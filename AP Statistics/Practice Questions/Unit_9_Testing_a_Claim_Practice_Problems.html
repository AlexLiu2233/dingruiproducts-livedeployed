一个随机样本含 150 个观测值，不到一个大总体的 10%，将用于检验 $H_0:p=0.30$ 对 $H_a:p\ne0.30$。关于单比例 $z$ 检验的条件，哪个陈述正确？

  2. (B) The sample must be random, and the large counts condition uses the hypothesized value: $150(0.30)=45$ and $150(0.70)=105$ are both at least 10.样本必须是随机的，且大计数条件使用假设值：$150(0.30)=45$ 和 $150(0.70)=105$ 都至少为 10。
  3. (C) The population of responses must be approximately normal, because the test statistic is a $z$ statistic.总体的回答必须近似正态，因为检验统计量是 $z$ 统计量。

Q 9第9题HARD较难3.6, 4.5 Interpreting a p-value3.6, 4.5 解释 p 值CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A student computes a p-value of $0.03$ for a significance test and writes: "There is a 3% chance that the null hypothesis is true." Which statement identifies the error?
一名学生算出某显著性检验的 p 值（p-value）为 $0.03$，并写道：“原假设（null hypothesis）为真的可能性是 3%。”哪个陈述指出了其中的错误？
  1. (A) There is no error: a p-value of $0.03$ means the null hypothesis has probability $0.03$.没有错误：p 值为 $0.03$ 就表示原假设的概率为 $0.03$。
  2. (B) The error is that the p-value should first be compared with the sample size to become a probability.错误在于 p 值要先与样本容量比较才能成为概率。
  3. (C) The error is that the p-value is the chance that the sample mean equals the null value, not the chance that the null is true.错误在于 p 值是样本均值等于原假设取值的可能性，而不是原假设为真的可能性。
  4. (D) The p-value is the probability of a result at least as extreme as the one observed, computed assuming the null hypothesis is true; it is not the probability that the null hypothesis is true.p 值是在假定原假设为真时算出的、得到至少与观察结果一样极端的结果的概率；它不是原假设为真的概率。
