A researcher randomly selects 80 of a school's 1,200 students and records each student's weekly study time. The sample mean is 6.2 hours. Which correctly identifies the observational unit and population parameter?研究者从某校 1,200 人中随机抽 80 人记录每周学习时间，样本均值为 6.2 小时。哪项正确识别观测单位和总体参数？

Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Each datum belongs to one student. The unknown population mean is the parameter; 6.2 hours is its sample estimate.每个数据属于一名学生。未知总体均值是参数，6.2 小时是样本估计。

Why the options attract (M 1)选项误区（M 1）
  1. Correct: unit and population summary are distinguished.正确区分单位及总体汇总。
  2. Calls the variable a unit and the statistic a parameter.把变量称为单位，把统计量称为参数。
  3. Calls the population a unit and its sample statistic a parameter.把总体称为单位，把样本统计量称为参数。
  4. 80 is the sample size, not a summary of study time.80 是样本量，不是学习时间的汇总。
A numerical estimate is not automatically a known population value. Attribute every summary to the group actually measured.数值估计不自动等于已知总体值；每个汇总都应归属于实际测量的群体。

Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Student IDs are labels, so they form a categorical variable (categorical variable) and a mean of them has no meaning. The question is about study time, so the mean must be of the weekly study hours, stated for the students measured.学号是标签，属于分类变量（categorical variable），对它求均值没有意义。问题关于学习时间，所以应对每周学习小时求均值，并说明所测的学生。

Why the options attract (M 1)选项误区（M 1）
  1. Correct: it names the measured quantity and its units.正确：指出了所测的量及其单位。
  2. Digits do not make a variable quantitative, and adding units to a mean of labels cannot give it meaning.用数字书写并不使变量成为定量变量，给标签的均值加单位也不能使它有意义。
  3. Resistance is about outliers, not variable type. A median of labels is just as meaningless as a mean of labels.稳健性针对离群值，与变量类型无关。标签的中位数与标签的均值一样没有意义。
  4. Rescaling labels does not turn them into measurements. A percent of the largest ID is still not hours of study.对标签重新缩放并不能使其成为测量值。占最大学号的百分比仍不是学习小时。
