Topic 3.1主题 3.1

Planning a Study规划一项研究

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define population, sample, census, parameter, and statistic, and identify each from a description.
  • Write a statistical (investigative) question that names the variable, the parameter and goal, and the scope.
  • Classify a study as a sample survey, an observational study (prospective or retrospective), or an experiment.
  • State the strongest conclusion each type of study can support.
  • 从情境描述中识别总体、样本、普查、参数与统计量。
  • 写出包含变量、参数与目标、适用范围三要素的统计（探究）问题。
  • 把研究分类为抽样调查、观察研究（前瞻性或回顾性）或实验。
  • 说明每类研究最多能支持的结论。

A statistical question is one that can be answered only by collecting data that vary. "How many siblings does Maya have?" has one answer; "What is the typical number of siblings among students at our school?" needs data from many students whose answers differ. The population is the whole group of individuals you want to learn about (size $N$). A sample is the part of the population you actually measure (size $n$). A census measures every member of the population; it is rarely practical because the population is large, measurement is costly or destructive (a crash-test of every car), or the population keeps changing.

统计问题是只能通过收集有差异的数据来回答的问题。“小玛雅有几个兄弟姐妹？”只有一个答案；“我校学生兄弟姐妹数的典型水平是多少？”则需要许多答案各异的学生数据。总体是你想了解的全部个体（大小 $N$）；样本是实际被测量的那部分（大小 $n$）；普查测量总体的每一个成员，但总体太大、测量代价高或具破坏性（碰撞测试每辆车）、总体不断变化时通常不可行。

A parameter is a number that describes the population (for example the population proportion $p$ or mean $\mu$). A statistic is a number computed from the sample (for example $\hat{p}$ or $\bar{x}$). A parameter is a fixed, usually unknown value; a statistic changes from sample to sample and is what we use to estimate the parameter.

参数是描述总体的数值（如总体比例 $p$、总体均值 $\mu$）；统计量是由样本计算的数值（如 $\hat{p}$、$\bar{x}$）。参数是固定且通常未知的值；统计量随样本而变，用来估计参数。

A good investigative question has three parts. (1) The variable or variables that will be measured. (2) The parameter and goal: estimate a value, or decide about a claim. (3) The scope: which population the answer is about, and whether a cause-and-effect claim could be justified. Writing these down before looking at any data keeps the study honest.

好的探究问题包含三部分：(1) 将要测量的变量；(2) 参数与目标：估计某个值，或对某个说法作判断；(3) 适用范围：答案针对哪个总体，以及能否得出因果结论。看数据之前先写下这三点，研究才站得住脚。

Sample surveyAsk a sample standard questions; impose nothingDescribes the population (if the sample is random); association only
Observational studyObserve and record; units choose their own groups. Prospective: follow units forward in time. Retrospective: look at past recordsAssociation, never cause and effect
ExperimentImpose a treatment on experimental unitsCause and effect, if treatments were randomly assigned

Worked Example 3.1: identify every component of a poll例题 3.1：识别一项民意调查的各个要素

A city has 48,000 registered voters. A polling firm selects 1,500 of them at random; 810 say they support a proposed transit tax.

  1. Population: all 48,000 registered voters ($N=48000$). Sample: the 1,500 voters polled ($n=1500$).
  2. Variable: support or not, categorical. Parameter: $p$, the proportion of all 48,000 registered voters who support the tax (unknown).
  3. Statistic: $\hat{p}=810/1500=0.54$.
  4. Type of study: a sample survey, which is observational: no treatment was imposed.
  5. In context: "54% of the 1,500 sampled voters support the tax, which is an estimate of the proportion of all registered voters in the city who support it." A census of 48,000 voters would be too slow and costly, and a different random sample would give a different $\hat{p}$.

某市有 48,000 名登记选民。民调机构随机抽取 1,500 人，其中 810 人支持一项交通税提案。

  1. 总体：全部 48,000 名登记选民（$N=48000$）。样本：被调查的 1,500 人（$n=1500$）。
  2. 变量：是否支持，分类变量。参数：$p$，全部登记选民中支持该税的比例（未知）。
  3. 统计量：$\hat{p}=810/1500=0.54$。
  4. 研究类型：抽样调查，属于观察研究：没有施加处理。
  5. 结合情境：“被抽中的 1,500 名选民中 54% 支持该税，这是全市登记选民支持比例的估计。”对 48,000 人普查太慢太贵，且换一个随机样本会得到不同的 $\hat{p}$。
Worked Example 3.2: classify three studies and state what each allows例题 3.2：分类三项研究并说明各自允许的结论

(a) Researchers select 600 adults at random from a city's residents and ask about coffee intake and hours of sleep. (b) Two hundred volunteers are randomly split into two groups; one group follows a low-sugar diet for 8 weeks and the other keeps its usual diet, and blood pressure is compared. (c) Hospital records of 150 people who had a heart attack are examined for their exercise habits over the previous ten years.

  1. (a) Sample survey (observational). Because the sample is random, results generalize to the city's adults, but coffee and sleep are only associated: no one was assigned to drink coffee.
  2. (b) Experiment. The researchers imposed the diets and used random assignment, so a difference in blood pressure larger than chance would produce can be attributed to the diet. The volunteers were not randomly selected, so the conclusion applies to people like these volunteers.
  3. (c) Retrospective observational study: units are identified now and their past is examined. It supports association only, and recall or record gaps can add error.

(a) 研究者从某市居民中随机抽取 600 名成年人，询问咖啡摄入量与睡眠时长。(b) 200 名志愿者被随机分成两组，一组 8 周内执行低糖饮食，另一组保持原有饮食，比较血压。(c) 查看 150 名心脏病发作者的病历，了解其此前十年的运动习惯。

  1. (a) 抽样调查（观察研究）。样本随机，结论可推广到该市成年人，但咖啡与睡眠只是有关联：没有人被指定喝咖啡。
  2. (b) 实验。研究者施加了饮食处理并使用随机分配，若血压差异大于随机性所能解释的范围，可归因于饮食。志愿者不是随机抽取的，所以结论适用于与这些志愿者相似的人。
  3. (c) 回顾性观察研究：先确定个体，再查看其过去。只支持关联，且回忆或记录缺失可能带来误差。
Worked Example 3.3: turn a vague idea into an investigative question例题 3.3：把模糊想法改写为探究问题

Rewrite "Are our school's students sleep-deprived?" as an investigative question.

  1. Variable: nightly sleep time in hours, quantitative.
  2. Parameter and goal: the population mean $\mu$; the goal is a decision about whether $\mu$ is below 8 hours (or, for estimation, a plausible range for $\mu$).
  3. Scope: all students enrolled at this school. No treatment is imposed, so the study is observational and no cause is claimed.
  4. Question: "Is the mean nightly sleep time of students at our school less than 8 hours?" Every part can be answered with data from a sample of students.

把“我校学生睡眠不足吗？”改写为探究问题。

  1. 变量：每晚睡眠时间（小时），定量变量。
  2. 参数与目标：总体均值 $\mu$；目标是判断 $\mu$ 是否低于 8 小时（若作估计，则给出 $\mu$ 的合理范围）。
  3. 适用范围：本校全体在校学生。没有施加处理，属于观察研究，不下因果结论。
  4. 问题：“我校学生每晚平均睡眠时间是否少于 8 小时？”每一部分都可用学生样本数据回答。
Exam tip考试提示

Name things in context. "The population is the students" is incomplete; "the population is all 1,200 students enrolled at Lincoln High" earns the point. When asked for a parameter, write its symbol and describe it in words with the population attached.

一定要结合情境。“总体是学生”不完整；“总体是林肯中学全部 1,200 名在读学生”才得分。被问到参数时，写出符号并用文字说明它描述的是哪个总体的什么量。

Common mistakes
  • Calling $\hat{p}$ a parameter because it came from a "real" poll. A summary of a sample is always a statistic.
  • Calling any study with a large sample an experiment. Only imposing a treatment makes it an experiment.
  • Writing "survey" as if it were a third category beside observational: a survey is a kind of observational study.
常见错误
  • 因为来自“真实”民调就把 $\hat{p}$ 叫参数。样本的概括数值永远是统计量。
  • 把样本大的研究都称为实验。只有施加处理才算实验。
  • 把“调查”当作与观察研究并列的第三类：调查是观察研究的一种。
AP FRQ-style response modelAP 简答题示范作答

Prompt. A random sample of 150 students at a school is surveyed. The 62 students who play an instrument average 7.4 hours of sleep; the other 88 average 7.9 hours. (a) Is this an experiment or an observational study? (b) Identify the population. (c) Can the researcher conclude that playing an instrument causes less sleep?

PointWhat earns it
(a)Observational: students chose whether to play an instrument and the researchers imposed no treatment.
(b)All students at that school (random sampling lets the result generalize to them).
(c)No. A confounding variable such as a heavy extracurricular schedule could lead to both playing an instrument and sleeping less. Without random assignment of the activity, only association is justified.

题目。某校随机抽取 150 名学生调查。62 名会乐器的学生平均睡 7.4 小时，其余 88 人平均睡 7.9 小时。(a) 这是实验还是观察研究？(b) 指出总体。(c) 能否得出“学乐器导致睡眠减少”？

得分点要点
(a)观察研究：学生自己选择是否学乐器，研究者没有施加处理。
(b)该校全体学生（随机抽样使结果可推广到他们）。
(c)不能。繁重的课外安排等混杂变量可能既使学生学乐器又使其睡得更少。活动不是随机分配的，只能得出关联。

1. A teacher computes the mean score of all 28 students in her class. Is 82.5 a parameter or a statistic?1. 一位老师计算了全班 28 名学生的平均分，82.5 是参数还是统计量？

A parameter, if the 28 students are the group of interest (a census of that class). It would be a statistic only if the 28 were a sample used to learn about a larger population.

若关心的就是这 28 名学生（对该班的普查），它是参数；只有当这 28 人是用来了解更大总体的样本时，它才是统计量。

Experiment: the researchers imposed the diets (treatments) on the mice. Random assignment means a large enough weight difference can be attributed to diet.

实验：研究者对小鼠施加了饮食处理。随机分配意味着足够大的增重差异可以归因于饮食。

3. Give two reasons a census may be impractical.3. 说出普查可能不可行的两个原因。

Any two of: the population is very large; measuring is costly or time-consuming; measuring destroys the unit (for example testing bulb lifetime); the population changes while you measure.

任意两点：总体太大；测量代价高或耗时；测量会破坏个体（如测灯泡寿命）；测量期间总体在变化。

Topic 3.2主题 3.2

Random Sampling and Data Collection随机抽样与数据收集

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe a procedure that selects a simple random sample (SRS) using a random number generator.
  • Distinguish simple random, stratified, cluster, and systematic sampling, and state when each is appropriate.
  • Compute the number selected from each stratum under proportional allocation.
  • Explain why random selection protects against bias in choosing units.
  • 用随机数生成器描述如何抽取简单随机样本（SRS）。
  • 区分简单随机、分层、整群和系统抽样，并说明各自适用的情形。
  • 在按比例分配时计算每层应抽取的个数。
  • 解释随机选择为什么能防止选取个体时产生偏差。

A sampling frame is the list of individuals from which the sample is drawn. A simple random sample (SRS) of size $n$ is chosen so that every group of $n$ individuals in the frame has the same chance of being the sample. Chance, not the researcher's judgment, picks the units. Samples chosen by judgment or ease are not random, however "representative" they feel.

抽样框是抽取样本所依据的个体名单。容量为 $n$ 的简单随机样本（SRS）要求框内每一组 $n$ 个个体成为样本的机会相同：由机会而非研究者的判断选出个体。凭判断或方便选出的样本不是随机样本，不管它看起来多么“有代表性”。

MethodProcedureUse it when
Simple random sampleLabel every individual; use random numbers to pick $n$ distinct labelsA complete frame exists and the population is fairly uniform
Stratified random sampleSplit into strata (groups alike on a relevant variable); take an SRS inside every stratum; combineGroups differ on the response and each must be represented; gives more precise estimates
Cluster sampleSplit into clusters that each look like the whole population; randomly choose some clusters; measure every individual in themNatural groups (classrooms, blocks) make a full list impractical or travel costly
Systematic random samplePick every $k$-th individual from the list after a random start between 1 and $k$, where $k\approx N/n$A list exists and is in no pattern that matches the interval $k$
方法步骤适用情形
简单随机样本给每个个体编号；用随机数选出 $n$ 个不同的编号有完整抽样框且总体较均匀
分层随机抽样按相关变量分成内部相似的层；在每一层内抽 SRS；再合并各组响应不同且都需被代表；估计更精确
整群抽样分成每个都像总体缩影的群；随机选若干群；测量所选群中的每一个个体自然分组（班级、街区）使完整名单难得或出行成本高
系统随机抽样在 1 到 $k$ 之间随机选起点，之后每隔 $k$ 个取一个，$k\approx N/n$有名单且名单排列没有与间隔 $k$ 吻合的周期

Stratified versus cluster. Strata are chosen so that individuals within a stratum are alike; you sample from all strata. Clusters are chosen so that each cluster is a mini-population; you sample some clusters and take everyone in them. Stratifying reduces variability; clustering saves cost. A convenience sample (whoever is easy to reach) is none of these.

分层与整群的区别。分层使层内个体相似，并从所有层中抽样；整群使每个群成为缩小的总体，只抽部分群并取其中全部个体。分层降低变异，整群节省成本。方便样本（谁容易找就取谁）不属于其中任何一种。

Also on the 2027 exam

The 2027 exam also separates sampling with replacement (a selected individual goes back and can be chosen again) from sampling without replacement (an individual can appear at most once). Most real sampling is without replacement. In an exam response, "ignore repeats until you have $n$ different numbers" shows you are sampling without replacement.

对应 2027 考试新增内容

2027 年考试还要求区分有放回抽样（被抽中的个体放回，可再次被抽到）与无放回抽样（每个个体最多出现一次）。现实中多为无放回。答题时写“忽略重复，直到得到 $n$ 个不同的数”，即表明采用无放回抽样。

Worked Example 3.4: a complete SRS procedure例题 3.4：完整的 SRS 步骤

Select an SRS of 80 students from a school list of 1,200 names.

  1. Label the students 0001 to 1200 using the alphabetical list.
  2. Randomize: use a random number generator to produce integers from 1 to 1,200, ignoring repeats, until 80 different numbers are obtained.
  3. Select the 80 students whose labels were generated.
  4. Why this earns credit: it names a labelling scheme, a chance mechanism, the range, how repeats are handled, and the stopping rule, so someone else could carry it out. "Randomly pick 80 students" names no mechanism and earns nothing.

从 1,200 人的学校名单中抽取 80 名学生的 SRS。

  1. 编号：按字母顺序名单给学生编号 0001 至 1200。
  2. 随机化：用随机数生成器产生 1 至 1,200 的整数，忽略重复，直到得到 80 个不同的数。
  3. 选取：取编号被生成的 80 名学生。
  4. 得分原因：写明了编号方式、随机机制、取值范围、重复的处理与停止规则，别人能照做。“随机抽 80 个学生”没写机制，不得分。
Worked Example 3.5: stratified allocation and why it helps例题 3.5：分层抽样的分配与优势

A high school has 520 freshmen, 480 sophomores, 500 juniors, and 500 seniors (2,000 students). The principal wants 200 students to estimate mean weekly homework hours, and expects hours to differ by grade.

  1. Strata: grade level, because homework load plausibly changes with grade.
  2. Proportional allocation: sample size in stratum = $n\times\dfrac{N_{\text{stratum}}}{N}$. Freshmen: $200\times 520/2000=52$; sophomores: $200\times 480/2000=48$; juniors: $200\times 500/2000=50$; seniors: $50$. Check: $52+48+50+50=200$.
  3. Selection: take a separate SRS of that size from each grade, then combine the four samples.
  4. Why: every grade is guaranteed to be represented in proportion, and because students within a grade are alike, the estimate varies less from sample to sample than an SRS of 200 from the whole school.

某高中有 520 名高一、480 名高二、500 名高三、500 名高四（共 2,000 人）。校长想抽 200 名学生估计每周作业平均时间，并预期不同年级作业时间不同。

  1. 分层：按年级，因为作业量大概率随年级变化。
  2. 按比例分配：某层样本量 $=n\times\dfrac{N_{\text{层}}}{N}$。高一：$200\times 520/2000=52$；高二：$200\times 480/2000=48$；高三：$200\times 500/2000=50$；高四：$50$。检验：$52+48+50+50=200$。
  3. 抽取：在每个年级分别抽相应数量的 SRS，再合并四个样本。
  4. 优势：保证每个年级按比例被代表；且年级内学生相似，估计值在样本之间的波动比从全校抽 200 人的 SRS 更小。
Worked Example 3.6: systematic and cluster samples, and why systematic is not an SRS例题 3.6：系统抽样、整群抽样，以及系统抽样为何不是 SRS

(a) From a list of 1,200 names choose 80 by a systematic random sample. (b) A district has 40 homerooms; 4 are chosen at random and every student in them is surveyed. (c) Explain why a systematic sample is not an SRS.

  1. (a) $k=1200/80=15$. Choose a random start from 1 to 15 (say 7), then take 7, 22, 37, ..., up to $7+15\times 79=1192$: exactly 80 names.
  2. (b) Cluster sample: homerooms are the clusters, and each homeroom mixes grades and backgrounds, so it resembles the district.
  3. (c) Take a list of 10 people and $n=5$. An SRS makes all $\binom{10}{5}=252$ possible samples equally likely. A systematic sample with $k=2$ can produce only two samples, {1,3,5,7,9} and {2,4,6,8,10}; people 1 and 2 can never be chosen together. Each person has a fair chance (1 in 2), but not every group does, so it is not an SRS. It also fails when the list has a pattern matching $k$: inspecting every 10th item from a machine with 10 heads always checks the same head.

(a) 从 1,200 人名单中用系统随机抽样抽 80 人。(b) 某学区有 40 个班级，随机选 4 个班并调查其中每名学生。(c) 解释为什么系统抽样不是 SRS。

  3. (c) 设名单有 10 人、$n=5$。SRS 使全部 $\binom{10}{5}=252$ 个可能样本机会均等；而 $k=2$ 的系统抽样只能产生两个样本 {1,3,5,7,9} 与 {2,4,6,8,10}，第 1 与第 2 号永远不会同时入选。每个人入选的机会都是 1/2，但并非每个组合机会都相同，所以不是 SRS。若名单有与 $k$ 吻合的周期也会失效：对 10 个机头的机器每隔 10 件检查一件，永远只检查同一个机头。

Common mistakes

Topic 3.3主题 3.3

Potential Problems with Sampling抽样中的潜在问题

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define bias and distinguish it from sampling variability.
  • Identify undercoverage, nonresponse, voluntary response, convenience, response (including question wording) and other biases from a description.
  • State the likely direction of a bias in context.
  • Explain why a larger sample reduces variability but does not remove bias.
  • 定义偏差，并区分偏差与抽样变异。
  • 从描述中识别覆盖不足、无应答、自愿回应、方便抽样、应答偏差（含问题措辞）等偏差。
  • 结合情境说明偏差的可能方向。
  • 解释为什么增大样本量能减小变异，却不能消除偏差。

Bias is a systematic error in the sampling method: it pushes the statistic the same way, sample after sample, so that the statistic is consistently too large or consistently too small. Sampling variability is the natural sample-to-sample change in a statistic even when the method is perfectly fair. Random selection removes the bias that comes from choosing the units, but many problems arise before or after the selection.

偏差是抽样方法中的系统性误差：它使统计量一次次朝同一方向偏移，长期偏大或长期偏小。抽样变异是即使方法完全公平，统计量在不同样本之间也会自然变化。随机选择消除了选取个体时的偏差，但许多问题发生在选择之前或之后。

Undercoverage versus nonresponse

If the people were never on the frame, it is undercoverage. If they were selected but did not answer, it is nonresponse. If the reply is on the record but untrue or pushed, it is response bias.

覆盖不足与无应答

从未进入抽样框是覆盖不足；被选中但没有回答是无应答；答案虽已记录却不真实或被引导是应答偏差。

Simulate it yourself: open the sampling bias simulator. Compare a fair random sample with a sample drawn from only one group, and raise $n$: the fair method centers on the truth with less spread, while the biased one tightens around the wrong value.

The revised course tests "bias is not variability" directly: say which bias, who is missing or influenced, and the direction of the error. Small sample size alone is never a bias; it causes high variability. Writing only "the sample is biased" earns no credit.

Worked Example 3.7: a famous failure: the 1936 Literary Digest poll例题 3.7：著名的失败：1936 年《文学文摘》民调

In 1936 the Literary Digest mailed about 10 million ballots to people drawn from telephone directories, automobile registrations, and its own subscribers, and received about 2.4 million back. It predicted that Alf Landon would win with 57% of the vote (Franklin Roosevelt 43%). Roosevelt won with about 61% of the popular vote.

  1. Size of the miss: Roosevelt was predicted at 43% and received about 61%, an error of about $61-43=18$ percentage points.
  2. Undercoverage: in 1936 telephones, cars and magazine subscriptions were more common among wealthier households, so poorer voters were underrepresented in the frame.
  3. Nonresponse: only about 24% of the ballots were returned, and those who replied were not typical of those who were sent one.
  4. Direction: support for Roosevelt was underestimated.
  5. Size does not fix bias: for a truly random sample of 2.4 million with $p\approx0.61$, the standard deviation of $\hat{p}$ is $\sqrt{0.61(0.39)/2{,}400{,}000}\approx 0.0003$, about 0.03 percentage points. The huge sample was extremely precise about the wrong quantity.

1936 年《文学文摘》向从电话簿、汽车登记簿及其订户中抽取的人邮寄了约 1,000 万张选票，回收约 240 万张。它预测兰登将以 57% 获胜（罗斯福 43%），而罗斯福实际获得约 61% 的普选票。

  1. 偏差大小：预测罗斯福 43%，实际约 61%，误差约 $61-43=18$ 个百分点。
  2. 覆盖不足：1936 年电话、汽车和杂志订阅在较富裕家庭中更常见，较贫穷选民在抽样框中代表性不足。
  3. 无应答：仅约 24% 的选票被寄回，回复者并不能代表收到选票的人。
  4. 方向：罗斯福的支持率被低估。
  5. 样本量无法修正偏差：即使是真正随机的 240 万样本，$p\approx0.61$ 时 $\hat{p}$ 的标准差为 $\sqrt{0.61(0.39)/2{,}400{,}000}\approx 0.0003$，约 0.03 个百分点。巨大的样本对错误的量非常精确。
Worked Example 3.8: undercoverage: the bias does not shrink with $n$例题 3.8：覆盖不足：偏差不会随 $n$ 变小

A school has 1,000 students: 750 non-athletes who average 6.0 hours of homework a week, and 250 athletes who average 4.0 hours. A survey frame accidentally omits the athletes.

  1. Parameter: $\mu=(750\times 6.0+250\times 4.0)/1000=5.5$ hours.
  2. What the survey estimates: only the non-athletes, so the sample means center on $6.0$, not $5.5$.
  3. Bias: $6.0-5.5=+0.5$ hours, an overestimate, whether $n$ is 50 or 500, because the athletes can never be chosen.
  4. Conclusion in context: the survey tends to overestimate mean weekly homework for all students at the school by about 0.5 hours, because athletes, who do less homework, were undercovered.

某校有 1,000 名学生：750 名非运动员平均每周作业 6.0 小时，250 名运动员平均 4.0 小时。调查的抽样框不慎漏掉了运动员。

  1. 参数：$\mu=(750\times 6.0+250\times 4.0)/1000=5.5$ 小时。
  2. 调查实际估计的：只有非运动员，所以样本均值围绕 $6.0$，而非 $5.5$。
  3. 偏差：$6.0-5.5=+0.5$ 小时，高估；无论 $n$ 是 50 还是 500，因为运动员永远不会被选中。
  4. 结合情境的结论：该调查倾向于把全校学生每周平均作业时间高估约 0.5 小时，因为作业较少的运动员被覆盖不足。
Worked Example 3.9: diagnose bias and state its direction例题 3.9：判断偏差类型并说明方向

(a) A local radio show asks listeners to call in about a new bike-lane plan. (b) A mailed questionnaire is sent to 2,000 residents and 340 are returned. (c) A pollster asks, "Given the shocking rise in crime, do you support more police patrols?"

  1. (a) Voluntary response bias: listeners with strong feelings call, so the opinions of the many with mild views are missing. If drivers who dislike the plan are the angriest, opposition is overestimated.
  2. (b) Nonresponse bias: the response rate is $340/2000=0.17$, or 17%, so 83% of those selected are missing and may differ from responders. State who is missing before stating a direction.
  3. (c) Response bias from leading wording: "shocking rise in crime" pushes answers toward support, so support for patrols is overestimated.

  3. (c) 诱导性措辞造成的应答偏差：“犯罪率惊人上升”把回答推向支持，对巡逻的支持被高估。
Exam tip考试提示

Use the three-part sentence: name the bias, say who is missing or influenced and why they differ, and state the direction ("the sample mean is likely larger than the population mean"). A bigger sample is never the fix for bias; a better method is (random selection from a complete frame, follow-ups to reduce nonresponse, neutral wording).

用三句式：点名偏差，说明谁缺失或受影响、为何不同，指出方向（“样本均值很可能大于总体均值”）。增大样本不是纠正偏差的办法，更好的方法才是（从完整抽样框随机选取、追访减少无应答、中性措辞）。

Common mistakes
  • Answering "the sample is too small" when asked about bias. Small samples give variability, not bias.
  • Calling any non-random sample "undercoverage". Check whether the people were left off the frame (undercoverage), were on it but did not answer (nonresponse), or chose to answer (voluntary response).
  • Saying random selection removes all bias. It does nothing for nonresponse, wording, or a defective frame.
常见错误
  • 被问偏差时回答“样本太小”。样本小导致变异，不是偏差。
  • 把所有非随机样本都叫“覆盖不足”。要分清：被漏在框外（覆盖不足）、在框内但没回答（无应答）、自己选择回答（自愿回应）。
  • 说随机选择能消除所有偏差。它对无应答、措辞和有缺陷的抽样框无能为力。

  • 说明良好实验的四个要素：比较、随机分配、重复与直接控制。
  • 解释对照组、安慰剂、安慰剂效应以及单盲与双盲的作用。

An experiment deliberately imposes a treatment on experimental units (called subjects or participants when they are people) and measures a response variable. The explanatory variable (or factor) is the variable whose levels the researcher sets. A treatment is one specific level, or one combination of levels when there are several factors. With factors having $a$ and $b$ levels there are $a\times b$ treatments.

实验是有意对实验单位（人则称受试者或参与者）施加处理，并测量响应变量。解释变量（或因素）是研究者设定其水平的变量。处理是某一个具体水平，多个因素时则是各水平的一个组合。因素分别有 $a$ 与 $b$ 个水平时，共有 $a\times b$ 个处理。

A confounding variable is related to both the explanatory and response variables in a way that makes it impossible to separate their effects: it offers a rival explanation for the observed association. An extraneous variable is any other variable that might affect the response but is not being studied. In an observational study, units choose their own groups, so confounding is nearly always possible. Real example: observational studies of women in the 1990s found that those taking hormone therapy had less heart disease, but healthier, wealthier women were more likely to take it. A large randomized trial later found no such protective effect, so the earlier link was at least partly confounding.

混杂变量同时与解释变量和响应变量相关，使人无法分开它们各自的效应：它为观察到的关联提供了另一种解释。外来变量是可能影响响应但并非研究对象的其他变量。观察研究中个体自己选择所在组，所以几乎总可能有混杂。真实例子：1990 年代的观察研究发现服用激素疗法的女性心脏病更少，但更健康、更富裕的女性更可能服用；后来一项大型随机试验并未发现这种保护作用，说明此前的关联至少部分来自混杂。

A control group gives a baseline for comparison; it may receive no treatment, a standard treatment, or a placebo, an inactive treatment that looks like the real one. The placebo effect is the change people show simply because they believe they are being treated. Blinding keeps expectations from influencing results: in a single-blind experiment either the subjects or the people who measure the response do not know the treatments; in a double-blind experiment neither group knows. In a double-blind drug trial an independent person holds the key to which pill is which, and the pills look identical.

Worked Example 3.10: identify every component of an experiment例题 3.10：识别实验的全部组成部分

A researcher wants to compare three fertilizers (F1, F2, F3) under two watering levels (low, high) on tomato yield. She has 48 identical plots and applies each combination to 8 plots, with assignment by chance.

  1. Experimental units: the 48 plots. Response variable: tomato yield (for example kg per plot).
  2. Explanatory variables (factors): fertilizer (3 levels) and watering (2 levels).
  3. Treatments: $3\times 2=6$ combinations, such as F1 with low water.
  4. Replication: $48/6=8$ plots per treatment.
  5. Elements: comparison among treatments; random assignment of plots to treatments; replication; direct control by using identical plots, same tomato variety, and same planting date.

研究者想比较三种肥料（F1、F2、F3）在两种浇水水平（低、高）下对番茄产量的影响。她有 48 块相同的地，把每种组合随机分配到 8 块地上。

  1. 实验单位：48 块地。响应变量：番茄产量（如每块地的千克数）。
  2. 解释变量（因素）：肥料（3 个水平）与浇水（2 个水平）。
  3. 处理：$3\times 2=6$ 种组合，如 F1 加低水量。
  4. 重复：每个处理 $48/6=8$ 块地。
  5. 要素：处理之间的比较；地块随机分配到处理；重复；通过使用相同地块、同一番茄品种和相同播种日期实现直接控制。
Worked Example 3.11: confounding in a badly designed experiment例题 3.11：设计糟糕的实验中的混杂

A teacher tests a new review game. She uses the game with her 9:00 class and the usual review with her 14:00 class, then compares unit-test means: 84.2 for the game class and 79.6 for the usual class.

  1. Difference in sample means: $84.2-79.6=4.6$ points.
  2. Is it an experiment? She imposed the treatment, so yes, but she did not randomly assign students, so it lacks that element.
  3. Confounding: time of day. Students in the morning class may be more alert, or the two classes may differ in prior ability. Both are tied to the class and to the test score, so the 4.6 points cannot be attributed to the game.
  4. Fix: pool all students, randomly assign half to each review method, and have both groups taught at the same time or by the same procedure.

一位老师测试新的复习游戏。她在 9:00 的班使用游戏，在 14:00 的班使用常规复习，然后比较单元测验平均分：游戏班 84.2，常规班 79.6。

  1. 样本均值之差：$84.2-79.6=4.6$ 分。
  2. 这是实验吗？她施加了处理，所以是；但她没有随机分配学生，缺少这一要素。
  3. 混杂：上课时间。上午班学生可能更清醒，或两个班原有水平不同。这些因素既与班级相关又与测验成绩相关，所以不能把这 4.6 分归因于游戏。
  4. 改进：把全体学生合并，随机分配一半用一种复习方法，并让两组在相同时间或以相同流程授课。
Worked Example 3.12: placebo, blinding, and the size of the placebo effect例题 3.12：安慰剂、盲法与安慰剂效应的大小

One hundred twenty headache patients are randomly assigned to three equal groups of 40: a new drug, a placebo pill, or no pill. Mean pain reduction on a 10-point scale is 4.1 for the drug, 2.6 for the placebo, and 1.0 for no pill.

  1. Placebo effect: placebo mean minus no-pill mean $=2.6-1.0=1.6$ points, the improvement from believing one is treated.
  2. Drug effect beyond the placebo: $4.1-2.6=1.5$ points. The right comparison for the drug is the placebo group, because both groups expect to be helped.
  3. Total improvement vs nothing: $4.1-1.0=3.1$ points, which is $1.6+1.5$.
  4. Blinding: the pills must look identical, and the staff who record pain ratings should not know which pill each patient received (double-blind), otherwise expectations could bias the ratings.
  5. Because the samples differ by chance, each difference is only an estimate; whether 1.5 points is too large to be chance is the question of statistical significance in Topic 3.6.

120 名头痛患者被随机分成三个各 40 人的组：新药、安慰剂药片或不服药。10 分制疼痛平均减轻：新药 4.1，安慰剂 2.6，不服药 1.0。

  1. 安慰剂效应：安慰剂组均值减不服药组均值 $=2.6-1.0=1.6$ 分，即相信自己受治疗所带来的改善。
  2. 新药超出安慰剂的效应：$4.1-2.6=1.5$ 分。药物应与安慰剂组比较，因为两组都期待得到帮助。
  3. 相对不处理的总改善：$4.1-1.0=3.1$ 分，等于 $1.6+1.5$。
  4. 盲法：药片外观必须相同，记录疼痛评分的工作人员也不应知道患者服的是哪种药（双盲），否则预期会使评分产生偏差。
  5. 由于样本之间有随机差异，每个差值只是估计；1.5 分是否大到不能用随机性解释，就是主题 3.6 中的统计显著性问题。

Real context. The Physicians' Health Study randomly assigned about 22,000 male physicians to take aspirin or an identical placebo every other day, double-blind, and followed them for years. Random assignment let researchers attribute the lower rate of heart attacks in the aspirin group to the aspirin, not to the doctors' lifestyles.

真实情境。“医生健康研究”把约 22,000 名男性医生随机分配为隔天服用阿司匹林或外观相同的安慰剂，双盲，并追踪多年。随机分配使研究者能把阿司匹林组较低的心脏病发作率归因于阿司匹林，而不是医生的生活方式。

Exam tip考试提示

When asked "why random assignment?" write: it tends to create groups that are similar in every respect, including variables we did not measure, so differences in response can be attributed to the treatment. "It makes the experiment fair" or "it removes bias" is too vague. For a confounding answer, name the variable and explain its link to both the explanatory and the response variable.

被问“为何要随机分配”时写：它倾向于使各组在所有方面（包括未测量的变量）都相似，因此响应的差异可归因于处理。“它使实验公平”或“消除偏差”太笼统。回答混杂时，要点出变量，并说明它与解释变量和响应变量两者的联系。

Common mistakes
  • Confusing treatment with explanatory variable. Treatments are the specific levels or level combinations that units receive.
  • Saying a placebo "does nothing". It produces the placebo effect, which is exactly why it is used.
  • Calling a variable confounding without linking it to both the explanatory and response variables.
常见错误
  • 混淆处理与解释变量。处理是单位实际接受的具体水平或水平组合。
  • 说安慰剂“什么也不做”。它会产生安慰剂效应，这正是使用它的原因。
  • 称某变量为混杂变量却没说明它与解释变量和响应变量的关系。

Block versus stratify

Blocking is to experiments what stratifying is to sampling: both group similar units to reduce variability. But stratification is followed by random selection within each stratum, while blocking is followed by random assignment within each block. Blocking is used when you cannot, or would not want to, hold the variable fixed; if you can hold it fixed, that is direct control.

区组与分层

区组之于实验，如同分层之于抽样：都是把相似单位分组以减少变异。但分层之后在每层内是随机抽取，区组之后在每个区组内是随机分配。当你无法或不愿将该变量固定时才用区组；若能固定，那就是直接控制。

Group sizes in a completely randomized design need not be equal, and you must be able to say why a design is used, not only name it.

Worked Example 3.13: a completely randomized design with a random procedure例题 3.13：含随机步骤的完全随机设计

Twenty-four volunteer students will compare a spaced-practice app with flashcards for learning 50 vocabulary words; the response is the score on a test one week later.

  1. Identify: units are the 24 students; factor is study method (app or flashcards); response is test score.
  2. Label the students 1 to 24.
  3. Randomize: use a random number generator to select 12 different numbers from 1 to 24 (ignore repeats). These students use the app; the other 12 use flashcards.
  4. Control: same words, same study time, same test for everyone.

Worked Example 3.14: a randomized block design and why blocking helps例题 3.14：随机区组设计及区组化的好处

Two fertilizers, A and B, are tested on 8 plots: 4 sunny and 4 shaded. Yields (kg) are A in sunny: 52, 55; B in sunny: 58, 60; A in shade: 31, 34; B in shade: 36, 38.

  1. Block by sunlight, a variable that strongly affects yield. Within each block, randomly assign 2 plots to A and 2 to B (this is what produced the layout above).
  2. Without blocking: all 8 yields have mean $(52+55+58+60+31+34+36+38)/8=45.5$ and standard deviation about $11.9$. A fertilizer difference of 5 kg is small next to that spread, so it is hidden.
  3. With blocking: sunny block means: A $=53.5$, B $=59.0$, difference $5.5$. Shaded block means: A $=32.5$, B $=37.0$, difference $4.5$. Within each block B beats A by a consistent amount; the average difference is $(5.5+4.5)/2=5.0$ kg.
  4. Conclusion: blocking removes sunlight differences from the comparison, so the fertilizer effect is estimated from similar plots. Random assignment inside each block still protects against other confounders.

在 8 块地上测试肥料 A 与 B：4 块向阳、4 块背阴。产量（千克）：向阳 A：52、55；向阳 B：58、60；背阴 A：31、34；背阴 B：36、38。

  1. 按光照分区组，光照强烈影响产量。每个区组内随机分配 2 块用 A、2 块用 B（得到上面的布局）。
  2. 不分区组：8 个产量的均值为 $(52+55+58+60+31+34+36+38)/8=45.5$，标准差约 $11.9$。5 千克的肥料差异相比这个离散程度很小，被掩盖了。
  3. 分区组：向阳区组均值：A $=53.5$，B $=59.0$，差 $5.5$；背阴区组均值：A $=32.5$，B $=37.0$，差 $4.5$。每个区组内 B 都比 A 高出相近的量；平均差为 $(5.5+4.5)/2=5.0$ 千克。
  4. 结论：区组化把光照差异从比较中去掉，肥料效应由相似地块估计；每个区组内的随机分配仍可防范其他混杂因素。
Worked Example 3.15: matched pairs: both treatments on every subject例题 3.15：配对设计：每名受试者接受两种处理

Eight students each complete a reaction-time task once without caffeine and once after a caffeinated drink. A coin flip decides each student's order. Times in milliseconds (no caffeine, caffeine): (312, 298), (287, 281), (335, 320), (301, 305), (296, 285), (318, 301), (290, 288), (324, 309).

  1. Design: matched pairs (each student is a pair with themselves). Randomizing the order prevents practice or fatigue from always favoring the second task.
  2. Differences (no caffeine minus caffeine): $14,\,6,\,15,\,-4,\,11,\,17,\,2,\,15$.
  3. Mean difference: $\bar{x}_d=76/8=9.5$ ms. Standard deviation of the differences: $s_d\approx 7.46$ ms.
  4. In context: on average these eight students responded 9.5 ms faster after caffeine. Analysis uses the differences, because the pairing removes person-to-person speed differences.

Topic 3.6主题 3.6

Inference and Experiments推断与实验

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Explain what it means for experimental results to be statistically significant.
  • State what random selection allows (generalization) and what random assignment allows (cause and effect).
  • Determine the scope of inference for a described study.
  • Distinguish statistical significance from practical importance.
  • 解释实验结果“统计显著”的含义。
  • 说明随机选择允许什么（推广）、随机分配允许什么（因果）。
  • 判断一项研究的推断范围。
  • 区分统计显著与实际重要。

Two different random processes answer two different questions. Random selection of units from a population allows the results to be generalized to that population. Random assignment of treatments to units allows a cause-and-effect conclusion about the treatment. Experiments often use volunteers, so the causal conclusion holds for units like the volunteers, not necessarily for everyone.

两个不同的随机过程回答两个不同的问题。从总体中随机选择个体，结果可以推广到该总体；对个体随机分配处理，可以对该处理得出因果结论。实验常常使用志愿者，所以因果结论适用于与志愿者相似的个体，不一定适用于所有人。

Statistical significance (preview). In a randomized experiment, the treatment groups will almost never have exactly equal means even when the treatment does nothing, because random assignment places units in groups by chance. A difference is statistically significant when it is larger than would be expected from the chance of random assignment alone, so chance is an unconvincing explanation. The question to ask is: if the treatment had no effect, how often would random assignment alone produce a difference at least this large? A small probability gives evidence of a treatment effect. Later units make this precise with $P$-values.

统计显著（预览）。在随机实验中，即使处理毫无作用，各处理组的均值也几乎不会恰好相等，因为随机分配是靠机会把单位放入各组的。若差异大于仅由随机分配的机会所能预期的程度，则称其统计显著，此时“只是机会”这个解释缺乏说服力。要问的问题是：如果处理没有作用，仅靠随机分配产生至少这么大差异的频率有多高？概率很小就为处理效应提供了证据。后续单元将用 $P$ 值把它精确化。

Also on the 2027 exam

The revised exam repeats this scope table and the sentence "random selection permits generalization; random assignment permits cause and effect." It says never to use the two terms interchangeably, and it expects conclusions to be limited to the population actually sampled or to units like those studied. A statistically significant result from a randomized experiment is evidence for a cause-and-effect conclusion, not for a population-wide claim.

Worked Example 3.16: how often would chance alone give this difference?例题 3.16：仅凭机会，多久会出现这样的差异？

Ten seedlings are randomly assigned, 5 to a new soil mix (T) and 5 to the usual mix (C). Heights after 4 weeks (cm): T: 14, 17, 15, 18, 16; C: 12, 13, 15, 11, 14.

  1. Observed difference: $\bar{x}_T=80/5=16$, $\bar{x}_C=65/5=13$, difference $=3$ cm.
  2. Suppose the soil does nothing. Then each seedling's height would be the same no matter which group it joined, and only the random assignment decides who is in T. There are $\binom{10}{5}=252$ equally likely ways to choose which 5 seedlings form group T.
  3. Count the assignments that give a difference of at least 3 cm: listing all 252 splits shows only $5$ of them do (the observed split is one of them).
  4. Probability: $5/252\approx 0.020$, about 2%. Random assignment alone would give a difference this large only about 2 times in 100.
  5. Conclusion: that is small, so the result is statistically significant at the 5% level: there is convincing evidence that the new mix increases height, for seedlings like these. Because assignment was random, the conclusion is causal; because these seedlings were not randomly selected from all seedlings, it does not generalize beyond similar seedlings.

10 株幼苗被随机分配，5 株用新土壤配方（T），5 株用常规配方（C）。4 周后高度（厘米）：T：14、17、15、18、16；C：12、13、15、11、14。

  1. 观察到的差：$\bar{x}_T=80/5=16$，$\bar{x}_C=65/5=13$，差 $=3$ 厘米。
  2. 假设土壤没有作用。则每株幼苗的高度与它在哪一组无关，只是随机分配决定了谁在 T 组。选出哪 5 株组成 T 组共有 $\binom{10}{5}=252$ 种等可能方式。
  3. 数出差至少为 3 厘米的分配：列出全部 252 种分法后发现只有 $5$ 种（观察到的分法是其中之一）。
  4. 概率：$5/252\approx 0.020$，约 2%。仅凭随机分配，每 100 次大约只有 2 次出现这么大的差。
  5. 结论：这个概率很小，因此结果在 5% 水平上统计显著：有令人信服的证据表明新配方使与这些幼苗相似的幼苗长得更高。因为是随机分配，结论是因果的；因为这些幼苗不是从所有幼苗中随机抽取的，结论不能推广到相似幼苗之外。
Worked Example 3.17: scope of inference for four studies例题 3.17：四项研究的推断范围

For each, state what can be concluded. (a) A random sample of 500 voters is surveyed: those who read a daily newspaper are more likely to vote. (b) 200 volunteers are randomly assigned to two diets and the low-sugar group shows a significantly larger weight loss. (c) From 1,000 randomly chosen adults in a city, 400 are randomly assigned to a mindfulness program; the program group scores significantly lower on a stress scale. (d) Students in a school club are compared with students not in the club.

  1. (a) Random selection, no random assignment: generalize the association to all voters in the population sampled; no cause and effect.
  2. (b) Random assignment, volunteers: the diet caused the extra weight loss for people like these volunteers; do not generalize to all people.
  3. (c) Both: cause and effect, generalized to the city's adults (subject to nonresponse and compliance, which the researchers must address).
  4. (d) Neither: association only, for students like those studied.

分别说明能得出什么结论。(a) 随机抽取 500 名选民调查：每天读报的人更可能投票。(b) 200 名志愿者被随机分配到两种饮食，低糖组体重减轻显著更多。(c) 从某市随机抽出 1,000 名成年人，其中 400 人被随机分配参加正念项目；项目组的压力量表得分显著更低。(d) 比较学校社团成员与非成员的学生。

  1. (a) 有随机抽样、无随机分配：把关联推广到被抽样的全体选民；不能下因果结论。
  2. (b) 有随机分配、志愿者：饮食导致了与这些志愿者相似的人多减重；不能推广到所有人。
  3. (c) 两者都有：因果结论，并可推广到该市成年人（研究者还须处理无应答和依从性问题）。
  4. (d) 两者都没有：只有关联，适用于与被研究学生相似的学生。

Practical importance. Statistical significance only says the difference is unlikely to be due to chance. A very large experiment can find a tiny, unimportant difference significant; a small experiment can miss a large, important one. Always state the size of the difference and whether it matters in context.

实际重要性。统计显著只说明差异不太可能由机会造成。很大的实验可能把微小而不重要的差异判为显著；很小的实验也可能错过巨大而重要的差异。务必说明差异的大小及其在情境中是否重要。

Try the sampling bias simulator again with this question in mind: random selection fixes who is measured, but it cannot assign a treatment, so it never turns an association into a cause.

带着这个问题再试试抽样偏差模拟器：随机选择决定谁被测量，但它不能分配处理，因此永远不能把关联变成因果。

写两句分开的话：一句关于因果（由随机分配回答），一句关于总体（由个体如何获得回答）。措辞要与证据相称：“有令人信服的证据表明该方法使与这些志愿者相似的学生成绩更高。”切勿说显著结果“证明”了什么。

Common mistakes
  • Saying random sampling allows cause and effect, or random assignment allows generalization to a population.
  • Concluding cause from a survey, however large and random.
  • Treating a statistically significant difference as automatically large or important.
常见错误
  • 说随机抽样允许因果结论，或说随机分配允许推广到总体。

  • 把统计显著的差异自动当作很大或很重要。

(a)区组：低、中、高收入学校。每个 6 所学校的区组内，给学校编号 1 至 6，用随机数生成器（忽略重复）选出 3 所实施项目，另 3 所作对照。响应：全年出勤率；所有学校测量方式相同。
(b)社区收入可能影响出勤，在同一收入水平内比较项目校与非项目校可去掉这一变异，使比较更精确。
(c)可得：有令人信服的证据表明项目使与这 18 所相似的学校出勤率提高（因果）。不可得：推广到全国所有学校，因为这 18 所学校不是随机抽取的。
(d)无应答偏差（65% 未回复）。态度强烈（也许是赞成）的家长更可能回复，所以对项目的支持很可能被高估。
