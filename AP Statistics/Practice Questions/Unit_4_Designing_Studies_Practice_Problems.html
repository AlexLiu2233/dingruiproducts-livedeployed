Q 9第9题MEDIUM中等1.11 Random sampling methods1.11 随机抽样方法CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A list has $N=20n$ names, where $n$ is the desired sample size. A researcher takes a systematic random sample: pick a random start from 1 to $k$, where $k=N/n$, then take every $k$th name. Which statement is correct?
一份名单有 $N=20n$ 个名字，其中 $n$ 是所需样本量。研究者采用系统随机抽样：在 1 到 $k$ 之间随机选起点（$k=N/n$），然后每隔 $k$ 个取一个名字。哪项陈述正确？
  1. (A) $k=20$, and all $\binom{20n}{n}$ possible samples are equally likely, so the sample is an SRS.$k=20$，全部 $\binom{20n}{n}$ 个可能的样本机会均等，所以是简单随机样本。
  2. (B) $k=n$, and exactly $n$ different samples are possible.$k=n$，恰好有 $n$ 个不同的样本可能出现。
  3. (C) $k=20$, and exactly $20n$ different samples are possible, one for each name as a start.$k=20$，恰好有 $20n$ 个不同的样本可能出现，每个名字对应一个起点。
  4. (D) $k=20$, and exactly 20 different samples are possible, so it is not an SRS.$k=20$，恰好有 20 个不同的样本可能出现，所以不是简单随机样本。

Q 21第21题HARD较难1.13 Experimental designs1.13 实验设计CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A researcher has $2n$ subjects ($n\ge2$) and two treatments. Design 1 is completely randomized with $n$ subjects in each group. Design 2 forms $n$ pairs and flips a coin in each pair to decide which member gets treatment A. Without computing numbers for a specific $n$, which statement compares the numbers of different possible random assignments?
研究者有 $2n$ 名受试者（$n\ge2$）和两种处理。设计 1 是完全随机设计，每组 $n$ 人。设计 2 组成 $n$ 对，每对抛一次硬币决定谁接受处理 A。不针对具体的 $n$ 做计算，哪项陈述正确地比较了两种设计可能的随机分配数？
  1. (A) Design 1 has more: it has $\binom{2n}{n}$ possible assignments, Design 2 has $2^n$, and every assignment of Design 2 is also an assignment of Design 1.设计 1 更多：它有 $\binom{2n}{n}$ 种可能的分配，设计 2 有 $2^n$ 种，而设计 2 的每种分配也都是设计 1 的一种分配。
  2. (B) Design 2 has more: $2^n$ grows faster than $\binom{2n}{n}$.设计 2 更多：$2^n$ 比 $\binom{2n}{n}$ 增长得更快。
  3. (C) They have equal numbers, because both put $n$ subjects on each treatment.两者数目相等，因为两者都让每种处理有 $n$ 名受试者。
  4. (D) Design 2 has more: it has $2n$ possible assignments, Design 1 has $n^2$.设计 2 更多：它有 $2n$ 种可能的分配，设计 1 有 $n^2$ 种。
