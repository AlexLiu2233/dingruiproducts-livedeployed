Human Anatomy and Physiology人体解剖与生理学

The human body is built from cells organized into tissues, organs, and organ systems, each system specialized for a distinct function yet intimately connected with all others. This guide covers the seven core systems: the digestive system (nutrient uptake), the circulatory system (transport), the respiratory system (gas exchange), the nervous system (control and signaling), the immune system (defense), and an overview of the endocrine and reproductive systems (chemical control and continuation of the species). Every section maps to your curriculum, from NGSS systems-model expectations through Ontario SBI3U Strand E and the rich system-by-system depth of BC Anatomy and Physiology 12.人体由细胞组成，细胞构成组织（tissue，组织），组织构成器官（organ，器官），器官组成系统（system，系统），每个系统各有专司，却又紧密相连。本指南涵盖七大核心系统：消化系统（digestive system，消化系统）负责营养摄取，循环系统（circulatory system，循环系统）负责物质运输，呼吸系统（respiratory system，呼吸系统）负责气体交换，神经系统（nervous system，神经系统）负责控制与信号传递，免疫系统（immune system，免疫系统）负责防御，以及内分泌系统（endocrine system，内分泌系统）与生殖系统概述。所有内容均与各大纲对应，从 NGSS 系统模型要求到安大略 SBI3U E 单元，再到 BC 解剖与生理 12 的系统逐一深入。

Levels of Organization: From Cell to Organism生命组织层次：从细胞到生物体

Learning objectives学习目标

  • 说出四种动物组织类型，并说明各自的功能。
  • 把一个结构归类为细胞、组织、器官或器官系统，并说明理由。

The structural hierarchy underlying all human anatomy (NGSS HS-LS1-2; BC Life Sciences 11 Content; SBI3U Big Idea).人体解剖学的结构层次（NGSS HS-LS1-2；BC Life Sciences 11 内容；SBI3U 大观念）。
  1. Cell: the basic structural and functional unit of all life. In the human body, there are over 200 specialized cell types (e.g. red blood cells, neurons, muscle cells).细胞：一切生命的基本结构与功能单位。人体有超过 200 种特化细胞类型（如红细胞、神经元、肌肉细胞）。
  2. Tissue: a group of similar cells performing the same function. Four tissue types in animals: epithelial (covering/lining), connective (support/binding), muscle (contraction), nervous (signaling).组织：执行相同功能的同类细胞群。动物有四种组织类型：上皮组织（覆盖/内衬）、结缔组织（支撑/连接）、肌肉组织（收缩）、神经组织（信号传导）。
  3. Organ: two or more tissue types working together for a common function. E.g. the stomach contains epithelial, muscle, connective, and nervous tissue.器官：两种或多种组织共同完成某种功能。如胃含有上皮、肌肉、结缔和神经组织。
  4. Organ system: organs working together for a broad function. E.g. the digestive system (stomach + intestines + liver + pancreas) processes food and absorbs nutrients.器官系统：器官共同完成某种宏观功能。如消化系统（胃 + 肠 + 肝脏 + 胰腺）处理食物并吸收营养。
  5. Organism: the complete individual, whose organ systems interact to maintain life and homeostasis.生物体：完整的个体，其器官系统相互作用以维持生命和稳态。

The eight human organ systems and their primary function (BC A&P 12 Content): digestive (nutrient uptake), cardiovascular/circulatory (transport), respiratory (gas exchange), nervous (control), endocrine (chemical signaling), immune/lymphatic (defense), urinary (waste removal), reproductive (continuation of the species).人体八大器官系统及其主要功能（BC 解剖与生理 12 内容）：消化系统（营养摄取）、心血管/循环系统（物质运输）、呼吸系统（气体交换）、神经系统（控制）、内分泌系统（化学信号传导）、免疫/淋巴系统（防御）、泌尿系统（废物排除）、生殖系统（种族延续）。

Worked Example 1: Ordering the levels of organization例题 1：排列组织层次

A student lists five items from the circulatory system: the heart, a cardiac muscle cell, the circulatory system, cardiac muscle tissue and a whole person. Put them in order from smallest to largest, name the level of each, and justify why the heart is an organ and not a tissue.一名学生列出循环系统的五个项目：心脏、一个心肌细胞、循环系统、心肌组织和一个完整的人。请按从小到大排序，说出每一项所属的层次，并说明为什么心脏是器官而不是组织。

Order: cardiac muscle cell (cell), then cardiac muscle tissue (tissue), then heart (organ), then circulatory system (organ system), then whole person (organism).顺序： 心肌细胞（细胞），心肌组织（组织），心脏（器官），循环系统（器官系统），完整的人（生物体）。

Why the heart is an organ: its wall is mostly cardiac muscle tissue, but it also contains connective tissue (the valves and the fibrous framework), epithelial tissue (the smooth lining of the chambers) and nervous tissue (nerves that adjust the heart rate). Two or more tissue types cooperating in one function, pumping blood, is the definition of an organ.为什么心脏是器官： 心脏壁主要由心肌组织构成，但也含有结缔组织（瓣膜和纤维支架）、上皮组织（心腔光滑的内衬）和神经组织（调节心率的神经）。两种或两种以上组织协同完成同一功能（泵血），正是器官的定义。

Why this works: each level is built from the one below, and a new level appears when different parts cooperate. Common error: calling the heart a tissue because it "is muscle"; muscle is only one of its tissues.为什么成立： 每个层次都由下一层次构成，不同部分协同工作时就出现新的层次。常见错误：因为心脏"是肌肉"就称它为组织；肌肉只是它的组织之一。

Worked Example 2: Identifying tissues from a description例题 2：根据描述识别组织

A slide of the small intestine wall shows four features: (a) one layer of tightly packed cells lining the inner surface; (b) layers of long cells packed with fibres that can shorten; (c) cells scattered in a gel-like matrix with strong fibres, holding the layers together; (d) branched cells with long extensions running between the muscle layers. Name the tissue type for each, state its function, and state whether the small intestine is a tissue or an organ.小肠壁的切片显示四个特征：(a) 一层紧密排列的细胞，衬在内表面；(b) 充满可缩短纤维的长细胞层；(c) 散布在含有坚韧纤维的凝胶状基质中的细胞，把各层连在一起；(d) 在肌肉层之间延伸的带有长突起的分支细胞。说出每个特征对应的组织类型及其功能，并判断小肠是组织还是器官。

(a) Epithelial tissue: it covers and lines surfaces; here it protects the wall and absorbs nutrients.(a) 上皮组织： 覆盖并衬在表面；这里起保护肠壁和吸收营养的作用。

(b) Muscle tissue: its cells contract; here the smooth muscle layers squeeze the contents along (peristalsis).(b) 肌肉组织： 细胞能收缩；这里的平滑肌层把内容物向前挤压（蠕动）。

(c) Connective tissue: cells in a matrix that supports and binds; here it holds the layers together and carries blood vessels.(c) 结缔组织： 细胞位于起支撑和连接作用的基质中；这里把各层连在一起，并容纳血管。

(d) Nervous tissue: branched cells with long extensions carry electrical signals; here they control the muscle and the secretion of juices.(d) 神经组织： 带长突起的分支细胞传导电信号；这里控制肌肉活动和消化液的分泌。

Level: the small intestine contains all four tissue types working together to digest and absorb food, so it is an organ in the digestive system.层次： 小肠含有全部四种组织，协同完成食物的消化和吸收，所以它是消化系统中的器官。

Why this works: tissues are recognised by cell shape, how the cells are arranged, and whether there is a matrix. Common error: choosing connective tissue for nerves because both "connect" things; the tissue type is named by its function, not by the word "connect".为什么成立： 组织要根据细胞形状、排列方式以及有无基质来识别。常见错误：因为神经和结缔都和"连接"有关就把神经选成结缔组织；组织类型由功能决定，与字面的"连接"无关。

Worked Example 4: Why each enzyme works only at its own site例题 4：为什么每种酶只在自己的部位起作用

The mouth has a pH of about 7, the stomach about 2, and the small intestine about 7 to 8 (pancreatic juice neutralises the stomach acid). A student says that salivary amylase keeps digesting starch while the food sits in the stomach. Use the pH values to decide whether this is correct, and state what happens to starch digestion next.口腔的 pH 约为 7，胃约为 2，小肠约为 7 至 8（胰液中和胃酸）。一名学生说，食物留在胃里时唾液淀粉酶仍在继续消化淀粉。请用这些 pH 值判断这种说法是否正确，并说明淀粉消化接下来如何进行。

Step 1, compare the conditions: salivary amylase works best near pH 7, the pH of the mouth. The stomach, at about pH 2, is far from that.第一步，比较条件： 唾液淀粉酶在 pH 7 附近（口腔的 pH）作用最好。胃的 pH 约为 2，与此相差很远。

Step 2, apply what you know about enzymes: strong acid changes the shape of an enzyme's active site (it denatures the enzyme), so salivary amylase stops working once it is mixed with stomach acid. The student's statement is not correct.第二步，运用酶的知识： 强酸会改变酶活性部位的形状（使酶变性），所以唾液淀粉酶一旦与胃酸混合就不再起作用。这名学生的说法不正确。

Step 3, what happens next: starch digestion pauses in the stomach. Common Questions常见问题

What are the levels of organization in the human body?人体的组织层次有哪些？

The body is organized from cells into tissues, then organs, then organ systems, and finally the whole organism. Each higher level is built from specialized structures at the level below.人体由细胞组成组织，再形成器官，进而构成器官系统，最终构成整个生物体。每个更高的层次都由其下一级的特化结构构成。

How does the circulatory system transport materials in the body?循环系统如何在体内运输物质？

The heart pumps blood through arteries, capillaries, and veins. Blood delivers oxygen and nutrients to cells and carries away carbon dioxide and wastes, linking it closely with the respiratory system.心脏将血液泵送通过动脉、毛细血管和静脉。血液将氧气和营养物质输送到细胞，并带走二氧化碳和废物，使其与呼吸系统紧密相连。

How do the nervous and endocrine systems differ in controlling the body?神经系统和内分泌系统在调控身体方面有何不同？

The nervous system sends fast, short-lived electrical signals through neurons, while the endocrine system releases hormones into the blood for slower, longer-lasting responses across the body.神经系统通过神经元发送快速、短暂的电信号，而内分泌系统则将激素释放到血液中，在全身产生较慢但更持久的反应。

If you are going for the top mark如果你目标顶分

Trace the path of a nutrient (mouth → esophagus → stomach → small intestine → blood), a breath (atmosphere → trachea → bronchioles → alveoli → blood), and a nerve impulse (receptor → afferent neuron → CNS → efferent neuron → effector). Link systems: the circulatory system connects every other system by delivering oxygen, hormones, and nutrients while removing wastes. Know at least one negative feedback loop (e.g. body temperature regulation) and how the endocrine and nervous systems cooperate to maintain it.追踪营养物质的路径（口腔 → 食道 → 胃 → 小肠 → 血液）、一次呼吸（大气 → 气管 → 细支气管 → 肺泡 → 血液）和一个神经冲动（感受器 → 传入神经元 → 中枢神经系统 → 传出神经元 → 效应器）。联系各系统：循环系统通过输送氧气、激素和营养并带走废物来连接其他所有系统。至少掌握一个负反馈回路（如体温调节），以及内分泌和神经系统如何协同维持稳态。
