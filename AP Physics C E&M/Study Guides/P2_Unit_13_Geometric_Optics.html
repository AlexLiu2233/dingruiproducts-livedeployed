Light as a Ray把光看作光线

Light is a wave, and a wave can be pictured by its wavefronts, surfaces joining points that are at the same stage of the wave, for example all the crests. A point source such as a small bulb sends out spherical wavefronts that grow outward.

Far from the source, a small patch of a wavefront is almost flat. A light ray is a straight line that is perpendicular to the wavefront (normal to the wave) and points in the direction the wave travels. A bulb therefore sends out rays in every direction, and a distant star sends almost parallel rays.

光是一种波，可以用波前（wavefront）来描绘：波前是由波的同一相位的点连成的面，例如所有波峰。小灯泡这样的点光源发出向外扩大的球面波前。

离光源很远时，波前上的一小块几乎是平面。光线（light ray）是一条直线，它垂直于波前，并指向波的传播方向。所以灯泡向各个方向发出光线，而遥远的恒星发出的光线几乎是平行的。

History: Ibn al-Haytham and the straight-line ray历史：伊本·海什木与沿直线传播的光线

In his Book of Optics, written in Cairo between about 1011 and 1021, Ibn al-Haytham (Alhazen) argued that we see because light travels in straight lines from objects into the eye. The older view held that the eye sends rays out.

伊本·海什木（Ibn al-Haytham，拉丁名阿尔哈曾）约在 1011 至 1021 年间于开罗写成《光学之书》（Book of Optics）。他论证：我们能看见东西，是因为光沿直线从物体进入眼睛。较早的观点则认为是眼睛向外发出光线。

He tested straight-line travel with a darkened room and a small hole (a camera obscura) and studied reflection from flat and curved mirrors. The Wikipedia article on the Book of Optics describes these experiments.

他用暗室和小孔（暗箱，camera obscura）检验光沿直线传播，并研究了平面镜和曲面镜的反射。维基百科《光学之书》条目（英文）介绍了这些实验。

When Rays Are Enough: Geometric Optics什么时候光线就够用：几何光学

Using rays to predict where light goes is called geometric optics. It works when the wave nature of light can be neglected, which is the case when light meets objects and openings that are much larger than its wavelength (about $4\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$ to $7\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$ for visible light). Mirrors, lenses, glasses and cameras are all analyzed this way in this unit.

用光线来预测光的去向，叫作几何光学（geometric optics）。当光的波动性可以忽略时它就适用，也就是光遇到的物体和孔径比光的波长（可见光约为 $4\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$ 到 $7\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$）大得多的时候。本单元中的镜子、透镜、眼镜和相机都用这种方法分析。

Rays are not enough to understand the spreading of light. When a beam passes through a very narrow slit it fans out, and when two beams overlap they can form bright and dark bands.

These effects, interference and diffraction, depend on the wave nature of light and are studied in Unit 14 of this course. No ray diagram can predict them, because a ray is a straight line and never spreads.

光线不足以解释光的扩散。光束通过非常窄的狭缝时会向外散开，两束光重叠时可以形成明暗条纹。

这些效应，即干涉（interference）和衍射（diffraction），取决于光的波动性，会在本课程第 14 单元学习。任何光线图都无法预测它们，因为光线是一条直线，永远不会扩散。

A laser is a common source of a single, narrow beam of coherent, monochromatic light: the waves keep a fixed phase relationship and have one wavelength. Such a beam is well modeled as a single ray, which is why lasers are the standard tool for demonstrating reflection and refraction.

The wave nature of lasers shows up only when the beam meets a very small opening, and that is treated in Unit 14.

激光（laser）是常见的光源，发出单一、狭窄的光束，其光相干、单色：各列波的相位关系固定，而且只有一种波长。这样的光束可以很好地建模为一条光线，所以激光是演示反射和折射的标准工具。

只有光束遇到极小的孔径时，激光的波动性才会显现，这部分在第 14 单元处理。

Ray Diagrams光线图

A ray diagram shows the path of light before and after an interaction with matter. Each ray is drawn as a straight line segment with an arrowhead showing its direction of travel.

The ray that arrives at the surface is the incident ray, and the ray that leaves it is the reflected ray (or the refracted ray, in Topic 13.3). Between interactions the ray is straight, and it changes direction only at a surface.

We also draw the normal, a dashed line perpendicular to the surface at the point where the ray meets it, because every angle in optics is measured from the normal, never from the surface.

光线图（ray diagram）表示光与物质相互作用前后的传播路径。每条光线画成一段带箭头的直线，箭头表示传播方向。

到达表面的光线是入射光线（incident ray），离开表面的是反射光线（reflected ray，在专题 13.3 中则是折射光线）。两次相互作用之间光线是直的，只在表面处改变方向。

我们还要画出法线（normal）：在光线与表面相交的点上垂直于表面的虚线，因为光学中所有的角都从法线量起，而不是从表面量起。

The Law of Reflection反射定律

When light that travels through one medium meets the surface of another, some of it can be reflected: it turns back into the first medium.

The law of reflection says that the angle between the incident ray and the normal equals the angle between the reflected ray and the normal. The incident ray, the normal and the reflected ray all lie in one plane.

当在一种介质中传播的光遇到另一种介质的表面时，有一部分光可以被反射（reflected）：它折回第一种介质。

反射定律（law of reflection）指出：入射光线与法线的夹角等于反射光线与法线的夹角。入射光线、法线和反射光线在同一个平面内。

Law of reflection反射定律
$$\theta_i = \theta_r$$

$\theta_i$ is the angle of incidence and $\theta_r$ is the angle of reflection, both in degrees, both measured from the normal.

$\theta_i$ 是入射角，$\theta_r$ 是反射角，单位都是度，都从法线量起。

Read Figure 13.1 from the normal outward. The angle of incidence is not the angle between the ray and the surface: here the incident ray makes $90^\circ - 40^\circ = 50^\circ$ with the surface. The angle between the incident and reflected rays at the point of incidence is $2\theta_i = 80^\circ$, and the direction of the light has turned through $180^\circ - 2\theta_i = 100^\circ$.

从法线向外读图 13.1。入射角不是光线与表面的夹角：这里入射光线与表面的夹角是 $90^\circ - 40^\circ = 50^\circ$。在入射点，入射光线与反射光线的夹角是 $2\theta_i = 80^\circ$，光的传播方向转过了 $180^\circ - 2\theta_i = 100^\circ$。

Common Mistake: Measuring the angle from the surface A ray that "hits the mirror at 25 degrees" is often one that makes $25^\circ$ with the mirror's surface, which is $65^\circ$ from the normal. The law of reflection uses the angle from the normal. Always draw the normal first, and then mark both angles from it. Using the surface instead gives the wrong answer for every direction except $45^\circ$.
常见错误：从表面而不是法线量角 "以 25 度射到镜面上"的光线，常常指光线与镜面的夹角为 $25^\circ$，这相当于与法线成 $65^\circ$。反射定律用的是与法线的夹角。一定要先画法线，再从法线量出两个角。若改用表面来量，除了 $45^\circ$ 之外所有方向都会得出错误答案。

First Principles: Why the Two Angles Are Equal第一性原理：为什么两个角相等

First principles: the law of reflection from the shortest path第一性原理：由最短路径导出反射定律

Principle. Light that goes from one point to a mirror and on to a second point takes the path of least time (Fermat's principle). In one medium the speed is constant, so least time means the shortest path.

原理。光从一点射到镜面再到另一点，走用时最短的路径（费马原理，Fermat's principle）。在同一种介质中光速不变，所以用时最短就是路程最短。

  1. Light goes from point A to a point P on the mirror and then to point B. Reflect B in the mirror to get the point B′.
  2. The mirror is the perpendicular bisector of BB′, so PB = PB′ for every P. Each path A → P → B is as long as A → P → B′.
  3. The shortest route from A to B′ is a straight line, so P is where the line AB′ crosses the mirror.
  4. At P the straight line AB′ makes equal (vertical) angles with the normal, and PB is the mirror image of PB′. So the angles in and out are equal: $\theta_i = \theta_r$.
  1. 光从 A 点射到镜面上的 P 点，再到 B 点。作 B 关于镜面的对称点 B′。
  2. 镜面是 BB′ 的垂直平分线，所以对任何 P 都有 PB = PB′。每条路径 A → P → B 都与 A → P → B′ 一样长。
  3. 从 A 到 B′ 的最短路线是直线，所以 P 就是直线 AB′ 与镜面的交点。
  4. 在 P 点，直线 AB′ 与法线所成的角相等（对顶角），而 PB 是 PB′ 的镜像。所以入射与出射的角相等：$\theta_i = \theta_r$。

Limiting check. For a ray along the normal ($\theta_i = 0$), A, P and B′ lie on one perpendicular line, so the light returns along the normal ($\theta_r = 0$), as observed. The construction also puts B′ as far behind the mirror as B is in front, which is the plane-mirror image rule of Topic 13.2.

极限检验。沿法线入射的光线（$\theta_i = 0$），A、P、B′ 在同一条垂线上，所以光沿法线返回（$\theta_r = 0$），与观察一致。这个作图还使 B′ 在镜后的距离等于 B 在镜前的距离，这正是专题 13.2 中平面镜成像的规律。

History: from shortest path to least time历史：从最短路径到最短时间

Hero of Alexandria (first century CE) showed that a reflected ray takes the shortest path between two points. In 1662 Pierre de Fermat replaced "shortest path" with "least time", a rule that also covers refraction.

亚历山大的希罗（Hero of Alexandria，公元 1 世纪）证明：反射光线走两点之间的最短路径。1662 年，皮埃尔·德·费马（Pierre de Fermat）把"最短路径"改为"最短时间"，这条规则也适用于折射。

Least time gives Snell's law only if light is slower in water or glass than in air, and experiments by Foucault and by Fizeau in 1850 confirmed this. See the Wikipedia article on Fermat's principle.

只有当光在水或玻璃中比在空气中慢时，最短时间原理才能给出斯涅尔定律；1850 年傅科（Foucault）和菲佐（Fizeau）的实验分别证实了这一点。参见维基百科“费马原理”条目（英文）。

Specular and Diffuse Reflection镜面反射与漫反射

The law of reflection holds at every point of every surface, but what we see depends on how the surface is shaped on a small scale. A smooth surface, such as a polished mirror or still water, has a normal that points in nearly the same direction everywhere over the patch of light.

Parallel incident rays therefore all reflect at the same angle and stay parallel, a behavior called specular reflection. You see a bright image or glare only from one direction, the direction of the reflected rays.

反射定律在任何表面的每一点都成立，但我们看到什么，取决于表面在小尺度上的形状。光滑表面，例如抛光的镜面或平静的水面，在光照射的那一小片区域内，法线几乎处处指向同一方向。

平行的入射光线因此都以相同的角度反射并保持平行，这种行为称为镜面反射（specular reflection）。只有沿着反射光线的那个方向才能看到明亮的像或眩光。

A rough surface, such as paper, cloth or a wall, is covered with tiny facets whose normals point in many different directions. Each ray still obeys the law of reflection at its own facet, but because the local normal varies from place to place, parallel incident rays leave in many different directions.

This is diffuse reflection. It is why a page can be read from any angle, and why you can see a non-luminous object from every side.

粗糙表面，例如纸、布或墙壁，布满微小的小平面，它们的法线指向许多不同的方向。每条光线在各自的小平面上仍然遵守反射定律，但由于局部法线处处不同，平行的入射光线会向许多不同的方向射出。

这就是漫反射（diffuse reflection）。这也是为什么书页从任何角度都能阅读，以及为什么我们能从各个方向看到不发光的物体。

Whether a surface counts as smooth or rough depends on the wavelength of the light: bumps much smaller than a wavelength do not matter. The key idea is the same in both cases: every ray obeys $\theta_i = \theta_r$ locally; the difference lies in whether the normal has a nearly constant direction or a varying one.

表面算光滑还是粗糙，取决于光的波长：比波长小得多的凹凸不起作用。两种情形的核心思想是一样的：每条光线在局部都遵守 $\theta_i = \theta_r$；区别只在于法线的方向是近似不变还是不断变化。

Worked Example: A Ray Given Relative to the Surface例题：给出与表面夹角的光线

A laser beam strikes a flat mirror and makes an angle of $25^\circ$ with the surface of the mirror. Find the angle of incidence, the angle of reflection, and the angle between the incident and reflected rays at the point of incidence.

一束激光射到平面镜上，与镜面的夹角为 $25^\circ$。求入射角、反射角，以及在入射点入射光线与反射光线之间的夹角。

Step 1: Convert to the angle from the normal第 1 步：换算成与法线的夹角
$$\theta_i = 90^\circ - 25^\circ = 65^\circ$$
The normal is perpendicular to the surface, so the two angles add to $90^\circ$.法线垂直于表面，所以两个角之和为 $90^\circ$。
Step 2: Apply the law of reflection第 2 步：应用反射定律
$$\theta_r = \theta_i = 65^\circ$$
Step 3: Angle between the rays第 3 步：两条光线之间的夹角
$$\theta_i + \theta_r = 65^\circ + 65^\circ = 130^\circ$$
Why this works: the two rays lie on opposite sides of the normal, so the angle between them is the sum of the two angles from the normal. As a check, the reflected ray also makes $25^\circ$ with the surface, and $25^\circ + 130^\circ + 25^\circ = 180^\circ$.为什么这样做：两条光线位于法线两侧，所以它们之间的夹角是各自与法线夹角之和。验算：反射光线与表面的夹角也是 $25^\circ$，而 $25^\circ + 130^\circ + 25^\circ = 180^\circ$。
Worked Example: Rotating a Mirror例题：转动镜面

A fixed laser beam strikes a flat mirror with an angle of incidence of $30^\circ$. The mirror is then rotated by $10^\circ$ about the point of incidence, so that the angle of incidence becomes $40^\circ$. By how many degrees does the reflected ray turn?

一束固定的激光以 $30^\circ$ 的入射角射到平面镜上。然后镜子绕入射点转动 $10^\circ$，入射角变为 $40^\circ$。反射光线转过了多少度？

Step 1: Direction change of the light before the rotation第 1 步：转动前光的方向改变量
$$180^\circ - 2(30^\circ) = 120^\circ$$
The light turns through $180^\circ - 2\theta_i$ at the surface, as in the discussion of Figure 13.1.光在表面处转过 $180^\circ - 2\theta_i$，如对图 13.1 的讨论。
Step 2: Direction change after the rotation第 2 步：转动后光的方向改变量
$$180^\circ - 2(40^\circ) = 100^\circ$$
Step 3: Difference第 3 步：差值
$$120^\circ - 100^\circ = 20^\circ = 2(10^\circ)$$
The reflected ray turns through $20^\circ$, twice the rotation of the mirror. Why this works: rotating the mirror by $\delta$ rotates the normal by $\delta$, which changes $\theta_i$ by $\delta$, and the reflected ray moves away from the normal by that same $\delta$ on top of the normal's own rotation. This doubling is what makes a small mirror rotation easy to see with a long lever arm of light.反射光线转过 $20^\circ$，是镜子转角的两倍。为什么这样做：镜子转过 $\delta$，法线也转过 $\delta$，入射角 $\theta_i$ 改变 $\delta$，而反射光线在法线自身转动的基础上又相对法线多偏离同样的 $\delta$。这个两倍关系使得借助很长的"光杠杆"就能清楚地看到镜子的微小转动。
Worked Example: Why a Matte Wall Looks the Same from Everywhere例题：为什么无光泽的墙从各处看起来都一样

Two parallel rays strike a rough wall, each at $35^\circ$ from the average normal of the wall. At one tiny facet the local normal is tilted by $8^\circ$ from the average normal, and at another facet it is tilted by $12^\circ$ in the opposite sense.

(a) Explain, using the law of reflection, why the reflected rays are not parallel. (b) Find the angle between the two reflected rays. Contrast this with a smooth mirror.

两条平行光线射到粗糙的墙上，每条与墙的平均法线成 $35^\circ$。在一个微小的小平面上，局部法线相对于平均法线偏转 $8^\circ$；在另一个小平面上，法线向相反方向偏转 $12^\circ$。

(a) 用反射定律解释为什么两条反射光线不平行。(b) 求两条反射光线之间的夹角。并与光滑镜面对比。

Step 1: Each ray obeys the law at its own facet第 1 步：每条光线在各自的小平面上遵守反射定律
At each facet $\theta_r = \theta_i$ measured from the local normal. The two local normals point in different directions, so the two angles of incidence differ even though the incident rays are parallel, and the reflected rays leave in different directions.在每个小平面上，$\theta_r = \theta_i$ 都是相对局部法线而言的。两个局部法线指向不同方向，所以即使入射光线平行，两个入射角也不同，反射光线便射向不同的方向。
Step 2: Relate the reflected direction to the tilt of the normal第 2 步：把反射方向与法线的偏转联系起来
From the rotating-mirror result, tilting the normal by an angle $\delta$ turns the reflected ray by $2\delta$.由转动镜面的结果，法线偏转 $\delta$，反射光线就转过 $2\delta$。
$$\Delta\theta = 2(8^\circ + 12^\circ) = 40^\circ$$
The normals are tilted in opposite senses, so the tilts add: the two normals differ by $20^\circ$ and the two reflected rays differ by twice that, $40^\circ$. On a smooth mirror both rays meet normals with the same direction, so both reflected rays leave at $35^\circ$ from the normal and stay parallel, and you see the image only from one direction. Why this works: diffuse reflection is just the law of reflection applied many times with many different normals.两个法线向相反方向偏转，所以偏转角相加：两个法线相差 $20^\circ$，两条反射光线的方向相差其两倍，即 $40^\circ$。在光滑镜面上，两条光线遇到的法线方向相同，所以两条反射光线都与法线成 $35^\circ$ 射出并保持平行，只有从一个方向才能看到像。为什么这样做：漫反射不过是把反射定律用在许多不同的法线上。

The Plane Mirror平面镜

A plane mirror is a flat, smooth reflecting surface. Light from a point on an object spreads out in all directions, and each ray that strikes the mirror reflects according to the law of reflection.

The reflected rays diverge, and when they enter your eye you perceive them as coming from a point behind the mirror, where the extensions of the reflected rays meet. That point is the image. Because no light is really there, the image is virtual.

平面镜是平坦光滑的反射面。物体上某一点发出的光向各个方向扩散，射到镜面上的每条光线都按反射定律反射。

反射光线是发散的，进入眼睛时，你觉得它们来自镜子后面的一点，即反射光线的反向延长线的交点。这一点就是像。由于那里并没有真正的光，这个像是虚像（virtual image）。

From Figure 13.2 you can read three facts about a plane mirror:

  • The image is as far behind the mirror as the object is in front of it, so the distance between the object and its image is twice the object's distance from the mirror.
  • The image is the same size as the object and upright.
  • The image is virtual.

A plane mirror does not bring parallel rays to a focus: parallel rays stay parallel after reflection, so the focal point of a plane mirror is at an infinite distance, and its focal length is infinite.

从图 13.2 可以读出关于平面镜的三个事实：

  • 像在镜后的距离等于物在镜前的距离，所以物与像之间的距离是物到镜面距离的两倍。
  • 像与物等大且正立。
  • 像是虚像。

平面镜不会使平行光线会聚到一点：平行光线反射后仍然平行，所以平面镜的焦点在无穷远处，焦距为无穷大。

Spherical Mirrors, Focal Point and Focal Length球面镜、焦点与焦距

A spherical mirror is a small piece of a spherical surface. The center of that sphere is the center of curvature $C$, and its radius is the radius of curvature $R$.

The principal axis is the line through $C$ and the middle of the mirror, a point called the vertex $V$ where the axis meets the mirror. A mirror whose reflecting surface faces the center of curvature is concave, and one whose reflecting surface faces away from it is convex.

球面镜是球面的一小块。该球面的球心是曲率中心 $C$，半径是曲率半径（radius of curvature）$R$。

主光轴（principal axis）是过 $C$ 和镜面中心的直线，主光轴与镜面的交点称为顶点 $V$。反射面朝向曲率中心的镜子是凹面镜（concave mirror），反射面背向曲率中心的是凸面镜（convex mirror）。

Light rays that arrive parallel to the principal axis of a concave (converging) mirror are reflected toward one common location on the axis, the focal point $F$, and the mirror makes a real focus.

Parallel rays that strike a convex (diverging) mirror reflect so that they spread apart and appear to have come from a common point behind the mirror: that point is the focal point of the convex mirror, and it is virtual.

For rays close to the axis, the focal point of a spherical mirror is approximately halfway between the surface of the mirror and the center of its radius of curvature. The distance from the mirror to the focal point is the focal length $f$.

平行于凹面镜（会聚镜）主光轴射来的光线被反射到轴上同一个位置，即焦点（focal point）$F$，凹面镜产生的是实焦点。

平行光线射到凸面镜（发散镜）上，反射后向外散开，看起来像是从镜后同一点发出的：这一点就是凸面镜的焦点，它是虚的。

对靠近主光轴的光线，球面镜的焦点近似位于镜面与曲率中心之间的中点。镜面到焦点的距离是焦距（focal length）$f$。

Focal length of a spherical mirror (approximate)球面镜的焦距（近似）
$$|f| = \frac{R}{2}$$

$f$ is the focal length and $R$ the radius of curvature, in meters or centimeters. The focal length is positive for a concave mirror and negative for a convex mirror (sign convention below).

$f$ 是焦距，$R$ 是曲率半径，单位为米或厘米。凹面镜的焦距为正，凸面镜的焦距为负（见下面的符号约定）。

First Principles: Why the Focal Length Is Half the Radius第一性原理：为什么焦距是半径的一半

First principles: deriving $|f| = R/2$ from the law of reflection第一性原理：由反射定律导出 $|f| = R/2$

Principle. The law of reflection holds at every point of the mirror, and the normal to a sphere at any point P runs along the radius CP, through the center of curvature C.

原理。反射定律在镜面上每一点都成立，而球面上任一点 P 的法线就沿半径 CP，经过曲率中心 C。

  1. A ray parallel to the axis at height $h$ meets a concave mirror at P. The normal CP makes an angle $\theta$ with the axis, where $\sin\theta = h/R$.
  2. The incident ray is parallel to the axis, so its angle of incidence is also $\theta$. By the law of reflection, the reflected ray makes $\theta$ with CP on the other side.
  3. The reflected ray crosses the axis at F. Triangle CPF has the angle $\theta$ at both C and P, so it is isosceles, and the foot of the perpendicular from F bisects CP: $CF = \dfrac{R}{2\cos\theta}$.
  4. For rays close to the axis, $\theta$ is small and $\cos\theta \approx 1$, so $CF \approx R/2$. F is halfway between C and the vertex: $f = R - R/2 = R/2$.
  1. 高度为 $h$、平行于主光轴的光线射到凹面镜上的 P 点。法线 CP 与主光轴成角 $\theta$，且 $\sin\theta = h/R$。
  2. 入射光线平行于主光轴，所以它的入射角也是 $\theta$。由反射定律，反射光线在 CP 另一侧与 CP 成 $\theta$。
  3. 反射光线在 F 点穿过主光轴。三角形 CPF 在 C 和 P 处的角都是 $\theta$，所以它是等腰三角形，从 F 作的垂线平分 CP：$CF = \dfrac{R}{2\cos\theta}$。
  4. 对靠近主光轴的光线，$\theta$ 很小，$\cos\theta \approx 1$，所以 $CF \approx R/2$。F 位于 C 与顶点的中点：$f = R - R/2 = R/2$。

Limiting check. As $h \to 0$, $\cos\theta \to 1$ and $f \to R/2$ exactly. A ray far from the axis, at $\theta = 30^\circ$, gives $CF = 0.577R$ and $f = 0.423R$, so it crosses the axis closer to the mirror. This blur is spherical aberration, which is why the formula is stated for rays close to the axis.

极限检验。当 $h \to 0$ 时，$\cos\theta \to 1$，$f \to R/2$ 精确成立。离轴较远的光线，例如 $\theta = 30^\circ$，给出 $CF = 0.577R$ 和 $f = 0.423R$，所以它在离镜面更近处穿过主光轴。这种模糊叫作球面像差（spherical aberration），所以这个公式只对靠近主光轴的光线成立。

Real and Virtual Images, and What We Say About an Image实像、虚像，以及如何描述一个像

A real image is formed when light rays that start from a common point on the object are reflected and then actually intersect at a common point. A screen placed there shows the image.

A virtual image is formed when the reflected rays diverge, so that they appear to have originated from a common point behind the mirror. No screen can capture a virtual image, but the eye can see it.

We describe every image by three pairs of words: real or virtual, upright or inverted (relative to the object), and reduced, enlarged or the same size as the object.

实像（real image）：从物体上同一点发出的光线经反射后真正相交于一点时形成。放在那里的屏幕上能显示出像。

虚像（virtual image）：反射光线发散，看起来像是从镜后同一点发出时形成。屏幕接不到虚像，但眼睛能看到它。

我们用三组词描述每个像：实或虚，相对于物体是正立（upright）还是倒立（inverted），以及比物体缩小、放大或等大。

Because the focal point, the object and the image can each lie on either side of the mirror, a sign convention says which sign each distance has.

像的位置取决于镜子的焦距，以及物体到镜面的距离。镜面方程（mirror equation）把物距 $s_\text{o}$、像距 $s_\text{i}$ 和焦距 $f$ 联系起来，它们都从镜面量起。

Ray Diagrams with the Three Principal Rays用三条主光线作图

A ray diagram gives the location, type, size and orientation of an image without calculation. Draw the principal axis and the mirror, and mark $F$ and $C$. Draw the object as an arrow standing on the axis. Then trace the three principal rays from the tip of the object:

  1. The ray parallel to the principal axis reflects through the focal point. For a convex mirror, it reflects as if it came from the focal point behind the mirror.
  2. The ray that passes through the focal point (or is aimed at it, for a convex mirror) reflects parallel to the axis.
  3. The ray that strikes the mirror at the vertex, where the principal axis meets it, reflects at the same angle on the other side of the axis.

The reflected rays, or their backward extensions drawn dashed, meet at the tip of the image. Virtual rays and virtual images are drawn dashed.

What Refraction Is and Why It Happens什么是折射，它为何发生

Refraction is the change in direction of a light ray as it passes from one medium into another, for example from air into water. It happens because the speed of light changes when light enters a new medium.

A wavefront picture shows why. Suppose a plane wavefront meets a boundary at an angle:

  1. The end of the wavefront that reaches the new medium first slows down (or speeds up) first.
  2. The other end is still traveling at the old speed, so the wavefront swings around.
  3. The ray is always perpendicular to the wavefront, so the ray changes direction.

Light that meets the boundary head-on has all of its wavefront change speed at the same moment. The wavefront does not swing around, and the ray keeps its direction.

折射（refraction）是光线从一种介质进入另一种介质（例如从空气进入水）时方向的改变。它的原因是光进入新介质时速度发生变化。

用波前来看就很清楚。设一个平面波前以某个角度到达界面：

  1. 波前上先到达新介质的那一端先减速（或加速）。
  2. 另一端仍以原来的速度前进，于是波前发生偏转。
  3. 光线始终垂直于波前，所以光线的方向改变了。

若光垂直射向界面，整个波前同时改变速度。波前不会偏转，光线保持原来的方向。

The Index of Refraction折射率

The speed of light is greatest in vacuum, $c = 3.00\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, and smaller in matter. The index of refraction $n$ of a medium compares the two. It is inversely proportional to the speed of light in the medium: the slower light travels in a material, the larger its $n$.

光速在真空中最大，$c = 3.00\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$，在物质中较小。介质的折射率（index of refraction）$n$ 就是用来比较这两者的量。它与光在介质中的速度成反比：光在材料中传播得越慢，$n$ 就越大。

Index of refraction折射率
$$n = \frac{c}{v}$$

$n$ is the index of refraction (no unit), $c$ the speed of light in vacuum and $v$ the speed of light in the medium, both in m/s. Since $v \le c$, always $n \ge 1$.

$n$ 是折射率（无单位），$c$ 是真空中的光速，$v$ 是光在介质中的速度，单位都是 m/s。由于 $v \le c$，总有 $n \ge 1$。

Typical values are about $1.00$ for air, $1.33$ for water, $1.50$ for glass and $2.42$ for diamond. These are given as data in problems.

When light enters a medium, its frequency stays the same, because the source sets how often wave crests arrive at the boundary, and the same number of crests must leave per second as arrive.

Since $v = f\lambda$, the speed and the wavelength change together: in a medium of index $n$ the wavelength is $\lambda = \lambda_0/n$, where $\lambda_0$ is the wavelength in vacuum (or in air, to a good approximation). Light of a given color is therefore squeezed into a shorter wavelength in glass, but its frequency, and so its color, is unchanged.

典型值：空气约为 $1.00$，水为 $1.33$，玻璃为 $1.50$，钻石为 $2.42$。这些在题目中作为数据给出。

光进入介质时，它的频率不变，因为光源决定了波峰到达界面的频繁程度，每秒离开界面的波峰数必须等于到达的波峰数。

由于 $v = f\lambda$，速度和波长一起改变：在折射率为 $n$ 的介质中，波长为 $\lambda = \lambda_0/n$，其中 $\lambda_0$ 是真空中（近似地，也可取空气中）的波长。因此某一颜色的光在玻璃中被"压缩"成较短的波长，但频率不变，所以颜色也不变。

Snell's Law斯涅尔定律

Snell's law relates the angles of incidence and refraction of a ray passing from one medium into another to the indices of refraction of the two media. As in reflection, both angles are measured from the normal. The incident ray, the normal and the refracted ray lie in one plane.

斯涅尔定律（Snell's law）把光线从一种介质进入另一种介质时的入射角和折射角，与两种介质的折射率联系起来。与反射一样，两个角都从法线量起。入射光线、法线和折射光线在同一个平面内。

Snell's law斯涅尔定律
$$n_1 \sin\theta_1 = n_2 \sin\theta_2$$

$n_1$ and $\theta_1$ are the index and the angle from the normal in the medium where the ray starts; $n_2$ and $\theta_2$ are the index and the angle from the normal in the medium the ray enters. Angles in degrees.

$n_1$ 和 $\theta_1$ 是光线出发的介质的折射率和与法线的夹角；$n_2$ 和 $\theta_2$ 是光线进入的介质的折射率和与法线的夹角。角度单位为度。

The law gives a rule for the direction of the bend. When a ray travels from a lower index into a higher index (light slows down), $\sin\theta_2 \lt \sin\theta_1$, so the ray refracts toward the normal. When a ray travels from a higher index into a lower index (light speeds up), the ray refracts away from the normal.

When the ray is incident along the normal, $\theta_1 = 0^\circ$, so $\sin\theta_2 = 0$ and $\theta_2 = 0^\circ$: the transmitted ray is not refracted and goes straight through, whatever the two indices are.

这个定律给出了弯折方向的规则。光线从折射率较小的介质进入折射率较大的介质（光减速）时，$\sin\theta_2 \lt \sin\theta_1$，所以光线偏向法线（toward the normal）折射。光线从折射率较大的介质进入折射率较小的介质（光加速）时，光线偏离法线（away from the normal）折射。

光线沿法线入射时，$\theta_1 = 0^\circ$，所以 $\sin\theta_2 = 0$，$\theta_2 = 0^\circ$：透射光线不发生折射，径直穿过，与两个折射率是多少无关。

First Principles: Snell's Law from Wavefronts第一性原理：由波前导出斯涅尔定律

First principles: Snell's law from Huygens' wavelets第一性原理：由惠更斯子波导出斯涅尔定律

Principle. Every point on a wavefront sends out wavelets, and rays are perpendicular to wavefronts (Huygens' principle). Light travels at $v_1 = c/n_1$ in medium 1 and at $v_2 = c/n_2$ in medium 2.

原理。波前上的每一点都发出子波，光线垂直于波前（惠更斯原理，Huygens' principle）。光在介质 1 中的速度为 $v_1 = c/n_1$，在介质 2 中为 $v_2 = c/n_2$。

  1. A plane wavefront AB meets the boundary, and edge A touches it first. The angle between the wavefront and the boundary equals $\theta_1$, the angle between the ray and the normal.
  2. In the time $t$ that edge B needs to reach the boundary at B′, it covers $v_1 t$. Meanwhile the wavelet from A spreads a distance $v_2 t$ into medium 2.
  3. Both wavefronts share the boundary segment AB′ of length $L$. The two right triangles give $\sin\theta_1 = v_1 t/L$ and $\sin\theta_2 = v_2 t/L$.
  4. Divide: $\dfrac{\sin\theta_1}{\sin\theta_2} = \dfrac{v_1}{v_2} = \dfrac{n_2}{n_1}$, so $n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2$.
  1. 平面波前 AB 到达界面，A 端先接触界面。波前与界面的夹角等于 $\theta_1$，即光线与法线的夹角。
  2. B 端到达界面上 B′ 点所需的时间 $t$ 内，它走过 $v_1 t$。与此同时，A 发出的子波在介质 2 中传播了 $v_2 t$。
  3. 两个波前共用界面上长度为 $L$ 的线段 AB′。两个直角三角形给出 $\sin\theta_1 = v_1 t/L$ 和 $\sin\theta_2 = v_2 t/L$。
  4. 两式相除：$\dfrac{\sin\theta_1}{\sin\theta_2} = \dfrac{v_1}{v_2} = \dfrac{n_2}{n_1}$，所以 $n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2$。

Limiting checks. If $n_1 = n_2$, then $\theta_2 = \theta_1$ and the ray goes straight on. If $\theta_1 = 0$, the whole wavefront arrives at once and $\theta_2 = 0$. If $v_2 \lt v_1$, the wavelet in medium 2 is shorter, so $\theta_2 \lt \theta_1$ and the ray bends toward the normal.

极限检验。若 $n_1 = n_2$，则 $\theta_2 = \theta_1$，光线直线前进。若 $\theta_1 = 0$，整个波前同时到达，$\theta_2 = 0$。若 $v_2 \lt v_1$，介质 2 中的子波较短，所以 $\theta_2 \lt \theta_1$，光线偏向法线。

History: a law found three times历史：被发现三次的定律

The Persian mathematician Ibn Sahl stated the law of refraction correctly in a treatise on burning mirrors and lenses, written in Baghdad around 984. Willebrord Snellius found it again in 1621 but never published it. René Descartes published it in 1637, so French textbooks call it the Snell-Descartes law.

波斯数学家伊本·萨赫勒（Ibn Sahl）约 984 年在巴格达写成一部关于取火镜和透镜的论著，其中正确地陈述了折射定律。斯涅尔（Willebrord Snellius）在 1621 年再次发现它，但从未发表。笛卡儿（René Descartes）在 1637 年发表了它，所以法国教材称之为斯涅尔-笛卡儿定律。

The Wikipedia article on Snell's law traces this history and shows how the law also follows from Fermat's principle.

维基百科“斯涅尔定律”条目（英文）梳理了这段历史，并说明这一定律也可以由费马原理导出。

Total Internal Reflection and the Critical Angle全反射与临界角

When light passes from a higher index $n_1$ into a lower index $n_2$, it refracts away from the normal, and the angle of refraction is larger than the angle of incidence. As the angle of incidence increases, the refracted ray swings toward the surface.

At one angle of incidence, the critical angle $\theta_c$, the refracted ray reaches an angle of $90^\circ$ and travels along the surface of the material.

Setting $\theta_2 = 90^\circ$ in Snell's law gives $n_1 \sin\theta_c = n_2 \sin 90^\circ = n_2$.

For any angle of incidence beyond the critical angle, Snell's law has no solution, because $\sin\theta_2$ would have to exceed 1, and no light is transmitted into the second medium: all the light is reflected back into the first. This is total internal reflection.

光从折射率较大的 $n_1$ 进入折射率较小的 $n_2$ 时，光线偏离法线折射，折射角大于入射角。随着入射角增大，折射光线向界面偏转。

在某一个入射角，即临界角（critical angle）$\theta_c$ 时，折射光线的折射角达到 $90^\circ$，沿材料表面传播。

在斯涅尔定律中令 $\theta_2 = 90^\circ$，得 $n_1 \sin\theta_c = n_2 \sin 90^\circ = n_2$。

入射角超过临界角后，斯涅尔定律无解，因为 $\sin\theta_2$ 必须大于 1，没有光透射进第二种介质：所有的光都被反射回第一种介质。这就是全反射（total internal reflection）。

Critical angle (derived from Snell's law)临界角（由斯涅尔定律导出）
$$\sin\theta_c = \frac{n_2}{n_1} \quad (n_1 \gt n_2)$$

$\theta_c$ is the critical angle of incidence in the denser medium (index $n_1$) for light going into the less dense medium (index $n_2$). It exists only when light goes from a higher index to a lower index.

$\theta_c$ 是光从折射率较大的介质（$n_1$）射向折射率较小的介质（$n_2$）时，在较密介质中的临界入射角。只有光从高折射率介质进入低折射率介质时才存在临界角。

Conditions for Total Internal Reflection and Optical Fibers全反射的条件与光纤

Three conditions to remember: total internal reflection needs light going toward a lower index (glass to air, water to air, not air to glass), it needs an angle of incidence greater than $\theta_c$, and when it happens the reflected ray obeys the law of reflection.

Total internal reflection is the principle of optical fibers: light traveling in a glass core strikes the boundary with the cladding beyond the critical angle again and again and stays trapped in the fiber.

要记住的三个条件：全反射要求光射向折射率较小的介质（玻璃到空气、水到空气，而不是空气到玻璃）；入射角要大于 $\theta_c$；发生全反射时，反射光线遵守反射定律。

全反射是光纤的原理：在玻璃纤芯中传播的光一次又一次地以大于临界角的角度射到与包层的界面上，因而被困在光纤中。

Common Mistake: Thinking the frequency or the color changes in a new medium "Light slows down in glass, so its frequency drops and the color changes" is wrong. The frequency is set by the source and is the same on both sides of the boundary. What changes is the speed ($v = c/n$) and the wavelength ($\lambda = \lambda_0/n$). A second error is to measure the angles from the surface: Snell's law uses angles from the normal, and a ray that is bent toward the normal is made more perpendicular to the surface, not more parallel to it.
常见错误：认为光进入新介质后频率或颜色会改变 "光在玻璃中变慢，所以频率降低、颜色改变"是错误的。频率由光源决定，在界面两侧相同。改变的是速度（$v = c/n$）和波长（$\lambda = \lambda_0/n$）。第二个错误是从界面量角：斯涅尔定律用的是与法线的夹角，光线偏向法线意味着它变得更接近垂直于界面，而不是更接近平行于界面。
Worked Example: Speed, Wavelength and Frequency in Glass例题：玻璃中的速度、波长和频率

Red light of wavelength $650\,\mathrm{nm}$ in air (use $n = 1.00$) enters glass of index $1.50$. Find its frequency, speed and wavelength in the glass. Take $c = 3.00\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. What would the wavelength be in water ($n = 1.33$)?

空气中波长为 $650\,\mathrm{nm}$ 的红光（取 $n = 1.00$）进入折射率为 $1.50$ 的玻璃。求它在玻璃中的频率、速度和波长。取 $c = 3.00\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$。它在水中（$n = 1.33$）的波长是多少？

Step 1: Frequency (unchanged across the boundary)第 1 步：频率（穿过界面时不变）
$$f = \frac{c}{\lambda_0} = \frac{3.00\times10^{8}}{650\times10^{-9}} = 4.62\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$$
Step 2: Speed in glass第 2 步：玻璃中的速度
$$v = \frac{c}{n} = \frac{3.00\times10^{8}}{1.50} = 2.00\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$$
Step 3: Wavelength in glass and in water第 3 步：玻璃和水中的波长
$$\lambda_\text{glass} = \frac{\lambda_0}{n} = \frac{650}{1.50} = 433\,\mathrm{nm}, \qquad \lambda_\text{water} = \frac{650}{1.33} = 489\,\mathrm{nm}$$
Check: $f\lambda = (4.62\times10^{14})(433\times10^{-9}) = 2.00\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, which agrees with $v$. Why this works: $f$ is fixed by the source, so when $v$ drops to $1/n$ of its value, the wavelength drops to $1/n$ of its value as well.验算：$f\lambda = (4.62\times10^{14})(433\times10^{-9}) = 2.00\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$，与 $v$ 一致。为什么这样做：$f$ 由光源决定，所以当 $v$ 减小为原来的 $1/n$ 时，波长也减小为原来的 $1/n$。

A ray in air strikes a flat glass surface ($n = 1.50$) with an angle of incidence of $50^\circ$. Find the angle of refraction and say whether the ray bends toward or away from the normal.

空气中的一条光线以 $50^\circ$ 的入射角射到平整的玻璃表面（$n = 1.50$）上。求折射角，并说明光线偏向还是偏离法线。

Step 2: Solve for the angle第 2 步：求出角度

Worked Example: Glass into Air and the Critical Angle例题：玻璃进入空气与临界角

A ray inside glass ($n = 1.50$) reaches the glass-air surface with an angle of incidence of $25^\circ$. (a) Find the angle of the ray in air. (b) Find the critical angle for the glass-air surface and decide what happens to a ray with an angle of incidence of $50^\circ$.

玻璃（$n = 1.50$）内部的一条光线以 $25^\circ$ 的入射角射到玻璃与空气的界面上。(a) 求光线在空气中的角度。(b) 求玻璃与空气界面的临界角，并判断入射角为 $50^\circ$ 的光线会怎样。

Step 1: Refraction at $25^\circ$第 1 步：$25^\circ$ 时的折射
$$\sin\theta_2 = \frac{1.50}{1.00}\sin 25^\circ = 0.6339 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \theta_2 = 39.3^\circ$$
The ray bends away from the normal, as expected for light going into a lower index.光线偏离法线，符合光射入折射率较小介质的预期。
Step 2: Critical angle第 2 步：临界角
$$\sin\theta_c = \frac{n_2}{n_1} = \frac{1.00}{1.50} = 0.667 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \theta_c = 41.8^\circ$$
Step 3: Compare第 3 步：比较
$50^\circ \gt 41.8^\circ$, so a ray incident at $50^\circ$ is totally reflected: all the light stays in the glass and none is transmitted. The ray at $25^\circ$ is below the critical angle, so it refracts out. Why this works: the same surface both transmits and totally reflects, depending only on the angle of incidence.$50^\circ \gt 41.8^\circ$，所以以 $50^\circ$ 入射的光线发生全反射：所有的光都留在玻璃内，没有光透射。$25^\circ$ 的光线低于临界角，所以折射射出。为什么这样做：同一个界面既可能透射也可能发生全反射，只取决于入射角。
Worked Example: Light Trapped in an Optical Fiber例题：被困在光纤中的光

An optical fiber has a core of index $1.48$ and a cladding of index $1.46$. Find the critical angle at the core-cladding boundary. A ray in the core meets this boundary at an angle of incidence of $85^\circ$. Does it stay in the core?

一根光纤的纤芯折射率为 $1.48$，包层折射率为 $1.46$。求纤芯与包层界面的临界角。纤芯中一条光线以 $85^\circ$ 的入射角射到该界面上。它会留在纤芯里吗？

Step 1: Critical angle (light goes from the core, the higher index, to the cladding)第 1 步：临界角（光从折射率较大的纤芯射向包层）
$$\sin\theta_c = \frac{1.46}{1.48} = 0.9865 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \theta_c = 80.6^\circ$$
Step 2: Compare第 2 步：比较
$85^\circ \gt 80.6^\circ$, so the ray is totally internally reflected and stays in the core. Only rays that travel nearly parallel to the fiber axis meet the boundary at such large angles of incidence, and that is why a fiber carries light around bends with almost no loss. Why this works: a small index difference gives a large critical angle, so the ray must meet the boundary at a glancing angle.$85^\circ \gt 80.6^\circ$，所以光线发生全反射，留在纤芯中。只有几乎平行于光纤轴传播的光线才会以这么大的入射角射到界面上，这就是光纤能让光绕过弯曲处而几乎没有损耗的原因。为什么这样做：折射率差很小时，临界角很大，所以光线必须以接近掠入射的角度射到界面。

Converging and Diverging Lenses会聚透镜与发散透镜

A lens is a piece of transparent material with curved surfaces that refracts light at both surfaces.

A thin lens is one whose thickness is small compared with the other distances in the problem, so we can pretend all the bending happens at a single plane through the middle of the lens, the lens midline, and we measure all distances from it.

The principal axis is the line through the center of the lens, perpendicular to the midline.

透镜（lens）是带曲面的透明材料，光在两个表面上都发生折射。

薄透镜（thin lens）的厚度比问题中的其他距离小得多，所以可以认为所有的折射都发生在穿过透镜中部的同一个平面上，即透镜的中线，所有距离都从中线量起。

主光轴（principal axis）是过透镜中心、垂直于中线的直线。

A thin convex lens (a converging lens) is thicker at the middle than at the edges. Incident rays parallel to the principal axis are refracted and converge toward one common location on the transmitted side of the lens, the focal point.

A thin concave lens (a diverging lens) is thinner at the middle. Incident rays parallel to the principal axis are refracted and diverge, as if they had originated from a focal point on the incident side of the lens.

Snell's law at each curved surface explains this: a ray that meets the surface away from the axis meets it at an angle of incidence that increases with the distance from the axis, so it is bent more, and the bending is toward the axis for a convex lens and away from the axis for a concave lens.

薄凸透镜（convex lens，会聚透镜，converging lens）中间比边缘厚。平行于主光轴入射的光线被折射后，会聚到透镜透射一侧的同一个位置，即焦点（focal point）。

薄凹透镜（concave lens，发散透镜，diverging lens）中间较薄。平行于主光轴入射的光线被折射后发散，看起来像是从透镜入射一侧的焦点发出的。

在每个曲面上用斯涅尔定律就能解释这一点：离轴越远的光线在表面上的入射角越大，所以折射得越多，对凸透镜是偏向轴，对凹透镜是偏离轴。

Two Focal Points, One Focal Length两个焦点，一个焦距

A lens has a focal point on both sides, one on each side of the lens, because light can enter from either side. Each side of the lens has a surface, curved or flat, and the shapes of both surfaces together set where the focal points lie.

For a thin lens surrounded by one medium such as air, the result is a single focal length: the two focal points are at the same distance $f$ from the midline, whatever the curvatures of the two surfaces (a lens with one flat side included).

The value of $f$ is set by the curvatures of the two surfaces and by the index of refraction of the lens material: more strongly curved surfaces and a larger index of refraction give a shorter $f$. The focal length $f$ is the distance from the midline to either focal point.

透镜两侧各有一个焦点（a focal point on both sides），因为光可以从任一侧射入。透镜的每一侧都有一个表面（曲面或平面），两个表面的形状共同决定焦点的位置。

对处于同一种介质（如空气）中的薄透镜，结果只有一个焦距：无论两个曲面的曲率如何（包括一侧为平面的透镜），两个焦点到中线的距离都相同，都是 $f$。

$f$ 的大小由两个曲面的曲率和透镜材料的折射率决定：曲面弯曲得越厉害、折射率越大，$f$ 越短。焦距（focal length）$f$ 是从中线到任一焦点的距离。

Real and Virtual Images from a Lens透镜形成的实像与虚像

A real image is formed by a lens when rays that start from a common point on the object are refracted so that they intersect at another common point on the transmitted side; a screen placed there shows the image.

A virtual image is formed when the refracted rays diverge, so that they appear to have originated from a common point; it can be seen by looking through the lens but cannot be projected on a screen.

As with mirrors, an image is described as real or virtual, upright or inverted, and reduced, enlarged (magnified) or the same size.

当从物体上同一点发出的光线经折射后，在透射一侧相交于另一点时，透镜形成实像（real image）；放在那里的屏幕上能显示出这个像。

当折射光线发散、看起来像是从同一点发出时，形成虚像（virtual image）；透过透镜能看到它，但不能把它投射到屏幕上。

与镜子一样，像要描述为实或虚、正立或倒立，以及缩小、放大（magnified）或等大。

The Thin-Lens Equation and Magnification薄透镜方程与放大率

For a thin lens, the location of the image depends on the focal length and on the distance between the object and the midline of the lens, as given by the thin-lens equation. It has the same form as the mirror equation and uses the same sign convention (Table 13.1 in Topic 13.2):

  • $s_\text{o}$ is positive for a real object on the incident side.
  • $s_\text{i}$ is positive for a real image, on the transmitted side of the lens, and negative for a virtual image, on the incident side.
  • $f$ is positive for a convex (converging) lens and negative for a concave (diverging) lens.
  • $M$ is negative for an inverted image and positive for an upright one.

对薄透镜，像的位置取决于焦距，以及物体到透镜中线的距离，由薄透镜方程（thin-lens equation）给出。它与镜面方程的形式相同，并使用相同的符号约定（sign convention，见专题 13.2 的表 13.1）：

  • 入射一侧实物体的 $s_\text{o}$ 为正。
  • 实像的 $s_\text{i}$ 为正，实像在透镜的透射一侧；虚像则为负，在入射一侧。
  • 凸（会聚）透镜的 $f$ 为正，凹（发散）透镜则为负。
  • 倒立像的 $M$ 为负，正立像则为正。
Thin-lens equation薄透镜方程
$$\frac{1}{s_\text{i}} + \frac{1}{s_\text{o}} = \frac{1}{f}$$

$s_\text{o}$ is the object distance, $s_\text{i}$ the image distance and $f$ the focal length, all measured from the midline of the lens, in the same unit (cm or m), and all signed by the convention above.

$s_\text{o}$ 是物距，$s_\text{i}$ 是像距，$f$ 是焦距，都从透镜中线量起，单位相同（cm 或 m），并按上述约定带符号。

Magnification of a thin lens薄透镜的放大率
$$M = \frac{h_\text{i}}{h_\text{o}} = -\frac{s_\text{i}}{s_\text{o}}$$

$M$ is the magnification (no unit), the ratio of the image height $h_\text{i}$ to the object height $h_\text{o}$. It depends on where the object and the image are located relative to the lens. A negative $M$ means an inverted image, and $|M| \gt 1$ means an enlarged one.

$M$ 是放大率（无单位），等于像高 $h_\text{i}$ 与物高 $h_\text{o}$ 之比。它取决于物体和像相对于透镜的位置。$M$ 为负表示倒立的像，$|M| \gt 1$ 表示放大的像。

Ray Diagrams with the Three Principal Rays用三条主光线作图

The three principal rays from the tip of the object are:

  1. The ray parallel to the principal axis is refracted through the focal point on the transmitted side. For a diverging lens, it is refracted as if it came from the focal point on the incident side.
  2. The ray that passes through the center of the lens, where the principal axis intersects the lens, continues in a straight line with almost no change of direction, because a thin lens is nearly flat there.
  3. The ray that passes through the focal point on the incident side (for a diverging lens, the ray aimed at the focal point on the transmitted side) is refracted parallel to the principal axis.

The image tip is where the refracted rays, or their dashed backward extensions, meet.

从物体顶端发出的三条主光线（principal rays）是：

  1. 平行于主光轴的光线，折射后经过透射一侧的焦点。对发散透镜，则折射后看起来像是从入射一侧的焦点发出。
  2. 经过透镜中心（主光轴与透镜的交点）的光线，由于薄透镜在那里近似为平板，它几乎不改变方向，沿直线继续前进。
  3. 经过入射一侧焦点的光线（对发散透镜，则是指向透射一侧焦点的光线），折射后平行于主光轴。

像的顶端在折射光线（或用虚线画出的反向延长线）的交点处。

Worked Example: Finding f from Data (Linearization)例题：由数据求 f（线性化）

Explain why a thin lens has a focal point on each side, and what determines the focal length.解释为什么薄透镜两侧各有一个焦点，以及是什么决定了焦距。

Light can arrive at a lens from either side, and for each direction rays that are parallel to the principal axis are brought together (or made to spread out as if from a point) by refraction at the two curved surfaces. So a lens has a focal point on both sides.

For a thin lens in one surrounding medium, the two focal points are at the same distance $f$ from the lens, whatever the curvatures of the two surfaces.

The focal length $f$ is set by the curvatures (shapes) of the two surfaces and by the index of refraction of the lens material: more strongly curved surfaces bend rays more and give a shorter $f$.

光可以从任一侧射到透镜上，对每个方向，平行于主光轴的光线都会因两个曲面上的折射而会聚（或散开成像是从一点发出）。所以透镜两侧都有焦点。

对处于同一种介质中的薄透镜，两个焦点到透镜的距离都是 $f$，与两个曲面的曲率无关。

焦距 $f$ 由两个曲面的曲率（形状）和透镜材料的折射率决定：曲面弯曲得越厉害，光线偏折得越多，$f$ 越短。

A converging lens with $f = 8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ is used as a magnifying glass for a stamp $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens. Find $s_\text{i}$ and $M$, and justify that the image is virtual.焦距 $f = 8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ 的会聚透镜用作放大镜，邮票距透镜 $6.0\,\mathrm{cm}$。求 $s_\text{i}$ 和 $M$，并论证这个像是虚像。

$1/s_\text{i} = 1/8.0 - 1/6.0 = -1/24$, so $s_\text{i} = -24\,\mathrm{cm}$, and $M = -(-24)/6.0 = +4.0$. The image distance is negative, so the image is virtual and on the same side as the stamp. This is expected because $s_\text{o} = 6.0\,\mathrm{cm} \lt f = 8.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. The image is upright and four times as large.

A converging lens projects a real image of a lamp onto a screen $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens, and the image is three times as tall as the lamp. Find the object distance and the focal length of the lens.会聚透镜把灯的实像投射到距透镜 $40\,\mathrm{cm}$ 的屏幕上，像的高度是灯的三倍。求物距和透镜的焦距。

Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Measuring angles from the surface instead of the normal.
  2. Dropping the minus sign of a virtual image distance or of a diverging focal length.
  3. Treating a convex mirror like a concave mirror, or a concave lens like a convex lens, in the sign of $f$.
  4. Claiming that the frequency of light changes in glass: the speed and the wavelength change, the frequency does not.
  5. Looking for total internal reflection going into a higher index: it needs a lower index and an angle beyond $\theta_c$.
  6. Thinking that covering half of a lens removes half of the image: it only makes the image dimmer.
最常见的错误
  1. 从界面而不是法线量角。
  2. 丢掉虚像像距或发散透镜焦距的负号。
  3. 在 $f$ 的符号上，把凸面镜当成凹面镜，或把凹透镜当成凸透镜。
  4. 声称光在玻璃中频率改变：改变的是速度和波长，频率不变。

What is a light ray?什么是光线？
A straight line perpendicular to the wavefront, pointing in the direction of travel. Geometric optics neglects the wave nature of light.垂直于波前、指向传播方向的直线。几何光学忽略光的波动性。
Law of reflection?反射定律？
$$\theta_i = \theta_r$$Both angles are measured from the normal.两个角都从法线量起。
Specular versus diffuse reflection?镜面反射与漫反射？
Specular: smooth surface, nearly constant normal, parallel rays stay parallel. Diffuse: rough surface, varying normal, rays scatter.镜面反射：表面光滑，法线近似不变，平行光线仍然平行。漫反射：表面粗糙，法线不断变化，光线向各方散射。

$$\frac{1}{s_\text{i}} + \frac{1}{s_\text{o}} = \frac{1}{f}$$Real image: $s_\text{i} \gt 0$. Virtual image: $s_\text{i} \lt 0$.实像：$s_\text{i} \gt 0$。虚像：$s_\text{i} \lt 0$。
Magnification?放大率？
$$M = \frac{h_\text{i}}{h_\text{o}} = -\frac{s_\text{i}}{s_\text{o}}$$$M \lt 0$ inverted, $M \gt 0$ upright, $|M| \gt 1$ enlarged.$M \lt 0$ 倒立，$M \gt 0$ 正立，$|M| \gt 1$ 放大。
Sign of $f$?$f$ 的符号？
Positive for a concave mirror and a convex lens (converging); negative for a convex mirror and a concave lens (diverging). For a spherical mirror $|f| = R/2$.凹面镜和凸透镜（会聚）为正；凸面镜和凹透镜（发散）为负。球面镜 $|f| = R/2$。
Plane mirror image?平面镜的像？
$$s_\text{i} = -s_\text{o}, \quad M = +1$$Virtual, upright, same size, as far behind the mirror as the object is in front.虚像、正立、等大，像在镜后的距离等于物在镜前的距离。
The three principal rays?三条主光线？
(1) Parallel to the axis, through the focal point. (2) Through the center of the mirror or lens. (3) Through the focal point, then parallel to the axis.(1) 平行于轴，经过焦点。(2) 经过镜面或透镜的中心。(3) 经过焦点，然后平行于轴。
Index of refraction?折射率？
$$n = \frac{c}{v}$$Speed and wavelength drop by $1/n$; the frequency is unchanged.速度和波长变为原来的 $1/n$；频率不变。
Snell's law?斯涅尔定律？
$$n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2$$Into a higher $n$: toward the normal. Into a lower $n$: away from the normal.进入较大的 $n$：偏向法线。进入较小的 $n$：偏离法线。
Critical angle and total internal reflection?临界角与全反射？
$$\sin\theta_c = \frac{n_2}{n_1}$$Only for $n_1 \gt n_2$. Beyond $\theta_c$ all the light is reflected.只适用于 $n_1 \gt n_2$。超过 $\theta_c$ 时所有的光都被反射。
Converging element, object beyond $f$ versus inside $f$?会聚元件：物体在 $f$ 之外与在 $f$ 之内？
Beyond $f$: real, inverted image. Inside $f$: virtual, upright, enlarged image. At $f$: no image.在 $f$ 之外：倒立实像。在 $f$ 之内：正立、放大的虚像。在 $f$ 处：不成像。
Diverging mirror or lens: the image of a real object?发散的镜子或透镜：实物体的像？
Always virtual, upright and reduced, so $s_\text{i} \lt 0$ and $0 \lt M \lt 1$.总是正立、缩小的虚像，所以 $s_\text{i} \lt 0$，$0 \lt M \lt 1$。

$3.00\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
$1.60\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
$1.88\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$
Correct! $v = c/n = (3.00\times10^{8})/1.60 = 1.88\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.正确！$v = c/n = (3.00\times10^{8})/1.60 = 1.88\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$。
The speed is inversely proportional to $n$: $v = c/n = 1.88\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Multiplying by $n$ gives $4.80\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, faster than $c$, which is impossible, and $1.60\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ just reuses the digits of $n$.速度与 $n$ 成反比：$v = c/n = 1.88\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$。乘以 $n$ 得 $4.80\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$，比 $c$ 还大，这是不可能的；$1.60\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 只是照搬了 $n$ 的数字。
7. A ray in air strikes the flat surface of a liquid at an angle of incidence of $60^\circ$ and refracts at $40^\circ$. What is the index of refraction of the liquid?空气中的一条光线以 $60^\circ$ 的入射角射到液体的平面上，折射角为 $40^\circ$。液体的折射率是多少？
$1.35$
$1.50$
$0.74$
$0.67$
Correct! $n = \sin 60^\circ/\sin 40^\circ = 0.8660/0.6428 = 1.35$.正确！$n = \sin 60^\circ/\sin 40^\circ = 0.8660/0.6428 = 1.35$。
Snell's law uses the sines of the angles: $n = \sin 60^\circ/\sin 40^\circ = 1.35$. Dividing the angles ($60/40 = 1.50$) is wrong because the law uses the sines, not the angles themselves. The value $0.74$ is the inverted sine ratio, $\sin 40^\circ/\sin 60^\circ$, and $0.67$ is the inverted angle ratio, $40/60$; both would make $n \lt 1$, which means light faster than $c$.斯涅尔定律用的是角的正弦：$n = \sin 60^\circ/\sin 40^\circ = 1.35$。直接用角度相除（$60/40 = 1.50$）是错的，因为定律用的是角的正弦，而不是角本身。$0.74$ 是把正弦之比倒过来，即 $\sin 40^\circ/\sin 60^\circ$；$0.67$ 是把角度之比倒过来，即 $40/60$；两者都会使 $n \lt 1$，即光速超过 $c$。
8. What is the critical angle for light going from a glass of index $1.60$ into air?光从折射率为 $1.60$ 的玻璃射入空气时，临界角是多少？
$51.3^\circ$
$32.0^\circ$
$38.7^\circ$
None exists不存在
Correct! $\sin\theta_c = n_2/n_1 = 1.00/1.60 = 0.625$, so $\theta_c = 38.7^\circ$.正确！$\sin\theta_c = n_2/n_1 = 1.00/1.60 = 0.625$，所以 $\theta_c = 38.7^\circ$。
Light goes from a higher to a lower index, so a critical angle exists: $\sin\theta_c = 1.00/1.60 = 0.625$ gives $38.7^\circ$. The value $51.3^\circ$ is $90^\circ - 38.7^\circ$, the angle with the surface, and $32.0^\circ$ comes from using the tangent instead of the sine.光从折射率较大的介质射向较小的介质，所以存在临界角：$\sin\theta_c = 1.00/1.60 = 0.625$，得 $38.7^\circ$。$51.3^\circ$ 是 $90^\circ - 38.7^\circ$，即与界面的夹角；$32.0^\circ$ 来自误用正切代替正弦。
9. Light passes from air into glass. Which of these quantities is the same on both sides of the boundary?光从空气进入玻璃。下列哪个量在界面两侧相同？
The wavelength波长
The frequency频率
The speed速度
None: all three change都不相同：三个量都改变
Correct! The source sets the frequency, so it is unchanged, while $v = c/n$ and $\lambda = \lambda_0/n$ both decrease.正确！频率由光源决定，所以不变；而 $v = c/n$ 和 $\lambda = \lambda_0/n$ 都减小。
Both the speed and the wavelength change by the factor $1/n$, but the number of wave crests that cross the boundary each second does not change, so the frequency is the same. Saying that all three change confuses the color of light, which is fixed by the frequency, with its speed.速度和波长都变为原来的 $1/n$，但每秒穿过界面的波峰数不变，所以频率相同。说三个量都改变，是把由频率决定的光的颜色与光的速度混为一谈。

10. A converging lens has a focal length of $20\,\mathrm{cm}$. An object is $30\,\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens. What is the image distance $s_\text{i}$?一个会聚透镜的焦距为 $20\,\mathrm{cm}$。物体距透镜 $30\,\mathrm{cm}$。像距 $s_\text{i}$ 是多少？
$+60\,\mathrm{cm}$
$+12\,\mathrm{cm}$
$-60\,\mathrm{cm}$
$+50\,\mathrm{cm}$
Correct! $1/s_\text{i} = 1/20 - 1/30 = 1/60$, so $s_\text{i} = +60\,\mathrm{cm}$ (real), with $M = -2$.正确！$1/s_\text{i} = 1/20 - 1/30 = 1/60$，所以 $s_\text{i} = +60\,\mathrm{cm}$（实像），$M = -2$。
The thin-lens equation gives $1/s_\text{i} = 1/f - 1/s_\text{o} = 1/60$. Adding the reciprocals of $f$ and $s_\text{o}$ gives $12\,\mathrm{cm}$, $f + s_\text{o} = 50\,\mathrm{cm}$ is not an equation of the lens, and a negative sign would mean a virtual image, which needs $s_\text{o} \lt f$.薄透镜方程给出 $1/s_\text{i} = 1/f - 1/s_\text{o} = 1/60$。把 $f$ 与 $s_\text{o}$ 的倒数相加会得到 $12\,\mathrm{cm}$；$f + s_\text{o} = 50\,\mathrm{cm}$ 不是透镜的方程；而负号表示虚像，那需要 $s_\text{o} \lt f$。
11. A magnifying glass is a converging lens of focal length $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. A coin is held $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ from it. What is the magnification $M$?放大镜是焦距 $5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ 的会聚透镜。把一枚硬币放在距它 $4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ 处。放大率 $M$ 是多少？
$-5.0$
$+1.25$
$+0.80$
$+5.0$
Correct! $1/s_\text{i} = 1/5.0 - 1/4.0 = -1/20$, so $s_\text{i} = -20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and $M = -(-20)/4.0 = +5.0$: virtual, upright and enlarged.正确！$1/s_\text{i} = 1/5.0 - 1/4.0 = -1/20$，所以 $s_\text{i} = -20\,\mathrm{cm}$，$M = -(-20)/4.0 = +5.0$：虚像、正立、放大。
The coin is inside the focal length, so the image is virtual ($s_\text{i} = -20\,\mathrm{cm}$) and upright, and $M = +5.0$. Using $-5.0$ forgets that a negative $s_\text{i}$ makes $M$ positive, and $1.25 = f/s_\text{o}$ and $0.80 = s_\text{o}/f$ are ratios that are not the magnification.硬币在焦距以内，所以像是虚像（$s_\text{i} = -20\,\mathrm{cm}$）且正立，$M = +5.0$。取 $-5.0$ 是忘了 $s_\text{i}$ 为负会使 $M$ 为正；$1.25 = f/s_\text{o}$ 和 $0.80 = s_\text{o}/f$ 是比值，并不是放大率。
12. Which description applies to the image of a real object formed by any diverging lens?下列哪个描述适用于任何发散透镜对实物体所成的像？
Real, inverted and reduced倒立、缩小的实像
Virtual, upright and reduced正立、缩小的虚像
Virtual, upright and enlarged正立、放大的虚像
Real, upright and enlarged正立、放大的实像
Correct! With $f \lt 0$ and $s_\text{o} \gt 0$, $1/s_\text{i} = 1/f - 1/s_\text{o}$ is negative, so $s_\text{i} \lt 0$ (virtual), and $|s_\text{i}| \lt s_\text{o}$ gives $0 \lt M \lt 1$ (upright, reduced).正确！$f \lt 0$ 且 $s_\text{o} \gt 0$ 时，$1/s_\text{i} = 1/f - 1/s_\text{o}$ 为负，所以 $s_\text{i} \lt 0$（虚像），而 $|s_\text{i}| \lt s_\text{o}$ 给出 $0 \lt M \lt 1$（正立、缩小）。
A diverging lens spreads the rays, so they cannot meet to form a real image, and it never enlarges. Enlarged images are given by a converging lens used inside $f$, which is a different case. For a diverging lens, $s_\text{i}$ is always negative and $M$ is always between $0$ and $1$.发散透镜使光线散开，不可能相交成实像，也从不放大。放大的像是会聚透镜在 $f$ 以内使用时得到的，那是另一种情形。对发散透镜，$s_\text{i}$ 总是负的，$M$ 总在 $0$ 与 $1$ 之间。
