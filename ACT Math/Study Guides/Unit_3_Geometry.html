Angle vocabulary and the three sums角的术语与三个基本和

An angle is acute if it is less than $90^\circ$, right if it equals $90^\circ$, obtuse if it is between $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$, and straight if it equals $180^\circ$. Two angles are complementary when they add to $90^\circ$ and supplementary when they add to $180^\circ$. Three sums come up constantly: angles on a straight line add to $180^\circ$ (a linear pair is supplementary), angles around a point add to $360^\circ$, and the angles of a triangle add to $180^\circ$ (see 3.2).

角小于 $90^\circ$ 为锐角 (acute)，等于 $90^\circ$ 为直角 (right)，介于 $90^\circ$ 与 $180^\circ$ 之间为钝角 (obtuse)，等于 $180^\circ$ 为平角 (straight)。两个角之和为 $90^\circ$ 时互余 (complementary)，之和为 $180^\circ$ 时互补 (supplementary)。三个和经常用到：一条直线上的角之和为 $180^\circ$（邻补角互补），一点周围的角之和为 $360^\circ$，三角形内角和为 $180^\circ$（见 3.2）。

$$\text{complement of }x=90^\circ-x,\qquad \text{supplement of }x=180^\circ-x$$
$$\text{complement of }x=90^\circ-x,\qquad \text{supplement of }x=180^\circ-x$$

Vertical angles and parallel lines with a transversal对顶角，以及平行线与截线

When two lines cross, the two angles that face each other are vertical angles and are equal; each angle is supplementary to the two angles next to it. A transversal is a line that crosses two or more other lines. When the two lines it crosses are parallel (never meet), the eight angles take only two values, one acute (or both right) and one obtuse, and the pairs in the table are related as shown. In Figure 3.1, angle 4 is $70^\circ$ and every other angle follows from it.

两条直线相交时，面对面的两个角是对顶角 (vertical angles)，大小相等；每个角与相邻的两个角互补。截线 (transversal) 是与另外两条或多条直线相交的直线。当被截的两条直线平行 (parallel，永不相交) 时，八个角只有两种大小，一种锐角（或都是直角），一种钝角，各对角的关系见下表。在图 3.1 中，4 号角为 $70^\circ$，其余各角都由它推出。

Worked Example 3.1.1: complement and supplement equations例题 3.1.1：余角与补角方程

An angle measures $15^\circ$ less than four times its complement. Find the angle.

  1. Let the angle be $x$. Its complement is $90-x$.
  2. Translate: $x=4(90-x)-15$.
  3. Expand: $x=360-4x-15$, so $5x=345$ and $x=69$.
  4. Check: the complement is $21^\circ$ and $4(21)-15=69$. The angle is $69^\circ$.

一个角比它的余角的四倍少 $15^\circ$。求这个角。

  1. 设这个角为 $x$，它的余角是 $90-x$。
  2. 列方程：$x=4(90-x)-15$。
  3. 展开：$x=360-4x-15$，所以 $5x=345$，$x=69$。
  4. 检验：余角为 $21^\circ$，$4(21)-15=69$。这个角是 $69^\circ$。
Worked Example 3.1.2: parallel lines with algebra例题 3.1.2：平行线与代数

Lines $l$ and $m$ are parallel and are cut by a transversal. A pair of same-side interior angles measures $(3x+10)^\circ$ and $(5x-30)^\circ$. Find $x$ and both angles.

  1. Same-side interior angles of parallel lines are supplementary, so $(3x+10)+(5x-30)=180$.
  2. Combine: $8x-20=180$, so $8x=200$ and $x=25$.
  3. The angles are $3(25)+10=85$ and $5(25)-30=95$, which add to $180$.
  4. Had the pair been alternate interior angles, the equation would have been $3x+10=5x-30$ instead. Naming the pair first decides which equation to write.

直线 $l$ 与 $m$ 平行，被一条截线所截。一对同旁内角的度数为 $(3x+10)^\circ$ 和 $(5x-30)^\circ$。求 $x$ 和这两个角。

  1. 平行线的同旁内角互补，所以 $(3x+10)+(5x-30)=180$。
  2. 合并：$8x-20=180$，所以 $8x=200$，$x=25$。
  3. 两个角分别为 $3(25)+10=85$ 和 $5(25)-30=95$，和为 $180$。
  4. 如果这对角是内错角，方程就应该是 $3x+10=5x-30$。先说出角的关系，才能决定写哪个方程。
Common Mistake: watch for these
  • Calling same-side interior angles equal. They are supplementary; alternate interior and corresponding angles are the equal ones.
  • Using a parallel-line rule when the problem never says the lines are parallel.
  • Reading an angle size off the diagram instead of from the stated numbers.
  • Confusing complementary ($90^\circ$) with supplementary ($180^\circ$).
常见错误：注意这些
  • 把同旁内角当作相等。它们互补；相等的是内错角和同位角。
  • 题目没有说两线平行，却使用平行线的性质。
  • 直接从图上“量”角度，而不是用题目给出的数值。
  • 混淆互余（$90^\circ$）与互补（$180^\circ$）。

Exam tip考试提示

Similar-triangle questions are fastest when you write the similarity statement with matching vertex order first, for example $\triangle ADE\sim\triangle ABC$, and then read the ratios straight off it. If a picture shows a segment parallel to one side, that parallel line is the reason the triangles are similar: say it to yourself and use whole sides ($AB$), not pieces ($DB$). A calculator helps with decimal proportions, but cross-multiplying by hand is usually quicker for whole numbers.

相似三角形题最快的做法：先按对应顶点顺序写出相似式，例如 $\triangle ADE\sim\triangle ABC$，再直接从中读出比例。如果图中有一条线段平行于某一边，这条平行线 (parallel) 就是三角形相似的理由：要用整条边（$AB$），不要用一段（$DB$）。计算器 (calculator) 适合小数比例，但整数时手算交叉相乘通常更快。

Angle sum, exterior angles and isosceles triangles内角和、外角与等腰三角形

The interior angles of every triangle add to $180^\circ$ (the angle sum). The reason comes from Section 3.1: draw a line through one vertex parallel to the opposite side; the two alternate interior angles it creates, together with the vertex angle, fill a straight line. An exterior angle is formed by extending one side. It forms a linear pair with its adjacent interior angle, so it equals the sum of the two remote (non-adjacent) interior angles.

每个三角形的内角和都是 $180^\circ$（angle sum）。理由来自第 3.1 节：过一个顶点作对边的平行线，产生的两个内错角与顶角恰好组成一个平角。把一条边延长就得到外角 (exterior angle)。它与相邻的内角互补，所以等于两个不相邻内角 (remote interior angles) 的和。

$$\angle A+\angle B+\angle C=180^\circ,\qquad \text{exterior angle at }C=\angle A+\angle B$$
$$\angle A+\angle B+\angle C=180^\circ,\qquad \text{exterior angle at }C=\angle A+\angle B$$

In an isosceles triangle two sides are equal, and the angles opposite those sides (the base angles) are equal. In an equilateral triangle all three sides are equal, so every angle is $60^\circ$. More generally, the longest side is opposite the largest angle.

等腰三角形 (isosceles) 有两条边相等，这两条边所对的角（底角）相等。等边三角形 (equilateral) 三边相等，所以每个角都是 $60^\circ$。更一般地，最长的边对着最大的角。

Worked Example 3.2.1: exterior angle equation例题 3.2.1：外角方程

Two interior angles of a triangle measure $(2x)^\circ$ and $(3x+10)^\circ$. The exterior angle at the third vertex measures $120^\circ$. Find all three interior angles.

  1. The exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles: $2x+(3x+10)=120$.
  2. Solve: $5x+10=120$, so $5x=110$ and $x=22$.
  3. The two angles are $2(22)=44^\circ$ and $3(22)+10=76^\circ$.
  4. The third interior angle forms a linear pair with the exterior angle: $180-120=60^\circ$.
  5. Check: $44+76+60=180$. Why this works: the exterior angle and the third angle add to $180^\circ$, and all three interior angles also add to $180^\circ$, so the exterior angle must equal the other two.

三角形的两个内角为 $(2x)^\circ$ 和 $(3x+10)^\circ$，第三个顶点处的外角为 $120^\circ$。求三个内角。

  1. 外角等于两个不相邻内角的和：$2x+(3x+10)=120$。
  2. 解方程：$5x+10=120$，所以 $5x=110$，$x=22$。
  3. 两个角为 $2(22)=44^\circ$ 和 $3(22)+10=76^\circ$。
  4. 第三个内角与外角互补：$180-120=60^\circ$。
  5. 检验：$44+76+60=180$。原理：外角与第三个内角之和是 $180^\circ$，三个内角之和也是 $180^\circ$，所以外角必然等于另外两个内角之和。

Congruent triangles全等三角形

Two triangles are congruent when all corresponding sides and all corresponding angles are equal: one is an exact copy of the other, possibly moved, turned or flipped. You do not need all six facts. Any of these is enough: SSS (three sides), SAS (two sides and the angle between them), ASA (two angles and the side between them), AAS (two angles and a non-included side), and HL for right triangles (hypotenuse and one leg). SSA is not a test, because two different triangles can share two sides and a non-included angle; AAA only proves the shape is the same (similar), not the size.

两个三角形的对应边和对应角都相等时，它们全等 (congruent)：一个是另一个的精确复制，可能经过平移、旋转或翻折。不需要六个条件全部知道，以下任意一种就够了：SSS（三边）、SAS（两边及其夹角）、ASA（两角及其夹边）、AAS（两角及一条非夹边），以及直角三角形的 HL（斜边和一条直角边）。SSA 不是判定方法，因为两边和一个非夹角可以对应两个不同的三角形；AAA 只能说明形状相同（相似），不能说明大小相同。

Once triangles are congruent, every pair of corresponding parts is equal, which is how a congruence lets you transfer a length or angle from one triangle to the other.

一旦三角形全等，所有对应部分都相等，这就是利用全等把一条边或一个角从一个三角形“搬”到另一个三角形的方法。

Similar triangles and the scale factor相似三角形与相似比

Triangles are similar when their corresponding angles are equal; then every pair of corresponding sides is in the same ratio $k$, the scale factor. Two pairs of equal angles (AA) are enough, since the third pair then matches by the angle sum. A segment drawn parallel to one side of a triangle cuts off a smaller triangle similar to the whole one, because the parallel line creates equal corresponding angles. Lengths scale by $k$, areas by $k^2$ (and, in Section 3.7, volumes by $k^3$).

对应角相等的三角形相似 (similar)；此时每一对对应边的比都相同，这个比 $k$ 叫相似比 (scale factor)。只要两组角相等（AA）就够了，因为由内角和可知第三组角也相等。在三角形中作一条平行于某边的线段，会截出一个与原三角形相似的小三角形，因为平行线产生相等的同位角。长度按 $k$ 缩放，面积按 $k^2$ 缩放（第 3.7 节中，体积按 $k^3$ 缩放）。

Worked Example 3.2.2: parallel segment inside a triangle例题 3.2.2：三角形内的平行线段

In Figure 3.2, $DE\parallel BC$, $AD=4$, $DB=6$, $DE=5$ and $AE=6$. Find $BC$, $EC$, and the ratio of the area of $\triangle ADE$ to the area of $\triangle ABC$.

  1. Similarity statement: $\triangle ADE\sim\triangle ABC$ (they share $\angle A$, and $\angle ADE=\angle ABC$ as corresponding angles).
  2. Use the whole side: $AB=4+6=10$, so $k=\dfrac{AB}{AD}=\dfrac{10}{4}=2.5$.
  3. $BC=2.5\times DE=2.5\times 5=12.5$.
  4. $AC=2.5\times AE=2.5\times 6=15$, so $EC=15-6=9$. (Check: $AD:DB=4:6$ and $AE:EC=6:9$ are both $2:3$.)
  5. Area ratio $=\left(\dfrac{4}{10}\right)^2=\dfrac{16}{100}=\dfrac{4}{25}$, so the small triangle has $\dfrac{4}{25}$ of the area of the large one.
  6. Why this works: equal angles force every side to stretch by the same factor, and area, being length times length, stretches by that factor squared.

在图 3.2 中，$DE\parallel BC$，$AD=4$，$DB=6$，$DE=5$，$AE=6$。求 $BC$、$EC$，以及 $\triangle ADE$ 与 $\triangle ABC$ 的面积比。

  1. 相似式：$\triangle ADE\sim\triangle ABC$（公共角 $\angle A$，且 $\angle ADE=\angle ABC$ 是同位角）。
  2. 用整条边：$AB=4+6=10$，所以 $k=\dfrac{AB}{AD}=\dfrac{10}{4}=2.5$。
  3. $BC=2.5\times DE=2.5\times 5=12.5$。

The Pythagorean theorem勾股定理

In a right triangle with legs $a$ and $b$, the side opposite the right angle is the hypotenuse $c$, always the longest side. The Pythagorean theorem says $a^2+b^2=c^2$. Why: four copies of the triangle arranged inside a square of side $a+b$ leave a tilted square of area $c^2$, and the same big square can be rearranged to leave $a^2+b^2$. The converse is also true: if $a^2+b^2=c^2$, the angle opposite $c$ is a right angle. Whole-number solutions (Pythagorean triples) appear often: $3,4,5$; $5,12,13$; $8,15,17$; $7,24,25$, and their multiples such as $6,8,10$.

在直角三角形中，两条直角边为 $a$、$b$，直角所对的边是斜边 (hypotenuse) $c$，它总是最长边。勾股定理 (Pythagorean theorem) 说 $a^2+b^2=c^2$。原因：在边长为 $a+b$ 的正方形中摆放四个这样的三角形，剩下一个面积为 $c^2$ 的斜放正方形；同一个大正方形重新摆放后剩下的是 $a^2+b^2$。逆定理同样成立：若 $a^2+b^2=c^2$，则 $c$ 所对的角是直角。常见的整数解（勾股数）有：$3,4,5$；$5,12,13$；$8,15,17$；$7,24,25$，以及它们的倍数，如 $6,8,10$。

Special right triangles: 45-45-90 and 30-60-90特殊直角三角形：45-45-90 与 30-60-90

Sine, cosine and tangent正弦、余弦与正切

For an acute angle $\theta$ in a right triangle, name the sides relative to $\theta$: opposite, adjacent and the hypotenuse. Because all right triangles with the same angle $\theta$ are similar, these ratios depend only on $\theta$, not on the triangle's size. That is why a calculator can give one value for each angle.

对于直角三角形中的锐角 $\theta$，相对 $\theta$ 给边命名：对边 (opposite)、邻边 (adjacent) 和斜边。由于含有同一个角 $\theta$ 的直角三角形都相似，这些比值只取决于 $\theta$，与三角形大小无关。所以计算器能对每个角给出唯一的值。

$$\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}},\qquad \cos\theta=\frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}},\qquad \tan\theta=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}$$
$$\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}},\qquad \cos\theta=\frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}},\qquad \tan\theta=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}$$

Two consequences are worth knowing: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$, and the two acute angles are complementary, so $\sin\theta=\cos(90^\circ-\theta)$. To find an angle from a ratio, use the inverse key: if $\tan\theta=0.75$, then $\theta=\tan^{-1}(0.75)\approx 36.87^\circ$.

有两个推论值得记住：$\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$；两个锐角互余，所以 $\sin\theta=\cos(90^\circ-\theta)$。已知比值求角时用反函数键：若 $\tan\theta=0.75$，则 $\theta=\tan^{-1}(0.75)\approx 36.87^\circ$。

Radians and the unit circle弧度与单位圆

A radian is the angle that cuts off an arc equal to the radius. A full turn is an arc of $2\pi r$, so $360^\circ=2\pi$ radians and $180^\circ=\pi$ radians. To convert degrees to radians multiply by $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$; to go back multiply by $\dfrac{180}{\pi}$. The unit circle has radius 1 and centre at the origin. For the point $P$ at angle $\theta$ measured counterclockwise from the positive $x$-axis, $P=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$: the right triangle under $P$ has hypotenuse 1, so its legs are exactly the cosine and sine. This definition works for any angle, and the signs follow the quadrant.

弧度 (radian) 是所对弧长等于半径的角。一整圈的弧长为 $2\pi r$，所以 $360^\circ=2\pi$ 弧度，$180^\circ=\pi$ 弧度。角度化弧度乘以 $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$；弧度化角度乘以 $\dfrac{180}{\pi}$。单位圆 (unit circle) 半径为 1，圆心在原点。从 $x$ 轴正方向逆时针转过角 $\theta$ 得到点 $P$，则 $P=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$：$P$ 下方的直角三角形斜边为 1，所以两条直角边恰好是余弦和正弦。这个定义适用于任何角，正负号由象限决定。

The quadrilateral family四边形家族

A parallelogram has both pairs of opposite sides parallel. From the parallel-line rules of Section 3.1 it follows that opposite sides are equal, opposite angles are equal, consecutive angles are supplementary, and the diagonals bisect each other. A rectangle is a parallelogram with four right angles (its diagonals are also equal); a rhombus is a parallelogram with four equal sides (its diagonals are perpendicular); a square is both. A trapezoid has a pair of parallel sides, called the bases; in an isosceles trapezoid the legs are equal and the base angles are equal.

平行四边形 (parallelogram) 的两组对边分别平行。由第 3.1 节的平行线性质可知：对边相等，对角相等，邻角互补，对角线互相平分。矩形 (rectangle) 是有四个直角的平行四边形（对角线也相等）；菱形 (rhombus) 是四边相等的平行四边形（对角线互相垂直）；正方形 (square) 两者兼具。梯形 (trapezoid) 有一组平行边，叫作底 (bases)；等腰梯形的两腰相等，底角相等。

Area and perimeter面积与周长

Perimeter is the distance around a figure: add the side lengths. Area counts unit squares inside it. A parallelogram can be cut along a height and the triangle slid to the other end to form a rectangle, so its area is base times perpendicular height, not base times slanted side. A triangle is half a parallelogram, and a trapezoid is the average of its bases times the height (two copies, one flipped, make a parallelogram of base $b_1+b_2$).

周长 (perimeter) 是图形一周的长度：把各边相加。面积 (area) 是图形内部单位正方形的个数。平行四边形沿高剪开，把三角形移到另一端就拼成矩形，所以面积是底乘垂直的高，而不是底乘斜边。三角形是平行四边形的一半；梯形面积是两底的平均数乘高（两个梯形，其中一个翻转，可拼成底为 $b_1+b_2$ 的平行四边形）。

$$A_{\text{rect}}=lw,\quad A_{\text{par}}=bh,\quad A_{\triangle}=\tfrac12 bh,\quad A_{\text{trap}}=\tfrac12(b_1+b_2)h$$

Circumference and area周长与面积

The ratio of any circle's circumference to its diameter is the same number, $\pi\approx 3.14159$, because all circles are similar. So $C=\pi d=2\pi r$. Cutting a circle into many thin sectors and laying them head to tail makes a near-rectangle of width $\pi r$ (half the circumference) and height $r$, which is why the area is $\pi r^2$.

任何圆的周长 (circumference) 与直径之比都是同一个数 $\pi\approx 3.14159$，因为所有圆都相似。所以 $C=\pi d=2\pi r$。把圆切成许多细扇形并首尾交错排列，得到近似的长方形，宽为 $\pi r$（半个周长），高为 $r$，这就是面积为 $\pi r^2$ 的原因。

Angles, chords and tangents角、弦与切线

A central angle has its vertex at the centre; an inscribed angle has its vertex on the circle. An inscribed angle is half the central angle that stands on the same arc, so any inscribed angle on a diameter (a semicircle, central angle $180^\circ$) is a right angle. A tangent line touches the circle at one point and is perpendicular to the radius drawn to that point; that right angle turns many tangent questions into Pythagorean theorem questions. Two tangent segments from the same outside point are equal in length.

顶点在圆心的角是圆心角 (central angle)；顶点在圆上的角是圆周角 (inscribed angle)。圆周角等于同弧所对圆心角的一半，所以直径（半圆，圆心角 $180^\circ$）所对的圆周角是直角。切线 (tangent) 与圆只有一个交点，并垂直于过该点的半径；这个直角能把很多切线题变成勾股定理题。从圆外同一点引出的两条切线段长度相等。

The equation of a circle圆的方程

A circle is every point at distance $r$ from its centre $(h,k)$. Writing that distance with the distance formula (Section 3.6) and squaring gives the equation of a circle in standard form. If an equation arrives expanded, complete the square in $x$ and in $y$ to recover the centre and radius.

$$(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$$
$$(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$$

Find the centre and radius of the circle $x^2+y^2-6x+4y-12=0$.

  5. Why this works: completing the square rewrites each quadratic as a perfect square, which matches the standard form; note that $y+2$ means $k=-2$.

Slope, parallel and perpendicular lines斜率、平行线与垂直线

Slope measures steepness: $m=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$, rise over run. Positive slope rises to the right, negative slope falls, a horizontal line has slope $0$ and a vertical line has undefined slope. Parallel lines have equal slopes. Perpendicular lines (neither vertical) have slopes whose product is $-1$, so each is the negative reciprocal of the other: turning a run-rise triangle through $90^\circ$ swaps run and rise and changes one sign.

斜率 (slope) 表示陡峭程度：$m=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$，竖直变化除以水平变化。正斜率向右上升，负斜率向右下降，水平线斜率为 $0$，竖直线斜率不存在。平行线斜率相等。垂直线（都不是竖直线时）斜率之积为 $-1$，即互为负倒数：把水平-竖直三角形旋转 $90^\circ$，水平量与竖直量互换，并且其中一个变号。

$$m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1},\qquad m_{\parallel}=m,\qquad m_{\perp}=-\frac{1}{m}$$

Prisms and cylinders棱柱与圆柱

A prism has two congruent parallel bases joined by rectangular faces; a cylinder is the same idea with circular bases. Stack copies of the base, one unit thick, up to height $h$, and you get the volume: base area $B$ times $h$. For a rectangular box this is $lwh$; for a cylinder, $\pi r^2h$.

棱柱 (prism) 有两个全等且平行的底面，由矩形侧面相连；圆柱 (cylinder) 是同样的结构，只是底面是圆。把一个单位厚的底面一层层叠到高度 $h$，就得到体积 (volume)：底面积 $B$ 乘 $h$。长方体为 $lwh$；圆柱为 $\pi r^2h$。

$$V_{\text{prism}}=Bh,\qquad V_{\text{box}}=lwh,\qquad V_{\text{cyl}}=\pi r^2h$$
$$V_{\text{prism}}=Bh,\qquad V_{\text{box}}=lwh,\qquad V_{\text{cyl}}=\pi r^2h$$

Pyramids, cones and spheres棱锥、圆锥与球

A pyramid or cone that comes to a point holds exactly one third of the prism or cylinder with the same base and height (three congruent pyramids can be fitted together to fill a cube). So $V=\tfrac13Bh$, and for a cone $V=\tfrac13\pi r^2h$, where $h$ is the perpendicular height. The height $h$, radius $r$ and slant height $l$ of a right cone form a right triangle, so $l^2=r^2+h^2$. A sphere of radius $r$ has $V=\tfrac43\pi r^3$ and $SA=4\pi r^2$.

收于一点的棱锥或圆锥，体积恰好是同底同高的棱柱或圆柱的三分之一（三个全等的棱锥可以拼成一个正方体）。所以 $V=\tfrac13Bh$，圆锥为 $V=\tfrac13\pi r^2h$，其中 $h$ 是垂直高。直圆锥的高 $h$、半径 $r$ 和斜高 $l$ (slant height) 构成直角三角形，所以 $l^2=r^2+h^2$。半径为 $r$ 的球 (sphere) 有 $V=\tfrac43\pi r^3$，$SA=4\pi r^2$。

$$V_{\text{cone}}=\tfrac13\pi r^2h,\qquad V_{\text{sphere}}=\tfrac43\pi r^3,\qquad SA_{\text{sphere}}=4\pi r^2$$
$$V_{\text{cone}}=\tfrac13\pi r^2h,\qquad V_{\text{sphere}}=\tfrac43\pi r^3,\qquad SA_{\text{sphere}}=4\pi r^2$$

Surface area from a net由展开图求表面积

Surface area is the total area of every face, the amount of material needed to wrap the solid. Unfold the solid into a net and add the pieces. A box has three pairs of rectangles: $2(lw+lh+wh)$. A cylinder unrolls into two circles and a rectangle whose width is the circumference $2\pi r$: $SA=2\pi r^2+2\pi rh$. A cone unrolls into its base circle and a sector of area $\pi rl$: $SA=\pi r^2+\pi rl$.

表面积 (surface area) 是所有面的面积总和，也就是包住这个立体需要的材料。把立体展开成展开图 (net)，把各块面积相加。长方体有三对矩形：$2(lw+lh+wh)$。圆柱展开为两个圆和一个矩形，矩形的宽是周长 $2\pi r$：$SA=2\pi r^2+2\pi rh$。圆锥展开为底面圆和一个面积为 $\pi rl$ 的扇形：$SA=\pi r^2+\pi rl$。

Scaling solids立体的缩放

If every length of a solid is multiplied by $k$, the new solid is similar to the old one. Each area is a product of two lengths, so surface area is multiplied by $k^2$; volume is a product of three lengths, so it is multiplied by $k^3$. Doubling the radius of a sphere therefore makes its surface area 4 times and its volume 8 times as large.

如果立体的所有长度都乘以 $k$，新立体与原立体相似。面积是两个长度的乘积，所以表面积乘以 $k^2$；体积是三个长度的乘积，所以乘以 $k^3$。因此球的半径加倍，表面积变为 4 倍，体积变为 8 倍。

Common Mistake: watch for these
  • Using the slant height in the cone's volume formula, or the perpendicular height in its surface area.
  • Forgetting the $\tfrac13$ for cones and pyramids.
  • Using the diameter as $r$, which multiplies a volume by 4 (cylinder) or 8 (sphere).
  • Answering in square units for a volume, or giving only the lateral area when the question asks for total surface area.
常见错误：注意这些
  • 圆锥体积公式中用了斜高，或表面积公式中用了垂直高。
  • 圆锥和棱锥忘记乘 $\tfrac13$。
  • 把直径当作 $r$，使体积变成 4 倍（圆柱）或 8 倍（球）。
  • 体积写成平方单位，或题目要总表面积却只算侧面积。
