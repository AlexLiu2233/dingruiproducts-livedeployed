Kinetic Theory of Temperature and Pressure温度与压强的分子动理论

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Explain how the pressure of a gas arises from the perpendicular forces of many atoms on a surface, and calculate it from $P = F_\perp/A$.
  • Explain why pressure exists throughout the gas and not only at the boundary with the container.
  • Describe temperature as a measure of the average kinetic energy of the atoms, and calculate $K_\text{avg}$ and $v_\text{rms}$ from $K_\text{avg} = \frac{3}{2}k_B T = \frac{1}{2}mv_\text{rms}^2$.
  • Sketch the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution of speeds at two temperatures and predict how its peak, spread and height change as the temperature rises.

  • 把温度描述（describe）为原子平均动能的量度，并由 $K_\text{avg} = \frac{3}{2}k_B T = \frac{1}{2}mv_\text{rms}^2$ 计算（calculate）$K_\text{avg}$ 与 $v_\text{rms}$。
  • 画出（sketch）两个温度下的麦克斯韦-玻尔兹曼速率分布，并预测（predict）温度升高时其峰位、宽度和高度如何变化。
  • 利用温度、原子质量与方均根速率之间的关系，论证（justify）关于两种气体的判断。

Atoms in a Gas: Collisions and Momentum气体中的原子：碰撞与动量

Kinetic theory explains the large-scale behavior of a gas from the motion of its atoms. In a gas the atoms are far apart compared with their own size, and they move in straight lines at high speed until they hit something. At room temperature a typical air molecule travels several hundred meters per second and undergoes billions of collisions every second, either with another atom or with the container wall. Each collision is a brief interaction in which the atoms exert forces on each other, so we can analyze it with the same tools as any other collision.

分子动理论（kinetic theory）从原子的运动出发解释气体的宏观行为。气体中原子之间的距离远大于原子自身的尺寸，原子沿直线高速运动，直到撞上别的东西。室温下，典型的空气分子每秒运动几百米，每秒要经历数十亿次碰撞，对象不是另一个原子就是容器壁。每次碰撞都是一次短暂的相互作用，原子之间互相施力，所以我们可以用分析其他碰撞时用的同样工具来处理它。

The key tool is conservation of momentum. For a pair of atoms colliding with no outside force on them, the total momentum vector before the collision equals the total momentum vector after it. In one dimension this is a single equation, $m_1 v_{1,i} + m_2 v_{2,i} = m_1 v_{1,f} + m_2 v_{2,f}$. In two dimensions the $x$ components and the $y$ components are each conserved separately, giving two equations. When the collision is also elastic, the total kinetic energy is conserved as well, which supplies one more equation. AP Physics 2 expects both qualitative and quantitative analysis of collisions in one and two dimensions, and the two worked examples below show what that looks like for atoms.

关键工具是动量守恒（conservation of momentum）。一对原子碰撞时若不受外力，碰撞前的总动量矢量等于碰撞后的总动量矢量。一维时这是一个方程：$m_1 v_{1,i} + m_2 v_{2,i} = m_1 v_{1,f} + m_2 v_{2,f}$。二维时 $x$ 分量和 $y$ 分量分别守恒，得到两个方程。如果碰撞还是弹性的，总动能也守恒，这又多提供一个方程。AP Physics 2 要求对一维和二维碰撞做定性和定量分析，下面两个例题展示了原子碰撞的分析方式。

A collision with the container wall is the same idea with one partner so massive that it hardly moves. An atom of mass $m$ that bounces straight back from a fixed wall with perpendicular speed $v$ reverses its velocity, so its momentum changes from $+mv$ to $-mv$, a change of magnitude $2mv$. By Newton's third law the atom pushes on the wall with an average force equal to that momentum change divided by the time over which it happens. One atom's push is far too small to notice, but a container holds an enormous number of atoms, so the wall receives a nearly steady push.

与容器壁的碰撞是同一思路，只是碰撞的另一方质量大到几乎不动。质量为 $m$ 的原子以垂直速率 $v$ 从固定的器壁上原路弹回，速度反向，动量从 $+mv$ 变为 $-mv$，变化量的大小为 $2mv$。根据牛顿第三定律，原子对器壁的平均推力等于这个动量变化除以所用的时间。单个原子的推力小到无法察觉，但容器里的原子数量极多，所以器壁受到的是几乎稳定的推力。

Pressure: Force Spread over an Area压强：分布在面积上的力

The pressure a gas exerts on a surface is the ratio of the sum of the magnitudes of the perpendicular components of the forces exerted by the gas's atoms on that surface to the area of the surface. Only the component perpendicular to the surface counts, because an atom that glances sideways along the wall adds nothing to the push on it. Pressure is a scalar with SI unit the pascal, $1\,\mathrm{Pa} = 1\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$.

气体对某个表面的压强（pressure）是气体原子对该表面施加的力的垂直分量大小之和与该表面面积之比。只有垂直于表面的分量才起作用，因为沿器壁擦过的原子对器壁的推力没有贡献。压强是标量，国际单位是帕斯卡：$1\,\mathrm{Pa} = 1\,\mathrm{N/m^2}$。

Pressure压强
$$P = \frac{F_\perp}{A}$$

$P$ is the pressure in Pa, $F_\perp$ is the sum of the magnitudes of the perpendicular force components in N, and $A$ is the surface area in m².

$P$ 为压强，单位 Pa；$F_\perp$ 为垂直力分量大小之和，单位 N；$A$ 为表面面积，单位 m²。

Pressure rises when the atoms hit the surface more often (more atoms in the same volume, so a larger number density) or hit it harder (higher speeds, so larger momentum changes per collision). That is why squeezing a gas into a smaller volume, or heating it in a rigid box, both raise the pressure on the walls. Topic 9.2 turns these two observations into the ideal gas law.

原子撞击表面更频繁（相同体积内原子更多，即数密度更大）或撞得更猛（速率更大，每次碰撞的动量变化更大）时，压强升高。所以把气体压进更小的体积，或在刚性容器中加热气体，都会使器壁受到的压强升高。专题 9.2 会把这两点观察变成理想气体定律。

Pressure exists throughout the gas itself, not just at the boundary between the gas and the container. Imagine a thin, tiny, massless sheet placed in the middle of the gas. Atoms strike both of its faces, so it feels the same perpendicular forces per unit area that a real wall would. The gas inside a sealed container therefore has a pressure at every point, and a pressure sensor works the same way whether it sits on the wall or floats in the middle.

压强存在于整个气体内部，而不只是在气体与容器的边界上。设想在气体中间放一块极薄、极小、没有质量的薄片：原子撞击它的两个面，所以它每单位面积受到的垂直力与真实器壁一样。因此密闭容器内的气体在每一点都有压强，压强传感器无论装在器壁上还是悬在气体中间，工作原理都一样。

Temperature and Average Kinetic Energy温度与平均动能

The atoms in a gas do not all move at the same speed. Collisions constantly speed some atoms up and slow others down, so at any instant there is a spread of speeds. The temperature of a system is characterized by the average kinetic energy of the atoms within that system. A hotter gas has atoms with a larger average kinetic energy. For an ideal gas the average translational kinetic energy per atom is proportional to the absolute temperature, and the same constant relates it to the root-mean-square speed.

气体中的原子并不都以同一速率运动。碰撞不断使一些原子加速、另一些减速，所以任一时刻速率都有一个分布。系统的温度（temperature）以系统内原子的平均动能为特征。温度更高的气体，原子的平均动能更大。对于理想气体，每个原子的平均平动动能与绝对温度成正比，同一个常数还把它与方均根速率联系起来。

Average kinetic energy and rms speed平均动能与方均根速率
$$K_\text{avg} = \frac{3}{2}k_B T = \frac{1}{2}mv_\text{rms}^2$$

$K_\text{avg}$ is the average kinetic energy per atom in J, $k_B = 1.38\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J/K}$ is Boltzmann's constant, $T$ is the temperature in K, $m$ is the mass of one atom in kg, and $v_\text{rms}$ is the root-mean-square speed in m/s.

$K_\text{avg}$ 为每个原子的平均动能，单位 J；$k_B = 1.38\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J/K}$ 为玻尔兹曼常数；$T$ 为温度，单位 K；$m$ 为单个原子的质量，单位 kg；$v_\text{rms}$ 为方均根速率，单位 m/s。

The root-mean-square speed is found by squaring every speed, averaging the squares, and taking the square root. It is the speed that corresponds to the average kinetic energy, and it is a little larger than the plain average speed. Solving the formula gives $v_\text{rms} = \sqrt{3k_B T/m}$. Three consequences follow directly. First, $T$ must be in kelvin, since a temperature of zero on the kelvin scale is the one with zero average kinetic energy. Second, doubling the kelvin temperature doubles $K_\text{avg}$ but multiplies $v_\text{rms}$ only by $\sqrt{2}$. Third, at the same temperature all ideal gases have the same $K_\text{avg}$, so lighter atoms must move faster: helium atoms are much quicker than nitrogen molecules in the same room.

方均根速率（root-mean-square speed）的求法是：先把每个速率平方，再求平方的平均值，最后开方。它是与平均动能对应的速率，比普通的平均速率略大。解这个公式得 $v_\text{rms} = \sqrt{3k_B T/m}$。由此直接得到三个结论。第一，$T$ 必须用开尔文，因为开尔文温标的零点对应平均动能为零。第二，开尔文温度加倍时，$K_\text{avg}$ 加倍，而 $v_\text{rms}$ 只变为 $\sqrt{2}$ 倍。第三，同一温度下所有理想气体的 $K_\text{avg}$ 相同，所以质量小的原子必然运动得更快：同一房间里氦原子比氮分子快得多。

Kelvin and Celsius degrees are the same size, so $T(\mathrm{K}) = T(^\circ\mathrm{C}) + 273$. Use this conversion in every kinetic-theory and gas-law calculation. Temperature is also not the same thing as the total energy of a sample: a bathtub of warm water holds far more energy than a cup of hot tea, even though the tea has the higher temperature, because temperature tracks the energy per particle and not the sum.

开尔文与摄氏度的每一格大小相同，所以 $T(\mathrm{K}) = T(^\circ\mathrm{C}) + 273$。每个分子动理论和气体定律的计算都要用这个换算。温度也不等于样品的总能量：一浴缸温水所含的能量远多于一杯热茶，尽管茶的温度更高，因为温度反映的是每个粒子的能量，而不是总和。

The Maxwell-Boltzmann Distribution麦克斯韦-玻尔兹曼分布

The Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution is a graphical representation of the speeds (or energies) of the atoms of a gas at a given temperature. The horizontal axis is speed and the height of the curve shows how many atoms, as a fraction of the total, have speeds near that value. You are not expected to know the functional form of the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution, only to recognize how the features of its graph are related to the temperature of the gas.

麦克斯韦-玻尔兹曼分布（Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution）是以图像表示一定温度下气体原子速率（或能量）的分布。横轴是速率，曲线的高度表示速率在该值附近的原子占总数的比例。不要求知道麦克斯韦-玻尔兹曼分布的函数形式（functional form），只需要认识这条曲线的各个特征如何与气体的温度相联系。

Read these features from Figure 9.1. Each curve starts at the origin, because essentially no atoms are at rest. Each is asymmetric: it rises steeply to a peak and then falls slowly into a long tail, because there is a lower limit of zero on the speed but no sharp upper limit, so a few atoms are always very fast. The area under each curve is the same, because it represents all the atoms and the number of atoms is fixed. When the temperature rises, the average kinetic energy rises, so the peak moves to a higher speed. The area must stay the same, so the curve spreads out into a broader shape, and its peak is lower. The fraction of atoms with very high speeds grows a great deal, which matters for anything that needs a minimum energy to happen.

从图 9.1 读出这些特征。每条曲线都从原点出发，因为几乎没有原子处于静止状态。每条曲线都不对称：它陡直上升到峰值，然后缓慢下降成一条长尾，因为速率有下限零而没有明确的上限，所以总会有少数原子速率很大。每条曲线下的面积相同，因为它代表全部原子，而原子总数不变。温度升高时平均动能增大，峰移向更高的速率；面积必须保持不变，所以曲线向两侧展开，变得更宽，峰值更低。速率很大的原子所占比例大幅增加，这对任何需要最低能量才能发生的过程都很重要。

Common Mistake: Treating every atom as having the average speed "The gas is at 300 K, so each atom moves at $v_\text{rms}$" is wrong. The temperature fixes only the average kinetic energy. Individual atoms have a whole distribution of speeds, some much slower and some much faster than $v_\text{rms}$, and collisions keep reshuffling who is fast and who is slow. Raising the temperature does not make every atom faster by the same amount either: the whole distribution shifts and broadens.
常见错误：认为每个原子都具有平均速率 "气体在 300 K，所以每个原子都以 $v_\text{rms}$ 运动"是错误的。温度只确定平均动能。单个原子的速率有一个完整的分布，有的比 $v_\text{rms}$ 慢得多，有的快得多，而碰撞不断重新分配谁快谁慢。温度升高也不是让每个原子都加快相同的量：整个分布整体移动并变宽。
Worked Example: A One-Dimensional Atomic Collision例题：一维原子碰撞

Atom A of mass $m$ moves at $600\,\mathrm{m/s}$ in the $+x$ direction and collides head-on with atom B of mass $2m$ at rest. The collision is elastic. Find the velocity of each atom afterward.

质量为 $m$ 的原子 A 以 $600\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 沿 $+x$ 方向运动，与静止的、质量为 $2m$ 的原子 B 正碰。碰撞是弹性的。求碰后两个原子各自的速度。

Step 1: Conserve momentum第 1 步：动量守恒
$$m(600) = m\,v_A + 2m\,v_B \quad\Rightarrow\quad v_A = 600 - 2v_B$$
Step 2: Conserve kinetic energy第 2 步：动能守恒
$$\tfrac{1}{2}m(600)^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}m\,v_A^2 + \tfrac{1}{2}(2m)\,v_B^2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 600^2 = v_A^2 + 2v_B^2$$
Step 3: Substitute and solve第 3 步：代入并求解
$$600^2 = (600 - 2v_B)^2 + 2v_B^2 \;\Rightarrow\; 0 = 6v_B^2 - 2400\,v_B \;\Rightarrow\; v_B = 400\,\mathrm{m/s},\; v_A = -200\,\mathrm{m/s}$$
The root $v_B = 0$ is the case of no collision, so the physical answer is the other one. Atom A bounces back at $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and atom B moves forward at $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Why this works: a momentum equation alone cannot fix two unknowns, and the elastic condition supplies the second equation. Check: total momentum $m(-200) + 2m(400) = 600m$, unchanged.根 $v_B = 0$ 对应没有发生碰撞，所以物理上的答案是另一个根。原子 A 以 $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 反弹，原子 B 以 $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 向前运动。为什么这样做：仅有动量方程无法确定两个未知量，弹性条件提供了第二个方程。检验：总动量 $m(-200) + 2m(400) = 600m$，与碰前相同。
Worked Example: A Two-Dimensional Atomic Collision例题：二维原子碰撞

An atom moving at $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$ along $+x$ strikes an identical atom at rest. After the collision the first atom moves at $346\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at $30^\circ$ above the $+x$ axis. Find the velocity of the second atom, and decide whether the collision was elastic.

一个原子以 $400\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 沿 $+x$ 方向运动，撞上一个静止的相同原子。碰后第一个原子以 $346\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 的速率沿 $+x$ 轴上方 $30^\circ$ 的方向运动。求第二个原子的速度，并判断这次碰撞是否为弹性碰撞。

Step 1: Conserve the $x$ component (the mass $m$ cancels)第 1 步：$x$ 分量守恒（质量 $m$ 约去）
$$400 = 346\cos 30^\circ + v_{2x} \;\Rightarrow\; v_{2x} = 400 - 300 = 100\,\mathrm{m/s}$$
Step 2: Conserve the $y$ component第 2 步：$y$ 分量守恒
$$0 = 346\sin 30^\circ + v_{2y} \;\Rightarrow\; v_{2y} = -173\,\mathrm{m/s}$$
Step 3: Speed and direction of atom 2第 3 步：原子 2 的速率与方向
$$v_2 = \sqrt{100^2 + 173^2} = 200\,\mathrm{m/s}, \qquad \theta = \tan^{-1}\!\frac{173}{100} = 60^\circ$$
The second atom moves at $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at an angle $\theta$ below the $+x$ axis.第二个原子以 $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 的速率运动，方向在 $+x$ 轴下方，夹角为 $\theta$。
Step 4: Compare kinetic energies第 4 步：比较动能
$$\frac{1}{2}m(400)^2 = 8.0\times10^{4}m, \qquad \frac{1}{2}m(346^2 + 200^2) = 8.0\times10^{4}m$$
The kinetic energy is unchanged, so the collision is elastic. Why this works: momentum is a vector, so each component is conserved on its own. The two atoms leave at $30^\circ + 60^\circ = 90^\circ$ to each other, as expected for an elastic collision of equal masses when one starts at rest.动能没有变化，所以这次碰撞是弹性碰撞。为什么这样做：动量是矢量，所以每个分量各自守恒。两个原子碰后的方向夹角为 $30^\circ + 60^\circ = 90^\circ$，这符合质量相等、其中一个原来静止的弹性碰撞的预期。
Worked Example: Kinetic Energy and rms Speed of Two Gases例题：两种气体的动能与方均根速率

Helium atoms ($m = 6.64\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$) and nitrogen molecules ($m = 4.65\times10^{-26}\,\mathrm{kg}$) are in the same room at $27^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Find the average kinetic energy and the rms speed of each, and compare them.

氦原子（$m = 6.64\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}$）和氮分子（$m = 4.65\times10^{-26}\,\mathrm{kg}$）在同一间 $27^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 的房间里。求每种粒子的平均动能和方均根速率，并加以比较。

Step 1: Convert to kelvin第 1 步：换算成开尔文
$$T = 27 + 273 = 300\,\mathrm{K}$$
Step 2: Average kinetic energy (the same for both)第 2 步：平均动能（两者相同）
$$K_\text{avg} = \tfrac{3}{2}k_B T = \tfrac{3}{2}(1.38\times10^{-23})(300) = 6.21\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{J}$$
Step 3: rms speeds第 3 步：方均根速率
$$v_\text{rms,He} = \sqrt{\frac{2K_\text{avg}}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{2(6.21\times10^{-21})}{6.64\times10^{-27}}} = 1.37\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{m/s}$$
$$v_\text{rms,N2} = \sqrt{\frac{2(6.21\times10^{-21})}{4.65\times10^{-26}}} = 517\,\mathrm{m/s}$$
The two gases have the same average kinetic energy, but helium is about 2.6 times faster, which is $\sqrt{m_{N_2}/m_\text{He}} = \sqrt{7.0}$. Why this works: at equal $K_\text{avg}$ the lighter atom needs more speed to carry the same energy.两种气体的平均动能相同，但氦约快 2.6 倍，即 $\sqrt{m_{N_2}/m_\text{He}} = \sqrt{7.0}$。为什么这样做：$K_\text{avg}$ 相同时，质量较小的原子需要更大的速率才能带有同样的能量。
Worked Example: Finding the Atomic Mass from a Graph例题：由图像求原子质量

A student measures the rms speed of the atoms of an unknown monatomic gas at four temperatures and records $v_\text{rms}^2$: $1.25\times10^6$ at $200\,\mathrm{K}$, $2.49\times10^6$ at $400\,\mathrm{K}$, $3.74\times10^6$ at $600\,\mathrm{K}$ and $4.99\times10^6$ at $800\,\mathrm{K}$, all in $\mathrm{m^2/s^2}$. Explain which graph should be linear, find the mass of one atom from its slope, and identify the gas.

The Classical Model of an Ideal Gas理想气体的经典模型

An ideal gas is a model that makes the atoms of a gas as simple as possible so that the physics can be worked out exactly. The classical model assumes four things. First, the instantaneous velocities of the atoms are random: at any moment the atoms move in every direction with a spread of speeds, and no direction is favoured. Second, the volumes of the atoms are negligible compared with the total volume occupied by the gas, so the atoms behave as points in a mostly empty container. Third, the atoms collide elastically, so collisions do not change the total kinetic energy. Fourth, the only appreciable forces on the atoms are those that occur during collisions: between collisions each atom moves in a straight line at constant velocity.

理想气体（ideal gas）是把气体原子尽量简化的模型，这样物理规律就可以精确求出。经典模型作四个假设。第一，原子的瞬时速度是随机的（random）：任一时刻原子朝各个方向运动，速率各不相同，没有哪个方向占优。第二，原子本身的体积与气体占据的总体积相比可以忽略（negligible），所以原子可以看作一个基本是空的容器里的点。第三，原子之间的碰撞是弹性的（elastic），碰撞不改变总动能。第四，作用在原子上的明显的力只出现在碰撞过程中：两次碰撞之间，每个原子沿直线匀速运动。

These assumptions are good for real gases at low density and moderate or high temperature, where the atoms are far apart and collisions are brief. They are poor when a gas is squeezed so tightly or cooled so far that the attractions between atoms start to matter. The model also has a consequence for energy that Topic 9.4 will use: since the atoms exert no appreciable forces on each other between collisions, there is no energy stored in the interactions, and the energy of the gas is all kinetic.

对于低密度、温度适中或较高的真实气体，这些假设是很好的近似，此时原子相距很远，碰撞很短暂。当气体被压得很紧或冷却到很低、原子间的吸引力开始起作用时，这些假设就不好了。这个模型对能量还有一个推论，专题 9.4 会用到：原子在两次碰撞之间没有明显的相互作用力，所以相互作用中没有储存能量，气体的能量全部是动能。

The Ideal Gas Law理想气体定律

An ideal gas is one in which the relationships among the pressure, volume, temperature and the amount of gas can be modeled with a single equation. The amount of gas can be counted in moles, $n$, or as a number of atoms, $N$. One mole contains Avogadro's number of atoms, $N_A = 6.02\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}$, so $N = nN_A$. The two forms of the law are connected by $R = N_A k_B$, since $(6.02\times10^{23})(1.38\times10^{-23}) = 8.31$.

理想气体是这样一种气体：压强、体积、温度和气体的量之间的关系可以用一个方程来描述。气体的量可以用物质的量（moles）$n$ 计量，也可以用原子数 $N$ 计量。一摩尔含有阿伏伽德罗常数个原子，$N_A = 6.02\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}$，所以 $N = nN_A$。两种形式由 $R = N_A k_B$ 联系起来，因为 $(6.02\times10^{23})(1.38\times10^{-23}) = 8.31$。

Ideal gas law理想气体定律
$$PV = nRT = Nk_B T$$

Here $P$ is the absolute pressure in Pa, $V$ is the volume of the container in m³, $n$ is the number of moles, $R = 8.31\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ is the gas constant, $N$ is the number of atoms, $k_B = 1.38\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J/K}$ is Boltzmann's constant, and $T$ is the temperature in kelvin. Use $PV = nRT$ when the amount is in moles and $PV = Nk_B T$ when it is a count of atoms. The law agrees with Topic 9.1: the pressure grows with the number of atoms per unit volume, $N/V$, and with the temperature, which sets how hard each atom hits the wall.

其中 $P$ 为绝对压强，单位 Pa；$V$ 为容器体积，单位 m³；$n$ 为物质的量，单位 mol；$R = 8.31\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ 为气体常量；$N$ 为原子数；$k_B = 1.38\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J/K}$ 为玻尔兹曼常数；$T$ 为开尔文温度。物质的量以摩尔计时用 $PV = nRT$，以原子个数计时用 $PV = Nk_B T$。这个定律与专题 9.1 一致：压强随单位体积内的原子数 $N/V$ 增大，也随温度升高而增大，因为温度决定每个原子撞击器壁的力度。

For a fixed amount of gas, $nR$ is a constant, so $PV/T$ has the same value in any two states. The ratio form follows:

对一定量的气体，$nR$ 是常量，所以任意两个状态下 $PV/T$ 的值相同。由此得到比值形式：

Ratio form for a fixed amount of gas定量气体的比值形式
$$\frac{P_1 V_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2 V_2}{T_2}$$

The ratio form is the fastest way to compare two states, since $R$ and $n$ cancel and only the changes matter. When one quantity is held fixed it reduces to a simple proportionality: at constant $T$, $P \propto 1/V$; at constant $V$, $P \propto T$; at constant $P$, $V \propto T$. In every case $T$ is the kelvin temperature.

比值形式是比较两个状态的最快方法，因为 $R$ 和 $n$ 都约去了，只有变化量起作用。当某个量保持不变时，它退化为简单的正比关系：$T$ 不变时 $P \propto 1/V$；$V$ 不变时 $P \propto T$；$P$ 不变时 $V \propto T$。无论哪种情形，$T$ 都是开尔文温度。

Common Mistake: Using Celsius temperatures in the gas law A sealed rigid container of gas at $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$ is heated to $100^\circ\mathrm{C}$. It is tempting to say the temperature went up by a factor of 5, so the pressure also went up by a factor of 5. That is wrong: the gas law needs absolute temperature, $293\,\mathrm{K}$ to $373\,\mathrm{K}$, a factor of only $1.27$. The Celsius zero is an arbitrary point on the scale, not the zero of kinetic energy.
常见错误：在气体定律中使用摄氏温度 一个密闭刚性容器里的气体从 $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 加热到 $100^\circ\mathrm{C}$。人们容易说温度升高到 5 倍，所以压强也升高到 5 倍。这是错的：气体定律需要绝对温度，即从 $293\,\mathrm{K}$ 到 $373\,\mathrm{K}$，倍数只有 $1.27$。摄氏温标的零点只是标度上一个任意的点，并不是动能为零的点。

Graphs of Pressure, Volume and Temperature压强、体积与温度的图像

Graphs let you read the properties of a gas from data, and the shape of each graph follows from the ideal gas law. At constant temperature and amount, $PV = nRT$ is a constant, so a graph of $P$ against $V$ is a curve in which pressure and volume are inversely proportional (an isotherm, as Topic 9.4 will name it). Plotting $P$ against $1/V$ straightens it into a line through the origin whose slope is $nRT$. At constant volume and amount, $P = (nR/V)\,T$, so a graph of $P$ against the kelvin temperature is a straight line through the origin with slope $nR/V$. At constant pressure and amount, $V = (nR/P)\,T$, so $V$ against $T$ is also a straight line through the origin, and the volume is directly proportional to $T$.

图像能让你从数据中读出气体的性质，而每种图像的形状都由理想气体定律决定。温度和物质的量不变时，$PV = nRT$ 是常量，所以 $P$-$V$ 图像是一条曲线，压强与体积成反比（这就是专题 9.4 要命名的等温线）。把 $P$ 对 $1/V$ 作图则变成过原点的直线，斜率为 $nRT$。体积和物质的量不变时，$P = (nR/V)\,T$，所以 $P$ 对开尔文温度的图像是过原点的直线，斜率为 $nR/V$。压强和物质的量不变时，$V = (nR/P)\,T$，所以 $V$ 对 $T$ 的图像也是过原点的直线，体积与 $T$ 成正比。

The slope of a straight-line graph is therefore a measuring tool. From a $P$ against $T$ line at known volume you can find $n$; from a $P$ against $1/V$ line at known temperature you can find $n$ or $T$. Look at which variable is on each axis and whether the temperature scale is kelvin before you interpret a slope or an intercept.

因此直线图像的斜率是一种测量工具。由体积已知时的 $P$-$T$ 直线可以求出 $n$；由温度已知时的 $P$-$1/V$ 直线可以求出 $n$ 或 $T$。解读斜率或截距之前，先看清每个坐标轴是什么变量，以及温度是否用开尔文。

Extrapolating to Absolute Zero外推到绝对零度

In a rigid container the pressure falls in step with the temperature. Suppose you measure the pressure of a fixed amount of gas at several temperatures above the freezing point of water and plot $P$ against the Celsius temperature. The points lie on a straight line. If you extend that line to lower temperatures, it reaches zero pressure at $-273^\circ\mathrm{C}$, and the extension is called an extrapolation. A temperature at which an ideal gas has zero pressure can therefore be found from a graph of pressure as a function of temperature. At this temperature the atoms have no translational kinetic energy left to hit the wall with. It defines absolute zero, $0\,\mathrm{K}$, the zero of the kelvin scale. A real gas condenses to a liquid before it gets that cold, so the line is an extrapolation of measurements made at higher temperatures, not a measurement at $0\,\mathrm{K}$.

在刚性容器中，压强随温度同步下降。设你测量了一定量气体在水的冰点以上若干温度下的压强，并以摄氏温度为横轴画出 $P$。这些点落在一条直线上。把这条直线向低温方向延长，它在 $-273^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 处达到零压强，这种延长叫做外推（extrapolation）。因此，理想气体压强为零的温度可以由压强随温度变化的图像求出。在这个温度下，原子已没有平动动能可以用来撞击器壁。它定义了绝对零度（absolute zero），即 $0\,\mathrm{K}$，开尔文温标的零点。真实气体在冷到这个程度之前就会凝结成液体，所以这条线是对较高温度下测量结果的外推，而不是在 $0\,\mathrm{K}$ 的测量。

Thermal Contact, Heating and Cooling热接触、加热与冷却

Two systems are in thermal contact if they may transfer energy by thermal processes. A cup of coffee and the air around it are in thermal contact, and so are two gases separated by a thin metal wall, or a thermometer and the liquid it is dipped into. A system sealed inside a perfectly insulating shell would not be in thermal contact with anything outside it.

如果两个系统可以通过热过程传递能量，它们就处于热接触（thermal contact）。一杯咖啡和周围的空气处于热接触，被一层薄金属壁隔开的两种气体也是，温度计和它所插入的液体同样如此。封在理想绝热外壳里的系统与外界不处于热接触。

Heating is the transfer of energy into a system by thermal processes, and cooling is the transfer of energy out of a system by thermal processes. These words name a process of energy transfer, not a stored quantity. A body does not contain "heat" in the way it contains mass. What a body does contain is internal energy, the sum of the kinetic and potential energies of its particles, and heating is one way of changing it. Three quantities are easy to confuse: temperature is a property of the state of a system and characterises the average kinetic energy of its atoms; heating is energy in transit by thermal processes between systems at different temperatures; and internal energy is the total energy of the particles in the system. The symbol $Q$ will stand for the energy transferred by heating, taken as positive when energy enters the system.

加热（heating）是通过热过程把能量传入系统，冷却（cooling）是通过热过程把能量传出系统。这两个词指的是能量传递的过程，而不是某种储存的量。物体并不像含有质量那样"含有热"。物体含有的是内能，即其中粒子的动能与势能之和，加热只是改变内能的一种方式。三个量很容易混淆：温度是系统状态的性质，表征原子的平均动能；加热是温度不同的系统之间通过热过程传递中的能量；内能（internal energy）是系统中粒子的总能量。符号 $Q$ 表示通过加热传递的能量，能量流入系统时取为正。

Conduction, Convection and Radiation热传导、对流与辐射

There are three thermal processes by which energy is transferred between systems at different temperatures.

在温度不同的系统之间，有三种热过程可以传递能量。

  • Conduction transfers energy through a material, or between materials in contact, without bulk motion of the material. Faster-moving atoms in a hot region collide with their slower neighbors and pass kinetic energy along. A metal spoon warming in hot soup is the standard case.
  • Convection transfers energy by the bulk motion of a fluid. Warm fluid is carried from one place to another by currents, and takes its energy with it. A heater warming a room by circulating air, or a pot of water with hot water rising in the middle, are examples.
  • Radiation transfers energy by electromagnetic waves, and needs no material between the two systems. The warmth from the Sun crossing empty space and the glow of a hot stove element are examples.
  • 热传导（conduction）通过物质内部，或相互接触的物质之间传递能量，物质本身没有整体运动。热区域中运动较快的原子与运动较慢的邻近原子碰撞，把动能传递下去。热汤中金属勺子逐渐变热是典型情形。
  • 对流（convection）靠流体的整体运动传递能量。温度较高的流体被流动带到别处，同时带走了它的能量。暖气通过空气循环使房间变暖，以及锅中热水在中间上升，都是例子。
  • 辐射（radiation）靠电磁波传递能量，两个系统之间不需要任何物质。太阳的热穿过真空到达地球，以及炉子上发红的加热元件，都是例子。

One situation often involves all three: a campfire warms your hands by radiation, the air above the fire carries energy upward by convection, and a stick left in the embers conducts energy along its length. To classify a process, ask whether the energy is carried by neighboring atoms bumping (conduction), by moving fluid (convection) or by electromagnetic waves (radiation).

同一个情境常常同时包含三种过程：篝火通过辐射使你的手变暖，火上方的空气通过对流把能量带向上方，留在余烬中的木棍则沿着自身长度传导能量。给一个过程分类时，问一问能量是靠相邻原子相互碰撞（传导）、靠流体运动（对流）还是靠电磁波（辐射）传递的。

Why Energy Flows from Hot to Cold能量为什么从热处流向冷处

Energy is transferred through thermal processes spontaneously from a higher-temperature system to a lower-temperature system: no outside agent is needed, and the reverse does not happen by itself. The reason comes from the atoms. When atoms from two different systems collide, the energy is most likely to be transferred from the higher-energy atom to the lower-energy atom. A fast atom hitting a slow one usually leaves with less kinetic energy and the slow one leaves with more. A single collision can go the other way, but over a very large number of collisions the net transfer is from the higher-energy atoms to the lower-energy atoms, which means from the system with the higher average kinetic energy to the one with the lower.

通过热过程传递能量时，能量自发地（spontaneously）从高温系统流向低温系统：不需要任何外界推动，反过来的过程不会自己发生。原因在于原子。两个不同系统的原子相撞时，能量最可能从能量较高的原子传向能量较低的原子。快的原子撞上慢的原子，通常是快的原子带着较少的动能离开，慢的原子则带着较多的动能离开。单次碰撞也可能是相反的情形，但在极大数量的碰撞之后，净传递是从能量较高的原子到能量较低的原子，也就是从平均动能较大的系统到平均动能较小的系统。

After many collisions, the energy is shared out among the atoms of both systems in the arrangement that is most probable. That most probable state is the one in which the two systems have the same temperature. It is not that energy is forbidden to flow the other way, but that the arrangements with equal average energy per atom are overwhelmingly more numerous than the arrangements in which one system holds a lopsided share. 经过许多次碰撞后，能量以最可能（probable）的方式在两个系统的原子之间分配。这个最可能的状态就是两个系统温度相同的状态。并不是不允许能量反向流动，而是每个原子平均能量相等的分配方式，在数量上压倒性地多于某一个系统占有不均衡份额的分配方式。下面的例题 2 在一个小模型中数出了这些分配方式。

Thermal Equilibrium热平衡

Thermal equilibrium results when no net energy is transferred by thermal processes between two systems in thermal contact with each other. Atoms still collide and trade energy in both directions, but the flows balance, so the average kinetic energy of each system stays constant. Two systems in thermal equilibrium are at the same temperature. This is why a thermometer works: it is left in thermal contact with an object until the two reach the same temperature, and then the reading of the thermometer is the temperature of the object. Figure 9.3 shows the approach to equilibrium of two identical blocks, one initially hot and one cold. The temperature of the hot block falls and that of the cold block rises, quickly at first while the difference is large, and then more slowly, until the curves meet.

热平衡（thermal equilibrium）是指两个相互热接触的系统之间没有净能量通过热过程传递。原子仍然相互碰撞并双向交换能量，但两个方向的流动相互抵消，所以每个系统的平均动能保持不变。处于热平衡的两个系统温度相同。这就是温度计的工作原理：把它与物体保持热接触，直到两者温度相同，此时温度计的读数就是物体的温度。图 9.3 显示了两块相同的物块趋向平衡的过程，一块起初较热，一块起初较冷。热块的温度下降，冷块的温度上升，温差大时变化较快，随后变慢，直到两条曲线相遇。

Common Mistake: Thinking that a hotter object holds more heat, or that cold flows in Objects do not store "heat" and "cold". A small cup of boiling water has a higher temperature than a swimming pool, yet the pool has far more internal energy. Energy flows spontaneously from the higher-temperature system to the lower-temperature system, and an ice cube warms because energy flows into it from the warmer surroundings, not because "cold" flows out. Equilibrium means equal temperature, not equal energy.
常见错误：认为温度高的物体含有更多热，或者认为"冷"会流进来 物体并不储存"热"和"冷"。一小杯沸水的温度比游泳池高，但游泳池的内能多得多。能量自发地从高温系统流向低温系统；冰块融化升温是因为较暖的周围环境把能量传入冰块，而不是"冷"流了出去。平衡的含义是温度相同，而不是能量相同。

$$\Delta K = 4.78\times10^{-21} - 1.20\times10^{-21} = 3.59\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{J}$$
The higher-energy atom loses $3.59\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{J}$ and the lower-energy atom gains it, so the energy moves from the hot gas to the cool gas. Why this works: the total kinetic energy of the pair is unchanged, only its distribution between the two atoms.能量较高的原子失去 $3.59\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{J}$，能量较低的原子得到同样多的能量，所以能量从热气体移向冷气体。为什么这样做：这一对原子的总动能没有变，变化的只是它在两个原子之间的分配。
Worked Example: Counting Arrangements to Find the Most Probable State例题：数出分配方式以求最可能的状态

A simple model has system A with 2 atoms and system B with 2 atoms in thermal contact, sharing 4 identical units of energy. Each atom can hold any number of units. For a given number $q_A$ of units in A, the number of arrangements is $(q_A + 1)(q_B + 1)$, where $q_B = 4 - q_A$. Find which split of energy is most probable and the probability that all the energy is in A.

一个简单模型中，系统 A 有 2 个原子，系统 B 有 2 个原子，两者热接触，共享 4 个相同的能量单位。每个原子可以持有任意数量的单位。A 中有 $q_A$ 个单位时，分配方式的数目为 $(q_A + 1)(q_B + 1)$，其中 $q_B = 4 - q_A$。求哪种能量分配最可能，以及全部能量都在 A 中的概率。

Step 1: Count each split第 1 步：数出每种分配的方式
$$q_A = 4, 3, 2, 1, 0 \;\Rightarrow\; (5)(1),\ (4)(2),\ (3)(3),\ (2)(4),\ (1)(5) = 5,\ 8,\ 9,\ 8,\ 5$$
Step 2: Total and probabilities第 2 步：总数与概率
$$N_\text{tot} = 5 + 8 + 9 + 8 + 5 = 35, \qquad P(q_A = 2) = \frac{9}{35} = 26\%, \qquad P(q_A = 4) = \frac{5}{35} = 14\%$$
Here $N_\text{tot}$ is the total number of arrangements.其中 $N_\text{tot}$ 是分配方式的总数。
The equal split, 2 units in each system, has the most arrangements, so it is the most probable. Even in a model this small, "all the energy in A" is less likely than the equal split. With the enormous numbers of atoms in real samples, the equal-sharing arrangements outnumber every lopsided one by an astronomical factor, which is why the equal-temperature state is overwhelmingly the most probable. Why this works: the energy flows toward the state that can be realized in the most ways.平均分配，即每个系统各 2 个单位，分配方式最多，所以最可能。即使在这么小的模型里，"全部能量都在 A 中"也比平均分配的可能性小。真实样品中原子数量极其庞大，平均分配的方式比任何不均衡分配的方式多出天文数字的倍数，这就是温度相同的状态压倒性地最可能的原因。为什么这样做：能量流向的是能以最多种方式实现的状态。
Worked Example: Two Gases Reach Thermal Equilibrium例题：两种气体达到热平衡

A sample of helium at $400\,\mathrm{K}$ and a sample of argon at $300\,\mathrm{K}$ contain the same number of atoms. They are separated by a thin conducting wall and isolated from everything else. (a) Which way is energy transferred? (b) Find the final temperature. (c) Compare the average kinetic energy per atom of each gas before and after.

一份 $400\,\mathrm{K}$ 的氦气和一份 $300\,\mathrm{K}$ 的氩气含有相同数目的原子。它们被一层薄的导热壁隔开，并与其他一切隔绝。(a) 能量向哪个方向传递？(b) 求最终温度。(c) 比较两种气体碰撞前后每个原子的平均动能。

Step 1: Direction第 1 步：方向
Energy is transferred spontaneously from the higher-temperature helium to the lower-temperature argon, by collisions of atoms with the wall and the wall with the atoms.能量自发地从温度较高的氦传向温度较低的氩，传递靠原子与器壁、器壁与原子之间的碰撞实现。
Step 2: Energy is conserved, with equal numbers of atoms第 2 步：能量守恒，两者原子数相同
$$N\tfrac{3}{2}k_B T_f + N\tfrac{3}{2}k_B T_f = N\tfrac{3}{2}k_B(400) + N\tfrac{3}{2}k_B(300) \;\Rightarrow\; T_f = 350\,\mathrm{K}$$
Step 3: Average kinetic energy per atom第 3 步：每个原子的平均动能
$$K_\text{He,i} = 8.28\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{J}, \quad K_\text{Ar,i} = 6.21\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{J}, \quad K_\text{f} = \tfrac{3}{2}(1.38\times10^{-23})(350) = 7.25\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{J}$$
The helium atoms lose an average of $1.03\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{J}$ each and the argon atoms gain the same on average. At equilibrium both gases have the same temperature and the same average kinetic energy per atom, although their atoms have different masses and so different speeds. Why this works: equal temperature means equal average kinetic energy per atom, not equal speed.氦原子平均每个失去 $1.03\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{J}$，氩原子平均每个得到同样多的能量。平衡时两种气体温度相同，每个原子的平均动能也相同，尽管它们的原子质量不同，所以速率不同。为什么这样做：温度相同意味着每个原子的平均动能相同，而不是速率相同。
Exam tip考试提示

When a question asks which way energy goes, answer with temperatures, not with the amount of energy: energy flows spontaneously from the higher-temperature system to the lower-temperature system until they are at the same temperature. Always name the process (conduction, convection or radiation) and say what carries the energy. In a justification, link the macroscopic statement to the atoms: collisions most likely pass energy from higher-energy atoms to lower-energy atoms, so the most probable final state is one temperature for both systems.

题目问能量朝哪个方向传递时，要用温度而不是能量的多少来回答：能量自发地从高温系统流向低温系统，直到两者温度相同。一定要说出过程的名称（传导、对流或辐射），并说明是什么携带了能量。作论证时，要把宏观陈述与原子联系起来：碰撞最可能把能量从能量较高的原子传给能量较低的原子，所以最可能的末态是两个系统温度相同。

Internal Energy内能

The internal energy of a system is the sum of the kinetic energy of the objects that make up the system and the potential energy of the configuration of those objects. For a gas the objects are its atoms or molecules, and the kinetic energy is their motion relative to the container. The potential energy comes from the forces between the particles.

系统的内能是组成该系统的物体的动能与这些物体所处构型的势能之和。对气体来说，这些物体就是原子或分子，动能是它们相对于容器的运动能量，势能来自粒子之间的相互作用力。

The atoms in an ideal gas do not interact with each other through conservative forces, and the internal structure of the atoms is not considered. Therefore an ideal gas has no internal potential energy, and the internal energy of an ideal monatomic gas is just the sum of the kinetic energies of its atoms. Using $K_\text{avg} = \frac{3}{2}k_B T$ from Topic 9.1 and multiplying by the number of atoms $N$ gives the result below. Since $N = nN_A$ and $R = N_A k_B$, the two forms are equal.

理想气体中的原子之间没有通过保守力（conservative forces）的相互作用，原子的内部结构也不考虑。因此，理想气体没有内部势能，理想单原子气体的内能就是各原子动能之和。把专题 9.1 中的 $K_\text{avg} = \frac{3}{2}k_B T$ 乘以原子数 $N$，得到下面的结果。由于 $N = nN_A$，$R = N_A k_B$，两种形式相等。

Internal energy of an ideal monatomic gas理想单原子气体的内能
$$U = \frac{3}{2}nRT = \frac{3}{2}Nk_B T$$

Here $U$ is the internal energy in J, $n$ is the number of moles, $R = 8.31\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$, $N$ is the number of atoms, $k_B = 1.38\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J/K}$ and $T$ is the kelvin temperature. Because $nRT = PV$, the same result can be written $U = \frac{3}{2}PV$, which is often the quickest form when a PV diagram supplies $P$ and $V$. The result shows that for an ideal monatomic gas the internal energy depends on the temperature alone: it is unchanged in any process in which $T$ is constant, and it rises or falls in step with $T$.

其中 $U$ 为内能，单位 J；$n$ 为物质的量；$R = 8.31\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$；$N$ 为原子数；$k_B = 1.38\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J/K}$；$T$ 为开尔文温度。因为 $nRT = PV$，同一个结果也可以写成 $U = \frac{3}{2}PV$，当 PV 图提供 $P$ 和 $V$ 时，这往往是最快的形式。这个结果表明，理想单原子气体的内能只取决于温度：凡是 $T$ 不变的过程中内能不变，内能随 $T$ 同步升降。

Changes to a system's internal energy can result in changes to the internal structure and internal behavior of that system without changing the motion of the system's center of mass. A sealed box of gas sitting on a table has a stationary center of mass, yet when it is heated the atoms move faster and its internal energy rises. When a car brakes to a stop, the kinetic energy of the car's motion becomes internal energy of the brakes, the tires and the road, which warm up. Keep these two kinds of energy apart: the energy of bulk motion of the whole object is not part of the internal energy.

系统内能的变化可以引起系统内部结构和内部行为的变化，而不改变系统质心（center of mass）的运动。放在桌上的密封气体盒子，质心静止，但加热时原子运动更快，内能升高。汽车刹车停下时，汽车运动的动能变成了刹车片、轮胎和路面的内能，它们因此变热。要把这两种能量分开：整个物体整体运动的能量不属于内能。

The First Law of Thermodynamics热力学第一定律

The first law of thermodynamics is a restatement of conservation of energy that accounts for energy transferred into or out of a system by work, heating or cooling. For an isolated system, which exchanges nothing with its surroundings, the total energy is constant. For a closed system, the change in internal energy is the sum of the energy transferred to or from the system by heating, and the work done on the system.

热力学第一定律（first law of thermodynamics）是能量守恒的另一种表述，它考虑了通过做功、加热或冷却传入或传出系统的能量。孤立系统（isolated system）与周围环境没有任何交换，其总能量保持不变。对封闭系统，内能的变化等于通过加热传入（或传出）系统的能量与外界对系统所做的功之和。

First law of thermodynamics热力学第一定律
$$\Delta U = Q + W$$

$\Delta U$ is the change in the internal energy of the system, $Q$ is the energy transferred to the system by heating, and $W$ is the work done on the system, all in joules. The sign convention used throughout this guide is the one in the equation: $Q$ is positive when energy enters the system by heating, and $Q$ is negative when it leaves by cooling; $W$ is positive when the surroundings do work on the system (for example, a piston compresses the gas), and $W$ is negative when the system does work on its surroundings (for example, the gas expands and pushes the piston outward). A positive $\Delta U$ means the system gained internal energy, which for an ideal gas means its temperature rose.

$\Delta U$ 为系统内能的变化，$Q$ 为通过加热传给系统的能量，$W$ 为外界对系统所做的功，单位均为焦耳。本指南始终使用方程中的符号约定：能量通过加热进入系统时 $Q$ 为正，通过冷却离开系统时 $Q$ 为负；外界对系统做功时 $W$ 为正（例如活塞压缩气体），系统对外界做功时 $W$ 为负（例如气体膨胀推动活塞向外运动）。$\Delta U$ 为正表示系统内能增加，对理想气体来说就是温度升高。

For an isolated system $Q = 0$ and $W = 0$, so $\Delta U = 0$: the energy may be redistributed inside the system but the total stays fixed.

对孤立系统，$Q = 0$，$W = 0$，所以 $\Delta U = 0$：能量可以在系统内部重新分配，但总量保持不变。

Work Done on a Gas外界对气体做的功

A gas does not simply sit in its container: when it expands it pushes a boundary outward, and when a piston compresses it the piston pushes inward. The work done on a system by a constant or average external pressure that changes the volume of the system, for example a piston compressing a gas in a container, is defined as follows.

气体并不只是待在容器里：它膨胀时会把边界向外推，活塞压缩它时则是活塞向内推。恒定的或平均的外界压强使系统体积发生变化时（例如活塞压缩容器中的气体），外界对系统所做的功定义如下。

Work done on a gas at constant pressure恒压下外界对气体做的功
$$W = -P\Delta V$$

$P$ is the external pressure in Pa and $\Delta V = V_f - V_i$ is the change in the volume of the gas in m³. If the gas is compressed, $\Delta V \lt 0$, so $W \gt 0$: the surroundings put energy into the gas. If the gas expands, $\Delta V \gt 0$, so $W \lt 0$: the gas gives up energy by pushing on its surroundings. If the volume is constant, $\Delta V = 0$ and $W = 0$, since a boundary that does not move does no work. Pressure times volume has units of $\mathrm{N/m^2 \times m^3 = N\,m = J}$.

$P$ 为外界压强，单位 Pa；$\Delta V = V_f - V_i$ 为气体体积的变化，单位 m³。气体被压缩时 $\Delta V \lt 0$，所以 $W \gt 0$：外界向气体输入能量。气体膨胀时 $\Delta V \gt 0$，所以 $W \lt 0$：气体推动周围环境而失去能量。体积不变时 $\Delta V = 0$，$W = 0$，因为不移动的边界不做功。压强乘体积的单位是 $\mathrm{N/m^2 \times m^3 = N\,m = J}$。

Common Mistake: Mixing sign conventions for work Some textbooks write the first law as $\Delta U = Q - W$ with $W$ the work done by the gas. This guide, and the equation $W = -P\Delta V$, use the work done on the gas. Choose one convention and use it in every step: with $\Delta U = Q + W$, an expanding gas has a negative $W$, and a compressed gas has a positive $W$. Check the sign of your final answer against the physical picture before you write it.
常见错误：混用功的符号约定 有些教材把第一定律写成 $\Delta U = Q - W$，其中 $W$ 是气体对外做的功。本指南以及方程 $W = -P\Delta V$ 使用的是外界对气体做的功。选定一种约定后，每一步都要用同一种：用 $\Delta U = Q + W$ 时，膨胀的气体 $W$ 为负，被压缩的气体 $W$ 为正。写出最后答案之前，要用物理图像检查它的符号。

PV Diagrams and the Work DonePV 图与功

A pressure-volume graph, also called a PV diagram, plots the pressure of a gas against its volume and represents a thermodynamic process as a path from one state to another. Lines of constant temperature on a PV diagram are called isotherms. For an ideal gas $PV = nRT$ is constant on an isotherm, so an isotherm is an inverse curve, and the higher the temperature, the farther the isotherm lies from the axes.

压强-体积图（pressure-volume graph），也叫 PV 图，以气体的体积为横轴、压强为纵轴，把热力学过程表示为从一个状态到另一个状态的路径。PV 图上温度恒定的线叫做等温线（isotherm）。对理想气体，等温线上 $PV = nRT$ 为常量，所以等温线是反比曲线，温度越高，等温线离坐标轴越远。

The absolute value of the work done on a gas when it expands or compresses is equal to the area under the curve of a plot of pressure against volume for the gas. For a constant pressure that is a rectangle of height $P$ and width $\Delta V$, which is just $P|\Delta V|$. For a path that changes the pressure, find the area under the path by splitting it into rectangles and triangles or by counting squares. The direction decides the sign: moving to the right (expansion) means the gas does work on its surroundings, so $W$ is negative; moving to the left (compression) means $W$ is positive. A vertical path has no width and no area under it, so it involves no work.

气体膨胀或被压缩时，外界对气体做的功的绝对值等于该气体压强-体积曲线下方的面积（area under the curve）。压强恒定时，它是高为 $P$、宽为 $\Delta V$ 的矩形，面积就是 $P|\Delta V|$。路径上压强变化时，把曲线下方的面积拆成矩形和三角形，或者数格子来求。方向决定符号：向右（膨胀）意味着气体对周围环境做功，所以 $W$ 为负；向左（压缩）意味着 $W$ 为正。竖直的路径没有宽度，下方也没有面积，所以不涉及功。

Special Processes几种特殊过程

Special cases of thermal processes depend on the relationship between the configuration of the system, the nature of the work done on it and the system's surroundings. Each one fixes one quantity or one transfer, and that simplifies the first law.

热过程的几种特殊情形取决于系统的构型、外界对它做功的性质以及系统周围的环境。每一种都固定了某个量或某种传递，从而简化了第一定律。

  • Constant volume (isovolumetric or isochoric). The boundary does not move, so $W = 0$ and $\Delta U = Q$. All the energy transferred by heating changes the internal energy. On a PV diagram it is a vertical line.
  • Constant temperature (isothermal). For an ideal gas, $T$ constant means $\Delta U = 0$, so $Q = -W$. Energy that leaves the gas as work on the surroundings has to enter it by heating, and energy put in by compression has to leave by cooling. On a PV diagram it follows an isotherm.
  • Constant pressure (isobaric). The work is $W = -P\Delta V$ and the first law is $\Delta U = Q - P\Delta V$. On a PV diagram it is a horizontal line.
  • No energy transfer by thermal processes (adiabatic). $Q = 0$, so $\Delta U = W$. A gas compressed adiabatically gains internal energy and warms; a gas that expands adiabatically does work at the expense of its internal energy and cools. Because the temperature changes, an adiabatic path crosses isotherms, and starting from the same point it lies below the isotherm for an expansion.
  • 体积恒定（等容，isovolumetric 或 isochoric）。边界不动，所以 $W = 0$，$\Delta U = Q$。通过加热传递的能量全部用来改变内能。在 PV 图上它是一条竖直线。
  • 温度恒定（等温，isothermal）。对理想气体，$T$ 不变意味着 $\Delta U = 0$，所以 $Q = -W$。以对外做功的形式离开气体的能量必须通过加热进入气体，而压缩时输入的能量必须通过冷却离开。在 PV 图上它沿着一条等温线。
  • 压强恒定（等压，isobaric）。功为 $W = -P\Delta V$，第一定律为 $\Delta U = Q - P\Delta V$。在 PV 图上它是一条水平线。
  • 没有通过热过程的能量传递（绝热，adiabatic）。$Q = 0$，所以 $\Delta U = W$。被绝热压缩的气体内能增加、温度升高；绝热膨胀的气体以消耗自身内能为代价对外做功，温度降低。因为温度在变化，绝热路径会穿过等温线，从同一起点出发，膨胀时它位于等温线下方。
Worked Example: Internal Energy of a Monatomic Gas例题：单原子气体的内能

A container holds $2.0\,\mathrm{mol}$ of an ideal monatomic gas at $300\,\mathrm{K}$. Find its internal energy, and the change in internal energy when it is heated to $400\,\mathrm{K}$. Explain why potential energy does not enter.

容器中有 $2.0\,\mathrm{mol}$ 的理想单原子气体，温度为 $300\,\mathrm{K}$。求它的内能，以及加热到 $400\,\mathrm{K}$ 时内能的变化。说明势能为什么不出现。

Step 1: Initial internal energy第 1 步：初始内能
$$U_i = \tfrac{3}{2}nRT_i = \tfrac{3}{2}(2.0)(8.31)(300) = 7.5\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$$
Step 2: Change in internal energy第 2 步：内能的变化
$$\Delta U = \tfrac{3}{2}nR\,(T_f - T_i) = \tfrac{3}{2}(2.0)(8.31)(100) = 2.5\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$$
The atoms of an ideal gas do not interact through conservative forces, so the internal energy is entirely kinetic. Why this works: a temperature rise of $100\,\mathrm{K}$ raises the average kinetic energy per atom, and $\Delta U$ is that increase summed over all atoms.理想气体的原子之间没有通过保守力的相互作用，所以内能完全是动能。为什么这样做：温度升高 $100\,\mathrm{K}$ 使每个原子的平均动能增大，而 $\Delta U$ 就是这个增量对全部原子求和。
Worked Example: Compression with Cooling例题：一边压缩一边冷却

A piston does $400\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on $0.20\,\mathrm{mol}$ of an ideal monatomic gas while the gas loses $150\,\mathrm{J}$ to the surroundings by cooling. Find the change in the internal energy and the change in the temperature.

活塞对 $0.20\,\mathrm{mol}$ 的理想单原子气体做了 $400\,\mathrm{J}$ 的功，同时气体通过冷却向周围环境放出 $150\,\mathrm{J}$ 的能量。求内能的变化和温度的变化。

Step 1: Assign signs第 1 步：确定符号
$$W = +400\,\mathrm{J}, \qquad Q = -150\,\mathrm{J}$$
Work is done on the gas, so $W$ is positive. Energy leaves by cooling, so $Q$ is negative.外界对气体做功，所以 $W$ 为正。能量通过冷却离开，所以 $Q$ 为负。
Step 2: First law第 2 步：第一定律
$$\Delta U = Q + W = -150 + 400 = +250\,\mathrm{J}$$
Step 3: Temperature change第 3 步：温度变化
$$\Delta T = \frac{\Delta U}{\tfrac{3}{2}nR} = \frac{250}{\tfrac{3}{2}(0.20)(8.31)} = +1.0\times10^{2}\,\mathrm{K}$$
The gas gets warmer even though it lost energy by cooling, because the work done on it was larger than the energy it lost. Why this works: the first law counts every transfer, so the net gain is $+250\,\mathrm{J}$.气体尽管通过冷却失去了能量，温度仍然升高，因为外界对它做的功大于它失去的能量。为什么这样做：第一定律把每一种传递都计入，所以净增加量是 $+250\,\mathrm{J}$。
Worked Example: Isobaric Expansion on a PV Diagram例题：PV 图上的等压膨胀

The magnitude, $200\,\mathrm{J}$, is the shaded area. It is negative because the gas expands and does work on its surroundings.大小 $200\,\mathrm{J}$ 就是阴影面积。因为气体膨胀并对周围环境做功，所以符号为负。

The gas needs $500\,\mathrm{J}$ by heating: $300\,\mathrm{J}$ raises its internal energy (it ends twice as hot, since $PV$ doubled) and $200\,\mathrm{J}$ goes out as work on the surroundings. Worked Example: Estimating the Work in an Isothermal Expansion例题：估算等温膨胀中的功

The same gas expands isothermally from A to the volume $2.0\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ along the isotherm of Figure 9.4, where the pressure falls to $1.0\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$. Bound the work done on the gas, and find $\Delta U$ and $Q$.

$$P_f|\Delta V| = 100\,\mathrm{J} \;\lt\; |W| \;\lt\; \tfrac{1}{2}(P_i + P_f)|\Delta V| = 150\,\mathrm{J}$$
The area under the curve is more than the rectangle at the lower final pressure and less than the trapezoid under the straight line joining the end points, because the isotherm sags below that line. Worked Example: A Closed Cycle例题：一个闭合循环

Find the work done on the gas along each leg, the net work, and the net energy transferred by heating over one cycle.

气体沿循环 A、B、C、D、A 变化：A（$2.0\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$，$1.0\,\mathrm{L}$）经等压过程到 B（$2.0\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$，$2.0\,\mathrm{L}$）；B 经等容过程到 C（$1.0\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$，$2.0\,\mathrm{L}$）；C 经等压过程到 D（$1.0\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$，$1.0\,\mathrm{L}$）；D 经等容过程回到 A。求每一段上外界对气体做的功、净功，以及一个循环中通过加热传递的净能量。

Step 1: Work on each leg第 1 步：每一段上的功
$$W_{AB} = -(2.0\times10^{5})(+1.0\times10^{-3}) = -200\,\mathrm{J}, \quad W_{BC} = 0$$
$$W_{CD} = -(1.0\times10^{5})(-1.0\times10^{-3}) = +100\,\mathrm{J}, \quad W_{DA} = 0$$

Here $\Delta U_\text{net}$ is the net change in internal energy over one full cycle.其中 $\Delta U_\text{net}$ 是一个完整循环中内能的净变化。
The gas does more work expanding at high pressure than is done on it compressing at low pressure, so the net work done on it is negative, $100\,\mathrm{J}$ in magnitude. The gas returns to its starting state, so $U$ is unchanged, and the net energy it absorbs by heating equals the net work it does. Why this works: $U$ depends only on the state, so around any closed path $\Delta U = 0$.气体在高压下膨胀时做的功多于在低压下被压缩时外界对它做的功，所以外界对它做的净功为负，大小为 $100\,\mathrm{J}$。气体回到起始状态，所以 $U$ 不变，它通过加热吸收的净能量等于它做的净功。为什么这样做：$U$ 只取决于状态，所以沿任何闭合路径 $\Delta U = 0$。

Specific Heat: Energy to Change a Temperature比热容：改变温度所需的能量

Different objects need different amounts of energy to warm up by the same number of degrees. A kilogram of water takes about eleven times as much energy to warm by one kelvin as a kilogram of copper. The energy required to change the temperature of a material is related to the material's specific heat $c$, the energy needed to raise the temperature of one kilogram of the material by one kelvin. It scales with how much material there is and with how large a temperature change you want.

不同的物体升高相同的温度需要的能量不同。把一千克水升高一开尔文所需的能量，大约是一千克铜的十一倍。改变材料温度所需的能量与该材料的比热容（specific heat）$c$ 有关，比热容是使一千克该材料温度升高一开尔文所需的能量。所需能量与材料的多少以及想要的温度变化量成正比。

Energy to change the temperature改变温度所需的能量
$$Q = mc\Delta T$$

$Q$ is the energy transferred to the object by heating in J (negative when energy leaves), $m$ is its mass in kg, $c$ is the specific heat in $\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$, and $\Delta T = T_f - T_i$ is the change in temperature. A change of one degree Celsius is the same size as a change of one kelvin, so $\Delta T$ may be taken in either unit; the temperatures $T_i$ and $T_f$ themselves must be on the same scale. Typical values, given as data in a problem when needed, are about $4.19\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$ for liquid water, $900$ for aluminum and $385$ for copper. The sign of $Q$ follows the sign of $\Delta T$: heating a substance that stays at one specific heat gives $Q \gt 0$ and $\Delta T \gt 0$, and cooling gives both negative.

$Q$ 为通过加热传给物体的能量，单位 J（能量流出时为负）；$m$ 为物体的质量，单位 kg；$c$ 为比热容，单位 $\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$；$\Delta T = T_f - T_i$ 为温度的变化。摄氏度的一格与开尔文的一格大小相同，所以 $\Delta T$ 用哪个单位都可以；而 $T_i$ 和 $T_f$ 本身必须在同一温标上。需要时题目会给出典型数值：液态水约为 $4.19\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$，铝为 $900$，铜为 $385$。$Q$ 的符号与 $\Delta T$ 一致：加热时 $Q \gt 0$、$\Delta T \gt 0$，冷却时两者都为负。

AP Physics 2 models specific heat as independent of temperature: the value of $c$ is treated as constant over the range of temperatures in a problem, so a single multiplication gives the energy for the whole change.

AP Physics 2 把比热容当作与温度无关来建模：在题目涉及的温度范围内，$c$ 的值视为常量，所以一次乘法就能得到整个变化过程所需的能量。

The specific heat of a material is an intrinsic property of that material: it depends on the type of material, and on the arrangement and interactions of the atoms that make it up, not on how much of it you have. A tiny copper pin and a huge copper beam have the same $c$, although the beam has a much larger heat capacity $mc$. The atoms explain the differences. In an ideal monatomic gas held at constant volume all the energy supplied goes into kinetic energy, since $Q = \Delta U = \frac{3}{2}nR\Delta T$, which is $\frac{3}{2}R = 12.5\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$ per mole. In a solid the atoms are bound to their neighbors, and part of the energy goes into the potential energy of the configuration as well, so more energy is needed per degree: copper takes about $385 \times 0.0635 = 24\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$, nearly twice the gas value.

材料的比热容是该材料的内在性质（intrinsic property）：它取决于材料的种类以及构成材料的原子的排列与相互作用，而与材料有多少无关。一根小铜针和一根巨大的铜梁 $c$ 相同，尽管铜梁的热容 $mc$ 大得多。原子层面的图像解释了这些差别。体积保持不变的理想单原子气体中，供给的能量全部变成动能，因为 $Q = \Delta U = \frac{3}{2}nR\Delta T$，即每摩尔 $\frac{3}{2}R = 12.5\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$。固体中原子与邻近原子相互束缚，供给的能量有一部分还变成了构型的势能，所以每升高一度需要更多的能量：铜约为 $385 \times 0.0635 = 24\,\mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}$，几乎是气体值的两倍。

Reaching Equilibrium: Mixing at Different Temperatures趋向平衡：不同温度的物体混合

When two objects at different temperatures are placed in thermal contact inside an insulated container, energy flows from the hotter to the cooler until the temperatures are equal. The two objects together form an isolated system, so the total energy is constant: the energy lost by one equals the energy gained by the other. Writing this with the sign convention of the first law, $Q_1 + Q_2 = 0$, so $m_1 c_1 (T_f - T_{1,i}) + m_2 c_2 (T_f - T_{2,i}) = 0$ with a single final temperature $T_f$, which lies between the two starting temperatures. Because the object with the larger heat capacity $mc$ changes temperature less, $T_f$ lies closer to its starting temperature.

把两个温度不同的物体放入隔热容器中使其热接触，能量从较热的物体流向较冷的物体，直到温度相等。这两个物体合起来构成孤立系统，所以总能量守恒：一个物体失去的能量等于另一个物体得到的能量。按第一定律的符号约定来写就是 $Q_1 + Q_2 = 0$，即 $m_1 c_1 (T_f - T_{1,i}) + m_2 c_2 (T_f - T_{2,i}) = 0$，其中末温 $T_f$ 只有一个，介于两个初温之间。热容 $mc$ 较大的物体温度变化较小，所以 $T_f$ 更接近它的初温。

Thermal Conductivity: The Rate of Conduction热导率：热传导的速率

Topic 9.3 introduced conduction qualitatively. The rate at which energy is transferred by conduction through a given material is related to the thermal conductivity $k$ of the material, the physical dimensions of the material (the cross-sectional area $A$ and the thickness $L$), and the temperature difference $\Delta T$ across the material.

专题 9.3 定性地介绍了热传导。通过一块材料的热传导传递能量的速率，与该材料的热导率（thermal conductivity）$k$、材料的几何尺寸（横截面积 $A$ 和厚度 $L$）以及材料两侧的温差 $\Delta T$ 有关。

Rate of conduction热传导速率
$$\frac{Q}{\Delta t} = \frac{kA\Delta T}{L}$$

Here $Q/\Delta t$ is the rate of energy transfer in watts ($1\,\mathrm{W} = 1\,\mathrm{J/s}$), $k$ is the thermal conductivity in $\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$, $A$ is the area through which the energy passes in m², $\Delta T$ is the temperature difference between the two faces in K, and $L$ is the thickness of the material in the direction of energy flow, in m. Read it as proportions. The rate is directly proportional to $k$, to $A$ and to $\Delta T$, and inversely proportional to the thickness $L$. A larger area gives more paths for energy to travel along; a greater temperature difference drives a faster flow; and a thicker layer slows the flow. Doubling $A$ doubles the rate, and doubling $L$ halves it.

其中 $Q/\Delta t$ 为能量传递的速率，单位瓦特（$1\,\mathrm{W} = 1\,\mathrm{J/s}$）；$k$ 为热导率，单位 $\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$；$A$ 为能量通过的面积，单位 m²；$\Delta T$ 为两个表面之间的温差，单位 K；$L$ 为材料沿能量流动方向的厚度，单位 m。把它当作比例关系来读：速率与 $k$、$A$ 和 $\Delta T$ 成正比，与厚度 $L$ 成反比。面积越大，能量可以通过的通道越多；温差越大，流动越快；厚度越大，流动越慢。$A$ 加倍则速率加倍，$L$ 加倍则速率减半。

The thermal conductivity of a material is an intrinsic property of the material that depends on the arrangement and interactions of the atoms that make it up. Metals have a high $k$ (copper is about $400\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$) because the energy is carried efficiently through the closely packed, strongly interacting atoms and the loosely bound electrons among them. Glass is about $1\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$ and still air only about $0.03\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$, since the atoms of a gas are far apart and collide only occasionally. That is why trapped air in wool, foam and double-glazed windows is such a good insulator. A material with low $k$ is a thermal insulator, and one with high $k$ is a thermal conductor.

材料的热导率是该材料的内在性质，取决于构成材料的原子的排列与相互作用。金属的 $k$ 很大（铜约为 $400\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$），因为能量可以通过紧密排列、相互作用很强的原子以及其间束缚较松的电子高效地传递。玻璃约为 $1\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$，静止空气只有约 $0.03\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$，因为气体原子相距很远，只是偶尔碰撞。这就是被困住的空气在羊毛、泡沫塑料和双层玻璃窗中如此有效地隔热的原因。$k$ 小的材料是热的绝缘体，$k$ 大的材料是热的良导体。

Common Mistake: Confusing specific heat with thermal conductivity The two properties answer different questions. Specific heat $c$ tells how much energy it takes to change the temperature of a given mass by one kelvin. Thermal conductivity $k$ tells how fast energy passes through a layer when there is a temperature difference across it. Water has a large $c$ but a small $k$; copper has a small $c$ and a large $k$. A material that is slow to change temperature is not necessarily a good insulator.
常见错误：混淆比热容与热导率 这两个性质回答的是不同的问题。比热容 $c$ 说明使给定质量的物体温度改变一开尔文需要多少能量。热导率 $k$ 说明一层材料两侧有温差时能量通过它的快慢。水的 $c$ 大而 $k$ 小；铜的 $c$ 小而 $k$ 大。温度变化慢的材料不一定是好的绝热体。
Worked Example: Heating Water例题：给水加热

A heater of power $1500\,\mathrm{W}$ warms $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water from $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $80^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Neglecting energy lost to the surroundings, find the energy needed and the time it takes. Use $c = 4.19\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$.

功率为 $1500\,\mathrm{W}$ 的加热器把 $0.50\,\mathrm{kg}$ 的水从 $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 加热到 $80^\circ\mathrm{C}$。忽略向周围环境损失的能量，求所需的能量和所用的时间。取 $c = 4.19\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$。

Step 1: Energy第 1 步：能量
$$Q = mc\Delta T = (0.50)(4.19\times10^{3})(60) = 1.26\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{J}$$
Step 2: Time from the power第 2 步：由功率求时间
$$\Delta t = \frac{Q}{P_h} = \frac{1.26\times10^{5}}{1500} = 84\,\mathrm{s}$$
Here $P_h = 1500\,\mathrm{W}$ is the power of the heater.其中 $P_h = 1500\,\mathrm{W}$ 是加热器的功率。
Why this works: power is energy per unit time, and $\Delta T = 60\,\mathrm{K}$ is the same number in kelvin and in degrees Celsius.为什么这样做：功率是单位时间内的能量，而 $\Delta T = 60\,\mathrm{K}$ 用开尔文和摄氏度表示数值相同。

Worked Example: Finding Specific Heat from a Heating Graph例题：由加热曲线求比热容

A student wants to measure the specific heat of an unknown liquid. She places $0.400\,\mathrm{kg}$ of it in an insulated cup and heats it with an electric heater of constant power $60.0\,\mathrm{W}$, stirring gently. The temperature is $20.0$, $23.8$, $27.4$, $31.3$ and $35.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$ at times $0$, $60$, $120$, $180$ and $240\,\mathrm{s}$. Describe the graph to plot, find $c$ from its slope, and say how heat lost to the room would affect the result.

一位学生想测量某种未知液体的比热容。她把 $0.400\,\mathrm{kg}$ 的液体放入隔热杯中，用恒定功率 $60.0\,\mathrm{W}$ 的电加热器加热，并轻轻搅拌。在 $0$、$60$、$120$、$180$、$240\,\mathrm{s}$ 时，温度依次为 $20.0$、$23.8$、$27.4$、$31.3$、$35.0^\circ\mathrm{C}$。说明应该画什么图，由斜率求 $c$，并说明向房间散失的热量会怎样影响结果。

Step 1: Linearize第 1 步：线性化
$$Q = P_h\,t = mc\Delta T \;\Rightarrow\; T = T_i + \frac{P_h}{mc}\,t$$
Plot temperature on the vertical axis against time on the horizontal axis. The graph is a straight line whose slope is $P_h/(mc)$, where $P_h$ is the power of the heater.以温度为纵轴、时间为横轴作图。图像是一条直线，斜率为 $P_h/(mc)$，其中 $P_h$ 是加热器的功率。

$$\beta = \frac{35.0 - 20.0}{240} = 0.0625\,\mathrm{K/s}, \qquad c = \frac{P_h}{m \times \beta} = \frac{60.0}{(0.400)(0.0625)} = 2.4\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$$
Here $\beta$ is the slope of the line.其中 $\beta$ 是直线的斜率。
Step 3: Effect of energy lost to the room第 3 步：向房间散失能量的影响
If some of the heater's energy escapes, the liquid gains less than $P_h\,t$, so the real slope is smaller than the ideal one. The calculation assumes all the energy is absorbed, so it would give a value of $c$ that is too large. Why this works: a smaller measured slope is read as a greater energy cost per kelvin. Insulating and stirring, and using a short time so the liquid stays near room temperature, reduce the error.如果加热器的部分能量散失了，液体得到的能量就小于 $P_h\,t$，所以真实斜率比理想斜率小。计算时假定全部能量都被吸收，所以得到的 $c$ 偏大。为什么这样做：测得的斜率偏小，被解读为每升高一开尔文需要的能量更多。做好隔热与搅拌，并使用较短的时间使液体温度保持接近室温，可以减小误差。
Worked Example: Heat Loss through a Window例题：通过窗户的热损失

A glass window of area $1.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and thickness $4.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ has $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$ on its inner face and $0^\circ\mathrm{C}$ on its outer face. For glass $k = 0.80\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$. Find the rate of energy transfer, the energy lost in one hour, and the rate if the glass is twice as thick.

一块面积为 $1.5\,\mathrm{m^2}$、厚度为 $4.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ 的玻璃窗，内表面温度为 $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$，外表面温度为 $0^\circ\mathrm{C}$。玻璃的 $k = 0.80\,\mathrm{W/(m\,K)}$。求能量传递的速率、一小时内损失的能量，以及玻璃厚度加倍时的速率。

The Second Law of Thermodynamics热力学第二定律

The first law says that energy is conserved, but it does not say which processes actually happen. A hot cup of coffee cools to room temperature, yet a cup of coffee at room temperature never spontaneously heats itself while the room gets cooler, although that would also conserve energy. A gas expands to fill a container, yet a gas that has spread through a room never gathers by itself into one corner. The second law of thermodynamics captures this one-way direction using a quantity called entropy. It states that the total entropy of an isolated system can never decrease, and is constant only when all the processes the system undergoes are reversible.

第一定律说的是能量守恒，但没有说哪些过程真的会发生。一杯热咖啡会冷却到室温，而室温下的咖啡从不会在房间变冷的同时自发升温，尽管这样也满足能量守恒。气体会膨胀充满容器，而已经散布到整个房间的气体从不会自己聚集到一个角落。热力学第二定律（second law of thermodynamics）用一个叫做熵（entropy）的量来描述这种单向性。它指出：孤立系统的总熵永不减小（never decrease），只有当系统经历的所有过程都是可逆的时，熵才保持不变。

A reversible process is an idealisation in which the system and its surroundings can both be returned exactly to their initial states, with no net change anywhere. A frictionless pendulum or a very slow, frictionless compression of a gas, always infinitesimally close to equilibrium, are the usual models. Real processes involve friction, sudden expansions, or energy flowing across a finite temperature difference, and are irreversible: for them the entropy of an isolated system increases. In AP Physics 2 the treatment of the second law and entropy is qualitative. You reason with the direction of change and with the ideas of spreading and availability, and you do not calculate entropy values.

可逆过程是一种理想化：系统和周围环境都可以精确地回到初始状态，任何地方都没有净变化。无摩擦的摆，或者极其缓慢、无摩擦、始终无限接近平衡的气体压缩，是常用的模型。真实过程都有摩擦、突然膨胀，或者能量在有限温差下流动，它们是不可逆的（irreversible）：对它们而言，孤立系统的熵增加。在 AP Physics 2 中，对第二定律和熵的处理是定性的（qualitative）。你要依据变化的方向以及扩散和可用性的思想来推理，不需要计算熵的数值。

Entropy as Energy Spreading Out熵：能量的扩散

Entropy can be described qualitatively in two connected ways. First, it is the tendency of energy to spread out: localized energy will tend to disperse and spread out. A hot spot cools as its energy is shared with the surroundings; a concentrated gas spreads into all the volume it can reach; the kinetic energy of an organized motion, such as a bouncing ball, becomes the random thermal motion of the atoms of the ball and the floor. Second, entropy is the unavailability of some of the system's energy to do work. A hot block next to a cold block can drive a process, because the temperature difference lets energy flow in a useful direction. After the two blocks have reached the same temperature, the same total energy is still there, but it is spread out and can no longer be used to do work. Each spontaneous spreading raises the entropy and reduces the energy that is available.

熵可以用两种相互联系的方式定性地描述。第一，它是能量趋向于扩散（spread out）的倾向：集中的能量会趋于分散开来。热点把能量分给周围环境而冷却；集中的气体扩散到它所能到达的全部体积；有序运动的动能（例如弹跳的球）变成球和地面原子的无规则热运动。第二，熵是系统中一部分能量无法用来做功（unavailable to do work）。紧挨着冷块的热块能够驱动某个过程，因为温差让能量可以沿着有用的方向流动。两块物体达到相同温度之后，总能量仍然在那里，但已经分散开，不能再用来做功。每一次自发的扩散都使熵增大，使可用的能量减少。

This is connected to the probability argument of Topic 9.3. The arrangements in which energy is spread evenly can be realized in vastly more ways than those in which it is concentrated, so an isolated system that is free to change moves toward the more probable, more spread-out state. A closely related fact is that a gas has no reason to stay in one half of a container. If each atom is equally likely to be in either half, the probability that all $N$ atoms are found in one chosen half is $(1/2)^N$, which for any macroscopic sample is so small that it never happens.

这与专题 9.3 的概率论证相联系。能量均匀分散的分配方式，可以用比能量集中的分配方式多得多的方法实现，所以可以自由变化的孤立系统会朝着更可能、更分散的状态移动。一个与此密切相关的事实是：气体没有理由待在容器的一半里。如果每个原子处于任一半的可能性相等，那么全部 $N$ 个原子都在指定的一半里的概率是 $(1/2)^N$，对任何宏观样品来说，这个概率小到永远不会发生。

State Function and Equilibrium状态函数与平衡

Entropy is a state function: it depends only on the current state or configuration of the system, and not on how the system reached that state. The same is true of the pressure, volume, temperature and internal energy of a gas. So if a gas goes from one state to another by two different paths, its entropy change is the same on both paths, even though the work and the energy transferred by heating are different. This is what allows you to compare the end states without knowing the path.

熵是状态函数（state function）：它只取决于系统当前的状态或构型，而与系统如何到达这个状态无关。气体的压强、体积、温度和内能也是如此。所以，如果气体沿两条不同的路径从一个状态变到另一个状态，两条路径上它的熵变相同，尽管功和通过加热传递的能量不同。这使你不必知道路径就能比较两个终态。

Maximum entropy occurs when a system is in thermodynamic equilibrium. In equilibrium the temperature is uniform and the gas is spread evenly, so no further spreading is possible. Isolated systems spontaneously move toward thermodynamic equilibrium. Once there, nothing happens spontaneously on the large scale, and the entropy stays at its maximum value.

系统处于热力学平衡（thermodynamic equilibrium）时熵最大（maximum entropy）。平衡时温度均匀，气体分布均匀，不可能再有进一步的扩散。孤立系统自发地趋向热力学平衡。一旦到达平衡，宏观上不再有自发的变化，熵保持在最大值。

Isolated Systems, Closed Systems and the Surroundings孤立系统、封闭系统与周围环境

The change in a system's entropy is determined by the system's interactions with its surroundings. An isolated system exchanges neither energy nor matter with its surroundings, so its entropy never decreases. A closed system can exchange energy with its surroundings (by heating or by work) but not matter. Because energy can be transferred into or out of a closed system, its entropy can decrease. A cup of coffee cooling in a room, a refrigerator's interior as it chills food, or a gas squeezed by a piston while energy flows out of it are all examples. In each case the entropy of the surroundings rises by at least as much as the entropy of the system falls, so the total for the system and surroundings together, which is an isolated system, does not decrease. The second law is never violated: the decrease in one place is paid for by an increase elsewhere.

系统的熵变由系统与周围环境（surroundings）的相互作用决定。孤立系统与周围环境既不交换能量也不交换物质，所以它的熵永不减小。封闭系统（closed system）可以与周围环境交换能量（通过加热或做功），但不交换物质。因为能量可以传入或传出封闭系统，它的熵可以减小。房间里逐渐冷却的咖啡、冷藏食物时冰箱的内部、被活塞压缩同时向外放出能量的气体，都是例子。每种情形中，周围环境的熵至少增加得和系统的熵减少得一样多，所以系统与周围环境合起来（这是一个孤立系统）的总熵不会减小。第二定律从未被违背：一处的减少，由另一处的增加来补偿。

Common Mistake: Applying "entropy never decreases" to every system The statement is for an isolated system, or for a system together with all of its surroundings. A closed system that loses energy to its surroundings can end with lower entropy: water frozen in a freezer, a hot drink cooling, or a gas being compressed and cooled. These are not violations of the second law, because the surroundings gain entropy. Do not say that a refrigerator or a growing plant breaks the second law; check where the energy goes and what the surroundings do.
常见错误：对每个系统都用"熵永不减小" 这个陈述针对的是孤立系统，或者系统连同它的全部周围环境。向周围环境放出能量的封闭系统，最终的熵可以更低：冰箱里结冰的水、变凉的热饮、被压缩并冷却的气体。这些并不违反第二定律，因为周围环境的熵增加了。不要说冰箱或者生长的植物违背了第二定律；要检查能量去了哪里，以及周围环境发生了什么。
Worked Example: Why a Gas Expands into a Vacuum and Never Back例题：为什么气体会膨胀进入真空而不会自己回来

An isolated container has a partition. Gas fills the left half and the right half is empty. The partition is removed. Predict what happens, find the probability that all the atoms are found in the left half at a later time for $N = 4$, $10$, and $100$, and explain the direction of change using entropy.

The atoms spread throughout the whole container, because an isolated system moves toward equilibrium, and the equilibrium state is the one with the gas evenly spread.原子会扩散到整个容器，因为孤立系统趋向平衡，而平衡状态就是气体均匀分布的状态。

For a mole of gas, $N \approx 6\times10^{23}$ and the probability is far below any chance of ever being seen. The spread-out state is overwhelmingly more probable, so it is the state with the maximum entropy, and the expansion increases the entropy of this isolated system. The reverse, a gas collecting itself in one half, would decrease the entropy of an isolated system, which the second law does not allow. Worked Example: Energy Moving from a Cold Region to a Hot One例题：能量从冷的地方到热的地方

A refrigerator removes $300\,\mathrm{J}$ by cooling the food inside (the cold region) and delivers $400\,\mathrm{J}$ to the warmer kitchen. Use the first law to find the work done on the refrigerator's working material, and use the second law to explain why this work input is required.

冰箱通过冷却内部的食物（低温区域）取走 $300\,\mathrm{J}$ 的能量，并向较暖的厨房释放 $400\,\mathrm{J}$ 的能量。用第一定律求对冰箱工作物质所做的功，并用第二定律解释为什么必须有这份功的输入。

Step 1: First law over one full cycle of the working material第 1 步：工作物质一个完整循环的第一定律
$$\Delta U = 0 = Q_\text{in} + Q_\text{out} + W \;\Rightarrow\; 0 = (+300) + (-400) + W \;\Rightarrow\; W = +100\,\mathrm{J}$$
The working material takes in $300\,\mathrm{J}$ by heating, gives out $400\,\mathrm{J}$ by cooling, and returns to its starting state with $\Delta U = 0$. Energy conservation therefore requires $100\,\mathrm{J}$ of work done on it, which the refrigerator's motor supplies.工作物质通过加热吸收 $300\,\mathrm{J}$，通过冷却放出 $400\,\mathrm{J}$，并带着 $\Delta U = 0$ 回到起始状态。所以能量守恒要求对它做 $100\,\mathrm{J}$ 的功，这由冰箱的电动机提供。
Step 2: Why the work is needed第 2 步：为什么需要这份功
Energy moving from the cold food to the warm kitchen is the opposite of the spontaneous direction. If it happened with no other change, the entropy of the isolated food-and-kitchen system would decrease, which the second law forbids. With the motor running, the electric energy supplied ends up as additional energy delivered to the kitchen, and the process as a whole, including the motor and the power source, increases the total entropy. The entropy of the food decreases, as it is a closed system that loses energy, and the entropy of the surroundings increases by more. Why this works: the second law does not forbid energy flowing from cold to hot, only doing so with nothing else changing.能量从冷的食物传到暖的厨房，与自发方向相反。如果这件事发生而没有任何其他变化，食物与厨房这个孤立系统的熵就会减小，这是第二定律所禁止的。电动机运转时，供给的电能最终变成多送到厨房的能量，包括电动机和电源在内的整个过程使总熵增大。食物的熵减小，因为它是失去能量的封闭系统，而周围环境的熵增加得更多。为什么这样做：第二定律并不禁止能量从冷处流向热处，只是禁止在没有任何其他变化的情况下发生。
Worked Example: Which Way Does an Isolated System Evolve?例题：孤立系统朝哪个方向演化？

Return to the counting model of Topic 9.3, with 2 atoms in each of systems A and B sharing 4 units of energy. The system starts with all 4 units in A ($q_A = 4$). Say which of the states $q_A = 4$ and $q_A = 2$ has the larger entropy, which way the isolated system changes, and whether it could change back to $q_A = 4$.

回到专题 9.3 的计数模型：系统 A 和 B 各有 2 个原子，共享 4 个能量单位。系统开始时 4 个单位全在 A 中（$q_A = 4$）。判断 $q_A = 4$ 和 $q_A = 2$ 哪个状态的熵较大，孤立系统朝哪个方向变化，以及它能否变回 $q_A = 4$。

Step 1: Compare the number of arrangements第 1 步：比较分配方式的数目
$$q_A = 4:\ (5)(1) = 5, \qquad q_A = 2:\ (3)(3) = 9$$
Step 2: Direction and reversibility第 2 步：方向与可逆性
The evenly shared state can be realized in more ways, so it is the state with greater entropy and it is the equilibrium state. The isolated system moves from $q_A = 4$ toward $q_A = 2$ as energy spreads from A to B. A return to $q_A = 4$ is possible in this tiny model, with probability $5/35$ at any moment, but the odds against it grow without limit as the number of atoms increases. Why this works: the direction of spontaneous change is toward more arrangements, which is toward the maximum entropy at equilibrium.平均分配的状态可以用更多的方式实现，所以它是熵较大的状态，也是平衡状态。能量从 A 扩散到 B，孤立系统从 $q_A = 4$ 向 $q_A = 2$ 变化。在这个微小的模型里，回到 $q_A = 4$ 是可能的，任一时刻的概率为 $5/35$，但随着原子数增加，这种可能性无限地变小。所以在大系统中这个过程是不可逆的：熵增大，不会自发地回落。为什么这样做：自发变化的方向是朝向分配方式更多的方向，也就是朝向平衡时的最大熵。
Worked Example: Entropy as a State Function例题：作为状态函数的熵

An ideal gas goes from state A to state B, where B has twice the volume and the same temperature as A. By path 1 the gas expands freely into a vacuum inside an insulated container. By path 2 the gas expands slowly against a piston while in contact with a warm reservoir, doing about $140\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on the piston. Compare $Q$, $W$, $\Delta U$ and the entropy change of the gas for the two paths.

理想气体从状态 A 变到状态 B，B 的体积是 A 的两倍，温度与 A 相同。路径 1：气体在隔热容器中自由膨胀进入真空。路径 2：气体与温度较高的热库接触，缓慢推动活塞膨胀，对活塞做了约 $140\,\mathrm{J}$ 的功。比较两条路径的 $Q$、$W$、$\Delta U$ 以及气体的熵变。

Step 1: Path 1, free expansion第 1 步：路径 1，自由膨胀
$$Q = 0, \qquad W = 0, \qquad \Delta U = 0$$
Step 2: Path 2, slow isothermal expansion第 2 步：路径 2，缓慢等温膨胀
$$W \approx -140\,\mathrm{J}, \qquad \Delta U = 0, \qquad Q = \Delta U - W \approx +140\,\mathrm{J}$$
Step 3: Compare the entropy第 3 步：比较熵
$Q$ and $W$ are different on the two paths, so they are not state functions. The gas has the same initial and final states on both paths, so its entropy change is the same: positive, because the energy has spread over a larger volume. On path 1 the system is isolated and the process is irreversible, so the entropy of the whole system increases. On path 2 the warm reservoir loses $140\,\mathrm{J}$ and its entropy falls, while the gas's entropy rises. Because this slow process is reversible, the two changes cancel and the total is constant. Why this works: entropy belongs to the state, but how much of the change is shared with the surroundings depends on the path.两条路径上 $Q$ 和 $W$ 不同，所以它们不是状态函数。两条路径上气体的初态和末态相同，所以它的熵变相同：为正，因为能量扩散到了更大的体积。在路径 1 中，系统是孤立的，过程不可逆，所以整个系统的熵增大。在路径 2 中，热库失去 $140\,\mathrm{J}$，它的熵减小，而气体的熵增加。由于这个缓慢的过程是可逆的，两个变化相互抵消，所以总熵保持恒定。为什么这样做：熵属于状态，但变化中有多少与周围环境分担，取决于路径。

Energy flows spontaneously from the hot block to the cold block until they have the same temperature. The box is an isolated system and this process is irreversible, so its entropy increases and reaches a maximum at thermal equilibrium. The energy has spread out over both blocks. At the beginning the temperature difference could drive a process that does work, but once the blocks are at the same temperature the same total energy is no longer available to do work.

能量自发地从热块流向冷块，直到两者温度相同。盒子是孤立系统，这个过程不可逆，所以它的熵增大，并在热平衡时达到最大值。能量已经扩散到两个物块上。开始时，温差可以驱动某个做功的过程，而两块物体温度相同之后，同样的总能量就不再能用来做功。

3. A closed jar of water placed in a freezer ends with lower entropy than it started. Justify why this does not violate the second law.3. 把一个密封的水罐放进冷冻室，最终它的熵比开始时更低。论证（justify）为什么这不违反第二定律。

The jar of water is a closed system, not an isolated one, because energy leaves it by cooling into the freezer. The second law requires only that the total entropy of an isolated system never decreases. The air and the refrigeration machinery of the freezer, which are the surroundings, gain more entropy than the water loses, so the total entropy of the water and its surroundings does not decrease.

水罐是封闭系统，而不是孤立系统，因为能量通过冷却离开它进入冷冻室。第二定律只要求孤立系统的总熵永不减小。冷冻室的空气和制冷设备是周围环境，它们增加的熵多于水减少的熵，所以水与其周围环境的总熵没有减小。

4. Calculate the probability that all $20$ atoms of a gas are found in the left half of a container at a particular instant, if each atom is equally likely to be in either half. Explain what this means for a mole of gas.4. 如果每个原子处于容器左半部分或右半部分的可能性相等，计算（calculate）某一时刻气体的全部 $20$ 个原子都在左半部分的概率。说明这对一摩尔气体意味着什么。

$P = (1/2)^{20} = 9.5\times10^{-7}$, about one in a million. For a mole of atoms the exponent is about $6\times10^{23}$, so the probability is so small that it is never observed. This is why a gas spreads out through all the volume available to it and does not collect in one half.

$P = (1/2)^{20} = 9.5\times10^{-7}$，约为百万分之一。对一摩尔原子，指数约为 $6\times10^{23}$，概率小到永远观察不到。这就是气体会扩散到它能到达的全部体积中，而不会聚集在一半里的原因。

5. Explain what it means to say that entropy is a state function, and use this to compare the entropy change of a gas that goes between the same two states by one irreversible path and one reversible path.5. 解释（explain）熵是状态函数是什么意思，并据此比较气体在相同的两个状态之间沿一条不可逆路径和一条可逆路径变化时的熵变。

A state function depends only on the current state, not on the way the system got there. The entropy of the gas therefore has the same initial and final values, and the same change, on both paths. What differs from path to path is the work and the energy transferred by heating, and how the entropy change is shared between the gas and its surroundings.

状态函数只取决于当前的状态，与系统如何到达该状态无关。所以气体的熵在两条路径上有相同的初值和末值，熵变也相同。不同路径上不同的是功和通过加热传递的能量，以及熵的变化在气体与周围环境之间如何分配。

6. A sealed, insulated box has a hot gas in one half and a cold gas in the other, separated by a thin conducting wall. Describe how it changes and what its final state looks like in terms of entropy.6. 一个密封隔热的盒子里，一半是热气体，另一半是冷气体，中间隔着一层导热薄壁。描述（describe）它如何变化，以及从熵的角度看最终状态是什么样的。

The box is an isolated system, so it moves spontaneously toward thermodynamic equilibrium: energy flows from the hot gas to the cold gas until both are at the same temperature. During this irreversible process the entropy of the box increases. At equilibrium the entropy has its maximum value and no further net energy transfer occurs.

盒子是孤立系统，所以它自发地趋向热力学平衡：能量从热气体流向冷气体，直到两者温度相同。在这个不可逆过程中，盒子的熵增大。平衡时熵取最大值，不再有净能量传递。

Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Substituting Celsius temperatures into $PV = nRT$, $K_\text{avg} = \frac{3}{2}k_B T$ or $U = \frac{3}{2}nRT$.
  2. Using gauge pressure in the ideal gas law, or forgetting to convert litres and cm³ to m³.
  3. Mixing the two sign conventions for work: with $\Delta U = Q + W$, an expanding gas has $W \lt 0$.
  4. Saying that a gas holds "heat", or that cold flows into a warm object, instead of describing energy transferred by heating from higher to lower temperature.
  5. Taking an adiabatic process to be isothermal: $Q = 0$ does not mean $\Delta T = 0$.
  6. Treating specific heat as thermal conductivity, or the reverse.
  7. Claiming that entropy decreases in an isolated system, or that a closed system's entropy can never decrease.
最常见的错误
  1. 把摄氏温度代入 $PV = nRT$、$K_\text{avg} = \frac{3}{2}k_B T$ 或 $U = \frac{3}{2}nRT$。
  2. 在理想气体定律中使用表压，或者忘记把升和 cm³ 换算成 m³。
  3. 混用功的两种符号约定：用 $\Delta U = Q + W$ 时，膨胀气体的 $W \lt 0$。
  4. 说气体"含有热"，或者说"冷"流进了温暖的物体，而不是描述能量通过加热从高温流向低温。
  5. 把绝热过程当作等温过程：$Q = 0$ 不代表 $\Delta T = 0$。
  6. 把比热容当作热导率，或者反过来。
  7. 声称孤立系统的熵会减小，或者声称封闭系统的熵永远不会减小。

Putting $27$ in for $T$ gives $8.1\times10^{23}$; using $1.5$ for $V$ as if litres were cubic meters gives $7.2\times10^{25}$; and $0.12$ is the number of moles, $n = PV/(RT)$, not the number of atoms.用 $N = PV/(k_B T)$，其中 $T = 300\,\mathrm{K}$，$V$ 用 m³。把 $T$ 代成 $27$ 得到 $8.1\times10^{23}$；把 $1.5$ 当作 $V$（好像升就是立方米）得到 $7.2\times10^{25}$；$0.12$ 是物质的量 $n = PV/(RT)$，不是原子数。
5. Helium gas and argon gas are in thermal equilibrium with each other. Which statement is true?氦气和氩气相互处于热平衡。下列哪个说法正确？
The atoms of both gases have the same rms speed.两种气体的原子有相同的方均根速率。
The helium atoms have the greater average kinetic energy because they are lighter.氦原子质量较小，所以平均动能较大。
The argon atoms have the greater average kinetic energy because they are heavier.氩原子质量较大，所以平均动能较大。
The atoms of both gases have the same average kinetic energy.两种气体的原子有相同的平均动能。
Correct! Thermal equilibrium means the same temperature, and $K_\text{avg} = \frac{3}{2}k_B T$ depends only on the temperature.正确！热平衡意味着温度相同，而 $K_\text{avg} = \frac{3}{2}k_B T$ 只取决于温度。
The average kinetic energy per atom depends only on the temperature, whatever the mass. Equal temperature does not mean equal speed: since $K_\text{avg}$ is the same, the lighter helium atoms must have the larger rms speed. Mass does not change which gas has more kinetic energy per atom.每个原子的平均动能只取决于温度，与质量无关。温度相同并不意味着速率相同：$K_\text{avg}$ 相同，所以质量较小的氦原子必须有较大的方均根速率。质量不会改变哪种气体每个原子的动能更多。
6. A small cup of $80^\circ\mathrm{C}$ water is placed in a large swimming pool at $25^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The pool holds far more internal energy than the cup. What happens, and why?把一小杯 $80^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 的水放进 $25^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 的大游泳池里。游泳池的内能远多于这杯水。会发生什么？为什么？
Energy flows from the pool to the cup, because the pool has more energy.能量从游泳池流向杯子，因为游泳池的能量更多。
Energy flows from the cup to the pool, because the cup is at the higher temperature.能量从杯子流向游泳池，因为杯子的温度更高。
No energy flows, because the pool has the larger amount of water.没有能量流动，因为游泳池里的水更多。
Energy flows both ways in equal amounts until the water in the cup is gone.能量双向等量流动，直到杯子里的水用完。
Correct! Energy is transferred spontaneously from the higher-temperature system to the lower-temperature system. The cup cools until it reaches the temperature of the pool.正确！能量自发地从高温系统传向低温系统。杯子逐渐冷却，直到与游泳池温度相同。
The direction of energy transfer is set by the temperatures, not by the total amount of internal energy. Atoms from the hotter cup, with higher average kinetic energy, most often pass energy to the cooler pool atoms. A pool with more water simply ends up a little warmer, and the cup ends at nearly the pool temperature.能量传递的方向由温度决定，而不是由内能的总量决定。较热的杯子里平均动能较高的原子，最常把能量传给较冷的池水原子。水更多的游泳池只会变得略微暖一点，杯子最终的温度接近游泳池的温度。
7. A gas at a constant pressure of $1.5\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ is compressed from $4.0\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ to $3.0\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$. What is the work done on the gas?压强恒为 $1.5\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{Pa}$ 的气体从 $4.0\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$ 被压缩到 $3.0\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$。外界对气体做的功是多少？
$+150\,\mathrm{J}$
$-150\,\mathrm{J}$
$+600\,\mathrm{J}$
$0\,\mathrm{J}$
Correct! $W = -P\Delta V = -(1.5\times10^{5})(3.0\times10^{-3} - 4.0\times10^{-3}) = +150\,\mathrm{J}$. Compression means work is done on the gas.正确！$W = -P\Delta V = -(1.5\times10^{5})(3.0\times10^{-3} - 4.0\times10^{-3}) = +150\,\mathrm{J}$。压缩意味着外界对气体做功。
Use the change in volume, $\Delta V = -1.0\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$, and the definition $W = -P\Delta V$ for work done on the gas. $-150\,\mathrm{J}$ drops the minus sign in the formula (it is the work done by the gas); $600\,\mathrm{J}$ multiplies the pressure by the whole initial volume instead of the change; and $0\,\mathrm{J}$ treats the pressure as constant, which does not mean no work.要用体积的变化 $\Delta V = -1.0\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}$，以及外界对气体做功的定义 $W = -P\Delta V$。$-150\,\mathrm{J}$ 漏掉了公式中的负号（那是气体对外做的功）；$600\,\mathrm{J}$ 用压强乘了整个初始体积而不是体积的变化；$0\,\mathrm{J}$ 把压强恒定当成了不做功。
8. An ideal gas in a well-insulated cylinder is compressed quickly by a piston. What happens to its temperature?隔热良好的汽缸中的理想气体被活塞快速压缩。它的温度如何变化？
It stays the same, because no energy is transferred by heating.保持不变，因为没有通过加热传递能量。
It falls, because the gas is squeezed into a smaller volume.降低，因为气体被压进了更小的体积。
It rises, because the work done on the gas increases its internal energy.升高，因为外界对气体做的功使它的内能增加。
It cannot be determined without the pressure.不知道压强就无法确定。
Correct! The process is adiabatic, $Q = 0$, and the work done on the gas is positive, so $\Delta U = W \gt 0$. For an ideal gas a higher $U$ means a higher $T$.正确！这个过程是绝热的，$Q = 0$，外界对气体做的功为正，所以 $\Delta U = W \gt 0$。对理想气体，$U$ 增大意味着 $T$ 升高。
$Q = 0$ does not mean the temperature is constant; that would be an isothermal process. With $Q = 0$ the first law gives $\Delta U = W$, and compression makes $W$ positive, so the internal energy and the temperature increase. The pressure is not needed to decide the direction of the change.$Q = 0$ 并不意味着温度恒定，那是等温过程。$Q = 0$ 时第一定律给出 $\Delta U = W$，压缩使 $W$ 为正，所以内能和温度都升高。判断变化方向不需要知道压强。
9. A gas absorbs $500\,\mathrm{J}$ by heating while it does $200\,\mathrm{J}$ of work on its surroundings. What is the change in its internal energy?气体通过加热吸收 $500\,\mathrm{J}$，同时对周围环境做了 $200\,\mathrm{J}$ 的功。它的内能变化是多少？
$+700\,\mathrm{J}$
$+300\,\mathrm{J}$
$-300\,\mathrm{J}$
$+500\,\mathrm{J}$
Correct! $Q = +500\,\mathrm{J}$ and the work done on the gas is $W = -200\,\mathrm{J}$, so $\Delta U = Q + W = +300\,\mathrm{J}$.正确！$Q = +500\,\mathrm{J}$，外界对气体做的功为 $W = -200\,\mathrm{J}$，所以 $\Delta U = Q + W = +300\,\mathrm{J}$。
Work done by the gas is negative in $\Delta U = Q + W$, where $W$ is the work done on the gas. $+700\,\mathrm{J}$ adds the work instead of subtracting it; $+500\,\mathrm{J}$ ignores the work the gas did; and $-300\,\mathrm{J}$ has the signs of both terms reversed.在 $\Delta U = Q + W$ 中，$W$ 是外界对气体做的功，气体对外做的功为负。$+700\,\mathrm{J}$ 把功加上而不是减去；$+500\,\mathrm{J}$ 忽略了气体做的功；$-300\,\mathrm{J}$ 把两项的符号都弄反了。
10. How much energy is needed to raise the temperature of $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ of water by $40\,\mathrm{K}$? ($c = 4.19\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$)使 $0.25\,\mathrm{kg}$ 的水温度升高 $40\,\mathrm{K}$ 需要多少能量？（$c = 4.19\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J/(kg\,K)}$）
$1.7\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{J}$
$4.2\times10^{7}\,\mathrm{J}$
$4.2\times10^{4}\,\mathrm{J}$
$1.05\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$
Correct! $Q = mc\Delta T = (0.25)(4.19\times10^{3})(40) = 4.2\times10^{4}\,\mathrm{J}$.正确！$Q = mc\Delta T = (0.25)(4.19\times10^{3})(40) = 4.2\times10^{4}\,\mathrm{J}$。
All three factors are needed: $Q = mc\Delta T$. $1.7\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{J}$ leaves out the mass; $4.2\times10^{7}\,\mathrm{J}$ uses the mass in grams, $250$, with $c$ per kilogram; and $1.05\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$ leaves out the temperature change.三个因子缺一不可：$Q = mc\Delta T$。$1.7\times10^{5}\,\mathrm{J}$ 漏掉了质量；$4.2\times10^{7}\,\mathrm{J}$ 用了以克为单位的质量 $250$，而 $c$ 是每千克的；$1.05\times10^{3}\,\mathrm{J}$ 漏掉了温度变化。
11. A slab conducts energy at a rate $P_0$. A slab of the same material has twice the area and half the thickness, with the same temperature difference. What is the new rate?一块平板的热传导速率为 $P_0$。同种材料的另一块平板，面积是前者的两倍，厚度是一半，温差相同。新的速率是多少？
$P_0$
$2P_0$
$P_0/4$
$4P_0$
Correct! The rate is proportional to $A/L$, and $(2)/(1/2) = 4$.正确！速率正比于 $A/L$，而 $(2)/(1/2) = 4$。
In $Q/\Delta t = kA\Delta T/L$ both changes increase the rate, so the factors multiply: $2 \times 2 = 4$. $2P_0$ counts only one of the two changes; $P_0$ assumes the two cancel, as if the rate depended on $A$ and $L$ in the same way; and $P_0/4$ puts $A$ and $L$ in the wrong places.在 $Q/\Delta t = kA\Delta T/L$ 中，两个变化都使速率增大，所以因子相乘：$2 \times 2 = 4$。$2P_0$ 只算了两个变化中的一个；$P_0$ 认为两者抵消，好像速率对 $A$ 和 $L$ 的依赖方式相同；$P_0/4$ 则把 $A$ 和 $L$ 放错了位置。
12. Which statement about entropy is correct?关于熵，下列哪个说法正确？
The entropy of every system must increase in every process.每个系统在每个过程中熵都必须增大。
The entropy of a closed system can decrease when it loses energy to its surroundings.封闭系统向周围环境放出能量时，熵可以减小。
An isolated system can lower its entropy, provided energy is conserved.孤立系统只要能量守恒，就可以降低它的熵。
The entropy of a gas depends on the path it took to reach its present state.气体的熵取决于它到达当前状态所经历的路径。
Correct! Energy can be transferred out of a closed system, so its entropy can fall, as long as the surroundings gain entropy and the total for the isolated combination does not decrease.正确！能量可以从封闭系统传出，所以它的熵可以降低，只要周围环境的熵增加，而这个孤立整体的总熵没有减小。
The second law applies to isolated systems, whose entropy never decreases, and conserving energy is not enough to allow a decrease. A closed system exchanges energy with its surroundings, so its own entropy can go down. Entropy is a state function, so it does not depend on the path.第二定律适用于孤立系统，孤立系统的熵永不减小，仅有能量守恒并不足以允许熵减小。封闭系统与周围环境交换能量，所以它自身的熵可以下降。熵是状态函数，所以与路径无关。
13. A gas inside an insulated rigid container is released from one half into a vacuum in the other half. Which description of the process is correct?绝热刚性容器中的气体被释放，从一半扩散到另一半的真空中。下列哪种描述正确？
It is irreversible and the entropy of the gas increases, while $Q = 0$ and $W = 0$.过程不可逆，气体的熵增大，而 $Q = 0$，$W = 0$。
It is reversible, because the gas can be pushed back to the first half.过程可逆，因为可以把气体推回第一半。
The entropy of the gas decreases, because it has more room to move.气体的熵减小，因为它有了更多的活动空间。
The entropy is unchanged, because the internal energy of the ideal gas is unchanged.熵不变，因为理想气体的内能不变。
Correct! The system is isolated, so $\Delta U = 0$ and the temperature of an ideal gas is unchanged, but the energy and the atoms spread over a larger volume, which is the more probable state. The entropy increases and the process does not run backward by itself.正确！系统是孤立的，所以 $\Delta U = 0$，理想气体的温度不变，但能量和原子分布到了更大的体积，这是更可能的状态。熵增大，过程不会自己倒着进行。
A gas that is pushed back needs work from outside, so the original process does not reverse itself and is irreversible. Spreading into more volume is the energy and atoms becoming more spread out, which raises the entropy, not lowers it. A constant internal energy does not mean a constant entropy: entropy is not a function of $U$ alone.要把气体推回去需要外界做功，所以原来的过程不会自己倒着进行，是不可逆的。扩散到更大的体积，是能量和原子变得更分散，这使熵增大而不是减小。内能不变并不意味着熵不变：熵并不只是 $U$ 的函数。
