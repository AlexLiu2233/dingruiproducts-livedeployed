Topic 8.1主题 8.1

Introducing Statistics: Why Be Normal?统计入门：为什么要用正态？

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe the sampling distribution of a sample proportion $\hat{p}$: its centre, spread and shape.
  • Calculate the mean and standard deviation of the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ from $p$ and $n$.
  • Check the conditions (random sample, 10% condition, at least 10 expected successes and 10 expected failures) that make the sampling distribution approximately normal.
  • Use a normal model to find the probability that a sample proportion falls above, below or between given values.
  • Explain why this sampling distribution is what makes confidence intervals and significance tests possible.
  • 描述样本比例 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布：中心、离散程度和形状。
  • 计算 $\hat{p}$ 抽样分布的均值与标准差（由 $p$ 和 $n$ 得出）。
  • 检验使抽样分布近似正态的条件（随机样本、10% 条件、至少 10 个预期成功与 10 个预期失败）。
  • 用正态模型求样本比例高于、低于或介于给定值之间的概率。
  • 解释为什么这个抽样分布使置信区间与显著性检验成为可能。

The sampling distribution of a sample proportion样本比例的抽样分布

A parameter such as the population proportion $p$ is one fixed number. A statistic such as the sample proportion $\hat{p}$ (read p-hat) changes from sample to sample, because each random sample contains different individuals. The sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ is the distribution of the values of $\hat{p}$ over all possible random samples of the same size $n$ from the same population. It is a model for how far a single sample result can be from the truth by chance alone.

参数（如总体比例 $p$）是一个固定的数。统计量（如样本比例 $\hat{p}$，读作 p-hat）则随样本而变，因为每个随机样本包含的个体不同。$\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布是从同一总体抽取的、容量同为 $n$ 的所有可能随机样本的 $\hat{p}$ 值的分布。它描述的是仅凭机会，单个样本结果可能离真值多远。

When the sample is random and not too large compared with the population, the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ has centre $p$ (the statistic is an unbiased estimator), and a spread that shrinks as $n$ grows:

当样本是随机的，且相对于总体不太大时，$\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布以 $p$ 为中心（该统计量是无偏估计量），其离散程度随 $n$ 增大而缩小：

$$\mu_{\hat{p}}=p,\qquad \sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\frac{p(1-p)}{n}}$$

The formula for $\sigma_{\hat{p}}$ is trustworthy when the 10% condition holds: the sample is no more than 10% of the population, so the individuals are close enough to independent. The shape is approximately normal when the sample is large enough to expect at least 10 successes and at least 10 failures, that is, $np\ge 10$ and $n(1-p)\ge 10$. If $p$ is near 0 or 1 and $n$ is small, the distribution is skewed and a normal model is not appropriate.

$\sigma_{\hat{p}}$ 的公式在10% 条件成立时才可靠：样本不超过总体的 10%，个体才近似独立。当样本足够大，使预期成功数与失败数都至少为 10，即 $np\ge 10$ 且 $n(1-p)\ge 10$ 时，其形状近似正态。若 $p$ 接近 0 或 1 而 $n$ 又小，分布会偏斜，不宜使用正态模型。

Why this makes inference work为什么这使推断成为可能

Because the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ is approximately normal, about 95% of sample proportions land within about two standard deviations of $p$. Read in reverse, a single observed $\hat{p}$ is probably within about two standard deviations of the unknown $p$, which is the idea behind a confidence interval. Read forward from an assumed value of $p$, an observed $\hat{p}$ far out in the tail would be surprising, which is the idea behind a significance test. The rest of this unit is these two ideas made precise.

因为 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布近似正态，约 95% 的样本比例落在 $p$ 的约两个标准差之内。反过来读，一个观察到的 $\hat{p}$ 大概率落在未知 $p$ 的约两个标准差之内，这就是置信区间的思想。从假定的 $p$ 值正向读，观察到的 $\hat{p}$ 若远在尾部就令人意外，这就是显著性检验的思想。本单元其余内容就是把这两个思想精确化。

Worked Example: probability of a sample proportion例题：样本比例的概率

In a city of 60,000 registered voters, 40% support a transit measure. A random sample of $n=100$ voters is taken. Find the probability that the sample proportion is at least $0.50$. (Illustrative scenario.)

  1. Conditions. Random sample; $100$ is far less than 10% of $60{,}000$; $np=100(0.40)=40\ge 10$ and $n(1-p)=100(0.60)=60\ge 10$. The sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ is approximately normal.
  2. Centre and spread. $\mu_{\hat{p}}=0.40$ and $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{100}}\approx 0.0490$.
  3. Standardize. $z=\dfrac{0.50-0.40}{0.0490}\approx 2.04$.
  4. Probability. $P(\hat{p}\ge 0.50)=P(Z\ge 2.04)\approx 0.021$ (normalcdf(2.04,1E99) or normalcdf(0.5,1E99,0.4,0.049)).
  5. In context. If 40% of all voters support the measure, about 2.1% of random samples of 100 voters would show 50% or more in support. Such a sample would be unusual but not impossible.

Why this works: the normal model for $\hat{p}$ turns "how unusual is this sample?" into an area under a curve.

某市有 60,000 名登记选民，其中 40% 支持一项公交提案。随机抽取 $n=100$ 名选民。求样本比例至少为 $0.50$ 的概率。（示意情境。）

  1. 条件。随机样本；$100$ 远小于 $60{,}000$ 的 10%；$np=100(0.40)=40\ge 10$，$n(1-p)=100(0.60)=60\ge 10$。$\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布近似正态。
  2. 中心与离散。$\mu_{\hat{p}}=0.40$，$\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{100}}\approx 0.0490$。
  3. 标准化。$z=\dfrac{0.50-0.40}{0.0490}\approx 2.04$。
  4. 概率。$P(\hat{p}\ge 0.50)=P(Z\ge 2.04)\approx 0.021$（normalcdf(2.04,1E99) 或 normalcdf(0.5,1E99,0.4,0.049)）。
  5. 结合情境。若全体选民中 40% 支持该提案，则约 2.1% 的 100 人随机样本会显示 50% 或更多人支持。这样的样本不常见，但并非不可能。

原理：$\hat{p}$ 的正态模型把“这个样本有多罕见”变成曲线下的一块面积。

Worked Example: sample size changes the spread, not the centre例题：样本容量改变离散程度而非中心

Same population as before ($p=0.40$). Compare the chance that $\hat{p}\ge 0.45$ for $n=100$ and $n=400$.

  1. $n=100$. $\sigma_{\hat{p}}\approx 0.0490$, so $z=\dfrac{0.45-0.40}{0.0490}\approx 1.02$ and $P(\hat{p}\ge 0.45)\approx 0.154$.
  2. $n=400$. $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{400}}\approx 0.0245$, so $z=\dfrac{0.45-0.40}{0.0245}\approx 2.04$ and $P(\hat{p}\ge 0.45)\approx 0.021$.
  3. Compare. Both distributions are centred at $0.40$. Quadrupling $n$ halves the standard deviation, so a sample proportion of $0.45$ goes from fairly ordinary (about 15%) to unusual (about 2%).

总体同前（$p=0.40$）。比较 $n=100$ 与 $n=400$ 时 $\hat{p}\ge 0.45$ 的概率。

  1. $n=100$。$\sigma_{\hat{p}}\approx 0.0490$，故 $z=\dfrac{0.45-0.40}{0.0490}\approx 1.02$，$P(\hat{p}\ge 0.45)\approx 0.154$。
  2. $n=400$。$\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{400}}\approx 0.0245$，故 $z=\dfrac{0.45-0.40}{0.0245}\approx 2.04$，$P(\hat{p}\ge 0.45)\approx 0.021$。
  3. 比较。两个分布都以 $0.40$ 为中心。$n$ 变为 4 倍，标准差减半，于是样本比例 $0.45$ 从较为平常（约 15%）变为不寻常（约 2%）。
Worked Example: when the normal model is not safe例题：正态模型何时不可靠

A manufacturer finds that 5% of its tablets are chipped. Is a normal model reasonable for $\hat{p}$ in random samples of $n=100$? Of $n=400$? (Illustrative figures.)

  1. $n=100$. $np=100(0.05)=5<10$. The condition fails, so the sampling distribution is skewed right and a normal model is not appropriate.
  2. $n=400$. $np=400(0.05)=20\ge 10$ and $n(1-p)=380\ge 10$. A normal model is reasonable, with $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.05(0.95)}{400}}\approx 0.0109$.
  3. Observe. For $n=100$ the standard deviation would be about $0.0218$, nearly half of $p$ itself, so $\hat{p}$ cannot drop far below $p$ but can stretch far above it. That lopsidedness is what a normal curve cannot capture.

某厂发现其 5% 的药片有缺角。对 $n=100$ 的随机样本，$\hat{p}$ 可以用正态模型吗？$n=400$ 呢？（示意数据。）

  1. $n=100$。$np=100(0.05)=5<10$。条件不满足，抽样分布右偏，不宜用正态模型。
  2. $n=400$。$np=400(0.05)=20\ge 10$ 且 $n(1-p)=380\ge 10$。可以用正态模型，$\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.05(0.95)}{400}}\approx 0.0109$。
  3. 观察。$n=100$ 时标准差约为 $0.0218$，接近 $p$ 本身的一半，所以 $\hat{p}$ 不可能比 $p$ 低很多，却可能比它高很多。这种不对称正是正态曲线无法刻画的。
Common Mistake: mixing up three distributions

The distribution of the population, the distribution of the one sample you collected, and the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ are three different things. The sampling distribution describes the statistic over many imagined samples. Also, a larger $n$ narrows the sampling distribution but never moves its centre away from $p$.

常见错误：混淆三种分布

总体的分布、你收集的那一个样本的分布，以及 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布是三件不同的事。抽样分布描述的是统计量在许多设想样本中的取值。另外，$n$ 增大会使抽样分布变窄，但绝不会使其中心偏离 $p$。

Exam tip考试提示

Write the check before using the normal model: "random sample, $100$ is less than 10% of all voters, $np=40\ge 10$ and $n(1-p)=60\ge 10$." State $\mu_{\hat{p}}$ and $\sigma_{\hat{p}}$ with the numbers substituted, and answer in context rather than leaving a bare probability.

使用正态模型之前先写出检验：“随机样本，$100$ 少于全体选民的 10%，$np=40\ge 10$ 且 $n(1-p)=60\ge 10$。”写出代入数值的 $\mu_{\hat{p}}$ 和 $\sigma_{\hat{p}}$，并结合情境作答，不要只留一个孤立的概率。

Topic 8.2主题 8.2

Constructing a Confidence Interval for a Population Proportion构造总体比例的置信区间

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate and state a one-sample $z$-interval for a population proportion, using the critical value $z^*$ and the standard error.
  • Check the conditions: random sample, 10% condition, and at least 10 successes and 10 failures in the sample.
  • Interpret a confidence interval and a confidence level in context, and say which one describes the method.
  • Find the margin of error and the sample size needed for a stated margin of error, including the conservative choice $0.5$.
  • Run the procedure on the TI-84 as 1-PropZInt and write it as State, Plan, Do, Conclude.
  • 利用临界值 $z^*$ 和标准误计算并写出总体比例的单样本 $z$ 区间。
  • 检验条件：随机样本、10% 条件，以及样本中至少 10 个成功与 10 个失败。
  • 结合情境解释置信区间与置信水平，并说明哪一个描述的是方法。
  • 求误差范围，以及达到指定误差范围所需的样本容量，包括保守取值 $0.5$。
  • 在 TI-84 上用 1-PropZInt 完成计算，并按 State、Plan、Do、Conclude 四步写出。

The one-proportion z-interval单比例 z 区间

A confidence interval gives a range of plausible values for the parameter $p$. The one-sample $z$-interval for a proportion (also called the one-proportion $z$-interval, 1-PropZInt on the TI-84) has the form statistic $\pm$ critical value $\times$ standard error:

置信区间给出参数 $p$ 的一组合理取值。总体比例的单样本 $z$ 区间（也叫单比例 $z$ 区间，TI-84 上的 1-PropZInt）的形式是统计量 $\pm$ 临界值 $\times$ 标准误：

$$\hat{p}\;\pm\; z^*\sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{n}}$$

The standard error $\sqrt{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})/n}$ estimates the standard deviation of $\hat{p}$; it uses $\hat{p}$ because the true $p$ is unknown. The critical value $z^*$ is the number of standard errors needed to capture the middle $C\%$ of a standard normal curve: $z^*\approx 1.645$ for 90%, $1.960$ for 95%, $2.576$ for 99% (invNorm). The product $z^*\times$ standard error is the margin of error.

标准误 $\sqrt{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})/n}$ 估计 $\hat{p}$ 的标准差；因为真实的 $p$ 未知，所以用 $\hat{p}$ 代入。临界值 $z^*$ 是在标准正态曲线上截取中间 $C\%$ 所需的标准误个数：90% 对应 $z^*\approx 1.645$，95% 对应 $1.960$，99% 对应 $2.576$（invNorm）。$z^*\times$ 标准误就是误差范围。

Conditions条件

Before using the interval, verify and state three conditions: (1) Random: the data come from a random sample or a randomized experiment. (2) 10% condition: when sampling without replacement, $n$ is at most 10% of the population. (3) At least 10 successes and 10 failures in the sample: $n\hat{p}\ge 10$ and $n(1-\hat{p})\ge 10$. For an interval the counts come from the sample, because $p$ is unknown.

使用区间之前，必须验证并写出三个条件：(1) 随机：数据来自随机样本或随机化实验。(2) 10% 条件：不放回抽样时，$n$ 至多为总体的 10%。(3) 样本中至少 10 个成功与 10 个失败：$n\hat{p}\ge 10$ 且 $n(1-\hat{p})\ge 10$。对区间而言，计数来自样本，因为 $p$ 未知。

Interpreting the interval and the confidence level解释区间与置信水平

Interpret the interval (in context, naming the population and the variable): "We are 95% confident that the interval from 0.323 to 0.417 captures the true proportion of all students at the school who walk to school." Interpret the confidence level: if we took many random samples of the same size and built an interval from each, about 95% of those intervals would capture the true proportion. The level describes the method's long-run success rate. It is not the probability that this one interval is correct, because $p$ is fixed and the interval either captures it or not.

解释区间（结合情境，点明总体和变量）：“我们有 95% 的把握认为，从 0.323 到 0.417 的区间覆盖了该校全体学生中步行上学的真实比例。”解释置信水平：若取许多相同容量的随机样本并分别构造区间，约 95% 的区间会覆盖真实比例。水平描述的是方法的长期成功率，不是这一个区间正确的概率，因为 $p$ 是固定的，区间要么覆盖它，要么不覆盖。

Worked Example: a 95% interval for a proportion例题：比例的 95% 置信区间

A school has 8,000 students. In a random sample of 400 students, 148 say they walk to school. Construct and interpret a 95% confidence interval for the proportion of all students at the school who walk. (Illustrative data.)

  1. State. Parameter: $p$, the proportion of all students at this school who walk to school. Confidence level 95%.
  2. Plan. One-sample $z$-interval for a proportion. Random: stated random sample. 10%: $400\le 0.10(8000)=800$ holds. Counts: $n\hat{p}=148\ge 10$ and $n(1-\hat{p})=252\ge 10$.
  3. Do. $\hat{p}=148/400=0.37$. Standard error $=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.37(1-0.37)}{400}}\approx 0.0241$. With $z^*=1.960$: margin of error $\approx 0.0473$. Interval: $0.37\pm 0.0473$, that is $(0.3227,\,0.4173)$. Calculator: 1-PropZInt with $x=148$, $n=400$, C-Level $0.95$.
  4. Conclude. We are 95% confident that the interval from $0.323$ to $0.417$ captures the true proportion of all students at this school who walk to school.

某校有 8,000 名学生。随机抽取 400 名学生，其中 148 人说自己步行上学。构造并解释该校全体学生中步行上学比例的 95% 置信区间。（示意数据。）

  1. State。参数：$p$，该校全体学生中步行上学的比例。置信水平 95%。
  2. Plan。比例的单样本 $z$ 区间。随机：已说明为随机样本。10%：$400\le 0.10(8000)=800$ 成立。计数：$n\hat{p}=148\ge 10$，$n(1-\hat{p})=252\ge 10$。
  3. Do。$\hat{p}=148/400=0.37$。标准误 $=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.37(1-0.37)}{400}}\approx 0.0241$。取 $z^*=1.960$：误差范围 $\approx 0.0473$。区间：$0.37\pm 0.0473$，即 $(0.3227,\,0.4173)$。计算器：1-PropZInt，$x=148$，$n=400$，C-Level $0.95$。
  4. Conclude。我们有 95% 的把握认为，从 $0.323$ 到 $0.417$ 的区间覆盖了该校全体学生中步行上学的真实比例。
Worked Example: a 90% interval and a condition check例题：90% 置信区间与条件检验

A warehouse manager randomly samples 250 packages from a very large shipment and finds 18 damaged. Check the conditions and give a 90% confidence interval for the proportion of damaged packages in the shipment. (Illustrative data.)

  1. Conditions. Random: stated. 10%: the shipment is very large, so $250$ is under 10% of it. Counts: $n\hat{p}=18\ge 10$ and $n(1-\hat{p})=232\ge 10$. All three hold.

Common Mistake: reading the confidence level as a probability for this interval

Do not write "there is a 95% probability that $p$ is in this interval" or "95% of the data lie in the interval." The parameter is fixed. The 95% is the success rate of the method over repeated samples; the sentence "we are 95% confident that the interval captures the true proportion" is the accepted way to say it for one interval. Also, a wider interval is not "more accurate"; it is more likely to capture $p$ but less informative.

常见错误：把置信水平当作这个区间的概率

Topic 8.3主题 8.3

Justifying a Claim Based on a Confidence Interval for a Population Proportion依据总体比例的置信区间论证说法

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Use a confidence interval to decide whether a claimed value of $p$ is plausible.
  • Justify a claim in context by stating whether the interval contains or does not contain the claimed value.
  • Explain why an interval that contains a value does not prove that value is the true proportion.
  • Use the position of the whole interval (all above or all below a value) to support a directional claim.
  • Relate the interval's conclusion to a two-sided significance test at the matching level.
  • 用置信区间判断声称的 $p$ 值是否合理。
  • 结合情境，通过说明区间含不含声称值来论证一个说法。
  • 解释为什么区间含有某值并不能证明该值就是真实比例。
  • 利用整个区间全在某值之上或之下的位置，支持有方向的说法。
  • 把区间的结论与相应水平的双侧显著性检验联系起来。

A confidence interval is the set of values of $p$ that are plausible (consistent with the data) at the stated confidence level. To justify a claim about a population proportion, compare the claimed value with the interval. If the interval contains the claimed value, the claim is plausible: the data are consistent with it, but they do not prove it, because many other values in the interval are plausible too. If the interval does not contain the claimed value, that value is not plausible, which is convincing evidence against the claim.

置信区间是在给定置信水平下合理（与数据相符）的 $p$ 值的集合。要论证一个关于总体比例的说法，把声称值与区间比较。若区间含有声称值，则该说法是合理的：数据与之相符，但并不能证明它，因为区间内其他许多值同样合理。若区间不含声称值，则该值不合理，这就是反对该说法的有力证据。

When the entire interval lies above (or below) the claimed value, you can also say which direction: every plausible value of $p$ is greater (or less) than the claim. A 95% interval and a two-sided test at significance level $\alpha=0.05$ usually reach the same decision, but the two use slightly different standard errors (the interval uses $\hat{p}$, the test uses the claimed $p_0$), so they can disagree when the claimed value sits very near an endpoint.

当整个区间都在声称值之上（或之下）时，还可以说明方向：$p$ 的每个合理取值都大于（或小于）该说法。95% 区间与显著性水平 $\alpha=0.05$ 的双侧检验通常得到相同决定，但二者的标准误略有不同（区间用 $\hat{p}$，检验用声称值 $p_0$），所以当声称值恰好靠近端点时，可能出现不一致。

Worked Example: an interval that rules out a claim例题：否定某个说法的区间

A shipping company claims that 90% of its orders arrive on time. A random sample of 300 orders (far fewer than 10% of all orders) has 250 on time. Use a 95% confidence interval to evaluate the claim. (Illustrative data.)

  1. Conditions. Random sample; 10% holds; $n\hat{p}=250\ge 10$ and $n(1-\hat{p})=50\ge 10$.

  3. Justify. The interval does not contain $0.90$, so $0.90$ is not a plausible value for the true proportion. Every value in the interval is below $0.90$.
  4. Conclude. We have convincing evidence that the true proportion of this company's orders that arrive on time is less than $0.90$; the claim is not supported. The interval estimates the true on-time rate to be between about $79\%$ and $88\%$.

Worked Example: several claims, one interval, and a change of level例题：多个说法、同一个区间与置信水平的改变

A city surveys a random sample of 400 adult residents (far fewer than 10% of the city's adults). Of these, 232 say they support a new bike lane. A council member says "a majority of adults in the city support the lane." A second member says "70% of adults in the city support it." Use a 95% confidence interval to evaluate both statements, then say whether a 99% interval would change the first conclusion. (Illustrative data.)

  1. Parameter and conditions. Let $p$ be the true proportion of adult residents of the city who support the lane. Random sample; 400 is under 10% of the adults; $232\ge 10$ and $168\ge 10$.
  2. Interval. $\hat{p}=232/400=0.58$; $SE=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.58(0.42)}{400}}\approx 0.0247$; $ME=1.960(0.0247)\approx 0.0484$. The 95% interval is $(0.532,\,0.628)$.
  3. Majority claim ($p>0.50$). Every value in the interval is above $0.50$, so $0.50$ is not plausible and neither is any smaller value.
  4. The 70% claim. The interval does not contain $0.70$, and every value in it is below $0.70$.
  5. A 99% interval. $z^*=2.576$, so $ME\approx 2.576(0.0247)\approx 0.0636$ and the interval is $(0.516,\,0.644)$. It is wider, but it still lies entirely above $0.50$, so the first conclusion does not change.

Common Mistake: treating "contains" as "proves"

An interval containing $0.50$ does not show that $p=0.50$; it only shows that $0.50$ cannot be ruled out. Equally, the interval is a range of plausible values, not a probability distribution: you cannot say that values near the middle are a stated percentage more likely.

常见错误：把“含有”当作“证明”

区间含有 $0.50$ 并不说明 $p=0.50$，只说明不能排除 $0.50$。同样，区间是一组合理取值，而不是概率分布：不能说靠近中部的值有某个百分比的“更大可能”。

Topic 8.4主题 8.4

Why Use t? Introducing the t Distribution为什么用 t？t 分布入门

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Calculate the standard error $s/\sqrt{n}$ of a sample mean and distinguish it from the standard deviation $\sigma$.
  • State the degrees of freedom for a one-sample $t$ procedure and describe how the shape of a $t$ distribution changes as $df$ grows.
  • Find $t^*$ critical values and $t$ probabilities with a table or a calculator (invT, tcdf).
  • Decide whether a graph and a sample size make a $t$ procedure reasonable.
  • 解释当总体标准差未知时，为什么对均值的推断使用 $t$ 分布而不是正态分布。
  • 计算样本均值的标准误 $s/\sqrt{n}$，并区分它与标准差 $\sigma$。
  • 写出单样本 $t$ 程序的自由度，并描述 $df$ 增大时 $t$ 分布形状的变化。
  • 用表或计算器（invT、tcdf）求 $t^*$ 临界值与 $t$ 分布的概率。
  • 根据图形与样本量判断 $t$ 程序是否合理。

Topics 8.1 to 8.3 built inference for a proportion on a simple fact: when $np$ and $n(1-p)$ are large, $\hat{p}$ varies in a nearly normal way with a standard deviation you can write down. For a population mean $\mu$ the sample statistic is $\bar{x}$, and its sampling distribution has mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\sigma/\sqrt{n}$. The difficulty is the population standard deviation $\sigma$: in practice it is almost never known. If you knew $\sigma$ you would usually already know $\mu$.

主题 8.1 至 8.3 为比例的推断建立在一个简单事实上：当 $np$ 与 $n(1-p)$ 都较大时，$\hat{p}$ 近似服从正态分布，其标准差可以直接写出。对于总体均值 $\mu$，样本统计量是 $\bar{x}$，其抽样分布的均值为 $\mu$，标准差为 $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\sigma/\sqrt{n}$。困难在于总体标准差 $\sigma$：实际中几乎从不已知。如果你知道 $\sigma$，通常也就已经知道 $\mu$ 了。

The fix is to replace $\sigma$ with the sample standard deviation $s$. The result, $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$, is the standard error of $\bar{x}$: an estimate, from the sample, of how far $\bar{x}$ typically falls from $\mu$. But $s$ itself changes from sample to sample, and that extra variation means the standardized statistic $\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}$ is not exactly normal. It follows a t-distribution (also called the t distribution), which was derived for exactly this situation.

解决办法是用样本标准差 $s$ 代替 $\sigma$。得到的 $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$ 叫做 $\bar{x}$ 的标准误（standard error）：用样本估计 $\bar{x}$ 通常偏离 $\mu$ 多远。但 $s$ 本身也随样本而变，这一额外的变异使标准化统计量 $\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}$ 不再严格服从正态分布，而是服从 t 分布（t-distribution），它正是为这种情形推导出来的。

Must know

Standard deviation of $\bar{x}$: $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$, requires the unknown population standard deviation. Standard error of $\bar{x}$: $s_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$, computed from the sample. When the conditions are met, $t=\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}$ follows a $t$ distribution with $df=n-1$.

必备知识

$\bar{x}$ 的标准差：$\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$，需要未知的总体标准差。$\bar{x}$ 的标准误：$s_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$，由样本计算。条件满足时，$t=\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}$ 服从自由度 $df=n-1$ 的 $t$ 分布。

Degrees of freedom and the shape of the t distribution自由度与 t 分布的形状

A $t$ distribution is a family of curves indexed by its degrees of freedom ($df$). For a one-sample procedure $df=n-1$: the sample standard deviation is computed from $n$ deviations about $\bar{x}$, but those deviations must sum to zero, so only $n-1$ of them are free to vary. Every $t$ curve is bell-shaped, symmetric, and centered at 0, like the standard normal curve. Compared with the normal curve it is wider and flatter in the center and has heavier tails (thicker tails), because an estimated $s$ sometimes comes out too small and produces an extreme $t$ value. The smaller the $df$, the heavier the tails (Figure 7.1).

$t$ 分布是由自由度（degrees of freedom，$df$）标记的一族曲线。单样本程序中 $df=n-1$：样本标准差由 $n$ 个相对 $\bar{x}$ 的离差算出，但这些离差之和必为零，所以只有 $n-1$ 个可以自由变化。每条 $t$ 曲线都是钟形、对称、中心在 0，与标准正态曲线类似。与正态曲线相比，它中心更矮更宽、尾部更重（heavier tails），因为估计出的 $s$ 有时偏小，会产生极端的 $t$ 值。$df$ 越小，尾部越重（图 7.1）。

As $df$ increases, the $t$ distribution approaches the normal distribution: with $df$ near 30 the two are hard to tell apart, and with $df=\infty$ they coincide. That is why a $t$ critical value is always larger than the matching $z^*$, and why the gap shrinks as the sample grows. For a 95% interval, $z^*=1.960$ while $t^*=2.776$ for $df=4$, $2.262$ for $df=9$, $2.064$ for $df=24$, and $1.984$ for $df=99$. The larger $t^*$ is the price of not knowing $\sigma$: wider intervals and less extreme evidence for the same $\bar{x}$.

随着 $df$ 增大，$t$ 分布趋近正态分布（approaches the normal distribution）：$df$ 接近 30 时两者已难以区分，$df=\infty$ 时完全重合。因此 $t$ 临界值总比对应的 $z^*$ 大，且样本越大差距越小。对于 95% 的区间，$z^*=1.960$，而 $df=4$ 时 $t^*=2.776$，$df=9$ 时 $2.262$，$df=24$ 时 $2.064$，$df=99$ 时 $1.984$。较大的 $t^*$ 是不知道 $\sigma$ 所付出的代价：区间更宽，同样的 $\bar{x}$ 所给出的证据也更不极端。

When may we use t? Graph and sample size何时可以使用 t？图形与样本量

The $t$ model assumes the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}$ is approximately normal. Two situations guarantee this well enough. If the population is approximately normal, $\bar{x}$ is approximately normal for any $n$. If $n\ge 30$, the Central Limit Theorem makes $\bar{x}$ approximately normal even for a skewed population. If $n<30$ and the shape of the population is unknown, look at a graph of the sample (dotplot, boxplot or histogram): proceed if it shows no strong skewness and no outliers, and stop if it does. With very small samples a graph cannot prove normality, so state what you saw and why it makes the procedure reasonable.

$t$ 模型假定 $\bar{x}$ 的抽样分布近似正态。有两种情形足以保证这一点：若总体近似正态，则任何 $n$ 下 $\bar{x}$ 都近似正态；若 $n\ge 30$，中心极限定理使 $\bar{x}$ 即使在总体偏斜时也近似正态。若 $n<30$ 且总体形状未知，要看样本的图形（点图、箱线图或直方图）：没有明显偏斜且没有离群点就可以继续，否则就不要继续。样本很小时，图形无法证明正态性，所以要写出你看到了什么以及为何这使程序合理。

单样本 t 区间

Interpreting the interval and the confidence level解释区间与置信水平

Interpret the interval: "We are 95% confident that the interval from 495.72 g to 501.08 g captures the true mean weight of all bags of rice produced that day." Interpret the level: if we took many random samples of the same size and built a 95% interval from each, about 95% of those intervals would capture the true mean. Do not say that there is a 95% probability that $\mu$ is in this particular interval (once computed it either captures $\mu$ or it does not), and do not say that 95% of the individual bags lie in the interval. The interval estimates a mean, not individual values. Always give the answer in context: name the population, the variable and the units.

解释区间：“我们有 95% 的把握认为，区间 495.72 克至 501.08 克包含当天生产的所有米袋的真实平均重量。”解释水平：如果用同样大小的样本重复抽样很多次，并各自构造 95% 的区间，大约 95% 的区间会包含真实均值。不要说“$\mu$ 落在这个具体区间内的概率是 95%”（区间一旦算出，要么包含 $\mu$，要么不包含），也不要说 95% 的单个米袋落在该区间内。区间估计的是均值，不是单个数值。务必结合情境（in context）作答：写出总体、变量与单位。

Margin of error and sample size误差范围与样本量

The margin of error $t^*\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$ shrinks when $n$ increases, when $s$ is smaller, or when the confidence level is lowered (a smaller $t^*$). To halve the margin of error you need about four times the sample size, because $n$ sits under a square root. To plan a study, set the target margin of error $ME$ and use a prior estimate of the spread (a pilot sample or earlier study) in place of $s$. Because $t^*$ depends on $n$, planning uses $z^*$ as an approximation: $n\ge\left(\dfrac{z^*\sigma}{ME}\right)^2$, rounded up.

误差范围 $t^*\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$ 在 $n$ 增大、$s$ 变小或降低置信水平（$t^*$ 变小）时缩小。要使误差范围减半，样本量大约要变成四倍，因为 $n$ 在根号下。规划研究时，先设定目标误差范围 $ME$，并用先前估计的离散程度（预试验样本或早期研究）代替 $s$。由于 $t^*$ 依赖于 $n$，规划时用 $z^*$ 作近似：$n\ge\left(\dfrac{z^*\sigma}{ME}\right)^2$，向上取整。

Topic 8.6主题 8.6

Justifying a Claim About a Population Mean Based on a Confidence Interval利用置信区间论证关于总体均值的说法

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Use a confidence interval to decide whether a claimed value of a mean is plausible.
  • Explain why an interval that contains a value does not prove the value is the mean.
  • Justify a claim that a mean is above or below a stated value using an entire interval.
  • Connect the confidence level of an interval to the significance level of a two-sided test.
  • 用置信区间判断所声称的均值是否合理。
  • 解释为什么区间包含某个值并不能证明该值就是均值。
  • 用整个区间论证均值高于或低于某个给定值的说法。
  • 把区间的置信水平与双侧检验的显著性水平联系起来。

A confidence interval is a set of plausible values for the parameter: values that are consistent with the data. This makes an interval a tool to justify a claim about a population mean. If the claimed value is inside the interval, the interval contains it: the claim is plausible, and the data do not provide convincing evidence against it. If the claimed value lies outside the interval (the interval does not contain it), the value is not plausible at that confidence level, and because the whole interval is on one side of the claim, you can say in which direction the mean seems to differ.

置信区间是参数的一组合理取值（plausible）：与数据相容的取值。这使区间成为论证关于总体均值的说法（justify a claim）的工具。若所声称的值在区间内，即区间包含（contains）它：该说法是合理的（consistent with 数据），数据没有提供令人信服的证据来反对它。若所声称的值在区间之外（区间不包含它），则在该置信水平下此值不合理；又因整个区间位于说法的一侧，可以指出均值看来朝哪个方向偏离。

Interval and claim

Claim $\mu=\mu_0$. If the interval contains $\mu_0$: $\mu_0$ is plausible; no convincing evidence that $\mu\ne\mu_0$. If the interval does not contain $\mu_0$: convincing evidence that $\mu\ne\mu_0$, and the interval shows the direction. A $C\%$ interval agrees with a two-sided test at significance level $\alpha=1-C/100$ (for example 95% and $\alpha=0.05$).

区间与说法

说法为 $\mu=\mu_0$。若区间包含 $\mu_0$：$\mu_0$ 合理，没有令人信服的证据表明 $\mu\ne\mu_0$。若区间不包含 $\mu_0$：有令人信服的证据表明 $\mu\ne\mu_0$，且区间显示方向。$C\%$ 的区间与显著性水平 $\alpha=1-C/100$ 的双侧检验结论一致（例如 95% 对应 $\alpha=0.05$）。

Notice what an interval cannot do. Containing 500 does not show that 500 is the mean: every value in the interval is also plausible, and a different sample would give a different interval. The only safe conclusion is that the claim is consistent with the data. The match between intervals and tests holds only for two-sided alternatives; a one-sided claim needs a one-sided test or a confidence level adjusted accordingly (a 90% interval corresponds to a one-sided test at $\alpha=0.05$).

注意区间做不到什么。包含 500 并不表明 500 就是均值：区间内的每个值都同样合理，换一个样本会得到不同的区间。唯一稳妥的结论是该说法与数据相容。区间与检验的对应只对双侧备择假设成立；单侧说法需要单侧检验，或相应调整置信水平（90% 的区间对应 $\alpha=0.05$ 的单侧检验）。

  3. What is not concluded: the interval does not prove the mean is 500 g, since values such as 497 g and 501 g are plausible too.
  4. In context: the sample gives no convincing evidence that the mean weight of the bags produced that day differs from the labeled 500 g.

标签称每袋含 500 克。由例题 7.2a，真实平均重量的 95% 区间为 $(495.72,\,501.08)$ 克。该区间支持还是反驳标签说法？

  1. 比较：$495.72<500<501.08$，所以区间包含 500。
  2. 结论：真实均值为 500 克是合理的；数据与说法相容。
  3. 不能得出的结论：区间并不证明均值就是 500 克，因为 497 克、501 克等值也同样合理。
  4. 结合情境：样本没有提供令人信服的证据表明当天所生产米袋的平均重量不同于标称的 500 克。
Worked Example 7.3b: a battery-life claim contradicted by the interval例题 7.3b：被区间驳斥的电池寿命说法

A manufacturer claims its batteries last a mean of 10 hours. A random sample of $n=30$ batteries from a large shipment has $\bar{x}=9.42$ hours and $s=1.1$ hours, and the sample graph shows no strong skew or outliers (also $n\ge 30$). Use a 95% interval to evaluate the claim.

  1. Conditions: random sample; 30 is under 10% of a large shipment; $n\ge 30$.
  2. Interval: $df=29$, $t^*=2.045$, $ME=2.045\cdot\dfrac{1.1}{\sqrt{30}}=0.411$, so $9.42\pm 0.411=(9.01,\,9.83)$ hours.
  3. Compare: the interval does not contain 10; every plausible value is below 10.
  4. In context: there is convincing evidence that the true mean life of batteries in this shipment is less than the claimed 10 hours; plausible means run from 9.01 to 9.83 hours.

制造商声称其电池平均寿命为 10 小时。从一大批货中随机抽取 $n=30$ 节电池，$\bar{x}=9.42$ 小时，$s=1.1$ 小时，样本图形无明显偏斜或离群点（且 $n\ge 30$）。用 95% 区间评价该说法。

  1. 条件：随机样本；30 节少于一大批货的 10%；$n\ge 30$。
  2. 区间：$df=29$，$t^*=2.045$，$ME=2.045\cdot\dfrac{1.1}{\sqrt{30}}=0.411$，所以 $9.42\pm 0.411=(9.01,\,9.83)$ 小时。
  3. 比较：区间不包含 10；所有合理取值都低于 10。
  4. 结合情境：有令人信服的证据表明这批电池的真实平均寿命低于所声称的 10 小时；合理的均值范围是 9.01 至 9.83 小时。
Exam tip考试提示

Use the phrases "consistent with" and "plausible" when the interval contains the claimed value, and "convincing evidence that the mean is less than (or greater than) ..." only when the whole interval is on one side of the claimed value. Never write that the interval "proves" the claim.

区间包含所声称的值时，用“与……相容”和“合理”；只有整个区间在所声称的值的一侧时，才写“有令人信服的证据表明均值小于（或大于）……”。绝不要写区间“证明”了某说法。

Common Mistake: concluding the claim is true because the interval contains it

Concluding "the claim is true" because the interval contains it. An interval can contain many values; each is plausible, none is proven.

常见错误：因区间含有声称值就断定说法为真

因为区间包含声称的值就得出“该说法为真”。区间可以包含许多值，每个都合理，没有一个被证明。
