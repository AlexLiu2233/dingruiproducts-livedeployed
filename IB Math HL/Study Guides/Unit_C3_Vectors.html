Unit C3: Vectors单元 C3：向量

The geometry of arrows in space. Vectors carry both magnitude and direction, and the algebra that operates on them (addition, scalar multiplication, dot product, cross product) translates classical Euclidean geometry into coordinates that a calculator can handle. The HL syllabus uses vectors for lines, planes, and distances in three dimensions. Every section follows the same arc: define the operation in components, prove the geometric meaning, then apply to a canonical exam set-up.本单元研究"空间中的箭头"的几何。向量同时携带大小与方向，作用于其上的代数（加法、数乘、点积、叉积）把欧氏几何译成计算器能处理的坐标。HL 大纲用向量处理三维空间中的直线、平面与距离。每节的叙事都遵循同一弧线：先以分量定义运算，再证其几何意义，最后应用到一类典型考题。

Vector Arithmetic, Magnitude, Unit Vectors向量运算、模长、单位向量 AHL 3.12

Learning objectives学习目标

  • 写出（state）位置向量（position vector）$\overrightarrow{OA}$ 的含义，并把位移写成 $\overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}$。
  • 在列向量形式与 $\mathbf{i}, \mathbf{j}, \mathbf{k}$ 形式下计算（calculate）向量的和、差与数乘。

Notation. A vector in three dimensions is written either in unit-vector form or as a column: $$ \mathbf{a} \;=\; a_1 \mathbf{i} + a_2 \mathbf{j} + a_3 \mathbf{k} \;=\; \begin{pmatrix} a_1 \\ a_2 \\ a_3 \end{pmatrix}. $$ Addition and scalar multiplication are componentwise. $$ \mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} \;=\; (a_1 + b_1, \; a_2 + b_2, \; a_3 + b_3), \qquad k \mathbf{a} \;=\; (k a_1, \; k a_2, \; k a_3). $$ Magnitude (length). $$ |\mathbf{v}| \;=\; \sqrt{v_1^{2} + v_2^{2} + v_3^{2}}. $$ Unit vector in the direction of $\mathbf{v}$. Provided $\mathbf{v} \ne \mathbf{0}$, $$ \hat{\mathbf{v}} \;=\; \frac{\mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{v}|}, \qquad |\hat{\mathbf{v}}| \;=\; 1. $$ The three standard basis vectors $\mathbf{i}, \mathbf{j}, \mathbf{k}$ are unit vectors along the $x, y, z$ axes.
记号。三维向量可写成单位向量组合或列向量形式： $$ \mathbf{a} \;=\; a_1 \mathbf{i} + a_2 \mathbf{j} + a_3 \mathbf{k} \;=\; \begin{pmatrix} a_1 \\ a_2 \\ a_3 \end{pmatrix}. $$ 加法与数乘按分量进行。 $$ \mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} \;=\; (a_1 + b_1, \; a_2 + b_2, \; a_3 + b_3), \qquad k \mathbf{a} \;=\; (k a_1, \; k a_2, \; k a_3). $$ 模长。 $$ |\mathbf{v}| \;=\; \sqrt{v_1^{2} + v_2^{2} + v_3^{2}}. $$ $\mathbf{v}$ 方向上的单位向量。设 $\mathbf{v} \ne \mathbf{0}$， $$ \hat{\mathbf{v}} \;=\; \frac{\mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{v}|}, \qquad |\hat{\mathbf{v}}| \;=\; 1. $$ 标准基向量 $\mathbf{i}, \mathbf{j}, \mathbf{k}$ 分别是沿 $x, y, z$ 轴方向的单位向量。
Worked Example C3.1: magnitude and unit vectorC3.1 例题：模长与单位向量

Given $\mathbf{v} = (3, 4, 12)$, find $|\mathbf{v}|$ and the unit vector $\hat{\mathbf{v}}$.设 $\mathbf{v} = (3, 4, 12)$，求 $|\mathbf{v}|$ 与单位向量 $\hat{\mathbf{v}}$。

Magnitude. Apply the formula componentwise:

模长。按分量套公式：

$$ |\mathbf{v}| \;=\; \sqrt{3^{2} + 4^{2} + 12^{2}} \;=\; \sqrt{9 + 16 + 144} \;=\; \sqrt{169} \;=\; 13. $$

Unit vector. Divide each component by the magnitude:

单位向量。每个分量都除以模长：

$$ \hat{\mathbf{v}} \;=\; \frac{1}{13} (3, 4, 12) \;=\; \left( \tfrac{3}{13}, \; \tfrac{4}{13}, \; \tfrac{12}{13} \right). $$

Check. $\left( \tfrac{3}{13} \right)^{2} + \left( \tfrac{4}{13} \right)^{2} + \left( \tfrac{12}{13} \right)^{2} = \tfrac{9 + 16 + 144}{169} = 1$, so $|\hat{\mathbf{v}}| = 1$ as required.

验证。$\left( \tfrac{3}{13} \right)^{2} + \left( \tfrac{4}{13} \right)^{2} + \left( \tfrac{12}{13} \right)^{2} = \tfrac{9 + 16 + 144}{169} = 1$，故 $|\hat{\mathbf{v}}| = 1$，符合定义。

Going deeper: why magnitude is a Pythagorean sum深入：为何模长是勾股和

In two dimensions, the segment from the origin to $(a_1, a_2)$ is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $a_1$ and $a_2$, so its length is $\sqrt{a_1^{2} + a_2^{2}}$ by Pythagoras. In three dimensions, apply Pythagoras twice: first in the $xy$-plane to get the projection length $\sqrt{a_1^{2} + a_2^{2}}$, then in the plane containing that projection and the $z$-axis to combine with $a_3$. The result is $\sqrt{(a_1^{2} + a_2^{2}) + a_3^{2}}$, which is the formula above. The pattern extends to $n$ dimensions: the magnitude of $(a_1, \dots, a_n)$ is $\sqrt{a_1^{2} + \cdots + a_n^{2}}$, which is the definition of the Euclidean norm.

二维时，从原点到 $(a_1, a_2)$ 的线段是腿长 $a_1$ 与 $a_2$ 的直角三角形的斜边，由勾股定理其长度为 $\sqrt{a_1^{2} + a_2^{2}}$。三维时连用两次勾股：先在 $xy$ 平面得投影长度 $\sqrt{a_1^{2} + a_2^{2}}$，再在含该投影与 $z$ 轴的平面中与 $a_3$ 合成，结果为 $\sqrt{(a_1^{2} + a_2^{2}) + a_3^{2}}$，即上面公式。此模式推广到 $n$ 维：$(a_1, \dots, a_n)$ 的模长为 $\sqrt{a_1^{2} + \cdots + a_n^{2}}$，即欧氏范数的定义。

Pitfall: the zero vector has no unit vector陷阱：零向量没有单位向量 If $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$, then $|\mathbf{v}| = 0$ and the formula $\hat{\mathbf{v}} = \mathbf{v} / |\mathbf{v}|$ divides by zero. The zero vector has no direction and therefore no unit vector. Always check that the vector you are normalising is nonzero before dividing.若 $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$，则 $|\mathbf{v}| = 0$，公式 $\hat{\mathbf{v}} = \mathbf{v} / |\mathbf{v}|$ 会除以零。零向量没有方向，因此没有单位向量。归一化之前务必检查向量非零。

Position vectors, collinearity and the section formula位置向量、共线与定比分点公式

Position vectors and displacements位置向量与位移

The position vector of a point $A$ is $\mathbf{a} = \overrightarrow{OA}$, the arrow from the origin $O$ to $A$; its components are the coordinates of $A$. The displacement from $A$ to $B$ is $\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{AO} + \overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}$ (end minus start), and the distance $AB$ is the magnitude $|\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}|$.

点 $A$ 的位置向量（position vector）是 $\mathbf{a} = \overrightarrow{OA}$，即从原点 $O$ 指向 $A$ 的箭头；它的分量就是 $A$ 的坐标。从 $A$ 到 $B$ 的位移为 $\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{AO} + \overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}$（终点减起点），距离 $AB$ 就是模长 $|\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}|$。

Collinear points. Three points $A, B, C$ are collinear (on one straight line) exactly when $\overrightarrow{AC} = k\,\overrightarrow{AB}$ for some scalar $k$. The two displacements are parallel and both start at $A$, so they lie on the same line. Two parallel vectors that start at different points do not prove collinearity on their own.

共线的点。三点 $A, B, C$ 共线（collinear，在同一条直线上）当且仅当存在标量 $k$ 使 $\overrightarrow{AC} = k\,\overrightarrow{AB}$。这两个位移平行且都从 $A$ 出发，所以落在同一条直线上。起点不同的两个平行向量本身不能证明共线。

Section (ratio) formula. The point $P$ on the segment $AB$ with $AP : PB = m : n$ has position vector $\mathbf{p} = \mathbf{a} + \tfrac{m}{m + n}(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}) = \dfrac{n\mathbf{a} + m\mathbf{b}}{m + n}$. The midpoint ($m = n$) is $\tfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b})$.

定比分点公式（section formula）。线段 $AB$ 上满足 $AP : PB = m : n$ 的点 $P$ 的位置向量为 $\mathbf{p} = \mathbf{a} + \tfrac{m}{m + n}(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}) = \dfrac{n\mathbf{a} + m\mathbf{b}}{m + n}$。中点（$m = n$）为 $\tfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b})$。

Common Mistake: start minus end常见错误：起点减终点

Writing $\overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}$ reverses the direction. The magnitude is unchanged, so the slip stays hidden until a later step (a line equation, a ratio point) uses the direction. Say "end minus start" each time you form a displacement.

写成 $\overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}$ 会把方向弄反。模长不变，所以这个错误会一直隐藏，直到后面的步骤（直线方程、定比分点）用到方向才暴露。每次构造位移时都默念"终点减起点"。

Worked Example C3.1b: position vectors, collinearity and a ratioC3.1b 例题：位置向量、共线与定比分点

The points $A(1, 2, -1)$, $B(4, 8, 5)$ and $C(7, 14, 11)$ are given. (a) Find $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and the distance $AB$. (b) Show that $A$, $B$ and $C$ are collinear. (c) Find the point $P$ on $AB$ with $AP : PB = 1 : 2$.已知点 $A(1, 2, -1)$、$B(4, 8, 5)$、$C(7, 14, 11)$。(a) 求 $\overrightarrow{AB}$ 与距离 $AB$。(b) 证明 $A$、$B$、$C$ 三点共线。(c) 求 $AB$ 上满足 $AP : PB = 1 : 2$ 的点 $P$。

(a) End minus start: $\overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a} = (4 - 1, \; 8 - 2, \; 5 - (-1)) = (3, 6, 6)$, so $AB = \sqrt{9 + 36 + 36} = \sqrt{81} = 9$.

(a) 终点减起点：$\overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a} = (4 - 1, \; 8 - 2, \; 5 - (-1)) = (3, 6, 6)$，所以 $AB = \sqrt{9 + 36 + 36} = \sqrt{81} = 9$。

(b) $\overrightarrow{AC} = \mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a} = (6, 12, 12) = 2\,\overrightarrow{AB}$. The vectors $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$ are parallel and share the point $A$, so $A$, $B$, $C$ are collinear. Since $k = 2$, $B$ is the midpoint of $AC$.

(b) $\overrightarrow{AC} = \mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a} = (6, 12, 12) = 2\,\overrightarrow{AB}$。$\overrightarrow{AB}$ 与 $\overrightarrow{AC}$ 平行且有公共点 $A$，所以 $A$、$B$、$C$ 共线。由于 $k = 2$，$B$ 是 $AC$ 的中点。

(c) $AP : PB = 1 : 2$ puts $P$ one third of the way from $A$ to $B$: $\mathbf{p} = \mathbf{a} + \tfrac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} = (1, 2, -1) + (1, 2, 2) = (2, 4, 1)$. Check with the section formula: $\tfrac{1}{3}(2\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) = \tfrac{1}{3}(2 + 4, \; 4 + 8, \; -2 + 5) = (2, 4, 1)$.

(c) $AP : PB = 1 : 2$ 表示 $P$ 位于从 $A$ 到 $B$ 的三分之一处：$\mathbf{p} = \mathbf{a} + \tfrac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} = (1, 2, -1) + (1, 2, 2) = (2, 4, 1)$。用定比分点公式验证：$\tfrac{1}{3}(2\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) = \tfrac{1}{3}(2 + 4, \; 4 + 8, \; -2 + 5) = (2, 4, 1)$。

Why this works. Every point on line $AB$ is $\mathbf{a}$ plus a multiple of $\overrightarrow{AB}$; collinearity and ratio points are both statements about that multiple.

为什么可行。直线 $AB$ 上的每一点都等于 $\mathbf{a}$ 加上 $\overrightarrow{AB}$ 的某个倍数；共线与定比分点说的都是这个倍数。

Dot Product and Angle Between Vectors点积与向量夹角 AHL 3.13

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate the scalar product $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}$ from components.
  • Calculate the angle between two vectors using $\cos\theta = \dfrac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{u}|\,|\mathbf{v}|}$.
  • Deduce from the sign of $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}$ whether two vectors are perpendicular, or meet at an acute or an obtuse angle.
  • Use the properties of the scalar product, including $\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v} = |\mathbf{v}|^{2}$, to expand and simplify expressions.
  • Calculate the scalar projection and the vector projection of one vector onto another.
  • 由分量计算（calculate）标量积（scalar product）$\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}$。
  • 用 $\cos\theta = \dfrac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{u}|\,|\mathbf{v}|}$ 计算两向量的夹角。
  • 由 $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}$ 的符号推断（deduce）两向量垂直，或成锐角、钝角。
  • 用标量积的性质（包括 $\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v} = |\mathbf{v}|^{2}$）展开并化简表达式。
  • 计算一个向量在另一个向量上的标量投影与向量投影。
Two equivalent formulas for the dot product. $$ \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} \;=\; u_1 v_1 + u_2 v_2 + u_3 v_3 \;=\; |\mathbf{u}|\,|\mathbf{v}|\,\cos\theta, $$ where $\theta$ is the angle between $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$, with $0 \le \theta \le \pi$.

The angle formula. Provided $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \ne \mathbf{0}$: $$ \cos\theta \;=\; \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{u}|\,|\mathbf{v}|}. $$ Perpendicularity test. $\mathbf{u} \perp \mathbf{v}$ if and only if $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0$ (for nonzero vectors).

Algebraic properties. The dot product is commutative ($\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{u}$), distributes over addition, and satisfies $\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v} = |\mathbf{v}|^{2}$.
点积的两种等价公式。 $$ \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} \;=\; u_1 v_1 + u_2 v_2 + u_3 v_3 \;=\; |\mathbf{u}|\,|\mathbf{v}|\,\cos\theta, $$ 其中 $\theta$ 为 $\mathbf{u}$ 与 $\mathbf{v}$ 之间的夹角，$0 \le \theta \le \pi$。

夹角公式。设 $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \ne \mathbf{0}$： $$ \cos\theta \;=\; \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{u}|\,|\mathbf{v}|}. $$ 垂直判别。对非零向量：$\mathbf{u} \perp \mathbf{v}$ 当且仅当 $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0$。

代数性质。点积满足交换律（$\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{u}$）、对加法的分配律，以及 $\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v} = |\mathbf{v}|^{2}$。

Start with the law of cosines applied to the triangle with sides $\mathbf{u}$, $\mathbf{v}$, $\mathbf{u} - \mathbf{v}$:

Properties of the scalar product标量积的性质

Rules you may use without proof可直接使用的规则
  • Commutative: $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{u}$.
  • Distributive: $\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v} + \mathbf{w}) = \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} + \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{w}$.
  • Scalars factor out: $(k\mathbf{u}) \cdot \mathbf{v} = k(\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v})$.
  • Self product: $\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v} = |\mathbf{v}|^{2}$.
  • Sign: for nonzero vectors, $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} > 0$ means an acute angle, $= 0$ means perpendicular, $< 0$ means an obtuse angle.
  • 交换律：$\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{u}$。
  • 分配律：$\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v} + \mathbf{w}) = \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} + \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{w}$。
  • 标量可提出：$(k\mathbf{u}) \cdot \mathbf{v} = k(\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v})$。
  • 自身点积：$\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v} = |\mathbf{v}|^{2}$。
  • 符号：对非零向量，$\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} > 0$ 表示锐角，$= 0$ 表示垂直，$< 0$ 表示钝角。

Why they matter. Together they let you expand like ordinary algebra: $|\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}|^{2} = (\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}) \cdot (\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}) = |\mathbf{u}|^{2} + 2\,\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} + |\mathbf{v}|^{2}$. When $\mathbf{u} \perp \mathbf{v}$ the middle term vanishes and this is Pythagoras.

为什么重要。这些规则让你可以像普通代数一样展开：$|\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}|^{2} = (\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}) \cdot (\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}) = |\mathbf{u}|^{2} + 2\,\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} + |\mathbf{v}|^{2}$。当 $\mathbf{u} \perp \mathbf{v}$ 时中间项为零，这就是勾股定理。

Common Mistake: cancelling a dot product常见错误：约去点积

$\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{w}$ does not imply $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{w}$. With $\mathbf{u} = (1, 0, 0)$, $\mathbf{v} = (0, 1, 0)$ and $\mathbf{w} = (0, 0, 1)$, both products are $0$ but $\mathbf{v} \ne \mathbf{w}$. It only gives $\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v} - \mathbf{w}) = 0$, so $\mathbf{u} \perp (\mathbf{v} - \mathbf{w})$ or $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{w}$. The scalar product is a number, so there is no "dividing by $\mathbf{u}$".

由 $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{w}$ 推不出 $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{w}$。取 $\mathbf{u} = (1, 0, 0)$、$\mathbf{v} = (0, 1, 0)$、$\mathbf{w} = (0, 0, 1)$，两个点积都是 $0$，但 $\mathbf{v} \ne \mathbf{w}$。它只能给出 $\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v} - \mathbf{w}) = 0$，即 $\mathbf{u} \perp (\mathbf{v} - \mathbf{w})$ 或 $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{w}$。标量积是一个数，不存在"除以 $\mathbf{u}$"这种运算。

Vector form. A line in three dimensions through point $\mathbf{a}$ with direction $\mathbf{d}$ (any nonzero vector parallel to the line) is the set of points $$ \mathbf{r} \;=\; \mathbf{a} + \lambda \mathbf{d}, \qquad \lambda \in \mathbb{R}. $$ Parametric form. Writing $\mathbf{r} = (x, y, z)$, $\mathbf{a} = (a_1, a_2, a_3)$, $\mathbf{d} = (d_1, d_2, d_3)$, the same line is $$ x = a_1 + \lambda d_1, \qquad y = a_2 + \lambda d_2, \qquad z = a_3 + \lambda d_3. $$ From two points. The line through $P$ and $Q$ uses $\mathbf{a} = \overrightarrow{OP}$ and $\mathbf{d} = \overrightarrow{PQ} = \overrightarrow{OQ} - \overrightarrow{OP}$.

Direction vector is not unique. Any nonzero scalar multiple of $\mathbf{d}$ describes the same line. Two lines are parallel exactly when one direction vector is a scalar multiple of the other.
向量式。三维空间中过点 $\mathbf{a}$、以 $\mathbf{d}$（任一与该直线平行的非零向量）为方向的直线，记为 $$ \mathbf{r} \;=\; \mathbf{a} + \lambda \mathbf{d}, \qquad \lambda \in \mathbb{R}. $$ 参数式。令 $\mathbf{r} = (x, y, z)$、$\mathbf{a} = (a_1, a_2, a_3)$、$\mathbf{d} = (d_1, d_2, d_3)$，同一直线写作 $$ x = a_1 + \lambda d_1, \qquad y = a_2 + \lambda d_2, \qquad z = a_3 + \lambda d_3. $$ 由两点求直线。过 $P$、$Q$ 的直线取 $\mathbf{a} = \overrightarrow{OP}$、$\mathbf{d} = \overrightarrow{PQ} = \overrightarrow{OQ} - \overrightarrow{OP}$。

方向向量不唯一。$\mathbf{d}$ 的任一非零数乘都描述同一条直线。两直线平行当且仅当其方向向量互为数乘。

Write the vector equation and the parametric equations of the line through $(1, 2, 3)$ with direction vector $(2, -1, 1)$. Does the point $(5, 0, 5)$ lie on this line?写出过 $(1, 2, 3)$、方向向量为 $(2, -1, 1)$ 的直线的向量方程与参数方程。判断点 $(5, 0, 5)$ 是否在该直线上。

Vector form.

向量式。

$$ \mathbf{r} \;=\; \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} + \lambda \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}. $$

Parametric form.

参数式。

$$ x = 1 + 2 \lambda, \qquad y = 2 - \lambda, \qquad z = 3 + \lambda. $$

Test the point $(5, 0, 5)$. If the point lies on the line, the same parameter value must satisfy all three equations. From $x = 5$: $1 + 2 \lambda = 5 \Rightarrow \lambda = 2$. Check the others: $y = 2 - 2 = 0$ ✓; $z = 3 + 2 = 5$ ✓. All three agree at $\lambda = 2$, so $(5, 0, 5)$ does lie on the line.

检验点 $(5, 0, 5)$。若点在直线上，则同一参数值需同时满足三个分量方程。由 $x = 5$：$1 + 2 \lambda = 5 \Rightarrow \lambda = 2$。验证其它：$y = 2 - 2 = 0$ ✓；$z = 3 + 2 = 5$ ✓。三式在 $\lambda = 2$ 时一致，故 $(5, 0, 5)$ 在直线上。

Reading the result of the test如何解读检验结果 If one value of $\lambda$ satisfies all three parametric equations, the point is on the line. If the three equations give different $\lambda$ values, the point is not on the line. Angle between two lines and motion along a line两直线的夹角与沿直线的运动

Angle between two lines两直线的夹角

The angle between two lines is the angle between their direction vectors $\mathbf{d}_1$ and $\mathbf{d}_2$, taken as the acute angle: $\cos\theta = \dfrac{|\mathbf{d}_1 \cdot \mathbf{d}_2|}{|\mathbf{d}_1|\,|\mathbf{d}_2|}$. Only the directions matter, so the formula works even for lines that never meet. The absolute value is there because reversing one direction vector describes the same line but would turn $\theta$ into $180^{\circ} - \theta$.

两直线的夹角是它们方向向量 $\mathbf{d}_1$ 与 $\mathbf{d}_2$ 的夹角，取锐角：$\cos\theta = \dfrac{|\mathbf{d}_1 \cdot \mathbf{d}_2|}{|\mathbf{d}_1|\,|\mathbf{d}_2|}$。只有方向起作用，所以即使两直线不相交，公式也适用。取绝对值是因为把一个方向向量反向仍表示同一条直线，却会把 $\theta$ 变成 $180^{\circ} - \theta$。

Motion along a line. If an object has position $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}_0 + t\mathbf{v}$ at time $t$, it moves along a straight line from $\mathbf{r}_0$ with constant velocity $\mathbf{v}$; its speed is the magnitude $|\mathbf{v}|$. Here the parameter has a physical meaning (time), and the direction vector is the velocity.

沿直线的运动。若物体在时刻 $t$ 的位置为 $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}_0 + t\mathbf{v}$，它就从 $\mathbf{r}_0$ 出发，以恒定速度（velocity）$\mathbf{v}$ 沿直线运动；速率（speed）是模长 $|\mathbf{v}|$。这里参数有物理意义（时间），方向向量就是速度。

Common Mistake: using position vectors in the angle formula常见错误：在夹角公式中用位置向量

For $L_1: \mathbf{r} = (2, 0, 1) + \lambda(2, 3, -2)$ the direction is $(2, 3, -2)$; the point $(2, 0, 1)$ plays no part in the angle. Feeding position vectors into the formula gives the angle between two arrows from the origin, which has nothing to do with the lines.

对 $L_1: \mathbf{r} = (2, 0, 1) + \lambda(2, 3, -2)$，方向是 $(2, 3, -2)$；点 $(2, 0, 1)$ 与夹角无关。把位置向量代入公式，求出的是从原点出发的两个箭头的夹角，与这两条直线毫无关系。

  • 解释（explain）三维空间中相交、平行、重合（coincident）与异面（skew）直线的区别。
  • 比较方向向量，推断（deduce）两直线是否平行。
  • 检验一个点，判断（determine）平行直线是重合还是不同的直线。
  • 联立两直线的参数方程求交点（point of intersection）。
  • 证明（show）两直线异面，并给出两条理由。
Three mutually exclusive cases in three dimensions.
  • Intersecting. The two lines share exactly one point. Direction vectors are not parallel; the system of parametric equations has a unique solution.
  • Parallel. Direction vectors are scalar multiples of each other. Either the lines coincide (same line, infinitely many common points) or they share no points.
  • Skew. The lines are neither parallel nor intersecting. They live in different parallel planes; no common point exists. Skew is the case that has no analogue in two dimensions.
The procedure. Set the parametric equations of the two lines equal (using different parameter names, typically $\lambda$ and $\mu$). This gives three equations in two unknowns. Solve two of them; substitute into the third.
  • If all three are simultaneously satisfied: intersecting. Substitute back to find the common point.
  • If no $(\lambda, \mu)$ satisfies all three: lines are skew (assuming non-parallel directions) or parallel without coincidence (parallel directions).
三维中相互排斥的三种情形。
  • 相交。两直线恰有一个公共点。方向向量不平行；参数方程组有唯一解。
  • 平行。方向向量互为数乘。要么重合（同一直线、无穷多公共点），要么无公共点。
  • 异面。既不平行也不相交。两直线落在两个平行平面内，无公共点。异面是二维所没有的情形。
判定流程。把两直线的参数方程相等（参数取不同字母，常用 $\lambda$ 与 $\mu$）。得三式两未知。取其中两式解出，代入第三式验证。
  • 若三式同时成立：相交，回代求公共点。
  • 若无 $(\lambda, \mu)$ 使三式同时成立：方向不平行则异面；方向平行则平行不重合。

Going deeper: why "two of three" is the right verification rule深入：为何用"三式中取两式"验证

Two parametric lines in three dimensions give a system of three linear equations in two unknowns $(\lambda, \mu)$. Generically, three equations in two unknowns are overdetermined: two independent equations among them determine $(\lambda, \mu)$ uniquely, and the remaining equation acts as a consistency check. If the third holds, the lines meet at that $(\lambda, \mu)$; if it fails, the system is inconsistent and there is no common point. The "two of three" procedure is the cleanest version of this argument, and it is easy to set out line by line.

三维空间中两条参数直线给出三式两未知 $(\lambda, \mu)$ 的线性方程组。三式两未知一般属"超定"：其中两个独立的方程可唯一确定 $(\lambda, \mu)$，剩下的一式则是相容性的验证。若第三式成立，两直线在该 $(\lambda, \mu)$ 处相交；若失败则方程组不一致，无公共点。"取两式解、第三式验"是上述论证最简洁的写法，也便于逐行写清楚。

Parallel lines and coincident lines平行直线与重合直线

When the directions are parallel方向平行时

If $\mathbf{d}_2 = k\,\mathbf{d}_1$, the lines are parallel, and one test decides the rest: take the known point of $L_2$ and check whether it lies on $L_1$. If it does, the lines are coincident (two equations for the same line, infinitely many common points). If it does not, they are distinct parallel lines with no common point. Solving for $\lambda$ and $\mu$ cannot finish this case on its own, because parallel directions make the system either inconsistent or dependent.

若 $\mathbf{d}_2 = k\,\mathbf{d}_1$，两直线平行，剩下的只需一次检验：取 $L_2$ 上的已知点，检查它是否在 $L_1$ 上。若在，两直线重合（coincident，同一条直线的两个方程，有无穷多公共点）。若不在，它们是不同的平行直线（parallel lines），没有公共点。仅靠解 $\lambda$ 与 $\mu$ 无法单独完成这种情形，因为方向平行时方程组要么不相容，要么相关。

Scalar (normal) form. A plane is fixed by a point $\mathbf{a}$ on it and a normal vector $\mathbf{n}$ perpendicular to it: $$ \mathbf{r} \cdot \mathbf{n} \;=\; \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{n} \;=\; d. $$ Cartesian form. Writing $\mathbf{n} = (a, b, c)$ and $\mathbf{r} = (x, y, z)$: $$ a x + b y + c z \;=\; d. $$ The coefficients of $x, y, z$ are the components of a normal vector.

Parametric (two-direction) form. A plane through $\mathbf{a}$ containing direction vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ (linearly independent, both lying in the plane) is $$ \mathbf{r} \;=\; \mathbf{a} + \lambda \mathbf{u} + \mu \mathbf{v}, \qquad \lambda, \mu \in \mathbb{R}. $$ Finding a normal from a plane through three points. Form two edge vectors $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$; their cross product $\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}$ is normal to the plane. Then $d = \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{n}$ using any of the three known points.

Distance from a point to a plane. For point $P$ and plane $a x + b y + c z = d$: $$ \text{dist}(P, \text{plane}) \;=\; \frac{|a x_P + b y_P + c z_P - d|}{\sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2} + c^{2}}}. $$
标量（法向量）式。平面由其上一点 $\mathbf{a}$ 与一法向量 $\mathbf{n}$ 决定： $$ \mathbf{r} \cdot \mathbf{n} \;=\; \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{n} \;=\; d. $$ 直角坐标式。令 $\mathbf{n} = (a, b, c)$、$\mathbf{r} = (x, y, z)$： $$ a x + b y + c z \;=\; d. $$ $x, y, z$ 的系数即为一个法向量的分量。

参数（双方向）式。过 $\mathbf{a}$、含线性无关方向向量 $\mathbf{u}$ 与 $\mathbf{v}$（均在面内）的平面： $$ \mathbf{r} \;=\; \mathbf{a} + \lambda \mathbf{u} + \mu \mathbf{v}, \qquad \lambda, \mu \in \mathbb{R}. $$ 由三点求法向量。构造两条边向量 $\overrightarrow{AB}$、$\overrightarrow{AC}$；其叉积 $\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}$ 即为法向量。再用任一已知点求 $d = \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{n}$。

点到平面的距离。点 $P$ 到平面 $a x + b y + c z = d$ 的距离： $$ \text{dist}(P, \text{plane}) \;=\; \frac{|a x_P + b y_P + c z_P - d|}{\sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2} + c^{2}}}. $$

Going deeper: why the distance formula is what it is深入：点到平面距离公式的来由

Take a point $P$ and a plane with unit normal $\hat{\mathbf{n}} = \mathbf{n} / |\mathbf{n}|$ and known point $\mathbf{a}$. The signed distance from $P$ to the plane is the scalar projection of $\overrightarrow{aP} = P - \mathbf{a}$ onto $\hat{\mathbf{n}}$:

取点 $P$ 与平面（单位法向量 $\hat{\mathbf{n}} = \mathbf{n} / |\mathbf{n}|$、面上已知点 $\mathbf{a}$）。$P$ 到平面的带符号距离即为 $\overrightarrow{aP} = P - \mathbf{a}$ 在 $\hat{\mathbf{n}}$ 上的标量投影：

$$ \text{signed dist} \;=\; (P - \mathbf{a}) \cdot \hat{\mathbf{n}} \;=\; \frac{(P - \mathbf{a}) \cdot \mathbf{n}}{|\mathbf{n}|} \;=\; \frac{\mathbf{n} \cdot P - \mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{a}}{|\mathbf{n}|}. $$

Writing $\mathbf{n} = (a, b, c)$ and $\mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{a} = d$ recovers the formula in the cram-cheat. The absolute value drops the sign, leaving the unsigned distance.

Angles and intersections: lines and planes角与交：直线与平面

Line and plane直线与平面

Substitute the parametric equations of the line into the Cartesian equation of the plane. This gives one equation in $\lambda$. A unique solution means the line cuts the plane at one point. A false statement such as $0 = 3$ means the line is parallel to the plane and never meets it. An identity such as $0 = 0$ means the line lies in the plane. A quick pre-test: $\mathbf{d} \cdot \mathbf{n} = 0$ means the line is parallel to the plane or lies in it.

把直线的参数方程代入平面的直角坐标方程，得到关于 $\lambda$ 的一个方程。有唯一解：直线与平面交于一点。得到 $0 = 3$ 这类矛盾：直线与平面平行，永不相交。得到 $0 = 0$ 这类恒等式：直线在平面内。快速预判：$\mathbf{d} \cdot \mathbf{n} = 0$ 说明直线与平面平行或在平面内。

Angle between a line and a plane. It is the angle $\varphi$ between the line and its shadow in the plane. The normal makes the angle $90^{\circ} - \varphi$ with the line, so $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{|\mathbf{d} \cdot \mathbf{n}|}{|\mathbf{d}|\,|\mathbf{n}|}$.

直线与平面的夹角。它是直线与其在平面内投影之间的夹角 $\varphi$。法向量与直线的夹角为 $90^{\circ} - \varphi$，所以 $\sin\varphi = \dfrac{|\mathbf{d} \cdot \mathbf{n}|}{|\mathbf{d}|\,|\mathbf{n}|}$。

Two planes. The angle between two planes is the acute angle between their normals: $\cos\theta = \dfrac{|\mathbf{n}_1 \cdot \mathbf{n}_2|}{|\mathbf{n}_1|\,|\mathbf{n}_2|}$. The intersection of two non-parallel planes is a line. Its direction $\mathbf{n}_1 \times \mathbf{n}_2$ is perpendicular to both normals, so it lies in both planes; find one point on it by setting one variable (for example $z = 0$) and solving the two plane equations.

两个平面。两平面的夹角是它们法向量所成的锐角：$\cos\theta = \dfrac{|\mathbf{n}_1 \cdot \mathbf{n}_2|}{|\mathbf{n}_1|\,|\mathbf{n}_2|}$。两个不平行平面的交线是一条直线。其方向 $\mathbf{n}_1 \times \mathbf{n}_2$ 同时垂直于两个法向量，因此位于两个平面内；令某个变量为定值（如 $z = 0$），解两个平面方程即可求出交线上的一点。

Three planes. Solve the three Cartesian equations as a system. A unique solution is a single common point; infinitely many solutions mean the planes share a line; an inconsistent system means no point lies on all three.

三个平面。把三个直角坐标方程联立求解。唯一解：只有一个公共点；无穷多解：三平面有一条公共直线；方程组不相容：没有点同时在三个平面上。

Common Mistake: cosine for a line and a plane常见错误：线面角用余弦

Putting $\mathbf{d}$ and $\mathbf{n}$ into the cosine formula gives the angle between the line and the normal, which is $90^{\circ} - \varphi$, not the angle with the plane. Use sine, or subtract your cosine answer from $90^{\circ}$.

把 $\mathbf{d}$ 与 $\mathbf{n}$ 代入余弦公式，得到的是直线与法向量的夹角 $90^{\circ} - \varphi$，而不是线面角。要用正弦，或者用 $90^{\circ}$ 减去余弦公式的结果。

Exam Tactics考试技巧

Exam Strategy and Common Pitfalls考试策略与常见陷阱

Setting up lines and planes建立直线与平面
  • State which point and which direction you are using. For a line, write "point $\mathbf{a}$, direction $\mathbf{d}$" so your method is clear. For a plane, name the point and the normal vector before writing the equation.
  • 明示所用的点与方向。直线题写出"点 $\mathbf{a}$、方向 $\mathbf{d}$"，让方法一目了然。平面题先点名"点 + 法向量"，再写方程。
  • Use different parameter letters for different lines. $L_1$ uses $\lambda$, $L_2$ uses $\mu$. Reusing the same letter is the canonical slip when checking for intersections.
  • 不同直线用不同参数字母。$L_1$ 用 $\lambda$、$L_2$ 用 $\mu$。求交点时若两线复用同一字母，是最容易出现的代数错。
Dot and cross products点积与叉积
  • Dot product is a scalar; cross product is a vector. Writing $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = (3, 0, 5)$ or $\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = 7$ is a notation error: a scalar cannot equal a vector.
  • 点积是标量、叉积是向量。写出 $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = (3, 0, 5)$ 或 $\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = 7$ 是记号错误：标量不能等于向量。
  • For "angle between" two vectors, take an absolute value if the question asks for the acute angle. $\cos\theta$ can be negative; if the question asks for the acute angle, use $\arccos \tfrac{|\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}|}{|\mathbf{u}||\mathbf{v}|}$.
  • 题目要求"锐角"时取绝对值。$\cos\theta$ 可为负；若题目要求锐角，应用 $\arccos \tfrac{|\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}|}{|\mathbf{u}||\mathbf{v}|}$。
Planes and distances平面与距离
  • The coefficients of $x, y, z$ in the Cartesian form are the normal. Conversely, given a normal $(a, b, c)$ and a known point, the equation is $a(x - x_0) + b(y - y_0) + c(z - z_0) = 0$.
  • 直角坐标方程中 $x, y, z$ 的系数即为法向量。反之，已知法向量 $(a, b, c)$ 与一点 $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$，方程为 $a(x - x_0) + b(y - y_0) + c(z - z_0) = 0$。
  • For distance from a point to a plane, divide by $|\mathbf{n}|$, not by $\mathbf{n}$ itself. The denominator is a scalar.
  • 点到平面的距离要除以 $|\mathbf{n}|$，不是除以 $\mathbf{n}$。分母是标量。
  • Verify by substituting the original points. If $A, B, C$ defined the plane, all three must satisfy your final equation. This is a cheap and reliable end-of-question check.
  • 用原始点逐一验证。若用 $A, B, C$ 三点定义平面，三点都应满足你的最终方程。这是廉价且可靠的末段自检。
