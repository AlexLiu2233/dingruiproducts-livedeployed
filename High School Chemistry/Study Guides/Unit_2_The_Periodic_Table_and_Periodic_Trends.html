The Periodic Table and Periodic Trends周期表与周期性

The periodic table is chemistry's master map: every element's position encodes its electron count, and that position predicts radius, ionization energy, electron affinity, electronegativity, and reactivity. This guide walks through the table's organization and history, the block structure (s, p, d, f), and the seven periodic trends, each explained through effective nuclear charge $Z_\text{eff}$ and shielding. Reactivity patterns in Groups 1, 17, and 18 close the unit. Worked examples and KaTeX formulas are used throughout.周期表（periodic table，元素周期表）是化学的总体地图：每种元素的位置编码了其电子数，而该位置能预测原子半径（atomic radius，原子半径）、电离能（ionization energy，电离能）、电子亲和能（electron affinity，电子亲和能）、电负性（electronegativity，电负性）和反应活性。本指南系统介绍周期表的组织结构与历史、s/p/d/f 区块划分，以及七大周期规律，每一项均通过有效核电荷 $Z_\text{eff}$ 和屏蔽效应加以解释。第 1、17、18 族的反应活性趋势作为本单元收尾。全程使用例题与公式。

Organization and History of the Periodic Table周期表的组织与历史

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Predict the properties of an undiscovered element from its neighbours in the table (Worked Example 1).
  • Find the period and group of an element from its atomic number and name its valence-electron count (Worked Example 2).
  • Explain why argon comes before potassium although argon has the larger average atomic mass (Worked Example 2).
  • Explain why the periods contain 2, 8, 8, 18, 18 and 32 elements.
  • 陈述周期律，并解释为什么现代周期表按原子序数而不是原子质量排列。
  • 根据相邻元素预测尚未发现元素的性质（例题 1）。
  • 由原子序数确定元素所在的周期和族，并说出价电子数（例题 2）。
  • 解释为什么氩的平均原子质量较大却排在钾之前（例题 2）。
  • 解释为什么各周期分别含有 2、8、8、18、18 和 32 种元素。
The periodic table arranges elements by increasing atomic number so that elements with similar properties fall in the same column.元素周期表按原子序数递增的顺序排列元素，使性质相似的元素位于同一列。
  • Mendeleev (1869)门捷列夫（1869年）: arranged elements by atomic mass, left gaps for undiscovered elements, and predicted their properties. When those elements were found (Ga, Sc, Ge), the predictions matched. This validated the table as a predictive model, exactly what NGSS HS-PS1-1 calls it: "Use the periodic table as a model to predict the relative properties of elements."：按原子质量排列元素，为未发现的元素留空，并预测其性质。当这些元素（Ga、Sc、Ge）被发现时，预测结果一致。这证明了周期表作为预测模型的有效性，正是 NGSS HS-PS1-1 所述："以元素周期表为模型预测元素的相对性质。"
  • Moseley (1913)莫塞莱（1913年）: used X-ray spectra to show atomic number $Z$ (proton count), not atomic mass, is the fundamental ordering principle. This resolved the anomalies in Mendeleev's table (e.g., Ar before K).：用 X 射线光谱证明原子序数 $Z$（质子数）而非原子质量是基本排列原则。这解决了门捷列夫周期表中的异常（如 Ar 排在 K 之前）。
  • Periodic law周期律: stated by SCH3U B3.3: "the physical and chemical properties of the elements are periodic functions of their atomic numbers." Repeat every 8 (periods 1–3) or 18 (periods 4–5) elements.：SCH3U B3.3 表述："元素的物理和化学性质是其原子序数的周期函数。"每 8 个（第 1–3 周期）或 18 个（第 4–5 周期）元素重复一次。
Worked Example 1: Predicting a missing element (Mendeleev style)例题 1：预测缺失元素（门捷列夫方式）

In 1869 Mendeleev left a gap below silicon (Si) in his table. He predicted the missing element ("eka-silicon") would have: atomic mass $\approx 72\ \mathrm{u}$; density $\approx 5.5\ \mathrm{g/cm^3}$; gray metallic appearance; four valence electrons; formula of oxide EX$_2$O$_4$. Germanium (Ge, $Z = 32$) was discovered in 1886. Its actual properties: atomic mass $72.6\ \mathrm{u}$; density $5.32\ \mathrm{g/cm^3}$; gray metalloid; four valence electrons; formula GeO$_2$. Explain how the periodic table functioned as a predictive model here.1869 年门捷列夫在他的表中留下了硅（Si）下方的空格。他预测缺失元素（"类硅"）将具有：原子质量 $\approx 72\ \mathrm{u}$；密度 $\approx 5.5\ \mathrm{g/cm^3}$；灰色金属外观；四个价电子；氧化物分子式 EX$_2$O$_4$。锗（Ge，$Z = 32$）于 1886 年被发现。其实际性质：原子质量 $72.6\ \mathrm{u}$；密度 $5.32\ \mathrm{g/cm^3}$；灰色类金属；四个价电子；分子式 GeO$_2$。解释元素周期表在此如何作为预测模型。

Column position encodes valence electrons.列的位置编码价电子数。 Si (Group 14) has 4 valence electrons; the element below it must also have 4. Four valence electrons predict oxide formula EO$_2$ and tetravalent bonding, exactly GeO$_2$.Si（第 14 族）有 4 个价电子；其下方的元素也必须有 4 个。四个价电子预测氧化物分子式 EO$_2$ 和四价成键，与 GeO$_2$ 完全吻合。

Row position encodes shell count and approximate size/density.行的位置编码壳层数和近似大小/密度。 Below Si (Period 3) and Sn (Period 5), the unknown was Period 4, with intermediate size and density. Mendeleev interpolated from neighbors to predict density $\approx 5.5$ vs actual $5.32$, a $3.4\%$ error.在 Si（第 3 周期）和 Sn（第 5 周期）之间，未知元素在第 4 周期，中等大小和密度。门捷列夫从相邻元素插值，预测密度 $\approx 5.5$，实际值 $5.32$，误差 $3.4\%$。

Conclusion.结论。 Periodic patterns in the table, not curve-fitting but structure from electron arrangement, allow quantitative predictions about undiscovered elements. This is the "model" invoked in NGSS HS-PS1-1 and stated as the periodic law in SCH3U B3.3.元素周期表中的周期规律，不是曲线拟合，而是来自电子排布的结构，允许对未发现元素进行定量预测。这就是 NGSS HS-PS1-1 所援引的"模型"，以及 SCH3U B3.3 所述的周期律。

Worked Example 2: Placing an element from its atomic number例题 2：由原子序数确定元素位置

(a) An element has atomic number $Z = 34$. State its period, its group, its number of valence electrons and one element it resembles chemically. (b) Argon (average atomic mass $39.95\ \mathrm{u}$) comes before potassium ($39.10\ \mathrm{u}$) in the table. Explain why.(a) 某元素的原子序数为 $Z = 34$。说明它的周期、族、价电子数，以及一种与它化学性质相似的元素。(b) 氩（平均原子质量 $39.95\ \mathrm{u}$）排在钾（$39.10\ \mathrm{u}$）之前。解释原因。

(a) Period.(a) 周期。 The noble gases have $Z = 2, 10, 18$, so $Z = 34$ lies beyond argon and is in Period 4. Period 4 starts at potassium ($Z = 19$).稀有气体的 $Z = 2, 10, 18$，所以 $Z = 34$ 超过了氩，位于第 4 周期。第 4 周期从钾（$Z = 19$）开始。

(a) Group.(a) 族。 Period 4 has 18 elements, one for each group 1 to 18. Element number $34 - 18 = 16$ of the period is in Group 16. Valence electrons for Groups 13 to 18 are the group number minus 10: $16 - 10 = 6$.第 4 周期有 18 种元素，恰好对应第 1 至 18 族。该周期的第 $34 - 18 = 16$ 种元素位于第 16 族。第 13 至 18 族的价电子数等于族数减 10：$16 - 10 = 6$。

(a) Family.(a) 同族元素。 The element is selenium (Se). It resembles sulfur ($Z = 16$) and oxygen ($Z = 8$), which are also in Group 16 with 6 valence electrons.该元素是硒（Se）。它与同在第 16 族、同样有 6 个价电子的硫（$Z = 16$）和氧（$Z = 8$）相似。

(b) Order by $Z$.(b) 按 $Z$ 排序。 Argon has $Z = 18$ and potassium has $Z = 19$, so argon comes first. Argon is heavier because it is mostly argon-40, while potassium is mostly potassium-39.氩的 $Z = 18$，钾的 $Z = 19$，所以氩在前。氩较重，是因为它主要是氩-40，而钾主要是钾-39。

Why this works.原理。 Chemical behaviour follows the valence-electron count, and that count follows $Z$, not mass. Ordering by mass would put potassium (a reactive Group 1 metal) in the noble-gas column.化学性质取决于价电子数，而价电子数取决于 $Z$，不取决于质量。若按质量排序，就会把活泼的第 1 族金属钾放进稀有气体那一列。

Going deeper: why the table has the shape it does: period lengths and electron shells深入：为何周期表是现在的形状：周期长度与电子壳层

Period 1 has 2 elements (filling the 1s subshell: 2 electrons). Periods 2 and 3 have 8 elements each (filling s + p subshells: 2 + 6 = 8). Periods 4 and 5 have 18 elements each (s + d + p: 2 + 10 + 6 = 18). Periods 6 and 7 have 32 elements (s + f + d + p: 2 + 14 + 10 + 6 = 32). Shell $n$ can hold at most $2n^2$ electrons (the same capacity formula as in the quantum-mechanical model), but period lengths follow the order in which subshells fill, not shell capacity alone. SCH4U C3.3 explicitly links element position to electron configuration and block assignment, making the shape of the table a direct consequence of quantum mechanics.第 1 周期有 2 种元素（填充 1s 亚壳层：2 个电子）。第 2、3 周期各有 8 种元素（填充 s + p 亚壳层：2 + 6 = 8）。第 4、5 周期各有 18 种元素（s + d + p：2 + 10 + 6 = 18）。第 6、7 周期有 32 种元素（s + f + d + p：2 + 14 + 10 + 6 = 32）。第 $n$ 壳层最多容纳 $2n^2$ 个电子（与量子力学模型中的容量公式相同），但周期的长度取决于亚壳层的填充顺序，而不只是壳层容量。SCH4U C3.3 明确将元素位置与电子排布和区块分配联系起来，使周期表的形状成为量子力学的直接结果。

Exam tip考试提示

Command words: state wants one fact (the periodic law, a group number); explain wants a reason tied to a cause; predict wants a value or property plus the neighbours it came from. Common errors: saying Mendeleev ordered the elements by atomic number (he used atomic mass), explaining the Ar and K order with "potassium is heavier" (it is lighter), and reading the period from the group number (the period is the row, which equals the number of occupied shells).

指令词：state（陈述）要求给出一个事实（周期律、族数）；explain（解释）要求把原因与成因联系起来；predict（预测）要求给出数值或性质，并说明依据哪些相邻元素。常见错误：说门捷列夫按原子序数排序（他用的是原子质量）；用“钾更重”解释 Ar 与 K 的顺序（钾其实更轻）；由族数读周期（周期是行，等于已占据的电子壳层数）。

1. Tellurium (Te, $127.6\ \mathrm{u}$) is placed before iodine (I, $126.9\ \mathrm{u}$). Explain why.1. 碲（Te，$127.6\ \mathrm{u}$）排在碘（I，$126.9\ \mathrm{u}$）之前。解释原因。

Tellurium has $Z = 52$ and iodine has $Z = 53$. The modern table is ordered by atomic number, so tellurium comes first even though it is heavier. This also puts iodine (7 valence electrons) in the halogen column, where it belongs.

碲的 $Z = 52$，碘的 $Z = 53$。现代周期表按原子序数排序，所以碲虽然较重，仍排在前面。这样碘（7 个价电子）也落在卤素那一列，位置正确。

2. An element is in Period 3, Group 16. Identify it, give its atomic number and state its valence-electron count.2. 某元素位于第 3 周期、第 16 族。确定该元素，给出其原子序数并说明价电子数。

Period 3 starts after neon ($Z = 10$) and contains Na, Mg, Al, Si, P, S, Cl, Ar. Group 16 is the sixth of these, so $Z = 10 + 6 = 16$: sulfur. Valence electrons: $16 - 10 = 6$.

第 3 周期紧接在氖（$Z = 10$）之后，依次为 Na、Mg、Al、Si、P、S、Cl、Ar。第 16 族是其中的第六个，所以 $Z = 10 + 6 = 16$：硫。价电子数：$16 - 10 = 6$。

3. Mendeleev predicted an atomic mass of $72\ \mathrm{u}$ for eka-silicon. Germanium has $72.6\ \mathrm{u}$. Find the percentage difference relative to the measured value and compare it with the $3.4\%$ density error.3. 门捷列夫预测类硅的原子质量为 $72\ \mathrm{u}$，锗的实测值为 $72.6\ \mathrm{u}$。求相对于实测值的百分差，并与密度的 $3.4\%$ 误差比较。

Worked Example 3: Configuration of a main-group element from its position例题 3：由位置写出主族元素的电子排布

An element is in Period 3, Group 16. Identify its block, write its condensed electron configuration and state its number of valence electrons.某元素位于第 3 周期、第 16 族。确定它所属的区块，写出简化电子排布，并说明价电子数。

Block.区块。 Groups 13 to 18 form the p-block, so the last electron enters a $3p$ subshell.第 13 至 18 族属于 p 区，所以最后一个电子进入 $3p$ 亚壳层。

Core.内层。 The noble gas before Period 3 is neon, so the core is $[\mathrm{Ne}]$ and the outer shell is $n = 3$.第 3 周期之前的稀有气体是氖，所以内层为 $[\mathrm{Ne}]$，最外层为 $n = 3$。

Outer electrons.外层电子。 Every p-block atom has $3s^{2}$. The number of $p$ electrons is the group number minus 12: $16 - 12 = 4$. The configuration is $[\mathrm{Ne}]\,3s^{2}\,3p^{4}$ (sulfur).每个 p 区原子都有 $3s^{2}$。$p$ 电子数等于族数减 12：$16 - 12 = 4$。电子排布为 $[\mathrm{Ne}]\,3s^{2}\,3p^{4}$（硫）。

Two competing forces determine size: nuclear charge pulling in, additional shells pushing out.两种竞争力决定大小：核电荷向内拉，额外壳层向外推。
  • Effective nuclear charge $Z_\text{eff}$有效核电荷 $Z_\text{eff}$ = net nuclear charge felt by a valence electron after core electrons shield part of the attraction: $Z_\text{eff} \approx Z - S$ (S = shielding constant). Valence electrons in the same shell shield each other poorly; inner shells shield effectively.= 价电子在芯电子屏蔽掉部分吸引力后感受到的净核电荷：$Z_\text{eff} \approx Z - S$（S = 屏蔽常数）。同一壳层的价电子相互屏蔽效果差；内层壳层屏蔽效果好。
  • Across a period (left to right)在同一周期内（从左到右） : same shell, proton count rises, so $Z_\text{eff}$ increases. The valence electrons are pulled inward. Atomic radius decreases.：相同壳层，质子数增加，因此 $Z_\text{eff}$ 升高。价电子被向内拉。原子半径减小。
  • Down a group在同一族向下 : a new shell is added, dramatically increasing atomic radius despite rising $Z$. Radius increases.：增加一个新壳层，尽管 $Z$ 升高，原子半径也急剧增大。半径增大。
  • Ionic radius离子半径 : cations (lost electrons) are smaller than the parent atom: fewer electrons, same $Z$, so $Z_\text{eff}$ per electron rises. Anions (gained electrons) are larger: more electrons, same $Z$, so repulsion expands the cloud. Isoelectronic series (same electron count): radius decreases as $Z$ increases (higher $Z_\text{eff}$ on the same electron cloud).：阳离子（失去电子）比母体原子小：电子更少，$Z$ 相同，故每个电子的 $Z_\text{eff}$ 升高。阴离子（获得电子）更大：电子更多，$Z$ 相同，故排斥膨胀了电子云。等电子体系列（相同电子数）：随 $Z$ 增大，半径减小（相同电子云上 $Z_\text{eff}$ 更高）。

Common Questions常见问题

Why does atomic radius decrease across a period?为什么原子半径在同一周期内会减小？

Moving left to right across a period, protons are added to the same energy level, so the increasing nuclear charge pulls the electrons in more tightly and the atomic radius shrinks.在同一周期从左向右，质子被添加到同一能级，核电荷增大将电子拉得更紧，因此原子半径缩小。

What is the trend in ionization energy on the periodic table?电离能在周期表上的变化趋势是什么？

Ionization energy increases across a period and decreases down a group, because electrons held closer to a more positive nucleus are harder to remove.电离能沿周期从左向右增大，沿族从上到下减小，因为离正电核更近、束缚更紧的电子更难被移走。

Which elements are the most reactive metals and nonmetals?哪些元素是最活泼的金属和非金属？

Group 1 alkali metals are the most reactive metals and Group 17 halogens are the most reactive nonmetals, while Group 18 noble gases are nearly inert due to full valence shells.第 1 族碱金属是最活泼的金属，第 17 族卤素是最活泼的非金属，而第 18 族稀有气体因价层全满而几乎不活泼。
