Topic 5.1

Scatterplots and association

Must know. A scatterplot displays paired values of two quantitative variables, one point for each observational unit. Describe form, direction, strength and unusual features in context.

Definition 5.1: paired quantitative data

An observational unit supplies both measurements. A cyclist’s weekly training hours and endurance minutes form one pair $(x,y)$. Do not independently sort the columns: doing so changes which values belong to the same cyclist and can manufacture an association. An ID number is a label, even if digits are used.

The explanatory variable goes on the horizontal axis when it is used to predict the response on the vertical axis. These roles are chosen by the investigative question. A scatterplot can describe a relationship even when there is no natural predictor, but a prediction model must identify its response.

Describing an association

  • Form: a straight trend, a curved trend, or no clear pattern. Calling a relation linear means that its approximate rate of change is constant.
  • Direction: larger x values tend to accompany larger y values (positive), or smaller y values (negative). A U-shaped relationship need not have a single direction.
  • Strength: how tightly points follow the stated form. A tight curve is a strong association even if its linear correlation is small.
  • Unusual features: separate clusters, gaps, or individual points that depart from the general pattern. Name what is unusual before deciding what to do about it.

Going deeper: association, aggregation and design

A combined scatterplot can hide distinct groups. Suppose two schools use different score scales and also have different average study times. A trend between school means need not match the trend within either school. Inspect group membership when context suggests it; do not infer an individual causal effect from aggregate points.

A random sample can support generalizing an association to its sampled population. Random assignment is needed to isolate a treatment effect in a well-designed experiment. Neither a tight scatterplot nor a large sample creates random assignment. Describe the observed relationship before addressing its scope.

主题 5.1

散点图与关联

必备知识。散点图展示两个定量变量的配对值，每个观测单位对应一个点。结合情境描述形态、方向、强度与异常特征。

定义 5.1：配对定量数据

同一观测单位提供两个测量值。例如，一名骑行者的每周训练小时数与耐力分钟数构成一对 $(x,y)$。不能分别对两列排序，否则会改变哪些数值属于同一名骑行者，并可能人为制造关联。编号即使用数字表示，也只是标签。

当解释变量用于预测响应变量时，解释变量放在横轴，响应变量放在纵轴。变量角色取决于研究问题。没有自然预测变量时，散点图仍能描述关系，但预测模型必须明确响应变量。

描述关联

  • 形态：直线趋势、曲线趋势，或无明显模式。近似恒定的变化率对应线性关系。
  • 方向：较大的 x 往往伴随较大的 y（正向），或较小的 y（负向）。U 形关系未必有单一方向。
  • 强度：点贴近所述形态的程度。紧密的曲线即使线性相关较小，也可表示强关联。
  • 异常特征：分离的聚类、空隙，或偏离一般模式的个别点。应先说明异常在哪里，再决定如何处理。

深入探究：关联、汇总与设计

合并散点图可能掩盖不同群体。假设两所学校使用不同分数尺度，且平均学习时间也不同，校均值之间的趋势未必与各校内部趋势一致。情境提示存在分组时应检查群体归属，不能由汇总点推断个体因果效应。

Topic 5.2

Correlation and its limitations

Must know. $-1\le r\le1$. The sign gives linear direction; $|r|$ describes linear strength. Correlation is unit-free, symmetric in x and y, and sensitive to unusual points. Correlation alone does not establish causation.

Definition 5.2: a linear summary

Correlation summarizes how two quantitative variables vary together in a straight pattern. It is defined only when both have positive variation. Values near 1 or −1 indicate strong positive or negative linear association; r = 0 indicates no linear association, not necessarily no relationship.

Technology computes r in this course. To understand why units cancel, let $z_{xi}=(x_i-\bar x)/s_x$ and $z_{yi}=(y_i-\bar y)/s_y$. The identity $r=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum z_{xi}z_{yi}$ expresses r as a standardized cross-product. This identity explains behavior under changes of scale; memorizing it is not a substitute for interpreting technology output.

Worked Example 5.2a: reading the sign and size

For age of a machine and its resale value, r = −0.88 indicates strong negative linear association: older machines tend to have lower resale values. It does not say that every extra year reduces value by 0.88 currency units. The slope, which has response units per explanatory unit, answers a different question.

Compare r = −0.88 and r = 0.60. The first has stronger linear association because its absolute magnitude is greater, despite being numerically smaller.

Worked Example 5.2b: the zero-correlation trap

For the five pairs from Example 5.1b, $\bar x=0$ and $\bar y=2$. Centered cross-products are −4, 1, 0, −1, 4, which sum to zero, so r = 0. Nevertheless, every point follows the quadratic rule exactly. Looking at the scatterplot prevents an incorrect “no relationship” conclusion.

Changes of units and variable roles

  • Adding a constant to either variable leaves r unchanged.
  • Multiplying a variable by a positive constant leaves r unchanged.
  • Multiplying exactly one variable by a negative constant reverses the sign but not the magnitude of r.
  • Interchanging x and y leaves r unchanged. It generally changes the prediction line because the response has changed.
Going deeper: sensitivity and causation

A remote point following the trend may increase the magnitude of r; a point departing from the trend may decrease it. There is no universal direction of change on deleting an unusual point. Recompute and compare the actual data.

Ice cream sales and sunburn counts can rise together on sunny hot days. Weather affects both demand for ice cream and exposure to ultraviolet radiation. This concrete common-cause pathway explains why the observed correlation does not isolate an effect of ice cream. A random observational sample would not change that causal limitation.

Even r near 1 can coexist with systematic curvature. Correlation reduces a plot to one number; inspect the original scatterplot and residuals before choosing a line.

主题 5.2

相关性及其局限

必备知识。$-1\le r\le1$。符号给出线性方向，$|r|$ 描述线性强度。相关系数无单位，对交换 x 与 y 对称，并对异常点敏感。仅凭相关不能确立因果。

定义 5.2：线性摘要

相关系数概括两个定量变量如何沿直线模式共同变化，仅在两者均有正变异时定义。接近 1 或 −1 表示强正向或负向线性关联；r = 0 表示无线性关联，并不一定表示没有关系。

本课程用技术工具计算 r。为理解单位如何抵消，令 $z_{xi}=(x_i-\bar x)/s_x$、$z_{yi}=(y_i-\bar y)/s_y$。恒等式 $r=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum z_{xi}z_{yi}$ 将 r 表示为标准化交叉乘积。此式解释尺度变换的性质，但背诵它不能代替解释技术输出。

例 5.2a：读取符号与大小

机器年龄与转售价的 r = −0.88 表示强负线性关联：较旧机器往往转售价较低。它不是说每多一年价格就降低 0.88 货币单位。斜率具有“响应单位/解释变量单位”，回答的是不同问题。

比较 r = −0.88 与 r = 0.60，前者虽然数值更小，但绝对值更大，因此线性关联更强。

例 5.2b：零相关陷阱

例 5.1b 的五对数据有 $\bar x=0$、$\bar y=2$。中心化交叉乘积为 −4、1、0、−1、4，和为零，因此 r = 0。然而每个点都精确遵循二次规律。观察散点图可避免错误的“没有关系”结论。

单位与变量角色变换

  • 任一变量加常数，r 不变。
  • 任一变量乘正常数，r 不变。
  • 恰有一个变量乘负常数，r 符号反转、绝对值不变。
  • 交换 x 与 y，r 不变；但响应变量已变，预测直线通常改变。
深入探究：敏感性与因果

沿趋势的远端点可能增大 r 的绝对值，偏离趋势的点可能减小它。删除异常点时没有普遍的变化方向，应根据实际数据重新计算与比较。

炎热晴朗天气可能同时增加冰淇淋销量与晒伤人数。天气既影响冰淇淋需求，也影响紫外线暴露。这条具体的共同原因路径说明观测相关无法隔离冰淇淋的作用。随机观察样本也不能改变该因果局限。

即使 r 接近 1，也可能存在系统曲率。相关系数把图压缩为一个数，选择直线前必须检查原散点图与残差。

检查理解

1. 树龄与年生长量之间的相关系数为 $r=-0.84$。哪种解释有依据？

答案：B 负号给出方向，绝对值 0.84 表示样本中较强的线性关联。相关系数无单位，也不是斜率。

  • A. 把相关系数误当成精确拟合的点所占比例。
  • B. 正确：结合情境，且限定为线性关联。
  • C. 给无单位系数加上响应变量单位。
  • D. 强度取决于绝对值而非符号。
2. 设 $c>0$ 且 $c\ne1$，保持 x 不变，将所有响应替换为 $y^*=cy+10$。原来两个变量均有变异，且 $r\ne0$。新相关系数与 r 相比如何？

答案：C 正比例缩放将中心化响应及其标准差同时乘以 c，故标准化响应不变。加 10 同时平移均值。因此 r 不变。

  • A. 把无单位的 r 当成响应单位的量。
  • B. 把位置平移误当成强度变化。
  • C. 正确：正仿射变换保留 r。
  • D. 负比例缩放才会翻转符号，此处 c 为正。
Topic 5.3

Linear regression models and prediction

Must know. $\hat y=a+bx$ predicts response y from explanatory x. a is the predicted response at x = 0; b is the predicted change in y per unit increase in x. Check the observed x range before predicting.

Definition 5.3: predicted versus observed

A hat marks a prediction, not an observation. The equation approximates a straight mean trend; individual responses need not equal the fitted values. In the CED convention, a is the intercept and b is the slope. Some technology uses the form ax + b and reverses the letter roles: read the displayed form rather than assuming which coefficient is slope.

Worked Example 5.3a: interpolation

For the cyclist data, technology gives $\hat y=2.2+0.9428571429x$. At x = 4 hours/week, the prediction is $2.2+0.9428571429(4)\approx5.97143$ minutes. The actual cyclist recorded 6 minutes. These are different quantities.

Each additional weekly training hour is associated with an increase of about 0.943 minute in predicted endurance. The intercept predicts 2.2 minutes at zero weekly training hours, but x = 0 is outside the observed 1 to 6 hour range. The numerical intercept is needed to locate the line even if its contextual interpretation is not supported by these data.

Interpolation and extrapolation

Interpolation uses an x inside the observed range. Extrapolation uses an x outside it. A formula can produce a value in either case, but the data support only the observed domain. Reliability usually decreases farther beyond that domain. Being in range does not guarantee accuracy if the model form is wrong or the new case differs from the sampled setting.

Worked Example 5.3b: a physically implausible prediction

A model for resale value in hundreds of dollars is $\hat y=120-3x$ for cars aged 1 to 8 years. Predicting at age 5 gives 105 hundreds of dollars, an interpolation. Predicting at age 50 gives −30, an extreme extrapolation with no sensible resale interpretation. This does not by itself show that the model is poor for ages 1 to 8.

Going deeper: reversing a prediction is not refitting

Solving $\hat y=a+bx$ for x at a specified predicted response is legitimate algebra when b is nonzero. However, fitting a regression that predicts x from observed y minimizes errors in x, while the original fit minimizes errors in y. These are different objectives and generally produce different lines.

For example, a y-on-x slope is $r s_y/s_x$, whereas an x-on-y slope is $r s_x/s_y$. The inverse of the first slope is generally not the second unless the data have perfect linear correlation. The equations are supplied here to explain the distinction; the required course calculation uses technology.

A prediction model contains no intervention by itself. “An additional hour is associated with a predicted increase” describes a fitted trend. “Assigning an additional hour causes that increase” requires experimental support and an appropriate target population.

主题 5.3

线性回归模型与预测

必备知识。$\hat y=a+bx$ 用解释变量 x 预测响应 y。a 为 x = 0 时的预测响应；b 为 x 每增加一单位时预测 y 的变化。预测前检查观测 x 范围。

定义 5.3：预测与实际观测

帽子符号表示预测而非观测。方程近似直线平均趋势，个体响应不必等于拟合值。CED 的约定中 a 是截距，b 是斜率。一些技术工具采用 ax + b，字母角色正好相反；应读取显示的形式，而非假定哪一个系数是斜率。

例 5.3a：内插

骑行者数据的技术输出为 $\hat y=2.2+0.9428571429x$。x = 4 小时/周时，预测值为 $2.2+0.9428571429(4)\approx5.97143$ 分钟。该骑行者的实际观测为 6 分钟，两者是不同的量。

每周训练每多一小时，预测耐力增加约 0.943 分钟。截距预测每周零训练小时者的耐力为 2.2 分钟，但 x = 0 超出观测的 1 至 6 小时范围。即使情境解释不受数据支持，数值截距仍是确定直线位置所必需的。

内插与外推

内插使用观测范围内的 x；外推使用范围外的 x。公式在两种情况下都能产生数值，但数据只支持观测域。离观测域越远通常越不可靠。若模型形态错误或新案例不同于抽样情境，范围内也不保证准确。

例 5.3b：缺乏物理意义的预测

对车龄 1 至 8 年的汽车，售价（百元）模型为 $\hat y=120-3x$。车龄 5 年时预测为 105 百元，属于内插；50 年时为 −30，属于缺乏合理售价解释的极端外推。这本身不能证明模型在 1 至 8 年范围内表现差。

深入探究：逆向求解不等于重新拟合

b 非零时，根据指定预测响应从 $\hat y=a+bx$ 解出 x 是合法代数。但以实际 y 预测 x 的回归最小化 x 方向误差，原拟合最小化 y 方向误差。两种目标不同，通常得到不同直线。

例如，y 关于 x 的斜率为 $r s_y/s_x$，x 关于 y 的斜率为 $r s_x/s_y$。前者的倒数通常不等于后者，除非数据完全线性相关。此处提供方程用于解释区别，课程要求的计算由技术工具完成。

预测模型本身不含干预。“多一小时对应预测增加”描述拟合趋势；“安排多一小时造成该增加”则需要实验支持与恰当的目标总体。

Topic 5.4

Residuals and model adequacy

Must know. $e=y-\hat y$. A positive residual means underprediction; a negative residual means overprediction. Residuals have the response units. Plot residuals against x or fitted values and look for structure.

Definition 5.4: vertical prediction error

A residual is the signed vertical distance from an observed point to its fitted line. It is not the shortest perpendicular distance. Observed response can be recovered as $y=\hat y+e$. A zero residual means that one prediction matches one observation, not that the whole model is perfect.

Topic 5.5

Least-squares regression and influence

Must know. Least squares chooses a and b to minimize $\sum(y_i-a-bx_i)^2$. The fitted line passes through $(\bar x,\bar y)$. $r^2$ is the proportion of response variation explained by the fitted linear relationship, not the fraction of points on the line.

Definition 5.5: the least-squares criterion

Technology computes the regression coefficients and correlation. The objective squares each vertical residual so that opposite signs cannot cancel. With varying x and an intercept, the least-squares line is unique. Minimizing signed residuals would not identify a useful best fit.

Worked Example 5.5a: reproduce technology

For the six cyclist pairs, $\bar x=3.5$, $\bar y=5.5$, $S_{xx}=\sum(x-\bar x)^2=17.5$, $S_{yy}=17.5$, and $S_{xy}=\sum(x-\bar x)(y-\bar y)=16.5$. The supplied checking identities give

$b=S_{xy}/S_{xx}=33/35\approx0.9428571429$, $a=\bar y-b\bar x=2.2$, and $r=S_{xy}/\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}=33/35$.

Therefore $r^2=1089/1225\approx0.8889795918$. About 88.90% of the variation in endurance among these six cyclists is explained by its fitted linear relationship with training hours. This does not say that 88.90% of the cyclists lie on the line, nor that training causes 88.90% of endurance.

The residual squared sum is $68/35\approx1.942857143$ square minutes. The total squared deviation from mean endurance is 17.5, and $1-(68/35)/17.5\approx0.8889795918$ agrees with r squared. Passing through (3.5,5.5) is a separate check: $2.2+(33/35)(3.5)=5.5$.

Worked Example 5.5b: equal signed sums, different squared errors

Residual sets (−3,0,3) and (−1,0,1) both sum to zero, but their squared sums are 18 and 2. The second has smaller total squared error. A signed zero sum hides the difference.

Unusual points: three separate questions

  • Residual outlier: is y unusually far vertically from its fitted value?
  • High leverage: is x unusually far from the center of the x values?
  • Influence: does removing the observation substantially change the fitted model or relevant predictions?

A high-leverage point has the potential for influence but need not change a line much if it follows the existing trend. A large residual at an ordinary x need not rotate the slope much. Compare fits with and without a point to assess actual influence; investigate errors and context before deciding whether exclusion is defensible.

Going deeper: why the mean point lies on the line

For a fixed slope b, write $a=\bar y-b\bar x+c$. Then the residual at observation i is $(y_i-\bar y)-b(x_i-\bar x)-c$. The first two centered terms sum to zero. Expanding the squared sum leaves a constant in c plus $nc^2$, which is minimized at c = 0. Hence $a=\bar y-b\bar x$ and the line passes through the mean point. This also explains the zero signed residual sum.

After centering, minimizing over b gives $b=S_{xy}/S_{xx}$. These identities provide a reproducible oracle, but course questions normally supply technology output and ask for contextual interpretation. Neither identity diagnoses whether a line is the right form.

主题 5.5

最小二乘回归与影响力

必备知识。最小二乘选择 a 与 b，使 $\sum(y_i-a-bx_i)^2$ 最小。拟合线通过 $(\bar x,\bar y)$。$r^2$ 表示响应变异中由拟合线性关系解释的比例，不是线上点所占比例。

定义 5.5：最小二乘准则

技术工具计算回归系数与相关系数。目标将每个竖直残差平方，使正负不能抵消。在 x 有变异且含截距时，最小二乘直线唯一。最小化带符号残差不能确定有用的最佳拟合。

例 5.5a：复核技术输出

六组骑行者数据的 $\bar x=3.5$、$\bar y=5.5$、$S_{xx}=\sum(x-\bar x)^2=17.5$、$S_{yy}=17.5$、$S_{xy}=\sum(x-\bar x)(y-\bar y)=16.5$。用于核验的恒等式给出

$b=S_{xy}/S_{xx}=33/35\approx0.9428571429$、$a=\bar y-b\bar x=2.2$、$r=S_{xy}/\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}=33/35$。

因此 $r^2=1089/1225\approx0.8889795918$。六名骑行者耐力变异的约 88.90% 可由其与训练小时的拟合线性关系解释。这不是说 88.90% 的骑行者在线上，也不是说训练造成耐力的 88.90%。

残差平方和为 $68/35\approx1.942857143$ 平方分钟。相对于平均耐力的总平方离差为 17.5，$1-(68/35)/17.5\approx0.8889795918$ 与 r 的平方一致。通过 (3.5,5.5) 是另一项核验：$2.2+(33/35)(3.5)=5.5$。

例 5.5b：带符号和相同，平方误差不同

残差组 (−3,0,3) 与 (−1,0,1) 的和均为零，但平方和分别为 18 与 2。第二组总平方误差较小，带符号零和掩盖了这个差异。

异常点：三个不同问题

  • 残差离群值：y 是否在竖直方向异常远离拟合值？
  • 高杠杆：x 是否异常远离 x 值中心？
  • 影响力：删除该观测是否会明显改变拟合模型或相关预测？

高杠杆点有潜在影响，但若沿原有趋势，可能不会使直线明显变化。普通 x 处的大残差也未必使斜率明显旋转。比较保留与删除时的拟合以评估实际影响；决定是否合理排除前，应调查错误与情境。

深入探究：为何直线通过均值点

固定斜率 b，令 $a=\bar y-b\bar x+c$。第 i 个残差为 $(y_i-\bar y)-b(x_i-\bar x)-c$。前两个中心化项的和为零，展开平方和得到与 c 无关的常数加 $nc^2$，在 c = 0 时最小。因此 $a=\bar y-b\bar x$，直线通过均值点。这也解释了带符号残差和为零。

中心化后对 b 最小化得到 $b=S_{xy}/S_{xx}$。这些恒等式提供可复现的数值核验，但课程题目通常提供技术输出并要求情境解释。两个恒等式都不能诊断直线形态是否正确。

A fitted relationship for nonconstant x and y is $\hat y=a+bx$, with nonzero b. Define $u=kx+h$ and $v=my+d$, where $k<0$ and $m>0$. All constants are known. A colleague claims that both the slope and r are unchanged because “units never matter.”

(a) [3]

Substitute $x=(u-h)/k$ into $\hat v=m(a+bx)+d$. Thus $\hat v=(ma+d-mbh/k)+(mb/k)u$. The transformed slope is mb/k and intercept is ma+d−mbh/k. 1 point for inverse substitution; 1 for slope; 1 for intercept.

(b) [3]

Negative scaling of x and positive scaling of y reverse the sign of r and preserve its magnitude, so new r = −r and new r-squared = r-squared. The slope changes to mb/k, of opposite sign for nonzero b. The claim is false. 1 point for r; 1 for squared r; 1 for slope/claim.

(c) [2]

The transformed line is v-hat = 49 − 3u, giving 43 at u = 2. Original x = (2 − 6)/(−2) = 2, y-hat = 14, and 3(14) + 1 = 43. 1 point for transformed prediction; 1 for independent substitution check.

(d) [2]

The observed domain is transformed along with x; a point outside it remains outside the transformed domain. Units do not change sampling or assignment, so confounding and causal limitations persist. 1 point for domain argument; 1 for design argument.

Unit quiz

1. A model for plant height is $\hat y=12+3x$, with x in weeks and height in cm. What is predicted at $x=4$?

Answer: B Substitute x = 4: predicted height is 12 + 3(4) = 24 cm. This is a model prediction, not a guarantee for one plant.

  • A. Adds the slope once, ignoring x.
  • B. Correct: multiplies slope by time, then adds intercept.
  • C. Multiplies the intercept by x.
  • D. Reports the rate rather than the predicted response.
2. For trees, $r=-0.84$ between age and yearly growth. Which interpretation is justified?

Answer: B The negative sign gives direction and magnitude 0.84 indicates a strong linear association in this sample. Correlation has no units and is not a slope.

  • A. Confuses a correlation with a percentage of exact fits.
  • B. Correct: contextual and restricted to linear association.
  • C. Assigns response units to a unit-free coefficient.
  • D. Strength depends on absolute magnitude, not sign.
3. For paired observations, $\bar x=4$, $\bar y=18$, $s_x=2$, $s_y=6$, and $r=0.5$. Using $b=r s_y/s_x$, find the LSRL $\hat y=a+bx$.

Answer: A Slope b = 0.5(6/2) = 1.5; intercept a = 18 − 1.5(4) = 12. The line passes through (4,18). The formula is supplied as an algebraic check; technology normally computes these coefficients.

  • A. Correct: computes slope then centers the line.
  • B. Treats the mean response as the intercept.
  • C. Uses r as the slope.
  • D. Omits r in calculating the slope.
4. Let $c>0$ with $c\ne1$, and replace every response by $y^*=cy+10$, leaving x unchanged. Both variables originally vary and $r\ne0$. Compare the new correlation with r.

Answer: C A positive rescaling multiplies the centered response and its SD by c, so standardized response values are unchanged. Adding 10 shifts the mean equally. Therefore r is unchanged.

  • A. Treats unit-free r as a response-unit quantity.
  • B. Mistakes a location shift for a strength change.
  • C. Correct: positive affine changes preserve r.
  • D. A negative scaling would reverse the sign, but c is positive.
5. A student says that the linear model is appropriate because residuals sum to zero. Inspect the schematic residual plot.

Which correction is best?

Answer: A Zero residual sum is an algebraic property of an LSRL with an intercept, not a fit diagnostic. The U-shaped pattern indicates systematic curvature, so a line is inadequate.

  • A. Correct: uses the plotted pattern.
  • B. Points above the line have positive residuals.
  • C. Signed residuals cancel even with large errors.
  • D. A diagnostic of form cannot establish cause.
6. A linear model predicting exam score from weekly practice time has $r^2=0.64$. Which is correct?

Answer: B The coefficient of determination describes explained variation in the response, relative to predicting every score by its mean. It is not a fraction of observations on a line or a causal share.

  • A. Confuses variation with a count of exact predictions.
  • B. Correct: identifies response variation and the linear relationship.
  • C. Turns a descriptive fit measure into causation.
  • D. A squared correlation has no slope units.
7. Across coastal towns, ice cream sales and reported sunburn cases have high positive correlation. A student concludes that reducing ice cream sales will prevent sunburn. Identify the best critique.

Answer: B Sunny weather is a plausible common cause: it affects outdoor UV exposure and demand for ice cream. An observational correlation cannot establish that intervening on sales changes sunburn. Random sampling could improve generalization but would not create random assignment.

  • A. No correlation threshold removes confounding.
  • B. Correct: connects the proposed confounder to both variables.
  • C. Larger n does not remove a design limitation.
  • D. Group-level association gives no individual guarantee.
8. For nonconstant paired x and y, let $x^*=kx$ with $k<0$ and leave y unchanged. I. The sign of r reverses when r is nonzero. II. The magnitude of r is unchanged. III. The new slope equals the old slope for every k. Which must hold?

Answer: C Negative scaling reverses standardized x values, reversing nonzero r while preserving magnitude. The slope changes from b to b/k, so III fails in general; b = 0 is an edge case where slopes remain equal.

  • A. Misses unchanged strength.
  • B. Includes the false general slope claim.
  • C. Correct: sign and strength behave differently from slope.
  • D. Ignores the units and sign change in slope.
9. A model is $\hat y=5+2x$. At $x=k$, an observation has $y=4k-1$ and residual 8. Find k and decide whether the model overpredicts or underpredicts this observation.

Answer: A Residual = (4k − 1) − (5 + 2k) = 2k − 6 = 8, so k = 7. Observed y = 27 and predicted y = 19. The positive residual means underprediction by 8 response units.

  • A. Correct: uses observed minus predicted and interprets the sign.
  • B. Mishandles the constant: solves 2k + 6 = 8.
  • C. Correct k, but reverses underprediction and overprediction.
  • D. Solves the reversed residual equation (predicted minus observed).
10. An LSRL with an intercept is fitted to nonconstant x and y. I. It passes through $(\bar x,\bar y)$. II. Its residuals sum to zero apart from numerical rounding. III. Its slope must equal r. Which statements must be true?

Answer: B With an intercept, least squares centers the fitted line on the mean point and makes the signed residual sum zero. In general b = r times sy/sx, so III holds only in special scaling cases (or at r = 0).

  • A. Misses the zero residual-sum property.
  • B. Correct: both intercept-based identities hold.
  • C. Drops the mean-point property and includes a false slope identity.
  • D. Mistakes a unit-free summary for a dimensional rate.

1. 植物高度模型为 $\hat y=12+3x$，x 的单位为周，高度单位为 cm。$x=4$ 时的预测值是多少？

答案：B 代入 x = 4：预测高度为 12 + 3(4) = 24 cm。这是模型预测，并非对某一株植物的保证。

  • A. 只加一次斜率，忽略 x。
  • B. 正确：用斜率乘时间，再加截距。
  • C. 用截距乘 x。
  • D. 报告了变化率而非预测响应值。
2. 树龄与年生长量之间的相关系数为 $r=-0.84$。哪种解释有依据？

答案：B 负号给出方向，绝对值 0.84 表示样本中较强的线性关联。相关系数无单位，也不是斜率。

  • A. 把相关系数误当成精确拟合的点所占比例。
  • B. 正确：结合情境，且限定为线性关联。
  • C. 给无单位系数加上响应变量单位。
  • D. 强度取决于绝对值而非符号。
3. 配对观测的 $\bar x=4$、$\bar y=18$、$s_x=2$、$s_y=6$、$r=0.5$。利用 $b=r s_y/s_x$ 求最小二乘回归线 $\hat y=a+bx$。

答案：A 斜率 b = 0.5(6/2) = 1.5；截距 a = 18 − 1.5(4) = 12。直线通过 (4,18)。题目提供该式作为代数核验，通常由技术工具计算系数。

  • A. 正确：先算斜率，再使直线经过均值点。
  • B. 把响应均值当成截距。
  • C. 把 r 当成斜率。
  • D. 计算斜率时遗漏 r。
4. 设 $c>0$ 且 $c\ne1$，保持 x 不变，将所有响应替换为 $y^*=cy+10$。原来两个变量均有变异，且 $r\ne0$。新相关系数与 r 相比如何？

答案：C 正比例缩放将中心化响应及其标准差同时乘以 c，故标准化响应不变。加 10 同时平移均值。因此 r 不变。

  • A. 把无单位的 r 当成响应单位的量。
  • B. 把位置平移误当成强度变化。
  • C. 正确：正仿射变换保留 r。
  • D. 负比例缩放才会翻转符号，此处 c 为正。

答案：B 决定系数描述响应变量中被解释的变异，相对于用均值预测所有分数而言。它不是线上观测所占比例，也不是因果份额。

  • A. 把变异误当成精确预测的个数。
  • B. 正确：明确响应变异与线性关系。
  • C. 把描述性拟合指标变成因果。
  • D. 相关系数的平方没有斜率单位。
7. 沿海城镇的冰淇淋销量与报告的晒伤人数高度正相关。学生据此认为减少冰淇淋销售能预防晒伤。哪项批评最恰当？

答案：B 晴朗天气是合理的共同原因：它影响户外紫外线暴露，也影响冰淇淋需求。观察性相关无法证明干预销量会改变晒伤。随机抽样可改善推广范围，却不会产生随机分配。

  • A. 任何相关阈值都不能消除混杂。
  • B. 正确：把提出的混杂因素与两个变量联系起来。
  • C. 更大的 n 不能消除设计局限。
  • D. 群体关联不能保证每个个体的结果。
8. 对均非恒定的配对变量 x 与 y，令 $x^*=kx$，其中 $k<0$，y 不变。I. 若 r 非零，其符号反转。II. r 的绝对值不变。III. 对任意 k，新斜率均等于原斜率。哪些必然成立？

答案：C 负比例缩放翻转标准化 x 值，故非零 r 的符号反转而绝对值不变。斜率由 b 变为 b/k，因此 III 一般不成立；b = 0 是斜率仍相等的特殊情况。

  • A. 遗漏强度不变。
  • B. 包含一般情况下错误的斜率结论。
  • C. 正确：符号与强度的变化不同于斜率。
  • D. 忽略斜率单位与符号变化。
9. 模型为 $\hat y=5+2x$。当 $x=k$ 时，某观测的 $y=4k-1$，残差为 8。求 k，并判断模型高估还是低估该观测。

答案：A 残差 = (4k − 1) − (5 + 2k) = 2k − 6 = 8，故 k = 7。实际 y = 27，预测 y = 19。正残差表示模型低估了 8 个响应单位。

  • A. 正确：用实际值减预测值，并解释符号。
  • B. 常数项处理错误：解了 2k + 6 = 8。
  • C. k 正确，却颠倒高估与低估。
  • D. 解了颠倒顺序的残差方程（预测值减实际值）。
10. 对均非恒定的 x 与 y 拟合含截距的最小二乘回归线。I. 它通过 $(\bar x,\bar y)$。II. 除数值舍入外，残差和为零。III. 斜率必等于 r。哪些必然正确？

答案：B 含截距最小二乘拟合使直线通过均值点，带符号残差和为零。一般 b = r 乘 sy/sx，故 III 只在特殊尺度下（或 r = 0 时）成立。

  • A. 遗漏残差和为零的性质。
  • B. 正确：两个依赖截距的恒等性质均成立。
  • C. 遗漏均值点性质，并加入错误的斜率恒等式。
  • D. 把无单位摘要误当成带单位变化率。
