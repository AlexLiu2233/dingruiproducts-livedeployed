Q 1第1题EASY基础1.11 Random sampling methods1.11 随机抽样方法CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A district randomly selects four of its 40 mixed-grade homerooms and surveys every student in each selected homeroom. Which sampling method is used?
学区从 40 个混合年级的班级中随机选出 4 个班，并调查被选班级中的每一名学生。采用的是哪种抽样方法？
Answer: C答案：C

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The homerooms are natural groups. Chance picks 4 of the 40 groups, and then every student in a chosen group is measured. Whole randomly chosen groups are measured completely, so the homerooms are clusters.班级是天然形成的群。由随机方式从 40 个群中选出 4 个，然后测量被选群中的每一名学生。随机选出的整个群被完整测量，所以班级是群（cluster）。

Why the options attract (M 7)选项误区（M 7）
  1. In an SRS of students any set of students could be the sample; here only sets made of four whole homerooms can occur.简单随机样本中任何一组学生都可能入选；这里只有由四个完整班级组成的学生组才可能出现。
  2. A stratified sample takes a separate random sample from every group; here 36 of the 40 homerooms contribute nobody.分层样本要从每一组中各抽一个随机样本；这里 40 个班中有 36 个班没有任何人入选。
  3. Correct: homerooms are the clusters.正确：班级是群。
  4. Systematic sampling needs an ordered list and a fixed interval $k$; nothing like that is used.系统抽样需要有序名单和固定间隔 $k$；这里没有用到。
Ask who is measured: a few randomly chosen groups measured completely is a cluster sample, while a random sample taken inside every group is a stratified sample.先问谁被测量：随机选出少数几个群并完整测量是整群抽样；在每一组内各抽一个随机样本则是分层抽样。

Q 2第2题EASY基础1.1, 1.2 Parameters, statistics and investigative questions1.1, 1.2 参数、统计量与探究问题CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A random sample of 400 of the 6,200 students at a university is selected, and 252 of the 400 say they own a bicycle. Which choice correctly identifies the parameter and the statistic?
从某大学 6,200 名学生中随机抽取 400 人，其中 252 人说自己有自行车。哪个选项正确指出了参数和统计量？
Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

A parameter describes the whole population of 6,200 students, so it is the unknown proportion $p$. A statistic is computed from the sample of 400: $\hat p=252/400=0.63$.参数（parameter）描述 6,200 名学生的全体，所以是未知的比例 $p$。统计量（statistic）由 400 人的样本算出：$\hat p=252/400=0.63$。

Why the options attract (M 1)选项误区（M 1）
  1. Correct: the sample value is the statistic and the population proportion is the parameter.正确：样本值是统计量，总体比例是参数。
  2. Swaps the two ideas: $0.63$ comes from a sample, so it is a statistic, and a parameter is never computed from a sample.把两者颠倒了：$0.63$ 来自样本，所以是统计量；参数不是由样本算出的。
  3. Divides the count by the population size, $252/6200\approx0.041$, instead of by the sample size $n=400$.用总体人数相除，$252/6200\approx0.041$，而不是用样本量 $n=400$ 相除。
  4. Sample size and a count are neither summaries of the population nor estimates of one; the variable here is owning a bicycle.样本量和计数既不是总体的概括，也不是总体的估计；这里的变量是是否有自行车。
Sample in hand, so statistic; the whole group you want to learn about, so parameter. Always write the parameter in words with its population.手里的是样本，就是统计量；想了解的全体对应参数。写参数时一定要用文字交代其总体。

Researchers ask 300 randomly selected adults how many hours per week they exercise and also measure each adult's resting heart rate. They then compare the heart rates of adults who exercise a lot with those who exercise little. What type of study is this?
研究者询问 300 名随机抽取的成年人每周锻炼多少小时，并测量每人的静息心率，然后比较锻炼多与锻炼少的成年人的心率。这是什么类型的研究？
Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The adults chose their own exercise habits; the researchers only recorded values. Imposing a treatment is what makes a study an experiment, so this is an observational study (a sample survey).成年人自己决定锻炼习惯，研究者只是记录数值。只有施加处理才算实验，所以这是观察研究（抽样调查，observational study）。

  2. Correct: the groups formed themselves.正确：各组是自行形成的。
  3. Random selection of subjects is not random assignment of treatments, and no treatment was assigned at all.随机选出受试者不是随机分配处理，而且这里根本没有分配任何处理。
  4. A census measures the whole population; 300 adults are a sample.普查测量整个总体；300 名成年人只是样本。

Q 6第6题MEDIUM中等1.12 Sampling bias1.12 抽样偏差CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A library convenience survey overestimates mean school study time. The investigator increases the sample from 50 to 500 library visitors and claims the estimate is now unbiased. What is the flaw?
一项在图书馆做的方便抽样调查高估了全校平均学习时间。调查者把样本从 50 名图书馆访客增加到 500 名，并宣称估计现在没有偏差。这个说法的错误在哪里？
Answer: D答案：D

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Bias comes from who is selected, not from how many. The same library visitors are still more likely to be frequent studiers, so the estimate still centers too high. A larger $n$ lowers sampling variability around that wrong center.偏差来自选了谁，而不是选了多少。图书馆访客仍然更可能是常学习的人，所以估计的中心仍然偏高。更大的 $n$ 只会减小围绕这个错误中心的抽样变异（sampling variability）。

Why the options attract (M 6)选项误区（M 6）
  1. No change in who is sampled gives no reason to expect the bias to reverse; it keeps its direction.被抽取的人群没变，没有理由认为偏差会反向；它的方向不变。
  2. The 10% condition is about independence when sampling without replacement; it says nothing about who is selected, so it cannot remove bias.10% 条件涉及不放回抽样时的独立性，与选了谁无关，因此不能消除偏差。
  3. No fraction of the students repairs a biased selection method: half of a library crowd is still a library crowd.无论抽取多大比例的学生，都不能修复有偏的选取方法：图书馆人群的一半仍然是图书馆人群。
  4. Correct: it separates systematic bias from variability.正确：它区分了系统偏差与变异。
Bigger samples cure variability, never bias. Fixing bias means changing the method: a complete frame, random selection, follow-up on nonresponse.增大样本只能减小变异，不能消除偏差。要消除偏差必须改进方法：完整的抽样框、随机选择、对无应答跟进。

某县卫生办公室向随机选出的 5,000 户家庭邮寄了一份很长的问卷。只有 800 户寄回，报告的平均每天蔬菜摄入量为 3.9 份。饮食较差的家庭不太愿意填完很长的问卷。哪项陈述最能解释这个 3.9？

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Where this goes wrong (M 12)常见错误（M 12）

(b) 3 points3 分

Blocks are fixed first: $50+40=90$. Half of each block gets each treatment: $25+25$ and $20+20$, so the groups are balanced inside each ward. Randomization is done separately in each block with labels and a random number generator that ignores repeats.先确定区组：$50+40=90$。每个区组内各一半接受每种处理：$25+25$ 与 $20+20$，使各病房内部的组别平衡。在每个区组内分别用编号和忽略重复的随机数生成器进行随机化。

(c) 2 points2 分

Blocking removes the ward-to-ward difference in anxiety from the comparison, making it more precise. The key contrast is selection versus assignment: strata are sampled from a population, blocks hold units already in the study that are then assigned to treatments.区组化把病房之间焦虑的差异从比较中去掉，使比较更精确。关键区别是选取与分配：层是从总体中抽样，区组则是已经在研究中的单位，再分配到各处理。

(d) 3 points3 分

Random assignment supports a cause-and-effect claim, stated as “convincing evidence” rather than “proof” and in context. Volunteers limit the scope to patients like them, so the result is not generalized to all surgery patients.随机分配支持因果结论，用“令人信服的证据”而非“证明”，并结合情境。志愿者限制了范围，只适用于与他们相似的患者，不能推广到所有手术患者。

Where this goes wrong (M 5)常见错误（M 5）

Describing blocking as random selection from each ward, or randomizing over all 90 patients and ignoring the blocks. Another slip is to generalize to all patients because the hospital is real, or to say the exercise “proves” lower anxiety.把区组化说成是从每个病房中随机选取，或者在 90 名患者中统一随机而忽略区组。另一种失误是因为医院是真实的就推广到所有患者，或者说呼吸练习“证明”焦虑更低。
