Gradient and Equations of a Straight Line斜率与直线方程 SL 2.1

Learning objectives学习目标

  • State the gradient-intercept form $y = mx + c$, the point-gradient form $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ and the general form $ax + by + d = 0$.
  • Find the equation of a line from a point and its gradient, or from two points.
  • Convert an equation between the three forms and read off the gradient and the intercepts.
  • Interpret the gradient and the $y$-intercept of a linear model in context.
  • 计算（calculate）过两点的直线的斜率（gradient），并把它解释为变化率。
  • 写出（state）斜截式 $y = mx + c$、点斜式 $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ 与一般式 $ax + by + d = 0$。
  • 由一点与斜率、或由两点求出（find）直线方程。
  • 在三种形式之间转换（convert），并读出斜率与截距。
  • 结合情境解释（interpret）线性模型的斜率与 $y$ 截距。

Gradient斜率

Definition. The gradient of the line through $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$, with $x_1 \neq x_2$, is $$ m \;=\; \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} \;=\; \frac{\text{change in } y}{\text{change in } x}. $$ Why it is one number for the whole line. Any two points on the same line form a right-angled "step" with the line as hypotenuse, and all these steps are similar triangles, so rise divided by run is the same wherever you measure it. That constant is the rate of change: $y$ increases by $m$ for every increase of $1$ in $x$.

Signs and special cases. $m > 0$: the line rises from left to right; $m < 0$: it falls; $m = 0$: horizontal line $y = k$. A vertical line $x = k$ has $x_2 - x_1 = 0$, so its gradient is undefined.
定义。过 $(x_1, y_1)$ 与 $(x_2, y_2)$（$x_1 \neq x_2$）的直线的斜率为 $$ m \;=\; \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} \;=\; \frac{\text{change in } y}{\text{change in } x}. $$ 为何整条直线只有一个斜率。同一直线上任取两点，都构成一个以直线为斜边的直角"台阶"；这些台阶彼此相似，所以无论在哪里量，"升高 ÷ 前进"都相同。这个常数就是变化率（rate of change）：$x$ 每增加 $1$，$y$ 增加 $m$。

符号与特殊情形。$m > 0$：直线从左到右上升；$m < 0$：下降；$m = 0$：水平线 $y = k$。竖直线 $x = k$ 的 $x_2 - x_1 = 0$，所以斜率无定义（undefined）。

Three forms of the equation直线方程的三种形式

Form形式Equation方程Best used when适用场合
Gradient-intercept斜截式（gradient-intercept）$y = mx + c$you need the gradient $m$ and the $y$-intercept $c$ at a glance, or want to graph the line需要一眼读出斜率 $m$ 与 $y$ 截距 $c$，或要画图时

The point-gradient form is just the gradient definition rearranged: a point $(x, y)$ is on the line through $(x_1, y_1)$ with gradient $m$ when $\dfrac{y - y_1}{x - x_1} = m$, and multiplying by $x - x_1$ gives $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ (which also includes the point $(x_1, y_1)$ itself). From the general form, $by = -ax - d$, so when $b \neq 0$ the gradient is $-\dfrac{a}{b}$ and the $y$-intercept is $-\dfrac{d}{b}$.

点斜式只是斜率定义的变形：点 $(x, y)$ 在过 $(x_1, y_1)$、斜率为 $m$ 的直线上，当 $\dfrac{y - y_1}{x - x_1} = m$；两边乘以 $x - x_1$ 得 $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$（该式也包含点 $(x_1, y_1)$ 本身）。由一般式得 $by = -ax - d$，所以 $b \neq 0$ 时斜率为 $-\dfrac{a}{b}$，$y$ 截距为 $-\dfrac{d}{b}$。

Find the equation of the line through $P(2, -1)$ and $Q(6, 7)$. Give it in gradient-intercept form and in the form $ax + by + d = 0$ with $a, b, d \in \mathbb{Z}$.求过 $P(2, -1)$ 与 $Q(6, 7)$ 的直线方程，分别写成斜截式与 $ax + by + d = 0$（$a, b, d \in \mathbb{Z}$）的形式。

Step 1. Gradient.

第 1 步：斜率。

$$ m \;=\; \frac{7 - (-1)}{6 - 2} \;=\; \frac{8}{4} \;=\; 2. $$

Step 2. Point-gradient form with $P$.

第 2 步：用 $P$ 写点斜式。

$$ y - (-1) \;=\; 2(x - 2) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y + 1 \;=\; 2x - 4 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y \;=\; 2x - 5. $$

Step 3. General form. Move every term to one side: $2x - y - 5 = 0$, so $a = 2$, $b = -1$, $d = -5$.

第 3 步：一般式。把各项移到一边：$2x - y - 5 = 0$，即 $a = 2$、$b = -1$、$d = -5$。

Check with $Q$. $2(6) - 5 = 7$, so $Q$ is on the line. With technology: entering the two points as lists and running linear regression on the GDC returns $a = 2$, $b = -5$ (two points fit a line exactly); this confirms the algebra but the working above is what shows the method.

用 $Q$ 检验。$2(6) - 5 = 7$，所以 $Q$ 在直线上。使用技术工具：把两点输入 GDC 列表并做线性回归，得 $a = 2$、$b = -5$（两点恰好确定一条直线）；这能确认代数结果，但上面的过程才体现方法。

Why this works. The gradient is the same between any two points of a line, so one point and $m$ pin the line down; using $Q$ instead of $P$ in Step 2 gives the same equation.

原理。直线上任意两点之间的斜率都相同，所以一点加上 $m$ 就确定了直线；第 2 步改用 $Q$ 代替 $P$ 也得到同一方程。

A phone plan charges a fixed monthly fee plus a constant amount per minute. A month with $60$ minutes costs $18$ dollars and a month with $140$ minutes costs $30$ dollars. (a) Find the cost $C$ dollars as a function of the time $t$ minutes. (b) Interpret the gradient and the $C$-intercept. (c) Write the model in the form $at + bC + d = 0$ with integer coefficients. (d) Predict the cost of $200$ minutes.某手机套餐每月收固定月费，另按分钟收固定单价。通话 $60$ 分钟的月份费用 $18$ 美元，通话 $140$ 分钟的月份费用 $30$ 美元。(a) 把费用 $C$（美元）写成时间 $t$（分钟）的函数。(b) 解释斜率与 $C$ 截距。(c) 把模型写成整数系数的 $at + bC + d = 0$。(d) 预测 $200$ 分钟的费用。

(a) 两个数据点为 $(60, 18)$ 与 $(140, 30)$。

$$ m \;=\; \frac{30 - 18}{140 - 60} \;=\; \frac{12}{80} \;=\; 0.15, \qquad C - 18 \;=\; 0.15(t - 60) \;\Longrightarrow\; C \;=\; 0.15t + 9. $$

(b) Gradient $0.15$: each extra minute costs $0.15$ dollars. Intercept $9$: the fixed monthly fee is $9$ dollars (the cost when $t = 0$).

(c) 把 $C = 0.15t + 9$ 乘以 $20$：$20C = 3t + 180$，所以 $3t - 20C + 180 = 0$。用 $(140, 30)$ 检验：$420 - 600 + 180 = 0$。

(d) $C = 0.15(200) + 9 = 39$ 美元。原理。"固定费用 + 固定单价"正是 $y = mx + c$ 的结构：单价是斜率，固定费用是截距。

Common Mistake: mixing the order in the gradient formula常见错误：斜率公式中顺序不一致 Writing $\dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_1 - x_2}$ flips the sign of the gradient. Subtract in the same order on top and bottom. Also note that $c$ in $y = mx + c$ is the $y$-intercept, but $d$ in $ax + by + d = 0$ is not: the $y$-intercept there is $-\dfrac{d}{b}$.写成 $\dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_1 - x_2}$ 会使斜率变号。分子分母要按同一顺序相减。另外，$y = mx + c$ 中的 $c$ 是 $y$ 截距，但 $ax + by + d = 0$ 中的 $d$ 不是：那里的 $y$ 截距是 $-\dfrac{d}{b}$。

Parallel and Perpendicular Lines; Midpoints and Distance平行线与垂直线；中点与距离 SL 2.1 SL 3.5

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State that parallel lines have equal gradients, $m_1 = m_2$.
  • State the perpendicular condition $m_1 \times m_2 = -1$ for two non-vertical lines, so each gradient is the negative reciprocal of the other.
  • Find the equation of the line through a given point parallel or perpendicular to a given line.
  • Calculate the midpoint of a line segment and the distance between two points.
  • Deduce whether a triangle has a right angle from the gradients of its sides.
  • 写出（state）平行线斜率相等：$m_1 = m_2$。
  • 写出（state）两条非竖直直线的垂直条件 $m_1 \times m_2 = -1$，即两斜率互为负倒数（negative reciprocal）。
  • 求出（find）过定点且与已知直线平行或垂直的直线方程。
  • 计算（calculate）线段的中点（midpoint）与两点间距离。
  • 由三角形各边的斜率推断（deduce）它是否有直角。
Parallel lines. Parallel lines rise by the same amount per unit across, so they have equal gradients: $m_1 = m_2$. (Two vertical lines are also parallel; their gradients are undefined.)

Perpendicular lines. Rotating a line through $90^{\circ}$ turns a step of "$1$ across, $m$ up" into a step of "$m$ back, $1$ up", whose gradient is $\dfrac{1}{-m} = -\dfrac{1}{m}$. So if two non-vertical lines are perpendicular, then $$ m_1 \times m_2 \;=\; -1, \qquad\text{that is}\qquad m_2 \;=\; -\frac{1}{m_1}, $$ and conversely, two lines whose gradients multiply to $-1$ are perpendicular. The exception: a horizontal line ($m = 0$) is perpendicular to a vertical line (no gradient), and no product can be formed.

Midpoint and distance. For $A(x_1, y_1)$ and $B(x_2, y_2)$: $$ M \;=\; \left( \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2},\; \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right), \qquad AB \;=\; \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^{2} + (y_2 - y_1)^{2}}. $$ The midpoint averages the coordinates; the distance is Pythagoras on the horizontal and vertical changes.
平行线。平行线每向右一个单位升高相同的量，所以斜率相等：$m_1 = m_2$。（两条竖直线也平行，它们的斜率无定义。）

垂直线。把直线旋转 $90^{\circ}$，"右移 $1$、上升 $m$"的台阶变成"左移 $m$、上升 $1$"，其斜率为 $\dfrac{1}{-m} = -\dfrac{1}{m}$。所以两条非竖直直线垂直时 $$ m_1 \times m_2 \;=\; -1, \qquad\text{that is}\qquad m_2 \;=\; -\frac{1}{m_1}, $$ 反过来，斜率之积为 $-1$ 的两条直线互相垂直。例外：水平线（$m = 0$）与竖直线（无斜率）垂直，此时无法构成乘积。

中点与距离。对 $A(x_1, y_1)$ 与 $B(x_2, y_2)$： $$ M \;=\; \left( \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2},\; \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right), \qquad AB \;=\; \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^{2} + (y_2 - y_1)^{2}}. $$ 中点是坐标的平均；距离是对水平与竖直变化量使用勾股定理。

原理。方向只由斜率决定，所以"平行"或"垂直"确定 $m$，已知点再确定直线。点 $(1.5, 2.5)$ 是从 $(6, 1)$ 向 $L$ 所作垂线的垂足。

Perpendicular Bisectors垂直平分线 SL 3.5

Learning objectives学习目标

  • 由中点与负倒数斜率求出（find）垂直平分线方程。
  • 由条件 $PA = PB$ 推导（derive）同一方程。

Definition and the equidistance property定义与等距性质

Definition. The perpendicular bisector of a segment $AB$ is the line that passes through the midpoint $M$ of $AB$ and is perpendicular to $AB$.

Why its points are equidistant. Take any point $P$ on the bisector. Triangles $PMA$ and $PMB$ have $MA = MB$ (midpoint), a right angle at $M$, and the side $PM$ in common, so they are congruent and $PA = PB$. Conversely, if $PA = PB$ then triangle $PAB$ is isosceles, and the line from $P$ to the midpoint of the base $AB$ is perpendicular to $AB$, so $P$ lies on the bisector. The perpendicular bisector is therefore exactly the set of points equidistant from $A$ and $B$; it splits the plane into the points closer to $A$ and the points closer to $B$.

Method.
  1. Midpoint $M$ of $AB$.
  2. Gradient $m_{AB}$, then the perpendicular gradient $-\dfrac{1}{m_{AB}}$.
  3. Point-gradient form through $M$; tidy into the form the question asks for.
Special cases: if $AB$ is horizontal the bisector is the vertical line $x = $ (midpoint $x$-coordinate); if $AB$ is vertical it is the horizontal line $y = $ (midpoint $y$-coordinate).
定义。线段 $AB$ 的垂直平分线是经过 $AB$ 的中点 $M$ 且与 $AB$ 垂直的直线。

为何其上的点等距。在垂直平分线上任取一点 $P$。三角形 $PMA$ 与 $PMB$ 中 $MA = MB$（中点），$M$ 处为直角，且公共边 $PM$，所以两三角形全等，$PA = PB$。反过来，若 $PA = PB$，则三角形 $PAB$ 是等腰三角形，从 $P$ 到底边 $AB$ 中点的连线垂直于 $AB$，所以 $P$ 在垂直平分线上。因此垂直平分线恰好是到 $A$、$B$ 等距（equidistant）的点的集合；它把平面分成离 $A$ 更近的点和离 $B$ 更近的点两部分。

方法。
  1. 求 $AB$ 的中点 $M$。
  2. 求斜率 $m_{AB}$，再求垂直斜率 $-\dfrac{1}{m_{AB}}$。
  3. 过 $M$ 写点斜式，再整理成题目要求的形式。
特殊情形：若 $AB$ 水平，垂直平分线是竖直线 $x = $（中点的 $x$ 坐标）；若 $AB$ 竖直，则是水平线 $y = $（中点的 $y$ 坐标）。

Deriving the equation from $PA = PB$由 $PA = PB$ 推导方程

For $P(x, y)$, $A(x_1, y_1)$ and $B(x_2, y_2)$, square both distances so the roots disappear:

对 $P(x, y)$、$A(x_1, y_1)$、$B(x_2, y_2)$，把两段距离都平方以去掉根号：

$$ (x - x_1)^{2} + (y - y_1)^{2} \;=\; (x - x_2)^{2} + (y - y_2)^{2}. $$

Expanding, $x^{2}$ and $y^{2}$ appear on both sides and cancel, leaving an equation of the form $ax + by + d = 0$. That is the algebraic reason the set of equidistant points is a straight line. Worked Example C1.3 does this with numbers and gets the same line as the midpoint method.

展开后两边都有 $x^{2}$ 与 $y^{2}$，相互抵消，剩下形如 $ax + by + d = 0$ 的方程。这就是"等距点的集合是一条直线"的代数原因。C1.3 例题用具体数字推导，得到与中点法相同的直线。

(a) Midpoint $M = \left(\dfrac{1 + 8}{2}, \dfrac{1 + 8}{2}\right) = (4.5, 4.5)$. Gradient $m_{AC} = \dfrac{8 - 1}{8 - 1} = 1$, so the perpendicular gradient is $-1$.

$$ y - 4.5 \;=\; -(x - 4.5) \;\Longrightarrow\; y \;=\; -x + 9 \;\Longrightarrow\; x + y - 9 \;=\; 0. $$

(b) Let $P(x, y)$ satisfy $PA^{2} = PC^{2}$:

(b) 设 $P(x, y)$ 满足 $PA^{2} = PC^{2}$：

so $x + y = 9$, the same line.

所以 $x + y = 9$，是同一条直线。

Why this works. Method (a) uses the definition (midpoint plus perpendicular); method (b) uses the equidistance property. They agree because the two descriptions name the same line.

原理。方法 (a) 用定义（中点加垂直），方法 (b) 用等距性质。两者一致，因为这两种描述指的是同一条直线。

Voronoi Diagrams: Sites, Cells, Edges and VerticesVoronoi 图：站点、单元、边与顶点 SL 3.6

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Explain why every edge lies on the perpendicular bisector of two neighbouring sites.
  • Construct the Voronoi diagram of three or four sites in a bounded region from perpendicular bisectors.

  • 解释（explain）为何每条边都在两个相邻站点的垂直平分线上。
  • 在有界区域内用垂直平分线作出（construct）三个或四个站点的 Voronoi 图。
  • 求出（find）一条边的方程及其端点坐标。
  • 通过说明某点到三个站点等距、且离其他站点都更远，验证（verify）它是顶点。

The four words四个关键词

Vocabulary of a Voronoi diagramVoronoi 图的术语
  • Sites: the given points (towns, wells, shops, weather stations).
  • Cell of a site: every point of the region that is closer to that site than to any other site. Each cell is a convex polygon (cut off by the edge of the region where needed), and every cell contains exactly one site.
  • Edge: a boundary between two neighbouring cells. Its points are equidistant from those two sites and no closer to any other site, so an edge is a piece of the perpendicular bisector of the two sites.
  • Vertex: a point where three (or more) edges meet. It is equidistant from three (or more) sites, so it is the intersection of their perpendicular bisectors.
  • 站点（sites）：给定的点（城镇、水井、商店、气象站）。
  • 某站点的单元（cell）：区域中离该站点比离其他任何站点都更近的所有点。每个单元是凸多边形（必要时被区域边界截断），每个单元恰好含一个站点。
  • 边（edge）：两个相邻单元的分界。其上的点到这两个站点等距，且不比它们更靠近其他站点，所以一条边是这两个站点的垂直平分线的一段。
  • 顶点（vertex）：三条（或更多）边的交汇点。它到三个（或更多）站点等距，所以是这些站点的垂直平分线的交点。

Why edges are bisector pieces. A point moves from the cell of $A$ into the cell of $B$ exactly where it stops being closer to $A$ and starts being closer to $B$, that is where $PA = PB$. By Topic C1.3 that set is the perpendicular bisector of $AB$. Only part of the bisector is an edge: beyond the edge's end, some third site is closer than both $A$ and $B$.

为何边是垂直平分线的一部分。一个点从 $A$ 的单元进入 $B$ 的单元，恰好发生在它不再离 $A$ 更近、开始离 $B$ 更近的地方，即 $PA = PB$ 处。由 C1.3，这个集合就是 $AB$ 的垂直平分线。只有其中一部分是边：越过边的端点后，会有第三个站点比 $A$、$B$ 都更近。

Constructing the diagram作图步骤

  1. Join neighbouring sites in pairs and draw the perpendicular bisector of each pair.
  2. For three sites, the three bisectors meet at one point (the point equidistant from all three): this is the vertex.
  3. From the vertex, keep the half of each bisector that points away from the third site; erase the other half, which lies in the third site's cell.
  4. Stop each edge at the boundary of the region (or at another vertex), then label the cells by their sites.
  1. 把相邻站点两两相连，作每一对的垂直平分线。
  2. 三个站点的三条垂直平分线交于一点（到三点等距的点）：这就是顶点。
  3. 从顶点出发，保留每条垂直平分线背离第三个站点的那一半；擦去另一半，因为它位于第三个站点的单元内。
  4. 每条边在区域边界（或另一个顶点）处截止，然后用站点名称标出各单元。

Adding a new site添加新站点

A new site only takes territory from the cells around it, so there is no need to redraw the whole diagram. Work outwards from the cell the new site falls in:

新站点只会从它周围的单元中"划走"区域，所以不必重画整张图。从新站点所在的单元开始向外处理：

  1. Find the cell containing the new site $N$; say it is the cell of $S$.
  2. Draw the perpendicular bisector of $N$ and $S$ inside that cell until it meets the cell's boundary.
  3. Where it meets an edge, cross into the neighbouring cell (site $T$) and continue with the bisector of $N$ and $T$.
  4. Repeat until the new boundary closes up or reaches the edge of the region.
  5. Erase every old edge that is now inside the new cell; the points where the new boundary met old edges are new vertices.
  1. 找出包含新站点 $N$ 的单元，设为 $S$ 的单元。
  2. 在该单元内画 $N$ 与 $S$ 的垂直平分线，直到碰到单元的边界。
  3. 在碰到某条边处进入相邻单元（站点 $T$），改画 $N$ 与 $T$ 的垂直平分线继续。
  4. 重复，直到新边界闭合或到达区域边界。
  5. 擦去现在位于新单元内的所有旧边；新边界与旧边的交点就是新的顶点。
Worked Example C1.5: adding a fourth townC1.5 例题：加入第四个城镇

A new town $D(1, 7)$ is added to the diagram of Worked Example C1.4 ($A(1, 1)$, $B(11, 1)$, $C(8, 8)$, region $0 \le x \le 12$, $0 \le y \le 10$). Update the Voronoi diagram, giving the equations of the new edges and the new vertex.在 C1.4 例题的 Voronoi 图（$A(1, 1)$、$B(11, 1)$、$C(8, 8)$，区域 $0 \le x \le 12$、$0 \le y \le 10$）中加入新城镇 $D(1, 7)$。更新 Voronoi 图，写出新边的方程与新顶点。

Step 1. Which cell is $D$ in? Squared distances from $D$: to $A$ $0 + 36 = 36$, to $C$ $49 + 1 = 50$, to $B$ $100 + 36 = 136$. So $D$ is in the cell of $A$.

第 1 步：$D$ 在哪个单元？$D$ 的距离平方：到 $A$ 为 $0 + 36 = 36$，到 $C$ 为 $49 + 1 = 50$，到 $B$ 为 $100 + 36 = 136$。所以 $D$ 在 $A$ 的单元内。

Step 2. Bisector of $A$ and $D$. $AD$ is vertical with midpoint $(1, 4)$, so the bisector is $y = 4$. Inside the cell of $A$ it runs from the boundary $(0, 4)$ to the edge $AC$ ($x + y = 9$), which it meets where $x = 5$: the point $(5, 4)$.

第 2 步：$A$ 与 $D$ 的垂直平分线。$AD$ 竖直，中点 $(1, 4)$，所以垂直平分线为 $y = 4$。在 $A$ 的单元内，它从边界点 $(0, 4)$ 延伸到边 $AC$（$x + y = 9$），交于 $x = 5$ 处：点 $(5, 4)$。

It passes through $(5, 4)$ (since $35 + 4 = 39$) and runs up to the top boundary $y = 10$ at $x = \tfrac{29}{7} \approx 4.14$.

第 3 步：进入 $C$ 的单元。$C$ 与 $D$ 的垂直平分线：中点 $(4.5, 7.5)$，$m_{CD} = \dfrac{8 - 7}{8 - 1} = \dfrac{1}{7}$，垂直斜率 $-7$：$y - 7.5 = -7(x - 4.5)$，即 $7x + y = 39$。它经过 $(5, 4)$（因为 $35 + 4 = 39$），向上延伸到上边界 $y = 10$，此时 $x = \tfrac{29}{7} \approx 4.14$。

Step 4. Erase and check. The old $AC$ edge from $(5, 4)$ to $(0, 9)$ is now inside the cell of $D$ (at $(0, 9)$, $D$ is $\sqrt{5}$ away while $A$ and $C$ are $\sqrt{65}$ away), so erase it. 原理。改归 $D$ 的点原本最靠近 $A$ 或 $C$，所以只有这两个单元改变；$B$ 的单元不受影响。$D$ 的单元是以 $(0, 4)$、$(5, 4)$、$(\tfrac{29}{7}, 10)$、$(0, 10)$ 为顶点的四边形。

Nearest-neighbour interpolation最近邻插值

Definition. Nearest-neighbour interpolation estimates an unknown value at a point (rainfall, temperature, price) by the value measured at the nearest site, that is the site of the cell containing the point. Mechanism: the Voronoi diagram does the "which site is nearest" work once, so every estimate is just "read the cell". Limitation: the estimate is constant across a cell and jumps at the edges, and it ignores every site except one; two points either side of an edge, $10$ m apart, can get very different estimates.

定义。最近邻插值用最近站点（即包含该点的单元的站点）测得的数值，来估计某点的未知值（降雨量、气温、价格）。机制：Voronoi 图一次性完成"哪个站点最近"的判断，所以每次估计只需"看单元"。局限：估计值在整个单元内不变、在边上跳变，并且只用了一个站点；一条边两侧相距 $10$ m 的两点，估计值可能差别很大。

The largest empty circle (toxic waste dump) problem最大空圆（有毒废物场选址）问题

The problem. Place a facility nobody wants nearby (a toxic waste dump, a noisy airport) at the point of the region that is as far as possible from its nearest site. Equivalently, find the largest circle whose centre is in the region and which has no site inside it: the centre is the location and the radius is the distance to the nearest site.

问题。要把没人愿意靠近的设施（有毒废物场、嘈杂的机场）放在区域内离最近站点尽可能远的位置。等价地：找一个圆心在区域内、圆内不含任何站点的最大的圆（最大空圆）：圆心就是选址，半径就是到最近站点的距离。

Why the answer is at a vertex or on the boundary. Suppose a point $X$ is inside the cell of $S$, not on an edge or on the region boundary. Moving $X$ a little directly away from $S$ keeps it in the same cell and increases its distance to $S$, which is its nearest site, so $X$ was not the best point. So the best point lies on an edge or on the boundary. On an edge between $S$ and $T$, moving along the edge away from the midpoint of $ST$ increases the distance to both, so the best point on an edge is at one of its ends: a Voronoi vertex, or where the edge meets the region boundary. Along the boundary inside one cell, the distance to the site is largest at an end of that stretch: a corner of the region or an edge meeting the boundary. The candidates are therefore:

为何答案在顶点或边界上。设点 $X$ 在 $S$ 的单元内部，不在边上也不在区域边界上。把 $X$ 沿背离 $S$ 的方向稍微移动，它仍在同一单元内，而到 $S$（它的最近站点）的距离增大，所以 $X$ 不是最优点。因此最优点在某条边上或区域边界上。在 $S$ 与 $T$ 之间的边上，沿边背离 $ST$ 的中点移动会使到两者的距离同时增大，所以边上的最优点在边的端点：Voronoi 顶点，或边与区域边界的交点。在区域边界上位于同一单元的一段里，到站点的距离在这一段的端点处最大：区域的角点，或边与边界的交点。因此候选点是：

  • every Voronoi vertex inside the region;
  • every point where an edge meets the region boundary;
  • every corner of the region.
  • 区域内的每个 Voronoi 顶点；
  • 每条边与区域边界的交点；
  • 区域的每个角点。

If the question only allows locations at vertices (a common simplification), compare the vertices alone; otherwise the boundary candidates must be checked too.

若题目只允许把设施放在顶点（常见的简化），就只比较顶点；否则还必须检查边界上的候选点。

Pitfalls to check before moving on继续之前要检查的陷阱
  • Wrong gradient for a bisector: it is the negative reciprocal of the segment's gradient, through the midpoint.
  • Wrong half of a bisector: from a vertex, an edge points away from the third site.
  • Redrawing everything when adding a site: only the cells next to the new site change; erase the old edges inside the new cell.
  • Forgetting boundary candidates in a largest empty circle problem, unless the question restricts the answer to vertices.
  • Answer form: "$ax + by + d = 0$ with integer coefficients" means no fractions or decimals.
  • 垂直平分线斜率用错：应是线段斜率的负倒数，并经过中点。
  • 保留了垂直平分线错误的一半：从顶点出发，边指向背离第三个站点的方向。
  • 添加站点时整张重画：只有新站点旁边的单元会改变；擦去新单元内的旧边。
  • 最大空圆问题中漏掉边界候选点，除非题目把答案限制在顶点。
  • 答案形式："整数系数的 $ax + by + d = 0$"意味着不能有分数或小数。
