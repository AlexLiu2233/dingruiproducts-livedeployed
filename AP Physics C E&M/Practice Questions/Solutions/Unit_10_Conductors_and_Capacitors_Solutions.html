Q1EASY 10.1 Field and Potential in a Conductor10.1 导体内的场与电势Calculator

A solid conducting sphere of radius $R=0.10~\mathrm{m}$ carries a net charge $Q=+5.0~\mathrm{nC}$ and is in electrostatic equilibrium. Take $V=0$ at infinity. At a point $0.050~\mathrm{m}$ from the center, the electric field magnitude $E$ and the potential $V$ are一个半径 $R=0.10~\mathrm{m}$ 的实心导体球带净电荷 $Q=+5.0~\mathrm{nC}$，处于静电平衡。取无穷远处 $V=0$。在距球心 $0.050~\mathrm{m}$ 处，电场强度大小 $E$ 与电势 $V$ 分别为

  • (A) $E=0$, $V=0$
  • (B) $E=0$, $V=4.5\times10^{2}~\mathrm{V}$
  • (C) $E=2.2\times10^{3}~\mathrm{N/C}$, $V=4.5\times10^{2}~\mathrm{V}$
  • (D) $E=0$, $V=9.0\times10^{2}~\mathrm{V}$
Answer:答案： (B)
$$E=0\ \ (r\lt R),\qquad V=\frac{kQ}{R}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(5.0\times10^{-9})}{0.10}=4.5\times10^{2}~\mathrm{V}$$
The field inside conducting material is zero, so the potential does not change from the surface inward. The whole sphere sits at its surface value $kQ/R$.导体材料内部场强为零，所以从表面向内电势不变，整个球体保持表面电势 $kQ/R$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) takes zero field to mean zero potential. (C) treats the sphere as a uniformly charged insulator, $E=kQr/R^{3}$, and keeps the surface potential $kQ/R$, mixing the two models. (D) uses the point-charge potential $kQ/r$ at $r=0.050~\mathrm{m}$, which holds only outside the sphere.(A) 把场强为零当成电势为零。 (C) 把导体球当成均匀带电的绝缘球，用了 $E=kQr/R^{3}$，却又保留表面电势 $kQ/R$，把两种模型混在了一起。 (D) 在 $r=0.050~\mathrm{m}$ 处用了点电荷电势 $kQ/r$，它只适用于球外。
Insight.要点。 Zero field means constant potential, not zero potential. For a conductor, state the field and the potential as two separate facts.场强为零意味着电势为常数，而不是零。对导体，要把场强和电势作为两个独立的事实分别写出。

Q2EASY 10.3 Parallel-Plate Capacitance10.3 平行板电容Calculator

A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum has plate area $A=0.060~\mathrm{m^2}$ and separation $d=1.5~\mathrm{mm}$. It is connected to a $24~\mathrm{V}$ battery. The magnitude of the charge on each plate is真空中的平行板电容器极板面积 $A=0.060~\mathrm{m^2}$，板间距 $d=1.5~\mathrm{mm}$，接在 $24~\mathrm{V}$ 电池上。每块极板上电荷的大小为

  • (A) $4.2~\mathrm{nC}$
  • (B) $0.85~\mathrm{nC}$
  • (C) $8.5~\mathrm{nC}$
  • (D) $3.5\times10^{-10}~\mathrm{C}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$C=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}=\frac{(8.85\times10^{-12})(0.060)}{1.5\times10^{-3}}=3.54\times10^{-10}~\mathrm{F}$$
$$Q=C\,\Delta V=(3.54\times10^{-10})(24)=8.5\times10^{-9}~\mathrm{C}=8.5~\mathrm{nC}$$
The battery fixes $\Delta V=24~\mathrm{V}$, and the charge on one plate is $Q=C\Delta V$.电池固定 $\Delta V=24~\mathrm{V}$，单块极板上的电荷为 $Q=C\Delta V$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $4.2~\mathrm{nC}$ inserts the $\tfrac12$ that belongs to the energy formula; $Q=C\Delta V$ has no factor of one half. (B) $0.85~\mathrm{nC}$ reads $d=1.5~\mathrm{mm}$ as $1.5~\mathrm{cm}$, which makes $C$ ten times too small. (D) is the capacitance $C$ in farads, reported as if it were a charge. It stops before multiplying by $\Delta V$.(A) $4.2~\mathrm{nC}$ 把属于能量公式的 $\tfrac12$ 带了进来；$Q=C\Delta V$ 没有二分之一因子。 (B) $0.85~\mathrm{nC}$ 把 $d=1.5~\mathrm{mm}$ 当成 $1.5~\mathrm{cm}$，使 $C$ 小了十倍。 (D) 这是以法拉为单位的电容 $C$，却被当作电荷给出，没有再乘以 $\Delta V$。
Insight.要点。 Convert millimeters to meters before using $C=\epsilon_0A/d$, and check the units of the answer: coulombs for charge, farads for capacitance.使用 $C=\epsilon_0A/d$ 之前先把毫米换算成米，并检查答案的单位：电荷用库仑，电容用法拉。

Q3EASY 10.2 Charge Sharing by Contact10.2 接触起电的电荷分配Calculator

Two identical small metal spheres on insulating stands carry charges $+8q$ and $-2q$. They are touched together and then separated. The charge on each sphere is now两个完全相同的小金属球放在绝缘支架上，分别带电 $+8q$ 和 $-2q$。让它们接触后再分开，每个球现在所带的电荷为

  • (A) $+3q$
  • (B) $+4q$
  • (C) $+5q$
  • (D) $+6q$
Answer:答案： (A)
$$Q_{\rm tot}=8q+(-2q)=+6q,\qquad Q_1=Q_2=\frac{6q}{2}=+3q$$
Identical spheres in contact reach the same potential only with equal charges, and the total charge is conserved.相同的球接触后只有电荷相等才能达到相同电势，并且总电荷守恒。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) $+4q$ takes half of $+8q$ and forgets that the sphere carrying $-2q$ also takes part. (C) $+5q$ averages the magnitudes, $(8q+2q)/2$, and ignores the sign of $-2q$. (D) $+6q$ is the total charge; it was not divided between the two spheres.(B) $+4q$ 只取了 $+8q$ 的一半，忘了带 $-2q$ 的球也参与分配。 (C) $+5q$ 对大小取平均，$(8q+2q)/2$，忽略了 $-2q$ 的符号。 (D) $+6q$ 是总电荷，没有在两个球之间分配。
Insight.要点。 Add the charges with their signs first, then split the sum. Equal sharing needs identical geometry; unequal spheres share in proportion to radius.先带符号相加，再把和平分。平分要求几何形状相同；大小不同的球按半径比例分配。

  • (A) cavity wall $-4.0~\mathrm{nC}$, outer surface $-1.0~\mathrm{nC}$空腔壁 $-4.0~\mathrm{nC}$，外表面 $-1.0~\mathrm{nC}$
  • (B) cavity wall $-4.0~\mathrm{nC}$, outer surface $+4.0~\mathrm{nC}$空腔壁 $-4.0~\mathrm{nC}$，外表面 $+4.0~\mathrm{nC}$
  • (C) cavity wall $-4.0~\mathrm{nC}$, outer surface $-5.0~\mathrm{nC}$空腔壁 $-4.0~\mathrm{nC}$，外表面 $-5.0~\mathrm{nC}$

(B) gives the outer surface only the induced $+4.0~\mathrm{nC}$ and drops the shell's own $-1.0~\mathrm{nC}$. (C) subtracts instead of adds, $-1.0-4.0$, so the sign of the induced outer charge is reversed.(A) 外表面仍取球壳自身的 $-1.0~\mathrm{nC}$，忘了被吸到壁上的 $-4.0~\mathrm{nC}$ 会让 $+4.0~\mathrm{nC}$ 被排到外面。 (B) 外表面只取了感应的 $+4.0~\mathrm{nC}$，漏掉了球壳自身的 $-1.0~\mathrm{nC}$。 (C) 做了减法而不是加法，$-1.0-4.0$，使外表面感应电荷的符号反了。
Insight.要点。 Name the surface, the sign and the reason: Gauss's law in the metal for the cavity wall, conservation of charge for the outer surface. The off-center position changes the wall pattern but not these totals.说明是哪个表面、符号和理由：空腔壁用金属内的高斯定律，外表面用电荷守恒。偏心位置只改变壁上的分布，不改变这些总量。

  • (B) $7.5\times10^{2}~\mathrm{N/C}$
  • (C) $1.5\times10^{3}~\mathrm{N/C}$
  • (D) $3.0\times10^{3}~\mathrm{N/C}$
Answer:答案： (B)
$$V(r)=\frac{kQ}{r}\ (r\ge R)\ \Rightarrow\ kQ=V(R)\,R=(300)(0.10)=30~\mathrm{V\,m}$$
$$E(0.20)=\frac{kQ}{r^{2}}=\frac{30}{(0.20)^{2}}=7.5\times10^{2}~\mathrm{N/C}\quad\left(=\frac{V}{r}=\frac{150}{0.20}\right)$$

  • (B) $2.7~\mathrm{nC}$
  • (C) $6.0~\mathrm{nC}$
  • (D) $2.0~\mathrm{nC}$

Q9MEDIUM 10.3 Concentric Spherical Capacitor10.3 同心球形电容器Calculator

  • (A) $3.7~\mathrm{pF}$
  • (B) $1.5~\mathrm{pF}$
  • (C) $5.6~\mathrm{pF}$
  • (D) $3.7\times10^{2}~\mathrm{pF}$
Answer:答案： (A)
$$C=4\pi\epsilon_0\frac{ab}{b-a}=4\pi(8.85\times10^{-12})\frac{(0.020)(0.050)}{0.030}=3.7\times10^{-12}~\mathrm{F}$$
Gauss's law gives $E=Q/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^2)$ between the shells, the line integral gives $\Delta V=\dfrac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\dfrac{b-a}{ab}$, and $Q$ cancels in $C=Q/\Delta V$.高斯定律给出壳间 $E=Q/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^2)$，线积分给出 $\Delta V=\dfrac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\dfrac{b-a}{ab}$，在 $C=Q/\Delta V$ 中 $Q$ 消去。

FRQ 1MEDIUM 10.3 Coaxial Capacitor Derivation10.3 同轴电容器的推导Calculator

(a) Take a coaxial cylinder of radius $r$ and length $L$ (length $L$ so that it spans the conductors). The field is radial by symmetry, so it is perpendicular to the curved side and constant over it. The end caps have $\vec E\parallel$ their surfaces, so $\vec E\cdot d\vec A=0$ there. The enclosed charge is $+Q$.取半径为 $r$、长度为 $L$ 的同轴圆柱面。由对称性，场沿径向，垂直于侧面且在侧面上大小不变。端面上 $\vec E$ 与端面平行，所以 $\vec E\cdot d\vec A=0$。包围的电荷为 $+Q$。
$$\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec A=E(2\pi rL)=\frac{Q}{\epsilon_0}\ \Rightarrow\ E(r)=\frac{Q}{2\pi\epsilon_0Lr}$$
(b) The magnitude of the potential difference is the integral of $E$ from $a$ to $b$:电势差的大小等于 $E$ 从 $a$ 到 $b$ 的积分：
$$\Delta V=\int_a^b\frac{Q}{2\pi\epsilon_0L\,r}\,dr=\frac{Q}{2\pi\epsilon_0L}\ln\frac ba,\qquad C=\frac{Q}{\Delta V}=\frac{2\pi\epsilon_0L}{\ln(b/a)}$$
$Q$ cancels, so $C$ depends only on the geometry, $L$ and $b/a$. The inner conductor is at the higher potential.$Q$ 被消去，所以 $C$ 只取决于几何形状，即 $L$ 与 $b/a$。内导体电势较高。
(c) Substitute the numbers:代入数值：
$$C=\frac{2\pi(8.85\times10^{-12})(2.5)}{\ln(4.0)}=\frac{1.390\times10^{-10}}{1.386}=1.0\times10^{-10}~\mathrm{F}=1.0\times10^{2}~\mathrm{pF}$$

$$U=\tfrac12C\,\Delta V^{2}=\tfrac12(1.0\times10^{-10})(250)^{2}=3.1\times10^{-6}~\mathrm{J}$$
$$E(a)=\frac{\Delta V}{a\ln(b/a)}=\frac{250}{(1.0\times10^{-3})(1.386)}=1.8\times10^{5}~\mathrm{V/m}$$
The field at the inner surface is four times the field at $r=b$, because $E\propto1/r$. Using $\Delta V/d$ with $d=3.0~\mathrm{mm}$ would give only $8.3\times10^{4}~\mathrm{V/m}$, which is wrong for a cylindrical gap.内表面处的场强是 $r=b$ 处的四倍，因为 $E\propto1/r$。若用 $\Delta V/d$（$d=3.0~\mathrm{mm}$）只能得到 $8.3\times10^{4}~\mathrm{V/m}$，对圆柱形间隙是错误的。

(d) Using $\Delta V/d$ for the field at the inner surface, or $Q^{2}/2C$ with the wrong $Q$.(a) 用球面或平面柱面代替同轴圆柱面，或把端面的通量也算进去。(b) 写成 $\Delta V=Ed$ 而丢掉对数，这假设了均匀场。(c) 用 $b-a$ 代替 $\ln(b/a)$。(d) 用 $\Delta V/d$ 求内表面处的场，或用错误的 $Q$ 代入 $Q^{2}/2C$。
Insight.要点。 Every capacitance derivation follows the same four steps: charge the plates, find $E$ from Gauss's law, integrate for $\Delta V$, and divide. A limiting check, such as $\ln(1+d/a)\approx d/a$ giving $\epsilon_0A/d$ for a thin gap, supports the algebra.每个电容推导都遵循同样的四步：给极板充电，用高斯定律求 $E$，积分求 $\Delta V$，再相除。极限检验，例如薄间隙时 $\ln(1+d/a)\approx d/a$ 给出 $\epsilon_0A/d$，可以印证代数。

The free charge on the plates is the same on both sides.介质板的分子被极板的场极化：正的束缚电荷向负极板偏移，负的束缚电荷向正极板偏移。于是靠近正极板的介质表面出现一层负的束缚电荷，靠近负极板的表面出现一层正的束缚电荷。束缚电荷产生与极板场方向相反的场，所以介质板内的净场为 $E_{\rm vac}/\kappa$，小于真空层中的场。两侧极板上的自由电荷相同。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) Using $d=2.0$ in millimeters in $C=\epsilon_0A/d$. (b) Multiplying $C_0$ by $\kappa$ as if the slab filled the whole gap, or using the parallel model for layers. (c) Using $\Delta V=60~\mathrm{V}$ after the battery is gone; $Q$ is what stays fixed. (d) Saying the work is negative because the energy decreases when a slab is inserted; here the slab is being removed, so the energy rises. (e) Saying the dielectric 'absorbs' the field, with no mention of bound charge.(a) 在 $C=\epsilon_0A/d$ 中用毫米数值代入 $d=2.0$。(b) 把 $C_0$ 乘以 $\kappa$，好像介质板充满整个间隙，或对各层用并联模型。(c) 电池断开后仍用 $\Delta V=60~\mathrm{V}$；保持不变的是 $Q$。(d) 说功为负，因为插入介质板时能量减少；这里是抽出，能量增加。(e) 说电介质“吸收”了场，却没有提到束缚电荷。
Insight.要点。 Always name what is held fixed: $\Delta V$ while the battery is connected, $Q$ after it is removed. Layers across the gap are in series, a polarized dielectric lowers the field only through bound charge, and the sign of the agent's work follows from the direction of the force.始终要说明什么量保持不变：接电池时是 $\Delta V$，断开后是 $Q$。沿间隙方向的层是串联，被极化的电介质只通过束缚电荷降低场，外力做功的符号由力的方向决定。

FRQ 6HARDFRQ LEVEL 10.3 Charge Sharing between Capacitors10.3 电容器之间的电荷分配Calculator

$C_1=4.0~\mu\mathrm{F}$ charged to $24~\mathrm{V}$ and disconnected, then joined in parallel to uncharged $C_2=12~\mu\mathrm{F}$.$C_1=4.0~\mu\mathrm{F}$ 充电到 $24~\mathrm{V}$ 后断开，再与不带电的 $C_2=12~\mu\mathrm{F}$ 并联。

(a) Charge and energy on $C_1$:$C_1$ 上的电荷和能量：
$$Q=C_1\Delta V_0=(4.0\times10^{-6})(24)=96~\mu\mathrm{C},\qquad U_i=\tfrac12C_1\Delta V_0^{2}=\tfrac12(4.0\times10^{-6})(24)^{2}=1.15\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{J}$$
(b) (1) Conservation of charge: the capacitors, once disconnected from the battery, form an isolated system, so the total free charge on the joined plates is $96~\mu\mathrm{C}$. (2) Equal potential: wires connect the plates, so they act as one conductor and both capacitors have the same $\Delta V$ (the wire is ideal and holds negligible charge).(1) 电荷守恒：两个电容器与电池断开后构成孤立系统，所以相连极板上的总自由电荷为 $96~\mu\mathrm{C}$。(2) 电势相等：导线把极板相连，它们成为一个导体，两个电容器的 $\Delta V$ 相同（导线理想，所带电荷可忽略）。
$$\Delta V_f=\frac{Q_{\rm tot}}{C_1+C_2}=\frac{96}{16}=6.0~\mathrm{V}$$
$$Q_1=C_1\Delta V_f=24~\mu\mathrm{C},\qquad Q_2=C_2\Delta V_f=72~\mu\mathrm{C},\qquad Q_1+Q_2=96~\mu\mathrm{C}$$
(c) Energy after, then the difference:先求之后的能量，再求差值：
$$U_f=\tfrac12(C_1+C_2)\Delta V_f^{2}=\tfrac12(16\times10^{-6})(6.0)^{2}=2.9\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{J}$$
$$U_i-U_f=1.15\times10^{-3}-2.9\times10^{-4}=8.6\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{J}\ \ (75\%\text{ of the initial energy})$$
(d) The claim is wrong. Charge conservation and energy conservation are separate statements, and the electrostatic energy of the capacitors alone is not conserved. The $8.6\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{J}$ is converted to thermal energy in the wire and to radiation (a spark or electromagnetic pulse) while charge flows. The answer to (c) passes an independent check:这一说法错误。电荷守恒与能量守恒是不同的陈述，仅电容器的静电能并不守恒。$8.6\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{J}$ 在电荷流动时变成了导线中的热能和辐射（火花或电磁脉冲）。(c) 的答案可以用独立的方法检验：
$$\Delta U=\tfrac12\,\frac{C_1C_2}{C_1+C_2}\,(\Delta V_0)^{2}=\tfrac12(3.0\times10^{-6})(24)^{2}=8.6\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{J}$$
(e) No. The energy lost depends only on the capacitances and the initial potential difference, as the formula in (d) shows. A larger resistance makes the charge flow more slowly and spreads the dissipation over a longer time, but the same total energy is dissipated.不会。损失的能量只取决于电容和初始电势差，如 (d) 中的公式所示。电阻更大使电荷流动更慢，把耗散分散到更长的时间里，但耗散的总能量相同。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) Using $C\Delta V^{2}$ without the $\tfrac12$. (b) Sharing the charge equally, or sharing the voltage instead of finding a common $\Delta V$; or treating the capacitors as in series. (c) Taking $U_f=U_i$ because the charge is conserved, or using the original $24~\mathrm{V}$ in $\tfrac12C\Delta V^{2}$ for the combination. (d) Accepting the claim and looking for an arithmetic error. (e) Claiming the energy loss grows with the resistance.(a) 用 $C\Delta V^{2}$ 而漏掉 $\tfrac12$。(b) 把电荷平分，或平分电压而不是求公共的 $\Delta V$；或把两个电容器当成串联。(c) 因电荷守恒取 $U_f=U_i$，或对组合仍用原来的 $24~\mathrm{V}$ 代入 $\tfrac12C\Delta V^{2}$。(d) 接受这一说法并去找算术错误。(e) 认为能量损失随电阻增大。
Insight.要点。 Charge sharing between capacitors mirrors charge sharing between conductors: equal potential plus conserved charge fix the state, and energy is lost in the process. A response earns the justification marks by naming the principle and its hypotheses, not by the numbers alone.电容器之间的电荷分配与导体之间的电荷分配相同：等电势加电荷守恒决定最终状态，过程中损失能量。回答要靠指明原理及其假设来获得论证分，而不只是数值。
