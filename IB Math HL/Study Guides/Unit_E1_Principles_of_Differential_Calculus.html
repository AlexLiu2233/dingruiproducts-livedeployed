The Idea of a Limit极限的概念 SL 5.1

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Explain what $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$ means, and why the value of $f$ at $x = a$ itself does not affect the limit.
  • Estimate a limit from a table of values on both sides of $a$.
  • Evaluate a limit by direct substitution when substitution gives a defined value.
  • Identify $\frac{0}{0}$ as an indeterminate form that signals more algebra, not an answer.
  • Calculate a $\frac{0}{0}$ limit by factorising and cancelling, or by rationalising with the conjugate.
  • 解释（explain）$\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$ 的含义，以及为何 $f$ 在 $x = a$ 处本身的取值不影响极限。
  • 由 $a$ 两侧的数值表估计（estimate）极限。
  • 代入得到确定值时，用直接代入法求（evaluate）极限。
  • 识别（identify）$\frac{0}{0}$ 是未定式（indeterminate form），提示还需代数化简，而不是答案。
  • 用因式分解约分或共轭有理化计算（calculate）$\frac{0}{0}$ 型极限。
$\displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$ means "$f(x)$ gets arbitrarily close to $L$ as $x$ gets arbitrarily close to $a$ (but not equal to $a$)." Three methods to evaluate:
  1. Direct substitution: if $f$ is continuous at $a$, just compute $f(a)$.
  2. Factor & cancel: if direct substitution gives $\tfrac{0}{0}$, factor numerator and denominator, cancel the common $(x - a)$, then substitute.
  3. Rationalise: when a square-root term causes $\tfrac{0}{0}$, multiply top and bottom by the conjugate.
Watch for the form $\tfrac{0}{0}$: it's not an answer, it's a flag that more work is needed.
$\displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$ 表示"当 $x$ 任意接近 $a$ 但不等于 $a$ 时，$f(x)$ 任意接近 $L$"。三种求法：
  1. 直接代入：若 $f$ 在 $a$ 处连续，算 $f(a)$ 即可。
  2. 因式分解 & 约分：若直接代入得 $\tfrac{0}{0}$，把分子分母分别分解、约掉公因子 $(x - a)$，再代入。
  3. 有理化（rationalise）：若根式造成 $\tfrac{0}{0}$，用共轭因子（conjugate）同乘分子分母。
$\tfrac{0}{0}$ 不是答案，而是"还需继续化简"的信号。

Reading a limit from a table. For $f(x) = \dfrac{x^{2} - 4}{x - 2}$, the value $f(2)$ does not exist, yet the outputs near $x = 2$ settle on one number from both sides: $f(1.9) = 3.9$, $f(1.99) = 3.99$, $f(2.01) = 4.01$, $f(2.1) = 4.1$. So $\lim_{x \to 2} f(x) = 4$. A table suggests the limit; the algebra in Worked Example E1.1a proves it.

由数值表读极限。对 $f(x) = \dfrac{x^{2} - 4}{x - 2}$，$f(2)$ 不存在，但 $x = 2$ 附近两侧的输出都趋于同一个数：$f(1.9) = 3.9$，$f(1.99) = 3.99$，$f(2.01) = 4.01$，$f(2.1) = 4.1$。故 $\lim_{x \to 2} f(x) = 4$。数值表只是提示极限，例题 E1.1a 的代数推导才是证明。

Worked Example E1.1a: Factor & cancelE1.1a 例题：因式分解 & 约分

Evaluate $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 2} \dfrac{x^{2} - 4}{x - 2}$.求 $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 2} \dfrac{x^{2} - 4}{x - 2}$。

Direct substitution fails. Plugging $x = 2$ gives $\tfrac{4 - 4}{2 - 2} = \tfrac{0}{0}$, which is indeterminate.

直接代入失败。代入 $x = 2$ 得 $\tfrac{4 - 4}{2 - 2} = \tfrac{0}{0}$，为未定式。

Factor. $x^{2} - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)$, so

分解。$x^{2} - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)$，故

$$ \frac{x^{2} - 4}{x - 2} \;=\; \frac{(x - 2)(x + 2)}{x - 2} \;=\; x + 2 \quad (x \ne 2). $$

Substitute. $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 2} (x + 2) = 2 + 2 = 4$.

代入。$\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 2} (x + 2) = 2 + 2 = 4$。

Worked Example E1.1b: RationaliseE1.1b 例题：有理化

Evaluate $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{\sqrt{x + 1} - 1}{x}$.求 $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{\sqrt{x + 1} - 1}{x}$。

Direct substitution gives $\tfrac{0}{0}$. Multiply top and bottom by the conjugate $\sqrt{x + 1} + 1$:

直接代入得 $\tfrac{0}{0}$。同乘共轭 $\sqrt{x + 1} + 1$：

$$ \frac{\sqrt{x + 1} - 1}{x} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{x + 1} + 1}{\sqrt{x + 1} + 1} \;=\; \frac{(x + 1) - 1}{x (\sqrt{x + 1} + 1)} \;=\; \frac{x}{x (\sqrt{x + 1} + 1)} \;=\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{x + 1} + 1}. $$

Substitute. $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x + 1} + 1} = \dfrac{1}{1 + 1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

代入。$\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x + 1} + 1} = \dfrac{1}{1 + 1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$。

▸ Going deeper: what "indeterminate" actually means▸ 深入："未定式"究竟是什么意思

"$\tfrac{0}{0}$" is not a number; it's a shape a limit can take that doesn't pin down the answer. The same shape can resolve to $0$, $1$, $\infty$, or any real number depending on how the numerator and denominator approach $0$ relative to each other. Example: $\lim_{x \to 0} \tfrac{x^{2}}{x} = 0$ but $\lim_{x \to 0} \tfrac{x}{x^{2}}$ diverges, even though both are "$\tfrac{0}{0}$" at face value. The algebraic manipulation (factor / rationalise) is how you uncover which case you're in.

"$\tfrac{0}{0}$"不是一个数，而是一种极限可能呈现的形态，本身并不锁定答案。同一形态可解出 $0$、$1$、$\infty$ 或任意实数，取决于分子分母各自趋近 $0$ 的速率。例：$\lim_{x \to 0} \tfrac{x^{2}}{x} = 0$，但 $\lim_{x \to 0} \tfrac{x}{x^{2}}$ 发散，二者表面都是"$\tfrac{0}{0}$"。代数化简（分解 / 有理化）就是揭示真实情形的工具。

1. Evaluate $\lim_{x \to 4} \dfrac{x^{2} - 16}{x - 4}$.1. 求 $\lim_{x \to 4} \dfrac{x^{2} - 16}{x - 4}$。

Substitution gives $\frac{0}{0}$. Factorise: $\dfrac{(x - 4)(x + 4)}{x - 4} = x + 4$ for $x \ne 4$. So the limit is $4 + 4 = 8$.

代入得 $\frac{0}{0}$。分解：$x \ne 4$ 时 $\dfrac{(x - 4)(x + 4)}{x - 4} = x + 4$。故极限为 $4 + 4 = 8$。

2. Evaluate $\lim_{x \to 9} \dfrac{\sqrt{x} - 3}{x - 9}$.2. 求 $\lim_{x \to 9} \dfrac{\sqrt{x} - 3}{x - 9}$。

Substitution gives $\frac{0}{0}$. Multiply top and bottom by the conjugate $\sqrt{x} + 3$: the numerator becomes $x - 9$, so the fraction is $\dfrac{x - 9}{(x - 9)(\sqrt{x} + 3)} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x} + 3}$ for $x \ne 9$. The limit is $\dfrac{1}{3 + 3} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.

代入得 $\frac{0}{0}$。分子分母同乘共轭 $\sqrt{x} + 3$：分子变为 $x - 9$，故 $x \ne 9$ 时分式为 $\dfrac{x - 9}{(x - 9)(\sqrt{x} + 3)} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x} + 3}$。极限为 $\dfrac{1}{3 + 3} = \dfrac{1}{6}$。

分子分母都分解：$x \ne 2$ 时 $\dfrac{(x + 3)(x - 2)}{(x + 2)(x - 2)} = \dfrac{x + 3}{x + 2}$。极限为 $\dfrac{5}{4}$。函数本身在 $x = 2$ 处无定义（分母为 $0$），说明函数无值之处极限仍可存在。

The Derivative as a Limit of Secant Gradients导数：割线斜率的极限 SL 5.1 · AHL 5.12

Learning objectives学习目标
  • (HL) State the first-principles definition $f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h}$.
  • Explain the difference quotient as the gradient of a secant, and $f'(x)$ as the gradient of the tangent (the instantaneous rate of change).
  • Calculate the gradient of a secant for a given $h$ and deduce the tangent gradient as $h \to 0$.
  • (HL) Derive the derivatives of simple polynomials and of $\frac{1}{x}$ from first principles.
  • Interpret $\frac{dy}{dx}$ as a rate of change with units.
  • （HL）写出（state）导数的定义（first principles）$f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h}$。
  • 解释（explain）差商是割线（secant）的斜率，$f'(x)$ 是切线的斜率（瞬时变化率）。
  • 对给定 $h$ 计算（calculate）割线斜率，并推断（deduce）$h \to 0$ 时的切线斜率。
  • （HL）用定义推导（derive）简单多项式与 $\frac{1}{x}$ 的导数。
  • 把 $\frac{dy}{dx}$ 解释（interpret）为带单位的变化率。
Definition. $$ f'(x) \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h}. $$ The fraction $\tfrac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$ is the slope of the secant line through $(x, f(x))$ and $(x + h, f(x + h))$. As $h \to 0$, the secant collapses to the tangent, and its slope becomes the instantaneous rate of change $f'(x)$.
  • Notation: $f'(x), \;\tfrac{dy}{dx}, \;\tfrac{d}{dx}[f(x)]$: all the same object.
  • Units: if $y$ is in metres and $x$ in seconds, $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$ is in m/s.
定义。 $$ f'(x) \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h}. $$ 差商（difference quotient）$\tfrac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$ 是过 $(x, f(x))$ 与 $(x + h, f(x + h))$ 的割线（secant line）的斜率。$h \to 0$ 时割线退化为切线（tangent），斜率即瞬时变化率 $f'(x)$。
  • 记号：$f'(x), \;\tfrac{dy}{dx}, \;\tfrac{d}{dx}[f(x)]$：同一对象。
  • 单位：若 $y$ 以米为单位、$x$ 以秒为单位，则 $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$ 以 m/s 为单位。
Worked Example E1.2a: estimate a gradient from secantsE1.2a 例题：用割线估计斜率

For $f(x) = x^{3}$, calculate the gradients of the secants from $x = 1$ to $x = 1 + h$ for $h = 0.1$, $0.01$ and $0.001$. Use them to estimate $f'(1)$.对 $f(x) = x^{3}$，计算从 $x = 1$ 到 $x = 1 + h$ 的割线斜率，其中 $h = 0.1$、$0.01$、$0.001$，并据此估计 $f'(1)$。

Secant gradient. It is the rise over the run $h$:

割线斜率。即竖直变化量除以水平距离 $h$：

$$ \frac{f(1 + h) - f(1)}{h} \;=\; \frac{(1 + h)^{3} - 1}{h}. $$

Evaluate for each $h$.

对每个 $h$ 求值。

$$ h = 0.1:\; \frac{1.331 - 1}{0.1} = 3.31, \qquad h = 0.01:\; \frac{1.030301 - 1}{0.01} = 3.0301, \qquad h = 0.001:\; \frac{1.003003001 - 1}{0.001} = 3.003001. $$

Read off the limit. As $h \to 0$ the secant gradients approach $3$, so $f'(1) = 3$: the tangent to $y = x^{3}$ at $(1, 1)$ has gradient $3$.

读出极限。$h \to 0$ 时割线斜率趋近 $3$，故 $f'(1) = 3$：$y = x^{3}$ 在 $(1, 1)$ 处的切线斜率为 $3$。

Worked Example E1.2b: a rate of change with unitsE1.2b 例题：带单位的变化率

Water flows into a tank so that the volume is $V(t) = \frac{t^{2}}{2} + 2t$ litres after $t$ minutes. Use secant gradients from $t = 4$ to estimate $V'(4)$, and interpret it.水箱中的水量为 $V(t) = \frac{t^{2}}{2} + 2t$ 升，其中 $t$ 为分钟数。用从 $t = 4$ 出发的割线斜率估计 $V'(4)$，并解释其含义。

Secant gradients. $V(4) = 8 + 8 = 16$. For $h = 1$, $0.1$ and $0.01$:

割线斜率。$V(4) = 8 + 8 = 16$。取 $h = 1$、$0.1$、$0.01$：

$$ \frac{V(5) - V(4)}{1} = \frac{22.5 - 16}{1} = 6.5, \qquad \frac{V(4.1) - V(4)}{0.1} = \frac{16.605 - 16}{0.1} = 6.05, \qquad \frac{V(4.01) - V(4)}{0.01} = \frac{16.06005 - 16}{0.01} = 6.005. $$

Limit. The gradients equal $6 + \frac{h}{2}$, which tends to $6$ as $h \to 0$, so $V'(4) = 6$.

极限。这些斜率等于 $6 + \frac{h}{2}$，$h \to 0$ 时趋于 $6$，故 $V'(4) = 6$。

Interpret, with units. At $t = 4$ minutes the volume is increasing at an instantaneous rate of $6$ litres per minute.

结合单位解释。在 $t = 4$ 分钟时，水量以每分钟 $6$ 升的瞬时变化率增加。

Worked Example E1.2c: Derive $\tfrac{d}{dx}(x^{2})$ from first principles (HL)E1.2c 例题：用定义推 $\tfrac{d}{dx}(x^{2})$（HL）

Show that the derivative of $f(x) = x^{2}$ is $f'(x) = 2x$.证明 $f(x) = x^{2}$ 的导数为 $f'(x) = 2x$。

Apply the definition.

套用定义。

$$ f'(x) \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{(x + h)^{2} - x^{2}}{h}. $$

Expand numerator and simplify.

展开分子并化简。

$$ (x + h)^{2} - x^{2} \;=\; x^{2} + 2xh + h^{2} - x^{2} \;=\; 2xh + h^{2} \;=\; h(2x + h). $$

Cancel $h$ and take the limit.

约去 $h$ 后取极限。

$$ f'(x) \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{h(2x + h)}{h} \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0} (2x + h) \;=\; 2x. \;\square $$
Worked Example E1.2d: Derive $\tfrac{d}{dx}(1/x)$ from first principles (HL)E1.2d 例题：用定义推 $\tfrac{d}{dx}(1/x)$（HL）

Show that the derivative of $f(x) = 1/x$ is $f'(x) = -1/x^{2}$ (for $x \ne 0$).证明 $f(x) = 1/x$ 的导数为 $f'(x) = -1/x^{2}$（$x \ne 0$）。

Definition.

定义。

$$ f'(x) \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{1}{h}\left[\frac{1}{x + h} - \frac{1}{x}\right]. $$

Common denominator inside the brackets.

方括号内通分。

$$ \frac{1}{x + h} - \frac{1}{x} \;=\; \frac{x - (x + h)}{x(x + h)} \;=\; \frac{-h}{x(x + h)}. $$

Substitute back and cancel $h$.

回代并约去 $h$。

$$ f'(x) \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{1}{h} \cdot \frac{-h}{x(x + h)} \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{-1}{x(x + h)} \;=\; -\frac{1}{x^{2}}. \;\square $$
Common Mistake (HL): substituting $h = 0$ before cancelling常见错误（HL）：约分前就代入 $h = 0$ The whole point of first-principles derivation is to extract a common factor of $h$ from the numerator so it cancels the denominator. Writing $\lim_{h \to 0} \tfrac{2xh + h^{2}}{h}$ and immediately substituting $h = 0$ gives $\tfrac{0}{0}$, which is undefined. The structural step "$h \cdot (2x + h) / h \to 2x + h$" is the move that turns the indeterminate form into a clean limit.从定义求导的整个目的就是从分子提出公因子 $h$，与分母约去。若写出 $\lim_{h \to 0} \tfrac{2xh + h^{2}}{h}$ 后立即代 $h = 0$，得 $\tfrac{0}{0}$，没有意义。"$h \cdot (2x + h) / h \to 2x + h$"这一结构性化简才是把未定式变成清晰极限的关键。
▸ Going deeper (HL): the alternate form using $x = a$▸ 深入（HL）：用 $x = a$ 的另一种形式

An equivalent definition computes $f'(a)$ at a specific point $a$:

在某固定点 $a$ 处，导数的等价定义为：

Power Rule (works for any real exponent $n$, including negative and fractional): $$ \frac{d}{dx}(x^{n}) \;=\; n x^{n - 1}. $$ Combined with linearity:
  • Sum rule: $\tfrac{d}{dx}[f(x) + g(x)] = f'(x) + g'(x)$.
  • Constant multiple: $\tfrac{d}{dx}[c \cdot f(x)] = c \cdot f'(x)$.
  • Derivative of a constant: $\tfrac{d}{dx}(c) = 0$.
Tangent line at $x = a$: $y - f(a) = f'(a)(x - a)$. Normal line at $x = a$: $y - f(a) = -\dfrac{1}{f'(a)}(x - a)$ (perpendicular to the tangent; defined only when $f'(a) \ne 0$).

  • 常数倍法则：$\tfrac{d}{dx}[c \cdot f(x)] = c \cdot f'(x)$。

Two lines are perpendicular iff their slopes are negative reciprocals: $m_{1} m_{2} = -1$. The tangent at $(a, f(a))$ has slope $f'(a)$; the normal, being perpendicular, has slope $-1/f'(a)$. Continuity & Differentiability连续性与可导性 HL AHL 5.12

Learning objectives学习目标

Continuity at $x = a$: $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = f(a)$. Equivalently, the left limit, the right limit, and the function value all agree.

Differentiability at $x = a$: the limit $f'(a) = \displaystyle\lim_{h \to 0} \tfrac{f(a + h) - f(a)}{h}$ exists. Equivalently, the left-derivative and the right-derivative both exist, are finite and are equal.

One-way implication. Differentiable $\Rightarrow$ continuous. (The converse is false.) Three canonical shapes are continuous but not differentiable at a point, and a fourth case is not even continuous:
  • Corner: left and right slopes differ. Example: $f(x) = |x|$ at $x = 0$.
  • Cusp: slopes go to $+\infty$ on one side and $-\infty$ on the other. Example: $f(x) = x^{2/3}$ at $x = 0$.
  • Vertical tangent: both slopes go to $+\infty$ (or both to $-\infty$). Example: $f(x) = \sqrt[3]{x}$ at $x = 0$.
  • Discontinuity: not even continuous, hence not differentiable. Example: a step function at the jump.
在 $x = a$ 处连续（continuous）：$\displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = f(a)$。等价地，左极限、右极限、函数值三者一致。

在 $x = a$ 处可导（differentiable）：极限 $f'(a) = \displaystyle\lim_{h \to 0} \tfrac{f(a + h) - f(a)}{h}$ 存在。等价地，左导数（left derivative）与右导数都存在、有限且相等。

单向蕴含。可导 $\Rightarrow$ 连续（反之不成立）。三种典型形状"连续但不可导"，第四种情形连连续都不满足：
  • 角点（corner）：左右斜率不同。例：$f(x) = |x|$ 在 $x = 0$。
  • 尖点（cusp）：一侧斜率趋于 $+\infty$，另一侧趋于 $-\infty$。例：$f(x) = x^{2/3}$ 在 $x = 0$。
  • 垂直切线（vertical tangent）：两侧斜率同趋于 $+\infty$（或同趋于 $-\infty$）。例：$f(x) = \sqrt[3]{x}$ 在 $x = 0$。
  • 不连续：连连续都不是，自然不可导。例：阶跃函数在跳跃处。
Worked Example E1.5: Show $f(x) = |x|$ is not differentiable at $0$E1.5 例题：证 $f(x) = |x|$ 在 $0$ 不可导

Show that $f(x) = |x|$ is continuous but not differentiable at $x = 0$.证明 $f(x) = |x|$ 在 $x = 0$ 处连续但不可导。

连续性。$\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0^{-}} |x| = 0$，$\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0^{+}} |x| = 0$，$f(0) = 0$。三者一致，故 $f$ 在 $0$ 处连续。✓

Differentiability: compute one-sided derivatives.

可导性：计算单侧导数。

$$ \lim_{h \to 0^{+}} \frac{|0 + h| - 0}{h} \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0^{+}} \frac{h}{h} \;=\; 1. $$ $$ \lim_{h \to 0^{-}} \frac{|0 + h| - 0}{h} \;=\; \lim_{h \to 0^{-}} \frac{-h}{h} \;=\; -1. $$

Left-derivative ($-1$) $\ne$ right-derivative ($+1$), so the two-sided limit does not exist. $f$ is not differentiable at $0$. ✗

Geometric reading. The graph of $|x|$ has a "corner" at the origin, so no single tangent direction exists.

几何理解。$|x|$ 的图像在原点形成"角点"，无法确定唯一的切线方向。

If $f'(a)$ exists, then

若 $f'(a)$ 存在，则

$$ \lim_{x \to a} \bigl[f(x) - f(a)\bigr] \;=\; \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x) - f(a)}{x - a} \cdot (x - a) \;=\; f'(a) \cdot 0 \;=\; 0. $$

So $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = f(a)$, exactly the definition of continuity at $a$. The converse fails because the difference quotient can have left/right limits that disagree even when $f(x) \to f(a)$.

即 $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = f(a)$，这正是连续性在 $a$ 处的定义。反向不成立：差商的左右极限可以不相等，即使 $f(x) \to f(a)$。

The second derivative $f''$ tells you the curvature:
  • $f''(x) > 0$ $\Rightarrow$ curve is concave up (opens like a cup; "smile"). $f'$ is increasing.
  • $f''(x) < 0$ $\Rightarrow$ curve is concave down (opens like a cap; "frown"). $f'$ is decreasing.

  • $f''(a) < 0$: local maximum.

A point of inflection is where $f''$ changes sign. If $f''$ is continuous (true for most IB functions you'll meet), the intermediate value theorem forces $f''(a) = 0$ at the crossing point. The converse fails: $f''(a) = 0$ without sign change gives a "flat" zero of $f''$ that's not an inflection (e.g. $f(x) = x^{4}$ at $x = 0$). So a complete answer verifies the sign change, not just the solution of $f''(x) = 0$.

First principles without technology (HL)不用技术工具的 first principles（HL）

  • Factor $h$ before taking the limit. Substituting $h = 0$ before cancelling gives $\tfrac{0}{0}$, which is not an answer.
  • 取极限之前先提因子 $h$。约分前代入 $h = 0$ 得 $\tfrac{0}{0}$，这不是答案。
  • State the limit explicitly. Write "as $h \to 0$, $(2x + h) \to 2x$"; the final answer alone does not show the limit was taken.

  • Compute the point from the curve. When only $x = a$ is given, the point is $(a, f(a))$; calculate $f(a)$ before writing the line.
  • 由曲线算出点。题目只给 $x = a$ 时，点为 $(a, f(a))$；写直线方程前先算 $f(a)$。
  • Use point-slope form first. $y - f(a) = m(x - a)$ needs no separate $y$-intercept step; rearrange at the end into the form the question asks for.
  • 先用点斜式。$y - f(a) = m(x - a)$ 无需单独求 $y$ 截距；最后再整理成题目要求的形式。
  • Normal: $m_{\text{normal}} = -1/m_{\text{tangent}}$. Both the minus sign and the reciprocal are needed; a gradient of $-m$ or $1/m$ gives a line that is not perpendicular.
  • 法线：$m_{\text{normal}} = -1/m_{\text{tangent}}$。负号与倒数都不能少；斜率写成 $-m$ 或 $1/m$ 得到的直线并不垂直。
Stationary point classification稳定点分类
  • Use the sign of $f'$ on both sides. The first derivative test always works: $+$ to $-$ is a local maximum, $-$ to $+$ a local minimum, no change a stationary point of inflection.
  • 看两侧 $f'$ 的符号。一阶导检验总是适用：由 $+$ 变 $-$ 为局部极大值，由 $-$ 变 $+$ 为局部极小值，不变号为水平拐点。
  • "Stationary" $\ne$ "extremum". A stationary point of inflection is stationary but not an extremum, so always verify the sign change.
  • "稳定"$\ne$"极值"。水平拐点是稳定点但不是极值，所以务必验证变号。
  • Answer with coordinates. Give each stationary point as $(x, y)$ and name its type.
  • 用坐标作答。每个稳定点都以 $(x, y)$ 给出，并说明类型。
Second derivative and inflection二阶导数与拐点
  • The second derivative test is quicker when it works. Use it unless $f''(a) = 0$; then switch to the sign of $f'$ on each side rather than stopping at "inconclusive".
  • 二阶导数检验能用时更快。除非 $f''(a) = 0$，否则优先用它；此时改看两侧 $f'$ 的符号，而不是停在"无法判断"。
  • Inflection needs a sign change of $f''$. Solving $f''(x) = 0$ only gives candidates.
  • 拐点要求 $f''$ 变号。解 $f''(x) = 0$ 只得到候选点。
HL extension: continuity and differentiabilityHL 拓展：连续性与可导性

  • 先连续，后可导。在分段函数的衔接点，先令函数值相等，再令单侧导数相等。
