Area and perimeter of plane figures平面图形的面积与周长

Perimeter is the distance around the boundary of a figure, so it is a length (units such as cm). Area is the amount of flat surface inside, so it is measured in square units (cm$^2$). A rectangle with length $\ell$ and width $w$ has $P=2\ell+2w$ and $A=\ell w$. A trapezoid has one pair of parallel sides, called the bases $b_1$ and $b_2$; its height $h$ is the perpendicular distance between them, not the length of a slanted leg.

周长（perimeter）是图形边界的总长，是长度（单位如 cm）。面积（area）是图形内部平面的大小，单位是平方单位（cm$^2$）。长为 $\ell$、宽为 $w$ 的矩形（rectangle）满足 $P=2\ell+2w$，$A=\ell w$。梯形（trapezoid）有一组平行边，称为底 $b_1$、$b_2$；高 $h$ 是两底之间的垂直距离，不是斜腰的长度。

Worked Example 4.1: area and perimeter of a trapezoid例题 4.1：梯形的面积与周长

An isosceles trapezoid has bases of length $8$ and $14$ and a height of $4$ (Figure 4.1). Find its area and its perimeter.

  1. Area: $A=\dfrac{b_1+b_2}{2}\,h=\dfrac{8+14}{2}\cdot 4=44$.
  2. The legs are not given. Drop perpendiculars from the top corners to the long base. Each overhang is $\dfrac{14-8}{2}=3$.
  3. Each leg is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $3$ and $4$, so the leg is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$.
  4. Perimeter: $8+14+5+5=32$. Check: the area $44$ lies between the area of an $8\times 4$ rectangle ($32$) and a $14\times 4$ rectangle ($56$).

一个等腰梯形的两底长为 $8$ 和 $14$，高为 $4$（图 4.1）。求它的面积和周长。

  1. 面积：$A=\dfrac{b_1+b_2}{2}\,h=\dfrac{8+14}{2}\cdot 4=44$。
  2. 腰长未给出。从上底两端向长底作垂线，每侧外伸 $\dfrac{14-8}{2}=3$。
  3. 每条腰是两条直角边为 $3$ 和 $4$ 的直角三角形的斜边，所以腰长为 $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$。
  4. 周长：$8+14+5+5=32$。检验：面积 $44$ 介于 $8\times 4$ 矩形的面积（$32$）与 $14\times 4$ 矩形的面积（$56$）之间。
Worked Example 4.2: a composite shape例题 4.2：组合图形

A rectangle is $10$ long and $6$ wide. A semicircle with diameter $6$ is attached to one of the $6$-unit ends. Find the area and the perimeter of the whole shape, to the nearest hundredth.

  1. The semicircle has radius $3$. Area: $10\cdot 6+\tfrac12\pi(3)^2=60+4.5\pi\approx 74.14$.
  2. The diameter edge where the semicircle joins the rectangle is a seam inside the shape, so it is not part of the perimeter.
  3. Boundary: two long sides ($10+10$), the far short side ($6$), and the semicircular arc $\tfrac12(2\pi\cdot 3)=3\pi$.
  4. Perimeter: $26+3\pi\approx 35.42$.

一个矩形长 $10$、宽 $6$。在一个长为 $6$ 的端边上接一个直径为 $6$ 的半圆。求整个图形的面积和周长，精确到百分位。

  1. 半圆的半径为 $3$。面积：$10\cdot 6+\tfrac12\pi(3)^2=60+4.5\pi\approx 74.14$。
  2. 半圆与矩形相接的直径边是图形内部的接缝，不属于周长。
  3. 边界：两条长边（$10+10$）、对面的短边（$6$），以及半圆弧 $\tfrac12(2\pi\cdot 3)=3\pi$。
  4. 周长：$26+3\pi\approx 35.42$。

Volume and surface area of solids立体的体积与表面积

Volume measures the space inside a solid (cubic units). Surface area adds the areas of all the faces, including any curved surface (square units). For a cylinder of radius $r$ and height $h$, the two circular ends have area $2\pi r^2$ and the curved side unrolls into a rectangle of area $2\pi r h$. A cone has a single circular base and a curved side that meets at the apex; its slant height $l$ runs along the side and satisfies $l^2=r^2+h^2$. A sphere is determined by its radius alone.

体积（volume）衡量立体内部的空间（立方单位）。表面积（surface area）是所有面的面积之和，包括曲面（平方单位）。半径为 $r$、高为 $h$ 的圆柱（cylinder）有两个圆形底面，面积共 $2\pi r^2$，侧面展开成面积为 $2\pi r h$ 的矩形。圆锥（cone）有一个圆形底面，侧面汇聚于顶点；母线（斜高） $l$ 沿侧面，满足 $l^2=r^2+h^2$。球（sphere）仅由半径决定。

A cone holds exactly one third of the cylinder with the same base and height; that is the reason for the $\tfrac13$ in its volume. When a question says a solid is poured into another, the volume is the quantity that carries over, so set the two volumes equal.

Worked Example 4.3: cone, cylinder and sphere side by side例题 4.3：圆锥、圆柱与球的对比

A cone and a cylinder both have radius $3$ and height $4$. A sphere has radius $3$. Find the volume and surface area of the cone, the volume of the cylinder, and the volume and surface area of the sphere. Leave answers in terms of $\pi$, then give a decimal.

  1. Slant height of the cone: $l=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$.
  2. Cone volume: $\tfrac13\pi(3)^2(4)=12\pi\approx 37.70$. Cone total surface area: $\pi(3)^2+\pi(3)(5)=9\pi+15\pi=24\pi\approx 75.40$.
  3. Cylinder volume: $\pi(3)^2(4)=36\pi\approx 113.10$, exactly three times the cone, as expected.
  4. Sphere: volume $\tfrac43\pi(3)^3=36\pi\approx 113.10$ and surface area $4\pi(3)^2=36\pi\approx 113.10$. The numbers agree only because $r=3$; the units differ (cubic and square).

一个圆锥和一个圆柱的半径都是 $3$、高都是 $4$；一个球的半径为 $3$。求圆锥的体积与表面积、圆柱的体积、球的体积与表面积。先用 $\pi$ 表示，再给出小数。

  1. 圆锥的母线：$l=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$。
  2. 圆锥体积：$\tfrac13\pi(3)^2(4)=12\pi\approx 37.70$。圆锥总表面积：$\pi(3)^2+\pi(3)(5)=9\pi+15\pi=24\pi\approx 75.40$。
  3. 圆柱体积：$\pi(3)^2(4)=36\pi\approx 113.10$，恰好是圆锥的三倍，与预期一致。
  4. 球：体积 $\tfrac43\pi(3)^3=36\pi\approx 113.10$，表面积 $4\pi(3)^2=36\pi\approx 113.10$。数值相同只是因为 $r=3$ 的巧合，单位并不相同（立方与平方）。
Worked Example 4.4: a solid displaces water例题 4.4：立体排开水

A cylindrical tank of radius $6$ cm holds water. A solid sphere of radius $3$ cm is lowered in until it is completely under water; nothing spills. By how many centimetres does the water level rise?

  1. The water that rises fills a thin cylinder with the tank's radius $6$ and height equal to the rise $d$. Its volume must equal the sphere's volume.
  2. Sphere volume: $\tfrac43\pi(3)^3=36\pi$.
  3. Set $\pi(6)^2 d=36\pi$, so $36\pi d=36\pi$ and $d=1$.
  4. The rise is $1$ cm. The $\pi$ cancels, which is a quick sign you are on the right track.

一个半径为 $6$ cm 的圆柱形水箱里盛有水。把一个半径为 $3$ cm 的实心球完全浸没在水中，水没有溢出。水面上升多少厘米？

  1. 上升的水体是一个以水箱半径 $6$ 为底、以上升高度 $d$ 为高的薄圆柱，其体积必须等于球的体积。
  2. 球的体积：$\tfrac43\pi(3)^3=36\pi$。
  3. 令 $\pi(6)^2 d=36\pi$，即 $36\pi d=36\pi$，所以 $d=1$。
  4. 水面上升 $1$ cm。$\pi$ 被约去，这是思路正确的快速信号。

Scale factors: similar figures比例因子与相似图形

Two figures are similar when one is an enlargement or reduction of the other: all corresponding angles are equal and all corresponding lengths are in the same ratio. That common ratio is the scale factor $k$. If the scale factor is $k$, every length (side, radius, height, perimeter) is multiplied by $k$, every area is multiplied by $k^2$, and every volume is multiplied by $k^3$. In words: the ratio of areas is the scale factor squared, and volumes scale by the scale factor cubed.

当一个图形是另一个图形的放大或缩小，即所有对应角相等、所有对应线段成同一比例时，两个图形相似（similar）。这个共同的比值叫比例因子（scale factor）$k$。若比例因子为 $k$，则所有长度（边长、半径、高、周长）乘 $k$，所有面积乘 $k^2$（面积比是比例因子的平方，squared），所有体积乘 $k^3$（体积按比例因子的立方，cubed）。

$$\dfrac{\text{new length}}{\text{old length}}=k,\qquad \dfrac{\text{new area}}{\text{old area}}=k^2,\qquad \dfrac{\text{new volume}}{\text{old volume}}=k^3$$
$$\dfrac{\text{new length}}{\text{old length}}=k,\qquad \dfrac{\text{new area}}{\text{old area}}=k^2,\qquad \dfrac{\text{new volume}}{\text{old volume}}=k^3$$
Worked Example 4.5: scaling length, area and volume例题 4.5：长度、面积与体积的缩放

Two similar solids have corresponding lengths in the ratio $2:5$ (small to large). The small solid has surface area $48$ and volume $40$. Find the surface area and volume of the large solid.

  1. Scale factor from small to large: $k=\dfrac52$.
  2. Surface area scales by $k^2=\dfrac{25}{4}$: $48\cdot\dfrac{25}{4}=300$.
  3. Volume scales by $k^3=\dfrac{125}{8}$: $40\cdot\dfrac{125}{8}=625$.
  4. Check: $\dfrac{300}{48}=6.25=2.5^2$ and $\dfrac{625}{40}=15.625=2.5^3$.

两个相似立体的对应长度之比为 $2:5$（小比大）。小立体的表面积为 $48$，体积为 $40$。求大立体的表面积和体积。

  1. 从小到大的比例因子：$k=\dfrac52$。
  2. 表面积按 $k^2=\dfrac{25}{4}$ 缩放：$48\cdot\dfrac{25}{4}=300$。
  3. 体积按 $k^3=\dfrac{125}{8}$ 缩放：$40\cdot\dfrac{125}{8}=625$。
  4. 检验：$\dfrac{300}{48}=6.25=2.5^2$，$\dfrac{625}{40}=15.625=2.5^3$。

Common mistakes
  • Using the diameter in $\pi r^2$. Halve it first.
  • Multiplying an area by $k$ instead of $k^2$, or a volume by $k$ or $k^2$ instead of $k^3$.
  • Using a slanted leg as the height of a trapezoid or triangle. The height is perpendicular to the base.
  • Using the height $h$ instead of the slant height $l$ in the cone's curved surface $\pi r l$.
  • Counting an internal seam in a composite perimeter.
常见错误
  • 把直径直接代入 $\pi r^2$。先把直径除以 2。
  • 面积乘 $k$ 而不是 $k^2$，或体积乘 $k$、$k^2$ 而不是 $k^3$。
  • 把斜腰当作梯形或三角形的高。高必须垂直于底。
  • 用高 $h$ 代替母线 $l$，计算圆锥侧面积 $\pi r l$。
  • 组合图形的周长中把内部接缝算进去。

Lines, Angles and Triangles直线、角与三角形

Lines and angles直线与角

Two angles are supplementary when they add to $180^\circ$ (for example the two angles that sit side by side on a straight line), and complementary when they add to $90^\circ$. When two lines cross, the opposite angles are vertical angles and are equal; each is supplementary to its neighbours. A transversal is a line that crosses two or more other lines. If the other two lines are parallel, the transversal creates only two different angle measures, one acute and one obtuse (or two right angles), and the relationships in the table hold.

Triangles: angle sum, exterior angle, isosceles三角形：内角和、外角、等腰

The three interior angles of any triangle add to $180^\circ$ (the angle sum, 180 degrees). An exterior angle is formed by extending one side; it equals the sum of the two interior angles not next to it (the remote interior angles), because it is also supplementary to the interior angle beside it. In an isosceles triangle two sides are equal, and the angles opposite those sides are equal; an equilateral triangle has three equal sides and three $60^\circ$ angles. In any triangle the longest side is opposite the largest angle.

任意三角形的三个内角之和为 $180^\circ$（内角和，angle sum）。延长一边得到外角（exterior angle）；外角等于不与它相邻的两个内角之和，因为它同时与相邻内角互补。等腰（isosceles）三角形有两条边相等，这两条边所对的角也相等；等边三角形三边相等，三个角都是 $60^\circ$。任何三角形中，最长边对着最大角。

In triangle $ABC$, $AB=AC$. Side $BC$ is extended past $C$ to point $D$, and the exterior angle $\angle ACD$ measures $130^\circ$ (Figure 4.4). Find the three interior angles.

  1. $\angle ACB$ and $\angle ACD$ lie on a straight line, so $\angle ACB=180^\circ-130^\circ=50^\circ$.

Congruent and similar triangles全等与相似三角形

Two triangles are congruent when they have the same shape and size, so all corresponding sides and angles are equal. Enough information to prove it is SSS (three sides), SAS (two sides and the included angle), ASA or AAS (two angles and a side). Knowing only three angles (AAA) gives the same shape but not the same size, and knowing two sides and a non-included angle (SSA) does not fix the triangle in general. Two triangles are similar triangles when corresponding angles are equal, which happens as soon as two pairs of angles match (AA); their corresponding sides are then proportional. A line drawn parallel to one side of a triangle cuts off a smaller triangle similar to the whole one.

形状和大小都相同、所有对应边和对应角都相等的两个三角形全等（congruent）。足以证明全等的条件有 SSS（三边）、SAS（两边及其夹角）、ASA 或 AAS（两角及一边）。只知道三个角（AAA）只能得到形状相同、大小未定；只知道两边及非夹角（SSA）一般也不能确定三角形。当对应角相等（只要有两对角相等即可，AA）时，两个三角形相似（similar triangles），对应边成比例。作一条平行于三角形一边的直线，截出的小三角形与原三角形相似。

Triangle inequality and sufficiency of information三角形不等式与信息充分性

The triangle inequality says every side of a triangle is shorter than the sum of the other two. For sides $a$ and $b$, the third side $c$ must satisfy $|a-b|<c<a+b$. A sufficiency question asks whether the given facts are enough to fix the answer. For triangles, ask which of SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS (congruence) or AA (similarity) the facts supply; if none, the facts are not sufficient, even when the diagram looks fixed.

三角形不等式（triangle inequality）指三角形每条边都小于另外两边之和。已知两边 $a$、$b$ 时，第三边 $c$ 须满足 $|a-b|<c<a+b$。充分性（sufficiency）问题问所给事实是否足以确定答案。对三角形而言，看这些事实提供了 SSS、SAS、ASA、AAS（全等）或 AA（相似）中的哪一个；一个也没有，就是信息不充分，即使图看起来是确定的。

The Pythagorean theorem勾股定理

In a right triangle the side opposite the right angle is the hypotenuse, the longest side. The Pythagorean theorem says the squares on the two legs add to the square on the hypotenuse: $a^2+b^2=c^2$. It works in two directions: given two sides you can find the third, and given three sides you can test whether the triangle is right-angled. Whole-number triples that appear often are $3\text{-}4\text{-}5$, $5\text{-}12\text{-}13$, $8\text{-}15\text{-}17$ and $7\text{-}24\text{-}25$, and every multiple of a triple is another triple (for example $6\text{-}8\text{-}10$). Spotting a triple saves a square root.

直角三角形中，直角所对的边是斜边（hypotenuse），也是最长的边。勾股定理（Pythagorean theorem）说两条直角边的平方和等于斜边的平方：$a^2+b^2=c^2$。它可以双向使用：已知两边求第三边；已知三边判断是否为直角三角形。常见的整数三元组有 $3\text{-}4\text{-}5$、$5\text{-}12\text{-}13$、$8\text{-}15\text{-}17$、$7\text{-}24\text{-}25$，三元组的任意倍数仍是三元组（如 $6\text{-}8\text{-}10$）。认出三元组可以省去开平方。

Special right triangles: 45-45-90 and 30-60-90特殊直角三角形：45-45-90 与 30-60-90

Two right triangles have fixed side ratios, so you can write their sides without the Pythagorean theorem. A 45-45-90 triangle is half of a square cut along its diagonal: the legs are equal ($s$) and the hypotenuse is $s\sqrt2$. A 30-60-90 triangle is half of an equilateral triangle cut along its height: if the short leg (opposite $30^\circ$) is $x$, the long leg (opposite $60^\circ$) is $x\sqrt3$ and the hypotenuse is $2x$.

有两种直角三角形的边长比是固定的，因此不用勾股定理也能写出各边。45-45-90 三角形是正方形沿对角线切开的一半：两条直角边相等（$s$），斜边为 $s\sqrt2$。30-60-90 三角形是等边三角形沿高切开的一半：若短直角边（$30^\circ$ 所对）为 $x$，则长直角边（$60^\circ$ 所对）为 $x\sqrt3$，斜边为 $2x$。

Sine, cosine, tangent and SOH-CAH-TOA正弦、余弦、正切与 SOH-CAH-TOA

For an acute angle $\theta$ in a right triangle, name the sides relative to $\theta$: the opposite side faces $\theta$, the adjacent side touches $\theta$ (and is not the hypotenuse), and the hypotenuse is opposite the right angle. The sine, cosine and tangent of $\theta$ are the ratios memorized as SOH-CAH-TOA.

对直角三角形中的锐角 $\theta$，按 $\theta$ 来命名边：对边（opposite）正对 $\theta$，邻边（adjacent）与 $\theta$ 相接（且不是斜边），斜边对着直角。$\theta$ 的正弦（sine）、余弦（cosine）、正切（tangent）就是口诀 SOH-CAH-TOA 所记的三个比值。

$$\sin\theta=\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}},\qquad \cos\theta=\dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}},\qquad \tan\theta=\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$$
$$\sin\theta=\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}},\qquad \cos\theta=\dfrac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}},\qquad \tan\theta=\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$$

To find a side, write the ratio that contains the known side and the unknown side, then solve. To find an angle, use the inverse: $\theta=\sin^{-1}(\cdot)$, $\cos^{-1}(\cdot)$ or $\tan^{-1}(\cdot)$. Note that $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$.

Complementary angles and cofunctions余角与余函数

The two acute angles of a right triangle are complementary (they add to $90^\circ$). The side opposite one acute angle is the side adjacent to the other, so the sine of one equals the cosine of the other: $\sin x^\circ=\cos(90-x)^\circ$. In radians the same fact reads $\sin x=\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x\right)$. Whenever a question sets a sine equal to a cosine of a different angle, suspect that the two angles add to $90^\circ$.

直角三角形的两个锐角互余（complementary，和为 $90^\circ$）。一个锐角的对边正是另一个锐角的邻边，所以一个角的正弦等于另一个角的余弦：$\sin x^\circ=\cos(90-x)^\circ$。用弧度写为 $\sin x=\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-x\right)$。当题目把某个正弦与另一个角的余弦设为相等时，要想到这两个角之和是 $90^\circ$。

A radian measures an angle by the arc it cuts off on a circle of radius $1$: an angle of $\theta$ radians cuts an arc of length $\theta r$ on a circle of radius $r$. Circumference, arc length and sector area周长、弧长与扇形面积

The circumference of a circle is $C=2\pi r=\pi d$ and its area is $A=\pi r^2$. A central angle has its vertex at the center. It cuts off an arc and, together with the two radii, a sector (a slice). Both the arc and the sector are the same fraction of the whole circle as the central angle is of a full turn, so for a central angle of $\theta^\circ$ the fraction is $\dfrac{\theta}{360}$.

圆的周长（circumference）为 $C=2\pi r=\pi d$，面积为 $A=\pi r^2$。圆心角（central angle）的顶点在圆心，它截出一段弧（arc），并与两条半径围成一个扇形（sector，像一块切片）。弧和扇形占整个圆的比例，等于圆心角占整圈的比例，所以圆心角为 $\theta^\circ$ 时，这个比例是 $\dfrac{\theta}{360}$。

$$\text{arc length}=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\cdot 2\pi r,\qquad \text{sector area}=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\cdot \pi r^2$$
$$\text{arc length}=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\cdot 2\pi r,\qquad \text{sector area}=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\cdot \pi r^2$$

In radians the central angle $\theta$ gives arc length $s=r\theta$ and sector area $\tfrac12 r^2\theta$. The two forms agree because $\theta^\circ=\theta\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{180}$ radians.

用弧度时，圆心角 $\theta$ 对应的弧长（arc length）为 $s=r\theta$，扇形面积为 $\tfrac12 r^2\theta$。两种写法一致，因为 $\theta^\circ=\theta\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{180}$ 弧度。

The equation of a circle and completing the square圆的方程与配方

The equation of a circle with center $(h,k)$ and radius $r$ is $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$. It is the distance formula in disguise: every point on the circle is exactly $r$ from the center. Watch the signs: $(x+4)^2$ means $h=-4$. When the equation is given expanded, such as $x^2+y^2+8x-2y+8=0$, use completing the square on the $x$ terms and on the $y$ terms to get back to the standard form: add $\left(\dfrac{b}{2}\right)^2$ to $x^2+bx$.

圆心为 $(h,k)$、半径为 $r$ 的圆的方程（equation of a circle）是 $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$。它其实就是距离公式：圆上每个点到圆心（center）的距离都恰好是半径（radius）$r$。注意符号：$(x+4)^2$ 表示 $h=-4$。当方程是展开形式，例如 $x^2+y^2+8x-2y+8=0$ 时，对 $x$ 项和 $y$ 项分别配方（completing the square）即可回到标准形式：给 $x^2+bx$ 加上 $\left(\dfrac{b}{2}\right)^2$。

The equation $x^2+y^2+8x-2y+8=0$ describes a circle. Find its center and radius.

  2. 分别配方。对 $x$：$\left(\dfrac82\right)^2=16$。对 $y$：$\left(\dfrac{-2}{2}\right)^2=1$。两边同时加上这两项：$(x^2+8x+16)+(y^2-2y+1)=-8+16+1$。

Tangents, chords and inscribed angles切线、弦与圆周角

A tangent touches the circle at exactly one point and is perpendicular to the radius drawn to that point. The two tangent segments from one outside point to a circle are equal in length. A chord joins two points of the circle; a perpendicular from the center to a chord bisects it, and a chord through the center is a diameter. An inscribed angle has its vertex on the circle; it is half of the central angle that cuts off the same arc, so an inscribed angle that cuts off a diameter is $90^\circ$.

切线（tangent）与圆恰有一个公共点，并垂直于过该点的半径。从圆外一点向圆作的两条切线段长度相等。弦（chord）连接圆上两点；从圆心向弦作的垂线平分该弦，过圆心的弦就是直径。圆周角（inscribed angle）的顶点在圆上，它等于对着同一段弧的圆心角的一半，所以对着直径的圆周角是 $90^\circ$。

Worked Example 4.21: tangent length and distance to a chord例题 4.21：切线长与弦心距

(a) Point $P$ is $13$ from the center $O$ of a circle of radius $5$ (Figure 4.11). Find the length of tangent $PT$. (b) In a circle of radius $10$, a chord is $16$ long. How far is the chord from the center?

  1. (a) The radius $OT$ is perpendicular to the tangent $PT$, so triangle $OTP$ is right-angled at $T$ with hypotenuse $OP=13$.
  2. $PT=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=\sqrt{144}=12$.
  3. (b) The perpendicular from the center bisects the chord, giving a right triangle with hypotenuse $10$ (the radius) and one leg $8$ (half the chord).
  4. The distance is $\sqrt{10^2-8^2}=\sqrt{36}=6$.

(a) 点 $P$ 到半径为 $5$ 的圆的圆心 $O$ 的距离为 $13$（图 4.11）。求切线段 $PT$ 的长。(b) 半径为 $10$ 的圆中，一条弦长 $16$。弦到圆心的距离是多少？

  1. (a) 半径 $OT$ 垂直于切线 $PT$，所以三角形 $OTP$ 在 $T$ 处是直角，斜边为 $OP=13$。
  2. $PT=\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=\sqrt{144}=12$。
  3. (b) 从圆心作的垂线平分弦，得到一个直角三角形：斜边为 $10$（半径），一条直角边为 $8$（弦的一半）。
  4. 距离为 $\sqrt{10^2-8^2}=\sqrt{36}=6$。
Worked Example 4.22: central and inscribed angles例题 4.22：圆心角与圆周角

In Figure 4.12 the central angle $\angle AOB$ is $100^\circ$. (a) Find the inscribed angle $\angle ACB$. (b) If $AB$ were a diameter, what would $\angle ACB$ be? (c) An inscribed angle of $35^\circ$ cuts off an arc; what central angle cuts off the same arc?

  1. (a) The inscribed angle is half the central angle on the same arc: $\dfrac{100^\circ}{2}=50^\circ$.
  2. (b) A diameter is a central angle of $180^\circ$, so the inscribed angle is $90^\circ$.
  3. (c) The central angle is double: $2\cdot 35^\circ=70^\circ$.

图 4.12 中圆心角 $\angle AOB$ 为 $100^\circ$。(a) 求圆周角 $\angle ACB$。(b) 若 $AB$ 是直径，$\angle ACB$ 是多少？(c) 一个 $35^\circ$ 的圆周角截出一段弧，对着同一段弧的圆心角是多少？

  1. (a) 圆周角是同弧所对圆心角的一半：$\dfrac{100^\circ}{2}=50^\circ$。
  2. (b) 直径对应的圆心角是 $180^\circ$，所以圆周角是 $90^\circ$。
  3. (c) 圆心角是它的两倍：$2\cdot 35^\circ=70^\circ$。

Common mistakes
  • Reading the center from $(x+4)^2$ as $x=4$. The sign flips: the center is at $-4$.
  • Giving $r^2$ as the radius. In $(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=25$ the radius is $5$.
  • Forgetting to add the completed-square amounts to the right side as well.
  • Mixing a degree fraction $\dfrac{\theta}{360}$ with a radian formula $r\theta$.
  • Doubling the inscribed angle when you should halve the central angle, or the reverse.
常见错误
  • 把 $(x+4)^2$ 的圆心读成 $x=4$。符号要反过来：圆心在 $-4$。
  • 把 $r^2$ 当作半径。在 $(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=25$ 中半径是 $5$。
  • 配方后忘记把加上的数同时加到等号右边。
  • 把度数比例 $\dfrac{\theta}{360}$ 与弧度公式 $r\theta$ 混用。
  • 该把圆心角减半时却把圆周角加倍，或者相反。
