Q 1第1题EASY基础3.15 The chi-square statistic and distribution3.15 卡方统计量与卡方分布CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A random sample of 70 customers each choose one of four lunch specials. If the four specials are equally popular, the expected count for each special is 17.5. The observed counts are 12, 18, 25 and 15. What is the value of the chi-square statistic $\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$?
从 70 名顾客的随机样本中，每人选择四种午餐特价套餐之一。如果四种套餐同样受欢迎，每种的期望计数（expected count）都是 17.5。观测计数（observed count）为 12、18、25 和 15。卡方统计量（chi-square statistic） $\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$ 的值是多少？
Answer: C答案：C

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Each category contributes $\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$ with $E=17.5$: $\frac{(12-17.5)^2}{17.5}=1.729$, $\frac{(18-17.5)^2}{17.5}=0.014$, $\frac{(25-17.5)^2}{17.5}=3.214$, $\frac{(15-17.5)^2}{17.5}=0.357$. Adding gives $\chi^2=5.314\approx5.31$.每个类别的贡献为 $\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$，其中 $E=17.5$：$\frac{(12-17.5)^2}{17.5}=1.729$，$\frac{(18-17.5)^2}{17.5}=0.014$，$\frac{(25-17.5)^2}{17.5}=3.214$，$\frac{(15-17.5)^2}{17.5}=0.357$。相加得 $\chi^2=5.314\approx5.31$。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. Divides each squared difference by the observed count $O$ instead of the expected count $E$: $\frac{30.25}{12}+\frac{0.25}{18}+\frac{56.25}{25}+\frac{6.25}{15}=5.20$.把每个平方差除以观测计数 $O$ 而不是期望计数 $E$：$\frac{30.25}{12}+\frac{0.25}{18}+\frac{56.25}{25}+\frac{6.25}{15}=5.20$。
  2. Drops the square and adds the sizes of the differences over $E$: $\frac{5.5+0.5+7.5+2.5}{17.5}=0.91$. Without the square the signed differences cancel to 0.漏掉平方，只把差的大小除以 $E$ 后相加：$\frac{5.5+0.5+7.5+2.5}{17.5}=0.91$。不平方时带符号的差会相互抵消为 0。
  3. Correct: the four contributions add to 5.314.正确：四项贡献之和为 5.314。
  4. Adds the squared differences and never divides by $E$: $30.25+0.25+56.25+6.25=93.0$.把平方差直接相加而没有除以 $E$：$30.25+0.25+56.25+6.25=93.0$。
Square the difference, divide by the expected count, then add: each step of the formula has a job.先平方、再除以期望计数、最后相加：公式的每一步都有它的作用。

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The p-value is the right-tail area, so the lower bound is the statistic and the upper bound is a huge number. With $k=5$ categories, $\text{df}=k-1=4$, so the command is $\chi^2\text{cdf}(9.2,\,1E99,\,4)=0.056$.p 值是右尾面积，所以下限取统计量，上限取一个很大的数。$k=5$ 个类别时 $\text{df}=k-1=4$，命令为 $\chi^2\text{cdf}(9.2,\,1E99,\,4)=0.056$。

Why the options attract (M 3)选项误区（M 3）
  1. Finds the left-tail area: $\chi^2\text{cdf}(0,9.2,4)=0.944$, which is $1-p$.求的是左尾面积：$\chi^2\text{cdf}(0,9.2,4)=0.944$，等于 $1-p$。
  2. Doubles the tail as for a two-sided z or t test, giving 0.113. A chi-square test has no two-sided version.像双侧 z 或 t 检验那样把尾部面积加倍，得到 0.113。卡方检验没有双侧版本。
  3. Uses $\text{df}=k=5$ instead of $k-1=4$, which gives 0.101.用 $\text{df}=k=5$ 而不是 $k-1=4$，得到 0.101。
  4. Correct: right tail, $\text{df}=4$, value 0.056.正确：右尾，$\text{df}=4$，值为 0.056。
Write the degrees of freedom before touching the calculator: categories minus one for goodness of fit.先写出自由度再按计算器：拟合优度检验的自由度是类别数减一。

Q 4第4题MEDIUM中等3.15 The chi-square statistic and distribution3.15 卡方统计量与卡方分布CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
Two chi-square tests each report the same statistic, $\chi^2=9.0$. Test A has $\text{df}=2$ and Test B has $\text{df}=8$. Without calculating either p-value, which statement is correct?
两项卡方检验报告了相同的统计量 $\chi^2=9.0$。检验 A 的 $\text{df}=2$，检验 B 的 $\text{df}=8$。不计算 p 值，下列哪项正确？
Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The mean of a chi-square distribution is its df, so compare the statistic with the mean of its own curve. Test A: the mean is 2, and $\frac{9.0}{2}=4.5$ times the mean puts the statistic far out in the right tail. Test B: the mean is 8, and $\frac{9.0}{8}=1.125$ times the mean is only slightly above the middle of the curve, which is typical. A statistic that is typical has a large right-tail area and one far out has a small area, so Test B has the larger p-value (about 0.342, against about 0.011 for Test A).卡方分布的均值等于自由度（degrees of freedom），所以要把统计量与它自己那条曲线的均值比较。检验 A：均值为 2，$\frac{9.0}{2}=4.5$ 倍于均值，统计量位于右尾很远处。检验 B：均值为 8，$\frac{9.0}{8}=1.125$ 倍于均值，只略高于曲线的中部，属于典型值。典型的统计量右尾面积大，远在尾部的统计量面积小，所以检验 B 的 p 值更大（约 0.342，检验 A 约 0.011）。

Why the options attract (M 3)选项误区（M 3）
  1. Correct: compare the statistic with the mean df of each curve.正确：把统计量与每条曲线的均值（即自由度）比较。
  2. Reverses the comparison: with $\text{df}=2$ the p-value is about 0.011, much smaller than 0.342 for $\text{df}=8$.把比较方向弄反了：$\text{df}=2$ 时 p 值约为 0.011，远小于 $\text{df}=8$ 时的 0.342。
  3. Treats the statistic alone as the evidence. The same $\chi^2$ is ordinary on one curve and extreme on another, so the df must be reported with it.只看统计量本身。同一个 $\chi^2$ 在一条曲线上很普通，在另一条上却很极端，所以必须同时给出自由度。
  4. The sample size enters only through the statistic. Once $\chi^2$ and df are known, the p-value is fixed.样本量只通过统计量起作用。已知 $\chi^2$ 与自由度后，p 值就确定了。
Always judge a chi-square value against its own curve: a statistic near the df is typical, one far above it is surprising.永远用卡方统计量所在的那条曲线来判断它：接近自由度的值是典型的，远高于自由度的值才令人意外。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）

  4. Correct: 1.309, with expected counts 75, 45 and 30.正确：1.309，期望计数为 75、45、30。
Build the expected counts first, $E=np$, and check that they add to $n$ before computing any contribution.先算期望计数 $E=np$，并检查它们的总和等于 $n$，再去算每项贡献。

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The question has a direction, so $H_a:p_1\gt p_2$ (with $p_i$ the completion proportion for App $i$ among volunteers like these). (d) 1 point1 分
