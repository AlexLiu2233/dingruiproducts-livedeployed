Q1EASY 12.1 Gauss's Law for Magnetism12.1 磁场的高斯定律Calculator可用计算器

A closed surface of any shape completely encloses a bar magnet. The net magnetic flux $\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A}$ through the surface is一个任意形状的闭合曲面将一根条形磁铁完全包围。通过该曲面的净磁通量 $\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A}$ 为

  • (A) zero零
  • (B) positive, because the north pole acts as a source of field lines正，因为北极是磁感线的源
  • (C) negative, because the south pole acts as a sink of field lines负，因为南极是磁感线的汇
  • (D) positive or negative depending on the strength of the magnet取决于磁铁强弱，可正可负
Answer:答案： (A)
Every magnetic field line is a closed loop, so each line that leaves the closed surface also enters it. Gauss's law for magnetism, $\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A}=0$, holds for every closed surface, whatever the magnet's strength or position.每条磁感线都是闭合曲线，所以离开闭合曲面的每条磁感线也必定穿入该曲面。磁场的高斯定律 $\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A}=0$ 对任意闭合曲面成立，与磁铁强弱和位置无关。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) treats the north pole like a positive electric charge in $\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A}=Q_\text{enc}/\epsilon_0$; there is no isolated magnetic charge, so no net outward flux. (C) is the same borrowed picture with the south pole as a negative charge. (D) makes the flux depend on strength, but a stronger magnet only packs more lines into the same closed loops, so the net count stays zero.(B) 把北极当成 $\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A}=Q_\text{enc}/\epsilon_0$ 中的正电荷；但不存在孤立的磁荷，所以没有净向外的磁通量。(C) 是同一种类比，把南极当成负电荷。(D) 认为磁通量取决于强弱，但更强的磁铁只是在同样的闭合回路里放了更多磁感线，净数仍为零。
Insight.要点。 Gauss's law for magnetism is the statement that there are no magnetic monopoles. Contrast it with the electric case: a closed surface can enclose net charge, but it can never enclose net magnetic charge.磁场的高斯定律表明不存在磁单极子。与电场对比：闭合曲面可以包围净电荷，却永远不能包围净磁荷。
Q1EASY 12.1 Poles of a Cut Magnet12.1 被切开的磁铁的磁极Calculator可用计算器

A bar magnet is sawed in half across its middle, so that each cut face was inside the original magnet. Which statement about the two pieces is correct?一根条形磁铁沿中部被锯成两半，每个切面原先都位于磁铁内部。关于这两块，下列说法正确的是

  • (A) One piece has only a north pole and the other piece has only a south pole.一块只有北极，另一块只有南极。
  • (B) Neither piece is magnetic, because the cut destroys the alignment of the atomic dipoles.两块都没有磁性，因为切割破坏了原子磁偶极子的排列。
  • (C) Each piece is a magnet with its own north pole and its own south pole.每一块都是磁铁，各有自己的北极和南极。
  • (D) Each piece has two poles, but its field lines no longer form closed loops.每一块都有两个磁极，但其磁感线不再构成闭合回路。
Answer:答案： (C)
No magnetic pole is ever found in isolation. The magnet is made of aligned magnetic dipoles, and cutting it does not change the alignment of the dipoles in either piece. Each piece therefore has a north pole and a south pole: a new south pole appears on the cut face of the piece that kept the original north end, and a new north pole appears on the cut face of the other piece.磁极从不孤立存在。磁铁由排列整齐的磁偶极子构成，切割并不改变每一块中偶极子的排列。因此每一块都有北极和南极：保留原北极端的那一块，切面上出现新的南极；另一块的切面上则出现新的北极。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) treats a magnetic pole like an electric charge that can be separated; no isolated north or south pole has ever been found. (B) assumes that the cut ruins the magnetization, but the alignment of the dipoles inside each piece is unchanged. (D) forgets that the field lines of any magnet close on themselves: the lines of each piece leave its north pole and return to its south pole.(A) 把磁极当成可以分开的电荷；从未发现过孤立的北极或南极。(B) 以为切割会破坏磁化，但每一块内部偶极子的排列并未改变。(D) 忘了任何磁铁的磁感线都是闭合的：每一块的磁感线从自己的北极出发，回到自己的南极。
Insight.要点。 Cutting a magnet makes two smaller magnets, never two isolated poles. This is why magnetic field lines always form closed loops.切开磁铁得到的是两块更小的磁铁，而不是两个孤立的磁极。这正是磁感线总是闭合回路的原因。

A piece of unmagnetized iron is brought near the north pole of a bar magnet and is pulled toward it. The best explanation is that把一块未磁化的铁靠近条形磁铁的北极，铁被吸向磁铁。最合理的解释是

  • (A) the iron carries a net electric charge, which the magnet attracts铁带有净电荷，被磁铁吸引
  • (B) the magnet's field aligns the iron's atomic dipoles, so the iron becomes magnetized with a south pole facing the magnet磁铁的磁场使铁的原子偶极子排列，铁被磁化，朝向磁铁的一端成为南极

  • (D) the magnet's field exerts a force on the iron's stationary electrons磁铁的磁场对铁中静止的电子施力
Answer:答案： (B)
A ferromagnet's atomic dipoles line up with an external field. The induced magnetization puts a south pole on the end nearest the magnet's north pole, so the pair attract. This is induced magnetism: the alignment is produced by the external field.铁磁体的原子偶极子会沿外磁场排列。感应磁化使最靠近磁铁北极的一端成为南极，因此二者相互吸引。这就是感应磁性：排列由外磁场产生。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) invents a net charge; the iron is neutral and a static magnet does not attract neutral matter electrically. (C) assumes isolated poles exist, but every piece of iron is made of dipoles with both poles. (D) fails because $\vec{F}=q\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$ is zero for a charge at rest.(A) 凭空设想净电荷；铁是电中性的，静止磁铁不会通过电学作用吸引中性物质。(C) 假定存在孤立磁极，但铁由同时有两极的偶极子构成。(D) 不成立，因为静止电荷所受的 $\vec{F}=q\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$ 为零。
Insight.要点。 Induced magnetism is alignment of existing dipoles by an outside field. That is why unmagnetized iron is attracted to either pole of a magnet.感应磁性是外场使已有偶极子排列。这就是为什么未磁化的铁会被磁铁的任一极吸引。

Q3EASY 12.2 Force on a Moving Charge12.2 运动电荷所受的力Calculator可用计算器

A proton moves in the $+x$ direction through a uniform magnetic field directed along $+z$. The magnetic force on the proton at that instant points in the direction一个质子沿 $+x$ 方向穿过沿 $+z$ 方向的匀强磁场。该时刻质子所受的磁力方向为

  • (A) $+y$
  • (B) $+z$
  • (C) $-y$
  • (D) the force is zero力为零
Answer:答案： (C)
For a positive charge $\vec{F}=q\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$, and $\hat{x}\times\hat{z}=-\hat{y}$. The force is along $-y$, perpendicular to both $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{B}$.对正电荷 $\vec{F}=q\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$，而 $\hat{x}\times\hat{z}=-\hat{y}$。力沿 $-y$，同时垂直于 $\vec{v}$ 和 $\vec{B}$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $+y$ comes from the reversed order $\hat{z}\times\hat{x}=+\hat{y}$, or from using the sign for an electron. (B) $+z$ puts the force along $\vec{B}$; the magnetic force is never parallel to the field. (D) confuses perpendicular with parallel: the force is zero only when $\vec{v}\parallel\vec{B}$, and is largest when they are perpendicular.(A) $+y$ 来自叉乘次序颠倒 $\hat{z}\times\hat{x}=+\hat{y}$，或误用了电子的符号。(B) $+z$ 让力沿 $\vec{B}$ 方向；磁力永远不会平行于磁场。(D) 混淆了垂直与平行：只有 $\vec{v}\parallel\vec{B}$ 时力才为零，二者垂直时力最大。
Insight.要点。 Write the cross product in the order $\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$ and use $\hat{x}\times\hat{y}=\hat{z}$ cyclically. The force direction flips for a negative charge.按 $\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$ 的次序写叉乘，并循环使用 $\hat{x}\times\hat{y}=\hat{z}$。负电荷的力方向相反。
Answer:答案： (C)
Use the right-hand rule for a positive charge: point the fingers along the velocity ($+x$) and curl them toward the field ($+z$). The thumb points along $-y$. The force is perpendicular to both $\vec v$ and $\vec B$, and its magnitude is $|q|vB$ because $\vec v\perp\vec B$.对正电荷用右手定则：四指沿速度方向（$+x$），向磁场方向（$+z$）弯曲，拇指指向 $-y$。力垂直于 $\vec v$ 和 $\vec B$，由于 $\vec v\perp\vec B$，其大小为 $|q|vB$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $+y$ comes from curling the fingers from the field toward the velocity, the wrong order, or from using the rule for a negative charge. (B) $+z$ puts the force along $\vec B$; the magnetic force is never parallel to the field. (D) confuses perpendicular with parallel: the force is zero only when $\vec v$ is parallel to $\vec B$, and is largest when they are perpendicular.(A) $+y$ 来自把四指从磁场方向向速度方向弯曲（顺序错了），或用了负电荷的规则。(B) $+z$ 让力沿 $\vec B$；磁力从不平行于磁场。(D) 把垂直和平行弄混了：只有 $\vec v$ 平行于 $\vec B$ 时力才为零，二者垂直时力最大。
Insight.要点。 Fingers along $\vec v$, curl toward $\vec B$, thumb gives the force on a positive charge. The force direction flips for a negative charge.四指沿 $\vec v$，向 $\vec B$ 弯曲，拇指给出正电荷所受力的方向。对负电荷，力的方向相反。
Q4MEDIUM 12.2 Circular Motion in a Field12.2 磁场中的圆周运动Calculator可用计算器

A proton and an alpha particle (mass $4m_p$, charge $+2e$) enter the same uniform magnetic field with the same speed, perpendicular to the field. The ratio of the alpha particle's orbit radius to the proton's, $r_\alpha/r_p$, is一个质子和一个 $\alpha$ 粒子（质量 $4m_p$，电荷 $+2e$）以相同的速率垂直射入同一个匀强磁场。$\alpha$ 粒子轨道半径与质子轨道半径之比 $r_\alpha/r_p$ 为

  • (A) $\tfrac{1}{2}$
  • (B) $1$
  • (C) $2$
  • (D) $4$
Answer:答案： (C)
The magnetic force supplies the centripetal force: $qvB=mv^2/r$, so $r=mv/(qB)$. At equal $v$ and $B$, $r\propto m/q$. Hence $r_\alpha/r_p=(4m_p/2e)/(m_p/e)=2$.磁力提供向心力：$qvB=mv^2/r$，故 $r=mv/(qB)$。在 $v$ 和 $B$ 相同时 $r\propto m/q$。因此 $r_\alpha/r_p=(4m_p/2e)/(m_p/e)=2$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $\tfrac12$ inverts the ratio and uses $q/m$ in place of $m/q$. (B) $1$ counts only the two protons for the mass (using $2m_p$ with $2e$), forgetting the two neutrons. (D) $4$ uses the mass ratio alone and forgets that the larger charge tightens the orbit.(A) $\tfrac12$ 把比值颠倒，用了 $q/m$ 而不是 $m/q$。(B) $1$ 只数了两个质子的质量（用 $2m_p$ 配 $2e$），忘了两个中子。(D) $4$ 只用了质量之比，忘记更大的电荷会使轨道变小。
Insight.要点。 The orbit radius depends on the particle's $m/q$ and momentum, not on its charge or mass separately. Compare $m/q$ first, then check the speed.轨道半径取决于粒子的 $m/q$ 和动量，而不是单独的电荷或质量。先比较 $m/q$，再看速度。
Q5MEDIUM 12.3 Field of a Circular Loop12.3 圆环的磁场Calculator可用计算器

A circular loop of radius $R$ carrying current $I$ produces a field of magnitude $B_0$ at its center. A second loop of radius $2R$ carries current $3I$. The field magnitude at the center of the second loop is半径为 $R$、电流为 $I$ 的圆环在其圆心处产生大小为 $B_0$ 的磁场。第二个圆环半径为 $2R$，电流为 $3I$。第二个圆环圆心处的磁场大小为

  • (B) $\tfrac{3}{2}B_0$

  • (D) $6B_0$

At the center of a loop, $B=\mu_0 I/(2R)$, so $B\propto I/R$. The second loop has three times the current and twice the radius: $B=(3/2)B_0$.圆环圆心处 $B=\mu_0 I/(2R)$，所以 $B\propto I/R$。第二个圆环电流是三倍、半径是两倍：$B=(3/2)B_0$。

(C) $3B_0$ keeps the factor $3$ from the current and forgets the radius. (D) $6B_0$ multiplies by the radius instead of dividing.(A) $\tfrac34B_0$ 把场当作像点电荷那样按 $1/R^2$ 下降；圆环圆心处只按 $1/R$ 下降。(C) $3B_0$ 只保留了电流的因子 $3$，忘了半径。(D) $6B_0$ 把半径乘了上去而不是除。

The field magnitude at a point a distance $3d$ from the second wire is一根通有电流 $I$ 的长直导线在距导线 $d$ 处产生大小为 $B_0$ 的磁场。第二根长直导线通有电流 $2I$。在距第二根导线 $3d$ 处，磁场的大小为

  • (B) $\tfrac{2}{3}B_0$
  • (C) $2B_0$
  • (D) $6B_0$

$$\frac{B}{B_0}=\frac{2I/(3d)}{I/d}=\frac{2}{3}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $\tfrac29B_0$ treats the field as falling like $1/r^2$, as for a point charge; the field of a long wire falls only as $1/r$. Insight.要点。 For a long straight wire read the scaling directly from $B\propto I/r$: current up raises the field, distance up lowers it, and each factor enters to the first power.对长直导线，可直接由 $B\propto I/r$ 读出比例关系：电流增大使磁场增大，距离增大使磁场减小，且两者都只是一次方。

(a) State the direction of the current in the rod that gives an upward magnetic force, and calculate the smallest current that makes the rod float in equilibrium.说明使铜棒受到向上磁力的电流方向，并计算使铜棒悬浮平衡的最小电流。 The force on a straight wire is along the thumb when the fingers point along the current and curl toward $\vec B$ ($+y$). For the thumb to point up ($+z$), the fingers must start along $+x$ and curl toward $+y$, so the current is along $+x$. For equilibrium $ILB=mg$:四指沿电流方向并向 $\vec B$（$+y$）弯曲时，拇指指向导线受力方向。要使拇指向上（$+z$），四指应从 $+x$ 出发向 $+y$ 弯曲，所以电流沿 $+x$。平衡时 $ILB=mg$：
$$I=\frac{mg}{LB}=\frac{(0.030)(9.8)}{(0.40)(0.25)}=2.9~\mathrm{A}$$
(b) The current is now increased to $5.0~\mathrm{A}$. Find the magnitude and direction of the rod's acceleration.现在把电流增大到 $5.0~\mathrm{A}$。求铜棒加速度的大小和方向。
$$a=\frac{ILB-mg}{m}=\frac{(5.0)(0.40)(0.25)-(0.030)(9.8)}{0.030}=\frac{0.500-0.294}{0.030}=6.9~\mathrm{m/s^2}$$
The acceleration is upward.加速度方向向上。
(c) The current is set to $8.0~\mathrm{A}$, and the field is rotated in the horizontal plane so that it makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the rod; its magnitude is still $0.25~\mathrm{T}$ and it still has a $+y$ component. Calculate the magnitude of the magnetic force on the rod and the rod's acceleration.电流调为 $8.0~\mathrm{A}$，并使磁场在水平面内转动，与铜棒成 $30^\circ$ 角；其大小仍为 $0.25~\mathrm{T}$，且仍有 $+y$ 分量。计算铜棒所受磁力的大小和铜棒的加速度。 Only the component of the field perpendicular to the rod acts, so the angle between the current and the field enters as $\sin\theta$ with $\theta=30^\circ$:只有垂直于铜棒的磁场分量起作用，所以电流与磁场的夹角以 $\sin\theta$ 的形式出现，$\theta=30^\circ$：
$$F=ILB\sin30^\circ=(8.0)(0.40)(0.25)(0.50)=0.40~\mathrm{N}$$
The force is vertical (perpendicular to both the rod and the field) and upward, because the field still has a $+y$ component.该力是竖直的（垂直于铜棒和磁场），且向上，因为磁场仍有 $+y$ 分量。
$$a=\frac{F-mg}{m}=\frac{0.40-0.294}{0.030}=3.5~\mathrm{m/s^2}$$
(d) The charge carriers in copper are electrons. For the situation in part (a), determine which face of the rod (top or bottom) becomes negatively charged and which face is at the higher electric potential, and explain how the rod as a whole still experiences an upward force.铜中的载流子是电子。对 (a) 中的情形，确定铜棒的哪个面（上面或下面）带负电、哪个面电势较高，并解释铜棒整体为何仍受到向上的力。 Current along $+x$ means the electrons drift along $-x$. A positive charge moving along $-x$ in a field along $+y$ would be pushed along $-z$ by the right-hand rule, so the negative electrons are pushed along $+z$, upward. The electrons collect on the top face, which becomes negative, and the bottom face is left positive, so the bottom face is at the higher potential.电流沿 $+x$ 意味着电子沿 $-x$ 漂移。沿 $-x$ 运动的正电荷在沿 $+y$ 的磁场中，由右手定则受到沿 $-z$ 的力，所以带负电的电子被推向 $+z$，即向上。电子聚集在上面，使上面带负电，下面留下正电，所以下面电势较高。 This charge separation sets up an electric field across the rod. That electric field acts on the positive lattice ions and pushes them upward, and the lattice carries the whole rod with it. The magnetic force on the moving electrons is passed to the rod through this electric field.这种电荷分离在铜棒内建立了一个横向电场。该电场作用在带正电的晶格离子上，把它们向上推，晶格带着整根铜棒一起运动。作用在运动电子上的磁力正是通过这个电场传递给铜棒的。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A student takes the current along $-x$ because the electrons move that way, reversing the force. (b) A student forgets the weight and gives $a=ILB/m=17~\mathrm{m/s^2}$. (c) A student uses $\cos30^\circ$ instead of $\sin30^\circ$, getting $F=0.69~\mathrm{N}$ and $a=13~\mathrm{m/s^2}$; the sine is the factor that goes with the angle between the current and the field. (d) A student states that the rod feels no force because the electric field cancels the magnetic force on the electrons, forgetting that this field also acts on the lattice ions.(a) 学生因为电子沿相反方向运动而取电流沿 $-x$，使力反向。(b) 学生忘了重力，给出 $a=ILB/m=17~\mathrm{m/s^2}$。(c) 学生用 $\cos30^\circ$ 代替 $\sin30^\circ$，得到 $F=0.69~\mathrm{N}$、$a=13~\mathrm{m/s^2}$；与电流和磁场夹角对应的因子是正弦。(d) 学生说铜棒不受力，因为电场抵消了电子所受的磁力，忘了这个电场同样作用在晶格离子上。
Insight.要点。 The force on a current-carrying wire is the sum of the magnetic forces on its moving charge carriers, passed to the lattice by the electric field of the charge separation (the Hall effect). Conventional current and electron drift are opposite, and only the angle between the current and the field enters through $\sin\theta$.载流导线所受的力等于其运动载流子所受磁力之和，并通过电荷分离产生的电场（霍尔效应）传递给晶格。常规电流与电子漂移方向相反，只有电流与磁场之间的夹角通过 $\sin\theta$ 起作用。
