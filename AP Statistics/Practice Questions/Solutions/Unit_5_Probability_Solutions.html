A bag holds 6 red, 5 blue and 9 green marbles (synthetic). One marble is drawn at random. What is the probability that it is not blue?

Answer: C答案：C

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

All 20 marbles are equally likely. $P(\text{blue})=5/20=0.25$, so by the complement rule $P(\text{not blue})=1-0.25=0.75$. Counting directly gives the same value: $(6+9)/20=15/20=0.75$.20 个球等可能。$P(\text{blue})=5/20=0.25$，由补集（complement）规则 $P(\text{not blue})=1-0.25=0.75$。直接计数结果相同：$(6+9)/20=15/20=0.75$。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. Reports $P(\text{blue})=5/20$ itself and never takes the complement.给出的是 $P(\text{blue})=5/20$ 本身，没有取补集。
  2. Divides the 5 blue marbles by the 15 non-blue marbles, $5/15=0.33$. That is a ratio of blue to not blue; the denominator of a probability is all 20 marbles.用 5 个蓝球除以 15 个非蓝球，$5/15=0.33$。这是蓝球与非蓝球之比；概率的分母应是全部 20 个球。
  3. Correct: the complement of 0.25.正确：0.25 的补集。
  4. Takes the complement of the wrong color: $1-6/20=0.70$ is the probability of not red.对错误的颜色取补集：$1-6/20=0.70$ 是“不是红球”的概率。

A student simulates free throws with random digits: 0 through 5 counts as a make and 6 through 9 as a miss, so the model probability of a make is 0.60 per attempt. In 200 simulated attempts, 118 were makes, a relative frequency of 0.59. Which interpretation is correct?
学生用随机数字模拟（simulation）罚球：0 至 5 记为命中、6 至 9 记为未中，模型给出的每次命中概率为 0.60。200 次模拟中命中 118 次，相对频数（relative frequency）为 0.59。哪种解释正确？
Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The digits 0 to 5 are 6 of 10 equally likely digits, so the model probability is $6/10=0.60$. The simulated relative frequency is $118/200=0.59$. It estimates the model probability, and a rerun with fresh digits would give a different value near 0.60.数字 0 至 5 是 10 个等可能数字中的 6 个，所以模型概率为 $6/10=0.60$。模拟的相对频数为 $118/200=0.59$。它是对模型概率的估计，用新的随机数字重跑会得到接近 0.60 的另一个值。

Why the options attract (M 1)选项误区（M 1）
  1. Treats the statistic $118/200=0.59$ as the true probability. Having 200 trials makes the estimate steadier but never makes it exact.把统计量 $118/200=0.59$ 当成真实概率。200 次试验使估计更稳定，但不会使它精确。
  2. Correct: a simulation proportion estimates the model probability and varies from run to run.正确：模拟得到的比例是对模型概率的估计，各次运行会有变化。
  3. Reads a probability as a guaranteed count in the next 100 attempts; the count is not fixed even if the probability were exactly 0.59.把概率当成下一批 100 次中必然出现的次数；即使概率恰好是 0.59，次数也不是固定的。
  4. The law-of-averages mistake: attempts are independent in the model, so earlier makes do not change the next attempt, which still has probability 0.60.“平均律”的错误：模型中各次尝试相互独立，此前的命中不改变下一次，其概率仍是 0.60。
A simulated proportion is an estimate of the model probability, not the probability itself.模拟得到的比例是对模型概率的估计，而不是概率本身。

Say in context that attending is associated with reporting better sleep; the sample data alone do not show that the workshop caused the change.独立要求 $P(S\mid W)=P(S)$。这里 $0.35\ne0.30$。乘积检验一致：$0.60\times0.30=0.18$ 不等于 $84/400=0.21$。结合情境说明参加讲座与报告睡眠改善有关联；仅凭样本数据不能说明讲座导致了这一变化。

Where this goes wrong (M 2)常见错误（M 2）

Reusing the given conditional as its reversed conditional (0.35); dividing 84 by the grand total, $84/400=0.21$, which is the joint probability; dividing by the 160 non-attendees, $84/160=0.525$; or comparing 0.35 with $P(W)=0.60$ instead of with $P(S)=0.30$ in the independence check.把已知的条件概率直接当作反向条件概率（0.35）；用 84 除以总计，$84/400=0.21$，这是联合概率；除以 160 名未参加者，$84/160=0.525$；或在独立性检验中把 0.35 与 $P(W)=0.60$ 而不是与 $P(S)=0.30$ 比较。

From a table, a conditional probability divides a cell by the row or column named after the bar; reversing the bar means choosing the other total.从表格求条件概率，是用单元格除以竖线之后所指的行或列合计；颠倒竖线就要换用另一个合计。

Q 28第28题HARD较难2.4 probability and long-run relative frequency2.4 概率与长期相对频率CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[10]
Two fair four-sided dice, each with faces 1, 2, 3 and 4, are rolled together (synthetic game).
同时掷两颗公平的四面骰子，每颗的面为 1、2、3、4（合成游戏）。
  1. Describe the sample space and find the probability that the sum of the two faces is 5.描述样本空间，并求两个面的和为 5 的概率。 [2]
  2. Use the complement rule to find the probability that at least one die shows a 4.用补集规则求至少有一颗骰子显示 4 的概率。 [2]
  3. A student rolls the pair 40 times and sees at least one 4 in 15 of the rolls, a relative frequency of 0.375. The student says, "0.375 is not 0.4375, so the calculation in part (b) must be wrong." Evaluate the claim.一名学生把这对骰子掷了 40 次，其中 15 次至少出现一个 4，相对频数为 0.375。学生说：“0.375 不等于 0.4375，所以 (b) 的计算一定错了。”评价这一说法。 [2]
  4. Interpret the probability you found in part (b) as a long-run relative frequency.把 (b) 中求得的概率解释为长期相对频率。 [1]
  5. Given that the sum is 5, find the probability that at least one die shows a 4. Are "the sum is 5" and "at least one die shows a 4" independent? Justify with numbers.已知和为 5，求至少有一颗骰子显示 4 的概率。“和为 5”与“至少一颗骰子显示 4”是否独立？用数字说明理由。 [3]
(a) The sample space has $4\times4=16$ equally likely ordered pairs $(a,b)$ with $a,b\in\{1,2,3,4\}$. The sum is 5 for $(1,4),(2,3),(3,2),(4,1)$, so $P=4/16=0.25$. (b) The complement is "no die shows a 4", which has $3\times3=9$ pairs, so $P(\text{at least one 4})=1-9/16=7/16=0.4375$. (c) The claim is not valid. A relative frequency from 40 rolls is an estimate that varies from sample to sample, so it need not equal 0.4375; 0.375 is a plausible result and does not show the calculation is wrong. More rolls would give a more stable estimate. (d) If the pair of dice were rolled a very large number of times, about 43.75% of the rolls would show at least one 4. (e) Let $S_5$ be "the sum is 5" and $A$ be "at least one 4". Given $S_5$ the sample space shrinks to $(1,4),(2,3),(3,2),(4,1)$, and two of these contain a 4, so $P(A\mid S_5)=2/4=0.50$. This differs from $P(A)=0.4375$, so the events are not independent; equivalently $P(S_5\cap A)=2/16=0.125\ne P(S_5)P(A)=0.25(0.4375)\approx0.109$.(a) 样本空间（sample space）有 $4\times4=16$ 个等可能的有序数对 $(a,b)$，$a,b\in\{1,2,3,4\}$。和为 5 的有 $(1,4),(2,3),(3,2),(4,1)$，所以 $P=4/16=0.25$。(b) 补集（complement）是“没有骰子显示 4”，含 $3\times3=9$ 个数对，所以 $P(\text{at least one 4})=1-9/16=7/16=0.4375$。(c) 该说法不成立。40 次掷骰得到的相对频数（relative frequency）是估计值，会随样本变化，不必等于 0.4375；0.375 是合理的结果，不能说明计算有误。多掷几次会得到更稳定的估计。(d) 如果把这对骰子掷很多很多次，约 43.75% 的次数会至少出现一个 4。(e) 设 $S_5$ 为“和为 5”，$A$ 为“至少一个 4”。已知 $S_5$，样本空间缩小为 $(1,4),(2,3),(3,2),(4,1)$，其中两个含 4，所以 $P(A\mid S_5)=2/4=0.50$。这与 $P(A)=0.4375$ 不同，所以两事件不独立（independent）；等价地 $P(S_5\cap A)=2/16=0.125\ne P(S_5)P(A)=0.25(0.4375)\approx0.109$。

(a) 2 points2 分

One point for describing the 16 equally likely ordered pairs, and one for counting the four pairs with sum 5 and dividing: $4/16=0.25$.一分给描述 16 个等可能的有序数对，一分给数出和为 5 的四个数对并相除：$4/16=0.25$。

(b) 2 points2 分

Counting "at least one 4" directly means listing 7 pairs. The complement is quicker: no 4 on either die has probability $(3/4)^2=9/16$, so the answer is $1-9/16=7/16=0.4375$.直接数“至少一个 4”要列出 7 个数对。补集更快：两颗骰子都不出 4 的概率为 $(3/4)^2=9/16$，所以答案为 $1-9/16=7/16=0.4375$。

(c) 2 points2 分

One point for saying that a relative frequency from 40 rolls is a statistic that varies, so a gap of $0.4375-0.375=0.0625$ is not evidence of an error. One point for the remedy: more rolls give a steadier estimate, by the law of large numbers.一分给说明 40 次掷骰得到的相对频数是会变化的统计量，所以 $0.4375-0.375=0.0625$ 的差距并非计算有误的证据。一分给改进办法：由大数定律（law of large numbers），多掷几次会得到更稳定的估计。

(d) 1 point1 分

The interpretation must name the event and use the long-run frame: in a very large number of rolls, about 43.75% show at least one 4. It does not say that 7 of every 16 rolls show a 4.解释必须点明事件并使用长期框架：在非常多次的掷骰中，约 43.75% 至少出现一个 4。这并不是说每 16 次中恰有 7 次出现 4。

(e) 3 points3 分

One point for restricting to the four outcomes with sum 5 and counting those with a 4 ($2/4=0.50$), one for comparing with $P(A)=0.4375$, and one for the conclusion that the events are not independent, supported by the product check $0.125\ne0.109$.一分给限制在和为 5 的四个结果并数出含 4 的个数（$2/4=0.50$），一分给与 $P(A)=0.4375$ 比较，一分给不独立的结论，并由乘积检验 $0.125\ne0.109$ 支持。

Where this goes wrong (M 1)常见错误（M 1）

Frequent errors: counting 4+4=8 ordered pairs with a 4 from $(4,b)$ and $(a,4)$, which counts $(4,4)$ twice (the true count is 7); using $1/4+1/4=0.5$ and ignoring the overlap; deciding that a simulation or sample result that differs from the exact probability proves the exact value wrong; and writing that exactly 7 of every 16 rolls will show a 4.常见错误：由 $(4,b)$ 与 $(a,4)$ 数出 4+4=8 个含 4 的有序数对，$(4,4)$ 被数了两次（真实个数是 7）；用 $1/4+1/4=0.5$，忽略重叠；认为与精确概率不同的模拟或样本结果能证明精确值错误；以及写成每 16 次中恰有 7 次出现 4。

An exact probability describes the long run; a short run is an estimate that wanders around it, so a mismatch is not evidence against the calculation.精确概率描述的是长期；短期结果只是围绕它波动的估计，所以不一致并不构成对计算的反驳。
