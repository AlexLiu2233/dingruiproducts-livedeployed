Q1EASYPaper 1A4.5 Sample Spaces[5 marks]

Two fair dice. (a) $P(\text{sum}=7)$. (b) $P(\text{sum even})$. (c) $P(\text{at least one }6)$.两枚均匀骰子。(a) $P(\text{sum}=7)$。(b) $P(\text{sum even})$。(c) $P(\text{at least one }6)$。

Answers:答案：  (a) $\dfrac{1}{6}$  ·  (b) $\dfrac{1}{2}$  ·  (c) $\dfrac{11}{36}$

(a) Sum equals $7$ M1·A1

Pairs giving sum $7$: $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$ — exactly $6$ outcomes out of $36$. $P = 6/36 = \boxed{\tfrac{1}{6}}.$

(b) Sum is even A1

Sum is even iff both dice match parity. Both odd: $3 \times 3 = 9$ pairs. Both even: $3 \times 3 = 9$ pairs. Total $18/36 = \boxed{\tfrac{1}{2}}$.

(c) At least one $6$ — complement trick M1·A1

$P(\text{no }6 \text{ on either die}) = (5/6)^2 = 25/36$. So $$ P(\text{at least one }6) \;=\; 1 - \tfrac{25}{36} \;=\; \boxed{\tfrac{11}{36}}. $$
Complement first. "At least one" almost always opens with the complement rule. Counting the at-least-one outcomes directly is error-prone (double-counting); $1 - P(\text{none})$ is bulletproof.

(a) 点数和等于 $7$ M1·A1

点数和为 $7$ 的样本空间（sample space）中的结果（outcome）：$(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$ —— $36$ 个结果中恰有 $6$ 个。$P = 6/36 = \boxed{\tfrac{1}{6}}.$

(b) 点数和为偶数 A1

点数和为偶数当且仅当两枚骰子奇偶性相同。两枚都为奇：$3 \times 3 = 9$ 对；两枚都为偶：$3 \times 3 = 9$ 对。共 $18/36 = \boxed{\tfrac{1}{2}}$。

(c) 至少一枚为 $6$ —— 余事件技巧 M1·A1

$P(\text{no }6 \text{ on either die}) = (5/6)^2 = 25/36$。因此用余事件（complement）： $$ P(\text{at least one }6) \;=\; 1 - \tfrac{25}{36} \;=\; \boxed{\tfrac{11}{36}}. $$
先用余事件。 题目里出现 "at least one" 几乎都从余事件入手。直接枚举 "至少一次" 容易重复计数；用 $1 - P(\text{none})$ 几乎不出错。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A common slip is to count only the three unordered pairs \((1,6),(2,5),(3,4)\), which gives \(3/36=1/12\) instead of the six ordered outcomes. (b) Another slip is to say an even sum means one even and one odd, which gives \(18/36=1/2\) from the wrong event. (c) A frequent error is to use \(1-\frac56\) for at least one \(6\), forgetting the square and getting \(1/6\).(a) 常见失误是只数到三个无序配对 \((1,6),(2,5),(3,4)\)，于是写成 \(3/36=1/12\)，把顺序对漏掉了。 (b) 另一种失误是把偶数和误看成一奇一偶，得到 \(18/36=1/2\)，但这是错的事件。 (c) 常见错误是写 \(1-\frac56\)，忘了两次都没有 \(6\)，要平方，所以会得到 \(1/6\)。
Markscheme, 5 marks.评分标准，共 5 分。 (a) 1: Lists the six ordered outcomes with sum \(7\) or counts \(6\) favourable pairs (M1) · 1: Converts to \(6/36=1/6\) (A1) · (b) 1: Uses parity, both odd or both even gives \(18/36\) or \(1/2\) (A1) · (c) 1: Uses complement \(P(\text{no }6)\) (M1) · 1: Calculates \(1-(5/6)^2=11/36\) (A1)(a) 1 分：列出 \(7\) 的六个有序结果，或正确数出 \(6\) 个有利配对 (M1) · 1 分：化成 \(6/36=1/6\) (A1) · (b) 1 分：用奇偶性，偶奇同号时得到 \(18/36\) 或 \(1/2\) (A1) · (c) 1 分：用补集 \(P(\text{没有 }6)\) (M1) · 1 分：算出 \(1-(5/6)^2=11/36\) (A1)
Q2EASYPaper 1A4.5 Complementary / Expected[5 marks]

$P(\text{rain}) = 0.4$, days independent. (a) $P(\text{no rain})$. (b) Expected rainy days in $30$. (c) $P(\ge 1\text{ rainy day in }3)$.$P(\text{rain}) = 0.4$，各天独立。(a) $P(\text{no rain})$。(b) $30$ 天内下雨天数的期望。(c) $P(\ge 1\text{ rainy day in }3)$。

Answers:答案：  (a) $0.6$  ·  (b) $12$  ·  (c) $0.784$

(a) A1

$P(R^{\,\prime}) = 1 - 0.4 = 0.6.$

(b) Expected frequency M1·A1

$E[\text{rainy days in }30] = n \cdot p = 30 \cdot 0.4 = \boxed{12}$ days.

(c) At least one rainy day in three M1·A1

$P(\text{no rain on any of 3 days}) = 0.6^3 = 0.216$. $P(\ge 1 \text{ rainy}) = 1 - 0.216 = \boxed{0.784}.$
Expected frequency vs probability. $E[X] = np$ is a count (units: "days"); $P$ is a probability (unitless, $[0,1]$). Keep them straight in answer writing.

(a) A1

$P(R^{\,\prime}) = 1 - 0.4 = 0.6.$（余事件 complement）

(b) 期望频数 M1·A1

$E[\text{rainy days in }30] = n \cdot p = 30 \cdot 0.4 = \boxed{12}$ 天。

(c) 三天中至少有一天下雨 M1·A1

$P(\text{no rain on any of 3 days}) = 0.6^3 = 0.216$。 $P(\ge 1 \text{ rainy}) = 1 - 0.216 = \boxed{0.784}.$
期望频数与概率的区别。 $E[X] = np$ 是一个计数（单位是 "天"）；$P$ 是概率（probability，无量纲，取值 $[0,1]$）。答题时务必区分清楚。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A natural mistake is to copy the rainy probability and write \(0.4\), but the not rainy probability is its complement. (b) Another slip is to multiply by \(0.6\) instead of \(0.4\), giving \(30\cdot0.6=18\) rainy days. (c) A common error is to use \(1-0.4^3\), which treats \(0.4\) as the chance of no rain and gives \(0.936\).(a) 常见失误是直接写成下雨概率 \(0.4\)，但不下雨概率应取补集。 (b) 另一种失误是把 \(0.4\) 写成 \(0.6\)，于是得到 \(30\cdot0.6=18\) 个雨天。 (c) 常见错误是写 \(1-0.4^3\)，把 \(0.4\) 当成三天都不下雨的概率，结果得到 \(0.936\)。
Markscheme, 5 marks.评分标准，共 5 分。 (a) 1: Complements rainfall, \(1-0.4=0.6\) (A1) · (b) 1: Uses \(E=np\) with \(30\cdot0.4\) (M1) · 1: Gives \(12\) (A1) · (c) 1: Uses complement for no rain over 3 days, \(0.6^3\) (M1) · 1: Calculates \(1-0.216=0.784\) (A1)(a) 1 分：取补集，\(1-0.4=0.6\) (A1) · (b) 1 分：用 \(E=np\)，写出 \(30\cdot0.4\) (M1) · 1 分：得到 \(12\) (A1) · (c) 1 分：对三天都不下雨用补集，写出 \(0.6^3\) (M1) · 1 分：算出 \(1-0.216=0.784\) (A1)
Q3MEDIUMPaper 1A4.6 Mutually Exclusive vs Independent[6 marks]

$P(A) = 0.3$, $P(B) = 0.5$. (a) ME: $P(A \cup B)$. (b) Ind: $P(A \cap B)$. (c) Ind: $P(A \cup B)$.$P(A) = 0.3$，$P(B) = 0.5$。(a) 互斥时 $P(A \cup B)$。(b) 独立时 $P(A \cap B)$。(c) 独立时 $P(A \cup B)$。

Answers:答案：  (a) $0.8$  ·  (b) $0.15$  ·  (c) $0.65$

(a) Mutually exclusive case M1·A1

ME means $A \cap B = \varnothing$, so $P(A \cap B) = 0$. Addition rule: $$ P(A \cup B) \;=\; P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B) \;=\; 0.3 + 0.5 - 0 \;=\; \boxed{0.8}. $$

(b) Independent: intersection M1·A1

Independence means $P(A \cap B) = P(A)\,P(B) = 0.3 \cdot 0.5 = \boxed{0.15}.$

(c) Independent: union M1·A1

Apply addition rule with the intersection from (b): $$ P(A \cup B) \;=\; 0.3 + 0.5 - 0.15 \;=\; \boxed{0.65}. $$
ME $\ne$ Independent. Mutually exclusive events with non-zero probability are never independent ($P(A \cap B) = 0 \ne P(A)P(B) > 0$). The two concepts are often confused. Use them as opposites of each other.

(a) 互斥（mutually exclusive）情形 M1·A1

互斥意味着 $A \cap B = \varnothing$，故 $P(A \cap B) = 0$。由加法公式（并 union）： $$ P(A \cup B) \;=\; P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B) \;=\; 0.3 + 0.5 - 0 \;=\; \boxed{0.8}. $$

(b) 独立（independent）时的交（intersection） M1·A1

独立意味着 $P(A \cap B) = P(A)\,P(B) = 0.3 \cdot 0.5 = \boxed{0.15}.$

(c) 独立时的并 M1·A1

用 (b) 的交代入加法公式： $$ P(A \cup B) \;=\; 0.3 + 0.5 - 0.15 \;=\; \boxed{0.65}. $$
互斥 $\ne$ 独立。 概率非零的互斥事件永远不可能独立（$P(A \cap B) = 0 \ne P(A)P(B) > 0$）。这两个概念常被混淆，应把它们视为对立的两种关系。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A common slip is to use the independent style subtraction \(0.3+0.5-0.15=0.65\), but mutual exclusivity means the intersection is \(0\). (b) Another slip is to add the probabilities and write \(0.3+0.5=0.8\) for the intersection, when multiplication is required for independence. (c) A frequent error is to forget to subtract the overlap and again write \(0.3+0.5=0.8\), even though the intersection is already counted once.(a) 常见失误是套用独立情形的减法 \(0.3+0.5-0.15=0.65\)，但互斥意味着交集为 \(0\)。 (b) 另一种失误是把交集写成加法 \(0.3+0.5=0.8\)，而独立时交集应由乘法求出。 (c) 常见错误是忘记减去交集，再次写成 \(0.3+0.5=0.8\)，但交集已经被算过一次了。
Markscheme, 6 marks.评分标准，共 6 分。 (a) 1: Sets \(P(A\cap B)=0\) for mutually exclusive events (M1) · 1: Applies addition rule to get \(0.3+0.5=0.8\) (A1) · (b) 1: Uses independence product \(P(A)P(B)\) (M1) · 1: Computes \(0.3\cdot0.5=0.15\) (A1) · (c) 1: Substitutes the intersection into the addition rule (M1) · 1: Computes \(0.3+0.5-0.15=0.65\) (A1)(a) 1 分：互斥时令 \(P(A\cap B)=0\) (M1) · 1 分：用加法公式得到 \(0.3+0.5=0.8\) (A1) · (b) 1 分：用独立性乘积 \(P(A)P(B)\) (M1) · 1 分：算出 \(0.3\cdot0.5=0.15\) (A1) · (c) 1 分：在加法公式中代入交集 (M1) · 1 分：算出 \(0.3+0.5-0.15=0.65\) (A1)
Q4MEDIUMPaper 1A4.6 Conditional from Table[6 marks]

Two-way table: $50$ M + $50$ F; $65$ pass total; $30$ M pass. (a) $P(\text{Pass})$. (b) $P(\text{Pass}\mid\text{Male})$. (c) $P(\text{Female}\mid\text{Pass})$. (d) Independence?列联表：$50$ 男 + $50$ 女；共 $65$ 人通过；男生通过 $30$ 人。(a) $P(\text{Pass})$。(b) $P(\text{Pass}\mid\text{Male})$。(c) $P(\text{Female}\mid\text{Pass})$。(d) 是否独立？

Answers:答案：  (a) $\tfrac{13}{20} = 0.65$  ·  (b) $\tfrac{3}{5} = 0.6$  ·  (c) $\tfrac{7}{13}$  ·  (d) not independent不独立

(a) A1

$P(\text{Pass}) = 65/100 = \boxed{0.65}.$

(b) M1·A1

Condition on Male; restrict to the male row: $P(\text{Pass}\mid\text{Male}) = 30/50 = \boxed{0.6}.$

(c) M1·A1

Condition on Pass; restrict to the pass column: $P(\text{Female}\mid\text{Pass}) = 35/65 = \boxed{7/13} \approx 0.538.$

(d) Independence check R1

$P(\text{Pass} \cap \text{Male}) = 30/100 = 0.3$. $P(\text{Pass})\,P(\text{Male}) = 0.65 \cdot 0.5 = 0.325$. Since $0.3 \ne 0.325$, the two events are not independent.
Conditional restricts the universe. $P(B \mid A)$ reads "given $A$"; the denominator switches from $n(U) = 100$ to $n(A)$. Both numerator and denominator change.

(a) A1

$P(\text{Pass}) = 65/100 = \boxed{0.65}.$

(b) M1·A1

以 Male 为条件（条件概率 conditional probability，P(A|B)）：仅看男生这一行，$P(\text{Pass}\mid\text{Male}) = 30/50 = \boxed{0.6}.$

(c) M1·A1

以 Pass 为条件：仅看通过这一列，$P(\text{Female}\mid\text{Pass}) = 35/65 = \boxed{7/13} \approx 0.538.$

(d) 独立性检验 R1

$P(\text{Pass} \cap \text{Male}) = 30/100 = 0.3$，$P(\text{Pass})\,P(\text{Male}) = 0.65 \cdot 0.5 = 0.325$。由 $0.3 \ne 0.325$ 可知两事件不独立。
条件概率改变样本范围。 $P(B \mid A)$ 读作 "在 $A$ 发生的条件下"，分母从 $n(U) = 100$ 变为 $n(A)$。分子与分母都同时改变。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A common slip is to read the wrong total and write \(50/100=0.5\), which is \(P(\text{Male})\), not \(P(\text{Pass})\). (b) Another slip is to use the whole class as the denominator, giving \(30/100=0.3\) instead of conditioning on males. (c) A frequent error is to divide the female pass count by \(100\), giving \(35/100=0.35\), instead of by the pass total \(65\). (d) For independence, some students compare \(0.6\) and \(0.65\) and say they are close enough, but the exact test is whether \(0.3=0.325\), which it is not.(a) 常见失误是读错总数，写成 \(50/100=0.5\)，这其实是 \(P(\text{Male})\)，不是 \(P(\text{Pass})\)。 (b) 另一种失误是直接用全班人数作分母，得到 \(30/100=0.3\)，而不是按男生条件化。 (c) 常见错误是把女生及格人数除以 \(100\)，得到 \(35/100=0.35\)，而不是除以及格总数 \(65\)。 (d) 有些同学只比较 \(0.6\) 和 \(0.65\) 就说差不多，但独立性的精确检验是看 \(0.3\) 是否等于 \(0.325\)，这里并不相等。
Markscheme, 6 marks.评分标准，共 6 分。 (a) 1: Uses total passes over total students, \(65/100\) (A1) · (b) 1: Conditions on males and uses the male row denominator \(50\) (M1) · 1: Computes \(30/50=0.6\) (A1) · (c) 1: Conditions on pass and uses the pass column denominator \(65\) (M1) · 1: Computes \(35/65=7/13\) (A1) · (d) 1: Compares \(P(\text{Pass}\cap\text{Male})\) with \(P(\text{Pass})P(\text{Male})\) and sees they differ (R1)(a) 1 分：用及格总数除以总人数，写出 \(65/100\) (A1) · (b) 1 分：按男生条件化，并用男生这一行的分母 \(50\) (M1) · 1 分：算出 \(30/50=0.6\) (A1) · (c) 1 分：按及格条件化，并用及格这一列的分母 \(65\) (M1) · 1 分：算出 \(35/65=7/13\) (A1) · (d) 1 分：比较 \(P(\text{Pass}\cap\text{Male})\) 与 \(P(\text{Pass})P(\text{Male})\)，发现不相等 (R1)

Q5MEDIUMPaper 1B4.6 Tree Diagram[7 marks]

$4$ red, $6$ blue. Two draws without replacement.$4$ 红、$6$ 蓝。不放回抽取两次。

Answers:答案：  (b) $\tfrac{2}{15}$  ·  (c) $\tfrac{8}{15}$  ·  (d) $\tfrac{2}{3}$

(a) Tree diagram M1·A1

                  3/9  R   →  RR : 4/10 · 3/9 = 12/90 = 2/15
              ___/
       4/10  R
       _____/    \___ 6/9  B   →  RB : 4/10 · 6/9 = 24/90 = 4/15
      |
      |
      |_____ 6/10  B
              ___       4/9  R   →  BR : 6/10 · 4/9 = 24/90 = 4/15
                 \___/
                      5/9  B   →  BB : 6/10 · 5/9 = 30/90 = 1/3
Award M1 for the structure (two-stage, branch probabilities reduced by $1$ on the second draw because no replacement); A1 for correctly written conditional probabilities.

(b) $P(RR)$ M1·A1

$P(RR) = \dfrac{4}{10} \cdot \dfrac{3}{9} = \dfrac{12}{90} = \boxed{\dfrac{2}{15}}.$

(c) One of each colour M1·A1

$P(RB) + P(BR) = \dfrac{4}{10}\cdot\dfrac{6}{9} + \dfrac{6}{10}\cdot\dfrac{4}{9} = \dfrac{24}{90} + \dfrac{24}{90} = \dfrac{48}{90} = \boxed{\dfrac{8}{15}}.$

(d) $P(\text{second blue} \mid \text{first red})$ A1

Given the first ball was red, the bag now has $3$ red and $6$ blue ($9$ total). $P(B_2 \mid R_1) = \boxed{\dfrac{6}{9} = \dfrac{2}{3}}.$
Symmetry check. The two "one-of-each" paths $RB$ and $BR$ give identical probabilities here ($24/90$). This is always true when independence (or in this case, just symmetric-sample-space) is involved — useful as an arithmetic check.

(a) 树状图（tree diagram） M1·A1

                  3/9  R   →  RR : 4/10 · 3/9 = 12/90 = 2/15
              ___/
       4/10  R
       _____/    \___ 6/9  B   →  RB : 4/10 · 6/9 = 24/90 = 4/15
      |
      |
      |_____ 6/10  B
              ___       4/9  R   →  BR : 6/10 · 4/9 = 24/90 = 4/15
                 \___/
                      5/9  B   →  BB : 6/10 · 5/9 = 30/90 = 1/3
给 M1 的依据是树的结构（两阶段；由于不放回，第二次抽取的分母减 $1$）；A1 给的是正确写出的条件概率。

(b) $P(RR)$ M1·A1

$P(RR) = \dfrac{4}{10} \cdot \dfrac{3}{9} = \dfrac{12}{90} = \boxed{\dfrac{2}{15}}.$

(c) 一红一蓝 M1·A1

$P(RB) + P(BR) = \dfrac{4}{10}\cdot\dfrac{6}{9} + \dfrac{6}{10}\cdot\dfrac{4}{9} = \dfrac{24}{90} + \dfrac{24}{90} = \dfrac{48}{90} = \boxed{\dfrac{8}{15}}.$

(d) $P(\text{second blue} \mid \text{first red})$ A1

已知第一球为红，袋中剩 $3$ 红 $6$ 蓝（共 $9$ 个）。$P(B_2 \mid R_1) = \boxed{\dfrac{6}{9} = \dfrac{2}{3}}.$
对称性检查。 此处两条 "一红一蓝" 路径 $RB$ 与 $BR$ 给出相同的概率（均为 $24/90$）。只要涉及独立性（或本题这样的对称样本空间）这都成立，可作为计算的快速检查。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A common tree error is to keep the second draw as if it were a replacement draw, so the second stage still uses $4/10$ and $6/10$ instead of $3/9$ and $6/9$. (b) Another slip is to forget one red ball is removed and write $4/10\cdot4/9=8/45$ for both red, which is the right idea in a neighbouring case but not here. 新先验 $P(D) = 1/6$，因此 $P(D^{\,\prime}) = 5/6$。用同样的检测参数再做一次贝叶斯： $$ P(+) \;=\; (0.99)\!\cdot\!\tfrac{1}{6} + (0.05)\!\cdot\!\tfrac{5}{6} \;=\; \tfrac{0.99 + 0.25}{6} \;=\; \tfrac{1.24}{6} \;\approx\; 0.2067. $$ $$ P(D \mid +,+) \;=\; \frac{0.99 \cdot \tfrac{1}{6}}{1.24/6} \;=\; \frac{0.99}{1.24} \;\approx\; \boxed{0.798}. $$ 从原始先验直接核对： $$ P(D \mid +,+) \;=\; \frac{P(D)\,[P(+\mid D)]^{2}}{P(D)[P(+\mid D)]^{2} + P(D^{\,\prime})[P(+\mid D^{\,\prime})]^{2}} \;=\; \frac{0.01 \cdot 0.9801}{0.01 \cdot 0.9801 + 0.99 \cdot 0.0025} \;\approx\; 0.798. \;\checkmark $$

(d) 为何 "一次阳性 $\to$ 两次阳性" 跃升如此剧烈 R1·R1

贝叶斯定理在赔率（odds）上是乘性的：每多一次独立阳性，先验赔率就乘以似然比（likelihood ratio）$\text{LR}^{+} = \dfrac{P(+\mid D)}{P(+\mid D^{\,\prime})} = \dfrac{0.99}{0.05} = 19.8$。先验赔率为 $0.01/0.99 \approx 1/99$。一次阳性：赔率 $\to 19.8/99 \approx 0.2$（对应概率 $\approx 1/6$）。两次阳性：赔率 $\to 19.8^{2}/99 \approx 3.96$（概率 $\approx 0.798$）。第二次阳性不是 "加"，而是 "乘" —— $\approx 20\times$ 的放大器连用两次，对赔率的总放大约为 $\approx 400\times$。

(e) 连续两次阳性的假阳性率 M1·A1·R1

在未患病的条件下两次检测条件独立： $$ P(+,+ \mid D^{\,\prime}) \;=\; [P(+\mid D^{\,\prime})]^{2} \;=\; (0.05)^{2} \;=\; \boxed{0.0025}. $$ 筛查解读：在 $100{,}000$ 名两次受检的健康人中，预计约 $250$ 人会纯属偶然地连续两次阳性 —— 对个人而言仍属罕见（$0.25\%$），但筛查规模一大绝对数量就显著，因此两次阳性的方案需要再做第三次确证或随访。

(f) 较弱特异度 $0.90$ —— 使后验 $> 0.95$ 的最小 $n$ M1·M1·A1

此时 $P(+\mid D^{\,\prime}) = 0.10$，因此 $\text{LR}^{+} = 0.99/0.10 = 9.9$。$n$ 次独立阳性后的后验赔率： $$ \text{odds}_n \;=\; \text{prior odds} \times (\text{LR}^{+})^{n} \;=\; \frac{0.01}{0.99} \cdot (9.9)^{n} \;=\; \frac{(9.9)^{n}}{99}. $$ 要后验概率 $> 0.95$ 等价于赔率 $> 19$： $$ \frac{(9.9)^{n}}{99} > 19 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; (9.9)^{n} > 1881 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; n > \frac{\log 1881}{\log 9.9} \approx \frac{3.274}{0.9956} \approx 3.29. $$ 用 GDC 迭代验证：
$n$赔率后验
$3$$9.9^{3}/99 \approx 9.80$$\approx 0.907$（未达标）
$4$$9.9^{4}/99 \approx 97.03$$\approx \boxed{0.990}$（达标）
最小 $n = \boxed{4}$，对应后验 $\approx 0.990$。
筛查检测的设计经验。 在低患病率场景下，提高特异度（降低假阳性）对后验能力的提升远快于提升灵敏度，因为分母 $P(+)$ 由健康多数中的假阳性主导。这就是为什么两阶段筛查（先做廉价的高灵敏度初筛，再做昂贵的高特异度确证）在同等成本下总能优于任何单一检测 —— 它们正是有意制造 (d) 中的乘性放大。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A common slip is to use the specificity $0.95$ itself as $P(+\mid D')$, which makes the false positive rate $0.95$ instead of $0.05$ and drives the posterior down near $0.01$ rather than $1/6$. (b) A natural mistake is to divide by $0.01$ or by $0.99$ instead of by $P(+)$, so the one positive posterior is not rebuilt from the full Bayes fraction. (c) Another error is to keep the original prior $0.01$ for the retest, so the second positive is treated as if it barely changes the belief; the correct move is to update the prior to $1/6$ and then apply Bayes again, or to square both likelihoods. (d) Saying only that the second test is stronger evidence is incomplete; each independent positive multiplies the odds by $19.8$ again, so the odds jump from about $1:5$ after one positive to about $4:1$ after two. (e) Squaring $0.99$ uses the diseased sensitivity, but the question asks for a non diseased patient, so the correct chance is $(0.05)^2=0.0025$. In a large screening program this means a small per person false alarm rate but hundreds of false alarms overall. (f) If you forget that specificity has dropped to $0.90$ and keep using $0.05$ as the false positive rate, the likelihood ratio is too large and you can stop at too small an $n$; the correct iteration first passes $0.95$ at $n=4$.(a) 常见失误是把特异性 $0.95$ 直接当成 $P(+\mid D')$，于是把假阳性率写成 $0.95$ 而不是 $0.05$，这样后验会被压到接近 $0.01$，而不是 $1/6$。 (b) 一个自然的错误是除以 $0.01$ 或 $0.99$，而不是除以 $P(+)$，所以“一次阳性后的后验”没有按完整的贝叶斯分式重算。 (c) 另一个错误是把原来的先验 $0.01$ 直接拿来做复测，所以第二次阳性被看成几乎不改变信念；正确做法是把先验更新成 $1/6$ 再套一次贝叶斯，或者把两次似然都平方。 (d) 只说“第二次检测证据更强”还不够；每一次独立阳性都会再次把赔率乘以 $19.8$，所以赔率会从一次阳性后的约 $1:5$ 跳到两次阳性后的约 $4:1$。 (e) 把 $0.99$ 平方用的是患病者的灵敏度，但这里问的是未患病者，所以正确概率是 $(0.05)^2=0.0025$。在大规模筛查中，这表示单个人的假警报率很小，但总体上会出现成百上千个假警报。 (f) 如果忘记特异性降到了 $0.90$，仍然把假阳性率当成 $0.05$，似然比就会过大，于是会过早停在太小的 $n$；正确的迭代第一次超过 $0.95$ 是在 $n=4$。

Q13HARDPaper 1BAHL 4.10 Bayes · Draws Without Replacement[17 marks]HL

Box A: 4R, 2B; box B: 2R, 4B; $P(A) = \tfrac{1}{3}$. Draw without replacement; update which box, then predict the next colour.盒 A：4 红 2 蓝；盒 B：2 红 4 蓝；$P(A) = \tfrac{1}{3}$。不放回抽取；更新是哪个盒子，再预测下一个颜色。

Answers:答案：  (a) $\tfrac{2}{5}$, $\tfrac{1}{15}$  ·  (b) $P(A \mid R) = \tfrac{1}{2}$, $P(A \mid RR) = \tfrac{3}{4}$  ·  (c) $\tfrac{3}{8}$  ·  (d) $\tfrac{1}{3}$

(a) Likelihoods without replacement M1·A1·M1·A1

$$ P(RR \mid A) = \frac{4}{6}\cdot\frac{3}{5} = \frac{2}{5}, \qquad P(RR \mid B) = \frac{2}{6}\cdot\frac{1}{5} = \frac{1}{15}. $$ The second factor uses five counters, because the first red has been removed.

(b) Posteriors M1·A1·M1·A1·A1

One red: joint weights $P(A \cap R) = \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot\tfrac{4}{6} = \tfrac{2}{9}$ and $P(B \cap R) = \tfrac{2}{3}\cdot\tfrac{2}{6} = \tfrac{2}{9}$, so $$ P(A \mid R) = \frac{2/9}{2/9 + 2/9} = \frac{1}{2}. $$ Two reds: $P(A \cap RR) = \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot\tfrac{2}{5} = \tfrac{2}{15}$ and $P(B \cap RR) = \tfrac{2}{3}\cdot\tfrac{1}{15} = \tfrac{2}{45}$, so $$ P(A \mid RR) = \frac{2/15}{2/15 + 2/45} = \frac{6/45}{8/45} = \frac{3}{4}. $$

(c) Predicting the third draw M1·M1·A1

After $RR$, box A has $2$ red among $4$ counters; box B has no red left. Weight each by its posterior: $$ P(R_{3} \mid RR) = \frac{3}{4}\cdot\frac{2}{4} + \frac{1}{4}\cdot 0 = \frac{3}{8}. $$ This is below $\tfrac{1}{2}$ even though A is now likely: two reds have depleted the red counters.

(d) Red then blue M1·A1·A1·R1·R1

$P(RB \mid A) = \tfrac{4}{6}\cdot\tfrac{2}{5} = \tfrac{4}{15}$ and $P(RB \mid B) = \tfrac{2}{6}\cdot\tfrac{4}{5} = \tfrac{4}{15}$. The likelihoods are equal, so the observation leaves the prior odds unchanged: $$ P(A \mid RB) = \frac{\tfrac{1}{3}\cdot\tfrac{4}{15}}{\tfrac{1}{3}\cdot\tfrac{4}{15} + \tfrac{2}{3}\cdot\tfrac{4}{15}} = \frac{1}{3}. $$ In two stages: after the red, $P(A \mid R) = P(B \mid R) = \tfrac{1}{2}$. The next draw is blue with probability $\tfrac{2}{5}$ from A but $\tfrac{4}{5}$ from B, so $$ P(A \mid RB) = \frac{\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{2}{5}}{\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{2}{5} + \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{4}{5}} = \frac{1}{3}. $$ The red raised A's probability from $\tfrac{1}{3}$ to $\tfrac{1}{2}$; the blue, being twice as likely from B, brought it back to $\tfrac{1}{3}$.
A posterior about the box is not a prediction about the counter. $P(A \mid RR) = \tfrac{3}{4}$ answers "which box?"; $P(R_{3} \mid RR) = \tfrac{3}{8}$ answers "what next?", and needs the depleted contents. The direct and two-stage calculations in (d) agree — updating one observation at a time is always legitimate, provided each likelihood uses the contents at that moment.

(a) 不放回下的似然 M1·A1·M1·A1

$$ P(RR \mid A) = \frac{4}{6}\cdot\frac{3}{5} = \frac{2}{5}，\qquad P(RR \mid B) = \frac{2}{6}\cdot\frac{1}{5} = \frac{1}{15}。 $$ 第二个因子的分母是 $5$，因为第一个红色已被取出。

(b) 后验概率 M1·A1·M1·A1·A1

一个红色：联合权重 $P(A \cap R) = \tfrac{2}{9}$，$P(B \cap R) = \tfrac{2}{9}$，故 $P(A \mid R) = \tfrac{1}{2}$。 两个红色：$P(A \cap RR) = \tfrac{2}{15}$，$P(B \cap RR) = \tfrac{2}{45}$，故 $$ P(A \mid RR) = \frac{2/15}{2/15 + 2/45} = \frac{3}{4}。 $$

(c) 预测第三次抽取 M1·M1·A1

$RR$ 之后，盒 A 剩 $4$ 个中有 $2$ 个红色；盒 B 已无红色。按后验加权： $$ P(R_{3} \mid RR) = \frac{3}{4}\cdot\frac{2}{4} + \frac{1}{4}\cdot 0 = \frac{3}{8}。 $$ 尽管此时 A 的可能性很大，结果仍小于 $\tfrac{1}{2}$：两次红色已消耗了红色筹码。

(d) 先红后蓝 M1·A1·A1·R1·R1

$P(RB \mid A) = \tfrac{4}{6}\cdot\tfrac{2}{5} = \tfrac{4}{15}$，$P(RB \mid B) = \tfrac{2}{6}\cdot\tfrac{4}{5} = \tfrac{4}{15}$。两个似然相等，观察结果不改变先验比，故 $P(A \mid RB) = \tfrac{1}{3}$。 分两步：抽到红色后 $P(A \mid R) = P(B \mid R) = \tfrac{1}{2}$；下一个为蓝色的概率在 A 中为 $\tfrac{2}{5}$、在 B 中为 $\tfrac{4}{5}$，所以 $$ P(A \mid RB) = \frac{\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{2}{5}}{\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{2}{5} + \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot\tfrac{4}{5}} = \frac{1}{3}。 $$ 红色把 A 的概率从 $\tfrac{1}{3}$ 提高到 $\tfrac{1}{2}$；蓝色在 B 中出现的可能性是 A 中的两倍，又把它拉回 $\tfrac{1}{3}$。
关于盒子的后验不等于关于筹码的预测。$P(A \mid RR) = \tfrac{3}{4}$ 回答"是哪个盒子"；$P(R_{3} \mid RR) = \tfrac{3}{8}$ 回答"下一个是什么"，需要用到消耗后的内容。(d) 中直接计算与分步计算一致——逐次更新总是合法的，只要每个似然都用当时的盒内内容。
