Magnetic Flux磁通量

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State the definition of magnetic flux through a surface as a dot product, $\Phi_B = \vec{B}\cdot\vec{A}$, and give its SI unit (the weber).
  • State the definition of magnetic flux through a flat surface in a uniform field, $\Phi_B = BA\cos\theta$, and give its SI unit (the weber).
  • Calculate the flux through a flat surface in a uniform field from $B$, the area, and the angle between $\vec{B}$ and the area vector.
  • Explain how the direction of the area vector (perpendicular to the surface, outward for a closed surface) fixes the sign of the flux.
  • Set up and evaluate $\Phi_B = \int \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A}$ when the field varies across the surface, choosing a differential area element.
  • Predict how the flux changes when the field strength, the area, or the orientation of the loop changes.
  • Justify why the net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero.
  • 陈述（state）磁通量作为点积的定义 $\Phi_B = \vec{B}\cdot\vec{A}$，并给出其 SI 单位（韦伯）。
  • 陈述（state）均匀磁场中平面磁通量的定义 $\Phi_B = BA\cos\theta$，并给出其 SI 单位（韦伯）。
  • 由 $B$、面积以及 $\vec{B}$ 与面积矢量的夹角，计算（calculate）均匀磁场中平面的磁通量。
  • 解释（explain）面积矢量的方向（垂直于表面；闭合曲面取向外）如何决定磁通量的正负。
  • 当磁场在表面上变化时，选取微元面积，列出并计算（set up and evaluate）$\Phi_B = \int \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A}$。
  • 预测（predict）当磁场强度、面积或线圈取向改变时磁通量如何变化。
  • 论证（justify）为什么通过任意闭合曲面的净磁通量为零。

Every induction effect in this unit starts from one quantity: magnetic flux. Flux measures how much magnetic field passes through a surface, counting only the component of the field perpendicular to that surface. A field that runs parallel to the surface never crosses it, so it contributes no flux, however strong it is.

本单元所有感应现象都从一个量出发：磁通量（magnetic flux）。磁通量衡量有多少磁场穿过一个表面，只计入垂直于该表面的场分量。平行于表面的磁场从不穿过表面，因此无论多强都不贡献磁通量。

Flux through a flat surface in a uniform field均匀磁场中平面的磁通量

Describe the surface by an area vector $\vec{A}$: its magnitude is the area $A$ and its direction is perpendicular (normal) to the surface. For a closed surface the convention is that $\vec{A}$ points outward.

用面积矢量（area vector）$\vec{A}$ 描述表面：大小等于面积 $A$，方向垂直于（法向）表面。对闭合曲面，约定 $\vec{A}$ 指向外侧。

For a flat loop that is not part of a closed surface, either normal is allowed, but you must choose one and keep it for the whole problem. If the field $\vec{B}$ is constant over the surface, the flux is the dot product:

对不属于闭合曲面的平面线圈，两个法向都可以，但必须选定一个并在整道题中保持不变。若磁场 $\vec{B}$ 在表面上处处相同，磁通量就是点积：

Magnetic flux, uniform field磁通量（均匀磁场）
$$\Phi_B = \vec{B}\cdot\vec{A} = BA\cos\theta$$

$\theta$ is the angle between $\vec{B}$ and $\vec{A}$. The unit is the weber: $1\;\text{Wb} = 1\;\text{T}\cdot\text{m}^2$.

$\theta$ 是 $\vec{B}$ 与 $\vec{A}$ 之间的夹角。单位是韦伯：$1\;\text{Wb} = 1\;\text{T}\cdot\text{m}^2$。

First principles: why the flux carries a cosine第一性原理：磁通量中为何出现余弦

Principle. Flux counts only the field that crosses the surface, so only the component of $\vec{B}$ along the normal counts.

出发点。磁通量只计入穿过表面的磁场，因此只计入 $\vec{B}$ 沿法线方向的分量。

  1. Split $\vec{B}$ into a part along the normal, $B\cos\theta$, and a part in the plane, $B\sin\theta$.
  2. The in-plane part runs along the surface and crosses none of it, so it adds no flux.
  3. The normal part crosses the whole area $A$, so $\Phi_B = (B\cos\theta)A = BA\cos\theta$.
  4. Keeping all of $B$ and using the area projected onto a plane perpendicular to $\vec{B}$, $A\cos\theta$, gives the same result.
  1. 把 $\vec{B}$ 分解为沿法线的分量 $B\cos\theta$ 和位于平面内的分量 $B\sin\theta$。
  2. 平面内的分量沿表面延伸，不穿过表面，因此不贡献磁通量。
  3. 法向分量穿过整个面积 $A$，所以 $\Phi_B = (B\cos\theta)A = BA\cos\theta$。
  4. 保留整个 $B$、改用表面在垂直于 $\vec{B}$ 的平面上的投影面积 $A\cos\theta$，得到相同结果。

Limiting-case check. At $\theta = 0$ the flux is the maximum $BA$; at $\theta = 90^\circ$ it is $0$; at $\theta = 180^\circ$ it is $-BA$. These match the sign rules above, and the unit is $\text{T}\cdot\text{m}^2 = \text{Wb}$.

极限检验。$\theta = 0$ 时磁通量取最大值 $BA$；$\theta = 90^\circ$ 时为 $0$；$\theta = 180^\circ$ 时为 $-BA$。这与上面的正负规则一致，单位为 $\text{T}\cdot\text{m}^2 = \text{Wb}$。

The sign of the flux and the orientation of the loop磁通量的正负与线圈的取向

The sign of the flux is the sign of the dot product. If $\vec{B}$ has a component along $\vec{A}$ (so $\theta < 90^\circ$) the flux is positive; if it points against $\vec{A}$ ($\theta > 90^\circ$) the flux is negative; if the field lies in the plane of the loop ($\theta = 90^\circ$) the flux is zero.

磁通量的正负就是点积的正负。若 $\vec{B}$ 有沿 $\vec{A}$ 的分量（$\theta < 90^\circ$），磁通量为正；若与 $\vec{A}$ 相反（$\theta > 90^\circ$），磁通量为负；若磁场位于线圈所在平面内（$\theta = 90^\circ$），磁通量为零。

Flipping the loop over reverses $\vec{A}$ and therefore reverses the sign of the flux without changing its magnitude.

把线圈翻转会使 $\vec{A}$ 反向，因此磁通量变号而大小不变。

Flux is proportional to the component of the field perpendicular to the surface and to the area of the surface. Doubling $B$ doubles $\Phi_B$, doubling $A$ doubles $\Phi_B$, and turning the loop changes only the factor $\cos\theta$.

磁通量与垂直于表面的磁场分量成正比，也与表面的面积成正比。$B$ 加倍则 $\Phi_B$ 加倍，$A$ 加倍则 $\Phi_B$ 加倍，转动线圈只改变因子 $\cos\theta$。

The sign of $\Phi_B$ is the sign of $\cos\theta$. A positive flux means the field has a component along the area vector $\vec{A}$; a negative flux means it has a component opposite to $\vec{A}$.

$\Phi_B$ 的正负就是 $\cos\theta$ 的正负。磁通量为正，表示磁场有沿面积矢量 $\vec{A}$ 的分量；磁通量为负，表示磁场有与 $\vec{A}$ 相反的分量。

Common Mistake: Using the angle to the plane The angle in $BA\cos\theta$ is measured from the normal to the loop, not from the plane of the loop. A loop whose plane is perpendicular to the field has $\theta = 0$ and the maximum flux $BA$. If a problem gives the angle between the field and the plane, the angle to use is its complement.
常见错误：用了与平面的夹角 $BA\cos\theta$ 中的角度是从线圈的法线量起的，而不是从线圈平面量起。平面垂直于磁场的线圈有 $\theta = 0$，磁通量最大，为 $BA$。若题目给出的是磁场与平面的夹角，应使用它的余角。
Worked Example: Flux Through a Tilted Loop例题：倾斜线圈的磁通量

A circular loop of radius $5.0\;\text{cm}$ sits in a uniform field $B = 0.40\;\text{T}$. The area vector makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with $\vec{B}$. Find the flux, then the flux after the loop is turned so that its plane contains the field, and after it is turned so that $\vec{A}$ points opposite to $\vec{B}$.

半径 $5.0\;\text{cm}$ 的圆形线圈放在均匀磁场 $B = 0.40\;\text{T}$ 中，面积矢量与 $\vec{B}$ 成 $30^\circ$ 角。求磁通量；再求线圈转到其平面包含磁场时的磁通量，以及转到 $\vec{A}$ 与 $\vec{B}$ 反向时的磁通量。

Step 1: Area第 1 步：面积
Convert the radius to metres first.先把半径换算成米。
$$A = \pi r^2 = \pi(0.050)^2 = 7.85\times10^{-3}\;\text{m}^2$$
Step 2: Flux at 30 degrees第 2 步：30° 时的磁通量
Use the dot product with the angle to the normal.用点积，角度取自法线。
$$\Phi_B = BA\cos\theta = (0.40)(7.85\times10^{-3})\cos30^\circ = 2.72\times10^{-3}\;\text{Wb}$$
Step 3: Other orientations第 3 步：其他取向
In the plane of the field $\theta = 90^\circ$, and with $\vec{A}$ reversed $\theta = 180^\circ$.在磁场所在平面内 $\theta = 90^\circ$；$\vec{A}$ 反向时 $\theta = 180^\circ$。
$$\Phi_B(90^\circ) = 0,\qquad \Phi_B(180^\circ) = -BA = -3.14\times10^{-3}\;\text{Wb}$$
Worked Example: Flux Through a Rectangular Coil as It Turns例题：转动的矩形线圈的磁通量

A rectangular coil with sides $0.20\;\text{m}$ and $0.30\;\text{m}$ is in a uniform field $B = 0.50\;\text{T}$. Find the flux when the area vector is parallel to $\vec{B}$, and the flux when the area vector makes $60^\circ$ with $\vec{B}$.

边长分别为 $0.20\;\text{m}$ 和 $0.30\;\text{m}$ 的矩形线圈处于均匀磁场 $B = 0.50\;\text{T}$ 中。求面积矢量与 $\vec{B}$ 平行时的磁通量，以及面积矢量与 $\vec{B}$ 成 $60^\circ$ 角时的磁通量。

Then find the flux after the coil is turned half a turn from the parallel position so that $\vec{A}$ is antiparallel to $\vec{B}$, and the change in flux during that half turn.

再求线圈从平行位置转过半圈、使 $\vec{A}$ 与 $\vec{B}$ 反平行后的磁通量，以及这半圈转动中磁通量的变化量。

Step 1: Area第 1 步：面积
Multiply the two sides.把两条边相乘。
$$A = (0.20)(0.30) = 6.00\times10^{-2}\;\text{m}^2$$
Step 2: Parallel第 2 步：平行
Here $\theta = 0$, so $\cos\theta = 1$.此时 $\theta = 0$，$\cos\theta = 1$。
$$\Phi_B = BA\cos 0^\circ = (0.50)(6.00\times10^{-2})(1) = 3.00\times10^{-2}\;\text{Wb}$$
Step 3: Sixty degrees第 3 步：60° 时
The angle is measured from the normal to the coil.角度从线圈的法线量起。
$$\Phi_B = (0.50)(6.00\times10^{-2})\cos 60^\circ = 1.50\times10^{-2}\;\text{Wb}$$
Step 4: Antiparallel and the change第 4 步：反平行与变化量
Now $\theta = 180^\circ$, so the flux is negative. The change is final minus initial, starting from the parallel position.此时 $\theta = 180^\circ$，磁通量为负。变化量等于末态减初态，初态取平行位置。
$$\Phi_B = BA\cos 180^\circ = -3.00\times10^{-2}\;\text{Wb},\qquad \Delta\Phi_B = (-3.00\times10^{-2}) - (3.00\times10^{-2}) = -6.00\times10^{-2}\;\text{Wb}$$
Step 5: Check第 5 步：检验
The flux goes from $+BA$ to $-BA$, so the magnitude of the change is $2BA = 6.00\times10^{-2}\;\text{Wb}$, as found.磁通量由 $+BA$ 变为 $-BA$，变化量的大小为 $2BA = 6.00\times10^{-2}\;\text{Wb}$，与计算结果一致。

A field that varies across the surface: the surface integral磁场在表面上变化：面积分

When $B$ is not the same everywhere on the surface, divide the surface into small elements $d\vec{A}$ small enough that the field is constant on each, then add the contributions. The total flux is the surface integral of the field:

当表面上各处的 $B$ 不同时，把表面分成足够小的面元 $d\vec{A}$，使每个面元上的磁场可视为恒定，再把各面元的贡献相加。总磁通量就是磁场的面积分（surface integral）：

Magnetic flux, general磁通量（一般情形）
$$\Phi_B = \int \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A}$$

Choose the element so that $B$ is constant across it: for a field that depends on one coordinate, take a thin strip perpendicular to that coordinate.

选取面元使 $B$ 在其上恒定：若磁场只依赖一个坐标，就取垂直于该坐标的细条。

Always state the differential element, its area in terms of one variable, the field at that location, and the integration limits. An integral with no stated geometry is not an explanation.

务必写明微元、用单一变量表示的微元面积、该处的磁场，以及积分上下限。没有交代几何的积分不是解释。

Worked Example: Flux of a Straight Wire Through a Rectangular Loop例题：直导线穿过矩形线圈的磁通量

A long straight wire carries $I = 5.0\;\text{A}$. A rectangular loop of length $\ell = 0.50\;\text{m}$ (parallel to the wire) lies in the plane of the wire with near side at $a = 0.10\;\text{m}$ and far side at $b = 0.30\;\text{m}$ from the wire. Find the flux through the loop. The wire's field at distance $r$ is $B = \mu_0 I/(2\pi r)$, perpendicular to the plane of the loop.

长直导线通有 $I = 5.0\;\text{A}$ 的电流。长 $\ell = 0.50\;\text{m}$（与导线平行）的矩形线圈与导线在同一平面内，近边距导线 $a = 0.10\;\text{m}$，远边距导线 $b = 0.30\;\text{m}$。求通过线圈的磁通量。导线在距离 $r$ 处的磁场为 $B = \mu_0 I/(2\pi r)$，垂直于线圈平面。

Step 1: Element第 1 步：微元
The field depends only on $r$, so use a strip of width $dr$ and length $\ell$, parallel to the wire. The field is perpendicular to the plane, so $\vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A} = B\,\ell\,dr$.磁场只依赖 $r$，所以取宽 $dr$、长 $\ell$ 且平行于导线的细条。磁场垂直于平面，故 $\vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A} = B\,\ell\,dr$。
$$d\Phi_B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}\,\ell\,dr$$
Step 2: Integrate第 2 步：积分
Integrate from the near side to the far side.从近边积到远边。
$$\Phi_B = \frac{\mu_0 I\ell}{2\pi}\int_a^b \frac{dr}{r} = \frac{\mu_0 I\ell}{2\pi}\ln\frac{b}{a}$$
Step 3: Numbers第 3 步：代入数值
$$\Phi_B = (2\times10^{-7})(5.0)(0.50)\ln 3 = 5.5\times10^{-7}\;\text{Wb}$$

Flux through a closed surface通过闭合曲面的磁通量

Magnetic field lines have no beginning or end: they form closed loops. Any line that enters a closed surface must leave it, so the outward flux over the whole closed surface cancels exactly. This is the statement that there are no isolated magnetic charges (monopoles):

磁感线没有起点和终点：它们是闭合曲线。任何进入闭合曲面的磁感线必定穿出，所以整个闭合曲面上的向外磁通量恰好抵消。这就是不存在孤立磁荷（磁单极子）的表述：

Closed surface闭合曲面
$$\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A} = 0$$

Contrast with the electric case: $\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A} = Q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$, which is nonzero when charge is enclosed.

与电学情形对比：$\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A} = Q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$，包围电荷时不为零。

Exam tip考试提示

On a flux question, write the area vector first and say which way it points, then write the angle between $\vec{B}$ and $\vec{A}$. Measure $\theta$ from the normal to the loop, not from its plane. Flux is a scalar with a sign, so state the direction of $\vec{A}$ before you give a negative value.

做磁通量题时，先写出面积矢量并说明它指向哪里，再写出 $\vec{B}$ 与 $\vec{A}$ 之间的夹角。角度 $\theta$ 要从线圈的法线量起，而不是从线圈平面量起。磁通量是带符号的标量，给出负值之前先说明 $\vec{A}$ 的方向。

Exam tip考试提示

On a flux question, write the area vector first and say which way it points, then write the angle you are using. Flux is a scalar with a sign, so a final answer such as $-3.14\times10^{-3}\;\text{Wb}$ is correct only if you stated the direction of $\vec{A}$. For a varying field, name the differential element and its limits before integrating.

做磁通量题时，先写出面积矢量并说明它指向哪里，再写出所用的角度。磁通量是带符号的标量，只有说明了 $\vec{A}$ 的方向，$-3.14\times10^{-3}\;\text{Wb}$ 这样的结果才有意义。对变化的磁场，积分前先写出微元及其上下限。

Check your understanding自我检测
A flat loop sits in a uniform field. List the orientations of the loop that give (a) the maximum positive flux, (b) zero flux, and (c) the maximum negative flux.平面线圈放在均匀磁场中。列出使 (a) 磁通量正向最大、(b) 磁通量为零、(c) 磁通量负向最大的线圈取向。

(a) The area vector parallel to $\vec{B}$ ($\theta = 0$), so $\Phi_B = +BA$. (b) The plane of the loop contains the field, so $\vec{A}\perp\vec{B}$ ($\theta = 90^\circ$) and $\Phi_B = 0$. (c) The area vector antiparallel to $\vec{B}$ ($\theta = 180^\circ$), so $\Phi_B = -BA$.

(a) 面积矢量与 $\vec{B}$ 平行（$\theta = 0$），$\Phi_B = +BA$。(b) 线圈平面包含磁场，$\vec{A}\perp\vec{B}$（$\theta = 90^\circ$），$\Phi_B = 0$。(c) 面积矢量与 $\vec{B}$ 反平行（$\theta = 180^\circ$），$\Phi_B = -BA$。

A loop of area $0.012\;\text{m}^2$ is in a uniform field of $0.50\;\text{T}$ and the flux through it is $3.0\times10^{-3}\;\text{Wb}$. Find the angle between $\vec{B}$ and the area vector.面积 $0.012\;\text{m}^2$ 的线圈处于 $0.50\;\text{T}$ 的均匀磁场中，通过它的磁通量为 $3.0\times10^{-3}\;\text{Wb}$。求 $\vec{B}$ 与面积矢量的夹角。

$\cos\theta = \Phi_B/(BA) = (3.0\times10^{-3})/[(0.50)(0.012)] = 0.50$, so $\theta = 60^\circ$ (the other solution, $300^\circ$, is the same orientation measured the other way).

$\cos\theta = \Phi_B/(BA) = (3.0\times10^{-3})/[(0.50)(0.012)] = 0.50$，所以 $\theta = 60^\circ$（另一解 $300^\circ$ 是同一取向按相反方向量取）。

A loop of area $0.040\;\text{m}^2$ is perpendicular to a uniform field of $0.25\;\text{T}$. Find (a) the flux through the loop, (b) the flux after the loop is rotated by $90^\circ$ about an axis in its plane, and (c) the flux if instead the field is doubled and the loop is not rotated.面积 $0.040\;\text{m}^2$ 的线圈垂直于 $0.25\;\text{T}$ 的均匀磁场。求 (a) 通过线圈的磁通量；(b) 线圈绕其平面内的轴转过 $90^\circ$ 后的磁通量；(c) 若改为磁场加倍而线圈不转动时的磁通量。

(a) The area vector is parallel to the field, so $\Phi_B = BA = (0.25)(0.040) = 1.0\times10^{-2}\;\text{Wb}$. (b) After the turn the field lies in the plane of the loop, $\theta = 90^\circ$, so $\Phi_B = 0$. (c) Flux is proportional to $B$, so doubling the field gives $2.0\times10^{-2}\;\text{Wb}$.

(a) 面积矢量与磁场平行，所以 $\Phi_B = BA = (0.25)(0.040) = 1.0\times10^{-2}\;\text{Wb}$。(b) 转动后磁场位于线圈平面内，$\theta = 90^\circ$，所以 $\Phi_B = 0$。(c) 磁通量与 $B$ 成正比，磁场加倍时磁通量为 $2.0\times10^{-2}\;\text{Wb}$。

A static magnetic field does nothing to a stationary loop of wire. The surprise discovered by Faraday is that a changing flux drives charge around the loop. The potential difference that does this is the induced emf. It exists whether or not the loop is closed; if the loop is closed it drives an induced current.

History: Faraday's ring and Lenz's rule历史：法拉第的铁环与楞次定则

On 29 August 1831 Michael Faraday wound two coils on one iron ring. A galvanometer on the second coil kicked only at the instant the first circuit was switched on or off, never while its current was steady. A change in magnetism, not magnetism itself, drove the current.

1831 年 8 月 29 日，迈克尔·法拉第在同一个铁环上绕了两个线圈。只有在第一个电路接通或断开的瞬间，第二个线圈上的电流计才会偏转；电流稳定时从不偏转。驱动电流的是磁的变化，而不是磁本身。

In 1834 Heinrich Lenz stated the direction rule that bears his name: the induced current always opposes the change that produces it.

Lenz's law楞次定律

Faraday's law gives the size of the emf; the minus sign tells you the direction. Lenz's law: the induced emf drives a current whose own magnetic field opposes the change in flux. It does not oppose the flux itself, only its change.

法拉第定律给出电动势的大小；负号告诉你方向。楞次定律：感应电动势驱动的电流，其自身磁场反抗磁通量的变化。它反抗的不是磁通量本身，而是磁通量的变化。

Use this three-step routine: (1) is the flux through the loop (along a chosen direction) increasing or decreasing? (2) the induced field must point against the increase, or along the decrease.

使用以下三步：(1) 沿选定方向，通过线圈的磁通量是在增大还是减小？(2) 感应磁场必须与增大的方向相反，或与减小的方向相同。

(3) use the right-hand rule on that required field to find the current direction (curl the fingers of the right hand in the direction of the current; the thumb gives the field inside the loop).

(3) 对所需的磁场用右手定则确定电流方向（右手四指沿电流方向弯曲，拇指指向线圈内部的磁场方向）。

Common Mistake: "The induced field opposes the field" The induced field points against the original field only when the original flux is increasing. If the flux is decreasing, the induced field points along the original field to resist the loss. If the flux is constant, there is no induced emf at all, even in a strong field.
常见错误：“感应磁场与原磁场相反” 只有当原磁通量增大时，感应磁场才与原磁场相反。若磁通量在减小，感应磁场与原磁场同向，以阻止其减小。若磁通量不变，则根本没有感应电动势，即使磁场很强。
First principles: Lenz's law from energy conservation第一性原理：由能量守恒得出楞次定律

Principle. Energy is conserved: the energy an induced current carries must be supplied by some outside agent.

出发点。能量守恒：感应电流携带的能量必须由某个外界作用提供。

  1. Push a magnet's north pole toward a closed loop, so the flux through the loop increases.
  2. Suppose the induced current helped the change: its field would attract the magnet and pull it in faster.
  3. A faster magnet changes the flux faster, so the current and the pull would both grow with no work put in.
  4. That runaway would create energy from nothing, so the induced current must oppose the change, and you must push to keep the magnet moving.
  1. 把磁铁的 N 极推向闭合线圈，线圈中的磁通量增大。
  2. 假设感应电流助长这一变化：它的磁场会吸引磁铁，使磁铁更快地进入。
  3. 磁铁越快，磁通量变化越快，电流和吸引力都会增大，却无需输入任何功。
  4. 这种失控增长会凭空产生能量，所以感应电流必须反抗变化，你必须推动磁铁才能使它继续运动。

Limiting-case check. Cut the loop open: an emf still appears, but no current flows, so the magnet feels no opposing force and nothing is heated. With the loop closed, the extra work you do pushing the magnet is what heats the wire.

极限检验。把线圈剪开：电动势仍然出现，但没有电流，所以磁铁不受反抗力，也没有发热。线圈闭合时，你推动磁铁多做的功正是导线中产生的热。

Motional emf动生电动势

A conducting rod of length $\ell$ slides at constant speed $v$ along two parallel rails in a uniform magnetic field $B$ that is perpendicular to the plane of the rails. This rod on rails is the standard example of electromagnetic induction.

长为 $\ell$ 的导体棒以恒定速率 $v$ 沿两条平行导轨滑动，均匀磁场 $B$ 垂直于导轨平面。导轨上的棒是电磁感应的标准例子。

In a time $\Delta t$ the rod moves a distance $v\,\Delta t$, so the area of the circuit grows by $\Delta A = \ell v\,\Delta t$ and the flux grows by $\Delta\Phi_B = B\,\Delta A = B\ell v\,\Delta t$. Dividing by $\Delta t$, Faraday's law for one loop gives the magnitude of the motional emf:

在时间 $\Delta t$ 内，棒移动距离 $v\,\Delta t$，回路面积增加 $\Delta A = \ell v\,\Delta t$，磁通量增加 $\Delta\Phi_B = B\,\Delta A = B\ell v\,\Delta t$。除以 $\Delta t$，对一个回路用法拉第定律，得到动生电动势的大小：

Motional emf动生电动势
$$\mathcal{E} = B\ell v$$

$B$ is the field strength, $\ell$ is the length of the rod between the rails, and $v$ is the speed of the rod. This holds when $\vec{B}$, the rod and its velocity are mutually perpendicular. If the rod completes a circuit of total resistance $R$, the induced current is $I = \mathcal{E}/R$.

$B$ 是磁场强度，$\ell$ 是两导轨之间棒的长度，$v$ 是棒的速率。适用于 $\vec{B}$、棒和速度三者互相垂直的情形。若棒与总电阻为 $R$ 的回路构成闭合电路，感应电流为 $I = \mathcal{E}/R$。

The direction comes from Lenz's law. As the rod moves, the flux through the circuit increases or decreases, and the induced current makes a field that opposes that change; the right-hand rule then gives the direction of the current in the circuit. Doubling the speed doubles the emf, and doubling the field or the length also doubles it.

方向由楞次定律决定。棒运动时，通过回路的磁通量增大或减小，感应电流产生的磁场反抗这一变化；再用右手定则得出回路中电流的方向。速率加倍，电动势加倍；磁场或棒长加倍，电动势同样加倍。

Induction creates a current, and a current in a magnetic field feels a force. This topic joins the two. When a conducting loop or rod moves through an external field, the changing flux induces an emf; the emf drives a current; and the field then pushes on that current with a magnetic force.

感应产生电流，而磁场中的电流会受力。本专题把两者结合起来。导体线圈或棒在外磁场中运动时，变化的磁通量感应出电动势；电动势驱动电流；磁场随后以磁力作用于该电流。

Lenz's law guarantees that this force opposes the motion that produced the flux change, which is how energy conservation is built into induction.

楞次定律保证这个力反抗产生磁通量变化的运动，这正是能量守恒在电磁感应中的体现。

The sliding rod on rails导轨上的滑动棒

A rod of length $\ell$, mass $m$ and the circuit resistance $R$ moves at speed $v$ on frictionless rails in a uniform field $B$ perpendicular to the plane. Follow the chain of causes: motional emf, then current, then force.

长 $\ell$、质量 $m$ 的棒，回路总电阻为 $R$，在无摩擦导轨上以速率 $v$ 运动，均匀磁场 $B$ 垂直于导轨平面。沿着因果链往下走：动生电动势，然后电流，然后磁力。

First principles: motional emf from the force on moving charges第一性原理：由运动电荷所受的力得出动生电动势

Principle. A charge $q$ moving at speed $v$ through a field $B$ feels a magnetic force of size $F = qvB\sin\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{B}$.

出发点。电荷 $q$ 以速率 $v$ 在磁场 $B$ 中运动时，受到大小为 $F = qvB\sin\theta$ 的磁力，其中 $\theta$ 是 $\vec{v}$ 与 $\vec{B}$ 的夹角。

  1. The charges in the rod move with it at speed $v$, with $\vec{v}\perp\vec{B}$, so each feels a force of size $qvB$ along the rod.
  2. On an isolated rod, charge piles up at one end until its electric field balances that push: $qE = qvB$, so $E = vB$.
  3. The emf is the work per unit charge along the rod: $\mathcal{E} = E\ell = B\ell v$.
  4. The flux rule agrees: the circuit area grows at the rate $\ell v$, so $\Delta\Phi_B/\Delta t = B\ell v$.
  1. 棒中的电荷随棒以速率 $v$ 运动，且 $\vec{v}\perp\vec{B}$，所以每个电荷受到沿棒方向、大小为 $qvB$ 的力。
  2. 对孤立的棒，电荷在一端堆积，直到其电场与该力平衡：$qE = qvB$，所以 $E = vB$。
  3. 电动势是沿棒移动单位电荷所做的功：$\mathcal{E} = E\ell = B\ell v$。
  4. 磁通量法则给出相同结果：回路面积以 $\ell v$ 的速率增大，所以 $\Delta\Phi_B/\Delta t = B\ell v$。

Limiting-case check. A rod at rest ($v = 0$), or one moving parallel to $\vec{B}$ ($\theta = 0$), has no magnetic force on its charges and no emf. Doubling $v$, $B$ or $\ell$ doubles $\mathcal{E}$.

极限检验。静止的棒（$v = 0$），或平行于 $\vec{B}$ 运动的棒（$\theta = 0$），其中电荷不受磁力，也没有电动势。$v$、$B$ 或 $\ell$ 加倍，$\mathcal{E}$ 加倍。

Induced current and force感应电流与磁力
$$I = \frac{B\ell v}{R},\qquad F = I\ell B = \frac{B^2\ell^2 v}{R}$$

The force is proportional to the speed, and by Lenz's law it points opposite to $\vec{v}$.

该力与速率成正比，并且由楞次定律，其方向与 $\vec{v}$ 相反。

Check the energy: the rate at which the magnetic force removes mechanical energy is $Fv = B^2\ell^2v^2/R$, and the power dissipated in the resistance is $I^2R = (B\ell v/R)^2R = B^2\ell^2v^2/R$. They are equal, so the work done against the magnetic force appears as thermal energy in the resistor, not in the magnetic field.

核对能量：磁力使机械能减少的速率为 $Fv = B^2\ell^2v^2/R$，电阻中耗散的功率为 $I^2R = (B\ell v/R)^2R = B^2\ell^2v^2/R$。两者相等，所以克服磁力所做的功转化为电阻中的热能，而不是磁场中的能量。

  • 论证（justify）为什么直导线被当作电感为零，而螺线管具有显著的电感。

A coil carrying current makes its own magnetic field, and therefore its own flux. If the current changes, that flux changes, and Faraday's law says the coil induces an emf in itself. This self-induced emf opposes the change in current: the coil resists being switched on or off quickly.

通电的线圈产生自己的磁场，因而有自己的磁通量。电流变化时，这个磁通量随之变化，由法拉第定律，线圈在自身中感应出电动势。这种自感电动势反抗电流的变化：线圈抗拒被快速接通或断开。

The tendency to oppose a change in current is called inductance, and a circuit element built to have a lot of it is an inductor.

反抗电流变化的这种趋势称为电感（inductance），专门做成具有大电感的电路元件称为电感器（inductor）。

Definition and the self-induced emf定义与自感电动势

Inductance and self-induced emf电感与自感电动势
$$L = \frac{N\Phi_B}{I},\qquad \mathcal{E}_L = -N\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt} = -L\frac{dI}{dt}$$

$L$ is positive and depends only on the geometry and the core material. The unit is the henry: $1\;\text{H} = 1\;\text{V}\cdot\text{s/A} = 1\;\text{Wb/A}$.

$L$ 为正，只取决于几何形状和铁芯材料。单位是亨利：$1\;\text{H} = 1\;\text{V}\cdot\text{s/A} = 1\;\text{Wb/A}$。

The emf depends on the rate of change of current, not on the current itself. An inductor carrying a large steady current has $dI/dt = 0$, so there is no potential difference across an ideal inductor.

电动势取决于电流的变化率，而不是电流本身。通有很大恒定电流的电感器有 $dI/dt = 0$，所以理想电感器两端没有电势差。

A straight wire has so little flux linkage per ampere that it is modeled as having zero inductance; the inductance of a circuit comes from deliberately coiled elements.

直导线每安培电流的磁链极少，被当作电感为零；电路中的电感来自特意绕成线圈的元件。

Inductance of a solenoid螺线管的电感

For a long solenoid of $N$ turns, length $\ell$, cross-sectional area $A$ and a core of permeability $\mu$ (so $\mu = \mu_0$ for air), the field inside is $B = \mu NI/\ell$. Each of the $N$ turns links the flux $BA$, so the total flux linkage is $N\Phi_B = N\cdot BA = \mu N^2AI/\ell$. Dividing by $I$:

设长螺线管有 $N$ 匝，长度 $\ell$，横截面积 $A$，铁芯磁导率为 $\mu$（空气芯时 $\mu = \mu_0$），内部磁场为 $B = \mu NI/\ell$。$N$ 匝中每匝的磁通量都是 $BA$，所以总磁链为 $N\Phi_B = N\cdot BA = \mu N^2AI/\ell$。除以 $I$：

Inductance of a solenoid螺线管的电感
$$L_{\text{sol}} = \frac{\mu_{\text{core}}N^2A}{\ell}$$

$L$ grows as $N^2$ (more turns raise both the field and the number of turns linked), grows with area and with a magnetic core, and falls as the length grows at fixed $N$.

$L$ 随 $N^2$ 增大（匝数增加既提高磁场，也增加被链接的匝数），随面积和磁性铁芯增大，在 $N$ 固定时随长度增大而减小。

Energy stored in an inductor电感器储存的能量

To raise the current from $0$ to $I$ the source must work against the self-induced emf. The power delivered is $P = |\mathcal{E}_L|\,I = LI\,dI/dt$, so the energy stored when the current reaches $I$ is

要把电流从 $0$ 增大到 $I$，电源必须克服自感电动势做功。输送的功率为 $P = |\mathcal{E}_L|\,I = LI\,dI/dt$，所以电流达到 $I$ 时储存的能量为

The energy is stored in the magnetic field generated by the current. When the current falls, the energy is returned: it can be dissipated in a resistor or used to charge a capacitor, and the total is conserved.

能量储存在电流产生的磁场中。电流减小时能量被释放：可以在电阻中耗散，也可以给电容器充电，总能量守恒。

The emf is $-L\,dI/dt$ and a constant current has $dI/dt = 0$, so the potential difference is zero. When it is connected to a resistor the current decays, and the energy $\tfrac{1}{2}LI^2$ is dissipated in the resistor as thermal energy, so energy is conserved.

电动势为 $-L\,dI/dt$，恒定电流的 $dI/dt = 0$，所以电势差为零。接到电阻上后电流衰减，能量 $\tfrac{1}{2}LI^2$ 在电阻中转化为热能耗散，能量守恒。

Why can a straight connecting wire be treated as having zero inductance, while the same length of wire wound into a solenoid cannot?为什么一段直的连接导线可以被当作电感为零，而同样长度的导线绕成螺线管后却不能？

Inductance is the flux linkage per ampere, $L = N\Phi_B/I$. A straight wire has $N = 1$ and the flux it links is tiny, so $L$ is negligible compared with the other elements.

电感是每安培电流的磁链，$L = N\Phi_B/I$。直导线的 $N = 1$，所链接的磁通量极小，所以 $L$ 与其他元件相比可以忽略。

Winding the wire into $N$ turns makes each turn link the flux of all the others and gives $L\propto N^2$, so a solenoid has a large flux linkage per ampere and a significant $L$.

把导线绕成 $N$ 匝后，每一匝都链接其余各匝产生的磁通量，使 $L\propto N^2$，所以螺线管每安培电流的磁链很大，电感 $L$ 显著。

An inductor does not allow the current through it to jump. When a battery is connected, the current in a series circuit with a resistor and an inductor must climb gradually from zero. The resistor sets the final current; the inductor sets how long the climb takes.

电感器不允许通过它的电流跳变。接上电池后，含电阻和电感器的串联电路中的电流必须从零逐渐上升。电阻决定最终电流；电感器决定上升需要多长时间。

The same two elements also control the decay when the battery is removed: the energy stored in the inductor is dissipated in the resistor.

撤去电池后的衰减同样由这两个元件控制：电感器储存的能量在电阻中耗散。

Loop rule and the growth of current回路定则与电流的增长

Close the switch at $t = 0$ in a circuit with a battery $\mathcal{E}$, resistor $R$ and inductor $L$ in series. Going around the loop in the direction of the current, the battery raises the potential by $\mathcal{E}$, the resistor drops it by $IR$, and the inductor drops it by $L\,dI/dt$ (its emf opposes the growth of $I$):

在电池 $\mathcal{E}$、电阻 $R$ 和电感器 $L$ 串联的电路中，于 $t = 0$ 闭合开关。沿电流方向绕回路一周，电池使电势升高 $\mathcal{E}$，电阻使电势降低 $IR$，电感器使电势降低 $L\,dI/dt$（其电动势反抗 $I$ 的增长）：

Loop equation of a series LR circuit串联 LR 电路的回路方程
$$\mathcal{E} = IR + L\frac{dI}{dt}$$

Separate the variables: $\dfrac{dI}{\mathcal{E}/R - I} = \dfrac{R}{L}\,dt$. Integrate from $I = 0$ at $t = 0$ to find the solution below.

分离变量：$\dfrac{dI}{\mathcal{E}/R - I} = \dfrac{R}{L}\,dt$。从 $t = 0$ 时的 $I = 0$ 起积分，得到下面的解。

Current growth, and the time constant电流增长与时间常数
$$I(t) = \frac{\mathcal{E}}{R}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right),\qquad \tau = \frac{L}{R}$$

The potential differences are $V_R = IR = \mathcal{E}(1 - e^{-t/\tau})$ and $V_L = L\,dI/dt = \mathcal{E}e^{-t/\tau}$, and they always sum to $\mathcal{E}$.

电势差为 $V_R = IR = \mathcal{E}(1 - e^{-t/\tau})$ 和 $V_L = L\,dI/dt = \mathcal{E}e^{-t/\tau}$，两者之和始终等于 $\mathcal{E}$。

What the time constant means时间常数的含义

The time constant $\tau = L/R$ (in seconds) is the single number that describes how quickly an LR circuit settles. Three equivalent readings are worth knowing. (1) Initial rate: at $t = 0$ the current changes at $\mathcal{E}/L$; if it kept changing at that rate it would reach its final value $\mathcal{E}/R$ after exactly $\tau$.

时间常数 $\tau = L/R$（单位为秒）是描述 LR 电路多快趋于稳定的唯一数值。有三种说法值得记住。(1) 初始变化率：在 $t = 0$ 时电流以 $\mathcal{E}/L$ 的速率变化；若一直以该速率变化，恰好经过 $\tau$ 就会达到最终值 $\mathcal{E}/R$。

(2) Growth from zero: after one time constant the current has risen to $1 - e^{-1} \approx 63\%$ of its final value. (3) Decay from an initial current: after one time constant the current has fallen to $e^{-1} \approx 37\%$ of its initial value.

(2) 从零增长：经过一个时间常数，电流上升到最终值的 $1 - e^{-1} \approx 63\%$。(3) 从初始电流衰减：经过一个时间常数，电流降到初始值的 $e^{-1} \approx 37\%$。

After $2\tau$ the growth is at $86.5\%$, after $5\tau$ at $99.3\%$, so a circuit is effectively steady after a few time constants.

经过 $2\tau$，增长达到 $86.5\%$，经过 $5\tau$ 达到 $99.3\%$，所以几个时间常数之后电路基本稳定。

Just after a switch changes, and a long time later开关动作的瞬间与很长时间之后

Immediately after a switch is closed or opened, the current through the inductor is unchanged from its value just before, so the inductor's induced emf is equal in magnitude and opposite in direction to the potential difference applied across its branch. With zero initial current the inductor therefore acts like an open circuit (a gap) at $t = 0^+$.

开关刚闭合或断开的瞬间，通过电感器的电流与动作前相同，所以电感器的感应电动势与加在其支路两端的电势差大小相等、方向相反。初始电流为零时，电感器在 $t = 0^+$ 相当于断路（一个缺口）。

After a time much longer than $\tau$ the current is steady, $dI/dt = 0$, there is no induced emf, and the inductor acts like a wire of zero resistance. The potential difference across the inductor, the current in it and the energy stored in it are all exponential in time, approaching asymptotes fixed by the initial conditions and the final steady state.

经过远大于 $\tau$ 的时间后，电流稳定，$dI/dt = 0$，没有感应电动势，电感器相当于电阻为零的导线。电感器两端的电势差、其中的电流以及储存的能量都随时间按指数变化，趋近由初始条件和最终稳态决定的渐近线。

Connect a charged capacitor to an inductor with no resistance and no battery. The capacitor begins to discharge, but the inductor does not let the current jump: the current builds up as the capacitor empties, and when the capacitor is empty the current is at its maximum and keeps charging the capacitor the other way.

把充了电的电容器接到没有电阻、没有电池的电感器上。电容器开始放电，但电感器不允许电流跳变：电流随电容器放空而增大，电容器放空时电流达到最大，并继续向相反方向给电容器充电。

The energy swings back and forth between the electric field of the capacitor and the magnetic field of the inductor, and the charge oscillates forever in the ideal circuit. A real circuit has some resistance that gradually dissipates the energy; this unit treats the ideal case.

能量在电容器的电场与电感器的磁场之间来回摆动，在理想电路中电荷永远振荡。实际电路有一定电阻，会逐渐耗散能量；本单元只处理理想情形。

History: from Maxwell's equations to Hertz's sparks历史：从麦克斯韦方程组到赫兹的电火花

In 1865 James Clerk Maxwell combined Faraday's law with his own displacement-current term and predicted electromagnetic waves travelling at the speed of light. In the late 1880s Heinrich Hertz made such waves with oscillating spark-gap circuits, whose frequency is set by their inductance and capacitance just as in an LC circuit.

1865 年，詹姆斯·克拉克·麦克斯韦把法拉第定律与他自己提出的位移电流项结合起来，预言了以光速传播的电磁波。19 世纪 80 年代后期，海因里希·赫兹用振荡的火花隙电路产生了这种电磁波；与 LC 电路一样，其频率由电感和电容决定。

Energy conservation in an LC circuitLC 电路中的能量守恒

With no resistance the total energy is constant. At the instant the capacitor holds its maximum charge $Q_0$ the current is zero, so all the energy is electric, $Q_0^2/(2C)$. When the capacitor is empty, the current is at its maximum $I_{\max}$ and all the energy is magnetic, $\tfrac{1}{2}LI_{\max}^2$. Equating the two:

没有电阻时总能量恒定。电容器电荷最大（$Q_0$）的瞬间电流为零，全部能量是电能 $Q_0^2/(2C)$。电容器放空时，电流最大（$I_{\max}$），全部能量是磁能 $\tfrac{1}{2}LI_{\max}^2$。令两者相等：

Maximum current from energy conservation由能量守恒求最大电流
$$\frac{Q_0^2}{2C} = \tfrac{1}{2}LI_{\max}^2\;\Rightarrow\; I_{\max} = \frac{Q_0}{\sqrt{LC}}$$

At a general instant, $\dfrac{q^2}{2C} + \tfrac{1}{2}LI^2 = \dfrac{Q_0^2}{2C}$, which lets you find the current when the charge has a given value.

在任意时刻，$\dfrac{q^2}{2C} + \tfrac{1}{2}LI^2 = \dfrac{Q_0^2}{2C}$，据此可以求出电荷为给定值时的电流。

The differential equation and the angular frequency微分方程与角频率

Apply the loop rule to the loop of the capacitor and inductor. The potential difference across the capacitor is $q/C$, and the inductor's induced emf is $-L\,dI/dt$ with $I$ the current that charges the capacitor, $I = dq/dt$. Take the loop direction along $I$, with $I = dq/dt$ defining the sign of $q$. The loop rule then gives $q/C + L\,dI/dt = 0$, and substituting $I = dq/dt$ gives $L\,d^2q/dt^2 + q/C = 0$:

对电容器与电感器构成的回路应用回路定则。电容器两端的电势差为 $q/C$，电感器的感应电动势为 $-L\,dI/dt$，其中 $I$ 是给电容器充电的电流，$I = dq/dt$。取回路方向与 $I$ 一致，并以 $I = dq/dt$ 确定 $q$ 的符号。回路定则给出 $q/C + L\,dI/dt = 0$，代入 $I = dq/dt$ 得 $L\,d^2q/dt^2 + q/C = 0$：

Differential equation of an LC circuitLC 电路的微分方程
$$L\frac{d^2q}{dt^2} + \frac{q}{C} = 0\;\Rightarrow\;\frac{d^2q}{dt^2} = -\frac{1}{LC}\,q$$

This has exactly the form $\ddot{x} = -\omega^2x$ of simple harmonic motion, so the charge oscillates sinusoidally.

它的形式恰好是简谐运动的 $\ddot{x} = -\omega^2x$，所以电荷做正弦振荡。

Angular frequency, period, and the solution角频率、周期与解
$$\omega = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}},\quad T = 2\pi\sqrt{LC},\quad q(t) = Q_0\cos(\omega t + \phi),\quad I = \frac{dq}{dt} = -\omega Q_0\sin(\omega t + \phi)$$

Compare coefficients with $\ddot{x} = -\omega^2x$: $\omega^2 = 1/(LC)$. The frequency depends on $L$ and $C$ only, not on the amplitude $Q_0$. The maximum current is $\omega Q_0 = Q_0/\sqrt{LC}$, matching the energy method.

与 $\ddot{x} = -\omega^2x$ 比较系数：$\omega^2 = 1/(LC)$。频率只取决于 $L$ 和 $C$，与振幅 $Q_0$ 无关。最大电流为 $\omega Q_0 = Q_0/\sqrt{LC}$，与能量法一致。

Starting from a fully charged capacitor ($q = Q_0$, $\phi = 0$), the charge and current are a quarter period out of step: when $|q|$ is maximum, $I = 0$; when $q = 0$, $|I|$ is maximum. The energies are $U_C = \dfrac{Q_0^2}{2C}\cos^2\omega t$ and $U_L = \dfrac{Q_0^2}{2C}\sin^2\omega t$, which add to a constant and each oscillate at twice the circuit's frequency.

从电容器充满电开始（$q = Q_0$，$\phi = 0$），电荷与电流相差四分之一周期：$|q|$ 最大时 $I = 0$；$q = 0$ 时 $|I|$ 最大。能量为 $U_C = \dfrac{Q_0^2}{2C}\cos^2\omega t$ 和 $U_L = \dfrac{Q_0^2}{2C}\sin^2\omega t$，两者之和为常数，且各自以电路频率的两倍振荡。

The mass-spring analogy质量-弹簧类比

The LC circuit is the electrical twin of a mass on a spring. Charge $q$ plays the role of position $x$, current $I$ the role of velocity $v$, the inductance $L$ the role of mass $m$ (inertia: it resists change in current just as mass resists change in velocity), and $1/C$ the role of the spring constant $k$.

LC 电路是弹簧振子的电学孪生。电荷 $q$ 对应位置 $x$，电流 $I$ 对应速度 $v$，电感 $L$ 对应质量 $m$（惯性：它抗拒电流的变化，正如质量抗拒速度的变化），$1/C$ 对应劲度系数 $k$。

Then $\omega = \sqrt{k/m}$ becomes $\omega = \sqrt{(1/C)/L} = 1/\sqrt{LC}$, the electric energy $q^2/(2C)$ is the spring energy $\tfrac{1}{2}kx^2$, and the magnetic energy $\tfrac{1}{2}LI^2$ is the kinetic energy $\tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$.

于是 $\omega = \sqrt{k/m}$ 变为 $\omega = \sqrt{(1/C)/L} = 1/\sqrt{LC}$，电能 $q^2/(2C)$ 对应弹性势能 $\tfrac{1}{2}kx^2$，磁能 $\tfrac{1}{2}LI^2$ 对应动能 $\tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$。

Worked Example: Oscillations of a Charged Capacitor and an Inductor例题：充电电容器与电感器的振荡

A $2.0\;\mu\text{F}$ capacitor is charged to $50\;\text{V}$ and connected across an ideal $8.0\;\text{mH}$ inductor. Find the maximum charge, the angular frequency, the frequency and period, the maximum current by two methods, and the time for the capacitor to discharge fully for the first time.

$2.0\;\mu\text{F}$ 的电容器充电到 $50\;\text{V}$，然后接到理想的 $8.0\;\text{mH}$ 电感器两端。求最大电荷、角频率、频率和周期、用两种方法求最大电流，以及电容器第一次完全放电所需的时间。

Step 1: Charge and frequency第 1 步：电荷与频率
$$Q_0 = CV = (2.0\times10^{-6})(50) = 1.0\times10^{-4}\;\text{C},\qquad \omega = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{(8.0\times10^{-3})(2.0\times10^{-6})}} = 7.9\times10^{3}\;\text{rad/s}$$
Step 2: Frequency and period第 2 步：频率与周期
$$f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} = 1.26\times10^{3}\;\text{Hz},\qquad T = \frac{1}{f} = 7.9\times10^{-4}\;\text{s}$$
Step 3: Maximum current第 3 步：最大电流
Use $I_{\max} = \omega Q_0$, and check with energy conservation using $U = \tfrac{1}{2}CV^2 = 2.5\times10^{-3}\;\text{J}$.用 $I_{\max} = \omega Q_0$，并用能量守恒核对，$U = \tfrac{1}{2}CV^2 = 2.5\times10^{-3}\;\text{J}$。
$$I_{\max} = \omega Q_0 = (7.9\times10^{3})(1.0\times10^{-4}) = 0.79\;\text{A},\qquad I_{\max} = \sqrt{\frac{2U}{L}} = \sqrt{\frac{5.0\times10^{-3}}{8.0\times10^{-3}}} = 0.79\;\text{A}$$
Step 4: First full discharge第 4 步：第一次完全放电
The charge goes from $Q_0$ to zero in a quarter period.电荷在四分之一周期内由 $Q_0$ 降为零。
$$t = \frac{T}{4} = 2.0\times10^{-4}\;\text{s}$$

For the same circuit, find the current at the instant the capacitor holds $6.0\times10^{-5}\;\text{C}$, and the fraction of the total energy then in the capacitor.

对同一电路，求电容器带电荷 $6.0\times10^{-5}\;\text{C}$ 的时刻的电流，以及此时电容器中的能量占总能量的比例。

Exam tip考试提示

On an LC question choose the method that matches the givens: use energy conservation when the question asks for a current or charge at a particular state, and use $\omega = 1/\sqrt{LC}$ when it asks for timing. The charge and current graphs are a quarter period apart, so label which curve is which and mark the period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{LC}$ on the time axis.

做 LC 题时，选与已知条件匹配的方法：当问某一状态下的电流或电荷时用能量守恒，当问时间关系时用 $\omega = 1/\sqrt{LC}$。电荷与电流的图像相差四分之一周期，所以要标明哪条曲线是哪个量，并在时间轴上标出周期 $T = 2\pi\sqrt{LC}$。

In an experiment, vary $C$ with $L$ fixed, plot $T^2$ against $C$, and read $L$ from the slope $4\pi^2L$; this linearization is how experimental-design questions on LC circuits are usually answered.

在实验中，保持 $L$ 不变改变 $C$，作 $T^2$ 对 $C$ 的图像，由斜率 $4\pi^2L$ 读出 $L$；LC 电路的实验设计题通常这样线性化作答。

If the capacitance in an ideal LC circuit is quadrupled with $L$ unchanged, what happens to the angular frequency and the period? Explain using the mass-spring analogy.理想 LC 电路中电容变为 4 倍而 $L$ 不变，角频率和周期如何变化？请用质量-弹簧类比解释。

$\omega = 1/\sqrt{LC}$ falls to $1/\sqrt4 = 1/2$ of its value, so $\omega$ halves and $T$ doubles. In the analogy $1/C$ is the spring constant, so quadrupling $C$ makes the spring four times softer and the oscillation slower.

$\omega = 1/\sqrt{LC}$ 减小为原来的 $1/\sqrt4 = 1/2$，所以 $\omega$ 减半，$T$ 加倍。在类比中 $1/C$ 对应劲度系数，$C$ 变 4 倍使弹簧变软 4 倍，振荡变慢。

$C = \dfrac{1}{\omega^2L} = \dfrac{1}{(2\pi\times10^{6})^2(5.0\times10^{-3})} = 5.1\times10^{-12}\;\text{F} = 5.1\;\text{pF}$.

$C = \dfrac{1}{\omega^2L} = \dfrac{1}{(2\pi\times10^{6})^2(5.0\times10^{-3})} = 5.1\times10^{-12}\;\text{F} = 5.1\;\text{pF}$。

In a lab, students measure the period $T$ of an LC circuit for several capacitors with the same inductor. A plot of $T^2$ against $C$ is a straight line of slope $0.395\;\text{s}^2/\text{F}$. Find $L$ and explain why plotting $T^2$ against $C$ is the right linearization.实验中，学生用同一个电感器和几个不同电容器测量 LC 电路的周期 $T$。$T^2$ 对 $C$ 的图像是斜率为 $0.395\;\text{s}^2/\text{F}$ 的直线。求 $L$，并解释为什么 $T^2$ 对 $C$ 作图是合适的线性化。

$T = 2\pi\sqrt{LC}$ gives $T^2 = 4\pi^2L\,C$, a line through the origin whose slope is $4\pi^2L$. So $L = 0.395/(4\pi^2) = 1.0\times10^{-2}\;\text{H} = 10\;\text{mH}$.

$T = 2\pi\sqrt{LC}$ 给出 $T^2 = 4\pi^2L\,C$，这是过原点、斜率为 $4\pi^2L$ 的直线。所以 $L = 0.395/(4\pi^2) = 1.0\times10^{-2}\;\text{H} = 10\;\text{mH}$。

Describe in words the graphs of $q(t)$, $I(t)$ and the two stored energies against time for an ideal LC circuit whose capacitor holds the full charge $Q_0$ at $t=0$.用文字描述理想 LC 电路中 $q(t)$、$I(t)$ 以及两种储能随时间变化的图像，已知 $t=0$ 时电容器带有全部电荷 $Q_0$。

$q(t)=Q_0\cos\omega t$ starts at its maximum $Q_0$ with zero slope and oscillates between $+Q_0$ and $-Q_0$ with period $T=2\pi\sqrt{LC}$. $I(t)=-\omega Q_0\sin\omega t$ is zero at $t=0$ and has amplitude $\omega Q_0$; $I$ is zero when $q$ is at an extreme and $I$ is at an extreme when $q=0$, a quarter period out of phase with $q$.

$q(t)=Q_0\cos\omega t$ 从最大值 $Q_0$ 开始，初始斜率为零，在 $+Q_0$ 与 $-Q_0$ 之间振荡，周期为 $T=2\pi\sqrt{LC}$。$I(t)=-\omega Q_0\sin\omega t$ 在 $t=0$ 时为零，振幅为 $\omega Q_0$；当 $q$ 取极值时 $I$ 为零，当 $q=0$ 时 $I$ 取极值，与 $q$ 相差四分之一周期。

$U_C=q^2/2C=(Q_0^2/2C)\cos^2\omega t$ and $U_L=\tfrac12LI^2=(Q_0^2/2C)\sin^2\omega t$ (using $L\omega^2=1/C$) each oscillate between $0$ and $Q_0^2/2C$ at twice the frequency of $q$, are out of phase with each other, and always sum to the constant $Q_0^2/2C$.

$U_C=q^2/2C=(Q_0^2/2C)\cos^2\omega t$ 与 $U_L=\tfrac12LI^2=(Q_0^2/2C)\sin^2\omega t$（利用 $L\omega^2=1/C$）都在 $0$ 与 $Q_0^2/2C$ 之间振荡，频率是 $q$ 的两倍，彼此反相，且总和始终为常数 $Q_0^2/2C$。

Direction questions: run the Lenz routine in order: the original field, whether the flux is increasing or decreasing, the induced field that opposes the change, and the right-hand rule for the current. Do it for every direction question, even when the answer looks obvious.

方向题：按顺序做楞次定律的流程：原磁场、磁通量增大还是减小、反抗该变化的感应磁场、用右手定则确定电流。每道方向题都这样做，即使答案看起来很明显。

Scaling questions: reason with proportionalities. The emf of a rod is proportional to $B$, $\ell$ and $v$; the magnetic force on the rod is proportional to $v/R$; the flux through a loop is proportional to $B$, $A$ and $\cos\theta$.

比例题：用比例关系推理。棒的电动势与 $B$、$\ell$、$v$ 成正比；棒所受的磁力与 $v/R$ 成正比；线圈的磁通量与 $B$、$A$、$\cos\theta$ 成正比。

Translation between representations: turn a graph of flux against time into a graph of emf against time. The emf is $N$ times the slope of the flux graph with a minus sign, so a straight segment gives a constant emf and a flat segment gives zero emf.

不同表征之间的转换：把磁通量随时间变化的图像转换成电动势随时间变化的图像。电动势等于磁通量图像斜率的 $N$ 倍再取负号，所以直线段对应恒定的电动势，水平段对应电动势为零。

Name the independent variable ($v$), the dependent variable ($\mathcal{E}$), the quantities held fixed ($B$ and $\ell$), and read $B\ell$ from the slope of the graph of $\mathcal{E}$ against $v$.

实验设计与分析：要检验 $\mathcal{E} = B\ell v$，可在几个不同速率下测量导轨上棒的电动势。要写明自变量（$v$）、因变量（$\mathcal{E}$）、保持不变的量（$B$ 和 $\ell$），并由 $\mathcal{E}$ 对 $v$ 的图像斜率读出 $B\ell$。

Direction questions: run the Lenz routine in order: original field, increasing or decreasing flux, required induced field, right-hand rule for the current. Do it for every direction question, even when the answer looks obvious.

方向题：按顺序做楞次定律的流程：原磁场、磁通量增大还是减小、所需的感应磁场、用右手定则确定电流。每道方向题都这样做，即使答案看起来很明显。

Scaling questions: reason with proportionalities. $F\propto v/R$ for a rod, $L\propto N^2A/\ell$ for a solenoid, $U_L\propto I^2$, $\omega\propto 1/\sqrt{LC}$.

比例题：用比例关系推理。棒：$F\propto v/R$；螺线管：$L\propto N^2A/\ell$；$U_L\propto I^2$；$\omega\propto 1/\sqrt{LC}$。

Time-constant questions: convert the time given into a multiple of $\tau$ first; then use $e^{-1}=0.37$, $e^{-2}=0.14$ and $1-e^{-1}=0.63$.

Mathematical routines: derive from the loop rule. For an LR circuit, write $\mathcal{E} = IR + L\,dI/dt$, separate variables and integrate; for an LC circuit, write $L\,d^2q/dt^2 + q/C = 0$ and read off $\omega$. Practise the flux integral for a non-uniform field with a stated strip.

数学过程：由回路定则推导。对 LR 电路，写出 $\mathcal{E} = IR + L\,dI/dt$，分离变量并积分；对 LC 电路，写出 $L\,d^2q/dt^2 + q/C = 0$ 并读出 $\omega$。练习用指定的细条计算非均匀磁场中的磁通量积分。

Translation between representations: sketch $I(t)$, $V_L(t)$ and $V_R(t)$ for a switch closing or opening, and $q(t)$ with $I(t)$ for an LC circuit; translate a graph of flux against time into a graph of emf against time (the emf is the negative slope).

不同表征之间的转换：画出开关闭合或断开时的 $I(t)$、$V_L(t)$、$V_R(t)$，以及 LC 电路的 $q(t)$ 与 $I(t)$；把磁通量随时间变化的图像转换成电动势随时间变化的图像（电动势是斜率的相反数）。

Experimental design and analysis: measure the peak emf of a magnet spinning in a coil against rotation frequency, area and number of turns; find $L$ from an LR time constant (with and without an iron core); linearize an LC circuit with $T^2$ against $C$. Name the independent variable, the dependent variable, the controls, and the quantity read from the slope.

实验设计与分析：测量线圈中转动磁铁的峰值电动势随转动频率、面积和匝数的变化；由 LR 时间常数求 $L$（有铁芯与无铁芯）；用 $T^2$ 对 $C$ 对 LC 电路线性化。要写明自变量、因变量、控制变量，以及由斜率读出的量。

Qualitative/quantitative translation: explain in words why a coasting rod slows down exponentially, then support the claim with $F = B^2\ell^2v/R$; explain why the inductor acts as a gap just after a switch closes and a wire a long time later.

定性与定量之间的转换：用文字解释为什么滑行的棒按指数规律减速，再用 $F = B^2\ell^2v/R$ 支持这一论断；解释为什么开关刚闭合时电感器相当于断路、很长时间后相当于导线。

Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Saying the induced field opposes the original field. It opposes the change in flux.
  2. Measuring the flux angle from the plane of the loop instead of from its normal.
  3. Forgetting the factor $N$ in $\mathcal{E} = -N\,\Delta\Phi_B/\Delta t$ for a coil.
  4. Using the final flux instead of the change in flux $\Delta\Phi_B$.
  5. Forgetting that a loop fully inside a uniform field has no induced current and no net force.
最常见的错误
  1. 说感应磁场与原磁场相反。它反抗的是磁通量的变化。
  2. 从线圈平面而不是从其法线量取磁通量的夹角。

  4. 用末态磁通量而不是磁通量的变化量 $\Delta\Phi_B$。
  5. 忘了完全处于均匀磁场中的线圈没有感应电流，也没有净磁力。
Top Mistakes to Avoid

  2. Measuring the flux angle from the plane of the loop instead of from its normal.
  3. Forgetting the factor $N$ in $\mathcal{E} = -N\,d\Phi_B/dt$ for a coil.
  4. Using $I = \mathcal{E}/R$ for an LR circuit at $t = 0$: the current is zero then, and the whole emf is across the inductor.

  2. 从线圈平面而不是从其法线量取磁通量的夹角。
  3. 线圈的 $\mathcal{E} = -N\,d\Phi_B/dt$ 中漏掉因子 $N$。
  4. 在 $t = 0$ 时对 LR 电路使用 $I = \mathcal{E}/R$：此时电流为零，全部电动势都加在电感器上。
  5. 把电感器的电动势当作与电流成正比，而不是与 $dI/dt$ 成正比。
  6. 把 LR 电路的两个极限搞混：$t = 0^+$ 时是断路，很长时间后是导线。
  7. 使用 $\tau = RL$ 或 $\tau = R/L$；正确的是 $L/R$（$RC$ 属于电容器电路）。
  8. 忘了完全处于均匀磁场中的线圈没有感应电流，也没有净磁力。
  9. 把 $f = 1/\sqrt{LC}$ 当作频率；那是 $\omega$。频率是 $f = \omega/2\pi$。
