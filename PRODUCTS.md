# Dingrui Scholars — Product Catalog

Dingrui Scholars publishes bilingual (English / 简体中文) STEM study products for the HS -> AP/IB -> first-year-university pipeline. Every page is a self-contained, print-ready single-file app: KaTeX-rendered math, an EN/ZH language toggle, a dark-mode toggle, and interactive quizzes where the product type calls for one.

Each unit ships up to three linked products — **Study Guide** (long-form lesson with worked examples), **Practice Questions** (problem set), and **Solutions** (full worked solutions to that practice set). The first few units of every course are open for free preview; deeper units unlock for enrolled students.
