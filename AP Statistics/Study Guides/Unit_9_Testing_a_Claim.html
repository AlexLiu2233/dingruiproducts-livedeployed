Topic 9.1主题 9.1

Setting Up a Test for a Population Proportion设置总体比例的检验

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State the null hypothesis $H_0$ and the alternative hypothesis $H_a$ for a claim about one population proportion, defining the parameter in words.
  • Decide whether the alternative is one-sided or two-sided from the wording of the question.
  • Explain the role of the significance level $\alpha$ and choose it before looking at the data.
  • Check the conditions for a test: random, 10% condition, and at least 10 expected successes and 10 expected failures using the hypothesized value $p_0$.
  • Explain why a test uses $p_0$ where an interval uses $\hat{p}$.
  • 针对一个总体比例的说法，写出原假设 $H_0$ 与备择假设 $H_a$，并用文字定义参数。
  • 根据题目措辞判断备择假设是单侧还是双侧。
  • 解释显著性水平 $\alpha$ 的作用，并在看数据之前确定它。
  • 检验条件：随机、10% 条件，以及用假设值 $p_0$ 算得的至少 10 个预期成功与 10 个预期失败。
  • 解释为什么检验用 $p_0$，而区间用 $\hat{p}$。

Hypotheses假设

A significance test weighs the evidence in a sample against a claim. The claim being tested is the null hypothesis $H_0$: it says the parameter equals a specific hypothesized value $p_0$, so it always contains an equals sign, as in $H_0: p=p_0$. The alternative hypothesis $H_a$ is what we suspect or want evidence for. It is one-sided if it points in one direction ($H_a: p>p_0$ or $H_a: p<p_0$) and two-sided if it says only that the value differs ($H_a: p\ne p_0$).

显著性检验用样本中的证据去衡量一个说法。被检验的说法是原假设 $H_0$：它断言参数等于某个具体的假设值 $p_0$，所以总含等号，如 $H_0: p=p_0$。备择假设 $H_a$ 是我们怀疑或想获得证据支持的内容。若它指向一个方向（$H_a: p>p_0$ 或 $H_a: p<p_0$）则是单侧的；若只说取值不同（$H_a: p\ne p_0$）则是双侧的。

The question asks whether$H_a$ isType
the proportion is greater than $p_0$ (increased, exceeds, more than)$p>p_0$one-sided (right-tailed)
the proportion is less than $p_0$ (decreased, fewer than, below)$p<p_0$one-sided (left-tailed)
the proportion is different from $p_0$ (changed, not equal to)$p\ne p_0$two-sided
题目问$H_a$ 为类型
比例大于 $p_0$（增加、超过、多于）$p>p_0$单侧（右尾）
比例小于 $p_0$（减少、少于、低于）$p<p_0$单侧（左尾）
比例不同于 $p_0$（改变、不等于）$p\ne p_0$双侧

Always define the parameter in words and in context: "$p$ = the proportion of all customers of this bakery who choose a gluten-free item." Hypotheses are about parameters, never about statistics, so $H_0: \hat{p}=0.25$ is wrong.

始终结合情境用文字定义参数：“$p$ = 这家面包店全体顾客中选择无麸质食品的比例。”假设讨论的是参数，绝不是统计量，所以写 $H_0: \hat{p}=0.25$ 是错的。

Significance level and conditions显著性水平与条件

The significance level $\alpha$ is the cut-off for how unlikely a result must be, if $H_0$ is true, before we call it convincing evidence against $H_0$. Common choices are $\alpha=0.05$ and $\alpha=0.01$; choose it before looking at the data. Its meaning as an error rate is the subject of topic 9.4.

显著性水平 $\alpha$ 是临界标准：若 $H_0$ 为真，结果必须多么罕见，才算对 $H_0$ 有令人信服的反对证据。常用 $\alpha=0.05$ 与 $\alpha=0.01$；要在看数据之前确定。它作为错误率的含义见主题 9.4。

The conditions mirror the interval but use the hypothesized value, because the test works in a world where $H_0$ is true: (1) random sample or randomized experiment; (2) 10% condition; (3) the expected number of successes and failures under $H_0$ is at least 10, that is, $np_0\ge 10$ and $n(1-p_0)\ge 10$. These checks use $p_0$, not $\hat{p}$.

条件与区间对应，但使用假设值，因为检验是在 $H_0$ 为真的世界里进行的：(1) 随机样本或随机化实验；(2) 10% 条件；(3) 在 $H_0$ 下预期的成功数与失败数至少为 10，即 $np_0\ge 10$ 且 $n(1-p_0)\ge 10$。这些检验用 $p_0$，不用 $\hat{p}$。

Worked Example: writing hypotheses from a claim例题：依据说法写出假设

Write $H_0$ and $H_a$ for each claim, defining the parameter. (a) A bakery states that 25% of customers choose a gluten-free item; the owner suspects the true share is lower. (b) A player's long-run free-throw rate is $70\%$; after a training program her coach believes it has improved. (c) A district reports that 48% of voters approve of a plan; a reporter thinks the figure is wrong. (Illustrative claims.)

  1. (a) $p$ = the proportion of all customers of this bakery who choose a gluten-free item. $H_0: p=0.25$, $H_a: p<0.25$ (one-sided; the owner suspects lower).
  2. (b) $p$ = the proportion of free throws this player makes after the training program. $H_0: p=0.70$, $H_a: p>0.70$ (one-sided; improvement).
  3. (c) $p$ = the proportion of all voters in the district who approve of the plan. $H_0: p=0.48$, $H_a: p\ne 0.48$ (two-sided; "wrong" does not say which way).

Why this works: the direction of $H_a$ comes from the question asked before the data are seen, and $H_0$ always names the single value being tested.

针对每个说法写出 $H_0$ 与 $H_a$ 并定义参数。(a) 某面包店称 25% 的顾客选择无麸质食品，店主怀疑真实占比更低。(b) 某球员长期罚球命中率为 $70\%$；训练后教练认为有所提高。(c) 某学区报告 48% 的选民赞成一项计划，记者认为这个数字不对。（示意说法。）

  1. (a) $p$ = 这家面包店全体顾客中选择无麸质食品的比例。$H_0: p=0.25$，$H_a: p<0.25$（单侧；店主怀疑更低）。
  2. (b) $p$ = 该球员训练后罚球的命中比例。$H_0: p=0.70$，$H_a: p>0.70$（单侧；提高）。
  3. (c) $p$ = 该学区全体选民中赞成这项计划的比例。$H_0: p=0.48$，$H_a: p\ne 0.48$（双侧；“不对”没有说明方向）。

原理：$H_a$ 的方向取决于看数据之前提出的问题，而 $H_0$ 总是指明被检验的那个具体值。

Worked Example: conditions use the hypothesized value例题：条件使用假设值

An inspector tests $H_0: p=0.15$ against $H_a: p\ne 0.15$ for the proportion of defective parts from a very large production run, using a random sample of $n=100$ parts in which 9 are defective. Check the conditions. (Illustrative data.)

  1. Random. The parts were randomly sampled (stated).
  2. 10% condition. The run is very large, so $100$ is less than 10% of it.
  3. Expected counts under $H_0$. $np_0=100(0.15)=15\ge 10$ and $n(1-p_0)=100(0.85)=85\ge 10$. Both hold, so the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ is approximately normal when $H_0$ is true.
  4. Note. The sample has only $9$ defective parts, but that is irrelevant for a test: the question is what the distribution looks like if $p=0.15$. (For an interval, the sample counts $n\hat{p}=9<10$ would be a problem.)

检查员对一大批产品中次品比例检验 $H_0: p=0.15$ 对 $H_a: p\ne 0.15$，随机抽取 $n=100$ 件，其中 9 件为次品。检验条件。（示意数据。）

  1. 随机。零件为随机抽取（已说明）。
  2. 10% 条件。这批产品数量很大，所以 $100$ 不到其 10%。
  3. $H_0$ 下的预期计数。$np_0=100(0.15)=15\ge 10$，$n(1-p_0)=100(0.85)=85\ge 10$。二者都成立，所以 $H_0$ 为真时 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布近似正态。
  4. 注意。样本中只有 $9$ 件次品，但这对检验无关紧要：问题是如果 $p=0.15$，分布是什么样。（对区间而言，样本计数 $n\hat{p}=9<10$ 就会是问题。）
Worked Example: a complete setup in context例题：情境中的完整设置

A subscription company says that 60% of its users renew after the first year. A product manager suspects the renewal rate is lower. In a random sample of 150 users (a small part of a much larger user base), 81 renewed. Write the full setup: hypotheses, procedure, conditions and test statistic. (Illustrative data.)

  1. Hypotheses. Let $p$ = the proportion of all this company's users who renew after the first year. $H_0: p=0.60$ and $H_a: p<0.60$ ("lower" makes the alternative one-sided). No level is given, so use $\alpha=0.05$.
  2. Procedure and conditions. One-sample $z$ test for a proportion. Random: stated. 10% condition: 150 is less than 10% of the users. Expected counts under $H_0$: $np_0=150(0.60)=90\ge 10$ and $n(1-p_0)=150(0.40)=60\ge 10$.
  3. Statistic. $\hat{p}=81/150=0.54$. The standard error uses $p_0$: $\sqrt{0.60(0.40)/150}=\sqrt{0.0016}=0.04$. So $z=\dfrac{0.54-0.60}{0.04}=-1.50$.
  4. Look ahead. The lower-tail area is $P(Z<-1.50)\approx 0.067$, which is greater than $0.05$. A complete answer states that the data do not provide convincing evidence that the proportion of all users who renew is less than 0.60.

Why this works: the claim fixes $H_0$ and $p_0$, the manager's question fixes the direction of $H_a$, and the sample enters only through $\hat{p}$ in the numerator of $z$.

一家订阅公司称其 60% 的用户在第一年后续订。产品经理怀疑续订率更低。在一个随机样本的 150 名用户（只占庞大用户群的一小部分）中，81 人续订。写出完整的检验设置：假设、方法、条件与检验统计量。（示意数据。）

  1. 假设。设 $p$ = 该公司全体用户中第一年后续订的比例。$H_0: p=0.60$，$H_a: p<0.60$（“更低”使备择假设为单侧）。题目未给水平，取 $\alpha=0.05$。
  2. 方法与条件。总体比例的单样本 $z$ 检验。随机：已说明。10% 条件：150 不到用户总数的 10%。$H_0$ 下的预期计数：$np_0=150(0.60)=90\ge 10$，$n(1-p_0)=150(0.40)=60\ge 10$。
  3. 统计量。$\hat{p}=81/150=0.54$。标准误使用 $p_0$：$\sqrt{0.60(0.40)/150}=\sqrt{0.0016}=0.04$。所以 $z=\dfrac{0.54-0.60}{0.04}=-1.50$。
  4. 预先看结果。下尾面积 $P(Z<-1.50)\approx 0.067$，大于 $0.05$。完整的答案应写出：数据没有提供令人信服的证据表明全体用户的续订比例低于 0.60。

原理：说法确定了 $H_0$ 与 $p_0$，经理的问题确定了 $H_a$ 的方向，样本只通过 $z$ 分子中的 $\hat{p}$ 进入计算。

Common Mistake: hypotheses about the sample

Writing $H_0: \hat{p}=0.25$, or "$H_0$: the sample has 25%," is a frequent error. The sample proportion is known; the hypotheses are statements about the unknown population proportion $p$. Also avoid inequalities in $H_0$ and do not switch the direction of $H_a$ after seeing which way the data point.

常见错误：关于样本的假设

写 $H_0: \hat{p}=0.25$ 或“$H_0$：样本中有 25%”是常见错误。样本比例是已知的；假设是关于未知总体比例 $p$ 的陈述。另外，$H_0$ 中不要用不等式，也不要在看到数据偏向后再改变 $H_a$ 的方向。

先写出带总体和变量的参数定义，再写带 $p_0$ 的 $H_0$ 和符号正确的 $H_a$。在 Plan 一步中，给出检验名称（单比例 $z$ 检验）、假设、显著性水平，以及带 $np_0$ 与 $n(1-p_0)$ 数值的三个条件。

Topic 9.2主题 9.2

Interpreting a p-Value解释 p 值

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define the p-value as the probability, assuming the null hypothesis is true, of a result at least as extreme as the observed one.
  • Find a p-value as a tail area for a right-tailed, left-tailed or two-sided alternative.
  • Interpret a p-value in context, naming the population, the null value and the direction of "extreme".
  • Compare a p-value with $\alpha$ to describe how strong the evidence against $H_0$ is.
  • Explain why the p-value is not the probability that $H_0$ is true.
  • 把 p 值定义为：假定原假设为真时，得到至少与观察结果一样极端的结果的概率。
  • 对右尾、左尾或双侧备择假设，把 p 值求为尾部面积。
  • 结合情境解释 p 值，点明总体、原假设取值以及“极端”的方向。
  • 把 p 值与 $\alpha$ 比较，说明反对 $H_0$ 的证据有多强。
  • 解释为什么 p 值不是 $H_0$ 为真的概率。

The p-value is the probability of getting a sample result at least as extreme as the one observed (in the direction of $H_a$), assuming the null hypothesis is true. It measures how surprising the data are in a world where $H_0$ holds. A small p-value means the observed result would be rare if $H_0$ were true, which is evidence against $H_0$; a large p-value means the result is ordinary under $H_0$.

p 值是在假定原假设为真的前提下，得到至少与所观察样本结果一样极端（沿 $H_a$ 的方向）的结果的概率。它衡量在 $H_0$ 成立的世界里数据有多令人意外。p 值小，说明若 $H_0$ 为真则该结果罕见，这是反对 $H_0$ 的证据；p 值大，说明该结果在 $H_0$ 下很平常。

To find it, standardize to the test statistic $z$ (topic 9.3) and take the area under the standard normal curve in the direction of $H_a$: the right tail for $H_a: p>p_0$, the left tail for $H_a: p<p_0$, and both tails (double the one-tail area) for $H_a: p\ne p_0$.

求 p 值时，先标准化为检验统计量 $z$（主题 9.3），再沿 $H_a$ 的方向取标准正态曲线下的面积：$H_a: p>p_0$ 取右尾，$H_a: p<p_0$ 取左尾，$H_a: p\ne p_0$ 取两侧尾部（单尾面积的两倍）。

The p-value is not the probability that H0 is true

The p-value is not the probability that the null hypothesis is true, and it is not the probability that the result happened "by chance." It is a probability about the data computed assuming $H_0$. A p-value of $0.03$ does not mean $H_0$ has a 3% chance of being true. Under the usual set-up $H_0$ is either true or false and has no probability in this framework.

p 值不是 H0 为真的概率

p 值不是原假设为真的概率，也不是结果“出于偶然”的概率。它是在假定 $H_0$ 成立下计算的、关于数据的概率。p 值为 $0.03$ 并不表示 $H_0$ 有 3% 的可能为真。在通常的框架里，$H_0$ 要么为真要么为假，没有概率可言。

Worked Example: finding p-values from z例题：由 z 求 p 值

Find the p-value for (a) $H_a: p>p_0$ with $z=1.84$, and (b) $H_a: p\ne p_0$ with $z=-2.30$.

  1. (a) Right-tailed. p-value $=P(Z\ge 1.84)\approx 0.0329$ (normalcdf(1.84,1E99)).
  2. (b) Two-sided. One tail is $P(Z\le -2.30)\approx 0.0107$; double it for both tails: p-value $\approx 0.0214$ (2*normalcdf(-1E99,-2.30)).
  3. Sense check. The farther $z$ is from 0 in the direction of $H_a$, the smaller the p-value. A two-sided test has twice the area of a one-sided test for the same $|z|$.

求 (a) $H_a: p>p_0$、$z=1.84$ 与 (b) $H_a: p\ne p_0$、$z=-2.30$ 时的 p 值。

  1. (a) 右尾。p 值 $=P(Z\ge 1.84)\approx 0.0329$（normalcdf(1.84,1E99)）。
  2. (b) 双侧。一侧为 $P(Z\le -2.30)\approx 0.0107$；两侧取两倍：p 值 $\approx 0.0214$（2*normalcdf(-1E99,-2.30)）。
  3. 合理性检查。$z$ 沿 $H_a$ 方向离 0 越远，p 值越小。对相同的 $|z|$，双侧检验的面积是单侧的两倍。
Worked Example: interpreting a p-value in context例题：结合情境解释 p 值

A pharmacy's old process had a 12% error rate. After retraining, a random sample of 400 prescriptions has 34 errors ($\hat{p}=0.085$). A test of $H_0: p=0.12$ against $H_a: p<0.12$ gives $z\approx -2.15$ and a p-value of about $0.016$. Interpret the p-value. (Illustrative data.)

  1. Direction of "extreme". $H_a$ is left-tailed, so results at least as extreme are sample proportions of $0.085$ or smaller.
  2. Interpretation. Assuming the true error rate is still $0.12$, there is about a $1.6\%$ chance of getting a sample proportion of $0.085$ or less in a random sample of 400 prescriptions.
  3. Meaning. That is rare, so the data provide evidence against $H_0$ (we will make the formal decision in topic 9.3).
  4. Not this. It is wrong to say "there is a $1.6\%$ chance the error rate is still $0.12$."

某药房旧流程的错误率为 12%。再培训后，随机抽取 400 张处方，有 34 张出错（$\hat{p}=0.085$）。检验 $H_0: p=0.12$ 对 $H_a: p<0.12$，得 $z\approx -2.15$，p 值约 $0.016$。解释该 p 值。（示意数据。）

Worked Example: one p-value, two alternatives例题：同一数据，两种备择假设

Historically 40% of adults in a panel say they stream video daily. In a random sample of 200 adults (under 10% of the panel), 94 say they stream daily, so $\hat{p}=0.47$. Find the p-value if the researcher expected an increase ($H_a: p>0.40$) and if she had no direction in mind ($H_a: p\ne 0.40$), then compare both at $\alpha=0.03$. (Illustrative data.)

  1. Statistic. $SE=\sqrt{0.40(0.60)/200}=\sqrt{0.0012}\approx 0.0346$, so $z=\dfrac{0.47-0.40}{0.0346}\approx 2.02$.
  2. One-sided. $p\text{-value}=P(Z>2.02)\approx 0.022$.
  3. Two-sided. Both tails count: $2(0.0217)\approx 0.043$.
  4. Compare with $\alpha=0.03$. $0.022\le 0.03$, so the one-sided test rejects $H_0$. $0.043>0.03$, so the two-sided test fails to reject.

  1. 统计量。$SE=\sqrt{0.40(0.60)/200}=\sqrt{0.0012}\approx 0.0346$，所以 $z=\dfrac{0.47-0.40}{0.0346}\approx 2.02$。
  2. 单侧。$p\text{-value}=P(Z>2.02)\approx 0.022$。
  3. 双侧。两侧尾部都要计入：$2(0.0217)\approx 0.043$。
  4. 与 $\alpha=0.03$ 比较。$0.022\le 0.03$，所以单侧检验拒绝 $H_0$。$0.043>0.03$，所以双侧检验不拒绝。

意义：两个 p 值相差一倍，所以 $H_a$ 的选择可能改变决定。因此方向必须在看数据之前由问题决定，研究者也不能在双侧检验不成功后改用单侧版本。

常见错误：把 p 值说成原假设为真的概率

误区：“p 值 $=0.016$，所以 $H_0$ 为真的可能性是 1.6%。”正确读法：若 $H_0$ 为真，这样极端的结果约有 1.6% 的时间会出现。另一个常见错误是解释 p 值时漏掉“假定 $H_0$”或尾部方向。

Topic 9.3主题 9.3

Concluding a Test for a Population Proportion得出总体比例检验的结论

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate the one-proportion $z$ test statistic using $p_0$ in the standard error.
  • Find the p-value for the correct tail(s) and compare it with $\alpha$.
  • State the decision (reject or fail to reject $H_0$) and a conclusion in context linked to the p-value.
  • Explain why we never accept $H_0$.
  • Carry out a complete four-step test and verify the answer with 1-PropZTest.
  • 计算单比例 $z$ 检验统计量，标准误中使用 $p_0$。
  • 求出对应尾部的 p 值并与 $\alpha$ 比较。
  • 写出决定（拒绝或不拒绝 $H_0$），以及与 p 值相联系的、结合情境的结论。
  • 解释为什么我们从不“接受” $H_0$。
  • 完成完整的四步检验，并用 1-PropZTest 核对答案。

The test statistic measures how many standard deviations the sample proportion is from the hypothesized value. For a one-proportion $z$-test (1-PropZTest):

检验统计量衡量样本比例离假设值有多少个标准差。对单比例 $z$ 检验（1-PropZTest）：

$$z=\dfrac{\hat{p}-p_0}{\sqrt{\dfrac{p_0(1-p_0)}{n}}}$$

Note that the denominator uses $p_0$, not $\hat{p}$, because the test assumes $H_0$ is true. Find the p-value in the direction of $H_a$, then decide: if the p-value is less than or equal to $\alpha$, reject $H_0$; if it is greater than $\alpha$, fail to reject $H_0$.

注意分母用 $p_0$ 而不是 $\hat{p}$，因为检验假定 $H_0$ 为真。沿 $H_a$ 的方向求 p 值，然后决定：若 p 值小于或等于 $\alpha$，拒绝 $H_0$；若 p 值大于 $\alpha$，则不拒绝 $H_0$。

Write the conclusion in context and tie it to the p-value. If you reject: "Because the p-value of ... is less than $\alpha=0.05$, we reject $H_0$. There is convincing evidence that the true proportion of ... is greater than (less than, different from) $p_0$." If you fail to reject: "Because the p-value of ... is greater than $\alpha=0.05$, we fail to reject $H_0$. There is not convincing evidence that ...". We never accept $H_0$: failing to reject says only that the data do not rule out $p_0$, which is not the same as showing it is true. A large p-value does not prove $H_0$.

结论要结合情境并与 p 值挂钩。若拒绝：“因为 p 值 ... 小于 $\alpha=0.05$，我们拒绝 $H_0$。有令人信服的证据表明……的真实比例大于（小于、不同于）$p_0$。”若不拒绝：“因为 p 值 ... 大于 $\alpha=0.05$，我们不拒绝 $H_0$。没有令人信服的证据表明……”。我们从不接受 $H_0$：不拒绝只是说数据没有排除 $p_0$，并不等于证明它为真。较大的 p 值并不证明 $H_0$。

A hospital's historical 30-day readmission rate is $18\%$. After a new discharge program, a random sample of 350 patients (a small fraction of the hospital's patients) has 49 readmitted. A test can reach the wrong conclusion in two ways. A Type I error is rejecting $H_0$ when $H_0$ is actually true (a false alarm). A Type II error is failing to reject $H_0$ when $H_0$ is actually false (a missed detection). Describe each in context, naming the parameter and the claim.

检验可能以两种方式得出错误结论。第一类错误是在 $H_0$ 实际为真时拒绝了 $H_0$（虚报）。第二类错误是在 $H_0$ 实际为假时没有拒绝 $H_0$（漏报）。要结合情境描述，点明参数和说法。

The probability of a Type I error equals the significance level $\alpha$: if $H_0$ is true and $\alpha=0.05$, the test falsely rejects about 5% of the time. The probability of a Type II error is written $\beta$. The power of a test is the probability that it correctly rejects a false $H_0$; power $=1-\beta$, and it depends on which alternative value of $p$ is true.

第一类错误的概率等于显著性水平 $\alpha$：若 $H_0$ 为真且 $\alpha=0.05$，检验约有 5% 的时间会错误地拒绝。第二类错误的概率记作 $\beta$。检验的功效是它正确拒绝一个为假的 $H_0$ 的概率；功效 $=1-\beta$，且取决于真实的 $p$ 取哪个备择值。

Power increases when (1) the sample size $n$ is larger, (2) the significance level $\alpha$ is larger (but then Type I errors become more likely), (3) the true value of $p$ is farther from $p_0$ (a larger effect size), and (4) the variability is smaller. Lowering the chance of one error by changing $\alpha$ raises the chance of the other; a larger sample can lower both error probabilities at once.

下列情形功效会增大：(1) 样本容量 $n$ 更大；(2) 显著性水平 $\alpha$ 更大（但第一类错误更易发生）；(3) $p$ 的真实值离 $p_0$ 更远（效应大小更大）；(4) 变异更小。通过改变 $\alpha$ 降低一类错误的可能性会提高另一类错误的可能性；增大样本则可以同时降低两类错误的概率。

Topic 9.5主题 9.5

Setting Up a Test for a Population Mean建立总体均值的检验

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define the parameter $\mu$ in words with its population and units.
  • Write a null hypothesis $H_0:\mu=\mu_0$ and the correct alternative hypothesis in symbols and in words.
  • Choose a one-sided or two-sided alternative from the wording of the question before seeing the data.
  • State the conditions for a one-sample $t$ test and what each one protects.
  • 结合总体与单位用文字定义参数 $\mu$。
  • 用符号与文字写出零假设 $H_0:\mu=\mu_0$ 与正确的备择假设。
  • 在看数据之前，根据题意选择单侧或双侧备择假设。
  • 陈述单样本 $t$ 检验的条件，以及每个条件保护什么。

A significance test asks whether the sample provides convincing evidence against a specific claim about a parameter. The claim is the null hypothesis $H_0$; the suspicion that something differs is the alternative hypothesis $H_a$. For a population mean, the null hypothesis always states that $\mu$ equals one hypothesized value $\mu_0$:

显著性检验询问样本是否提供了令人信服的证据来反对某个关于参数的具体说法。该说法是零假设（null hypothesis）$H_0$；怀疑有差异的想法是备择假设（alternative hypothesis）$H_a$。对总体均值，零假设总是说 $\mu$ 等于某个假设值 $\mu_0$（hypothesized value）：

Hypotheses for one mean

$H_0:\mu=\mu_0$ against one of $H_a:\mu<\mu_0$, $H_a:\mu>\mu_0$, or $H_a:\mu\ne\mu_0$. Define $\mu$ as the population mean of a named variable for a named population, with units. Hypotheses are about parameters ($\mu$), never about statistics ($\bar{x}$).

单个均值的假设

$H_0:\mu=\mu_0$，对立面为 $H_a:\mu<\mu_0$、$H_a:\mu>\mu_0$ 或 $H_a:\mu\ne\mu_0$ 之一。要把 $\mu$ 定义为某指定总体中某指定变量的总体均值（population mean），并带上单位。假设涉及参数（$\mu$），绝不涉及统计量（$\bar{x}$）。

Choose the form of $H_a$ from the research question, before looking at the data. Words such as "less than", "fewer" or "lower" give $H_a:\mu<\mu_0$; "greater than", "exceeds" or "more" give $H_a:\mu>\mu_0$; "differs from", "is different" or "has changed" give $H_a:\mu\ne\mu_0$. Choosing the direction after seeing which way $\bar{x}$ fell is not allowed because it halves the p-value dishonestly.

根据研究问题，在看数据之前确定 $H_a$ 的形式。“少于”“更少”“更低”等词对应 $H_a:\mu<\mu_0$；“大于”“超过”“更多”对应 $H_a:\mu>\mu_0$；“不同于”“有差异”“发生变化”对应 $H_a:\mu\ne\mu_0$。看到 $\bar{x}$ 的方向后再选方向是不允许的，因为这样会不诚实地把 p 值减半。

The conditions for the one-sample $t$ test are the same as for the interval, and you check them the same way. Random: a random sample or randomized experiment, so the sample represents the population. 10% condition: $n<10\%$ of the population, so observations are nearly independent. Normal or large sample: population approximately normal, or $n\ge 30$, or a sample graph without strong skew and outliers.

单样本 $t$ 检验的条件与区间的相同，检验方法也相同。随机：随机样本或随机化实验，使样本代表总体。10% 条件：$n<10\%$（占总体的比例），使观测近似独立。正态或大样本：总体近似正态，或 $n\ge 30$，或样本图形没有明显偏斜和离群点。

Worked Example 7.4a: a two-sided test about a labeled weight例题 7.4a：关于标称重量的双侧检验

The rice plant wants to know whether the mean bag weight differs from the labeled 500 g.

  1. Parameter: $\mu$ = the true mean weight (g) of all bags of rice produced at the plant that day (the population mean).
  2. Null hypothesis: $H_0:\mu=500$ g, so $\mu_0=500$.
  3. Alternative hypothesis: "differs from" is two-sided, $H_a:\mu\ne 500$ g.

大米厂想知道平均每袋重量是否不同于标称的 500 克。

  1. 参数：$\mu$ = 该厂当天生产的全部米袋的真实平均重量（克）（总体均值）。
  2. 零假设：$H_0:\mu=500$ 克，所以 $\mu_0=500$。
  3. 备择假设：“不同于”是双侧的，$H_a:\mu\ne 500$ 克。
Worked Example 7.4b: a one-sided test about battery life例题 7.4b：关于电池寿命的单侧检验

A consumer group suspects that batteries from a shipment last fewer than the claimed 10 hours.

  1. Parameter: $\mu$ = the true mean life (hours) of all batteries in the shipment.
  2. Hypotheses: $H_0:\mu=10$ hours; $H_a:\mu<10$ hours (the suspicion is "fewer").
  3. In words: the null says the mean life is exactly the claimed 10 hours; the alternative says it is less than 10 hours.

消费者组织怀疑一批电池的寿命少于所声称的 10 小时。

  1. 参数：$\mu$ = 这批货中全部电池的真实平均寿命（小时）。
  2. 假设：$H_0:\mu=10$ 小时；$H_a:\mu<10$ 小时（怀疑的是“更少”）。
  3. 用文字表述：零假设说平均寿命恰为所声称的 10 小时；备择假设说平均寿命少于 10 小时。
Worked Example 7.4c: correcting hypotheses that are written wrong例题 7.4c：改正写错的假设

A student writes "$H_0:\bar{x}=45$ and $H_a:\bar{x}\ge 45$" for a teacher's claim that the mean homework time is 45 minutes, which students think is too low.

老师声称平均作业时间是 45 分钟，学生认为偏低。一名学生写成“$H_0:\bar{x}=45$，$H_a:\bar{x}\ge 45$”。

Checking the Normal condition with a small sample小样本时检查正态条件

The $t$ procedures need the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}$ to be approximately normal. With $n\ge 30$ the central limit theorem usually supplies this, even for a skewed population. With a smaller sample the population itself must be approximately normal, and all you can do is look at the sample. Make a dotplot, histogram or boxplot of the data. For a very small sample (roughly $n<15$) the graph should be close to symmetric with no outliers; for a moderate sample a mild skew is tolerable, but strong skew or an outlier is not.

A graph cannot prove that a population is normal; it can only reveal a reason to doubt it. So report what you see: "The dotplot of the 12 readings is roughly symmetric with no outliers, so the Normal condition is reasonable." If a small sample shows strong skew or an outlier, say that a $t$ test is not appropriate for these data instead of carrying on.

$t$ 方法要求 $\bar{x}$ 的抽样分布近似正态。当 $n\ge 30$ 时，中心极限定理通常能保证这一点，即使总体偏斜。样本较小时，总体本身必须近似正态，而你能做的只是观察样本。画出数据的点图、直方图或箱线图。对于很小的样本（大致 $n<15$），图形应接近对称且没有离群值；对于中等样本，轻度偏斜可以接受，但强烈偏斜或存在离群值则不可以。

图形不能证明总体是正态的，只能揭示怀疑的理由。所以要写出你看到的情况：“12 个读数的点图大致对称且没有离群值，所以正态条件是合理的。”如果小样本显示强烈偏斜或有离群值，应说明这些数据不适合用 $t$ 检验，而不是继续计算。

Exam tip考试提示

Always begin with a sentence defining $\mu$: "Let $\mu$ = the true mean ___ (units) of all ___." A missing or vague definition is an incomplete answer even when the symbols are right. The null hypothesis contains only an equals sign, and the alternative must match the direction in the question.

总是先用一句话定义 $\mu$：“设 $\mu$ = 全体 ___ 的真实平均 ___（单位）。”即使符号正确，缺少或含糊的定义也是不完整的作答。零假设只含等号，备择假设必须与题目中的方向一致。

Common Mistake: sample results in the hypotheses and a late choice of direction
  • Using $\bar{x}$ or a sample result in $H_0$ or $H_a$.
  • Writing $H_0:\mu\ge\mu_0$ or an alternative with $\ge$ or $\le$.
  • Deciding between one-sided and two-sided after seeing the data.
常见错误：把样本结果写进假设与事后选择方向
  • 在 $H_0$ 或 $H_a$ 中使用 $\bar{x}$ 或样本结果。

  • 看过数据后才决定单侧还是双侧。

One-sample t test

$$t=\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu_0}{s/\sqrt{n}},\qquad df=n-1$$ The p-value is the probability, computed assuming $H_0$ is true, of getting a sample mean at least as far from $\mu_0$ as the observed one, in the direction(s) of $H_a$. 单样本 t 检验

Carry out a test in four steps. State the hypotheses with $\mu$ defined and a significance level $\alpha$ (use $0.05$ if none is given). Plan by naming the procedure (one-sample $t$ test for $\mu$) and checking the conditions. Do the mathematics: $\bar{x}$, $s$, $n$, $t$, $df$ and the p-value. Conclude by comparing the p-value with $\alpha$ and answering in context.

Interpreting the p-value and writing the conclusion解释 p 值并写出结论

If the p-value is less than or equal to $\alpha$, reject $H_0$: the data provide convincing evidence for $H_a$. If the p-value is greater than $\alpha$, fail to reject $H_0$: the data do not provide convincing evidence for $H_a$. Failing to reject is never the same as accepting $H_0$ or proving it true; it only says the sample was not extreme enough. A p-value is not the probability that $H_0$ is true; it is the probability of data this extreme if $H_0$ were true.

若 p 值小于或等于 $\alpha$，则拒绝 $H_0$：数据提供了支持 $H_a$ 的令人信服的证据（convincing evidence）。若 p 值大于 $\alpha$，则不拒绝（fail to reject）$H_0$：数据没有提供支持 $H_a$ 的令人信服的证据。不拒绝绝不等于接受或证明 $H_0$ 为真，它只说明样本不够极端。p 值不是 $H_0$ 为真的概率，而是假如 $H_0$ 为真时出现如此极端数据的概率。

Worked Example 7.5a: a left-tailed t test for battery life例题 7.5a：电池寿命的左侧 t 检验

Use the battery sample ($n=30$, $\bar{x}=9.42$ h, $s=1.1$ h) to test whether the true mean life is less than the claimed 10 hours at $\alpha=0.05$.

  1. State: $\mu$ = true mean life (hours) of all batteries in the shipment. $H_0:\mu=10$; $H_a:\mu<10$; $\alpha=0.05$.
  2. Plan: one-sample $t$ test for $\mu$. Random sample (given); 30 is under 10% of a large shipment; $n\ge 30$.
  3. Do: $t=\dfrac{9.42-10}{1.1/\sqrt{30}}=\dfrac{-0.58}{0.2008}=-2.89$, $df=29$. p-value $=$ tcdf(-1E99,-2.89,29) $\approx 0.0036$. (T-Test: $\mu_0=10$, $\bar{x}=9.42$, Sx$=1.1$, $n=30$, $\mu<\mu_0$.)
  4. Conclude: the p-value 0.0036 is less than $0.05$, so reject $H_0$. There is convincing evidence that the true mean life of batteries in this shipment is less than 10 hours.

用电池样本（$n=30$，$\bar{x}=9.42$ 小时，$s=1.1$ 小时）在 $\alpha=0.05$ 下检验真实平均寿命是否少于所声称的 10 小时。

Worked Example 7.5c: the test agrees with the interval例题 7.5c：检验与区间一致

Test the label claim $H_0:\mu=500$ g against $H_a:\mu\ne 500$ g with the rice sample ($n=25$, $\bar{x}=498.4$, $s=6.5$), $\alpha=0.05$.

  1. Statistic: $t=\dfrac{498.4-500}{6.5/\sqrt{25}}=\dfrac{-1.6}{1.3}=-1.23$, $df=24$.
  2. p-value: $2\cdot$tcdf(-1E99,-1.23,24) $\approx 0.230$.
  3. Conclude: 0.230 is greater than $0.05$, so fail to reject $H_0$. There is not convincing evidence that the mean weight of bags produced that day differs from 500 g.
  4. Check with an interval: the 95% confidence interval $498.4\pm 2.064(1.3)=(495.72,\,501.08)$ contains 500, which is the same decision as a two-sided 0.05 test.

Reading calculator or computer output for a $t$ test读懂 $t$ 检验的计算器或计算机输出

A lab says its sensor reads $50$ units on a calibration standard; a technician suspects it reads low. Twenty random readings (from an approximately normal distribution of readings) give $\bar{x}=48.2$ and $s=3.77$. The output of T-Test or statistics software lists: $\mu<50$, $t=-2.14$, $p=0.023$, $\bar{x}=48.2$, $s_x=3.77$, $n=20$.

  1. Check the first line: $\mu<50$ must match the alternative in the question.
  2. Interpret $t$: the sample mean is about $2.14$ standard errors below $50$. You can confirm it: $\dfrac{48.2-50}{3.77/\sqrt{20}}\approx -2.14$, and $df=n-1=19$.
  3. Use the p-value as printed: $0.023$ is already one-sided because the alternative is. A calculator's two-sided output has the doubling built in, so never double it again.
  4. Conclude: since $0.023\le 0.05$, reject $H_0$; the data provide convincing evidence that the true mean reading of this sensor is less than $50$ units.

Output gives numbers, not a decision. You still write the hypotheses, check the conditions and state the conclusion in context.

  1. 先看第一行：$\mu<50$ 必须与题目中的备择假设一致。

(a)$p$ = 该校全体学生中现在购买午餐的真实比例。$H_0: p=0.55$，$H_a: p>0.55$。
(b)单比例 $z$ 检验。随机样本；$240\le 0.10(3000)=300$；$np_0=240(0.55)=132\ge 10$，$n(1-p_0)=108\ge 10$。$\hat{p}=150/240=0.625$；$z=\dfrac{0.625-0.55}{\sqrt{0.55(0.45)/240}}=\dfrac{0.075}{0.0321}\approx 2.34$（由 $2.3355$ 取整）；p 值 $\approx 0.0098$，由未取整的 $z$ 求得。
(c)因为 p 值约 $0.010$ 小于 $0.05$，我们拒绝 $H_0$。有令人信服的证据表明，菜单调整后该校购买午餐的学生真实比例大于 $0.55$。
(d)条件同上，$n\hat{p}=150$，$n(1-\hat{p})=90$。$0.625\pm1.960\sqrt{0.625(0.375)/240}=0.625\pm0.0612$，即 $(0.564,\,0.686)$。我们有 95% 的把握认为，从 $0.564$ 到 $0.686$ 的区间覆盖了现在购买午餐的学生真实比例。它额外给出了增幅大小的范围；区间内每个值都高于 $0.55$。
(e)在购买午餐比例实际上仍是 $0.55$ 时，断定它已上升到 $0.55$ 以上（例如样本偶然偏高）。

8. 双侧单样本 $t$ 检验得 $t=-2.10$，$df=24$。p 值最接近

B。单侧约 0.023；双侧备择假设需加倍，得 0.046。

9. For $H_0: p=0.40$ with $n=200$ and $\hat{p}=0.47$, the standard error used in the test statistic is closest to

10. A two-sided $z$ test gives $z=1.80$. The p-value is closest to

D. The upper tail is $P(Z>1.80)\approx 0.036$; a two-sided alternative doubles it to $0.072$. A is only one tail, B is the area below $z=1.80$, and C is the area between $-1.80$ and $1.80$.

10. 双侧 $z$ 检验得 $z=1.80$。p 值最接近

D。上尾为 $P(Z>1.80)\approx 0.036$；双侧备择假设需加倍，得 $0.072$。A 只有一侧，B 是 $z=1.80$ 左侧的面积，C 是 $-1.80$ 到 $1.80$ 之间的面积。

11. A test of $H_0: p=0.30$ against $H_a: p\ne 0.30$ gives a p-value of $0.12$ with $\alpha=0.05$. Which conclusion is best?

B. Since $0.12>0.05$ we fail to reject $H_0$, which means the evidence is not convincing, not that $H_0$ is proven (A). The p-value is computed assuming $H_0$ is true, so it is not the probability that $H_0$ is true (C). D would need a p-value of at most $0.05$.

11. 检验 $H_0: p=0.30$ 对 $H_a: p\ne 0.30$，得 p 值 $0.12$，$\alpha=0.05$。哪个结论最好？

B。因为 $0.12>0.05$，不拒绝 $H_0$，意思是证据不够有说服力，而不是 $H_0$ 得到证明（A）。p 值是在 $H_0$ 为真的假设下算出的，所以不是 $H_0$ 为真的概率（C）。D 需要 p 值不超过 $0.05$。

12. A $95\%$ $t$ interval for $\mu$ is $(24.1,\,27.3)$. For $H_0:\mu=28$ against $H_a:\mu\ne 28$ at $\alpha=0.05$, the matching one-sample $t$ test

A. A two-sided $t$ test at level $\alpha$ rejects $H_0:\mu=\mu_0$ exactly when $\mu_0$ is outside the $100(1-\alpha)\%$ interval. Being close does not matter, and the interval already summarizes the data needed.

12. $\mu$ 的 $95\%$ $t$ 区间为 $(24.1,\,27.3)$。对 $H_0:\mu=28$ 对 $H_a:\mu\ne 28$，在 $\alpha=0.05$ 下，对应的单样本 $t$ 检验

A。显著性水平为 $\alpha$ 的双侧 $t$ 检验，当且仅当 $\mu_0$ 落在 $100(1-\alpha)\%$ 区间之外时拒绝 $H_0:\mu=\mu_0$。接近区间并无影响，区间已经概括了所需的数据。
