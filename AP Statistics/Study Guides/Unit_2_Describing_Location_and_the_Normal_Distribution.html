Topic 2.1主题 2.1

The Normal Distribution and the Empirical Rule正态分布与经验法则

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe a normal distribution $N(\mu,\sigma)$ and use the 68-95-99.7 rule.
  • Standardize with z-scores and find areas with normalcdf, and values with invNorm.
  • Answer percentile and cutoff questions about a normal model, with a labeled sketch.
  • 描述正态分布 $N(\mu,\sigma)$ 并运用 68-95-99.7 法则。
  • 用 z 分数标准化，用 normalcdf 求面积，用 invNorm 求数值。
  • 回答关于正态模型的百分位和分界值问题，并附带标注的草图。
What you must know

A density curve is a curve that is on or above the horizontal axis with total area 1; the area under it between two values is the proportion of data in that interval. The normal distribution $N(\mu,\sigma)$ is symmetric and bell-shaped, centered at $\mu$, with inflection points at $\mu\pm\sigma$. The empirical (68-95-99.7) rule says that approximately 68%, 95% and 99.7% of the values lie within 1, 2 and 3 standard deviations of the mean.

$$z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\qquad x=\mu+z\sigma$$

The standardized value $z$ has the standard normal distribution $N(0,1)$. The model is an approximation: a real data set is only "approximately normal".

必备知识

密度曲线是位于横轴上方（或与横轴相接）、总面积为 1 的曲线；它在两个数值之间的面积就是该区间内数据所占的比例。正态分布 $N(\mu,\sigma)$ 对称、呈钟形，中心为 $\mu$，拐点在 $\mu\pm\sigma$ 处。经验法则（68-95-99.7）指出：约 68%、95%、99.7% 的数值落在与均值相差 1、2、3 个标准差的范围内。

$$z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\qquad x=\mu+z\sigma$$

标准化后的 $z$ 服从标准正态分布 $N(0,1)$。该模型只是近似：真实数据只能说「近似正态」。

Worked Example 8.1: Empirical rule例题 8.1：经验法则

Battery lifetimes are approximately normal with mean 50 hours and standard deviation 6 hours. Use the empirical rule to find (a) the interval holding the middle 95% and (b) the proportion lasting more than 62 hours.

  1. (a) $\mu\pm2\sigma=50\pm12$, so about 95% of batteries last between 38 and 62 hours.
  2. (b) $62=\mu+2\sigma$. The remaining $100\%-95\%=5\%$ is split equally in the two tails, so about $2.5\%$ last more than 62 hours (exact area 0.0228).
  3. (c) A lifetime of 44 hours has $z=(44-50)/6=-1$; the proportion between 44 and 56 hours is about 68%, so about $16\%$ last more than 56 hours.
  4. Statement in context: "About 2.5% of these batteries last longer than 62 hours."

电池寿命近似服从均值 50 小时、标准差 6 小时的正态分布。用经验法则求 (a) 包含中间 95% 的区间，(b) 寿命超过 62 小时的比例。

  1. (a) $\mu\pm2\sigma=50\pm12$，所以约 95% 的电池寿命在 38 到 62 小时之间。
  2. (b) $62=\mu+2\sigma$。其余的 $100\%-95\%=5\%$ 平分在两侧尾部，所以约 $2.5\%$ 的电池寿命超过 62 小时（精确面积 0.0228）。
  3. (c) 寿命 44 小时的 $z=(44-50)/6=-1$；44 到 56 小时之间的比例约为 68%，所以约 $16\%$ 的电池寿命超过 56 小时。
  4. 结合情境表述：「约 2.5% 的这类电池寿命超过 62 小时。」
Worked Example 8.2: Area with normalcdf例题 8.2：用 normalcdf 求面积

Scores on a placement test are approximately normal with $\mu=72$ and $\sigma=9$. (a) What proportion score above 85? (b) What proportion score between 60 and 80?

  1. (a) $z=(85-72)/9=1.44$. Calculator: normalcdf(85,1E99,72,9) $=0.0743$. About 7.4% of test takers score above 85.
  2. (b) $z_{60}=(60-72)/9=-1.33$ and $z_{80}=(80-72)/9=0.89$. normalcdf(60,80,72,9) $=0.7218$. About 72.2% score between 60 and 80.
  3. Always sketch and shade the normal curve first (Figure 9), label the mean 72 and the cutoff 85, and write the probability statement $P(X>85)$ before using the calculator.

某分班测试成绩近似服从 $\mu=72$、$\sigma=9$ 的正态分布。(a) 得分超过 85 的比例是多少？(b) 得分在 60 到 80 之间的比例是多少？

  1. (a) $z=(85-72)/9=1.44$。计算器：normalcdf(85,1E99,72,9) $=0.0743$。约 7.4% 的考生得分超过 85。
  2. (b) $z_{60}=(60-72)/9=-1.33$，$z_{80}=(80-72)/9=0.89$。normalcdf(60,80,72,9) $=0.7218$。约 72.2% 的考生得分在 60 到 80 之间。
  3. 务必先画正态曲线并涂阴影（图 9），标出均值 72 和分界值 85，并先写出概率式 $P(X>85)$，再使用计算器。
Worked Example 8.3: Cutoff with invNorm例题 8.3：用 invNorm 求分界值

Using the same $N(72,9)$ model, what score is needed to be in the top 10%?

  1. The top 10% means 90% of scores lie below the cutoff, so the left-tail area is 0.90.
  2. Calculator: invNorm(0.90,72,9) $=83.53$. By hand: $z=1.2816$, $x=72+1.2816(9)=83.53$.
  3. Conclusion: "A score of about 83.5 or higher places a test taker in the top 10%."
  4. Check: $z=(83.53-72)/9=1.28$ and $P(X>83.53)\approx0.10$.

仍用 $N(72,9)$ 模型，要进入前 10% 需要多少分？

  1. 前 10% 意味着有 90% 的成绩低于分界值，所以左尾面积为 0.90。
  2. 计算器：invNorm(0.90,72,9) $=83.53$。手算：$z=1.2816$，$x=72+1.2816(9)=83.53$。
  3. 结论：「得分约 83.5 或更高，就进入了前 10%。」
  4. 核对：$z=(83.53-72)/9=1.28$，$P(X>83.53)\approx0.10$。
Worked Example 8.4: Is a normal model reasonable?例题 8.4：正态模型是否合理？

Fifteen exam scores (62 to 97, $\bar{x}=80.6$, $s_x=9.78$) are shown in Topic 1.5. Check the empirical rule.

  1. Within 1 SD: $80.6\pm9.78$ is 70.8 to 90.4. Scores 71 through 88 are 10 of 15, or about 67%, close to 68%.
  2. Within 2 SD: $80.6\pm19.56$ is 61.0 to 100.2: all 15 scores, or 100%, above 95%, as expected for a small sample.
  3. The histogram is unimodal and roughly symmetric (2, 5, 5, 3), so a normal model is a reasonable approximation; with only 15 values the match cannot be exact.
  4. Do not call the data "normal"; write "approximately normal" and give the evidence.

主题 1.5 给出了 15 个考试成绩（62 至 97，$\bar{x}=80.6$，$s_x=9.78$）。检验经验法则。

  1. 1 个标准差内：$80.6\pm9.78$ 即 70.8 到 90.4。71 到 88 的成绩有 10 个，共 15 个，约占 67%，接近 68%。
  2. 2 个标准差内：$80.6\pm19.56$ 即 61.0 到 100.2：全部 15 个成绩，占 100%，高于 95%，对小样本是预期之内的。
  3. 直方图单峰、大致对称（2、5、5、3），所以正态模型是合理的近似；只有 15 个值，吻合不可能完全精确。
  4. 不要把数据称为「正态」；应写「近似正态」并给出依据。

What a z-score tells youz 分数告诉你什么

A z-score converts a value into a count of standard deviations from the mean, $z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$. Because the units cancel, a z-score has no units, which is why it can compare heart rates with test scores. The sign gives the direction ($z>0$ above the mean, $z<0$ below, $z=0$ exactly at the mean) and the size gives the distance. To interpret one, name the value, the direction, the number of standard deviations and the mean it is measured from: "A heart rate of 87 is 2.5 standard deviations above the mean heart rate of 72." Working backward uses $x=\mu+z\sigma$.

z 分数把一个数值换算成它与均值相差的标准差个数，$z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$。单位相互抵消，所以 z 分数没有单位，这就是它能把心率和考试成绩放在一起比较的原因。符号表示方向（$z>0$ 高于均值，$z<0$ 低于均值，$z=0$ 恰好在均值），大小表示距离。解释时要写出数值、方向、标准差个数以及所对照的均值：「心率 87 比平均心率 72 高 2.5 个标准差。」反过来求数值用 $x=\mu+z\sigma$。

In an approximately Normal distribution about 95% of values have $|z|<2$, so a value with $|z|>2$ is often called unusual. That label is a convention about rarity, not a verdict that the value is an error: a z-score of 2.5 is a perfectly valid observation that is seen about 1.2% of the time (both tails together). Be careful with skewed data, where large z-scores on the long-tail side are much more common than the Normal model predicts.

在近似正态的分布中，约 95% 的数值满足 $|z|<2$，所以 $|z|>2$ 的数值常被称为不寻常（unusual）。这个说法只是关于罕见程度的约定，并不意味着数据有误：z 分数为 2.5 是完全有效的观测值，大约 1.2% 的情形会出现（两侧尾部合计）。对偏斜数据要小心：长尾一侧出现大 z 分数的情形，比正态模型预测的要常见得多。

Worked Example 8.5: Interpreting and reversing a z-score例题 8.5：解释并反推 z 分数

Resting heart rates at one clinic have mean 72 beats per minute (bpm) and standard deviation 6 bpm. (a) Find and interpret the z-score of 87 bpm. (b) Find the heart rate with $z=-1.5$. (c) At a second clinic the mean is 68 bpm and the SD is 4 bpm. What heart rate at the second clinic is just as unusual as 87 bpm is at the first? Is 80 bpm at the second clinic more or less unusual than that?

  1. (a) $z=\dfrac{87-72}{6}=2.5$. A heart rate of 87 bpm is 2.5 standard deviations above the clinic mean of 72 bpm.
  2. (b) $x=\mu+z\sigma=72+(-1.5)(6)=63$ bpm, which is 9 bpm below the mean.
  3. (c) A matching position needs $z=2.5$ at the second clinic, so $x=68+(2.5)(4)=78$ bpm. For 80 bpm, $z=\dfrac{80-68}{4}=3$. Since $3>2.5$, a rate of 80 bpm is further from its own clinic mean in SD units, so it is more unusual than 87 bpm at the first clinic, even though 87 is the larger raw number and 80 is only 12 bpm above its mean while 87 is 15 above its own.
  4. Why this works: each z-score uses its own mean and SD, so it measures position within its own distribution, and only positions measured in SD units can be compared.

某诊所的静息心率均值为每分钟 72 次（bpm），标准差为 6 bpm。(a) 求 87 bpm 的 z 分数并解释。(b) 求 $z=-1.5$ 对应的心率。(c) 第二家诊所的均值为 68 bpm，标准差为 4 bpm。第二家诊所中，多少 bpm 的心率与第一家的 87 bpm 同样不寻常？第二家诊所的 80 bpm 比这个值更不寻常还是更不那么不寻常？

  1. (a) $z=\dfrac{87-72}{6}=2.5$。87 bpm 比该诊所的平均心率 72 bpm 高 2.5 个标准差。
  2. (b) $x=\mu+z\sigma=72+(-1.5)(6)=63$ bpm，比均值低 9 bpm。
  3. (c) 位置相当需要第二家诊所的 $z=2.5$，所以 $x=68+(2.5)(4)=78$ bpm。对 80 bpm，$z=\dfrac{80-68}{4}=3$。因为 $3>2.5$，以标准差为单位，80 bpm 离其所在诊所均值更远，所以它比第一家的 87 bpm 更不寻常；虽然 87 是更大的原始数值，但 80 只比均值高 12 bpm，而 87 比自己的均值高 15 bpm。
  4. 原理：每个 z 分数使用各自的均值和标准差，衡量的是在自己分布中的位置，只有以标准差为单位的位置才能相互比较。

Percentiles and cumulative relative frequency百分位数与累积相对频率

The percentile of a value is the percent of the observations that are at or below it (this guide counts "at or below"). A student at the 80th percentile did at least as well as 80% of the group; this says nothing about the percent of questions answered correctly. From raw data, the percentile of $x$ is $\dfrac{k}{n}\times100$, where $k$ is the number of values at or below $x$. From grouped data, build a cumulative relative frequency column: the running total of the relative frequencies, read at the upper end of each class. A percentile inside a class is found by linear interpolation, which assumes the values are spread evenly across that class, so it is an estimate. The median is the value at 50%, and the quartiles are at 25% and 75%.

一个数值的百分位数（percentile）是不高于它的观测值所占的百分比（本指南按「不高于」计）。处在第 80 百分位数的学生，成绩不低于全组 80% 的人；这并不表示他答对了 80% 的题目。由原始数据求百分位数：$x$ 的百分位数为 $\dfrac{k}{n}\times100$，其中 $k$ 是不高于 $x$ 的个数。分组数据则建立累积相对频率（cumulative relative frequency）一列：相对频率的逐项累加，读取位置在每一组的上端点。组内的百分位数用线性插值求得，它假设数值在该组内均匀分布，所以只是估计。中位数对应 50%，四分位数对应 25% 和 75%。

Worked Example 8.6: Percentiles from a cumulative relative frequency table例题 8.6：由累积相对频率表求百分位数

The minutes of homework done last night by 50 students are grouped as follows: 0 to under 20: 4 students; 20 to under 40: 11; 40 to under 60: 18; 60 to under 80: 12; 80 to under 100: 5. (a) Build the cumulative relative frequency column. (b) Estimate the percentile of 50 minutes. (c) Estimate the median. (d) What is the 90th percentile?

  1. (a) Cumulative counts are 4, 15, 33, 45, 50. Dividing by 50 gives cumulative relative frequencies 8%, 30%, 66%, 90%, 100% at the upper ends 20, 40, 60, 80, 100.
  2. (b) 50 lies halfway through the class 40 to under 60, which adds $\dfrac{50-40}{20}(36\%)=18\%$ to the 30% reached at 40. Estimated percentile: $30\%+18\%=48\%$, about the 48th percentile.
  3. (c) The median needs 50%, which is 20 percentage points past the 30% at 40, out of the 36% in the class: $40+\dfrac{20}{36}(20)\approx51.1$ minutes.
  4. (d) The cumulative relative frequency reaches exactly 90% at the end of the class ending at 80, so the 90th percentile is 80 minutes.
  5. Statement: "About half of the students did less than 51 minutes of homework, and a student who did 50 minutes is at about the 48th percentile."

50 名学生昨晚完成作业的分钟数分组如下：0 至不足 20：4 人；20 至不足 40：11 人；40 至不足 60：18 人；60 至不足 80：12 人；80 至不足 100：5 人。(a) 建立累积相对频率一列。(b) 估计 50 分钟的百分位数。(c) 估计中位数。(d) 第 90 百分位数是多少？

  1. (a) 累积频数为 4、15、33、45、50。除以 50 得累积相对频率 8%、30%、66%、90%、100%，对应上端点 20、40、60、80、100。
  2. (b) 50 位于 40 至不足 60 这一组的中间，在 40 处已达到的 30% 之上再加 $\dfrac{50-40}{20}(36\%)=18\%$。估计的百分位数：$30\%+18\%=48\%$，约为第 48 百分位数。
  3. (c) 中位数需要 50%，比 40 处的 30% 多 20 个百分点，而该组共占 36%：$40+\dfrac{20}{36}(20)\approx51.1$ 分钟。
  4. (d) 累积相对频率在上端点 80 处恰好达到 90%，所以第 90 百分位数是 80 分钟。
  5. 结论：「约有一半学生的作业时间少于 51 分钟；做了 50 分钟的学生约处在第 48 百分位数。」

Density curves, area, and the mean and median密度曲线、面积与均值、中位数

A density curve describes a whole distribution by the area under it, so areas are proportions. Because a single point has no width, the area at one value is 0: for a density curve $P(X=a)=0$ and $P(X<a)=P(X\le a)$. The median is the point with half the area on each side. The mean is the balance point of the curve. In a symmetric curve they coincide; in a right-skewed curve the long right tail pulls the mean above the median, and in a left-skewed curve it pulls the mean below. The simplest density curve is a uniform one on $[a,b]$, a rectangle of height $\dfrac{1}{b-a}$, where every area is a width times that height.

密度曲线用曲线下的面积描述整个分布，所以面积就是比例。单个点没有宽度，其面积为 0：对密度曲线有 $P(X=a)=0$，且 $P(X<a)=P(X\le a)$。中位数是两侧各占一半面积的点，均值是曲线的平衡点。对称曲线中二者重合；右偏曲线中，较长的右尾把均值拉到中位数之上；左偏曲线则把均值拉到中位数之下。最简单的密度曲线是 $[a,b]$ 上的均匀分布（uniform）：高为 $\dfrac{1}{b-a}$ 的矩形，任何面积都等于宽度乘以这个高。

Worked Example 8.7: Areas under a uniform density curve例题 8.7：均匀密度曲线下的面积

The wait for a shuttle bus is modelled by a uniform density curve on 0 to 12 minutes. (a) Verify that it is a density curve. (b) Find the proportion of waits between 3 and 9 minutes and the proportion over 10 minutes. (c) Find the median and the mean. (d) What is $P(X=6)$?

  1. (a) The height is $\dfrac{1}{12}$ everywhere on [0, 12], so the total area is $12\cdot\dfrac{1}{12}=1$, and the curve is never below the axis: it is a density curve.
  2. (b) Between 3 and 9: $(9-3)\cdot\dfrac{1}{12}=0.5$. Over 10: $(12-10)\cdot\dfrac{1}{12}\approx0.167$. About half of waits are between 3 and 9 minutes, and about 16.7% exceed 10 minutes.
  3. (c) The curve is symmetric about 6, which splits the area in halves, so the median and the mean both equal 6 minutes.
  4. (d) A single point has no width, so $P(X=6)=0$. This does not mean a wait of exactly 6 minutes is impossible, only that its probability is 0 in a continuous model, so $P(X<6)=P(X\le6)=0.5$.

公交接驳车的等待时间用 0 到 12 分钟上的均匀密度曲线建模。(a) 验证它是密度曲线。(b) 求等待时间在 3 到 9 分钟之间的比例，以及超过 10 分钟的比例。(c) 求中位数和均值。(d) $P(X=6)$ 是多少？

  1. (a) 在 [0, 12] 上高度处处为 $\dfrac{1}{12}$，总面积为 $12\cdot\dfrac{1}{12}=1$，且曲线从不低于横轴：它是密度曲线。
  2. (b) 3 到 9 之间：$(9-3)\cdot\dfrac{1}{12}=0.5$。超过 10：$(12-10)\cdot\dfrac{1}{12}\approx0.167$。约一半的等待时间在 3 到 9 分钟之间，约 16.7% 超过 10 分钟。
  3. (c) 曲线关于 6 对称，6 把面积平分，所以中位数和均值都等于 6 分钟。
  4. (d) 单个点没有宽度，所以 $P(X=6)=0$。这并不表示恰好等待 6 分钟不可能发生，只是在连续模型中其概率为 0，所以 $P(X<6)=P(X\le6)=0.5$。
Common Mistake: Reading a percentile as a percent score

A student reports, "I was at the 85th percentile, so I got 85% right." The percentile counts how many students scored at or below the score; it says nothing about the percent of questions correct. On an easy test the 85th percentile may be 97% correct, and on a hard test it may be 52%. Write "about 85% of the students scored at or below this score" and, if needed, quote the score separately.

常见错误：把百分位数当成百分制得分

Topic 2.2主题 2.2

The Normal Distribution, Revisited正态分布回顾

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Use the empirical rule (68-95-99.7) to describe how data from a Normal distribution are spread around the mean.
  • Calculate and interpret a z-score, and use it to compare values from different distributions.
  • Find a Normal probability with normalcdf, and a percentile or cutoff value with invNorm.
  • Sketch and shade the Normal curve for a probability question before using the calculator.
  • Write the conclusion of a Normal calculation in context, with units.
  • 用经验法则（68-95-99.7）描述正态分布数据围绕均值的分布。
  • 计算并解释 z 分数，用它比较来自不同分布的数值。
  • 用 normalcdf 求正态概率，用 invNorm 求百分位数或临界值。
  • 使用计算器前先画正态曲线并涂色。
  • 结合情境并带单位写出正态计算的结论。

A Normal distribution has a symmetric bell-shaped curve fixed by its mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$. The empirical rule (68-95-99.7) says that about 68% of values lie within 1 standard deviation of the mean, about 95% within 2, and about 99.7% within 3. The z-score standardizes a value: $z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$ counts how many standard deviations $x$ lies above ($z>0$) or below ($z<0$) the mean. Standardizing lets values on different scales be compared.

正态分布（Normal distribution）的曲线对称、呈钟形，由均值 $\mu$ 与标准差 $\sigma$ 决定。经验法则（empirical rule，68-95-99.7）指出：约 68% 的值落在均值 1 个标准差之内，约 95% 在 2 个之内，约 99.7% 在 3 个之内。z 分数（z-score）将数值标准化：$z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$ 表示 $x$ 高于（$z>0$）或低于（$z<0$）均值多少个标准差。标准化使不同尺度的数值可以相互比较。

On the TI-84 (2nd VARS, DISTR): normalcdf(lower, upper, mean, SD) returns the area between two values; for a one-sided area use -1E99 or 1E99 as the open end. invNorm(area, mean, SD) returns the value with that area to its left. Always sketch the curve, label the boundary on the axis, and shade the region before touching the calculator; the picture tells you which direction the area is.

TI-84（2nd VARS，DISTR）：normalcdf(lower, upper, mean, SD) 返回两个值之间的面积；单侧面积则用 -1E99 或 1E99 作为开放端。invNorm(area, mean, SD) 返回左侧面积为给定值的那个数值。使用计算器之前，务必先画图、在轴上标出边界并涂色；图会告诉你面积在哪一侧。

Standardize first, then adjust: a right tail is $P(Z>z)=1-P(Z<z)$; an interval is the difference $P(Z<b)-P(Z<a)$; and by symmetry about 0, $P(Z<-a)=P(Z>a)=1-P(Z<a)$. A few values worth recognizing: $P(Z<0.50)=0.6915$, $P(Z<1.50)=0.9332$, $P(Z<1.96)=0.9750$, $P(Z<2.00)=0.9772$. Worked Example 5.6: Using the table and symmetry例题 5.6：使用查表与对称性

Times to finish an online task are approximately Normal with mean 250 seconds and SD 20 seconds. Using Table A, find (a) $P(X>285)$, (b) $P(230<X<275)$ and (c) $P(X<215)$.

  1. (a) $z=\dfrac{285-250}{20}=1.75$. The table gives $P(Z<1.75)=0.9599$, so $P(X>285)=1-0.9599=0.0401$. About 4.0% of times exceed 285 seconds.
  2. (b) $z$ runs from $\dfrac{230-250}{20}=-1.00$ to $\dfrac{275-250}{20}=1.25$. $P(Z<1.25)=0.8944$ and $P(Z<-1.00)=0.1587$, so the area is $0.8944-0.1587=0.7357$. About 73.6% of times are between 230 and 275 seconds.
  3. (c) $z=\dfrac{215-250}{20}=-1.75$. By symmetry $P(Z<-1.75)=P(Z>1.75)=0.0401$, the same as part (a), because 215 and 285 are the same distance from 250.
  4. Why this works: every Normal question reduces to areas under one standard curve once the boundary is expressed in SD units, and the table stores those areas; symmetry halves the table you need.

  2. (b) $z$ 从 $\dfrac{230-250}{20}=-1.00$ 到 $\dfrac{275-250}{20}=1.25$。$P(Z<1.25)=0.8944$，$P(Z<-1.00)=0.1587$，所以面积为 $0.8944-0.1587=0.7357$。约 73.6% 的用时在 230 到 275 秒之间。
  3. (c) $z=\dfrac{215-250}{20}=-1.75$。由对称性 $P(Z<-1.75)=P(Z>1.75)=0.0401$，与 (a) 相同，因为 215 和 285 与 250 的距离相等。
  4. 原理：边界一旦换成以标准差为单位，每个正态问题都化为同一条标准曲线下的面积，表中存的正是这些面积；对称性让需要查的表减少一半。

Choosing the procedure: empirical rule, z-score or calculator选择方法：经验法则、z 分数还是计算器

Match the question to the tool. A boundary that is a whole number of standard deviations from the mean and a request for an approximate percent: the empirical rule. "How far from typical?" or a comparison across scales: a z-score. A proportion for any other boundary: normalcdf (or the table). A value that cuts off a given proportion: invNorm. A middle $C\%$ interval: put $\dfrac{100-C}{2}\%$ in each tail, so the cutoffs are invNorm of $\dfrac{1-C/100}{2}$ and of $1-\dfrac{1-C/100}{2}$. For middle 90% that is $z=\pm1.645$, for middle 95% it is $z=\pm1.960$ (the empirical rule's 2 is a rounded 1.96).

让题目与工具匹配。边界与均值相差整数个标准差，且只要近似百分比：用经验法则。「离典型值有多远」或跨尺度比较：用 z 分数。其他边界的比例：用 normalcdf（或查表）。切出给定比例的数值：用 invNorm。中间 $C\%$ 的区间：每侧尾部占 $\dfrac{100-C}{2}\%$，所以分界值是 $\dfrac{1-C/100}{2}$ 与 $1-\dfrac{1-C/100}{2}$ 的 invNorm。中间 90% 对应 $z=\pm1.645$，中间 95% 对应 $z=\pm1.960$（经验法则中的 2 是 1.96 取整的结果）。

Assessing normality: shape, percents and the Normal probability plot评估正态性：形状、百分比与正态概率图

No real data set is exactly Normal, so the question is whether a Normal model is a reasonable approximation. Use three checks together. Shape: a histogram or dotplot that is unimodal, roughly symmetric and without extreme outliers. Percents: the shares of values within 1, 2 and 3 SDs of the mean should be close to 68%, 95% and 99.7%; for a Normal distribution the quartiles also sit about $0.6745$ SDs from the mean, so $IQR\approx1.349\,s$. Normal probability plot: plot each ordered value against the z-score that a Normal distribution would give it; points that lie close to a straight line support a Normal model, while systematic curvature signals skewness and a point far from the line signals an outlier. Write the conclusion as "approximately Normal" with the evidence, never "the data are Normal".

实际数据都不是严格正态的，所以问题是正态模型是否是合理的近似。同时使用三种检验。形状：直方图或点图单峰、大致对称、没有极端离群值。百分比：落在均值 1、2、3 个标准差内的比例应接近 68%、95%、99.7%；对正态分布，四分位数大约距均值 $0.6745$ 个标准差，所以 $IQR\approx1.349\,s$。正态概率图（Normal probability plot）：把每个有序数值与正态分布应给它的 z 分数作图；点大致落在一条直线上支持正态模型，系统性的弯曲说明偏斜，远离直线的点说明有离群值。结论要写成「近似正态」并给出依据，不能写「数据是正态的」。

Worked Example 5.8: Weighing the evidence for a Normal model例题 5.8：权衡正态模型的证据

A sample of 60 study times has mean 48 min, SD 22 min, median 41, $Q_1=30$, $Q_3=60$, minimum 12 and maximum 130. A second sample of 80 times has 54 values within 1 SD of its mean, 76 within 2 SDs and all 80 within 3 SDs. Is a Normal model reasonable for each?

  1. First sample. $IQR=60-30=30$, close to $1.349(22)\approx29.7$, which alone looks Normal. But the maximum is $\dfrac{130-48}{22}\approx3.73$ SDs above the mean while the minimum is only $\dfrac{12-48}{22}\approx-1.64$ SDs below, so the data stretch much further to the right.
  2. The upper fence is $Q_3+1.5(IQR)=60+45=105$, and $130>105$ is an outlier; it is also more than 2 SDs above the mean ($48+2(22)=92$). The mean exceeds the median (48 versus 41), consistent with a right tail. A histogram should confirm, but the evidence points to right skew, so a Normal model is not reasonable as it stands.
  3. Second sample. $54/80=67.5\%$ within 1 SD, $76/80=95\%$ within 2 SDs and $80/80=100\%$ within 3 SDs, all close to 68%, 95% and 99.7%. With a roughly symmetric histogram, a Normal model is reasonable.
  4. Conclusion: "The study times in the first sample are not approximately Normal because of right skew and an outlier at 130; the second sample is approximately Normal because its percents within 1, 2 and 3 SDs match the empirical rule."

60 个学习时间的样本，均值 48 分钟，标准差 22 分钟，中位数 41，$Q_1=30$，$Q_3=60$，最小值 12，最大值 130。第二个样本含 80 个时间，其中 54 个在均值 1 个标准差内，76 个在 2 个标准差内，全部 80 个在 3 个标准差内。对每个样本，正态模型合理吗？

  1. 第一个样本。$IQR=60-30=30$，接近 $1.349(22)\approx29.7$，仅看这一点像正态。但最大值比均值高 $\dfrac{130-48}{22}\approx3.73$ 个标准差，而最小值只比均值低 $\dfrac{12-48}{22}\approx-1.64$ 个标准差，所以数据向右延伸得远得多。
  2. 上栅栏为 $Q_3+1.5(IQR)=60+45=105$，$130>105$ 是离群值；它也比均值高出 2 个标准差以上（$48+2(22)=92$）。均值大于中位数（48 对 41），与右尾一致。直方图应能确认，但证据指向右偏，所以正态模型在目前的形式下并不合理。
  3. 第二个样本。1 个标准差内 $54/80=67.5\%$，2 个标准差内 $76/80=95\%$，3 个标准差内 $80/80=100\%$，都接近 68%、95%、99.7%。若直方图大致对称，则正态模型是合理的。
  4. 结论：「第一个样本的学习时间由于右偏和 130 处的离群值，不是近似正态；第二个样本因 1、2、3 个标准差内的百分比与经验法则吻合，是近似正态。」

How the mean and the SD change the answer均值与标准差如何改变答案

Changing $\mu$ slides the whole curve left or right without changing its shape. Changing $\sigma$ rescales it: a larger SD makes the curve lower and wider, so more of the distribution lies in both tails. This matters in comparisons: a group with the higher mean can still have more values below a fixed cutoff if its spread is larger. To compare two Normal models, compute each proportion with its own mean and SD; do not compare means alone.

改变 $\mu$ 会让整条曲线左右平移而形状不变。改变 $\sigma$ 会改变曲线的尺度：标准差越大，曲线越矮越宽，两侧尾部的比例越大。这在比较中很重要：均值更高的一组，如果离散程度更大，低于固定分界值的数值仍可能更多。比较两个正态模型时，要用各自的均值和标准差分别计算比例，不能只比较均值。

1. A test score of 82 has mean 70 and standard deviation 8. What is the z-score?

Answer: A. $z=(82-70)/8=1.5$.

1. 某测验成绩 82，均值 70，标准差 8。z 分数是多少？

答案：A. $z=(82-70)/8=1.5$。

2. Lifetimes are approximately $N(50,6)$ hours. About what proportion exceed 62 hours?

Answer: B. 62 is 2 SD above the mean; about 5% lies outside 2 SD, half above: 2.5%.

2. 寿命近似服从 $N(50,6)$（小时）。约多大比例超过 62 小时？

答案：B. 62 比均值高 2 个标准差；约 5% 在 2 个标准差之外，其中一半在上侧：2.5%。

3. Which command gives the proportion of an $N(72,9)$ distribution above 85?

Answer: B. Lower bound 85, upper bound a large number, then mean and SD.

3. 哪个命令能求出 $N(72,9)$ 分布中大于 85 的比例？

答案：B. 下界 85，上界取很大的数，然后是均值和标准差。

4. Which command finds the score that separates the top 10% of an $N(72,9)$ distribution?

Answer: B. The area to the left of the cutoff is 0.90.

4. 哪个命令能求出把 $N(72,9)$ 分布前 10% 分开的分数？

答案：B. 分界值左侧的面积为 0.90。

5. A student's z-score on a test is $-0.5$. Which statement is correct?

Answer: C. A z-score counts standard deviations from the mean; the negative sign means below.

5. 某学生在一次测验中的 z 分数为 $-0.5$。下列哪项正确？

答案：C. z 分数数的是与均值相差的标准差个数；负号表示低于均值。

6. A student scored 74, which is at the 80th percentile of the class. Which statement is correct?

Answer: B. A percentile is the percent of observations at or below the value; it is not a percent correct.

6. 某学生得 74 分，处在全班的第 80 百分位数。下列哪项正确？

答案：B. 百分位数是不高于该数值的观测所占的百分比，不是答对的百分比。

7. IQ scores are approximately $N(100,15)$. What proportion of people score between 85 and 130?

Answer: C. $z=-1$ and $z=2$: normalcdf(85,130,100,15) $=0.9772-0.1587=0.8186$. Choice A is only $\pm1$ SD and D is only $\pm2$ SD.

7. 智商分数近似服从 $N(100,15)$。得分在 85 到 130 之间的人占多大比例？

答案：C. $z=-1$ 与 $z=2$：normalcdf(85,130,100,15) $=0.9772-0.1587=0.8186$。选项 A 只是 $\pm1$ 个标准差，D 只是 $\pm2$ 个标准差。

8. A Normal probability plot of a data set lies close to a straight line. Which is the best conclusion?

Answer: B. A roughly linear plot supports "approximately Normal"; real data are never exactly Normal, and the plot does not prove there are no outliers.

8. 某数据集的正态概率图非常接近一条直线。最佳结论是哪项？

答案：B. 大致呈直线的图支持「近似正态」；真实数据绝不会恰好正态，该图也不能证明没有离群值。

9. Scores are approximately $N(500,100)$. What score separates the lowest 5% from the rest?

Answer: C. The left-tail area is 0.05: invNorm(0.05,500,100) $=335.5$. Choice A is the top 5% cutoff and B uses 2 SDs from the empirical rule, which leaves 2.5%.

9. 成绩近似服从 $N(500,100)$。把最低的 5% 与其余分开的分数是多少？

答案：C. 左尾面积为 0.05：invNorm(0.05,500,100) $=335.5$。选项 A 是前 5% 的分界值，B 用的是经验法则的 2 个标准差，那只留下 2.5%。

10. A uniform density curve covers 0 to 20. What is the proportion of values above 15?

Answer: D. The height is $\dfrac{1}{20}$ and the width above 15 is 5, so the area is $5\cdot\dfrac{1}{20}=0.25$.

10. 一个均匀密度曲线覆盖 0 到 20。大于 15 的比例是多少？

答案：D. 高度为 $\dfrac{1}{20}$，15 以上的宽度为 5，所以面积为 $5\cdot\dfrac{1}{20}=0.25$。

11. A Normal distribution has SD 6 and 10% of its values are above 50. What is the mean?

Answer: D. The area to the left of 50 is 0.90, so $z=1.2816$ and $\mu=50-1.2816(6)=42.3$. Choice A adds instead of subtracts; B uses $z=1$; C uses $z=2$.

11. 某正态分布的标准差为 6，且有 10% 的数值大于 50。均值是多少？

答案：D. 50 左侧的面积为 0.90，所以 $z=1.2816$，$\mu=50-1.2816(6)=42.3$。选项 A 把减写成了加；B 用了 $z=1$；C 用了 $z=2$。

12. A teacher adds 5 points to every score on a test. What happens to a student's z-score and to the SD of the scores?

Answer: A. Adding a constant shifts the mean by the same amount and leaves the spread alone, so the distance from the mean in SD units, the z-score, does not change.

12. 老师给一次测验的每个分数都加了 5 分。学生的 z 分数和分数的标准差会怎样？

答案：A. 加一个常数使均值平移相同的量，而离散程度不变，所以与均值相差的标准差个数，即 z 分数，不变。

13. Heights are approximately $N(170,8)$ cm. About what percent of heights are below 154 cm?

Answer: A. $154=170-2(8)$, 2 SDs below the mean. About 95% lie within 2 SDs, so the remaining 5% is split between the tails: 2.5% below. Choice C is the tail beyond 1 SD.

13. 身高近似服从 $N(170,8)$ 厘米。约百分之几的身高低于 154 厘米？

答案：A. $154=170-2(8)$，比均值低 2 个标准差。约 95% 在 2 个标准差之内，其余 5% 由两侧尾部平分：低于 154 的占 2.5%。选项 C 是 1 个标准差之外的尾部。
