Q 2第2题MEDIUM中等2.3 simulating random processes2.3 模拟随机过程CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A student simulates free throws with random digits: 0 through 5 counts as a make and 6 through 9 as a miss, so the model probability of a make is 0.60 per attempt. In 200 simulated attempts, 118 were makes, a relative frequency of 0.59. Which interpretation is correct?
学生用随机数字模拟（simulation）罚球：0 至 5 记为命中、6 至 9 记为未中，模型给出的每次命中概率为 0.60。200 次模拟中命中 118 次，相对频数（relative frequency）为 0.59。哪种解释正确？
  1. (A) The true probability is 0.59 because 200 trials are enough to remove simulation variability.真实概率是 0.59，因为 200 次试验已足以消除模拟变异。
  2. (B) The model assigns probability 0.60 to each attempt; 0.59 is this run's relative frequency, an estimate that would change on a rerun.模型给每次尝试的概率为 0.60；0.59 是本次运行的相对频数，重跑会得到变化的估计值。
  3. (C) Exactly 59 of the next 100 simulated attempts will be makes.接下来 100 次模拟中恰好有 59 次命中。
  4. (D) The next attempt must be a make because most attempts so far were makes.由于此前多数尝试命中，下一次必定命中。

Q 11第11题HARD较难2.5 mutually exclusive events and addition2.5 互斥事件与加法CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
In a synthetic survey of households, 40% own a cat, 35% own a dog and 12% own both. A student writes: Step 1: $P(\text{cat or dog})=P(\text{cat})+P(\text{dog})$. Step 2: $=0.40+0.35=0.75$. Step 3: So 25% of households own neither. Which evaluation is correct?
一项合成的家庭调查中，40% 的家庭养猫，35% 养狗，12% 两者都养。学生写道：步骤 1：$P(\text{cat or dog})=P(\text{cat})+P(\text{dog})$。步骤 2：$=0.40+0.35=0.75$。步骤 3：所以 25% 的家庭两者都不养。哪种评价正确？
  1. (A) Step 2 is the first error: $0.40+0.35$ is not 0.75.步骤 2 是第一处错误：$0.40+0.35$ 不等于 0.75。
  2. (B) Step 1 is the first error: the events are not mutually exclusive, so the 12% owning both is counted twice; the correct value is $0.40+0.35-0.12=0.63$, and 37% own neither.步骤 1 是第一处错误：两事件不互斥，两者都养的 12% 被重复计算；正确值为 $0.40+0.35-0.12=0.63$，两者都不养的占 37%。
  3. (C) Step 1 is correct, because 12% own both, which shows the events are independent.步骤 1 正确，因为有 12% 两者都养，这说明两事件独立。
  4. (D) Step 3 is the first error: the complement of "cat or dog" cannot be found by subtraction from 1.步骤 3 是第一处错误：“养猫或养狗”的补集不能用 1 减去它来求。
