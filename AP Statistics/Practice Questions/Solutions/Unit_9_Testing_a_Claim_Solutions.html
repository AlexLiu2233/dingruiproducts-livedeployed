Q 1第1题EASY基础3.5 Setting up a test for a population proportion3.5 总体比例检验的设置CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A school cafeteria manager says that 40% of students buy lunch on a typical day. A reporter suspects the true percentage is lower and will survey a random sample of 120 students from a large school. Let $p$ be the proportion of all students at the school who buy lunch. Which choice names the correct test and states the correct hypotheses?
学校食堂经理说，普通一天有 40% 的学生购买午餐。一名记者怀疑真实百分比更低，将从一所规模很大的学校随机抽取 120 名学生调查。设 $p$ 为全校学生中购买午餐的比例。哪个选项给出了正确的检验和假设？
Answer: C答案：C

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The data are one yes/no variable from one sample, so the procedure is a one-proportion $z$ test. The null hypothesis names the claimed value of the parameter with an equals sign, $H_0:p=0.40$. The reporter suspects the percentage is lower, and that suspicion is stated before any data are seen, so the alternative is one-sided: $H_a:p\lt0.40$.数据是一个样本中的一个“是/否”变量，所以程序是单比例 $z$ 检验。原假设（null hypothesis）用等号写出所声称的参数值，$H_0:p=0.40$。记者怀疑比例更低，这一怀疑在看数据之前就已提出，所以备择假设（alternative hypothesis）是单侧（one-sided）的：$H_a:p\lt0.40$。

Why the options attract (M 1)选项误区（M 1）
  1. Writes the hypotheses about the sample proportion $\hat p$. A sample proportion is observed, not unknown; hypotheses are about the population proportion $p$.把假设写成关于样本比例 $\hat p$。样本比例是观察到的，不是未知的；假设应针对总体比例 $p$。
  2. Uses a two-sided alternative although the question points in one direction. The direction comes from the research question, so "lower" gives $p\lt0.40$.题目指向一个方向，却用了双侧备择假设。方向来自研究问题，“更低”对应 $p\lt0.40$。
  3. Correct: a parameter in both hypotheses, equals sign in $H_0$, direction taken from the reporter's suspicion.正确：两个假设都针对参数，$H_0$ 含等号，方向取自记者的怀疑。
  4. Puts an inequality in $H_0$. The null hypothesis always states one value of the parameter with an equals sign, because the test works in a world where $p=0.40$.在 $H_0$ 中使用了不等号。原假设总是用等号给出参数的一个取值，因为检验是在 $p=0.40$ 的世界里进行的。
Write $H_0$ as "parameter = claimed value" and let the research question, read before the data, choose the direction of $H_a$.把 $H_0$ 写成“参数 = 所声称的值”，由看数据之前读到的研究问题来决定 $H_a$ 的方向。

Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

A one-proportion $z$ test needs a random sample, the 10% condition when sampling without replacement (given here), and large counts evaluated at the hypothesized proportion: $np_0=150(0.30)=45$ and $n(1-p_0)=150(0.70)=105$, both at least 10. The test works in a world where $H_0$ is true, so $p_0$ drives both the standard error and the counts check.单比例 $z$ 检验需要随机样本、不放回抽样时的 10% 条件（此处已给出），以及用假设比例计算的大计数（large counts）：$np_0=150(0.30)=45$ 与 $n(1-p_0)=150(0.70)=105$，都至少为 10。检验在 $H_0$ 为真的世界里进行，所以标准误（standard error）和计数检查都由 $p_0$ 决定。

Why the options attract (M 20)选项误区（M 20）
  1. Checks the counts with $\hat p$, which is the rule for an interval. For a test the hypothesized value governs the sampling distribution, so the counts use $p_0$ (the guide's example of 9 defective parts in a sample shows why).用 $\hat p$ 检查计数，这是区间的规则。检验中由假设值决定抽样分布，所以计数用 $p_0$（讲义中样本只有 9 件次品的例子说明了原因）。
  2. Correct: random sample, with counts computed from $p_0=0.30$.正确：随机样本，计数由 $p_0=0.30$ 算出。
  3. Asks for a normal population. The normal model applies to the sampling distribution of $\hat p$, not to the yes/no responses, which cannot be normal.要求总体正态。正态模型适用于 $\hat p$ 的抽样分布，而不是“是/否”回答，后者不可能是正态的。
  4. Carries over the $n\ge30$ rule for means. Proportions need counts of successes and failures, not a bare sample size.把均值的 $n\ge30$ 规则搬了过来。比例需要成功数与失败数的计数，而不是单纯的样本容量。
For a test, check the counts at $p_0$; for an interval, check them at $\hat p$. The question is always "what does the sampling distribution look like in the world being tested?".检验时在 $p_0$ 处检查计数；区间则在 $\hat p$ 处检查。问题始终是“在被检验的那个世界里，抽样分布是什么样？”

A random sample of $n=60$ observations, less than 10% of a large population, will be used for a one-proportion $z$ test of $H_0:p=p_0$. For which values of $p_0$ is the large counts condition satisfied?
一个含 $n=60$ 个观测值的随机样本（不到一个大总体的 10%）将用于单比例 $z$ 检验 $H_0:p=p_0$。当 $p_0$ 取哪些值时，大计数（large counts）条件成立？
Answer: D答案：D

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Both expected counts must be at least 10. Expected successes: $60p_0\ge10$ gives $p_0\ge\tfrac{10}{60}=\tfrac16$. Expected failures: $60(1-p_0)\ge10$ gives $1-p_0\ge\tfrac16$, so $p_0\le\tfrac56$. Both inequalities must hold together.两个预期计数都必须至少为 10。预期成功数：$60p_0\ge10$ 得 $p_0\ge\tfrac{10}{60}=\tfrac16$。预期失败数：$60(1-p_0)\ge10$ 得 $1-p_0\ge\tfrac16$，即 $p_0\le\tfrac56$。两个不等式需同时成立。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. Treats the 10 in "at least 10" as 10% of the probability scale. The threshold is a count, so it is divided by $n=60$, not by 100.把“至少 10”中的 10 当成概率尺度上的 10%。阈值是计数，应除以 $n=60$，而不是除以 100。
  2. Solves only $60p_0\ge10$. Expected failures $60(1-p_0)$ must also be at least 10, which caps $p_0$ at $\tfrac56$.只解了 $60p_0\ge10$。预期失败数 $60(1-p_0)$ 也必须至少为 10，这把 $p_0$ 限制在 $\tfrac56$ 以内。
  3. Requires only 1 expected success and 1 expected failure ($60p_0\ge1$), a threshold of 1 instead of 10.只要求 1 个预期成功和 1 个预期失败（$60p_0\ge1$），阈值是 1 而不是 10。
  4. Correct: solves both inequalities, $60p_0\ge10$ and $60(1-p_0)\ge10$.正确：解出两个不等式 $60p_0\ge10$ 与 $60(1-p_0)\ge10$。
Write each count as an inequality in $p_0$ and solve both: the condition is an interval of $p_0$ values that shrinks as $n$ falls.把每个计数写成关于 $p_0$ 的不等式并同时求解：条件对应一个 $p_0$ 的区间，且 $n$ 越小区间越窄。

Q 4第4题MEDIUM中等3.6 p-values3.6 p 值CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A test of $H_0:p=p_0$ against $H_a:p\gt p_0$ produces a standardized test statistic of $z=1.90$. What is the p-value?
检验 $H_0:p=p_0$ 对 $H_a:p\gt p_0$ 得到标准化检验统计量（test statistic） $z=1.90$。p 值（p-value）是多少？
Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The alternative $p\gt p_0$ is right-tailed, so the p-value is the area to the right of 1.90: $P(Z\ge1.90)=1-0.9713=0.0287$ (normalcdf(1.90,1E99)). It is the probability, assuming $H_0$ is true, of a result at least this large.备择假设（alternative hypothesis） $p\gt p_0$ 是右尾的，所以 p 值是 1.90 右侧的面积：$P(Z\ge1.90)=1-0.9713=0.0287$（normalcdf(1.90,1E99)）。它是假定 $H_0$ 为真时，得到至少这么大的结果的概率。

Why the options attract (M 13)选项误区（M 13）
  1. Correct: the upper-tail area beyond 1.90.正确：1.90 右侧的上尾面积。
  2. Doubles the tail, $2(0.0287)=0.0574$. Doubling is for a two-sided alternative; this one is right-tailed.把尾部面积加倍，$2(0.0287)=0.0574$。加倍适用于双侧备择假设；这里是右尾。
  3. Reports the area to the left of 1.90, $P(Z\lt1.90)=0.9713$: the complement of the tail, not the tail.给出的是 1.90 左侧的面积 $P(Z\lt1.90)=0.9713$：是尾部的补集，不是尾部。
  4. Halves the tail, $0.0287/2=0.0144$. No rule divides a one-sided area by two.把尾部面积减半，$0.0287/2=0.0144$。没有任何规则把单侧面积除以二。
The alternative hypothesis fixes the tail before any arithmetic: $\gt$ right tail, $\lt$ left tail, $\ne$ both tails.在做任何计算之前，备择假设就决定了尾部：$\gt$ 取右尾，$\lt$ 取左尾，$\ne$ 取两侧尾部。
