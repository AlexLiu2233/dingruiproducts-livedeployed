Topic 10.1主题 10.1

Confidence Intervals for the Difference of Two Proportions两个比例之差的置信区间

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Construct a two-proportion $z$-interval for $p_1-p_2$ using the unpooled standard error.
  • Check the conditions: independent groups, random sampling or random assignment, the 10% condition, and enough successes and failures in each group.
  • Interpret the interval for a difference in context, keeping track of which group is first.
  • Distinguish a randomized experiment from two independent random samples and say what each allows.
  • Run 2-PropZInt on the TI-84 and write the result as State, Plan, Do, Conclude.
  • 用未合并的标准误构造 $p_1-p_2$ 的两比例 $z$ 区间。
  • 检验条件：两组独立、随机抽样或随机分配、10% 条件，以及每组有足够的成功与失败。
  • 结合情境解释差异的区间，并注意哪一组在前。
  • 区分随机化实验与两个独立随机样本，并说明各自允许的结论。
  • 在 TI-84 上用 2-PropZInt 计算，并按 State、Plan、Do、Conclude 书写。

To compare two groups, estimate the difference in population proportions $p_1-p_2$ with $\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2$. The sampling distribution of this difference is centred at $p_1-p_2$ and, because the groups are independent, the variances add. The two-proportion $z$-interval (2-PropZInt) is

要比较两组，用 $\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2$ 估计总体比例之差 $p_1-p_2$。该差的抽样分布以 $p_1-p_2$ 为中心，且因为两组独立，方差相加。两比例 $z$ 区间（2-PropZInt）为

$$(\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2)\;\pm\; z^*\sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}_1(1-\hat{p}_1)}{n_1}+\frac{\hat{p}_2(1-\hat{p}_2)}{n_2}}$$

The standard error is unpooled: each group keeps its own $\hat{p}$. (Pooling belongs to the test in topic 10.4, because only a test assumes $p_1=p_2$.)

标准误是未合并的：每组保留自己的 $\hat{p}$。（合并属于主题 10.4 的检验，因为只有检验才假定 $p_1=p_2$。）

Conditions for two groups两组的条件

(1) Independent groups, produced either by two independent random samples or by a randomized experiment with random assignment of units to two treatments. (2) 10% condition for each sample when sampling without replacement (not needed for random assignment to treatments). (3) At least 10 successes and 10 failures in each group: $n_1\hat{p}_1$, $n_1(1-\hat{p}_1)$, $n_2\hat{p}_2$ and $n_2(1-\hat{p}_2)$ are all at least 10. Random sampling supports generalizing to the sampled populations; random assignment supports a cause-and-effect conclusion about the treatments for the units in the study.

(1) 两组独立，来自两个独立随机样本，或来自把试验单位随机分配到两种处理的随机化实验。(2) 不放回抽样时每个样本满足 10% 条件（随机分配到处理时不需要）。(3) 每组至少有 10 个成功与 10 个失败：$n_1\hat{p}_1$、$n_1(1-\hat{p}_1)$、$n_2\hat{p}_2$、$n_2(1-\hat{p}_2)$ 都至少为 10。随机抽样支持推广到被抽样的总体；随机分配支持对研究中的单位作出处理的因果结论。

Worked Example: two independent random samples例题：两个独立随机样本

A random sample of 400 students at East High (large school) includes 212 who usually eat breakfast at home; a separate random sample of 500 students at West High (large school) has 215. Construct a 95% confidence interval for $p_1-p_2$, where $p_1$ and $p_2$ are the proportions of all East and West students who eat breakfast at home. (Illustrative data.)

  1. State. $p_1-p_2$ = difference between the proportions of all East High and all West High students who usually eat breakfast at home. Confidence level 95%.
  2. Plan. Two-proportion $z$-interval. Two independent random samples, each under 10% of its school. Counts: $212$, $188$, $215$, $285$ are all at least 10.
  3. Do. $\hat{p}_1=212/400=0.53$, $\hat{p}_2=215/500=0.43$, difference $=0.10$. Standard error $=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.53(1-0.53)}{400}+\dfrac{0.43(1-0.43)}{500}}=\sqrt{0.001113}\approx 0.0334$. Margin of error $=1.960(0.0334)\approx 0.0654$. Interval: $(0.0346,\,0.1654)$ (2-PropZInt).
  4. Conclude. We are 95% confident that the interval from $0.035$ to $0.165$ captures the true difference (East minus West) in the proportions of students who usually eat breakfast at home.

东高中（大校）随机抽取 400 名学生，其中 212 人通常在家吃早餐；另外独立地从西高中（大校）随机抽取 500 名学生，其中 215 人。构造 $p_1-p_2$ 的 95% 置信区间，其中 $p_1$、$p_2$ 分别是东高中与西高中全体学生在家吃早餐的比例。（示意数据。）

  1. State。$p_1-p_2$ = 东高中与西高中全体学生中通常在家吃早餐的比例之差。置信水平 95%。
  2. Plan。两比例 $z$ 区间。两个独立随机样本，各自不到本校学生的 10%。计数：$212$、$188$、$215$、$285$ 都至少为 10。
  3. Do。$\hat{p}_1=212/400=0.53$，$\hat{p}_2=215/500=0.43$，差 $=0.10$。标准误 $=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.53(1-0.53)}{400}+\dfrac{0.43(1-0.43)}{500}}=\sqrt{0.001113}\approx 0.0334$。误差范围 $=1.960(0.0334)\approx 0.0654$。区间：$(0.0346,\,0.1654)$（2-PropZInt）。
  4. Conclude。我们有 95% 的把握认为，从 $0.035$ 到 $0.165$ 的区间覆盖了通常在家吃早餐的学生比例的真实差（东高中减西高中）。
Worked Example: a randomized experiment例题：随机化实验

180 volunteers in an online course are randomly assigned, 90 each, to receive reminders by text message (group 1) or by email (group 2). Afterwards 72 of the text group and 58 of the email group completed the course. Construct a 90% confidence interval for $p_1-p_2$, where $p_i$ is the true proportion who would complete the course under reminder method $i$. (Illustrative data.)

  1. Plan. Randomized experiment with random assignment, so the groups are independent and the 10% condition is not needed. Counts: $72$, $18$, $58$, $32$ are all at least 10.
  2. Do. $\hat{p}_1=72/90=0.8000$ and $\hat{p}_2=58/90\approx 0.6444$; difference $\approx 0.1556$. Standard error $=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.8(0.2)}{90}+\dfrac{0.6444(1-0.6444)}{90}}\approx 0.0658$. With $z^*=1.645$, margin of error $\approx 0.1082$. Interval: $(0.0474,\,0.2637)$.
  3. Conclude. We are 90% confident that the interval from $0.047$ to $0.264$ captures the true difference (text minus email) in the proportions of volunteers like these who would complete the course.
  4. Scope. Random assignment allows a cause-and-effect conclusion about the reminder method for these volunteers; because the volunteers were not randomly selected, the result is not generalized to all online students.

某网络课程的 180 名志愿者被随机分配（每组 90 人）到短信提醒（第 1 组）或邮件提醒（第 2 组）。之后短信组 72 人、邮件组 58 人完成了课程。构造 $p_1-p_2$ 的 90% 置信区间，其中 $p_i$ 是采用提醒方式 $i$ 时完成课程的真实比例。（示意数据。）

  1. Plan。有随机分配的随机化实验，所以两组独立，不需要 10% 条件。计数：$72$、$18$、$58$、$32$ 都至少为 10。
  2. Do。$\hat{p}_1=72/90=0.8000$，$\hat{p}_2=58/90\approx 0.6444$；差 $\approx 0.1556$。标准误 $=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.8(0.2)}{90}+\dfrac{0.6444(1-0.6444)}{90}}\approx 0.0658$。取 $z^*=1.645$，误差范围 $\approx 0.1082$。区间：$(0.0474,\,0.2637)$。
  3. Conclude。我们有 90% 的把握认为，从 $0.047$ 到 $0.264$ 的区间覆盖了与这些志愿者相似的人完成课程比例的真实差（短信减邮件）。
  4. 适用范围。随机分配允许对这些志愿者就提醒方式作出因果结论；因为志愿者不是随机选出的，结果不推广到所有网络课程学生。

Common Mistake: pooling in an interval or adding standard errors

Do not pool the two proportions for an interval, and do not add the two standard errors: add the variances and then take the square root. Also, the interval is about the difference $p_1-p_2$, so interpret it as a difference ("East minus West"), not as two separate intervals.

常见错误：在区间里合并，或把标准误直接相加

构造区间时不要合并两个比例，也不要把两个标准误直接相加：应先把方差相加再开方。另外，区间针对的是差 $p_1-p_2$，所以要按差来解释（“东高中减西高中”），而不是当作两个单独的区间。

Exam tip考试提示

Name both groups and the order: "the difference (East minus West) in the proportions of all students who ...". Check four counts, not two, and say whether the groups come from two random samples or from random assignment, because that decides what the conclusion may claim.

点明两组及其顺序：“全体学生中……比例之差（东高中减西高中）”。要检查四个计数而不是两个，并说明两组来自两个随机样本还是随机分配，因为这决定结论能声称什么。

从两个总体分别独立随机抽样，或在随机化实验中把单位随机分配到两种处理。

Topic 10.2主题 10.2

Justifying a Claim Based on a Confidence Interval for a Difference of Proportions依据比例之差的置信区间论证说法

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Decide whether an interval for $p_1-p_2$ contains 0 and what that says about a difference.
  • Justify a claim about a difference using the sign and location of the whole interval.
  • Explain why an interval containing 0 does not show that the proportions are equal.
  • Report the size of a plausible difference, with direction, in context.
  • State what the study design (random sampling or random assignment) allows the claim to say.
  • 判断 $p_1-p_2$ 的区间是否含 0，以及这对差异意味着什么。
  • 利用整个区间的符号与位置论证关于差异的说法。
  • 解释为什么区间含 0 并不说明两个比例相等。
  • 结合情境，带方向地报告合理差异的大小。
  • 说明研究设计（随机抽样或随机分配）允许结论说什么。

Zero is the key number: $p_1-p_2=0$ means the two population proportions are equal. If the interval for the difference contains 0, then "no difference" is plausible and the data do not provide convincing evidence of a difference. If the interval does not contain 0 and lies entirely above it, there is convincing evidence that $p_1>p_2$; entirely below it, that $p_1<p_2$. The endpoints also say how large the difference could plausibly be, which is more informative than a yes or no.

0 是关键数：$p_1-p_2=0$ 表示两个总体比例相等。若差的区间含 0，则“无差异”是合理的，数据没有提供差异的令人信服的证据。若区间不含 0且全在 0 之上，则有令人信服的证据表明 $p_1>p_2$；全在 0 之下则表明 $p_1<p_2$。端点还说明了差异可能有多大，这比单纯的“是”或“否”信息更丰富。

Worked Example: an interval that excludes 0例题：不含 0 的区间

Using the 95% interval $(0.035,\,0.165)$ for East minus West from topic 10.1, evaluate the student council's claim that a larger proportion of East students than West students eat breakfast at home.

  1. Location. The interval does not contain 0, and every value in it is positive.
  2. Justify. Because every plausible value of $p_1-p_2$ is greater than 0, the claim is supported: there is convincing evidence that the true proportion of East students who eat breakfast at home is greater than that of West students.
  3. Size. We estimate the East proportion exceeds the West proportion by between about $3.5$ and $16.5$ percentage points. Because the samples were random (not randomly assigned), this shows an association between school and habit, not that the school causes it.

利用主题 10.1 中东高中减西高中的 95% 区间 $(0.035,\,0.165)$，评价学生会的说法：东高中在家吃早餐的学生比例高于西高中。

  1. 位置。区间不含 0，且其中每个值都为正。
  2. 论证。因为 $p_1-p_2$ 的每个合理取值都大于 0，该说法得到支持：有令人信服的证据表明，东高中学生在家吃早餐的真实比例大于西高中。
  3. 大小。我们估计东高中的比例比西高中高约 $3.5$ 到 $16.5$ 个百分点。因为样本是随机抽取的（不是随机分配的），这表明学校与习惯之间有关联，而不是学校导致了该习惯。
Worked Example: an interval that contains 0例题：含 0 的区间

A random sample of 120 students in one grade has 54 who prefer morning study sessions; a separate random sample of 150 students in another grade has 57. A teacher claims the two grades differ in the proportion preferring mornings. Evaluate the claim with a 95% interval. (Illustrative data.)

  1. Conditions. Independent random samples; each under 10% of its grade (assumed); counts $54$, $66$, $57$, $93$ are all at least 10.
  2. Interval. $\hat{p}_1=0.45$, $\hat{p}_2=0.38$, difference $0.07$. Standard error $=\sqrt{0.003633}\approx 0.0603$; margin of error $\approx 0.1181$. Interval: $(-0.0481,\,0.1881)$.
  3. Justify. The interval contains 0, so "no difference" is plausible. The interval also contains positive and negative values.
  4. Conclude. The interval does not provide convincing evidence that the true proportions of students preferring morning study sessions differ between the two grades. This does not show the proportions are equal: differences from about $-0.05$ to $0.19$ remain plausible.

某年级随机抽取 120 名学生，其中 54 人喜欢早上自习；另一年级独立随机抽取 150 名学生，其中 57 人。老师称两个年级喜欢早上自习的比例不同。用 95% 区间评价该说法。（示意数据。）

  1. 条件。独立随机样本；各自不到本年级的 10%（假设）；计数 $54$、$66$、$57$、$93$ 都至少为 10。
  2. 区间。$\hat{p}_1=0.45$，$\hat{p}_2=0.38$，差 $0.07$。标准误 $=\sqrt{0.003633}\approx 0.0603$；误差范围 $\approx 0.1181$。区间：$(-0.0481,\,0.1881)$。
  3. 论证。区间含 0，所以“无差异”是合理的。区间内既有正值也有负值。
  4. Conclude。该区间没有提供令人信服的证据表明，两个年级喜欢早上自习的学生真实比例不同。这并不说明两个比例相等：从约 $-0.05$ 到 $0.19$ 的差异仍然合理。
Worked Example: using the experiment interval to judge "equally effective"例题：用实验区间判断“同样有效”

From topic 10.1, the 90% interval for text minus email completion rates is $(0.047,\,0.264)$. A course designer claims the two reminder methods are equally effective. Evaluate the claim.

  1. Location. The interval does not contain 0; it lies entirely above 0.
  2. Conclude. There is convincing evidence that the true completion proportion is higher with text reminders than with email reminders for volunteers like these, so "equally effective" is not plausible.
  3. Why causal. The volunteers were randomly assigned to the two methods, so a difference this large is unlikely to be due to who ended up in which group.

由主题 10.1，短信减邮件完成率的 90% 区间为 $(0.047,\,0.264)$。课程设计者声称两种提醒方式同样有效。评价该说法。

  1. 位置。区间不含 0，完全在 0 之上。
  2. Conclude。有令人信服的证据表明，对与这些志愿者相似的人，短信提醒下的真实完成比例高于邮件提醒，所以“同样有效”并不合理。
  3. 为何可下因果结论。志愿者被随机分配到两种方式，所以如此大的差异不太可能是由谁分到哪一组造成的。
Common Mistake: concluding "equal" from an interval that contains 0

An interval containing 0 means a difference of zero cannot be ruled out, not that the two proportions are the same. Another frequent mistake is to compare the two groups by checking whether two separate one-sample intervals overlap; use one interval for the difference.

常见错误：由含 0 的区间断定“相等”

区间含 0 只说明不能排除差为零，并不说明两个比例相同。另一个常见错误是通过两个单独的单样本区间是否重叠来比较两组；应当使用差的那一个区间。

Exam tip考试提示

State where 0 sits relative to the interval, then link it to the claim in context, with the direction in the correct order ("East is greater than West"). Mention causation only for randomized experiments, and say what population the result applies to.

说明 0 相对区间的位置，再联系情境中的说法，并以正确的顺序写出方向（“东高中大于西高中”）。只有随机化实验才可以谈因果，并说明结果适用于哪个总体。

Topic 10.3主题 10.3

Setting Up a Test for the Difference of Two Population Proportions设置两个总体比例之差的检验

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Write $H_0$ and $H_a$ for a comparison of two proportions, defining $p_1$ and $p_2$ in words.
  • Choose a one-sided or two-sided alternative from the question wording.
  • Explain why $H_0$ is $p_1=p_2$ and what that says about the difference.
  • Compute the pooled (combined) proportion and use it for the expected-count check.
  • State the conditions for a two-proportion $z$-test.
  • 为两个比例的比较写出 $H_0$ 与 $H_a$，并用文字定义 $p_1$ 和 $p_2$。
  • 依据题目措辞选择单侧或双侧备择假设。
  • 解释为什么 $H_0$ 是 $p_1=p_2$，以及它对差意味着什么。
  • 计算合并（联合）比例，并用它检查预期计数。
  • 写出两比例 $z$ 检验的条件。

The null hypothesis for comparing two groups says there is no difference: $H_0: p_1=p_2$, which is the same as $H_0: p_1-p_2=0$. The alternative hypothesis is $H_a: p_1>p_2$, $H_a: p_1<p_2$ or $H_a: p_1\ne p_2$, chosen from the question before seeing the data. Define each parameter in words, with the group, population and variable, and keep the order of the groups the same throughout: if $p_1$ is the treatment group, $H_a: p_1>p_2$ means the treatment does better.

比较两组的原假设说明没有差异：$H_0: p_1=p_2$，等价于 $H_0: p_1-p_2=0$。备择假设是 $H_a: p_1>p_2$、$H_a: p_1<p_2$ 或 $H_a: p_1\ne p_2$，须在看数据之前由问题决定。要用文字定义每个参数，写明组别、总体和变量，并在全过程中保持组的顺序不变：若 $p_1$ 是处理组，则 $H_a: p_1>p_2$ 表示处理效果更好。

The pooled proportion and conditions合并比例与条件

Because $H_0$ says both groups share one proportion, the best single estimate of that common value combines the two samples. The pooled (combined) proportion is $\hat{p}_c=\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2}$, where $x_1$ and $x_2$ are the success counts. The conditions are: (1) independent groups from two random samples or a randomized experiment with random assignment; (2) the 10% condition for each sample when sampling without replacement (not needed for random assignment); (3) the expected counts under $H_0$, $n_1\hat{p}_c$, $n_1(1-\hat{p}_c)$, $n_2\hat{p}_c$ and $n_2(1-\hat{p}_c)$, are all at least 10. (Checking the observed successes and failures in each group is a common alternative; in the examples here both checks agree. For a confidence interval in topic 10.1 the check uses the separate counts of each group instead of the pooled proportion.)

因为 $H_0$ 说两组共享同一个比例，对这个共同值最好的单一估计要把两个样本合在一起。合并（联合）比例为 $\hat{p}_c=\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2}$，其中 $x_1$、$x_2$ 为成功计数。条件是：(1) 两组独立，来自两个随机样本或有随机分配的随机化实验；(2) 不放回抽样时每个样本的 10% 条件（随机分配时不需要）；(3) $H_0$ 下的预期计数 $n_1\hat{p}_c$、$n_1(1-\hat{p}_c)$、$n_2\hat{p}_c$、$n_2(1-\hat{p}_c)$ 都至少为 10。（检查每组观察到的成功数与失败数是常见的替代做法；本节例子里两种检查结论一致。对主题 10.1 的置信区间，则用每组各自的计数检查，而不用合并比例。）

Topic 10.4主题 10.4

Carrying Out a Test for the Difference of Two Population Proportions执行两个总体比例之差的检验

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate the two-proportion $z$ test statistic using the pooled proportion.
  • Find the p-value in the direction of $H_a$ and make a decision at the stated $\alpha$.
  • Write a conclusion in context about the difference, with an appropriate statement about causation or generalization.
  • Carry out the test as State, Plan, Do, Conclude and verify it with 2-PropZTest.
  • Explain when to pool (tests) and when not to pool (intervals).
  • 用合并比例计算两比例 $z$ 检验统计量。
  • 沿 $H_a$ 的方向求 p 值，并在规定的 $\alpha$ 下作出决定。
  • 结合情境就差异写出结论，并恰当说明因果或推广。
  • 按 State、Plan、Do、Conclude 完成检验，并用 2-PropZTest 核对。
  • 解释何时合并（检验）、何时不合并（区间）。

The two-proportion $z$-test (2-PropZTest) measures how many standard errors the observed difference $\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2$ is from the null value 0. The standard error uses the pooled proportion $\hat{p}_c$ because $H_0$ says both groups share one proportion:

两比例 $z$ 检验（2-PropZTest）衡量观察到的差 $\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2$ 离原假设值 0 有多少个标准误。标准误使用合并比例 $\hat{p}_c$，因为 $H_0$ 说两组共享同一个比例：

$$z=\dfrac{\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2}{\sqrt{\hat{p}_c(1-\hat{p}_c)\left(\dfrac{1}{n_1}+\dfrac{1}{n_2}\right)}},\qquad \hat{p}_c=\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2}$$

Then find the p-value in the direction of $H_a$ (double the tail for $p_1\ne p_2$), compare it with $\alpha$, and write the conclusion in context. Use the four-step structure: State the hypotheses, parameters and $\alpha$; Plan by naming the test and checking conditions; Do the calculation; Conclude in context, tied to the p-value. Reminder: use the pooled $\hat{p}_c$ only in the test; a confidence interval for $p_1-p_2$ keeps the two separate $\hat{p}$ values.

然后沿 $H_a$ 的方向求 p 值（$p_1\ne p_2$ 时尾部面积加倍），与 $\alpha$ 比较，并结合情境写出结论。使用四步结构：State 写假设、参数和 $\alpha$；Plan 写检验名称并检验条件；Do 完成计算；Conclude 结合情境并与 p 值挂钩。提醒：合并的 $\hat{p}_c$ 只用于检验；$p_1-p_2$ 的置信区间保留两个独立的 $\hat{p}$。

Worked Example: a randomized experiment with a one-sided test例题：单侧检验的随机化实验

A researcher randomly assigns 200 volunteers to use a reminder app (group 1, $n_1=100$) or standard reminders (group 2, $n_2=100$). In the app group 68 followed their plan; in the standard group 54 did. Is there convincing evidence that the app increases the proportion who follow their plan? Use $\alpha=0.05$. (Illustrative data.)

  1. State. $p_1$ = true proportion who would follow their plan with the app; $p_2$ = true proportion with standard reminders. $H_0: p_1=p_2$, $H_a: p_1>p_2$, $\alpha=0.05$.
  2. Plan. Two-proportion $z$-test. Random assignment, so independent groups. Pooled $\hat{p}_c=122/200=0.610$; expected counts $61.0$ and $39.0$ in each group, all at least 10.
  3. Do. $\hat{p}_1=0.68$, $\hat{p}_2=0.54$, difference $=0.14$. $z=\dfrac{0.14}{\sqrt{0.610(1-0.610)\left(\dfrac{1}{100}+\dfrac{1}{100}\right)}}=\dfrac{0.14}{0.0690}\approx 2.03$. p-value $=P(Z\ge 2.03)\approx 0.0212$ (2-PropZTest, $p_1>p_2$).
  4. Conclude. Because the p-value of about $0.021$ is less than $\alpha=0.05$, we reject $H_0$. There is convincing evidence that the true proportion who follow their plan is higher with the app than with standard reminders for volunteers like these. Random assignment supports the cause-and-effect wording; the volunteers were not randomly selected, so we do not generalize beyond similar volunteers.

  3. Do。$\hat{p}_1=0.68$，$\hat{p}_2=0.54$，差 $=0.14$。$z=\dfrac{0.14}{\sqrt{0.610(1-0.610)\left(\dfrac{1}{100}+\dfrac{1}{100}\right)}}=\dfrac{0.14}{0.0690}\approx 2.03$。p 值 $=P(Z\ge 2.03)\approx 0.0212$（2-PropZTest，$p_1>p_2$）。

Common Mistake: wrong standard error or wrong direction

Using the unpooled standard error in a test, or the pooled one in an interval, is a frequent slip. Another is reversing the order: if $\hat{p}_1<\hat{p}_2$ the statistic $z$ is negative, which matters for a one-sided $H_a: p_1>p_2$ (the p-value would then be large, not small).

常见错误：标准误或方向用错

在检验中用未合并的标准误，或在区间中用合并的标准误，都是常见失误。另一个是顺序颠倒：若 $\hat{p}_1<\hat{p}_2$，则统计量 $z$ 为负，这对单侧的 $H_a: p_1>p_2$ 很关键（此时 p 值会很大，而不是很小）。

Topic 10.5主题 10.5

Confidence Interval for the Difference of Two Population Means两个总体均值之差的置信区间

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Construct a two-sample $t$ interval for $\mu_1-\mu_2$ by hand and with 2-SampTInt.
  • State and check the conditions for two independent groups.
  • Use the calculator's degrees of freedom and explain the conservative alternative.
  • Distinguish a two-sample design from paired data.
  • Interpret an interval for a difference of means in context.
  • 手算并用 2-SampTInt 构造 $\mu_1-\mu_2$ 的双样本 $t$ 区间。
  • 陈述并检验两个独立组的条件。
  • 使用计算器给出的自由度，并解释保守的替代做法。
  • 区分双样本设计与配对数据。
  • 结合情境解释均值之差的区间。

To compare two populations (or two treatments), estimate the difference $\mu_1-\mu_2$ using the difference of the sample means $\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2$. The standard error of this difference combines the variation in both groups, and the interval is the two-sample t-interval:

比较两个总体（或两种处理）时，用样本均值之差 $\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2$ 估计 $\mu_1-\mu_2$。该差的标准误综合了两组的变异，区间称为双样本 t 区间（two-sample t-interval）：

Two-sample t interval for $\mu_1-\mu_2$

$$(\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2)\pm t^*\sqrt{\dfrac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{s_2^2}{n_2}}$$ Use the unpooled standard error. The degrees of freedom come from the calculator (STAT → TESTS → 0:2-SampTInt, with Pooled: No); a safe by-hand choice is the smaller of $n_1-1$ and $n_2-1$, which gives a slightly wider interval.

$\mu_1-\mu_2$ 的双样本 t 区间

$$(\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2)\pm t^*\sqrt{\dfrac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{s_2^2}{n_2}}$$ 使用非合并（unpooled）标准误。自由度由计算器给出（STAT → TESTS → 0:2-SampTInt，选 Pooled: No）；手算时稳妥的选择是 $n_1-1$ 与 $n_2-1$ 中较小者，得到的区间略宽。

Conditions: independent groups, each sample条件：独立的组，每个样本

The groups must be independent: knowing the values in one group tells you nothing about the other. Random: the data come from two independent random samples, or from a randomized experiment with units randomly assigned to two treatments. 10% condition: for random samples, each sample is less than 10% of its own population (not needed for randomized experiments). Normal or large sample: check each sample or group separately: approximately normal population, or $n\ge 30$ in each group, or a graph of each group with no strong skew and no outliers. Both samples must meet the condition.

两组必须独立（independent）：知道一组的数值，对另一组毫无信息。随机：数据来自两个独立的随机样本，或来自把单位随机分配到两种处理的随机化实验。10% 条件：对随机样本，每个样本都少于各自总体的 10%（随机化实验不需要）。正态或大样本：对每个样本或每组分别检验：总体近似正态，或每组 $n\ge 30$，或每组的图形没有明显偏斜和离群点。两个样本（both samples）都必须满足条件。

Two samples or paired data?双样本还是配对数据？

Before using this interval, decide how the data were collected. In a two-sample design the groups are separate collections of different individuals; no value in one group is linked to a value in the other. In paired (matched pairs) data, every value in one set is naturally linked to exactly one value in the other: the same subject measured twice, twins, or two matched individuals. Paired data are analyzed as one sample of differences (Topic 10.8). Using the two-sample formula on paired data ignores the pairing and usually wastes power.

使用该区间之前，先判断数据是如何收集的。双样本设计中，两组是不同个体的独立集合；一组中的任何数值都与另一组无关联。配对（paired，匹配对）数据中，一组的每个数值都自然地与另一组的恰好一个数值相连：同一对象测两次、双胞胎，或两个匹配的个体。配对数据按一个差值样本分析（主题 10.8）。对配对数据使用双样本公式会忽略配对关系，通常浪费检验功效。

Topic 10.6主题 10.6

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Use an interval for $\mu_1-\mu_2$ to justify a claim about a difference in means.
  • Decide from the interval whether 0 is a plausible value for the difference.
  • Describe the direction and likely size of a difference using the whole interval.
  • Match a confidence level to a two-sided significance level.
  • 用 $\mu_1-\mu_2$ 的区间论证关于均值差异的说法。
  • 根据区间判断 0 是否为差的合理取值。
  • 利用整个区间描述差异的方向与可能的大小。
  • 把置信水平与双侧显著性水平对应起来。

If there were no difference between two population means, $\mu_1-\mu_2$ would equal 0. So, to evaluate a claim about a difference in means, look at where 0 falls relative to the confidence interval for $\mu_1-\mu_2$ (Figure 7.4). If the interval contains 0, then 0 is a plausible value for the difference: the data do not provide convincing evidence that the means differ. If the interval does not contain 0, the interval is entirely positive or entirely negative, and you can justify the claim that the means differ, in the direction the interval shows.

如果两个总体均值没有差异，则 $\mu_1-\mu_2$ 等于 0。因此要评价关于均值差异（difference）的说法，就看 0 相对于 $\mu_1-\mu_2$ 置信区间的位置（图 7.4）。若区间包含 0（contains 0），则 0 是差的合理取值：数据没有提供令人信服的证据表明均值不同。若区间不包含 0，则区间全为正或全为负，可以论证（justify）均值不同的说法，其方向由区间显示。

The interval also tells you how large the difference might be, something a test alone does not. An interval such as $(0.21,\,8.59)$ minutes excludes zero but includes tiny differences; whether the difference matters in practice is a judgment about context. Remember that the interval concerns the populations (or treatments) as defined by the design: a randomized experiment supports a causal claim for the units studied, a random sample supports generalization to its population, and an observational comparison supports only association.

区间还告诉你差异可能有多大，这是单独的检验给不了的。像 $(0.21,\,8.59)$ 分钟这样的区间不含零，但包含很小的差异；差异在实际中是否重要，要结合情境判断。记住区间针对的是设计所界定的总体（或处理）：随机化实验支持对所研究单位的因果结论，随机样本支持推广到其总体，而观察性比较只支持关联。

Topic 10.7主题 10.7

Setting Up a Test for the Difference of Two Population Means建立两个总体均值之差的检验

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Write $H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$ (equivalently $\mu_1-\mu_2=0$) with the correct alternative hypothesis.
  • Choose a one-sided or two-sided alternative from the wording of the question.
  • Set up the hypotheses for paired data in terms of the mean difference $\mu_d$.
  • 在情境中定义 $\mu_1$ 与 $\mu_2$，并确定谁是第 1 组。
  • 写出 $H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$（等价于 $\mu_1-\mu_2=0$）及正确的备择假设。

  • 用平均差值 $\mu_d$ 为配对数据建立假设。

A test comparing two means asks whether the sample data provide convincing evidence of a difference. The null hypothesis says there is no difference between the population means, and the alternative hypothesis says there is a difference (in one direction or either):

比较两个均值的检验询问样本数据是否提供了令人信服的差异证据。零假设说两个总体均值没有差异，备择假设（alternative hypothesis）说存在差异（某一方向或任一方向）：

Hypotheses for two means

$H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$ (that is, $\mu_1-\mu_2=0$) against $H_a:\mu_1<\mu_2$, $H_a:\mu_1>\mu_2$, or $H_a:\mu_1\ne\mu_2$. Describe $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ as population means (or true treatment means) with units, and say which group is which. For paired data, use one parameter instead: $\mu_d$, the true mean of the differences; $H_0:\mu_d=0$.

两个均值的假设

$H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$（即 $\mu_1-\mu_2=0$），对立面为 $H_a:\mu_1<\mu_2$、$H_a:\mu_1>\mu_2$ 或 $H_a:\mu_1\ne\mu_2$。把 $\mu_1$ 与 $\mu_2$ 描述为带单位的总体均值（或处理的真实均值），并说明谁是谁。配对数据则只用一个参数：$\mu_d$，差值的真实均值；$H_0:\mu_d=0$。

Choose the direction from the question's wording, and keep the order consistent: if $\mu_1$ is the new method and $\mu_2$ the old, "the new method gives higher scores" is $H_a:\mu_1>\mu_2$. Reversing the labels reverses the inequality. Two-sample t test

$$t=\dfrac{(\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2)-0}{\sqrt{\dfrac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{s_2^2}{n_2}}}$$ The degrees of freedom come from the calculator (by hand, the smaller of $n_1-1$ and $n_2-1$ is a safe, slightly conservative alternative). TI-84: STAT → TESTS → 4:2-SampTTest with Pooled: No; the output gives the test statistic $t$, the p-value, and $df$. Use the same four steps as in Topic 9.6.

双样本 t 检验

$$t=\dfrac{(\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2)-0}{\sqrt{\dfrac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{s_2^2}{n_2}}}$$ 自由度由计算器给出（手算时，取 $n_1-1$ 与 $n_2-1$ 中较小者是稳妥而略保守的替代做法）。TI-84：STAT → TESTS → 4:2-SampTTest，选 Pooled: No；输出给出检验统计量 $t$、p 值与 $df$。步骤与主题 9.6 相同的四步。

The two-sample t-test compares the observed difference of sample means with the value 0 that the null hypothesis predicts, in units of the standard error of the difference. The test statistic measures how many standard errors the difference is from 0. A p-value that is small means a difference this large would rarely occur by chance (or random assignment) if $\mu_1=\mu_2$. Conclusions use the same wording as Topic 9.6: compare the p-value with $\alpha$, then reject or fail to reject $H_0$ and state the evidence for $H_a$ in context.

双样本 t 检验（two-sample t-test）以差的标准误为单位，把观测到的样本均值之差与零假设预测的 0 比较。检验统计量（test statistic）衡量差值距离 0 有多少个标准误。p 值小说明若 $\mu_1=\mu_2$，如此大的差异很少会仅由机会（或随机分配）产生。结论的措辞与主题 9.6 相同：比较 p 值与 $\alpha$，然后拒绝或不拒绝（fail to reject）$H_0$，并结合情境陈述对 $H_a$ 的证据。

Paired data: a one-sample t test on the differences配对数据：对差值做单样本 t 检验

When the data are paired (matched pairs), compute the difference within each pair and treat the differences as a single sample. The parameter is $\mu_d$, the true mean of the differences. The inference is a one-sample $t$ procedure applied to the differences: $t=\dfrac{\bar{x}_d-0}{s_d/\sqrt{n}}$ with $df=n-1$, where $n$ is the number of pairs. Conditions apply to the differences: the pairs are a random sample (or the order or assignment within pairs was random), the number of pairs is under 10% of the population, and the differences are approximately normal or the number of pairs is at least 30. The mean of the differences equals the difference of the means, but the standard deviation $s_d$ is usually much smaller than the spread within either set, which makes the pairing pay off.

当数据是配对（matched pairs）时，先在每一对内计算差值，再把差值当作一个样本。参数是 $\mu_d$，差值的真实均值。推断是对差值（differences）使用单样本 $t$ 程序：$t=\dfrac{\bar{x}_d-0}{s_d/\sqrt{n}}$，$df=n-1$，其中 $n$ 是对数。条件针对差值：各对是随机样本（或对内的顺序或分配是随机的）、对数少于总体的 10%，且差值近似正态或对数至少为 30。差值的均值（mean difference）等于均值之差，但 $s_d$ 通常远小于任一组内的离散程度，这正是配对的好处。

Worked Example 7.9d: what goes wrong if you ignore the pairing例题 7.9d：忽略配对会出什么问题

Run the wrong test on the data in Worked Example 7.9c: treat "before" (mean 54.6, $s=5.23$) and "after" (mean 51.3, $s=4.32$) as two independent samples of 10.

  1. Two-sample statistic: $t=\dfrac{54.6-51.3}{\sqrt{\dfrac{5.23^2}{10}+\dfrac{4.32^2}{10}}}=1.54$, $df\approx 17.4$, p-value $\approx 0.071$ (one-sided, $H_a:\mu_{\text{before}}>\mu_{\text{after}}$; a two-sided p-value would be 0.142).
  2. Result: 0.071 is greater than $0.05$, so the incorrect procedure fails to reject $H_0$ and misses the effect.
  3. Why: students differ a lot in typing speed (the spread within each set is about 4 to 5 seconds), and that variation swamps the 3.3 second mean change. The paired test removes it, since each student is compared with himself or herself: $s_d=1.829$ only.

(d)Since 0.0310 is less than $0.05$, reject $H_0$. There is convincing evidence that the new fertilizer gives a greater mean pea-plant height after 30 days than the standard fertilizer.
(e)Because seedlings were randomly assigned, the difference can be attributed to the fertilizer (cause and effect). The seedlings were not a random sample of all pea plants, so the conclusion applies to seedlings like these.

题目。某园艺公司想知道新肥料能否使豌豆苗 30 天后的平均高度增加。公司把 24 株幼苗随机分配，12 株用新肥料，12 株用标准肥料。高度（厘米）：新肥料，$\bar{x}_1=31.8$，$s_1=4.6$；标准肥料，$\bar{x}_2=27.9$，$s_2=5.1$。两组点图都大致对称、无离群点。(a) 陈述假设。(b) 检验条件并写出程序名称。(c) 求检验统计量与 p 值。(d) 在 $\alpha=0.05$ 下作结论。(e) 该设计允许得出哪类结论？

部分完整答案要点
(a)$\mu_1$、$\mu_2$ 是与这些幼苗相似的幼苗使用新肥料与标准肥料 30 天后的真实平均高度（厘米）。$H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$；$H_a:\mu_1>\mu_2$。
(b)针对 $\mu_1-\mu_2$ 的双样本 $t$ 检验。幼苗被随机分配到处理；每组 $n=12<30$，但点图大致对称且无离群点，故正态性合理。（实验不需要 10% 条件。）
(c)$t=\dfrac{3.90}{\sqrt{\dfrac{4.6^2}{12}+\dfrac{5.1^2}{12}}}=\dfrac{3.90}{1.983}=1.97$，$df\approx 21.8$，p 值 $\approx 0.0310$（2-SampTTest，$\mu_1>\mu_2$，Pooled: No）。
(d)由于 0.0310 小于 $0.05$，拒绝 $H_0$。有令人信服的证据表明新肥料使豌豆苗 30 天后的平均高度大于标准肥料。
(e)因为幼苗是随机分配的，差异可归因于肥料（因果）。幼苗不是所有豌豆苗的随机样本，所以结论适用于与这些相似的幼苗。

6. In which situation is the 10% condition not needed?

B. The 10% condition protects independence when sampling without replacement; random assignment to treatments does not sample a population.

6. 在哪种情形下不需要 10% 条件？

B。10% 条件保护不放回抽样时的独立性；随机分配到处理并不是从总体中抽样。

7. For a 95% interval for $p_1-p_2$ with $\hat{p}_1=0.40$, $n_1=100$, $\hat{p}_2=0.30$, $n_2=100$, which standard error is used?

B. An interval keeps each group's own $\hat{p}$ and adds the variances. The pooled value 0.0675 belongs to a test of $H_0: p_1=p_2$, and standard errors are never added directly.

7. 对 $\hat{p}_1=0.40$、$n_1=100$、$\hat{p}_2=0.30$、$n_2=100$ 的 $p_1-p_2$ 的 95% 区间，使用哪个标准误？

B。区间保留每组各自的 $\hat{p}$ 并把方差相加。合并值 0.0675 属于 $H_0: p_1=p_2$ 的检验，标准误绝不能直接相加。
