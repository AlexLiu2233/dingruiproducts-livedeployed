  2. Correct: about 95% lies within 2 standard deviations, $32\pm2(3)$, which is 26 to 38.正确：约 95% 落在 2 个标准差之内，$32\pm2(3)$，即 26 到 38。

Answer: D答案：D

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The z-score is $z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}=\dfrac{84-72}{8}=1.5$, so the score is 1.5 standard deviations above the mean.z 分数为 $z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}=\dfrac{84-72}{8}=1.5$，即该分数比均值高 1.5 个标准差。

  3. Subtracts in the wrong order, $72-84$; a score above the mean has a positive z-score.减的顺序反了（$72-84$）；高于均值的分数 z 分数为正。
