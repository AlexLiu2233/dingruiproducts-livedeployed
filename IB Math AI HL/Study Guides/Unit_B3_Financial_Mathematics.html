Topic B3.1 · Compound Interest and DepreciationTopic B3.1 · 复利与折旧

Compound Interest, Effective Rate and Depreciation复利、实际利率与折旧 SL 1.4

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State the compound interest formula $FV = PV\left(1 + \frac{r}{100k}\right)^{kn}$ and identify $PV$, $r$, $k$ and $n$ in a context.
  • Calculate the future value of an investment compounded annually, quarterly or monthly, giving money to 2 d.p. at the end only.
  • Calculate the effective annual rate for a nominal rate and use it to compare two accounts.
  • Model depreciation as repeated multiplication by $1 - \frac{r}{100}$ and find a value after $n$ years or the first year it falls below a target.
  • Use the same growth model for inflation, and find a future price or the real value of a sum of money.
  • 写出（state）复利公式 $FV = PV\left(1 + \frac{r}{100k}\right)^{kn}$，并在情境中指出 $PV$、$r$、$k$ 与 $n$。
  • 计算（calculate）按年、按季或按月复利的投资终值，货币金额只在最后保留 2 位小数。
  • 计算（calculate）名义利率对应的实际年利率（effective annual rate），并用它比较两个账户。
  • 把折旧（depreciation）建模为反复乘以 $1 - \frac{r}{100}$，求 $n$ 年后的价值，或首次低于目标值的年份。
  • 用同一增长模型处理通货膨胀（inflation），求未来价格或一笔钱的实际价值（real value）。
Compound interest. Interest is added to the balance at the end of every compounding period, so the next period earns interest on the interest as well. For a starting amount $PV$, a nominal annual rate $r\%$, $k$ compounding periods per year and $n$ years, $$ FV \;=\; PV\left(1 + \frac{r}{100k}\right)^{kn}. $$ The rate per compounding period is $\frac{r}{100k}$ and the number of compounding periods is $N = kn$. "Compounded monthly" means $k = 12$, "compounded quarterly" means $k = 4$, "compounded half-yearly" means $k = 2$, and "compounded annually" means $k = 1$.
复利。每个计息期末，利息都并入余额，因此下一期的利息也在利息上再产生利息。设本金为 $PV$，名义年利率为 $r\%$，每年计息 $k$ 次，共 $n$ 年，则 $$ FV \;=\; PV\left(1 + \frac{r}{100k}\right)^{kn}. $$ 每个计息期的利率为 $\frac{r}{100k}$，计息期总数为 $N = kn$。"按月复利"（compounded monthly）即 $k = 12$，"按季复利"即 $k = 4$，"按半年复利"即 $k = 2$，"按年复利"即 $k = 1$。
Worked Example: compound interest with monthly compounding例题：按月复利的复利计算

A savings account pays a nominal annual interest rate of $3.6\%$ compounded monthly. A deposit of 8000 USD is left untouched for 5 years. Find the value of the account at the end.某储蓄账户的名义年利率为 $3.6\%$，按月复利。存入 8000 美元并保持 5 年不动。求期末账户的价值。

Step 1. Read the data. $PV = 8000$, $r = 3.6$, $k = 12$, $n = 5$, so the number of compounding periods is $N = kn = 60$ and the rate per month is $\frac{3.6}{1200} = 0.003$.

第 1 步：读取数据。$PV = 8000$，$r = 3.6$，$k = 12$，$n = 5$，所以计息期数 $N = kn = 60$，月利率为 $\frac{3.6}{1200} = 0.003$。

Step 2. Formula route.

第 2 步：公式法。

$$ FV \;=\; 8000\left(1 + \frac{3.6}{100 \times 12}\right)^{60} \;=\; 8000 \times 1.003^{60} \;\approx\; 9575.16 \text{ USD}. $$

GDC finance solver (TVM). Enter N = 60, I% = 3.6, PV = -8000, PMT = 0, FV = ?, P/Y = 12, C/Y = 12. Solve for FV: the GDC returns FV = 9575.16. The deposit is money paid out, so PV is negative and the answer comes back positive (money received at the end).

GDC 金融求解器（TVM）。输入 N = 60, I% = 3.6, PV = -8000, PMT = 0, FV = ?, P/Y = 12, C/Y = 12。对 FV 求解：GDC 给出 FV = 9575.16。存款是付出的钱，所以 PV 为负，结果为正（期末收回的钱）。

Why this works. Each month multiplies the balance by $1.003$, and there are $60$ months. The money is rounded to 2 d.p. once, at the end: $9575.158\ldots$ becomes $9575.16$ USD. Interest earned is $9575.16 - 8000 = 1575.16$ USD.

原理。每个月余额乘以 $1.003$，共 $60$ 个月。金额只在最后取整一次：$9575.158\ldots$ 记为 $9575.16$ 美元。所得利息为 $9575.16 - 8000 = 1575.16$ 美元。

Effective annual rate实际年利率

A nominal rate hides the effect of compounding. The effective annual rate is the single yearly rate that gives the same growth in one year: $\left(1 + \frac{r}{100k}\right)^{k} - 1$. For a nominal $6\%$ compounded quarterly it is $1.015^{4} - 1 \approx 0.06136$, that is $6.136\%$. The more often interest is compounded, the larger the effective rate for the same nominal rate. Some GDCs have a built-in conversion between nominal and effective rates; the formula always works.

名义利率掩盖了复利的影响。实际年利率（effective annual rate）是一年内产生相同增长的单一年利率：$\left(1 + \frac{r}{100k}\right)^{k} - 1$。名义利率 $6\%$ 按季复利时，它为 $1.015^{4} - 1 \approx 0.06136$，即 $6.136\%$。名义利率相同时，复利越频繁，实际利率越大。部分 GDC 内置名义利率与实际利率的换算；公式法始终适用。

Worked Example: comparing two accounts with the effective annual rate例题：用实际年利率比较两个账户

Account A offers a nominal annual rate of $5.9\%$ compounded monthly. Account B offers $6.0\%$ compounded half-yearly. Which account has the greater effective annual rate?账户 A 的名义年利率为 $5.9\%$，按月复利；账户 B 为 $6.0\%$，按半年复利。哪个账户的实际年利率更大？

Step 1. Account A ($k = 12$).

第 1 步：账户 A（$k = 12$）。

$$ \left(1 + \frac{5.9}{1200}\right)^{12} - 1 \;\approx\; 0.06062 \;=\; 6.062\%. $$

Step 2. Account B ($k = 2$).

第 2 步：账户 B（$k = 2$）。

$$ \left(1 + \frac{6.0}{200}\right)^{2} - 1 \;=\; 1.03^{2} - 1 \;=\; 0.0609 \;=\; 6.090\%. $$

Step 3. Compare. $6.090\% > 6.062\%$, so Account B has the greater effective annual rate, even though it compounds less often, because its nominal rate is higher. To check with the GDC finance solver, enter N = 12, PV = -1, PMT = 0, P/Y = 12, C/Y = 12 with I% = 5.9 and read FV; the effective rate is FV minus 1. The same works for B with N = 2, I% = 6, P/Y = 2, C/Y = 2, using one year of compounding periods.

第 3 步：比较。$6.090\% > 6.062\%$，所以账户 B 的实际年利率更大；尽管它复利次数更少，但名义利率更高。用 GDC 金融求解器检验：输入 N = 12, PV = -1, PMT = 0, P/Y = 12, C/Y = 12 与 I% = 5.9，读出 FV；实际利率等于 FV 减 1。账户 B 同理：N = 2、I% = 6、P/Y = 2, C/Y = 2，即一年内的计息期数。

Depreciation折旧

Depreciation is compound growth with a negative rate: an asset loses a fixed percentage of its current value in each period. After $n$ years at $r\%$ per year, $V = V_{0}\left(1 - \frac{r}{100}\right)^{n}$. The value falls by a smaller amount each year because the percentage applies to a smaller number. With $k$ reductions per year the formula becomes $V = V_{0}\left(1 - \frac{r}{100k}\right)^{kn}$.

折旧（depreciation）就是增长率为负的复合变化：资产每个周期损失其当前价值的固定百分比。以每年 $r\%$ 计，$n$ 年后 $V = V_{0}\left(1 - \frac{r}{100}\right)^{n}$。由于百分比作用在越来越小的数上，每年下降的绝对值逐年变小。若每年折旧 $k$ 次，则 $V = V_{0}\left(1 - \frac{r}{100k}\right)^{kn}$。

Worked Example: depreciation of a vehicle例题：车辆的折旧

A delivery van costs 25000 USD new and loses $12\%$ of its value at the end of each year. (a) Find its value after 4 years. (b) After how many whole years is the value first below 10000 USD?一辆送货车新车价 25000 美元，每年年末损失其价值的 $12\%$。(a) 求 4 年后的价值。(b) 价值首次低于 10000 美元发生在第几个整年？

Step 1. Formula for (a). The yearly multiplier is $1 - 0.12 = 0.88$.

第 1 步：(a) 的公式。每年的乘数为 $1 - 0.12 = 0.88$。

$$ V \;=\; 25000 \times 0.88^{4} \;\approx\; 14992.38 \text{ USD}. $$

GDC finance solver (TVM). Enter N = 4, I% = -12, PV = -25000, PMT = 0, FV = ?, P/Y = 1, C/Y = 1. The GDC returns FV = 14992.38. A negative I% models a loss; if your GDC does not accept it, use the formula.

GDC 金融求解器（TVM）。输入 N = 4, I% = -12, PV = -25000, PMT = 0, FV = ?, P/Y = 1, C/Y = 1。GDC 给出 FV = 14992.38。负的 I% 表示损失；若你的 GDC 不接受负值，就用公式。

Step 2. Part (b). Solve $25000 \times 0.88^{n} < 10000$ with the GDC: graph $y = 25000 \times 0.88^{x}$ and $y = 10000$ and find the intersection, or build a table of values. The curves meet at $x \approx 7.17$. Because the value changes only at the end of each year, check the whole years on either side: after 7 years $25000 \times 0.88^{7} \approx 10216.89 > 10000$, after 8 years $25000 \times 0.88^{8} \approx 8990.86 < 10000$.

第 2 步：(b) 小问。用 GDC 解 $25000 \times 0.88^{n} < 10000$：画出 $y = 25000 \times 0.88^{x}$ 与 $y = 10000$ 求交点，或列数值表。两曲线交于 $x \approx 7.17$。由于价值只在每年年末变化，检查两侧的整年：7 年后 $25000 \times 0.88^{7} \approx 10216.89 > 10000$，8 年后 $25000 \times 0.88^{8} \approx 8990.86 < 10000$。

Conclusion. The van is first worth less than 10000 USD after 8 years.

结论。这辆车在第 8 年后价值首次低于 10000 美元。

Inflation and real value通货膨胀与实际价值

Inflation is a general rise in prices, usually quoted as a percentage per year. It uses the same compound model. A price that is $P$ today becomes $P\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^{n}$ after $n$ years of inflation at $r\%$. Because money buys less when prices rise, a sum of money $S$ received in $n$ years has a real value in today's prices of $S \div \left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^{n}$. Only the money is nominal; the real value says how much it can buy.

通货膨胀（inflation）指物价总体上升，通常以每年的百分比表示，用的仍是复合模型。今天价格为 $P$ 的物品，在年通胀率 $r\%$ 下 $n$ 年后价格为 $P\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^{n}$。物价上升时同样的钱买到的东西更少，所以 $n$ 年后收到的一笔钱 $S$，按今天价格计的实际价值（real value）为 $S \div \left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^{n}$。名义金额不变，实际价值说明它能买到多少东西。

Worked Example: inflation: a future price and a real value例题：通货膨胀：未来价格与实际价值

Inflation is $2.5\%$ per year. (a) A basket of goods costs 120 USD today. Find its price after 10 years. (b) Inflation is $3\%$ per year. Find the value in today's prices of 5000 USD received in 8 years.通货膨胀率为每年 $2.5\%$。(a) 一篮子商品今天价格为 120 美元，求 10 年后的价格。(b) 若通胀率为每年 $3\%$，求 8 年后收到的 5000 美元按今天价格计的价值。

Step 1. Part (a), growth.

第 1 步：(a) 小问，增长。

$$ 120 \times 1.025^{10} \;\approx\; 153.61 \text{ USD}. $$

GDC finance solver (TVM). Enter N = 10, I% = 2.5, PV = -120, PMT = 0, FV = ?, P/Y = 1, C/Y = 1. The GDC returns FV = 153.61.

GDC 金融求解器（TVM）。输入 N = 10, I% = 2.5, PV = -120, PMT = 0, FV = ?, P/Y = 1, C/Y = 1。GDC 给出 FV = 153.61。

Step 2. Part (b), real value. Divide by the growth in prices over 8 years.

第 2 步：(b) 小问，实际价值。除以 8 年中的物价增长倍数。

$$ \frac{5000}{1.03^{8}} \;\approx\; 3947.05 \text{ USD}. $$

GDC finance solver (TVM). Enter N = 8, I% = 3, PV = ?, PMT = 0, FV = 5000, P/Y = 1, C/Y = 1. The GDC returns PV = -3947.05. The negative sign only says that this is the amount you would pay in today's money; the real value is 3947.05 USD.

GDC 金融求解器（TVM）。输入 N = 8, I% = 3, PV = ?, PMT = 0, FV = 5000, P/Y = 1, C/Y = 1。GDC 给出 PV = -3947.05。负号只表示这是你今天要付出的金额；实际价值为 3947.05 美元。

Why this works. Prices are $1.03^{8}$ times higher in 8 years, so 5000 USD then buys what 3947.05 USD buys today.

原理。8 年后物价是现在的 $1.03^{8}$ 倍，所以那时的 5000 美元只能买到今天 3947.05 美元能买到的东西。

Common Mistake: dividing by $k$ but forgetting to multiply the exponent常见错误：除以 $k$ 却忘了乘指数 With compounding $k$ times a year, both parts change: the rate per period is $\frac{r}{100k}$ and the exponent is $kn$. Using $\left(1 + \frac{3.6}{1200}\right)^{5}$ for the 5-year monthly example computes only 5 months of interest. Also keep unrounded values in the GDC and round only the final money amount to 2 d.p.每年复利 $k$ 次时，两处都要改：每期利率是 $\frac{r}{100k}$，并且指数是 $kn$。在 5 年按月复利的例子里写成 $\left(1 + \frac{3.6}{1200}\right)^{5}$，只算了 5 个月的利息。另外 GDC 中保留未舍入的值，只对最终金额取 2 位小数。

写出代入数值的公式以及你在金融求解器中输入的值（N、I%、PV、PMT、FV、P/Y、C/Y），这样即使某个输入有误，你的方法也能被看清。写明货币单位；除非题目另有要求，金额只在最后取 2 位小数。并检查答案是否合理：复利增长的余额必须大于本金，折旧后的价值必须小于购买价。

After six months the interest earned is added to the balance, so the second half-year earns interest on that interest as well; this extra growth makes the yearly rate larger than the nominal rate.

$\left(1 + \frac{4}{200}\right)^{2} - 1 = 1.02^{2} - 1 = 0.0404$，所以实际年利率为 $4.04\%$。半年后赚到的利息并入余额，下半年就在这笔利息上再产生利息；这部分额外增长使年利率大于名义利率。

年乘数为 $0.85$。$V = 18000 \times 0.85^{3} = 18000 \times 0.614125 = 11054.25$ 美元。TVM：N = 3, I% = -15, PV = -18000, PMT = 0, P/Y = 1, C/Y = 1 得 FV = 11054.25。

The amount is still 1000 USD, but prices are $1.02^{5} \approx 1.1041$ times higher, so it buys less. Real value $= \frac{1000}{1.02^{5}} \approx 905.73$ USD in today's prices.

金额仍是 1000 美元，但物价是现在的 $1.02^{5} \approx 1.1041$ 倍，所以买到的东西变少。实际价值 $= \frac{1000}{1.02^{5}} \approx 905.73$ 美元（按今天价格）。

The seven entries七个输入项

The finance solver (the TVM app, short for time value of money) on the GDC solves one equation that links seven values. You fill in all but one and the GDC solves for the missing one. Menu names differ between models, but the entries are the same.

GDC 上的金融求解器（finance solver，即 TVM 应用，TVM 是货币时间价值 time value of money 的缩写）求解一个联系七个量的方程。你填入除一个以外的所有值，GDC 求出缺失的那一个。不同型号的菜单名称不同，但输入项相同。

Payments are made at the end of each period (END mode) in this unit. When P/Y and C/Y are equal to $m$, the solver is working with the rate per period $i = \frac{I\%}{100m}$ and the equation

本单元的付款发生在每期的期末（END 模式）。当 P/Y 与 C/Y 都等于 $m$ 时，求解器使用每期利率 $i = \frac{I\%}{100m}$ 以及方程

这就是下面每个例子的公式法。三个金额项之和必须为零，这正是符号如此重要的原因。

Sign convention for money in and out资金流入与流出的符号约定

Sign convention符号约定

Money you pay out is negative; money you receive is positive. Decide the point of view first (yours, not the bank's) and keep it for all of PV, PMT and FV.

你付出的钱为负，你收到的钱为正。先确定视角（你的视角，不是银行的视角），并在 PV、PMT、FV 上始终保持一致。

  • Lump-sum investment: you pay in now, PV negative, PMT = 0, and you receive the result, so FV is positive.
  • 一次性投资：你现在存入，PV 为负，PMT = 0，期末收回，所以 FV 为正。
  • Regular savings plan: you pay in every period, PMT negative (and PV negative or 0), and you receive the total at the end, FV positive.
  • 定期储蓄计划：你每期存入，PMT 为负（PV 为负或 0），期末收回总额，FV 为正。
  • Loan: you receive the loan now, PV positive, you pay each repayment, PMT negative, and the loan ends fully repaid, FV = 0.
  • 贷款：你现在收到贷款，PV 为正；每期还款，PMT 为负；贷款最终还清，FV = 0。
  • Withdrawal annuity: you pay a fund in now, PV negative, and then receive regular payments, PMT positive, with FV = 0 if the fund is used up.
  • 取款年金：你现在向基金存入，PV 为负；之后定期收到款项，PMT 为正；基金用完时 FV = 0。

Loan and amortization. A loan of $PV$ is repaid by equal repayments at the end of each period for $N$ periods. Amortization means that every repayment pays the interest owed for that period first, and the rest reduces the balance. With rate per period $i = \frac{r}{100k}$, $$ PMT \;=\; \frac{PV\,i}{1 - (1+i)^{-N}}, \qquad \text{interest} = \text{balance} \times i, \qquad \text{principal} = PMT - \text{interest}. $$ The balance after $k$ payments is $PV(1+i)^{k} - PMT\,\frac{(1+i)^{k} - 1}{i}$, and the total interest over the loan is $N \times PMT - PV$.
贷款与分期摊还。贷款（loan）$PV$ 通过在每期期末等额还款（repayment）、共 $N$ 期还清。分期摊还（amortization）指每次还款先付清该期应付的利息，余下部分用来减少余额。设每期利率 $i = \frac{r}{100k}$，则 $$ PMT \;=\; \frac{PV\,i}{1 - (1+i)^{-N}}, \qquad \text{interest} = \text{balance} \times i, \qquad \text{principal} = PMT - \text{interest}. $$ 还款 $k$ 次后的余额为 $PV(1+i)^{k} - PMT\,\frac{(1+i)^{k} - 1}{i}$，贷款全期总利息为 $N \times PMT - PV$。

Annuity. An annuity is a sequence of equal payments made at equal time intervals. In this unit every payment is at the end of its period. Two situations use the same equation:

Savings plan. You pay $PMT$ into an account each period. With rate per period $i$ and $N$ payments, $FV = PMT\,\dfrac{(1+i)^{N} - 1}{i}$ (plus $PV(1+i)^{N}$ if you also start with a lump sum).

Withdrawal annuity. A fund $PV$ pays you $PMT$ each period until it is used up: $PMT = \dfrac{PV\,i}{1 - (1+i)^{-N}}$, the same formula as for a loan, with you in the role of the bank.
年金。年金（annuity）是在等间隔的时间点上发生的一系列等额付款。本单元的每次付款都在其周期的期末。两种情形用同一个方程：

储蓄计划。你每期向账户存入 $PMT$。设每期利率为 $i$、共 $N$ 次存款，则 $FV = PMT\,\dfrac{(1+i)^{N} - 1}{i}$（若还有起始的一次性存款，再加 $PV(1+i)^{N}$）。

取款年金。基金 $PV$ 每期支付你 $PMT$，直到用完：$PMT = \dfrac{PV\,i}{1 - (1+i)^{-N}}$，与贷款的公式相同，只是你扮演银行的角色。

System of linear equations. A system of linear equations (simultaneous equations) in three unknowns $x$, $y$, $z$ has the form $$ a_{1}x + b_{1}y + c_{1}z = d_{1}, \qquad a_{2}x + b_{2}y + c_{2}z = d_{2}, \qquad a_{3}x + b_{3}y + c_{3}z = d_{3}. $$ A GDC solves it from the nine coefficients and three constants. Three outcomes are possible: exactly one solution, no solution, or infinitely many solutions.

Polynomial equation. A polynomial equation such as $x^{3} + x^{2} - 5x + 1 = 0$ rarely factorises. The GDC finds all real roots from the coefficients (polynomial solver), or from the graph of $y = p(x)$ (zeros), or from the intersection of $y = p(x)$ with a horizontal line.
线性方程组。含三个未知数 $x$、$y$、$z$ 的线性方程组（联立方程）形如 $$ a_{1}x + b_{1}y + c_{1}z = d_{1}, \qquad a_{2}x + b_{2}y + c_{2}z = d_{2}, \qquad a_{3}x + b_{3}y + c_{3}z = d_{3}. $$ GDC 根据九个系数和三个常数求解。可能出现三种结果：恰有一个解、无解、有无穷多解。

多项式方程。像 $x^{3} + x^{2} - 5x + 1 = 0$ 这样的多项式方程很少能因式分解。GDC 可以由系数（多项式求解器）、由 $y = p(x)$ 的图像（零点），或由 $y = p(x)$ 与某条水平线的交点求出所有实根。

When there is no unique solution没有唯一解的情形

If the GDC reports an error, "no unique solution", or shows a row of zeros in a reduced form, the system does not have exactly one solution. Compare $x + y + z = 6$, $x + 2y + 3z = 14$ and $2x + 3y + 4z = c$. Adding the first two equations gives $2x + 3y + 4z = 20$ for every solution. If $c = 21$ the third equation contradicts this, so there is no solution. If $c = 20$ the third equation says nothing new, so there are infinitely many solutions: $z = t$, $y = 8 - 2t$, $x = t - 2$ for every real $t$ (for example $t = 3$ gives $x = 1$, $y = 2$, $z = 3$, and $1 + 2 + 3 = 6$, $1 + 4 + 9 = 14$, $2 + 6 + 12 = 20$).

若 GDC 报错、提示"无唯一解"，或在简化形式中出现一行全零，则方程组不是恰有一个解。比较 $x + y + z = 6$，$x + 2y + 3z = 14$，$2x + 3y + 4z = c$。前两式相加，对每个解都有 $2x + 3y + 4z = 20$。若 $c = 21$，第三个方程与此矛盾，所以无解。若 $c = 20$，第三个方程没有提供新信息，所以有无穷多解：对每个实数 $t$，$z = t$，$y = 8 - 2t$，$x = t - 2$（例如 $t = 3$ 给出 $x = 1$，$y = 2$，$z = 3$，且 $1 + 2 + 3 = 6$，$1 + 4 + 9 = 14$，$2 + 6 + 12 = 20$）。

How to use this guide本指南使用说明

B3 covers the 2021 sub-topics SL 1.4 (compound interest and depreciation), SL 1.7 (amortization and annuities with technology) and SL 1.8 (technology for systems of linear equations and polynomial equations). The sections build on each other: compound growth (B3.1) is the engine behind the finance solver (B3.2), which then solves loans (B3.3) and annuities (B3.4); B3.5 turns to the other tools of the GDC. Every example shows the formula route and the GDC route side by side.

B3 涵盖 2021 年大纲子主题 SL 1.4（复利与折旧）、SL 1.7（借助技术工具的分期摊还与年金）和 SL 1.8（借助技术工具解线性方程组与多项式方程）。各节层层递进：复合增长（B3.1）是金融求解器（B3.2）的基础，求解器接着处理贷款（B3.3）与年金（B3.4）；B3.5 转向 GDC 的其他工具。每个例子都并列给出公式法与 GDC 方法。

Memorise the seven finance-solver entries and the sign rule (money out negative, money in positive). Know the three set-ups: lump sum (PMT = 0), loan (PV positive, FV = 0) and savings plan (PV = 0, PMT negative). Practise entering a three-equation system without a sign slip.

背熟金融求解器的七个输入项和符号规则（流出为负，流入为正）。掌握三种设定：一次性存款（PMT = 0）、贷款（PV 为正，FV = 0）和储蓄计划（PV = 0，PMT 为负）。练习输入三元方程组而不出现符号失误。
