Topic 3.1主题 3.1

Representing Two Quantitative Variables: Scatterplots表示两个定量变量：散点图

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Identify the explanatory and response variables and place them on the correct axes.
  • Construct a scatterplot of paired quantitative data.
  • Describe a scatterplot completely and in context: form, direction, strength, and unusual features.
  • Justify a claim about a relationship using a scatterplot.
  • 识别解释变量与响应变量，并放在正确的坐标轴上。
  • 为成对的定量数据作散点图。
  • 结合情境完整描述散点图：形式、方向、强度和异常特征。
  • 用散点图为关于变量关系的说法提供依据。

Bivariate quantitative data consist of ordered pairs $(x,y)$ recorded on the same individuals. The explanatory variable $x$ is the variable used to explain or predict; the response variable $y$ is the outcome being explained. A scatterplot puts the explanatory variable on the horizontal axis and the response on the vertical axis, with one point per individual. Which variable is which depends on the question asked, not on the data alone.

二元定量数据由同一批个体上记录的有序数对 $(x,y)$ 组成。解释变量 $x$ 用来解释或预测；响应变量 $y$ 是被解释的结果。散点图把解释变量放在横轴、响应变量放在纵轴，每个个体对应一个点。哪个是解释变量取决于所问的问题，而不只取决于数据本身。

Describe every scatterplot with four features用四个特征描述每张散点图
  • Form: linear or nonlinear (curved). A relationship is linear if the points follow a straight band, that is, the rate of change is roughly constant.
  • Direction: positive if $y$ tends to increase as $x$ increases, negative if $y$ tends to decrease.
  • Strength: how closely the points follow the form: strong, moderate or weak.
  • Unusual features: outliers (points far from the pattern), clusters, and gaps.

Write in context with "tends to" language, naming both variables with units.

  • 形式：线性或非线性（弯曲）。点沿一条直带分布，即变化率大致恒定，则为线性。
  • 方向：$x$ 增大时 $y$ 倾向增大为正向，倾向减小为负向。
  • 强度：点与该形式的贴合程度：强、中等或弱。
  • 异常特征：离群点（远离整体模式的点）、聚类与间隙。

AP exam tipAP 考试提示

Never write only "positive linear". Always add the context ("students who study more hours tend to score higher") and mention outliers, clusters or gaps, or say there are none. Use the word "association", not "correlation", unless you are discussing the number $r$.

不要只写“正向线性”。要加上情境（“每周学习更久的学生倾向得分更高”），并说明有无离群点、聚类或间隙。除非讨论数值 $r$，否则用“关联”而不是“相关”。

Common mistakes常见错误
  • Putting the response variable on the horizontal axis (it is the explanatory variable that goes on $x$).
  • Calling a tight curved pattern "weak". Strength measures how closely points follow the stated form, so a tight curve is a strong nonlinear association.
  • Describing direction for a U-shaped pattern as "positive" or "negative". Say the form is nonlinear and describe where it decreases and increases.
  • Using the word "line" as the reason for linearity (for example "it is linear because it makes a line"). Say that the rate of change is roughly constant.
  • 把响应变量放在横轴（应由解释变量作 $x$）。
  • 把紧密的弯曲模式叫作“弱”。强度度量点与所述形式的贴合程度，紧密的曲线是强的非线性关联。
  • 对 U 形模式说“正向”或“负向”。应指出形式是非线性，并说明在哪里下降、在哪里上升。
  • 用“line”本身来解释线性（如“因为它形成一条线所以是线性”）。应说明变化率大致恒定。

Topic 3.2主题 3.2

Correlation相关系数

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Interpret the correlation coefficient $r$ as the strength and direction of a linear association.
  • Calculate $r$ from standardized values and with technology, and state its properties.
  • Explain why $r$ does not describe nonlinear patterns, is affected by outliers, and does not prove causation.
  • Describe how changing units or switching $x$ and $y$ affects $r$.
  • 把相关系数 $r$ 解释为线性关联的强度与方向。
  • 用标准化值和技术手段计算 $r$，并说出它的性质。
  • 解释为什么 $r$ 不能描述非线性模式、受离群点影响、且不能证明因果。
  • 说明改变单位或交换 $x$、$y$ 对 $r$ 的影响。

The correlation coefficient $r$ measures the direction and strength of the linear association between two quantitative variables. It is the average of the products of the standardized values, using $n-1$ in the denominator:

相关系数 $r$ 度量两个定量变量之间线性关联的方向与强度。它是标准化值乘积的平均（分母用 $n-1$）：

$$r=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\frac{x_i-\bar{x}}{s_x}\right)\left(\frac{y_i-\bar{y}}{s_y}\right)$$
Property of $r$$r$ 的性质What it means含义
$-1\le r\le1$; the sign gives direction$-1\le r\le1$；符号表示方向Positive $r$: positive association. Negative $r$: negative association.$r>0$ 为正向关联，$r<0$ 为负向关联。
$|r|$ near 1 means strong linear association$|r|$ 接近 1 表示线性关联强$r=\pm1$ only when all points lie exactly on a line; $r=0$ means no linear association.只有所有点恰好在一条直线上时 $r=\pm1$；$r=0$ 表示无线性关联。
Unit-free无单位Changing units (feet to meters, dollars to thousands of dollars) or adding a constant does not change $r$.改变单位（英尺换成米，元换成千元）或加常数，$r$ 不变。
Symmetric in $x$ and $y$$x$ 与 $y$ 对称Swapping the variables gives the same $r$ (but a different regression line).交换两个变量，$r$ 不变（但回归线会变）。
Measures only linear association只度量线性关联A strong curved pattern can have $r$ near 0.很强的弯曲模式也可能 $r$ 接近 0。
Not resistant不具有抗性One outlier can raise or lower $r$ sharply (see Topic 3.6).一个离群点就能使 $r$ 大幅升降（见主题 3.6）。

Worked Example 2.9: r near 0 does not mean no relationship; r does not depend on units例题 2.9：$r$ 接近 0 不代表没有关系；$r$ 与单位无关

Part A. The five points $(-2,4),(-1,1),(0,0),(1,1),(2,4)$ lie exactly on $y=x^2$.

  1. $\bar{x}=0$ and $\bar{y}=2$. The products $(x-\bar{x})(y-\bar{y})$ are $(-2)(2)=-4$, $(-1)(-1)=1$, $(0)(-2)=0$, $(1)(-1)=-1$, $(2)(2)=4$.
  2. They sum to $-4+1+0-1+4=0$, so $r=0$ exactly.
  3. Yet $y$ is perfectly determined by $x$. Zero correlation rules out a linear association only. Always look at the scatterplot first.

Part B. The asking prices in Example 2.8 are converted from thousands of dollars to dollars ($y^*=1000y$), and ages from years to months ($x^*=12x$).

  1. Multiplying by positive constants rescales $s_x$ and $s_y$ by the same factors, so every standardized value $z$ is unchanged.
  2. Therefore $r$ is still $-0.976$. If the price were measured as a discount below a list price, so that the sign of $y$ flips ($y^*=-y$), then $r$ would change sign to $+0.976$.

甲部分。五个点 $(-2,4),(-1,1),(0,0),(1,1),(2,4)$ 恰好落在 $y=x^2$ 上。

  1. $\bar{x}=0$，$\bar{y}=2$。乘积 $(x-\bar{x})(y-\bar{y})$ 依次为 $(-2)(2)=-4$，$(-1)(-1)=1$，$(0)(-2)=0$，$(1)(-1)=-1$，$(2)(2)=4$。
  2. 它们之和为 $-4+1+0-1+4=0$，所以 $r=0$。
  3. 但 $y$ 完全由 $x$ 决定。零相关只排除了线性关联。一定要先看散点图。

乙部分。把例题 2.8 中的标价由千美元换成美元（$y^*=1000y$），车龄由年换成月（$x^*=12x$）。

  1. 乘以正常数后，$s_x$ 与 $s_y$ 按相同倍数缩放，所以每个标准化值 $z$ 都不变。
  2. 因此 $r$ 仍为 $-0.976$。如果把价格改为低于标价的折扣，使 $y$ 变号（$y^*=-y$），则 $r$ 变号为 $+0.976$。
Correlation does not imply causation相关不等于因果

A strong association can come from a third variable. Across coastal towns, ice cream sales and sunburn cases may have a high positive correlation because hot, sunny weather increases both. This variable is a confounding (lurking) variable. Only a well-designed randomized experiment can support a cause-and-effect conclusion; an observational correlation cannot, however large $|r|$ is. Reserve the word "correlation" for two quantitative variables.

很强的关联可能来自第三个变量。在沿海城镇中，冰淇淋销量与晒伤人数可能高度正相关，因为炎热晴朗的天气同时增加两者。这个变量是混杂（潜在）变量。只有设计良好的随机化实验才能支持因果结论；无论 $|r|$ 多大，观察性相关都不能。“相关”一词只用于两个定量变量。

Topic 3.3主题 3.3

Linear Regression Models线性回归模型

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Write a linear regression model $\hat{y}=a+bx$ and identify $a$ (intercept) and $b$ (slope).
  • Interpret the slope and the y-intercept in context, using "predicted".
  • Predict a response value, and distinguish interpolation from extrapolation.
  • Explain why the intercept sometimes has no sensible interpretation.
  • 写出线性回归模型 $\hat{y}=a+bx$，并指出 $a$（截距）与 $b$（斜率）。
  • 结合情境解释斜率与 $y$ 截距，并使用“预测”一词。
  • 预测响应变量的值，并区分内插与外推。
  • 解释截距为什么有时没有合理的含义。

If a scatterplot is roughly linear, we can use a linear regression model to predict the response from the explanatory variable:

如果散点图大致呈线性，就可以用线性回归模型由解释变量预测响应变量：

$$\hat{y}=a+bx$$

Here $\hat{y}$ ("y-hat") is the predicted value of the response, $x$ is the explanatory value, $b$ is the slope (the predicted change in $y$ for a one-unit increase in $x$) and $a$ is the y-intercept (the predicted value of $y$ when $x=0$). The line is fitted by the least-squares method of Topic 3.5. For the data of Table 2.2 technology gives $\hat{y}=51.21+3.694x$.

其中 $\hat{y}$（读作“y 帽”）是响应变量的预测值，$x$ 是解释变量的值，$b$ 是斜率（$x$ 增加一个单位时 $y$ 的预测变化量），$a$ 是 y 截距（$x=0$ 时 $y$ 的预测值）。这条直线用主题 3.5 的最小二乘法拟合。对表 2.2 的数据，技术手段给出 $\hat{y}=51.21+3.694x$。

Interpretation templates解释模板
  • Slope: "For each additional [unit of $x$], the predicted [response] increases (decreases) by about $|b|$ [units of $y$]."
  • Intercept: "When [$x$ is 0], the predicted [response] is about $a$ [units of $y$]." Then say whether $x=0$ is within the data and whether this makes sense.

Always include context, units, and the word "predicted" (or "on average"). The model describes a trend, not an exact rule for every individual.

  • 斜率：“[$x$ 的单位] 每增加一个，[响应变量] 的预测值增加（减少）约 $|b|$ [$y$ 的单位]。”
  • 截距：“当 [$x$ 为 0] 时，[响应变量] 的预测值约为 $a$ [$y$ 的单位]。”然后说明 $x=0$ 是否在数据范围内、这个值是否合理。

一定要写情境、单位和“预测”（或“平均”）。模型描述的是趋势，不是对每个个体都精确成立的规则。

A residual is the prediction error for one individual:

残差是单个个体的预测误差：

$$\text{residual}=e=y-\hat{y}=\text{observed}-\text{predicted}$$

A positive residual means the point is above the line: the model underpredicts (underestimates) the response. A negative residual means the point is below the line: the model overpredicts (overestimates). Residuals have the units of $y$. For a least-squares line with an intercept, the residuals always sum to zero (apart from rounding), so a zero sum is not evidence of a good fit.

正残差表示点在直线上方：模型低估了响应变量。负残差表示点在直线下方：模型高估了响应变量。残差的单位与 $y$ 相同。对含截距的最小二乘线，残差之和总为 0（舍入误差除外），所以和为零并不能说明拟合良好。

Common mistakes常见错误
  • Computing predicted minus observed. This reverses the sign and the meaning (over/underprediction).
  • Saying a residual of $-6.6$ means the student is "6.6% below". Residuals are in the units of $y$, not percentages.
  • Concluding the model is good because the residuals sum to 0 (they always do).
  • Calling a residual plot "random" when it has an obvious curve, or "bad" because a single point is large.
  • Judging linearity from $r$ alone.
  • 算成预测值减观测值。这会使符号和含义（高估/低估）反过来。
  • 说残差 $-6.6$ 表示学生“低 6.6%”。残差的单位与 $y$ 相同，不是百分比。
  • 因残差和为 0 就认为模型好（残差和总是 0）。
  • 把有明显弯曲的残差图说成“随机”，或因某一个点较大就说“不好”。

The least-squares regression line (LSRL) is the line $\hat{y}=a+bx$ that makes the sum of the squared residuals, $\sum(y-\hat{y})^2$, as small as possible. Squaring stops positive and negative residuals from cancelling and penalizes large errors heavily. Technology finds $a$ and $b$, but you must know the relationships among the summary statistics:

最小二乘回归线（LSRL）是使残差平方和 $\sum(y-\hat{y})^2$ 最小的直线 $\hat{y}=a+bx$。平方使正负残差不会相互抵消，并重罚大的误差。$a$ 和 $b$ 由技术手段求得，但你必须掌握汇总统计量之间的关系：

$$b=r\,\frac{s_y}{s_x}\qquad a=\bar{y}-b\,\bar{x}\qquad \text{the line passes through }(\bar{x},\bar{y})$$

For the same data, the sum of squared residuals is $\sum(y-\hat{y})^2=162.41$ and the total variation in $y$ is $\sum(y-\bar{y})^2=(n-1)s_y^2=9(9.539)^2=818.9$.

  3. $s=\sqrt{162.41/(10-2)}=\sqrt{20.30}=4.506$ points. The divisor is $n-2$ because two parameters ($a$ and $b$) were estimated from the data.
  4. Compare with $s_y=9.539$: knowing the study time reduces the typical prediction error from about 9.5 points to about 4.5 points.

Preview: inference for the slope (a later unit)预告：斜率的推断（后续单元）

Transformations to achieve linearity用变换使数据线性化

If the scatterplot is curved and the residual plot of a straight-line fit is curved, transform a variable so the pattern becomes straight, fit a line to the transformed data, and check the new residual plot.

  • Exponential model $y=a\,b^{x}$ (growth or decay by a constant factor): $\ln y$ against $x$ is linear. Fit $\widehat{\ln y}=a^*+b^*x$, then $\hat{y}=e^{a^*}\left(e^{b^*}\right)^x$.
  • Power model $y=a\,x^{p}$: $\ln y$ against $\ln x$ is linear. Fit $\widehat{\ln y}=a^*+p\ln x$, then $\hat{y}=e^{a^*}x^{p}$.

Common logarithms (base 10) work the same way. The linear model is chosen because its residual plot has no pattern, not because $r$ is large.

若散点图弯曲，且直线拟合的残差图也弯曲，就对某个变量作变换使模式变直，对变换后的数据拟合直线，并检查新的残差图。

  • 指数模型 $y=a\,b^{x}$（按恒定倍数增长或衰减）：$\ln y$ 对 $x$ 呈线性。拟合 $\widehat{\ln y}=a^*+b^*x$，再得 $\hat{y}=e^{a^*}\left(e^{b^*}\right)^x$。
  • 幂函数模型 $y=a\,x^{p}$：$\ln y$ 对 $\ln x$ 呈线性。拟合 $\widehat{\ln y}=a^*+p\ln x$，再得 $\hat{y}=e^{a^*}x^{p}$。

常用对数（以 10 为底）用法相同。选择线性模型的依据是残差图没有模式，而不是 $r$ 很大。

For an unusual point, name the type and describe the effect with numbers or direction: "This point has high leverage (its $x$ is far from the others) and lies far below the pattern, so it is influential: it pulls the slope down and lowers $r$." Never recommend simply deleting a point; say to investigate it and compare the line with and without it. When you transform, justify the choice with the residual plot.

谈到异常点时，要指明类型并用数值或方向说明影响：“该点杠杆高（$x$ 远离其他点）且远低于整体模式，所以是强影响点：它把斜率拉低并降低 $r$。”不要简单地建议删除；应说明先调查，并比较含与不含该点的直线。作变换时，要用残差图说明选择的理由。

Common mistakes常见错误
  • Saying every outlier is influential, or every high-leverage point changes the line. A high-leverage point on the trend changes it little.
  • Deleting a point only because it is an outlier, with no investigation.
  • Choosing a transformation because $r$ is larger, with no residual plot.
  • Forgetting to back-transform: $\ln\hat{y}=7.8$ is not a predicted count.
  • Interpreting the slope of a $\ln y$ model as an additive change in $y$. It is a multiplicative change: $y$ is multiplied by $e^{b^*}$.
  • 说每个离群点都是强影响点，或每个高杠杆点都会改变直线。位于趋势上的高杠杆点对直线影响很小。
  • 仅仅因为是离群点就删除，不作调查。
  • 因为 $r$ 更大就选择某个变换，却不看残差图。
  • 忘记反变换：$\ln\hat{y}=7.8$ 不是预测的数量。
  • 把 $\ln y$ 模型的斜率解释成 $y$ 的加性变化。它是乘性变化：$y$ 乘以 $e^{b^*}$。

Formulas and facts公式与要点
  • Conditional relative frequency $=$ cell count $/$ total of the given row or column; joint uses the grand total.
  • $r=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum z_xz_y$, $-1\le r\le1$, unit-free, symmetric, not resistant.
  • $\hat{y}=a+bx$; $b=r\,s_y/s_x$; $a=\bar{y}-b\bar{x}$; the line passes through $(\bar{x},\bar{y})$.
  • Residual $=y-\hat{y}$ (observed minus predicted); the residuals sum to 0.
  • $r^2=1-\sum(y-\hat{y})^2/\sum(y-\bar{y})^2$; $s=\sqrt{\sum(y-\hat{y})^2/(n-2)}$.
  • Exponential: $\ln y$ vs $x$ linear. Power: $\ln y$ vs $\ln x$ linear.
  • 条件相对频率 $=$ 单元格计数 $/$ 所给行或列的合计；联合相对频率用总计作分母。
  • $r=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum z_xz_y$，$-1\le r\le1$，无单位，对称，不具抗性。
  • $\hat{y}=a+bx$；$b=r\,s_y/s_x$；$a=\bar{y}-b\bar{x}$；直线经过 $(\bar{x},\bar{y})$。
  • 残差 $=y-\hat{y}$（观测减预测）；残差之和为 0。
  • $r^2=1-\sum(y-\hat{y})^2/\sum(y-\bar{y})^2$；$s=\sqrt{\sum(y-\hat{y})^2/(n-2)}$。
  • 指数：$\ln y$ 对 $x$ 线性。幂函数：$\ln y$ 对 $\ln x$ 线性。
