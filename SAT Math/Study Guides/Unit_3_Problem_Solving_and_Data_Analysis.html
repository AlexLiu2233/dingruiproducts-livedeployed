Ratios, Rates, Proportional Relationships and Units比、率、正比例关系与单位

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define a ratio and a rate and write each as a fraction with units.
  • Identify a proportional relationship from a table, an equation or a graph.
  • Calculate the constant of proportionality $k$ in $y=kx$ and use it to predict.
  • Divide a total in a given ratio.
  • Carry out a unit conversion by dimensional analysis, cancelling units.
  • Interpret a rate in context and state its units.
  • 定义比 (ratio) 与率 (rate)，并把它们写成带单位的分数。
  • 从表格、方程或图像判断是否为正比例关系 (proportional relationship)。
  • 计算比例常数 (constant of proportionality) $k$（在 $y=kx$ 中）并用于预测。
  • 按给定的比分配总量。
  • 用量纲分析 (dimensional analysis) 进行单位换算 (unit conversion)，约去单位。
  • 结合情境解释率并写出单位。

Ratios and rates比与率

A ratio compares two quantities by division, such as $3:7$ or $\dfrac{3}{7}$. A rate is a ratio of two quantities with different units, such as $\dfrac{18\text{ acres}}{45\text{ minutes}}$. A unit rate has a denominator of 1, here $0.4$ acres per minute. The units are part of the answer: a rate with the wrong units is a wrong rate.

比 (ratio) 用除法比较两个量，例如 $3:7$ 或 $\dfrac{3}{7}$。率 (rate) 是两个不同单位的量之比，例如 $\dfrac{18\text{ acres}}{45\text{ minutes}}$。单位率 (unit rate) 的分母为 1，这里是每分钟 $0.4$ 英亩。单位是答案的一部分：单位错了，率就错了。

Proportional relationships and the constant of proportionality正比例关系与比例常数

Two quantities have a proportional relationship when $y=kx$ for one fixed number $k$. That number is the constant of proportionality, and $k=\dfrac{y}{x}$ for every pair in the table. The graph is a straight line through the origin $(0,0)$ whose slope is $k$. If the ratio $\dfrac{y}{x}$ changes from row to row, or the line misses the origin, the relationship is not proportional.

当 $y=kx$ 对某个固定的数 $k$ 成立时，两个量成正比例关系 (proportional relationship)。这个数就是比例常数 (constant of proportionality)，表中每一对数都满足 $k=\dfrac{y}{x}$。图像是过原点 $(0,0)$ 的直线，斜率为 $k$。如果各行的 $\dfrac{y}{x}$ 在变，或直线不过原点，就不是正比例关系。

Worked Example: a constant scanning rate例题：恒定的扫描速率

A fictional survey drone scans 18 acres in 45 minutes at a constant rate. (a) Find the rate in acres per minute. (b) How many acres does it scan in 2 hours? (c) How long does it take to scan 30 acres?

  1. Rate: $k=\dfrac{18}{45}=0.4$ acres per minute, so $y=0.4x$ with $x$ in minutes.
  2. Two hours is $120$ minutes: $y=0.4\times120=48$ acres.
  3. For 30 acres: $30=0.4x$, so $x=\dfrac{30}{0.4}=75$ minutes.

Why it works: the relationship is proportional, so $y\div x$ stays $0.4$; Figure 1 shows the same line.

一架虚构的测绘无人机以恒定速率在 45 分钟内扫描 18 英亩。(a) 求每分钟扫描的英亩数。(b) 2 小时扫描多少英亩？(c) 扫描 30 英亩需要多长时间？

  1. 速率：$k=\dfrac{18}{45}=0.4$ 英亩每分钟，所以 $y=0.4x$，其中 $x$ 以分钟计。
  2. 2 小时是 $120$ 分钟：$y=0.4\times120=48$ 英亩。
  3. 扫描 30 英亩：$30=0.4x$，所以 $x=\dfrac{30}{0.4}=75$ 分钟。

为什么可行：关系是正比例，所以 $y\div x$ 始终是 $0.4$；图 1 画的就是这条直线。

Worked Example: dividing a total in a ratio例题：按比分配总量

Red and blue paint are mixed in the ratio $3:7$ to make 40 liters. How many liters of each?

  1. Total parts: $3+7=10$.
  2. One part is $\dfrac{40}{10}=4$ liters.
  3. Red: $3\times4=12$ liters. Blue: $7\times4=28$ liters.

Check: $12+28=40$ and $\dfrac{12}{28}=\dfrac{3}{7}$.

红漆与蓝漆按 $3:7$ 混合，共 40 升。各需多少升？

  1. 总份数：$3+7=10$。
  2. 每份是 $\dfrac{40}{10}=4$ 升。
  3. 红漆：$3\times4=12$ 升。蓝漆：$7\times4=28$ 升。

检验：$12+28=40$，且 $\dfrac{12}{28}=\dfrac{3}{7}$。

Unit conversion and dimensional analysis单位换算与量纲分析

Unit conversion multiplies by a conversion factor, a fraction equal to 1 such as $\dfrac{1000\text{ m}}{1\text{ km}}$. In dimensional analysis you write each factor so the unwanted unit sits on the opposite side of the fraction bar and cancels. Whatever units are left must be the units the question asks for. For squared or cubed units, convert each length factor, so $1\text{ m}^2=100^2\text{ cm}^2$.

单位换算 (unit conversion) 就是乘以换算因子 (conversion factor)，即值为 1 的分数，例如 $\dfrac{1000\text{ m}}{1\text{ km}}$。在量纲分析 (dimensional analysis) 中，把每个因子写成让不需要的单位处在分数线另一侧而被约去；剩下的单位必须是题目所问的单位。面积或体积单位要对每个长度因子都换算，所以 $1\text{ m}^2=100^2\text{ cm}^2$。

Worked Example: kilometers per hour to meters per second例题：千米每小时换算为米每秒

A cyclist averages 27 km per hour. (a) Convert to meters per second. (b) How many seconds does 450 m take?

  1. $27\dfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}\times\dfrac{1000\text{ m}}{1\text{ km}}\times\dfrac{1\text{ h}}{3600\text{ s}}=\dfrac{27000}{3600}\dfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}=7.5\dfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}$. The km and h cancel.
  2. Time $=\dfrac{450\text{ m}}{7.5\text{ m/s}}=60$ s.

Why it works: each factor equals 1, so the speed is unchanged; only the units change.

一名骑行者平均速度为 27 千米每小时。(a) 换算成米每秒。(b) 行驶 450 米需要多少秒？

  1. $27\dfrac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}\times\dfrac{1000\text{ m}}{1\text{ km}}\times\dfrac{1\text{ h}}{3600\text{ s}}=\dfrac{27000}{3600}\dfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}=7.5\dfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}$。km 与 h 被约去。
  2. 时间 $=\dfrac{450\text{ m}}{7.5\text{ m/s}}=60$ 秒。

为什么可行：每个因子都等于 1，速度本身不变，变的只是单位。

Common Mistake: flipping a conversion factor

Convert $27$ km per hour to meters per second. If the length factor is flipped, you get $27\times\dfrac{1\text{ km}}{1000\text{ m}}\times\dfrac{1\text{ h}}{3600\text{ s}}=0.0000075$, and the units left over (km per m, h and s) are not meters per second. The correct setup gives $7.5$ m/s. A correct setup leaves exactly the requested units and nothing else.

常见错误：把换算因子写反

把 $27$ 千米每小时换算成米每秒。如果把长度因子写反，就得到 $27\times\dfrac{1\text{ km}}{1000\text{ m}}\times\dfrac{1\text{ h}}{3600\text{ s}}=0.0000075$，剩下的单位（千米每米、小时和秒）并不是米每秒。正确的算式得到 $7.5$ 米每秒。正确的算式只留下题目要求的单位，不剩别的。

Exam tip考试提示

Questions in context are about 30% of the digital SAT Math section, so many ratio and rate items are set in context. Underline the unit the question asks for before you calculate (minutes or hours, meters or kilometers) and end with a units check. If you enter a student-produced response, it has a single entry field of up to six characters, a leading negative sign is allowed, and a fraction is typed with a slash; a result such as $7.5$ is fine as it stands. A question with no solution or several solutions in a student-produced response format still has exactly one numeric entry, so read what single number is requested.

在情境中提问的题目约占数字 SAT 数学部分的 30%，所以许多比与率的题都设在具体情境中。计算之前先划出题目要求的单位（分钟还是小时，米还是千米），算完再做单位检查。若是学生作答题 (SPR)，只有一个输入框，最多六个字符，可以以负号开头，分数用斜杠输入；像 $7.5$ 这样的结果直接输入即可。若学生作答题的情形是无解或有多个解，仍然只有一个数值输入，所以要看清题目要求的是哪一个数。

Section 3.23.2 节

Percentages百分数

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate a percent of a quantity.
  • Calculate percent change and say whether it is an increase or a decrease.
  • Combine successive percent changes by multiplying growth factors.
  • Find the original value when the new value and the percent change are known.
  • Distinguish percent change from percentage points.
  • 计算一个量的百分之几 (percent of)。
  • 计算百分数变化 (percent change)，并说明是增加还是减少。
  • 通过乘以增长因子来合并连续的百分数变化 (successive changes)。
  • 已知新值和百分数变化时，求原值 (original value)。
  • 区分百分数变化与百分点 (percentage points)。

Percent of a quantity一个量的百分之几

Percent of a quantity means a part out of 100: $p\%$ of $N$ is $\dfrac{p}{100}\times N$. To find what percent one number is of another, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100.

一个量的百分之几 (percent of) 表示一百份中的几份：$N$ 的 $p\%$ 是 $\dfrac{p}{100}\times N$。求一个数占另一个数的百分之几，用部分除以整体再乘 100。

Percent change百分数变化

Percent change $=\dfrac{\text{new}-\text{original}}{\text{original}}\times100$. The denominator is always the original value. A positive result is an increase, a negative result is a decrease. As a multiplier, an increase of $r\%$ multiplies by $1+\dfrac{r}{100}$ and a decrease multiplies by $1-\dfrac{r}{100}$.

百分数变化 (percent change) $=\dfrac{\text{new}-\text{original}}{\text{original}}\times100$。分母永远是原值 (original value)。结果为正是增加，为负是减少。用乘数表示，增加 $r\%$ 就乘以 $1+\dfrac{r}{100}$，减少就乘以 $1-\dfrac{r}{100}$。

Worked Example: percent of and percent change例题：百分之几与百分数变化

(a) A fictional school has 240 students and 35% take the bus. How many take the bus? (b) A town grows from 250 to 310 residents. Find the percent change.

  1. (a) $0.35\times240=84$ students.
  2. (b) $\dfrac{310-250}{250}\times100=\dfrac{60}{250}\times100=24$, so a $24\%$ increase.

Why it works: the change of 60 is measured against the original 250, not the new 310.

(a) 某虚构学校有 240 名学生，其中 35% 乘校车。有多少人乘校车？(b) 某镇居民从 250 人增加到 310 人。求百分数变化。

  1. (a) $0.35\times240=84$ 名学生。
  2. (b) $\dfrac{310-250}{250}\times100=\dfrac{60}{250}\times100=24$，即增加 $24\%$。

为什么可行：变化量 60 是相对于原来的 250 来衡量的，而不是新的 310。

Successive changes连续变化

Successive percent changes multiply; they do not add. An increase of 15% followed by a decrease of 15% is not a net change of 0, because the second percentage applies to a different base.

连续 (successive) 的百分数变化是相乘，而不是相加。先增加 15% 再减少 15% 并不是净变化为 0，因为第二个百分数作用在不同的基数上。

Worked Example: an increase then a decrease例题：先涨后降

A jacket costs USD 80. The price rises 15%, then the new price falls 15%. Find the final price and the net percent change.

  1. Multipliers: $1.15$ and $0.85$.
  2. Final price: $80\times1.15\times0.85=92\times0.85=78.20$, that is USD 78.20.
  3. Net change: $\dfrac{78.20-80}{80}\times100=-2.25$, a decrease of $2.25\%$.

Why it works: the 15% decrease is taken from USD 92, a bigger base than USD 80, so it removes more than the increase added.

一件夹克售价 80 美元。价格上涨 15%，然后新价格下降 15%。求最终价格与净百分数变化。

  1. 乘数：$1.15$ 与 $0.85$。
  2. 最终价格：$80\times1.15\times0.85=92\times0.85=78.20$，即 78.20 美元。
  3. 净变化：$\dfrac{78.20-80}{80}\times100=-2.25$，减少 $2.25\%$。

为什么可行：15% 的降幅是对 92 美元 计算的，基数比 80 美元 大，所以减少的量多于增加的量。

Working back to the original value反求原值

When the final value and the percent change are given, divide by the multiplier: $\text{original}=\dfrac{\text{final}}{\text{multiplier}}$. A 20% discount has multiplier $0.80$, so the sale price is 80% of the original, not the original minus 20% of the sale price.

已知最终值和百分数变化时，除以乘数：$\text{original}=\dfrac{\text{final}}{\text{multiplier}}$。折扣 20% 的乘数是 $0.80$，所以售价是原价的 80%，而不是售价再减去其 20%。

Worked Example: find the original price例题：求原价

After a 20% discount a calculator sells for USD 52. What was the original price?

  1. Multiplier $=1-0.20=0.80$.
  2. $0.80\times\text{original}=52$, so original $=\dfrac{52}{0.80}=65$, that is USD 65.

Check: $0.80\times65=52$. (The tempting wrong answer USD 62.40 comes from adding 20% to 52.)

打八折（折扣 20%）后，一台计算器售价 52 美元。原价是多少？

  1. 乘数 $=1-0.20=0.80$。
  2. $0.80\times\text{original}=52$，所以原价 $=\dfrac{52}{0.80}=65$，即 65 美元。

检验：$0.80\times65=52$。（容易错选的 62.40 美元 来自对 52 再加 20%。）

Common Mistake: percent versus percentage points

If support rises from 40% to 46%, it rose by 6 percentage points, but the percent change is $\dfrac{6}{40}\times100=15\%$. Also, a fall from 40 to 30 is $-25\%$, while a rise from 30 back to 40 is $+33.3\%$, because the original value differs.

常见错误：百分数变化与百分点

支持率从 40% 升到 46%，上升了 6 个百分点，但百分数变化是 $\dfrac{6}{40}\times100=15\%$。另外，从 40 降到 30 是 $-25\%$，而从 30 回升到 40 是 $+33.3\%$，因为原值不同。

Exam tip考试提示

Backsolve (multiple-choice only, since a student-produced response has no options): for an original-price question, test the middle option first. If USD 65 is offered, check $0.80\times65=52$ and you are done; if the result is too small, the original must be larger. The calculator is allowed on every question, so type chains such as $80\times1.15\times0.85$ in Desmos in one line, but set up the multipliers yourself first. The official average is 1.59 minutes per question, so do not spend longer than that on a one-step percent.

代入选项法 (Backsolve，仅限选择题，因为学生作答题没有选项)：求原价的题先试中间的选项。若选项里有 65 美元，检验 $0.80\times65=52$ 即可；若结果太小，原价应更大。每道题都允许使用计算器，所以可以在 Desmos 中一行输入 $80\times1.15\times0.85$ 这样的连乘，但乘数要先自己列好。官方平均每题 1.59 分钟，一步完成的百分数题不要花更长时间。

One-Variable Data: Distributions, Center and Spread单变量数据：分布、中心与离散程度

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Read a histogram, a dot plot and a box plot and describe the distribution.
  • Calculate the mean, median, mode and range of a data set.
  • Explain how an outlier changes the mean, the median and the range.
  • Compare the spread of two data sets with the same mean using standard deviation.
  • State how adding a constant or multiplying by a constant changes center and spread.
  • 读懂直方图 (histogram)、点图 (dot plot) 和箱线图 (box plot)，并描述分布 (distribution)。
  • 计算数据集的平均数 (mean)、中位数 (median)、众数 (mode) 与极差 (range)。
  • 说明离群值 (outlier) 如何改变平均数、中位数与极差。

Reading a distribution: histogram, dot plot, box plot读懂分布：直方图、点图、箱线图

The distribution of a variable says which values occur and how often. A histogram groups values into bins and the bar height is the count (or relative frequency) in each bin. A dot plot puts one dot per observation above a number line, so every value can be read exactly. A box plot shows only five numbers: minimum, first quartile $Q_1$, median, third quartile $Q_3$ and maximum. The box holds the middle 50% of the data, and the interquartile range is $\text{IQR}=Q_3-Q_1$. In this guide $Q_1$ and $Q_3$ are the medians of the lower and upper halves.

变量的分布 (distribution) 说明哪些取值出现以及出现多少次。直方图 (histogram) 把取值分组，柱高是每组的频数（或相对频率）。点图 (dot plot) 在数轴上方每个观测画一个点，所以每个取值都能精确读出。箱线图 (box plot) 只显示五个数：最小值、第一四分位数 $Q_1$、中位数、第三四分位数 $Q_3$ 和最大值。箱内包含中间 50% 的数据，四分位距为 $\text{IQR}=Q_3-Q_1$。本指南中 $Q_1$ 与 $Q_3$ 取下半部与上半部的中位数。

Mean, median, mode, range and the effect of an outlier平均数、中位数、众数、极差与离群值的影响

The mean is the sum divided by the count. The median is the middle value of the ordered list (the average of the two middle values when the count is even). The mode is the most frequent value. The range is maximum minus minimum. An outlier is a value far from the rest. It pulls the mean and enlarges the range, but moves the median very little, so for skewed data or data with outliers the median describes the typical value better.

平均数 (mean) 是总和除以个数。中位数 (median) 是排序后的中间值（个数为偶数时取中间两个数的平均）。众数 (mode) 是出现最多的值。极差 (range) 是最大值减最小值。离群值 (outlier) 是远离其余数据的值。它会拉动平均数、增大极差，但对中位数影响很小，所以数据偏斜或含离群值时，中位数更能代表典型值。

Worked Example: an outlier moves the mean, not the median例题：离群值拉动平均数而非中位数

Seven fictional clinic waiting times (minutes) are $5,6,6,8,10,13,15$. Find the mean, median, mode and range. Then the 15 is recorded as 50 by mistake; recompute.

  1. Mean: $\dfrac{5+6+6+8+10+13+15}{7}=\dfrac{63}{7}=9$. Median (4th value): $8$. Mode: $6$. Range: $15-5=10$.
  2. With 50: sum $=98$, mean $=\dfrac{98}{7}=14$. Median still $8$. Mode still $6$. Range: $50-5=45$.

Why it works: the mean uses every value so it moved from 9 to 14, while the median depends only on the middle position.

为什么可行：平均数用到每个值，所以从 9 变到 14；中位数只取决于中间位置。

Standard deviation: comparing the spread of two data sets标准差：比较两组数据的离散程度

Standard deviation (SD) measures the typical distance of the values from the mean, so it measures spread. Two data sets can have the same mean and different SDs. A set clustered near the mean has a small SD; a set with values far from the mean has a large SD. Adding a constant $c$ to every value shifts the mean and median by $c$ but leaves the SD unchanged; multiplying every value by $k$ multiplies the mean and the median by $k$ and the SD by $|k|$.

标准差 (standard deviation，SD) 衡量各值与平均数的典型距离，所以它衡量的是离散程度 (spread)。两组数据可以有相同的平均数 (same mean) 而标准差不同。数据聚集在平均数附近则标准差小；数据远离平均数则标准差大。每个值加上常数 $c$，平均数与中位数都加 $c$，但标准差不变；每个值乘以 $k$，平均数与中位数都乘以 $k$，标准差乘以 $|k|$。

Worked Example: which set has the larger standard deviation例题：哪一组的标准差更大

Set A is $4,5,6,7,8$ and set B is $2,4,6,8,10$. Both have mean $6$. Compare the standard deviations (population formula, divide by $n=5$).

  1. Set A deviations from 6: $-2,-1,0,1,2$. Squares: $4,1,0,1,4$, sum $10$. Variance $\dfrac{10}{5}=2$, SD $=\sqrt2\approx1.41$.
  2. Set B deviations: $-4,-2,0,2,4$. Squares: $16,4,0,4,16$, sum $40$. Variance $\dfrac{40}{5}=8$, SD $=\sqrt8\approx2.83$.
  3. Set B has the larger SD. Indeed every B deviation is double the matching A deviation, so $\text{SD}_B=2\,\text{SD}_A$.

Why it works: B is A stretched by a factor of 2 about the same center, so the spread doubles.

A 组是 $4,5,6,7,8$，B 组是 $2,4,6,8,10$。两组平均数都是 $6$。比较标准差（总体公式，除以 $n=5$）。

  2. B 组偏差：$-4,-2,0,2,4$。平方：$16,4,0,4,16$，和为 $40$。方差 $\dfrac{40}{5}=8$，标准差 $=\sqrt8\approx2.83$。
  3. B 组的标准差更大。事实上 B 组每个偏差都是 A 组对应偏差的两倍，所以 $\text{SD}_B=2\,\text{SD}_A$。

为什么可行：B 组是 A 组围绕同一中心拉伸 2 倍，所以离散程度加倍。

Scatterplots and the line of best fit散点图与最佳拟合直线

A scatterplot shows two numerical variables as points $(x,y)$. The trend is positive if $y$ tends to rise with $x$, negative if it tends to fall, and there is no trend if the points show no pattern. The line of best fit $\hat y=a+bx$ runs through the cloud of points so that the points are balanced above and below it. Its slope $b$ is the predicted change in $y$ for one more unit of $x$, and the intercept $a$ is the predicted $y$ when $x=0$ (meaningful only if $x=0$ makes sense). A residual is $y-\hat y$: actual minus predicted.

散点图 (scatterplot) 把两个数值变量画成点 $(x,y)$。若 $y$ 倾向于随 $x$ 增大，趋势 (trend) 为正；倾向于减小则为负；点没有规律则无趋势。最佳拟合直线 (line of best fit) $\hat y=a+bx$ 穿过点群，使点在直线上下大致平衡。斜率 $b$ 是 $x$ 每增加一个单位时 $y$ 的预测变化量，截距 $a$ 是 $x=0$ 时的预测 $y$（仅当 $x=0$ 有意义时才有解释价值）。残差 (residual) 是 $y-\hat y$：实际值减预测值。

Worked Example: use the line to predict and interpret例题：用直线预测并解释

Ten fictional students recorded weekly practice hours $x$ and test score $y$. The line of best fit is $\hat y=47.1+5.04x$ (Figure 4). (a) Predict the score for $6.5$ hours. (b) Interpret the slope. (c) One student practised $2$ hours and scored $61$. Find the residual.

  1. (a) $\hat y=47.1+5.04\times6.5=47.1+32.76=79.86\approx79.9$.
  2. (b) For each additional hour of weekly practice, the predicted score rises by about $5.04$ points.
  3. (c) $\hat y=47.1+5.04\times2=57.18$. Residual $=61-57.18=3.82\approx3.8$, so this student scored about $3.8$ above the prediction.

Why it works: the model is a rule $\hat y=a+bx$, so a prediction is a substitution, and the slope is the rate of change.

  2. (b) 每周练习每多一小时，预测得分约上升 $5.04$ 分。
  3. (c) $\hat y=47.1+5.04\times2=57.18$。残差 $=61-57.18=3.82\approx3.8$，即该学生比预测值高约 $3.8$ 分。

为什么可行：模型就是规则 $\hat y=a+bx$，所以预测就是代入，斜率就是变化率。

Interpolation and extrapolation

Predicting inside the range of the data (here $1$ to $8$ hours) is interpolation and is usually reliable. Predicting outside it is extrapolation: at $x=12$ the line gives $47.1+5.04\times12=107.58$, which is impossible for a test scored out of $100$. A strong trend also does not prove that $x$ causes $y$ (see Section 3.7).

内插与外推

在数据范围内（这里是 $1$ 到 $8$ 小时）预测称为内插，通常可靠。超出范围的预测称为外推：当 $x=12$ 时直线给出 $47.1+5.04\times12=107.58$，对满分 $100$ 的测试而言不可能。强趋势也不能证明 $x$ 导致 $y$（见 3.7 节）。

Linear model versus exponential model线性模型与指数模型

A linear model $y=a+bx$ adds a constant rate $b$ for each step in $x$. An exponential model $y=a\cdot r^{x}$ multiplies by a constant ratio $r$ (a constant percent change) for each step. Test a table with equal steps in $x$: if the differences in $y$ are equal the model is linear; if the ratios of consecutive $y$ values are equal the model is exponential, and $r=1+\dfrac{\text{percent}}{100}$.

线性模型 (linear model) $y=a+bx$ 在 $x$ 每走一步时加上恒定的变化率 (constant rate) $b$。指数模型 (exponential model) $y=a\cdot r^{x}$ 每走一步乘以恒定的比值 (constant ratio) $r$，也就是恒定的百分数 (constant percent) 变化。检验 $x$ 等间隔的表格：若 $y$ 的差相等则为线性；若相邻 $y$ 的比相等则为指数，且 $r=1+\dfrac{\text{percent}}{100}$。

Worked Example: linear or exponential from a table例题：由表格判断线性或指数

Fictional account X has balances $500,\ 540,\ 580,\ 620$ after years $0,1,2,3$. Account Y has $500,\ 540,\ 583.20,\ 629.86$. Which is linear, which exponential, and what are the balances at year $10$?

  1. X: differences $40,40,40$. Constant rate, so $y=500+40t$. At $t=10$: $500+400=900$.
  2. Y: ratios $\dfrac{540}{500}=1.08$, $\dfrac{583.20}{540}=1.08$, $\dfrac{629.86}{583.20}\approx1.08$. Constant ratio of $1.08$ (8% growth), so $y=500(1.08)^t$. At $t=10$: $500\times1.08^{10}\approx1079.46$.

Why it works: a constant difference means repeated addition; a constant ratio means repeated multiplication. Figure 5 shows how they separate.

  1. X：差为 $40,40,40$。变化率恒定，所以 $y=500+40t$。$t=10$ 时：$500+400=900$。
  2. Y：比值 $\dfrac{540}{500}=1.08$，$\dfrac{583.20}{540}=1.08$，$\dfrac{629.86}{583.20}\approx1.08$。比值恒为 $1.08$（增长 8%），所以 $y=500(1.08)^t$。$t=10$ 时：$500\times1.08^{10}\approx1079.46$。

为什么可行：差恒定意味着反复相加；比恒定意味着反复相乘。图 5 显示两者如何拉开。

Probability and relative frequency概率与相对频率

When all outcomes are equally likely, the probability of an event is $\dfrac{\text{number of favorable outcomes}}{\text{size of the sample space}}$. The sample space is the set of all possible outcomes. Probabilities lie between $0$ and $1$, and $P(\text{not }A)=1-P(A)$. When outcomes cannot be listed, the relative frequency $\dfrac{\text{times the event occurred}}{\text{number of trials}}$ from many trials estimates the probability, and the expected count in $N$ trials is about $P\times N$.

当所有结果等可能时，事件的概率 (probability) 为 $\dfrac{\text{number of favorable outcomes}}{\text{size of the sample space}}$。样本空间 (sample space) 是所有可能结果的集合。概率介于 $0$ 与 $1$ 之间，且 $P(\text{not }A)=1-P(A)$。当结果无法一一列出时，用多次试验得到的相对频率 (relative frequency) $\dfrac{\text{times the event occurred}}{\text{number of trials}}$ 估计概率，$N$ 次试验中的期望次数约为 $P\times N$。

Worked Example: estimate from relative frequency例题：用相对频率估计

A fictional spinner lands on blue 140 times in 500 spins. Estimate the probability of blue and the expected number of blue results in 2000 spins.

  1. Relative frequency: $\dfrac{140}{500}=0.28$, so $P(\text{blue})\approx0.28$.
  2. Expected count: $0.28\times2000=560$.

Why it works: over many trials the relative frequency settles near the true probability, so scaling it to $N$ trials gives the expected count.

一个虚构转盘在 500 次旋转中有 140 次停在蓝色。估计停在蓝色的概率，以及 2000 次旋转中蓝色的期望次数。

  2. 期望次数：$0.28\times2000=560$。

为什么可行：试验次数很多时相对频率会稳定在真实概率附近，所以把它放大到 $N$ 次试验就得到期望次数。

Inference from Sample Statistics and Margin of Error由样本统计量作推断与误差范围

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Distinguish a population from a sample, and a parameter from a statistic.
  • Explain why only a random sample supports generalizing to the population.
  • Use a sample statistic to estimate a population value and scale it to a count.
  • Interpret a margin of error as a confidence interval around the estimate.
  • Decide whether a claim about the population is supported by an interval.
  • 区分总体 (population) 与样本 (sample)，区分参数与统计量。
  • 解释为什么只有随机样本 (random sample) 才能推广 (generalize) 到总体。
  • 用样本统计量估计总体数值，并换算成总数。
  • 把误差范围 (margin of error) 解释为估计值周围的置信区间 (confidence interval)。
  • 判断一个区间 (interval) 是否支持关于总体的说法。

From sample to population从样本到总体

The population is the whole group you want to learn about; the sample is the part you actually measure. A number computed from the sample (a sample proportion or sample mean) estimates the matching population value. If the sample is a random sample, every member has a known chance of being chosen, and the results can be generalized to the population the sample was drawn from, and only to that population. A volunteer or convenience sample is likely to differ from the population, so no margin of error can repair it.

总体 (population) 是你想了解的全体；样本 (sample) 是你实际测量的那一部分。由样本算出的数（样本比例或样本平均数）用来估计对应的总体数值。如果样本是随机样本 (random sample)，每个成员被选中的机会已知，结果就可以推广 (generalize) 到抽样所来自的总体，而且只能推广到那个总体。志愿者样本或方便样本很可能与总体不同，任何误差范围都无法修复。

Margin of error and confidence interval误差范围与置信区间

A margin of error tells how far the sample estimate could plausibly be from the true population value because of random sampling. The confidence interval is $\text{estimate}-\text{margin}$ to $\text{estimate}+\text{margin}$: it is the interval of plausible values for the population value. Larger random samples give smaller margins of error; as a rule of thumb, four times the sample size roughly halves the margin. The margin of error covers sampling variability only. It does not correct a biased sample.

误差范围 (margin of error) 说明由于随机抽样，样本估计值与真实总体值可能相差多远。置信区间 (confidence interval) 是 $\text{estimate}-\text{margin}$ 到 $\text{estimate}+\text{margin}$：它是总体数值的合理取值区间 (interval)。随机样本越大，误差范围越小；经验上样本量变为四倍，误差范围大约减半。误差范围只涵盖抽样变异，不能纠正有偏的样本。

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Explain what random assignment does and why it supports cause and effect.
  • Explain what random sampling does and why it supports generalizing.
  • Match a study design to the conclusion it can support.
  • Identify a possible confounding variable in an observational study.
  • 区分观察研究 (observational study) 与实验 (experiment)。
  • 解释随机分配 (random assignment) 的作用，以及它为何支持因果结论。
  • 解释随机抽样的作用，以及它为何支持推广。
  • 把研究设计与它能支持的结论对应起来。
  • 在观察研究中指出可能的混杂变量。

Observational study versus experiment观察研究与实验

In an observational study researchers measure what is already happening and do not assign anyone to a group. In an experiment researchers impose a treatment on the subjects. A difference found in an observational study may come from a confounding variable (a third factor linked to both variables), so it shows an association only.

在观察研究 (observational study) 中，研究者只测量已经发生的情况，不把任何人分到某一组。在实验 (experiment) 中，研究者对受试者施加处理。观察研究中发现的差异可能来自混杂变量 (confounding variable，与两个变量都有联系的第三个因素)，所以它只显示关联。

Random sampling versus random assignment随机抽样与随机分配

Random assignment places subjects into treatment groups by chance, so the groups are alike apart from the treatment; a difference in outcome can then be attributed to the treatment. That is what supports causation (cause and effect). Random sampling chooses the subjects from a population by chance, and that is what supports generalizing to the population. The two are independent, so a study can have either, both or neither.

随机分配 (random assignment) 用抽签式的机会把受试者分入各处理组，所以各组除处理外相似；结果上的差异就可以归因于处理。这正是支持因果关系 (causation，cause and effect) 的依据。随机抽样用机会从总体中选取受试者，这是支持推广到总体的依据。两者相互独立，所以一项研究可以两者都有、只有其一或都没有。

1. Researchers record the screen time and sleep hours of $300$ randomly chosen teenagers and find that more screen time goes with less sleep. Can they conclude that screen time reduces sleep?1. 研究者记录了随机选出的 $300$ 名青少年的屏幕时间与睡眠时间，发现屏幕时间越多睡眠越少。能否断定屏幕时间减少了睡眠？

No. It is an observational study: nobody was assigned to a screen-time level, so a confounding variable (for example, a heavy homework load) may explain the link. They can generalize the association to teenagers like those sampled, but they cannot claim causation.

不能。这是观察研究：没有人被分配到某个屏幕时间水平，所以混杂变量（例如作业量大）可能解释这种联系。他们可以把这种关联推广到与样本相似的青少年，但不能声称因果关系。

2. What does random assignment do that random sampling does not?2. 随机分配能做到而随机抽样做不到的是什么？

Random assignment makes the treatment groups comparable, so a difference in outcomes can be attributed to the treatment (cause and effect). Random sampling instead makes the sample representative of the population, which supports generalizing.

随机分配使各处理组具有可比性，所以结果的差异可以归因于处理（因果关系）。随机抽样则使样本能代表总体，支持推广。

1. A juice mix uses concentrate and water in the ratio $2:7$. How many liters of concentrate are in 63 liters of the mix?

C. Parts $2+7=9$; one part is $\dfrac{63}{9}=7$ liters; concentrate is $2\times7=14$. (49 is the water.)

1. 某果汁按浓缩液与水 $2:7$ 的比例配制。63 升混合液中有多少升浓缩液？

C。共 $2+7=9$ 份；每份 $\dfrac{63}{9}=7$ 升；浓缩液为 $2\times7=14$ 升。（49 是水的量。）

2. A train travels at 72 kilometers per hour. What is this speed in meters per second?

B. $72\times\dfrac{1000}{3600}=20$ meters per second.

B。$72\times\dfrac{1000}{3600}=20$ 米每秒。

3. A price of USD 50 rises 10%, then the new price falls 10%. What is the final price?

B. $50\times1.10\times0.90=49.5$. Successive changes multiply, so the two do not cancel.

B。$50\times1.10\times0.90=49.5$。连续变化是相乘，所以两者不会抵消。
