Topic 4.1主题 4.1

Introducing probability概率入门

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe a random process and list its sample space and events.
  • Interpret a probability as a long-run relative frequency and state the law of large numbers.
  • Use equally likely outcomes and the complement rule to find probabilities.
  • Explain why short-run streaks do not change the probability of the next trial.
  • 描述随机过程，并列出其样本空间与事件。
  • 把概率解释为长期相对频率，并陈述大数定律。
  • 利用等可能结果与补集规则求概率。
  • 说明为何短期的连续结果不会改变下一次试验的概率。

A random process produces outcomes that cannot be predicted individually but follow a regular pattern over many repetitions. The sample space $S$ is the list of all possible outcomes, and an event is any collection of outcomes. The probability $P(A)$ of an event is the proportion of times $A$ would occur in the long run if the random process were repeated indefinitely under the same conditions.

随机过程的单次结果无法预测，但在大量重复下呈现规律。样本空间 $S$ 是所有可能结果的列表，事件是若干结果的集合。事件 $A$ 的概率 $P(A)$ 是：在相同条件下无限次重复该随机过程时，$A$ 发生的长期比例。

Must know

Every probability satisfies $0\le P(A)\le 1$, and $P(S)=1$. When all $N$ outcomes are equally likely, $P(A)=\dfrac{\text{number of outcomes in }A}{N}$. The complement $A^{c}$ ("not $A$") has $P(A^{c})=1-P(A)$.

必备知识

任一概率满足 $0\le P(A)\le 1$，且 $P(S)=1$。当 $N$ 个结果等可能时，$P(A)=\dfrac{A\text{ 含的结果数}}{N}$。补集 $A^{c}$（“非 $A$”）满足 $P(A^{c})=1-P(A)$。

The law of large numbers大数定律

The law of large numbers states that as the number of repetitions grows, the relative frequency of an event approaches its probability. Figure 1 shows one simulated run of fair-coin flips: the running proportion of heads is erratic for small $n$ and settles near $0.5$ for large $n$. Note what the law does not say: the count of heads does not have to "catch up" after a streak. The coin has no memory, so each flip still has probability $0.5$ of heads.

大数定律指出：重复次数越多，事件的相对频率越接近其概率。图 1 是公平硬币抛掷的一次模拟：$n$ 较小时正面比例起伏不定，$n$ 较大时稳定在 $0.5$ 附近。注意定律并没有说连续出现某结果后正面的次数要“补回来”。硬币没有记忆，每次抛掷正面的概率仍是 $0.5$。

Worked examples例题

Worked Example 4.1a - Two dice and the complement rule

Two fair six-sided dice are rolled. Find $P(\text{sum}=7)$, $P(\text{sum}\ge 10)$ and $P(\text{at least one 6})$.

  1. The sample space has $6\times 6=36$ equally likely ordered pairs, so each pair has probability $1/36$.
  2. Sum 7: $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$ gives 6 pairs, so $P=6/36=1/6\approx0.1667$.
  3. Sum at least 10: $(4,6),(5,5),(6,4),(5,6),(6,5),(6,6)$ gives 6 pairs, so $P=6/36=1/6\approx0.1667$.
  4. At least one 6: count the complement "no 6" first. There are $5\times5=25$ pairs with no 6, so $P=1-25/36=11/36\approx0.3056$.
例题 4.1a - 两颗骰子与补集规则

掷两颗公平的六面骰子。求 $P(\text{点数和}=7)$、$P(\text{点数和}\ge 10)$ 与 $P(\text{至少一个 6})$。

  1. 样本空间含 $6\times 6=36$ 个等可能的有序数对，每个的概率为 $1/36$。
  2. 和为 7：$(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$ 共 6 对，所以 $P=6/36=1/6\approx0.1667$。
  3. 和不小于 10：$(4,6),(5,5),(6,4),(5,6),(6,5),(6,6)$ 共 6 对，所以 $P=6/36=1/6\approx0.1667$。
  4. 至少一个 6：先数补集“没有 6”。无 6 的数对有 $5\times5=25$ 个，所以 $P=1-25/36=11/36\approx0.3056$。
Worked Example 4.1b - A committee of two

A club has four officers: Ana and Ben (seniors), Cai and Dee (juniors). Two officers are chosen at random for a committee. Find the probability that the committee has at least one senior.

  1. List the sample space (order does not matter): $\{AB, AC, AD, BC, BD, CD\}$, six equally likely committees.
  2. "At least one senior" fails only for $CD$, so use the complement: $P(\text{no senior})=1/6$.
  3. $P(\text{at least one senior})=1-1/6=5/6\approx0.833$. Check by counting directly: $AB, AC, AD, BC, BD$ are 5 of the 6 committees.
例题 4.1b - 两人委员会

某社团有四名干部：Ana 和 Ben（高三），Cai 和 Dee（高二）。随机选两人组成委员会。求委员会中至少有一名高三学生的概率。

  1. 列出样本空间（不计顺序）：$\{AB, AC, AD, BC, BD, CD\}$，共六种等可能的委员会。
  2. “至少一名高三学生”只有 $CD$ 不满足，故用补集：$P(\text{没有高三学生})=1/6$。
  3. $P(\text{至少一名高三学生})=1-1/6=5/6\approx0.833$。直接数可验证：$AB, AC, AD, BC, BD$ 共 6 种中的 5 种。
Common mistake

The "law of averages" is not the law of large numbers. After five heads in a row, the next flip is still $50\%$ likely to be heads, and a probability of $0.3$ does not guarantee that exactly 3 of 10 trials succeed.

常见错误

“平均律”不是大数定律。连续五次正面之后，下一次仍有 $50\%$ 的概率为正面；概率为 $0.3$ 也不保证 10 次试验中恰有 3 次成功。

AP exam tipAP 考试提示

Always interpret a probability as a long-run proportion and name the event: "If many such days were observed under the same conditions, about $30\%$ would have rain." Do not write that it "will" rain on $3$ of $10$ days.

解释概率时，始终说成长期比例并点明事件：“在相同条件下观察大量这样的日子，约有 $30\%$ 会下雨。”不要写成“10 天中会有 3 天下雨”。

1. A forecast says there is a $30\%$ chance of rain tomorrow. Interpret this probability.1. 预报称明天有 $30\%$ 的降雨概率。解释这个概率。

In the long run, on about $30\%$ of days with conditions like tomorrow's, it rains. It does not mean it rains for $30\%$ of the day.

在长期中，与明天条件相似的日子里约有 $30\%$ 会下雨。这不是说一天中有 $30\%$ 的时间在下雨。

2. A fair coin has landed heads $5$ times in a row. What is the probability of heads on the next flip, and why?2. 一枚公平硬币已连续 $5$ 次正面朝上。下一次正面的概率是多少？为什么？

$0.5$. Flips are independent and the coin has no memory; the law of large numbers concerns the long-run proportion, not a correction of streaks.

$0.5$。各次抛掷相互独立，硬币没有记忆；大数定律讲的是长期比例，而非对连续结果的补偿。

$P(\text{红})=5/10=0.5$，所以 $P(\text{不是红})=1-0.5=0.5$（等价于 $(3+2)/10$）。

Topic 4.2主题 4.2

Mutually exclusive events and the addition rule互斥事件与加法规则

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Decide whether two events are mutually exclusive.
  • Apply the addition rule for mutually exclusive events.
  • Explain why mutually exclusive events with positive probability are not independent.
  • Use a Venn diagram to organize events.
  • 判断两个事件是否互斥。
  • 对互斥事件应用加法规则。
  • 说明为什么概率为正的互斥事件不是独立的。
  • 用维恩图整理事件。

Two events are mutually exclusive (also called disjoint) if they cannot occur at the same time: they share no outcomes, so $P(A\text{ and }B)=0$. For such events the probabilities simply add. The same rule extends to any list of events no two of which overlap.

若两个事件不可能同时发生，则称它们互斥（也称不相交）：二者没有共同结果，故 $P(A\text{ 且 }B)=0$。此时概率直接相加。该规则可推广到任意两两不重叠的一组事件。

Must know

If $A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive, $P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A)+P(B)$. For events with overlap, the general addition rule in Topic 4.4 applies instead. If $P(A)>0$ and $P(B)>0$ and $A,B$ are mutually exclusive, then $P(B\mid A)=0\ne P(B)$, so $A$ and $B$ are dependent.

必备知识

若 $A$、$B$ 互斥，则 $P(A\text{ 或 }B)=P(A)+P(B)$。事件有重叠时，应使用主题 4.4 的一般加法规则。若 $P(A)>0$、$P(B)>0$ 且 $A,B$ 互斥，则 $P(B\mid A)=0\ne P(B)$，所以 $A$ 与 $B$ 不独立。

Worked examples例题

Worked Example 4.2a - How students get to school

In a synthetic survey of students, each student reports one main commute mode: $P(\text{walk})=0.18$, $P(\text{bike})=0.12$, $P(\text{bus})=0.35$ and $P(\text{car})=0.35$. Find $P(\text{walk or bike})$, $P(\text{not car})$ and $P(\text{walk or bus})$.

  1. The categories are mutually exclusive (one main mode per student) and exhaustive: $0.18+0.12+0.35+0.35=1.00$.
  2. $P(\text{walk or bike})=0.18+0.12=0.30$.
  3. $P(\text{not car})=1-0.35=0.65$, which equals $0.18+0.12+0.35=0.65$.
  4. $P(\text{walk or bus})=0.18+0.35=0.53$.
例题 4.2a - 学生如何上学

在一项合成的学生调查中，每名学生只报告一种主要上学方式：$P(\text{步行})=0.18$，$P(\text{骑车})=0.12$，$P(\text{公交})=0.35$，$P(\text{汽车})=0.35$。求 $P(\text{步行或骑车})$、$P(\text{不乘汽车})$ 与 $P(\text{步行或公交})$。

  1. 各类别互斥（每人只有一种主要方式）且穷尽：$0.18+0.12+0.35+0.35=1.00$。
  2. $P(\text{步行或骑车})=0.18+0.12=0.30$。
  3. $P(\text{不乘汽车})=1-0.35=0.65$，也等于 $0.18+0.12+0.35=0.65$。
  4. $P(\text{步行或公交})=0.18+0.35=0.53$。
Worked Example 4.2b - Counts by class year

A school council survey lists $300$ students: $90$ first-years, $80$ second-years, $70$ juniors and $60$ seniors. A student is chosen at random. Find $P(\text{first-year or senior})$ and $P(\text{not a junior})$, and decide whether "first-year" and "plays a sport" are mutually exclusive.

  1. Class years are mutually exclusive, so $P(\text{first-year or senior})=\dfrac{90}{300}+\dfrac{60}{300}=\dfrac{150}{300}=0.50$.
  2. $P(\text{not a junior})=1-\dfrac{70}{300}=\dfrac{230}{300}\approx0.767$.
  3. "First-year" and "plays a sport" are not mutually exclusive: a first-year student can play a sport, so the events can occur together and the simple sum would overcount.
例题 4.2b - 按年级的人数

某校学生会调查列出 $300$ 名学生：$90$ 名一年级、$80$ 名二年级、$70$ 名三年级、$60$ 名四年级。随机选一名学生。求 $P(\text{一年级或四年级})$、$P(\text{不是三年级})$，并判断“一年级”与“参加体育运动”是否互斥。

  1. 年级互斥，故 $P(\text{一年级或四年级})=\dfrac{90}{300}+\dfrac{60}{300}=\dfrac{150}{300}=0.50$。
  2. $P(\text{不是三年级})=1-\dfrac{70}{300}=\dfrac{230}{300}\approx0.767$。
  3. “一年级”与“参加体育运动”不互斥：一年级学生可以参加体育运动，两事件可同时发生，直接相加会重复计数。
Common mistake

Mutually exclusive does not mean independent. If you know one of two mutually exclusive events occurred, you know the other did not, which is the strongest possible dependence.

常见错误

互斥不等于独立。若已知两个互斥事件之一发生，就知道另一个没有发生，这是最强的相依关系。

AP exam tipAP 考试提示

A free-response answer that adds probabilities must say why the events are mutually exclusive ("a student has only one main commute mode"). Without that justification the addition may be marked as unsupported.

在简答题中若要把概率相加，必须说明事件互斥的理由（“每名学生只有一种主要上学方式”）。缺少这一说明，加法步骤可能被判为无依据。

Check your understanding自我检测
1. A die is rolled. Let $A=\{1,2\}$, $B=\{5,6\}$, $C=\{2,4,6\}$. Which pairs are mutually exclusive, and what is $P(A\text{ or }B)$?1. 掷一颗骰子。设 $A=\{1,2\}$，$B=\{5,6\}$，$C=\{2,4,6\}$。哪几对事件互斥？$P(A\text{ 或 }B)$ 是多少？

Only $A,B$ share no outcome. $A,C$ share $2$ and $B,C$ share $6$. $P(A\text{ or }B)=2/6+2/6=4/6\approx0.667$.

只有 $A,B$ 没有共同结果；$A,C$ 共有 $2$，$B,C$ 共有 $6$。$P(A\text{ 或 }B)=2/6+2/6=4/6\approx0.667$。

2. For a randomly chosen student, $P(\text{first-year})=0.28$ and $P(\text{sophomore})=0.26$. Find the probability the student is neither.2. 随机选一名学生，$P(\text{一年级})=0.28$，$P(\text{二年级})=0.26$。求该学生两者都不是的概率。

The two events are mutually exclusive, so $P(\text{first-year or sophomore})=0.54$ and $P(\text{neither})=1-0.54=0.46$.

两事件互斥，所以 $P(\text{一年级或二年级})=0.54$，$P(\text{两者都不是})=1-0.54=0.46$。

3. Can two events with positive probabilities be both mutually exclusive and independent?3. 概率为正的两个事件能否既互斥又独立？

No. Mutually exclusive gives $P(A\text{ and }B)=0$, but independence requires $P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)P(B)>0$. Both cannot hold.

不能。互斥意味着 $P(A\text{ 且 }B)=0$，而独立要求 $P(A\text{ 且 }B)=P(A)P(B)>0$。二者不可能同时成立。

Topic 4.3主题 4.3

Conditional probability, the multiplication rule, and tree and two-way tables条件概率、乘法规则，以及树状图与二维表格

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Compute conditional probabilities from a two-way table and from a definition.
  • Use the general multiplication rule $P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\,P(B\mid A)$.
  • Build and read a tree diagram, including reversing a conditional with the tree.
  • Write a complete AP free-response solution that names events and interprets the answer.
  • 从二维表格和定义计算条件概率。
  • 使用一般乘法规则 $P(A\text{ 且 }B)=P(A)\,P(B\mid A)$。
  • 绘制并读取树状图，包括用树状图把条件概率反转。
  • 写出完整的 AP 简答题解答：说明事件并解释结果。

The conditional probability of $B$ given $A$ is the probability that $B$ occurs when we already know that $A$ occurred. Knowing $A$ shrinks the sample space to the outcomes in $A$.

在已知 $A$ 发生的条件下，$B$ 发生的概率称为条件概率。已知 $A$ 后，样本空间缩小为 $A$ 中的结果。

$$P(B\mid A)=\frac{P(A\text{ and }B)}{P(A)},\qquad P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\,P(B\mid A)$$
Must know

The condition goes in the denominator. In a two-way table, $P(B\mid A)$ is the cell count divided by the total of the row or column for $A$, not by the grand total. Also, in general $P(B\mid A)\ne P(A\mid B)$.

必备知识

条件放在分母。在二维表格中，$P(B\mid A)$ 是单元格计数除以 $A$ 所在行或列的合计，而不是除以总计。一般地，$P(B\mid A)\ne P(A\mid B)$。

Also on the 2027 exam: two-way table summaries (topics 2.1 and 2.2)

The revised course also asks you to describe two categorical variables with joint, marginal and conditional relative frequencies. They are probabilities in disguise: a joint relative frequency is $P(A\text{ and }B)$ (cell divided by the table total), a marginal relative frequency is $P(A)$ (row or column total divided by the table total), and a conditional relative frequency is $P(B\mid A)$ (cell divided by its row or column total). The variables are associated when the conditional distributions differ between groups, for example in a segmented bar chart.

对应 2027 考试新增内容：二维表格的汇总（主题 2.1 与 2.2）

新课程还要求用联合、边际与条件相对频率描述两个分类变量。它们其实就是概率：联合相对频率是 $P(A\text{ 且 }B)$（单元格除以总计），边际相对频率是 $P(A)$（行或列合计除以总计），条件相对频率是 $P(B\mid A)$（单元格除以其所在行或列的合计）。当各组的条件分布不同时（例如在分段条形图中），两变量存在关联。

Tree diagrams树状图

A tree diagram lays out a sequence of stages. Each branch carries a conditional probability, so the probabilities leaving any one node sum to $1$. Multiply along a path to get a joint probability, and add the paths that share the event you want. To reverse a conditional, divide one joint probability by the total probability of the given event.

树状图按阶段排列。每条分支标注条件概率，因此从同一节点发出的概率之和为 $1$。沿一条路径相乘得联合概率；把包含目标事件的各路径相加。要把条件概率反转，就用一个联合概率除以所给事件的总概率。

Topic 4.4主题 4.4

Independent events and unions of events独立事件与并集

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Apply the general addition rule $P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\text{ and }B)$.
  • Test whether two events are independent using probabilities or a two-way table.
  • Compute "at least one" probabilities for independent trials with the complement.
  • Distinguish independent from mutually exclusive events.
  • 应用一般加法规则 $P(A\text{ 或 }B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\text{ 且 }B)$。
  • 用概率或二维表格检验两个事件是否独立。
  • 利用补集计算独立试验中“至少一次”的概率。
  • 区分独立事件与互斥事件。

The union "$A$ or $B$" means $A$ occurs, $B$ occurs, or both. The general addition rule subtracts the overlap that was counted twice. Events are independent if knowing that one occurred does not change the probability of the other.

并集“$A$ 或 $B$”指 $A$ 发生、$B$ 发生，或二者都发生。一般加法规则要减去被重复计算的重叠部分。若得知一个事件发生并不改变另一个事件的概率，则这两个事件独立。

$$P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\text{ and }B)$$
Must know

Events $A$ and $B$ are independent exactly when any one of these holds: $P(B\mid A)=P(B)$, or $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$, or $P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\,P(B)$. For independent events, $P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\,P(B)$ extends to any number of events. Independence is a statement about probabilities, not about whether the events seem unrelated.

必备知识

事件 $A$、$B$ 独立，当且仅当下列任一式成立：$P(B\mid A)=P(B)$，或 $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$，或 $P(A\text{ 且 }B)=P(A)\,P(B)$。对独立事件，$P(A\text{ 且 }B)=P(A)\,P(B)$ 可推广到任意多个事件。独立是关于概率的陈述，而不是凭直觉判断事件“看起来是否无关”。

When sampling without replacement from a large population, the trials are not exactly independent, but they are treated as approximately independent when the sample is less than $10\%$ of the population (the $10\%$ condition).

从大总体中不放回抽样时，各次试验并非严格独立；当样本量不超过总体的 $10\%$（$10\%$ 条件）时，可近似视为独立。

Simulation模拟

Learning objectives学习目标

A simulation imitates a random process with a model that has the same probabilities, so that it can be repeated cheaply many times. It is used when an exact calculation is hard or when you want to check one. A complete description has five parts, in order.

模拟用概率相同的模型模仿随机过程，因而可以低成本地重复多次。当精确计算困难，或想检验精确计算时就可使用。完整的描述依次包括五部分。

  1. State the model and its assumptions (for example, each box holds one of 5 prizes with equal probability, independently).
  2. Assign digits so the digit probabilities match the model, and say what to ignore (for example, digits 6 to 9 and 0 are skipped).
  3. Define one trial: what is generated, when it stops, and what is recorded.
  4. Repeat for many trials (state the number).
  5. Estimate with the proportion of trials in which the event occurred, and conclude in context.
  1. 说明模型及其假设（例如，每个盒子独立地等可能含 5 种奖品之一）。
  2. 分配数字，使数字的概率与模型一致，并说明忽略哪些数字（例如跳过 6 到 9 和 0）。
  3. 定义一次试验：生成什么、何时停止、记录什么。
  4. 重复多次试验（写明次数）。
  5. 估计：用事件发生的试验比例估计，并结合情境作结论。

Topic 4.6主题 4.6

Random variables and probability distributions随机变量与概率分布

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define a random variable and classify it as discrete or continuous.
  • Construct and check a discrete probability distribution (table and histogram).
  • Find probabilities of events such as $P(X\ge 3)$ from a distribution.
  • Compute and interpret an expected value, including the net gain of a game.
  • 定义随机变量，并区分离散与连续型。
  • 构造并检验离散概率分布（表格与直方图）。
  • 由分布求 $P(X\ge 3)$ 之类事件的概率。
  • 计算并解释期望值，包括游戏的净收益。

A random variable $X$ assigns a number to each outcome of a random process. It is discrete if its possible values can be listed (usually counts) and continuous if it can take any value in an interval (measurements). A probability distribution of a discrete variable lists every value with its probability.

随机变量 $X$ 给随机过程的每个结果赋一个数。若可能取值可以一一列出（通常是计数），则为离散型；若可取某区间内的任意值（测量值），则为连续型。离散变量的概率分布列出每个取值及其概率。

Must know

A valid discrete distribution has every $P(X=x)$ between $0$ and $1$, and $\sum P(X=x)=1$. For a continuous variable, probability is an area under a curve and $P(X=x)=0$ for any single value, so $P(X\le a)=P(X<a)$.

必备知识

合法的离散分布满足每个 $P(X=x)$ 在 $0$ 到 $1$ 之间，且 $\sum P(X=x)=1$。对连续变量，概率是曲线下的面积，任一单点的概率 $P(X=x)=0$，因此 $P(X\le a)=P(X<a)$。

$$E(X)=\mu_X=\sum x\cdot P(X=x)$$

The expected value $E(X)$ is the mean of the distribution: the long-run average of $X$ over many repetitions. It need not be a possible value of $X$. Topic 4.7 adds the standard deviation.

期望值 $E(X)$ 是分布的均值：大量重复下 $X$ 的长期平均值。它不一定是 $X$ 的可能取值。主题 4.7 会补充标准差。

Topic 4.7主题 4.7

Mean and standard deviation of discrete random variables离散随机变量的均值与标准差

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate the mean and standard deviation of a discrete random variable by formula and by calculator.
  • Interpret the mean and standard deviation in context.
  • Distinguish population parameters $\mu,\sigma$ from sample statistics $\bar x, s$.
  • 用公式和计算器求离散随机变量的均值与标准差。
  • 结合情境解释均值与标准差。
  • 区分总体参数 $\mu,\sigma$ 与样本统计量 $\bar x, s$。

The mean $\mu_X$ locates the center of the distribution and the standard deviation $\sigma_X$ measures the typical distance of $X$ from $\mu_X$, in the same units as $X$. These are parameters computed from probabilities, not statistics computed from a sample.

均值 $\mu_X$ 刻画分布的中心；标准差 $\sigma_X$ 衡量 $X$ 到 $\mu_X$ 的典型距离，单位与 $X$ 相同。它们是由概率算出的参数，而不是由样本算出的统计量。

$$\mu_X=\sum x\,P(x),\qquad \sigma_X^{2}=\sum (x-\mu_X)^{2}P(x),\qquad \sigma_X=\sqrt{\sigma_X^{2}}$$

A binomial setting counts the number of successes in a fixed number of trials. Then $X\sim B(n,p)$ counts the successes.

二项设定计的是固定次数试验中的成功次数。需检验四个条件，记作 BINS：Binary 结果只有成功或失败，Independent 各次试验独立，Number 试验次数固定，Same 每次成功概率相同。此时 $X\sim B(n,p)$ 表示成功次数。

$$\mu_X=np,\qquad \sigma_X=\sqrt{np(1-p)}$$

These shortcuts come from adding $n$ independent success-or-failure variables, each with mean $p$ and variance $p(1-p)$, using the rules of Topic 4.8: means add to $np$ and variances add to $np(1-p)$. Interpret $\mu$ as the long-run average number of successes over many sets of $n$ trials.

这两个简便公式来自把 $n$ 个独立的“成功或失败”变量相加（每个均值为 $p$、方差为 $p(1-p)$），再用主题 4.8 的规则：均值相加为 $np$，方差相加为 $np(1-p)$。$\mu$ 的含义是：重复很多组 $n$ 次试验时，成功次数的长期平均。

Must know

The shape of $B(n,p)$ is skewed right for small $p$, skewed left for large $p$, and symmetric at $p=0.5$; it becomes more bell-shaped as $n$ increases. A normal approximation is reasonable when both $np\ge10$ and $n(1-p)\ge10$. Then $X$ is approximately $N\big(np,\sqrt{np(1-p)}\big)$.

必备知识

$B(n,p)$ 的形状：$p$ 小时右偏，$p$ 大时左偏，$p=0.5$ 时对称；随 $n$ 增大越来越接近钟形。当 $np\ge10$ 且 $n(1-p)\ge10$ 时，正态近似是合理的，此时 $X$ 近似服从 $N\big(np,\sqrt{np(1-p)}\big)$。

The geometric distribution几何分布

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Recognize a geometric setting and state its conditions.
  • Compute geometric probabilities with the formula and with geometpdf and geometcdf.
  • Find and interpret the mean $1/p$ and standard deviation.
  • Distinguish geometric from binomial settings.
  • 识别几何分布的设定并陈述其条件。
  • 用公式以及 geometpdf、geometcdf 计算几何概率。
  • 求并解释均值 $1/p$ 与标准差。
  • 区分几何设定与二项设定。

A geometric setting repeats independent trials, each with the same success probability $p$, until the first success. The random variable $X$ is the number of the trial on which the first success occurs, so $X=1,2,3,\dots$ with no upper limit. Compare binomial: a fixed $n$ and a count of successes.

几何设定重复进行独立试验，每次成功概率相同为 $p$，直到首次成功为止。随机变量 $X$ 是首次成功发生在第几次试验，故 $X=1,2,3,\dots$ 且没有上限。与二项分布对比：二项的 $n$ 固定，计的是成功次数。

$$P(X=k)=(1-p)^{k-1}p,\quad P(X>k)=(1-p)^{k},\quad P(X\le k)=1-(1-p)^{k}$$
$$\mu_X=\frac1p,\qquad \sigma_X=\frac{\sqrt{1-p}}{p}$$
Must know

Conditions: binary trials, independent, the same $p$, and counting trials until the first success. The probabilities fall steadily, so the distribution is always skewed right and the most likely value is $1$, yet the mean $1/p$ can be much larger. The mean is the long-run average number of trials needed.

必备知识

条件：二元试验、相互独立、$p$ 相同，并且计的是直到首次成功的试验次数。概率逐步下降，所以分布总是右偏，最可能的取值是 $1$，但均值 $1/p$ 可以大得多。均值是所需试验次数的长期平均。

A normal distribution is symmetric and described by $\mu$ and $\sigma$. Standardize with $z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$. The empirical rule gives about $68\%$, $95\%$ and $99.7\%$ within $1$, $2$ and $3$ standard deviations. Go deeper: differences of two independent statistics

The rules of Topic 4.8 extend the sampling distributions to differences. For independent samples, $\mu_{\hat p_1-\hat p_2}=p_1-p_2$ with standard deviation $\sqrt{\dfrac{p_1(1-p_1)}{n_1}+\dfrac{p_2(1-p_2)}{n_2}}$, and $\mu_{\bar x_1-\bar x_2}=\mu_1-\mu_2$ with standard deviation $\sqrt{\dfrac{\sigma_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{\sigma_2^2}{n_2}}$. Bias, unbiased estimators and variability偏差、无偏估计量与变异

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define an unbiased estimator and give examples.
  • Distinguish bias from variability and explain how sample size affects each.
  • Verify unbiasedness by listing a small sampling distribution.
  • Explain why a larger sample does not fix a biased sampling method.
  • 定义无偏估计量并举例。
  • 区分偏差与变异，并说明样本量对二者的影响。
  • 通过列出一个小型抽样分布来验证无偏性。
  • 说明为什么更大的样本不能纠正有偏的抽样方法。

A statistic used to estimate a parameter is an estimator. It is unbiased if the mean of its sampling distribution equals the parameter it estimates. Variability describes the spread of the sampling distribution. A good estimator has little bias and little variability (Figure 8).

用来估计参数的统计量称为估计量。若它的抽样分布的均值等于所估计的参数，则称其无偏。变异描述抽样分布的离散程度。好的估计量偏差小且变异小（图 8）。

Must know

The sample proportion $\hat p$ is an unbiased estimator of $p$, and the sample mean $\bar x$ is an unbiased estimator of $\mu$, when samples are random. The sample variance $s^2$ (dividing by $n-1$) is unbiased for $\sigma^2$, although $s$ is slightly biased low for $\sigma$. A larger random sample reduces variability but is not what removes bias; bias comes from the sampling method or the estimator itself.

必备知识

在随机抽样下，样本比例 $\hat p$ 是 $p$ 的无偏估计量，样本均值 $\bar x$ 是 $\mu$ 的无偏估计量。样本方差 $s^2$（除以 $n-1$）是 $\sigma^2$ 的无偏估计量，但 $s$ 对 $\sigma$ 略有偏低。更大的随机样本可降低变异，但消除不了偏差；偏差来自抽样方法或估计量本身。

Worked Example 4.13a - Listing a sampling distribution by hand

A tiny population has the three values $2,\,4,\,6$ (so $\mu=4$ and population variance $\sigma^2=8/3$). Take every possible sample of size $2$ with replacement ($9$ equally likely samples). Show that $\bar x$ is unbiased, and show that the sample maximum is a biased estimator of the population maximum $6$.

  1. Sample means for $(2,2),(2,4),(2,6),(4,2),(4,4),(4,6),(6,2),(6,4),(6,6)$ are $2,3,4,3,4,5,4,5,6$.
  2. $\mu_{\bar x}=(2+3+4+3+4+5+4+5+6)/9=36/9=4=\mu$. So $\bar x$ is unbiased. Also $\sigma_{\bar x}^2=\frac{4+1+0+1+0+1+0+1+4}{9}=\frac{12}{9}=\frac43=\frac{\sigma^2}{n}=\frac{8/3}{2}$.
  3. Sample maxima are $2,4,6,4,4,6,6,6,6$, with mean $44/9\approx4.89$. This is below the true maximum $6$, so the sample maximum is biased low (it can never exceed the population maximum and is usually below it).
  4. Check on variance: sample variances $s^2=(a-b)^2/2$ are $0,2,8,2,0,2,8,2,0$ with mean $24/9=8/3=\sigma^2$, so dividing by $n-1$ is unbiased. Dividing by $n$ would give mean $4/3$, which is biased low.

Worked Example 4.13b - A big sample with a biased method

  1. If the sample had been random, $\sigma_{\hat p}=\sqrt{0.6(0.4)/1500}=\sqrt{0.00016}\approx0.0126$.

Unit quiz单元自测

1. Events $A$ and $B$ are independent with $P(A)=0.4$ and $P(B)=0.5$. What is $P(A\text{ or }B)$?

Answer: B. Independence gives $P(A\text{ and }B)=0.4\times0.5=0.20$, so $P(A\text{ or }B)=0.4+0.5-0.2=0.70$. Choice A forgets the overlap.

1. 事件 $A$ 与 $B$ 独立，$P(A)=0.4$，$P(B)=0.5$。$P(A\text{ 或 }B)$ 是多少？

答案：B。独立给出 $P(A\text{ 且 }B)=0.4\times0.5=0.20$，所以 $P(A\text{ 或 }B)=0.4+0.5-0.2=0.70$。选项 A 忘记扣除重叠部分。

2. $X\sim B(20,0.3)$. What is $\sigma_X$?

Answer: B. $\sigma=\sqrt{np(1-p)}=\sqrt{20(0.3)(0.7)}=\sqrt{4.2}\approx2.05$. Choice A is the mean $np$, and C is the variance.

2. $X\sim B(20,0.3)$。$\sigma_X$ 是多少？

答案：B。$\sigma=\sqrt{np(1-p)}=\sqrt{20(0.3)(0.7)}=\sqrt{4.2}\approx2.05$。选项 A 是均值 $np$，C 是方差。

3. A geometric random variable has $p=0.25$. What is its mean?

Answer: A. $\mu=1/p=1/0.25=4$ trials on average until the first success.

3. 几何随机变量的 $p=0.25$。它的均值是多少？

答案：A。$\mu=1/p=1/0.25=4$，即平均需要 $4$ 次试验才首次成功。

4. $X$ has mean $10$ and SD $2$; $Y$ has mean $6$ and SD $1.5$; they are independent. What is the SD of $X-Y$?

Answer: C. Variances add even for a difference: $\sqrt{2^2+1.5^2}=\sqrt{6.25}=2.5$. Choice A subtracts the SDs, B adds them, and D is the variance.

4. $X$ 的均值为 $10$、标准差为 $2$；$Y$ 的均值为 $6$、标准差为 $1.5$；二者独立。$X-Y$ 的标准差是多少？

答案：C。即使求差，方差也是相加：$\sqrt{2^2+1.5^2}=\sqrt{6.25}=2.5$。选项 A 把标准差相减，B 把标准差相加，D 是方差。

5. A population has $p=0.5$. For random samples of $n=100$, what is the standard deviation of $\hat p$?

Answer: B. $\sigma_{\hat p}=\sqrt{0.5(0.5)/100}=\sqrt{0.0025}=0.05$.

5. 总体 $p=0.5$。对 $n=100$ 的随机样本，$\hat p$ 的标准差是多少？

答案：B。$\sigma_{\hat p}=\sqrt{0.5(0.5)/100}=\sqrt{0.0025}=0.05$。

6. Which statement about an unbiased estimator is correct?

Answer: D. Unbiased describes the center of the sampling distribution only. Individual samples vary, sample size affects variability rather than bias, and unbiased says nothing about the smallest variability.

6. 关于无偏估计量，下列哪项正确？

答案：D。无偏只描述抽样分布的中心。单个样本会变化，样本量影响的是变异而非偏差，且无偏并不意味着变异最小。
