Topic 7.1主题 7.1

Why Use t? Introducing the t Distribution为什么用 t？t 分布入门

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Explain why inference about a mean uses a $t$ distribution rather than a normal distribution when the population standard deviation is unknown.
  • Calculate the standard error $s/\sqrt{n}$ of a sample mean and distinguish it from the standard deviation $\sigma$.
  • State the degrees of freedom for a one-sample $t$ procedure and describe how the shape of a $t$ distribution changes as $df$ grows.
  • Find $t^*$ critical values and $t$ probabilities with a table or a calculator (invT, tcdf).
  • Decide whether a graph and a sample size make a $t$ procedure reasonable.
  • 解释当总体标准差未知时，为什么对均值的推断使用 $t$ 分布而不是正态分布。
  • 计算样本均值的标准误 $s/\sqrt{n}$，并区分它与标准差 $\sigma$。
  • 写出单样本 $t$ 程序的自由度，并描述 $df$ 增大时 $t$ 分布形状的变化。
  • 用表或计算器（invT、tcdf）求 $t^*$ 临界值与 $t$ 分布的概率。
  • 根据图形与样本量判断 $t$ 程序是否合理。

Unit 6 built inference for a proportion on a simple fact: when $np$ and $n(1-p)$ are large, $\hat{p}$ varies in a nearly normal way with a standard deviation you can write down. For a population mean $\mu$ the sample statistic is $\bar{x}$, and its sampling distribution has mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\sigma/\sqrt{n}$. The difficulty is the population standard deviation $\sigma$: in practice it is almost never known. If you knew $\sigma$ you would usually already know $\mu$.

第 6 单元为比例的推断建立在一个简单事实上：当 $np$ 与 $n(1-p)$ 都较大时，$\hat{p}$ 近似服从正态分布，其标准差可以直接写出。对于总体均值 $\mu$，样本统计量是 $\bar{x}$，其抽样分布的均值为 $\mu$，标准差为 $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\sigma/\sqrt{n}$。困难在于总体标准差 $\sigma$：实际中几乎从不已知。如果你知道 $\sigma$，通常也就已经知道 $\mu$ 了。

The fix is to replace $\sigma$ with the sample standard deviation $s$. The result, $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$, is the standard error of $\bar{x}$: an estimate, from the sample, of how far $\bar{x}$ typically falls from $\mu$. But $s$ itself changes from sample to sample, and that extra variation means the standardized statistic $\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}$ is not exactly normal. It follows a t-distribution (also called the t distribution), which was derived for exactly this situation.

解决办法是用样本标准差 $s$ 代替 $\sigma$。得到的 $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$ 叫做 $\bar{x}$ 的标准误（standard error）：用样本估计 $\bar{x}$ 通常偏离 $\mu$ 多远。但 $s$ 本身也随样本而变，这一额外的变异使标准化统计量 $\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}$ 不再严格服从正态分布，而是服从 t 分布（t-distribution），它正是为这种情形推导出来的。

Must know

Standard deviation of $\bar{x}$: $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$, requires the unknown population standard deviation. Standard error of $\bar{x}$: $s_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$, computed from the sample. When the conditions are met, $t=\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}$ follows a $t$ distribution with $df=n-1$.

必备知识

$\bar{x}$ 的标准差：$\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$，需要未知的总体标准差。$\bar{x}$ 的标准误：$s_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$，由样本计算。条件满足时，$t=\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}$ 服从自由度 $df=n-1$ 的 $t$ 分布。

Degrees of freedom and the shape of the t distribution自由度与 t 分布的形状

A $t$ distribution is a family of curves indexed by its degrees of freedom ($df$). For a one-sample procedure $df=n-1$: the sample standard deviation is computed from $n$ deviations about $\bar{x}$, but those deviations must sum to zero, so only $n-1$ of them are free to vary. Every $t$ curve is bell-shaped, symmetric, and centered at 0, like the standard normal curve. Compared with the normal curve it is wider and flatter in the center and has heavier tails (thicker tails), because an estimated $s$ sometimes comes out too small and produces an extreme $t$ value. The smaller the $df$, the heavier the tails (Figure 7.1).

$t$ 分布是由自由度（degrees of freedom，$df$）标记的一族曲线。单样本程序中 $df=n-1$：样本标准差由 $n$ 个相对 $\bar{x}$ 的离差算出，但这些离差之和必为零，所以只有 $n-1$ 个可以自由变化。每条 $t$ 曲线都是钟形、对称、中心在 0，与标准正态曲线类似。与正态曲线相比，它中心更矮更宽、尾部更重（heavier tails），因为估计出的 $s$ 有时偏小，会产生极端的 $t$ 值。$df$ 越小，尾部越重（图 7.1）。

As $df$ increases, the $t$ distribution approaches the normal distribution: with $df$ near 30 the two are hard to tell apart, and with $df=\infty$ they coincide. That is why a $t$ critical value is always larger than the matching $z^*$, and why the gap shrinks as the sample grows. For a 95% interval, $z^*=1.960$ while $t^*=2.776$ for $df=4$, $2.262$ for $df=9$, $2.064$ for $df=24$, and $1.984$ for $df=99$. The larger $t^*$ is the price of not knowing $\sigma$: wider intervals and less extreme evidence for the same $\bar{x}$.

随着 $df$ 增大，$t$ 分布趋近正态分布（approaches the normal distribution）：$df$ 接近 30 时两者已难以区分，$df=\infty$ 时完全重合。因此 $t$ 临界值总比对应的 $z^*$ 大，且样本越大差距越小。对于 95% 的区间，$z^*=1.960$，而 $df=4$ 时 $t^*=2.776$，$df=9$ 时 $2.262$，$df=24$ 时 $2.064$，$df=99$ 时 $1.984$。较大的 $t^*$ 是不知道 $\sigma$ 所付出的代价：区间更宽，同样的 $\bar{x}$ 所给出的证据也更不极端。

When may we use t? Graph and sample size何时可以使用 t？图形与样本量

The $t$ model assumes the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}$ is approximately normal. Two situations guarantee this well enough. If the population is approximately normal, $\bar{x}$ is approximately normal for any $n$. If $n\ge 30$, the Central Limit Theorem makes $\bar{x}$ approximately normal even for a skewed population. If $n<30$ and the shape of the population is unknown, look at a graph of the sample (dotplot, boxplot or histogram): proceed if it shows no strong skewness and no outliers, and stop if it does. With very small samples a graph cannot prove normality, so state what you saw and why it makes the procedure reasonable.

$t$ 模型假定 $\bar{x}$ 的抽样分布近似正态。有两种情形足以保证这一点：若总体近似正态，则任何 $n$ 下 $\bar{x}$ 都近似正态；若 $n\ge 30$，中心极限定理使 $\bar{x}$ 即使在总体偏斜时也近似正态。若 $n<30$ 且总体形状未知，要看样本的图形（点图、箱线图或直方图）：没有明显偏斜且没有离群点就可以继续，否则就不要继续。样本很小时，图形无法证明正态性，所以要写出你看到了什么以及为何这使程序合理。

Worked Example 7.1a: standard error and degrees of freedom例题 7.1a：标准误与自由度

A random sample of $n=16$ students has a mean commute time of $\bar{x}=24.5$ minutes with $s=9.2$ minutes. The population standard deviation is not known.

  1. Standard error: $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}=\dfrac{9.2}{\sqrt{16}}=\dfrac{9.2}{4}=2.3$ minutes.
  2. Degrees of freedom: $df=n-1=15$.
  3. In context: the sample mean commute time typically differs from the true mean commute time of all students in the population by about 2.3 minutes. We cannot write $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$ because $\sigma$ is unknown; this is why the standard error is used.

Why this works: $s$ estimates $\sigma$, and dividing by $\sqrt{n}$ shrinks the spread of the mean as the sample grows.

随机抽取 $n=16$ 名学生，平均通勤时间 $\bar{x}=24.5$ 分钟，$s=9.2$ 分钟。总体标准差未知。

  1. 标准误：$\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}=\dfrac{9.2}{\sqrt{16}}=\dfrac{9.2}{4}=2.3$ 分钟。
  2. 自由度：$df=n-1=15$。
  3. 结合情境：样本平均通勤时间与总体中全体学生的真实平均通勤时间通常相差约 2.3 分钟。因 $\sigma$ 未知，不能写 $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$，所以使用标准误。

为什么可行：$s$ 估计 $\sigma$，除以 $\sqrt{n}$ 反映样本越大均值的波动越小。

Worked Example 7.1b: a t probability is bigger than the normal one例题 7.1b：t 概率比正态概率大

A one-sided test of a mean from a sample of $n=10$ gives $t=2.10$. Find $P(T>2.10)$ and compare it with the normal answer.

  1. Degrees of freedom: $df=10-1=9$.
  2. Calculator: tcdf(2.10,1E99,9) $\approx 0.0326$.
  3. Compare: the normal curve gives $P(Z>2.10)=0.0179$, about half as large.
  4. Meaning: using $z$ instead of $t$ would make the evidence look nearly twice as strong as it is.

Why this works: the heavier tails of $t_{9}$ put more area beyond 2.10.

对 $n=10$ 的样本做均值的单侧检验，得 $t=2.10$。求 $P(T>2.10)$ 并与正态结果比较。

  1. 自由度：$df=10-1=9$。
  2. 计算器：tcdf(2.10,1E99,9) $\approx 0.0326$。
  3. 比较：正态曲线给出 $P(Z>2.10)=0.0179$，约小一半。
  4. 含义：用 $z$ 代替 $t$ 会使证据看起来比实际强近一倍。

为什么可行：$t_{9}$ 的重尾使 2.10 之外的面积更大。

Exam tip考试提示

Write which distribution you use and why: "unknown $\sigma$, so use $t$ with $df=n-1$." When $n<30$ and a graph is not provided, say that you cannot check normality and ask for a graph; when $n\ge 30$, cite the large sample size. Never use $z$ for a mean when only $s$ is given.

写明所用分布及理由：“$\sigma$ 未知，故用 $df=n-1$ 的 $t$。”当 $n<30$ 且没有给出图形时，要说明无法检验正态性；当 $n\ge 30$ 时，引用大样本。只给了 $s$ 时，绝不能对均值使用 $z$。

Common mistake

Saying the $t$ curve has a larger standard deviation than the normal curve is true, but writing "$df=n$" or reading a table row for $n$ instead of $n-1$ gives a $t^*$ that is too small.

常见错误

说 $t$ 曲线的标准差比正态曲线大是对的，但写成“$df=n$”或在表中取 $n$ 而不是 $n-1$ 所在的行，会得到偏小的 $t^*$。

Topic 7.2主题 7.2

Constructing a Confidence Interval for a Population Mean构造总体均值的置信区间

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State and check the conditions for a one-sample $t$ interval: random, 10% condition, Normal or large sample.
  • Construct a one-sample $t$ interval by hand and with TInterval.
  • Interpret a confidence interval and a confidence level in context.
  • Calculate the margin of error and explain how $n$, $s$ and the confidence level affect it.
  • Estimate the sample size needed for a target margin of error.
  • 陈述并检验单样本 $t$ 区间的条件：随机、10% 条件、正态或大样本。
  • 手算并用 TInterval 构造单样本 $t$ 区间。
  • 结合情境解释置信区间与置信水平。
  • 计算误差范围，并说明 $n$、$s$ 与置信水平如何影响它。
  • 估计达到目标误差范围所需的样本量。

A one-sample t-interval estimates a population mean $\mu$ from a sample. The interval has the familiar form statistic $\pm$ margin of error, with the standard error in place of $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$:

单样本 t 区间（one-sample t-interval）用样本估计总体均值 $\mu$。区间具有熟悉的形式：统计量 $\pm$ 误差范围，其中用标准误代替 $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$：

One-sample t interval

$$\bar{x}\pm t^*\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}},\qquad df=n-1$$ Here $t^*$ is the critical value (the number of standard errors) that captures the middle $C\%$ of a $t$ distribution with $n-1$ degrees of freedom. The margin of error is $ME=t^*\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$. On the TI-84: STAT → TESTS → 8:TInterval, then choose Data or Stats.

单样本 t 区间

$$\bar{x}\pm t^*\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}},\qquad df=n-1$$ 其中 $t^*$ 是临界值（标准误的个数），它截取自由度为 $n-1$ 的 $t$ 分布中间 $C\%$ 的面积。误差范围为 $ME=t^*\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$。TI-84 路径：STAT → TESTS → 8:TInterval，再选择 Data 或 Stats。

Conditions条件

Check three conditions, in context, before computing. Random: the data come from a random sample from the population of interest, or from a randomized experiment. 10% condition: when sampling without replacement, $n$ is less than 10% of the population, so observations are close to independent. Normal or large sample: the population is approximately normal, or $n\ge 30$, or (for $n<30$) a graph of the sample shows no strong skewness and no outliers. For small samples, sketch or describe the graph; writing "it is approximately normal" without evidence is not a justification; describe the graph.

计算前要结合情境检验三个条件。随机：数据来自感兴趣总体的随机样本，或来自随机化实验。10% 条件（10% condition）：不放回抽样时，$n$ 小于总体的 10%，使各观测近似独立。正态或大样本：总体近似正态（approximately normal），或 $n\ge 30$，或（$n<30$ 时）样本图形没有明显偏斜和离群点。小样本要画出或描述图形；没有依据就写“近似正态”不构成理由，要描述图形。

Interpreting the interval and the confidence level解释区间与置信水平

Interpret the interval: "We are 95% confident that the interval from 495.72 g to 501.08 g captures the true mean weight of all bags of rice produced that day." Interpret the level: if we took many random samples of the same size and built a 95% interval from each, about 95% of those intervals would capture the true mean. Do not say that there is a 95% probability that $\mu$ is in this particular interval (once computed it either captures $\mu$ or it does not), and do not say that 95% of the individual bags lie in the interval. The interval estimates a mean, not individual values. Always give the answer in context: name the population, the variable and the units.

解释区间：“我们有 95% 的把握认为，区间 495.72 克至 501.08 克包含当天生产的所有米袋的真实平均重量。”解释水平：如果用同样大小的样本重复抽样很多次，并各自构造 95% 的区间，大约 95% 的区间会包含真实均值。不要说“$\mu$ 落在这个具体区间内的概率是 95%”（区间一旦算出，要么包含 $\mu$，要么不包含），也不要说 95% 的单个米袋落在该区间内。区间估计的是均值，不是单个数值。务必结合情境（in context）作答：写出总体、变量与单位。

Margin of error and sample size误差范围与样本量

The margin of error $t^*\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$ shrinks when $n$ increases, when $s$ is smaller, or when the confidence level is lowered (a smaller $t^*$). To halve the margin of error you need about four times the sample size, because $n$ sits under a square root. To plan a study, set the target margin of error $ME$ and use a prior estimate of the spread (a pilot sample or earlier study) in place of $s$. Because $t^*$ depends on $n$, planning uses $z^*$ as an approximation: $n\ge\left(\dfrac{z^*\sigma}{ME}\right)^2$, rounded up.

误差范围 $t^*\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$ 在 $n$ 增大、$s$ 变小或降低置信水平（$t^*$ 变小）时缩小。要使误差范围减半，样本量大约要变成四倍，因为 $n$ 在根号下。规划研究时，先设定目标误差范围 $ME$，并用先前估计的离散程度（预试验样本或早期研究）代替 $s$。由于 $t^*$ 依赖于 $n$，规划时用 $z^*$ 作近似：$n\ge\left(\dfrac{z^*\sigma}{ME}\right)^2$，向上取整。

Worked Example 7.2a: a 95% t interval from summary statistics例题 7.2a：由汇总统计量构造 95% t 区间

A packing plant produces thousands of bags of rice per day, each labeled 500 g. A random sample of $n=25$ bags has $\bar{x}=498.4$ g and $s=6.5$ g. A dotplot of the 25 weights is roughly symmetric with no outliers. Construct and interpret a 95% confidence interval for the mean weight $\mu$ of all bags produced that day.

Topic 7.3主题 7.3

Justifying a Claim About a Population Mean Based on a Confidence Interval利用置信区间论证关于总体均值的说法

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Use a confidence interval to decide whether a claimed value of a mean is plausible.
  • Explain why an interval that contains a value does not prove the value is the mean.
  • Justify a claim that a mean is above or below a stated value using an entire interval.
  • Connect the confidence level of an interval to the significance level of a two-sided test.
  • 用置信区间判断所声称的均值是否合理。
  • 解释为什么区间包含某个值并不能证明该值就是均值。
  • 用整个区间论证均值高于或低于某个给定值的说法。
  • 把区间的置信水平与双侧检验的显著性水平联系起来。

A confidence interval is a set of plausible values for the parameter: values that are consistent with the data. This makes an interval a tool to justify a claim about a population mean. If the claimed value is inside the interval, the interval contains it: the claim is plausible, and the data do not provide convincing evidence against it. If the claimed value lies outside the interval (the interval does not contain it), the value is not plausible at that confidence level, and because the whole interval is on one side of the claim, you can say in which direction the mean seems to differ.

置信区间是参数的一组合理取值（plausible）：与数据相容的取值。这使区间成为论证关于总体均值的说法（justify a claim）的工具。若所声称的值在区间内，即区间包含（contains）它：该说法是合理的（consistent with 数据），数据没有提供令人信服的证据来反对它。若所声称的值在区间之外（区间不包含它），则在该置信水平下此值不合理；又因整个区间位于说法的一侧，可以指出均值看来朝哪个方向偏离。

Interval and claim

Claim $\mu=\mu_0$. If the interval contains $\mu_0$: $\mu_0$ is plausible; no convincing evidence that $\mu\ne\mu_0$. If the interval does not contain $\mu_0$: convincing evidence that $\mu\ne\mu_0$, and the interval shows the direction. A $C\%$ interval agrees with a two-sided test at significance level $\alpha=1-C/100$ (for example 95% and $\alpha=0.05$).

区间与说法

说法为 $\mu=\mu_0$。若区间包含 $\mu_0$：$\mu_0$ 合理，没有令人信服的证据表明 $\mu\ne\mu_0$。若区间不包含 $\mu_0$：有令人信服的证据表明 $\mu\ne\mu_0$，且区间显示方向。$C\%$ 的区间与显著性水平 $\alpha=1-C/100$ 的双侧检验结论一致（例如 95% 对应 $\alpha=0.05$）。

Notice what an interval cannot do. Containing 500 does not show that 500 is the mean: every value in the interval is also plausible, and a different sample would give a different interval. The only safe conclusion is that the claim is consistent with the data. The match between intervals and tests holds only for two-sided alternatives; a one-sided claim needs a one-sided test or a confidence level adjusted accordingly (a 90% interval corresponds to a one-sided test at $\alpha=0.05$).

注意区间做不到什么。包含 500 并不表明 500 就是均值：区间内的每个值都同样合理，换一个样本会得到不同的区间。唯一稳妥的结论是该说法与数据相容。区间与检验的对应只对双侧备择假设成立；单侧说法需要单侧检验，或相应调整置信水平（90% 的区间对应 $\alpha=0.05$ 的单侧检验）。

Topic 7.4主题 7.4

Setting Up a Test for a Population Mean建立总体均值的检验

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define the parameter $\mu$ in words with its population and units.
  • Write a null hypothesis $H_0:\mu=\mu_0$ and the correct alternative hypothesis in symbols and in words.
  • Choose a one-sided or two-sided alternative from the wording of the question before seeing the data.
  • State the conditions for a one-sample $t$ test and what each one protects.
  • 结合总体与单位用文字定义参数 $\mu$。
  • 用符号与文字写出零假设 $H_0:\mu=\mu_0$ 与正确的备择假设。
  • 在看数据之前，根据题意选择单侧或双侧备择假设。
  • 陈述单样本 $t$ 检验的条件，以及每个条件保护什么。

A significance test asks whether the sample provides convincing evidence against a specific claim about a parameter. The claim is the null hypothesis $H_0$; the suspicion that something differs is the alternative hypothesis $H_a$. For a population mean, the null hypothesis always states that $\mu$ equals one hypothesized value $\mu_0$:

显著性检验询问样本是否提供了令人信服的证据来反对某个关于参数的具体说法。该说法是零假设（null hypothesis）$H_0$；怀疑有差异的想法是备择假设（alternative hypothesis）$H_a$。对总体均值，零假设总是说 $\mu$ 等于某个假设值 $\mu_0$（hypothesized value）：

Hypotheses for one mean

$H_0:\mu=\mu_0$ against one of $H_a:\mu<\mu_0$, $H_a:\mu>\mu_0$, or $H_a:\mu\ne\mu_0$. Define $\mu$ as the population mean of a named variable for a named population, with units. Hypotheses are about parameters ($\mu$), never about statistics ($\bar{x}$).

单个均值的假设

$H_0:\mu=\mu_0$，对立面为 $H_a:\mu<\mu_0$、$H_a:\mu>\mu_0$ 或 $H_a:\mu\ne\mu_0$ 之一。要把 $\mu$ 定义为某指定总体中某指定变量的总体均值（population mean），并带上单位。假设涉及参数（$\mu$），绝不涉及统计量（$\bar{x}$）。

Choose the form of $H_a$ from the research question, before looking at the data. Words such as "less than", "fewer" or "lower" give $H_a:\mu<\mu_0$; "greater than", "exceeds" or "more" give $H_a:\mu>\mu_0$; "differs from", "is different" or "has changed" give $H_a:\mu\ne\mu_0$. Choosing the direction after seeing which way $\bar{x}$ fell is not allowed because it halves the p-value dishonestly.

根据研究问题，在看数据之前确定 $H_a$ 的形式。“少于”“更少”“更低”等词对应 $H_a:\mu<\mu_0$；“大于”“超过”“更多”对应 $H_a:\mu>\mu_0$；“不同于”“有差异”“发生变化”对应 $H_a:\mu\ne\mu_0$。看到 $\bar{x}$ 的方向后再选方向是不允许的，因为这样会不诚实地把 p 值减半。

The conditions for the one-sample $t$ test are the same as for the interval, and you check them the same way. Random: a random sample or randomized experiment, so the sample represents the population. 10% condition: $n<10\%$ of the population, so observations are nearly independent. Normal or large sample: population approximately normal, or $n\ge 30$, or a sample graph without strong skew and outliers.

单样本 $t$ 检验的条件与区间的相同，检验方法也相同。随机：随机样本或随机化实验，使样本代表总体。10% 条件：$n<10\%$（占总体的比例），使观测近似独立。正态或大样本：总体近似正态，或 $n\ge 30$，或样本图形没有明显偏斜和离群点。

  2. Null hypothesis: $H_0:\mu=500$ g, so $\mu_0=500$.
  3. Alternative hypothesis: "differs from" is two-sided, $H_a:\mu\ne 500$ g.

大米厂想知道平均每袋重量是否不同于标称的 500 克。

  1. 参数：$\mu$ = 该厂当天生产的全部米袋的真实平均重量（克）（总体均值）。
  2. 零假设：$H_0:\mu=500$ 克，所以 $\mu_0=500$。
  3. 备择假设：“不同于”是双侧的，$H_a:\mu\ne 500$ 克。

After the hypotheses and conditions are set, measure how far the sample mean is from the hypothesized mean in units of standard error. The test statistic is

在假设与条件确定后，用标准误为单位衡量样本均值与假设均值的距离。检验统计量是

One-sample t test

$$t=\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu_0}{s/\sqrt{n}},\qquad df=n-1$$ The p-value is the probability, computed assuming $H_0$ is true, of getting a sample mean at least as far from $\mu_0$ as the observed one, in the direction(s) of $H_a$. TI-84: STAT → TESTS → 2:T-Test; or use tcdf(lower,upper,df). For $H_a:\mu\ne\mu_0$, double the one-tail area.

单样本 t 检验

$$t=\dfrac{\bar{x}-\mu_0}{s/\sqrt{n}},\qquad df=n-1$$ p 值（p-value）是在 $H_0$ 为真的前提下，得到与 $\mu_0$ 的距离至少和观测值一样远的样本均值（按 $H_a$ 的方向）的概率。TI-84：STAT → TESTS → 2:T-Test；或用 tcdf(下限,上限,df)。对 $H_a:\mu\ne\mu_0$，要把单侧面积加倍。

Carry out a test in four steps. State the hypotheses with $\mu$ defined and a significance level $\alpha$ (use $0.05$ if none is given). 检验分四步进行。陈述假设，定义 $\mu$，并给出显著性水平 $\alpha$（未给出时用 $0.05$）。计划：写出程序名称（针对 $\mu$ 的单样本 $t$ 检验）并检验条件。计算：$\bar{x}$、$s$、$n$、$t$、$df$ 与 p 值。结论：把 p 值与 $\alpha$ 比较，并结合情境（in context）回答。

Interpreting the p-value and writing the conclusion解释 p 值并写出结论

If the p-value is less than or equal to $\alpha$, reject $H_0$: the data provide convincing evidence for $H_a$. If the p-value is greater than $\alpha$, fail to reject $H_0$: the data do not provide convincing evidence for $H_a$. Failing to reject is never the same as accepting $H_0$ or proving it true; it only says the sample was not extreme enough. A p-value is not the probability that $H_0$ is true; it is the probability of data this extreme if $H_0$ were true.

若 p 值小于或等于 $\alpha$，则拒绝 $H_0$：数据提供了支持 $H_a$ 的令人信服的证据（convincing evidence）。若 p 值大于 $\alpha$，则不拒绝（fail to reject）$H_0$：数据没有提供支持 $H_a$ 的令人信服的证据。不拒绝绝不等于接受或证明 $H_0$ 为真，它只说明样本不够极端。p 值不是 $H_0$ 为真的概率，而是假如 $H_0$ 为真时出现如此极端数据的概率。

Topic 7.6主题 7.6

Confidence Interval for the Difference of Two Population Means两个总体均值之差的置信区间

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Construct a two-sample $t$ interval for $\mu_1-\mu_2$ by hand and with 2-SampTInt.
  • State and check the conditions for two independent groups.
  • Use the calculator's degrees of freedom and explain the conservative alternative.
  • Distinguish a two-sample design from paired data.
  • Interpret an interval for a difference of means in context.
  • 手算并用 2-SampTInt 构造 $\mu_1-\mu_2$ 的双样本 $t$ 区间。
  • 陈述并检验两个独立组的条件。
  • 使用计算器给出的自由度，并解释保守的替代做法。
  • 区分双样本设计与配对数据。
  • 结合情境解释均值之差的区间。

To compare two populations (or two treatments), estimate the difference $\mu_1-\mu_2$ using the difference of the sample means $\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2$. The standard error of this difference combines the variation in both groups, and the interval is the two-sample t-interval:

比较两个总体（或两种处理）时，用样本均值之差 $\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2$ 估计 $\mu_1-\mu_2$。该差的标准误综合了两组的变异，区间称为双样本 t 区间（two-sample t-interval）：

Two-sample t interval for $\mu_1-\mu_2$

$$(\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2)\pm t^*\sqrt{\dfrac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{s_2^2}{n_2}}$$ Use the unpooled standard error. The degrees of freedom come from the calculator (STAT → TESTS → 0:2-SampTInt, with Pooled: No); a safe by-hand choice is the smaller of $n_1-1$ and $n_2-1$, which gives a slightly wider interval.

$\mu_1-\mu_2$ 的双样本 t 区间

$$(\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2)\pm t^*\sqrt{\dfrac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{s_2^2}{n_2}}$$ 使用非合并（unpooled）标准误。自由度由计算器给出（STAT → TESTS → 0:2-SampTInt，选 Pooled: No）；手算时稳妥的选择是 $n_1-1$ 与 $n_2-1$ 中较小者，得到的区间略宽。

Conditions: independent groups, each sample条件：独立的组，每个样本

The groups must be independent: knowing the values in one group tells you nothing about the other. Random: the data come from two independent random samples, or from a randomized experiment with units randomly assigned to two treatments. 10% condition: for random samples, each sample is less than 10% of its own population (not needed for randomized experiments). Normal or large sample: check each sample or group separately: approximately normal population, or $n\ge 30$ in each group, or a graph of each group with no strong skew and no outliers. Both samples must meet the condition.

两组必须独立（independent）：知道一组的数值，对另一组毫无信息。随机：数据来自两个独立的随机样本，或来自把单位随机分配到两种处理的随机化实验。10% 条件：对随机样本，每个样本都少于各自总体的 10%（随机化实验不需要）。正态或大样本：对每个样本或每组分别检验：总体近似正态，或每组 $n\ge 30$，或每组的图形没有明显偏斜和离群点。两个样本（both samples）都必须满足条件。

Two samples or paired data?双样本还是配对数据？

Before using this interval, decide how the data were collected. In a two-sample design the groups are separate collections of different individuals; no value in one group is linked to a value in the other. In paired (matched pairs) data, every value in one set is naturally linked to exactly one value in the other: the same subject measured twice, twins, or two matched individuals. Paired data are analyzed as one sample of differences (Topic 7.9). Using the two-sample formula on paired data ignores the pairing and usually wastes power.

使用该区间之前，先判断数据是如何收集的。双样本设计中，两组是不同个体的独立集合；一组中的任何数值都与另一组无关联。配对（paired，匹配对）数据中，一组的每个数值都自然地与另一组的恰好一个数值相连：同一对象测两次、双胞胎，或两个匹配的个体。配对数据按一个差值样本分析（主题 7.9）。对配对数据使用双样本公式会忽略配对关系，通常浪费检验功效。

Topic 7.7主题 7.7

Justifying a Claim About the Difference of Two Population Means Based on a Confidence Interval利用置信区间论证关于两个总体均值之差的说法

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Use an interval for $\mu_1-\mu_2$ to justify a claim about a difference in means.
  • Decide from the interval whether 0 is a plausible value for the difference.
  • Describe the direction and likely size of a difference using the whole interval.
  • Match a confidence level to a two-sided significance level.
  • 用 $\mu_1-\mu_2$ 的区间论证关于均值差异的说法。
  • 根据区间判断 0 是否为差的合理取值。
  • 利用整个区间描述差异的方向与可能的大小。
  • 把置信水平与双侧显著性水平对应起来。

If there were no difference between two population means, $\mu_1-\mu_2$ would equal 0. So, to evaluate a claim about a difference in means, look at where 0 falls relative to the confidence interval for $\mu_1-\mu_2$ (Figure 7.4). If the interval contains 0, then 0 is a plausible value for the difference: the data do not provide convincing evidence that the means differ. If the interval does not contain 0, the interval is entirely positive or entirely negative, and you can justify the claim that the means differ, in the direction the interval shows.

如果两个总体均值没有差异，则 $\mu_1-\mu_2$ 等于 0。因此要评价关于均值差异（difference）的说法，就看 0 相对于 $\mu_1-\mu_2$ 置信区间的位置（图 7.4）。若区间包含 0（contains 0），则 0 是差的合理取值：数据没有提供令人信服的证据表明均值不同。若区间不包含 0，则区间全为正或全为负，可以论证（justify）均值不同的说法，其方向由区间显示。

The interval also tells you how large the difference might be, something a test alone does not. An interval such as $(0.21,\,8.59)$ minutes excludes zero but includes tiny differences; whether the difference matters in practice is a judgment about context. Remember that the interval concerns the populations (or treatments) as defined by the design: a randomized experiment supports a causal claim for the units studied, a random sample supports generalization to its population, and an observational comparison supports only association.

区间还告诉你差异可能有多大，这是单独的检验给不了的。像 $(0.21,\,8.59)$ 分钟这样的区间不含零，但包含很小的差异；差异在实际中是否重要，要结合情境判断。记住区间针对的是设计所界定的总体（或处理）：随机化实验支持对所研究单位的因果结论，随机样本支持推广到其总体，而观察性比较只支持关联。

Topic 7.8主题 7.8

Setting Up a Test for the Difference of Two Population Means建立两个总体均值之差的检验

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ in context and decide which group is group 1.
  • Write $H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$ (equivalently $\mu_1-\mu_2=0$) with the correct alternative hypothesis.
  • Choose a one-sided or two-sided alternative from the wording of the question.
  • Set up the hypotheses for paired data in terms of the mean difference $\mu_d$.
  • 在情境中定义 $\mu_1$ 与 $\mu_2$，并确定谁是第 1 组。
  • 写出 $H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$（等价于 $\mu_1-\mu_2=0$）及正确的备择假设。
  • 根据题意选择单侧或双侧备择假设。
  • 用平均差值 $\mu_d$ 为配对数据建立假设。

A test comparing two means asks whether the sample data provide convincing evidence of a difference. The null hypothesis says there is no difference between the population means, and the alternative hypothesis says there is a difference (in one direction or either):

比较两个均值的检验询问样本数据是否提供了令人信服的差异证据。零假设说两个总体均值没有差异，备择假设（alternative hypothesis）说存在差异（某一方向或任一方向）：

Hypotheses for two means

$H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$ (that is, $\mu_1-\mu_2=0$) against $H_a:\mu_1<\mu_2$, $H_a:\mu_1>\mu_2$, or $H_a:\mu_1\ne\mu_2$. Describe $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ as population means (or true treatment means) with units, and say which group is which. For paired data, use one parameter instead: $\mu_d$, the true mean of the differences; $H_0:\mu_d=0$.

两个均值的假设

$H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$（即 $\mu_1-\mu_2=0$），对立面为 $H_a:\mu_1<\mu_2$、$H_a:\mu_1>\mu_2$ 或 $H_a:\mu_1\ne\mu_2$。把 $\mu_1$ 与 $\mu_2$ 描述为带单位的总体均值（或处理的真实均值），并说明谁是谁。配对数据则只用一个参数：$\mu_d$，差值的真实均值；$H_0:\mu_d=0$。

Choose the direction from the question's wording, and keep the order consistent: if $\mu_1$ is the new method and $\mu_2$ the old, "the new method gives higher scores" is $H_a:\mu_1>\mu_2$. Reversing the labels reverses the inequality. The conditions are those of Topic 7.6: independent random samples or randomized assignment, the 10% condition for samples, and Normality or large sample in each group.

根据题目措辞确定方向，并保持顺序一致：若 $\mu_1$ 是新方法、$\mu_2$ 是旧方法，“新方法得分更高”就是 $H_a:\mu_1>\mu_2$。交换标签会使不等号反向。条件与主题 7.6 相同：独立随机样本或随机分配、样本的 10% 条件，以及每组的正态性或大样本。

Two-sample t test

$$t=\dfrac{(\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2)-0}{\sqrt{\dfrac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{s_2^2}{n_2}}}$$ The degrees of freedom come from the calculator. TI-84: STAT → TESTS → 4:2-SampTTest with Pooled: No; the output gives the test statistic $t$, the p-value, and $df$. Use the same four steps as in Topic 7.5.

$$t=\dfrac{(\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2)-0}{\sqrt{\dfrac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{s_2^2}{n_2}}}$$ 自由度由计算器给出。TI-84：STAT → TESTS → 4:2-SampTTest，选 Pooled: No；输出给出检验统计量 $t$、p 值与 $df$。步骤与主题 7.5 相同的四步。

The two-sample t-test compares the observed difference of sample means with the value 0 that the null hypothesis predicts, in units of the standard error of the difference. The test statistic measures how many standard errors the difference is from 0. A p-value that is small means a difference this large would rarely occur by chance (or random assignment) if $\mu_1=\mu_2$. Conclusions use the same wording as Topic 7.5: compare the p-value with $\alpha$, then reject or fail to reject $H_0$ and state the evidence for $H_a$ in context.

Paired data: a one-sample t test on the differences配对数据：对差值做单样本 t 检验

When the data are paired (matched pairs), compute the difference within each pair and treat the differences as a single sample. The parameter is $\mu_d$, the true mean of the differences. The inference is a one-sample $t$ procedure applied to the differences: $t=\dfrac{\bar{x}_d-0}{s_d/\sqrt{n}}$ with $df=n-1$, where $n$ is the number of pairs. Conditions apply to the differences: the pairs are a random sample (or the order or assignment within pairs was random), the number of pairs is under 10% of the population, and the differences are approximately normal or the number of pairs is at least 30. The mean of the differences equals the difference of the means, but the standard deviation $s_d$ is usually much smaller than the spread within either set, which makes the pairing pay off.

当数据是配对（matched pairs）时，先在每一对内计算差值，再把差值当作一个样本。参数是 $\mu_d$，差值的真实均值。推断是对差值（differences）使用单样本 $t$ 程序：$t=\dfrac{\bar{x}_d-0}{s_d/\sqrt{n}}$，$df=n-1$，其中 $n$ 是对数。条件针对差值：各对是随机样本（或对内的顺序或分配是随机的）、对数少于总体的 10%，且差值近似正态或对数至少为 30。差值的均值（mean difference）等于均值之差，但 $s_d$ 通常远小于任一组内的离散程度，这正是配对的好处。

Worked Example 7.9c: paired test of a typing-training program例题 7.9c：打字训练项目的配对检验

Ten randomly chosen students are timed typing the same paragraph before and after training. Before: 52, 47, 61, 55, 49, 58, 63, 50, 57, 54; after: 48, 45, 55, 53, 47, 52, 60, 49, 52, 52 (seconds). Worked Example 7.10b: a full selection argument with conditions例题 7.10b：附条件的完整选择论证

A teacher gives the same 25 randomly chosen students a reading-speed test on paper and on a tablet, and asks whether the mean speed differs between formats.

  1. Which procedure? The variable is quantitative (words per minute), so a mean. Each student gives two linked measurements, so the data are paired. Choose a paired $t$ test of $H_0:\mu_d=0$ versus $H_a:\mu_d\ne 0$, with $d=\text{paper}-\text{tablet}$.
  2. Why not two-sample? The two sets are not independent: the same students appear in both.
  3. Conditions: random sample of students; 25 is under 10% of the students at a large school; with $n=25<30$, a graph of the 25 differences must show no strong skew or outliers.
  4. Why not a proportion? Speed is measured, not classified as success or failure.

老师让随机选出的同一批 25 名学生分别在纸质和平板上做阅读速度测验，并问两种形式的平均速度是否不同。

  1. 用哪个程序？变量是定量的（每分钟词数），所以是均值。每名学生提供两个相关联的测量，因此数据是配对的。选择配对 $t$ 检验，$H_0:\mu_d=0$ 对 $H_a:\mu_d\ne 0$，$d=\text{paper}-\text{tablet}$。
  2. 为什么不用双样本？两组数据不独立：同样的学生出现在两组中。
  3. 条件：学生为随机样本；25 人少于大型学校学生数的 10%；$n=25<30$，所以 25 个差值的图形必须没有明显偏斜或离群点。
  4. 为什么不用比例？速度是测量值，不是成功或失败的分类。
Exam tip考试提示

Choose the procedure by the data collection, not by the numbers given. Phrases that signal pairs: "the same subjects", "before and after", "twins", "matched". Phrases that signal two samples: "two independent random samples", "randomly assigned to two groups". Name the procedure in your answer; naming it shows your reasoning.

根据数据收集方式，而不是给出的数字，选择程序。提示配对的措辞：“同一批对象”“前后”“双胞胎”“匹配”。提示双样本的措辞：“两个独立随机样本”“随机分到两组”。作答时写出程序名称；写出名称能体现你的推理。

Common mistakes
  • Using $z$ for a mean because $n$ is large and $s$ is close to $\sigma$.
  • Treating a mean difference as a proportion because the answer is a percent.
  • Picking the procedure without checking its conditions.
常见错误
  • 因为 $n$ 大且 $s$ 接近 $\sigma$，就对均值用 $z$。
  • 因为答案是百分数，就把均值之差当作比例。
  • 选了程序却不检验其条件。

Prompt. A gardening company wants to know whether a new fertilizer increases the mean height of a pea plant after 30 days. It randomly assigns 24 seedlings, 12 to the new fertilizer and 12 to the standard fertilizer. Heights (cm): new fertilizer, $\bar{x}_1=31.8$, $s_1=4.6$; standard fertilizer, $\bar{x}_2=27.9$, $s_2=5.1$. Dotplots of both groups are roughly symmetric with no outliers. (a) State the hypotheses. (b) Check the conditions and name the procedure. (c) Find the test statistic and p-value. (d) Conclude at $\alpha=0.05$. (e) What kind of conclusion does the design allow?

PartModel answer
(a)$\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ are the true mean heights (cm) after 30 days of seedlings like these given the new and the standard fertilizer. $H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$; $H_a:\mu_1>\mu_2$.
(b)Two-sample $t$ test for $\mu_1-\mu_2$. Random assignment of seedlings to treatments; each group has $n=12<30$ but its dotplot is roughly symmetric with no outliers, so Normality is reasonable. (The 10% condition is not needed in an experiment.)
(c)$t=\dfrac{3.90}{\sqrt{\dfrac{4.6^2}{12}+\dfrac{5.1^2}{12}}}=\dfrac{3.90}{1.983}=1.97$, $df\approx 21.8$, p-value $\approx 0.0310$ (2-SampTTest, $\mu_1>\mu_2$, Pooled: No).
(d)Since 0.0310 is less than $0.05$, reject $H_0$. There is convincing evidence that the new fertilizer gives a greater mean pea-plant height after 30 days than the standard fertilizer.
(e)Because seedlings were randomly assigned, the difference can be attributed to the fertilizer (cause and effect). The seedlings were not a random sample of all pea plants, so the conclusion applies to seedlings like these.

题目。某园艺公司想知道新肥料能否使豌豆苗 30 天后的平均高度增加。公司把 24 株幼苗随机分配，12 株用新肥料，12 株用标准肥料。高度（厘米）：新肥料，$\bar{x}_1=31.8$，$s_1=4.6$；标准肥料，$\bar{x}_2=27.9$，$s_2=5.1$。两组点图都大致对称、无离群点。(a) 陈述假设。(b) 检验条件并写出程序名称。(c) 求检验统计量与 p 值。(d) 在 $\alpha=0.05$ 下作结论。(e) 该设计允许得出哪类结论？

部分参考作答
(a)$\mu_1$、$\mu_2$ 是与这些幼苗相似的幼苗使用新肥料与标准肥料 30 天后的真实平均高度（厘米）。$H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2$；$H_a:\mu_1>\mu_2$。
(b)针对 $\mu_1-\mu_2$ 的双样本 $t$ 检验。幼苗被随机分配到处理；每组 $n=12<30$，但点图大致对称且无离群点，故正态性合理。（实验不需要 10% 条件。）
(c)$t=\dfrac{3.90}{\sqrt{\dfrac{4.6^2}{12}+\dfrac{5.1^2}{12}}}=\dfrac{3.90}{1.983}=1.97$，$df\approx 21.8$，p 值 $\approx 0.0310$（2-SampTTest，$\mu_1>\mu_2$，Pooled: No）。
(d)由于 0.0310 小于 $0.05$，拒绝 $H_0$。有令人信服的证据表明新肥料使豌豆苗 30 天后的平均高度大于标准肥料。
(e)因为幼苗是随机分配的，差异可归因于肥料（因果）。幼苗不是所有豌豆苗的随机样本，所以结论适用于与这些相似的幼苗。
