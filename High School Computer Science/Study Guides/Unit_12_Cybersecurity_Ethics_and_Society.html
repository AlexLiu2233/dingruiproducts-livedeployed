Section 1 · Cybersecurity threats第 1 节 · 网络安全威胁

Cybersecurity Threats网络安全威胁

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Identify malware, phishing and social engineering in a short scenario and justify the label with evidence from the scenario.
  • Distinguish a virus, worm, Trojan, ransomware and spyware by how each spreads or what it does.
  • Spot the red flags in a suspicious email, including a link whose real host differs from its visible text.
  • Recommend one defence for each threat and explain which weakness it closes.
  • 识别短场景中的恶意软件、钓鱼和社会工程学（social engineering），并用场景中的证据说明理由。
  • 按传播方式或作用区分病毒、蠕虫、木马、勒索软件和间谍软件。
  • 发现可疑电子邮件中的危险信号，包括真实主机与显示文字不一致的链接。
  • 为每种威胁推荐一项防御措施，并解释它堵住了哪个弱点。
Three threat categories: know all three cold.三类威胁：完全掌握这三类。
  • Malware恶意软件: malicious software designed to damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access. Subtypes: virus (attaches to files and self-replicates), worm (spreads across networks without a host file), Trojan (disguises itself as legitimate software), ransomware (encrypts victim data and demands payment), spyware (records activity without consent). BC CS10 names malware, Trojans, viruses, phishing scams, identity fraud, and ransomware verbatim.：旨在破坏、干扰或获取未授权访问权限的恶意软件（malware）。子类型：病毒（附着于文件并自我复制）、蠕虫（无需宿主文件在网络中传播）、木马（伪装成合法软件）、勒索软件（加密受害者数据并索取赎金）、间谍软件（未经同意记录活动）。BC CS10 原文命名了恶意软件、木马、病毒、钓鱼诈骗、身份欺诈和勒索软件。
  • Phishing钓鱼: fraudulent attempts to obtain sensitive information (passwords, credit card numbers) by impersonating a trusted entity via email, SMS, or a fake website. Spear phishing targets a specific individual using personalised details; pharming redirects DNS to a fake site. Ontario ICS4U D2.1 lists phishing as an example ethical issue.：通过冒充可信实体（电子邮件、短信或虚假网站）获取敏感信息（密码、信用卡号）的欺诈行为（phishing，钓鱼）。鱼叉式钓鱼利用个人化细节针对特定个人；域欺骗将 DNS 重定向到虚假网站。安大略 ICS4U D2.1 将钓鱼列为伦理问题示例。
  • Social engineering社会工程学: manipulating people into revealing confidential information or taking actions that compromise security, by exploiting trust, authority, urgency, or fear rather than technical exploits. Examples: pretexting (fabricating a scenario), baiting (leaving an infected USB drive), tailgating (following someone through a secure door). CSTA 3A-NI-05 says: "Give examples to illustrate how sensitive data can be affected by malware and other attacks."：通过利用信任、权威、紧迫感或恐惧（而非技术漏洞）来操纵人们泄露机密信息或采取危害安全的行动（social engineering，社会工程学）。示例：借口攻击（捏造场景）、诱骗（留下受感染的 USB 驱动器）、尾随（跟随他人通过安全门）。CSTA 3A-NI-05 要求："举例说明敏感数据如何受到恶意软件和其他攻击的影响。"
Key distinction: malware exploits software vulnerabilities; social engineering exploits human vulnerabilities. Both are addressed in the threat model because the weakest link is often the user, not the code.关键区别：恶意软件利用软件漏洞；社会工程学利用人类漏洞。两者都纳入威胁模型，因为最薄弱的环节往往是用户，而非代码。
Worked Example 1: Identifying the attack type例题 1：识别攻击类型

Read each scenario and identify the threat category (malware, phishing, social engineering).阅读每个场景并识别威胁类别（恶意软件、钓鱼、社会工程学）。

Scenario A.场景 A。 You receive an email that looks like it is from your bank, asking you to "verify your account" by clicking a link and entering your password. The link goes to a site that looks identical to your bank but has a slightly different URL.你收到一封看起来来自你的银行的电子邮件，要求你通过点击链接并输入密码来"验证账户"。该链接指向一个看起来与你的银行相同但 URL 略有不同的网站。

Threat type: Phishing.威胁类型：钓鱼。 The attacker impersonates a trusted entity (the bank) to steal credentials. The fake URL is the tell-tale sign. Defence: inspect URLs carefully; go directly to the bank's site rather than clicking email links.攻击者冒充可信实体（银行）来窃取凭据。虚假 URL 是识别信号。防御：仔细检查 URL；直接访问银行网站而非点击电子邮件链接。

Scenario B.场景 B。 Someone downloads a free game from an unofficial site. When they run it, their files become encrypted and a message demands payment to restore them.有人从非官方网站下载了一款免费游戏。运行后，他们的文件被加密，并出现一条消息要求付款以恢复文件。

Threat type: Malware (ransomware).威胁类型：恶意软件（勒索软件）。 The game was a Trojan that delivered ransomware. Defence: download software only from trusted sources; maintain backups.该游戏是一个传递勒索软件的木马。防御：仅从可信来源下载软件；保持备份。

Scenario C.场景 C。 A caller claims to be from IT support and tells an employee their account has been compromised. They ask the employee to read out their password so IT can "fix" the problem immediately.一名来电者自称来自 IT 支持，告诉员工其账户已被入侵。他们要求员工读出密码，以便 IT 可以立即"修复"问题。

Threat type: Social engineering (pretexting).威胁类型：社会工程学（借口攻击）。 No malware is needed; the attacker exploits authority and urgency. Legitimate IT support will never ask for a password. Defence: verify identities through official channels; never share passwords verbally.不需要恶意软件，攻击者利用权威和紧迫感。合法的 IT 支持永远不会要求提供密码。防御：通过官方渠道验证身份；切勿口头分享密码。

An email reaches a student's inbox, shown in Figure 1. List its red flags, name the attack category, and say what the student should do.一名学生的收件箱里收到一封电子邮件，见图 1。列出其中的危险信号，说出攻击类别，并说明学生应该怎么做。

Step 1. The sender. The address ends in northbank-alerts.example, but the bank's own domain is northbank.example. A display name can say anything; the domain after the @ is what identifies the sender. Red flag 1: a look-alike domain.步骤 1. 发件人。地址以 northbank-alerts.example 结尾，而银行自己的域名是 northbank.example。显示名称可以随便写；@ 后面的域名才能标识发件人。危险信号 1：外观相似的域名。

Step 2. Pressure. The subject threatens a lock within 24 hours. Urgency and fear are social engineering levers: they push the reader to act before checking. Red flag 2.步骤 2. 施压。主题威胁账户将在 24 小时内被锁定。紧迫感和恐惧是社会工程学的杠杆：它们促使读者在核实之前就行动。危险信号 2。

Step 3. The greeting. "Dear customer" is generic, so the sender may not know who you are. This flag is weak on its own, because real organisations also send mass emails, but it adds to the pattern. Red flag 3.步骤 3. 称呼。"尊敬的客户"是通用称呼，说明发件人可能并不知道你是谁。这个信号单独出现时较弱，因为正规机构也会群发邮件，但它会让整体模式更可疑。危险信号 3。

Step 4. The link. The visible text reads www.northbank.example/login, but hovering over the link shows the real destination, northbank.example.verify-now.example/login. Read a host name from right to left: the last two labels, verify-now.example, name who runs the site (for a multi-part suffix such as co.uk, take three labels), and everything to their left is a subdomain that the site's owner can choose freely. So this link leads to the owner of verify-now.example, not to the bank. Red flags 4 and 5: the text and the target differ, and the real host is not the bank's.步骤 4. 链接。显示文字是 www.northbank.example/login，但鼠标悬停在链接上会显示真实目的地 northbank.example.verify-now.example/login。主机名要从右往左读：最后两段 verify-now.example 说明谁在运营这个网站（遇到 co.uk 这类多段后缀时，取最后三段），其左边的部分只是该网站所有者可以随意取的子域名。因此这个链接指向 verify-now.example 的所有者，而不是银行。危险信号 4 和 5：显示文字与目标不一致，且真实主机不是银行的。

Step 5. Category and response. This is phishing: an impersonation attempt by email that tries to collect a password. No software has run yet, so it is not malware (an attachment could change that). The student should not click, reply or type a password. Instead, open the bank's site by typing its address or use its official app, and report the email. If a password was already entered, change it at the real site at once, change it anywhere it was reused, and turn on MFA. Why this works: the safe response leaves the attacker's channel entirely and checks with the real organisation.步骤 5. 类别与应对。这是钓鱼（phishing）：通过电子邮件冒充他人、试图骗取密码。目前还没有任何软件运行，所以它不是恶意软件（附件可能改变这一点）。学生不应点击、回复或输入密码，而应自己输入网址或使用官方应用打开银行网站，并举报这封邮件。如果已经输入了密码，要立刻在真正的网站上修改，凡是重复使用过该密码的地方也都要修改，并开启 MFA。为什么有效：安全的做法完全离开攻击者的渠道，直接向真正的机构核实。

Exam tip考试提示

Command words: identify means name the category (malware, phishing or social engineering); justify means quote the evidence from the scenario, such as a sender domain that differs from the bank's; recommend means name one defence and the weakness it closes. Common errors: calling every attack a virus or "hacking", writing "it looks fake" without evidence, and giving a defence that does not match the threat (a strong password does not help against ransomware that is already on the machine).

指令词：identify（识别）是指说出类别（恶意软件、钓鱼或社会工程学）；justify（说明理由）是指引用场景中的证据，例如与银行不一致的发件域名；recommend（推荐）是指说出一项防御措施及其堵住的弱点。常见错误：把所有攻击都叫作病毒或"黑客攻击"，只写"看起来是假的"而不给证据，以及给出与威胁不匹配的防御（强密码对已经在机器上的勒索软件没有帮助）。

Check your understanding自我检测
1. What separates a worm from a virus?1. 蠕虫与病毒的区别是什么？

A virus attaches itself to a host file and spreads when that file is run or shared. A worm is a standalone program that copies itself across a network without needing a host file or a user to open anything, so it can spread much faster.

病毒附着在宿主文件上，在该文件被运行或共享时传播。蠕虫是独立的程序，无需宿主文件，也无需用户打开任何东西，就能通过网络自我复制，因此传播速度可以快得多。

2. A text message says a parcel is held and asks you to follow a link to pay a small fee. Name the category and one defence.2. 一条短信说包裹被扣留，要求你点击链接支付一笔小额费用。说出攻击类别和一项防御措施。

Phishing by text message (sometimes called smishing): it impersonates a delivery company to collect payment details. Defence: do not follow the link; check the delivery through the company's official app or website, typed in yourself.

短信钓鱼（有时称为 smishing）：冒充快递公司来骗取付款信息。防御：不要点击链接；通过自己输入网址或官方应用，向快递公司核实包裹情况。

3. A link's real host is login.northbank.example.verify-now.example. Who runs the site?3. 某链接的真实主机是 login.northbank.example.verify-now.example。谁在运营这个网站？

Read from the right: the last two labels are verify-now.example, so the owner of that domain runs it. The labels login.northbank.example to the left are just a subdomain name that owner chose, and they are chosen to look like the bank.

从右往左读：最后两段是 verify-now.example，所以该域名的所有者在运营它。左边的 login.northbank.example 只是所有者取的子域名，目的是看起来像银行。

4. Why do backups defend against ransomware but not against spyware?4. 为什么备份能防御勒索软件，却不能防御间谍软件？

Ransomware attacks availability: it locks your files, and a clean backup lets you restore them without paying. Spyware attacks confidentiality: it copies what you do or type to someone else, and restoring files does not take back what was already stolen. Spyware needs prevention and removal, such as updates and trusted sources.

勒索软件攻击的是可用性：它锁住你的文件，而干净的备份让你无需付款就能恢复。间谍软件攻击的是保密性：它把你的操作或输入复制给别人，恢复文件无法收回已被窃取的内容。对付间谍软件需要预防和清除，例如及时更新和只用可信来源。

Going deeper: threat actors and the attack surface深入：威胁行为者和攻击面

CSTA 3A-NI-06 says: "Recommend security measures to address various scenarios based on factors such as efficiency, feasibility, and ethical impacts." Understanding who attacks and why is the first step in choosing proportionate defences. Threat actors range from opportunistic script-kiddies running pre-built malware kits, to organised criminal groups monetising ransomware, to state-sponsored actors conducting espionage. The attack surface is the sum of all the different points where an attacker could try to enter or extract data: network ports, software interfaces, physical hardware, and most importantly, people. Reducing the attack surface means keeping software patched, disabling unused services, and training users to recognise social engineering, because the human is consistently the easiest point of entry. BC Computer Studies 10 Curricular Competency "Examine how cultural beliefs, values, and ethical positions affect the development and use of technologies" applies here: norms around sharing passwords, downloading software, and clicking email links are cultural, not purely technical.CSTA 3A-NI-06 要求："根据效率、可行性和伦理影响等因素，推荐针对各种场景的安全措施。"了解谁在攻击以及原因是选择适当防御措施的第一步。威胁行为者范围从运行预建恶意软件套件的机会主义脚本小子，到将勒索软件货币化的有组织犯罪团伙，再到进行间谍活动的国家支持行为者。攻击面是攻击者可能尝试进入或提取数据的所有不同点的总和：网络端口、软件接口、物理硬件，以及最重要的：人。减少攻击面意味着保持软件补丁更新、禁用未使用的服务，以及培训用户识别社会工程学，因为人类始终是最容易进入的点。BC Computer Studies 10 课程能力"检查文化信仰、价值观和伦理立场如何影响技术的开发和使用"在这里适用：关于共享密码、下载软件和点击电子邮件链接的规范是文化性的，而非纯粹技术性的。

Encryption and Authentication加密与身份验证

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Explain the difference between symmetric and asymmetric encryption and state which key does what.
  • Encrypt and decrypt with a Caesar cipher and explain why its tiny key space makes it insecure.
  • Trace a message from sender to recipient using public-key encryption, saying which key is used at each step.
  • Explain why HTTPS uses both kinds of encryption.
  • Describe what a hash is, why a salt is added, and how a hash differs from encryption.
  • Decide whether a login uses multi-factor authentication by naming the factor type of each step.
  • 解释对称加密与非对称加密的区别，并说明各个密钥的作用。
  • 用凯撒密码加密和解密，并解释其极小的密钥空间为何使它不安全。
  • 追踪一条使用公钥加密的消息从发送方到接收方的过程，并说明每一步使用哪个密钥。
  • 解释为什么 HTTPS 同时使用两种加密。
  • 描述什么是哈希（hash），为什么要加盐，以及哈希与加密有何不同。
  • 通过说出每一步的因素类型，判断一次登录是否使用了多因素认证。
Two encryption models: know the difference cold.两种加密模型：熟记区别。
  • Symmetric encryption对称加密: the same key is used to encrypt and decrypt. Fast; suitable for large data (e.g., encrypting a hard drive with AES). Problem: how do you share the key securely in the first place?：使用相同的密钥进行加密和解密。速度快；适合大数据（如使用 AES 加密硬盘）。问题：如何在一开始就安全地共享密钥？
  • Asymmetric encryption非对称加密: uses a mathematically linked key pair: a public key (shareable with anyone) and a private key (kept secret). Worked Example 3: The padlock analogy for asymmetric encryption例题 3：非对称加密的挂锁类比

Worked Example 4: Caesar cipher and key space例题 4：凯撒密码与密钥空间

A Caesar cipher shifts every letter forward by a key k and wraps from Z back to A. (a) Encrypt MEET AT NOON with k = 3. (b) Decrypt the result. (c) An observer sees PHHW DW QRRQ and does not know the key. How many keys must be tried at most, and what does that say about security?凯撒密码把每个字母向后移动密钥 k 位，并从 Z 回绕到 A。(a) 用 k = 3 加密 MEET AT NOON。(b) 解密结果。(c) 观察者看到 PHHW DW QRRQ，但不知道密钥。最多需要试多少个密钥？这说明了什么安全问题？

(a) Encrypt. Number the letters A = 0, B = 1, ..., Z = 25. To encrypt, add the key and wrap around: $c = (p + k) \bmod 26$. With $k = 3$: M (12) becomes 15 = P, E (4) becomes 7 = H, T (19) becomes 22 = W, N (13) becomes 16 = Q, O (14) becomes 17 = R, A becomes D. Spaces stay. So MEET AT NOON becomes PHHW DW QRRQ. Wrapping matters near the end of the alphabet: X (23) plus 3 is 26, which wraps to 0 = A.(a) 加密。给字母编号 A = 0，B = 1，……，Z = 25。加密时加上密钥并循环回绕：$c = (p + k) \bmod 26$。取 $k = 3$：M（12）变为 15 = P，E（4）变为 7 = H，T（19）变为 22 = W，N（13）变为 16 = Q，O（14）变为 17 = R，A 变为 D。空格保持不变。于是 MEET AT NOON 变成 PHHW DW QRRQ。接近字母表末尾时要回绕：X（23）加 3 得 26，回绕为 0 = A。

(b) Decrypt. Subtract the same key: $p = (c - k) \bmod 26$. The same secret key does both jobs, so this is symmetric encryption. The code below does both directions and also tries every key.(b) 解密。减去同一个密钥：$p = (c - k) \bmod 26$。同一个秘密密钥完成两项工作，所以这是对称加密。下面的代码完成两个方向，并尝试所有密钥。

def shift_letter(ch, k):
    if "A" <= ch <= "Z":
        return chr((ord(ch) - ord("A") + k) % 26 + ord("A"))
    return ch          # spaces and punctuation stay as they are

def caesar(text, k):
    return "".join(shift_letter(ch, k) for ch in text)

cipher = caesar("MEET AT NOON", 3)
print(cipher)                 # encrypt with key 3
print(caesar(cipher, -3))     # decrypt with the same key, reversed

# brute force: try every possible key
for k in range(1, 26):
    guess = caesar("PHHW DW QRRQ", -k)
    if k <= 4:
        print(k, guess)
print(26 - 1, "possible keys")

# Output:
# PHHW DW QRRQ
# MEET AT NOON
# 1 OGGV CV PQQP
# 2 NFFU BU OPPO
# 3 MEET AT NOON
# 4 LDDS ZS MNNM
# 25 possible keys

(c) How many keys, and is this secure? A shift of 0 or 26 leaves the text unchanged, so there are only 25 useful keys. Trying all 25 takes a computer far less than a second, and the readable line appears at key 3. A modern symmetric cipher such as AES with a 128-bit key has $2^{128} \approx 3.4 \times 10^{38}$ possible keys. At an assumed $10^{12}$ guesses per second that would take about $10^{19}$ years to search. The lesson: a cipher's security should rest on a huge key space and a secret key, not on keeping the method secret. The Caesar cipher is a teaching tool, not protection.(c) 有多少个密钥，安全吗？位移 0 或 26 不改变文本，所以只有 25 个有用的密钥。试遍 25 个密钥，计算机用不了一秒钟，在密钥 3 时就会出现可读的句子。现代对称密码（例如使用 128 位密钥的 AES）有 $2^{128} \approx 3.4 \times 10^{38}$ 个可能的密钥。假设每秒猜测 $10^{12}$ 次，要搜索完大约需要 $10^{19}$ 年。启示：密码的安全性应建立在巨大的密钥空间和保密的密钥之上，而不是保密算法本身。凯撒密码只是教学工具，不能提供保护。

Why this works. Counting keys turns "is it secure?" into arithmetic: 25 keys can be tried in an instant, $2^{128}$ keys cannot.为什么有效。数一数密钥个数，就把"它安全吗"变成了算术问题：25 个密钥瞬间即可试完，$2^{128}$ 个则不可能。

Going deeper: passwords, hashing, and salting深入：密码、哈希与加盐

Websites should not store passwords in plaintext. Instead, they store a hash: the output of a one-way function that converts the password into a fixed-length string (e.g., SHA-256 produces a 256-bit output). When you log in, the site hashes your input and compares it to the stored hash; the original password is never stored. The problem: if two users have the same password, they produce the same hash, making it easy for an attacker with the hash database to use precomputed "rainbow tables." The solution is salting: add a unique random string (the salt) to each password before hashing, making each stored hash unique even for identical passwords. CSTA 3A-NI-07 on security tradeoffs and CSTA 3B-NI-04 ("Compare ways software developers protect devices and information from unauthorized access") cover this design tradeoff. Ontario ICS4U D2.1 lists "sharing passwords" as an ethical issue; understanding why plaintext password storage is negligent is the flip side of that ethics question.网站不应以明文存储密码。相反，它们存储哈希值：一种将密码转换为固定长度字符串的单向函数的输出（如 SHA-256 产生 256 位输出）。登录时，网站对你的输入进行哈希并与存储的哈希进行比较；原始密码从不存储。问题：如果两个用户有相同的密码，它们产生相同的哈希，使得拥有哈希数据库的攻击者可以使用预先计算的"彩虹表"。解决方案是加盐：在哈希之前向每个密码添加唯一的随机字符串（盐），使每个存储的哈希唯一，即使对于相同的密码也是如此。CSTA 3A-NI-07 关于安全权衡和 CSTA 3B-NI-04（"比较软件开发人员保护设备和信息免受未授权访问的方式"）涵盖了这种设计权衡。安大略 ICS4U D2.1 将"共享密码"列为伦理问题，理解为什么明文密码存储是疏忽大意，是这个伦理问题的另一面。

Group B fails $0.12 / 0.02 = 6$ times as often.(a) 错误率。总体：$70 / 1000 = 0.07$，即 7%。A 组：$10 / 500 = 0.02$，即 2%。B 组：$60 / 500 = 0.12$，即 12%。B 组的失败频率是 A 组的 $0.12 / 0.02 = 6$ 倍。

(b) Why the overall rate misleads. A single 7% looks acceptable but hides a six-fold gap, because the average mixes a good result with a poor one. Always report the rate per group.(b) 为什么总体错误率会误导人。单独一个 7% 看起来可以接受，却掩盖了六倍的差距，因为平均值把好结果和差结果混在了一起。一定要分别报告每个组的错误率。

(c) Finding the source. The numbers show that bias exists, not why. Check the three entry points: Was one group underrepresented in the training data? Does a feature behave differently for the groups, such as the camera or lighting set-up? Was the test team diverse enough to notice? Test each cause rather than assume one.(c) 找出来源。这些数字说明偏见存在，却没有说明原因。检查三个进入点：训练数据中某个群体是否代表性不足？某个特征对不同群体的表现是否不同，例如摄像头或光线设置？测试团队是否足够多元，能够注意到问题？应逐一检验各种原因，而不是先假定其中一个。

(d) Responses. Add representative training images, set a target that every group must meet before release, retest per group, and offer a fallback such as a PIN so a failure does not lock a person out. Why this works: splitting the results by group turns a vague worry ("is it fair?") into a measurable gap that can be tracked after each fix.(d) 应对办法。增加有代表性的训练图像，规定每个群体在发布前都必须达到的目标，按群体重新测试，并提供 PIN 码等备用方式，使失败不会把人挡在门外。为什么有效：按群体拆分结果，把模糊的担忧（"公平吗？"）变成可测量的差距，可以在每次改进后持续跟踪。

Data centres that power the internet and cloud computing are a significant and growing energy consumer. What is phishing and how do I protect against it?什么是网络钓鱼，如何防范？

Phishing is a fraudulent attempt to obtain sensitive information like passwords or credit card numbers by impersonating a trusted entity through email, SMS, or a fake website. To protect yourself, hover over links to preview the real URL, verify sender addresses, and be sceptical of unexpected attachments.网络钓鱼是通过电子邮件、短信或假网站冒充可信实体，企图骗取密码或信用卡号等敏感信息的欺诈行为。要防范，可将鼠标悬停在链接上预览真实网址、核实发件人地址，并对意外的附件保持警惕。

What are the different types of malware?恶意软件有哪些不同类型？

Malware is malicious software designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorised access, and its main types include viruses that attach to files, worms that self-replicate across a network, Trojans disguised as legitimate software, ransomware that encrypts your files for payment, and spyware that secretly monitors you.恶意软件是旨在破坏、损害或非法获取访问权限的软件，主要类型包括附着在文件上的病毒、在网络上自我复制的蠕虫、伪装成合法软件的特洛伊木马、加密文件勒索付款的勒索软件，以及秘密监视你的间谍软件。

What is the difference between copyright and fair use?版权和合理使用有什么区别？

Copyright is automatic legal protection for original creative works from the moment of creation, giving the creator control over copying and distribution, and software is protected by copyright by default. Fair use is a limited exception in the US allowing use of copyrighted material for purposes like education, criticism, and parody; Canada and the UK have a related but separate doctrine called fair dealing.版权是对原创作品从创作之时起的自动法律保护，赋予创作者对复制和分发的控制权，软件默认受版权保护。合理使用是美国的一项有限例外，允许出于教育、评论和讽刺等目的使用受版权保护的材料；加拿大和英国有相关但不同的制度，称为公平处理（fair dealing）。
