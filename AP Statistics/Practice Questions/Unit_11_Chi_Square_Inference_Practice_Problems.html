Q 4第4题MEDIUM中等3.15 The chi-square statistic and distribution3.15 卡方统计量与卡方分布CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
Two chi-square tests each report the same statistic, $\chi^2=9.0$. Test A has $\text{df}=2$ and Test B has $\text{df}=8$. Without calculating either p-value, which statement is correct?
两项卡方检验报告了相同的统计量 $\chi^2=9.0$。检验 A 的 $\text{df}=2$，检验 B 的 $\text{df}=8$。不计算 p 值，下列哪项正确？
  1. (A) Test B has the larger p-value, because the mean of a chi-square distribution equals its degrees of freedom, and 9.0 is close to 8 but far above 2.检验 B 的 p 值更大，因为卡方分布的均值等于自由度，9.0 接近 8，却远高于 2。
  2. (B) Test A has the larger p-value, because fewer degrees of freedom means fewer ways for the counts to differ from the null hypothesis.检验 A 的 p 值更大，因为自由度越小，计数偏离原假设的方式越少。
  3. (C) The two p-values are equal, because both come from the same statistic.两个 p 值相等，因为它们来自同一个统计量。
  4. (D) The p-values cannot be compared unless the sample sizes are given.除非给出样本量，否则无法比较两个 p 值。
