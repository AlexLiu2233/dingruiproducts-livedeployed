Topic 11.1专题 11.1

Electric Current电流

Learning objectives学习目标

  • 把电流定义（define）为电荷通过横截面的平均速率 $I=\Delta q/\Delta t$，并在给定电流下计算（calculate）通过的电荷量或所需时间。

Every circuit in this unit begins with the same question: how fast does charge move past a point, and what carries it? Electric current is the rate at which charge passes through a cross-sectional area of a wire. Its SI unit is the ampere, $1\,\text{A}=1\,\text{C/s}$.

本单元的每个电路都从同一个问题开始：电荷以多快的速率通过某一点，又由谁来携带？电流（electric current）是电荷通过导线横截面的速率，其 SI 单位是安培，$1\,\text{A}=1\,\text{C/s}$。

Current as a rate电流是变化率
$$I=\frac{dq}{dt}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad q=\int_{t_1}^{t_2} I\,dt$$

If $I$ changes with time, the charge that crosses the section between $t_1$ and $t_2$ is the area under the $I$-$t$ graph.

若 $I$ 随时间变化，则在 $t_1$ 到 $t_2$ 之间通过该截面的电荷量等于 $I$-$t$ 图像下的面积。

Current as an average rate电流是平均变化率
$$I=\frac{\Delta q}{\Delta t}$$

$\Delta q$ is the charge that passes a cross section of the wire during the time interval $\Delta t$, so $I$ is the average current over that interval and $\Delta q=I\,\Delta t$. The unit is the ampere, $1\,\text{A}=1\,\text{C/s}$. For a steady current the average equals the instantaneous value.

$\Delta q$ 是在时间间隔 $\Delta t$ 内通过导线横截面的电荷量，因此 $I$ 是该间隔内的平均电流，且 $\Delta q=I\,\Delta t$。单位是安培，$1\,\text{A}=1\,\text{C/s}$。对于稳恒电流，平均值等于瞬时值。

Drift velocity and $I=nqv_dA$漂移速度与 $I=nqv_dA$

A metal contains a fixed number of mobile charge carriers per unit volume, $n$, each with charge $q$. With no field they move randomly and there is no net flow. A potential difference across the wire sets up an electric field inside it, and each carrier acquires a small average drift velocity $v_d$ superimposed on the random motion.

金属中单位体积内有固定数目 $n$ 的可移动载流子，每个带电荷 $q$。无电场时它们做无规运动、没有净流动。导线两端的电势差在导线内建立电场，使每个载流子在无规运动之上获得一个很小的平均漂移速度（drift velocity）$v_d$。

To find the current, count the charge that crosses a cross section of area $A$ during a short time $dt$. Only carriers within a distance $v_d\,dt$ upstream reach it, and they occupy a volume $A\,v_d\,dt$. The charge in that volume is therefore $dq=(nA\,v_d\,dt)\,q$, and dividing by $dt$ gives the result.

要求电流，就数一数通过面积为 $A$ 的截面的电荷，时间取很短的 $dt$。只有位于上游距离 $v_d\,dt$ 以内的载流子能到达该截面，它们占据的体积为 $A\,v_d\,dt$。因此该体积内的电荷为 $dq=(nA\,v_d\,dt)\,q$，再除以 $dt$ 即得结果。

Current from drift由漂移求电流
$$I=\frac{dq}{dt}=nqv_dA$$

$n$ is the carrier density (m$^{-3}$), $q$ the charge per carrier, $v_d$ the drift speed and $A$ the cross-sectional area.

$n$ 为载流子数密度（m$^{-3}$），$q$ 为每个载流子的电荷，$v_d$ 为漂移速率，$A$ 为横截面积。

Common Mistake: electrons race around the circuit The drift speed in a household wire is a fraction of a millimetre per second. The lamp lights at once because the field is established along the whole wire almost instantly and every carrier starts to drift together, not because one electron travels from the switch to the bulb. If the current in a section is zero, the net motion of the carriers is zero, but individual carriers still move randomly at high speed.
常见错误：电子在电路里飞奔 家用导线中的漂移速率只有每秒零点几毫米。灯立即亮起，是因为电场几乎瞬间沿整条导线建立、所有载流子同时开始漂移，而不是某个电子从开关跑到灯泡。若某段导线中电流为零，说明载流子的净运动为零，但单个载流子仍以很高的速率无规运动。

Current density电流密度

The current through a wire says nothing about how it is spread across the cross section. Current density $\vec J$ is the current per unit area, and it is a vector that points in the direction a positive carrier would drift. For a uniform current, $J=I/A$. Because $I=nqv_dA$, the current density in terms of the carriers is $\vec J=nq\vec v_d$.

导线中的电流并不说明电流在截面上如何分布。电流密度（current density）$\vec J$ 是单位面积上的电流，它是矢量，方向为正载流子的漂移方向。电流均匀时 $J=I/A$。由于 $I=nqv_dA$，用载流子表示的电流密度为 $\vec J=nq\vec v_d$。

A potential difference across a conductor creates a field inside it. The field and the current density in an ohmic material are proportional, with the resistivity $\rho$ as the constant of proportionality:

导体两端的电势差在导体内产生电场。在欧姆材料中，场与电流密度成正比，比例常数是电阻率（resistivity）$\rho$：

Field, resistivity and current density电场、电阻率与电流密度
$$\vec E=\rho\,\vec J$$

A larger $\rho$ needs a stronger field to drive the same current density. Topic 11.3 turns this into $V=IR$.

$\rho$ 越大，驱动同样的电流密度所需的电场越强。专题 11.3 会把它转化为 $V=IR$。

When $J$ varies across the cross section, the total current is the flux of $\vec J$ through the area, $I=\int\vec J\cdot d\vec A$. For a cylindrical wire in which $J$ depends only on the distance $r$ from the axis, the area element is a thin ring, $dA=2\pi r\,dr$.

当 $J$ 在截面上变化时，总电流是 $\vec J$ 通过该面积的通量 $I=\int\vec J\cdot d\vec A$。对于 $J$ 只依赖于到轴线距离 $r$ 的圆柱形导线，面积元是一个细圆环，$dA=2\pi r\,dr$。

Total current from a current density由电流密度求总电流
$$I=\int \vec J\cdot d\vec A=\int_0^R J(r)\,2\pi r\,dr$$

Integrate over rings of radius $r$ and thickness $dr$; this is the same strategy as the shell integrals of Unit 8.

在半径 $r$、厚度 $dr$ 的圆环上积分；策略与第 8 单元的球壳积分相同。

Charge carriers and the potential difference载流子与电势差

Charge moves through a circuit in response to an electric potential difference, which is sometimes called electromotive force, or emf. A battery supplies this potential difference, and the charge carriers in the wires move around the circuit as a result. In common circuits the carriers that actually move are electrons, which carry negative charge.

电荷在电势差的作用下在电路中运动，电势差有时也称为电动势（emf）。电池提供这个电势差，导线中的载流子因此在电路中运动。在常见电路中，真正运动的载流子是电子，它们带负电荷。

Common Mistake: zero current means nothing is moving If the current in a section of wire is zero, the net motion of the charge carriers in that wire is zero. Individual carriers still move randomly and do not have zero speed. Zero current means no net flow of charge, not motionless carriers.
常见错误：电流为零就是什么都不动 若某段导线中的电流为零，则该段导线中载流子的净运动为零。单个载流子仍在无规运动，速率并不为零。电流为零表示没有电荷的净流动，而不是载流子静止不动。
History: Volta's pile历史：伏打电堆

In 1800 Alessandro Volta described his "pile" in a letter to the Royal Society of London: a stack of zinc and copper (or silver) discs separated by cloth or card soaked in brine. It was the first source of a steady current, so for the first time charge could be kept moving around a circuit.

1800 年，亚历山德罗·伏打（Alessandro Volta）在致伦敦皇家学会的信中描述了他的“电堆”：把锌片和铜片（或银片）交替叠放，中间隔着浸过盐水的布或纸板。它是第一个能提供稳定电流的电源，人们第一次能让电荷持续地在电路中流动。

Voltaic pile (Wikipedia): how the stack was built and why it works, a five-minute read.

伏打电堆（英文维基百科 Voltaic pile）：电堆的构造与工作原理，约五分钟阅读。

Current is a scalar with a direction电流是有方向的标量

Although we draw arrows on wires, current does not obey vector addition and has no components. Its direction is relative to the carriers and the wire, not to space, so a current turning a corner is still just $I$. Currents add as numbers: two wires carrying $2\,\text{A}$ and $3\,\text{A}$ into a junction supply $5\,\text{A}$ regardless of the angle between them.

虽然我们在导线上画箭头，但电流不遵守矢量加法，也没有分量。它的方向是相对于载流子和导线而言的，而不是相对于空间，所以电流拐弯后仍只是 $I$。电流按数值相加：两根分别通有 $2\,\text{A}$ 和 $3\,\text{A}$ 的导线流入节点，不论夹角多大，总共供给 $5\,\text{A}$。

The conventional current points the way positive charge would move, from the high-potential terminal around the circuit to the low-potential terminal. In metals the carriers are electrons, so the carriers move opposite to the conventional current. Unless stated otherwise, schematics in this unit use conventional current.

常规电流（conventional current）指向正电荷运动的方向，即从高电势端出发经电路流向低电势端。金属中的载流子是电子，所以电子的运动方向与常规电流相反。除非另有说明，本单元的电路图均采用常规电流。

Worked Example: Drift speed in a copper wire例题：铜导线中的漂移速率

A copper wire of diameter $2.0\,\text{mm}$ carries $5.0\,\text{A}$. Copper has $n=8.5\times10^{28}\,\text{m}^{-3}$ free electrons and $q=1.6\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$ in magnitude, and $\rho=1.7\times10^{-8}\,\Omega\cdot\text{m}$. Find $v_d$, the current density and the field in the wire.

直径 $2.0\,\text{mm}$ 的铜导线通有 $5.0\,\text{A}$。铜的自由电子数密度 $n=8.5\times10^{28}\,\text{m}^{-3}$，电荷大小 $q=1.6\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$，电阻率 $\rho=1.7\times10^{-8}\,\Omega\cdot\text{m}$。求 $v_d$、电流密度以及导线内的电场。

Step 1: Cross-sectional area第 1 步：横截面积
The radius is $1.0\times10^{-3}\,\text{m}$.半径为 $1.0\times10^{-3}\,\text{m}$。
$$A=\pi r^2=\pi(1.0\times10^{-3})^2=3.1\times10^{-6}\,\text{m}^2$$
Step 2: Drift speed第 2 步：漂移速率
Solve $I=nqv_dA$ for $v_d$.由 $I=nqv_dA$ 解出 $v_d$。
$$v_d=\frac{I}{nqA}=\frac{5.0}{(8.5\times10^{28})(1.6\times10^{-19})(3.1\times10^{-6})}=1.2\times10^{-4}\,\text{m/s}$$
Step 3: Current density第 3 步：电流密度
The current is uniform.电流均匀分布。
$$J=\frac{I}{A}=\frac{5.0}{3.1\times10^{-6}}=1.6\times10^{6}\,\text{A/m}^2$$
Step 4: Field in the wire第 4 步：导线内电场
Use $E=\rho J$.利用 $E=\rho J$。
$$E=\rho J=(1.7\times10^{-8})(1.6\times10^{6})=2.7\times10^{-2}\,\text{V/m}$$
Why this works为什么这样做可行
The drift speed is about $0.12\,\text{mm/s}$ even though the current is large, because $n$ is enormous. The small field is why a $1\,\text{m}$ length of this wire has only about $0.03\,\text{V}$ across it.即使电流很大，漂移速率也只有约 $0.12\,\text{mm/s}$，因为 $n$ 极大。电场很小，所以 $1\,\text{m}$ 长的这种导线两端只有约 $0.03\,\text{V}$ 的电势差。
Worked Example: Current from a nonuniform current density例题：由非均匀电流密度求电流

In a cylindrical wire of radius $R=1.5\,\text{mm}$ the current density grows linearly from the axis to the surface, $J(r)=J_0\,r/R$, with $J_0=4.0\times10^{6}\,\text{A/m}^2$. Find the total current and compare the average current density with $J_0$.

半径 $R=1.5\,\text{mm}$ 的圆柱形导线中，电流密度从轴线到表面线性增大，$J(r)=J_0\,r/R$，其中 $J_0=4.0\times10^{6}\,\text{A/m}^2$。求总电流，并比较平均电流密度与 $J_0$。

Step 1: Set up the integral第 1 步：列出积分
Use rings of area $dA=2\pi r\,dr$ and the given $J(r)$.取面积为 $dA=2\pi r\,dr$ 的圆环，代入已知的 $J(r)$。
$$I=\int_0^R \frac{J_0 r}{R}\,2\pi r\,dr=\frac{2\pi J_0}{R}\int_0^R r^2\,dr$$
Step 2: Evaluate第 2 步：积分
The integral of $r^2$ is $R^3/3$.$r^2$ 的积分为 $R^3/3$。
$$I=\frac{2\pi J_0 R^2}{3}=\frac{2\pi(4.0\times10^{6})(1.5\times10^{-3})^2}{3}=19\,\text{A}$$
Step 3: Average current density第 3 步：平均电流密度
Divide by the full area $\pi R^2$.除以整个截面积 $\pi R^2$。
$$J_{\text{avg}}=\frac{I}{\pi R^2}=\frac{2}{3}J_0=2.7\times10^{6}\,\text{A/m}^2$$
Why this works为什么这样做可行
The current density is largest at the surface, but the rings near the surface have the most area, so the average ($\tfrac23J_0$) lies between $0$ and $J_0$ and is closer to $J_0$ than a simple average of the endpoints.电流密度在表面最大，而靠近表面的圆环面积最大，因此平均值 $\tfrac23J_0$ 介于 $0$ 与 $J_0$ 之间，且比两端点的简单平均更接近 $J_0$。
Worked Example: Charge through a lamp filament例题：通过灯丝的电荷量

A steady current of $0.500\,\text{A}$ flows in a lamp filament for $2.00\,\text{min}$. Find the charge that passes a cross section of the filament and the number of electrons that make up this charge. Use $e=1.60\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$.

灯丝中有 $0.500\,\text{A}$ 的稳恒电流，持续 $2.00\,\text{min}$。求通过灯丝某一横截面的电荷量，以及构成这些电荷的电子数。取 $e=1.60\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$。

Step 1: Convert the time第 1 步：换算时间
Use seconds so that the unit of current is C/s.用秒作单位，这样电流的单位就是 C/s。
$$\Delta t=2.00\,\text{min}=120\,\text{s}$$
Step 2: Charge that passes第 2 步：通过的电荷量
Rearrange $I=\Delta q/\Delta t$.把 $I=\Delta q/\Delta t$ 变形。
$$\Delta q=I\,\Delta t=(0.500)(120)=60.0\,\text{C}$$
Step 3: Number of electrons第 3 步：电子数
Each electron carries a charge of magnitude $e$.每个电子所带电荷的大小为 $e$。
$$N=\frac{\Delta q}{e}=\frac{60.0}{1.60\times10^{-19}}=3.75\times10^{20}$$
Why this works为什么这样做可行
The charge is the current times the time. Half an ampere corresponds to $3.13\times10^{18}$ electrons per second, which is why the number over two minutes is so large.电荷量等于电流乘以时间。半安培的电流对应每秒 $3.13\times10^{18}$ 个电子，所以两分钟内的电子数才这么大。
Worked Example: Average current from a count of electrons例题：由电子数求平均电流

In a wire, $2.50\times10^{18}$ electrons pass a cross section moving to the right in $0.400\,\text{s}$. Find the average current and the direction of the conventional current. Use $e=1.60\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$.

导线中有 $2.50\times10^{18}$ 个电子向右通过某一横截面，用时 $0.400\,\text{s}$。求平均电流以及常规电流的方向。取 $e=1.60\times10^{-19}\,\text{C}$。

Step 1: Charge that passes第 1 步：通过的电荷量
Multiply the number of electrons by the charge of each.电子数乘以每个电子的电荷。
$$\Delta q=Ne=(2.50\times10^{18})(1.60\times10^{-19})=0.400\,\text{C}$$
Step 2: Average current第 2 步：平均电流
Divide by the time interval.除以时间间隔。
$$I=\frac{\Delta q}{\Delta t}=\frac{0.400}{0.400}=1.00\,\text{A}$$
Step 3: Direction第 3 步：方向
Electrons carry negative charge. The conventional current is chosen to be the direction in which positive charge would move, so it points opposite to the electron motion: to the left.电子带负电荷。常规电流取正电荷运动的方向，因此与电子运动方向相反：指向左。
Why this works为什么这样做可行
Current is a scalar: $1.00\,\text{A}$ says how much charge passes per second, and its direction is stated only along the wire.电流是标量：$1.00\,\text{A}$ 表示每秒通过多少电荷，其方向只沿导线来说明。
Exam tip考试提示

When a free-response question gives you a current density that depends on position, write $dI=J\,dA$ and choose the area element that matches the symmetry: a ring $2\pi r\,dr$ for a round wire. State the limits of integration before evaluating.

自由作答题若给出随位置变化的电流密度，先写 $dI=J\,dA$，再选与对称性匹配的面积元：圆形导线用圆环 $2\pi r\,dr$，并在计算前写明积分限。

If a question asks about the direction of the current, say whether you mean conventional current or electron motion, and give the direction relative to the wire.

若题目问电流方向，要说明指的是常规电流还是电子运动，并给出相对于导线的方向。

The relations $I=dq/dt$, $I=nqv_dA$ and $\vec E=\rho\vec J$ are worth knowing, and you should check which relations the provided reference information lists, so spend your time on the setup rather than recall.

  • 把电阻描述（describe）为物体对电荷运动的阻碍程度，并把它与电阻率、长度和截面积联系起来。

Resistance measures how strongly an object opposes the movement of charge. It depends on the object (its shape) and on the material (its resistivity). Think of a long, narrow pipe packed with sand: a longer pipe and a narrower pipe both make flow harder.

电阻（resistance）衡量物体阻碍电荷运动的程度。它既取决于物体（形状），也取决于材料（电阻率）。设想一根装满沙子的细长管子：管子越长、越细，水流越困难。

Resistance from geometry由几何形状决定的电阻

Resistance of a uniform resistor均匀电阻的阻值
$$R=\frac{\rho L}{A}$$

$\rho$ is the resistivity of the material (unit $\Omega\cdot\text{m}$), $L$ the length along the current and $A$ the cross-sectional area. $R$ is proportional to $\rho$ and $L$ and inversely proportional to $A$.

$\rho$ 为材料的电阻率（单位 $\Omega\cdot\text{m}$），$L$ 为沿电流方向的长度，$A$ 为横截面积。$R$ 与 $\rho$、$L$ 成正比，与 $A$ 成反比。

For a resistor with uniform geometry, the resistance is proportional to the resistivity of the material and to its length, and inversely proportional to its cross-sectional area. Doubling the length doubles the resistance, and doubling the cross-sectional area halves it.

对几何形状均匀的电阻，电阻与材料的电阻率和长度成正比，与横截面积成反比。长度加倍则电阻加倍，横截面积加倍则电阻减半。

Resistivity is a fundamental property of a material that depends on its atomic and molecular structure and quantifies how strongly the material opposes charge motion. The resistivity of a conductor typically increases with temperature, because the lattice ions vibrate more and scatter the carriers more often.

电阻率是材料的基本属性，取决于其原子与分子结构，量化材料阻碍电荷运动的强弱。导体的电阻率通常随温度升高而增大，因为晶格离子振动加剧，更频繁地散射载流子。

A tungsten filament, for example, has a much larger resistance when glowing than when cold.

例如钨丝发光时的电阻远大于冷态时的电阻。

If the material is uniform in cross section but its resistivity changes along the length, slice the resistor into thin disks of thickness $dx$. Each disk has resistance $dR=\rho(x)\,dx/A$ and the disks are in series, so the resistances add and the sum becomes an integral.

若截面均匀但电阻率沿长度变化，就把电阻切成厚度为 $dx$ 的薄片。每片的电阻为 $dR=\rho(x)\,dx/A$，各片串联，电阻相加，求和变成积分。

Resistance with varying resistivity电阻率变化时的电阻
$$R=\int dR=\frac{1}{A}\int_0^L \rho(x)\,dx$$

Valid when $A$ is uniform and the current runs along $x$. For constant $\rho$ it reduces to $\rho L/A$.

适用于 $A$ 均匀且电流沿 $x$ 方向的情形。$\rho$ 为常数时退化为 $\rho L/A$。

Ohm's law and ohmic materials欧姆定律与欧姆材料

Ohm's law欧姆定律
$$\Delta V=IR$$

The potential difference across a conductive element equals the current times its resistance.

导电元件两端的电势差等于电流乘以其电阻。

Materials that obey Ohm's law have a constant resistance for all currents and are called ohmic. The resistivity of an ohmic material is constant (it does not change with the current).

遵守欧姆定律的材料在任何电流下电阻都保持不变，称为欧姆材料（ohmic）。欧姆材料的电阻率是常数（不随电流改变）。

A resistor also converts electrical energy to thermal energy, which can raise the temperature of the resistor and its surroundings. If the temperature rise is large enough to change $\rho$, the element stops behaving ohmically, as a bulb filament does.

电阻也会把电能转化为热能，使电阻及其环境的温度升高。若温升大到足以改变 $\rho$，该元件就不再表现为欧姆元件，灯丝就是如此。

On a graph of current $I$ against potential difference $\Delta V$, an ohmic element gives a straight line through the origin. The slope is $I/\Delta V=1/R$, so the resistance is the reciprocal of the slope. A curved graph shows a nonohmic element: its resistance $\Delta V/I$ at each point differs.

在电流 $I$ 对电势差 $\Delta V$ 的图像上，欧姆元件给出一条过原点的直线。斜率为 $I/\Delta V=1/R$，因此电阻是斜率的倒数。曲线图像表示非欧姆元件：各点的电阻 $\Delta V/I$ 不同。

Common Mistake: reading R as the slope On an $I$-$\Delta V$ graph, the slope is $1/R$ (conductance), not $R$. On a $\Delta V$-$I$ graph, the slope is $R$. Check which variable is on which axis before you read the resistance. For a curve, $R$ at a point is the ratio $\Delta V/I$ there, not the local slope.

History: Ohm's measurements历史：欧姆的测量

In 1827 Georg Simon Ohm published Die galvanische Kette, mathematisch bearbeitet. Using a thermocouple as a steady source and test wires of different lengths, he found that the current fell as the wire got longer, in the way $\Delta V=IR$ describes. Many physicists dismissed the work at first; the Royal Society awarded him the Copley Medal in 1841.

1827 年，格奥尔格·西蒙·欧姆（Georg Simon Ohm）发表了《伽伐尼电路的数学研究》（Die galvanische Kette, mathematisch bearbeitet）。他用温差电偶作稳定电源，接入不同长度的导线，发现导线越长电流越小，规律正如 $\Delta V=IR$ 所描述。起初许多物理学家并不接受这项工作；1841 年英国皇家学会授予他科普利奖章。

Apply $R=\rho L/A$.代入 $R=\rho L/A$。

Electric Power电功率

Charge moving through a potential difference changes its electric potential energy. A charge $dq$ that drops through $\Delta V$ loses energy $dU=dq\,\Delta V$ (Unit 9). The rate of this energy transfer is the power. With $dq=I\,dt$ it follows directly:

电荷通过电势差时，电势能会改变。电荷 $dq$ 经过电势降 $\Delta V$ 时失去能量 $dU=dq\,\Delta V$（第 9 单元）。这种能量转移的速率就是功率（power）。代入 $dq=I\,dt$ 即得：

Electric power电功率
$$P=\frac{dU}{dt}=I\,\Delta V$$

For any element: $\Delta V$ is the potential difference across it and $I$ the current through it. The unit is the watt, $1\,\text{W}=1\,\text{J/s}=1\,\text{V}\cdot\text{A}$.

对任何元件：$\Delta V$ 是其两端的电势差，$I$ 是通过它的电流。单位是瓦特，$1\,\text{W}=1\,\text{J/s}=1\,\text{V}\cdot\text{A}$。

Charge moving through a potential difference changes its electric potential energy: a charge $\Delta q$ that moves through a potential difference $\Delta V$ changes its energy by $\Delta U=\Delta q\,\Delta V$. Power is the rate at which a circuit element transfers, converts or dissipates energy. Because the charge that passes in a time $\Delta t$ is $\Delta q=I\,\Delta t$, the power follows directly:

电荷通过电势差时，电势能会改变：电荷 $\Delta q$ 通过电势差 $\Delta V$，能量改变 $\Delta U=\Delta q\,\Delta V$。功率（power）是电路元件转移、转化或耗散能量的速率。因为在时间 $\Delta t$ 内通过的电荷为 $\Delta q=I\,\Delta t$，所以功率可直接得出：

Electric power电功率
$$P=I\Delta V$$

For any element: $\Delta V$ is the potential difference across it and $I$ the current through it. Equivalently $P=\Delta U/\Delta t$. The unit is the watt, $1\,\text{W}=1\,\text{J/s}=1\,\text{V}\cdot\text{A}$.

对任何元件：$\Delta V$ 是其两端的电势差，$I$ 是通过它的电流。等价地，$P=\Delta U/\Delta t$。单位是瓦特，$1\,\text{W}=1\,\text{J/s}=1\,\text{V}\cdot\text{A}$。

For an ohmic resistor, $\Delta V=IR$ lets you rewrite the power in terms of whichever quantity you know:

对欧姆电阻，利用 $\Delta V=IR$ 可以把功率改写成已知量表示的形式：

Power in a resistor电阻上的功率
$$P=I\,\Delta V=I^2R=\frac{(\Delta V)^2}{R}$$

Use $P=I^2R$ when the current is the same (series) and $P=(\Delta V)^2/R$ when the potential difference is the same (parallel).

电流相同（串联）时用 $P=I^2R$；电势差相同（并联）时用 $P=(\Delta V)^2/R$。

First principles: power from energy per charge第一性原理：由单位电荷的能量推出功率

First principles第一性原理

Principle. Potential difference is energy per unit charge, so a charge $\Delta q$ that crosses $\Delta V$ transfers energy $\Delta U=\Delta q\,\Delta V$.

原理。电势差是单位电荷的能量，所以电荷 $\Delta q$ 跨过 $\Delta V$ 时转移能量 $\Delta U=\Delta q\,\Delta V$。

  1. Divide by the time taken: $P=\dfrac{\Delta U}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{\Delta q}{\Delta t}\,\Delta V=I\,\Delta V$.
  2. For a resistor, substitute $\Delta V=IR$: $P=I^2R$.
  3. Or substitute $I=\Delta V/R$: $P=(\Delta V)^2/R$.
  1. 除以所用时间：$P=\dfrac{\Delta U}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{\Delta q}{\Delta t}\,\Delta V=I\,\Delta V$。
  2. 对电阻代入 $\Delta V=IR$：$P=I^2R$。
  3. 或代入 $I=\Delta V/R$：$P=(\Delta V)^2/R$。

Limiting check. Units: $\text{V}\cdot\text{A}=(\text{J/C})(\text{C/s})=\text{W}$. An open switch gives $I=0$ and $P=0$. At fixed $\Delta V$, $P=(\Delta V)^2/R$ grows without bound as $R\to0$, which is why a short circuit is dangerous.

极限检验。单位：$\text{V}\cdot\text{A}=(\text{J/C})(\text{C/s})=\text{W}$。开关断开时 $I=0$，$P=0$。$\Delta V$ 固定时，$R\to0$ 会使 $P=(\Delta V)^2/R$ 无限增大，这就是短路危险的原因。

Power from sources, energy and brightness电源功率、能量与亮度

A battery delivering current $I$ at emf $\mathcal E$ supplies power $P=I\mathcal E$. The sign tells the direction of the transfer: a resistor or bulb absorbs energy from the circuit and dissipates it, a battery that is discharging delivers energy, and a motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.

电池输出电流 $I$、电动势为 $\mathcal E$ 时，提供的功率为 $P=I\mathcal E$。符号表明能量转移的方向：电阻或灯泡从电路吸收能量并耗散，放电的电池输出能量，电动机把电能转化为机械能。

The brightness of a bulb increases with the power it dissipates, so power predicts brightness qualitatively. The energy over a time interval is $U=\int P\,dt$, which is simply $P\,t$ when the power is constant.

灯泡的亮度随其耗散功率增大，所以功率可以定性地预测亮度。一段时间内的能量为 $U=\int P\,dt$，功率恒定时就是 $P\,t$。

Boundary: which energy transfers are analyzed This course analyzes the transfer of mechanical and electrical energy. You should be aware that electrical energy can also be dissipated as thermal energy, which is what a resistor does, but problems do not ask for thermal-energy accounting beyond that.
范围说明：分析哪些能量转移 本课程分析的是机械能与电能之间的转移。你应当知道电能还可以以热能的形式耗散（电阻正是如此），但题目不会要求超出这一点的热能核算。

A battery delivering current $I$ at emf $\mathcal E$ supplies power $P=I\mathcal E$. Whether an element absorbs or delivers energy depends on whether charge moves through it to lower or to higher potential:

电池输出电流 $I$、电动势为 $\mathcal E$ 时，提供的功率为 $P=I\mathcal E$。元件是吸收还是输出能量，取决于电荷通过它时是移向较低电势还是较高电势：

  • a resistor or bulb, through which charge moves to lower potential, absorbs energy from the circuit and dissipates it;
  • a battery that is discharging, through which charge moves to higher potential, delivers energy;
  • a motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
  • 电荷通过电阻或灯泡时移向较低电势，它们从电路吸收能量并耗散；
  • 放电的电池使电荷移向较高电势，输出能量；
  • 电动机把电能转化为机械能。

The brightness of a bulb increases with the power it dissipates, so power can be used to predict brightness qualitatively. At constant power, the energy transferred in a time $\Delta t$ is $\Delta U=P\,\Delta t$.

灯泡的亮度随其耗散功率增大，所以功率可以定性地预测亮度。功率恒定时，在时间 $\Delta t$ 内转移的能量为 $\Delta U=P\,\Delta t$。

Common Mistake: brighter in series means brighter in parallel Bulb brightness follows power, and the formula to use depends on the circuit. In series the same current flows, so the larger $R$ dissipates more ($P=I^2R$). In parallel the same $\Delta V$ is across each, so the smaller $R$ dissipates more ($P=(\Delta V)^2/R$). 常见错误：串联中更亮的灯泡在并联中也更亮 灯泡亮度取决于功率，而该用哪个公式取决于电路。串联时电流相同，$R$ 较大者耗散更多（$P=I^2R$）；并联时每个灯泡两端的 $\Delta V$ 相同，$R$ 较小者耗散更多（$P=(\Delta V)^2/R$）。排序恰好相反。

Real circuits mix elements. The equivalent resistance $R_{eq}$ of a group of resistors is the single resistance that would draw the same current from the same potential difference. Two connections are the building blocks.

实际电路由多种元件混合而成。一组电阻的等效电阻（equivalent resistance）$R_{eq}$ 是在相同电势差下取用相同电流的单个电阻。有两种基本连接方式。

Series: the equivalent resistance is the sum of the individual resistances. Parallel: the inverse of the equivalent resistance is the sum of the inverses of the individual resistances. First principles: the series and parallel rules第一性原理：串联与并联规则

First principles第一性原理

Principle. Charge is conserved, so one path carries one current and currents that split at a junction add. Energy is conserved, so potential differences along one path add and branches with the same endpoints share one $\Delta V$.

原理。电荷守恒，所以单一通路中电流处处相同，在节点处分开的电流相加。能量守恒，所以沿同一通路的电势差相加，端点相同的各支路电势差 $\Delta V$ 相同。

  1. Series: $\Delta V=IR_1+IR_2=IR_{eq}$, so $R_{eq}=R_1+R_2$.
  2. Parallel: $I=\dfrac{\Delta V}{R_1}+\dfrac{\Delta V}{R_2}=\dfrac{\Delta V}{R_{eq}}$, so $\dfrac{1}{R_{eq}}=\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_2}$.
  3. For two in parallel this is $R_{eq}=\dfrac{R_1R_2}{R_1+R_2}$.
  1. 串联：$\Delta V=IR_1+IR_2=IR_{eq}$，所以 $R_{eq}=R_1+R_2$。
  2. 并联：$I=\dfrac{\Delta V}{R_1}+\dfrac{\Delta V}{R_2}=\dfrac{\Delta V}{R_{eq}}$，所以 $\dfrac{1}{R_{eq}}=\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_2}$。
  3. 两个电阻并联时即 $R_{eq}=\dfrac{R_1R_2}{R_1+R_2}$。

Limiting check. Cut one parallel branch ($R_2\to\infty$): $R_{eq}\to R_1$, as if it were absent. Short it ($R_2\to0$): $R_{eq}\to0$ and all the current bypasses $R_1$. For $n$ equal resistors, series gives $nR$ and parallel gives $R/n$.

极限检验。断开一条并联支路（$R_2\to\infty$）：$R_{eq}\to R_1$，相当于该支路不存在。把它短路（$R_2\to0$）：$R_{eq}\to0$，电流全部绕过 $R_1$。$n$ 个相同电阻串联得 $nR$，并联得 $R/n$。

Batteries with internal resistance, and ideal assumptions有内阻的电池与理想化假设

Unless a problem says otherwise, batteries, wires and meters are ideal. An ideal battery has no internal resistance and an ideal wire has no resistance.

除非题目另有说明，电池、导线和仪表都按理想情形处理。理想电池没有内阻，理想导线没有电阻。

The resistance of a good conducting wire is normally neglected because it is much smaller than the other resistances, but only when the circuit contains other elements that do have resistance; a wire across an ideal battery alone would have no defined current.

良导体导线的电阻通常可以忽略，因为它远小于电路中其他元件的电阻，但前提是电路中还有别的有电阻的元件；单独一根导线接在理想电池两端，电流是没有定义的。

A real battery behaves like an ideal battery with emf $\mathcal E$ in series with a small internal resistance $r$. The emf is the potential difference across the terminals when there is no current in the battery. 实际电池的行为相当于电动势（emf）为 $\mathcal E$ 的理想电池与一个很小的内阻（internal resistance）$r$ 串联。电动势是电池中无电流时两极间的电势差。电池输出电流 $I$ 时，由回路定则得到降低了的端电压：

Terminal potential difference端电压
$$\Delta V_{\text{terminal}}=\mathcal E-Ir$$

The terminal potential difference equals $\mathcal E$ only when $I=0$; the larger the current drawn, the more it falls.

端电压等于 $\mathcal E$ 仅当 $I=0$ 时成立；取用的电流越大，端电压下降越多。

$$\Delta V_t=\mathcal{E}-Ir$$

$\Delta V_t$ is the terminal potential difference, $\mathcal{E}$ the emf and $r$ the internal resistance. The terminal potential difference equals $\mathcal{E}$ only when there is no current in the battery; when the battery delivers current $I$, the terminal potential difference is reduced, and the larger the current, the larger the reduction.

$\Delta V_t$ 是端电压，$\mathcal{E}$ 是电动势，$r$ 是内阻。只有电池中没有电流时，端电压才等于 $\mathcal{E}$；电池输出电流 $I$ 时，端电压会降低，电流越大，降低得越多。

Measuring current and potential difference测量电流与电势差

An ammeter measures the current at a point, so it is connected in series with the element and must have zero resistance if ideal, so it does not change the current.

电流表（ammeter）测量某点的电流，因此与元件串联，理想电流表电阻为零，这样不会改变电流。

A voltmeter measures the potential difference between two points, so it is connected in parallel across the element and has infinite resistance if ideal, so no charge flows through it. A nonideal ammeter adds resistance in series and a nonideal voltmeter draws some current, so either one changes the circuit it measures.

电压表（voltmeter）测量两点之间的电势差，因此并联在元件两端，理想电压表电阻无穷大，没有电荷流过它。非理想电流表在串联处增加电阻，非理想电压表会取用一部分电流，所以二者都会改变被测电路。

An ammeter measures the current at a specific point in a circuit, so it must be connected in series with the element in which the current is measured. An ideal ammeter has zero resistance, so it does not affect the current in the element.

电流表（ammeter）测量电路中某一点的电流，因此必须与被测电流的元件串联。理想电流表电阻为零，所以不会影响该元件中的电流。

电压表（voltmeter）测量两点之间的电势差，因此必须与被测元件并联。理想电压表电阻无穷大，所以没有电荷流过它。

Nonideal ammeters and voltmeters change the circuit they measure: a nonideal ammeter adds resistance in series and a nonideal voltmeter draws some current.

非理想的电流表和电压表会改变被测电路：非理想电流表在电路中串入电阻，非理想电压表会分走一部分电流。

$$R_{23}=\left(\frac1{12}+\frac1{6.0}\right)^{-1}=4.0\,\Omega$$

The pair is in series with $R_1$.该并联组与 $R_1$ 串联。
$$R_{eq}=4.0+4.0=8.0\,\Omega,\qquad I=\frac{24}{8.0}=3.0\,\text{A}$$

The full current is in $R_1$; the rest of the $24\,\text{V}$ is across the pair.全部电流通过 $R_1$；$24\,\text{V}$ 中其余部分加在并联组上。
$$\Delta V_1=(3.0)(4.0)=12\,\text{V},\qquad \Delta V_2=\Delta V_3=24-12=12\,\text{V}$$

Same potential difference across each parallel path.并联各支路两端电势差相同。

The branch currents add to the total ($1.0+2.0=3.0\,\text{A}$), and the powers add to the source power ($36+12+24=72\,\text{W}=24\times3.0$). Kirchhoff's loop rule基尔霍夫回路定则
$$\sum_{\text{loop}}\Delta V_i=0$$

The sum of the potential differences across all elements in a single closed loop equals zero.

单个闭合回路中所有元件两端电势差的代数和为零。

First principles: why the loop sum is zero第一性原理：回路电势差之和为何为零

First principles第一性原理

Principle. The electrostatic field in a steady circuit is conservative: $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell=0$ around any closed path (Unit 9).

原理。稳恒电路中的静电场是保守场：沿任意闭合路径 $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell=0$（第 9 单元）。

  1. Potential is defined by $V_b-V_a=-\int_a^b\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell$.
  2. Split the closed path element by element: $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell=-\sum_i\Delta V_i$.
  3. So $\sum_i\Delta V_i=0$. For one loop, $\mathcal E-Ir-IR=0$ gives $I=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R+r}$.
  1. 电势的定义为 $V_b-V_a=-\int_a^b\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell$。
  2. 把闭合路径按元件分段：$\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell=-\sum_i\Delta V_i$。
  3. 所以 $\sum_i\Delta V_i=0$。对单个回路，$\mathcal E-Ir-IR=0$ 给出 $I=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R+r}$。

First principles第一性原理

Principle. Energy is conserved, and a charge $q$ that crosses a potential difference $\Delta V$ changes its electric potential energy by $\Delta U=q\Delta V$.

  1. Follow $q$ once around a loop: $\Delta U_{\text{total}}=q\sum_i\Delta V_i$.
  2. Back at its starting point the charge has its starting potential energy, so $\Delta U_{\text{total}}=0$.

Gustav Kirchhoff stated both circuit rules in 1845, while still a student at Königsberg, extending Ohm's work to networks with many loops. The loop rule is energy conservation and the junction rule (11.7) is charge conservation.

Charge cannot be created or destroyed, and in a steady circuit it cannot pile up at a junction, a point where three or more wires meet. Whatever charge arrives per second must leave per second. This is Kirchhoff's junction rule, a statement of conservation of electric charge.

电荷不能被创造或消灭，而且在稳恒电路中电荷也不会在节点（三条或更多导线相会处）堆积。每秒到达的电荷必须每秒离开。这就是基尔霍夫节点定则，它表述的是电荷守恒。

$$\sum I_{\text{in}}=\sum I_{\text{out}}$$

单位时间内进入节点的总电荷等于离开节点的总电荷。电流是标量，按数值相加。

Capacitors in series and in parallel电容的串联与并联

A group of capacitors may be replaced by one equivalent capacitance $C_{eq}$. In parallel the same potential difference $\Delta V$ is across each, and the charges add: $C_{eq}\Delta V=C_1\Delta V+C_2\Delta V+\cdots$.

一组电容可以用一个等效电容（equivalent capacitance）$C_{eq}$ 代替。并联时每个电容两端的电势差 $\Delta V$ 相同，电荷相加：$C_{eq}\Delta V=C_1\Delta V+C_2\Delta V+\cdots$。

In series the charges on the plates are the same: the inner plates of neighbors are isolated, so by conservation of charge their net charge stays zero and each capacitor holds the same magnitude $Q$ on each plate. The potential differences add, $Q/C_{eq}=Q/C_1+Q/C_2+\cdots$.

串联时各极板上的电荷相同：相邻电容的内侧极板是孤立的，由电荷守恒其净电荷保持为零，因此每个电容每块极板上的电荷大小都是 $Q$。电势差相加，$Q/C_{eq}=Q/C_1+Q/C_2+\cdots$。

$$C_{eq}^{\text{parallel}}=\sum_i C_i\qquad\qquad \frac{1}{C_{eq}^{\text{series}}}=\sum_i\frac{1}{C_i}$$

这与电阻的规则正好相反。串联组合总小于其中最小的电容。

Charging and discharging behavior充电与放电过程

Right after an uncharged capacitor is connected, there is no potential difference across it, so it acts like a wire and charge flows easily onto the plates. As it charges, the potential difference across the capacitor, the charge, the current in its branch and the stored energy $q^2/2C$ all change with time and approach their steady-state values asymptotically.

未充电的电容刚接入电路时，两端没有电势差，相当于导线，电荷很容易流到极板上。随着充电，电容两端的电势差、电荷、所在支路的电流以及储能 $q^2/2C$ 都随时间变化，并渐近趋向稳态值。

After a time much greater than $\tau$, a charging capacitor is fully charged, with maximum potential difference and zero current in its branch: it acts like an open circuit.

经过远大于 $\tau$ 的时间后，充电的电容已充满，电势差最大，所在支路电流为零：相当于开路。

A discharging capacitor, immediately after the discharge begins, starts to lose its charge and stored energy, and the charge, potential difference and current all decrease toward zero. For times much longer than $\tau$ the circuit may be analyzed with steady-state conditions: the capacitor branch is open.

放电的电容在放电刚开始时就开始失去电荷和储能，电荷、电势差和电流都向零减小。对远大于 $\tau$ 的时间，可以用稳态条件分析电路：电容支路视为开路。

While a capacitor charges, the potential difference across it, its charge and the energy stored in it increase, and the current in its branch decreases; all of these approach their steady-state values asymptotically.

电容充电时，它两端的电势差、电荷和储存的能量增大，所在支路的电流减小；这些量都渐近地趋向各自的稳态值。

While a capacitor discharges, its charge, the potential difference across it and the current in its branch all decrease toward zero. For times much greater than the time constant, the circuit may be analyzed with steady-state conditions.

电容放电时，它的电荷、两端的电势差和所在支路的电流都减小并趋向于零。经过远大于时间常数的时间后，可以用稳态条件分析电路。

First principles: what the time constant measures第一性原理：时间常数衡量的是什么

First principles第一性原理

Principle. The loop rule for a charging RC circuit: $\mathcal E-R\dfrac{dq}{dt}-\dfrac{q}{C}=0$.

原理。RC 充电电路的回路定则：$\mathcal E-R\dfrac{dq}{dt}-\dfrac{q}{C}=0$。

  1. At $t=0$, $q=0$, so the initial slope is $\dfrac{dq}{dt}=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R}$.
  2. Kept at that rate, the charge would reach $C\mathcal E$ at $t=\dfrac{C\mathcal E}{\mathcal E/R}=RC$: $\tau$ is where the initial tangent meets the final value.
  3. The rate actually falls as $q$ builds, so $q=C\mathcal E(1-e^{-t/RC})$ and $q(\tau)=(1-e^{-1})C\mathcal E\approx0.632\,C\mathcal E$.
  1. $t=0$ 时 $q=0$，所以初始斜率为 $\dfrac{dq}{dt}=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R}$。
  2. 若一直保持该速率，电荷会在 $t=\dfrac{C\mathcal E}{\mathcal E/R}=RC$ 时达到 $C\mathcal E$：$\tau$ 就是初始切线与最终值相交之处。
  3. 实际上速率随 $q$ 增大而减小，所以 $q=C\mathcal E(1-e^{-t/RC})$，$q(\tau)=(1-e^{-1})C\mathcal E\approx0.632\,C\mathcal E$。

Limiting check. Units: $\Omega\cdot\text{F}=(\text{V/A})(\text{C/V})=\text{s}$. As $t\to\infty$, $q\to C\mathcal E$ and $I\to0$ (open branch). As $R\to0$, $\tau\to0$: the capacitor charges almost instantly.

极限检验。单位：$\Omega\cdot\text{F}=(\text{V/A})(\text{C/V})=\text{s}$。$t\to\infty$ 时 $q\to C\mathcal E$，$I\to0$（支路开路）。$R\to0$ 时 $\tau\to0$：电容几乎瞬间充满。

First principles第一性原理

Principle. The loop rule holds at every instant, with $\Delta V_C=q/C$ across the capacitor and $\Delta V_R=IR$ across the resistor.

原理。回路定则在每一时刻都成立，电容两端 $\Delta V_C=q/C$，电阻两端 $\Delta V_R=IR$。

  1. Just after closing: $q=0$, so $\Delta V_C=0$ and $I_0=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R}$.
  2. After a long time: $I=0$, so $\Delta V_R=0$, $\Delta V_C=\mathcal E$ and $Q=C\mathcal E$.
  3. At the initial rate the capacitor would gain $Q$ in $\dfrac{Q}{I_0}=\dfrac{C\mathcal E}{\mathcal E/R}=RC$. The current shrinks as charge builds, so by then it holds only about $63\%$ of $Q$.
  1. 刚闭合时：$q=0$，所以 $\Delta V_C=0$，$I_0=\dfrac{\mathcal E}{R}$。
  2. 很长时间后：$I=0$，所以 $\Delta V_R=0$，$\Delta V_C=\mathcal E$，$Q=C\mathcal E$。
  3. 若保持初始速率，电容获得 $Q$ 需要 $\dfrac{Q}{I_0}=\dfrac{C\mathcal E}{\mathcal E/R}=RC$。电流随电荷积累而减小，所以那时电容只有约 $63\%$ 的 $Q$。

Limiting check. Units: $\Omega\cdot\text{F}=(\text{V/A})(\text{C/V})=\text{s}$. As $R\to0$, $\tau\to0$: charging is almost instant. A larger $C$ needs more charge at the same initial current, so it charges more slowly.

极限检验。单位：$\Omega\cdot\text{F}=(\text{V/A})(\text{C/V})=\text{s}$。$R\to0$ 时 $\tau\to0$：几乎瞬间充满。$C$ 越大，在相同初始电流下需要的电荷越多，所以充电越慢。

Mistakes and self-check错误与自查

Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Treating the drift speed as the speed of the signal or as a fast electron: $v_d$ is below a millimetre per second.
  2. Reading $R$ as the slope of an $I$-$\Delta V$ graph: the slope is $1/R$.
  3. Using $P=I^2R$ for parallel bulbs or $P=(\Delta V)^2/R$ for series bulbs without checking what is shared.
  4. Forgetting that a real battery has $\Delta V_t=\mathcal E-Ir$, so $\Delta V_t<\mathcal E$ when current flows.
  5. Sign errors in the loop rule: a drop $-IR$ only along the current, and $\pm\mathcal E$ by the terminal you cross first.
  6. Treating a capacitor in a circuit as always open or always a wire: it is a wire at $t=0$ if uncharged and open after a long time.
  7. Believing the time constant is the full charging time: $t=\tau$ is about $63\%$ of the final charge.
最常见的错误
  1. 把漂移速率当作信号速度或高速电子：$v_d$ 不到每秒一毫米。
  2. 把 $R$ 当作 $I$-$\Delta V$ 图像的斜率：斜率是 $1/R$。
  3. 对并联灯泡用 $P=I^2R$、对串联灯泡用 $P=(\Delta V)^2/R$，而没有检查哪个量是共有的。
  4. 忘记实际电池满足 $\Delta V_t=\mathcal E-Ir$，因此有电流时 $\Delta V_t<\mathcal E$。
  5. 回路定则的符号错误：只有沿电流方向才是电阻降 $-IR$，而 $\pm\mathcal E$ 取决于先经过哪一极。
  6. 把电路中的电容始终当作开路或始终当作导线：未充电时 $t=0$ 相当于导线，长时间后相当于开路。
  7. 认为时间常数是完整的充电时间：$t=\tau$ 时只达到最终电荷的约 $63\%$。

Convert prefixes before multiplying: $50\,\text{k}\Omega=5.0\times10^{4}\,\Omega$ and $40\,\mu\text{F}=4.0\times10^{-5}\,\text{F}$. The product is $2.0\,\text{s}$.相乘前先换算前缀：$50\,\text{k}\Omega=5.0\times10^{4}\,\Omega$，$40\,\mu\text{F}=4.0\times10^{-5}\,\text{F}$，乘积为 $2.0\,\text{s}$。
5. A battery with $\mathcal E=6.0\,\text{V}$ and $r=0.40\,\Omega$ delivers $2.5\,\text{A}$. Its terminal potential difference is:电动势 $\mathcal E=6.0\,\text{V}$、内阻 $r=0.40\,\Omega$ 的电池输出 $2.5\,\text{A}$。其端电压为：
$6.0\,\text{V}$
$5.0\,\text{V}$
$7.0\,\text{V}$
$1.0\,\text{V}$
Correct! $\Delta V_t=\mathcal E-Ir=6.0-(2.5)(0.40)=5.0\,\text{V}$.正确！$\Delta V_t=\mathcal E-Ir=6.0-(2.5)(0.40)=5.0\,\text{V}$。
With current flowing, the internal drop $Ir=1.0\,\text{V}$ is subtracted from the emf, so the terminal potential difference is $5.0\,\text{V}$: less than $\mathcal E$, never more.有电流时要从电动势中减去内阻上的电势降 $Ir=1.0\,\text{V}$，所以端电压为 $5.0\,\text{V}$：小于 $\mathcal E$，绝不会大于。
6. A $15\,\text{V}$ ideal battery, a $5.0\,\Omega$ total resistance and a capacitor in series: at one instant the capacitor potential difference is $6.0\,\text{V}$. The current at that instant is:$15\,\text{V}$ 理想电池、总阻值 $5.0\,\Omega$ 的电阻和一个电容串联：某时刻电容两端的电势差为 $6.0\,\text{V}$。此刻的电流为：
$3.0\,\text{A}$
$1.8\,\text{A}$
$4.2\,\text{A}$
$0.60\,\text{A}$
Correct! The loop rule gives $15-IR-6.0=0$, so $I=9.0/5.0=1.8\,\text{A}$.正确！回路定则给出 $15-IR-6.0=0$，所以 $I=9.0/5.0=1.8\,\text{A}$。
The capacitor opposes the battery by $6.0\,\text{V}$, so only $15-6.0=9.0\,\text{V}$ drives current through the resistance. The value $3.0\,\text{A}$ ignores the capacitor and $4.2\,\text{A}$ adds its potential difference instead of subtracting it.电容以 $6.0\,\text{V}$ 反抗电池，所以只有 $15-6.0=9.0\,\text{V}$ 驱动电流通过电阻。$3.0\,\text{A}$ 忽略了电容，$4.2\,\text{A}$ 把电容的电势差加上去而不是减去。

Questions 7 and 8第 7 和 8 题

7. Currents of $3.0\,\text{A}$ and $2.0\,\text{A}$ enter a junction, and $4.0\,\text{A}$ leaves along one wire. The current in the fourth wire is:$3.0\,\text{A}$ 与 $2.0\,\text{A}$ 的电流流入某节点，其中一条导线流出 $4.0\,\text{A}$。第四条导线中的电流为：
$1.0\,\text{A}$ out
$1.0\,\text{A}$ in
$9.0\,\text{A}$ out
$0$
Correct! In: $3.0+2.0=5.0\,\text{A}$. Out: $4.0+I$. So $I=1.0\,\text{A}$ out of the junction.正确！流入：$3.0+2.0=5.0\,\text{A}$。流出：$4.0+I$。所以 $I=1.0\,\text{A}$，流出节点。
The junction rule requires equal totals in and out. Five amperes enter and only four leave, so the missing $1.0\,\text{A}$ must leave through the fourth wire.节点定则要求流入与流出总量相等。流入五安培而只流出四安培，缺少的 $1.0\,\text{A}$ 必须从第四条导线流出。
8. An initially uncharged capacitor in series with a resistor is connected to a battery. Which statement describes the circuit correctly?最初未充电的电容与电阻串联后接到电池上。下列哪个说法正确描述了该电路？
At $t=0$ the capacitor behaves like an open circuit and the current is zero.$t=0$ 时电容相当于开路，电流为零。
At $t=0$ the capacitor behaves like a wire; after a long time it behaves like an open circuit.$t=0$ 时电容相当于导线；很长时间后相当于开路。
The capacitor behaves like a wire at all times.电容在任何时候都相当于导线。
The current is smallest at $t=0$ and largest after a long time.$t=0$ 时电流最小，很长时间后最大。
Correct! At $t=0$ the capacitor has no potential difference and the current is $\mathcal E/R$. After many time constants it is fully charged and the current is zero.正确！$t=0$ 时电容两端无电势差，电流为 $\mathcal E/R$。经过许多个时间常数后充满电，电流为零。
The current in the capacitor branch starts at its maximum and decays exponentially to zero. The wire-then-open behavior is the key to steady-state analysis.电容支路中的电流从最大值开始，按指数衰减到零。先像导线、后像开路，这是稳态分析的关键。

Questions 9 and 10第 9 和 10 题

9. A wire is drawn out to three times its original length at constant volume and constant resistivity. Its resistance becomes:一根导线在体积和电阻率不变的条件下被拉长到原长的三倍。它的电阻变为：
$3R$
$9R$
$R/3$
$6R$
Correct! $L\to3L$ forces $A\to A/3$, so $R'=\rho(3L)/(A/3)=9R$.正确！$L\to3L$ 迫使 $A\to A/3$，所以 $R'=\rho(3L)/(A/3)=9R$。
The length factor and the area factor each contribute a factor of $3$. Using only the length gives $3R$, which forgets that the cross section shrinks.长度因子与截面积因子各贡献一个 $3$。只考虑长度得到 $3R$，忘了截面积在缩小。
10. To find the power dissipated in a resistor with an ideal ammeter and an ideal voltmeter, you connect:要用理想电流表和理想电压表求某电阻耗散的功率，应这样连接：
Ammeter in parallel with the resistor, voltmeter in series电流表与电阻并联，电压表串联
Ammeter in series with the resistor, voltmeter in parallel across it电流表与电阻串联，电压表并联在其两端
Both meters in series with the resistor两个表都与电阻串联
Both meters in parallel across the resistor两个表都并联在电阻两端
Correct! The ammeter (resistance zero) is in series to read $I$, and the voltmeter (resistance infinite) is in parallel to read $\Delta V$. Then $P=I\,\Delta V$.正确！电流表（电阻为零）串联以读出 $I$，电压表（电阻无穷大）并联以读出 $\Delta V$。然后 $P=I\,\Delta V$。
A series voltmeter would block the current and a parallel ammeter would short the resistor. Ideal meters must be connected so they do not change the circuit.串联的电压表会阻断电流，并联的电流表会把电阻短路。理想仪表的接法必须使它们不改变电路。
