A flat loop of area $0.020~\mathrm{m^2}$ is placed in a uniform magnetic field $B=0.50~\mathrm{T}$. The area vector of the loop makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with $\vec B$. The magnetic flux through the loop is一个面积为 $0.020~\mathrm{m^2}$ 的平面线圈放在均匀磁场 $B=0.50~\mathrm{T}$ 中，线圈的面积矢量与 $\vec B$ 的夹角为 $60^\circ$。通过线圈的磁通量为

  • (A) $1.0\times10^{-2}~\mathrm{Wb}$
  • (B) $8.7\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{Wb}$
  • (C) $5.0\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{Wb}$
  • (D) $0$
Answer:答案： (C)
The flux is the dot product $\Phi_B=\vec B\cdot\vec A=BA\cos\theta$, with $\theta$ measured from the area vector. $\Phi_B=(0.50)(0.020)\cos60^\circ=5.0\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{Wb}$.磁通量是点积 $\Phi_B=\vec B\cdot\vec A=BA\cos\theta$，其中 $\theta$ 从面积矢量量起。$\Phi_B=(0.50)(0.020)\cos60^\circ=5.0\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{Wb}$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) ignores the angle and reports the maximum flux $BA=1.0\times10^{-2}~\mathrm{Wb}$, which needs $\theta=0$. (B) uses $\sin60^\circ$, as if $60^\circ$ were the angle between the field and the plane of the loop. (D) zero flux requires the field to lie in the plane of the loop ($\theta=90^\circ$); at $60^\circ$ part of the field still crosses the loop.(A) 忽略夹角，给出最大磁通量 $BA=1.0\times10^{-2}~\mathrm{Wb}$，而这需要 $\theta=0$。 (B) 使用了 $\sin60^\circ$，好像 $60^\circ$ 是磁场与线圈平面之间的夹角。 (D) 磁通量为零要求磁场位于线圈平面内（$\theta=90^\circ$）；在 $60^\circ$ 时仍有一部分磁场穿过线圈。

Which statement best justifies the result that the net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero, $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec A=0$?下列哪一项最好地论证了通过任意闭合曲面的净磁通量为零，即 $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec A=0$？

  • (A) Magnetic field lines have no beginning or end, so every line that enters the surface also leaves it.磁感线没有起点也没有终点，所以每一条进入曲面的磁感线也会离开曲面。
  • (B) The magnetic field is uniform everywhere inside any closed surface.在任意闭合曲面内部，磁场处处均匀。
  • (C) Magnetic forces do no work on moving charges, so no field can cross a boundary.磁力对运动电荷不做功，所以磁场不能穿过边界。
  • (D) Magnetic field lines are always parallel to the surface, so $\vec B\cdot d\vec A=0$ at every point.磁感线总是平行于曲面，所以在每一点都有 $\vec B\cdot d\vec A=0$。
Answer:答案： (A)
There are no isolated magnetic charges, so field lines close on themselves. Each line that enters a closed surface must leave it, and the outward and inward contributions to $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec A$ cancel.不存在孤立的磁荷，磁感线自身闭合。每一条进入闭合曲面的磁感线都必须离开，所以 $\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec A$ 中向外和向内的贡献相互抵消。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) uniformity would give zero for a special case only; the field near a magnet is far from uniform and the net flux is still zero. (C) a true fact (the magnetic force does no work) that has nothing to do with flux; field lines cross surfaces freely. (D) field lines cross a surface at all kinds of angles. The cancellation is global, over the whole surface, not point by point.(B) 均匀性只能解释特殊情形；磁铁附近的磁场远非均匀，净磁通量仍为零。 (C) 这是一个正确的事实（磁力不做功），但与磁通量无关；磁感线可以自由穿过曲面。 (D) 磁感线可以以各种角度穿过曲面。抵消发生在整个曲面上，而不是逐点成立。
Insight.要点。 Contrast Gauss's law for $\vec E$: electric lines can start or stop on charge, so $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec A=Q_{\mathrm{enc}}/\epsilon_0$ need not vanish. Magnetic lines never do.对比电场的高斯定律：电场线可以起止于电荷，所以 $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec A=Q_{\mathrm{enc}}/\epsilon_0$ 不必为零。磁感线则从不起止。

A coil of $200$ turns, each of area $4.0\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{m^2}$, has its axis along a uniform magnetic field. The field falls uniformly from $0.30~\mathrm{T}$ to zero in $0.060~\mathrm{s}$. The magnitude of the induced emf in the coil is一个 $200$ 匝的线圈，每匝面积 $4.0\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{m^2}$，其轴线沿均匀磁场方向。磁场在 $0.060~\mathrm{s}$ 内均匀地从 $0.30~\mathrm{T}$ 降为零。线圈中感应电动势的大小为

  • (A) $0.020~\mathrm{V}$
  • (B) $4.0~\mathrm{V}$
  • (C) $0.24~\mathrm{V}$
  • (D) $1.0\times10^{3}~\mathrm{V}$
Answer:答案： (B)
$|\mathcal E|=N\left|\dfrac{\Delta\Phi_B}{\Delta t}\right|=NA\dfrac{\Delta B}{\Delta t}=(200)(4.0\times10^{-3})\dfrac{0.30}{0.060}=4.0~\mathrm{V}$.$|\mathcal E|=N\left|\dfrac{\Delta\Phi_B}{\Delta t}\right|=NA\dfrac{\Delta B}{\Delta t}=(200)(4.0\times10^{-3})\dfrac{0.30}{0.060}=4.0~\mathrm{V}$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) treats the coil as a single turn, $A\,\Delta B/\Delta t=0.020~\mathrm{V}$, dropping the factor $N$. (C) computes $NA\,\Delta B=0.24~\mathrm{V}\cdot\mathrm{s}$ and forgets to divide by the time; the emf is a rate. (D) leaves out the area, $N\,\Delta B/\Delta t=1.0\times10^{3}$, which does not even have the right units.(A) 把线圈当作单匝，$A\,\Delta B/\Delta t=0.020~\mathrm{V}$，漏掉了因子 $N$。 (C) 算出 $NA\,\Delta B=0.24$ 后忘记除以时间；电动势是变化率。 (D) 漏掉了面积，$N\,\Delta B/\Delta t=1.0\times10^{3}$，单位都不对。
Insight.要点。 With constant area and orientation, $|\mathcal E|=NA\,|dB/dt|$. Count turns, area and a rate, in that order.面积和取向不变时，$|\mathcal E|=NA\,|dB/dt|$。依次数清匝数、面积和变化率。

A bar magnet is held to the left of a flat conducting loop with its north pole facing the loop, so the field through the loop points to the right. The magnet is then pulled away to the left. Viewed from the magnet's side, the induced current in the loop is一根条形磁铁位于一个平面导线圈的左侧，其北极朝向线圈，所以穿过线圈的磁场指向右方。随后磁铁向左被拉远。从磁铁一侧看，线圈中的感应电流为

  • (A) counterclockwise, and the loop repels the magnet逆时针，线圈排斥磁铁
  • (B) clockwise, and the loop repels the magnet顺时针，线圈排斥磁铁
  • (C) counterclockwise, and the loop attracts the magnet逆时针，线圈吸引磁铁
  • (D) clockwise, and the loop attracts the magnet顺时针，线圈吸引磁铁
Answer:答案： (D)
The rightward flux through the loop is decreasing, so the induced field must point right to resist the loss. A field pointing away from a viewer on the left comes from a clockwise current. That current makes the loop's left face a south pole, which attracts the retreating north pole and opposes the motion.穿过线圈的向右磁通量在减小，所以感应磁场必须指向右方以阻碍这种减小。对左侧的观察者而言，指向远离自己的磁场由顺时针电流产生。该电流使线圈左面成为南极，吸引正在远离的北极，从而阻碍运动。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) treats the pull-away like an approach (induced field to the left, counterclockwise) and then gets the force wrong as well. (B) the clockwise sense is right but the force is not: a force that repels a retreating magnet would help the motion, violating Lenz's law. (C) the attraction is right but the current sense is reversed; counterclockwise is the sense for an approaching magnet, where the flux increases.(A) 把拉远当成靠近（感应磁场向左，逆时针），而且力的判断也错了。 (B) 顺时针方向是对的，但力不对：排斥正在远离的磁铁会助长运动，违背楞次定律。 (C) 吸引是对的，但电流方向反了；逆时针是磁铁靠近（磁通量增大）时的方向。
Insight.要点。 Decide whether the flux along a chosen direction is growing or shrinking first. A shrinking flux is resisted by an induced field along the original one.先判断沿选定方向的磁通量是在增大还是减小。减小的磁通量会被与原磁场同向的感应磁场所阻碍。
