The periodic table arranges every known element by increasing atomic number. Each period corresponds to the number of occupied electron shells in a ground-state atom; each group (for the s- and p-blocks) corresponds to the number of valence electrons. Elements fall into four blocks according to which subshell is being filled: the s-block (groups 1 to 2), the p-block (groups 13 to 18), the d-block (the transition elements), and the f-block (the lanthanoids and actinoids).

元素周期表按原子序数递增排列所有已知元素。周期（period）对应基态原子中被占据的电子层数；族（group，对 s 区和 p 区而言）对应价电子数。元素按正在被填充的亚层分为四个区：s 区（第 1 到 2 族）、p 区（第 13 到 18 族）、d 区（过渡元素）以及 f 区（镧系与锕系元素）。

Metals, Metalloids and Non-metals金属、类金属与非金属

Elements fall into three broad classes according to where they sit in the table and how they behave. Metals occupy the left and centre of the table (all of the s-, d- and f-block except hydrogen and helium, plus the lower p-block). Non-metals sit in the upper right of the p-block, together with hydrogen. Metalloids lie along the diagonal "staircase" between them and are usually listed as B, Si, Ge, As, Sb and Te; their properties are intermediate.

元素按在周期表中的位置和表现分为三大类。金属（metals）位于周期表左侧和中部（除氢、氦外的 s 区、d 区、f 区，以及 p 区下部）。非金属（non-metals）位于 p 区右上方，另加氢。类金属（metalloids）沿二者之间的对角“阶梯线”分布，通常列为 B、Si、Ge、As、Sb、Te，性质介于金属与非金属之间。

Common misconception: the boundaries are not sharp. Aluminium has an amphoteric oxide but is still classed as a metal because its overall behaviour (metallic bonding, good conductor, forms Al$^{3+}$) is metallic. Always classify by several properties together rather than by one.

常见误区：分界并不截然。铝的氧化物是两性的，但仍归为金属，因为其整体表现（金属键、良导体、形成 Al$^{3+}$）是金属性的。分类应综合多项性质，而不是只看一项。

Worked Example - Classifying Elements from Their Properties

Element X is a shiny, brittle solid. Its conductivity is low at 25 °C but increases markedly on heating, and its oxide XO$_2$ is weakly acidic. Element Y is a lustrous, malleable solid whose conductivity falls on heating and whose oxide is basic. Classify X and Y and state where each lies in the table.

Step 1 - Element X
Conductivity that rises with temperature marks a semiconductor. Together with a brittle but shiny solid and a weakly acidic oxide, this fits a metalloid (for example Si or Ge), found on the diagonal staircase.
Step 2 - Element Y
Conductivity that falls on heating is metallic: lattice vibrations scatter the delocalized electrons more strongly. Malleability, lustre and a basic oxide confirm a metal on the left or centre of the table.
Step 3 - Why this works
No single property is decisive; the temperature dependence of conductivity and the acid-base character of the oxide together separate metalloids from metals.
例题 - 根据性质对元素分类

元素 X 是有光泽的脆性固体，25 °C 时导电性低，加热后明显增强，其氧化物 XO$_2$ 呈弱酸性。元素 Y 是有光泽、可锻的固体，加热后导电性下降，其氧化物呈碱性。对 X 和 Y 分类，并说明各自在周期表中的位置。

第 1 步 - 元素 X
电导率随温度升高而增大是半导体的标志。再加上有光泽但质脆的固体和弱酸性氧化物，这符合类金属（如 Si 或 Ge），位于对角阶梯线上。
第 2 步 - 元素 Y
加热后电导率下降是金属的特征：晶格振动更强烈地散射离域电子。可锻性、光泽和碱性氧化物进一步确认它是位于周期表左侧或中部的金属。
第 3 步 - 原理
没有哪一项性质是决定性的；电导率随温度的变化以及氧化物的酸碱性合起来才能区分类金属与金属。

Periodicity and Effective Nuclear Charge周期性与有效核电荷

Most periodic properties are governed by the effective nuclear charge ($Z_{eff}$): the net positive charge actually felt by a valence electron once the shielding (screening) of inner-shell electrons is taken into account. Moving across a period, protons are added to the same outer shell while inner-shell shielding stays roughly constant, so $Z_{eff}$ rises steadily. Moving down a group, a whole new outer shell is added, so the valence electrons sit farther from the nucleus and are shielded by an additional inner shell.

多数周期性性质都由有效核电荷（effective nuclear charge，$Z_{eff}$）决定：这是价电子在扣除内层电子屏蔽（shielding）效应后实际感受到的净正电荷。同一周期从左到右，质子不断加到同一外层，而内层屏蔽大致不变，因此 $Z_{eff}$ 持续升高。同一族从上到下，则整整多出一个外层，价电子离核更远，且多受一层内层电子的屏蔽。

Effective Nuclear Charge
$$Z_{eff} = Z - S$$
$Z$ = proton number; $S$ = shielding by inner-shell electrons

Atomic Radius and Ionic Radius原子半径与离子半径

Radius Trends Atomic radius: decreases across a period (rising $Z_{eff}$ pulls the same shell in tighter); increases down a group (an entirely new, farther shell is occupied, which outweighs the increase in $Z_{eff}$). Ionic radius: cations are smaller than their parent atom (fewer electrons and, often, one fewer occupied shell, e.g. Na$^+$ < Na). Anions are larger than their parent atom (extra electrons increase electron-electron repulsion with no extra protons, e.g. Cl$^-$ > Cl). Isoelectronic series: species with the same electron count but different proton counts. For a fixed number of electrons, radius shrinks as nuclear charge rises: O$^{2-}$ > F$^-$ > Ne > Na$^+$ > Mg$^{2+}$ > Al$^{3+}$ (all 10 electrons).
半径变化规律 原子半径：同周期从左到右减小（$Z_{eff}$ 升高，把同一电子层拉得更紧）；同族从上到下增大（多占据一整层、离核更远，这一效应超过 $Z_{eff}$ 增大的影响）。 离子半径：阳离子比其母原子小（电子数更少，往往还少一个占据电子层，如 Na$^+$ < Na）；阴离子比其母原子大（电子数增多而质子数不变，电子间排斥增强，如 Cl$^-$ > Cl）。 等电子体系列：电子数相同但质子数不同的粒子。电子数固定时，核电荷越高，半径越小：O$^{2-}$ > F$^-$ > Ne > Na$^+$ > Mg$^{2+}$ > Al$^{3+}$（均为 10 个电子）。

First Ionization Energy第一电离能

First ionization energy is the energy needed to remove one mole of electrons from one mole of gaseous atoms in their ground state: $X(g) \rightarrow X^+(g) + e^-$. It generally increases across a period (rising $Z_{eff}$ holds electrons more tightly) and decreases down a group (valence electrons are farther out and more shielded).

第一电离能是从一摩尔气态基态原子中移去一摩尔电子所需的能量：$X(g) \rightarrow X^+(g) + e^-$。它在同一周期内总体随原子序数增大而升高（$Z_{eff}$ 升高使电子被抓得更紧），在同一族内则随原子序数增大而降低（价电子离核更远、屏蔽更多）。

Two Dips to Memorize Group 2 to group 13 (e.g. Be to B, Mg to Al): the single outer electron in group 13 occupies a higher-energy p-subshell, which is also shielded slightly by the filled s-subshell beneath it. It is easier to remove than an s-electron, despite the higher nuclear charge. Group 15 to group 16 (e.g. N to O, P to S): group 15 has a stable, half-filled p-subshell ($p^3$) with one electron in each of three orbitals. Group 16 must place two electrons in the same p-orbital; the extra electron-electron repulsion in that doubly occupied orbital makes an electron easier to remove, despite the higher nuclear charge.
两处需要记住的反常下降 第 2 族到第 13 族（如 Be 到 B，Mg 到 Al）：第 13 族多出的那一个电子位于能量更高的 p 亚层，并受到其下方已填满的 s 亚层的少量屏蔽。尽管核电荷更高，这个 p 电子仍比 s 电子更容易移走。 第 15 族到第 16 族（如 N 到 O，P 到 S）：第 15 族拥有稳定的半充满 p 亚层（$p^3$，三个轨道各占一个电子）。第 16 族必须把两个电子放进同一个 p 轨道，这个双占轨道中额外的电子间排斥，使得移走一个电子更容易，尽管核电荷更高。

Common misconception: the N $\rightarrow$ O drop is often blamed only on the "stability of a half-filled subshell". The mark-scheme reason is the repulsion between the two electrons paired in the same p orbital, which makes one of them easier to remove even though the nuclear charge is higher.

常见误区：N $\rightarrow$ O 的下降常被单纯归因于“半充满亚层稳定”。评分标准要求的理由是同一 p 轨道中成对的两个电子之间的排斥，它使其中一个电子更易移走，尽管核电荷更高。

Worked Example - Explaining the N to O Discontinuity

The first ionization energy of nitrogen is 1402 kJ mol$^{-1}$ and that of oxygen is 1314 kJ mol$^{-1}$. Explain why oxygen's value is lower.

Step 1 - Write the configurations
N is $1s^2 2s^2 2p^3$: the three 2p electrons occupy three separate orbitals. O is $1s^2 2s^2 2p^4$: one 2p orbital now holds a pair of electrons.
Step 2 - Compare the electron removed
N loses an electron from a singly occupied orbital. O loses one from the doubly occupied orbital, where the two electrons repel each other.
Step 3 - Conclude
The extra repulsion lowers the energy needed to remove that electron, so the value falls by $1402 - 1314 = 88\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ even though O has one more proton and the electron is removed from the same shell with similar shielding.
例题 - 解释 N 到 O 的反常下降

氮的第一电离能为 1402 kJ mol$^{-1}$，氧为 1314 kJ mol$^{-1}$。解释为什么氧的数值更低。

第 1 步 - 写出电子组态
N 为 $1s^2 2s^2 2p^3$：三个 2p 电子分占三个不同轨道。O 为 $1s^2 2s^2 2p^4$：有一个 2p 轨道容纳了一对电子。
第 2 步 - 比较被移走的电子
N 从单占轨道失去电子。O 从双占轨道失去电子，该轨道中的两个电子相互排斥。
第 3 步 - 得出结论
额外的排斥降低了移走该电子所需的能量，因此数值下降了 $1402 - 1314 = 88\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$，尽管 O 多一个质子，且被移走的电子位于同一电子层、屏蔽相近。

Electron Affinity, Electronegativity, and Metallic Character电子亲和能、电负性与金属性

Three Related but Distinct Trends Electron affinity: the energy change when one mole of electrons is added to one mole of gaseous atoms, $X(g) + e^- \rightarrow X^-(g)$. Generally becomes more exothermic (more negative) across a period, since higher $Z_{eff}$ attracts the incoming electron more strongly. Electronegativity: the relative ability of a bonded atom to attract the shared pair of electrons in a covalent bond (Pauling scale, no units). Increases across a period and decreases down a group; fluorine is the most electronegative element. Metallic character: the tendency to lose electrons and form cations. Increases down a group and decreases across a period, mirroring the fall in ionization energy and electronegativity toward the lower-left of the table.
三个相关但不同的概念 电子亲和能：一摩尔气态原子获得一摩尔电子时的能量变化，$X(g) + e^- \rightarrow X^-(g)$。同一周期内总体随原子序数增大而更放热（更负），因为更高的 $Z_{eff}$ 更强烈地吸引外来电子。 电负性：成键原子吸引共价键中共用电子对的相对能力（Pauling 标度，无单位）。同周期从左到右增大，同族从上到下减小；氟的电负性最大。 金属性：失去电子形成阳离子的倾向。同族从上到下增强，同周期从左到右减弱，这与电离能、电负性在周期表左下方降低的趋势一致。

Metal and Non-metal Oxides; Acid Deposition金属与非金属氧化物；酸沉降

Across period 3 the bonding in the oxides changes from ionic (Na$_2$O, MgO) through ionic with covalent character (Al$_2$O$_3$) and giant covalent (SiO$_2$) to simple molecular (P$_4$O$_{10}$, SO$_2$, SO$_3$, Cl$_2$O$_7$). The same switch runs through the whole table: metal oxides are basic and non-metal oxides are acidic.

在第三周期中，氧化物的键型由离子型（Na$_2$O、MgO），经带共价成分的离子型（Al$_2$O$_3$）和巨型共价（SiO$_2$），变为简单分子型（P$_4$O$_{10}$、SO$_2$、SO$_3$、Cl$_2$O$_7$）。整个周期表遵循同样的规律：金属氧化物呈碱性，非金属氧化物呈酸性。

Four Types of Oxide Basic oxides (metal oxides; the O$^{2-}$ ion accepts H$^+$): CaO + H$_2$O $\rightarrow$ Ca(OH)$_2$; MgO + 2HCl $\rightarrow$ MgCl$_2$ + H$_2$O. Acidic oxides (non-metal oxides): P$_4$O$_{10}$ + 6H$_2$O $\rightarrow$ 4H$_3$PO$_4$; Cl$_2$O$_7$ + H$_2$O $\rightarrow$ 2HClO$_4$; CO$_2$ + H$_2$O $\rightleftharpoons$ H$_2$CO$_3$; SO$_2$ + 2NaOH $\rightarrow$ Na$_2$SO$_3$ + H$_2$O. Amphoteric oxides react with both acids and bases: Al$_2$O$_3$ and ZnO. ZnO + 2HCl $\rightarrow$ ZnCl$_2$ + H$_2$O; ZnO + 2NaOH + H$_2$O $\rightarrow$ Na$_2$[Zn(OH)$_4$]. Neutral oxides (CO, NO, N$_2$O) do not react with water, acids or bases under normal conditions.

Acid deposition is the transfer of acidic substances from the atmosphere to the ground as wet deposition (acid rain, snow, fog) or dry deposition (acidic gases and particles). Unpolluted rain is already slightly acidic, about pH 5.6, because dissolved CO$_2$ forms H$_2$CO$_3$; rain below this value is called acid rain. The main culprits are sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides (NO$_x$ = NO + NO$_2$).

酸沉降（acid deposition）是大气中的酸性物质以湿沉降（酸雨、酸雪、酸雾）或干沉降（酸性气体和颗粒）的形式降落到地面。未受污染的雨水本身就略呈酸性，约 pH 5.6，因为溶解的 CO$_2$ 生成 H$_2$CO$_3$；低于此值的雨称为酸雨（acid rain）。主要元凶是二氧化硫和氮氧化物（NO$_x$ = NO + NO$_2$）。

Sources, Reactions, Effects and Control SO$_2$ source: burning sulfur-containing coal and oil, S + O$_2$ $\rightarrow$ SO$_2$, and roasting sulfide ores, 2ZnS + 3O$_2$ $\rightarrow$ 2ZnO + 2SO$_2$. Acid formation: SO$_2$ + H$_2$O $\rightleftharpoons$ H$_2$SO$_3$; in the air 2SO$_2$ + O$_2$ $\rightarrow$ 2SO$_3$, then SO$_3$ + H$_2$O $\rightarrow$ H$_2$SO$_4$. NO$_x$ source: high-temperature combustion (car engines, power stations) forces N$_2$ and O$_2$ in the air to react, N$_2$ + O$_2$ $\rightarrow$ 2NO, followed by 2NO + O$_2$ $\rightarrow$ 2NO$_2$. Acid formation: 4NO$_2$ + O$_2$ + 2H$_2$O $\rightarrow$ 4HNO$_3$. Effects: acidified lakes harm fish; Al$^{3+}$ is leached from soil and is toxic; forests are damaged; limestone and marble are eroded, CaCO$_3$ + H$_2$SO$_4$ $\rightarrow$ CaSO$_4$ + H$_2$O + CO$_2$; metal structures corrode faster. Control: remove sulfur from fuel; flue-gas desulfurization with CaCO$_3$ or CaO (SO$_2$ is converted to solid calcium sulfite/sulfate); catalytic converters turn NO into N$_2$, 2NO + 2CO $\rightarrow$ N$_2$ + 2CO$_2$; adding lime to acidified lakes.

Worked Example - Sulfur in Coal and Acid Deposition

A coal sample contains 2.0% sulfur by mass. Calculate the mass of SO$_2$ released on burning 1.00 kg of it, and the maximum mass of H$_2$SO$_4$ that this SO$_2$ could form. ($M$: S = 32.06, SO$_2$ = 64.06, H$_2$SO$_4$ = 98.08 g mol$^{-1}$.)

Step 1 - Moles of S
$m(\mathrm{S}) = 1000\ \mathrm{g} \times 0.020 = 20.0\ \mathrm{g}$, so $n(\mathrm{S}) = 20.0 / 32.06 = 0.624\ \mathrm{mol}$.
Step 2 - Mass of SO$_2$
S + O$_2$ $\rightarrow$ SO$_2$ is 1 : 1, so $n(\mathrm{SO_2}) = 0.624\ \mathrm{mol}$ and $m = 0.624 \times 64.06 = 40.0\ \mathrm{g}$.
Step 3 - Mass of H$_2$SO$_4$
SO$_2$ $\rightarrow$ SO$_3$ $\rightarrow$ H$_2$SO$_4$ is 1 : 1 : 1, so $m(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}) = 0.624 \times 98.08 = 61.2\ \mathrm{g}$.
Step 4 - Why this works
Each S atom ends up in one H$_2$SO$_4$ molecule, so the mole ratio is 1 : 1 throughout; the mass grows only because O and H are added. Answers keep three significant figures, matching the data.

HL Only - Transition Elements, Spectra, and Complexes Line spectra: atoms absorb or emit light only at specific frequencies corresponding to transitions between quantized (discrete) energy levels, producing a line spectrum of sharp separate lines, unlike the continuous spectrum of white light. Energy levels converge at higher $n$; the frequency at which the lines converge corresponds to the ionization energy of that electron. Transition elements: a d-block element that forms at least one stable ion with an incomplete d-subshell. By this definition, scandium (only Sc$^{3+}$, $3d^0$) and zinc (only Zn$^{2+}$, $3d^{10}$) are d-block but NOT transition elements. Variable oxidation states: the 4s and 3d subshells are close in energy, so transition elements can lose different numbers of electrons from both, giving several stable oxidation states, e.g. Fe$^{2+}$/Fe$^{3+}$, Cu$^+$/Cu$^{2+}$. Complex ions: a transition metal ion surrounded by ligands (species with a lone pair, e.g. H$_2$O, NH$_3$, Cl$^-$, CN$^-$) bonded by coordinate (dative) bonds, e.g. [Cu(H$_2$O)$_6$]$^{2+}$. Colour: ligands split the five d orbitals into two groups of different energy. An electron absorbs a photon of visible light and is promoted between these split orbitals; the observed colour is complementary to the colour absorbed. Ions with an empty ($d^0$) or completely full ($d^{10}$) d-subshell, such as Sc$^{3+}$ or Zn$^{2+}$, cannot make this transition and are colourless. Catalysis: transition metals catalyse reactions by providing a surface for adsorption (heterogeneous, e.g. Fe in the Haber process) or by using their variable oxidation states to open a lower-activation-energy pathway (homogeneous, e.g. Fe$^{2+}$/Fe$^{3+}$ catalysing the reaction between I$^-$ and S$_2$O$_8^{2-}$). Magnetism: a species with unpaired electrons is paramagnetic (weakly attracted into a magnetic field); one with all electrons paired is diamagnetic (weakly repelled). Fe$^{3+}$ ($3d^5$, unpaired) is paramagnetic; Zn$^{2+}$ ($3d^{10}$, all paired) is diamagnetic.
HL 专属 - 过渡元素、光谱与配合物 线状光谱：原子只在与量子化（离散）能级跃迁相对应的特定频率处吸收或发射光，产生由分立谱线组成的线状光谱，不同于白光那样的连续光谱。能级随 $n$ 增大而逐渐收敛；谱线收敛处对应的频率即给出该电子的电离能。 过渡元素：指能形成至少一种 d 亚层不满的稳定离子的 d 区元素。按此定义，钪（只形成 Sc$^{3+}$，$3d^0$）与锌（只形成 Zn$^{2+}$，$3d^{10}$）属于 d 区元素，但不算过渡元素。 可变氧化态：4s 与 3d 亚层能量相近，过渡元素可从两者中失去不同数目的电子，因而呈现多种稳定氧化态，例如 Fe$^{2+}$/Fe$^{3+}$、Cu$^+$/Cu$^{2+}$。 配离子：过渡金属离子被配体（带孤对电子的分子或离子，如 H$_2$O、NH$_3$、Cl$^-$、CN$^-$）以配位键（dative bond）包围而成，例如 [Cu(H$_2$O)$_6$]$^{2+}$。 颜色：配体使五个简并 d 轨道分裂为能量不同的两组。电子吸收一个可见光光子后在分裂的轨道间跃迁，观察到的颜色是被吸收光的互补色。d 亚层全空（$d^0$）或全满（$d^{10}$）的离子（如 Sc$^{3+}$、Zn$^{2+}$）无法发生这种跃迁，因而无色。 催化作用：过渡金属可通过提供吸附表面来催化反应（多相催化，如 Haber 法中的 Fe），也可利用可变氧化态开辟活化能更低的反应路径（均相催化，如 Fe$^{2+}$/Fe$^{3+}$ 催化 I$^-$ 与 S$_2$O$_8^{2-}$ 之间的反应）。 磁性：含未成对电子的粒子具有顺磁性（paramagnetic，被磁场微弱吸引）；电子全部成对的粒子具有逆磁性（diamagnetic，被磁场微弱排斥）。Fe$^{3+}$（$3d^5$，有未成对电子）顺磁；Zn$^{2+}$（$3d^{10}$，全部成对）逆磁。

(HL) Complex Ions, Ligands, Colour and Magnetism（HL）配离子、配体、颜色与磁性

A ligand is a molecule or ion with a lone pair that it donates to a central metal ion, forming a coordinate (dative) bond. The metal ion plus its ligands is a complex ion, and the number of coordinate bonds to the metal is its coordination number. Six (octahedral) is the commonest, then four (tetrahedral or square planar) and two (linear, as in [Ag(NH$_3$)$_2$]$^+$). The coordination number counts bonds, not ligands: [Co(en)$_3$]$^{3+}$ has three bidentate ethane-1,2-diamine ligands but coordination number 6.

配体（ligand）是带有孤对电子、并将其提供给中心金属离子形成配位键（coordinate bond）的分子或离子。金属离子加上配体构成配离子（complex ion），与金属形成的配位键数目即配位数（coordination number）。最常见的是 6（八面体），其次是 4（四面体或平面正方形）和 2（直线形，如 [Ag(NH$_3$)$_2$]$^+$）。配位数数的是键而非配体个数：[Co(en)$_3$]$^{3+}$ 含三个双齿 ethane-1,2-diamine 配体，但配位数为 6。

The charge of a complex is the metal's oxidation state plus the sum of the ligand charges. Neutral ligands (H$_2$O, NH$_3$) add nothing; each Cl$^-$ or CN$^-$ adds $-1$. So in [Fe(CN)$_6$]$^{4-}$: $x + 6(-1) = -4$, giving Fe oxidation state $+2$.

配离子的电荷等于金属的氧化态加上各配体电荷之和。中性配体（H$_2$O、NH$_3$）不贡献电荷；每个 Cl$^-$ 或 CN$^-$ 贡献 $-1$。因此在 [Fe(CN)$_6$]$^{4-}$ 中：$x + 6(-1) = -4$，得 Fe 的氧化态为 $+2$。

HL Only - Why Complexes Are Coloured In a free ion the five 3d orbitals have equal energy. When ligands approach, their lone pairs repel the d electrons unequally, so the d orbitals split into two groups of different energy, separated by an energy gap $\Delta E$ (in an octahedral complex, three lower and two higher). A d electron can absorb a photon whose energy matches the gap, $\Delta E = hf = hc/\lambda$, and is promoted to the upper set (a d-d transition). The light that is not absorbed is transmitted, so the observed colour is the complementary colour of the colour absorbed (opposite on the colour wheel: red-green, orange-blue, yellow-violet). No colour from d$^0$ or d$^{10}$: Sc$^{3+}$ ($3d^0$) has no d electron to promote and Zn$^{2+}$ ($3d^{10}$) has no vacancy in the upper set, so neither can undergo a d-d transition and both are colourless.
HL 专属 - 配合物为何有颜色 在自由离子中，五个 3d 轨道能量相等。配体接近时，其孤对电子对 d 电子的排斥不均等，使 d 轨道分裂（d-orbital splitting）为能量不同的两组，二者间的能隙为 $\Delta E$（八面体配合物中为三个较低、两个较高）。 d 电子可吸收能量恰好等于能隙的光子，$\Delta E = hf = hc/\lambda$，跃迁到较高的一组轨道（d-d 跃迁）。未被吸收的光透过，因此观察到的颜色是被吸收颜色的互补色（complementary colour，在色轮上相对：红-绿、橙-蓝、黄-紫）。 d$^0$ 与 d$^{10}$ 无色：Sc$^{3+}$（$3d^0$）没有可跃迁的 d 电子，Zn$^{2+}$（$3d^{10}$）上层轨道已无空位，因此都不能发生 d-d 跃迁，均为无色。
HL Only - Factors That Change the Splitting (and So the Colour) Identity of the ligand: stronger-field ligands split the orbitals more. Roughly I$^-$ < Br$^-$ < Cl$^-$ < H$_2$O < NH$_3$ < CN$^-$. Replacing H$_2$O by NH$_3$ in copper(II) turns pale blue [Cu(H$_2$O)$_6$]$^{2+}$ into deep blue [Cu(NH$_3$)$_4$(H$_2$O)$_2$]$^{2+}$ because a larger $\Delta E$ means shorter-wavelength light is absorbed. Oxidation state of the metal: a higher charge pulls ligands closer and increases the splitting (e.g. Fe$^{2+}$ and Fe$^{3+}$ aqua complexes differ in colour). Identity of the metal and the coordination number/geometry: pink octahedral [Co(H$_2$O)$_6$]$^{2+}$ and blue tetrahedral [CoCl$_4$]$^{2-}$ differ in both ligand and shape, and therefore in colour.
HL 专属 - 影响分裂程度（进而影响颜色）的因素 配体种类：场强越强的配体使轨道分裂越大。大致顺序 I$^-$ < Br$^-$ < Cl$^-$ < H$_2$O < NH$_3$ < CN$^-$。在铜(II)中以 NH$_3$ 取代 H$_2$O，使淡蓝色的 [Cu(H$_2$O)$_6$]$^{2+}$ 变成深蓝色的 [Cu(NH$_3$)$_4$(H$_2$O)$_2$]$^{2+}$，因为 $\Delta E$ 越大，吸收的光波长越短。 金属的氧化态：电荷越高，越能把配体拉近，分裂越大（如 Fe$^{2+}$ 与 Fe$^{3+}$ 的水合配离子颜色不同）。 金属种类以及配位数/几何构型：粉红色八面体 [Co(H$_2$O)$_6$]$^{2+}$ 与蓝色四面体 [CoCl$_4$]$^{2-}$ 的配体和形状都不同，因而颜色不同。
HL Only - Paramagnetism and Diamagnetism from Unpaired Electrons Draw the d electrons in boxes (Hund's rule: fill singly before pairing). A species with one or more unpaired electrons is paramagnetic (drawn weakly into a magnetic field); one with all electrons paired is diamagnetic (weakly repelled). More unpaired electrons means a stronger paramagnetic effect. Cr$^{3+}$ ($3d^3$): three unpaired electrons, paramagnetic. Zn$^{2+}$ ($3d^{10}$): no unpaired electrons, diamagnetic. Ti$^{4+}$ ($3d^0$): diamagnetic.
HL 专属 - 由未成对电子决定的顺磁性与逆磁性 把 d 电子画入方框（洪特规则：先单占再配对）。含一个或多个未成对电子的粒子是顺磁性的（被磁场微弱吸引）；电子全部成对的粒子是逆磁性的（被磁场微弱排斥）。未成对电子越多，顺磁性越强。 Cr$^{3+}$（$3d^3$）：三个未成对电子，顺磁性。Zn$^{2+}$（$3d^{10}$）：无未成对电子，逆磁性。Ti$^{4+}$（$3d^0$）：逆磁性。

Worked Example - Colour from the Energy Gap

A complex ion absorbs light of wavelength 600 nm. Calculate the d-orbital splitting $\Delta E$ in kJ mol$^{-1}$ and state the colour observed. ($h = 6.63 \times 10^{-34}$ J s, $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}$ m s$^{-1}$, $L = 6.02 \times 10^{23}$ mol$^{-1}$.)

Step 1 - Energy per ion
$\Delta E = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda} = \dfrac{(6.63\times10^{-34})(3.00\times10^{8})}{600\times10^{-9}} = 3.32\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{J}$.
Step 2 - Per mole
$3.32\times10^{-19} \times 6.02\times10^{23} = 2.00\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}} = 200\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
Step 3 - Observed colour
600 nm is orange light. The ion transmits the rest, so it appears the complementary colour, blue.
Step 4 - Why this works
One photon promotes one electron, so the photon energy equals the gap. Converting the wavelength to joules per photon and multiplying by $L$ gives the molar gap.
例题 - 由能隙求颜色

某配离子吸收波长 600 nm 的光。计算 d 轨道分裂能 $\Delta E$（单位 kJ mol$^{-1}$），并说明观察到的颜色。（$h = 6.63 \times 10^{-34}$ J s，$c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}$ m s$^{-1}$，$L = 6.02 \times 10^{23}$ mol$^{-1}$。）

第 1 步 - 每个离子的能量
$\Delta E = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda} = \dfrac{(6.63\times10^{-34})(3.00\times10^{8})}{600\times10^{-9}} = 3.32\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{J}$。
第 2 步 - 每摩尔
$3.32\times10^{-19} \times 6.02\times10^{23} = 2.00\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}} = 200\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$。
第 3 步 - 观察到的颜色

第 4 步 - 原理

HL Only - Anomalous Configurations: Chromium and Copper Strict aufbau filling predicts [Ar] $4s^2\,3d^4$ for chromium and [Ar] $4s^2\,3d^9$ for copper. Both are exceptions: chromium is actually [Ar] $4s^1\,3d^5$, and copper is actually [Ar] $4s^1\,3d^{10}$. Why the exception occurs: a half-filled ($d^5$, one electron in each of five orbitals) or completely filled ($d^{10}$) d-subshell is an unusually stable, lower-energy arrangement, because it minimizes electron-electron repulsion between orbitals. Promoting a single 4s electron into the 3d subshell to reach that more stable arrangement costs less energy than it releases, so the atom adopts the anomalous configuration. The exception does not change ion-formation rules: electrons are still removed from 4s before 3d when a transition metal ion forms, exactly as for every other first-row transition element. This is easy to misapply precisely because the neutral atom's configuration already looks unusual. Classification consequence: Cr and Cu remain ordinary d-block elements and transition elements by the IB definition, since each forms stable ions with an incomplete d-subshell (e.g. Cr$^{3+}$ and Cu$^{2+}$, below). The anomaly affects only the neutral-atom configuration, not which category the element belongs to.
HL 专属 - 反常电子组态：铬与铜 严格按能量最低原理（aufbau）推测，铬应为 [Ar] $4s^2\,3d^4$，铜应为 [Ar] $4s^2\,3d^9$。但两者都是例外：铬实际为 [Ar] $4s^1\,3d^5$，铜实际为 [Ar] $4s^1\,3d^{10}$。 反常原因：半充满（$d^5$，五个轨道各占一个电子）或全充满（$d^{10}$）的 d 亚层是异常稳定、能量更低的排布方式，因为它使轨道间的电子排斥最小化。把一个 4s 电子提升到 3d 亚层以达到这种更稳定的排布，所付出的能量代价小于由此获得的稳定化能量，因此原子采用了这种反常组态。 该反常不改变离子形成规则：过渡金属形成离子时，仍然先失去 4s 电子，再失去 3d 电子，与其他第一行过渡元素完全相同。正因为基态原子本身的组态已经看起来不合常规，这条规则在考试中很容易被误用。 对分类的影响：按 IB 的定义，Cr 与 Cu 仍然是普通的 d 区元素与过渡元素，因为二者各自都能形成 d 亚层不满的稳定离子（如下文的 Cr$^{3+}$ 与 Cu$^{2+}$）。这一反常只影响基态原子的电子组态，不影响该元素所属的分类。
Worked Example - Predicting Ion Configurations for Cr and Cu

Give the full electron configurations of Cr, Cr$^{3+}$, Cu, and Cu$^{2+}$.

Step 1 - Ground-state Cr
Aufbau would predict [Ar] $4s^2\,3d^4$, but the half-filled 3d subshell is more stable, so one 4s electron shifts into 3d: Cr is [Ar] $4s^1\,3d^5$.
Step 2 - Cr$^{3+}$
Remove the single $4s^1$ electron first, leaving [Ar] $3d^5$, then remove two more electrons from 3d, giving Cr$^{3+}$ = [Ar] $3d^3$. A common mistake is removing all three electrons from 3d directly, since Cr already "looks" d-heavy; that gives the wrong answer [Ar] $4s^1\,3d^2$. The 4s electron is removed first no matter how few electrons occupy it.
Step 3 - Ground-state Cu and Cu$^{2+}$
By the same logic, Cu is [Ar] $4s^1\,3d^{10}$, not the aufbau prediction [Ar] $4s^2\,3d^9$. Removing the single $4s^1$ electron first leaves [Ar] $3d^{10}$; the second electron for Cu$^{2+}$ must then come from 3d, since 4s is already empty, giving Cu$^{2+}$ = [Ar] $3d^9$.
Step 4 - Why this matters
Despite the anomalous neutral-atom configurations, the ion-formation rule (4s before 3d) never changes. Only the ground-state starting point looks unusual, not the mechanism of ionization, and both Cr and Cu still satisfy the IB definition of a transition element via the incomplete d-subshells of Cr$^{3+}$ and Cu$^{2+}$.

先移去唯一的 $4s^1$ 电子，得到 [Ar] $3d^5$，再从 3d 中移去两个电子，得到 Cr$^{3+}$ = [Ar] $3d^3$。常见错误是直接从 3d 中移去全部三个电子（因为 Cr 组态"看起来"已经以 d 为主），得到错误答案 [Ar] $4s^1\,3d^2$。无论 4s 上剩几个电子，都要先移去 4s 电子。

Worked Example - A Period 3 Ionization Energy Anomaly

Explain why the first ionization energy of aluminium (578 kJ mol$^{-1}$) is lower than that of magnesium (738 kJ mol$^{-1}$), even though Al has a greater nuclear charge.

Step 1 - Electron configurations
Mg: $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2$. Al: $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^1$.
Step 2 - Compare the outermost electron
Al's outermost electron occupies the 3p subshell, which is higher in energy than the filled 3s subshell of Mg, and is slightly shielded by the 3s$^2$ pair.
Step 3 - Conclusion
Despite Al's higher nuclear charge (+13 vs +12), its 3p electron is easier to remove than Mg's 3s electron, so Al has the lower first ionization energy. This is the group 2 to group 13 dip.

Worked Example — Ranking an Isoelectronic Series by Radius

Rank N$^{3-}$, O$^{2-}$, F$^-$, and Na$^+$ in order of decreasing radius. All four species have 10 electrons.

Step 1 — Confirm they are isoelectronic
N$^{3-}$: 7 + 3 = 10. O$^{2-}$: 8 + 2 = 10. F$^-$: 9 + 1 = 10. Na$^+$: 11 − 1 = 10. All four have exactly 10 electrons, so the only variable affecting radius is nuclear charge.
Step 2 — Apply the isoelectronic-series rule
With electron count fixed, more protons pull the same 10 electrons in more tightly, shrinking the radius. Proton numbers: N = 7, O = 8, F = 9, Na = 11.
Step 3 — Order by increasing proton number = decreasing radius
$$\text{N}^{3-} > \text{O}^{2-} > \text{F}^{-} > \text{Na}^{+}$$
Notice this ranking needs no data booklet values at all — only the electron count (fixed) and the proton count (from the periodic table). This is a purely reasoning-based question, not a lookup one.

Common traps: a carbonyl is just C=O (aldehydes and ketones), while a carboxyl is C=O and OH on the same carbon. IUPAC NomenclatureIUPAC 命名法

To name a compound: (1) identify the longest continuous carbon chain that contains the principal functional group; (2) number the chain to give the lowest possible locant to the principal group, then to substituents; (3) name substituents as prefixes, listed alphabetically with locants; (4) express the principal functional group as a suffix (carboxylic acids, esters, and amides instead determine the parent name directly).

化合物命名步骤：（1）找出包含主官能团的最长连续碳链；（2）为主链编号，使主官能团获得最小编号，其次是取代基；（3）取代基作前缀，按字母顺序列出并标明编号；（4）主官能团以后缀表示（羧酸、酯、酰胺则直接决定母体名称）。

Worked Example - Naming a Branched Haloalkane

Give the IUPAC name of CH$_3$CHClCH(CH$_3$)CH$_2$CH$_3$.

Step 1 - Find the longest chain containing both substituents
Counting carbons across the drawn structure gives a continuous chain of 5 carbons carrying both a chlorine and a methyl branch, so the parent chain is pentane. Choosing a shorter chain that puts the methyl-bearing carbon at an endpoint would drop a substituent from the chain entirely, a common source of naming errors.
Step 2 - Number for the lowest locant set
Numbering from the chlorine end gives locants {2, 3} for chloro and methyl. Numbering from the other end gives {3, 4}. Comparing the two sets at the first point of difference, 2 < 3, so numbering starts from the chlorine end: C1 is the CH$_3$ nearest the Cl.
Step 3 - Order the prefixes alphabetically
"Chloro" is listed before "methyl" (c before m in the alphabet) regardless of which locant is numerically smaller, giving 2-chloro-3-methyl- as the substituent block.
Step 4 - Assemble the name
The full name is 2-chloro-3-methylpentane. Halogens are always cited as prefixes, never as a suffix, so there is no separate suffix step here beyond the parent name "-pentane".

Structural, Condensed, and Skeletal Formulas结构式、缩合式与骨架式

A full structural formula shows every atom and every bond explicitly. A condensed formula groups atoms carbon by carbon, e.g. CH$_3$CH$_2$OH. A skeletal formula shows only the carbon skeleton as a zig-zag line: each line-end or vertex represents a carbon atom (with hydrogens on carbon implied), while heteroatoms and functional groups are drawn explicitly.

完整结构式明确画出所有原子和所有键。缩合式按碳原子逐个分组书写，例如 CH$_3$CH$_2$OH。骨架式（skeletal formula）只用锯齿折线表示碳骨架：每条线段的端点或转折点代表一个碳原子（碳上的氢被省略不写），杂原子和官能团则明确画出。

Saturated and Unsaturated Compounds饱和与不饱和化合物

A saturated compound contains only single bonds between carbon atoms (alkanes, for example), so each carbon holds the maximum number of other atoms. An unsaturated compound contains at least one C=C double bond or C$\equiv$C triple bond (alkenes, alkynes, and benzene rings) and could add more atoms across it. Rings do not make a compound unsaturated in this sense, but they do count in the degree of unsaturation below.

饱和（saturated）化合物的碳原子之间只有单键（例如烷烃），每个碳连接的原子数达到最大。不饱和（unsaturated）化合物含有至少一个 C=C 双键或 C$\equiv$C 三键（烯烃、炔烃和苯环），可以在其上加成更多原子。环不会使化合物在这个意义上成为不饱和，但会计入下文的不饱和度。

Test: bromine water is decolourised rapidly by an unsaturated hydrocarbon because Br$_2$ adds across the double bond, C$_2$H$_4$ + Br$_2$ $\rightarrow$ C$_2$H$_4$Br$_2$ (1,2-dibromoethane); a saturated alkane gives no immediate change in the dark. The C=O of an aldehyde or ketone does not decolourise bromine water this way.

检验：不饱和烃能使溴水迅速褪色，因为 Br$_2$ 加成到双键上，C$_2$H$_4$ + Br$_2$ $\rightarrow$ C$_2$H$_4$Br$_2$（1,2-二溴乙烷）；饱和烷烃在暗处没有即时变化。醛或酮的 C=O 不会以这种方式使溴水褪色。

氢缺指数（index of hydrogen deficiency，IHD），即不饱和度，可直接由分子式算出。每一个环或每一个 $\pi$ 键（双键记 1，三键记 2）都对 IHD 贡献 1。

Worked Example — Using IHD to Narrow Down a Structure

A compound has molecular formula C$_4$H$_6$O. Calculate its IHD and state what combinations of rings/$\pi$ bonds are consistent with that value.

Step 1 — Apply the formula (O is ignored)
$$\text{IHD} = \frac{2(4) + 2 + 0 - 6}{2} = \frac{8 + 2 - 6}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2$$
Step 2 — Translate IHD = 2 into structural possibilities
A value of 2 could mean: two $\pi$ bonds and no ring (e.g. a diene, or a C=C plus a C=O), one ring and one $\pi$ bond (e.g. a cyclic ketone), or one triple bond (which alone counts as 2).
Step 3 — Why this matters
IHD narrows down candidate structures before you even start drawing them, but it never uniquely identifies one structure by itself — it must be combined with other evidence (IR, NMR, or a description of reactivity) to settle which arrangement is actually correct.
例题 — 用 IHD 缩小结构范围

第 1 步 — 代入公式（O 不计入）
$$\text{IHD} = \frac{2(4) + 2 + 0 - 6}{2} = \frac{8 + 2 - 6}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2$$
第 2 步 — 把 IHD = 2 转化为结构上的可能性
数值为 2 可能对应：两个 π 键、无环（例如二烯，或一个 C=C 加一个 C=O）；一个环加一个 π 键（例如环状酮）；或一个三键（三键本身就计 2）。
第 3 步 — 为何这很重要
IHD 能在动笔画结构之前就缩小候选范围，但单靠它永远无法唯一确定某个结构——必须结合其他证据（红外光谱、核磁共振，或反应活性的描述）才能确定到底是哪种排列。

Structural Isomers结构异构体

Three Types of Structural Isomerism Chain isomers: same molecular formula, different carbon skeleton (branching), e.g. butane and 2-methylpropane (both C$_4$H$_{10}$). Position isomers: same skeleton and same functional group, different position of that group, e.g. propan-1-ol and propan-2-ol (both C$_3$H$_8$O). Functional group isomers: same molecular formula, entirely different functional groups, e.g. ethanol and methoxymethane (both C$_2$H$_6$O); ethanoic acid and methyl methanoate (both C$_2$H$_4$O$_2$).
结构异构的三种类型 碳链异构：分子式相同，碳骨架（支链方式）不同，例如 butane（丁烷）与 2-methylpropane（2-甲基丙烷），两者都是 C$_4$H$_{10}$。 位置异构：碳骨架与官能团种类相同，官能团所在位置不同，例如 propan-1-ol 与 propan-2-ol（两者都是 C$_3$H$_8$O）。 官能团异构：分子式相同，官能团种类完全不同，例如 ethanol（乙醇）与 methoxymethane（甲氧基甲烷），两者都是 C$_2$H$_6$O；ethanoic acid（乙酸）与 methyl methanoate（甲酸甲酯），两者都是 C$_2$H$_4$O$_2$。

Primary, Secondary and Tertiary Classification伯、仲、叔的分类

Alcohols and halogenoalkanes are classified by the number of carbon atoms bonded to the carbon that carries the functional group: primary (1 carbon, or 0 in methanol), secondary (2 carbons) and tertiary (3 carbons). Amines follow a different rule: they are classified by the number of carbon groups bonded to the nitrogen: primary RNH$_2$, secondary R$_2$NH, tertiary R$_3$N. The class controls reactivity, for example whether an alcohol can be oxidised (primary and secondary can; tertiary cannot by acidified dichromate).

醇和卤代烷按与带官能团的碳相连的碳原子数分类：伯（primary，1 个碳，甲醇为 0 个）、仲（secondary，2 个碳）和叔（tertiary，3 个碳）。胺采用不同规则：按与氮原子相连的烃基数目分类：伯胺 RNH$_2$、仲胺 R$_2$NH、叔胺 R$_3$N。类别决定反应性，例如醇能否被氧化（伯醇和仲醇可以；叔醇不能被酸化重铬酸盐氧化）。

HL Only - Stereoisomerism and Benzene Stereoisomers share the same structural formula (connectivity) but differ in the spatial arrangement of atoms. Cis-trans / E-Z isomerism: arises around a C=C double bond (or in a ring) because rotation about it is restricted, provided each doubly bonded carbon carries two different substituents. E/Z naming uses Cahn-Ingold-Prelog priority: on each carbon, rank the two substituents by atomic number. Z (zusammen) means the higher-priority groups are on the same side; E (entgegen) means they are on opposite sides. Optical isomerism: arises at a chiral carbon, a carbon bonded to four different groups. A chiral molecule and its mirror image, called enantiomers, are non-superimposable, like left and right hands. Enantiomers share identical physical and chemical properties except that they rotate the plane of plane-polarized light in opposite directions, and they can react differently with other chiral species. A 50:50 mixture of both enantiomers (a racemic mixture) is optically inactive because the rotations cancel. Benzene delocalization: benzene, C$_6$H$_6$, is a planar ring in which the six p-orbitals (one per carbon) overlap sideways to form a delocalized $\pi$ system above and below the ring. All six C-C bonds are equal in length, intermediate between a single and a double bond. This delocalization stabilizes the ring, so benzene typically undergoes electrophilic substitution rather than the addition reactions typical of alkenes.
HL 专属 - 立体异构与苯 立体异构体连接方式（结构式）相同，只是原子的空间排列不同。 顺反 / E-Z 异构：由于绕 C=C 双键（或环内某键）不能自由旋转，只要每个双键碳都连有两个不同的取代基，就会出现这种异构。E/Z 命名依据 Cahn-Ingold-Prelog 优先级规则：在每个碳上，按原子序数比较两个取代基。Z（zusammen，意为"共同"）表示两个优先级较高的基团在同侧；E（entgegen，意为"相对"）表示它们在异侧。 光学异构：产生于手性碳（chiral carbon），即连接四个互不相同基团的碳原子。手性分子与其镜像互为对映体（enantiomers），二者不能重合，如同左右手。对映体的物理、化学性质完全相同，唯独使平面偏振光的旋转方向相反，并且与其他手性物质的反应可能不同。等量（50:50）的两种对映体混合而成的外消旋混合物（racemic mixture）因旋光相互抵消而不显光学活性。 苯的离域：苯（C$_6$H$_6$）是一个平面环状分子，六个 p 轨道（每个碳一个）侧面重叠，在环上、下方形成离域的 $\pi$ 电子体系。六条 C-C 键长度完全相等，介于单键与双键之间。这种离域使苯环更稳定，因此苯通常发生亲电取代反应，而不像烯烃那样容易发生加成反应。

Mass Spectrometry: Molecular Ion and Fragmentation质谱：分子离子与碎片

In mass spectrometry a vaporised sample is bombarded with high-energy electrons, which knock out an electron to give a positive molecular ion, M$^{+\bullet}$ (written M$^+$). A compound C$_4$H$_8$O has peaks at $m/z$ 72, 57, 43 and 29. Assign each peak.

Infrared Spectroscopy红外光谱

Covalent bonds stretch and bend at characteristic frequencies. A bond absorbs infrared radiation of matching frequency, but only if the vibration changes the bond's dipole moment, so O=O and N$\equiv$N in O$_2$ and N$_2$ do not absorb. A spectrum plots transmittance against wavenumber (cm$^{-1}$, equal to $1/\lambda$), so absorptions show as downward troughs. Stronger bonds and lighter atoms vibrate at higher wavenumbers. The region below about 1500 cm$^{-1}$ is the fingerprint region, unique to each compound and used only for matching against reference spectra. The wavenumber ranges are given in the data booklet.

共价键以特征频率伸缩和弯曲。化学键吸收红外（infrared）辐射的前提是振动改变键的偶极矩，因此 O$_2$ 中的 O=O 和 N$_2$ 中的 N$\equiv$N 不吸收。谱图以透光率对波数（wavenumber，单位 cm$^{-1}$，等于 $1/\lambda$）作图，因此吸收表现为向下的谷。键越强、原子越轻，振动波数越高。约 1500 cm$^{-1}$ 以下为指纹区，每种化合物各不相同，只用于与标准谱图比对。波数范围见数据手册。

Worked Example - Using IR to Identify a Functional Group

An unknown compound of formula C$_2$H$_4$O$_2$ shows a very broad absorption from 2500 to 3000 cm$^{-1}$ and a strong sharp peak at 1710 cm$^{-1}$. Identify the functional group and say why an alcohol plus a ketone does not fit.

Step 1 - Assign the peaks
1710 cm$^{-1}$ lies in the C=O range (1700–1750). The very broad 2500–3000 cm$^{-1}$ band is the O$-$H of a carboxylic acid, so the group is carboxyl, $-$COOH: the compound is ethanoic acid.
Step 2 - Rule out the alternative
An alcohol O$-$H would absorb at 3200–3600 cm$^{-1}$, a higher wavenumber than observed, and its band is less broad. The isomer methyl methanoate would show C=O but no broad O$-$H band.

$^1$H NMR: Environments, Chemical Shift and Integration$^1$H NMR：化学环境、化学位移与积分

In $^1$H NMR spectroscopy hydrogen nuclei in a strong magnetic field absorb radio-frequency radiation. The frequency depends on the electron density around each hydrogen, so hydrogens in different chemical environments give separate signals, while equivalent hydrogens (for example the three H of one CH$_3$, or the two CH$_3$ groups of propane related by symmetry) give one. The number of signals equals the number of distinct environments.

在 $^1$H NMR 谱中，处于强磁场内的氢核吸收射频辐射。吸收频率取决于每个氢周围的电子密度，所以不同化学环境的氢给出不同的信号，等价的氢（例如同一个 CH$_3$ 上的三个 H，或丙烷中因对称而等价的两个 CH$_3$）只给出一个信号。信号数等于不同环境的数目。

Reading a $^1$H NMR Spectrum Chemical shift ($\delta$, ppm) measures the position of a signal relative to TMS (tetramethylsilane, (CH$_3$)$_4$Si), defined as $\delta = 0$. TMS is the standard because its 12 equivalent hydrogens give one sharp signal, it is inert and volatile, and it absorbs at lower shift than almost all organic hydrogens. Electronegative atoms and C=O groups nearby withdraw electron density, so the hydrogen is deshielded and appears at higher $\delta$. Integration is the area under each signal (shown as a step height on the integration trace). It is proportional to the number of hydrogens in that environment, so the ratio of areas is the ratio of hydrogens. Solvent: samples are dissolved in a solvent with no $^1$H, such as CDCl$_3$ or CCl$_4$, so the solvent does not add signals.
读 $^1$H NMR 谱 化学位移（$\delta$，ppm）表示信号相对于 TMS（四甲基硅 tetramethylsilane，(CH$_3$)$_4$Si，规定 $\delta = 0$）的位置。选 TMS 作标准，是因为它的 12 个等价氢只给出一个尖锐信号，化学性质惰性且易挥发，并且比几乎所有有机氢的位移都低。附近的电负性原子和 C=O 吸引电子密度，使氢去屏蔽而出现在较高的 $\delta$ 处。 积分（integration）是每个信号下的面积（在积分曲线上表现为台阶高度）。它与该环境中氢的数目成正比，所以面积之比就是氢原子数之比。 溶剂：样品溶于不含 $^1$H 的溶剂，如 CDCl$_3$ 或 CCl$_4$，因此溶剂不会增加信号。

(HL) High-Resolution NMR: Splitting and the n + 1 Rule（HL）高分辨 NMR：裂分与 n + 1 规则

At high resolution a signal splits into a multiplet because the hydrogens on neighbouring carbons slightly change the magnetic field felt. The $n+1$ rule: a hydrogen environment next to carbons bearing $n$ equivalent hydrogens appears as $n+1$ lines. Hydrogens on the same carbon, or equivalent to each other, do not split one another. O$-$H and N$-$H signals normally appear as unsplit singlets at this level.

高分辨率下，信号会裂分为多重峰（multiplet），因为相邻碳上的氢轻微改变了所感受的磁场。$n+1$ 规则：某个氢环境的相邻碳上共有 $n$ 个等价氢时，该信号表现为 $n+1$ 条谱线。同一个碳上的氢，或彼此等价的氢，互不引起裂分。在此水平上，O$-$H 和 N$-$H 的信号通常为不裂分的单峰。

(HL) Combining Spectroscopic Data and X-ray Crystallography（HL）综合光谱数据与 X 射线晶体学

No single technique identifies an unknown. Mass spectrometry gives the relative molecular mass and fragments (with elemental analysis, the molecular formula); the formula gives the degree of unsaturation. IR shows which functional groups are present. $^1$H NMR shows how many hydrogen environments there are, how many hydrogens in each, and (from splitting) which groups are neighbours. A candidate structure must explain all of the data.

没有哪一种技术能单独鉴定未知物。质谱给出相对分子质量和碎片（结合元素分析得到分子式）；由分子式可算出不饱和度。红外显示存在哪些官能团。$^1$H NMR 显示有多少种氢环境、每种有多少个氢，以及（由裂分）哪些基团相邻。候选结构必须解释全部数据。

X-ray crystallography directs X-rays at a single crystal. The X-rays, whose wavelength is similar to interatomic distances (about $10^{-10}$ m), are diffracted by the electrons around the atoms, and the diffraction pattern is converted into a map of electron density. This gives the exact positions of atoms, hence bond lengths, bond angles and the full three-dimensional structure, including stereochemistry; it is how the structures of proteins and DNA were determined. Its limits are that a good crystal is required, it shows the solid-state structure, and hydrogen (one electron) is hard to locate.

X 射线晶体学（X-ray crystallography）把 X 射线射向单晶。X 射线的波长与原子间距相近（约 $10^{-10}$ m），被原子周围的电子衍射，衍射图样再被转换为电子密度图。由此得到原子的精确位置，进而得到键长、键角和完整的三维结构（包括立体化学）；蛋白质和 DNA 的结构就是这样测定的。它的局限是需要质量好的晶体、显示的是固态结构，且氢（只有一个电子）很难定位。

Worked Example - Identifying an Unknown from MS, IR and NMR

A compound contains only C, H and O: 54.5% C, 9.2% H, 36.3% O by mass. Its mass spectrum has M$^+$ at $m/z$ 88 and strong peaks at 43, 45 and 29. IR: strong sharp peak at 1740 cm$^{-1}$; no absorption between 2500 and 3600 cm$^{-1}$. $^1$H NMR: $\delta$ 1.2 (triplet, 3H), $\delta$ 2.0 (singlet, 3H), $\delta$ 4.1 (quartet, 2H). Deduce the structure.

Step 1 - Molecular formula
Moles per 100 g: C 54.5/12.01 = 4.54; H 9.2/1.008 = 9.13; O 36.3/16.00 = 2.27. Dividing by 2.27 gives C : H : O = 2 : 4 : 1, empirical formula C$_2$H$_4$O (44). With M$^+$ = 88, the molecular formula is C$_4$H$_8$O$_2$, and $\text{IHD} = (2\times4 + 2 - 8)/2 = 1$, one C=O or one ring.
Step 2 - IR
1740 cm$^{-1}$ is C=O (1700–1750). No band at 2500–3600 cm$^{-1}$ means no O$-$H, so it is not an acid or alcohol. Two oxygens with C=O and no O$-$H indicate an ester.
Step 3 - NMR
Areas 3 : 3 : 2 sum to 8 H, matching the formula. The singlet at 2.0 is CH$_3$ on C=O with no neighbouring H. The quartet at 4.1 (2H) is an O$-$CH$_2$ next to a CH$_3$ ($n = 3$); the triplet at 1.2 (3H) is that CH$_3$ next to the CH$_2$ ($n = 2$). So the fragments are CH$_3$CO$-$ and $-$OCH$_2$CH$_3$.
Step 4 - Structure and check
The compound is ethyl ethanoate, CH$_3$COOCH$_2$CH$_3$. MS check: $88 - 45 = 43$ (loss of OC$_2$H$_5$ gives CH$_3$CO$^+$, the 43 peak); 45 is C$_2$H$_5$O$^+$; 29 is C$_2$H$_5^+$. The isomer methyl propanoate would have its CH$_3$ singlet near 3.7 ppm and its CH$_2$ quartet near 2.3 ppm, so the observed 2.0 singlet and 4.1 quartet rule it out.

Ethanoic acid shows a very broad O$-$H absorption at 2500–3000 cm$^{-1}$ as well as C=O at 1700–1750 cm$^{-1}$. Methyl methanoate shows C=O but has no O$-$H band.

乙酸除了在 1700–1750 cm$^{-1}$ 有 C=O 吸收外，还在 2500–3000 cm$^{-1}$ 有很宽的 O$-$H 吸收。Methyl methanoate 有 C=O 但没有 O$-$H 谱带。

4. (HL) A compound C$_3$H$_6$O has a strong IR peak at 1715 cm$^{-1}$ and a single $^1$H NMR signal at $\delta$ 2.1. Deduce its structure and explain why propanal is ruled out.4. （HL）化合物 C$_3$H$_6$O 在红外 1715 cm$^{-1}$ 处有强峰，$^1$H NMR 只有一个信号，$\delta$ 2.1。推断其结构，并解释为什么排除丙醛。

IHD = (6 + 2 − 6)/2 = 1, and 1715 cm$^{-1}$ is C=O. One NMR signal means all six hydrogens are equivalent, so the structure is symmetrical: propanone, CH$_3$COCH$_3$ (a singlet, 6H, at 2.1 ppm typical of CH$_3$C=O). Propanal, CH$_3$CH$_2$CHO, would give three signals (a triplet, a multiplet and an aldehyde H near 9.4–10.0 ppm), not one.

IHD = (6 + 2 − 6)/2 = 1，1715 cm$^{-1}$ 为 C=O。只有一个 NMR 信号说明六个氢全部等价，结构对称：丙酮 CH$_3$COCH$_3$（6H 单峰，2.1 ppm，符合 CH$_3$C=O 的典型位移）。丙醛 CH$_3$CH$_2$CHO 会给出三个信号（一个三重峰、一个多重峰和 9.4–10.0 ppm 附近的醛基氢），而不是一个。

Preparation

Exam Strategy考试策略

Paper 1 (Multiple Choice)Paper 1（多项选择）

Periodic trend questions are very common: memorize the two ionization-energy dips (group 2 to 13, group 15 to 16) and be ready to identify which one is being tested. Acid-base oxide questions almost always test Al$_2$O$_3$'s amphoteric behaviour. For organic questions, practise recognizing all the common functional groups on sight, and be able to classify an isomer pair as chain, position, or functional group in seconds.

周期性趋势题非常常见：把两处电离能反常下降（第 2 到 13 族，第 15 到 16 族）背熟，并能迅速判断题目考的是哪一种。氧化物酸碱性题几乎总会考 Al$_2$O$_3$ 的两性行为。有机题方面，练习一眼识别常见官能团，并能在几秒内判断一对异构体属于碳链、位置还是官能团异构。

Paper 2 (Extended Response)Paper 2（论述题）

When asked to "explain" a periodic trend, always mention effective nuclear charge (or shielding) explicitly. "Ionization energy decreases because it gets easier to remove an electron" earns few marks; "ionization energy decreases because the valence electron is farther from the nucleus and more shielded, so effective nuclear charge experienced by that electron is lower" earns full marks. For organic "explain" questions on stereoisomerism, always state the structural condition first (restricted rotation about C=C, or four different groups on a carbon) before describing the consequence.

遇到解释周期性趋势的题目，务必明确提到有效核电荷（或屏蔽效应）。只写 "电离能下降是因为更容易移走电子" 得分有限；写成 "电离能下降是因为价电子离核更远、屏蔽更强，该电子所感受到的有效核电荷更低" 才能拿满分。有机化学中解释立体异构的题目，先说明结构条件（C=C 键旋转受限，或某碳连有四个不同基团），再描述其结果。

Watch Out

Common Mistakes常见错误

Mistake 1 - Explaining Trends Without Effective Nuclear Charge Saying "atomic radius decreases because there are fewer electrons" is vague and often wrong. The correct explanation invokes effective nuclear charge: across a period, protons are added to the same shell while shielding stays roughly constant, so the increasing $Z_{eff}$ pulls the valence shell in tighter.
错误 1 - 解释趋势时不提有效核电荷 只写 "原子半径减小是因为电子变少" 既模糊又常常错误。正确的解释必须提到有效核电荷：同一周期内质子不断加到同一电子层，而屏蔽大致不变，因此不断升高的 $Z_{eff}$ 把价电子层拉得更紧。
Mistake 2 - Confusing Electronegativity with Electron Affinity Electronegativity describes an atom's pull on a shared pair of electrons within a covalent bond (a relative, unitless scale). Electron affinity is a measured energy change (kJ mol$^{-1}$) for adding an isolated electron to a gaseous atom. The two trends run in similar directions but are not interchangeable in an explanation.
错误 2 - 把电负性和电子亲和能混为一谈 电负性描述原子在共价键中吸引共用电子对的能力（相对、无单位的标度）。电子亲和能是气态原子获得一个孤立电子时实测的能量变化（kJ mol$^{-1}$）。两者变化方向相似，但在解释题中不能互相替代。
Mistake 3 - Assuming Cis-Trans and E-Z Labels Always Match Cis/trans labels only work cleanly when it is obvious which substituents count as "the same." When the substituents differ, use Cahn-Ingold-Prelog priority to assign E/Z. A "cis" isomer is not automatically the Z isomer once the substituents get more complex.
错误 3 - 认为顺反标记与 E/Z 标记总是一致 只有在明显能判断哪些取代基算 "相同" 时，顺式/反式（cis/trans）标记才好用。一旦取代基更复杂，就必须用 Cahn-Ingold-Prelog 优先级来判定 E/Z。"顺式" 异构体不一定就是 Z 异构体。
Mistake 4 - Misclassifying Al$_2$O$_3$ as Purely Basic or Acidic Because aluminium is a metal, students often assume Al$_2$O$_3$ is simply basic. It is amphoteric: it reacts with both acids (forming a salt and water) and bases (forming an aluminate complex and water). Always show both reactions if asked to justify the classification.
错误 4 - 把 Al$_2$O$_3$ 误判为纯碱性或纯酸性 由于铝是金属，学生常常想当然地认为 Al$_2$O$_3$ 是碱性氧化物。实际上它是两性氧化物：既能与酸反应（生成盐和水），也能与碱反应（生成铝酸盐配离子和水）。题目要求说明分类理由时，两个反应都要写出来。

What do periods and groups correspond to?周期与族分别对应什么？
A period is the number of occupied electron shells; a group (s- and p-block) is the number of valence electrons.周期数等于被占据的电子层数；族（对 s 区、p 区而言）等于价电子数。
Define effective nuclear charge.什么是有效核电荷？
$Z_{eff} = Z - S$: the net positive charge felt by a valence electron once inner-shell shielding is subtracted from the proton number.$Z_{eff} = Z - S$：从质子数中减去内层屏蔽后，价电子实际感受到的净正电荷。
Why does first IE dip from Mg to Al?为什么第一电离能从 Mg 到 Al 出现下降？
Al's outer electron is in the higher-energy 3p subshell (shielded by 3s$^2$), easier to remove than Mg's 3s electron despite Al's higher nuclear charge.Al 的最外层电子位于能量更高的 3p 亚层（受 3s$^2$ 屏蔽），尽管核电荷更高，仍比 Mg 的 3s 电子更容易移走。
Why does first IE dip from N to O?为什么第一电离能从 N 到 O 出现下降？
O's $2p^4$ configuration forces two electrons into one p-orbital; the extra repulsion makes removal easier than from N's stable, half-filled $2p^3$.O 的 $2p^4$ 组态迫使两个电子挤进同一个 p 轨道，额外的排斥使其比 N 稳定的半充满 $2p^3$ 更容易失去电子。
How is Al$_2$O$_3$ classified, and why?Al$_2$O$_3$ 如何分类？为什么？
Amphoteric: it reacts with both acids and bases, sitting between the basic oxides of groups 1 to 2 and the acidic oxides of groups 14 to 16.两性氧化物：既能与酸反应也能与碱反应，介于第 1 到 2 族的碱性氧化物与第 14 到 16 族的酸性氧化物之间。
HL: What is the IB definition of a transition element, and who is excluded?HL：IB 对过渡元素的定义是什么？哪些元素被排除？
A d-block element forming at least one stable ion with an incomplete d-subshell. Sc ($3d^0$ as Sc$^{3+}$) and Zn ($3d^{10}$ as Zn$^{2+}$) are d-block but not transition elements.能形成至少一种 d 亚层不满的稳定离子的 d 区元素。Sc（Sc$^{3+}$ 为 $3d^0$）与 Zn（Zn$^{2+}$ 为 $3d^{10}$）属于 d 区但不是过渡元素。
What defines a homologous series?同系列的定义是什么？
A family of compounds with the same general formula, differing by a fixed CH$_2$ increment, with similar chemistry and gradually changing physical properties.一组具有相同通式的化合物，彼此相差固定的 CH$_2$ 单元，化学性质相似，物理性质随链长平滑变化。
HL: What makes a carbon chiral, and what special property do enantiomers show?HL：什么样的碳是手性碳？对映体有什么特殊性质？
A chiral carbon is bonded to four different groups. Its enantiomers are non-superimposable mirror images that rotate plane-polarized light in opposite directions.手性碳连接四个互不相同的基团。其对映体是不能重合的镜像，能使平面偏振光向相反方向旋转。
