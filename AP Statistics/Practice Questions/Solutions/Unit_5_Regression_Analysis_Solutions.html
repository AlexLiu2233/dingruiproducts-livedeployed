A fitted relationship for nonconstant x and y is $\hat y=a+bx$, with nonzero b. Define $u=kx+h$ and $v=my+d$, where $k<0$ and $m>0$. All constants are known. A colleague claims that both the slope and r are unchanged because “units never matter.”对均非恒定的 x 与 y，拟合关系为 $\hat y=a+bx$，其中 b 非零。定义 $u=kx+h$、$v=my+d$，其中 $k<0$、$m>0$，所有常数已知。同事称“单位从不影响结果”，因此斜率与 r 均不变。

  1. Derive the fitted equation predicting v from u, with slope and intercept.推导由 u 预测 v 的拟合方程，给出斜率与截距。 [3]
  2. Determine the transformed r and r-squared and evaluate the colleague’s claim.确定变换后的 r 与 r-squared，并评价同事的主张。 [3]
  3. For a = 10, b = 2, k = −2, h = 6, m = 3, d = 1, predict v at u = 2 and confirm by the original model.当 a = 10、b = 2、k = −2、h = 6、m = 3、d = 1 时，预测 u = 2 对应的 v，并用原模型核验。 [2]
  4. Explain whether transforming units removes extrapolation or changes a descriptive relationship into a causal one.说明单位变换是否能消除外推，或把描述关系变成因果关系。 [2]
See the part-by-part responses below.见下方逐部分解答。

(a) 3 points3 分

Substitute $x=(u-h)/k$ into $\hat v=m(a+bx)+d$. Thus $\hat v=(ma+d-mbh/k)+(mb/k)u$. The transformed slope is mb/k and intercept is ma+d−mbh/k. 1 point for inverse substitution; 1 for slope; 1 for intercept.将 $x=(u-h)/k$ 代入 $\hat v=m(a+bx)+d$，得 $\hat v=(ma+d-mbh/k)+(mb/k)u$。变换斜率为 mb/k，截距为 ma+d−mbh/k。逆代入 1 分；斜率 1 分；截距 1 分。

(b) 3 points3 分

Negative scaling of x and positive scaling of y reverse the sign of r and preserve its magnitude, so new r = −r and new r-squared = r-squared. The slope changes to mb/k, of opposite sign for nonzero b. The claim is false. 1 point for r; 1 for squared r; 1 for slope/claim.x 的负比例缩放与 y 的正比例缩放使 r 符号反转、绝对值不变，因此新 r = −r，新 r-squared = 原 r-squared。斜率变为 mb/k，b 非零时符号相反。该主张错误。r 1 分；r 的平方 1 分；斜率与主张 1 分。

(c) 2 points2 分

The transformed line is v-hat = 49 − 3u, giving 43 at u = 2. Original x = (2 − 6)/(−2) = 2, y-hat = 14, and 3(14) + 1 = 43. 1 point for transformed prediction; 1 for independent substitution check.变换直线为 v-hat = 49 − 3u，u = 2 时预测 43。原 x = (2 − 6)/(−2) = 2，y-hat = 14，且 3(14) + 1 = 43。变换预测 1 分；独立代入核验 1 分。

(d) 2 points2 分

The observed domain is transformed along with x; a point outside it remains outside the transformed domain. Units do not change sampling or assignment, so confounding and causal limitations persist. 1 point for domain argument; 1 for design argument.观测定义域随 x 一起变换，域外点在变换后仍为域外点。单位不会改变抽样或分配，因此混杂与因果局限仍然存在。定义域论证 1 分；设计论证 1 分。

Where this goes wrong (M24)常见错误（M24）

Unit-free r is invariant only under positive rescaling; a negative scaling reverses its sign. A dimensional slope follows both response and explanatory scales.无单位 r 只在正比例缩放下保持不变；负比例缩放使符号反转。带单位斜率随响应与解释变量两者的尺度变化。

One symbolic transformation ties together prediction, coefficient units, correlation and domain.一次符号变换串联预测、系数单位、相关与适用域。
