Topic E5.1 · VocabularyTopic E5.1 · 术语

What is a Differential Equation何谓微分方程 HL AHL 5.18

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State the order of a differential equation and identify a first-order DE.
  • Distinguish the general solution (one arbitrary constant) from a particular solution fixed by an initial condition.
  • Verify that a given function satisfies a DE by substituting it into both sides.
  • Write down a DE from a verbal statement about a rate of change, with an initial condition and consistent units.
  • Deduce the value of the arbitrary constant from an initial condition.
  • 写出（state）微分方程的阶数，并识别一阶微分方程。
  • 区分通解（general solution，含一个任意常数）与由初始条件（initial condition）确定的特解（particular solution）。
  • 把给定函数代入方程两边，验证（verify）它满足微分方程。
  • 由关于变化率的文字叙述写出微分方程，附上初始条件，并保持单位一致。
  • 由初始条件推断（deduce）任意常数的值。
The vocabulary set.
  • A differential equation (DE) is an equation involving an unknown function $y(x)$ together with one or more of its derivatives.
  • The order of a DE is the order of the highest derivative that appears. First-order means only $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$ shows up.
  • To solve a DE is to find an explicit formula $y = $ (expression in $x$), or an implicit relation $F(x, y) = 0$, that satisfies the equation.
  • The general solution contains one arbitrary constant (for a first-order DE). A particular solution is the general solution with that constant fixed by an initial condition, such as $y(0) = 1$.
  • To verify a candidate $y(x)$, substitute it into both sides of the DE and check that the two sides agree as functions of $x$.
术语表。
  • 微分方程（DE）是把未知函数 $y(x)$ 与其若干阶导数联系起来的方程。
  • 阶数是方程中所出现的最高阶导数的阶数。一阶表示只出现 $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$。
  • 解微分方程即求得显式 $y = $（$x$ 的表达式）或隐式关系 $F(x, y) = 0$ 满足该方程。
  • 通解含一个任意常数（一阶 DE）。特解是用初始条件（如 $y(0) = 1$）把通解中常数确定后的解。
  • 验证候选解 $y(x)$：代入方程两边，检查作为 $x$ 的函数是否相等。
Worked Example E5.1: verify a solutionE5.1 例题：验证解

Verify that $y = e^{x}$ is a solution of the differential equation $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = y$.验证 $y = e^{x}$ 是微分方程 $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = y$ 的解。

Differentiate the candidate. $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = \tfrac{d}{dx}(e^{x}) = e^{x}$.

对候选解求导。$\tfrac{dy}{dx} = \tfrac{d}{dx}(e^{x}) = e^{x}$。

Compare with the right-hand side. The DE says $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = y$. Substituting the candidate, $y = e^{x}$, so the right-hand side is $e^{x}$.

与右边比较。方程为 $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = y$。把候选解 $y = e^{x}$ 代入，右边为 $e^{x}$。

Conclusion. Left-hand side $e^{x}$ equals right-hand side $e^{x}$ for every $x$. Therefore $y = e^{x}$ satisfies the DE.

结论。左右两边对所有 $x$ 均为 $e^{x}$。故 $y = e^{x}$ 满足该方程。

Remark. $y = e^{x}$ is one particular solution. The general solution is $y = A e^{x}$ for any constant $A$, because differentiating $A e^{x}$ still gives $A e^{x}$. An initial condition such as $y(0) = 1$ would fix $A = 1$ and recover the candidate above.

注记。$y = e^{x}$ 只是一个特解。通解为 $y = A e^{x}$（$A$ 为任意常数），因为 $A e^{x}$ 的导数仍为 $A e^{x}$。初始条件 $y(0) = 1$ 可确定 $A = 1$，恰好得到上述候选解。

Going deeper: why first-order DEs need exactly one constant深入：一阶 DE 为何只含一个常数

Integrating a first-order DE involves exactly one antiderivative step, and every antiderivative carries an additive constant of integration. Higher-order DEs require more antiderivative steps and pick up more constants: a second-order DE has a general solution with two arbitrary constants, and so on. In E5 we work with first-order equations only, so one initial condition is always enough to pin down a unique particular solution.

解一阶微分方程恰好做一次反求导（积分），每次反求导都带出一个积分常数。更高阶 DE 需要更多次反求导，从而引入更多任意常数：二阶 DE 的通解含两个任意常数，依此类推。E5 只处理一阶方程，所以一个初始条件总足以唯一确定特解。

Worked Example E5.1b: verify a solution AND its initial conditionE5.1b 例题：同时验证解与初始条件

Verify that $y = 3e^{2x} - 1$ is the particular solution of $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = 2y + 2$ satisfying $y(0) = 2$.验证 $y = 3e^{2x} - 1$ 是 $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = 2y + 2$ 满足 $y(0) = 2$ 的特解。

Differentiate the candidate. $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = 3 \cdot 2 e^{2x} = 6e^{2x}$.

对候选解求导。$\tfrac{dy}{dx} = 3 \cdot 2 e^{2x} = 6e^{2x}$。

Substitute into the right-hand side. $2y + 2 = 2(3e^{2x} - 1) + 2 = 6e^{2x} - 2 + 2 = 6e^{2x}$.

代入右边。$2y + 2 = 2(3e^{2x} - 1) + 2 = 6e^{2x} - 2 + 2 = 6e^{2x}$。

The DE is satisfied: both sides equal $6e^{2x}$ for every $x$. But this only confirms $y = 3e^{2x} - 1$ is a solution, not necessarily the one matching $y(0) = 2$.

方程成立：两边对所有 $x$ 都等于 $6e^{2x}$。但这只说明 $y = 3e^{2x} - 1$ 是某个解，还未确认它就是满足 $y(0) = 2$ 的那一个。

Check the initial condition separately. $y(0) = 3e^{0} - 1 = 3 - 1 = 2$. ✓

单独检验初始条件。$y(0) = 3e^{0} - 1 = 3 - 1 = 2$。 ✓

Conclusion. Both checks must pass independently: verifying the DE alone does not confirm the constant is correct, and verifying $y(0)=2$ alone does not confirm the function actually solves the DE.

结论。两项检验缺一不可：仅验证方程本身并不能确认常数选对了；仅验证 $y(0)=2$ 也不能确认该函数确实满足方程。

Setting up a differential equation from a context由情境建立微分方程

Many questions describe a rate in words and leave the equation to you. Translate phrase by phrase. "The rate of change of $Q$" is $\tfrac{dQ}{dt}$. "Is proportional to $X$" means $= kX$ for a constant $k$. "Decreases" or "is lost" means the rate is negative, so write $-kX$ with $k > 0$ rather than leaving the sign inside $k$. A starting value ("initially", "at $t = 0$") becomes the initial condition. Finally check units: both sides must carry the same unit, such as kg per minute.

很多题目用文字描述变化率，方程要你自己写。逐句翻译。"$Q$ 的变化率"即 $\tfrac{dQ}{dt}$。"与 $X$ 成正比"（is proportional to）表示 $= kX$，$k$ 为常数。"减少"或"流失"表示变化率为负，所以写成 $-kX$ 并注明 $k > 0$，不要把符号藏在 $k$ 里。起始值（"最初"、"$t = 0$ 时"）就是初始条件。最后检查单位：两边单位必须相同，例如千克每分钟。

Common misconception. "Proportional to $X$" is not "equal to $X$": without the constant $k$ the model has no free parameter to fit to data, and a later part that asks you to find $k$ cannot be answered.

常见误解。"与 $X$ 成正比"不等于"等于 $X$"：没有常数 $k$，模型就没有可用数据拟合的参数，后面要求求 $k$ 的小问也就无从下手。

Worked Example E5.1c: set up a DE from a mixing contextE5.1c 例题：由混合问题建立微分方程

A tank holds $100$ litres of brine containing $5$ kg of dissolved salt. Pure water flows in at $2$ litres per minute and the well-mixed solution flows out at $2$ litres per minute. Let $S$ kg be the mass of salt in the tank after $t$ minutes. (a) Write down a differential equation and an initial condition for $S$. (b) Verify that $S = 5e^{-t/50}$ satisfies both. (c) Find the mass of salt after $10$ minutes.一个水箱装有 $100$ 升盐水，其中溶有 $5$ 千克盐。纯水以每分钟 $2$ 升流入，充分混合后的溶液以每分钟 $2$ 升流出。设 $t$ 分钟后箱中盐的质量为 $S$ 千克。(a) 写出 $S$ 满足的微分方程与初始条件。(b) 验证 $S = 5e^{-t/50}$ 同时满足二者。(c) 求 $10$ 分钟后箱中盐的质量。

(a) Rate in minus rate out. The inflow is pure water, so salt enters at $0$ kg per minute. In and out flows are equal, so the volume stays $100$ litres and the concentration is $\tfrac{S}{100}$ kg per litre. Salt leaves at $2 \times \tfrac{S}{100} = \tfrac{S}{50}$ kg per minute. Hence

(a) 流入速率减流出速率。流入的是纯水，所以盐以每分钟 $0$ 千克进入。流入与流出相等，所以体积保持 $100$ 升，浓度为每升 $\tfrac{S}{100}$ 千克。盐以每分钟 $2 \times \tfrac{S}{100} = \tfrac{S}{50}$ 千克流出。于是

$$ \frac{dS}{dt} \;=\; -\frac{S}{50}, \qquad S(0) \;=\; 5. $$

Units: the left side is kg per minute; on the right, $\tfrac{S}{50}$ is kg times (litres per minute divided by litres), also kg per minute.

单位：左边为千克每分钟；右边 $\tfrac{S}{50}$ 为千克乘以（升每分钟除以升），同样是千克每分钟。

(b) Verify. $\tfrac{dS}{dt} = 5 \cdot \left(-\tfrac{1}{50}\right) e^{-t/50} = -\tfrac{1}{10} e^{-t/50}$, and $-\tfrac{S}{50} = -\tfrac{5e^{-t/50}}{50} = -\tfrac{1}{10} e^{-t/50}$. The two sides agree for every $t$. Also $S(0) = 5e^{0} = 5$.

(b) 验证。$\tfrac{dS}{dt} = 5 \cdot \left(-\tfrac{1}{50}\right) e^{-t/50} = -\tfrac{1}{10} e^{-t/50}$，而 $-\tfrac{S}{50} = -\tfrac{5e^{-t/50}}{50} = -\tfrac{1}{10} e^{-t/50}$。两边对所有 $t$ 相等。且 $S(0) = 5e^{0} = 5$。

(c) $S(10) = 5e^{-10/50} = 5e^{-0.2} \approx 4.09$ kg.

(c) $S(10) = 5e^{-10/50} = 5e^{-0.2} \approx 4.09$ 千克。

Why this works. A conservation statement (rate in minus rate out) is already a differential equation; the only modelling step is expressing each rate in terms of $S$.

为什么可行。守恒关系（流入速率减流出速率）本身就是一个微分方程；唯一的建模步骤是把每个速率用 $S$ 表示出来。

Exam tip考试提示

When asked to "verify" or "show that" a function solves a DE, work on each side separately until both reach the same expression, then state that they are equal; do not start by assuming the equation holds. Check the initial condition as a separate line. When you set up a model, define every variable with its unit, introduce the constant of proportionality explicitly, and state its sign ($k > 0$).

题目要求"验证"（verify）或"证明"（show that）某函数是微分方程的解时，要分别化简两边直到得到同一表达式，再说明二者相等；不要一开始就假设等式成立。初始条件单独写一行检验。建立模型时，定义每个变量及其单位，明确引入比例常数，并说明其符号（$k > 0$）。

Check your understanding自我检测
1. State the order of $\left(\tfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^{3} + y = x$.1. 写出 $\left(\tfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^{3} + y = x$ 的阶数。

First order. The highest derivative present is $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$; the cube is a power of that derivative, not a higher derivative.

一阶。所出现的最高阶导数是 $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$；立方只是该导数的幂，并不是更高阶的导数。

2. Show that $y = Ax^{2}$ satisfies $x \tfrac{dy}{dx} = 2y$ for every constant $A$, and find the particular solution with $y(2) = 12$.2. 证明对任意常数 $A$，$y = Ax^{2}$ 满足 $x \tfrac{dy}{dx} = 2y$，并求满足 $y(2) = 12$ 的特解。

$\tfrac{dy}{dx} = 2Ax$, so the left side is $x \cdot 2Ax = 2Ax^{2}$, and the right side is $2y = 2Ax^{2}$. They agree. Then $y(2) = 4A = 12$ gives $A = 3$, so $y = 3x^{2}$.

$\tfrac{dy}{dx} = 2Ax$，所以左边为 $x \cdot 2Ax = 2Ax^{2}$，右边为 $2y = 2Ax^{2}$，两边相等。再由 $y(2) = 4A = 12$ 得 $A = 3$，所以 $y = 3x^{2}$。

3. In a school of $800$ students, $N$ students have heard a rumour after $t$ days. The rate at which $N$ increases is proportional to the product of the number who have heard it and the number who have not. Initially $10$ students have heard it. Write down the DE and initial condition, and find the initial rate if the constant of proportionality is $0.001$.3. 某校有 $800$ 名学生，$t$ 天后有 $N$ 人听到一则传言。$N$ 的增长速率与"已听到的人数"和"未听到的人数"之积成正比。最初有 $10$ 人听到。写出微分方程与初始条件；若比例常数为 $0.001$，求初始增长速率。

$\tfrac{dN}{dt} = kN(800 - N)$ with $k > 0$, and $N(0) = 10$. With $k = 0.001$: $\tfrac{dN}{dt} = 0.001 \times 10 \times 790 = 7.9$ students per day at $t = 0$.

$\tfrac{dN}{dt} = kN(800 - N)$，$k > 0$，且 $N(0) = 10$。取 $k = 0.001$：$t = 0$ 时 $\tfrac{dN}{dt} = 0.001 \times 10 \times 790 = 7.9$ 人每天。

Topic E5.2 · Separable VariablesTopic E5.2 · 分离变量

Separable Variables分离变量法 HL AHL 5.18

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Identify a separable DE $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x)\,g(y)$, including ones that need factorising first, such as $e^{x - y} = e^{x} e^{-y}$.
  • Solve a separable DE by separating, integrating both sides with one constant, and applying an initial condition.
  • Explain why $\int \tfrac{dy}{y} = \ln|y| + C$ leads to $y = Ae^{(\dots)}$, and identify constant solutions lost by dividing.
  • Recognise a homogeneous DE $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = F\!\left(\tfrac{y}{x}\right)$ and reduce it to a separable DE with the substitution $y = vx$.
  • Verify a solution, explicit or implicit, by substituting back into the DE.
  • 识别可分离（separable）方程 $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x)\,g(y)$，包括需要先因式分解的情形，例如 $e^{x - y} = e^{x} e^{-y}$。
  • 分离变量、两边积分（只加一个常数）并代入初始条件，求解（solve）可分离方程。
  • 解释（explain）为何由 $\int \tfrac{dy}{y} = \ln|y| + C$ 得到 $y = Ae^{(\dots)}$，并找出因除法而丢失的常值解。
  • 识别齐次方程（homogeneous DE）$\tfrac{dy}{dx} = F\!\left(\tfrac{y}{x}\right)$，并用换元 $y = vx$ 化为可分离方程。
  • 把显式解或隐式解代回方程进行验证（verify）。
The recipe. A first-order DE is separable if it can be written as $$ \frac{dy}{dx} \;=\; f(x) \, g(y). $$ Rearrange so all $y$ pieces sit on the left and all $x$ pieces on the right, then integrate both sides: $$ \int \frac{dy}{g(y)} \;=\; \int f(x) \, dx. $$ Add a single arbitrary constant $C$ (on either side; conventionally on the right). Solve for $y$ if the algebra allows; otherwise leave the answer implicit. Apply the initial condition after integrating to fix $C$.

Three traps.
  • If $g(y) = 0$ at some value of $y$, that constant function is also a solution. Check whether dividing by $g(y)$ lost a solution.
  • $\int \tfrac{dy}{y} = \ln |y| + C$, not $\ln y + C$. The absolute value disappears only after exponentiating into $y = A e^{(\dots)}$ with $A$ a real constant that may be positive or negative.
  • Constants combine: $\ln |y| = h(x) + C$ becomes $|y| = e^{C} e^{h(x)}$, then $y = A e^{h(x)}$ with $A = \pm e^{C} \neq 0$ (the case $A = 0$ comes from the lost constant solution $y = 0$).
套路。一阶 DE 若可写成 $$ \frac{dy}{dx} \;=\; f(x) \, g(y), $$ 则称可分离。整理使所有 $y$ 项在左、所有 $x$ 项在右，再两边积分： $$ \int \frac{dy}{g(y)} \;=\; \int f(x) \, dx. $$ 只需一个任意常数 $C$（习惯写在右边）。能解出 $y$ 就解出，否则留作隐式解。初始条件须在积分之后代入以确定 $C$。

三个陷阱。
  • 若 $g(y) = 0$ 在某 $y$ 值成立，则该常值函数也是解。检查"除以 $g(y)$"是否丢解。
  • $\int \tfrac{dy}{y} = \ln |y| + C$，不是 $\ln y + C$。绝对值在指数化为 $y = A e^{(\dots)}$（$A$ 可正可负）后才消失。
  • 常数合并：$\ln |y| = h(x) + C$ 变为 $|y| = e^{C} e^{h(x)}$，再写作 $y = A e^{h(x)}$，其中 $A = \pm e^{C} \ne 0$（$A = 0$ 对应被丢掉的常值解 $y = 0$）。
Worked Example E5.2: separable with initial conditionE5.2 例题：含初始条件

Solve $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = x y$ subject to $y(0) = 1$.求解 $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = x y$，初始条件 $y(0) = 1$。

Identify the form. Here $f(x) = x$ and $g(y) = y$, so the equation is separable.

识别形式。$f(x) = x$，$g(y) = y$，方程可分离。

Separate.

分离变量。

$$ \frac{dy}{y} \;=\; x \, dx. $$

Integrate both sides.

两边积分。

$$ \int \frac{dy}{y} \;=\; \int x \, dx \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \ln |y| \;=\; \frac{x^{2}}{2} + C. $$

Solve for $y$. Exponentiate both sides:

Homogeneous equations: the substitution y = vx齐次方程：换元 y = vx

Definition. A first-order DE is homogeneous if the right-hand side can be written as a function of the ratio $\tfrac{y}{x}$ alone: $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = F\!\left(\tfrac{y}{x}\right)$. A quick test: replace $x$ by $\lambda x$ and $y$ by $\lambda y$; if the $\lambda$ cancels completely, the equation is homogeneous. For example $\tfrac{x^{2} + y^{2}}{xy}$ is homogeneous, but $x + y$ is not.

定义。若一阶方程的右边可以只写成比值 $\tfrac{y}{x}$ 的函数，即 $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = F\!\left(\tfrac{y}{x}\right)$，则称其为齐次方程（homogeneous）。快速检验：把 $x$ 换成 $\lambda x$、$y$ 换成 $\lambda y$，若 $\lambda$ 完全消去，方程就是齐次的。例如 $\tfrac{x^{2} + y^{2}}{xy}$ 是齐次的，而 $x + y$ 不是。

Mechanism. Put $y = vx$, where $v$ is a new function of $x$. By the product rule $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = v + x\tfrac{dv}{dx}$, so the DE becomes $v + x\tfrac{dv}{dx} = F(v)$, that is $x\tfrac{dv}{dx} = F(v) - v$. This is separable in $v$ and $x$: $\int \tfrac{dv}{F(v) - v} = \int \tfrac{dx}{x}$. Solve for $v$, then replace $v$ by $\tfrac{y}{x}$.

原理。令 $y = vx$，其中 $v$ 是 $x$ 的新函数。由乘积法则 $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = v + x\tfrac{dv}{dx}$，方程化为 $v + x\tfrac{dv}{dx} = F(v)$，即 $x\tfrac{dv}{dx} = F(v) - v$。这是关于 $v$ 与 $x$ 的可分离方程：$\int \tfrac{dv}{F(v) - v} = \int \tfrac{dx}{x}$。解出 $v$ 后再把 $v$ 换回 $\tfrac{y}{x}$。

Common misconception. Writing $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = \tfrac{dv}{dx}$ or $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = v$ after substituting. Because $v$ depends on $x$, the product rule term $x\tfrac{dv}{dx}$ is essential; dropping it destroys the method.

常见误解。换元后写成 $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = \tfrac{dv}{dx}$ 或 $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = v$。由于 $v$ 依赖于 $x$，乘积法则给出的 $x\tfrac{dv}{dx}$ 项必不可少；漏掉它整个方法就失效了。

The standard form. A first-order DE is linear if it can be written $$ \frac{dy}{dx} + P(x) \, y \;=\; Q(x). $$ Here $y$ appears to the first power, $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$ appears to the first power, and there are no products of $y$ with $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$.

The integrating factor. $$ \mu(x) \;=\; \exp\Bigl( \int P(x) \, dx \Bigr). $$ Multiplying the standard form by $\mu(x)$ collapses the left-hand side into a product-rule derivative: $$ \frac{d}{dx} \bigl( \mu(x) \, y \bigr) \;=\; \mu(x) \, Q(x). $$ Integrate both sides with respect to $x$ and divide by $\mu(x)$ to recover $y$.

Three-line workflow. Identify $P, Q$. Compute $\mu = e^{\int P \, dx}$ (no $+ C$ needed inside the exponent, since any constant just rescales $\mu$). Write $\mu y = \int \mu Q \, dx + C$ and solve for $y$.
标准形式。一阶 DE 若可写为 $$ \frac{dy}{dx} + P(x) \, y \;=\; Q(x), $$ 则称线性。此处 $y$ 与 $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$ 均为一次幂，且不含两者的乘积。

积分因子。 $$ \mu(x) \;=\; \exp\Bigl( \int P(x) \, dx \Bigr). $$ 标准形式两边乘 $\mu(x)$ 后，左边化为乘积法则的导数： $$ \frac{d}{dx} \bigl( \mu(x) \, y \bigr) \;=\; \mu(x) \, Q(x). $$ 对 $x$ 积分两边、再除以 $\mu(x)$ 即得 $y$。

三行流程。识别 $P, Q$；算 $\mu = e^{\int P \, dx}$（指数内不必加 $+ C$，常数只缩放 $\mu$）；写 $\mu y = \int \mu Q \, dx + C$ 并解出 $y$。

Going deeper: why the integrating factor works深入：积分因子为何奏效

We want a function $\mu(x)$ such that $\mu y' + \mu P y$ is the derivative of some product. The product rule gives $\tfrac{d}{dx}(\mu y) = \mu' y + \mu y'$. Matching coefficients on $y$ requires $\mu' = \mu P$, which is itself a separable DE for $\mu$. Solving $\tfrac{d \mu}{\mu} = P \, dx$ gives $\ln |\mu| = \int P \, dx$, hence $\mu = e^{\int P \, dx}$ (the absolute value and overall sign are absorbed because any non-zero scalar multiple of $\mu$ still works). By construction, multiplying the standard form by this $\mu$ makes the left-hand side an exact derivative.

我们希望找到 $\mu(x)$ 使 $\mu y' + \mu P y$ 能写成某乘积的导数。由乘积法则 $\tfrac{d}{dx}(\mu y) = \mu' y + \mu y'$，要让 $y$ 的系数对得上需 $\mu' = \mu P$，这本身是一个关于 $\mu$ 的可分离方程。解 $\tfrac{d \mu}{\mu} = P \, dx$ 得 $\ln |\mu| = \int P \, dx$，故 $\mu = e^{\int P \, dx}$（绝对值与正负号可吸收，因为 $\mu$ 的任一非零数乘仍可作积分因子）。如此构造下，标准形式乘 $\mu$ 后左边恰为某函数的导数。

Setup. Given a first-order initial-value problem $$ \frac{dy}{dx} \;=\; f(x, y), \qquad y(x_{0}) \;=\; y_{0}, $$ Euler's method advances the approximation by a fixed step size $h$ using the linear tangent at the current point: $$ x_{n + 1} \;=\; x_{n} + h, \qquad y_{n + 1} \;=\; y_{n} + h \cdot f(x_{n}, y_{n}). $$ Interpretation. $y_{n + 1}$ is read off the tangent line at $(x_{n}, y_{n})$ after stepping $h$ units in $x$. The method is a polygonal approximation to the true solution curve.

Error behaviour. Halving $h$ roughly halves the total error (Euler is first-order accurate). The trade-off is twice the arithmetic. For exam problems with two or three steps, hand computation is expected; show every $f(x_{n}, y_{n})$ value.

What a slope field is. For the DE $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x, y)$, at each grid point $(x, y)$ draw a short line segment whose slope equals $f(x, y)$. The collection of segments is the slope field (also called the direction field). A solution curve through any starting point follows the local slope at every step, so the curve must be tangent to the segments it passes through.

Reading a slope field.
  • Horizontal segments ($f = 0$) mark equilibrium or turning behaviour. Constant solutions $y = c$ live where $f(x, c) = 0$ for all $x$.
  • Steep upward segments ($f$ large positive) indicate fast growth; steep downward ($f$ large negative) indicate fast decay.
  • If $f(x, y)$ depends only on $y$ (the DE is autonomous), all segments at the same $y$-level have the same slope: the field is invariant under horizontal shifts.
Why use it. The slope field gives the qualitative behaviour of every solution without solving algebraically. Useful when the DE is not separable, not linear, or simply too messy to integrate.
斜率场是什么。对方程 $\tfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x, y)$，在每个格点 $(x, y)$ 画一段短线段，斜率为 $f(x, y)$。这些线段的集合称为斜率场（或方向场）。过任一起点的解曲线必处处沿当地斜率，故曲线在所经过的每段线段处都与之相切。

读图要点。
  • 水平线段（$f = 0$）标记平衡或极值。常值解 $y = c$ 出现在 $f(x, c) = 0$ 对所有 $x$ 成立处。
  • 陡峭向上线段（$f$ 大正）表示快速增长；陡峭向下（$f$ 大负）表示快速衰减。
  • 若 $f(x, y)$ 只依赖 $y$（自治方程），同一 $y$ 高度上所有线段斜率相同：斜率场在水平方向平移不变。
用途。无须解析求解即可获得所有解的定性行为。当方程不可分离、不线性、或难以积分时尤其有用。

Three canonical models.
  • Exponential growth or decay. $\tfrac{dy}{dt} = k y$ with $k$ constant. Separable. Solution $y(t) = y_{0} e^{k t}$. Positive $k$ gives growth; negative $k$ gives decay.
  • Newton's law of cooling. $\tfrac{dT}{dt} = -k (T - T_{\text{env}})$ with $k > 0$ and $T_{\text{env}}$ the ambient temperature. Separable after the substitution $u = T - T_{\text{env}}$. Solution $T(t) = T_{\text{env}} + (T_{0} - T_{\text{env}}) e^{-k t}$.
  • Logistic growth. $\tfrac{dP}{dt} = k P (M - P)$ with $M > 0$ the carrying capacity. Separable; the $P$-side needs partial fractions. Solution $P(t) = \dfrac{M}{1 + B e^{-k M t}}$ for some constant $B$ fixed by the initial condition.
The recurring trick. Newton's law and the logistic equation both yield to a clean substitution that turns the right-hand side into something straightforwardly separable. For Newton's law, set $u = T - T_{\text{env}}$. For logistic, use partial fractions on $\tfrac{1}{P(M - P)} = \tfrac{1}{M} \bigl( \tfrac{1}{P} + \tfrac{1}{M - P} \bigr)$.
三个经典模型。
  • 指数增长或衰减。$\tfrac{dy}{dt} = k y$（$k$ 为常数）。可分离。解 $y(t) = y_{0} e^{k t}$。$k > 0$ 增长，$k < 0$ 衰减。
  • 牛顿冷却律。$\tfrac{dT}{dt} = -k (T - T_{\text{env}})$（$k > 0$，$T_{\text{env}}$ 为环境温度）。换元 $u = T - T_{\text{env}}$ 后可分离。解 $T(t) = T_{\text{env}} + (T_{0} - T_{\text{env}}) e^{-k t}$。
  • 逻辑斯蒂增长。$\tfrac{dP}{dt} = k P (M - P)$（$M > 0$ 为环境容量）。可分离；$P$ 一侧需部分分式。解 $P(t) = \dfrac{M}{1 + B e^{-k M t}}$，$B$ 由初始条件确定。
反复用到的技巧。牛顿冷却与逻辑斯蒂方程都靠一次干净的换元，把右边化为容易分离的形式。牛顿冷却取 $u = T - T_{\text{env}}$；逻辑斯蒂对 $\tfrac{1}{P(M - P)} = \tfrac{1}{M} \bigl( \tfrac{1}{P} + \tfrac{1}{M - P} \bigr)$ 做部分分式。

Exam Tactics考试技巧

Exam Strategy and Common Pitfalls考试策略与常见陷阱

Classify before solving先分类再求解
  • Test for separability first. If $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$ factors as $f(x) g(y)$, separation is the cleanest route. Worth checking even when the DE is also linear, because separation usually takes fewer steps than an integrating factor.
  • 先测可分离性。若 $\tfrac{dy}{dx}$ 能分解为 $f(x) g(y)$，分离法最干净。即便方程也是线性的，仍值得先试分离，因为分离法通常比积分因子法步骤更少。
  • If the right-hand side is a function of $\tfrac{y}{x}$, substitute $y = vx$. The homogeneous equation becomes separable in $v$ and $x$.
  • 若右边是 $\tfrac{y}{x}$ 的函数，代入 $y = vx$。齐次方程随即化为关于 $v$ 与 $x$ 的可分离方程。
  • If not separable, try the linear form. Reshape into $\tfrac{dy}{dx} + P(x) y = Q(x)$ and read off $P, Q$. The integrating factor $\mu = e^{\int P \, dx}$ then unlocks the equation.
  • 若不可分离，尝试线性形式。整理为 $\tfrac{dy}{dx} + P(x) y = Q(x)$ 后读出 $P, Q$。积分因子 $\mu = e^{\int P \, dx}$ 即可破题。
Constants of integration积分常数
  • Write $+ C$ explicitly. Without the constant you have found one solution, not the general solution, and the initial condition has nothing to fix. Show it appearing on the first integration step and then solve for it after the initial condition.
  • $+ C$ 必须显式写出。没有常数，你只求出了一个解，而不是通解，初始条件也就无从确定任何东西。在第一步积分时就写出，再由初始条件解出。
  • Combine constants by renaming. $e^{C_{1}}$ becomes a new constant $A$; do not carry chains of $C_{1}, C_{2}, C_{3}$ through the algebra. One symbol per final constant is cleaner.
  • 合并常数时重命名。$e^{C_{1}}$ 重命名为新常数 $A$；不要把 $C_{1}, C_{2}, C_{3}$ 链条带到最后。一个最终常数对应一个符号更清晰。
  • For separable DEs, $\int \tfrac{dy}{y}$ is $\ln |y|$. Drop the absolute value only after exponentiating into $y = A e^{(\dots)}$ where $A$ ranges over all real numbers.
  • 分离法中 $\int \tfrac{dy}{y}$ 写作 $\ln |y|$。取指数化为 $y = A e^{(\dots)}$（$A$ 可正可负可零）后才能去掉绝对值。
Euler and slope fields欧拉法与斜率场
  • For Euler, tabulate. Columns $n, x_{n}, y_{n}, f(x_{n}, y_{n}), h \cdot f, y_{n + 1}$. The table shows the method and makes slips easy to find; a bare final number is an incomplete answer.
  • 欧拉法务必列表。列 $n, x_{n}, y_{n}, f(x_{n}, y_{n}), h \cdot f, y_{n + 1}$。表格展示方法，也让错误容易查出；只写一个最终数字是不完整的答案。
  • Compare Euler to the exact solution when possible. Say whether $y_{\text{Euler}}$ undershoots or overshoots, and justify it with the concavity of the exact curve.
  • 有可能时把欧拉值与精确解比较。说明 $y_{\text{Euler}}$ 偏低还是偏高，并用精确解的凹凸性加以说明。
  • For slope-field questions, start at the zeros. Where $f(x, y) = 0$ marks horizontal segments, which is the fastest way to identify or rule out a candidate diagram.
  • 斜率场题先看零点。$f(x, y) = 0$ 处是水平线段，是辨识或排除候选图最快的特征。
