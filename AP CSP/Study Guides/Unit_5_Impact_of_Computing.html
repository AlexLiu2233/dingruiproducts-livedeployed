Unit 5: Impact of Computing

单元 5：计算的影响

Computing innovations change society in ways their creators never fully predict. This unit teaches you to evaluate those effects, from privacy and security to equity and ethics.

Topic 5.1
小节 5.1

Beneficial and Harmful Effects

有益与有害影响

Must know
  • People create computing innovations, and people often change how they complete tasks to use them. (IOC-1.A.1, IOC-1.A.2)
  • Some effects are unanticipated. One effect can be beneficial and harmful to different people or even to the same person. (IOC-1.A.3, IOC-1.A.4)
  • Computing advances can increase creativity in other fields and produce unintended beneficial advances there. (IOC-1.A.5, IOC-1.B.5)
  • Innovations can be used beyond their intended purpose, sometimes harming society, the economy, or culture. (IOC-1.B.1, IOC-1.B.2)
  • Responsible programmers consider possible unintended uses and their benefits and harms, but cannot predict every use. (IOC-1.B.3, IOC-1.B.4)
  • Rapid sharing or use by many people can produce significant impacts beyond a programmer's purpose or control. (IOC-1.B.6)
必备知识
  • 人们创造计算创新（computing innovation），也常常改变完成任务的方式来使用它们。（IOC-1.A.1, IOC-1.A.2）
  • 有些影响无法事先预料。同一种影响对不同的人，甚至对同一个人，既可能有益也可能有害。（IOC-1.A.3, IOC-1.A.4）
  • 计算技术的进步可以提高其他领域的创造力，也可能在这些领域带来意料之外的有益进展。（IOC-1.A.5, IOC-1.B.5）
  • 创新可能被用于超出原定目的的用途，有时会对社会、经济或文化造成危害。（IOC-1.B.1, IOC-1.B.2）
  • 负责任的程序员会考虑可能出现的非预期用途及其利弊，但无法预测所有用途。（IOC-1.B.3, IOC-1.B.4）
  • 程序的快速传播或大量用户运行程序，可能产生超出程序员原定目的或控制范围的重大影响。（IOC-1.B.6）

Purpose and effects

目的与影响

People design computing innovations for purposes, but using an innovation can change how people do tasks and produce effects its creators did not anticipate. Advances in computing have also supported creativity in medicine, engineering, communications, and the arts.

人们为了特定目的设计计算创新，但使用创新可能改变人们完成任务的方式，也可能产生创造者未曾预料的影响。计算技术的进步还促进了医学、工程、通信和艺术领域的创造力。

On an exam, identify a specific effect and explain who benefits and who is harmed. The same effect can have both sides for one person. Do not assume that an effect was intended just because it happened.

考试中，要指出一种具体影响，并解释谁受益、谁受害。同一种影响对同一个人也可能同时有利有弊。不要因为某种影响已经发生，就认为它是原本预期的。

Uses beyond the intended purpose

超出原定目的的用途

Targeted advertising can help businesses reach audiences, but it can also be misused against individuals or groups. Machine learning and data mining have enabled innovation in medicine, business, and science. Information discovered through them has also been used to discriminate against groups.

定向广告（targeted advertising）可以帮助企业接触目标受众，但也可能被滥用，对个人或群体造成伤害。机器学习（machine learning）和数据挖掘（data mining）促进了医学、商业和科学领域的创新。通过这些方法发现的信息也曾被用于歧视某些群体。

New uses can also bring unintended benefits, such as advances in another field. Responsible programmers consider possible uses and their effects, but cannot foresee all of them. Rapid sharing or many users can make an impact significant and difficult for the programmer to control.

新用途也可能带来意料之外的好处，例如促进另一个领域的进步。负责任的程序员会考虑可能的用途及其影响，但无法预见所有情况。程序快速传播或用户数量众多时，其影响可能变得重大，而且程序员难以控制。

Worked Example: Targeted advertising例题：定向广告

A team creates a service to help businesses show ads to people likely to be interested. A user receives relevant ads, but the targeting also reveals an inferred interest that the user wanted to keep private. An advertiser later uses the service to exclude a group from seeing opportunities.

一个团队创建了一项服务，帮助企业向可能感兴趣的人展示广告。一位用户收到了相关广告，但定向投放也暴露了这位用户希望保密的某项推断兴趣。后来，一名广告主利用该服务，让某个群体看不到一些机会。

  1. The intended purpose is to help businesses reach interested audiences.
  2. One effect is that ads are targeted using inferred interests. It benefits the user by making ads relevant, but harms the same user by exposing an interest.
  3. Excluding a group is a use beyond the team's stated purpose. It can harm that group by limiting the opportunities its members see.
  4. If the service spreads rapidly, these effects could reach many people and extend beyond the team's control. The answer should distinguish the intended purpose from both the beneficial and harmful effects.
  1. 原定目的是帮助企业接触感兴趣的受众。
  2. 其中一种影响是根据推断的兴趣定向投放广告。这使广告更相关，对该用户有利；但它也暴露了用户的兴趣，对同一用户有害。
  3. 排除某个群体是超出团队所述目的的用途。它可能限制该群体成员看到的机会，从而造成伤害。
  4. 如果服务快速传播，这些影响可能波及许多人，并超出团队的控制范围。答案应区分原定目的，以及有益和有害的影响。
Exam trap

A common wrong answer says an innovation is simply beneficial because it fulfills its intended purpose. Its actual effects may also be harmful or unintended. Explain the effect and the people affected instead of labeling the whole innovation as good or bad.

考试陷阱

常见的错误答案是：创新实现了原定目的，所以它只有好处。实际影响也可能是有害的或非预期的。应解释具体影响以及受到影响的人，而不是直接把整个创新判定为好或坏。

Going deeper

An unintended effect is not necessarily harmful. A computing advance developed for one purpose might enable creative work in medicine or the arts. Consider the use, the people affected, and the scale separately: a result that affects one person can become a broader social or cultural impact when many people use or share the program.

深入理解

非预期影响不一定有害。为某一目的开发的计算技术进步，也可能促进医学或艺术领域的创造性工作。应分别考虑用途、受到影响的人和规模：当许多人使用或分享程序时，影响一个人的结果可能演变成更广泛的社会或文化影响。

Topic 5.2
小节 5.2

Digital Divide

数字鸿沟（digital divide）

Must know
  • Internet access varies with socioeconomic, geographic, and demographic characteristics, and between countries. (IOC-1.C.1)
  • The digital divide is the difference in access to computing devices and the Internet based on these characteristics. (IOC-1.C.2)
  • The divide can affect both individuals and groups. (IOC-1.C.3)
  • It raises issues of equity, access, and influence locally and globally. (IOC-1.C.4)
  • Actions by individuals, organizations, and governments can affect the divide. (IOC-1.C.5)
必备知识
  • 互联网接入情况因社会经济、地理和人口特征而异，国家之间也有差异。(IOC-1.C.1)
  • 数字鸿沟是指基于这些特征而产生的计算设备和互联网接入差异。(IOC-1.C.2)
  • 数字鸿沟既会影响个人，也会影响群体。(IOC-1.C.3)
  • 数字鸿沟在本地和全球范围内引发公平、接入和影响力问题。(IOC-1.C.4)
  • 个人、组织和政府的行动都可能影响数字鸿沟。(IOC-1.C.5)

Recognizing the divide

识别数字鸿沟

Look for differences in access to devices or the Internet. For example, cost may limit access for some households, while location may limit Internet availability for others. Access can also differ across demographic groups and countries.

要关注计算设备或互联网接入方面的差异。例如，费用可能限制一些家庭的接入，而地理位置可能限制另一些家庭接入互联网的机会。不同人口群体和国家之间的接入情况也可能不同。

Exam pattern: connect access to impact

考试模式：把接入与影响联系起来

Identify who has less access, explain the barrier, and describe a possible effect. Unequal access can make it harder to use online resources or participate in online discussions. This raises questions about equity and whose voices have influence.

先确定谁的接入机会较少，再解释障碍，最后描述可能的影响。接入不平等可能使人们更难使用在线资源或参与在线讨论。这会引发公平性以及谁的声音具有影响力等问题。

Individuals, organizations, and governments can take actions that reduce or increase access differences. A proposed action should be evaluated by considering which barriers it addresses and which people it reaches.

个人、组织和政府的行动可能缩小或扩大接入差异。评估一项提议时，应考虑它解决了哪些障碍，以及哪些人能从中受益。

Worked Example: Online school resources例题：学校在线资源

A school posts learning materials online. Some students lack a suitable device at home, while others have a device but no reliable Internet connection. The school lends devices and makes printed copies available. How does this action affect the digital divide?

一所学校在网上发布学习资料。有些学生家里没有合适的设备，另一些学生有设备，但没有可靠的互联网连接。学校借出设备并提供纸质资料。这一行动如何影响数字鸿沟？

  1. The access barriers are different: some students lack devices, while others lack reliable Internet.
  2. Lending devices can reduce the device-access gap for students who receive them, but it does not by itself provide Internet access.
  3. Printed copies help students obtain the materials without Internet access, but they do not give those students Internet access.
  4. Answer: The school can reduce some unequal effects of the divide, but these actions do not eliminate every access barrier.
  1. 接入障碍各不相同：有些学生缺少设备，另一些学生缺少可靠的互联网连接。
  2. 借出设备可以缩小收到设备的学生与其他学生之间的设备接入差距，但仅靠借设备无法提供互联网接入。
  3. 纸质资料帮助学生在没有互联网接入的情况下获取材料，但并没有让他们接入互联网。
  4. 答案：学校可以减少数字鸿沟造成的部分不平等影响，但这些行动不能消除所有接入障碍。
Exam trap

Wrong answer: “Giving everyone a device eliminates the digital divide.” Device access is only one part of the issue. People may still lack Internet access, so check which barrier the action actually addresses.

考试陷阱

错误答案：“给每个人一台设备就能消除数字鸿沟。”设备接入只是问题的一部分。人们可能仍然无法接入互联网，因此要看这项行动实际解决了哪种障碍。

Going deeper

A difference between groups or countries does not mean every individual in each group has the same access. Consider both local and global effects, and distinguish improving access to a resource from providing access to the Internet itself.

深入理解

群体或国家之间存在差异，并不意味着每个群体中的所有个人都有相同的接入条件。既要考虑本地影响，也要考虑全球影响；还要区分改善资源获取与提供互联网接入本身。

Computing Bias

计算偏见（computing bias）

Must know
  • Computing innovations can reflect human bias through their algorithms or the data they use. (IOC-1.D.1)
  • Programmers should take action to reduce algorithmic bias and combat existing human biases. (IOC-1.D.2)
  • Bias can be embedded at all levels of software development. (IOC-1.D.3)
  • Exam pattern: identify the source of bias, explain who may be affected, and propose a way to reduce it.
必备知识
  • 计算创新（computing innovation）可能通过其算法（algorithm）或使用的数据反映人类偏见。 (IOC-1.D.1)
  • 程序员应采取行动减少算法偏见，以对抗已有的人类偏见。 (IOC-1.D.2)
  • 偏见可能被嵌入软件开发的各个层面。 (IOC-1.D.3)
  • 考试模式：指出偏见的来源，解释谁可能受到影响，并提出减少偏见的方法。

How bias enters a computing innovation

偏见如何进入计算创新

An algorithm can contain a rule that favors one group. Data can also reflect existing human biases or leave some groups underrepresented. An innovation that uses such data can reflect those biases in its results.

算法中的规则可能偏向某一群体。数据也可能反映已有的人类偏见，或使某些群体的代表性不足。使用这类数据的计算创新可能在结果中反映这些偏见。

Bias can enter at different stages, from choosing data and designing rules to implementing and testing software. Programmers should examine both the data and the algorithm rather than assume that a computer's result is neutral.

偏见可能在不同阶段进入软件，包括选择数据、设计规则、实现和测试。程序员应同时检查数据和算法，而不是假设计算机给出的结果是中立的。

Worked Example: Screening applicants例题：筛选申请人

A hiring tool uses past application data that mostly describes people from School A. Its designers also write this rule. The Boolean attendedSchoolA is already set for each applicant.

一个招聘工具使用的历史申请数据大多描述来自 School A 的人。设计者还编写了下面的规则。每位申请人的布尔值（Boolean）attendedSchoolA 已经设定。

IF(attendedSchoolA) {
  DISPLAY("priority")
} ELSE {
  DISPLAY("standard")
}
  1. Two applicants have the same relevant qualifications, but only one attended School A. The rule gives that applicant priority solely because of the school.
  2. The rule can disadvantage equally qualified applicants from other schools. The data may also underrepresent those applicants, so it may not adequately reflect them.
  3. Programmers could remove the school preference, seek more representative data, and test outcomes for different groups. These actions can reduce bias, but do not guarantee its elimination.
  4. Answer: Both the written rule and the data are possible sources of bias. Examine and address both.
  1. 两位申请人的相关资质相同，但只有一人就读过 School A。规则仅因为学校不同就给予该申请人优先权。
  2. 该规则可能使来自其他学校、资质相同的申请人处于不利地位。数据也可能使这些申请人的代表性不足，因此不能充分反映他们的情况。
  3. 程序员可以移除学校优先规则，寻找更有代表性的数据，并测试不同群体的结果。这些行动可以减少偏见，但不能保证完全消除偏见。
  4. 答案：编写的规则和数据都可能是偏见来源，应同时检查并处理两者。
Exam trap

Wrong answer: “A computing result cannot be biased because a computer produced it.” The algorithm and data can reflect human biases, so automation alone does not remove them.

考试陷阱

错误答案：“计算结果由计算机产生，因此不可能有偏见。”算法和数据都可能反映人类偏见，所以自动化本身不能消除偏见。

Going deeper

Fixing one rule does not prove the whole innovation is unbiased. Bias may remain in the data or enter elsewhere in development. Check outcomes across groups and revisit both data and design when a problem appears.

深入理解

修正一条规则并不能证明整个计算创新没有偏见。偏见可能仍存在于数据中，也可能在开发的其他环节进入。应检查不同群体的结果，并在发现问题时重新审视数据和设计。

小节 5.4

Crowdsourcing

众包（crowdsourcing）

Must know
  • Access to information and public data helps people identify problems, develop solutions, and share results. (IOC-1.E.1)
  • Distributed and citizen science help solve scientific problems. (IOC-1.E.2)
  • In citizen science, distributed individuals, many of whom may not be scientists, contribute relevant data using their own computing devices. (IOC-1.E.3)
  • Crowdsourcing obtains input or information from many people via the Internet. (IOC-1.E.4)
  • Collaboration through computing can enhance human capabilities. (IOC-1.E.5)
  • Crowdsourcing can connect businesses or social causes with funding. (IOC-1.E.6)
必备知识
  • 广泛获取信息和公共数据有助于人们发现问题、开发解决方案并传播结果。（IOC-1.E.1）
  • 分布式科学（distributed science）和公民科学（citizen science）有助于解决科学问题。（IOC-1.E.2）
  • 在公民科学中，分散在各地的个人使用自己的计算设备贡献相关数据，其中许多人可能并非科学家。（IOC-1.E.3）
  • 众包通过互联网从大量人员那里获取意见或信息。（IOC-1.E.4）
  • 通过计算进行协作可以增强人的能力。（IOC-1.E.5）
  • 众包可以将企业或社会公益事业与资金来源联系起来。（IOC-1.E.6）

Participation at scale

大规模参与

When information and public data are widely available, people can use them to spot a problem and work on solutions. Computing also helps participants share their results with others. Collaboration can let a group contribute more observations or ideas than one person could easily gather alone.

当信息和公共数据能被广泛获取时，人们可以利用它们发现问题并共同寻找解决方案。计算技术也有助于参与者向他人分享结果。通过协作，群体可以提供比一个人独自收集更丰富的观察结果或想法。

Citizen science and other crowdsourcing

公民科学与其他众包形式

Citizen science is research conducted wholly or partly by distributed individuals who contribute relevant data with their own devices. Many participants may not be scientists. Crowdsourcing is broader: a project can ask many people online for information or input, or connect a business or social cause with funding.

公民科学是全部或部分由分散在各地的个人开展的科学研究。参与者使用自己的设备贡献相关数据，其中许多人可能并非科学家。众包的范围更广：项目可以在线向大量人员征求信息或意见，也可以将企业或社会公益事业与资金来源联系起来。

Worked Example: Monitoring stream litter例题：监测溪流垃圾

A research team sees public reports of litter near streams. It asks people in different locations to use their devices to submit photos and locations of litter. The team reviews the submissions, summarizes the observations, and shares the results publicly.

一个研究团队看到关于溪流附近垃圾的公开报告，于是邀请不同地点的人使用自己的设备提交垃圾照片和位置信息。团队审核提交内容，汇总观察结果，并公开分享研究结果。

  1. The public reports help the team identify a problem to study.
  2. People contribute observations from different locations using their own devices. This is citizen science, even if some contributors are not scientists.
  3. The Internet request for input from many people is also crowdsourcing. Reviewing submissions helps the team assess the data before sharing results.
  4. Answer: Computing supports collaboration at scale, from identifying the problem through collecting data and disseminating results.
  1. 公开报告帮助团队发现一个值得研究的问题。
  2. 人们使用自己的设备贡献不同地点的观察数据。即使部分贡献者不是科学家，这仍属于公民科学。
  3. 通过互联网向大量人员征集信息也属于众包。审核提交内容有助于团队在分享结果前评估数据。
  4. 答案：计算技术支持从发现问题、收集数据到传播结果的大规模协作。
Exam trap

Wrong answer: Citizen science requires every participant to be a scientist. Many contributors may not be scientists; they participate by providing relevant data for research.

考试陷阱

错误答案：公民科学要求每位参与者都是科学家。许多贡献者可能并非科学家；他们通过为研究提供相关数据来参与。

Going deeper

More contributions can extend what a team observes, but a large number of submissions does not automatically make the data accurate or representative. Consider whether observations need review and whether some locations are missing before drawing conclusions.

深入理解

更多贡献可以扩大团队的观察范围，但提交数量多并不自动意味着数据准确或具有代表性。得出结论前，应考虑是否需要审核观察数据，以及是否缺少某些地点的数据。

Legal and Ethical Concerns

法律与伦理问题

Must know
  • Computer-created material is intellectual property of its creator or an organization. Easy copying and distribution raise concerns about ownership, value, and use, so intellectual property should be safeguarded. (IOC-1.F.1, IOC-1.F.2, IOC-1.F.3)
  • Presenting someone else's material as your own without permission is plagiarism and may have legal consequences. Always cite material created by someone else. (IOC-1.F.4, IOC-1.F.6)
  • Creative Commons licenses, open-source programs, and open-access research provide ways to access and use others' work. Check the applicable terms. (IOC-1.F.5, IOC-1.F.7)
  • Using computing to harm people raises legal and ethical concerns. Computing can also affect social and political issues. (IOC-1.F.8, IOC-1.F.9)
  • The digital divide raises ethical concerns. Innovations such as media download or streaming software and biased algorithms, or devices that continuously monitor activities to collect and analyze data, can also raise legal and ethical concerns. (IOC-1.F.10, IOC-1.F.11)
必备知识
  • 用计算机创作的材料是创作者或某个组织的知识产权（intellectual property）。数字材料易于复制和传播，这会引发关于所有权、价值和使用的担忧，因此应采取措施保护知识产权。（IOC-1.F.1、IOC-1.F.2、IOC-1.F.3）
  • 未经许可使用他人材料并将其当作自己的作品，是剽窃（plagiarism），可能产生法律后果。使用他人创作的材料时始终要注明出处。（IOC-1.F.4、IOC-1.F.6）
  • 知识共享许可（Creative Commons license）、开源（open source）程序和开放获取（open access）研究提供了获取和使用他人作品的途径。应检查适用条款。（IOC-1.F.5、IOC-1.F.7）
  • 利用计算技术伤害个人或群体会引发法律与伦理问题。计算技术也会影响社会和政治议题。（IOC-1.F.8、IOC-1.F.9）
  • 数字鸿沟（digital divide）会引发伦理问题。媒体下载或流媒体软件、有偏见的算法、持续监测活动以收集和分析数据的设备等创新也可能引发法律与伦理问题。（IOC-1.F.10、IOC-1.F.11）

Ownership, permission, and citation

所有权、许可与引用

Digital work can be copied and shared easily, but availability does not establish permission to use it. Creators and organizations can safeguard their work by stating ownership and licensing terms and controlling access where appropriate. When using another person's material, check permission and cite the source. Citation identifies the creator; it does not by itself grant permission.

数字作品容易被复制和分享，但能够获取并不意味着获得了使用许可。创作者和组织可以通过声明所有权和许可条款，并在适当情况下控制访问，来保护作品。使用他人材料时，应检查使用许可并注明出处。引用能够标明创作者，但本身并不授予使用许可。

Ways to share and reuse

分享与再利用的方式

Creative Commons is a set of public copyright licenses that creators can use to grant sharing and other rights. Specific licenses have different conditions, so check the license before using or changing a work. Open-source programs are freely available and may be redistributed and modified, subject to their license terms. Open-access research is free to access and has fewer restrictions on use, but access does not mean every reuse is allowed. These approaches have broadened access to digital information. Copyright is the legal protection of a creator's work: copying it without permission can be infringement even when you cite it, while plagiarism is presenting someone else's work as your own, even if no law is broken.

知识共享是一组公共版权许可，创作者可以用它授予分享等权利。不同许可的条件不同，因此使用或修改作品前要检查许可。开源程序可以免费获取，也可以在遵守许可条款的情况下再分发和修改。开放获取的研究成果可免费访问，使用限制也较少，但能访问并不意味着允许所有再利用方式。这些方式扩大了人们获取数字信息的机会。版权（copyright）是对创作者作品的法律保护：即使注明出处，未经许可复制仍可能构成侵权；剽窃（plagiarism）则是把他人的作品当作自己的作品，即使没有违法也属不当。

Effects on people and society

对个人与社会的影响

Evaluate both what an innovation enables and whom it affects. Software that enables digital media downloads or streaming can raise questions about rights to the media. An algorithm with bias can affect groups unfairly. Computing can also influence social or political issues, while unequal access to computing can limit who benefits. Harm to individuals or groups can raise both legal and ethical concerns.

评估一项创新时，既要考虑它能实现什么，也要考虑它会影响谁。允许下载或在线播放数字媒体的软件可能引发媒体使用权问题。有偏见的算法可能不公平地影响某些群体。计算技术也可能影响社会或政治议题，而计算资源获取不平等可能限制哪些人能够受益。对个人或群体造成伤害可能同时引发法律和伦理问题。

Worked Example: Choosing an image例题：选择图片

A student wants an image for a noncommercial class website. Image A is online but has no stated permission for reuse. Image B has a Creative Commons license permitting this use if the creator is credited. The student can meet all of Image B's license conditions. Which image can the student use?

一名学生想为非商业性的班级网站选择图片。图片 A 在网上可以看到，但没有注明允许再利用。图片 B 使用知识共享许可，允许这种用途，条件是注明创作者。学生能够满足图片 B 的所有许可条件。学生可以使用哪张图片？

  1. Image A's availability does not establish permission. Crediting its creator alone would not establish permission either.
  2. Image B's license permits the proposed use when its conditions are met.
  3. Credit Image B's creator and follow all license conditions. Answer: use Image B.
  1. 图片 A 可以获取，但这并不能证明已获得使用许可。仅注明其创作者也不能证明获得了许可。
  2. 只要满足图片 B 的许可条件，该许可就允许题目中的用途。
  3. 注明图片 B 的创作者并遵守所有许可条件。答案：使用图片 B。
Exam trap

The wrong answer is “It is online, so I can use it however I want” or “A citation alone gives me permission.” Online access is not permission, and attribution and permission address different concerns. Check the terms and cite the creator.

考试陷阱

错误答案是“它在网上，所以我可以随意使用”或“只要注明出处就获得了许可”。在线可获取不等于获得许可，注明出处与获得许可解决的是不同问题。应检查条款并注明创作者。

Going deeper

A use can raise more than one concern. For example, media-sharing software may broaden access while also raising questions about creators' rights. A service may benefit users with access but leave others out because of the digital divide. Evaluate the affected groups and the specific use rather than assuming an innovation is entirely good or bad.

深入理解

一种用途可能同时引发多种问题。例如，媒体分享软件可能扩大获取范围，同时引发对创作者权利的担忧。一项服务可能让能够访问它的用户受益，却因数字鸿沟而使其他人无法受益。应考虑受影响的群体和具体用途，而不是认定一项创新完全有益或完全有害。

小节 5.6

Safe Computing

安全计算

Must know
  • Personally identifiable information (PII) identifies, links, relates, or describes an individual, such as a Social Security number, phone number, medical information, or biometric data (IOC-2.A.1).
  • Search engines, websites, devices, and networks can record search history, page views, and user location (IOC-2.A.2, IOC-2.A.3, IOC-2.A.4).
  • Disparate data such as geolocation, cookies, and browsing history can be aggregated to build knowledge about a person, and information placed online is difficult to delete (IOC-2.A.7, IOC-2.A.13).
  • Authentication measures such as strong passwords and multifactor authentication protect devices and information from unauthorized access (IOC-2.B.1).
  • Encryption encodes data to prevent unauthorized access; symmetric key encryption uses one key, while public key encryption uses a public key to encrypt and a private key to decrypt (IOC-2.B.5).
  • Unauthorized access is gained through phishing, keylogging, rogue access points, malicious links, and untrustworthy downloads (IOC-2.C.1, IOC-2.C.2, IOC-2.C.4, IOC-2.C.5, IOC-2.C.7).
  • Certificate authorities validate key ownership; virus and malware scanning, regular software updates, and reviewing program permissions protect a system (IOC-2.B.6-B.11). Data on public networks can be intercepted (IOC-2.C.3).
必备知识
  • 个人可识别信息（PII）是能够识别、关联、描述个人的信息，例如社会安全号码、电话号码、医疗信息和生物特征数据（IOC-2.A.1）。
  • 搜索引擎、网站、设备和网络可以记录搜索历史、页面浏览记录和用户位置（IOC-2.A.2、IOC-2.A.3、IOC-2.A.4）。
  • 地理位置、cookies 和浏览历史等分散的数据可以被聚合（aggregate）起来，形成关于个人的知识；信息一旦放到网上就很难删除（IOC-2.A.7、IOC-2.A.13）。
  • 身份验证措施（authentication measures），如强密码和多因素身份验证，可以保护设备和信息免受未经授权的访问（IOC-2.B.1）。
  • 加密（encryption）是对数据进行编码以防止未经授权的访问；对称密钥加密使用一个密钥，而公钥加密用公钥加密、用私钥解密（IOC-2.B.5）。
  • 未经授权的访问通过网络钓鱼、键盘记录、恶意接入点、恶意链接和不可信的下载等途径实现（IOC-2.C.1、IOC-2.C.2、IOC-2.C.4、IOC-2.C.5、IOC-2.C.7）。
  • 证书颁发机构验证密钥所有权；病毒和恶意软件扫描、定期软件更新以及检查程序权限可保护系统（IOC-2.B.6-B.11）。公共网络上的数据可能被截获（IOC-2.C.3）。

Privacy Risks from Data Collection

数据收集带来的隐私风险

PII is information about an individual that identifies, links, relates, or describes them (IOC-2.A.1). Examples include a Social Security number, phone numbers, medical information, and biometric data. Technology enables the collection, use, and exploitation of information about, by, and for individuals, groups, and institutions (IOC-2.A.5).

PII 是能够识别、关联或描述个人的信息（IOC-2.A.1）。例子包括社会安全号码、电话号码、医疗信息和生物特征数据（biometric data）。技术使得关于个人、群体和机构的信息能够被收集、使用和利用（IOC-2.A.5）。

Search engines can record and maintain a history of searches made by users (IOC-2.A.2), and websites can record and maintain a history of individuals who have viewed their pages (IOC-2.A.3). Devices, websites, and networks can collect information about a user's location (IOC-2.A.4). Programs can collect your location and record where you have been, how you got there, and how long you were at a given location (IOC-2.A.14).

搜索引擎可以记录并保存用户的搜索历史（IOC-2.A.2），网站可以记录并保存浏览过其页面的个人的历史（IOC-2.A.3）。设备、网站和网络可以收集关于用户位置的信息（IOC-2.A.4）。程序可以收集你的位置，并记录你去过哪里、如何到达以及在那里停留了多长时间（IOC-2.A.14）。

Search engines can use search history to suggest websites or for targeted marketing (IOC-2.A.6). Disparate personal data, such as geolocation, cookies, and browsing history, can be aggregated to create knowledge about an individual (IOC-2.A.7). Information posted to social media services can be used by others, and combining information posted on social media and other sources can be used to deduce private information about you (IOC-2.A.15).

搜索引擎可以利用搜索历史来推荐网站或进行定向营销（targeted marketing）（IOC-2.A.6）。地理位置、cookies 和浏览历史等分散的个人数据可以被聚合起来，形成关于个人的知识（IOC-2.A.7）。发布在社交媒体服务上的信息可以被他人使用，把社交媒体上的信息与其他来源的信息结合起来，可以推断出关于你的私人信息（IOC-2.A.15）。

Data collection is not only harmful. PII and other information placed online can be used to enhance a user's online experiences (IOC-2.A.8), and PII stored online can be used to simplify making online purchases (IOC-2.A.9). The risk is that commercial and governmental curation of information may be exploited if privacy and other protections are ignored (IOC-2.A.10).

数据收集并不只有害处。放在网上的 PII 和其他信息可以用来改善用户的在线体验（IOC-2.A.8），存储在网上的 PII 可以简化网上购物的流程（IOC-2.A.9）。风险在于，如果忽视隐私和其他保护措施，商业和政府管理的信息可能会被利用（IOC-2.A.10）。

Information placed online can be used in ways that were not intended and that may have a harmful impact (IOC-2.A.11). For example, an email message may be forwarded, tweets can be retweeted, and social media posts can be viewed by potential employers. PII can be used to stalk or steal the identity of a person or to aid in the planning of other criminal acts (IOC-2.A.12). Once information is placed online, it is difficult to delete (IOC-2.A.13).

放在网上的信息可能会以意想不到的方式被使用，并可能产生有害影响（IOC-2.A.11）。例如，电子邮件可能被转发，推文可能被转推，社交媒体帖子可能被潜在雇主看到。PII 可能被用来跟踪或窃取一个人的身份，或帮助策划其他犯罪行为（IOC-2.A.12）。信息一旦放到网上，就很难删除（IOC-2.A.13）。

Protecting Computing Resources

保护计算资源

Authentication measures protect devices and information from unauthorized access (IOC-2.B.1). Examples include strong passwords and multifactor authentication. A strong password is something that is easy for a user to remember but would be difficult for someone else to guess based on knowledge of that user (IOC-2.B.2).

身份验证措施可以保护设备和信息免受未经授权的访问（IOC-2.B.1）。例子包括强密码（strong password）和多因素身份验证（multifactor authentication）。强密码是用户自己容易记住、但了解该用户的其他人难以猜到的密码（IOC-2.B.2）。

Multifactor authentication is a method of computer access control in which a user is only granted access after successfully presenting several separate pieces of evidence to an authentication mechanism (IOC-2.B.3). The evidence typically comes from at least two of three categories: knowledge (something they know), possession (something they have), and inherence (something they are). Multifactor authentication requires at least two steps to unlock protected information; each step adds a new layer of security that must be broken to gain unauthorized access (IOC-2.B.4).

多因素身份验证是一种计算机访问控制方法：用户只有成功地向验证机制提供多个独立的证据后才能获得访问权限（IOC-2.B.3）。这些证据通常来自以下三类中的至少两类：知识（他们知道的东西）、持有（他们拥有的东西）和固有特征（他们本身是什么）。多因素身份验证至少需要两个步骤才能解锁受保护的信息；每一步都增加了一个新的安全层，攻击者必须逐层突破才能获得未经授权的访问（IOC-2.B.4）。

Encryption is the process of encoding data to prevent unauthorized access, and decryption is the process of decoding the data (IOC-2.B.5). Symmetric key encryption involves one key for both encryption and decryption. Public key encryption pairs a public key for encryption and a private key for decryption: the sender does not need the receiver's private key to encrypt a message, but the receiver's private key is required to decrypt the message. The specific mathematical procedures for encryption and decryption are beyond the scope of this course and the AP Exam.

加密是对数据进行编码以防止未经授权访问的过程，解密（decryption）是对数据进行解码的过程（IOC-2.B.5）。对称密钥加密（symmetric key encryption）使用同一个密钥进行加密和解密。公钥加密（public key encryption）把用于加密的公钥和用于解密的私钥配对：发送者不需要接收者的私钥就能加密消息，但解密消息必须使用接收者的私钥。加密和解密的具体数学过程超出了本课程和 AP 考试的范围。

Certificate authorities issue digital certificates that validate the ownership of encryption keys used in secure communications and are based on a trust model (IOC-2.B.6). Computer virus and malware scanning software can help protect a computing system against infection (IOC-2.B.7).

证书颁发机构（certificate authorities）签发数字证书，用于验证安全通信中所用加密密钥的所有权，并基于信任模型（trust model）运作（IOC-2.B.6）。计算机病毒和恶意软件扫描软件可以帮助保护计算系统免受感染（IOC-2.B.7）。

A computer virus is a malicious program that can copy itself and gain access to a computer in an unauthorized way (IOC-2.B.8). Computer viruses often attach themselves to legitimate programs and start running independently on a computer. Malware is software intended to damage a computing system or to take partial control over its operation (IOC-2.B.9).

计算机病毒（computer virus）是一种能够自我复制并以未经授权的方式访问计算机的恶意程序（IOC-2.B.8）。计算机病毒常常附着在合法程序上，并在计算机上开始独立运行。恶意软件（malware）是旨在破坏计算系统或部分控制其运行的软件（IOC-2.B.9）。

All real-world systems have errors or design flaws that can be exploited to compromise them (IOC-2.B.10). Regular software updates help fix errors that could compromise a computing system. Users can control the permissions programs have for collecting user information, and users should review the permission settings of programs to protect their privacy (IOC-2.B.11).

所有现实世界的系统都存在可能被利用来攻破它们的错误或设计缺陷（IOC-2.B.10）。定期的软件更新有助于修复可能危及计算系统的错误。用户可以控制程序收集用户信息的权限，并应该检查程序的权限设置以保护自己的隐私（IOC-2.B.11）。

How Unauthorized Access Is Gained

未经授权的访问是如何实现的

Phishing is a technique that attempts to trick a user into providing personal information (IOC-2.C.1). That personal information can then be used to access sensitive online resources, such as bank accounts and emails. Keylogging is the use of a program to record every keystroke made by a computer user in order to gain fraudulent access to passwords and other confidential information (IOC-2.C.2).

网络钓鱼（phishing）是一种试图诱骗用户提供个人信息的技术（IOC-2.C.1）。这些个人信息随后可能被用来访问敏感的在线资源，例如银行账户和电子邮件。键盘记录（keylogging）是使用程序记录计算机用户的每一次按键，以骗取对密码和其他机密信息的访问（IOC-2.C.2）。

Data sent over public networks can be intercepted, analyzed, and modified (IOC-2.C.3). One way that this can happen is through a rogue access point. A rogue access point is a wireless access point that gives unauthorized access to secure networks (IOC-2.C.4).

通过公共网络发送的数据可能被截获、分析和修改（IOC-2.C.3）。其中一种方式是恶意接入点（rogue access point）。恶意接入点是一个无线接入点，它让攻击者能够未经授权地访问安全网络（IOC-2.C.4）。

A malicious link can be disguised on a web page or in an email message (IOC-2.C.5). Unsolicited emails, attachments, links, and forms in emails can be used to compromise the security of a computing system (IOC-2.C.6). These can come from unknown senders or from known senders whose security has been compromised. Untrustworthy (often free) downloads from freeware or shareware sites can contain malware (IOC-2.C.7).

恶意链接（malicious link）可以伪装在网页或电子邮件中（IOC-2.C.5）。垃圾邮件、附件、链接和邮件中的表单都可能被用来危害计算系统的安全（IOC-2.C.6）。它们可能来自未知发件人，也可能来自安全已被攻破的熟人发件人。来自免费软件（freeware）或共享软件（shareware）网站的不可信（通常是免费的）下载可能包含恶意软件（IOC-2.C.7）。

Worked Example: Which Attack Is This?例题：这属于哪种攻击？

A student receives an email that appears to come from their bank. The email asks them to click a link and enter their account password on a form. Later, the student connects a laptop to a free public wireless network and notices that login information typed on the network has been captured. Finally, a friend downloads a free game from a shareware site, and the game installs a program that damages the computer. Identify the security threat in each part and one protection that would help.

一名学生收到一封看似来自其银行的电子邮件。邮件要求他点击一个链接，并在表单中输入账户密码。之后，这名学生把笔记本电脑连接到一个免费的公共无线网络，发现自己在该网络中输入的登录信息被截获了。最后，一位朋友从共享软件网站下载了一个免费游戏，这个游戏安装了一个破坏计算机的程序。指出每一部分中的安全威胁，以及一种有帮助的防护措施。

  1. The email tries to trick the student into providing personal information. This is phishing (IOC-2.C.1), and the disguised link is a malicious link (IOC-2.C.5). The stolen password could be used to access sensitive resources such as bank accounts and emails.
  2. Data sent over public networks can be intercepted, analyzed, and modified, for example through a rogue access point (IOC-2.C.3, IOC-2.C.4). Encryption protects the data by encoding it to prevent unauthorized access (IOC-2.B.5).
  3. The free game is an untrustworthy download from a freeware or shareware site that contains malware (IOC-2.C.7). Malware is software intended to damage a computing system or take partial control over its operation (IOC-2.B.9). Virus and malware scanning software can help protect against infection (IOC-2.B.7).
  4. Additional protections: multifactor authentication adds layers of security even if a password is stolen (IOC-2.B.4), and regular software updates fix errors that could compromise the system (IOC-2.B.10).
  1. 这封邮件试图诱骗学生提供个人信息。这是网络钓鱼（IOC-2.C.1），伪装的链接是恶意链接（IOC-2.C.5）。被盗的密码可能被用来访问银行账户和电子邮件等敏感资源。
  2. 通过公共网络发送的数据可能被截获、分析和修改，例如通过恶意接入点（IOC-2.C.3、IOC-2.C.4）。加密通过对数据进行编码来防止未经授权的访问，从而保护数据（IOC-2.B.5）。
  3. 这个免费游戏是来自免费软件或共享软件网站的不可信下载，其中包含恶意软件（IOC-2.C.7）。恶意软件是旨在破坏计算系统或部分控制其运行的软件（IOC-2.B.9）。病毒和恶意软件扫描软件可以帮助防止感染（IOC-2.B.7）。
  4. 其他防护措施：即使密码被盗，多因素身份验证也能增加安全层（IOC-2.B.4）；定期的软件更新可以修复可能危及系统的错误（IOC-2.B.10）。

Answer: phishing with a malicious link, interception through a rogue access point, and malware from an untrustworthy download; encryption, scanning software, multifactor authentication, and software updates are matching protections.

答案：带恶意链接的网络钓鱼、通过恶意接入点的数据截获，以及来自不可信下载的恶意软件；加密、扫描软件、多因素身份验证和软件更新是对应的防护措施。

Exam trap

Do not confuse symmetric key encryption with public key encryption. Symmetric key encryption uses one key for both encryption and decryption, while public key encryption uses a public key to encrypt and the receiver's private key to decrypt (IOC-2.B.5). The sender never needs the receiver's private key to encrypt a message. Also, do not assume a dangerous email only comes from strangers: unsolicited emails, attachments, and links can come from known senders whose security has been compromised (IOC-2.C.6). Multifactor authentication needs evidence from at least two different categories (knowledge, possession, inherence); two passwords are both knowledge and do not count (IOC-2.B.3).

考试陷阱

不要混淆对称密钥加密和公钥加密。对称密钥加密用同一个密钥进行加密和解密，而公钥加密用公钥加密、用接收者的私钥解密（IOC-2.B.5）。发送者加密消息时永远不需要接收者的私钥。另外，不要以为危险的邮件只来自陌生人：垃圾邮件、附件和链接也可能来自安全已被攻破的熟人发件人（IOC-2.C.6）。多因素身份验证需要来自至少两个不同类别（知识、持有、固有）的证据；两个密码都属于知识因素，不算多因素（IOC-2.B.3）。

Going deeper

Aggregation is the quiet multiplier of privacy risk. A single post or one location record may seem harmless, but disparate data such as geolocation, cookies, and browsing history can be combined to create detailed knowledge about an individual (IOC-2.A.7), and combining social media with other sources can reveal private information you never posted directly (IOC-2.A.15). Because information placed online is difficult to delete (IOC-2.A.13) and can be used in unintended ways, such as posts viewed by potential employers (IOC-2.A.11), the safest habit is to treat everything you share as permanent and to review program permission settings regularly (IOC-2.B.11).

深入理解

数据聚合是隐私风险的隐形放大器。一条帖子或一次位置记录看起来可能无害，但地理位置、cookies 和浏览历史等分散的数据可以被组合起来，形成关于个人的详细知识（IOC-2.A.7）；把社交媒体信息与其他来源结合，还能推断出你从未直接发布过的私人信息（IOC-2.A.15）。由于网上的信息很难删除（IOC-2.A.13），并且可能以意想不到的方式被使用，例如帖子被潜在雇主看到（IOC-2.A.11），最安全的习惯是把你分享的一切内容都当作永久存在的，并定期检查程序的权限设置（IOC-2.B.11）。

Unit 5 MCQ Patterns That Show Up Every Year

单元 5 每年都会出现的选择题套路

Big Idea 5 makes up 21 to 26 percent of the MCQ, the largest single share after Big Idea 3. Questions are mostly scenario based: you read about a computing innovation or a security situation and identify the effect, risk, or protection being described. Memorize the vocabulary (PII, phishing, rogue access point, Creative Commons) and practice classifying scenarios quickly.

第五大概念占选择题的 21% 到 26%，是除第三大概念外占比最高的部分。题目大多基于情境：阅读关于计算创新或安全场景的描述，然后判断其中体现的影响、风险或防护措施。熟记术语（PII、网络钓鱼、恶意接入点、知识共享许可），并练习快速归类情境。

Pattern 1: Same innovation, two opposite effects The stem describes one effect of an innovation and asks for another view. Remember IOC-1.A.4: a single effect can be seen as both beneficial and harmful, by different people or even the same person. If an option says an effect is purely good or purely bad for everyone, it is usually wrong. (See Topic 5.1.)
套路 1：同一创新，正反两种影响 题干描述某项创新的一个影响，要求你选出另一种视角。记住 IOC-1.A.4：同一个影响对不同人（甚至同一个人）可以既有益又有害。如果某选项说某个影响对所有人纯粹有利或纯粹有害，通常是错的。（见主题 5.1。）
Pattern 2: Spot the digital divide variable Questions give data about Internet or device access and ask what explains the gap. The answer almost always references socioeconomic, geographic, or demographic differences, or differences between countries. The divide affects both groups and individuals, and actions by individuals, organizations, and governments can widen or narrow it. (See Topic 5.2.)
套路 2：识别数字鸿沟的变量 题目给出关于网络或设备使用情况的数据，问差距的原因。答案几乎总是涉及社会经济、地理或人口特征的差异，或国家之间的差异。数字鸿沟既影响群体也影响个人，个人、组织和政府的行为可以扩大或缩小它。（见主题 5.2。）
Pattern 3: Where did the bias come from A scenario describes an unfair output, like a hiring or loan algorithm rejecting one group more often. The correct answer points to bias written into the algorithm or bias in the training data, and the fix is programmer action to reduce bias. Bias can be embedded at all levels of development, not just in the data. (See Topic 5.3.)
套路 3：偏见从何而来 情境描述不公平的输出，例如招聘或贷款算法更频繁地拒绝某一群体。正确答案指向写入算法的偏见或训练数据中的偏见，解决办法是程序员主动减少偏见。偏见可能嵌入开发的各个层面，不只是数据。（见主题 5.3。）
Pattern 4: Name the attack or the defense The exam loves vocabulary matching: a fake email asking for credentials is phishing, a program recording keystrokes is keylogging, a fake wireless hotspot is a rogue access point, self-copying malicious code is a virus. Learn each term with one concrete example so you can classify in seconds. (See Topic 5.6.)
套路 4：说出攻击或防御的名称 考试喜欢考术语配对：伪造邮件骗取账号密码是网络钓鱼（phishing），记录按键的程序是键盘记录（keylogging），伪造无线热点是恶意接入点（rogue access point），能自我复制的恶意代码是病毒（virus）。每个术语记一个具体例子，几秒钟就能归类。（见主题 5.6。）
Pattern 5: Legal use of someone else's work Stems ask which use of digital material is legal or ethical. Creative Commons licenses let creators grant sharing rights, open source code can be redistributed and modified, open access research is free of access restrictions. Using material without permission and presenting it as your own is plagiarism, and any borrowed material should be cited. (See Topic 5.5.)
套路 5：合法使用他人作品 题干问哪种使用数字材料的方式合法或合乎伦理。知识共享许可（Creative Commons）允许创作者授予分享权，开源（open source）代码可以再分发和修改，开放获取（open access）研究成果没有访问限制。未经许可使用他人材料并据为己有是抄袭（plagiarism），任何借用的材料都应注明出处。（见主题 5.5。）
