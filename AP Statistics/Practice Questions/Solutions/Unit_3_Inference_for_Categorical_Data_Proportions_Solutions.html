Sample12345678910
$\hat p$0.380.420.360.440.400.340.460.400.420.38

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The sample proportion is unbiased, so the sampling distribution is centered at $p=0.40$.   1. Correct: center $p=0.40$, spread $\sqrt{0.40(0.60)/50}\approx0.069$.正确：中心 $p=0.40$，离散度 $\sqrt{0.40(0.60)/50}\approx0.069$。
  2. Uses $p(1-p)=0.24$, the variance of a single trial, as if it were the spread of the sampling distribution.把单次试验的方差 $p(1-p)=0.24$ 当作抽样分布的离散度。
  3. Sample-to-sample variability is expected; unbiased means the center equals $p$, not that every sample matches it.样本间存在波动是正常的；无偏指中心等于 $p$，而非每个样本都相同。
  4. Halving the sample size increases the spread, because the standard deviation is $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$.样本量减半会增大离散度，因为标准差为 $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$。
A sampling distribution of a proportion is identified by its center $p$ and its spread $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$.比例的抽样分布由中心 $p$ 与离散度 $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$ 共同刻画。

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

A student writes: "The sampling distribution of the sample proportion is centered at the population proportion $p$, and its spread shrinks as the sample size grows. So if I take a large enough sample, my sample proportion will equal $p$." According to the argument, the conclusion is wrong. Where is the flaw?某学生写道：“样本比例的抽样分布以总体比例 $p$ 为中心，且样本量越大离散度越小。所以只要样本足够大，我的样本比例就会等于 $p$。”该论证的结论错误，缺陷在哪里？

Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Unbiasedness fixes the center and variability shrinks like $1/\sqrt{n}$, but the sampling distribution still has positive spread for every finite sample, so a single sample proportion rarely equals $p$ exactly.无偏性固定了中心，离散度按 $1/\sqrt{n}$ 缩小，但对任何有限样本，抽样分布的离散度仍为正，故单个样本比例几乎不会恰等于 $p$。

Why the options attract (M 3)选项误区（M 3）
  1. Correct: the spread shrinks but never reaches 0, so equality is not guaranteed.正确：离散度不断缩小但不会变为 0，故无法保证相等。
  2. The center stays at $p$ for every sample size; unbiasedness is not the flawed step.无论样本量多大，中心始终为 $p$；无偏性并非错误步骤。
  3. The spread does depend on $n$: it equals $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$ and shrinks as $n$ grows.离散度确实依赖 $n$：它等于 $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$，随 $n$ 增大而缩小。
  4. The sample proportion stays unbiased for every sample size; growing $n$ does not introduce bias.样本比例在任何样本量下都无偏；增大 $n$ 不会引入偏差。
Unbiased center and shrinking spread describe precision, not certainty: an estimate can be trustworthy without becoming exact.无偏的中心与不断缩小的离散度描述的是精确程度而非确定性：可信的估计未必精确相等。

Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

With $\hat p=126/180=0.70$ the standard error is $\sqrt{0.70(0.30)/180}\approx0.034$, so the 95% interval is $0.70\pm1.96(0.034)$, about $(0.633, 0.767)$.由 $\hat p=126/180=0.70$，标准误为 $\sqrt{0.70(0.30)/180}\approx0.034$，故 95% 区间为 $0.70\pm1.96(0.034)$，约为 $(0.633, 0.767)$。

Why the options attract (M 4)选项误区（M 4）
  1. Correct: one standard error times the 95% multiplier $z^{*}=1.96$, added and subtracted from $\hat p$.正确：标准误乘以 95% 对应的 $z^{*}=1.96$，在 $\hat p$ 上下加减。
  2. Uses one standard error as the margin: the confidence level supplies the multiplier 1.96.用 1 个标准误作为边际：置信水平还要提供乘数 1.96。
  3. Uses $z^{*}=1.645$, the multiplier for 90% confidence, instead of 1.96.误用 90% 置信水平对应的乘数 $z^{*}=1.645$，而非 1.96。
  4. Doubles the margin of error; $\pm1.96$ standard errors is already the full 95% interval.把边际误差翻倍；$\pm1.96$ 个标准误本身已是完整的 95% 区间。
A confidence interval is estimate plus and minus $z^{*}$ standard errors, with $z^{*}$ fixed by the confidence level.置信区间＝估计值 ± $z^{*}$ 个标准误，其中 $z^{*}$ 由置信水平决定。
