Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Each datum belongs to one student. The unknown population mean is the parameter; 6.2 hours is its sample estimate.每个数据属于一名学生。未知总体均值是参数，6.2 小时是样本估计。

Why the options attract (M 1)选项误区（M 1）

  2. Calls the variable a unit and the statistic a parameter.把变量称为单位，把统计量称为参数。
  3. Calls the population a unit and its sample statistic a parameter.把总体称为单位，把样本统计量称为参数。
  4. 80 is the sample size, not a summary of study time.80 是样本量，不是学习时间的汇总。
A numerical estimate is not automatically a known population value. Attribute every summary to the group actually measured.数值估计不自动等于已知总体值；每个汇总都应归属于实际测量的群体。

Volunteers are not a random sample of all teenagers, so the claim about all teenagers is unsupported.随机实验比较原则适用：每区组内部随机分配两处理、有重复和直接控制，因此显著分数差支持相似志愿者的处理因果差异。随机分配平均而言平衡混杂，不是保证。志愿者不是全青少年随机样本，故全体结论无依据。

Where this goes wrong (M 5)常见错误（M 5）

Random assignment is not random selection. Neither volunteering nor grade blocking converts the participants into a representative sample of every teenager.随机分配不是随机选取；志愿参加或年级区组都不能把参与者变为全青少年的代表样本。

Blocking changes which comparisons are informative, while recruitment determines whose outcomes those comparisons can represent.区组决定哪些比较更有信息，招募决定比较可以代表谁。
Instructional AP-style rubric.教学 AP 形式评分标准。

Follow-through: a consistent alternative equal allocation earns later reasoning points if randomization within both blocks and replication are preserved; an unrandomized allocation cannot earn the causal-condition point.各1分：正确组成部分；问题含均值方向及有限因果/总体范围；年级区组；每区组可执行随机15人分配；重复及两控制；年级相关变异解释；选取与分配区别；因果前核查区组内随机、控制及重复；显著情境因果结论；志愿来源的外推限制。跟进给分：替代等人数方案若仍在两区组随机且有重复，可给后续推理分；无随机方案不给因果条件分。

  2. Explain a context-specific advantage over an SRS of 120, stating when that advantage is expected.说明相较120人 SRS 的具体优势，并说何时预期此优势。 [2]
  3. If heavy-workload selected students do not respond, identify the bias and likely direction. Would surveying ten times as many students with the same nonresponse pattern fix it?若被选的重作业学生不答，识别偏差及可能方向。把样本增至十倍但相同无应答模式会修复吗？ [3]
Sample 30,40,50 by independent within-grade SRS. Stratification can reduce variation when grade predicts workload. Nonresponse likely biases the homework mean downward; larger size alone does not fix it.各年级独立 SRS 抽30、40、50人；若年级预测作业量，分层可减变异。无应答可能使均值向下偏，大样本本身不能修复。

Allocate 120 times each grade's population share:30,40,50. Number every student within grade and draw the required number of different labels uniformly, ignoring repeats; combine the three independent samples.120乘各年级总体比例得30、40、50；各年级完整编号，均匀随机抽所需不同编号，忽略重复，再合并。

(b) 2 points2 分

All grades receive proportional representation. If homework times are more similar within grade than across the school, separating grade differences tends to reduce variability in the estimated overall mean. This is a conditional advantage, not a guarantee for any stratification.各年级按比例代表。若年级内作业时间比全校更相似，分离年级差异倾向于减少总体均值估计变异；不是任何分层都必然更好。

Increasing sample size may reduce random variability but leaves the systematic response imbalance.这是无应答：已选高作业者缺失，受访者少代表高时间，可能低估总体均值。增加大小可减随机变异，但系统性应答不平衡仍在。

Calling this undercoverage ignores that the heavy-workload students were on the frame and were selected; the loss occurred at response.称为覆盖不足忽略了重作业者在抽样框内且已被选，损失发生在应答阶段。

  3. Evaluate: I. Standardization guarantees a normal distribution. II. Larger standardized values always indicate better performance. Give a contextual counterexample where needed.评价：I. 标准化保证正态分布。II. 标准分越大总表示表现越好。必要时给情境反例。 [2]
$x=82$; converted value 174,mean 150,SD 16; z remains 1.5 for every positive scale transformation. Neither normality nor better performance is guaranteed.$x=82$，换算值174、均值150、标准差16；正比例变换下 z 恒为1.5。不保证正态或表现更好。

(a) 3 points3 分

$x=70+1.5(8)=82$. Then $y=10+2(82)=174$, $\mu_Y=10+2(70)=150$, $\sigma_Y=2(8)=16$.$x=82$，$y=174,\mu_Y=150,\sigma_Y=16$。

(b) 3 points3 分

$z_Y=(a+bx-(a+b\mu))/(b\sigma)=b(x-\mu)/(b\sigma)=z_X=1.5$. The positive-scale condition preserves ordering; nonzero spread permits division.$z_Y=(a+bx-(a+b\mu))/(b\sigma)=z_X=1.5$。正比例保持顺序，非零标准差确保可除。

(c) 2 points2 分
