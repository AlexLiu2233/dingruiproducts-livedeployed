Which description of the sampling distribution of the sample mean delivery time is correct?某城市包裹送达时间的均值为 $22$ 分钟、标准差为 $8$ 分钟，且单次送达时间的分布明显右偏。调度员随机抽取 $64$ 次送达。关于样本均值送达时间的抽样分布，下列描述哪项正确？

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The sample mean is unbiased for $\mu$, so its center is $22$ minutes; its standard deviation is $\sigma/\sqrt{n}=8/\sqrt{64}=1$ minute; and the central limit theorem makes the sampling distribution approximately normal once $n$ is large, which $n=64$ satisfies.样本均值对 $\mu$ 无偏，故中心为 $22$ 分钟；其标准差为 $\sigma/\sqrt{n}=8/\sqrt{64}=1$ 分钟；当 $n$ 较大时中心极限定理保证抽样分布近似正态，$n=64$ 满足这一点。

Why the options attract (M 10)选项误区（M 10）

  2. Uses the population standard deviation as the spread of the sample mean, ignoring the $1/\sqrt{n}$ factor.把总体标准差当作样本均值的离散度，忽略了 $1/\sqrt{n}$ 因子。
  3. Divides the standard deviation by $n$ instead of $\sqrt{n}$.把标准差除以 $n$ 而不是 $\sqrt{n}$。
  4. Treats the shape of the population as the shape of the sampling distribution no matter how large $n$ is.无论 $n$ 多大，都把总体形状当作抽样分布的形状。
