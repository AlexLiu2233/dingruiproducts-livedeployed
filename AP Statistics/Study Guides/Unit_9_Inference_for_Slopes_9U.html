Topic 9.1主题 9.1

Sampling Distributions for the Slope斜率的抽样分布

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Distinguish the population slope $\beta$ from the sample slope $b$, and explain that $b$ varies from sample to sample.
  • Describe the sampling distribution of $b$: its centre, its spread $SE_b$ and its shape.
  • Calculate the standard error of the slope $SE_b = \dfrac{s}{s_x\sqrt{n-1}}$ from summary statistics and explain why the degrees of freedom are $n-2$.
  • State the four LINE conditions (Linear, Independent, Normal, Equal variance) and say which display checks each.
  • Explain how sample size, scatter about the line and the spread of $x$ change $SE_b$.
  • 区分总体斜率 $\beta$ 与样本斜率 $b$，并解释 $b$ 会随样本而变。
  • 描述 $b$ 的抽样分布：中心、离散程度 $SE_b$ 与形状。
  • 由汇总统计量计算斜率的标准误 $SE_b = \dfrac{s}{s_x\sqrt{n-1}}$，并解释自由度为何是 $n-2$。
  • 写出 LINE 四个条件（线性、独立、正态、等方差）并说明各用哪种图形检查。
  • 解释样本量、围绕直线的散布以及 $x$ 的离散程度如何改变 $SE_b$。

Earlier you fitted a least-squares line to the points in a scatterplot and described those points. Inference asks a larger question: if the points are a random sample from a big population, what does the line say about the whole population? The answer begins with a model. For each value of $x$, the response $y$ varies around a mean that depends linearly on $x$. The line of those means, $\mu_y = \alpha + \beta x$, is the population regression line, and its slope $\beta$ is the population slope. Individual values scatter about it: $y = \alpha + \beta x + \varepsilon$, where the random departures $\varepsilon$ are Normal with mean 0 and the same standard deviation $\sigma$ at every $x$.

此前你对散点图中的点拟合最小二乘直线，并描述这些点。推断提出更大的问题：如果这些点是大总体中的随机样本，这条直线对整个总体说明了什么？答案从模型开始。对每个 $x$ 值，响应变量 $y$ 围绕一个随 $x$ 线性变化的均值波动。这些均值构成的直线 $\mu_y = \alpha + \beta x$ 是总体回归直线（population regression line），其斜率 $\beta$ 是总体斜率。个别观测围绕它散布：$y = \alpha + \beta x + \varepsilon$，随机偏差 $\varepsilon$ 服从均值为 0 的正态分布，且在每个 $x$ 处标准差同为 $\sigma$。

The sample gives us estimates, and a sample statistic differs from the parameter it estimates. The sample slope $b$ (written $b_1$ in some books) estimates $\beta$, the intercept $a$ estimates $\alpha$, the residual standard deviation $s$ estimates $\sigma$, and the sample correlation $r$ estimates the population correlation $\rho$.

样本给出估计值，而样本统计量与它所估计的参数并不相同。样本斜率 $b$（有些教材写作 $b_1$）估计 $\beta$，截距 $a$ 估计 $\alpha$，残差标准差 $s$ 估计 $\sigma$，样本相关系数 $r$ 估计总体相关系数 $\rho$。

The sampling distribution of the sample slope样本斜率的抽样分布

Take another random sample of 15 students and the points change, so the least-squares line and the slope $b$ change too. If we could repeat the sampling many times, the values of $b$ would form the sampling distribution of $b$. Three facts describe it when the LINE conditions hold. Centre: the mean of the sample slopes is $\beta$, so $b$ is an unbiased estimator. Spread: the typical distance between $b$ and $\beta$ is measured by the standard deviation of $b$, which we estimate with the standard error of the slope $SE_b$. Shape: Normal when the errors are Normal; because $\sigma$ must be estimated by $s$, the standardized slope follows a $t$ distribution instead of a Normal one.

再随机抽取 15 名学生，数据点就变了，最小二乘直线与斜率 $b$ 也随之改变。若能反复抽样，这些 $b$ 值就构成 $b$ 的抽样分布（sampling distribution）。当 LINE 条件成立时，它有三个特征。中心：样本斜率的均值是 $\beta$，所以 $b$ 是无偏估计量。离散程度：$b$ 与 $\beta$ 的典型距离由 $b$ 的标准差衡量，我们用斜率的标准误 $SE_b$ 来估计它。形状：误差服从正态时为正态；由于必须用 $s$ 估计 $\sigma$，标准化后的斜率服从 $t$ 分布而不是正态分布。

Standard error of the slope

$$SE_b = \dfrac{s}{s_x\sqrt{n-1}}, \qquad s = \sqrt{\dfrac{\sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2}{n-2}}$$

Here $s_x$ is the standard deviation of the $x$-values and $n$ is the number of points. The residual standard deviation $s$ divides by $n-2$ because two numbers, $a$ and $b$, were estimated from the data before the residuals were found. That is why inference for a slope uses a $t$ distribution with $df = n-2$.

斜率的标准误

$$SE_b = \dfrac{s}{s_x\sqrt{n-1}}, \qquad s = \sqrt{\dfrac{\sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2}{n-2}}$$

其中 $s_x$ 是 $x$ 值的标准差，$n$ 是数据点个数。残差标准差 $s$ 除以 $n-2$，是因为求残差之前已经由数据估计了 $a$ 和 $b$ 两个数。所以斜率推断使用自由度 $df = n-2$ 的 $t$ 分布。

The formula shows three ways to get a more precise estimate of $\beta$ (a smaller $SE_b$): points closer to the line (smaller $s$), $x$-values spread over a wider range (larger $s_x$), and a larger sample (larger $n$).

公式说明了三种得到更精确的 $\beta$ 估计（更小的 $SE_b$）的途径：点更靠近直线（$s$ 更小）、$x$ 值分布范围更宽（$s_x$ 更大）、样本更大（$n$ 更大）。

Worked Example: identify the parameter and the statistic in context例题：在情境中识别参数与统计量

Using the 15 students above, the least-squares line is $\hat{y} = 51.66 + 2.617x$.

  1. Population and sample: the population is all 1,200 students at the school; the sample is the 15 students chosen at random.
  2. Parameter: $\beta$ is the slope of the population regression line, that is, the true mean change in test score (points) for each additional hour of weekly study among all students at the school. It is fixed and unknown.
  3. Statistic: the sample slope is $b = 2.617$ points per hour. It estimates $\beta$.
  4. In context: in this sample, each additional hour of weekly study goes with a predicted increase of about 2.62 points in test score. A different random sample of 15 students would give a different $b$, which is why we need the sampling distribution of $b$ before we can make a statement about $\beta$.

用上面 15 名学生的数据，最小二乘直线为 $\hat{y} = 51.66 + 2.617x$。

  1. 总体与样本：总体是该校全部 1,200 名学生；样本是随机抽取的 15 名学生。
  2. 参数：$\beta$ 是总体回归直线的斜率，即该校全体学生每周学习时间每多一小时，测验分数（分）的真实均值变化量。它是固定而未知的。
  3. 统计量：样本斜率 $b = 2.617$ 分/小时，用来估计 $\beta$。
  4. 结合情境：在这个样本中，每周学习时间每增加一小时，预测测验分数约增加 2.62 分。换一个 15 人的随机样本会得到不同的 $b$，所以要对 $\beta$ 作结论，必须先了解 $b$ 的抽样分布。
Worked Example: compute $b$ and $SE_b$ from summary statistics例题：由汇总统计量计算 $b$ 与 $SE_b$

For the same 15 students the summary statistics are $n=15$, $s_x = 3.335$ hours, $s_y = 9.741$ points, $r = 0.8961$ and $s = 4.486$ points. Find $b$ and $SE_b$.

  1. Slope: $b = r\,\dfrac{s_y}{s_x} = 0.8961 \times \dfrac{9.741}{3.335} = 2.617$ points per hour.
  2. Standard error: $SE_b = \dfrac{s}{s_x\sqrt{n-1}} = \dfrac{4.486}{3.335\sqrt{14}} = \dfrac{4.486}{12.479} = 0.3595$.
  3. Interpretation: the sample slope of 2.617 typically differs from the true slope $\beta$ by about 0.36 points per hour when we take random samples of 15 students.

Why this works: $b$ rescales the correlation by the ratio of standard deviations, and $SE_b$ measures the scatter of the points about the line ($s$) relative to the spread of the $x$-values and the number of points.

仍是这 15 名学生，汇总统计量为 $n=15$，$s_x = 3.335$ 小时，$s_y = 9.741$ 分，$r = 0.8961$，$s = 4.486$ 分。求 $b$ 与 $SE_b$。

  1. 斜率：$b = r\,\dfrac{s_y}{s_x} = 0.8961 \times \dfrac{9.741}{3.335} = 2.617$ 分/小时。
  2. 标准误：$SE_b = \dfrac{s}{s_x\sqrt{n-1}} = \dfrac{4.486}{3.335\sqrt{14}} = \dfrac{4.486}{12.479} = 0.3595$。
  3. 解释：对 15 名学生的随机样本，样本斜率 2.617 与真实斜率 $\beta$ 的典型差距约为 0.36 分/小时。

为何成立：$b$ 用标准差之比对相关系数重新定标；$SE_b$ 则衡量各点围绕直线的散布（$s$）相对于 $x$ 值的离散程度与点数的大小。

Worked Example: how sample size changes $SE_b$, and the independence check例题：样本量如何改变 $SE_b$，以及独立性检查

Suppose a second study of the same school takes 60 students instead of 15 and finds the same $s = 4.486$ and $s_x = 3.335$.

  1. $SE_b = \dfrac{4.486}{3.335\sqrt{59}} = \dfrac{4.486}{25.617} = 0.1751$.
  2. Compare with 0.3595: quadrupling $n$ cuts $SE_b$ roughly in half, because $SE_b$ shrinks like $1/\sqrt{n-1}$ ($\sqrt{14/59} \approx 0.49$).
  3. Independence: the students were chosen at random and 60 is less than 10% of 1,200 (which is 120), so the 10% condition holds. The same check passes for $n = 15$.

Why this works: a larger sample averages out more of the chance scatter, so sample slopes cluster more tightly around $\beta$.

假设对该校做第二项研究，抽取 60 名而不是 15 名学生，得到相同的 $s = 4.486$ 与 $s_x = 3.335$。

  1. $SE_b = \dfrac{4.486}{3.335\sqrt{59}} = \dfrac{4.486}{25.617} = 0.1751$。
  2. 与 0.3595 比较：$n$ 增为 4 倍，$SE_b$ 约减半，因为 $SE_b$ 随 $1/\sqrt{n-1}$ 缩小（$\sqrt{14/59} \approx 0.49$）。
  3. 独立性：学生为随机抽取，且 60 小于 1,200 的 10%（即 120），所以 10% 条件成立；对 $n = 15$ 同样通过。

为何成立：样本越大，越能抵消偶然的散布，样本斜率就越紧密地聚集在 $\beta$ 附近。

Exam tip考试提示

Always define $\beta$ in words with the population attached: "the true slope of the population regression line relating mean test score to weekly study hours for all students at this school." A bare "the slope" is incomplete, and a statement about $b$ where the question asks about $\beta$ is a statement about the wrong thing.

一定要用文字定义 $\beta$ 并写明总体：“该校全体学生中，平均测验分数与每周学习小时数之间总体回归直线的真实斜率。”只写“斜率”不完整；题目问 $\beta$ 时却写 $b$，就是答非所问。

Common Mistake: mixing up the sample and the population
  • Writing hypotheses or intervals about $b$. Inference is about the parameter $\beta$; $b$ is the known statistic that estimates it.
  • Using $n-1$ degrees of freedom (the one-sample $t$ value). For a slope, $df = n-2$.
  • Checking Normality on the $y$-values alone. The condition is on the residuals, the scatter about the line.
常见错误：混淆样本与总体
  • 对 $b$ 写假设或区间。推断针对参数 $\beta$；$b$ 是已知的、用来估计它的统计量。
  • 使用 $n-1$ 自由度（单样本 $t$ 的值）。斜率推断的自由度是 $df = n-2$。
  • 只对 $y$ 值单独检查正态性。条件针对的是残差，即围绕直线的散布。

Confidence Intervals for the Slope斜率的置信区间

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Write the formula for a $t$-interval for the slope, $b \pm t^* \cdot SE_b$, with $df = n-2$.
  • Read the coefficient, SE Coef, T, P, $S$ and R-Sq entries of regression computer output.
  • Calculate a confidence interval for $\beta$ by hand from computer output and with the TI-84 LinRegTInt.
  • Interpret a confidence interval for a slope in context, including the units and the phrase "per additional".
  • Explain how the confidence level changes the width of the interval.
  • 写出斜率的 $t$ 区间公式 $b \pm t^* \cdot SE_b$，其中 $df = n-2$。
  • 读懂回归计算机输出中的 Coef、SE Coef、T、P、$S$ 与 R-Sq。
  • 由计算机输出手算 $\beta$ 的置信区间，并用 TI-84 的 LinRegTInt 求得。
  • 结合情境解释斜率的置信区间，包括单位与“每增加一个单位”的说法。
  • 解释置信水平如何改变区间的宽度。

Because the standardized slope follows a $t$ distribution with $n-2$ degrees of freedom when the LINE conditions hold, a confidence interval for $\beta$ has the familiar form statistic plus or minus critical value times standard error. This is the t-interval for the slope.

当 LINE 条件成立时，标准化后的斜率服从自由度为 $n-2$ 的 $t$ 分布，所以 $\beta$ 的置信区间采用熟悉的形式：统计量 加减 临界值乘标准误。这就是斜率的 t 区间（t-interval for the slope）。

t-interval for the slope

$$b \pm t^* \cdot SE_b, \qquad df = n-2$$

$t^*$ is the critical value of the $t$ distribution with $n-2$ degrees of freedom that leaves the middle $C\%$ of the area. Find it with a table or with invT on the calculator.

Topic 9.3主题 9.3

Justifying a Claim About a Slope论证关于斜率的说法

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Explain why a slope of 0 means no linear relationship between $x$ and $y$ in the population.
  • Use a confidence interval for $\beta$ to decide whether there is convincing evidence of a linear relationship.
  • Justify a claim about the value of the slope by checking whether the claimed value lies inside the interval.
  • Link the confidence level $C$ of a two-sided interval to the significance level $\alpha = 1 - C$ of a two-sided test.
  • Write a conclusion that avoids "no relationship" when the interval contains 0.
  • 解释为什么总体斜率为 0 意味着 $x$ 与 $y$ 之间没有线性关系。
  • 用 $\beta$ 的置信区间判断是否有令人信服的证据表明存在线性关系。
  • 检查所声称的值是否落在区间内，从而论证关于斜率取值的说法。
  • 把双侧区间的置信水平 $C$ 与双侧检验的显著性水平 $\alpha = 1 - C$ 联系起来。
  • 当区间含 0 时，写出不使用“没有关系”的结论。

The interval from Topic 9.2 does more than estimate $\beta$. It lists the values of the slope that are plausible given the data, so we can use it to judge a claim about the slope. The most important value to check is zero. If $\beta = 0$, the population regression line is flat: the mean of $y$ does not change as $x$ changes, which means there is no linear relationship (no linear association) between $x$ and $y$ in the population.

9.2 节的区间不只是估计 $\beta$。它列出了在这组数据下斜率合理的取值，因此可以用来判断关于斜率的说法（claim）。最重要的检查值是零。如果 $\beta = 0$，总体回归直线是水平的：$x$ 变化时 $y$ 的均值不变，这意味着总体中 $x$ 与 $y$ 之间没有线性关系（线性关联）。

Decision from an interval
  • The interval does not contain 0: 0 is not a plausible value, so there is convincing evidence of a linear relationship. The sign of the endpoints gives the direction.
  • The interval contains 0 (it includes 0): 0 is plausible, so there is not convincing evidence of a linear relationship. This does not prove that the slope is 0.
由区间作决定
  • 区间不含 0：0 不是合理取值，所以有令人信服的证据表明存在线性关系。端点的符号给出方向。
  • 区间含 0：0 是合理取值，所以没有令人信服的证据表明存在线性关系。这并不证明斜率等于 0。

A two-sided interval and a two-sided test give the same decision. A $C\%$ interval that excludes 0 matches rejecting $H_0\!: \beta = 0$ at significance level $\alpha = 1 - C$ (a 95% interval goes with $\alpha = 0.05$). A one-sided test at $\alpha = 0.05$ matches a 90% two-sided interval, provided the sign of the estimate agrees with the alternative.

双侧区间与双侧检验给出相同的决定。不含 0 的 $C\%$ 区间，对应在显著性水平 $\alpha = 1 - C$ 下拒绝 $H_0\!: \beta = 0$（95% 区间对应 $\alpha = 0.05$）。在 $\alpha = 0.05$ 下的单侧检验对应 90% 双侧区间，前提是估计值的符号与备择假设一致。

The same logic works for any claimed value, not just 0. A claim that $\beta$ equals some number is consistent with the data if that number lies inside the interval, and it is contradicted if the number lies outside.

同样的逻辑适用于任何被声称的值，不只是 0。如果某个数落在区间内，则“$\beta$ 等于该数”的说法与数据相符；如果落在区间外，则说法被数据否定。

  • 弄错方向：只含负值的区间表示负关系，尽管数值带有负号。

Hypotheses for a test of the slope斜率检验的假设

A significance test asks whether sample evidence is strong enough to show that a linear relationship exists in the population. The null hypothesis says there is none: the population slope is zero, $H_0\!: \beta = 0$. Checking the conditions with plots用图形检查条件

Three displays do the checking. A scatterplot of $y$ against $x$ shows whether the pattern is roughly linear and whether any point is far from the rest. A residual plot (residuals against $x$, or against the fitted values) is the sharper tool: it should look like a horizontal band of random scatter around 0, with about the same vertical spread everywhere. A Normal probability plot of the residuals should be close to a straight line; a dotplot or histogram of the residuals should be roughly symmetric with no strong skew or outliers. Independence comes from how the data were collected (random sample or random assignment, with the 10% condition), not from a plot.

三种图形完成检查。散点图（scatterplot）显示 $y$ 对 $x$ 的模式是否大致线性，以及是否有点远离其他点。残差图（residual plot，残差对 $x$ 或对拟合值）更为敏锐：它应像一条围绕 0 的水平随机散布带，各处纵向散布大致相同。残差的正态概率图（normal probability plot）应接近一条直线；残差的点图或直方图应大致对称，无明显偏斜或离群点。独立性来自数据收集方式（随机抽样或随机分配，并满足 10% 条件），而不是来自某张图。

  • Curved residual pattern: the true relationship is not linear, so a straight-line model and its slope $t$ procedures are not appropriate. Consider a different model or a transformation.
  • Fan shape: the variability of $y$ changes with $x$, so $s$ and $SE_b$ do not describe every part of the data. Probabilities from the $t$ procedures are unreliable.
  • Strong skew or outliers in the residuals: the Normal condition fails. With a small sample this matters; with a large sample the $t$ procedures are more forgiving of mild non-Normality, but outliers still need investigation.
  • 残差呈弯曲模式：真实关系不是线性的，直线模型及其斜率 $t$ 程序不适用。可考虑其他模型或变换。

Tests for the Slope斜率的检验

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Find a $p$-value from computer output or with the TI-84 LinRegTTest, and convert a two-sided $p$-value to a one-sided one.

  • 写出斜率的检验统计量 $t = \dfrac{b}{SE_b}$，并说明其自由度为 $n-2$。
  • 由计算机输出或 TI-84 的 LinRegTTest 得到 $p$ 值，并把双侧 $p$ 值换算为单侧。

The test of $H_0\!: \beta = 0$ standardizes the sample slope: how many standard errors is $b$ from the null value 0? When the LINE conditions hold and $H_0$ is true, this statistic follows a $t$ distribution with $n-2$ degrees of freedom. The $p$-value is the probability, assuming $H_0$ is true, of a value of $t$ at least as extreme as the one observed, in the direction of $H_a$.

检验 $H_0\!: \beta = 0$ 要把样本斜率标准化：$b$ 离原假设值 0 有多少个标准误？当 LINE 条件成立且 $H_0$ 为真时，这个统计量服从自由度为 $n-2$ 的 $t$ 分布。$p$ 值（p-value）是在 $H_0$ 为真的假定下，得到至少与观测值一样极端（沿 $H_a$ 的方向）的 $t$ 值的概率。

t-test for the slope

$$t = \dfrac{b}{SE_b}, \qquad df = n-2$$

Two-sided $H_a\!: \beta \neq 0$: the $p$-value is the area in both tails beyond $\pm t$. One-sided $H_a\!: \beta > 0$ or $\beta < 0$: the area in one tail only. Reject $H_0$ when the $p$-value is less than the significance level $\alpha$.

斜率的 t 检验

$$t = \dfrac{b}{SE_b}, \qquad df = n-2$$

双侧 $H_a\!: \beta \neq 0$：$p$ 值是 $\pm t$ 之外两个尾部的面积。单侧 $H_a\!: \beta > 0$ 或 $\beta < 0$：只取一个尾部的面积。当 $p$ 值小于显著性水平 $\alpha$ 时拒绝 $H_0$。

In computer output the P in the slope row is the two-sided $p$-value. If the alternative is one-sided and the sign of $b$ agrees with it, divide that $P$ by 2. If the sign of $b$ disagrees with the alternative, the one-sided $p$-value is more than 0.5 and $H_0$ is not rejected. The calculator command LinRegTTest (STAT, TESTS) takes Xlist, Ylist, Freq and the alternative (β & ρ: $\neq 0$, $< 0$ or $> 0$) and returns $t$, the $p$-value for the alternative you chose, $df$, $a$, $b$, $s$, $r^2$ and $r$.

计算机输出中斜率行的 P 是双侧 $p$ 值。若备择假设是单侧且 $b$ 的符号与其一致，把这个 $P$ 除以 2。若 $b$ 的符号与备择假设相反，单侧 $p$ 值大于 0.5，不拒绝 $H_0$。计算器命令 LinRegTTest（STAT，TESTS）需要 Xlist、Ylist、Freq 与备择假设（β & ρ：$\neq 0$、$< 0$ 或 $> 0$），返回 $t$、所选备择假设对应的 $p$ 值、$df$、$a$、$b$、$s$、$r^2$ 与 $r$。

Association and causation关联与因果

A small $p$-value says the linear relationship in the data is too strong to be explained by chance alone. It does not say why the variables are related. Cause and effect can be concluded only when the explanatory variable was assigned to the units by random assignment in an experiment; in an observational study a lurking variable may explain the relationship. And a conclusion extends to a larger population only if the units were a random sample from it.

小的 $p$ 值说明数据中的线性关系强到不能仅用偶然解释，但并不说明变量为什么相关。只有在实验中通过随机分配把解释变量指派给个体时，才能得出因果结论；在观察研究中，潜在变量也可能解释这种关系。而结论能否推广到更大总体，则取决于个体是否是该总体的随机样本。

Knowing how to run a procedure is half the job; the other half is deciding which procedure to run. Ask three questions in order. (1) What type of variable or variables: categorical or quantitative? (3) What parameter does the question ask about: a proportion, a mean, a difference, an association or a slope?

Two traps deserve a warning. First, a paired design and a slope both involve two measurements on each individual, but they answer different questions: the paired $t$ procedure asks how much the measurements differ on average, while the slope procedure asks how one measurement changes with the other. Second, a chi-square test needs counts of categories, never two quantitative variables.

有两个陷阱需要提醒。第一，配对设计与斜率都涉及每个个体的两次测量，但回答的问题不同：配对 $t$ 程序问两次测量平均相差多少，斜率程序问一个测量如何随另一个测量变化。第二，卡方检验需要类别的计数，不能用于两个定量变量。

Twelve randomly selected students take a pretest and, after a workshop, a posttest. Which procedure answers each question?

  1. Question 1: "Did scores improve, on average?" The parameter is the mean difference $\mu_d$ (posttest minus pretest) for all students who could take the workshop. Use a paired $t$ procedure.
  2. Question 2: "Can the pretest score be used to predict the posttest score?" The parameter is the slope $\beta$ of the population regression line of posttest on pretest. Use a $t$ procedure for the slope with $H_0\!: \beta = 0$ and $df = 12 - 2 = 10$.
  3. The data layout (two numbers per student) is identical, so the layout does not choose the procedure. The question and the parameter do.

随机选出的 12 名学生先做前测，参加研讨班后再做后测。下列问题各用哪个程序？

  1. 问题 1：“成绩平均是否提高？”参数是所有可参加研讨班的学生的均值差 $\mu_d$（后测减前测）。使用配对 $t$ 程序。
  2. 问题 2：“前测成绩能否用来预测后测成绩？”参数是后测对前测的总体回归直线斜率 $\beta$。使用斜率的 $t$ 程序，$H_0\!: \beta = 0$，$df = 12 - 2 = 10$。
  3. 两个问题的数据布局（每名学生两个数）完全相同，所以不是布局决定程序，而是问题和参数决定。
Worked Example: choose, then carry out, the procedure例题：先选择程序，再完整执行

A random sample of 25 used cars of one model is taken from the many more than 250 advertised for sale in a region, and records mileage $x$ (thousands of miles) and asking price $y$ (thousands of dollars). The regression output gives $b = -0.0850$ (thousand dollars per thousand miles) and $SE_b = 0.0215$; plots show no problems with the conditions. Is price linearly related to mileage? Use $\alpha = 0.05$.

  1. Which procedure? Both variables are quantitative, measured on the same cars in one sample, and the question is about a linear relationship, so choose a $t$-test for the slope. (Not two-sample or paired, because there are not two groups of one variable; not chi-square, because the variables are not categorical.)
  2. Hypotheses: $\beta$ is the true slope relating asking price to mileage for this model of car. $H_0\!: \beta = 0$, $H_a\!: \beta \neq 0$.
  3. Conditions: plots support Linear, Normal and Equal variance; a random sample from more than 250 cars, so $n = 25$ is under the 10% limit.
  4. Test statistic: $t = \dfrac{-0.0850}{0.0215} = -3.95$, $df = 25 - 2 = 23$, and the two-sided $p$-value is $0.0006$.
  5. Conclusion: $p < 0.05$, so we reject $H_0$. There is convincing evidence of a negative linear relationship between mileage and asking price for this model of used car.

从一个地区在售的某款车（多于 250 辆）中随机抽取 25 辆，记录里程 $x$（千英里）与要价 $y$（千美元）。回归输出给出 $b = -0.0850$（千美元/千英里），$SE_b = 0.0215$；图形显示条件无问题。要价与里程是否线性相关？取 $\alpha = 0.05$。

  1. 用哪个程序？两个变量都是定量的，在同一批车上、一个样本中测量，问题是关于线性关系，所以选择斜率的 $t$ 检验。（不是两样本或配对，因为不是同一变量的两组；也不是卡方，因为变量不是分类的。）
  2. 假设：$\beta$ 是该款车要价对里程的真实斜率。$H_0\!: \beta = 0$，$H_a\!: \beta \neq 0$。
  3. 条件：图形支持线性、正态与等方差；随机样本取自多于 250 辆的在售车，所以 $n = 25$ 未超过 10% 的限度。
  4. 检验统计量：$t = \dfrac{-0.0850}{0.0215} = -3.95$，$df = 25 - 2 = 23$，双侧 $p$ 值为 $0.0006$。
  5. 结论：$p < 0.05$，拒绝 $H_0$。有令人信服的证据表明该款二手车的里程与要价之间存在负的线性关系。

State the procedure and defend it with the data: "I will use a $t$-test for the slope because both variables are quantitative and measured on the same cars." Then write the parameter in words, the hypotheses, and the conditions that go with that procedure (LINE for a slope, expected counts for chi-square, and so on). Common Mistake: letting the layout choose the procedure
  • Using a two-sample $t$-test because there are "two columns of numbers." Two variables on the same individuals call for a slope (or a paired $t$ if the question is about a mean difference), not two independent groups.
  • Running a chi-square test on two quantitative variables, or a slope test on two categorical variables.
  • Ignoring the conditions that belong to the chosen procedure and quoting the ones from a different test.
常见错误：让数据布局来决定程序
  • 因为“有两列数”就用两样本 $t$ 检验。同一批个体上的两个变量应用斜率（若问的是均值差则用配对 $t$），而不是两个独立组。
  • 对两个定量变量做卡方检验，或对两个分类变量做斜率检验。
  • 忽略所选程序自己的条件，却引用另一种检验的条件。
