Chemical Bonding化学键

Why do atoms stick together at all? And why do the resulting substances have such different properties? The answer lies in the electrons: specifically, in how atoms gain, lose, or share electrons to reach a lower-energy, more stable configuration. This guide moves from the driving force for bonding (the octet rule), through the two primary bond types (ionic and covalent), to the tools chemists use to represent and predict bonding (Lewis structures, VSEPR geometry, electronegativity and polarity), and finally to the weaker forces that act between molecules (intermolecular forces) and through metals (metallic bonding). Worked examples and KaTeX formulas are used throughout.原子为什么会结合在一起？由此形成的物质为何拥有如此不同的性质？答案在于电子：具体来说，在于原子如何得失或共享电子以达到能量更低、更稳定的状态。本指南从成键的驱动力（八隅规则）出发，经过两种主要键型（离子键与共价键），到化学家用来表示和预测成键的工具（路易斯结构、VSEPR 分子几何、电负性与极性），最终落脚于分子间的较弱作用力（分子间作用力）和金属中的结合力（金属键）。全程使用例题与公式。

Why Atoms Bond: The Octet Rule原子为何成键：八隅规则

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Determine the number of valence electrons of a main-group atom from its group number.
  • Predict whether an atom tends to lose, gain or share electrons, and the ion charge it forms.
  • Explain why bonding lowers the energy of a system of atoms.
  • Decide whether two elements form an ionic or a covalent bond.
  • 陈述八隅规则以及氢的二隅规则。
  • 根据族数确定主族原子的价电子数。
  • 预测原子倾向于失去、获得还是共享电子，以及形成的离子电荷。
  • 解释成键为何使原子体系的能量降低。
  • 判断两种元素形成离子键还是共价键。
Atoms bond to reach a lower-energy, more stable electron configuration.原子成键是为了达到能量更低、更稳定的电子构型。
  • Driving force:驱动力： isolated atoms are higher in energy than bonded atoms. Bond formation releases energy; bond breaking absorbs energy. Atoms bond because the product has a lower potential energy than the separated reactants.孤立原子的能量高于成键原子。成键释放能量；断键吸收能量。原子成键是因为生成物的势能低于分离反应物的势能。
  • Octet rule:八隅规则： main-group atoms tend to gain, lose, or share valence electrons until they have 8 valence electrons (a full outer shell, like a noble gas). Hydrogen is the key exception: it aims for 2 electrons (helium configuration). Exceptions beyond hydrogen include: atoms in Period 3 and below can expand their octet (more than 8 electrons around the central atom) using d-orbital capacity; some molecules are electron-deficient (e.g. BF$_3$, with only 6 electrons around B).主族原子倾向于得失或共享价电子，直到拥有 8 个价电子（满外层，类似稀有气体）。氢是关键例外：它的目标是 2 个电子（氦的构型）。氢以外的例外包括：第 3 周期及以下的原子可以利用 d 轨道容量扩展八隅（中心原子周围超过 8 个电子）；一些分子电子不足（如 BF$_3$，B 周围只有 6 个电子）。
  • Valence electrons:价电子： the electrons in the outermost shell; they are the ones involved in bonding. The group number of a main-group element equals its valence-electron count (Group 1 has 1, Group 17 has 7, Group 18 has 8).最外层壳层中的电子；这些电子参与成键。主族元素的族数等于其价电子数（第 1 族有 1 个，第 17 族有 7 个，第 18 族有 8 个）。
NGSS HS-PS1-2 grounds bonding in "outermost electron states of atoms." AB Chemistry 20 GO1 defines "valence electron" as a key concept. SCH3U B3.4 names the octet as the conceptual basis for both ionic and covalent bond formation.NGSS HS-PS1-2 把成键建立在"原子最外层电子状态"的基础上。AB Chemistry 20 GO1 将"价电子"列为关键概念。SCH3U B3.4 把八隅规则列为离子键和共价键形成的概念基础。
Two ways to reach an octet.达到八隅的两种方式。
  • Transfer electrons:转移电子： one atom gives electron(s) to another. This creates oppositely charged ions that attract each other: an ionic bond. Typical between metals and non-metals.一个原子将电子给予另一个原子。这产生相互吸引的异号带电离子：离子键。典型地发生在金属与非金属之间。
  • Share electrons:共享电子： two atoms each contribute electrons to a shared pair that both nuclei attract: a covalent bond. Typical between non-metals.两个原子各贡献电子形成两个原子核共同吸引的共用电子对：共价键。典型地发生在非金属之间。

Why bonded atoms are lower in energy为何成键后的原子能量更低

A bond forms when the potential energy of two atoms falls as they come together: each nucleus attracts the electrons of the other atom, and this attraction outweighs the repulsion between the two nuclei and between the electron clouds. The energy is lowest at one separation, the bond length, and the energy released on reaching it is the reason the bonded arrangement is stable. A full outer shell (an octet, or a duet for hydrogen) is the pattern of valence electrons that gives this low energy for main-group atoms, which is why the octet rule predicts so many bonds.当两个原子靠近时，若体系的势能降低，就会成键：每个原子核吸引另一原子的电子，这种吸引力超过两个原子核之间以及电子云之间的排斥。势能在某一间距（键长）处最低，到达该间距时释放的能量就是成键排布稳定的原因。满的外层（八隅，氢为二隅）是使主族原子能量较低的价电子排布，因此八隅规则能预测很多键。

Common error: writing that atoms bond "because they want a full shell". An atom has no wants. Write the cause as energy: the bonded atoms have lower potential energy than the separated atoms, and the octet is the electron pattern that goes with it. Remember also that hydrogen follows the duet rule, and that a few molecules such as $\mathrm{BF_{3}}$ stop at 6 electrons around the central atom.常见错误：写成原子成键"是因为想要满壳层"。原子没有意愿。应把原因写成能量：成键原子的势能低于分离的原子，八隅是与之对应的电子排布。还要记住氢遵循二隅规则，少数分子（如 $\mathrm{BF_{3}}$）中心原子周围只有 6 个电子。

Worked Example 1: Valence electrons and ion charge of Al, S and Ar例题 1：Al、S、Ar 的价电子数与离子电荷

Use the group number to state the valence electrons of aluminium (Group 13), sulfur (Group 16) and argon (Group 18). Predict whether each atom tends to lose, gain or not form ions, and give the charge and the noble-gas configuration reached.利用族数写出铝（第 13 族）、硫（第 16 族）和氩（第 18 族）的价电子数。预测每种原子倾向于失去、获得电子还是不形成离子，并给出电荷及达到的稀有气体构型。

Valence electrons.价电子数。 For main-group elements the number of valence electrons equals the last digit of the group number (Group 13 gives 3, Group 16 gives 6, Group 18 gives 8). So Al has 3, S has 6 and Ar has 8.主族元素的价电子数等于族数的个位数（第 13 族为 3，第 16 族为 6，第 18 族为 8）。所以 Al 有 3 个，S 有 6 个，Ar 有 8 个。

Aluminium.铝。 Losing 3 electrons is easier than gaining 5, so Al forms $\mathrm{Al^{3+}}$. It has $13 - 3 = 10$ electrons, the neon configuration.失去 3 个电子比获得 5 个容易，所以 Al 形成 $\mathrm{Al^{3+}}$。它有 $13 - 3 = 10$ 个电子，是氖的构型。

Sulfur.硫。 Gaining 2 electrons is easier than losing 6, so S forms $\mathrm{S^{2-}}$. It has $16 + 2 = 18$ electrons, the argon configuration.获得 2 个电子比失去 6 个容易，所以 S 形成 $\mathrm{S^{2-}}$。它有 $16 + 2 = 18$ 个电子，是氩的构型。

Argon.氩。 Ar already has 8 valence electrons (a full octet), so it neither gains nor loses electrons and forms no ion. ✓Ar 已有 8 个价电子（满八隅），因此既不得也不失电子，不形成离子。✓

Why this works.原理。 The atom takes the route that reaches a noble-gas configuration with the smaller energy change, which for a metal (few valence electrons) is losing and for a non-metal (many valence electrons) is gaining.原子选择能量变化较小、可达到稀有气体构型的途径：金属（价电子少）倾向于失去，非金属（价电子多）倾向于获得。

Worked Example 2: Which bond forms: Li with F, F with F, H with Cl例题 2：形成哪种键：Li 与 F、F 与 F、H 与 Cl

Decide whether each pair of atoms bonds by transfer or by sharing of electrons, and show how each atom reaches a noble-gas pattern: (a) lithium and fluorine, (b) two fluorine atoms, (c) hydrogen and chlorine.判断下列每一对原子是通过转移还是共享电子成键，并说明每个原子如何达到稀有气体的排布：(a) 锂和氟，(b) 两个氟原子，(c) 氢和氯。

(a) Li and F.(a) Li 与 F。 Li (Group 1) has 1 valence electron; F (Group 17) has 7. A metal and a non-metal: Li transfers 1 electron to F. Li becomes $\mathrm{Li^{+}}$ ($1s^2$, helium pattern) and F becomes $\mathrm{F^{-}}$ (8 valence electrons). This is an ionic bond.Li（第 1 族）有 1 个价电子；F（第 17 族）有 7 个。金属与非金属：Li 将 1 个电子转移给 F。Li 变为 $\mathrm{Li^{+}}$（$1s^2$，氦的排布），F 变为 $\mathrm{F^{-}}$（8 个价电子）。这是离子键。

(b) F and F.(b) F 与 F。 Two non-metals with equal pull on electrons: neither can take an electron from the other, so each F shares 1 electron. The shared pair counts for both atoms: each F has $6 + 2 = 8$ electrons (3 lone pairs and 1 bonding pair). This is a covalent bond, a single bond in $\mathrm{F_{2}}$.两个非金属对电子的吸引力相同：谁也无法夺走对方的电子，因此各共享 1 个电子。共用电子对对两个原子都计数：每个 F 有 $6 + 2 = 8$ 个电子（3 个孤电子对和 1 个成键对）。这是共价键，即 $\mathrm{F_{2}}$ 中的单键。

(c) H and Cl.(c) H 与 Cl。 Both are non-metals, so they share one pair. H counts 2 electrons (the duet) and Cl counts $6 + 2 = 8$ (an octet). The bond is covalent in $\mathrm{HCl}$. ✓二者都是非金属，因此共享一对电子。H 计 2 个电子（二隅），Cl 计 $6 + 2 = 8$ 个（八隅）。$\mathrm{HCl}$ 中的键是共价键。✓

Why this works.原理。 Transfer happens when the atoms differ greatly in how strongly they hold electrons (metal with non-metal); sharing happens when both atoms attract electrons about equally or both need electrons (two non-metals).当两个原子对电子的束缚力差别很大（金属与非金属）时发生转移；当两个原子对电子的吸引力相近，或都需要电子（两个非金属）时发生共享。

2. Explain why sodium forms $\mathrm{Na^{+}}$ and not $\mathrm{Na^{2+}}$.2. 解释钠为何形成 $\mathrm{Na^{+}}$ 而不是 $\mathrm{Na^{2+}}$。

Na has 1 valence electron. Losing it leaves the neon configuration, $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6$, a full second shell with lower energy. A second electron would have to come out of that full inner shell, which is held much more tightly by the nucleus, so far more energy would be needed and the ion would be less stable.

Na 有 1 个价电子。失去它后得到氖的构型 $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6$，即满的第二层，能量较低。第二个电子必须从这个满的内层中移出，而内层受原子核的束缚强得多，需要的能量大得多，所得离子也不稳定。

3. Predict the ion formed by Ca and by N, and name the noble gas each ion matches.3. 预测 Ca 和 N 形成的离子，并写出每种离子对应的稀有气体。

Ca (Group 2) loses 2 electrons to form $\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}$ with $20 - 2 = 18$ electrons, the argon configuration. N (Group 15, 5 valence electrons) gains 3 electrons to form $\mathrm{N^{3-}}$ with $7 + 3 = 10$ electrons, the neon configuration.

Ca（第 2 族）失去 2 个电子形成 $\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}$，有 $20 - 2 = 18$ 个电子，是氩的构型。N（第 15 族，5 个价电子）获得 3 个电子形成 $\mathrm{N^{3-}}$，有 $7 + 3 = 10$ 个电子，是氖的构型。

Ionic bond: metal transfers electron(s) to non-metal; opposites attract.离子键：金属将电子转移给非金属；异号电荷相互吸引。
  • Formation:形成： a metal atom loses one or more valence electrons to become a cation (positive ion); a non-metal atom gains electrons to become an anion (negative ion). The electrostatic attraction between cation and anion is the ionic bond.金属原子失去一个或多个价电子成为阳离子（正离子）；非金属原子获得电子成为阴离子（负离子）。阳离子与阴离子之间的静电吸引力就是离子键。

  • Properties of ionic compounds:离子化合物的性质： high melting/boiling points (strong lattice forces); brittle (shifting layers misalign like-charge rows); conduct electricity when dissolved or molten (free ions); soluble in polar solvents like water.高熔沸点（强晶格力）；脆性（层错位时同性电荷排列相对）；溶解或熔融时导电（自由离子）；溶于极性溶剂（如水）。

Worked Example 3: Ionic bond formation of sodium chloride例题 3：氯化钠的离子键形成

Explain, using valence electrons and the octet rule, how sodium (Group 1) and chlorine (Group 17) form an ionic bond. Identify the resulting ions and their electron configurations.用价电子和八隅规则解释钠（第 1 族）和氯（第 17 族）如何形成离子键。确定生成的离子及其电子排布。

Sodium's situation.钠的情况。 Na has electron configuration $1s^2\, 2s^2\, 2p^6\, 3s^1$, which is 1 valence electron. Losing it gives the neon configuration ($1s^2\, 2s^2\, 2p^6$, full outer shell) and creates Na$^+$ (cation, charge $+1$).Na 的电子排布为 $1s^2\, 2s^2\, 2p^6\, 3s^1$，即 1 个价电子。失去它得到氖的构型（$1s^2\, 2s^2\, 2p^6$，满外层），形成 Na$^+$（阳离子，电荷 $+1$）。

Chlorine's situation.氯的情况。 Cl has configuration $[\text{Ne}]\, 3s^2\, 3p^5$, which is 7 valence electrons. Gaining 1 electron gives the argon configuration ($[\text{Ne}]\, 3s^2\, 3p^6$, full outer shell) and creates Cl$^-$ (anion, charge $-1$).Cl 的构型为 $[\text{Ne}]\, 3s^2\, 3p^5$，即 7 个价电子。获得 1 个电子得到氩的构型（$[\text{Ne}]\, 3s^2\, 3p^6$，满外层），形成 Cl$^-$（阴离子，电荷 $-1$）。

The bond.化学键。 Na$^+$ and Cl$^-$ attract electrostatically. In the solid, each Na$^+$ is surrounded by 6 Cl$^-$ and vice versa (a face-centred cubic lattice). The formula unit NaCl represents the 1:1 ratio that gives net charge zero. ✓Na$^+$ 与 Cl$^-$ 静电吸引。在固体中，每个 Na$^+$ 被 6 个 Cl$^-$ 包围，反之亦然（面心立方晶格）。化学式 NaCl 表示净电荷为零的 1:1 比值。✓

Worked Example 4: Formula, melting point and conductivity of ionic compounds例题 4：离子化合物的化学式、熔点与导电性

Aluminium (Group 13) and oxygen (Group 16) form an ionic compound. (a) Deduce its formula. (b) Explain why MgO (melting point 2852 °C) melts at a much higher temperature than NaCl (801 °C). (c) State whether solid, molten and dissolved NaCl conduct electricity.铝（第 13 族）和氧（第 16 族）形成离子化合物。(a) 推导其化学式。(b) 解释 MgO（熔点 2852 °C）的熔点为何远高于 NaCl（801 °C）。(c) 说明固态、熔融和溶解的 NaCl 是否导电。

(a) Formula.(a) 化学式。 Al forms $\mathrm{Al^{3+}}$ (charge $+3$) and O forms $\mathrm{O^{2-}}$ (charge $-2$). The smallest equal total charges are $2 \times (+3) = +6$ and $3 \times (-2) = -6$, so the ratio is 2 Al : 3 O and the formula is $\mathrm{Al_{2}O_{3}}$. The charge balance is $+6 - 6 = 0$. ✓Al 形成 $\mathrm{Al^{3+}}$（电荷 $+3$），O 形成 $\mathrm{O^{2-}}$（电荷 $-2$）。最小的相等总电荷是 $2 \times (+3) = +6$ 和 $3 \times (-2) = -6$，所以比例为 2 个 Al : 3 个 O，化学式为 $\mathrm{Al_{2}O_{3}}$。电荷平衡：$+6 - 6 = 0$。✓

(b) Melting points.(b) 熔点。 Melting must pull the ions out of the lattice, so it depends on the strength of the electrostatic attraction. The ions in MgO carry charges $2+$ and $2-$ and are smaller than $\mathrm{Na^{+}}$ and $\mathrm{Cl^{-}}$ respectively, while NaCl has charges $1+$ and $1-$. Larger charges and smaller ions give a stronger attraction between neighbours, so more energy is needed to separate them and MgO melts at the higher temperature.熔化必须把离子从晶格中拉开，因此取决于静电吸引力的强弱。MgO 中的离子电荷为 $2+$ 和 $2-$，且分别比 $\mathrm{Na^{+}}$ 和 $\mathrm{Cl^{-}}$ 小，而 NaCl 的电荷为 $1+$ 和 $1-$。电荷越大、离子越小，相邻离子间的吸引力越强，分离它们所需的能量越多，所以 MgO 的熔点更高。

(c) Conductivity.(c) 导电性。 Solid NaCl does not conduct: the ions are fixed in the lattice and there are no free electrons. Molten NaCl conducts, because the ions are free to move and carry charge. Dissolved NaCl conducts for the same reason: the lattice has separated into mobile $\mathrm{Na^{+}}$ and $\mathrm{Cl^{-}}$ ions. ✓固态 NaCl 不导电：离子固定在晶格中，没有自由电子。熔融 NaCl 导电，因为离子可以自由移动并携带电荷。溶解的 NaCl 导电的原因相同：晶格已分离成可移动的 $\mathrm{Na^{+}}$ 和 $\mathrm{Cl^{-}}$。✓

Why this works.原理。 Conduction needs charged particles that can move. In an ionic compound those particles are the ions, and they are free only when the lattice is broken by melting or dissolving.导电需要能够移动的带电粒子。离子化合物中的这类粒子是离子，只有在熔化或溶解使晶格被破坏后离子才能自由移动。

A lattice is not a molecule晶格不是分子

An ionic lattice is a repeating three-dimensional array of cations and anions in which every ion is surrounded by ions of opposite charge. There are no separate "NaCl molecules": the formula $\mathrm{NaCl}$ is a formula unit, the simplest whole-number ratio of ions. The lattice is brittle because a blow shifts one layer by a single ion, bringing ions of like charge face to face, and the repulsion splits the crystal.离子晶格是阳离子和阴离子重复排列的三维阵列，每个离子被异号离子包围。不存在单独的"NaCl 分子"：化学式 $\mathrm{NaCl}$ 表示化学式单元，即离子的最简整数比。晶格易脆，因为撞击使一层移动一个离子的距离，同号离子相对，排斥力使晶体碎裂。

Bond order, bond length and bond energy键级、键长与键能

Each extra shared pair pulls the two nuclei closer and holds them more strongly. For carbon–carbon bonds the measured values are: C–C 154 pm and 347 kJ/mol, C=C 134 pm and 614 kJ/mol, C≡C 120 pm and 839 kJ/mol. The length falls and the energy rises with bond order. The rise is not proportional: C=C has $614 \div 347 = 1.77$ times the energy of C–C, not 2 times, because the second pair is not as effective as the first.每增加一个共用电子对，两个原子核被拉得更近，结合得更牢。碳碳键的测量值为：C–C 154 pm、347 kJ/mol，C=C 134 pm、614 kJ/mol，C≡C 120 pm、839 kJ/mol。键级越高，键长越短，键能越大。增幅并不成正比：C=C 的键能是 C–C 的 $614 \div 347 = 1.77$ 倍，而不是 2 倍，因为第二对电子的效果不如第一对。

Worked Example 7: Lewis structure of water (H$_2$O)例题 7：水（H$_2$O）的路易斯结构

Draw the Lewis structure of water. Count valence electrons, arrange atoms, and verify the octet rule is satisfied.绘制水的路易斯结构。计算价电子数，排列原子，并验证八隅规则是否满足。

Worked Example 8: Lewis structure of carbon dioxide (CO₂)例题 8：二氧化碳（CO₂）的路易斯结构

Draw the Lewis structure of $\mathrm{CO_{2}}$. Count the valence electrons, build the skeleton, complete the octets, and use a multiple bond if the central atom is short of electrons.画出 $\mathrm{CO_{2}}$ 的路易斯结构。计算价电子数，搭好骨架，完成八隅；若中心原子电子不足，则使用多重键。

Step 1: count valence electrons.第 1 步：计算价电子数。 C (Group 14) contributes 4 and each O (Group 16) contributes 6. Total: $4 + 6 + 6 = 16$ valence electrons.C（第 14 族）贡献 4 个，每个 O（第 16 族）贡献 6 个。总计：$4 + 6 + 6 = 16$ 个价电子。

Step 2: skeleton.第 2 步：骨架。 C is the least electronegative atom, so it is central: O–C–O. Two single bonds use $2 \times 2 = 4$ electrons, leaving $16 - 4 = 12$.C 的电负性最小，所以是中心原子：O–C–O。两个单键用去 $2 \times 2 = 4$ 个电子，剩下 $16 - 4 = 12$ 个。

Step 3: octets.第 3 步：八隅。 Put 3 lone pairs on each O, which uses all 12 remaining electrons. Each O has $2 + 6 = 8$ electrons, but C has only $2 \times 2 = 4$.在每个 O 上放 3 个孤电子对，恰好用完剩下的 12 个电子。每个 O 有 $2 + 6 = 8$ 个电子，但 C 只有 $2 \times 2 = 4$ 个。

Step 4: multiple bonds.第 4 步：多重键。 Move one lone pair from each O into the C–O region, making two double bonds, O=C=O. Now C has $2 \times 4 = 8$ electrons, and each O has $4 + 4 = 8$ (a double bond and 2 lone pairs).把每个 O 上的一个孤电子对移入 C–O 之间，形成两个双键，即 O=C=O。现在 C 有 $2 \times 4 = 8$ 个电子，每个 O 有 $4 + 4 = 8$ 个（一个双键和 2 个孤电子对）。

Check.核对。 Shared electrons: 2 double bonds $\times 4 = 8$; lone-pair electrons: 4 lone pairs $\times 2 = 8$. Total $8 + 8 = 16$, equal to Step 1. ✓共享电子：2 个双键 $\times 4 = 8$；孤对电子：4 个孤电子对 $\times 2 = 8$。总数 $8 + 8 = 16$，与第 1 步相同。✓

Going deeper: resonance structures and formal charges深入：共振结构与形式电荷

Some molecules cannot be described by a single Lewis structure. Ozone (O$_3$) and carbonate (CO$_3^{2-}$) are classic examples. In ozone, two equivalent Lewis structures can be drawn that differ only in which O–O bond is single and which is double. The actual molecule is an average (resonance hybrid) of both: both bonds are identical, with a bond order of 1.5. Resonance structures are drawn with a double-headed arrow between them ($\leftrightarrow$). Formal charge helps identify the best Lewis structure: Formal charge $= $ (valence electrons) $-$ (lone-pair electrons) $-$ $\frac{1}{2}$(bonding electrons). The best structure minimises formal charges and places any negative formal charge on the most electronegative atom. IMFs are the forces between molecules (not inside them). They are weaker than covalent and ionic bonds but govern physical properties.分子间作用力是分子之间（而非分子内部）的力。它们比共价键和离子键弱，但决定物质的物理性质。
  • London (dispersion) forces:伦敦（色散）力： present in ALL molecules (polar or not). Arise from instantaneous dipoles caused by random electron motion. Strength increases with molar mass and surface area. Example: CH$_4$ (bp −161°C) vs C$_4$H$_{10}$ (bp −1°C): the larger molecule has stronger London forces.存在于所有分子中（无论极性与否）。由电子随机运动产生的瞬间偶极矩引起。强度随摩尔质量和表面积增大而增强。示例：CH$_4$（沸点 −161°C）vs C$_4$H$_{10}$（沸点 −1°C）：分子更大，伦敦力更强。
  • Dipole-dipole forces:偶极-偶极力： between polar molecules. The positive end of one molecule attracts the negative end of a neighbour. Stronger than London forces for molecules of similar size. Example: HCl (polar) has higher boiling point than Ar (non-polar) of similar molar mass.存在于极性分子之间。一个分子的正端吸引邻近分子的负端。对于大小相似的分子，比伦敦力更强。示例：HCl（极性）的沸点高于摩尔质量相近的 Ar（非极性）。
  • Hydrogen bonding:氢键： a special, unusually strong dipole-dipole attraction between an H bonded to F, O, or N and a lone pair on an adjacent F, O, or N. Ranking: hydrogen bonding > dipole-dipole > London (dispersion). Metallic bonding: in metals, valence electrons are delocalised in a "sea" of electrons throughout the lattice. Four types of solid四种类型的固体

The particles in a solid and the force holding them decide its melting point and conductivity. An ionic solid is held by ionic bonds throughout the lattice. A molecular solid is made of separate molecules held by intermolecular forces. In a network covalent solid (also called a covalent network) every atom is joined to its neighbours by covalent bonds through the whole crystal, so melting must break those bonds. A metallic solid has cations in a sea of delocalised electrons.固体中的粒子及其相互作用力决定其熔点和导电性。离子固体由贯穿整个晶格的离子键结合。分子固体由独立的分子通过分子间作用力结合。网状共价固体（又称共价网络）中每个原子都通过共价键与相邻原子相连，贯穿整个晶体，因此熔化必须破坏这些键。金属固体由浸在离域电子"海"中的阳离子组成。

A metal is a lattice of cations surrounded by delocalised valence electrons that belong to the whole piece of metal. The attraction between the cations and the electron sea is the metallic bond. Layers of cations can slide over one another while the electron sea keeps holding them, so metals are malleable and ductile instead of brittle like ionic solids.金属是阳离子晶格，周围是属于整块金属的离域价电子。阳离子与电子"海"之间的吸引力就是金属键。施加电压时电子可以移动，所以金属在固态时就能导电。阳离子层可以互相滑动，电子"海"仍然把它们联系在一起，因此金属具有延展性而不像离子固体那样易脆。

Common Questions常见问题

What is the difference between ionic and covalent bonds?离子键和共价键有什么区别？

Ionic bonds form when electrons transfer from a metal to a nonmetal, creating oppositely charged ions, while covalent bonds form when two nonmetals share electrons to complete their octets.离子键是电子从金属转移到非金属形成带相反电荷的离子时产生的，而共价键是两个非金属共享电子以达到八隅体时产生的。

How do you predict the shape of a molecule with VSEPR?如何用价层电子对互斥理论预测分子的形状？

Count the electron domains (bonds and lone pairs) around the central atom; VSEPR theory states they arrange to minimize repulsion, giving shapes such as linear, trigonal planar, and tetrahedral.数出中心原子周围的电子对区域（成键对与孤对电子），价层电子对互斥理论认为它们会排布成相互排斥最小的方式，从而形成直线形、平面三角形、四面体形等形状。

What makes a molecule polar?是什么使分子具有极性？

A molecule is polar when it contains polar bonds (from an electronegativity difference) that do not cancel out due to an asymmetric shape, leaving a net dipole.当分子含有极性键（源于电负性差异），且因形状不对称而无法相互抵消、留下净偶极时，分子就具有极性。
