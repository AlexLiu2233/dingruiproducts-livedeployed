Counting and Probability计数与概率

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Apply the fundamental counting principle to multi-stage choices.
  • Decide whether order matters, then calculate a permutation or a combination.
  • Calculate a probability as favorable outcomes over equally likely total outcomes, and use the complement.
  • Multiply probabilities for independent events and adjust the second factor when drawing without replacement.
  • Use the addition rule for “A or B” without double counting.
  • 对多阶段选择应用计数基本原理 (fundamental counting principle)。
  • 先判断顺序是否重要，再计算排列 (permutation) 或组合 (combination)。
  • 把概率 (probability) 算成有利结果数除以等可能的总结果数，并会使用补事件。
  • 对独立 (independent) 事件把概率相乘，并在不放回抽取时调整第二个因子。
  • 用加法法则计算“A 或 B”，避免重复计数。

The counting principle计数基本原理

If one choice can be made in $m$ ways and, after it, a second choice can be made in $n$ ways, the pair of choices can be made in $m\times n$ ways. This fundamental counting principle extends to any number of stages: $4$ shirts, $3$ pairs of pants and $2$ pairs of shoes give $4\times 3\times 2=24$ outfits. The principle works because every option at one stage combines with every option at the next, so the counts multiply rather than add.

若第一个选择有 $m$ 种方法，之后第二个选择有 $n$ 种方法，则这一对选择共有 $m\times n$ 种方法。这条计数基本原理 (fundamental counting principle) 可推广到任意多个阶段：$4$ 件衬衫、$3$ 条裤子、$2$ 双鞋共可搭配 $4\times 3\times 2=24$ 套。原理成立的原因是：每个阶段的每个选项都能与下一阶段的每个选项组合，所以个数相乘而不是相加。

Permutations and combinations排列与组合

A permutation is an arrangement in which order matters (first, second and third place are different jobs). A combination is a selection in which order does not matter (a committee of three is the same committee in any order). Each combination of $r$ objects can be arranged in $r!$ orders, so dividing the permutation count by $r!$ removes the repeats.

排列 (permutation) 是顺序有关的安排（第一、第二、第三名是不同的位置）。组合 (combination) 是顺序无关的选取（三人委员会无论按什么顺序都是同一个委员会）。每个由 $r$ 个对象组成的组合可以排成 $r!$ 种顺序，所以把排列数除以 $r!$ 就去掉了重复。

Counting formulas
Permutations of $r$ from $n$: ${}_{n}P_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{(n-r)!}$
Combinations of $r$ from $n$: ${}_{n}C_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}$
Example: ${}_{8}P_{3}=8\times7\times6=336$ and ${}_{8}C_{3}=\dfrac{336}{3!}=56$.
计数公式
从 $n$ 个中取 $r$ 个的排列：${}_{n}P_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{(n-r)!}$
从 $n$ 个中取 $r$ 个的组合：${}_{n}C_{r}=\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}$
例：${}_{8}P_{3}=8\times7\times6=336$，${}_{8}C_{3}=\dfrac{336}{3!}=56$。
Worked Example: officers versus a committee例题：职位与委员会

A club has $10$ members. (a) In how many ways can it choose a president, a vice president and a secretary (three different people)? (b) In how many ways can it choose a $3$-person committee?

  1. (a) Order matters, because the three jobs are different. President: $10$ choices, then vice president: $9$, then secretary: $8$.
  2. Counting principle: $10\times9\times8=720$, which is ${}_{10}P_{3}$.
  3. (b) Order does not matter. Each committee was counted $3!=6$ times in part (a), once for each way to hand out the three jobs.
  4. Divide: $\dfrac{720}{6}=120$, which is ${}_{10}C_{3}$.

Why it works: a combination is a permutation with the order forgotten, so it is always the permutation count divided by $r!$.

某社团有 $10$ 名成员。(a) 选出主席、副主席和秘书（三个不同的人）有多少种方法？(b) 选出一个 $3$ 人委员会有多少种方法？

  1. (a) 三个职位不同，所以顺序重要。主席有 $10$ 种选法，接着副主席 $9$ 种，秘书 $8$ 种。
  2. 由计数原理：$10\times9\times8=720$，即 ${}_{10}P_{3}$。
  3. (b) 顺序无关。每个委员会在 (a) 中被数了 $3!=6$ 次，对应三个职位的每种分配方式。
  4. 相除：$\dfrac{720}{6}=120$，即 ${}_{10}C_{3}$。

原理：组合就是忘掉顺序的排列，所以组合数总是排列数除以 $r!$。

Probability and the complement概率与补事件

When all outcomes are equally likely, the probability of an event is $P(A)=\dfrac{\text{favorable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}}$, a number from $0$ (impossible) to $1$ (certain). The complement “not $A$” has $P(\text{not }A)=1-P(A)$. The complement is the fast route for “at least one”: the only way to get none is a single outcome path, so $P(\text{at least one})=1-P(\text{none})$.

当所有结果等可能时，事件的概率 (probability) 为 $P(A)=\dfrac{\text{favorable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}}$（有利结果数除以总结果数），取值从 $0$（不可能）到 $1$（必然）。补事件 (complement)“非 $A$”满足 $P(\text{not }A)=1-P(A)$。补事件是处理“至少一个”的捷径：“一个也没有”只有一条结果路径，所以 $P(\text{at least one})=1-P(\text{none})$。

Independent events, “and” and “or”独立事件、“且”与“或”

Two events are independent when the outcome of one does not change the probability of the other, as with a coin flip and a die roll. Then $P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\times P(B)$. Drawing without replacement makes the draws dependent: after a red marble leaves the bag, both the red count and the total drop by one, so the second factor changes (Figure 4.1). For “or”, use $P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\text{ and }B)$; the subtraction removes the overlap that was counted twice.

若一个事件的结果不改变另一个事件的概率，两事件独立 (independent)，例如抛硬币与掷骰子。此时 $P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\times P(B)$。不放回抽取使各次抽取不独立：取出一个红球后，红球数和总数都少了一个，所以第二个因子改变（图 4.1）。对“或”，用 $P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\text{ and }B)$；减去的部分就是被重复计算的重叠。

Worked Example: two draws without replacement例题：不放回抽两次

Using the bag in Figure 4.1 ($3$ red, $2$ blue, two draws without replacement), find (a) $P(\text{both the same color})$ and (b) $P(\text{at least one blue})$.

  1. Path products: $P(\text{RR})=\tfrac35\cdot\tfrac24=\tfrac{6}{20}=\tfrac{3}{10}$ and $P(\text{BB})=\tfrac25\cdot\tfrac14=\tfrac{2}{20}=\tfrac{1}{10}$.
  2. (a) “Same color” is RR or BB, two separate paths: $\tfrac{3}{10}+\tfrac{1}{10}=\tfrac{4}{10}=\tfrac25$.
  3. (b) “At least one blue” fails only on RR: $1-\tfrac{3}{10}=\tfrac{7}{10}$.
  4. Check by adding paths: RB $+$ BR $+$ BB $=\tfrac{3}{10}+\tfrac{3}{10}+\tfrac{1}{10}=\tfrac{7}{10}$.

Why it works: paths in a tree never overlap, so their probabilities add with no subtraction, and the complement replaces three paths with one.

用图 4.1 中的袋子（$3$ 红、$2$ 蓝，不放回抽两次），求 (a) $P(\text{both the same color})$（两球同色）和 (b) $P(\text{at least one blue})$（至少一个蓝球）。

  1. 路径乘积：$P(\text{RR})=\tfrac35\cdot\tfrac24=\tfrac{6}{20}=\tfrac{3}{10}$，$P(\text{BB})=\tfrac25\cdot\tfrac14=\tfrac{2}{20}=\tfrac{1}{10}$。
  2. (a) “同色”是 RR 或 BB 两条不同的（互斥的）路径：$\tfrac{3}{10}+\tfrac{1}{10}=\tfrac{4}{10}=\tfrac25$。
  3. (b) “至少一个蓝球”只在 RR 时不成立：$1-\tfrac{3}{10}=\tfrac{7}{10}$。
  4. 用路径相加检验：RB $+$ BR $+$ BB $=\tfrac{3}{10}+\tfrac{3}{10}+\tfrac{1}{10}=\tfrac{7}{10}$。

原理：树形图中的路径互不重叠，所以概率可以直接相加；补事件把三条路径换成一条。

Worked Example: independent trials and “at least one”例题：独立试验与“至少一次”

A fair six-sided die is rolled $3$ times. Find the probability of rolling at least one $6$. Give the exact fraction and a decimal to three places.

  1. The rolls are independent, and $P(\text{not }6)=\tfrac56$ on each roll.
  2. No $6$ in three rolls: $\left(\tfrac56\right)^3=\tfrac{125}{216}$.
  3. Complement: $1-\tfrac{125}{216}=\tfrac{91}{216}\approx 0.421$.

Why it works: “at least one” covers seven different patterns of sixes, but “none” is one pattern, and independence lets its three factors multiply. Note that the answer is not $3\times\tfrac16=\tfrac12$, which double counts rolls with two or three sixes.

一个均匀的六面骰子掷 $3$ 次。求至少掷出一次 $6$ 的概率，给出精确分数和保留三位的小数。

  1. 各次投掷相互独立，每次 $P(\text{not }6)=\tfrac56$。
  2. 三次都没有 $6$：$\left(\tfrac56\right)^3=\tfrac{125}{216}$。
  3. 补事件：$1-\tfrac{125}{216}=\tfrac{91}{216}\approx 0.421$。

原理：“至少一次”包含七种不同的出现六点的模式，而“一次也没有”只有一种模式，独立性让它的三个因子可以相乘。注意答案不是 $3\times\tfrac16=\tfrac12$，那样会重复计算出现两次或三次六点的情形。

Common Mistake: counting and probability
  • Using a combination when the positions are different jobs or places. If swapping two people gives a new outcome, order matters.
  • Keeping the same denominator on the second draw when the problem says “without replacement”.
  • Adding $P(A)+P(B)$ for “or” when $A$ and $B$ can happen together; subtract the overlap.
  • Multiplying probabilities of events that are not independent without adjusting the second factor.
常见错误：计数与概率
  • 位置是不同职务或名次时却用了组合。若交换两个人会得到新的结果，顺序就重要。
  • 题目说“不放回” (without replacement)，第二次抽取却仍用原来的分母。
  • $A$ 与 $B$ 可同时发生时，求“或”却直接 $P(A)+P(B)$；应减去重叠部分。
  • 事件不独立时直接把概率相乘，而没有调整第二个因子。

1. How many $4$-digit codes can be made from the digits $0$ to $9$ if digits may repeat? If they may not repeat?1. 用数字 $0$ 到 $9$ 组成 $4$ 位密码，数字可以重复时有多少个？不可以重复时呢？

With repeats, each of the $4$ places has $10$ choices: $10^4=10{,}000$. Without repeats: $10\times9\times8\times7=5040$ (order matters, so this is ${}_{10}P_{4}$).

可重复时，$4$ 个位置各有 $10$ 种选择：$10^4=10{,}000$。不可重复时：$10\times9\times8\times7=5040$（顺序重要，即 ${}_{10}P_{4}$）。

2. A pizza shop offers $5$ toppings. How many different $2$-topping pizzas are possible?2. 一家披萨店有 $5$ 种配料。含 $2$ 种配料的不同披萨有多少种？

顺序无关（蘑菇加橄榄与橄榄加蘑菇是同一种披萨）：${}_{5}C_{2}=\dfrac{5\times4}{2}=10$。

3. Two fair dice are rolled. What is the probability that the sum is $7$?3. 掷两个均匀骰子。点数之和为 $7$ 的概率是多少？

There are $6\times6=36$ equally likely ordered outcomes. Sum $7$: $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$, which is $6$ outcomes. $P=\tfrac{6}{36}=\tfrac16$.

$P(\text{no red})=0.7\times0.7=0.49$，所以 $P(\text{at least one red})=1-0.49=0.51$。直接 $0.3+0.3=0.6$ 是错的，因为红红被算了两次：$0.6-0.3\times0.3=0.51$，结果一致。

Conditional probability: “given that”条件概率：“已知”

Conditional probability $P(A\mid B)$ is the probability of $A$ given that $B$ is known to have happened. The condition shrinks the group you are choosing from to the $B$ row or column, so the denominator is that row or column total, not the grand total: $P(A\mid B)=\dfrac{P(A\text{ and }B)}{P(B)}=\dfrac{\text{count in both}}{\text{count in }B}$. The order matters. $P(\text{drama}\mid\text{junior})$ asks what fraction of juniors are in drama; $P(\text{junior}\mid\text{drama})$ asks what fraction of drama members are juniors. They have different denominators, so they are usually different numbers.

条件概率 (conditional probability) $P(A\mid B)$ 是在已知 $B$ 已发生 (given that) 的情况下 $A$ 的概率。条件把可选的人群缩小到 $B$ 所在的行或列，所以分母是该行或列的合计，而不是总数：$P(A\mid B)=\dfrac{P(A\text{ and }B)}{P(B)}=\dfrac{\text{count in both}}{\text{count in }B}$。顺序很重要：$P(\text{drama}\mid\text{junior})$ 问的是11 年级学生中有多大比例在戏剧社；$P(\text{junior}\mid\text{drama})$ 问的是戏剧社成员中有多大比例是11 年级学生。两者分母不同，所以通常是不同的数。

Testing for independence检验独立性

Events $A$ and $B$ are independent exactly when knowing $B$ does not change the chance of $A$: $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$, which is the same as $P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\,P(B)$. In the table, $P(\text{robotics})=\tfrac{50}{200}=0.25$ and $P(\text{robotics}\mid\text{senior})=\tfrac{20}{80}=0.25$, so robotics membership and being a senior are independent. 事件 $A$ 与 $B$ 独立，当且仅当知道 $B$ 不改变 $A$ 的机会：$P(A\mid B)=P(A)$，等价于 $P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\,P(B)$。在表中，$P(\text{robotics})=\tfrac{50}{200}=0.25$，$P(\text{robotics}\mid\text{senior})=\tfrac{20}{80}=0.25$，所以参加机器人社与是12 年级学生相互独立。而 $P(\text{drama}\mid\text{senior})=\tfrac{20}{80}=0.25$，$P(\text{drama})=0.35$，所以参加戏剧社与是12 年级学生不独立。

Expected value期望值

The expected value of a numerical outcome is the probability-weighted average of its values: $E(X)=\sum x\,P(x)$. It is the long-run average per trial if the experiment is repeated many times, so it need not be a value that can actually occur. A game is fair when the expected net gain is $0$; a negative expected value means the player loses money on average.

Worked Example: expected value of a raffle ticket例题：抽奖券的期望值

A school sells $500$ raffle tickets at $2$ dollars each. One ticket wins $300$ dollars and two tickets win $50$ dollars each. What is the expected net gain for a person who buys one ticket?

  1. Prize values and probabilities: $300$ dollars with $P=\tfrac{1}{500}$, $50$ dollars with $P=\tfrac{2}{500}$, $0$ dollars with $P=\tfrac{497}{500}$.
  2. Expected winnings: $300\cdot\tfrac{1}{500}+50\cdot\tfrac{2}{500}+0=\tfrac{300+100}{500}=\tfrac{400}{500}=0.80$ dollars.
  3. Subtract the ticket price: $0.80-2=-1.20$ dollars.

Why it works: over all $500$ tickets the school pays out $400$ dollars and collects $1000$ dollars, a loss to buyers of $600$ dollars, which is $\tfrac{600}{500}=1.20$ dollars per ticket.

Worked Example: expected value from a distribution table例题：由分布表求期望值

  2. Multiply each value by its probability: $0(0.2)+1(0.4)+2(0.3)+3(0.1)=0+0.4+0.6+0.3$.
  3. Add: $E(X)=1.3$ siblings.

Why it works: $E(X)$ is a weighted average with the probabilities as weights. No student has $1.3$ siblings; the value means that over many randomly chosen students, the average number of siblings is about $1.3$.

原理：$E(X)$ 是以概率为权重的加权平均。没有学生有 $1.3$ 个兄弟姐妹；它表示在大量随机选出的学生中，平均兄弟姐妹人数约为 $1.3$。

Measures of center: mean, median and mode集中趋势：平均数、中位数与众数

The mean is the sum of the values divided by how many there are. The median is the middle value once the data are sorted; with an even count it is the mean of the two middle values. The mode is the most frequent value (a set can have none, one, or several). The mean uses every value's size, so a few large values drag it toward them; the median only uses position, so it resists outliers. In a right-skewed set (a long tail of large values) the mean is usually greater than the median, as in Figure 4.2.

平均数 (mean) 是各值之和除以个数。中位数 (median) 是排序后位于中间的值；个数为偶数时取中间两个值的平均。众数 (mode) 是出现次数最多的值（可能没有、有一个或有多个）。平均数用到每个值的大小，所以少数很大的值会把它拉过去；中位数只用到位置，所以不受离群值 (outlier) 影响。在右偏的数据中（大值形成长尾），平均数通常大于中位数，如图 4.2 所示。

For the data in Figure 4.2, find the mean, median, mode and range. Then the value $10$ is replaced by $30$. Which of the four measures change, and to what?

Spread: range and standard deviation离散程度：极差与标准差

The range (maximum minus minimum) uses only the two extremes. The standard deviation measures the typical distance of the values from the mean: find each deviation $x-\bar{x}$, square it, average the squares, then take the square root. For $2,4,4,4,5,5,7,9$ the mean is $5$, the squared deviations are $9,1,1,1,0,0,4,16$ with sum $32$, and $\sqrt{32/8}=2$. (A sample standard deviation divides by $n-1$ instead, giving $\sqrt{32/7}\approx2.14$; many calculators report both.) Most questions only ask you to compare: values bunched near the mean give a small standard deviation, values far from the mean give a large one, and a set of identical values has standard deviation $0$.

极差 (range)（最大值减最小值）只用到两个极端值。标准差 (standard deviation) 衡量各值与平均数的典型距离：求出每个偏差 $x-\bar{x}$，平方，对平方求平均，再开平方。对 $2,4,4,4,5,5,7,9$，平均数为 $5$，偏差平方为 $9,1,1,1,0,0,4,16$，和为 $32$，$\sqrt{32/8}=2$。（样本标准差改为除以 $n-1$，得 $\sqrt{32/7}\approx2.14$；许多计算器两个都会给出。）多数题目只要求比较：数值集中在平均数附近，标准差小；远离平均数，标准差大；所有值都相同时，标准差为 $0$。

Add $c$ to every value: mean, median and mode increase by $c$; range and standard deviation do not change.
Multiply every value by $k>0$: mean, median, range and standard deviation are all multiplied by $k$.

每个值都加 $c$：平均数、中位数、众数都增加 $c$；极差和标准差不变。

Worked Example: curving test scores例题：调整考试分数

A class's test scores have mean $72$ and standard deviation $8$. (a) The teacher adds $5$ points to every score. (b) Instead, the teacher multiplies every score by $1.1$. Find the new mean and standard deviation in each case.

  1. (a) Adding $5$ moves every score, and so the center, up by $5$: new mean $72+5=77$.
  2. Every distance from the mean is unchanged, so the standard deviation stays $8$.
  3. (b) Multiplying by $1.1$ scales the center: new mean $72\times1.1=79.2$.
  4. It also stretches every distance from the mean by $1.1$: new standard deviation $8\times1.1=8.8$.

Why it works: standard deviation is built from distances between values, and adding a constant does not change a distance, while multiplying does.

  2. 每个分数到平均数的距离不变，所以标准差仍为 $8$。
  3. (b) 乘以 $1.1$ 会缩放中心：新平均数 $72\times1.1=79.2$。
  4. 它也把每个到平均数的距离拉伸 $1.1$ 倍：新标准差 $8\times1.1=8.8$。

原理：标准差由数值之间的距离构成；加常数不改变距离，乘以常数则会改变。

Scatterplots, correlation and the line of best fit散点图、相关性与最佳拟合直线

A scatterplot shows paired data $(x,y)$ as points. Describe it by direction (positive: $y$ tends to rise as $x$ rises; negative: $y$ tends to fall), form (linear or curved) and strength (how tightly the points follow the pattern). The correlation coefficient $r$, between $-1$ and $1$, measures the strength and direction of a linear association: near $\pm1$ is strong, near $0$ is weak. Correlation does not show causation; a third variable can drive both.

散点图 (scatterplot) 把成对数据 $(x,y)$ 画成点。描述时说明方向（正：$x$ 增大时 $y$ 倾向于增大；负：$y$ 倾向于减小）、形式（线性或弯曲）和强弱（点贴近趋势的程度）。相关系数 (correlation) $r$ 介于 $-1$ 与 $1$ 之间，衡量线性关联的强弱和方向：接近 $\pm1$ 为强，接近 $0$ 为弱。相关不等于因果；可能有第三个变量同时影响两者。

A line of best fit runs through the middle of the points; a calculator's linear regression gives the least-squares line. Its slope is the predicted change in $y$ for each one-unit increase in $x$, and its $y$-intercept is the predicted $y$ when $x=0$ (meaningful only if $x=0$ makes sense). A residual is actual minus predicted. Predicting far outside the observed $x$ values (extrapolation) is unreliable.

最佳拟合直线 (line of best fit) 穿过点群的中间；计算器的线性回归给出最小二乘直线。其斜率是 $x$ 每增加一个单位时 $y$ 的预测变化量；$y$ 截距是 $x=0$ 时的预测值 $y$（只有当 $x=0$ 有意义时才有意义）。残差 (residual) 等于实际值减预测值。在观测到的 $x$ 范围之外很远处做预测（外推）是不可靠的。

Ratios and rates比与速率

A ratio compares two quantities of the same kind, such as $3$ boys to $5$ girls, written $3:5$ or $\tfrac35$. Read the order carefully: part-to-part ($3:5$) is different from part-to-whole ($3:8$, because $3+5=8$ parts make the whole). A rate compares quantities with different units, such as $270$ miles in $4.5$ hours. A unit rate is the rate per one unit of the second quantity: $\tfrac{270}{4.5}=60$ miles per hour. Unit rates make comparisons fair, because both options are measured against the same “one”.

比 (ratio) 比较两个同类的量，例如 $3$ 个男生比 $5$ 个女生，写作 $3:5$ 或 $\tfrac35$。注意顺序：部分比部分（$3:5$）不同于部分比整体（$3:8$，因为 $3+5=8$ 份构成整体）。速率 (rate) 比较单位不同的量，例如 $4.5$ 小时行驶 $270$ 英里。单位速率 (unit rate) 是相对于第二个量的每一个单位的速率：$\tfrac{270}{4.5}=60$ 英里每小时。单位速率让比较更公平，因为两个选项都以同一个“一”为基准。

Worked Example: splitting a total in a ratio例题：按比分配总量

A class of $32$ students has boys and girls in the ratio $3:5$. (a) How many boys and girls are there? (b) If $4$ more boys join, what is the new ratio of boys to girls in simplest form?

  1. (a) Total parts: $3+5=8$. One part is $\tfrac{32}{8}=4$ students.
  2. Boys: $3\times4=12$. Girls: $5\times4=20$. Check: $12+20=32$.
  3. (b) Boys become $12+4=16$; girls stay $20$.
  4. New ratio: $16:20$; divide both by $4$: $4:5$.

Why it works: a ratio $3:5$ says the whole is made of $8$ equal parts, so finding the size of one part turns the ratio into actual counts.

原理：比 $3:5$ 表示整体由 $8$ 个相等的份组成，求出每份的大小，就能把比变成实际人数。

Proportions and proportional relationships比例与正比例关系

A proportion states that two ratios are equal, $\tfrac{a}{b}=\tfrac{c}{d}$, and is solved by cross-multiplying: $ad=bc$. Two quantities are in a proportional relationship when $y=kx$ for a constant $k$: doubling $x$ doubles $y$, the ratio $\tfrac{y}{x}$ is always $k$, and the graph is a straight line through the origin whose slope $k$ is the unit rate. A relationship with a fixed fee, such as $y=1.2x+2$, is linear but not proportional, because $\tfrac{y}{x}$ changes and the line misses the origin (Figure 4.4).

比例 (proportion) 表示两个比相等，$\tfrac{a}{b}=\tfrac{c}{d}$，用交叉相乘求解：$ad=bc$。若存在常数 $k$ 使 $y=kx$，两个量成正比例关系 (proportional relationship)：$x$ 加倍则 $y$ 加倍，比值 $\tfrac{y}{x}$ 始终为 $k$，图像是过原点的直线，其斜率 $k$ 就是单位速率。含固定费用的关系，如 $y=1.2x+2$，是线性的但不成正比例，因为 $\tfrac{y}{x}$ 会变化，直线也不过原点（图 4.4）。

Unit conversion单位换算

To convert units, multiply by fractions that equal $1$, such as $\tfrac{5280\text{ ft}}{1\text{ mi}}$ or $\tfrac{1\text{ h}}{3600\text{ s}}$, arranged so the unwanted units cancel. Writing the units in every factor is the check: if they do not cancel to the units you want, a fraction is upside down.

换算单位时，乘以等于 $1$ 的分数，如 $\tfrac{5280\text{ ft}}{1\text{ mi}}$ 或 $\tfrac{1\text{ h}}{3600\text{ s}}$，并安排好使不需要的单位约去。在每个因子中都写上单位就是检验：若约不出想要的单位，说明某个分数倒置了。

Worked Example: a map scale and a speed conversion例题：地图比例尺与速度换算

(a) On a map, $1.5$ inches represents $20$ miles. Two towns are $4.2$ inches apart on the map. How far apart are they? (b) Convert $60$ miles per hour to feet per second ($1$ mile $=5280$ feet).

  2. Cross-multiply: $1.5d=4.2\times20=84$, so $d=\tfrac{84}{1.5}=56$ miles.
  3. (b) $60\,\dfrac{\text{mi}}{\text{h}}\times\dfrac{5280\text{ ft}}{1\text{ mi}}\times\dfrac{1\text{ h}}{3600\text{ s}}$.
  4. Miles and hours cancel: $\dfrac{60\times5280}{3600}=\dfrac{316800}{3600}=88$ feet per second.

Why it works: in (a) the scale ratio is the same everywhere on the map; in (b) each conversion fraction equals $1$, so the speed is unchanged while its units change.

Percents, percent change and interest百分数、百分比变化与利息

“$p$ percent of $W$” means $\tfrac{p}{100}\times W$, so $15\%$ of $80$ is $0.15\times80=12$. The percent change compares the change with the original value: $\text{percent change}=\dfrac{\text{new}-\text{original}}{\text{original}}\times100\%$. The fastest tool is the multiplier: an increase of $r\%$ multiplies by $1+\tfrac{r}{100}$ and a decrease multiplies by $1-\tfrac{r}{100}$. Successive changes multiply, they do not add: up $20\%$ then down $20\%$ gives $1.2\times0.8=0.96$, a $4\%$ decrease overall. A discount is a percent decrease and sales tax a percent increase. Simple interest is $I=Prt$ (rate per year, $t$ in years); interest compounded once a year gives $A=P(1+r)^t$.

Worked Example: discount, tax and a reverse percent例题：折扣、税与逆向百分数

(a) A jacket marked $80$ dollars is $25\%$ off, and $8\%$ sales tax is added to the sale price. What is the final cost? (b) After a $15\%$ increase, a ticket costs $92$ dollars. What was the original price?

  1. (a) Discount multiplier $1-0.25=0.75$; tax multiplier $1+0.08=1.08$.
  2. Final cost: $80\times0.75\times1.08=60\times1.08=64.80$ dollars.
  3. (b) The new price is the original times $1.15$: $1.15x=92$.
  4. Divide: $x=\tfrac{92}{1.15}=80$ dollars. Check: $80\times1.15=92$.

Why it works: each percent change is a multiplication, so a chain of changes is a product, and undoing a change means dividing by its multiplier, not subtracting $15\%$ of the new price (which would give $78.20$ dollars, the wrong answer).

(a) 标价 $80$ 美元的夹克降价 $25\%$，并在折后价上加 $8\%$ 销售税。最终价格是多少？(b) 涨价 $15\%$ 后一张票为 $92$ 美元。原价是多少？

  1. (a) 折扣乘数 $1-0.25=0.75$；税的乘数 $1+0.08=1.08$。
  2. 最终价格：$80\times0.75\times1.08=60\times1.08=64.80$ 美元。
  3. (b) 新价格等于原价乘以 $1.15$：$1.15x=92$。
  4. 相除：$x=\tfrac{92}{1.15}=80$ 美元。检验：$80\times1.15=92$。

原理：每个百分比变化都是一次乘法，所以一连串变化是乘积；撤销一个变化要除以它的乘数，而不是从新价格中减去 $15\%$（那样得 $78.20$ 美元，是错误答案）。

Worked Example: percent change and interest例题：百分比变化与利息

(a) A town's population grows from $1250$ to $1400$. Find the percent change. (b) $2000$ dollars is invested at $3\%$ per year for $4$ years. Find the balance with simple interest and with interest compounded annually, to the nearest cent.

  1. (a) Change: $1400-1250=150$. Divide by the original: $\tfrac{150}{1250}=0.12$, a $12\%$ increase.
  2. (b) Simple: $I=2000\times0.03\times4=240$, balance $2240$ dollars.
  3. Compound: $A=2000(1.03)^4=2000\times1.12550881=2251.02$ dollars (to the nearest cent).
  4. Compound interest earns $2251.02-2240=11.02$ dollars more, because each year's interest is computed on a larger balance.

Why it works: percent change always divides by the starting value; compounding multiplies by $1.03$ once per year, so the growth is exponential rather than linear.

  2. (b) 单利：$I=2000\times0.03\times4=240$，余额 $2240$ 美元。
  3. 复利：$A=2000(1.03)^4=2000\times1.12550881=2251.02$ 美元（精确到分）。
  4. 复利多得 $2251.02-2240=11.02$ 美元，因为每年的利息都按更大的余额计算。

The average and the sum of the values平均数与各值之和

The average (arithmetic mean) is $\bar{x}=\dfrac{\text{sum of the values}}{\text{number of values}}$. Rearranged, $\text{sum of the values}=\bar{x}\times n$. This second form is the key to most average problems: averages cannot be added or subtracted directly, but sums can. To find a missing value, compute the sum the target average requires, subtract the sum you already have, and what is left is the missing value.

平均数 (average，算术平均) 为 $\bar{x}=\dfrac{\text{sum of the values}}{\text{number of values}}$。变形得 $\text{sum of the values}=\bar{x}\times n$（各值之和 = 平均数 × 个数）。第二种形式是解决大多数平均数问题的关键：平均数不能直接相加减，但总和可以。求缺失值 (missing value) 时，先算出目标平均数所需的总和，减去已有的总和，剩下的就是缺失值。

Worked Example: the score needed on the next test例题：下一次考试需要的分数

A student's average on $4$ tests is $85$. What score on the $5$th test makes the average of all $5$ tests exactly $87$?

  1. Current sum: $85\times4=340$.
  2. Required sum for an average of $87$ over $5$ tests: $87\times5=435$.
  3. Missing value: $435-340=95$.
  4. Check: $\tfrac{340+95}{5}=\tfrac{435}{5}=87$.

Why it works: the new score must supply the whole gap between the sums; equivalently, it must be $87$ plus $2$ points for each of the $4$ earlier tests that were $2$ below $87$: $87+4\times2=95$.

某学生 $4$ 次考试的平均分为 $85$。第 $5$ 次考多少分，才能使 $5$ 次的平均分恰好为 $87$？

  1. 现有总和：$85\times4=340$。
  2. $5$ 次平均 $87$ 所需总和：$87\times5=435$。
  3. 缺失值：$435-340=95$。
  4. 检验：$\tfrac{340+95}{5}=\tfrac{435}{5}=87$。

原理：新分数必须补上两个总和之间的全部差距；等价地说，它必须等于 $87$，再为之前每次比 $87$ 低 $2$ 分的 $4$ 次考试各补 $2$ 分：$87+4\times2=95$。

Weighted averages and average speed加权平均数与平均速度

A weighted average gives each value a weight: $\bar{x}=\dfrac{w_1x_1+w_2x_2+\dots}{w_1+w_2+\dots}$. When the weights are percentages that add to $100\%$, the denominator is $1$ and you simply add the products. Combining two groups is a weighted average by group size: a class of $20$ averaging $80$ and a class of $30$ averaging $90$ have a combined average of $\tfrac{20(80)+30(90)}{50}=\tfrac{4300}{50}=86$, not $85$, because the larger class pulls harder. Average speed is also a weighted idea: it is total distance divided by total time, not the average of the speeds.

加权平均数 (weighted average) 给每个值一个权重：$\bar{x}=\dfrac{w_1x_1+w_2x_2+\dots}{w_1+w_2+\dots}$。若权重是总和为 $100\%$ 的百分数，分母为 $1$，直接把各乘积相加即可。合并两组就是按人数加权：$20$ 人平均 $80$ 分的班与 $30$ 人平均 $90$ 分的班，合并平均为 $\tfrac{20(80)+30(90)}{50}=\tfrac{4300}{50}=86$，而不是 $85$，因为人数多的班影响更大。平均速度也是加权的概念：它是总路程除以总时间，而不是各速度的平均。

Worked Example: a weighted course grade例题：加权课程成绩

A course grade is $20\%$ homework, $50\%$ tests and $30\%$ final exam. A student has $92$ on homework, $78$ on tests and $85$ on the final. What is the course grade?

  1. Convert the weights to decimals: $0.20$, $0.50$, $0.30$ (they add to $1$).
  2. Multiply: $0.20(92)=18.4$, $0.50(78)=39$, $0.30(85)=25.5$.
  3. Add: $18.4+39+25.5=82.9$.

Why it works: each category contributes its share of the total. The unweighted mean $\tfrac{92+78+85}{3}=85$ is too high because the lowest score, tests, carries half the weight.

某课程成绩中作业占 $20\%$、测验占 $50\%$、期末考试占 $30\%$。某学生作业 $92$ 分、测验 $78$ 分、期末 $85$ 分。课程成绩是多少？

  1. 把权重化为小数：$0.20$、$0.50$、$0.30$（和为 $1$）。
  2. 相乘：$0.20(92)=18.4$，$0.50(78)=39$，$0.30(85)=25.5$。
  3. 相加：$18.4+39+25.5=82.9$。

原理：每个类别按其份额贡献总成绩。不加权的平均 $\tfrac{92+78+85}{3}=85$ 偏高，因为最低的测验分数占了一半的权重。

Worked Example: average speed for a round trip例题：往返的平均速度

A cyclist rides $60$ km to a lake at $30$ km/h and returns the same $60$ km at $60$ km/h. What is the average speed for the whole trip?

  1. Time out: $\tfrac{60}{30}=2$ h. Time back: $\tfrac{60}{60}=1$ h.
  2. Total distance: $60+60=120$ km. Total time: $2+1=3$ h.
  3. Average speed: $\tfrac{120}{3}=40$ km/h.

Why it works: the cyclist spends twice as long at the slower speed, so the slower speed gets twice the weight; $\tfrac{30+60}{2}=45$ would treat the two legs as equally long in time.

一名骑行者以 $30$ km/h 骑 $60$ km 到湖边，再以 $60$ km/h 沿原路返回 $60$ km。全程平均速度是多少？

  1. 去程时间：$\tfrac{60}{30}=2$ h；回程时间：$\tfrac{60}{60}=1$ h。
  2. 总路程：$60+60=120$ km；总时间：$2+1=3$ h。
  3. 平均速度：$\tfrac{120}{3}=40$ km/h。

原理：骑行者以较慢速度骑行的时间是两倍，所以慢速的权重也是两倍；$\tfrac{30+60}{2}=45$ 相当于把两段的时间当成一样长。

Fractions, decimals and percents分数、小数与百分数

The same number can be written as a fraction, a decimal or a percent. Fraction to decimal: divide the numerator by the denominator ($\tfrac38=0.375$). Decimal to percent: multiply by $100$ ($0.375=37.5\%$). Percent to fraction: write over $100$ and simplify ($35\%=\tfrac{35}{100}=\tfrac{7}{20}$). Some fractions give repeating decimals: $\tfrac23=0.666\ldots$, which is about $0.667$. To order numbers given in mixed forms, convert all of them to decimals first (Figure 4.5). Very large and very small numbers use scientific notation $a\times10^n$ with $1\le a\lt10$: $4.5\times10^{-3}=0.0045$ and $3.2\times10^{5}=320{,}000$.

同一个数可以写成分数 (fraction)、小数 (decimal) 或百分数 (percent)。分数化小数：分子除以分母（$\tfrac38=0.375$）。小数化百分数：乘以 $100$（$0.375=37.5\%$）。百分数化分数：写成分母为 $100$ 的分数再化简（$35\%=\tfrac{35}{100}=\tfrac{7}{20}$）。有些分数是循环小数：$\tfrac23=0.666\ldots$，约为 $0.667$。给混合形式的数排序时，先全部化为小数（图 4.5）。很大和很小的数用科学记数法 $a\times10^n$ 表示，其中 $1\le a\lt10$：$4.5\times10^{-3}=0.0045$，$3.2\times10^{5}=320{,}000$。

Estimation估算

Estimation means rounding to friendly numbers to predict the size of an answer. It catches keying errors (a misplaced decimal point changes the estimate by a factor of $10$) and can rule out answer choices with no calculation. For example, $0.498\times61.2\approx0.5\times60=30$; the exact value is $30.4776$, so a calculator display of $304.776$ means a slipped decimal point.

估算 (estimation) 就是把数凑成好算的数来预测答案的大小。它能发现按键错误（小数点错一位会使估计值相差 $10$ 倍），也能在不计算的情况下排除选项。例如 $0.498\times61.2\approx0.5\times60=30$；精确值是 $30.4776$，所以若计算器显示 $304.776$，说明小数点错位了。

Worked Example: order mixed forms and estimate例题：给混合形式排序并估算

(a) Order from least to greatest: $\tfrac23$, $0.6$, $62\%$, $\tfrac58$. (b) Estimate $19.8\%$ of $401$, then compare with the exact value.

  1. (a) Convert to decimals: $\tfrac23\approx0.667$, $0.6$, $62\%=0.62$, $\tfrac58=0.625$.
  2. Order: $0.6\lt0.62\lt0.625\lt0.667$, that is $0.6$, $62\%$, $\tfrac58$, $\tfrac23$.
  3. (b) Round: $19.8\%\approx20\%=0.2$ and $401\approx400$, so the estimate is $0.2\times400=80$.
  4. Exact: $0.198\times401=79.398$, close to $80$ as expected.

Why it works: decimals put every number on the same scale, and rounding changed the factors by only about $1\%$ and $0.25\%$, so the estimate stays within about $1\%$ of the truth.

(a) 从小到大排序：$\tfrac23$，$0.6$，$62\%$，$\tfrac58$。(b) 估算 $401$ 的 $19.8\%$，再与精确值比较。

  1. (a) 化为小数：$\tfrac23\approx0.667$，$0.6$，$62\%=0.62$，$\tfrac58=0.625$。
  2. 顺序：$0.6\lt0.62\lt0.625\lt0.667$，即 $0.6$，$62\%$，$\tfrac58$，$\tfrac23$。
  3. (b) 取整：$19.8\%\approx20\%=0.2$，$401\approx400$，估计值为 $0.2\times400=80$。
  4. 精确值：$0.198\times401=79.398$，与 $80$ 很接近，符合预期。

原理：小数把所有数放在同一尺度上；取整只使两个因子分别改变约 $1\%$ 和 $0.25\%$，所以估计值与真实值相差约 $1\%$ 以内。

From word problem to model从文字题到模型

A model is a mathematical description of a real situation: an expression, equation, inequality, function, table or graph. ACT describes Modeling as questions that involve producing, interpreting, understanding, evaluating and improving models, and it counts across all the categories rather than as a separate topic. A reliable routine for any word problem has five steps: (1) list the quantities and their units; (2) define a variable in words, such as “let $m$ be the number of months”; (3) write the relationship; (4) solve; (5) interpret the answer in the context and check that it is sensible (a whole number of people, a positive time, a value inside the data range).

模型 (model) 是对真实情境的数学描述：表达式、方程、不等式、函数、表格或图像。ACT 把 Modeling（建模）描述为涉及建立、解释、理解、评价和改进模型的题目，它贯穿所有类别，而不是一个单独的主题。处理任何文字题 (word problem) 都可以用五步法：(1) 列出各个量及其单位；(2) 用文字定义变量 (variable)，例如“设 $m$ 为月数”；(3) 写出关系式；(4) 求解；(5) 结合情境解释 (interpret) 答案并检查是否合理（人数为整数、时间为正、取值在数据范围内）。

Linear models: rate and starting value线性模型：变化率与初始值

When a quantity changes by the same amount for each unit of input, a linear model $y=b+mx$ fits: $b$ is the starting value (often a fixed fee) and $m$ is the rate of change, with units such as dollars per month. Comparing two linear models means finding where they are equal, the break-even point, and then reading which one is lower on each side (Figure 4.6).

当输入每增加一个单位，某个量的变化量都相同时，适合用线性模型 $y=b+mx$：$b$ 是初始值（常为固定费用），$m$ 是变化率，单位如“美元每月”。比较两个线性模型，就是找出它们相等的地方，即持平点 (break-even point)，再读出在其两侧哪个更低（图 4.6）。

Worked Example: build and compare two cost models例题：建立并比较两个费用模型

Gym A charges a $50$-dollar joining fee plus $25$ dollars per month. Gym B charges $35$ dollars per month and no fee. Write a model for each, find when they cost the same, and state which is cheaper for a $12$-month membership.

  1. Variable: let $m$ be the number of months and $C$ the total cost in dollars.
  2. Models: Gym A $C=50+25m$; Gym B $C=35m$.
  3. Equal cost: $50+25m=35m\Rightarrow 50=10m\Rightarrow m=5$ months, when both cost $35(5)=175$ dollars.
  4. For $12$ months: A costs $50+25(12)=350$ dollars and B costs $35(12)=420$ dollars, so A is cheaper by $70$ dollars.

Why it works: B starts lower but rises $10$ dollars per month faster, so it takes $\tfrac{50}{10}=5$ months for B to use up its $50$-dollar head start; after that A is cheaper.

  1. 变量：设 $m$ 为月数，$C$ 为总费用（美元）。
  2. 模型：A 为 $C=50+25m$；B 为 $C=35m$。
  3. 费用相等：$50+25m=35m\Rightarrow 50=10m\Rightarrow m=5$ 个月，此时两者都是 $35(5)=175$ 美元。
  4. $12$ 个月时：A 为 $50+25(12)=350$ 美元，B 为 $35(12)=420$ 美元，所以 A 便宜 $70$ 美元。

原理：B 起点较低，但每月多涨 $10$ 美元，所以需要 $\tfrac{50}{10}=5$ 个月才能用完它 $50$ 美元的领先；此后 A 更便宜。

Linear or exponential?线性还是指数？

Look at equal steps in the input. If the outputs have a constant difference, the model is linear. If they have a constant ratio, the model is exponential, $y=a\cdot b^x$, where $a$ is the starting value and $b$ is the growth factor; a growth of $r\%$ per step means $b=1+\tfrac{r}{100}$. Percent language (“grows $10\%$ per year”) signals exponential; “grows by $80$ per year” signals linear.

观察输入等间隔变化时的输出。若输出的差恒定，模型是线性的；若输出的比恒定，模型是指数型的，$y=a\cdot b^x$，其中 $a$ 是初始值，$b$ 是增长因子；每步增长 $r\%$ 意味着 $b=1+\tfrac{r}{100}$。百分数的说法（“每年增长 $10\%$”）提示指数模型；“每年增加 $80$”提示线性模型。

Worked Example: choose, use and evaluate a model from a table例题：由表格选择、使用并评价模型

A fictional town's number of e-bike riders is $800$ in year $0$, $880$ in year $1$, $968$ in year $2$ and $1064.8$ in year $3$ (values from a smoothed estimate). Find a model, predict year $5$, and comment on using it for year $50$.

  1. Differences: $80$, $88$, $96.8$, not constant, so not linear.
  2. Ratios: $\tfrac{880}{800}=1.1$, $\tfrac{968}{880}=1.1$, $\tfrac{1064.8}{968}=1.1$, constant, so exponential with $a=800$, $b=1.1$: $R=800(1.1)^t$, which is $10\%$ growth per year.
  3. Year $5$: $800(1.1)^5=800\times1.61051\approx1288$ riders.
  4. Year $50$: $800(1.1)^{50}\approx93{,}900$ riders, far beyond what a small town could hold. Growth like this cannot continue, so the model should only be used near the data, or improved with a model that levels off.

Why it works: a constant ratio means each year multiplies by the same factor, which is the definition of exponential growth; evaluating the model means testing its predictions against what is physically possible.

  2. 比：$\tfrac{880}{800}=1.1$，$\tfrac{968}{880}=1.1$，$\tfrac{1064.8}{968}=1.1$，恒定，所以是指数模型，$a=800$，$b=1.1$：$R=800(1.1)^t$，即每年增长 $10\%$。
  3. 第 $5$ 年：$800(1.1)^5=800\times1.61051\approx1288$ 人。
  4. 第 $50$ 年：$800(1.1)^{50}\approx93{,}900$ 人，远超一个小镇所能容纳的人数。这样的增长不可能持续，所以该模型只应在数据附近使用，或改用会趋于平稳的模型。

原理：比值恒定意味着每年都乘以同一个因子，这正是指数增长的定义；评价模型就是检验其预测是否符合现实可能。

Inequality models and sensible answers不等式模型与合理的答案

Words such as “at most”, “no more than” and “within a budget” lead to $\le$; “at least” and “minimum” lead to $\ge$. After solving, round in the direction the context forces: a budget limit on people rounds down, a requirement to carry everyone rounds up.

“至多” (at most)、“不超过”和“在预算之内”等字眼对应 $\le$；“至少” (at least) 和“最少”对应 $\ge$。求解后，按情境要求的方向取整：预算限制下的人数向下取整，要求运送所有人时向上取整。

Worked Example: a budget inequality例题：预算不等式

A club has a budget of $400$ dollars for a trip. The bus costs $150$ dollars plus $12$ dollars per student for entry tickets. What is the greatest number of students who can go?

  1. Variable: let $n$ be the number of students.
  2. Model: $150+12n\le400$.
  3. Solve: $12n\le250$, so $n\le\tfrac{250}{12}\approx20.83$.
  4. Interpret: $n$ must be a whole number and the budget cannot be exceeded, so round down: at most $20$ students. Check: $150+12(20)=390\le400$, while $21$ students would cost $402$ dollars.

Why it works: the inequality keeps every allowed value, and the context (whole students, a hard limit) decides which way to round.

某社团出游预算为 $400$ 美元。包车 $150$ 美元，另外每名学生门票 $12$ 美元。最多能去多少名学生？

  1. 变量：设 $n$ 为学生人数。
  2. 模型：$150+12n\le400$。
  3. 求解：$12n\le250$，所以 $n\le\tfrac{250}{12}\approx20.83$。
  4. 解释：$n$ 必须是整数且不能超出预算，所以向下取整：最多 $20$ 名学生。检验：$150+12(20)=390\le400$，而 $21$ 名学生需 $402$ 美元。

原理：不等式保留了所有允许的值，而情境（人数为整数、硬性上限）决定了取整的方向。

Common Mistake: modeling
  • Using a variable without defining it, then answering for the wrong quantity.
  • Choosing a linear model for “increases by $10\%$ each year”; a constant percent is exponential.
  • Rounding $20.83$ students up to $21$ when the budget is a hard limit.
  • Trusting a model's prediction far outside the data it was built from.
常见错误：建模
  • 使用变量却没有定义，最后回答了错误的量。
  • 对“每年增长 $10\%$”选用线性模型；恒定百分比是指数型的。
  • 预算是硬性上限时，把 $20.83$ 名学生向上取整为 $21$。
  • 相信模型在其所依据的数据范围之外很远处的预测。

$3.5$ is the fixed starting charge in dollars (the fare at $d=0$); $2.25$ is the cost in dollars per mile. For $8$ miles: $3.5+2.25(8)=3.5+18=21.5$ dollars.

1. A coach must choose $4$ of $9$ swimmers to form a relay squad, without assigning positions. How many different squads are possible?

Order does not matter, so use combinations: ${}_{9}C_{4}=\dfrac{9\times8\times7\times6}{4!}=\dfrac{3024}{24}=126$. $3024$ is ${}_{9}P_{4}$, which counts orders; $36$ is ${}_{9}C_{2}$; $24$ is $4!$.

2. A bag holds $4$ green and $6$ yellow tokens. Two tokens are drawn at random without replacement. What is the probability that both are green?

The condition is “senior”, so the denominator is $25$: $\frac{15}{25}=\frac{3}{5}$. $\frac{1}{4}=\frac{15}{60}$ uses the grand total; $\frac{5}{12}=\frac{25}{60}$ is the chance of choosing a senior; $\frac{2}{5}$ is the chance the senior does not drive.

Expected winnings: $8(0.25)+0(0.75)=2$. Net: $2-3=-1$ dollar per game. $2$ forgets the cost; $5=8-3$ ignores the probability; $-3$ ignores the prize.

The sum must be $12\times5=60$. The known values add to $8+10+15+20=53$, so $x=60-53=7$. $13.25=\frac{53}{4}$ is the mean of the four known values; $53$ is their sum.

6. A data set has mean $20$ and standard deviation $3$. If $4$ is added to every value, what are the new mean and standard deviation?

Adding $4$ moves every value and the mean up by $4$, to $24$, but leaves every distance from the mean unchanged, so the standard deviation stays $3$. Mean $80$ and standard deviation $12$ would come from multiplying by $4$.
