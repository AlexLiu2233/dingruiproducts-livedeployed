Q1EASY 14.1 Wave Pulses14.1 波脉冲No Calculator

A transverse pulse travels along a long, taut string. Which statement about the pulse is correct?一个横向脉冲沿一根很长的拉紧的弦传播。关于这个脉冲，下列哪一项说法是正确的？

  • (A) It carries both energy and string material from one end to the other.它把能量和弦的物质一起从一端带到另一端。
  • (B) It carries energy along the string, but the string material is not carried to the far end.它沿弦传递能量，但弦的物质并不会被带到远端。
  • (C) It carries string material along the string but transfers no energy.它沿弦带动弦的物质，但不传递能量。
  • (D) Each piece of the string moves parallel to the direction in which the pulse travels.弦上每一小段都沿脉冲传播的方向平行运动。
Answer:答案： (B)
A wave or pulse transfers energy between two places without transferring matter between them. Each piece of the string moves up and down briefly as the pulse passes and then returns to its original position, so the string material does not travel to the far end.波或脉冲在两地之间传递能量，而不传递物质。脉冲经过时，弦上每一小段只短暂地上下运动，随后回到原来的位置，所以弦的物质不会被带到远端。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) pictures the pulse as a lump of string moving along the string, which confuses the motion of the disturbance with the motion of the material. (C) reverses the facts: it is the energy that is transferred, not the material. (D) describes a longitudinal wave; in a transverse pulse each piece moves perpendicular to the direction of travel.(A) 把脉冲想成一团弦沿着弦移动，混淆了扰动的传播与物质的运动。 (C) 把事实说反了：被传递的是能量，而不是物质。 (D) 描述的是纵波；在横向脉冲中，每一小段的运动方向垂直于传播方向。
Insight.要点。 The disturbance moves, the medium does not: waves carry energy, not matter.移动的是扰动，不是介质：波传递的是能量，而不是物质。
Q2EASY 14.1 Wave Speed on a String14.1 弦上的波速Calculator

A string has mass per unit length $0.010~\mathrm{kg/m}$ and is stretched with tension $36~\mathrm{N}$. The speed of a pulse on the string is closest to一根弦的单位长度质量为 $0.010~\mathrm{kg/m}$，张力为 $36~\mathrm{N}$。弦上脉冲的速度最接近

  • (A) $0.60~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $6.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $60~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $3.6\times10^{3}~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (C)
Here $F_T$ is the tension and $\mu$ is the mass per unit length of the string.其中 $F_T$ 是张力，$\mu$ 是弦的单位长度质量。
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{F_T}{\mu}} = \sqrt{\frac{36}{0.010}} = \sqrt{3600} = 60~\mathrm{m/s}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) multiplies instead of dividing: $\sqrt{F_T\mu}=\sqrt{0.36}=0.60~\mathrm{m/s}$. (B) leaves out the mass per unit length: $\sqrt{F_T}=\sqrt{36}=6.0~\mathrm{m/s}$. (D) forgets the square root: $F_T/\mu=3600=3.6\times10^{3}$.(A) 把除法错写成乘法：$\sqrt{F_T\mu}=\sqrt{0.36}=0.60~\mathrm{m/s}$。 (B) 漏掉单位长度质量：$\sqrt{F_T}=\sqrt{36}=6.0~\mathrm{m/s}$。 (D) 忘了开平方：$F_T/\mu=3600=3.6\times10^{3}$。
Insight.要点。 Speed on a string rises with tension and falls with mass per unit length, and both sit under a square root.弦上的波速随张力增大而增大，随单位长度质量增大而减小，两者都在根号之下。
Q3EASY 14.2 Wave Speed14.2 波速No Calculator

A periodic wave passes a point. Successive crests pass the point $0.50~\mathrm{s}$ apart, and the crests are $2.0~\mathrm{m}$ apart in space. The speed of the wave is一列周期波经过某一点。相邻波峰经过该点的时间间隔为 $0.50~\mathrm{s}$，相邻波峰在空间中相距 $2.0~\mathrm{m}$。这列波的波速为

  • (A) $0.25~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (D)
The time between crests is the period $T=0.50~\mathrm{s}$ and the distance between crests is the wavelength $\lambda=2.0~\mathrm{m}$.波峰之间的时间间隔是周期 $T=0.50~\mathrm{s}$，波峰之间的距离是波长 $\lambda=2.0~\mathrm{m}$。
$$v = \frac{\lambda}{T} = \frac{2.0}{0.50} = 4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) inverts the ratio: $T/\lambda=0.50/2.0=0.25$. (B) multiplies instead of dividing: $\lambda T=(2.0)(0.50)=1.0$. (C) reports the wavelength $2.0$ as if it were the speed, without dividing by the period.(A) 把比值倒过来：$T/\lambda=0.50/2.0=0.25$。 (B) 把除法错写成乘法：$\lambda T=(2.0)(0.50)=1.0$。 (C) 把波长 $2.0$ 当成波速，没有除以周期。
Insight.要点。 A wave moves one wavelength in one period, so $v=\lambda/T=f\lambda$.波在一个周期内前进一个波长，所以 $v=\lambda/T=f\lambda$。
Q4MEDIUM 14.2 Amplitude and Frequency14.2 振幅与频率No Calculator

A tuning fork is struck harder, so the amplitude of the sound wave doubles while the frequency of the fork stays the same. Compared with the softer sound, the louder sound用更大的力敲击音叉，使声波的振幅变为原来的两倍，而音叉的频率保持不变。与较轻的声音相比，较响的声音

  • (A) is louder and higher in pitch.更响，音调更高。
  • (B) is louder, with the same pitch and the same wavelength.更响，音调相同，波长相同。
  • (C) is louder and the sound travels twice as fast.更响，且声音传播得快一倍。
  • (D) has the same loudness, because amplitude does not depend on the frequency.响度不变，因为振幅与频率无关。
Answer:答案： (B)
Loudness increases with amplitude, so the sound is louder. Pitch is set by frequency, which did not change. The speed of sound is set by the air, which did not change, so $\lambda=v/f$ is unchanged as well.响度随振幅增大而增大，所以声音更响。音调由频率决定，而频率没有变。声速由空气决定，也没有变，所以 $\lambda=v/f$ 同样不变。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) mixes up loudness with pitch; pitch follows frequency, not amplitude. (C) lets the source change the wave speed, but the speed depends on the medium. (D) correctly says amplitude is independent of frequency, then wrongly concludes that amplitude has no effect on loudness.(A) 把响度和音调混为一谈；音调取决于频率，而不是振幅。 (C) 以为波源会改变波速，但波速由介质决定。 (D) 正确地指出振幅与频率无关，却错误地推出振幅对响度没有影响。
Insight.要点。 Amplitude sets loudness and energy; frequency sets pitch; the medium sets speed.振幅决定响度和能量；频率决定音调；介质决定波速。
Q5HARD 14.2 Reading Two Graphs14.2 读取两幅图像Calculator

A sinusoidal wave moves along a string. A photograph of the whole string (a graph of displacement against position) shows two neighboring crests at $x=0.10~\mathrm{m}$ and $x=0.70~\mathrm{m}$. A separate record of one point on the string (a graph of displacement against time) shows two neighboring crests at $t=0$ and $t=0.30~\mathrm{s}$. The speed of the wave on the string is closest to一列正弦波沿弦传播。对整根弦拍的照片（位移对位置的图像）显示，相邻两个波峰位于 $x=0.10~\mathrm{m}$ 和 $x=0.70~\mathrm{m}$。弦上某一点的另一份记录（位移对时间的图像）显示，相邻两个波峰出现在 $t=0$ 和 $t=0.30~\mathrm{s}$。弦上波速最接近

  • (A) $0.18~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $0.50~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $3.3~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (C)
The graph against position gives the wavelength; the graph against time gives the period.位置图像给出波长；时间图像给出周期。
$$\lambda = 0.70-0.10 = 0.60~\mathrm{m},\qquad T = 0.30~\mathrm{s}$$
$$v = \frac{\lambda}{T} = \frac{0.60}{0.30} = 2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) multiplies the two repeat distances: $\lambda T=(0.60)(0.30)=0.18$. (B) inverts the ratio: $T/\lambda=0.30/0.60=0.50$. (D) takes the frequency $f=1/T=3.3~\mathrm{Hz}$ as the speed and ignores the wavelength.(A) 把两个重复量相乘：$\lambda T=(0.60)(0.30)=0.18$。 (B) 把比值倒过来：$T/\lambda=0.30/0.60=0.50$。 (D) 把频率 $f=1/T=3.3~\mathrm{Hz}$ 当作波速，忽略了波长。
Insight.要点。 Read the horizontal axis first: a repeat in meters is a wavelength, a repeat in seconds is a period, and the speed needs one of each.先看横轴：以米为单位的重复距离是波长，以秒为单位的重复时间是周期，求波速两者都要用到。
Q6EASY 14.3 Reflection at a Boundary14.3 边界处的反射No Calculator

An upright pulse travels along a light string toward a junction with a heavier string. Pulses travel more slowly on the heavier string. After the pulse reaches the junction,一个正立的脉冲沿一根轻弦传向它与一根较重的弦的连接处。脉冲在较重的弦上传播得更慢。脉冲到达连接处之后，

  • (A) the reflected pulse is upright and the transmitted pulse is upright.反射脉冲是正立的，透射脉冲也是正立的。
  • (B) the reflected pulse is inverted and the transmitted pulse is upright.反射脉冲是倒置的，透射脉冲是正立的。
  • (C) the reflected pulse is upright and the transmitted pulse is inverted.反射脉冲是正立的，透射脉冲是倒置的。
  • (D) both the reflected pulse and the transmitted pulse are inverted.反射脉冲和透射脉冲都是倒置的。
Answer:答案： (B)
The transmitted pulse goes into a medium where the wave speed is smaller, so the reflected pulse is inverted. The transmitted pulse is not inverted.透射脉冲进入的是波速更小的介质，所以反射脉冲是倒置的。透射脉冲不倒置。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) assumes a reflection never flips a pulse, which holds only when the pulse enters a medium with a greater speed. (C) swaps the two pulses: the inversion belongs to the reflected pulse. (D) applies the inversion to both pulses; the transmitted pulse keeps its orientation.(A) 以为反射永远不会使脉冲翻转，这只在脉冲进入波速更大的介质时才成立。 (C) 把两个脉冲弄反了：倒置的是反射脉冲。 (D) 把倒置同时用在两个脉冲上；透射脉冲保持原来的朝向。
Insight.要点。 The reflected pulse is inverted when the transmitted wave is slower, and upright when the transmitted wave is faster.透射波较慢时，反射脉冲倒置；透射波较快时，反射脉冲正立。
Q7MEDIUM 14.3 Wavelength After a Boundary14.3 越过边界后的波长Calculator

A wave of frequency $80~\mathrm{Hz}$ travels along string 1 at $48~\mathrm{m/s}$ and passes into string 2, where its speed is $72~\mathrm{m/s}$. The wavelength of the transmitted wave in string 2 is closest to一列频率为 $80~\mathrm{Hz}$ 的波在弦 1 上以 $48~\mathrm{m/s}$ 的速度传播，然后进入弦 2，在弦 2 中的速度为 $72~\mathrm{m/s}$。透射波在弦 2 中的波长最接近

  • (A) $0.40~\mathrm{m}$
  • (B) $0.60~\mathrm{m}$
  • (C) $0.90~\mathrm{m}$
  • (D) $5.8\times10^{3}~\mathrm{m}$
Answer:答案： (C)
The frequency does not change at a boundary, so $f=80~\mathrm{Hz}$ in string 2.波在边界处频率不变，所以在弦 2 中 $f=80~\mathrm{Hz}$。
$$\lambda_2 = \frac{v_2}{f} = \frac{72}{80} = 0.90~\mathrm{m}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) scales the wavelength inversely with speed: $(0.60)(48/72)=0.40~\mathrm{m}$. (B) keeps the old wavelength $\lambda_1=48/80=0.60~\mathrm{m}$, as if the wavelength were fixed by the source. (D) multiplies instead of dividing: $v_2f=(72)(80)=5.8\times10^{3}$.(A) 让波长与波速成反比：$(0.60)(48/72)=0.40~\mathrm{m}$。 (B) 沿用原来的波长 $\lambda_1=48/80=0.60~\mathrm{m}$，好像波长由波源决定。 (D) 把除法错写成乘法：$v_2f=(72)(80)=5.8\times10^{3}$。
Insight.要点。 Across a boundary the frequency stays fixed; speed and wavelength change in the same proportion.越过边界时频率不变；波速和波长按相同比例变化。
Q8MEDIUM 14.3 Polarization14.3 偏振No Calculator

A beam of light passes through two polarizing filters in a row. Rotating the second filter about the beam changes the intensity of the transmitted light. A student rotates a slotted barrier in the path of a beam of sound in air, and the sound intensity never changes. Which conclusion is best supported by these observations?一束光依次通过两块偏振片。把第二块偏振片绕光束转动，透射光的强度会改变。一名学生在空气中的声束路径上转动一块开有狭缝的挡板，声强始终不变。下列哪个结论最能由这些观察得到支持？

  • (A) Both light and sound are transverse waves.光和声都是横波。
  • (B) Light is a transverse wave and sound is a longitudinal wave.光是横波，声是纵波。
  • (C) Light is a longitudinal wave and sound is a transverse wave.光是纵波，声是横波。
  • (D) Light is a mechanical wave and sound is an electromagnetic wave.光是机械波，声是电磁波。
Answer:答案： (B)
Only transverse waves can be polarized, because only they oscillate in a plane that a filter can select. The intensity of light depends on the filter angle, so light is transverse. Sound shows no dependence on the barrier angle, which fits a longitudinal wave, and sound is modeled as a longitudinal wave.只有横波才能被偏振，因为只有横波的振动处在一个可以被滤光片选择的平面内。光的强度与偏振片的角度有关，所以光是横波。声音与挡板的角度无关，这与纵波相符，声被建模为纵波。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) ignores the sound observation: a transverse sound wave could be polarized and would change with the barrier angle. (C) swaps the two wave types, reading the angle dependence of light as a sign of a longitudinal wave. (D) mixes up the categories: sound needs a medium (mechanical), while light does not (electromagnetic).(A) 忽略了声音的观察结果：横向的声波可以被偏振，其强度会随挡板角度变化。 (C) 把两种波的类型弄反了，把光对角度的依赖当成纵波的标志。 (D) 混淆了类别：声音需要介质（机械波），而光不需要（电磁波）。
Insight.要点。 Polarization is a test for transverse waves; a longitudinal wave cannot be polarized.偏振是检验横波的方法；纵波不能被偏振。

  • (C) $2.0\times10^{6}~\mathrm{m}$
  • (D) $1.8\times10^{23}~\mathrm{m}$
Answer:答案： (B)
$$\lambda = \frac{c}{f} = \frac{3.00\times10^{8}}{6.0\times10^{14}} = 5.0\times10^{-7}~\mathrm{m}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) enters $c$ as $3.00\times10^{5}$, the speed in km/s, so the result is a thousand times too small: $3.00\times10^{5}/6.0\times10^{14}=5.0\times10^{-10}$. Insight.要点。 The wave relation $v=f\lambda$ with $v=c$ gives $\lambda=c/f$ in a vacuum; use meters and hertz.波的关系式 $v=f\lambda$ 在真空中取 $v=c$，得 $\lambda=c/f$；单位用米和赫兹。
Q11MEDIUM 14.5 Doppler Effect14.5 多普勒效应No Calculator

A siren emits sound of a fixed frequency while it drives straight toward a stationary listener. Compared with the sound heard when the siren is at rest, the sound reaching the listener has一个警报器发出固定频率的声音，同时径直驶向一位静止的听者。与警报器静止时听到的声音相比，到达听者的声音

  • (A) a higher frequency, a shorter wavelength, and the same speed.频率更高，波长更短，波速相同。
  • (B) a higher frequency, the same wavelength, and a greater speed.频率更高，波长相同，波速更大。
  • (C) a higher frequency, a longer wavelength, and the same speed.频率更高，波长更长，波速相同。
  • (D) the same frequency, a shorter wavelength, and a lower speed.频率相同，波长更短，波速更小。
Answer:答案： (A)
Between two successive wavefronts the siren moves forward, so the wavefronts ahead of it are emitted closer to each other and are packed more closely together. Each wavefront travels at the speed set by the air, so the listener meets the closely spaced wavefronts more often: a shorter wavelength, a higher frequency, and the same speed.在相继发出两个波前之间，警报器向前移动，所以它前方的波前彼此靠得更近、排得更密。每个波前都以空气决定的速度传播，所以听者更频繁地遇到间距更小的波前：波长更短，频率更高，波速不变。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) lets the motion of the source change the wave speed, but the speed is set by the air. It also keeps the wavelength fixed, which cannot raise the frequency at the same speed. (b) A string fixed at both ends has a node at each end, so the fundamental fits half a wavelength on the string:两端固定的弦在两端各有一个波节，所以基频时弦上正好容纳半个波长：
$$\lambda_1 = 2L = 2(1.20) = 2.40~\mathrm{m}$$
$$f_1 = \frac{v}{\lambda_1} = \frac{50}{2.40} = 20.8~\mathrm{Hz}$$

A stationary siren emits sound of frequency $700~\mathrm{Hz}$ in air, where the speed of sound is $343~\mathrm{m/s}$. A listener stands beside the road. Later, the siren is mounted on a truck that drives straight toward the listener and then past the listener, with the siren still emitting the same frequency.一个静止的警报器在空气中发出频率为 $700~\mathrm{Hz}$ 的声音，空气中的声速为 $343~\mathrm{m/s}$。一位听者站在路边。后来，警报器被装在一辆卡车上，卡车径直驶向听者并从听者身旁驶过，警报器仍发出相同频率的声音。

(a) Here $v$ is the speed of sound, $f$ the frequency, $\lambda$ the wavelength and $T$ the period:其中 $v$ 是声速，$f$ 是频率，$\lambda$ 是波长，$T$ 是周期：
$$\lambda = \frac{v}{f} = \frac{343}{700} = 0.490~\mathrm{m}$$
$$T = \frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{700} = 1.43\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{s}$$
(b) The listener hears a higher frequency than $700~\mathrm{Hz}$. The siren emits wavefronts at equal time intervals, but between two emissions the truck moves toward the listener, so each new wavefront starts closer to the one before it. The wavefronts between the truck and the listener are therefore packed more closely together than for a stationary siren, so the wavelength in that direction is shorter. The speed of sound is set by the air, so the wavefronts still travel at $343~\mathrm{m/s}$. Closely spaced wavefronts moving at the same speed reach the listener more often, which means a higher frequency and a higher pitch.听者听到的频率高于 $700~\mathrm{Hz}$。警报器每隔相等的时间发出一个波前，但在两次发射之间卡车朝听者前进，所以每个新波前都从离前一个波前更近的位置开始。因此，卡车与听者之间的波前比静止警报器的波前排得更密，该方向上的波长更短。声速由空气决定，所以波前仍以 $343~\mathrm{m/s}$ 传播。间距较小、速度不变的波前更频繁地到达听者，这意味着频率更高、音调更高。
(c) The heard frequency is below $700~\mathrm{Hz}$. Behind the truck, each new wavefront starts farther from the one before it, so the wavefronts are spread out more than for a stationary siren. At the same speed they reach the listener less often, so the frequency drops.听到的频率低于 $700~\mathrm{Hz}$。在卡车后方，每个新波前都从离前一个波前更远的位置开始，所以波前比静止警报器的波前分得更开。在速度相同的情况下，它们到达听者的频率较低，所以频率下降。
(d) The beat frequency is the number of beats per unit time:拍频是单位时间内的拍数：
$$f_\mathrm{beat} = \frac{12}{3.0~\mathrm{s}} = 4.0~\mathrm{Hz}$$
The beat frequency is the difference of the two frequencies, so the truck's sound is either $696~\mathrm{Hz}$ or $704~\mathrm{Hz}$. From part (b), the approaching truck is heard above $700~\mathrm{Hz}$, so the listener hears $704~\mathrm{Hz}$.拍频等于两个频率之差，所以卡车的声音频率是 $696~\mathrm{Hz}$ 或 $704~\mathrm{Hz}$。由 (b) 知，驶近的卡车听起来频率高于 $700~\mathrm{Hz}$，所以听者听到的是 $704~\mathrm{Hz}$。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 False moves: (a) giving $\lambda=f/v=2.04~\mathrm{m}^{-1}$ with the ratio inverted; (b) claiming that the sound waves travel faster because the truck moves, or that the siren itself emits a higher frequency; (d) giving $696~\mathrm{Hz}$ without using the direction of the truck, or inverting the count to $3.0/12=0.25~\mathrm{Hz}$.常见错误：(a) 把比值倒过来，得到 $\lambda=f/v=2.04~\mathrm{m}^{-1}$；(b) 说因为卡车在动所以声波传播得更快，或者说警报器本身发出的频率更高；(d) 不考虑卡车的运动方向而给出 $696~\mathrm{Hz}$，或把次数倒过来算成 $3.0/12=0.25~\mathrm{Hz}$。
Insight.要点。 For the Doppler effect, reason with wavefront spacing and relative motion: the medium sets the speed, and the spacing sets how often wavefronts arrive.对多普勒效应，要用波前间距和相对运动来推理：介质决定波速，间距决定波前到达的频繁程度。
AP rubric, 9 points.AP 评分标准，共 9 分。 (a) 1: $\lambda=v/f=0.490~\mathrm{m}$ · 1: $T=1/f=1.43\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{s}$ · (b) 1: states that the heard frequency is higher · 1: explains that the wavefronts ahead of the truck are closer together · 1: states that the speed of sound is unchanged because the air sets it, so the closely spaced wavefronts arrive more often · (c) 1: states that the heard frequency is lower · 1: explains that the wavefronts behind the truck are spread farther apart · (d) 1: $f_\mathrm{beat}=4.0~\mathrm{Hz}$ · 1: selects $704~\mathrm{Hz}$ because the approaching siren is heard higher. (a) 1 分：$\lambda=v/f=0.490~\mathrm{m}$ · 1 分：$T=1/f=1.43\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{s}$ · (b) 1 分：指出听到的频率更高 · 1 分：解释卡车前方的波前靠得更近 · 1 分：指出声速不变（由空气决定），间距小的波前更频繁地到达 · (c) 1 分：指出听到的频率更低 · 1 分：解释卡车后方的波前分得更开 · (d) 1 分：$f_\mathrm{beat}=4.0~\mathrm{Hz}$ · 1 分：因驶近的警报器听起来更高，选定 $704~\mathrm{Hz}$。
FRQ 5HARD 14.9 Mathematical Routines: Thin Films14.9 数学运算：薄膜Calculator

A camera lens of glass with index of refraction $1.52$ carries an antireflection coating of index of refraction $1.38$. Light is incident normally from air, and the coating is designed for light of wavelength $550~\mathrm{nm}$ in a vacuum. Use $1.00$ for air.折射率为 $1.52$ 的玻璃相机镜头上镀有折射率为 $1.38$ 的增透膜。光从空气垂直入射，增透膜是为真空中波长 $550~\mathrm{nm}$ 的光设计的。空气的折射率取 $1.00$。

(a) A reflected ray shifts by $180^\circ$ when the light reflects from a medium of higher index of refraction than the one it travels in. At the air-coating surface the light goes from $1.00$ to $1.38$, so that ray shifts by $180^\circ$. At the coating-glass surface it goes from $1.38$ to $1.52$, so that ray also shifts by $180^\circ$. Both rays shift by the same amount, so these shifts produce no net phase difference between the two reflected rays.光从折射率较低的介质射向折射率较高的介质并在界面反射时，反射光线有 $180^\circ$ 的相位变化。在空气与薄膜的表面，光从 $1.00$ 到 $1.38$，所以这条光线有 $180^\circ$ 的相位变化。在薄膜与玻璃的表面，光从 $1.38$ 到 $1.52$，所以这条光线也有 $180^\circ$ 的相位变化。两条光线的相位变化相同，所以这些相位变化不会在两条反射光线之间产生净相位差。
(b) Here $n$ is the index of the coating, $\lambda_\mathrm{film}$ the wavelength in the coating and $t$ its thickness:其中 $n$ 是薄膜的折射率，$\lambda_\mathrm{film}$ 是薄膜中的波长，$t$ 是膜厚：
$$\lambda_\mathrm{film} = \frac{\lambda}{n} = \frac{550}{1.38} = 399~\mathrm{nm}$$
With no net shift, the rays cancel when the extra path $2t$ is half a wavelength in the coating:没有净相位差时，当额外路程 $2t$ 等于薄膜中的半个波长时两条光线相消：
$$2t = \frac{\lambda_\mathrm{film}}{2}\ \Rightarrow\ t = \frac{\lambda_\mathrm{film}}{4} = \frac{398.6}{4} = 99.6~\mathrm{nm}$$
(c) For $440~\mathrm{nm}$ light, the wavelength in the coating is $440/1.38=318.8~\mathrm{nm}$, and the extra path is $2t=2(99.6)=199.2~\mathrm{nm}$:对于 $440~\mathrm{nm}$ 的光，薄膜中的波长为 $440/1.38=318.8~\mathrm{nm}$，额外路程为 $2t=2(99.6)=199.2~\mathrm{nm}$：
$$\frac{2t}{\lambda_\mathrm{film}} = \frac{199.2}{318.8} = 0.625$$
Full cancellation needs this ratio to be $0.5$ (or $1.5$, $2.5$, and so on). At $0.625$ the cancellation is only partial, so some of this light is reflected.完全相消要求这个比值为 $0.5$（或 $1.5$、$2.5$ 等）。比值为 $0.625$ 时只是部分相消，所以这种光仍有一部分被反射。
(d) At the front surface the light goes from air to soap film (a higher index), so that reflected ray shifts by $180^\circ$. At the back surface it goes from film to air (a lower index), so that ray does not shift. Exactly one ray shifts. When the film is much thinner than a wavelength, the extra path $2t$ is nearly zero, so the two rays differ in phase by essentially $180^\circ$ and cancel. The film looks dark.在前表面，光从空气射向肥皂膜（折射率更高），这条反射光线有 $180^\circ$ 的相位变化。在后表面，光从薄膜射向空气（折射率更低），这条光线没有相位变化。恰好只有一条光线有相位变化。当膜比一个波长薄得多时，额外路程 $2t$ 几乎为零，所以两条光线的相位差基本上是 $180^\circ$，相互抵消。薄膜看起来是暗的。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 False moves: (a) saying only the air-coating reflection shifts; (b) using the vacuum wavelength, $t=550/4=138~\mathrm{nm}$, or $t=\lambda_\mathrm{film}/2=199~\mathrm{nm}$; (d) saying the film is dark because no light enters it, or forgetting that the back surface has no shift.常见错误：(a) 说只有空气与薄膜表面的反射有相位变化；(b) 用真空波长，$t=550/4=138~\mathrm{nm}$，或取 $t=\lambda_\mathrm{film}/2=199~\mathrm{nm}$；(d) 说薄膜是暗的因为没有光进入，或忘了后表面没有相位变化。
Insight.要点。 Decide the shifts for each surface first, then choose the condition. The coating needs $t=\lambda/(4n)$ when both rays shift; a thin soap film is dark because only one does.先判断每个表面的相位变化，再选择条件。两条光线都有相位变化时，增透膜需要 $t=\lambda/(4n)$；薄肥皂膜之所以暗，是因为只有一条有相位变化。
AP rubric, 9 points.AP 评分标准，共 9 分。 (a) 1: states that both reflections shift by $180^\circ$ with the reason (higher index each time) · 1: concludes that there is no net phase difference · (b) 1: $\lambda_\mathrm{film}=550/1.38=399~\mathrm{nm}$ · 1: uses $2t=\lambda_\mathrm{film}/2$ for destructive interference · 1: $t=99.6~\mathrm{nm}$ · (c) 1: computes the ratio $2t/\lambda_\mathrm{film}=0.625$ · 1: concludes that the cancellation is only partial since the ratio is not $0.5$ · (d) 1: states that exactly one reflection (front surface) shifts by $180^\circ$ · 1: argues that $2t\approx0$ gives a net phase difference of $180^\circ$ and so destructive interference. (a) 1 分：指出两次反射都有 $180^\circ$ 的相位变化，理由是每次都是进入更高折射率 · 1 分：得出没有净相位差 · (b) 1 分：$\lambda_\mathrm{film}=550/1.38=399~\mathrm{nm}$ · 1 分：用 $2t=\lambda_\mathrm{film}/2$ 作为相消条件 · 1 分：$t=99.6~\mathrm{nm}$ · (c) 1 分：算出比值 $2t/\lambda_\mathrm{film}=0.625$ · 1 分：因比值不是 $0.5$，得出只是部分相消 · (d) 1 分：指出恰好只有一次反射（前表面）有 $180^\circ$ 的相位变化 · 1 分：论证 $2t\approx0$ 时净相位差为 $180^\circ$，因而相消。
FRQ 6MEDIUM 14.6 Translation Between Representations: Standing Waves in Pipes14.6 表征转换：管中的驻波Calculator

Two pipes each have length $0.85~\mathrm{m}$. Pipe 1 is open at both ends and pipe 2 is closed at one end and open at the other. The speed of sound in the air in the pipes is $343~\mathrm{m/s}$.两根管的长度都是 $0.85~\mathrm{m}$。管 1 两端开口，管 2 一端封闭、一端开口。管内空气中的声速为 $343~\mathrm{m/s}$。

(a) An open end is a displacement antinode, so pipe 1 has an antinode at each end, and the $n$th harmonic has $n$ displacement nodes and $n+1$ displacement antinodes: the fundamental has $1$ node and $2$ antinodes, and the third harmonic has $3$ nodes and $4$ antinodes.开口端是位移波腹，所以管 1 两端各有一个位移波腹，第 $n$ 谐波有 $n$ 个位移波节和 $n+1$ 个位移波腹：基频有 $1$ 个波节和 $2$ 个波腹，第三谐波有 $3$ 个波节和 $4$ 个波腹。 In the fundamental the single node is at the middle of the pipe. In the third harmonic the nodes are at $L/6$, $L/2$ and $5L/6$ from one end.基频时唯一的波节在管的中点。第三谐波时，波节位于距一端 $L/6$、$L/2$ 和 $5L/6$ 处。
(b) Here $L$ is the length of the pipe and $v$ the speed of sound. The wavelengths are $\lambda_n=2L/n$:其中 $L$ 是管长，$v$ 是声速。波长为 $\lambda_n=2L/n$：
$$\lambda_1 = 2L = 1.70~\mathrm{m},\qquad f_1 = \frac{v}{\lambda_1} = \frac{343}{1.70} = 202~\mathrm{Hz}$$
$$\lambda_3 = \frac{2L}{3} = 0.567~\mathrm{m},\qquad f_3 = 3f_1 = 605~\mathrm{Hz}$$
(c) Pipe 2 has a displacement node at the closed end and an antinode at the open end, so $L=n\lambda/4$ with odd $n$ and $f_n=nv/(4L)$:管 2 的封闭端是位移波节，开口端是位移波腹，所以 $L=n\lambda/4$（$n$ 为奇数），$f_n=nv/(4L)$：
$$f_1 = \frac{343}{4(0.85)} = 101~\mathrm{Hz},\qquad f_3 = 3f_1 = 303~\mathrm{Hz}$$
(d) The claim is false. With a node at one end and an antinode at the other, the pipe holds an odd number of quarter wavelengths, so only odd harmonics can form. The second-lowest resonance is the third harmonic, $3(101)=303~\mathrm{Hz}$, three times the lowest. A frequency of twice the lowest, $202~\mathrm{Hz}$, would need a pipe with the same type of end at both ends, as in pipe 1.这一说法不正确。一端是波节、另一端是波腹时，管内容纳的是四分之一波长的奇数倍，所以只能形成奇次谐波。第二低的共振是第三谐波，$3(101)=303~\mathrm{Hz}$，是最低共振频率的三倍。要得到最低频率的两倍 $202~\mathrm{Hz}$，管的两端必须是同一类型，如管 1。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 False moves: (a) giving a node at each open end; (b) using $\lambda_1=4L$ for the open-open pipe, which gives $101~\mathrm{Hz}$; (c) using $f=v/(2L)$ for pipe 2, which gives $202~\mathrm{Hz}$; (d) agreeing with the claim because the open-open pipe has all harmonics.常见错误：(a) 在每个开口端画波节；(b) 对两端开口的管用 $\lambda_1=4L$，得到 $101~\mathrm{Hz}$；(c) 对管 2 用 $f=v/(2L)$，得到 $202~\mathrm{Hz}$；(d) 因为两端开口的管有全部谐波就同意这一说法。
Insight.要点。 Draw the displacement nodes and antinodes allowed by the ends first, then count quarter wavelengths or half wavelengths to the length of the pipe.先画出两端条件允许的位移波节和波腹，再按管长数出四分之一波长或半波长的个数。
AP rubric, 8 points.AP 评分标准，共 8 分。 (a) 1: states $1$ node and $2$ antinodes for the fundamental (node at the center) · 1: states $3$ nodes and $4$ antinodes for the third harmonic · (b) 1: $\lambda_1=2L=1.70~\mathrm{m}$ and $f_1=202~\mathrm{Hz}$ · 1: $\lambda_3=0.567~\mathrm{m}$ and $f_3=605~\mathrm{Hz}$ · (c) 1: uses a node at the closed end and an antinode at the open end, so $f_n=nv/(4L)$ with odd $n$ · 1: obtains $101~\mathrm{Hz}$ and $303~\mathrm{Hz}$ · (d) 1: rejects the claim, stating that the second-lowest resonance is $3f_1$ · 1: justifies this with the boundary conditions, so only odd harmonics occur. (a) 1 分：指出基频有 $1$ 个波节、$2$ 个波腹（波节在中点） · 1 分：指出第三谐波有 $3$ 个波节、$4$ 个波腹 · (b) 1 分：$\lambda_1=2L=1.70~\mathrm{m}$，$f_1=202~\mathrm{Hz}$ · 1 分：$\lambda_3=0.567~\mathrm{m}$，$f_3=605~\mathrm{Hz}$ · (c) 1 分：利用封闭端为波节、开口端为波腹，得 $f_n=nv/(4L)$（$n$ 为奇数） · 1 分：得到 $101~\mathrm{Hz}$ 和 $303~\mathrm{Hz}$ · (d) 1 分：否定该说法，指出第二低的共振是 $3f_1$ · 1 分：用边界条件说明只会出现奇次谐波。
