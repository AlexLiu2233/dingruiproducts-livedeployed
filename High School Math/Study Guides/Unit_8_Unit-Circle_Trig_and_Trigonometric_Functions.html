Unit-Circle Trigonometry and Trigonometric Functions单位圆三角学与三角函数

Right-triangle trig defines $\sin, \cos, \tan$ only for acute angles. The unit circle, a circle of radius $1$ centred at the origin, extends those three ratios to every real angle by reading $\cos\theta = x$ and $\sin\theta = y$ off the point $(x, y)$ where the angle's terminal arm meets the circle. Radian measure, $\pi = 180^{\circ}$, makes calculus possible. Then the same picture, rolled out horizontally, gives the periodic graphs of $\sin x$, $\cos x$, $\tan x$ and the transformation toolkit $y = A \sin(B(x - C)) + D$ that physics, signal processing, and AP Pre-Calc all rely on.直角三角形三角学只为锐角定义 $\sin, \cos, \tan$。单位圆（unit circle，半径为 $1$、以原点为圆心的圆）通过让角的终边与圆相交于点 $(x, y)$，并令 $\cos\theta = x$、$\sin\theta = y$，把这三个三角比扩展到所有实数角。弧度制（radians）以 $\pi = 180^{\circ}$ 为换算桥梁，是后续微积分的基础。把单位圆沿水平方向展开，便得到 $\sin x$、$\cos x$、$\tan x$ 的周期图像，以及 $y = A \sin(B(x - C)) + D$ 这一变换工具组，物理、信号处理与 AP Pre-Calc 都离不开它。

Arc length from a central angle由圆心角求弧长

A central angle has its vertex at the centre of a circle and is said to be subtended by the arc between its two sides. By the definition of a radian, an angle of $1$ radian cuts off an arc exactly one radius long. So an angle of $\theta$ radians cuts off $\theta$ radii of arc:圆心角（central angle）的顶点在圆心，它所对的弧夹在两边之间。按弧度的定义，$1$ 弧度的圆心角截下的弧恰好等于一个半径。所以 $\theta$ 弧度的圆心角截下 $\theta$ 个半径长的弧：

$$ a = r\theta \qquad \text{or} \qquad \theta = \frac{a}{r}, \qquad (\theta \text{ in radians}). $$

In degrees the same idea reads $a = \dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}} \cdot 2\pi r$, because the arc is the fraction $\theta/360^{\circ}$ of the circumference $2\pi r$. Both versions give the same length; the first needs the angle in radians.用角度时同一思想写作 $a = \dfrac{\theta}{360^{\circ}} \cdot 2\pi r$，因为弧长是周长 $2\pi r$ 的 $\theta/360^{\circ}$。两种写法结果相同；第一种要求角用弧度。

Common Mistake: degrees inside $a = r\theta$常见错误：把角度直接代入 $a = r\theta$

For a radius of $12$ cm and an angle of $150^{\circ}$, writing $a = 12 \cdot 150 = 1800$ cm is wrong: the formula needs radians. Convert first ($150^{\circ} = 5\pi/6$), then $a = 12 \cdot 5\pi/6 = 10\pi \approx 31.42$ cm.半径 $12$ cm、角 $150^{\circ}$ 时，写 $a = 12 \cdot 150 = 1800$ cm 是错的：公式要求弧度。先换算（$150^{\circ} = 5\pi/6$），再算 $a = 12 \cdot 5\pi/6 = 10\pi \approx 31.42$ cm。

A point on the terminal side need not lie on the unit circle. If $P(x, y)$ is any point on the terminal side other than the origin, let $r = \sqrt{x^{2} + y^{2}}$ be its distance from the origin. The point $(x/r, y/r)$ lies on the same ray and on the unit circle, because $(x/r)^{2} + (y/r)^{2} = 1$. Common Questions常见问题

How do I convert between degrees and radians?如何在角度与弧度之间转换？

Multiply degrees by pi over 180 to get radians, and multiply radians by 180 over pi to get degrees, since a full circle is 360 degrees or 2 pi radians.角度乘以 pi 除以 180 得到弧度，弧度乘以 180 除以 pi 得到角度，因为一整圈是 360 度即 2 pi 弧度。

What do A, B, C, and D do in y = A sin(B(x - C)) + D?在 y = A sin(B(x - C)) + D 中 A、B、C、D 各起什么作用？

A sets the amplitude, B sets the period as 2 pi over B, C is the horizontal phase shift, and D is the vertical shift of the midline.A 决定振幅，B 决定周期为 2 pi 除以 B，C 是水平相移，D 是中线的竖直平移。

Why does the tangent graph have asymptotes?正切函数图像为什么有渐近线？

Tangent is sine over cosine, so wherever cosine equals zero the function is undefined, which creates vertical asymptotes at pi over 2 plus multiples of pi.正切是正弦除以余弦，所以当余弦为零时函数无定义，从而在 pi 除以 2 加上 pi 的整数倍处产生竖直渐近线。
