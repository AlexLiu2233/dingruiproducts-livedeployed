Q 1第1题EASY基础3.12, 3.13 Setting up and carrying out a two-proportion test3.12, 3.13 两比例检验的设立与实施CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
In a randomized experiment, 48 of the 120 volunteers in group 1 and 39 of the 130 volunteers in group 2 succeed at a task. For a test of $H_0:p_1=p_2$, what is the pooled proportion $\hat p_c$?
在一项随机化实验中，第 1 组 120 名志愿者中有 48 人完成任务，第 2 组 130 名志愿者中有 39 人完成任务。检验 $H_0:p_1=p_2$ 时，合并比例（pooled proportion） $\hat p_c$ 是多少？
Answer: C答案：C

Pool the counts合并计数1 point1 分

Under $H_0$ both groups share one proportion, so the best single estimate combines all successes and all participants: $\hat p_c=\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2}=\dfrac{48+39}{120+130}=\dfrac{87}{250}=0.348$.在 $H_0$ 下两组共享同一个比例，所以最好的单一估计要合并全部成功数和全部人数：$\hat p_c=\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2}=\dfrac{48+39}{120+130}=\dfrac{87}{250}=0.348$。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. Averages the two sample proportions, $(0.40+0.30)/2=0.350$. That ignores that the groups have different sizes, so group 2 is under-weighted.把两个样本比例取平均，$(0.40+0.30)/2=0.350$。这忽略了两组人数不同，使第 2 组权重偏低。
  2. Divides the total successes by the average group size, $87/125=0.696$. The denominator must be the total number of participants, $n_1+n_2=250$.用总成功数除以平均组容量，$87/125=0.696$。分母必须是总人数 $n_1+n_2=250$。
  3. Correct: total successes over total participants.正确：总成功数除以总人数。
  4. Subtracts the two sample proportions, $0.40-0.30=0.100$. That is the observed difference, the numerator of the test statistic, not a pooled estimate.把两个样本比例相减，$0.40-0.30=0.100$。这是观测到的差，是检验统计量的分子，而不是合并估计。
The pooled proportion is one number for both groups: all successes divided by all participants.合并比例是对两组共用的一个数：全部成功数除以全部人数。

A synthetic experiment randomly assigned 190 students from a school of 3,000 students to two teaching methods. Under Method 1, 52 of 100 students reach a mastery threshold; under Method 2, 39 of 90 students do. Which 95% confidence interval for $p_1-p_2$ is correct?
一项合成实验将某校 3,000 名学生中的 190 人随机分配（random assignment）到两种教学方法：方法 1 下 100 名学生中有 52 人达标；方法 2 下 90 名学生中有 39 人达标。$p_1-p_2$ 的 95% 置信区间（confidence interval）哪项正确？
Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

With $\hat p_1=0.52$ and $\hat p_2=39/90=0.433$, the standard error is $\sqrt{0.52(0.48)/100+0.433(0.567)/90}\approx0.0723$, so the margin is $1.96(0.0723)\approx0.142$ and the interval is $0.087\pm0.142$, about $(-0.055, 0.228)$.由 $\hat p_1=0.52$、$\hat p_2=39/90=0.433$，标准误（standard error）为 $\sqrt{0.52(0.48)/100+0.433(0.567)/90}\approx0.0723$，边际为 $1.96(0.0723)\approx0.142$，区间为 $0.087\pm0.142$，约 $(-0.055, 0.228)$。

Why the options attract (M 20)选项误区（M 20）
  1. Uses $z^{*}=1.645$, which belongs to 90% confidence.误用属于 90% 置信的 $z^{*}=1.645$。
  2. Correct: the difference of estimates with the combined standard error and the 95% multiplier.正确：估计值之差，配以合并标准误与 95% 乘数。
  3. Halves the margin, producing an interval far too narrow for 95% confidence.把边际减半，得到对 95% 置信而言过窄的区间。
  4. Adds the two separate margins; a difference of proportions uses one combined standard error.把两个单独边际相加；比例之差使用一个合并标准误。
A difference of proportions adds the two variances inside one square root and uses one multiplier.比例之差把两个方差相加置于同一个根号内，只用一个乘数。

Answer: D答案：D

Direction follows the order of the groups方向要跟随分组顺序1 point1 分

The null hypothesis is no difference, $p_1=p_2$. The company hopes the app (group 1) lowers the proportion who miss a payment, so the alternative is $p_1\lt p_2$. The direction comes from the wording, and it must match the order in which $p_1$ and $p_2$ were defined.原假设（null hypothesis）是没有差异，$p_1=p_2$。公司希望应用（第 1 组）降低逾期付款的比例，所以备择假设（alternative hypothesis）是 $p_1\lt p_2$。方向来自题目的措辞，并且必须与定义 $p_1$ 与 $p_2$ 的顺序一致。

Why the options attract (M 1)选项误区（M 1）
  1. Reverses the direction relative to the order of the groups. $p_1\gt p_2$ would say the app raises the proportion who miss a payment.相对分组顺序把方向弄反了。$p_1\gt p_2$ 表示应用提高了逾期付款的比例。
  2. Writes the hypotheses about sample proportions. Hypotheses are about the parameters $p_1$ and $p_2$, since the sample proportions will almost never be exactly equal.把假设写成关于样本比例。假设针对的是参数 $p_1$ 与 $p_2$，因为样本比例几乎不可能恰好相等。
  3. Uses a two-sided alternative although the company states a direction in advance. The direction is chosen from the question before seeing the data.公司已事先说明方向，却使用了双侧备择假设。方向应当在看数据之前，从题目中选定。
  4. Correct: equality under $H_0$, and a lower proportion for the app group under $H_a$.正确：$H_0$ 下相等，$H_a$ 下应用组比例更低。
Define the parameters in words, keep the group order fixed, and let the question's wording choose the direction of $H_a$.用文字定义参数，固定分组顺序，让题目的措辞决定 $H_a$ 的方向。

某研究报告 $p_1-p_2$（两总体比例之差）的 95% 置信区间（confidence interval）为 $(-0.02, 0.14)$。哪种解释正确？
Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The interval lists the values of the difference that are plausible given the data and the method. Since 0 is inside, a difference of zero remains plausible, so the study has not demonstrated that the proportions differ.该区间列出在数据与方法下仍属合理的差值取值范围。由于 0 在区间内，差值为零仍属合理，故研究未能证明两比例有差异。

  1. Correct: 0 remains a plausible value, so no difference is established.正确：0 仍是合理取值，故未确立差异。

A month later, 28 of group 1 and 22 of group 2 renew their memberships. The owner will build a 90% confidence interval for $p_1-p_2$, where $p_i$ is the proportion who would renew under schedule $i$. Match each condition to the design按设计逐条核对条件1 point1 分

Independence comes from random assignment of the volunteers to two treatments, so the 10% condition (which protects sampling without replacement) is not needed. The large-counts check uses all four counts: successes $28$ and $22$, failures $40-28=12$ and $40-22=18$, all at least 10. Because the volunteers were not randomly selected, the result applies to volunteers like these, but the conditions for the interval are met.独立性来自把志愿者随机分配到两种处理，所以不需要 10% 条件（它用于保护不放回抽样）。大计数（large counts）检验用到全部四个计数：成功 $28$ 与 $22$，失败 $40-28=12$ 与 $40-22=18$，都不小于 10。志愿者不是随机选出的，所以结论适用于类似的志愿者，但区间的条件已经满足。

Why the options attract (M 5)选项误区（M 5）
  1. Applies the 10% condition to an experiment. It protects sampling without replacement from a population; here independence comes from random assignment, so the check ($40\lt0.10\times400=40$ is false) is irrelevant.把 10% 条件用到实验上。它保护的是从总体中不放回抽样；这里独立性来自随机分配，所以这个检验（$40\lt0.10\times400=40$ 不成立）无关。

Three sample-size plans are considered: (a) 100 from each population, (b) 400 from each population, (c) 100 from the first and 400 from the second. Which plan gives the smallest standard error of $\hat p_1-\hat p_2$, and why?
某研究将比较两个总体比例，规划值取 $p_1=0.40$、$p_2=0.30$。考虑三种抽样方案：(a) 两个总体各取 100；(b) 各取 400；(c) 第一个总体取 100、第二个取 400。哪个方案使 $\hat p_1-\hat p_2$ 的标准误（standard error）最小，为什么？

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The standard error is $\sqrt{p_1(1-p_1)/n_1+p_2(1-p_2)/n_2}$, and both terms shrink as their sample size grows: plan (b) gives about 0.0335, plan (c) about 0.0541, and plan (a) about 0.0671.标准误为 $\sqrt{p_1(1-p_1)/n_1+p_2(1-p_2)/n_2}$，两项都随各自样本量增大而缩小：方案 (b) 约为 0.0335，方案 (c) 约为 0.0541，方案 (a) 约为 0.0671。

  2. Variability adds across the two groups; a small sample on one side keeps its own large contribution.两组的变异性是相加的；某一侧样本小，就会保留其较大的贡献。
  3. Correct: more data in either group lowers the standard error, and (b) uses the most.正确：任一组数据增多都会降低标准误，而 (b) 的数据最多。

The standard error of a difference is built from two separate $1/n$ terms: shrinking either one helps, shrinking both helps most.差值标准误由两个独立的 $1/n$ 项构成：缩小任一项都有益，同时缩小两项收益最大。

A two-proportion test pools the successes: $\hat p_c=(45+44)/260=0.342$. Pooling happens inside the standard error, but each sample contributes its own $1/n$: unequal sizes are exactly where the shortcut fails.合并发生在标准误内部，但每个样本各贡献自身的 $1/n$：样本量不等时捷径必然出错。

A synthetic driver-training study takes a random sample of $9$ drivers from a company with more than $200$ drivers and records braking reaction time, in milliseconds, before and after a workshop. The differences (after minus before) have sample mean $12.0$ ms and sample standard deviation $6.0$ ms, and a dotplot of the nine differences is roughly symmetric with no outliers. The researchers want a $95$ percent confidence interval for the mean change.
某合成驾驶培训研究从一家拥有超过 $200$ 名驾驶员的公司中随机抽取 $9$ 名驾驶员，记录其参加工作坊前后的刹车反应时间（毫秒）。差值（后减前）的样本均值为 $12.0$ 毫秒、样本标准差为 $6.0$ 毫秒，九个差值的点图大致对称且无离群值。研究者希望得到平均变化的 $95$% 置信区间（confidence interval）。

The two measurements come from the same driver, so they are paired and the analysis works with the nine differences: the parameter is $\mu_d$, the mean change in braking reaction time for the population of drivers from which the sample was drawn. Conditions: the nine drivers are a random sample and 9 is less than 10% of more than 200 drivers ($9\lt20$); the dotplot of the differences is roughly symmetric with no outliers, so a $t$ interval is reasonable. With $\text{df}=8$, $t^\ast=2.306$. The standard error is $6.0/\sqrt{9}=2.0$ ms, the margin of error is $2.306(2.0)\approx4.612$ ms, and the interval is $12.0\pm4.612$, about $(7.388, 16.612)$ ms. We are $95$ percent confident that the mean braking reaction time increases by between about $7.4$ and $16.6$ ms after the workshop. (c) 2 points2 分
