Graph Language: Vertices, Edges, Degree, Kinds of Graph, Subgraphs and Trees图的语言：顶点、边、度、图的种类、子图与树 HL AHL 3.14

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Distinguish simple, complete, weighted and directed graphs, and state the number of edges of the complete graph $K_n$.
  • State the handshake lemma and use it to explain why a graph with given degrees cannot exist.
  • Identify subgraphs, decide whether a graph is connected, and recognise a tree.
  • Calculate in-degrees and out-degrees in a directed graph.
  • 定义（define）顶点、边、相邻、环、重边以及顶点的度。

  • 计算（calculate）有向图中各顶点的入度与出度。

Vertices, edges and degree顶点、边与度

A graph is a set of vertices (also called nodes) together with a set of edges; each edge joins two vertices. Two vertices joined by an edge are adjacent. An edge that starts and ends at the same vertex is a loop, and two or more edges joining the same pair of vertices are multiple edges. Only the connections matter: a graph can be redrawn with the vertices moved and the edges bent, and it is still the same graph.

图（graph）由一组顶点（vertices，也叫结点）和一组边（edges）组成；每条边连接两个顶点。由一条边连接的两个顶点称为相邻（adjacent）。起点和终点是同一顶点的边叫环（loop），连接同一对顶点的两条或更多条边叫重边（multiple edges）。图只关心连接关系：移动顶点、弯曲边重新画出来，仍然是同一个图。

The degree of a vertex $v$, written $\deg(v)$, is the number of edge ends at $v$. An ordinary edge adds $1$ to the degree of each of its two end vertices; a loop adds $2$ to the degree of its vertex, because both of its ends are there. A vertex is odd or even according to its degree.

顶点 $v$ 的度（degree），记作 $\deg(v)$，是落在 $v$ 上的边端点个数。普通的边使两个端点的度各加 $1$；环使所在顶点的度加 $2$，因为它的两个端点都在这个顶点上。按度的奇偶，顶点分为奇顶点（odd vertex）与偶顶点（even vertex）。

Kinds of graph图的种类

  • A simple graph has no loops and no multiple edges. In a simple graph with $n$ vertices each vertex has degree at most $n - 1$, because it can be joined to each of the other $n - 1$ vertices at most once.
  • A complete graph $K_n$ is a simple graph on $n$ vertices in which every pair of distinct vertices is joined by an edge. Every vertex has degree $n - 1$, and $K_n$ has $\tfrac{n(n-1)}{2}$ edges (one for each pair of vertices).
  • A weighted graph has a number, its weight, on each edge: a distance, a time or a cost. The weight of a route is the sum of the weights of its edges.
  • A directed graph (digraph) has a direction on each edge, drawn as an arrow; a directed edge is also called an arc. The in-degree of a vertex is the number of arcs arriving at it and the out-degree is the number leaving it.
  • 简单图（simple graph）没有环也没有重边。在 $n$ 个顶点的简单图中，每个顶点的度至多为 $n - 1$，因为它与其余 $n - 1$ 个顶点各至多连一条边。
  • 完全图（complete graph）$K_n$ 是 $n$ 个顶点、每一对不同顶点之间都有一条边的简单图。每个顶点的度都是 $n - 1$，$K_n$ 共有 $\tfrac{n(n-1)}{2}$ 条边（每对顶点一条）。
  • 带权图（weighted graph）的每条边上都标有一个数，称为权（weight），可以是距离、时间或费用。一条路线的权是其各边权之和。
  • 有向图（directed graph，digraph）的每条边都有方向，用箭头表示；有向边也叫弧（arc）。顶点的入度（in-degree）是指向它的弧数，出度（out-degree）是从它出发的弧数。

The handshake lemma握手引理

Every edge has exactly two ends, and each end adds $1$ to the degree of the vertex it sits on. Counting edge ends in two ways gives the handshake lemma:

每条边恰有两个端点，每个端点使其所在顶点的度加 $1$。用两种方法数边的端点，就得到握手引理（handshake lemma）：

$$ \sum_{v} \deg(v) \;=\; 2 \times (\text{number of edges}). $$

So the sum of the degrees is always even, and therefore the number of odd vertices is always even (the even degrees add to an even number, so the odd degrees must also add to an even number, which needs an even count of them). In a directed graph each arc adds $1$ to one out-degree and $1$ to one in-degree, so the sum of the in-degrees and the sum of the out-degrees both equal the number of arcs.

因此度数之和总是偶数，从而奇顶点的个数总是偶数（偶数度之和是偶数，所以奇数度之和也必须是偶数，这要求奇数度的个数为偶数）。在有向图中，每条弧使某个出度加 $1$、某个入度加 $1$，所以入度之和与出度之和都等于弧的条数。

Common misconception. "The degrees add up to an even number, so the graph exists" is not a valid argument. An even sum is necessary, not sufficient: degrees $3, 3, 1, 1$ add to $8$, yet no simple graph has them (each degree-3 vertex must be joined to all three other vertices, so each degree-1 vertex would get degree 2).

常见误解。"度数之和是偶数，所以这个图存在"不是有效的论证。和为偶数是必要条件，而不是充分条件：度数 $3, 3, 1, 1$ 之和为 $8$，但没有简单图具有这组度数（每个度为 3 的顶点都必须与其余三个顶点相连，于是每个度为 1 的顶点的度都会变成 2）。

Worked Example C5.1a: degrees and the handshake lemma例题 C5.1a：度与握手引理

A simple graph has vertices $P, Q, R, S, T$ and edges $PQ$, $PR$, $PS$, $QR$, $RS$, $ST$. (a) Write down the degree of each vertex. (b) Verify the handshake lemma. (c) Explain why no graph can have six vertices of degrees $4, 3, 3, 2, 2, 1$.一个简单图的顶点为 $P, Q, R, S, T$，边为 $PQ$、$PR$、$PS$、$QR$、$RS$、$ST$。(a) 写出每个顶点的度。(b) 验证握手引理。(c) 解释为什么没有任何图能有六个顶点、度数分别为 $4, 3, 3, 2, 2, 1$。

(a) Count edge ends. $P$ lies on $PQ, PR, PS$, so $\deg(P) = 3$. $Q$ lies on $PQ, QR$: $\deg(Q) = 2$. $R$ lies on $PR, QR, RS$: $\deg(R) = 3$. $S$ lies on $PS, RS, ST$: $\deg(S) = 3$. $T$ lies on $ST$ only: $\deg(T) = 1$.

(a) 数边的端点。$P$ 在 $PQ, PR, PS$ 上，所以 $\deg(P) = 3$。$Q$ 在 $PQ, QR$ 上：$\deg(Q) = 2$。$R$ 在 $PR, QR, RS$ 上：$\deg(R) = 3$。$S$ 在 $PS, RS, ST$ 上：$\deg(S) = 3$。$T$ 只在 $ST$ 上：$\deg(T) = 1$。

(b) Verify. Sum of degrees $= 3 + 2 + 3 + 3 + 1 = 12$, and there are $6$ edges, so $2 \times 6 = 12$. The two counts agree. There are four odd vertices ($P, R, S, T$), an even number, as expected.

(b) 验证。度数之和 $= 3 + 2 + 3 + 3 + 1 = 12$，共有 $6$ 条边，$2 \times 6 = 12$。两者一致。奇顶点有四个（$P, R, S, T$），是偶数个，与预期相符。

(c) Explain. $4 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 15$, which is odd. By the handshake lemma the sum of the degrees equals twice the number of edges and so must be even. Hence no graph (simple or not) has these degrees. Another way to see it: the list has three odd degrees, and the number of odd vertices must be even.

(c) 解释。$4 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 15$，是奇数。由握手引理，度数之和等于边数的两倍，必须是偶数。因此没有任何图（无论是否简单）具有这组度数。另一种看法：这组度数中有三个奇数，而奇顶点的个数必须是偶数。

Why this works. Each edge is counted exactly twice when you add the degrees, once from each end.

为什么可行。把度数相加时，每条边恰好被数两次，两个端点各一次。

Worked Example C5.1b: complete graphs and directed graphs例题 C5.1b：完全图与有向图

(a) Find the number of edges of $K_6$ and the degree of each of its vertices. (b) Explain why there is no simple graph with $6$ vertices and $16$ edges. (c) A directed graph has arcs $P \to Q$, $Q \to R$, $R \to P$, $P \to R$ and a loop $R \to R$. Find the in-degree and out-degree of each vertex and check the totals.(a) 求 $K_6$ 的边数及每个顶点的度。(b) 解释为什么不存在 $6$ 个顶点、$16$ 条边的简单图。(c) 某有向图的弧为 $P \to Q$、$Q \to R$、$R \to P$、$P \to R$，以及环 $R \to R$。求每个顶点的入度与出度，并检验总数。

(a) $K_6$ has one edge for each pair of vertices: $\tfrac{6 \times 5}{2} = 15$ edges. Each vertex is joined to the other $5$, so every degree is $5$. Check: $6 \times 5 = 30 = 2 \times 15$.

(a) $K_6$ 每对顶点有一条边：共 $\tfrac{6 \times 5}{2} = 15$ 条边。每个顶点与其余 $5$ 个顶点相连，所以每个度都是 $5$。检验：$6 \times 5 = 30 = 2 \times 15$。

(b) A simple graph on $6$ vertices has at most one edge per pair of vertices, so at most $15$ edges, the number in $K_6$. Sixteen edges would need a repeated pair (a multiple edge) or a loop, so the graph would not be simple.

(b) $6$ 个顶点的简单图每对顶点至多一条边，所以至多 $15$ 条边，正是 $K_6$ 的边数。十六条边就需要某对顶点重复连接（重边）或出现环，那么这个图就不是简单图。

(c) Out-degrees (arcs leaving): $P$ has $P \to Q, P \to R$, so $2$; $Q$ has $Q \to R$, so $1$; $R$ has $R \to P, R \to R$, so $2$. In-degrees (arcs arriving): $P$ receives $R \to P$, so $1$; $Q$ receives $P \to Q$, so $1$; $R$ receives $Q \to R, P \to R, R \to R$, so $3$. Totals: out $2 + 1 + 2 = 5$ and in $1 + 1 + 3 = 5$, both equal to the $5$ arcs. The loop counts once as leaving $R$ and once as arriving at $R$.

(c) 出度（离开的弧）：$P$ 有 $P \to Q, P \to R$，为 $2$；$Q$ 有 $Q \to R$，为 $1$；$R$ 有 $R \to P, R \to R$，为 $2$。入度（到达的弧）：$P$ 收到 $R \to P$，为 $1$；$Q$ 收到 $P \to Q$，为 $1$；$R$ 收到 $Q \to R, P \to R, R \to R$，为 $3$。总数：出度 $2 + 1 + 2 = 5$，入度 $1 + 1 + 3 = 5$，都等于 $5$ 条弧。环既算作离开 $R$ 一次，也算作到达 $R$ 一次。

Why this works. The edge count of $K_n$ is the number of ways to choose $2$ vertices from $n$; on a GDC this is $\binom{n}{2}$ (nCr).

为什么可行。$K_n$ 的边数就是从 $n$ 个顶点中选 $2$ 个的方法数；在 GDC 上就是 $\binom{n}{2}$（nCr）。

Subgraphs, connected graphs and trees子图、连通图与树

A subgraph of a graph $G$ is a graph whose vertices are all vertices of $G$ and whose edges are all edges of $G$ (each joining two vertices of the subgraph). Deleting some edges, or some vertices together with every edge at them, leaves a subgraph. A graph is connected if there is a route along edges between every pair of vertices; a graph in two or more separate pieces is disconnected. A cycle is a closed route that returns to its start without repeating any other vertex (C5.4 makes this exact).

图 $G$ 的子图（subgraph）是这样的图：它的顶点都是 $G$ 的顶点，边都是 $G$ 的边（每条边连接子图中的两个顶点）。删去若干条边，或删去若干顶点以及与它们相连的所有边，剩下的就是子图。若任意两个顶点之间都有沿边走的路线，则图是连通的（connected）；分成两块或更多块的图是不连通的。圈（cycle）是回到起点、且不重复其他任何顶点的闭合路线（C5.4 给出精确定义）。

A tree is a connected graph with no cycles. A tree with $n$ vertices has exactly $n - 1$ edges: removing any one edge disconnects it, and adding any edge between two of its vertices creates a cycle. A spanning tree of a connected graph $G$ is a subgraph that is a tree and contains every vertex of $G$; in a weighted graph the spanning tree of least total weight is the minimum spanning tree of C5.5.

树（tree）是没有圈的连通图。有 $n$ 个顶点的树恰有 $n - 1$ 条边：删去任何一条边都会使它不连通，在它的两个顶点之间再加任何一条边都会产生圈。连通图 $G$ 的生成树（spanning tree）是包含 $G$ 的全部顶点且本身是树的子图；在带权图中，总权最小的生成树就是 C5.5 的最小生成树。

Common misconception. "It has $n - 1$ edges, so it is a tree" needs the graph to be connected as well: two separate pieces (a triangle and a single vertex) have $4$ vertices and $3$ edges but are not a tree. A connected graph with $n$ vertices and $n - 1$ edges is a tree.

常见误解。"它有 $n - 1$ 条边，所以是树"还需要图是连通的：一个三角形加一个孤立顶点，有 $4$ 个顶点、$3$ 条边，却不是树。有 $n$ 个顶点、$n - 1$ 条边的连通图才是树。

Exam tip考试提示

When a question asks you to "explain why" a graph cannot exist, name the result you use ("by the handshake lemma, the sum of the degrees must be even") and show the sum; a bare "impossible" is an incomplete answer. Read the wording carefully: "simple" rules out loops and multiple edges, and a loop adds 2 to a degree. For a directed graph, give in-degree and out-degree separately when asked for "degree".

题目要求"解释为什么"（explain why）某个图不可能存在时，要写出所用的结论（"由握手引理，度数之和必须是偶数"）并写出求和过程；只写"不可能"是不完整的答案。仔细读题："简单"（simple）排除了环和重边，环使度加 2。对有向图，被问到"度"时要分别写出入度和出度。

The adjacency matrix邻接矩阵

Label the vertices $1, 2, \dots, n$ in a fixed order. The adjacency matrix $A$ is the $n \times n$ matrix whose entry $a_{ij}$ is the number of edges from vertex $i$ to vertex $j$. For an undirected graph each edge can be travelled either way, so $a_{ij} = a_{ji}$ and $A$ is symmetric. For a directed graph $a_{ij}$ counts only arcs from $i$ to $j$, so $A$ is usually not symmetric; its row sums are the out-degrees and its column sums are the in-degrees. Multiple edges give entries greater than $1$. A loop at vertex $i$ is entered as $1$ in $a_{ii}$ in this guide (it is one way to step from $i$ back to $i$, which is what walk counting needs), even though it adds $2$ to the degree; check which convention a question states.

按固定顺序把顶点编号为 $1, 2, \dots, n$。邻接矩阵（adjacency matrix）$A$ 是 $n \times n$ 矩阵，其元素 $a_{ij}$ 等于从顶点 $i$ 到顶点 $j$ 的边数。无向图的每条边两个方向都能走，所以 $a_{ij} = a_{ji}$，$A$ 是对称的（symmetric）。有向图的 $a_{ij}$ 只数从 $i$ 指向 $j$ 的弧，所以 $A$ 通常不对称；它的行和是出度，列和是入度。重边使元素大于 $1$。本指南中顶点 $i$ 处的环在 $a_{ii}$ 中记为 $1$（它是从 $i$ 回到 $i$ 的一种走法，这正是通路计数所需要的），尽管它使度加 $2$；做题时要看题目说明采用哪种约定。

Walks and powers of the adjacency matrix通路与邻接矩阵的幂

A walk is a sequence of vertices in which each consecutive pair is joined by an edge; vertices and edges may repeat. A walk of length $k$ uses $k$ edges. The key result is:

通路（walk）是一串顶点，其中每相邻两个顶点之间都有边相连；顶点和边都可以重复。长度为 $k$ 的通路（walk of length k）经过 $k$ 条边。核心结论是：

$$ (A^k)_{ij} \;=\; \text{the number of walks of length } k \text{ from vertex } i \text{ to vertex } j. $$

Why it works. A walk of length $2$ from $i$ to $j$ goes $i \to m \to j$ through some middle vertex $m$. There are $a_{im}$ ways to make the first step and $a_{mj}$ ways to make the second, so $a_{im} a_{mj}$ walks pass through $m$. Adding over every $m$ gives $\sum_m a_{im} a_{mj}$, which is exactly the $(i, j)$ entry of $A \times A$. The same argument, one step at a time, gives $A^3, A^4, \dots$. To count walks of length at most $k$, add the matrices: $A + A^2 + \dots + A^k$. Weighted adjacency tables带权邻接表

A weighted adjacency table lists the vertices along the top and down the side; the entry in row $i$, column $j$ is the weight of the edge joining $i$ and $j$, and a dash (or a blank) means there is no edge. For an undirected graph the table is symmetric, so every edge appears twice; count only the entries above the diagonal when you add up the edges. The table is the natural input for Prim's algorithm (C5.5) and for the travelling salesman bounds (C5.6).

带权邻接表（weighted adjacency table）把顶点列在表头和左侧；第 $i$ 行第 $j$ 列的元素是连接 $i$ 与 $j$ 的边的权，短横线（或空白）表示没有边。无向图的表是对称的，每条边出现两次；统计边时只数对角线上方的元素。这种表正是 Prim 算法（C5.5）和旅行商上下界（C5.6）的自然输入。

Common misconception. The entry for an edge is the weight of that edge, not the shortest distance between the two vertices. A direct road can be longer than a route through another town, as the next example shows.

常见误解。表中某条边的元素是那条边的权，而不是两个顶点之间的最短距离。直达的道路可能比经过别的城镇的路线还长，下一个例题就是这样。

Transition matrices for a random walk on a graph图上随机游走的转移矩阵

In a random walk on a graph, a walker at a vertex moves at each step along one of the edges at that vertex, each edge being equally likely. From a vertex of degree $d$ in a simple graph, each neighbour is chosen with probability $\tfrac{1}{d}$. The transition matrix $T$ records these probabilities. In this guide (as in Unit B5) column $j$ holds the probabilities of moving from vertex $j$, so $T_{ij} = P(\text{next at } i \mid \text{now at } j)$ and every column sums to $1$. If the column vector $s_0$ gives the starting probabilities, then after $n$ steps

在图上的随机游走（random walk）中，位于某顶点的游走者每一步沿该顶点处的某一条边移动，各条边的可能性相同。在简单图中，从度为 $d$ 的顶点出发，每个邻点被选中的概率为 $\tfrac{1}{d}$。转移矩阵（transition matrix）$T$ 记录这些概率。本指南（与单元 B5 一致）用第 $j$ 列表示从顶点 $j$ 出发的概率，所以 $T_{ij} = P(\text{next at } i \mid \text{now at } j)$，并且每一列之和为 $1$。若列向量 $s_0$ 给出初始概率，则 $n$ 步之后

$$ s_n = T^n s_0 . $$

A steady state is a probability vector $s$ with $Ts = s$. For a random walk on a connected graph there is exactly one, and its entries are proportional to the degrees: $s_i = \tfrac{\deg(i)}{\sum \deg} = \tfrac{\deg(i)}{2 \times \text{edges}}$. It gives the long-run proportion of time the walker spends at each vertex. If the graph also contains a cycle of odd length (for example a triangle), $T^n s_0$ approaches the steady state from any start; on a graph with no odd cycle, such as a square, the walker alternates between two sides and $T^n s_0$ need not settle. Markov chains in general are the subject of Unit B5; a random walk on a graph is the special case whose matrix is built from the graph.

稳态（steady state）是满足 $Ts = s$ 的概率向量 $s$。连通图上的随机游走恰有一个稳态，其各分量与度成正比：$s_i = \tfrac{\deg(i)}{\sum \deg} = \tfrac{\deg(i)}{2 \times \text{edges}}$。它给出长期来看游走者在每个顶点停留的时间比例。若图还含有奇数长度的圈（例如三角形），则从任何初始状态出发，$T^n s_0$ 都趋于稳态；在没有奇圈的图上（例如正方形），游走者在两侧之间来回交替，$T^n s_0$ 不一定稳定下来。一般的马尔可夫链（Markov chains）是单元 B5 的内容；图上的随机游走是由图构造矩阵的特例。

Verify the first row of $Ts$: $\tfrac12 \times 0.25 + \tfrac13 \times 0.375 = 0.125 + 0.125 = 0.25$. Third row: $\tfrac12 \times 0.25 + \tfrac12 \times 0.25 + 1 \times 0.125 = 0.375$. The other rows check the same way. Because G contains the triangle $ABC$, $T^n s_0$ approaches this vector: a GDC gives $T^{30} s_0 \approx (0.25, 0.25, 0.375, 0.125)^{T}$.

Why this works. In the long run each edge is crossed equally often in each direction, so a vertex is visited in proportion to the number of edges at it. Interpretation: the walker spends $37.5\%$ of the time at $C$.

为什么可行。长期来看，每条边在两个方向上被经过的频率相同，所以顶点被访问的频率与它所连的边数成正比。解读：游走者有 $37.5\%$ 的时间位于 $C$。

Eulerian trails and circuits欧拉迹与欧拉回路

An Eulerian trail is a trail that uses every edge of the graph exactly once. An Eulerian circuit is an Eulerian trail that starts and ends at the same vertex. Whether they exist is settled by the degrees, for a connected graph:

欧拉迹（Eulerian trail）是恰好经过图中每条边一次的迹。欧拉回路（Eulerian circuit）是起点与终点相同的欧拉迹。对连通图，它们是否存在由度决定：

Degree conditions (connected graph)度条件（连通图）
  • Every vertex even: if a connected graph has every vertex of even degree, it has an Eulerian circuit, and the circuit can start at any vertex. Conversely, if a graph has an Eulerian circuit, every vertex has even degree.
  • Exactly two odd vertices: if a connected graph has exactly two vertices of odd degree, it has an Eulerian trail, every Eulerian trail starts at one odd vertex and ends at the other, and there is no Eulerian circuit. Conversely, if a graph has an Eulerian trail that is not closed, its start and end are the only odd vertices.
  • More than two odd vertices: there is no Eulerian trail and no Eulerian circuit.
  • 所有顶点都是偶顶点：若连通图的每个顶点的度都是偶数，则它有欧拉回路，且回路可以从任一顶点出发。反过来，若一个图有欧拉回路，则它的每个顶点的度都是偶数。
  • 恰有两个奇顶点：若连通图恰有两个奇顶点，则它有欧拉迹，每条欧拉迹都从一个奇顶点出发、在另一个奇顶点结束，并且没有欧拉回路。反过来，若一个图有不闭合的欧拉迹，则其起点和终点是仅有的两个奇顶点。
  • 奇顶点多于两个：没有欧拉迹，也没有欧拉回路。

Why the degrees decide it. Each time a trail passes through a vertex it uses two edges there, one in and one out. So at every vertex other than the start and end, the edges are used in pairs and the degree must be even. In a closed trail the start and end are the same vertex, whose first edge out pairs with its last edge in, so every degree is even. In an open trail the start and end each have one unpaired edge, so exactly those two vertices are odd. The graph must be connected: two separate triangles have every degree even but no single trail can cover both.

为什么由度决定。迹每经过一个顶点一次，就在该处用掉两条边，一进一出。所以除起点和终点以外，每个顶点处的边都是成对使用的，度必须是偶数。闭合的迹起点和终点是同一个顶点，它的第一条出边与最后一条入边配成一对，所以每个度都是偶数。不闭合的迹起点和终点各有一条不成对的边，所以恰好这两个顶点是奇顶点。图必须连通：两个分开的三角形每个度都是偶数，但没有一条迹能同时覆盖两者。

Hamiltonian paths and cycles哈密顿路径与哈密顿圈

A Hamiltonian path visits every vertex of the graph exactly once. A Hamiltonian cycle visits every vertex exactly once and returns to its start (the start vertex is the only one repeated). Unlike the Eulerian case, no simple degree condition decides whether a Hamiltonian cycle exists. To show one exists, write it down and check it. To show one does not, give a reason: for example, a vertex of degree $1$ cannot lie on any cycle (a cycle enters and leaves each of its vertices along two different edges), so a graph with a vertex of degree $1$ has no Hamiltonian cycle. Every complete graph $K_n$ with $n \ge 3$ has a Hamiltonian cycle: visit the vertices in any order and return.

哈密顿路径（Hamiltonian path）恰好经过图的每个顶点一次。哈密顿圈（Hamiltonian cycle）恰好经过每个顶点一次并回到起点（只有起点重复）。与欧拉情形不同，没有简单的度条件能判定哈密顿圈是否存在。要说明存在，就写出一个并检验。要说明不存在，就给出理由：例如度为 $1$ 的顶点不可能在任何圈上（圈进入和离开它的每个顶点要用两条不同的边），所以有度为 $1$ 的顶点的图没有哈密顿圈。每个 $n \ge 3$ 的完全图 $K_n$ 都有哈密顿圈：按任意顺序访问各顶点再返回即可。

Common misconception. Mixing up the two names. Euler covers every Edge; Hamilton visits every vertex. A graph can have one kind of route and not the other.

常见误解。把两个名称混淆。欧拉（Euler）覆盖每一条边（Edge）；哈密顿（Hamilton）访问每一个顶点。一个图可以只有其中一种路线。

What a minimum spanning tree is什么是最小生成树

A minimum spanning tree (MST) of a connected weighted graph is a spanning tree (a tree containing every vertex, C5.1) whose total weight is as small as possible. It answers questions such as "which cables connect every building for the least total length?". With $n$ vertices it always has $n - 1$ edges. If all the edge weights are different, the minimum spanning tree is unique; with equal weights there may be several, all with the same total.

连通带权图的最小生成树（minimum spanning tree，MST）是总权尽可能小的生成树（包含全部顶点的树，见 C5.1）。它回答诸如"用哪些电缆把所有建筑连起来、总长度最短？"的问题。有 $n$ 个顶点时，它总有 $n - 1$ 条边。若所有边的权互不相同，最小生成树是唯一的；若有相等的权，可能有多棵，但总权相同。

Kruskal's algorithmKruskal 算法

  1. List the edges in order of increasing weight.
  2. Take the next edge in the list. Add it to the tree unless it would form a cycle with edges already chosen; in that case reject it.
  3. Stop when $n - 1$ edges have been added.
  1. 把所有边按权从小到大排列。
  2. 取列表中的下一条边。若它与已选的边构成圈，则拒绝它；否则把它加入树中。
  3. 加入 $n - 1$ 条边后停止。

The chosen edges may be in separate pieces in the middle of the algorithm; they join into one tree by the end.

算法进行到中途时，已选的边可能分成几块；到最后它们会连成一棵树。

Prim's algorithmPrim 算法

  1. Choose a starting vertex; it is the tree so far.
  2. Among all edges joining a vertex in the tree to a vertex not yet in the tree, add the one of least weight (and its new vertex).
  3. Repeat until every vertex is in the tree.
  1. 选一个起始顶点，它就是目前的树。
  2. 在所有连接"树内顶点"与"树外顶点"的边中，加入权最小的那条（连同它的新顶点）。
  3. 重复，直到所有顶点都在树中。

Prim's tree is connected at every stage, so it can never form a cycle: the new edge always reaches a vertex outside the tree. This is why Prim's algorithm works well on a table, where cycles are hard to see.

Prim 算法的树在每一步都是连通的，所以永远不会形成圈：新加的边总是通向树外的顶点。这也是 Prim 算法适合在表格上操作的原因，因为在表格中很难看出圈。

Prim's algorithm on a table在表格上用 Prim 算法

When the graph is given as a weighted adjacency table, run Prim's algorithm on the table itself. (1) Choose a start vertex: cross out its row and number its column $1$. (2) Look down every numbered column for the smallest entry that is not crossed out; that entry is the next edge, and its row gives the new vertex. (3) Cross out the new vertex's row and give its column the next number. Repeat (2) and (3) until every column is numbered. Crossing out rows stops you from choosing an edge back into the tree, so no cycle can form.

当图以带权邻接表给出时，直接在表上运行 Prim 算法。(1) 选起始顶点：划掉它所在的行，并把它的列编号为 $1$。(2) 在所有已编号的列中找出未被划掉的最小元素；这个元素就是下一条边，它所在的行给出新顶点。(3) 划掉新顶点所在的行，并给它的列编下一个号。重复 (2)(3)，直到所有列都编了号。划掉行可以防止选到通回树内的边，所以不会形成圈。

The Chinese postman problem中国邮递员问题

A postman, a snow plough or a road inspector must travel along every edge at least once and return to the start, for the least total weight. This is the Chinese postman problem (also called the route inspection problem). If every vertex is even, an Eulerian circuit (C5.4) does the job and the least weight is simply the total weight of the graph. Otherwise some edges must be travelled twice. Repeating the edges of a route between two odd vertices makes both of them even, so the method is:

邮递员、扫雪车或道路巡查员必须把每一条边至少走一次并回到起点，且总权最小。这就是中国邮递员问题（Chinese postman problem，也叫道路巡查问题 route inspection）。若每个顶点都是偶顶点，一条欧拉回路（C5.4）即可完成，最小权就是图的总权。否则必须有一些边走两次。把两个奇顶点之间一条路线上的边重复一遍，就能使这两个顶点都变成偶顶点，所以方法是：

  1. List the odd vertices (there is always an even number of them).
  2. List every way of pairing them up. Four odd vertices $W, X, Y, Z$ give three pairings: $WX\,|\,YZ$, $WY\,|\,XZ$, $WZ\,|\,XY$.
  3. For each pair, find the shortest route between the two vertices (it need not be the direct edge). Add the two shortest routes for each pairing.
  4. Choose the pairing with the least sum. Least route weight $=$ total weight of the graph $+$ that sum. The repeated edges are the edges of the chosen shortest routes.
  1. 列出奇顶点（奇顶点总是偶数个）。
  2. 列出所有配对方式。四个奇顶点 $W, X, Y, Z$ 有三种配对：$WX\,|\,YZ$、$WY\,|\,XZ$、$WZ\,|\,XY$。
  3. 对每一对，求两顶点之间的最短路线（不一定是直接的边）。把每种配对中两条最短路线的长度相加。
  4. 选总和最小的配对。最小路线权 $=$ 图的总权 $+$ 该总和。重复走的边就是所选最短路线上的边。

With exactly two odd vertices there is only one pair: add the shortest route between them. If the route may start and end at different vertices, start at one odd vertex and finish at another; then only the remaining odd vertices need pairing, so leave unrepeated the pair whose removal saves the most.

若恰有两个奇顶点，只有一对：加上二者之间的最短路线即可。若路线可以在不同顶点开始和结束，就从一个奇顶点出发、在另一个奇顶点结束；这样只有其余的奇顶点需要配对，因此应选择使节省最多的那一对不重复。

The travelling salesman problem旅行商问题

In the travelling salesman problem (TSP) a salesperson must visit every vertex and return to the start for the least total weight. In the classical problem each vertex is visited exactly once, so the answer is a Hamiltonian cycle of least weight. In the practical problem each vertex is visited at least once, so passing through a vertex again is allowed when it is shorter. If a table has an entry longer than a route through another vertex (as $XZ = 6$ against $X \to Y \to Z = 5$ in C5.3), first replace every entry by the least distance between the two vertices; the bounds below are then found on that table of least distances. For $n$ vertices there are $\tfrac{(n-1)!}{2}$ different Hamiltonian cycles in $K_n$, which grows too fast to check them all, so we trap the answer between two bounds.

在旅行商问题（travelling salesman problem，TSP）中，推销员必须访问每一个顶点并回到起点，且总权最小。在经典（classical）问题中每个顶点恰好访问一次，所以答案是权最小的哈密顿圈。在实际（practical）问题中每个顶点至少访问一次，所以若更短，允许再次经过某个顶点。若表中某个元素比经过另一个顶点的路线还长（如 C5.3 中 $XZ = 6$ 而 $X \to Y \to Z = 5$），先把每个元素替换为两顶点之间的最短距离；下面的界就在这张最短距离表上求。对 $n$ 个顶点，$K_n$ 中有 $\tfrac{(n-1)!}{2}$ 个不同的哈密顿圈，增长太快无法逐一检查，所以我们用两个界把答案夹住。

Two bounds两个界
  • Upper bound: nearest-neighbour algorithm. Start at a vertex. Repeatedly move to the nearest vertex not yet visited. When every vertex has been visited, return to the start. The length of this tour is an upper bound: it is a real tour, so the best tour is no longer. Different starting vertices can give different tours; the smallest is the best upper bound.
  • Lower bound: deleted-vertex algorithm. Delete one vertex $X$ and every edge at it. Find the weight of a minimum spanning tree of what remains. Add the two smallest weights of edges at $X$. The total is a lower bound. Deleting different vertices can give different values; the largest is the best lower bound.
  • 上界：最近邻算法（nearest-neighbour algorithm）。从一个顶点出发，每次移动到尚未访问的最近顶点。所有顶点都访问后回到起点。这条回路的长度是上界（upper bound）：它是一条真实的回路，所以最优回路不会更长。不同的起点可能给出不同的回路；其中最小的是最好的上界。
  • 下界：删点算法（deleted-vertex algorithm）。删去一个顶点 $X$ 以及与它相连的所有边。求剩余部分最小生成树的权。再加上 $X$ 处两条最小边的权。总和就是下界（lower bound）。删去不同的顶点可能得到不同的值；其中最大的是最好的下界。

Why the lower bound works. Take an optimal tour and remove $X$ from it. What is left is a path through all the other vertices, which is a spanning tree of them, so its weight is at least the minimum spanning tree weight. The two tour edges at $X$ weigh at least the two smallest edges at $X$. So the optimal tour weighs at least the sum. Together: lower bound $\le$ optimal tour $\le$ upper bound. If a lower bound equals an upper bound, the tour that gave the upper bound is optimal.

为什么下界成立。取一条最优回路并从中去掉 $X$。剩下的是经过其余所有顶点的一条路径，它是这些顶点的一棵生成树，所以其权不小于最小生成树的权。回路在 $X$ 处的两条边的权不小于 $X$ 处最小的两条边。所以最优回路的权不小于这个和。合起来：下界 $\le$ 最优回路 $\le$ 上界。若某个下界等于某个上界，则给出该上界的回路就是最优的。

Worked Example C5.6b: nearest-neighbour upper bound例题 C5.6b：最近邻上界

A driver must visit the five warehouses of Worked Example C5.5b (same table) and return. Each entry is no longer than any route through another warehouse, so this is a classical problem. Use the nearest-neighbour algorithm starting at $P$, and then starting at $S$, to find upper bounds.司机必须访问例题 C5.5b 中的五个仓库（同一张表）并返回。表中每个元素都不长于经过另一仓库的路线，所以这是经典问题。分别从 $P$ 和 $S$ 出发，用最近邻算法求上界。

从 $P$ 出发。离 $P$ 最近的是 $R\,(5)$。从 $R$ 出发，未访问的有 $Q\,4$、$S\,6$、$T\,9$：去 $Q\,(4)$。从 $Q$ 出发，未访问的有 $S\,7$、$T\,10$：去 $S\,(7)$。从 $S$ 出发只剩 $T$：$T\,(3)$。返回 $T \to P\,(12)$。回路 $P \to R \to Q \to S \to T \to P$，长度 $5 + 4 + 7 + 3 + 12 = 31$ km。

Conclusion. Both are upper bounds; the better (smaller) one is $31$ km, so the optimal tour is at most $31$ km.

结论。两者都是上界；较好（较小）的是 $31$ km，所以最优回路不超过 $31$ km。

Why this works. Nearest neighbour is greedy: it never looks ahead, so the last edge back to the start can be long (here $TP = 12$). That is why it gives only a bound, and why trying other starts can help.

为什么可行。最近邻算法是贪心的：它从不往前看，所以回到起点的最后一条边可能很长（这里 $TP = 12$）。这就是它只能给出一个界的原因，也是换其他起点可能有帮助的原因。

Exam Strategy and Common Pitfalls考试策略与常见陷阱

Match the problem to the tool按问题选工具
  • Connect every vertex as cheaply as possible (cables, pipes): minimum spanning tree, by Kruskal's or Prim's algorithm.
  • Travel every edge (post, snow ploughs, inspections): Chinese postman; look at the odd vertices.
  • Visit every vertex (deliveries, tours): travelling salesman; give the nearest-neighbour upper bound and the deleted-vertex lower bound.
  • Count routes of a given length: powers of the adjacency matrix. Long-run behaviour of a random walk: transition matrix and steady state.
  • 以最低代价连接所有顶点（电缆、管道）：最小生成树，用 Kruskal 或 Prim 算法。
  • 走遍每一条边（投递、扫雪、巡查）：中国邮递员问题；先看奇顶点。
  • 访问每一个顶点（送货、巡回）：旅行商问题；给出最近邻上界和删点下界。
  • 计数给定长度的路线：邻接矩阵的幂。随机游走的长期行为：转移矩阵与稳态。
Common mistakes常见错误
  • Counting a loop as $1$ in a degree (it adds $2$), or forgetting that "simple" forbids loops and multiple edges.
  • Reading $(A^k)_{ij}$ as a number of paths: it counts walks, which may repeat vertices and edges.
  • Building a transition matrix with rows from and columns to, then multiplying $T s_0$ as if the columns summed to $1$.
  • Saying a graph is Eulerian without checking it is connected, or confusing Eulerian (edges) with Hamiltonian (vertices).
  • In Chinese postman, using the direct edge between two odd vertices when a route through another vertex is shorter.
  • Calling the larger nearest-neighbour tour the "better" upper bound, or the smaller deleted-vertex value the "better" lower bound.
  • 把环在度中算作 $1$（它使度加 $2$），或忘记"简单"不允许环和重边。
  • 把 $(A^k)_{ij}$ 当成路径数：它数的是通路，顶点和边都可以重复。
  • 把转移矩阵建成"行表示出发、列表示到达"，却仍按列和为 $1$ 的方式计算 $T s_0$。
  • 没检查连通就说图是欧拉图，或把欧拉（边）与哈密顿（顶点）混淆。
  • 在中国邮递员问题中，两个奇顶点之间明明经过另一顶点的路线更短，却用了直接的边。
  • 把较大的最近邻回路称为"更好的"上界，或把较小的删点值称为"更好的"下界。
Using the GDC使用 GDC

The GDC raises matrices to powers, adds them, and finds $T^n s_0$ for large $n$; enter the matrix once and reuse it. The graph algorithms (Kruskal, Prim, pairings, nearest neighbour, deleted vertex) are written out by hand step by step, with the GDC used for the sums.

GDC 可以计算矩阵的幂、把它们相加，并对较大的 $n$ 求 $T^n s_0$；矩阵输入一次即可反复使用。图算法（Kruskal、Prim、配对、最近邻、删点）要手写、逐步写出，GDC 只用来求和。
