Topic 6.1主题 6.1

Introducing Statistics: Why Be Normal?统计入门：为什么要用正态？

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe the sampling distribution of a sample proportion $\hat{p}$: its centre, spread and shape.
  • Calculate the mean and standard deviation of the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ from $p$ and $n$.
  • Check the conditions (random sample, 10% condition, at least 10 expected successes and 10 expected failures) that make the sampling distribution approximately normal.
  • Use a normal model to find the probability that a sample proportion falls above, below or between given values.
  • Explain why this sampling distribution is what makes confidence intervals and significance tests possible.
  • 描述样本比例 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布：中心、离散程度和形状。
  • 计算 $\hat{p}$ 抽样分布的均值与标准差（由 $p$ 和 $n$ 得出）。
  • 检验使抽样分布近似正态的条件（随机样本、10% 条件、至少 10 个预期成功与 10 个预期失败）。
  • 用正态模型求样本比例高于、低于或介于给定值之间的概率。
  • 解释为什么这个抽样分布使置信区间与显著性检验成为可能。

The sampling distribution of a sample proportion样本比例的抽样分布

A parameter such as the population proportion $p$ is one fixed number. A statistic such as the sample proportion $\hat{p}$ (read p-hat) changes from sample to sample, because each random sample contains different individuals. The sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ is the distribution of the values of $\hat{p}$ over all possible random samples of the same size $n$ from the same population. It is a model for how far a single sample result can be from the truth by chance alone.

参数（如总体比例 $p$）是一个固定的数。统计量（如样本比例 $\hat{p}$，读作 p-hat）则随样本而变，因为每个随机样本包含的个体不同。$\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布是从同一总体抽取的、容量同为 $n$ 的所有可能随机样本的 $\hat{p}$ 值的分布。它描述的是仅凭机会，单个样本结果可能离真值多远。

When the sample is random and not too large compared with the population, the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ has centre $p$ (the statistic is an unbiased estimator), and a spread that shrinks as $n$ grows:

当样本是随机的，且相对于总体不太大时，$\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布以 $p$ 为中心（该统计量是无偏估计量），其离散程度随 $n$ 增大而缩小：

$$\mu_{\hat{p}}=p,\qquad \sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\frac{p(1-p)}{n}}$$

The formula for $\sigma_{\hat{p}}$ is trustworthy when the 10% condition holds: the sample is no more than 10% of the population, so the individuals are close enough to independent. The shape is approximately normal when the sample is large enough to expect at least 10 successes and at least 10 failures, that is, $np\ge 10$ and $n(1-p)\ge 10$. If $p$ is near 0 or 1 and $n$ is small, the distribution is skewed and a normal model is not appropriate.

$\sigma_{\hat{p}}$ 的公式在10% 条件成立时才可靠：样本不超过总体的 10%，个体才近似独立。当样本足够大，使预期成功数与失败数都至少为 10，即 $np\ge 10$ 且 $n(1-p)\ge 10$ 时，其形状近似正态。若 $p$ 接近 0 或 1 而 $n$ 又小，分布会偏斜，不宜使用正态模型。

Why this makes inference work为什么这使推断成为可能

Because the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ is approximately normal, about 95% of sample proportions land within about two standard deviations of $p$. Read in reverse, a single observed $\hat{p}$ is probably within about two standard deviations of the unknown $p$, which is the idea behind a confidence interval. Read forward from an assumed value of $p$, an observed $\hat{p}$ far out in the tail would be surprising, which is the idea behind a significance test. The rest of this unit is these two ideas made precise.

因为 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布近似正态，约 95% 的样本比例落在 $p$ 的约两个标准差之内。反过来读，一个观察到的 $\hat{p}$ 大概率落在未知 $p$ 的约两个标准差之内，这就是置信区间的思想。从假定的 $p$ 值正向读，观察到的 $\hat{p}$ 若远在尾部就令人意外，这就是显著性检验的思想。本单元其余内容就是把这两个思想精确化。

Worked Example: probability of a sample proportion例题：样本比例的概率

In a city of 60,000 registered voters, 40% support a transit measure. A random sample of $n=100$ voters is taken. Find the probability that the sample proportion is at least $0.50$. (Illustrative scenario.)

  1. Conditions. Random sample; $100$ is far less than 10% of $60{,}000$; $np=100(0.40)=40\ge 10$ and $n(1-p)=100(0.60)=60\ge 10$. The sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ is approximately normal.
  2. Centre and spread. $\mu_{\hat{p}}=0.40$ and $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{100}}\approx 0.0490$.
  3. Standardize. $z=\dfrac{0.50-0.40}{0.0490}\approx 2.04$.
  4. Probability. $P(\hat{p}\ge 0.50)=P(Z\ge 2.04)\approx 0.021$ (normalcdf(2.04,1E99) or normalcdf(0.5,1E99,0.4,0.049)).
  5. In context. If 40% of all voters support the measure, about 2.1% of random samples of 100 voters would show 50% or more in support. Such a sample would be unusual but not impossible.

Why this works: the normal model for $\hat{p}$ turns "how unusual is this sample?" into an area under a curve.

某市有 60,000 名登记选民，其中 40% 支持一项公交提案。随机抽取 $n=100$ 名选民。求样本比例至少为 $0.50$ 的概率。（示意情境。）

  1. 条件。随机样本；$100$ 远小于 $60{,}000$ 的 10%；$np=100(0.40)=40\ge 10$，$n(1-p)=100(0.60)=60\ge 10$。$\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布近似正态。
  2. 中心与离散。$\mu_{\hat{p}}=0.40$，$\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{100}}\approx 0.0490$。
  3. 标准化。$z=\dfrac{0.50-0.40}{0.0490}\approx 2.04$。
  4. 概率。$P(\hat{p}\ge 0.50)=P(Z\ge 2.04)\approx 0.021$（normalcdf(2.04,1E99) 或 normalcdf(0.5,1E99,0.4,0.049)）。
  5. 结合情境。若全体选民中 40% 支持该提案，则约 2.1% 的 100 人随机样本会显示 50% 或更多人支持。这样的样本不常见，但并非不可能。

原理：$\hat{p}$ 的正态模型把“这个样本有多罕见”变成曲线下的一块面积。

Worked Example: sample size changes the spread, not the centre例题：样本容量改变离散程度而非中心

Same population as before ($p=0.40$). Compare the chance that $\hat{p}\ge 0.45$ for $n=100$ and $n=400$.

  1. $n=100$. $\sigma_{\hat{p}}\approx 0.0490$, so $z=\dfrac{0.45-0.40}{0.0490}\approx 1.02$ and $P(\hat{p}\ge 0.45)\approx 0.154$.
  2. $n=400$. $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{400}}\approx 0.0245$, so $z=\dfrac{0.45-0.40}{0.0245}\approx 2.04$ and $P(\hat{p}\ge 0.45)\approx 0.021$.
  3. Compare. Both distributions are centred at $0.40$. Quadrupling $n$ halves the standard deviation, so a sample proportion of $0.45$ goes from fairly ordinary (about 15%) to unusual (about 2%).

总体同前（$p=0.40$）。比较 $n=100$ 与 $n=400$ 时 $\hat{p}\ge 0.45$ 的概率。

  1. $n=100$。$\sigma_{\hat{p}}\approx 0.0490$，故 $z=\dfrac{0.45-0.40}{0.0490}\approx 1.02$，$P(\hat{p}\ge 0.45)\approx 0.154$。
  2. $n=400$。$\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{400}}\approx 0.0245$，故 $z=\dfrac{0.45-0.40}{0.0245}\approx 2.04$，$P(\hat{p}\ge 0.45)\approx 0.021$。
  3. 比较。两个分布都以 $0.40$ 为中心。$n$ 变为 4 倍，标准差减半，于是样本比例 $0.45$ 从较为平常（约 15%）变为不寻常（约 2%）。
Worked Example: when the normal model is not safe例题：正态模型何时不可靠

A manufacturer finds that 5% of its tablets are chipped. Is a normal model reasonable for $\hat{p}$ in random samples of $n=100$? Of $n=400$? (Illustrative figures.)

  1. $n=100$. $np=100(0.05)=5<10$. The condition fails, so the sampling distribution is skewed right and a normal model is not appropriate.
  2. $n=400$. $np=400(0.05)=20\ge 10$ and $n(1-p)=380\ge 10$. A normal model is reasonable, with $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.05(0.95)}{400}}\approx 0.0109$.
  3. Observe. For $n=100$ the standard deviation would be about $0.0218$, nearly half of $p$ itself, so $\hat{p}$ cannot drop far below $p$ but can stretch far above it. That lopsidedness is what a normal curve cannot capture.

某厂发现其 5% 的药片有缺角。对 $n=100$ 的随机样本，$\hat{p}$ 可以用正态模型吗？$n=400$ 呢？（示意数据。）

  1. $n=100$。$np=100(0.05)=5<10$。条件不满足，抽样分布右偏，不宜用正态模型。
  2. $n=400$。$np=400(0.05)=20\ge 10$ 且 $n(1-p)=380\ge 10$。可以用正态模型，$\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{0.05(0.95)}{400}}\approx 0.0109$。
  3. 观察。$n=100$ 时标准差约为 $0.0218$，接近 $p$ 本身的一半，所以 $\hat{p}$ 不可能比 $p$ 低很多，却可能比它高很多。这种不对称正是正态曲线无法刻画的。
Common Mistake: mixing up three distributions

The distribution of the population, the distribution of the one sample you collected, and the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ are three different things. The sampling distribution describes the statistic over many imagined samples. Also, a larger $n$ narrows the sampling distribution but never moves its centre away from $p$.

常见错误：混淆三种分布

总体的分布、你收集的那一个样本的分布，以及 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布是三件不同的事。抽样分布描述的是统计量在许多设想样本中的取值。另外，$n$ 增大会使抽样分布变窄，但绝不会使其中心偏离 $p$。

Exam tip考试提示

Write the check before using the normal model: "random sample, $100$ is less than 10% of all voters, $np=40\ge 10$ and $n(1-p)=60\ge 10$." State $\mu_{\hat{p}}$ and $\sigma_{\hat{p}}$ with the numbers substituted, and answer in context rather than leaving a bare probability.

使用正态模型之前先写出检验：“随机样本，$100$ 少于全体选民的 10%，$np=40\ge 10$ 且 $n(1-p)=60\ge 10$。”写出代入数值的 $\mu_{\hat{p}}$ 和 $\sigma_{\hat{p}}$，并结合情境作答，不要只留一个孤立的概率。

Topic 6.2主题 6.2

Constructing a Confidence Interval for a Population Proportion构造总体比例的置信区间

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate and state a one-sample $z$-interval for a population proportion, using the critical value $z^*$ and the standard error.
  • Check the conditions: random sample, 10% condition, and at least 10 successes and 10 failures in the sample.
  • Interpret a confidence interval and a confidence level in context, and say which one describes the method.
  • Find the margin of error and the sample size needed for a stated margin of error, including the conservative choice $0.5$.
  • Run the procedure on the TI-84 as 1-PropZInt and write it as State, Plan, Do, Conclude.
  • 利用临界值 $z^*$ 和标准误计算并写出总体比例的单样本 $z$ 区间。
  • 检验条件：随机样本、10% 条件，以及样本中至少 10 个成功与 10 个失败。
  • 结合情境解释置信区间与置信水平，并说明哪一个描述的是方法。
  • 求误差范围，以及达到指定误差范围所需的样本容量，包括保守取值 $0.5$。
  • 在 TI-84 上用 1-PropZInt 完成计算，并按 State、Plan、Do、Conclude 四步写出。

The one-proportion z-interval单比例 z 区间

A confidence interval gives a range of plausible values for the parameter $p$. The one-sample $z$-interval for a proportion (also called the one-proportion $z$-interval, 1-PropZInt on the TI-84) has the form statistic $\pm$ critical value $\times$ standard error:

置信区间给出参数 $p$ 的一组合理取值。总体比例的单样本 $z$ 区间（也叫单比例 $z$ 区间，TI-84 上的 1-PropZInt）的形式是统计量 $\pm$ 临界值 $\times$ 标准误：

$$\hat{p}\;\pm\; z^*\sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{n}}$$

The standard error $\sqrt{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})/n}$ estimates the standard deviation of $\hat{p}$; it uses $\hat{p}$ because the true $p$ is unknown. The critical value $z^*$ is the number of standard errors needed to capture the middle $C\%$ of a standard normal curve: $z^*\approx 1.645$ for 90%, $1.960$ for 95%, $2.576$ for 99% (invNorm). The product $z^*\times$ standard error is the margin of error.

标准误 $\sqrt{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})/n}$ 估计 $\hat{p}$ 的标准差；因为真实的 $p$ 未知，所以用 $\hat{p}$ 代入。临界值 $z^*$ 是在标准正态曲线上截取中间 $C\%$ 所需的标准误个数：90% 对应 $z^*\approx 1.645$，95% 对应 $1.960$，99% 对应 $2.576$（invNorm）。$z^*\times$ 标准误就是误差范围。

Conditions条件

Before using the interval, verify and state three conditions: (1) Random: the data come from a random sample or a randomized experiment. (2) 10% condition: when sampling without replacement, $n$ is at most 10% of the population. (3) At least 10 successes and 10 failures in the sample: $n\hat{p}\ge 10$ and $n(1-\hat{p})\ge 10$. For an interval the counts come from the sample, because $p$ is unknown.

使用区间之前，必须验证并写出三个条件：(1) 随机：数据来自随机样本或随机化实验。(2) 10% 条件：不放回抽样时，$n$ 至多为总体的 10%。(3) 样本中至少 10 个成功与 10 个失败：$n\hat{p}\ge 10$ 且 $n(1-\hat{p})\ge 10$。对区间而言，计数来自样本，因为 $p$ 未知。

Interpreting the interval and the confidence level解释区间与置信水平

Interpret the interval (in context, naming the population and the variable): "We are 95% confident that the interval from 0.323 to 0.417 captures the true proportion of all students at the school who walk to school." Interpret the confidence level: if we took many random samples of the same size and built an interval from each, about 95% of those intervals would capture the true proportion. The level describes the method's long-run success rate. It is not the probability that this one interval is correct, because $p$ is fixed and the interval either captures it or not.

解释区间（结合情境，点明总体和变量）：“我们有 95% 的把握认为，从 0.323 到 0.417 的区间覆盖了该校全体学生中步行上学的真实比例。”解释置信水平：若取许多相同容量的随机样本并分别构造区间，约 95% 的区间会覆盖真实比例。水平描述的是方法的长期成功率，不是这一个区间正确的概率，因为 $p$ 是固定的，区间要么覆盖它，要么不覆盖。

Worked Example: a 95% interval for a proportion例题：比例的 95% 置信区间

Topic 6.3主题 6.3

Justifying a Claim Based on a Confidence Interval for a Population Proportion依据总体比例的置信区间论证说法

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Use a confidence interval to decide whether a claimed value of $p$ is plausible.
  • Justify a claim in context by stating whether the interval contains or does not contain the claimed value.
  • Explain why an interval that contains a value does not prove that value is the true proportion.
  • Use the position of the whole interval (all above or all below a value) to support a directional claim.
  • Relate the interval's conclusion to a two-sided significance test at the matching level.
  • 用置信区间判断声称的 $p$ 值是否合理。
  • 结合情境，通过说明区间含不含声称值来论证一个说法。
  • 解释为什么区间含有某值并不能证明该值就是真实比例。
  • 利用整个区间全在某值之上或之下的位置，支持有方向的说法。
  • 把区间的结论与相应水平的双侧显著性检验联系起来。

A confidence interval is the set of values of $p$ that are plausible (consistent with the data) at the stated confidence level. To justify a claim about a population proportion, compare the claimed value with the interval. If the interval contains the claimed value, the claim is plausible: the data are consistent with it, but they do not prove it, because many other values in the interval are plausible too. If the interval does not contain the claimed value, that value is not plausible, which is convincing evidence against the claim.

置信区间是在给定置信水平下合理（与数据相符）的 $p$ 值的集合。要论证一个关于总体比例的说法，把声称值与区间比较。若区间含有声称值，则该说法是合理的：数据与之相符，但并不能证明它，因为区间内其他许多值同样合理。若区间不含声称值，则该值不合理，这就是反对该说法的有力证据。

When the entire interval lies above (or below) the claimed value, you can also say which direction: every plausible value of $p$ is greater (or less) than the claim. A 95% interval and a two-sided test at significance level $\alpha=0.05$ usually reach the same decision, but the two use slightly different standard errors (the interval uses $\hat{p}$, the test uses the claimed $p_0$), so they can disagree when the claimed value sits very near an endpoint.

当整个区间都在声称值之上（或之下）时，还可以说明方向：$p$ 的每个合理取值都大于（或小于）该说法。95% 区间与显著性水平 $\alpha=0.05$ 的双侧检验通常得到相同决定，但二者的标准误略有不同（区间用 $\hat{p}$，检验用声称值 $p_0$），所以当声称值恰好靠近端点时，可能出现不一致。

Hypotheses假设

A significance test weighs the evidence in a sample against a claim. The claim being tested is the null hypothesis $H_0$: it says the parameter equals a specific hypothesized value $p_0$, so it always contains an equals sign, as in $H_0: p=p_0$. The alternative hypothesis $H_a$ is what we suspect or want evidence for. It is one-sided if it points in one direction ($H_a: p>p_0$ or $H_a: p<p_0$) and two-sided if it says only that the value differs ($H_a: p\ne p_0$).

显著性检验用样本中的证据去衡量一个说法。被检验的说法是原假设 $H_0$：它断言参数等于某个具体的假设值 $p_0$，所以总含等号，如 $H_0: p=p_0$。备择假设 $H_a$ 是我们怀疑或想获得证据支持的内容。若它指向一个方向（$H_a: p>p_0$ 或 $H_a: p<p_0$）则是单侧的；若只说取值不同（$H_a: p\ne p_0$）则是双侧的。

the proportion is less than $p_0$ (decreased, fewer than, below)$p<p_0$one-sided (left-tailed)
the proportion is different from $p_0$ (changed, not equal to)$p\ne p_0$two-sided

比例不同于 $p_0$（改变、不等于）$p\ne p_0$双侧

Always define the parameter in words and in context: "$p$ = the proportion of all customers of this bakery who choose a gluten-free item." Hypotheses are about parameters, never about statistics, so $H_0: \hat{p}=0.25$ is wrong.

始终结合情境用文字定义参数：“$p$ = 这家面包店全体顾客中选择无麸质食品的比例。”假设讨论的是参数，绝不是统计量，所以写 $H_0: \hat{p}=0.25$ 是错的。

Significance level and conditions显著性水平与条件

The significance level $\alpha$ is the cut-off for how unlikely a result must be, if $H_0$ is true, before we call it convincing evidence against $H_0$. Common choices are $\alpha=0.05$ and $\alpha=0.01$; choose it before looking at the data. Its meaning as an error rate is the subject of topic 6.7.

显著性水平 $\alpha$ 是临界标准：若 $H_0$ 为真，结果必须多么罕见，才算对 $H_0$ 有令人信服的反对证据。常用 $\alpha=0.05$ 与 $\alpha=0.01$；要在看数据之前确定。它作为错误率的含义见主题 6.7。

The conditions mirror the interval but use the hypothesized value, because the test works in a world where $H_0$ is true: (1) random sample or randomized experiment; (2) 10% condition; (3) the expected number of successes and failures under $H_0$ is at least 10, that is, $np_0\ge 10$ and $n(1-p_0)\ge 10$. These checks use $p_0$, not $\hat{p}$.

条件与区间对应，但使用假设值，因为检验是在 $H_0$ 为真的世界里进行的：(1) 随机样本或随机化实验；(2) 10% 条件；(3) 在 $H_0$ 下预期的成功数与失败数至少为 10，即 $np_0\ge 10$ 且 $n(1-p_0)\ge 10$。这些检验用 $p_0$，不用 $\hat{p}$。

Topic 6.5主题 6.5

Interpreting a p-Value解释 p 值

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define the p-value as the probability, assuming the null hypothesis is true, of a result at least as extreme as the observed one.
  • Find a p-value as a tail area for a right-tailed, left-tailed or two-sided alternative.
  • Interpret a p-value in context, naming the population, the null value and the direction of "extreme".
  • Compare a p-value with $\alpha$ to describe how strong the evidence against $H_0$ is.
  • Explain why the p-value is not the probability that $H_0$ is true.
  • 把 p 值定义为：假定原假设为真时，得到至少与观察结果一样极端的结果的概率。
  • 对右尾、左尾或双侧备择假设，把 p 值求为尾部面积。
  • 结合情境解释 p 值，点明总体、原假设取值以及“极端”的方向。
  • 把 p 值与 $\alpha$ 比较，说明反对 $H_0$ 的证据有多强。
  • 解释为什么 p 值不是 $H_0$ 为真的概率。

The p-value is the probability of getting a sample result at least as extreme as the one observed (in the direction of $H_a$), assuming the null hypothesis is true. It measures how surprising the data are in a world where $H_0$ holds. A small p-value means the observed result would be rare if $H_0$ were true, which is evidence against $H_0$; a large p-value means the result is ordinary under $H_0$.

p 值是在假定原假设为真的前提下，得到至少与所观察样本结果一样极端（沿 $H_a$ 的方向）的结果的概率。它衡量在 $H_0$ 成立的世界里数据有多令人意外。p 值小，说明若 $H_0$ 为真则该结果罕见，这是反对 $H_0$ 的证据；p 值大，说明该结果在 $H_0$ 下很平常。

To find it, standardize to the test statistic $z$ (topic 6.6) and take the area under the standard normal curve in the direction of $H_a$: the right tail for $H_a: p>p_0$, the left tail for $H_a: p<p_0$, and both tails (double the one-tail area) for $H_a: p\ne p_0$.

求 p 值时，先标准化为检验统计量 $z$（主题 6.6），再沿 $H_a$ 的方向取标准正态曲线下的面积：$H_a: p>p_0$ 取右尾，$H_a: p<p_0$ 取左尾，$H_a: p\ne p_0$ 取两侧尾部（单尾面积的两倍）。

The p-value is not the probability that H0 is true

The p-value is not the probability that the null hypothesis is true, and it is not the probability that the result happened "by chance." It is a probability about the data computed assuming $H_0$. A p-value of $0.03$ does not mean $H_0$ has a 3% chance of being true. Under the usual set-up $H_0$ is either true or false and has no probability in this framework.

p 值不是 H0 为真的概率

p 值不是原假设为真的概率，也不是结果“出于偶然”的概率。它是在假定 $H_0$ 成立下计算的、关于数据的概率。p 值为 $0.03$ 并不表示 $H_0$ 有 3% 的可能为真。在通常的框架里，$H_0$ 要么为真要么为假，没有概率可言。

Worked Example: finding p-values from z例题：由 z 求 p 值

Find the p-value for (a) $H_a: p>p_0$ with $z=1.84$, and (b) $H_a: p\ne p_0$ with $z=-2.30$.

  1. (a) Right-tailed. p-value $=P(Z\ge 1.84)\approx 0.0329$ (normalcdf(1.84,1E99)).
  2. (b) Two-sided. One tail is $P(Z\le -2.30)\approx 0.0107$; double it for both tails: p-value $\approx 0.0214$ (2*normalcdf(-1E99,-2.30)).
  3. Sense check. The farther $z$ is from 0 in the direction of $H_a$, the smaller the p-value. A two-sided test has twice the area of a one-sided test for the same $|z|$.

求 (a) $H_a: p>p_0$、$z=1.84$ 与 (b) $H_a: p\ne p_0$、$z=-2.30$ 时的 p 值。

  1. (a) 右尾。p 值 $=P(Z\ge 1.84)\approx 0.0329$（normalcdf(1.84,1E99)）。
  2. (b) 双侧。一侧为 $P(Z\le -2.30)\approx 0.0107$；两侧取两倍：p 值 $\approx 0.0214$（2*normalcdf(-1E99,-2.30)）。
  3. 合理性检查。$z$ 沿 $H_a$ 方向离 0 越远，p 值越小。对相同的 $|z|$，双侧检验的面积是单侧的两倍。

Misconception: "p-value $=0.016$, so there is a 1.6% chance $H_0$ is true." Correct reading: if $H_0$ were true, results this extreme would occur about 1.6% of the time. Another common mistake is a p-value interpretation that leaves out "assuming $H_0$" or the direction of the tail.

常见错误：把 p 值说成原假设为真的概率

误区：“p 值 $=0.016$，所以 $H_0$ 为真的可能性是 1.6%。”正确读法：若 $H_0$ 为真，这样极端的结果约有 1.6% 的时间会出现。另一个常见错误是解释 p 值时漏掉“假定 $H_0$”或尾部方向。

Exam tip考试提示

Leave out the assumption and the interpretation is incomplete.

完整的 p 值解释有四个部分：假设前提（“若……的真实比例为 $p_0$”）、样本统计量及其方向（“样本比例为……或更低/更高”）、样本容量与情境，以及概率。漏掉前提，解释就不完整。

Topic 6.6主题 6.6

Concluding a Test for a Population Proportion得出总体比例检验的结论

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate the one-proportion $z$ test statistic using $p_0$ in the standard error.
  • Find the p-value for the correct tail(s) and compare it with $\alpha$.
  • State the decision (reject or fail to reject $H_0$) and a conclusion in context linked to the p-value.
  • Explain why we never accept $H_0$.
  • Carry out a complete four-step test and verify the answer with 1-PropZTest.
  • 计算单比例 $z$ 检验统计量，标准误中使用 $p_0$。
  • 求出对应尾部的 p 值并与 $\alpha$ 比较。
  • 写出决定（拒绝或不拒绝 $H_0$），以及与 p 值相联系的、结合情境的结论。
  • 解释为什么我们从不“接受” $H_0$。
  • 完成完整的四步检验，并用 1-PropZTest 核对答案。

The test statistic measures how many standard deviations the sample proportion is from the hypothesized value. For a one-proportion $z$-test (1-PropZTest):

检验统计量衡量样本比例离假设值有多少个标准差。对单比例 $z$ 检验（1-PropZTest）：

$$z=\dfrac{\hat{p}-p_0}{\sqrt{\dfrac{p_0(1-p_0)}{n}}}$$

Note that the denominator uses $p_0$, not $\hat{p}$, because the test assumes $H_0$ is true. Find the p-value in the direction of $H_a$, then decide: if the p-value is less than or equal to $\alpha$, reject $H_0$; if it is greater than $\alpha$, fail to reject $H_0$.

注意分母用 $p_0$ 而不是 $\hat{p}$，因为检验假定 $H_0$ 为真。沿 $H_a$ 的方向求 p 值，然后决定：若 p 值小于或等于 $\alpha$，拒绝 $H_0$；若 p 值大于 $\alpha$，则不拒绝 $H_0$。

Write the conclusion in context and tie it to the p-value. If you reject: "Because the p-value of ... is less than $\alpha=0.05$, we reject $H_0$. There is convincing evidence that the true proportion of ... is greater than (less than, different from) $p_0$." If you fail to reject: "Because the p-value of ... is greater than $\alpha=0.05$, we fail to reject $H_0$. There is not convincing evidence that ...". We never accept $H_0$: failing to reject says only that the data do not rule out $p_0$, which is not the same as showing it is true. A large p-value does not prove $H_0$.

结论要结合情境并与 p 值挂钩。若拒绝：“因为 p 值 ... 小于 $\alpha=0.05$，我们拒绝 $H_0$。有令人信服的证据表明……的真实比例大于（小于、不同于）$p_0$。”若不拒绝：“因为 p 值 ... 大于 $\alpha=0.05$，我们不拒绝 $H_0$。没有令人信服的证据表明……”。我们从不接受 $H_0$：不拒绝只是说数据没有排除 $p_0$，并不等于证明它为真。较大的 p 值并不证明 $H_0$。

Worked Example: a left-tailed test, reject H0例题：左尾检验，拒绝 H0

A hospital's historical 30-day readmission rate is $18\%$. After a new discharge program, a random sample of 350 patients (a small fraction of the hospital's patients) has 49 readmitted. Is there convincing evidence that the program lowered the readmission rate? Use $\alpha=0.05$. (Illustrative data.)

  1. State. $p$ = the true proportion of patients under the new program who are readmitted within 30 days. $H_0: p=0.18$, $H_a: p<0.18$, $\alpha=0.05$.
  2. Plan. One-proportion $z$-test. Random: stated. 10%: $350$ is less than 10% of the hospital's patients. Expected counts: $np_0=350(0.18)=63\ge 10$ and $n(1-p_0)=350(0.82)=287\ge 10$.
  3. Do. $\hat{p}=49/350=0.14$. $z=\dfrac{0.14-0.18}{\sqrt{\dfrac{0.18(0.82)}{350}}}=\dfrac{-0.04}{0.0205}\approx -1.95$. p-value $=P(Z\le -1.95)\approx 0.0257$ (1-PropZTest: $p_0=0.18$, $x=49$, $n=350$, $<p_0$).
  4. Conclude. Because the p-value of about $0.026$ is less than $\alpha=0.05$, we reject $H_0$. There is convincing evidence that the true proportion of patients readmitted within 30 days under the new program is less than $0.18$.

某医院历史上的 30 天再入院率为 $18\%$。实行新的出院方案后，随机抽取 350 名患者（只占该院患者的一小部分），其中 49 人再入院。是否有令人信服的证据表明该方案降低了再入院率？取 $\alpha=0.05$。（示意数据。）

Topic 6.7主题 6.7

Potential Errors When Performing Tests检验中可能出现的错误

Learning objectives学习目标

A test can reach the wrong conclusion in two ways. A Type I error is rejecting $H_0$ when $H_0$ is actually true (a false alarm). A Type II error is failing to reject $H_0$ when $H_0$ is actually false (a missed detection). Describe each in context, naming the parameter and the claim.

检验可能以两种方式得出错误结论。第一类错误是在 $H_0$ 实际为真时拒绝了 $H_0$（虚报）。第二类错误是在 $H_0$ 实际为假时没有拒绝 $H_0$（漏报）。要结合情境描述，点明参数和说法。

$H_0$ is true$H_0$ is false
Reject $H_0$Type I errorcorrect (power)
Fail to reject $H_0$correctType II error
$H_0$ 为真$H_0$ 为假
拒绝 $H_0$第一类错误正确（功效）
不拒绝 $H_0$正确第二类错误

The probability of a Type I error equals the significance level $\alpha$: if $H_0$ is true and $\alpha=0.05$, the test falsely rejects about 5% of the time. The probability of a Type II error is written $\beta$. The power of a test is the probability that it correctly rejects a false $H_0$; power $=1-\beta$, and it depends on which alternative value of $p$ is true.

第一类错误的概率等于显著性水平 $\alpha$：若 $H_0$ 为真且 $\alpha=0.05$，检验约有 5% 的时间会错误地拒绝。第二类错误的概率记作 $\beta$。检验的功效是它正确拒绝一个为假的 $H_0$ 的概率；功效 $=1-\beta$，且取决于真实的 $p$ 取哪个备择值。

Power increases when (1) the sample size $n$ is larger, (2) the significance level $\alpha$ is larger (but then Type I errors become more likely), (3) the true value of $p$ is farther from $p_0$ (a larger effect size), and (4) the variability is smaller. Lowering the chance of one error by changing $\alpha$ raises the chance of the other; a larger sample is the only change that helps both.

下列情形功效会增大：(1) 样本容量 $n$ 更大；(2) 显著性水平 $\alpha$ 更大（但第一类错误更易发生）；(3) $p$ 的真实值离 $p_0$ 更远（效应大小更大）；(4) 变异更小。通过改变 $\alpha$ 降低一类错误的可能性会提高另一类错误的可能性；只有增大样本才能同时有益于两者。

To compare two groups, estimate the difference in population proportions $p_1-p_2$ with $\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2$. The sampling distribution of this difference is centred at $p_1-p_2$ and, because the groups are independent, the variances add. The two-proportion $z$-interval (2-PropZInt) is

要比较两组，用 $\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2$ 估计总体比例之差 $p_1-p_2$。该差的抽样分布以 $p_1-p_2$ 为中心，且因为两组独立，方差相加。两比例 $z$ 区间（2-PropZInt）为

$$(\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2)\;\pm\; z^*\sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}_1(1-\hat{p}_1)}{n_1}+\frac{\hat{p}_2(1-\hat{p}_2)}{n_2}}$$

The standard error is unpooled: each group keeps its own $\hat{p}$. (Pooling belongs to the test in topic 6.11, because only a test assumes $p_1=p_2$.)

标准误是未合并的：每组保留自己的 $\hat{p}$。（合并属于主题 6.11 的检验，因为只有检验才假定 $p_1=p_2$。）

Conditions for two groups两组的条件

(1) Independent groups, produced either by two independent random samples or by a randomized experiment with random assignment of units to two treatments. (2) 10% condition for each sample when sampling without replacement (not needed for random assignment to treatments). (3) At least 10 successes and 10 failures in each group: $n_1\hat{p}_1$, $n_1(1-\hat{p}_1)$, $n_2\hat{p}_2$ and $n_2(1-\hat{p}_2)$ are all at least 10. Random sampling supports generalizing to the sampled populations; random assignment supports a cause-and-effect conclusion about the treatments for the units in the study.

(1) 两组独立，来自两个独立随机样本，或来自把试验单位随机分配到两种处理的随机化实验。(2) 不放回抽样时每个样本满足 10% 条件（随机分配到处理时不需要）。(3) 每组至少有 10 个成功与 10 个失败：$n_1\hat{p}_1$、$n_1(1-\hat{p}_1)$、$n_2\hat{p}_2$、$n_2(1-\hat{p}_2)$ 都至少为 10。随机抽样支持推广到被抽样的总体；随机分配支持对研究中的单位作出处理的因果结论。

Zero is the key number: $p_1-p_2=0$ means the two population proportions are equal. If the interval for the difference contains 0, then "no difference" is plausible and the data do not provide convincing evidence of a difference. 0 是关键数：$p_1-p_2=0$ 表示两个总体比例相等。若差的区间含 0，则“无差异”是合理的，数据没有提供差异的令人信服的证据。若区间不含 0且全在 0 之上，则有令人信服的证据表明 $p_1>p_2$；全在 0 之下则表明 $p_1<p_2$。端点还说明了差异可能有多大，这比单纯的“是”或“否”信息更丰富。

Topic 6.10主题 6.10

Setting Up a Test for the Difference of Two Population Proportions设置两个总体比例之差的检验

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Write $H_0$ and $H_a$ for a comparison of two proportions, defining $p_1$ and $p_2$ in words.
  • Choose a one-sided or two-sided alternative from the question wording.
  • Explain why $H_0$ is $p_1=p_2$ and what that says about the difference.
  • Compute the pooled (combined) proportion and use it for the expected-count check.
  • State the conditions for a two-proportion $z$-test.
  • 为两个比例的比较写出 $H_0$ 与 $H_a$，并用文字定义 $p_1$ 和 $p_2$。
  • 依据题目措辞选择单侧或双侧备择假设。
  • 解释为什么 $H_0$ 是 $p_1=p_2$，以及它对差意味着什么。
  • 计算合并（联合）比例，并用它检查预期计数。
  • 写出两比例 $z$ 检验的条件。

The null hypothesis for comparing two groups says there is no difference: $H_0: p_1=p_2$, which is the same as $H_0: p_1-p_2=0$. The alternative hypothesis is $H_a: p_1>p_2$, $H_a: p_1<p_2$ or $H_a: p_1\ne p_2$, chosen from the question before seeing the data. Define each parameter in words, with the group, population and variable, and keep the order of the groups the same throughout: if $p_1$ is the treatment group, $H_a: p_1>p_2$ means the treatment does better.

比较两组的原假设说明没有差异：$H_0: p_1=p_2$，等价于 $H_0: p_1-p_2=0$。备择假设是 $H_a: p_1>p_2$、$H_a: p_1<p_2$ 或 $H_a: p_1\ne p_2$，须在看数据之前由问题决定。要用文字定义每个参数，写明组别、总体和变量，并在全过程中保持组的顺序不变：若 $p_1$ 是处理组，则 $H_a: p_1>p_2$ 表示处理效果更好。

The pooled proportion and conditions合并比例与条件

Because $H_0$ says both groups share one proportion, the best single estimate of that common value combines the two samples. The pooled (combined) proportion is $\hat{p}_c=\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2}$, where $x_1$ and $x_2$ are the success counts. The conditions are: (1) independent groups from two random samples or a randomized experiment with random assignment; (2) the 10% condition for sampling; (3) the expected counts under $H_0$, $n_1\hat{p}_c$, $n_1(1-\hat{p}_c)$, $n_2\hat{p}_c$ and $n_2(1-\hat{p}_c)$, are all at least 10. (Checking the observed successes and failures in each group is a common alternative; in the examples here both checks agree.)

因为 $H_0$ 说两组共享同一个比例，对这个共同值最好的单一估计要把两个样本合在一起。合并（联合）比例为 $\hat{p}_c=\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2}$，其中 $x_1$、$x_2$ 为成功计数。条件是：(1) 两组独立，来自两个随机样本或有随机分配的随机化实验；(2) 抽样的 10% 条件；(3) $H_0$ 下的预期计数 $n_1\hat{p}_c$、$n_1(1-\hat{p}_c)$、$n_2\hat{p}_c$、$n_2(1-\hat{p}_c)$ 都至少为 10。（检查每组观察到的成功数与失败数是常见的替代做法；本节例子里两种检查结论一致。）

Topic 6.11主题 6.11

Carrying Out a Test for the Difference of Two Population Proportions执行两个总体比例之差的检验

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate the two-proportion $z$ test statistic using the pooled proportion.
  • Find the p-value in the direction of $H_a$ and make a decision at the stated $\alpha$.
  • Write a conclusion in context about the difference, with an appropriate statement about causation or generalization.
  • Carry out the test as State, Plan, Do, Conclude and verify it with 2-PropZTest.
  • Explain when to pool (tests) and when not to pool (intervals).
  • 用合并比例计算两比例 $z$ 检验统计量。
  • 沿 $H_a$ 的方向求 p 值，并在规定的 $\alpha$ 下作出决定。
  • 结合情境就差异写出结论，并恰当说明因果或推广。
  • 按 State、Plan、Do、Conclude 完成检验，并用 2-PropZTest 核对。
  • 解释何时合并（检验）、何时不合并（区间）。

The two-proportion $z$-test (2-PropZTest) measures how many standard errors the observed difference $\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2$ is from the null value 0. The standard error uses the pooled proportion $\hat{p}_c$ because $H_0$ says both groups share one proportion:

两比例 $z$ 检验（2-PropZTest）衡量观察到的差 $\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2$ 离原假设值 0 有多少个标准误。标准误使用合并比例 $\hat{p}_c$，因为 $H_0$ 说两组共享同一个比例：

$$z=\dfrac{\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2}{\sqrt{\hat{p}_c(1-\hat{p}_c)\left(\dfrac{1}{n_1}+\dfrac{1}{n_2}\right)}},\qquad \hat{p}_c=\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2}$$

Then find the p-value in the direction of $H_a$ (double the tail for $p_1\ne p_2$), compare it with $\alpha$, and write the conclusion in context. Use the four-step structure: State the hypotheses, parameters and $\alpha$; Plan by naming the test and checking conditions; Do the calculation; Conclude in context, tied to the p-value. Reminder: use the pooled $\hat{p}_c$ only in the test; a confidence interval for $p_1-p_2$ keeps the two separate $\hat{p}$ values.

然后沿 $H_a$ 的方向求 p 值（$p_1\ne p_2$ 时尾部面积加倍），与 $\alpha$ 比较，并结合情境写出结论。使用四步结构：State 写假设、参数和 $\alpha$；Plan 写检验名称并检验条件；Do 完成计算；Conclude 结合情境并与 p 值挂钩。提醒：合并的 $\hat{p}_c$ 只用于检验；$p_1-p_2$ 的置信区间保留两个独立的 $\hat{p}$。

Worked Example: a randomized experiment with a one-sided test例题：单侧检验的随机化实验

A researcher randomly assigns 200 volunteers to use a reminder app (group 1, $n_1=100$) or standard reminders (group 2, $n_2=100$). In the app group 68 followed their plan; in the standard group 54 did. Is there convincing evidence that the app increases the proportion who follow their plan? Use $\alpha=0.05$. (Illustrative data.)

  1. State. $p_1$ = true proportion who would follow their plan with the app; $p_2$ = true proportion with standard reminders. $H_0: p_1=p_2$, $H_a: p_1>p_2$, $\alpha=0.05$.
  2. Plan. Two-proportion $z$-test. Random assignment, so independent groups. Pooled $\hat{p}_c=122/200=0.610$; expected counts $61.0$ and $39.0$ in each group, all at least 10.
  3. Do. $\hat{p}_1=0.68$, $\hat{p}_2=0.54$, difference $=0.14$. $z=\dfrac{0.14}{\sqrt{0.610(1-0.610)\left(\dfrac{1}{100}+\dfrac{1}{100}\right)}}=\dfrac{0.14}{0.0690}\approx 2.03$. p-value $=P(Z\ge 2.03)\approx 0.0212$ (2-PropZTest, $p_1>p_2$).
  4. Conclude. Because the p-value of about $0.021$ is less than $\alpha=0.05$, we reject $H_0$. There is convincing evidence that the true proportion who follow their plan is higher with the app than with standard reminders for volunteers like these. Random assignment supports the cause-and-effect wording; the volunteers were not randomly selected, so we do not generalize beyond similar volunteers.

Common Mistake: wrong standard error or wrong direction

Using the unpooled standard error in a test, or the pooled one in an interval, is a frequent slip. Another is reversing the order: if $\hat{p}_1<\hat{p}_2$ the statistic $z$ is negative, which matters for a one-sided $H_a: p_1>p_2$ (the p-value would then be large, not small).

常见错误：标准误或方向用错

在检验中用未合并的标准误，或在区间中用合并的标准误，都是常见失误。另一个是顺序颠倒：若 $\hat{p}_1<\hat{p}_2$，则统计量 $z$ 为负，这对单侧的 $H_a: p_1>p_2$ 很关键（此时 p 值会很大，而不是很小）。

(b)单比例 $z$ 检验。随机样本；$240\le 0.10(3000)=300$；$np_0=240(0.55)=132\ge 10$，$n(1-p_0)=108\ge 10$。$\hat{p}=150/240=0.625$；$z=\dfrac{0.625-0.55}{\sqrt{0.55(0.45)/240}}=\dfrac{0.075}{0.0321}\approx 2.34$；p 值 $\approx 0.0098$。
(c)因为 p 值约 $0.010$ 小于 $0.05$，我们拒绝 $H_0$。有令人信服的证据表明，菜单调整后该校购买午餐的学生真实比例大于 $0.55$。
(d)条件同上，$n\hat{p}=150$，$n(1-\hat{p})=90$。$0.625\pm1.960\sqrt{0.625(0.375)/240}=0.625\pm0.0612$，即 $(0.564,\,0.686)$。我们有 95% 的把握认为，从 $0.564$ 到 $0.686$ 的区间覆盖了现在购买午餐的学生真实比例。它额外给出了增幅大小的范围；区间内每个值都高于 $0.55$。
(e)在购买午餐比例实际上仍是 $0.55$ 时，断定它已上升到 $0.55$ 以上（例如样本偶然偏高）。
