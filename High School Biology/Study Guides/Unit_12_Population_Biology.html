Population Biology种群生物学

A population is a group of individuals of the same species living in the same area at the same time. This guide covers how populations are described (density, dispersion, age structure), how they grow under unlimited conditions (exponential growth, $\tfrac{dN}{dt}=rN$), how carrying capacity puts the brakes on growth (logistic model), what factors regulate population size, the r vs K life-history trade-off, human population growth and the demographic transition, and how conservation biology responds to biodiversity loss. Worked examples and quiz questions use real data throughout.种群（population，种群）是同一时间、同一地点、同一物种个体的集合。本指南涵盖种群的描述方式（种群密度、分布格局、年龄结构）、无限条件下的增长方式（指数增长，$\tfrac{dN}{dt}=rN$）、环境容纳量如何制约增长（逻辑斯蒂模型）、种群大小的调节因素、r 对策与 K 对策的生活史权衡，以及人口增长与人口转变理论，最后探讨保护生物学如何应对生物多样性丧失。全部例题与测验均以真实数据为据。

Population Characteristics: Density, Dispersion, and Age Structure种群特征：种群密度、分布格局与年龄结构

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State population density as $D_p = N/A$ and calculate it from a count and an area, quoting the unit.
  • Classify a described pattern as clumped, uniform or random dispersion and give a cause for it.
  • Interpret an age-structure pyramid to predict whether a population will grow, stay stable or decline.
  • Estimate a population size with the Lincoln-Petersen formula $N \approx n_1 n_2 / m$ and state its assumptions.
  • 陈述种群密度 $D_p = N/A$，并由个体数和面积计算密度，写出单位。
  • 把所描述的格局判断为集群、均匀或随机分布，并给出一个原因。
  • 解读年龄结构金字塔，预测种群将增长、稳定还是衰退。
  • 用林肯-彼得森公式 $N \approx n_1 n_2 / m$ 估算种群大小，并陈述其假设条件。
Three ways to describe any population.描述任何种群的三种方式。
  • Population density:种群密度： number of individuals per unit area or volume. $D_p = N/A$. High density increases competition and disease transmission; low density risks extinction (Allee effect).单位面积或体积内的个体数目。$D_p = N/A$。高密度加剧竞争与疾病传播；低密度有灭绝风险（阿利效应）。
  • Population dispersion:分布格局：
    • Clumped: individuals cluster near resources or social groups (e.g. wolves in a pack, trees near water). Most common in nature.集群分布：个体聚集于资源或社会群体附近（如狼群、水边树木）。自然界中最常见。
    • Uniform: individuals are evenly spaced due to competition or territoriality (e.g. nesting penguins, creosote bushes).均匀分布：因竞争或领地行为个体均匀分布（如筑巢企鹅、木馏油灌木）。
    • Random: positions are unpredictable; resources are uniformly available and individuals do not interact (e.g. dandelion seeds blown by wind). Rarest in nature.随机分布：位置无法预测；资源均匀分布且个体间无互动（如风吹散的蒲公英种子）。自然界中最罕见。
  • Age structure:年龄结构： proportion of individuals in pre-reproductive, reproductive, and post-reproductive age classes. A broad base (many young) predicts future growth; a narrow base predicts decline. Tracked via age-structure pyramids.处于繁殖前、繁殖中和繁殖后年龄组的个体比例。宽基底（幼年个体多）预示未来增长；窄基底预示种群衰退。通过年龄结构金字塔追踪。

(b) Growing population.(b) 增长型种群。 The pre-reproductive class (60 = 50%) outnumbers both reproductive (40 = 33%) and post-reproductive (20 = 17%) classes. Reading age-structure pyramids读懂年龄结构金字塔

An age-structure pyramid stacks bars for the pre-reproductive, reproductive and post-reproductive classes. The bottom bar shows who will be breeding next, so its width compared with the middle bar predicts the direction of change: wider than the middle is an expanding population, about equal is stable, narrower is declining. Common error: reading a wide middle bar as "growing". A wide middle means many breeders now, but if the base is narrow there are too few young to replace them. The prediction also assumes birth and death rates stay the same, and migration can change it.年龄结构金字塔把繁殖前期、繁殖期和繁殖后期三个年龄组叠放成条形。最下面的条形代表下一批繁殖者，因此把它与中间条形比较即可预测变化方向：比中间宽为增长型，大致相等为稳定型，比中间窄为衰退型。常见错误：把宽的中间条形当作"增长"。中间宽只说明现在繁殖个体多；若基底窄，则幼年个体太少，无法接替。该预测还假设出生率和死亡率不变，迁移则可能改变结果。

Going deeper: mark-recapture method for estimating population size (Lincoln-Petersen)深入：标记重捕法估算种群大小（林肯-彼得森法）

When direct counting is impractical, ecologists use mark-recapture. Catch a sample ($n_1$), mark and release. Later catch a second sample ($n_2$); count how many are marked ($m$). The Lincoln-Petersen estimate is: $$ N \approx \frac{n_1 \times n_2}{m} $$ Assumptions: the population is closed (no births/deaths/migration between samples), marks do not affect survival, and marks do not wash off. SBI4U F2 and Biology 30 D3 both expect students to apply this formula and state its assumptions.当直接计数不可行时，生态学家使用标记重捕法。捕获一个样本（$n_1$），标记后放回。之后再捕第二个样本（$n_2$），统计其中有标记的个体数（$m$）。林肯-彼得森估算公式为：$$ N \approx \frac{n_1 \times n_2}{m} $$ 假设条件：两次取样之间种群封闭（无出生/死亡/迁移），标记不影响生存，标记不会脱落。SBI4U F2 和 Biology 30 D3 均要求学生运用该公式并陈述其假设条件。

Common errors: swapping $m$ into the numerator, and dividing by the wrong area.

指令词：calculate（计算）要写出公式、代入过程和单位（每 km² 的个体数，而不是光写数字）；state（陈述）标记重捕法的假设时，每条用一个短句（种群封闭、标记不脱落且不影响存活、带标记个体重新混入种群）；predict（预测）时通过比较基底与中间条形来判断。常见错误：把 $m$ 放到分子；除以错误的面积。

Section 2 · Exponential growth第 2 节 · 指数增长

Exponential Growth: The J-Curve指数增长：J 型曲线

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate the change in population size $\Delta N = (\text{births} + \text{immigration}) - (\text{deaths} + \text{emigration})$ from counts.
  • Calculate the per-capita rate $r = \Delta N / (N \Delta t)$ and the growth rate $\Delta N / \Delta t = rN$, with units.
  • Project a population with $N_t = N_0 e^{rt}$ and calculate a doubling time with $\ln 2 / r$ or the rule of 70, saying which you used.
  • Explain why exponential growth gives a J-curve and why it cannot continue.
  • 由计数计算种群数量变化 $\Delta N = (\text{births} + \text{immigration}) - (\text{deaths} + \text{emigration})$。
  • 计算人均增长率 $r = \Delta N / (N \Delta t)$ 与增长速率 $\Delta N / \Delta t = rN$，并写出单位。
  • 用 $N_t = N_0 e^{rt}$ 预测种群数量，并用 $\ln 2 / r$ 或 70 法则计算倍增时间，说明用的是哪一种。
  • 解释指数增长为何呈 J 型曲线，以及为何无法持续。
Exponential growth: what happens when resources are unlimited.指数增长：资源无限时会发生什么。
  • Biotic potential ($r$): the maximum per-capita rate of increase a population can achieve under ideal conditions. $r = \text{birth rate} - \text{death rate}$ (per individual per unit time).种群增长潜力（$r$）：在理想条件下种群能达到的最大人均增长率。$r = \text{birth rate} - \text{death rate}$（出生率减去死亡率，每个体每单位时间）。
  • Exponential growth equation: $\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN$. Each individual adds $r$ net new individuals per unit time (births minus deaths), so the growth rate accelerates as $N$ increases. The graph is a J-shaped curve.指数增长方程：$\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN$。每个个体每单位时间净增加 $r$ 个个体（出生减去死亡），因此增长率随 $N$ 增大而加速。图形呈 J 型曲线。
  • Two ways to calculate: over a short step, $\Delta N \approx r \cdot N \cdot \Delta t$; over longer times use the exact continuous solution $N_t = N_0 e^{rt}$ (the form used in this guide). A population with $r = 0$ is stable; $r > 0$ grows; $r < 0$ declines.两种计算方式：短时间步内 $\Delta N \approx r \cdot N \cdot \Delta t$；较长时间使用精确的连续解 $N_t = N_0 e^{rt}$（本指南采用的形式）。$r = 0$ 时种群稳定；$r > 0$ 时增长；$r < 0$ 时衰退。
  • Why it can't last: no real population has unlimited resources. Exponential growth is only a starting model; logistic growth (§3) adds the realistic brake.为何无法持续：现实中没有资源无限的种群。指数增长只是起点模型；逻辑斯蒂增长（§3）加入了现实的制约。
Exponential growth equation指数增长方程
$$ \frac{dN}{dt} = rN $$

$N$ = population size   $r$ = per-capita rate of increase   $t$ = time. Growth rate $\Delta N / \Delta t$ increases with $N$, a hallmark of exponential growth.$N$ = 种群大小 $r$ = 人均增长率 $t$ = 时间。增长速率 $\Delta N / \Delta t$ 随 $N$ 增大而增加，这是指数增长的标志。

From counts to a growth rate由计数得到增长率

Four ways a population changes种群变化的四个途径 Individuals are added by births ($B$) and immigration ($I$) and removed by deaths ($D$) and emigration ($E$). In one time step: $$ \Delta N = (B + I) - (D + E), \qquad r = \frac{\Delta N}{N\,\Delta t} $$ The per-capita rate $r$ is the change per individual per unit time (for example per month), while $\Delta N / \Delta t$ is the change in the whole population. Common errors: reporting $r$ as if it were $\Delta N / \Delta t$, and mixing a percentage with a decimal. A rate of $1.25\%$ per month is $r = 0.0125$ per month. This guide uses the continuous form $N_t = N_0 e^{rt}$ with $r$ as a decimal; for small $r$ the discrete form $N_0(1+r)^t$ gives almost the same answer.个体通过出生（$B$）和迁入（$I$）增加，通过死亡（$D$）和迁出（$E$）减少。在一个时间步内：$$ \Delta N = (B + I) - (D + E), \qquad r = \frac{\Delta N}{N\,\Delta t} $$ 人均增长率 $r$ 是每个个体每单位时间（如每月）的变化，而 $\Delta N / \Delta t$ 是整个种群的变化。常见错误：把 $r$ 当作 $\Delta N / \Delta t$ 报告；混用百分数和小数。每月 $1.25\%$ 即 $r = 0.0125$ 每月。本指南使用连续形式 $N_t = N_0 e^{rt}$（$r$ 取小数）；$r$ 较小时，离散形式 $N_0(1+r)^t$ 的结果几乎相同。
Worked Example 3: Exponential growth calculation例题 3：指数增长计算

A bacterial colony starts with $N_0 = 500$ cells. The per-capita growth rate is $r = 0.4$ per hour. (a) Calculate the growth rate $\Delta N / \Delta t$ at this moment. (b) Predict $N$ after 1 hour using $\Delta N = rN\Delta t$.一个细菌菌落初始 $N_0 = 500$ 个细胞，人均增长率 $r = 0.4$/小时。(a) 计算此时的增长率 $\Delta N / \Delta t$。(b) 使用 $\Delta N = rN\Delta t$ 预测 1 小时后的 $N$。

(a) $$ \frac{dN}{dt} = rN = 0.4 \times 500 = 200 \text{ cells/hour} $$

(b) $$ \Delta N = r \cdot N \cdot \Delta t = 0.4 \times 500 \times 1 = 200 $$ $$ N_1 \approx 500 + 200 = 700 \text{ cells (approximately)} $$

The one-step estimate is approximate because $r\,\Delta t = 0.4$ is not small: the exact exponential value is $500\,e^{0.4} \approx 746$ cells, so the one-step estimate is about $46$ cells too low.单步估算只是近似，因为 $r\,\Delta t = 0.4$ 并不小：精确的指数值为 $500\,e^{0.4} \approx 746$ 个细胞，所以单步估算约低了 $46$ 个。

The r vs K trade-off: two ends of a continuum.r 对策与 K 对策的权衡：连续体的两端。
  • r-selected species:r 对策生物： maximise $r$, the intrinsic growth rate. Traits: short lifespan, early maturity, many small offspring, low parental investment, high mortality. Populations boom rapidly and often overshoot $K$. Common in unstable/disturbed environments. Examples: insects, mice, annual plants, bacteria.最大化内禀增长率 $r$。特征：寿命短、成熟早、后代多而小、亲本投资少、死亡率高。种群快速爆发，常超过 $K$。多见于不稳定/受扰动的环境。例如：昆虫、小鼠、一年生植物、细菌。
  • K-selected species:K 对策生物： populations maintained near $K$. Traits: long lifespan, late maturity, few large offspring, high parental investment, low mortality. Stable populations close to carrying capacity. Common in stable, competitive environments. Examples: elephants, whales, humans, oak trees.种群维持在接近 $K$ 的水平。特征：寿命长、成熟晚、后代少而大、亲本投资多、死亡率低。种群稳定，接近环境容纳量。多见于稳定、竞争激烈的环境。例如：大象、鲸、人类、橡树。

Survivorship curves存活曲线

A survivorship curve follows a cohort from birth and plots how many are still alive at each age; $l_x$ is the number alive at the start of age interval $x$. The y-axis is usually logarithmic, so a straight line means a constant death rate per individual. Type I (many large mammals, humans with good care): few die young and most die near the maximum lifespan. Type II (many songbirds, some lizards): a steady chance of dying at every age, a straight line. Type III (most fish, marine invertebrates, trees that shed thousands of seeds): most die very young, but those that survive the early stage then die slowly. Type I usually goes with few offspring and heavy parental care (K-selected); Type III with huge numbers of offspring and no care (r-selected). Common errors: treating Type III as "short-lived adults" (it is early mortality), and treating the types as exact labels when real species fall between them.存活曲线追踪一个同生群从出生起的存活情况，画出每个年龄仍存活的个体数；$l_x$ 表示年龄区间 $x$ 开始时仍存活的个体数。纵轴通常取对数，所以直线表示每个个体的死亡率恒定。I 型（许多大型哺乳动物，以及得到良好照护的人类）：幼年死亡少，多数个体死于接近最大寿命时。II 型（许多鸣禽、部分蜥蜴）：各年龄的死亡概率恒定，曲线为直线。III 型（多数鱼类、海洋无脊椎动物、产生成千上万种子的树木）：大多数个体在很年幼时死亡，但度过早期的个体此后死亡缓慢。I 型通常对应后代少、亲本照顾多（K 对策）；III 型对应后代极多、无照顾（r 对策）。常见错误：把 III 型当作"成体寿命短"（其实是早期死亡率高）；把三种类型当作精确标签，而真实物种往往介于其间。

Natural increase and population momentum自然增长与人口惯性

Natural increase自然增长 Demographers give the crude birth rate (CBR) and crude death rate (CDR) per $1000$ people per year. Natural increase $= \text{CBR} - \text{CDR}$ per $1000$, which divided by $10$ is a percent per year (ignoring migration). In Stage 2 the gap is wide; in Stages 1 and 4 it is close to zero. Population momentum: a country with many young people keeps having many births for decades after the number of children per woman falls, because there are so many new parents; this is why the global total still rises while the percent rate falls. The model is a description of many countries, not a law that every country must follow.人口学家用每年每 $1000$ 人的粗出生率（CBR）和粗死亡率（CDR）来表示。自然增长 $= \text{CBR} - \text{CDR}$（每 $1000$ 人），除以 $10$ 即为每年的百分比（不计迁移）。第 2 阶段两者差距很大；第 1 阶段和第 4 阶段差距接近零。人口惯性：年轻人口众多的国家，即使每位妇女的生育数下降，出生人数仍会持续数十年居高不下，因为新增的父母很多；这就是全球总人口在百分比增长率下降时仍在上升的原因。该模型是对许多国家的描述，而不是每个国家都必须遵循的规律。

Assume the density at $K$ stays the same in the remaining habitat, that the current population is $150$, and that a minimum viable population (MVP) of $100$ is assumed for this species. (a) Calculate $K$ before and after. (b) By how much does the current population exceed the new $K$? (c) How does the new $K$ compare with the MVP?一个 $500\ \text{km}^2$ 的保护区在种群处于 $K$ 时，每 km$^2$ 可养活 $0.4$ 只类似鹿的动物。采伐移除了 $60\%$ 的栖息地。假设剩余栖息地中处于 $K$ 时的密度不变，现有种群为 $150$ 只，并假定该物种的最小可存活种群（MVP）为 $100$。(a) 计算前后的 $K$。(b) 现有种群比新的 $K$ 多多少？(c) 新的 $K$ 与 MVP 比较如何？

(a) Before: $K = 0.4 \times 500 = 200$ animals. After: the area is $500 \times 0.40 = 200\ \text{km}^2$, so $K = 0.4 \times 200 = 80$ animals.(a) 之前：$K = 0.4 \times 500 = 200$ 只。之后：面积为 $500 \times 0.40 = 200\ \text{km}^2$，所以 $K = 0.4 \times 200 = 80$ 只。

(b) $150 - 80 = 70$ animals above the new $K$, so $r < 0$ and the population should fall toward $80$ (an overshoot).(b) $150 - 80 = 70$ 只高于新的 $K$，所以 $r < 0$，种群应向 $80$ 下降（超调）。

(c) $80$ is below the assumed MVP of $100$. Even at its carrying capacity, the population would be too small to persist for the long term, because of inbreeding depression, random events and Allee effects. Protecting or restoring habitat is needed, not only counting animals.(c) $80$ 低于假定的 MVP $100$。即使达到环境容纳量，种群也过小，无法长期存续，原因包括近交衰退、随机事件和阿利效应。需要保护或恢复栖息地，而不仅仅是清点个体。

Why this works: $K$ scales with usable area, so cutting habitat cuts $K$ in the same proportion. The result is an idealised estimate that ignores edge effects and fragmentation.为什么这样做：$K$ 与可用面积成正比，所以减少栖息地会使 $K$ 按同样比例降低。该结果是理想化的估计，未考虑边缘效应和栖息地破碎。

Worked Example 14: Recovery time from captive breeding例题 14：圈养繁殖的恢复时间

A captive-bred population of $12$ animals is released into protected habitat. Assume exponential growth with $r = 0.15$ per year and no limits. (a) How long to reach $100$ animals? (b) What is the doubling time? (c) How long if $r = 0.05$ per year, as for a slow-breeding species? (d) Give two reasons real recovery may be slower than (a).将一个由 $12$ 只个体组成的圈养繁殖种群放归保护栖息地。假设指数增长，$r = 0.15$ 每年，且无任何限制。(a) 到达 $100$ 只需要多长时间？(b) 倍增时间是多少？(c) 若 $r = 0.05$ 每年（繁殖缓慢的物种），需要多长时间？(d) 给出真实恢复可能比 (a) 更慢的两个原因。

(a) $t = \dfrac{\ln(100/12)}{0.15} = \dfrac{2.12}{0.15} \approx 14.1$ years.(a) $t = \dfrac{\ln(100/12)}{0.15} = \dfrac{2.12}{0.15} \approx 14.1$ 年。

(b) $t_d = \ln 2 / 0.15 = 4.6$ years.(b) $t_d = \ln 2 / 0.15 = 4.6$ 年。

(c) $t = 2.12 / 0.05 \approx 42.4$ years, three times as long, because $t$ is inversely proportional to $r$.(c) $t = 2.12 / 0.05 \approx 42.4$ 年，是前者的三倍，因为 $t$ 与 $r$ 成反比。

(d) For example: inbreeding depression lowers survival and fertility in a small founder group; Allee effects make it hard to find mates at low density; habitat and food limit growth as $N$ approaches $K$, so $r$ does not stay constant.(d) 例如：近交衰退使很小的奠基群体存活率和生育力下降；阿利效应使低密度时难以找到配偶；$N$ 接近 $K$ 时栖息地和食物限制增长，$r$ 不会保持恒定。

Common Questions常见问题

What is the difference between exponential and logistic growth?指数增长和逻辑斯蒂增长有什么区别？

Exponential growth produces a J-curve with no limits on resources, while logistic growth produces an S-curve that levels off at the carrying capacity, the maximum population an environment can sustain.指数增长产生不受资源限制的 J 形曲线，而逻辑斯蒂增长则产生在环境容纳量处趋于平缓的 S 形曲线，环境容纳量即环境所能维持的最大种群数量。

What is carrying capacity in population biology?种群生物学中的环境容纳量是什么？

Carrying capacity, written as K, is the largest population size an environment can support long term given its resources. Growth slows as a population nears K and stabilizes around it.环境容纳量，记作 K，是指环境凭借其资源所能长期维持的最大种群数量。当种群接近 K 时增长放缓，并在 K 附近趋于稳定。

What is the difference between r-selected and K-selected species?r 选择物种和 K 选择物种有什么区别？

r-selected species reproduce quickly with many offspring and little parental care, thriving in unstable environments. K-selected species have few offspring with high care and do well in stable ones.r 选择物种繁殖快、后代多、亲代照顾少，在不稳定的环境中繁盛。K 选择物种后代少但照顾多，在稳定的环境中表现良好。
