Density and Pressure密度与压强

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate the density of an object or fluid from $\rho = m/V$, and convert between g/cm³ and kg/m³.
  • Calculate the pressure exerted by a force spread over an area from $P = F_\perp/A$, in pascals.
  • Explain why pressure is a scalar and why the force it produces always acts perpendicular to a surface.
  • Distinguish absolute pressure from gauge pressure and convert between them using $P_\text{gauge} = P - P_\text{atm}$.
  • Compare the pressures produced by the same force on different contact areas.
  • 由 $\rho = m/V$ 计算（calculate）物体或流体的密度，并在 g/cm³ 与 kg/m³ 之间换算。
  • 由 $P = F_\perp/A$ 计算（calculate）力分布在一定面积上产生的压强，单位为帕斯卡（pascal）。
  • 解释（explain）为什么压强是标量，以及为什么它产生的力总是垂直于表面。
  • 区分（distinguish）绝对压强与表压（gauge pressure），并用 $P_\text{gauge} = P - P_\text{atm}$ 互相换算。
  • 比较（compare）同一个力作用在不同接触面积上产生的压强。

Density密度

A fluid is any substance that flows: liquids and gases. Because a fluid has no fixed shape, we describe it by quantities per unit volume rather than by its total mass. The first of these is density, the mass per unit volume. Its SI unit is kg/m³. Fresh water has a density of about $1000\;\text{kg/m}^3$ (that is, $1.00\;\text{g/cm}^3$), seawater about $1025\;\text{kg/m}^3$, and air at sea level only about $1.2\;\text{kg/m}^3$.

流体（fluid）是任何能流动的物质：液体和气体。流体没有固定形状，所以我们用"单位体积的量"而不是总质量来描述它。第一个这样的量是密度（density），即单位体积的质量，国际单位为 kg/m³。淡水的密度约为 $1000\;\text{kg/m}^3$（即 $1.00\;\text{g/cm}^3$），海水约为 $1025\;\text{kg/m}^3$，而海平面附近的空气只有约 $1.2\;\text{kg/m}^3$。

Density密度
$$\rho = \frac{m}{V}$$

Density is a property of the material, not of the sample: cutting a block of aluminum in half halves both $m$ and $V$, so $\rho$ is unchanged. To convert, use $1\;\text{g/cm}^3 = 1000\;\text{kg/m}^3$, because $1\;\text{g} = 10^{-3}\;\text{kg}$ and $1\;\text{cm}^3 = 10^{-6}\;\text{m}^3$.

密度是材料的性质，而不是某一块样品的性质：把一块铝切成两半，$m$ 和 $V$ 都减半，$\rho$ 不变。换算时用 $1\;\text{g/cm}^3 = 1000\;\text{kg/m}^3$，因为 $1\;\text{g} = 10^{-3}\;\text{kg}$，$1\;\text{cm}^3 = 10^{-6}\;\text{m}^3$。

Pressure压强

Pressure is the magnitude of the perpendicular force per unit area. Its SI unit is the pascal: $1\;\text{Pa} = 1\;\text{N/m}^2$. The same force produces a large pressure on a small area and a small pressure on a large area, which is why a knife edge cuts and snowshoes do not sink.

压强（pressure）是单位面积上垂直作用力的大小，国际单位为帕斯卡：$1\;\text{Pa} = 1\;\text{N/m}^2$。同一个力作用在小面积上压强大、作用在大面积上压强小，这就是刀刃能切东西而雪鞋不会陷下去的原因。

Pressure压强
$$P = \frac{F_\perp}{A}$$

Inside a fluid at rest, pressure at a point is the same in every direction, so pressure is a scalar: it has no direction of its own. The force that a fluid exerts on any surface it touches, however, is a vector, and it always points perpendicular to that surface, pushing on it. That is why water pushes sideways on the wall of a pool and upward on the bottom of a boat.

在静止流体内部，某一点的压强在各个方向上都相同，所以压强是标量（scalar）：它本身没有方向。但流体对它接触的任何表面施加的力是矢量，而且总是垂直于该表面、推向该表面。这就是水会侧向推泳池的池壁、向上推船底的原因。

Absolute and Gauge Pressure绝对压强与表压

The air above us presses on everything with atmospheric pressure, $P_\text{atm} \approx 1.01 \times 10^5\;\text{Pa}$ at sea level. The absolute pressure $P$ is the total pressure measured from a perfect vacuum. Most gauges (a tire gauge, a blood-pressure cuff) read only the amount above atmospheric pressure, called the gauge pressure.

我们头顶的空气以大气压强压在一切物体上，海平面处 $P_\text{atm} \approx 1.01 \times 10^5\;\text{Pa}$。绝对压强（absolute pressure）$P$ 是从理想真空算起的总压强。大多数压力表（轮胎气压表、血压计）只读出高于大气压的部分，称为表压（gauge pressure）。

Gauge Pressure表压
$$P_\text{gauge} = P - P_\text{atm}$$
Common Mistake: Mixing absolute and gauge pressure A tire gauge that reads $2.0 \times 10^5\;\text{Pa}$ means the air inside is at an absolute pressure of about $3.0 \times 10^5\;\text{Pa}$. Read the question for the words "absolute" or "gauge", and use one kind consistently through a calculation.
常见错误：混用绝对压强与表压 轮胎气压表读数为 $2.0 \times 10^5\;\text{Pa}$，意味着胎内空气的绝对压强约为 $3.0 \times 10^5\;\text{Pa}$。注意题目中的"绝对"（absolute）或"表压"（gauge）字样，并在一次计算中始终使用同一种压强。

一块长方体金属块的尺寸为 $10.0\;\text{cm} \times 5.0\;\text{cm} \times 2.0\;\text{cm}$，质量为 $0.270\;\text{kg}$。求它的密度（单位 kg/m³），并判断它在水中是否下沉。

一个 $60\;\text{kg}$ 的人穿平底鞋站立，两只鞋与地面的总接触面积为 $0.030\;\text{m}^2$。之后这个人把全部体重压在一只面积为 $1.0\;\text{cm}^2$ 的细高跟上。分别求两种情况下对地面的压强。取 $g = 9.8\;\text{m/s}^2$。

Step 1: Force on the floor第 1 步：对地面的压力
$$F_\perp = mg = (60)(9.8) = 588\;\text{N}$$
Step 2: Flat shoes第 2 步：平底鞋
$$P = \frac{588}{0.030} = 1.96 \times 10^{4}\;\text{Pa}$$

  • Sketch and interpret a graph of pressure against depth, identifying the slope as $\rho g$ and the intercept as $P_0$.
  • State Pascal's principle and apply it to a hydraulic lift, calculating the force on each piston and the distance each moves.
  • 由一段处于平衡的流体柱的受力推导（derive）$P = P_0 + \rho g h$。
  • 计算（calculate）液体中某一深度处的绝对压强与表压。
  • 解释（explain）为什么某一深度处的压强与容器形状无关。

Pressure Increases with Depth压强随深度增加

Picture a vertical column of liquid of cross-sectional area $A$ and height $h$, with its top at the surface, where the pressure is $P_0$. The column is at rest, so the forces on it balance. Downward: the push $P_0 A$ on its top and its weight $mg = \rho (Ah) g$. Upward: the push $PA$ from the liquid below its bottom. Setting $PA = P_0 A + \rho A h g$ and dividing by $A$ gives the hydrostatic pressure equation.

设想一段截面积为 $A$、高为 $h$ 的竖直液柱，它的顶面在液面处，那里的压强为 $P_0$。液柱静止，所以受力平衡。向下：顶面受到的推力 $P_0 A$ 和液柱的重力 $mg = \rho (Ah) g$。向上：下方液体对底面的推力 $PA$。令 $PA = P_0 A + \rho A h g$，两边除以 $A$，就得到静水压强（hydrostatic pressure）公式。

Pressure at Depth h深度 h 处的压强
$$P = P_0 + \rho g h$$

Here $\rho$ is the density of the liquid, $h$ is the vertical depth below the surface, and $P_0$ is the pressure at the surface (for an open container, $P_0 = P_\text{atm}$). The term $\rho g h$ on its own is the gauge pressure at depth $h$. Because the liquid is treated as incompressible, $\rho$ is the same at every depth and $P$ rises linearly with $h$: a straight line of slope $\rho g$, as Figure 8.1 shows.

其中 $\rho$ 是液体的密度，$h$ 是液面以下的竖直深度，$P_0$ 是液面处的压强（敞口容器中 $P_0 = P_\text{atm}$）。单独一项 $\rho g h$ 就是深度 $h$ 处的表压。由于液体被视为不可压缩，各深度处 $\rho$ 相同，$P$ 随 $h$ 线性增大：一条斜率为 $\rho g$ 的直线，如图 8.1 所示。

Key Insight: Only depth matters In a connected body of one liquid at rest, every point at the same depth has the same pressure, whatever the shape of the container. A narrow tube and a wide tank filled to the same height have the same pressure at the bottom, even though the tank holds far more water. The wide tank's bottom does feel a larger total force, $F = PA$, but only because its area $A$ is larger; the pressure is the same. In a container with sloped or stepped walls, those walls carry part of the water's weight, so the bottom force can differ from the weight of the water.
关键洞察：只与深度有关 在连通的、静止的同一种液体中，同一深度处各点的压强相同，与容器形状无关。注满到同一高度的细管和宽水箱，底部压强相同，尽管水箱装的水多得多。宽水箱底面受到的总压力 $F = PA$ 确实更大，但这只是因为它的面积 $A$ 更大；压强是相同的。器壁倾斜或呈台阶状的容器中，器壁承担了部分水的重力，所以底面受到的压力可以不等于水的重力。
Worked Example: Pressure on a Diver例题：潜水员受到的压强

A diver swims $15\;\text{m}$ below the surface of the sea ($\rho = 1025\;\text{kg/m}^3$). Find the gauge pressure and the absolute pressure on the diver. Take $P_\text{atm} = 1.01 \times 10^5\;\text{Pa}$ and $g = 9.8\;\text{m/s}^2$.

一名潜水员在海面以下 $15\;\text{m}$ 处游动（海水 $\rho = 1025\;\text{kg/m}^3$）。求潜水员受到的表压和绝对压强。取 $P_\text{atm} = 1.01 \times 10^5\;\text{Pa}$，$g = 9.8\;\text{m/s}^2$。

Step 1: Gauge pressure第 1 步：表压
$$P_\text{gauge} = \rho g h = (1025)(9.8)(15) = 1.51 \times 10^{5}\;\text{Pa}$$
Step 2: Absolute pressure第 2 步：绝对压强
$$P = P_\text{atm} + \rho g h = 1.01 \times 10^{5} + 1.51 \times 10^{5} = 2.52 \times 10^{5}\;\text{Pa}$$
The unrounded gauge value is $150{,}675\;\text{Pa}$, so the absolute pressure is about $2.5$ times atmospheric pressure. Why this works: the water above the diver adds $\rho g h$ on top of the air pressing on the sea surface.表压的未取整值为 $150{,}675\;\text{Pa}$，所以绝对压强约为大气压的 $2.5$ 倍。为什么这样做：潜水员上方的海水在压在海面上的大气压之外又增加了 $\rho g h$。

Pascal's Principle and the Hydraulic Lift帕斯卡原理与液压机

Pascal's principle: a change in pressure applied to an enclosed, incompressible fluid is transmitted undiminished to every part of the fluid and to the walls of its container. This follows from $P = P_0 + \rho g h$: if $P_0$ rises by $\Delta P$, the pressure at every depth rises by the same $\Delta P$.

帕斯卡原理（Pascal's principle）：施加在封闭的不可压缩流体上的压强变化，会大小不变地传递到流体的每一部分以及容器壁上。这可以由 $P = P_0 + \rho g h$ 得出：如果 $P_0$ 增加 $\Delta P$，每个深度处的压强都增加同样的 $\Delta P$。

A hydraulic lift uses this. A small force $F_1$ on a small piston of area $A_1$ raises the pressure by $F_1/A_1$; the same pressure acts on a large piston of area $A_2$ at the same level, which pushes up with $F_2 = (F_1/A_1)A_2$. The force is multiplied by $A_2/A_1$. Energy is not: the liquid volume pushed out of the small cylinder, $A_1 d_1$, equals the volume pushed into the large one, $A_2 d_2$, so the small piston must move much farther, and $F_1 d_1 = F_2 d_2$.

Where the Buoyant Force Comes From浮力从何而来

Hold a block of height $H$ and face area $A$ fully under water. The water pushes down on its top face at depth $h$ with force $(P_0 + \rho g h)A$ and up on its bottom face at depth $h + H$ with force $(P_0 + \rho g (h+H))A$. The sideways pushes on the vertical faces cancel. The net upward push is the difference, $\rho g H A = \rho g V$: the weight of a volume $V$ of the fluid. This upward net force from the fluid is the buoyant force. Notice that $h$ cancels: once an object is fully submerged in a liquid, the buoyant force does not change with depth.

把一块高为 $H$、底面积为 $A$ 的物块完全浸没在水中。水在深度 $h$ 处以 $(P_0 + \rho g h)A$ 的力向下压它的顶面，在深度 $h + H$ 处以 $(P_0 + \rho g (h+H))A$ 的力向上推它的底面。竖直侧面受到的水平推力相互抵消。向上的合力就是两者之差 $\rho g H A = \rho g V$：等于体积为 $V$ 的流体的重力。流体施加的这个向上的合力就是浮力（buoyant force）。注意 $h$ 被约掉了：物体一旦完全浸没在液体中，浮力就不随深度改变。

Archimedes' Principle阿基米德原理
$$F_B = \rho_\text{fluid}\,g\,V_\text{disp}$$

Archimedes' principle: the buoyant force on an object equals the weight of the fluid it displaces. $V_\text{disp}$ is the volume of the object that is below the fluid surface: the whole volume for a submerged object, only the part under the waterline for a floating one. The density in the formula is the density of the fluid.

阿基米德原理（Archimedes' principle）：物体受到的浮力等于它排开的流体的重力。$V_\text{disp}$ 是物体位于流体表面以下部分的体积：浸没物体取整个体积，漂浮物体只取水线以下的部分。公式中的密度是流体的密度。

Floating漂浮

A floating object is in equilibrium, so the buoyant force equals its weight: $\rho_\text{fluid} V_\text{sub} g = \rho_\text{obj} V g$. Cancelling $g$ gives the fraction of the object below the surface. An object denser than the fluid cannot displace enough fluid to balance its weight, so it sinks; a less dense object floats with part of it above the surface.

漂浮的物体处于平衡状态，所以浮力等于它的重力：$\rho_\text{fluid} V_\text{sub} g = \rho_\text{obj} V g$。约去 $g$ 就得到物体位于液面以下部分所占的比例。密度大于流体的物体无法排开足够的流体来平衡自身重力，所以下沉；密度较小的物体漂浮，有一部分露出液面。

Fraction Submerged (floating object)浸没比例（漂浮物体）
$$\frac{V_\text{sub}}{V} = \frac{\rho_\text{obj}}{\rho_\text{fluid}}$$

The Ideal-Fluid Model理想流体模型

Real fluids are complicated, so for moving fluids we use a model with four assumptions. Each equation below is only as good as these assumptions.

真实流体很复杂，所以研究流动的流体时我们使用带有四条假设的模型。下面每个方程的适用程度都取决于这些假设。

  • Incompressible: the density $\rho$ is the same everywhere in the fluid (a good model for liquids).
  • Non-viscous: no internal friction, so no mechanical energy is turned into thermal energy.
  • Steady flow: the velocity at each point in the pipe does not change with time.
  • Along a streamline: Bernoulli's equation compares two points on the same path that a bit of fluid follows.
  • 不可压缩（incompressible）：流体各处密度 $\rho$ 相同（对液体是很好的模型）。
  • 无黏性（non-viscous）：没有内摩擦，所以没有机械能转化为内能。
  • 定常流动（steady flow）：管道中每一点的流速不随时间变化。
  • 沿流线（streamline）：伯努利方程比较的是同一小团流体所经过路径上的两点。

The Continuity Equation连续性方程

The volume flow rate $Q$ is the volume of fluid passing a cross-section per second. In a time $\Delta t$ the fluid moves a distance $v\,\Delta t$, sweeping out a volume $Av\,\Delta t$, so $Q = Av$ (unit m³/s). Fluid cannot pile up or vanish inside a full pipe, and an incompressible fluid cannot be squeezed, so the same volume must pass every cross-section each second. Where the pipe narrows, the fluid speeds up.

体积流量（volume flow rate）$Q$ 是每秒通过某一截面的流体体积。在时间 $\Delta t$ 内流体移动距离 $v\,\Delta t$，扫过体积 $Av\,\Delta t$，所以 $Q = Av$（单位 m³/s）。流体在充满的管道中不会堆积也不会消失，不可压缩流体也不能被压缩，所以每秒通过每个截面的体积必须相同。管道变窄的地方，流体流速变大。

Continuity Equation (incompressible fluid)连续性方程（不可压缩流体）
$$Q = A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$$

$$Q = A_1 v_1 = \pi(0.010)^2(1.5) = 4.7 \times 10^{-4}\;\text{m}^3/\text{s}$$

Bernoulli's Equation伯努利方程

Follow a small parcel of fluid of volume $V$ along a streamline. The fluid behind it pushes it forward and the fluid ahead pushes back; if the pressure behind is larger, the net work done on the parcel by the surrounding fluid is $(P_1 - P_2)V$. With no friction, that work becomes the parcel's change in kinetic energy, $\tfrac{1}{2}\rho V(v_2^2 - v_1^2)$, plus its change in gravitational potential energy, $\rho V g(y_2 - y_1)$. Dividing by $V$ and collecting each point's terms gives Bernoulli's equation: the work-energy theorem written per unit volume.

沿一条流线跟踪一小团体积为 $V$ 的流体。后面的流体推它向前，前面的流体推它向后；若后方压强较大，周围流体对它做的净功为 $(P_1 - P_2)V$。没有摩擦时，这部分功转化为这团流体的动能变化 $\tfrac{1}{2}\rho V(v_2^2 - v_1^2)$ 与重力势能变化 $\rho V g(y_2 - y_1)$ 之和。除以 $V$ 并把同一点的项归在一起，就得到伯努利方程：按单位体积写出的动能定理。

Bernoulli's Equation (ideal fluid, along a streamline)伯努利方程（理想流体，沿流线）
$$P_1 + \tfrac{1}{2}\rho v_1^2 + \rho g y_1 = P_2 + \tfrac{1}{2}\rho v_2^2 + \rho g y_2$$

Two special cases are worth knowing. If the fluid is at rest ($v_1 = v_2 = 0$), Bernoulli's equation reduces to $P = P_0 + \rho g h$ from Part 2. If the flow is horizontal ($y_1 = y_2$), then where the speed is higher, the pressure is lower: combined with continuity, the narrow part of a horizontal pipe has the fastest flow and the lowest pressure.

有两个特殊情形值得掌握。如果流体静止（$v_1 = v_2 = 0$），伯努利方程就退化为第 2 部分的 $P = P_0 + \rho g h$。如果流动是水平的（$y_1 = y_2$），那么流速越大的地方压强越小：结合连续性方程，水平管道最窄处流速最快、压强最低。

Worked Example: Pressure in a Narrowing Pipe例题：变窄管道中的压强

Water flows at $2.0\;\text{m/s}$ through a horizontal pipe where the absolute pressure is $2.00 \times 10^5\;\text{Pa}$. The pipe then narrows to half its cross-sectional area. Find the speed and the pressure in the narrow section.

水以 $2.0\;\text{m/s}$ 的速度流过一段水平管道，该处绝对压强为 $2.00 \times 10^5\;\text{Pa}$。管道随后变窄到截面积的一半。求窄段的流速和压强。

Step 1: Continuity第 1 步：连续性方程
$$v_2 = v_1\frac{A_1}{A_2} = (2.0)(2) = 4.0\;\text{m/s}$$
Step 2: Bernoulli with $y_1 = y_2$第 2 步：伯努利方程，$y_1 = y_2$
$$P_2 = P_1 + \tfrac{1}{2}\rho\left(v_1^2 - v_2^2\right) = 2.00 \times 10^{5} + \tfrac{1}{2}(1000)(4.0 - 16)$$
$$P_2 = 2.00 \times 10^{5} - 6.0 \times 10^{3} = 1.94 \times 10^{5}\;\text{Pa}$$
The fluid speeds up entering the narrow section, so something must push it forward: the higher pressure behind it. Why this works: the pressure drop of $6.0\;\text{kPa}$ is exactly the gain in kinetic energy per unit volume.流体进入窄段时加速，必须有东西推它向前：那就是它后方较高的压强。为什么这样做：$6.0\;\text{kPa}$ 的压强下降正好等于单位体积动能的增加量。

Torricelli's Result托里拆利结论

Water leaks from a small hole a depth $h$ below the surface of a large open tank. Compare point 1 on the surface with point 2 just outside the hole. Both are open to the air, so $P_1 = P_2 = P_\text{atm}$. Because the tank is much wider than the hole, continuity makes the surface fall very slowly, so $v_1 \approx 0$. With $y_1 - y_2 = h$, Bernoulli's equation leaves $\rho g h = \tfrac{1}{2}\rho v_2^2$.

水从大型敞口水箱液面以下深度 $h$ 处的小孔漏出。比较液面上的点 1 和小孔外侧的点 2。两点都与空气相通，所以 $P_1 = P_2 = P_\text{atm}$。由于水箱比小孔宽得多，由连续性方程可知液面下降极慢，所以 $v_1 \approx 0$。由 $y_1 - y_2 = h$，伯努利方程化为 $\rho g h = \tfrac{1}{2}\rho v_2^2$。

Torricelli's Result托里拆利结论
$$v = \sqrt{2gh}$$

This is the same speed an object reaches falling freely through height $h$ from rest, which makes sense: each bit of water that leaves effectively replaces a bit at the surface that dropped by $h$.

这与物体从静止开始自由下落高度 $h$ 时获得的速度相同，这是合理的：每流出一小团水，相当于液面处有一小团水下降了 $h$。

Before using Bernoulli's equation, choose two points on the same streamline and list $P$, $v$ and $y$ at each; usually one unknown remains. Use continuity first to link the two speeds. Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Using the object's density in $F_B = \rho g V$: it is always the fluid's density.
  2. Mixing absolute and gauge pressure within one calculation.
  3. Measuring $h$ along a slanted tube or from the bottom of the container: it is the vertical depth below the surface.
  4. Using the diameter ratio in continuity: speeds scale with the area ratio, which is the diameter ratio squared.
  5. Claiming a hydraulic lift multiplies energy: it multiplies force, and the small piston moves farther by the same factor.
  6. Applying Bernoulli's equation without checking its assumptions (incompressible, non-viscous, steady, same streamline).
最常见的错误
  1. 在 $F_B = \rho g V$ 中代入物体的密度：这里永远是流体的密度。
  2. 在一次计算中混用绝对压强与表压。
  3. 沿倾斜管道或从容器底部量 $h$：它是液面以下的竖直深度。
  4. 在连续性方程中用直径之比：流速与面积之比有关，即直径之比的平方。
  5. 声称液压机放大了能量：它放大的是力，小活塞移动的距离按同样倍数增大。
  6. 不检查假设（不可压缩、无黏性、定常、同一流线）就使用伯努利方程。

Correct! Continuity makes the speed highest in the narrow section, and with $y$ constant Bernoulli's equation then makes $P$ lowest there.正确！由连续性方程，窄段流速最大；$y$ 不变时，由伯努利方程可知那里 $P$ 最低。
