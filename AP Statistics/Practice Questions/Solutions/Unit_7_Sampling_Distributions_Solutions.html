Q 1第1题EASY基础2.12 Sampling distributions and the CLT2.12 抽样分布与中心极限定理CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A county has 5,000 registered voters. A pollster takes a random sample of 200 of them, and 124 say they support a proposal, so $\hat p=124/200=0.62$. Which choice describes the sampling distribution of $\hat p$?
某县有 5,000 名登记选民。民调人员随机抽取其中 200 人，124 人表示支持某项提案，所以 $\hat p=124/200=0.62$。哪个选项描述了 $\hat p$ 的抽样分布？
Answer: C答案：C

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

A sampling distribution is the distribution of a statistic over all possible samples of the same size. Here the statistic is $\hat p$ and the sample size is 200, so it is the values of $\hat p$ over all possible random samples of 200 voters. Each other option describes a different object.抽样分布（sampling distribution）是同样大小的所有可能样本上某个统计量的分布。这里的统计量是 $\hat p$，样本量为 200，所以它是所有可能的 200 人随机样本所得 $\hat p$ 的取值。其余选项各自描述的是别的对象。

Why the options attract (M 17)选项误区（M 17）
  1. These are the data of one sample, the distribution of one sample. They are 200 individual answers, not values of a statistic.这是一个样本的数据，即单个样本的分布。它们是 200 个个体的回答，不是统计量的取值。
  2. This is the population distribution: the answers of every individual voter, with no sample drawn.这是总体分布：每位选民的回答，没有抽样。
  3. Correct: a sampling distribution collects the statistic from every possible sample.正确：抽样分布汇集了每个可能样本的统计量。
  4. $0.62$ is one value of the statistic from one sample. A single number has no distribution.$0.62$ 是一个样本的一个统计量取值。单个数值没有分布。
Keep three distributions apart: the population (individuals), one sample ($n$ values you collected), and the sampling distribution (a statistic over all samples of size $n$).区分三个分布：总体（个体）、单个样本（你收集的 $n$ 个值）和抽样分布（容量为 $n$ 的所有样本上的统计量）。

Q 2第2题EASY基础4.1 Sampling distributions for sample means4.1 样本均值的抽样分布CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
Simple random samples of size $n$ are drawn from a population with mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$, and the sample size is less than $10$ percent of the population. Which expression gives the standard deviation of the sampling distribution of the sample mean $\bar x$?
从均值为 $\mu$、标准差为 $\sigma$ 的总体中抽取容量为 $n$ 的简单随机样本，样本量不超过总体的百分之十。哪个表达式给出样本均值 $\bar x$ 的抽样分布的标准差？
Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

For a random sample under $10\%$ of the population, the standard deviation of the sampling distribution of $\bar x$ is the population spread divided by the square root of the sample size: $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$.当随机样本不超过总体的 $10\%$ 时，$\bar x$ 的抽样分布（sampling distribution）的标准差是总体标准差除以样本量的平方根：$\sigma/\sqrt{n}$。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. Divides by $n$ instead of $\sqrt{n}$, so the spread would shrink far too fast: quadrupling $n$ would divide it by 4, not 2.除以 $n$ 而不是 $\sqrt{n}$，离散程度缩小得太快：$n$ 增为 4 倍会使它除以 4，而不是 2。
  2. Correct: $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$.正确：$\sigma/\sqrt{n}$。
  3. Uses the sample statistic $s$ where the population value $\sigma$ is given; $s$ is only an estimate of $\sigma$.在应该用总体值 $\sigma$ 的地方用了样本统计量 $s$；$s$ 只是 $\sigma$ 的估计。
  4. This is the spread of individual values. It leaves out the reduction that averaging gives, which is the whole point of $\bar x$.这是个体取值的离散程度，没有体现取平均带来的缩小，而这正是 $\bar x$ 的关键。
A mean has less spread than a single value, by the factor $\sqrt{n}$. Decide whether the question is about one value ($\sigma$) or the mean of a sample ($\sigma/\sqrt{n}$).均值比单个取值的离散程度小，缩小因子为 $\sqrt{n}$。先判断问题问的是单个取值（$\sigma$）还是样本均值（$\sigma/\sqrt{n}$）。

Q 3第3题EASY基础3.1, 3.2 Estimators and sampling distributions for proportions3.1, 3.2 估计量与样本比例的抽样分布CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A random sample of $n=120$ items is taken from a very large production run in which the proportion of defective items is $p=0.30$. What is the standard deviation of the sampling distribution of $\hat p$?
从很大的一批产品中随机抽取 $n=120$ 件，其中次品比例为 $p=0.30$。$\hat p$ 的抽样分布的标准差是多少？
Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The sample is random and the run is very large, so $\sigma_{\hat p}=\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}=\sqrt{0.30(0.70)/120}=\sqrt{0.00175}\approx0.0418$.样本是随机的，这批产品数量很大，所以 $\sigma_{\hat p}=\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}=\sqrt{0.30(0.70)/120}=\sqrt{0.00175}\approx0.0418$。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. Correct: substitute into the formula and take the square root.正确：代入公式并开平方。
  2. Stops at $0.00175\approx0.0018$, which is the variance of $\hat p$. The standard deviation needs the square root.停在 $0.00175\approx0.0018$，这是 $\hat p$ 的方差。标准差还需要开平方。
  3. Forgets to divide by $n$, giving the standard deviation of one yes or no trial rather than of a proportion from $120$ items.忘记除以 $n$，得到的是单次“是或否”试验的标准差，而不是 $120$ 件产品的比例的标准差。
  4. Uses $p$ in place of $p(1-p)$ under the root: $\sqrt{0.30/120}=0.05$.在根号下用 $p$ 代替 $p(1-p)$：$\sqrt{0.30/120}=0.05$。
The standard deviation of $\hat p$ is $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$: the product $p(1-p)$ on top, $n$ below, and one square root over everything.$\hat p$ 的标准差是 $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$：分子是 $p(1-p)$，分母是 $n$，整体开一次平方根。

  4. Reverses the effect of $n$: $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$ grows when $n$ falls, so samples of 25 are more variable.把 $n$ 的影响弄反了：$n$ 减小时 $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$ 增大，所以容量为 25 的样本变异更大。
A sampling distribution of a proportion is identified by its center $p$ and its spread $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$; ten simulated values should be close to both.样本比例的抽样分布由中心 $p$ 和离散程度 $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$ 确定；十个模拟值应当接近这两者。

A simulation runs 200 samples of size 50 from a very large population with proportion $0.30$. The 200 sample proportions have mean $0.30$ and standard deviation $0.065$. Which interpretation of $0.065$ is correct?
一次模拟从比例为 $0.30$ 的很大总体中抽取 200 个容量为 50 的样本。这 200 个样本比例的平均为 $0.30$，标准差为 $0.065$。对 $0.065$ 的哪种解释是正确的？
Answer: D答案：D

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The standard deviation of the sampling distribution measures the typical distance between a sample proportion and the true proportion. Here $\sqrt{0.30(0.70)/50}\approx0.065$, so a typical $\hat p$ is about $0.065$ from $0.30$. The standard deviation of the individual yes or no variable is $\sqrt{0.21}\approx0.458$.抽样分布（sampling distribution）的标准差衡量样本比例与真实比例之间的典型距离。这里 $\sqrt{0.30(0.70)/50}\approx0.065$，所以典型的 $\hat p$ 离 $0.30$ 约 $0.065$。个体“是或否”变量的标准差是 $\sqrt{0.21}\approx0.458$。

Why the options attract (M 17)选项误区（M 17）
  1. Reads the standard deviation as the probability of landing exactly on the center. A spread is measured in the units of $\hat p$, not in percent of samples.把标准差当作恰好落在中心的概率。离散程度的单位是 $\hat p$ 的单位，不是样本的百分比。
  2. Treats the spread of sample proportions across samples as variation among population proportions. The population proportion is one fixed number, $0.30$.把样本比例在样本之间的离散当作总体比例之间的变化。总体比例是一个固定的数 $0.30$。
  3. Confuses the standard deviation of the sampling distribution with the standard deviation of the individual values, which is $\sqrt{0.30(0.70)}\approx0.458$.把抽样分布的标准差与个体取值的标准差混为一谈；后者是 $\sqrt{0.30(0.70)}\approx0.458$。
  4. Correct: it is a typical distance from the true proportion, not a probability.正确：它是与真实比例的典型距离，而不是概率。
A standard deviation of a sampling distribution is a typical error of the statistic, so say “typical distance of $\hat p$ from $p$”, never “typical individual”.抽样分布的标准差是统计量的典型误差，要说“$\hat p$ 与 $p$ 的典型距离”，而不是“典型个体”。

On the day studied there are more than $10{,}000$ deliveries, and a dispatcher selects a random sample of $64$ deliveries. Which description of the sampling distribution of the sample mean delivery time is correct?
某城市包裹的配送时间均值为 $22$ 分钟、标准差为 $8$ 分钟，单次配送时间的分布为中度右偏，没有极端离群值。研究当天有超过 $10{,}000$ 次配送，调度员随机抽取 $64$ 次配送。关于样本平均配送时间的抽样分布，哪种描述是正确的？

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The sample is random and $64\le0.10(10000)=1000$, so the 10% condition holds. The center is $\mu_{\bar x}=22$ and the spread is $\sigma/\sqrt{n}=8/\sqrt{64}=1$ minute. The population is only moderately skewed and $n=64\ge30$, so by the central limit theorem $\bar x$ is approximately normal.样本是随机的，且 $64\le0.10(10000)=1000$，所以 10% 条件成立。中心为 $\mu_{\bar x}=22$，离散程度为 $\sigma/\sqrt{n}=8/\sqrt{64}=1$ 分钟。总体只是中度偏斜且 $n=64\ge30$，由中心极限定理（central limit theorem），$\bar x$ 近似正态。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. Correct: center $22$, spread $8/\sqrt{64}=1$, approximately normal at $n=64$.正确：中心 $22$，离散程度 $8/\sqrt{64}=1$，$n=64$ 时近似正态。
  2. Uses $\sigma$ as the spread of $\bar x$, ignoring the factor $1/\sqrt{n}$.把 $\sigma$ 当作 $\bar x$ 的离散程度，忽略了因子 $1/\sqrt{n}$。

Mean: $\mu_1-\mu_2=14-11.5=2.5$ minutes. Standard deviation: $\sqrt{\dfrac{6^2}{45}+\dfrac{5^2}{36}}=\sqrt{0.8+0.6944}=\sqrt{1.4944}\approx1.22$ minutes.均值：$\mu_1-\mu_2=14-11.5=2.5$ 分钟。标准差：$\sqrt{\dfrac{6^2}{45}+\dfrac{5^2}{36}}=\sqrt{0.8+0.6944}=\sqrt{1.4944}\approx1.22$ 分钟。

(c) 3 points3 分

Sampling distribution of $\bar x_1-\bar x_2$: approximately normal with mean $2.5$ and standard deviation $1.2225$. $z=\dfrac{4-2.5}{1.2225}\approx1.23$ (rounded), and $P(\bar x_1-\bar x_2\gt4)=$ normalcdf(4, 1E99, 2.5, 1.2225) $\approx0.1099$. In context: about $11\%$ of pairs of random samples would show a Stop 1 mean wait more than $4$ minutes longer than the Stop 2 mean wait.$\bar x_1-\bar x_2$ 的抽样分布：近似正态，均值 $2.5$，标准差 $1.2225$。$z=\dfrac{4-2.5}{1.2225}\approx1.23$（取整），$P(\bar x_1-\bar x_2\gt4)=$ normalcdf(4, 1E99, 2.5, 1.2225) $\approx0.1099$。结合情境：约 $11\%$ 的随机样本对会显示 1 号站的平均等待时间比 2 号站长 $4$ 分钟以上。

(d) 1 point1 分

The formula $\sigma_1^2/n_1+\sigma_2^2/n_2$ needs independent samples. The same riders at both stops give paired data, whose two means are related, so the variances cannot simply be added.公式 $\sigma_1^2/n_1+\sigma_2^2/n_2$ 要求样本独立。同一批乘客在两站的数据是配对数据，两个均值相关，方差不能简单相加。

Where this goes wrong (M 16)常见错误（M 16）

Adding the standard deviations $0.894+0.833$ instead of the variances; using $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_2$ without dividing by the sample sizes; and checking the central limit theorem condition for only one of the two samples.把标准差 $0.894+0.833$ 直接相加而不是方差相加；用 $\sigma_1$ 和 $\sigma_2$ 而不除以样本量；以及只检查两个样本中一个的中心极限定理条件。

For a difference of means, justify normality for each sample separately, say the samples are independent, then subtract the centers and add the variances.对均值之差，要分别为每个样本说明正态性的理由，说明样本相互独立，然后中心相减、方差相加。
