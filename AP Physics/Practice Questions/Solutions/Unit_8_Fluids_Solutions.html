Q1EASY 8.1 Density8.1 密度Calculator

A solid block has mass $0.60~\mathrm{kg}$ and volume $250~\mathrm{cm^3}$. Its density is closest to一个固体块质量为 $0.60~\mathrm{kg}$，体积为 $250~\mathrm{cm^3}$。其密度最接近

  • (A) $4.2\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
  • (B) $2.4\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
  • (C) $2.4\times10^{3}~\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
  • (D) $2.4\times10^{6}~\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$V = 250~\mathrm{cm^3}\times\frac{10^{-6}~\mathrm{m^3}}{1~\mathrm{cm^3}} = 2.5\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{m^3}$$
$$\rho = \frac{m}{V} = \frac{0.60}{2.5\times10^{-4}} = 2.4\times10^{3}~\mathrm{kg/m^3}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) inverts the ratio and computes $V/m = 2.5\times10^{-4}/0.60$, so the quotient has the wrong units. (B) divides $0.60~\mathrm{kg}$ by $250$ without converting the volume, which gives kg per cm$^3$, not kg per m$^3$. (D) converts the volume with $1~\mathrm{cm^3}=10^{-9}~\mathrm{m^3}$ instead of $10^{-6}~\mathrm{m^3}$, so $V$ comes out a thousand times too small.(A) 把比值倒过来，算成 $V/m = 2.5\times10^{-4}/0.60$，商的单位也不对。 (B) 直接用 $0.60~\mathrm{kg}$ 除以 $250$，没有换算体积，得到的是 kg/cm$^3$，不是 kg/m$^3$。 (D) 用 $1~\mathrm{cm^3}=10^{-9}~\mathrm{m^3}$ 而不是 $10^{-6}~\mathrm{m^3}$ 换算体积，使 $V$ 小了一千倍。
Insight.要点。 Convert each length or volume to SI before substituting: $1~\mathrm{cm^3}=10^{-6}~\mathrm{m^3}$, and $1~\mathrm{g/cm^3}=1000~\mathrm{kg/m^3}$.代入前先把长度、体积换成国际单位：$1~\mathrm{cm^3}=10^{-6}~\mathrm{m^3}$，$1~\mathrm{g/cm^3}=1000~\mathrm{kg/m^3}$。

A uniform solid cube has mass $m$ and density $\rho$. It is cut into $8$ identical smaller cubes. Each small cube has mass and density, respectively,一个均匀实心立方体质量为 $m$，密度为 $\rho$。把它切成 $8$ 个完全相同的小立方体。每个小立方体的质量和密度分别为

  • (A) $m/8$ and $\rho/8$
  • (B) $m/8$ and $\rho$
  • (C) $m/8$ and $8\rho$
  • (D) $m$ and $\rho$
Answer:答案： (B)
$$m_\text{small} = \frac{m}{8},\qquad \rho_\text{small} = \frac{m/8}{V/8} = \rho$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) treats density as if it shared out like mass; but the volume also drops to $V/8$, so $\rho=m/V$ is unchanged. (C) divides the volume by $8$ but forgets that the mass is divided by $8$ too, giving $\rho'=m/(V/8)=8\rho$. (D) assumes cutting does not change the mass of a piece; each piece holds only one eighth of the material.(A) 把密度当成像质量一样被均分；但体积也变为 $V/8$，所以 $\rho=m/V$ 不变。 (C) 只把体积除以 $8$，忘了质量也被除以 $8$，得到 $\rho'=m/(V/8)=8\rho$。 (D) 以为切开后每块的质量不变；实际上每块只含八分之一的材料。
Insight.要点。 Density is a property of the material, not of the sample: mass and volume scale together, so $\rho$ does not change.密度是材料的性质，而不是样品的性质：质量和体积同比例变化，所以 $\rho$ 不变。
Q3EASY 8.2 Pressure8.2 压强No Calculator

A brick rests on a table on its largest face, exerting pressure $P$ on the table. It is turned to rest on its smallest face, whose area is one fifth of the largest face. The pressure it now exerts on the table is一块砖以最大面平放在桌上，对桌面的压强为 $P$。把它翻转，改用最小面着桌，最小面的面积是最大面的五分之一。此时它对桌面的压强为

  • (A) $\tfrac{1}{5}P$
  • (B) $P$
  • (C) $5P$
  • (D) $25P$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$P = \frac{F_\perp}{A},\qquad F_\perp = mg\ \text{unchanged},\qquad P' = \frac{mg}{A/5} = 5P$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) inverts the area ratio; a smaller contact area gives a larger pressure, not a smaller one. (B) confuses pressure with force: the force on the table is still the weight $mg$, but the pressure depends on how that force is spread over the area. (D) squares the area ratio; $P=F_\perp/A$ is inversely proportional to $A$, not $A^2$.(A) 把面积比颠倒了；接触面积更小，压强更大，而不是更小。 (B) 把压强与力混为一谈：桌面受的力仍是重力 $mg$，但压强取决于这个力分布在多大面积上。 (D) 把面积比平方了；$P=F_\perp/A$ 与 $A$ 成反比，而不是与 $A^2$ 成反比。
Insight.要点。 At fixed force, pressure is inversely proportional to contact area: one fifth of the area means five times the pressure.力一定时，压强与接触面积成反比：面积变为五分之一，压强变为五倍。

A tire gauge reads a gauge pressure of $2.2\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$. Take $P_\text{atm}=1.01\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$. The absolute pressure of the air in the tire is closest to轮胎气压表读数（表压）为 $2.2\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$。取 $P_\text{atm}=1.01\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$。胎内空气的绝对压强最接近

  • (A) $1.2\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$
  • (B) $2.2\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$
  • (C) $3.2\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$
  • (D) $2.2\times10^{10}~\mathrm{Pa}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$P = P_\text{gauge} + P_\text{atm} = 2.2\times10^{5} + 1.01\times10^{5} = 3.21\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}\approx 3.2\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) subtracts, $2.2\times10^{5}-1.01\times10^{5}\approx1.2\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$; gauge pressure is measured above atmospheric pressure, so the atmosphere is added back. (B) reads the gauge value as the absolute pressure and leaves out the $1.01\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$ of the atmosphere. (D) multiplies the two pressures, $(2.2\times10^{5})(1.01\times10^{5})\approx2.2\times10^{10}$, instead of adding them.(A) 做了减法，$2.2\times10^{5}-1.01\times10^{5}\approx1.2\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$；表压是高出大气压的部分，应当加回大气压。 (B) 把表压当成绝对压强，漏掉了大气压的 $1.01\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$。 (D) 把两个压强相乘，$(2.2\times10^{5})(1.01\times10^{5})\approx2.2\times10^{10}$，而不是相加。
Insight.要点。 $P_\text{gauge}=P-P_\text{atm}$. Check whether a problem says gauge or absolute before you add or drop $P_\text{atm}$.$P_\text{gauge}=P-P_\text{atm}$。先看题目说的是表压还是绝对压强，再决定是否加上或去掉 $P_\text{atm}$。
Q5MEDIUM 8.2 Pressure from Collisions8.2 碰撞产生的压强No Calculator

A sample of gas is sealed in a rigid container and heated. Which statement best explains why the pressure on the container walls increases?一定量气体密封在刚性容器中并被加热。下列哪项最能解释容器壁所受压强增大？

  • (A) Each particle becomes more massive, so it exerts a larger force on the wall.每个粒子的质量变大，所以对器壁的作用力更大。
  • (B) The particles move faster, so they hit the walls more often and with a larger momentum change per collision.粒子运动得更快，所以撞击器壁更频繁，而且每次碰撞的动量变化更大。
  • (C) More particles enter the container from outside.有更多粒子从容器外进入容器。
  • (D) The particles expand, so they take up more of the container's volume.粒子膨胀，占据了容器更多的体积。
Answer:答案： (B)

Heating raises the average speed of the particles. A particle with perpendicular speed $v$ that rebounds elastically changes its momentum by $2mv$, so faster particles give a larger change per collision, and they also arrive at the wall more often. The wall's force per unit area, the pressure, rises.加热使粒子的平均速率增大。垂直速率为 $v$ 的粒子弹性反弹时动量变化为 $2mv$，所以速率越大，每次碰撞的动量变化越大，而且撞击器壁也更频繁。器壁单位面积所受的力，即压强，随之增大。

Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) particle mass does not change when a gas is heated; only the particle speeds change. (C) the container is sealed, so the number of particles is fixed; the extra pressure comes from the faster motion. (D) particle size does not change with temperature in the particle model; the particles are far apart compared with their size.(A) 加热气体不会改变粒子的质量，改变的只是粒子的速率。 (C) 容器是密封的，粒子数不变；压强增大来自更快的运动。 (D) 在粒子模型中粒子大小不随温度改变；气体粒子之间的距离远大于粒子本身的尺寸。
Insight.要点。 Pressure is the large-scale result of countless collisions: more frequent or more forceful collisions mean greater pressure.压强是无数次碰撞的宏观结果：碰撞更频繁或更有力，压强就更大。

$d~(\mathrm{m})$$0$$2.0$$5.0$
$P~(10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa})$$1.01$$1.26$$1.63$

  • (C) $3.3\times10^{3}~\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
  • (D) $1.3\times10^{3}~\mathrm{kg/m^3}$
Answer:答案： (D)
$$P = P_0 + \rho g d\ \Rightarrow\ \rho = \frac{P - P_0}{g\,d} = \frac{(1.63-1.01)\times10^{5}}{(9.8)(5.0)}\approx 1.3\times10^{3}~\mathrm{kg/m^3}$$

Q8MEDIUM 8.2 Pascal's Principle8.2 帕斯卡原理No Calculator

A hydraulic lift has a small piston of area $4.0~\mathrm{cm^2}$ and a large piston of area $200~\mathrm{cm^2}$, connected by an incompressible fluid at the same height. A force of $150~\mathrm{N}$ is applied to the small piston. The largest weight the large piston can hold up is一台液压升降机的小活塞面积为 $4.0~\mathrm{cm^2}$，大活塞面积为 $200~\mathrm{cm^2}$，二者由不可压缩的流体连通且位于同一高度。对小活塞施加 $150~\mathrm{N}$ 的力。大活塞能托起的最大重量为

  • (A) $3.0~\mathrm{N}$
  • (B) $150~\mathrm{N}$
  • (C) $7500~\mathrm{N}$
  • (D) $3.0\times10^{4}~\mathrm{N}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$\frac{F_1}{A_1} = \frac{F_2}{A_2}\ \Rightarrow\ F_2 = F_1\frac{A_2}{A_1} = (150)\frac{200}{4.0} = 7500~\mathrm{N}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) inverts the area ratio, $F_2=F_1A_1/A_2=3.0~\mathrm{N}$; the larger piston carries the larger force. (B) takes the force as unchanged, as if the fluid only passed the force along; it is the pressure that is transmitted, and the force scales with area. (D) multiplies $F_1$ by $A_2$ without dividing by $A_1$, $(150)(200)=3.0\times10^{4}$, which has the wrong units.(A) 把面积比颠倒，$F_2=F_1A_1/A_2=3.0~\mathrm{N}$；面积较大的活塞承受较大的力。 (B) 以为流体只是原样传递力；实际传递的是压强，力与面积成正比。 (D) 把 $F_1$ 乘以 $A_2$ 却没有除以 $A_1$，$(150)(200)=3.0\times10^{4}$，单位也不对。
Insight.要点。 Pascal's principle: a pressure change applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted undiminished, so $F_2/F_1=A_2/A_1$.帕斯卡原理：施加在封闭流体上的压强变化会不减弱地传递，所以 $F_2/F_1=A_2/A_1$。

(d) For $y=2.0~\mathrm{m}$ the depth is $h=1.0~\mathrm{m}$:当 $y=2.0~\mathrm{m}$ 时，深度 $h=1.0~\mathrm{m}$：
$$v = \sqrt{2(9.8)(1.0)} = 4.43~\mathrm{m/s},\qquad t = \sqrt{\frac{2(2.0)}{9.8}} = 0.639~\mathrm{s},\qquad x = vt \approx 2.8~\mathrm{m}$$
Both streams land at the same place. In general $x=\sqrt{2gh}\sqrt{2y/g}=2\sqrt{hy}$, which is unchanged when $h$ and $y$ are swapped.两股水流落在同一位置。一般地，$x=\sqrt{2gh}\sqrt{2y/g}=2\sqrt{hy}$，把 $h$ 与 $y$ 互换后结果不变。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 False moves: (a) quoting $v=\sqrt{2gh}$ without saying why $v_1\approx0$ or why the pressures are equal; (b) using the height above the floor, $\sqrt{2(9.8)(1.0)}$, instead of the depth below the surface; (c) using $t=\sqrt{2h/g}$ with the wrong height.常见错误：(a) 直接写出 $v=\sqrt{2gh}$，没有说明为什么 $v_1\approx0$ 或为什么两处压强相等；(b) 用离地高度而不是水面以下的深度，算成 $\sqrt{2(9.8)(1.0)}$；(c) 用 $t=\sqrt{2h/g}$，高度取错。
Insight.要点。 Two separate pieces of physics join here: fluid flow (Bernoulli) sets the exit speed from the depth, and projectile motion sets the fall time from the height above the floor.这里把两部分物理结合起来：流体流动（伯努利方程）由深度决定出口速率，抛体运动由离地高度决定下落时间。
