The Parabola and Its Key Features抛物线及其关键特征

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Calculate the y-intercept and the x-intercepts of $y = a x^{2} + b x + c$.

  • Decide whether the vertex is a maximum or a minimum from the sign of $a$.
  • Sketch a parabola from its vertex, axis of symmetry and intercepts.
  • 由方程写出抛物线的顶点（vertex）、对称轴（axis of symmetry）与开口方向。
  • 计算 $y = a x^{2} + b x + c$ 的 y 截距（y-intercept）与 x 截距。
  • 写出二次函数的定义域（domain）与值域（range）。
  • 由 $a$ 的符号判断顶点是最大值还是最小值。

Anatomy of $y = a x^{2} + b x + c$.$y = a x^{2} + b x + c$ 的结构。
  • Opens up开口向上 if $a > 0$; opens down if $a < 0$. The sign of $a$ controls whether the vertex is a minimum or maximum.当 $a > 0$；开口向下当 $a < 0$。$a$ 的符号决定顶点是最小值还是最大值。
  • Axis of symmetry:对称轴： the vertical line $x = -b/(2 a)$. The parabola is symmetric about this line.竖直线 $x = -b/(2 a)$。抛物线关于此线对称。
  • Vertex:顶点： the point on the parabola where the axis of symmetry meets it. Its $x$-coordinate is $-b/(2 a)$; its $y$-coordinate is found by substituting back into the equation.对称轴与抛物线的交点。$x$ 坐标为 $-b/(2 a)$；$y$ 坐标由代回方程求得。
  • $y$-intercept:$y$ 截距： always $(0, c)$, just plug in $x = 0$.恒为 $(0, c)$，代入 $x = 0$ 即得。
  • $x$-intercepts (roots):$x$ 截距（实根）： where $y = 0$. There are $0$, $1$, or $2$ of them depending on the discriminant.$y = 0$ 处。根据判别式不同，可能有 $0$、$1$ 或 $2$ 个。
Maximum vs. minimum.最大值与最小值。 If $a > 0$, the vertex is the minimum point of the function (the parabola opens up). If $a < 0$, the vertex is the maximum point. There is no other extremum: a parabola has exactly one turning point.$a > 0$ 时，顶点是函数的最小值点（开口向上）；$a < 0$ 时，顶点是最大值点。再无其他极值：抛物线恰有一个转折点。
Worked Example 1: Key features from standard form例题 1：由一般式读取关键特征

For the quadratic $f(x) = -2 x^{2} + 8 x - 3$, find (a) the axis of symmetry, (b) the vertex, (c) the $y$-intercept, and (d) the direction of opening.对二次函数 $f(x) = -2 x^{2} + 8 x - 3$，求（a）对称轴，（b）顶点，（c）$y$ 截距，（d）开口方向。

Identify.辨识。 Standard form $a = -2$, $b = 8$, $c = -3$.一般式中 $a = -2$，$b = 8$，$c = -3$。

(a) Axis of symmetry.（a）对称轴。

$$ x \;=\; -\frac{b}{2 a} \;=\; -\frac{8}{2 \cdot (-2)} \;=\; 2. $$

(b) Vertex.（b）顶点。 Substitute $x = 2$ into $f$:代入 $x = 2$：

$$ f(2) \;=\; -2 \cdot 4 + 8 \cdot 2 - 3 \;=\; -8 + 16 - 3 \;=\; 5. $$

So the vertex is $(2, 5)$.所以顶点为 $(2, 5)$。

(c) $y$-intercept.（c）$y$ 截距。 Set $x = 0$: $f(0) = -3$, so the $y$-intercept is $(0, -3)$.令 $x = 0$：$f(0) = -3$，$y$ 截距为 $(0, -3)$。

(d) Direction.（d）开口方向。 Because $a = -2 < 0$, the parabola opens downward and the vertex $(2, 5)$ is the maximum point.由 $a = -2 < 0$，抛物线向下开口，顶点 $(2, 5)$ 为最大值点。

Evaluate.小结。 The maximum value of $f$ is $5$, achieved at $x = 2$. The parabola crosses the $y$-axis at $(0, -3)$ on its way up to the vertex.$f$ 的最大值为 $5$，在 $x = 2$ 处取得。抛物线在 $(0, -3)$ 处交 $y$ 轴，沿途上升至顶点。

Worked Example 2: Features of a parabola with no x-intercepts例题 2：没有 x 截距的抛物线的特征

For $h(x) = x^{2} + 4 x + 7$, find the axis of symmetry, the vertex, the y-intercept and any x-intercepts, then state the domain and range.对 $h(x) = x^{2} + 4 x + 7$，求对称轴、顶点、y 截距与全部 x 截距，并写出定义域和值域。

Identify.辨识。 $a = 1$, $b = 4$, $c = 7$. Since $a > 0$ the parabola opens upward and the vertex is the minimum.$a = 1$，$b = 4$，$c = 7$。$a > 0$，抛物线开口向上，顶点为最小值点。

Axis of symmetry.对称轴。

$$ x \;=\; -\frac{b}{2 a} \;=\; -\frac{4}{2} \;=\; -2. $$

Vertex.顶点。 Substitute $x = -2$:代入 $x = -2$：

$$ h(-2) \;=\; (-2)^{2} + 4(-2) + 7 \;=\; 4 - 8 + 7 \;=\; 3. $$

The vertex is $(-2, 3)$.顶点为 $(-2, 3)$。

y-intercept.y 截距。 Set $x = 0$: $h(0) = 7$, so the y-intercept is $(0, 7)$.令 $x = 0$：$h(0) = 7$，y 截距为 $(0, 7)$。

x-intercepts.x 截距。 Check the discriminant: $\Delta = b^{2} - 4 a c = 16 - 28 = -12 < 0$. There are no real x-intercepts, which matches a minimum value of $3$ above the x-axis.检查判别式：$\Delta = b^{2} - 4 a c = 16 - 28 = -12 < 0$，没有实 x 截距，与最小值 $3$ 位于 x 轴上方一致。

Domain and range.定义域与值域。 Any real number can be an input, so the domain is all real numbers, $(-\infty, \infty)$. The lowest output is the vertex value $3$, so the range is $y \ge 3$, that is $[3, \infty)$.任何实数都可作输入，定义域为全体实数 $(-\infty, \infty)$。最小输出为顶点处的 $3$，值域为 $y \ge 3$，即 $[3, \infty)$。

Evaluate.核验。 The mirror image of the y-intercept $(0, 7)$ in the axis $x = -2$ is $(-4, 7)$, and $h(-4) = 16 - 16 + 7 = 7$. The symmetry checks out.y 截距 $(0, 7)$ 关于对称轴 $x = -2$ 的镜像点为 $(-4, 7)$，且 $h(-4) = 16 - 16 + 7 = 7$，对称性吻合。

Going deeper · Why the axis of symmetry is $x = -b / (2 a)$深入 · 为何对称轴为 $x = -b / (2 a)$

Start from $f(x) = a x^{2} + b x + c$ and apply the algebraic identity for completing the square:从 $f(x) = a x^{2} + b x + c$ 出发，套用配方法的代数恒等式：

$$ f(x) \;=\; a \left( x^{2} + \tfrac{b}{a} x \right) + c \;=\; a \left( x + \tfrac{b}{2 a} \right)^{2} - a \cdot \tfrac{b^{2}}{4 a^{2}} + c. $$

The first term $a (x + b/(2 a))^{2}$ is non-negative when $a > 0$ and equals zero only at $x = -b/(2 a)$. The remaining constant $c - b^{2}/(4 a)$ is the value of $f$ at that point. Symmetry of the squared term about $x = -b/(2 a)$ is what makes that vertical line the axis of symmetry of the whole parabola: $f(-b/(2 a) + h) = f(-b/(2 a) - h)$ for every $h$. The argument runs in reverse for $a < 0$ with "non-negative" replaced by "non-positive," and the same line is the axis of symmetry.第一项 $a (x + b/(2 a))^{2}$ 当 $a > 0$ 时非负，仅在 $x = -b/(2 a)$ 处为零；其余常数 $c - b^{2}/(4 a)$ 为该点的 $f$ 值。平方项关于 $x = -b/(2 a)$ 对称，使该竖直线成为整条抛物线的对称轴：对任意 $h$，$f(-b/(2 a) + h) = f(-b/(2 a) - h)$。$a < 0$ 时把"非负"换成"非正"，结论一致。

Domain, range and intercepts of a parabola抛物线的定义域、值域与截距

The domain of a function is the set of allowed inputs $x$; the range is the set of outputs $y$ it actually produces. A quadratic polynomial accepts every real number, so its domain is always $(-\infty, \infty)$. The range is decided by the vertex: a parabola has exactly one turning point and then rises (or falls) forever, so the $y$-coordinate $k$ of the vertex is the boundary of the range. If $a > 0$ the range is $y \ge k$; if $a < 0$ it is $y \le k$.函数的定义域（domain）是允许输入的 $x$ 的集合；值域（range）是实际得到的输出 $y$ 的集合。二次多项式接受任何实数，所以定义域恒为 $(-\infty, \infty)$。值域由顶点决定：抛物线只有一个转折点，之后无限上升（或下降），所以顶点的 $y$ 坐标 $k$ 是值域的边界。$a > 0$ 时值域为 $y \ge k$；$a < 0$ 时为 $y \le k$。

The y-intercept is where the graph meets the y-axis: set $x = 0$ and read $(0, c)$. The x-intercepts are where it meets the x-axis: set $y = 0$ and solve $a x^{2} + b x + c = 0$. There can be two, one or none, and the discriminant in §4 tells you which before you solve. For $f(x) = -2 x^{2} + 8 x - 3$ in Worked Example 1, the vertex is $(2, 5)$, so the domain is $(-\infty, \infty)$ and the range is $(-\infty, 5]$.y 截距（y-intercept）是图像与 y 轴的交点：令 $x = 0$，得 $(0, c)$。x 截距是图像与 x 轴的交点：令 $y = 0$，解 $a x^{2} + b x + c = 0$。可能有两个、一个或没有，§4 的判别式能在求解前告诉你是哪种。例题 1 中 $f(x) = -2 x^{2} + 8 x - 3$ 的顶点为 $(2, 5)$，故定义域为 $(-\infty, \infty)$，值域为 $(-\infty, 5]$。

Common error. Writing the range with the vertex's x-coordinate ("range $x \le 2$"), or taking the y-intercept for the maximum. The range is always a statement about $y$, and the extreme value is the vertex's $y$-coordinate, not $c$. In the figure the y-intercept is $-3$ but the maximum is $5$.常见错误。用顶点的横坐标写值域（"值域 $x \le 2$"），或把 y 截距当作最大值。值域永远是关于 $y$ 的陈述，极值是顶点的 $y$ 坐标而不是 $c$。图中 y 截距为 $-3$，最大值却是 $5$。

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Describe the vertical stretch or compression, the reflection and the translation that map $y = x^{2}$ onto $y = a (x - h)^{2} + k$.

  • 写出一般式（standard form）、顶点式（vertex form）与因式分解式（factored form），并说明各自直接显示的特征。
  • 在一般式、顶点式与因式分解式之间互相转换。
  • 由两个根和另一个点写出抛物线的方程。
  • 描述把 $y = x^{2}$ 变为 $y = a (x - h)^{2} + k$ 的纵向拉伸或压缩、反射与平移。
  • 由顶点式画出抛物线草图。
The three forms.三种形式。
  • Standard form:一般式： $y = a x^{2} + b x + c$. Best for reading the $y$-intercept ($c$) and computing the axis ($x = -b/(2 a)$).$y = a x^{2} + b x + c$。最适合读取 $y$ 截距（$c$）与计算对称轴（$x = -b/(2 a)$）。
  • Vertex form:顶点式： $y = a (x - h)^{2} + k$. The vertex is $(h, k)$, read directly. Best for transformations and for stating the max/min value.$y = a (x - h)^{2} + k$。顶点为 $(h, k)$，可直接读出。最适合处理变换与给出极值。
  • Factored form:因式分解式： $y = a (x - r_{1}) (x - r_{2})$. The roots are $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$, read directly. Best for graphing from roots and for solving the equation.$y = a (x - r_{1}) (x - r_{2})$。实根为 $r_{1}$ 与 $r_{2}$，可直接读出。最适合由根作图与求解方程。
Conversions.互转。 All three describe the same parabola; learn the moves between them cold.三种形式描述同一条抛物线；要把彼此之间的转换练到熟极。
  • Standard to vertex:一般式 → 顶点式： complete the square (see §5) or use $h = -b/(2 a)$, $k = f(h)$.配方法（见 §5），或用 $h = -b/(2 a)$、$k = f(h)$。
  • Standard to factored:一般式 → 因式分解式： factor the quadratic (§3) or use the roots from the formula (§4).对二次因式分解（§3），或用求根公式得到的根（§4）。
  • Vertex to standard:顶点式 → 一般式： expand $a (x - h)^{2} + k$.展开 $a (x - h)^{2} + k$。
  • Factored to standard:因式分解式 → 一般式： expand $a (x - r_{1})(x - r_{2})$. The constant term is $a r_{1} r_{2}$; the middle coefficient is $-a (r_{1} + r_{2})$.展开 $a (x - r_{1})(x - r_{2})$。常数项为 $a r_{1} r_{2}$；一次项系数为 $-a (r_{1} + r_{2})$。
When is the factored form unavailable?何时无法用因式分解式？ When the parabola has no real roots ($\Delta < 0$) you cannot factor over the real numbers. Vertex and standard forms always exist.当抛物线无实根（$\Delta < 0$）时，无法在实数范围内因式分解。一般式与顶点式始终存在。
Worked Example 3: Move between all three forms例题 3：三种形式之间互转

Take $f(x) = 2 x^{2} - 12 x + 10$ and write it in (a) vertex form and (b) factored form.取 $f(x) = 2 x^{2} - 12 x + 10$，写出（a）顶点式与（b）因式分解式。

(a) Vertex form.（a）顶点式。 First find the vertex via $h = -b/(2 a) = 12/4 = 3$. Then先用 $h = -b/(2 a) = 12/4 = 3$ 求顶点：

$$ k \;=\; f(3) \;=\; 2 \cdot 9 - 12 \cdot 3 + 10 \;=\; 18 - 36 + 10 \;=\; -8. $$

A perfect square trinomial is what you get by squaring a binomial: $(x + p)^{2} = x^{2} + 2 p x + p^{2}$. Notice the pattern: the middle coefficient is twice $p$, and the constant is $p^{2}$. So if you are given $x^{2} + b x$, the constant that completes it is the square of half the coefficient of $x$:完全平方三项式（perfect square trinomial）是把二项式平方得到的：$(x + p)^{2} = x^{2} + 2 p x + p^{2}$。注意规律：一次项系数是 $p$ 的两倍，常数项是 $p^{2}$。因此给定 $x^{2} + b x$，补全它所需的常数就是 $x$ 的系数的一半（half the coefficient）的平方：

$$ x^{2} + b x + \left(\tfrac{b}{2}\right)^{2} \;=\; \left(x + \tfrac{b}{2}\right)^{2}. $$

For example, $x^{2} + 6 x$ needs $(6/2)^{2} = 9$ to become $(x + 3)^{2}$. The reason is geometric: a square of side $x$ plus a $6 \times x$ strip is an L shape, and the missing corner is a $3 \times 3$ square. Algebraically, adding $9$ to a quantity changes its value, so to keep an expression equal you must subtract $9$ again; to keep an equation balanced you must add $9$ to both sides.例如 $x^{2} + 6 x$ 需要加上 $(6/2)^{2} = 9$ 才成为 $(x + 3)^{2}$。原因是几何的：边长为 $x$ 的正方形加上 $6 \times x$ 的长条是 L 形，缺的角是 $3 \times 3$ 的方块。代数上，加 $9$ 会改变值：要保持表达式不变就必须再减去 $9$；要保持等式平衡就必须在两边都加 $9$。

Solving by completing the square. Solve $x^{2} + 6 x - 7 = 0$. Move the constant: $x^{2} + 6 x = 7$. Add $9$ to both sides: $x^{2} + 6 x + 9 = 16$, that is $(x + 3)^{2} = 16$. Take both square roots: $x + 3 = \pm 4$, so $x = 1$ or $x = -7$. Check: $1 + 6 - 7 = 0$ and $49 - 42 - 7 = 0$.用配方法解方程。解 $x^{2} + 6 x - 7 = 0$。移项：$x^{2} + 6 x = 7$。两边加 $9$：$x^{2} + 6 x + 9 = 16$，即 $(x + 3)^{2} = 16$。取两个平方根：$x + 3 = \pm 4$，故 $x = 1$ 或 $x = -7$。检验：$1 + 6 - 7 = 0$，$49 - 42 - 7 = 0$。

Common Questions常见问题

How do I complete the square?如何使用配方法？

Move the constant aside, take half of the x-coefficient and square it, add that value to both sides, then write the left side as a squared binomial to reach vertex form.先把常数项移到一边，取 x 系数的一半并平方，将该值加到两边，然后把左边写成一个完全平方式，从而得到顶点式。

What does the discriminant tell you?判别式能告诉我们什么？

The discriminant b squared minus 4ac shows the number and type of roots: positive gives two real roots, zero gives one repeated root, and negative gives two complex roots.判别式 b 平方减 4ac 决定根的数量与类型：为正时有两个实根，为零时有一个重根，为负时有两个复根。

When should I use the quadratic formula instead of factoring?什么时候该用求根公式而不是因式分解？

Use factoring when the quadratic factors with whole numbers, but use the quadratic formula when the expression does not factor nicely or the roots are irrational.当二次式能用整数因式分解时用因式分解，但当表达式难以分解或根为无理数时应使用求根公式。
