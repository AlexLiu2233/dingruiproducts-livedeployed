  • (A) is larger along the longer path, because the force acts over more distance沿较长路径时较大，因为力作用的距离更长

Answer:答案： (B)
The electrostatic force is conservative, so the work it does between two points is $W=-\Delta U$, which depends only on the initial and final positions and not on the path.静电力是保守力，因此它在两点之间做的功为 $W=-\Delta U$，只取决于初末位置，与路径无关。

  • (A) $18~\mathrm{V}$
  • (B) $200~\mathrm{V}$
  • (C) $60~\mathrm{V}$
  • (D) $600~\mathrm{V}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$V=\frac{kQ}{r}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(2.0\times10^{-9})}{0.30}\approx 60~\mathrm{V}$$
The potential of a point charge falls as $1/r$ and is positive for a positive charge. The result carries two significant figures because $Q$ and $r$ do.点电荷的电势按 $1/r$ 下降，正电荷的电势为正。因为 $Q$ 和 $r$ 都是两位有效数字，结果保留两位。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $18~\mathrm{V}$ is $kQ=17.98~\mathrm{V\,m}$, the product with the distance never divided in. (B) $200~\mathrm{V}$ is $kQ/r^2=200~\mathrm{N/C}$, the electric field magnitude; it has the wrong units and the wrong power of $r$. (D) $600~\mathrm{V}$ is a power-of-ten slip in the division by $0.30$.(A) $18~\mathrm{V}$ 是 $kQ=17.98~\mathrm{V\,m}$，没有除以距离。(B) $200~\mathrm{V}$ 是 $kQ/r^2=200~\mathrm{N/C}$，即电场强度，单位和 $r$ 的幂次都不对。(D) $600~\mathrm{V}$ 是除以 $0.30$ 时的数量级错误。
Insight.要点。 Potential goes as $1/r$ and field goes as $1/r^2$. Check the units of your final number: volts for $V$, volts per metre for $E$.电势按 $1/r$ 变化，电场按 $1/r^2$ 变化。检查最终结果的单位：$V$ 为伏特，$E$ 为伏每米。

A proton is released from rest and accelerates through a potential difference of $500~\mathrm{V}$. Its kinetic energy afterwards is closest to ($e=1.60\times10^{-19}~\mathrm{C}$)一个质子由静止释放，并通过 $500~\mathrm{V}$ 的电势差加速。之后它的动能最接近（$e=1.60\times10^{-19}~\mathrm{C}$）

  • (A) $8.0\times10^{-17}~\mathrm{J}$
  • (B) $3.2\times10^{-22}~\mathrm{J}$
  • (C) $5.0\times10^{2}~\mathrm{J}$
  • (D) $8.0\times10^{-15}~\mathrm{J}$
Answer:答案： (A)
$$K=q\,\Delta V=(1.60\times10^{-19})(500)=8.0\times10^{-17}~\mathrm{J}$$
Energy conservation gives $\Delta K=-\Delta U=-q\,\Delta V$; the proton moves to lower potential, so $K$ rises by $q|\Delta V|$. This is also $500~\mathrm{eV}$.能量守恒给出 $\Delta K=-\Delta U=-q\,\Delta V$；质子向低电势运动，因此动能增加 $q|\Delta V|$。这也等于 $500~\mathrm{eV}$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) $3.2\times10^{-22}~\mathrm{J}$ divides $e$ by $500$ instead of multiplying. (C) $5.0\times10^{2}~\mathrm{J}$ forgets the charge and reports the potential difference as if it were energy. (D) $8.0\times10^{-15}~\mathrm{J}$ is a power-of-ten error, as if $\Delta V$ were $50{,}000~\mathrm{V}$.(B) $3.2\times10^{-22}~\mathrm{J}$ 把 $e$ 除以了 $500$，而不是相乘。(C) $5.0\times10^{2}~\mathrm{J}$ 忘记乘电荷，把电势差当成了能量。(D) $8.0\times10^{-15}~\mathrm{J}$ 是数量级错误，相当于把 $\Delta V$ 取成 $50{,}000~\mathrm{V}$。
Insight.要点。 One elementary charge through one volt gains exactly one electron-volt, $1.60\times10^{-19}~\mathrm{J}$. Multiply, never divide, by the charge.一个元电荷经过一伏电势差恰好获得一电子伏特，即 $1.60\times10^{-19}~\mathrm{J}$。应当乘以电荷，而不是除以电荷。

  • (A) $\dfrac{kq^2}{d}$
  • (B) $\dfrac{2kq^2}{d}$
  • (C) $\dfrac{3kq^2}{d}$
  • (D) $\dfrac{6kq^2}{d}$
Answer:答案： (C)
The first charge costs nothing. The second feels one neighbour: $kq^2/d$. The third feels two neighbours, each at distance $d$: $2kq^2/d$. The total is the sum over the three distinct pairs.第一个电荷不需要做功。第二个电荷受一个邻居作用：$kq^2/d$。第三个电荷受两个邻居作用，距离均为 $d$：$2kq^2/d$。总功等于三个不同电荷对的能量之和。
$$W=0+\frac{kq^2}{d}+\frac{2kq^2}{d}=\frac{3kq^2}{d}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) counts only one pair. (B) stops after the third charge's work and forgets the second charge's $kq^2/d$. (D) counts each pair twice, once from each member, which double-counts the shared energy.(A) 只算了一对电荷。(B) 只算了第三个电荷的功，漏掉了第二个电荷的 $kq^2/d$。(D) 把每一对电荷算了两次（从每个成员各算一次），重复计算了共享的能量。

The average field between two points is $|\Delta V|/\Delta x$, directed toward lower potential.一组点电荷的电势是各项 $kq/r$ 的代数和。在远离这组电荷的地方，各个距离几乎相同，所以这组电荷就像一个等于总电荷的单个电荷。两点之间的平均电场为 $|\Delta V|/\Delta x$，方向指向电势较低处。

FRQ 6HARD 9.2 Semicircular Arc and Comparison9.2 半圆弧与比较Calculator

A thin insulating rod is bent into a semicircular arc of radius $R=0.25~\mathrm{m}$ and carries a uniformly distributed total charge $Q=+3.0~\mathrm{nC}$. Point $C$ is the centre of curvature of the arc. Take $V=0$ at infinity. Use $m_e=9.11\times10^{-31}~\mathrm{kg}$ and $e=1.60\times10^{-19}~\mathrm{C}$.一根细绝缘棒弯成半径 $R=0.25~\mathrm{m}$ 的半圆弧，均匀带总电荷 $Q=+3.0~\mathrm{nC}$。点 $C$ 是该圆弧的圆心。取无穷远处 $V=0$。取 $m_e=9.11\times10^{-31}~\mathrm{kg}$，$e=1.60\times10^{-19}~\mathrm{C}$。

(a) Every element is a distance $R$ from $C$.每个电荷元到 $C$ 的距离都是 $R$。
$$V_C=\frac{kQ}{R}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(3.0\times10^{-9})}{0.25}\approx1.1\times10^{2}~\mathrm{V}$$
(b) Resolve each element's field; the components perpendicular to the symmetry axis cancel.分解每个电荷元的电场；垂直于对称轴的分量相互抵消。 Let $\theta$ be measured from the symmetry axis, $\lambda=Q/(\pi R)$ and $dq=\lambda R\,d\theta$. The axial component of the field of one element is $dE=\dfrac{k\,dq}{R^2}\cos\theta$:令 $\theta$ 从对称轴起算，$\lambda=Q/(\pi R)$，$dq=\lambda R\,d\theta$。每个电荷元电场的轴向分量为 $dE=\dfrac{k\,dq}{R^2}\cos\theta$：
$$E=\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\frac{k\lambda}{R}\cos\theta\,d\theta=\frac{2k\lambda}{R}=\frac{2kQ}{\pi R^2}$$
$$E=\frac{2(8.99\times10^{9})(3.0\times10^{-9})}{\pi(0.25)^2}\approx2.7\times10^{2}~\mathrm{N/C}$$
The field at $C$ points away from the arc's midpoint, toward the open side of the semicircle.$C$ 处的电场背离圆弧的中点，指向半圆的开口一侧。
(c) The force on the electron is opposite to $\vec E$.电子受力与 $\vec E$ 相反。
$$a=\frac{eE}{m_e}=\frac{(1.60\times10^{-19})(274.7)}{9.11\times10^{-31}}\approx4.8\times10^{13}~\mathrm{m/s^2}$$
The electron accelerates toward the arc's midpoint, since it is attracted to the positive charge.电子向圆弧中点加速，因为它被正电荷吸引。
(d) Potential is a scalar sum; field is a vector sum.电势是标量和；电场是矢量和。
$$V_C=\frac{k(2Q)}{R}=\frac{2(8.99\times10^{9})(3.0\times10^{-9})}{0.25}\approx2.2\times10^{2}~\mathrm{V},\qquad E_C=0$$
The potential doubles because the total charge doubles at the same distance. The field at the centre of a uniform ring is zero because the two arcs' fields have equal magnitude and opposite direction. Scalars add, vectors cancel.电势加倍，因为在相同距离处总电荷加倍。均匀圆环中心的电场为零，因为两段圆弧的电场大小相等、方向相反。标量相加，矢量抵消。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) Halving the answer because the charge is on half a circle. (b) Integrating $\theta$ from $0$ to $\pi$ and getting zero, or omitting the $\cos\theta$ weighting and getting $kQ/R^2$. (c) Sending the electron away from the arc, or using the proton mass. (d) Concluding that $V=0$ because $E=0$, or that $E$ doubles with the charge.(a) 因为电荷只在半个圆上就把答案减半。(b) 把 $\theta$ 从 $0$ 积到 $\pi$ 得到零，或漏掉 $\cos\theta$ 权重而得到 $kQ/R^2$。(c) 让电子背离圆弧运动，或用了质子的质量。(d) 因为 $E=0$ 就得出 $V=0$，或认为 $E$ 随电荷加倍。
Insight.要点。 The same charges give a scalar sum for $V$ and a vector sum for $E$. A semicircle at its centre is the standard test: $V=kQ/R$ is easy, while $E=2kQ/\pi R^2$ needs the cosine integral.同样的电荷，对 $V$ 做标量求和，对 $E$ 做矢量求和。半圆弧圆心处是标准的检验：$V=kQ/R$ 很容易，而 $E=2kQ/\pi R^2$ 需要余弦积分。

FRQ 6HARD 10.5 Two Point Charges on a Circle10.5 圆周上的两个点电荷Calculator

Two identical point charges, each $+1.5~\mathrm{nC}$, are fixed on a circle of radius $R=0.25~\mathrm{m}$ centered at the origin $C$, at $(R,0)$ and $(0,R)$. Take $V=0$ at infinity. Use $m_e=9.11\times10^{-31}~\mathrm{kg}$ and $e=1.60\times10^{-19}~\mathrm{C}$.两个相同的点电荷，每个 $+1.5~\mathrm{nC}$，固定在以原点 $C$ 为圆心、半径 $R=0.25~\mathrm{m}$ 的圆周上，位置为 $(R,0)$ 和 $(0,R)$。取无穷远处 $V=0$。使用 $m_e=9.11\times10^{-31}~\mathrm{kg}$ 和 $e=1.60\times10^{-19}~\mathrm{C}$。

(a) Add the two scalar potentials.把两个标量电势相加。
$$V=2\,\frac{kq}{R}=\frac{2(8.99\times10^{9})(1.5\times10^{-9})}{0.25}\approx108~\mathrm{V}$$
(b) Add the two field vectors.把两个电场矢量相加。 Each charge produces a field of magnitude每个电荷产生的电场大小为
$$E_1=\frac{kq}{R^2}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(1.5\times10^{-9})}{(0.25)^2}\approx216~\mathrm{N/C}$$
The fields at $C$ point away from their charges, along $-x$ and $-y$, so they are perpendicular:$C$ 处的两个电场背离各自的电荷，分别沿 $-x$ 和 $-y$，所以二者相互垂直：
$$E=\sqrt{E_1^2+E_1^2}=\sqrt2\,E_1\approx305~\mathrm{N/C}$$
The field points away from the pair, along the line from the midpoint of the two charges through $C$ (at $45^\circ$ between $-x$ and $-y$).电场背离这对电荷，沿从两电荷中点经过 $C$ 的直线方向（在 $-x$ 与 $-y$ 之间成 $45^\circ$）。
(c) Force on the electron.电子所受的力。
$$a=\frac{eE}{m_e}=\frac{(1.60\times10^{-19})(305)}{9.11\times10^{-31}}\approx5.36\times10^{13}~\mathrm{m/s^2}$$
The electron is negative, so the force is opposite to the field: the acceleration points toward the pair of charges (toward the midpoint between them, at $45^\circ$ between $+x$ and $+y$).电子带负电，所以受力与电场方向相反：加速度指向这对电荷（指向它们的中点，在 $+x$ 与 $+y$ 之间成 $45^\circ$）。
(d) Four charges: the potential doubles, the field vanishes.四个电荷：电势加倍，电场消失。
$$V=4\,\frac{kq}{R}\approx216~\mathrm{V},\qquad \vec E=0$$
Potentials of like charges are scalars of the same sign, so each added charge raises $V$ and the value doubles. The added charges at $(-R,0)$ and $(0,-R)$ produce fields along $+x$ and $+y$ that exactly cancel the first pair, so the vector sum is zero.同号电荷的电势是同号的标量，所以每增加一个电荷都使 $V$ 升高，数值加倍。加在 $(-R,0)$ 和 $(0,-R)$ 处的电荷产生沿 $+x$ 和 $+y$ 的电场，恰好抵消第一对电荷的电场，所以矢量和为零。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A student uses one charge only and gets $kq/R\approx54~\mathrm{V}$. (b) The student adds the field magnitudes, $2kq/R^2\approx432~\mathrm{N/C}$, forgetting that the two fields are perpendicular. (c) The student gives the acceleration along the field, away from the pair, instead of opposite to it. (d) The student expects the field to double like the potential.(a) 学生只用一个电荷，得到 $kq/R\approx54~\mathrm{V}$。(b) 学生把电场的大小直接相加，$2kq/R^2\approx432~\mathrm{N/C}$，忘了两个电场相互垂直。(c) 学生把加速度方向写成沿电场方向（背离这对电荷），而不是与电场相反。(d) 学生以为电场会像电势一样加倍。
Insight.要点。 Potentials of like charges add as numbers, so more charge always raises $V$. Field vectors can cancel by symmetry, so adding charges can bring $E$ to zero while $V$ rises.同号电荷的电势按数值相加，所以电荷越多，$V$ 越高。电场矢量可以因对称而抵消，所以增加电荷可能使 $E$ 变为零，而 $V$ 升高。
