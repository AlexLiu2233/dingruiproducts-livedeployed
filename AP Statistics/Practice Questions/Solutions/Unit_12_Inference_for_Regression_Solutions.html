Q 1第1题EASY基础Sampling distribution of the slope斜率的抽样分布CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A random sample of 35 students from a school of 1,500 students gives the least-squares line $\hat y=48.2+3.1x$, where $x$ is weekly study time in hours and $y$ is quiz score in points. Which statement about the number 3.1 is correct?
从某校 1,500 名学生中随机抽取 35 人，得到最小二乘直线 $\hat y=48.2+3.1x$，其中 $x$ 为每周学习时间（小时），$y$ 为测验分数（分）。关于数字 3.1，哪项正确？
Answer: C答案：C

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The line is fitted to a sample of 35 students, so 3.1 is a statistic: the sample slope $b$, the predicted change in score per extra weekly hour in this sample. The matching parameter $\beta$ is the slope of the population regression line for all 1,500 students, fixed but unknown, and $b$ is only an estimate that varies with the sample.这条直线由 35 名学生的样本拟合，所以 3.1 是统计量，即样本斜率 $b$：在这个样本中，每周多学一小时对应的预测分数变化。对应的参数 $\beta$ 是全部 1,500 名学生的总体回归直线（population regression line）斜率，固定而未知；$b$ 只是随样本而变的估计。

Why the options attract (M 1)选项误区（M 1）
  1. Mistakes the slope for a predicted value; the prediction at $x=1$ is $48.2+3.1(1)=51.3$ points, and 3.1 is the change per hour.把斜率当成预测值；$x=1$ 时的预测值是 $48.2+3.1(1)=51.3$ 分，3.1 是每小时的变化量。
  2. Confuses the slope with $s$, the standard deviation of the residuals; the typical prediction error comes from the scatter about the line, not from the line's coefficients.把斜率与残差标准差 $s$ 混淆；典型预测误差来自点围绕直线的散布，而不是直线的系数。
  3. Correct: a fitted line from a sample gives the statistic $b$.正确：由样本拟合的直线给出统计量 $b$。
  4. Treats the sample slope as the population slope and as exact for every student; $\beta$ is unknown, $b$ only estimates it, and the slope describes the mean change, not each student.把样本斜率当成总体斜率，并当成对每名学生都精确成立；$\beta$ 未知，$b$ 只是估计，而且斜率描述的是均值的变化，不是每名学生的变化。
In every regression report, ask first whether a number describes the sample ($b$, $r$, $s$) or the population ($\beta$, $\rho$, $\sigma$), and whether it is a rate of change or a predicted value.每次读回归结果，先问这个数描述的是样本（$b$、$r$、$s$）还是总体（$\beta$、$\rho$、$\sigma$），以及它是变化率还是预测值。

Q 2第2题EASY基础Sampling distribution of the slope斜率的抽样分布CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A regression line is fitted to a random sample of $n=26$ points. The standard deviation of the residuals is $s=3.2$ and the standard deviation of the $x$-values is $s_x=2.5$. What is the standard error of the slope, $SE_b$?
对 $n=26$ 个点的随机样本拟合回归直线。残差的标准差 $s=3.2$，$x$ 值的标准差 $s_x=2.5$。斜率的标准误（standard error） $SE_b$ 是多少？
Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

$SE_b=\dfrac{s}{s_x\sqrt{n-1}}=\dfrac{3.2}{2.5\sqrt{25}}=\dfrac{3.2}{12.5}=0.256$.$SE_b=\dfrac{s}{s_x\sqrt{n-1}}=\dfrac{3.2}{2.5\sqrt{25}}=\dfrac{3.2}{12.5}=0.256$。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. $3.2/(2.5\sqrt{26})\approx0.251$ uses $\sqrt n$; the slope formula uses $\sqrt{n-1}$.$3.2/(2.5\sqrt{26})\approx0.251$ 用了 $\sqrt n$；斜率公式用的是 $\sqrt{n-1}$。
  2. Correct: $3.2/(2.5\cdot5)=0.256$.正确：$3.2/(2.5\cdot5)=0.256$。
  3. $3.2/\sqrt{25}=0.640$ leaves $s_x$ out of the denominator, so the spread of the $x$-values is ignored.$3.2/\sqrt{25}=0.640$ 漏掉分母中的 $s_x$，忽略了 $x$ 值的离散程度。
  4. $3.2/(2.5\cdot25)=0.0512$ divides by $n-1$ instead of $\sqrt{n-1}$.$3.2/(2.5\cdot25)=0.0512$ 除以的是 $n-1$ 而不是 $\sqrt{n-1}$。
$SE_b$ falls when the points hug the line (small $s$), when the $x$-values are spread out (large $s_x$) and when $n$ is large.$SE_b$ 在点贴近直线（$s$ 小）、$x$ 值分布宽（$s_x$ 大）、样本量大时变小。

Q 3第3题EASY基础Confidence intervals for the slope斜率的置信区间CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A researcher takes a random sample of 18 laptops from a very large inventory and fits a least-squares line of battery life on screen brightness. Conditions have been checked, and she builds a 95% confidence interval for the population slope. Which critical value should she use?
研究者从庞大的库存中随机抽取 18 台笔记本电脑，用屏幕亮度对电池续航拟合最小二乘直线。条件已检验，她要构造总体斜率的 95% 置信区间（confidence interval）。应使用哪个临界值（critical value）？
Answer: D答案：D

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

For a slope the degrees of freedom are $n-2=18-2=16$, because two numbers, $a$ and $b$, were estimated before the residuals were found. For 95% confidence $t^*$ leaves 2.5% in each tail of the $t$ distribution with 16 degrees of freedom, so $t^*=2.120$ (invT(0.975, 16) on the TI-84).斜率推断的自由度（degrees of freedom）为 $n-2=18-2=16$，因为求残差之前已估计了 $a$ 与 $b$ 两个数。95% 置信水平下，$t^*$ 在自由度 16 的 $t$ 分布每侧留下 2.5%，故 $t^*=2.120$（TI-84：invT(0.975, 16)）。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. Uses $df=n-1=17$, the one-sample mean rule, which ignores the second estimated number.用了 $df=n-1=17$（单样本均值的规则），忽略了第二个被估计的数。
  2. Uses $df=n=18$; the degrees of freedom are never equal to the sample size here.用了 $df=n=18$；这里自由度不会等于样本量。
  3. Uses a Normal critical value, but $\sigma$ is estimated by $s$, so a $t$ distribution is needed.用了正态临界值，但 $\sigma$ 由 $s$ 估计，必须用 $t$ 分布。
  4. Correct: $df=n-2=16$ and $t^*=2.120$.正确：$df=n-2=16$，$t^*=2.120$。
Every slope procedure uses $t$ with $df=n-2$: check the sample size first, then subtract two.斜率的所有程序都用自由度 $n-2$ 的 $t$ 分布：先看样本量，再减去 2。

Two studies select random samples from the same large population and fit a regression of $y$ on $x$. Study 1 uses $n$ points and Study 2 uses $4n-3$ points, with $n\gt1$. Suppose both studies happen to have the same residual standard deviation $s$ and the same standard deviation $s_x$. How does the standard error of the slope in Study 2 compare with that in Study 1?
两项研究从同一大总体中随机抽样，并用 $x$ 对 $y$ 拟合回归。研究 1 用 $n$ 个点，研究 2 用 $4n-3$ 个点，$n\gt1$。设两项研究的残差标准差 $s$ 与 $x$ 值标准差 $s_x$ 恰好相同。研究 2 的斜率标准误（standard error）与研究 1 相比如何？
Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

$SE_b=\dfrac{s}{s_x\sqrt{n-1}}$. For Study 1 the denominator contains $\sqrt{n-1}$; for Study 2 it contains $\sqrt{(4n-3)-1}=\sqrt{4(n-1)}=2\sqrt{n-1}$. With $s$ and $s_x$ unchanged, $SE_b$ is divided by 2 for every $n\gt1$.$SE_b=\dfrac{s}{s_x\sqrt{n-1}}$。研究 1 的分母含 $\sqrt{n-1}$；研究 2 的分母含 $\sqrt{(4n-3)-1}=\sqrt{4(n-1)}=2\sqrt{n-1}$。$s$ 与 $s_x$ 不变时，对任意 $n\gt1$，$SE_b$ 都变为一半。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. Correct: $n-1$ is multiplied by 4, so $\sqrt{n-1}$ is multiplied by 2.正确：$n-1$ 变为 4 倍，$\sqrt{n-1}$ 变为 2 倍。
  2. Quadrupling the data divides $SE_b$ by $\sqrt4=2$, not by 4; the standard error shrinks like a square root.数据量变为 4 倍，$SE_b$ 除以 $\sqrt4=2$ 而不是 4；标准误按平方根缩小。
  3. Holding $s$ and $s_x$ fixed does not freeze the standard error: $n$ still enters through $\sqrt{n-1}$.固定 $s$ 与 $s_x$ 并不会使标准误不变：$n$ 仍通过 $\sqrt{n-1}$ 起作用。
  4. Looks for a dependence on $n$, but $4n-3-1=4(n-1)$ makes the ratio exactly $\tfrac12$ for every $n$.去找对 $n$ 的依赖，但 $4n-3-1=4(n-1)$ 使比值对每个 $n$ 都恰好是 $\tfrac12$。
When the sample size changes, track $n-1$ inside the square root: multiplying $n-1$ by $k$ divides $SE_b$ by $\sqrt k$.样本量变化时，要盯住根号里的 $n-1$：$n-1$ 乘以 $k$，$SE_b$ 就除以 $\sqrt k$。

Q 5第5题HARD较难Sampling distribution of the slope斜率的抽样分布CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
Random samples of size $n$ are repeatedly drawn from a population in which the LINE conditions hold, and a least-squares slope $b$ is computed from each. Consider three statements. I. The mean of all the possible values of $b$ equals the population slope $\beta$. II. For fixed $n$ and fixed $s$, using $x$-values that are more spread out makes $SE_b$ smaller. III. A sample of size $2n$ is guaranteed to give a value of $b$ closer to $\beta$ than any sample of size $n$. Which statements must be true?
从满足 LINE 条件的总体中反复抽取样本量为 $n$ 的随机样本，并从每个样本算出最小二乘斜率 $b$。考虑三个陈述。I. $b$ 所有可能取值的均值等于总体斜率 $\beta$。II. 在 $n$ 与 $s$ 固定时，$x$ 值分布更宽会使 $SE_b$ 更小。III. 样本量为 $2n$ 的样本必定比任何样本量为 $n$ 的样本给出更接近 $\beta$ 的 $b$。哪些陈述必然成立？
Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

I is true: $b$ is an unbiased estimator of $\beta$, so the centre of its sampling distribution is $\beta$. II is true: $SE_b=s/(s_x\sqrt{n-1})$ has $s_x$ in the denominator, so a larger $s_x$ gives a smaller $SE_b$. III is false: a larger sample makes the sampling distribution narrower, but one particular small sample can still land closer to $\beta$ than one particular large sample. The answer is I and II only.I 成立：$b$ 是 $\beta$ 的无偏估计量，其抽样分布（sampling distribution）的中心是 $\beta$。II 成立：$SE_b=s/(s_x\sqrt{n-1})$ 的分母含 $s_x$，$s_x$ 越大 $SE_b$ 越小。III 不成立：更大的样本使抽样分布更窄，但某个特定的小样本仍可能比某个特定的大样本更接近 $\beta$。答案为仅 I 与 II。

Why the options attract (M 1)选项误区（M 1）
  1. I only drops II; the formula for $SE_b$ shows the spread of the $x$-values matters.仅 I 漏掉了 II；$SE_b$ 的公式说明 $x$ 值的离散程度有影响。
  2. Correct: I and II are true, III is false.正确：I 与 II 成立，III 不成立。
  3. Rejects I, although the unbiasedness of $b$ is exactly why its sampling distribution is centred at $\beta$, and accepts III, which turns 'typically closer' into 'always closer'.否定了 I，而 $b$ 的无偏性正是其抽样分布以 $\beta$ 为中心的原因；又接受了 III，把“通常更接近”说成“一定更接近”。
  4. Includes III: a larger sample narrows the distribution of $b$ but gives no guarantee for any single pair of samples.把 III 也算进来：更大的样本使 $b$ 的分布变窄，但对任意一对具体样本并无保证。
'Typically closer' is a statement about a sampling distribution; 'guaranteed closer' is a claim about one sample, and statistics never makes it.“通常更接近”说的是抽样分布；“一定更接近”说的是某一个样本，统计学从不作这种保证。

对条件已检验的 $n=22$ 个点的随机样本，检验 $H_0:\beta=0$ 得到样本斜率 $b=1.92$ 和检验统计量（test statistic） $t=3.2$。$\beta$ 的 95% 置信区间（confidence interval）是哪一个？
