Topic 14.1专题 14.1

Properties of Wave Pulses and Waves波脉冲与波的性质

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe how a wave pulse and a wave transfer energy between two locations without transferring matter, and distinguish a single pulse from a periodic wave.
  • Explain why mechanical waves need a medium while electromagnetic waves do not, and state the universal speed $c$ of electromagnetic waves in a vacuum.
  • Calculate the speed of a wave on a string from $v = \sqrt{F_T/\mu}$ and predict how it changes when the tension or the mass per unit length changes.
  • Compare transverse and longitudinal waves, and describe sound as a longitudinal wave made of compressions and rarefactions.
  • Justify how amplitude, loudness and wave energy are related, including the pressure amplitude of a sound wave.
  • Predict how the speed of sound in a medium changes with the temperature of that medium.
  • 描述（describe）波脉冲和波如何在两个位置之间传递能量而不传递物质，并区分单个脉冲与周期波。
  • 解释（explain）为什么机械波需要介质而电磁波不需要，并写出电磁波在真空中的普适速度 $c$。
  • 由 $v = \sqrt{F_T/\mu}$ 计算（calculate）弦上的波速，并预测（predict）张力或单位长度质量变化时波速如何变化。

Waves Carry Energy, Not Matter波传递能量，不传递物质

Flick one end of a long rope and a bump runs along it. Each piece of rope moves up and back down near its own place, yet the bump, and the energy it carries, travels all the way to the far end.

轻弹一根长绳的一端，一个凸起沿绳传播。绳上每一小段只是在自己的位置附近上下运动，但凸起以及它携带的能量却一直传到远端。

This is the defining property of waves: waves transfer energy between two locations without transferring matter between those locations. A cork floating on a pond bobs up and down as ripples pass but is not carried to the shore.

这就是波的根本特征：波在两个位置之间传递能量，而不在这两个位置之间传递物质。漂在池塘上的软木塞在涟漪经过时上下浮动，却不会被带到岸边。

Two idealized forms are used. A wave pulse is a single disturbance that transfers energy without transferring matter, like the one bump from a single flick.

常用两种理想化形式。波脉冲（wave pulse）是单个扰动，传递能量而不传递物质，就像一次轻弹产生的那个凸起。

A wave is modeled as a continuous, periodic disturbance with a well-defined wavelength and frequency, like the steady train of crests made by shaking the rope back and forth at a steady rate. The pulse is the building block: a periodic wave can be thought of as a long, regular sequence of pulses.

波被建模为具有明确波长和频率的连续、周期性扰动，就像以稳定节奏来回抖动绳子产生的一串波峰。脉冲是基本单元：周期波可以看作一长串规则排列的脉冲。

Mechanical and Electromagnetic Waves, and Wave Speed机械波、电磁波与波速

Mechanical waves and mechanical wave pulses require a medium (a rope, water, air, a solid) in which to propagate: the disturbance passes from one bit of the medium to the next through the forces between neighbors, so with no medium there is nothing to carry it.

机械波和机械波脉冲需要介质（medium，如绳、水、空气、固体）才能传播：扰动靠相邻部分之间的作用力从介质的一处传到下一处，没有介质就没有东西来传递它。

Sound is therefore a mechanical wave and cannot cross a vacuum. Electromagnetic waves (light, radio, X-rays) do not require a medium. They can travel through empty space, which is how sunlight reaches Earth.

因此声音是机械波，不能穿过真空。电磁波（光、无线电波、X 射线）不需要介质，可以在真空中传播，阳光就是这样到达地球的。

The speed at which a wave or pulse propagates depends on the type of wave and the properties of the medium, not on how hard or how often the source disturbs the medium. For all electromagnetic waves in a vacuum the speed is the same universal physical constant,

波或脉冲的传播速度取决于波的类型和介质的性质，而不取决于波源扰动介质的力度或频繁程度。真空中所有电磁波的速度都是同一个普适物理常量，

Speed of electromagnetic waves in a vacuum真空中电磁波的速度
$$c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$$

For sound, the speed in a given medium increases with the temperature of the medium: warmer particles move faster, so a disturbance is handed from particle to particle more quickly.

对声音而言，给定介质中的声速随介质温度升高而增大：温度越高，粒子运动越快，扰动在粒子之间传递得也越快。

In this guide the speed of sound in air is $343\,\mathrm{m/s}$, a value that holds for air at about $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$; in colder air it is lower and in hotter air it is higher.

本指南中空气里的声速取 $343\,\mathrm{m/s}$，这个值对应约 $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 的空气；空气更冷时声速更小，更热时更大。

Speed of a Wave on a String弦上的波速

For a pulse or wave on a stretched string, the speed depends on two properties of the string: the tension $F_T$ in it and its mass per unit length $\mu$ (also called the linear mass density), where $\mu = m_\text{string}/L$.

对拉紧的弦上的脉冲或波，波速取决于弦的两个性质：弦中的张力 $F_T$ 和单位长度的质量 $\mu$（也叫线密度），其中 $\mu = m_\text{string}/L$。

Wave speed on a string弦上的波速
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{F_T}{\mu}}$$

Here $v$ is the wave speed in m/s, $F_T$ is the tension in N, and $\mu$ is the mass per unit length in kg/m. The square root means the speed responds to the square root of tension: quadrupling the tension doubles $v$.

其中 $v$ 为波速（m/s），$F_T$ 为张力（N），$\mu$ 为单位长度的质量（kg/m）。开平方意味着波速随张力的平方根变化：张力变为 4 倍，$v$ 变为 2 倍。

A heavier string (larger $\mu$) is more sluggish, so $v$ is smaller. The reason is the same as for any oscillator: a larger restoring force (tension pulling each bit of string back toward the straight line) speeds up the response, and larger mass slows it.

更重的弦（$\mu$ 更大）反应更迟缓，所以 $v$ 更小。原因与任何振子相同：回复力越大（张力把每一小段弦拉回直线），响应越快；质量越大，响应越慢。

First Principles: Why $v = \sqrt{F_T/\mu}$第一性原理：为什么 $v = \sqrt{F_T/\mu}$

Principle: Newton's second law applied to a tiny piece of string at the crest of a small pulse.

出发原理：对小脉冲波峰处的一小段弦应用牛顿第二定律（Newton's second law）。

  1. Ride along with the pulse at speed $v$. In this frame the string slides through the curved crest at speed $v$, like a car over a hilltop of radius $R$.
  2. A piece of length $\Delta\ell = 2R\theta$ has mass $\mu\,\Delta\ell$. Tension pulls each end, tilted by a small angle $\theta$, so the net pull toward the center is $2F_T\sin\theta \approx F_T\,\Delta\ell/R$.
  3. That net pull is the centripetal force: $\mu\,\Delta\ell\,v^2/R = F_T\,\Delta\ell/R$.
  4. Cancel $\Delta\ell$ and $R$: $v^2 = F_T/\mu$, so $v = \sqrt{F_T/\mu}$.
  1. 以速度 $v$ 随脉冲一起运动。在这个参考系中，弦以速度 $v$ 滑过弯曲的波峰，就像汽车驶过半径为 $R$ 的山顶。
  2. 长度为 $\Delta\ell = 2R\theta$ 的一小段质量为 $\mu\,\Delta\ell$。张力拉着它的两端，各倾斜一个小角 $\theta$，所以指向圆心的合力为 $2F_T\sin\theta \approx F_T\,\Delta\ell/R$。
  3. 这个合力提供向心力：$\mu\,\Delta\ell\,v^2/R = F_T\,\Delta\ell/R$。
  4. 消去 $\Delta\ell$ 和 $R$：$v^2 = F_T/\mu$，所以 $v = \sqrt{F_T/\mu}$。

Limiting check: the units are $\sqrt{\mathrm{N}/(\mathrm{kg/m})} = \mathrm{m/s}$. A slack string ($F_T \to 0$) or an infinitely heavy one ($\mu \to \infty$) gives $v \to 0$. The crest radius $R$ cancelled, so the speed does not depend on the pulse's shape or size.

极限检验：单位为 $\sqrt{\mathrm{N}/(\mathrm{kg/m})} = \mathrm{m/s}$。松弛的弦（$F_T \to 0$）或无限重的弦（$\mu \to \infty$）都给出 $v \to 0$。波峰半径 $R$ 被消去，所以波速与脉冲的形状和大小无关。

Worked Example: Pulse Speed on a Stretched String例题：拉紧的弦上的脉冲速度

A string of mass $0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$ and length $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ is held under a tension of $40\,\mathrm{N}$. Find the speed of a pulse on it, the time the pulse needs to travel the whole length, and the new speed if the tension is raised to $160\,\mathrm{N}$.

一根质量为 $0.050\,\mathrm{kg}$、长为 $2.5\,\mathrm{m}$ 的弦受到 $40\,\mathrm{N}$ 的张力。求弦上脉冲的速度、脉冲走完全长所需的时间，以及张力增大到 $160\,\mathrm{N}$ 时的新速度。

Step 1: Mass per unit length第 1 步：单位长度的质量
$$\mu = \frac{m}{L} = \frac{0.050}{2.5} = 0.020\,\mathrm{kg/m}$$
Step 2: Speed and travel time第 2 步：速度与传播时间
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{40}{0.020}} = \sqrt{2000} = 44.7\,\mathrm{m/s}, \qquad t = \frac{L}{v} = \frac{2.5}{44.7} = 0.056\,\mathrm{s}$$
Step 3: Raise the tension to 160 N第 3 步：张力增大到 160 N
$$v' = \sqrt{\frac{160}{0.020}} = 89.4\,\mathrm{m/s}$$
The tension grew by a factor of 4, so the speed grew by $\sqrt{4} = 2$. Why this works: $v$ depends on the square root of $F_T/\mu$, and the string's $\mu$ did not change.张力变为 4 倍，所以速度变为 $\sqrt{4} = 2$ 倍。为什么这样做：$v$ 取决于 $F_T/\mu$ 的平方根，而弦的 $\mu$ 没有变。

Transverse and Longitudinal Waves横波与纵波

The two basic kinds of wave differ in how the disturbance of the medium compares with the direction in which the wave moves (its direction of propagation). In a transverse wave the disturbance is perpendicular to the direction of propagation: a rope shaken up and down carries a wave along the rope.

两类基本的波的区别在于介质的扰动方向与波的传播方向之间的关系。在横波（transverse wave）中，扰动垂直于传播方向：上下抖动的绳子沿绳传递波。

In a longitudinal wave the disturbance is parallel to the direction of propagation: the coils of a stretched spring pushed back and forth along its length carry a wave along the spring.

在纵波（longitudinal wave）中，扰动平行于传播方向：沿弹簧长度方向来回推拉的弹簧圈沿弹簧传递波。

Sound waves are modeled as mechanical longitudinal waves. A vibrating speaker cone pushes air particles forward, crowding them into a region of high pressure called a compression, and pulls back, leaving a region of low pressure called a rarefaction. These alternate along the direction the sound travels, and each air particle simply oscillates back and forth along that line.

声波被建模为机械纵波。振动的扬声器纸盆把空气粒子向前推，使它们挤在一起形成高压区，称为压缩区（compression，密部）；纸盆回拉时留下低压区，称为稀疏区（rarefaction，疏部）。它们沿声音传播的方向交替出现，每个空气粒子只是沿这条直线来回振动。

Amplitude, Loudness and Energy振幅、响度与能量

Amplitude is the maximum displacement of a wave from its equilibrium position. For a transverse wave on a rope it is the greatest height of a crest above the rest line.

振幅（amplitude）是波离开平衡位置的最大位移。对绳上的横波，它是波峰高出静止线的最大高度。

A longitudinal pressure wave such as sound has no sideways height to measure, so its amplitude is found from the maximum increase or decrease in pressure from the equilibrium pressure (the pressure amplitude): a bigger gap between the compressions and rarefactions and the pressure of undisturbed air means a larger amplitude.

声波这样的纵向压强波没有横向高度可量，所以它的振幅由压强相对平衡压强的最大增加量或减少量（压强振幅）确定：压缩区、稀疏区与未被扰动的空气之间的压强差越大，振幅就越大。

Amplitude controls how much energy the wave carries. The energy carried by a wave increases with increasing amplitude, and for sound the loudness increases with increasing amplitude. A louder sound is one whose pressure swings are larger, not one that travels faster: the speed is set by the medium, not by the amplitude.

振幅决定波携带多少能量。波携带的能量随振幅增大而增大，对声音来说，响度随振幅增大而增大。更响的声音是压强起伏更大，而不是传播得更快：速度由介质决定，而不是由振幅决定。

Common Mistake: Thinking a bigger or more frequent disturbance makes the wave faster Shaking the end of a rope harder raises the amplitude and shaking it more often raises the frequency, but neither changes how fast the pulses travel along a given rope. The speed is fixed by the medium (for a string, by $F_T$ and $\mu$). To change the speed you must change the tension or the string, not the source.
常见错误：认为扰动更大或更频繁，波就传得更快 用更大的力抖动绳端会增大振幅，抖得更频繁会增大频率，但这两者都不会改变脉冲沿给定的绳传播的快慢。速度由介质决定（对弦而言由 $F_T$ 和 $\mu$ 决定）。要改变波速，必须改变张力或换弦，而不是改变波源。
Worked Example: Distance to a Lightning Strike例题：与闪电的距离

On a day when the air is at about $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$, thunder is heard $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$ after the flash of a lightning strike is seen. Estimate the distance to the strike and decide whether the travel time of the light matters. Use $343\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for sound in air and $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ for light.

在空气约为 $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 的一天，看到闪电的闪光 $6.0\,\mathrm{s}$ 后听到雷声。估算与落雷点的距离，并判断光的传播时间是否重要。声音在空气中取 $343\,\mathrm{m/s}$，光取 $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$。

Step 1: Distance from the sound delay第 1 步：由声音的延迟求距离
$$d = v_\text{sound}\,t = (343)(6.0) = 2.06 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 2.1\,\mathrm{km}$$
Step 2: Travel time of the light第 2 步：光的传播时间
$$t_\text{light} = \frac{d}{c} = \frac{2.06 \times 10^{3}}{3.00 \times 10^{8}} = 6.9 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{s}$$
The light arrives about $7\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ after the strike, roughly a million times sooner than the sound, so treating the flash as instantaneous is justified. Why this works: both signals cover the same distance, but the electromagnetic wave travels about a million times faster than the mechanical sound wave.光在落雷后约 $7\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ 到达，比声音早约一百万倍，所以把闪光看作瞬时到达是合理的。为什么这样做：两种信号走过的距离相同，但电磁波的速度约为机械声波的一百万倍。

Exam tip考试提示

When a question changes a string's tension or mass per unit length, answer with a factor, not a new number: speed scales as $\sqrt{F_T}$ and as $1/\sqrt{\mu}$.

当题目改变弦的张力或单位长度质量时，用倍数作答，而不是给出新的数值：波速按 $\sqrt{F_T}$ 以及 $1/\sqrt{\mu}$ 变化。

When a question asks you to compare waves, say which property of the medium sets the speed and which property of the source sets the frequency, and never claim that a larger amplitude or a higher frequency speeds a wave up.

当题目要求比较波时，要说清楚是介质的哪个性质决定波速、波源的哪个性质决定频率，千万不要说振幅更大或频率更高会使波传得更快。

For a sound wave, name the quantity that is oscillating (pressure, or the displacement of air along the line of travel).

对声波，要指明在振荡的是哪个量（压强，或空气沿传播方向的位移）。

A ball floats on a lake. A wave pulse passes under it. Describe what happens to the ball and to the energy of the pulse, and justify why this shows that waves do not transfer matter.一只球漂在湖面上，一个波脉冲从它下面经过。描述球和脉冲能量的情况，并论证（justify）这说明波不传递物质。

The ball rises and falls near its starting position and is not carried along with the pulse, while the pulse and its energy move on to the shore. The water, and the ball with it, only oscillates locally; the pulse transfers energy between two locations without transferring matter between them.

球在原位置附近上下起伏，并不随脉冲前进，而脉冲及其能量继续向岸边传播。水和球只是在局部振荡；脉冲在两个位置之间传递了能量，却没有在它们之间传递物质。

The tension in a guitar string is increased by a factor of 9 and the same string is used. By what factor does the wave speed change? What factor of the tension would cut the speed in half?吉他弦的张力增大为原来的 9 倍，仍用同一根弦。波速变为原来的多少倍？张力变为原来的多少倍才会使波速减半？

Since $v \propto \sqrt{F_T}$, a factor of 9 in tension gives $\sqrt{9} = 3$, so the speed triples. To halve the speed we need $\sqrt{F_T'/F_T} = 1/2$, so $F_T'/F_T = 1/4$: the tension must be cut to one quarter.

由于 $v \propto \sqrt{F_T}$，张力变为 9 倍时波速变为 $\sqrt{9} = 3$ 倍，即波速变为原来的 3 倍。要使波速减半，需要 $\sqrt{F_T'/F_T} = 1/2$，即 $F_T'/F_T = 1/4$：张力必须减小到原来的四分之一。

Speed: $v = 36/0.80 = 45\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Solving $v = \sqrt{F_T/\mu}$ for the tension gives $F_T = \mu v^2 = (0.10)(45)^2 = 2.0 \times 10^{2}\,\mathrm{N}$.

Period and Frequency周期与频率

A periodic wave repeats itself in a regular way, so it can be described by how long one repetition takes and how often repetitions occur.

周期波以规则的方式重复，所以可以用一次重复需要多长时间和重复多频繁来描述。

The period $T$ is the time for one complete oscillation of the wave: the time for any point of the medium to go through one full cycle, or equivalently for one full wavelength to pass a fixed point.

周期（period）$T$ 是波完成一次完整振荡所用的时间：介质中任一点完成一个完整循环的时间，等价地，也是一个完整波长经过某一固定点所用的时间。

Its SI unit is the second. The frequency $f$ is the rate at which the wave repeats, the number of complete cycles per second, measured in hertz ($1\,\mathrm{Hz} = 1\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$). The two are reciprocals of each other.

其国际单位为秒。频率（frequency）$f$ 是波重复的快慢，即每秒完成的完整循环数，单位为赫兹（$1\,\mathrm{Hz} = 1\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$）。二者互为倒数。

Period and frequency周期与频率
$$T = \frac{1}{f}, \qquad f = \frac{1}{T}$$

Here $T$ is in s and $f$ is in Hz. A source that vibrates $50$ times each second has $f = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $T = 1/50 = 0.020\,\mathrm{s}$. The source sets the frequency: the wave has exactly the frequency at which the source shakes the medium.

其中 $T$ 的单位为 s，$f$ 的单位为 Hz。每秒振动 $50$ 次的波源，$f = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$，$T = 1/50 = 0.020\,\mathrm{s}$。频率由波源决定：波的频率恰好等于波源扰动介质的频率。

What Frequency Means for Sound, Amplitude and Energy频率对声音、振幅与能量意味着什么

For a sound wave the frequency is related to its pitch: a higher frequency is heard as a higher pitch. A piccolo plays notes of high frequency and high pitch; a tuba plays notes of low frequency and low pitch.

对声波而言，频率与音调有关：频率越高，听起来音调越高。短笛演奏高频、高音调的音；大号演奏低频、低音调的音。

Pitch is a matter of how fast the air pressure oscillates, not how large the oscillation is. How large it is belongs to the amplitude (and, for sound, the loudness).

音调取决于气压振荡有多快，而不是振荡有多大；振荡有多大属于振幅（对声音来说则是响度）。

Two properties of a wave are easy to confuse. The amplitude of a wave is independent of its period and frequency: you can shake a rope with a large or small amplitude at any frequency, and the amplitude does not change with the frequency (the two quantities are set by different features of the source).

波的两个性质很容易混淆。波的振幅与其周期和频率无关：你可以以任意频率用大或小的振幅抖动绳子，振幅不随频率改变（这两个量由波源的不同特征决定）。

Yet the energy of a wave increases with increasing frequency, as it does with amplitude: for the same amplitude, a source that shakes the medium more rapidly delivers more energy per second to the wave.

然而波的能量随频率增大而增大，就像它随振幅增大而增大一样：振幅相同时，振动介质更快的波源每秒传给波的能量更多。

Wavelength and the Wave Equation $v = f\lambda$波长与波速公式 $v = f\lambda$

Wavelength $\lambda$ is the distance between successive corresponding positions on a wave, such as crest to crest or trough to trough (or compression to compression in a sound wave).

波长（wavelength）$\lambda$ 是波上相邻对应位置之间的距离，例如波峰到波峰、波谷到波谷（声波中则是压缩区到压缩区）。

It is the length of one full cycle of the wave pattern in space, measured in meters. In one period $T$ the wave pattern advances by exactly one wavelength, so the wave speed is $v = \lambda/T$. Replacing $1/T$ by $f$ gives the relationship used throughout the unit.

它是波形在空间中一个完整循环的长度，单位为米。在一个周期 $T$ 内，波形恰好向前推进一个波长，所以波速 $v = \lambda/T$。把 $1/T$ 换成 $f$，就得到本单元反复使用的关系式。

Wave speed, frequency and wavelength波速、频率与波长
$$v = f\lambda, \qquad \lambda = \frac{v}{f}$$

With $v$ in m/s, $f$ in Hz and $\lambda$ in m, the wavelength is proportional to the wave's speed and inversely proportional to its frequency. So for waves in the same medium (same $v$), doubling the frequency halves the wavelength.

$v$ 的单位为 m/s，$f$ 的单位为 Hz，$\lambda$ 的单位为 m；波长与波速成正比，与频率成反比。所以在同一种介质（$v$ 相同）中，频率加倍则波长减半。

In a given wave the two properties are linked by the medium and the source: the medium sets $v$ (Topic 14.1), the source sets $f$, and the wavelength is whatever fits $\lambda = v/f$. When the same wave enters a medium with a different speed (Topic 14.3), $f$ stays fixed and $\lambda$ changes in proportion to $v$.

对给定的波而言，这些量由介质和波源联系起来：介质决定 $v$（专题 14.1），波源决定 $f$，波长则由 $\lambda = v/f$ 随之确定。同一列波进入波速不同的介质时（专题 14.3），$f$ 保持不变，$\lambda$ 与 $v$ 成比例变化。

Describing a Sinusoidal Wave: Two Graphs, Two Equations描述正弦波：两种图像，两个方程

The simplest periodic wave is a sinusoidal (sine) wave. It can be pictured in two different ways, and the two pictures answer different questions. A snapshot shows the displacement $y$ of every point along the medium at one instant: a graph of $y$ as a function of position, whose repeat length is the wavelength $\lambda$.

最简单的周期波是正弦波（sinusoidal wave）。它可以用两种不同的方式来描绘，两幅图回答不同的问题。快照显示某一时刻沿介质各点的位移 $y$：这是 $y$ 随位置变化的函数图像，其重复长度是波长 $\lambda$。

A time record follows the displacement $y$ of one point as the wave passes: a graph of $y$ as a function of time, whose repeat time is the period $T$. The amplitude $A$ is read from either graph.

时间记录则跟踪某一点在波经过时的位移 $y$：这是 $y$ 随时间变化的函数图像，其重复时间是周期 $T$。振幅 $A$ 在两幅图上都能读出。

The Two Equations of a Sinusoidal Wave正弦波的两个方程

Each picture has its own equation. For the displacement from equilibrium at one specific location as a function of time, and for the displacement at one specific time as a function of position, a sinusoidal wave of amplitude $A$ can be written

每幅图都有各自的方程。对于某一特定位置的位移随时间变化，以及某一特定时刻的位移随位置变化，振幅为 $A$ 的正弦波可以写成

Sinusoidal wave: two representations正弦波的两种表示
$$y(t) = A\sin\!\left(\frac{2\pi t}{T}\right) = A\sin(2\pi f t), \qquad y(x) = A\sin\!\left(\frac{2\pi x}{\lambda}\right)$$

In both, $y$ is the displacement from equilibrium (m), $A$ the amplitude (m), $t$ the time (s) and $x$ the position (m); the argument of the sine is an angle in radians, and one full cycle is $2\pi$ radians.

两式中，$y$ 是离开平衡位置的位移（m），$A$ 是振幅（m），$t$ 是时间（s），$x$ 是位置（m）；正弦函数的自变量是以弧度为单位的角，一个完整循环为 $2\pi$ 弧度。

A cosine may be used instead when the position or time origin is chosen at a crest. The reading rule is: the first equation is a function of time and its repeat time is $T$; the second is a function of position and its repeat length is $\lambda$.

当把位置或时间的原点选在波峰时，也可以改用余弦函数。读图规则是：第一个方程是时间的函数，重复的时间是 $T$；第二个方程是位置的函数，重复的长度是 $\lambda$。

Common Mistake: Reading the wrong graph for the wavelength or the period On a graph whose horizontal axis is time, the distance between crests is a period, not a wavelength. On a graph whose horizontal axis is position, it is a wavelength, not a period. Before you read a repeat distance, look at the units on the horizontal axis (seconds or meters) and use the matching symbol. To get the speed you need one of each: $v = \lambda/T$.
常见错误：读错图像，把周期当成波长（或相反） 在横轴为时间的图上，相邻波峰之间的距离是周期，而不是波长；在横轴为位置的图上，它是波长，而不是周期。读重复距离之前，先看横轴的单位（秒还是米），再选用对应的符号。要得到波速，两者各需要一个：$v = \lambda/T$。

先换算波长：$650\,\mathrm{nm} = 6.50 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$。则 $f = c/\lambda = (3.00 \times 10^{8})/(6.50 \times 10^{-7}) = 4.6 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$，$T = 1/f = 2.2 \times 10^{-15}\,\mathrm{s}$。

A point on a wave has displacement $y(t) = 0.020\sin(2\pi (5.0)\,t)$ in meters. State the amplitude, frequency and period, and find the displacement at $t = 0.050\,\mathrm{s}$.波上某点的位移为 $y(t) = 0.020\sin(2\pi (5.0)\,t)$（单位为米）。写出振幅、频率和周期，并求 $t = 0.050\,\mathrm{s}$ 时的位移。

Comparing with $y = A\sin(2\pi f t)$: $A = 0.020\,\mathrm{m}$, $f = 5.0\,\mathrm{Hz}$, so $T = 1/f = 0.20\,\mathrm{s}$. At $t = 0.050\,\mathrm{s}$ the argument is $2\pi(5.0)(0.050) = \pi/2$, so $y = 0.020\sin(\pi/2) = 0.020\,\mathrm{m}$: the point is at a crest, a quarter of a period after starting.

Reflection and Transmission at a Boundary边界处的反射与透射

A wave that travels from one medium into another can be transmitted into the second medium, reflected back into the first, or both, depending on the properties of the boundary that separates them.

从一种介质传入另一种介质的波可以透射（transmitted）进入第二种介质，也可以被反射（reflected）回第一种介质，或者两者兼有，这取决于分隔两种介质的边界的性质。

The usual case is both. A wave passing along a string where a low-mass (light) string is joined to a high-mass (heavy) string, for example, produces a reflected wave that returns along the first string and a transmitted wave that continues along the second.

通常两者兼有。例如，波沿着一根弦传播，弦上轻（低质量）弦与重（高质量）弦相连，就会产生一列沿第一根弦返回的反射波和一列继续沿第二根弦前进的透射波。

Sound arriving at a wall and light arriving at a window behave in the same way: part bounces back, part passes on (and some may be absorbed).

声音遇到墙壁、光遇到窗户时也是如此：一部分弹回，一部分通过（还可能有一部分被吸收）。

Inverted or Upright: The Reflection Rule倒转还是正立：反射规则

The shape of the reflected pulse depends on how the wave speed changes at the boundary. The rule has two cases, and each can be understood from the string.

反射脉冲的形状取决于波速在边界处如何变化。规则有两种情形，都可以从弦的情形来理解。

A heavy string is harder to move than a light one, so when a pulse on the light string reaches the heavy string, the heavy string cannot follow the upward lift and instead pushes back down on the light string, sending back a downward pulse.

重弦比轻弦更难被拉动，所以轻弦上的脉冲到达重弦时，重弦跟不上向上的提拉，反而向下推轻弦，于是送回一个向下的脉冲。

  • The transmitted wave travels into a medium where the wave speed decreases (light string into heavy string, with the same tension): the reflected wave is inverted. The transmitted pulse is not inverted.
  • The transmitted wave travels into a medium where the wave speed increases (heavy string into light string): the reflected wave is not inverted.
  • 透射波进入波速减小的介质（张力相同时，轻弦进入重弦）：反射波倒转。透射脉冲不倒转。
  • 透射波进入波速增大的介质（重弦进入轻弦）：反射波不倒转。

For strings under the same tension the speed is $v = \sqrt{F_T/\mu}$, so the heavy string, with the larger $\mu$, is the slower medium. This links the picture to the rule: light to heavy means speed decreases, so the reflected pulse is inverted.

张力相同的弦上波速为 $v = \sqrt{F_T/\mu}$，所以 $\mu$ 较大的重弦是波速较小的介质。这把图像与规则联系起来：轻弦到重弦意味着波速减小，所以反射脉冲倒转。

(A string tied to a rigid wall is the extreme case of a very heavy medium: the pulse comes back inverted. A string with a loose end, free to slide on a pole, comes back upright.)

（系在刚性墙上的弦是"极重介质"的极端情形：脉冲返回时倒转。末端松开、可在杆上自由滑动的弦，脉冲返回时不倒转。）

The Frequency Does Not Change; the Wavelength Does频率不变，波长改变

At the boundary the end of one string is attached to the start of the other, so the two ends move together. The point where they join is driven at the frequency of the incoming wave, and it drives the transmitted wave at that same frequency.

在边界处，一根弦的末端与另一根弦的起点相连，两个端点一起运动。它们的连接点以入射波的频率被驱动，并以同一频率驱动透射波。

The frequency of a wave does not change when it travels from one medium to another. The reflected wave has this frequency too.

波从一种介质进入另一种介质时，频率不变。反射波的频率也相同。

What changes is the speed (set by the new medium) and therefore the wavelength, because $\lambda = v/f$ with $f$ fixed: the wavelength changes in the same proportion as the speed. A wave that slows down at a boundary becomes more closely spaced; a wave that speeds up is stretched out.

改变的是波速（由新介质决定），因此波长也变，因为 $f$ 固定时 $\lambda = v/f$：波长与波速按同样的比例变化。在边界处减速的波，波形变得更密；加速的波，波形被拉开。

Worked Example: A Wave Passing from a Light to a Heavy String例题：波从轻弦传入重弦

A light string of mass per unit length $0.010\,\mathrm{kg/m}$ is joined to a heavy string of mass per unit length $0.040\,\mathrm{kg/m}$, and the tension is $25\,\mathrm{N}$ throughout. A sinusoidal wave of frequency $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ travels along the light string toward the joint. Find the speed and wavelength in each string and decide whether the reflected wave is inverted.

质量为 $0.010\,\mathrm{kg/m}$（单位长度）的轻弦与单位长度质量为 $0.040\,\mathrm{kg/m}$ 的重弦相连，整条弦的张力均为 $25\,\mathrm{N}$。频率为 $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ 的正弦波沿轻弦向连接处传播。求每根弦中的波速和波长，并判断反射波是否倒转。

Step 1: Speed in each string第 1 步：每根弦中的波速
$$v_1 = \sqrt{\frac{25}{0.010}} = 50\,\mathrm{m/s}, \qquad v_2 = \sqrt{\frac{25}{0.040}} = 25\,\mathrm{m/s}$$
Step 2: Wavelengths with the same frequency第 2 步：频率相同时的波长
$$\lambda_1 = \frac{v_1}{f} = \frac{50}{50} = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}, \qquad \lambda_2 = \frac{v_2}{f} = \frac{25}{50} = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}$$
Step 3: Reflected wave第 3 步：反射波
The speed decreases from $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ to $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$ as the wave is transmitted, so the reflected wave is inverted. Why this works: the frequency is $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$ in both strings and the reflected wave, so only $v$ and $\lambda$ change, and $\lambda_2/\lambda_1 = v_2/v_1 = 1/2$.波被透射时速度从 $50\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 降到 $25\,\mathrm{m/s}$，所以反射波倒转。为什么这样做：两根弦中以及反射波的频率都是 $50\,\mathrm{Hz}$，因此只有 $v$ 和 $\lambda$ 变化，且 $\lambda_2/\lambda_1 = v_2/v_1 = 1/2$。
Worked Example: A Pulse Going from the Heavy String to the Light String例题：脉冲从重弦传向轻弦

The same two strings and the $25\,\mathrm{N}$ tension are used, but now a pulse lasting $0.020\,\mathrm{s}$ travels in the heavy string toward the joint with the light string. Compare the length of the pulse in the two strings and decide how the reflected pulse looks.

仍用上述两根弦和 $25\,\mathrm{N}$ 的张力，但现在持续 $0.020\,\mathrm{s}$ 的脉冲在重弦中向与轻弦的连接处传播。比较脉冲在两根弦中的长度，并判断反射脉冲的样子。

Step 1: Pulse length in each string第 1 步：脉冲在每根弦中的长度
$$\ell_\text{heavy} = v_2\,\Delta t = (25)(0.020) = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}, \qquad \ell_\text{light} = v_1\,\Delta t = (50)(0.020) = 1.0\,\mathrm{m}$$
Step 2: Reflected pulse第 2 步：反射脉冲
The transmitted pulse enters the medium where the speed increases ($25 \to 50\,\mathrm{m/s}$), so the reflected pulse is not inverted: it comes back upright along the heavy string with the length $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ it had before. The transmitted pulse is stretched to $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$ in the light string. Why this works: the pulse takes the same time $\Delta t$ to pass any point, so its length is proportional to the local speed.透射脉冲进入波速增大的介质（$25 \to 50\,\mathrm{m/s}$），所以反射脉冲不倒转：它以原来的 $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ 长度正立地沿重弦返回。透射脉冲在轻弦中被拉长到 $1.0\,\mathrm{m}$。为什么这样做：脉冲经过任一点所用的时间 $\Delta t$ 相同，所以它的长度与该处波速成正比。
Common Mistake: Thinking the frequency or the inversion follows the amplitude Students often say that a wave "changes frequency" when it enters a new medium because it looks different there. It looks different because the wavelength changed; the frequency is fixed by the source and the joint. Likewise the reflected pulse is inverted because the wave speed in the second medium is lower, not because the pulse is smaller. Always compare the two wave speeds first.
常见错误：认为频率或倒转与振幅有关 学生常说波进入新介质后"频率变了"，因为它看起来不一样了。它看起来不一样是因为波长变了；频率由波源和连接点决定，是固定的。同样，反射脉冲倒转是因为第二种介质中的波速较小，而不是因为脉冲变小了。要始终先比较两种波速。

Polarization偏振

A transverse wave oscillates perpendicular to its direction of travel, but "perpendicular" leaves many possible directions. Transverse waves can be polarized, meaning they oscillate in a single plane. A rope threaded through a narrow vertical slot shows this: a vertical shake passes through, but a horizontal shake is stopped.

横波的振动垂直于传播方向，但"垂直"仍留下许多可能的方向。横波可以被偏振（polarized），即只在单一平面内振动。穿过狭窄竖直缝隙的绳子能说明这一点：竖直抖动能通过，水平抖动则被挡住。

Transverse waves that are reflected from a surface, refracted through a medium, or passed through specific openings (a narrow slot, or a polarizing filter for light) may be polarized.

被表面反射、被介质折射或通过特定开口（狭缝，或用于光的偏振片）的横波都可能被偏振。

Longitudinal waves cannot be polarized. A sound wave in air oscillates along its line of travel, so there is only one direction of oscillation and no choice of plane to select. This is a clean test: if a wave can be polarized, it is transverse. Light can be polarized, which is evidence that light is a transverse wave.

纵波不能被偏振。空气中的声波沿传播方向振动，所以只有一个振动方向，没有可以选择的平面。这是一个清晰的判据：能被偏振的波必定是横波。光可以被偏振，这就是光为横波的证据。

Intensity and the Effect of a Polarizer强度与偏振片的作用

Intensity is a measure of the amount of power transferred per unit area. For a wave it is the average power per unit area over one period of the wave, measured at a surface perpendicular to the direction of travel.

强度（intensity）衡量单位面积上传递的功率的大小。对波而言，它是在垂直于传播方向的表面上，一个周期内单位面积的平均功率。

Intensity强度
$$I = \frac{P}{A}$$

Here $I$ is the intensity in W/m$^2$, $P$ is the average power in W carried through the area, and $A$ is the area in m$^2$ perpendicular to the wave's direction. A louder sound or a brighter beam delivers more power through the same area.

其中 $I$ 为强度（W/m$^2$），$P$ 为通过该面积的平均功率（W），$A$ 为垂直于波传播方向的面积（m$^2$）。更响的声音或更亮的光束在同样的面积上传递更多功率。

Because a polarizing filter lets through only the oscillations along its transmission axis, polarization of a wave may result in a reduction of the wave's intensity: the part of the oscillation in other planes is blocked, so less power passes through the same area.

因为偏振片只让沿其透振方向的振动通过，所以波的偏振可能使波的强度减小：其他平面内的那部分振动被挡住，通过同一面积的功率就变小了。

Worked Example: Intensity Before and After a Polarizing Filter例题：通过偏振片前后的强度

A light sensor of area $2.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ facing a lamp measures a power of $4.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W}$. A polarizing filter placed in front of the sensor reduces the measured power to $1.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W}$. Find the intensity before and after the filter and the fraction of the intensity that is blocked.

面积为 $2.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ 的光传感器正对一盏灯，测得功率为 $4.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W}$。在传感器前放一块偏振片后，测得功率降为 $1.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{W}$。求通过偏振片前后的强度，以及被挡住的强度所占的比例。

Step 1: Intensity before第 1 步：偏振片之前的强度
$$I_1 = \frac{P_1}{A} = \frac{4.0 \times 10^{-3}}{2.0 \times 10^{-4}} = 20\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$$
Step 2: Intensity after第 2 步：偏振片之后的强度
$$I_2 = \frac{P_2}{A} = \frac{1.0 \times 10^{-3}}{2.0 \times 10^{-4}} = 5.0\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$$
Step 3: Fraction blocked第 3 步：被挡住的比例
$$\frac{I_1 - I_2}{I_1} = \frac{20 - 5.0}{20} = 0.75$$
The filter lets through one quarter of the intensity and blocks three quarters. Why this works: the sensor area is the same in both measurements, so the intensity changes in the same ratio as the power.偏振片让四分之一的强度通过，挡住了四分之三。为什么这样做：两次测量中传感器面积相同，所以强度按功率相同的比例变化。

遇到边界问题，先写出每种介质中的波速，再用波速表述倒转规则（"波速减小，所以反射脉冲倒转"），最后写上"频率不变，所以波长与波速成比例变化"。

题目要求画图时，把反射脉冲和透射脉冲画在边界的正确一侧，并标明它们的朝向。

For polarization, the key justification is that longitudinal waves oscillate along only one line, so they cannot be polarized, and that intensity is power per unit area, so a drop in measured power through the same area is a drop in intensity.

对于偏振，关键论证是：纵波只沿一条直线振动，所以不能被偏振；强度是单位面积的功率，所以通过同一面积的功率下降就是强度下降。

What an Electromagnetic Wave Is什么是电磁波

An electromagnetic wave consists of oscillating electric and magnetic fields that are mutually perpendicular. The electric field points along one direction, the magnetic field points along a second direction at right angles to it, and the wave travels along a third direction perpendicular to both.

电磁波（electromagnetic wave）由相互垂直的振荡电场和振荡磁场构成。电场沿一个方向，磁场沿与之成直角的第二个方向，而波沿与二者都垂直的第三个方向传播。

An oscillating electric field creates an oscillating magnetic field, which in turn creates an oscillating electric field, and this chain carries the wave forward without any material to push on.

振荡的电场产生振荡的磁场，振荡的磁场又产生振荡的电场，这个链条推动波向前传播，不需要任何物质来承载。

Because the oscillations of the electric and magnetic fields are perpendicular to the direction of propagation, electromagnetic waves are transverse waves. This is why light can be polarized (Topic 14.3).

由于电场和磁场的振荡都垂直于传播方向，电磁波是横波。这就是光可以被偏振的原因（专题 14.3）。

And because the wave consists of fields rather than the motion of matter, electromagnetic waves do not need a medium through which to propagate: they cross a vacuum, at the speed $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$ that is the same for every electromagnetic wave there. In a material such as glass or water they travel more slowly than $c$.

又因为这种波由场而不是物质的运动构成，所以电磁波不需要介质就能传播：它们能穿过真空，速度为 $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$，在真空中所有电磁波的这一速度都相同。在玻璃或水等材料中，它们的传播速度比 $c$ 小。

Plane Waves平面波

A wavefront is a surface joining points of the wave that are at the same stage of their oscillation (all at a crest, for instance). Near a small source the wavefronts are curved, like spheres spreading out. Far from the source, a small piece of a huge sphere is very nearly flat.

波前（wavefront）是把波上处于振荡同一阶段的点（例如都在波峰）连起来的曲面。在小波源附近，波前是弯曲的，像向外扩展的球面。离波源很远时，巨大球面上的一小块几乎是平的。

Electromagnetic waves are therefore commonly assumed to be plane waves, which are characterized by planar wavefronts: parallel flat sheets all moving in the same direction. Drawing the direction of travel as parallel straight rays is the simplest way to picture them, and it is the picture used for the light incident on slits and thin films later in this unit.

因此电磁波通常被假设为平面波（plane wave），其特征是平面波前：相互平行、朝同一方向运动的平坦薄片。把传播方向画成平行的直线（射线）是最简单的描绘方式，也是本单元后面讨论光入射到狭缝和薄膜时所用的图像。

The Electromagnetic Spectrum: Categories by Wavelength电磁波谱：按波长分类

Electromagnetic waves are grouped into categories that are characterized by their wavelengths. In order of decreasing wavelength, spanning a range from kilometers to picometres, the categories are radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays.

电磁波按其波长特征分成若干类别。按波长由长到短（范围从千米到皮米）排列，这些类别是无线电波（radio waves）、微波（microwaves）、红外线（infrared）、可见光（visible）、紫外线（ultraviolet）、X 射线（X-rays）和伽马射线（gamma rays）。

All of them are the same kind of wave, with the same speed $c$ in a vacuum; they differ in wavelength and frequency. Visible electromagnetic waves are further broken into colors. In order of decreasing wavelength they are red, orange, yellow, green, blue and violet: red light has the longest wavelength we can see, and violet the shortest.

它们都是同一种波，真空中速度都是 $c$；差别在于波长和频率。可见电磁波又细分为各种颜色。按波长由长到短依次为红、橙、黄、绿、蓝、紫：红光是我们能看见的波长最长的光，紫光最短。

Electromagnetic wave in a vacuum真空中的电磁波
$$c = f\lambda$$

This is the wave relation $v = f\lambda$ with $v = c$. Here $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, $f$ is in Hz and $\lambda$ is in m.

这就是波的关系式 $v = f\lambda$ 取 $v = c$ 的情形。其中 $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{m/s}$，$f$ 的单位为 Hz，$\lambda$ 的单位为 m。

Since $c$ is the same for all categories, a longer wavelength means a lower frequency, so the order of decreasing wavelength is also the order of increasing frequency: gamma rays have the shortest wavelength and the highest frequency, radio waves the longest wavelength and the lowest frequency.

因为各类别的 $c$ 都相同，波长越长频率越低，所以波长由长到短的顺序同时也是频率由低到高的顺序：伽马射线波长最短、频率最高，无线电波波长最长、频率最低。

The course expects you to know this order of the spectrum (including visible light), but you are not expected to memorize the exact wavelength range of each category.

本课程要求你知道电磁波谱的顺序（包括可见光），而不要求记住每一类别的确切波长范围。

The Word "Light""光"一词

Visible electromagnetic waves are also called light. Sometimes, though, electromagnetic waves of all wavelengths are collectively referred to as light or electromagnetic radiation. Read the context: "the speed of light" applies to all electromagnetic waves in a vacuum, and "the colors of light" refers to the visible part only.

可见的电磁波也叫做光（light）。不过有时也把所有波长的电磁波统称为光或电磁辐射（electromagnetic radiation）。要看上下文："光速"适用于真空中的所有电磁波，而"光的颜色"只指可见部分。

Common Mistake: Thinking different parts of the spectrum travel at different speeds X-rays are not "faster" than radio waves, and violet light is not faster than red light in a vacuum. All electromagnetic waves travel at $c$ there. What differs is the wavelength and the frequency, which trade off through $c = f\lambda$. Another slip is to treat electromagnetic waves like sound: they are transverse, they need no medium, and they cross the empty space between the Sun and Earth.
常见错误：认为电磁波谱的不同部分传播速度不同 X 射线并不比无线电波"更快"，真空中紫光也不比红光快。在真空中所有电磁波的速度都是 $c$。不同的是波长和频率，二者通过 $c = f\lambda$ 此消彼长。另一个失误是把电磁波当成声波：电磁波是横波，不需要介质，能穿过太阳与地球之间的真空。
Worked Example: From Frequency to Wavelength例题：由频率求波长

A radio station broadcasts at $1.00 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Hz}$ ($100\,\mathrm{MHz}$). A beam of visible light has a frequency of $5.0 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Find the wavelength of each in a vacuum and compare them.

一个电台以 $1.00 \times 10^{8}\,\mathrm{Hz}$（$100\,\mathrm{MHz}$）广播。一束可见光的频率为 $5.0 \times 10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$。求二者在真空中的波长并比较。

Step 1: Radio wave第 1 步：无线电波
$$\lambda = \frac{c}{f} = \frac{3.00 \times 10^{8}}{1.00 \times 10^{8}} = 3.00\,\mathrm{m}$$
Step 2: Visible light第 2 步：可见光
$$\lambda = \frac{3.00 \times 10^{8}}{5.0 \times 10^{14}} = 6.0 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m} = 600\,\mathrm{nm}$$
The radio wave's wavelength is about five million times longer, and its frequency about five million times lower. This matches the spectrum order, with radio waves at the long-wavelength end and visible light much farther toward short wavelengths. Why this works: both are electromagnetic waves with the same $c$, so $\lambda \propto 1/f$.无线电波的波长约为可见光的五百万倍，而频率约为它的五百万分之一。这与波谱的顺序一致：无线电波在长波一端，可见光则远为靠近短波一端。为什么这样做：二者都是电磁波，$c$ 相同，所以 $\lambda \propto 1/f$。
Worked Example: Sunlight Crossing Empty Space例题：穿过真空的阳光

Rest Frequency and Observed Frequency静止频率与观测频率

An ambulance siren sounds higher in pitch as the ambulance races toward you and drops as it speeds away. The siren itself has not changed: the driver hears the same steady note throughout. What changed is the relative motion between the source and the observer.

救护车的警笛在救护车冲向你时音调升高，驶离时音调降低。警笛本身没有变：司机从头到尾听到的是同一个稳定的音。变化的是波源与观察者之间的相对运动。

The Doppler effect describes the relationship between three things: the rest frequency of the source (the frequency it emits, measured when source and observer are not moving relative to each other), the observed frequency (the frequency the observer actually detects), and the relative velocity of the source and the observer.

多普勒效应（Doppler effect）描述三个量之间的关系：波源的静止频率（rest frequency，波源与观察者之间没有相对运动时测得的、波源发出的频率）、观测频率（observed frequency，观察者实际探测到的频率），以及波源与观察者的相对速度。

This topic is treated qualitatively in AP Physics 2: you are asked to reason about whether the observed frequency is higher, lower or the same, and how large the change is compared with another case. No Doppler formula and no Doppler calculation is required, so this section builds a picture with wavefronts rather than an equation.

History: Doppler and the Musicians on a Train历史：多普勒与火车上的乐手

In 1842 the Austrian physicist Christian Doppler proposed that a moving source changes the frequency an observer receives. He hoped it would explain the colors of double stars; stars move too slowly for that, but the effect is real.

1842 年，奥地利物理学家克里斯蒂安·多普勒提出，运动的波源会改变观测者接收到的频率。他希望用它解释双星的颜色；恒星运动太慢，无法造成这种颜色差别，但这个效应是真实存在的。

In 1845 the Dutch scientist C. H. D. Buys Ballot tested it with sound: musicians on a moving train held a steady note, and listeners beside the track heard the pitch rise on approach and fall as the train left. Read more: Doppler effect (Wikipedia).

1845 年，荷兰科学家白贝罗（C. H. D. Buys Ballot）用声音检验了它：行驶的火车上的乐手奏出稳定的音，铁轨旁的听者听到火车驶近时音调升高、离去时音调降低。延伸阅读：多普勒效应（Wikipedia）。

Why It Happens: Wavefront Spacing为何发生：波前间距

Picture a source that emits one wavefront every period. Each wavefront spreads out from the place where it was emitted, at the wave speed fixed by the medium, no matter how the source moves afterward.

设想一个波源每个周期发出一个波前。每个波前都从它被发出的位置向外扩展，速度由介质决定，与波源之后如何运动无关。

If the source is at rest, the wavefronts are concentric circles, equally spaced in every direction by one wavelength, and every observer meets them at the source's rest frequency.

如果波源静止，波前是同心圆，在各个方向上等间距，间距为一个波长，每个观察者遇到波前的频率都等于波源的静止频率。

Now let the source move. Each new wavefront is centered on the point where the source was when it emitted that wavefront, and the source has moved on since. Ahead of the source the wavefronts are squeezed together, because the source follows close behind the wave it just emitted.

现在让波源运动。每个新波前以波源发出该波前时所在的位置为中心，而波源此后已经向前运动了。在波源前方，波前被挤压在一起，因为波源紧跟在它刚发出的波的后面。

Behind the source the wavefronts are stretched apart, because the source is moving away from the waves it emitted earlier. An observer ahead meets closely spaced wavefronts, so they arrive more often: the observed frequency is higher.

在波源后方，波前被拉开，因为波源正在远离先前发出的波。前方的观察者遇到间距很小的波前，它们到达得更频繁：观测频率更高。

An observer behind meets widely spaced wavefronts, which arrive less often: the observed frequency is lower. The wave speed in the medium is unchanged; it is the spacing of the wavefronts, and so the wavelength the observer sees, that differs.

后方的观察者遇到间距很大的波前，到达得不那么频繁：观测频率更低。介质中的波速不变；不同的是波前的间距，也就是观察者看到的波长。

The Spacing Argument in Symbols用符号表示的间距论证

First Principles: Why Wavefronts Bunch Up第一性原理：为什么波前会挤在一起

Principle: once emitted, each wavefront travels at the speed $v$ set by the medium, however the source moves.

出发原理：波前一旦发出，就以介质决定的速度 $v$ 传播，与波源如何运动无关。

  1. The source emits one wavefront every period $T = 1/f$.
  2. In that time the last wavefront moves $vT$ ahead, while the source, moving toward you at $v_s$, follows it by $v_sT$.
  3. The spacing ahead of the source is $\lambda' = (v - v_s)T$, shorter than $\lambda = vT$. Behind it the spacing is $(v + v_s)T$.
  4. For a listener at rest the fronts still arrive at speed $v$, so $f' = v/\lambda' = f\,v/(v - v_s)$, above $f$.
  1. 波源每经过一个周期 $T = 1/f$ 发出一个波前。
  2. 在这段时间内，上一个波前向前走了 $vT$，而以 $v_s$ 朝你运动的波源跟在后面走了 $v_sT$。
  3. 波源前方的间距为 $\lambda' = (v - v_s)T$，比 $\lambda = vT$ 短。波源后方的间距为 $(v + v_s)T$。
  4. 对静止的听者，波前仍以速度 $v$ 到达，所以 $f' = v/\lambda' = f\,v/(v - v_s)$，高于 $f$。

Limiting check: $v_s = 0$ gives $f' = f$, the no-relative-motion rule. An $800\,\mathrm{Hz}$ siren approaching at $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ through air at $343\,\mathrm{m/s}$ is heard at about $877\,\mathrm{Hz}$. AP Physics 2 asks only for the direction of the shift, so use this chain to justify "higher" or "lower".

极限检验：$v_s = 0$ 时 $f' = f$，即无相对运动的规则。一个 $800\,\mathrm{Hz}$ 的警笛以 $30\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 在声速为 $343\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 的空气中驶近，听到的约为 $877\,\mathrm{Hz}$。AP Physics 2 只要求判断频移的方向，所以用这条推理来论证"更高"或"更低"。

The Rules: Toward, Away, and No Relative Motion规则：靠近、远离与无相对运动

Reasoning from the wavefront picture gives a short set of rules, all about the relative motion of source and observer.

根据波前图可以得出一组简短的规则，它们都与波源和观察者之间的相对运动有关。

  • Source moving toward the observer: the observed frequency is greater than the rest frequency.
  • Source moving away from the observer: the observed frequency is less than the rest frequency.
  • Source and observer moving at the same velocity (no relative motion, moving together): the observed frequency equals the rest frequency.
  • A greater relative velocity gives a greater measured difference between the observed and rest frequencies: a faster approach raises the pitch more, and a faster retreat lowers it more.
  • 波源朝向观察者运动：观测频率大于静止频率。
  • 波源远离观察者运动：观测频率小于静止频率。
  • 波源与观察者以相同速度运动（无相对运动，一起运动）：观测频率等于静止频率。
  • 相对速度越大，观测频率与静止频率之间测得的差越大：接近得越快，音调升得越多；退离得越快，音调降得越多。

The rules depend only on the relative velocity, so it does not matter whether the source moves toward a stationary observer or the observer moves toward a stationary source: either way they are approaching, and the observer meets the wavefronts more often and observes a higher frequency. The same reasoning applies to waves in general, not only to sound.

这些规则只取决于相对速度，所以无论是波源朝静止的观察者运动，还是观察者朝静止的波源运动，结果都一样：双方在靠近，观察者遇到波前更频繁，观测频率更高。同样的推理适用于一般的波，而不只是声波。

Key Insight: The source's own frequency never changes The rest frequency is a property of the source. The Doppler effect changes only the frequency that is observed, because of relative motion. A siren does not "sound different to itself", and a driver moving along with the siren hears it unchanged.
关键洞察：波源自身的频率从不改变 静止频率是波源的性质。多普勒效应只改变因相对运动而被观测到的频率。警笛对自己来说并没有"变声"，随警笛一起运动的司机听到的声音也没有变化。
Common Mistake: Confusing the pitch shift with loudness, or with a change in wave speed A siren is also louder when it is near, but the Doppler effect is about the frequency, not the loudness, and it happens at any distance. Nor does the moving source change the speed of the wave in the medium; that speed is set by the medium. Finally, it is not the distance but the relative motion toward or away that matters: a source at a fixed distance, or moving along with you, produces no shift.
常见错误：把音调的变化与响度或波速的变化混为一谈 警笛靠近时也更响，但多普勒效应讲的是频率，而不是响度，而且在任何距离上都会发生。运动的波源也不会改变波在介质中的速度；那个速度由介质决定。最后，起作用的不是距离，而是朝向或远离的相对运动：距离固定的波源，或随你一起运动的波源，不会产生频率变化。

Superposition: Waves Pass Through Each Other and Add叠加：波相互穿过并相加

Wave interference is the interaction of two or more wave pulses or waves. When pulses or waves meet, they travel through each other and overlap rather than bouncing off each other, and afterwards each continues exactly as before.

波的干涉（wave interference）是两个或多个波脉冲或波之间的相互作用。脉冲或波相遇时，它们相互穿过并重叠，而不是相互弹开，之后每一个都完全照原样继续前进。

While they overlap, the resulting displacement at every point is found by adding the individual displacements. This is called superposition: the net displacement is the sum of the displacements the waves would have had alone, with sign.

重叠期间，每一点的合位移通过把各个位移相加得到。这称为叠加原理（superposition）：合位移是各列波单独存在时的位移之和（要带符号）。

The sign decides the outcome. When the displacements of the superposed pulses or waves are in the same direction, the interaction is constructive interference, and the resultant displacement is larger than either alone.

符号决定结果。当叠加的脉冲或波的位移同向时，这种相互作用是相长干涉（constructive interference），合位移比任何一列单独的都大。

When they are in opposite directions, it is destructive interference, and the resultant displacement is smaller; equal and opposite displacements cancel to zero at that instant. In terms of the phase of two periodic waves, in phase means the displacements are in the same direction at the same place and time, and out of phase means opposite.

当位移反向时，是相消干涉（destructive interference），合位移变小；大小相等、方向相反的位移在该时刻抵消为零。就两列周期波的相位而言，同相意味着同一地点同一时刻位移同向，反相则意味着位移反向。

Interference means two interacting traveling waves can produce amplitude variations in the resultant wave. Drawing the pulses, and adding them point by point, is the standard way to find the result.

干涉意味着两列相互作用的行波可以使合成波的振幅发生变化。把脉冲画出来并逐点相加，是求结果的标准方法。

Worked Example: Adding Displacements Point by Point例题：逐点相加位移

Two pulses overlap on a string. At three positions, pulse 1 alone would give displacements of $+2.0$, $+4.0$ and $+2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$, and pulse 2 alone would give $+1.0$, $-4.0$ and $-1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Find the net displacement at each position, classify the interference there, and say what happens to the pulses after they pass.

两个脉冲在弦上重叠。在三个位置，脉冲 1 单独存在时的位移分别为 $+2.0$、$+4.0$ 和 $+2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$，脉冲 2 单独存在时的位移分别为 $+1.0$、$-4.0$ 和 $-1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$。求各位置的合位移，判断那里的干涉类型，并说明脉冲通过之后会怎样。

Step 1: Add with sign第 1 步：带符号相加
$$y_1 + y_2 = 2.0 + 1.0 = +3.0\,\mathrm{cm}, \qquad 4.0 - 4.0 = 0\,\mathrm{cm}, \qquad 2.0 - 1.0 = +1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$$
Step 2: Classify and conclude第 2 步：分类并得出结论
At the first position the displacements are in the same direction, so the interference is constructive and the net displacement ($3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$) is larger than either. At the second the displacements are equal and opposite, so the interference is destructive and cancels to zero. At the third they are opposite but unequal, so the result is smaller than the larger one. After the overlap the pulses pass through each other and continue unchanged. Why this works: superposition adds the displacements the waves would have alone, and the waves do not bounce off or destroy each other.在第一个位置，位移同向，所以是相长干涉，合位移（$3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$）比任何一个都大。在第二个位置，位移大小相等、方向相反，所以是相消干涉，抵消为零。在第三个位置，位移反向但大小不等，所以结果比较大的那个小。重叠之后，脉冲相互穿过并保持原样继续前进。为什么这样做：叠加原理把各列波单独存在时的位移相加，波并不会相互弹开或相互毁灭。
Common Mistake: Thinking waves bounce off each other, or that cancelling destroys the energy Colliding pulses do not rebound like colliding balls: they pass through each other and emerge unchanged. At the instant of complete destructive interference the string is flat, but both pulses are still there, and they reappear a moment later. The wave's energy has not vanished; it is momentarily stored in the motion of the string.
常见错误：认为波会相互弹开，或认为抵消就是能量消失 相遇的脉冲不会像相撞的球那样反弹：它们相互穿过并毫无改变地继续前进。完全相消的那一刻弦是平直的，但两个脉冲仍然存在，稍后又会重新出现。波的能量并没有消失；它暂时储存在弦的运动之中。

Beats拍

Beats arise from the addition of two waves of slightly different frequency. Waves with different frequencies are sometimes in phase and sometimes out of phase at a given place.

拍（beats）源于两列频率略有不同的波相加。频率不同的波，在某一位置有时同相、有时反相。

When they are in phase the interference is constructive and the combined wave is large; when they drift out of phase it is destructive and the combined wave is small.

同相时干涉相长，合成波很大；漂移到反相时干涉相消，合成波很小。

The result is a periodic amplitude change in the resultant wave: for sound, a loudness that swells and fades. The rate of the swelling is the beat frequency, and it is the difference of the two frequencies.

结果是合成波的振幅发生周期性变化：对声音来说，就是响度忽强忽弱。强弱变化的快慢就是拍频（beat frequency），它等于两个频率之差。

Tuning forks are commonly used to demonstrate beat frequencies, and musicians use the beats between a reference and a string to tune an instrument: beats slow down and vanish as the two frequencies approach each other.

音叉常用来演示拍频，乐手也利用参考音与琴弦之间的拍来调音：当两个频率接近时，拍变慢并消失。

Beat frequency拍频
$$f_\text{beat} = |f_1 - f_2|$$

Here $f_1$ and $f_2$ are the two frequencies in Hz, and the absolute value makes the beat frequency positive whichever is larger.

其中 $f_1$ 和 $f_2$ 是两个频率（Hz），取绝对值使拍频总是正数，不论哪个频率更大。

The beat frequency tells you how far apart the two frequencies are but not which one is larger, which is why a second piece of information (such as a known change in one source) is often needed to decide.

拍频告诉你两个频率相差多少，却不告诉你哪个更大，所以通常需要另一条信息（例如已知其中一个声源发生了某种变化）才能判断。

Worked Example: Tuning Forks and Beats例题：音叉与拍

Step 1: Part (a)第 1 步：(a) 问

Standing Waves: Nodes and Antinodes驻波：波节与波腹

When two waves of the same frequency travel in opposite directions through the same region, they interfere to form a standing wave.

当两列频率相同的波沿相反方向穿过同一区域时，它们干涉形成驻波（standing wave）。

This happens naturally when a wave is confined: it runs to a boundary, reflects, and the reflected wave travels back through the incoming one. The pattern does not travel along. Each point of the medium oscillates up and down with its own fixed amplitude.

当波被限制在一定范围内时，这种情形会自然发生：波传到边界后被反射，反射波又穿过入射波返回。图样并不沿着介质前进，介质中每一点都以各自固定的振幅上下振动。

Standing waves have nodes and antinodes. A node is a point on the standing wave where the amplitude is always zero: the two waves always cancel there. An antinode is a point where the amplitude is always at its maximum: the waves always reinforce there.

驻波有波节（node）和波腹（antinode）。波节是驻波上振幅始终为零的点：两列波在那里总是相互抵消。波腹是振幅始终最大的点：两列波在那里总是相互加强。

Nodes and antinodes alternate, with one quarter of a wavelength between a node and the next antinode, so the distance between two successive nodes is half a wavelength.

波节与波腹交替出现，相邻波节与波腹之间相隔四分之一波长，所以相邻两个波节之间的距离是半个波长。

The possible wavelengths of a standing wave are determined by the size and the boundary conditions of the region to which it is confined. A fixed end of a string (or the closed end of a pipe) forces a node there, because the medium cannot move.

驻波可能的波长由它所受限制的区域的大小和边界条件决定。弦的固定端（或管的闭合端）迫使该处为波节，因为介质不能运动。

A loose end of a string (free to slide) or the open end of a pipe forces an antinode, because the medium is free to move there. For a pipe, these are the nodes and antinodes of the displacement of the air; the pressure variation is the opposite, with a pressure antinode where there is a displacement node.

弦的自由端（可自由滑动）或管的开口端迫使该处为波腹，因为介质在那里可以自由运动。对管而言，这里说的是空气位移的波节和波腹；压强的变化则正好相反，位移波节处是压强波腹。

Common regions are strings with fixed or loose ends and pipes with open or closed ends. Only wavelengths that fit the region with the right ending at each end can form a standing wave.

常见的区域有端点固定或自由的弦，以及两端开口或闭合的管。只有恰好能嵌入区域、且每一端都符合要求的波长才能形成驻波。

Harmonics of a String Fixed at Both Ends两端固定的弦的谐波

Take a string of length $L$ fixed at both ends, so both ends are nodes. The standing wave with the longest possible wavelength is the fundamental or first harmonic: one antinode in the middle, so $L$ is half a wavelength.

取长度为 $L$、两端固定的弦，两端都是波节。波长最长的驻波是基频或第一谐波（fundamental，first harmonic）：中间只有一个波腹，所以 $L$ 是半个波长。

The second-longest wavelength is the second harmonic, with one more node in the middle, so $L$ is one whole wavelength, and the third-longest is the third harmonic, with $L$ equal to three half-wavelengths, and so on. Counting half-wavelengths that fit between the two nodes gives, for the $n$th harmonic, $L = n\lambda_n/2$.

波长第二长的是第二谐波，中间多一个波节，所以 $L$ 是一个整波长；第三长的是第三谐波，$L$ 等于三个半波长，依此类推。数一数两个波节之间能放下几个半波长，第 $n$ 个谐波有 $L = n\lambda_n/2$。

Solving $L = n\lambda_n/2$ for the wavelength, and then using $f = v/\lambda$ with the wave speed $v$ on the string (Topic 14.1), gives the allowed wavelengths and frequencies. The frequencies are whole-number multiples of the fundamental, $f_n = nf_1$.

由 $L = n\lambda_n/2$ 解出波长，再结合弦上的波速 $v$（专题 14.1），用 $f = v/\lambda$ 即得允许的波长和频率。这些频率是基频的整数倍，$f_n = nf_1$。

Both ends fixed (or both ends open): all harmonics两端固定（或两端开口）：所有谐波
$$L = n\,\frac{\lambda_n}{2}, \qquad \lambda_n = \frac{2L}{n}, \qquad f_n = \frac{v}{\lambda_n} = n\,\frac{v}{2L} = n f_1, \qquad n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$$

Here $L$ is the length of the region in m, $\lambda_n$ the wavelength of the $n$th harmonic in m, $v$ the wave speed in m/s, $f_n$ the frequency in Hz and $n$ the harmonic number.

其中 $L$ 为区域长度（m），$\lambda_n$ 为第 $n$ 个谐波的波长（m），$v$ 为波速（m/s），$f_n$ 为频率（Hz），$n$ 为谐波序数。

For the fixed-fixed string the $n$th harmonic has $n$ loops, that is $n$ antinodes. A pipe open at both ends has a displacement antinode at each end (the air is free to move there), which is the same pattern as a string with both ends loose.

对两端固定的弦，第 $n$ 个谐波有 $n$ 个环，即 $n$ 个波腹。两端开口的管，两端各有一个位移波腹（那里的空气可以自由运动），与两端自由的弦图样相同。

Its $n$th harmonic has $n$ displacement nodes and $n+1$ antinodes counting the two ends, and it has the same series of harmonics as the fixed-fixed string, with $v$ the speed of sound in the air column.

它的第 $n$ 个谐波有 $n$ 个位移波节，算上两端共有 $n+1$ 个波腹，并且与两端固定的弦有相同的谐波序列，其中 $v$ 取空气柱中的声速。

One Fixed (Closed) End: Only Odd Harmonics一端固定（闭合）：只有奇次谐波

For a standing wave with a node at one end and an antinode at the other end, such as a string fixed at one end and free at the other, or a pipe closed at one end and open at the other, the allowed patterns are different.

对于一端为波节、另一端为波腹的驻波，例如一端固定另一端自由的弦，或一端闭合另一端开口的管，允许的图样不同。

The shortest fit is a quarter of a wavelength (node to antinode), so $L = \lambda/4$ for the fundamental. Adding a half-wavelength each time keeps a node at one end and an antinode at the other, so the next possible fit has $L = 3\lambda/4$, then $5\lambda/4$, and so on.

最短的嵌入方式是四分之一波长（从波节到波腹），所以基频有 $L = \lambda/4$。每次再增加半个波长，仍保持一端为波节、另一端为波腹，所以下一种可能的嵌入方式是 $L = 3\lambda/4$，然后是 $5\lambda/4$，依此类推。

Only odd harmonics can be established: the first, third, fifth and so on. The even harmonics would put an antinode at both ends or a node at both, which the boundary conditions do not allow.

只能建立奇次谐波：第 1、3、5 个等。偶次谐波会使两端都是波腹或都是波节，这是边界条件所不允许的。

One end closed (node), one end open (antinode): odd harmonics only一端闭合（波节）、一端开口（波腹）：只有奇次谐波
$$L = n\,\frac{\lambda_n}{4}, \qquad \lambda_n = \frac{4L}{n}, \qquad f_n = \frac{v}{\lambda_n} = n\,\frac{v}{4L}, \qquad n = 1, 3, 5, \dots$$
Key Insight: Count the ends, then count the fit Two ends of the same type, both nodes (a fixed-fixed string) or both antinodes (an open-open pipe), fit a whole number of half-wavelengths, so every harmonic $n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$ occurs. Two ends of different types, one node and one antinode (a string fixed at one end only, or a closed-open pipe), fit an odd number of quarter-wavelengths, so only the odd harmonics $n = 1, 3, 5, \dots$ occur. For a diagram, label each end, and the relationship between $L$, $\lambda$, $f$ and $v$ follows.
关键洞察：先数端点，再数嵌入的段数 两端类型相同，即都是波节（两端固定的弦）或都是波腹（两端开口的管），嵌入整数个半波长，所以 $n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$ 的每个谐波都会出现。两端类型不同，即一端波节、一端波腹（只有一端固定的弦，或一端闭合一端开口的管），嵌入奇数个四分之一波长，所以只有奇次谐波 $n = 1, 3, 5, \dots$ 出现。画图时标出每个端点的类型，$L$、$\lambda$、$f$ 和 $v$ 之间的关系就随之得出。

闭合端是波节、开口端是波腹，所以只能建立奇次谐波，接下来的两个是 $3f_1 = 300\,\mathrm{Hz}$ 和 $5f_1 = 500\,\mathrm{Hz}$。$200\,\mathrm{Hz}$ 会是第二谐波，这种图样两端要么都是波节、要么都是波腹，边界条件不允许。

A string with a wave speed of $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$ and length $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ is fixed at both ends. Find its fundamental frequency. By what factor does the fundamental change if the tension is quadrupled?一根波速为 $200\,\mathrm{m/s}$、长 $0.50\,\mathrm{m}$ 的弦两端固定。求它的基频。若张力变为 4 倍，基频变为原来的多少倍？

$f_1 = v/(2L) = 200/1.0 = 200\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Quadrupling the tension doubles the wave speed (Topic 14.1: $v \propto \sqrt{F_T}$), while $L$ is unchanged, so the fundamental frequency doubles to $400\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

$f_1 = v/(2L) = 200/1.0 = 200\,\mathrm{Hz}$。张力变为 4 倍使波速加倍（专题 14.1：$v \propto \sqrt{F_T}$），而 $L$ 不变，所以基频加倍，变为 $400\,\mathrm{Hz}$。

Explain, in terms of the two waves that make a standing wave, why a node stays at zero amplitude while an antinode oscillates with maximum amplitude.用构成驻波的两列波来解释：为什么波节的振幅始终为零，而波腹以最大振幅振动。

The standing wave is the superposition of two waves traveling in opposite directions. At a node the two waves always arrive with opposite displacements, so they always cancel: the amplitude is always zero. At an antinode they always arrive with the same displacement, so they always reinforce: the amplitude is a maximum.

驻波是沿相反方向传播的两列波的叠加。在波节处，两列波到达时的位移总是相反，所以总是相互抵消：振幅始终为零。在波腹处，它们到达时的位移总是相同，所以总是相互加强：振幅最大。

A string of length $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ has one end fixed and the other end loose, free to slide on a pole. The wave speed is $120\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Which harmonics can it carry, and what is the fundamental frequency?一根长 $0.60\,\mathrm{m}$ 的弦，一端固定，另一端自由，可在杆上滑动。波速为 $120\,\mathrm{m/s}$。它能承载哪些谐波？基频是多少？

The fixed end is a node and the loose end is an antinode, the same pattern as a pipe closed at one end, so only odd harmonics occur, $\lambda_n = 4L/n$ with $n = 1, 3, 5$. The fundamental has $\lambda_1 = 4L = 2.4\,\mathrm{m}$, so $f_1 = v/\lambda_1 = 120/2.4 = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$. The next resonances are $150\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $250\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

固定端是波节，自由端是波腹，与一端闭合的管图样相同，所以只出现奇次谐波，$\lambda_n = 4L/n$，$n = 1, 3, 5$。基频的 $\lambda_1 = 4L = 2.4\,\mathrm{m}$，所以 $f_1 = v/\lambda_1 = 120/2.4 = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}$。接下来的共振频率是 $150\,\mathrm{Hz}$ 和 $250\,\mathrm{Hz}$。

A pulse of height $+4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a pulse of height $-1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ travel toward each other on a string, and at one instant their peaks lie at the same point. Sketch or describe the string at that point and instant, and say what the pulses look like afterward.一个高度为 $+4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ 的脉冲和一个高度为 $-1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ 的脉冲在弦上相向传播，某一时刻它们的峰值位于同一点。画出或描述该时刻该点处弦的形状，并说明之后脉冲的样子。

The displacements add with sign: $(+4.0) + (-1.0) = +3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. At that instant the string has a peak of $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ upward at that point, lower than the large pulse alone because the interference there is partly destructive. The pulses pass through each other, so afterward the $+4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ pulse and the $-1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ pulse continue with their original shapes.

位移带符号相加：$(+4.0) + (-1.0) = +3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$。在该时刻，该点处弦有一个向上 $3.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ 的峰，比大脉冲单独存在时低，因为那里的干涉是部分相消。脉冲相互穿过，所以之后 $+4.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ 的脉冲和 $-1.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ 的脉冲继续以原来的形状前进。

What Diffraction Is and When It Matters什么是衍射，何时重要

Diffraction is the spreading of a wave around the edges of an obstacle or through an opening. A wave that meets a gap does not simply continue as a narrow beam with sharp edges: it fans out into the region behind the barrier.

衍射（diffraction）是波绕过障碍物的边缘或穿过开口时的扩展。遇到缝隙的波并不会简单地继续沿着边缘清晰的窄束前进：它会向屏障后面的区域扇形展开。

Diffraction is most pronounced when the size of the opening is comparable to the wavelength of the wave. If the opening is much larger than the wavelength, the wave passes almost straight through and the spreading is hardly noticeable; if it is about the size of a wavelength, the wave spreads widely.

当开口的大小与波的波长相当时，衍射最明显。如果开口比波长大得多，波几乎直线通过，扩展几乎察觉不到；如果开口约为一个波长大小，波就会大范围扩展。

This explains an everyday contrast. Sound has wavelengths of the order of a meter, comparable to a doorway, so it diffracts strongly and you can hear a conversation from around a corner.

这解释了日常生活中的一个对比。声音的波长约为一米，与门口相当，所以衍射很强，你能听到拐角另一边的谈话。

Visible light has wavelengths of the order of $10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$, enormously smaller than a doorway, so it hardly diffracts and you cannot see around the corner. To see light diffract, you need an opening of micrometer scale, such as a narrow slit.

可见光的波长约为 $10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$，比门口小得多，所以几乎不发生衍射，你看不到拐角后面。要看到光的衍射，需要微米量级的开口，例如一条很窄的狭缝。

History: Huygens's Wavelets and the Bright Spot in a Shadow历史：惠更斯子波与阴影中的亮斑

In his 1690 Treatise on Light, Christiaan Huygens proposed that every point on a wavefront is a source of secondary wavelets, and the next wavefront is the surface touching them all. Wavelets from the edges of an opening spread sideways, which is diffraction.

克里斯蒂安·惠更斯在 1690 年的《光论》（Treatise on Light）中提出，波前上的每一点都是次级子波的波源，下一个波前就是与所有子波相切的面。开口边缘发出的子波向侧面展开，这就是衍射。

In 1818 Augustin Fresnel added interference between the wavelets. Simeon Poisson objected that the theory predicted a bright spot at the center of a round disc's shadow; Francois Arago looked and found it. See Huygens-Fresnel principle and Arago spot (Wikipedia).

1818 年，菲涅耳在子波之间加入了干涉。泊松反驳说，这个理论预言圆盘阴影的中心会有一个亮斑；阿拉戈去观察，果然找到了它。参见 惠更斯-菲涅耳原理 和 阿拉戈亮斑（Wikipedia）。

The Single-Slit Pattern单缝图样

Send monochromatic light of wavelength $\lambda$ at a narrow opening of width $a$, with a screen a distance $L$ behind it. Diffraction of multiple wavefronts through a single opening leads to observable interference patterns. Every point across the opening acts as a source of wavefronts spreading toward the screen.

用波长为 $\lambda$ 的单色光照射宽度为 $a$ 的窄开口，屏在其后距离 $L$ 处。多个波前通过单个开口的衍射会形成可观察到的干涉图样。开口上的每一点都是向屏扩展的波前的波源。

At a given point on the screen, the waves from all these points arrive by paths of slightly different lengths, so they interfere. Where they reinforce, the screen is bright; where they cancel, it is dark. The pattern is a wide, bright central maximum with dark bands (minima) on both sides, separated by much dimmer bright bands.

在屏上某一点，来自这些点的波经过略有不同长度的路径到达，所以发生干涉。它们相互加强处，屏是亮的；相互抵消处，屏是暗的。图样是一条又宽又亮的中央亮纹（central maximum），两侧是暗纹（极小），暗纹之间是暗得多的亮纹。

The amount of interference between two wavefronts depends on their path length difference $\Delta$. Take the angle $\theta$ between the direction in which the light travels toward a point on the screen and the normal to the opening.

两个波前之间的干涉程度取决于它们的路程差（path length difference）$\Delta$。取光射向屏上某点的方向与开口法线之间的夹角 $\theta$。

Compare the wave from the top edge of the slit with the wave from the middle of the slit: the lower one has to travel an extra distance. For the two edges of the opening the path length difference is $\Delta = a\sin\theta$.

比较缝上边缘发出的波与缝中间发出的波：下面的那个要多走一段距离。对于开口的两个边缘，路程差为 $\Delta = a\sin\theta$。

Pairing the Two Halves: The Dark-Band Condition两半配对：暗纹条件

To find a dark band, split the opening into two halves: the wave from each point in the top half is paired with the wave from the point a distance $a/2$ below it.

要找暗纹，把开口分成上下两半：上半部分每一点发出的波，与比它低 $a/2$ 的那一点发出的波配对。

If this pair has a path length difference of half a wavelength, $(a/2)\sin\theta = \lambda/2$, every pair cancels and the screen is dark. That is $a\sin\theta = \lambda$. Splitting into four quarters in the same way gives $a\sin\theta = 2\lambda$, and so on.

如果这一对的路程差是半个波长，即 $(a/2)\sin\theta = \lambda/2$，那么每一对都相互抵消，屏上就是暗的。也就是 $a\sin\theta = \lambda$。用同样的方法分成四份，得到 $a\sin\theta = 2\lambda$，依此类推。

Single slit: dark bands (minima)单缝：暗纹（极小）
$$a\sin\theta = m\lambda, \qquad m = 1, 2, 3, \dots$$

Here $a$ is the slit width (m), $\theta$ the angle from the normal to the $m$th dark band, $\lambda$ the wavelength (m) and $m$ the order of the minimum ($m = 1$ is the first dark band on either side of the center).

其中 $a$ 为缝宽（m），$\theta$ 为法线与第 $m$ 条暗纹之间的夹角，$\lambda$ 为波长（m），$m$ 为极小的级次（$m = 1$ 是中央两侧的第一条暗纹）。

This equation locates minima, not maxima. It is easy to confuse with the double-slit equation of the next topic, $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$, which locates maxima; the symbols look alike, but the meaning differs, and $a$ is the width of one slit while $d$ is the spacing between two slits.

这个方程确定的是极小，而不是极大。它很容易与下一专题的双缝方程 $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$ 混淆，后者确定的是极大；两者的符号很像，含义却不同，而且 $a$ 是单个缝的宽度，$d$ 是两条缝之间的间距。

Positions on the Screen: The Small-Angle Form屏上的位置：小角近似形式

On the screen, the $m$th minimum sits a distance $y_\text{min}$ from the middle of the central bright fringe, and the geometry gives $\tan\theta = y_\text{min}/L$. For small angles, where $\theta \lt 10^\circ$, the small-angle approximation says $\sin\theta \approx \tan\theta$ (with $\theta$ in radians), so $a\,(y_\text{min}/L) = m\lambda$.

在屏上，第 $m$ 级极小与中央亮纹中心的距离为 $y_\text{min}$，由几何关系 $\tan\theta = y_\text{min}/L$。对于小角，即 $\theta \lt 10^\circ$ 时，小角近似（small-angle approximation）为 $\sin\theta \approx \tan\theta$（$\theta$ 以弧度计），所以 $a\,(y_\text{min}/L) = m\lambda$。

Solving gives the form used in most problems. The approximation is valid only when the angle is small: when $\theta$ is not small, use $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$ directly.

解出后得到多数题目使用的形式。只有当角度很小时这个近似才成立：当 $\theta$ 不小时，直接用 $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$。

Single slit, small angles: position of the $m$th minimum单缝，小角：第 $m$ 级极小的位置
$$y_\text{min} = \frac{m\lambda L}{a}$$

Here $y_\text{min}$ is measured on the screen from the center of the pattern, $L$ is the slit-to-screen distance, and all lengths share one unit. Two consequences follow. The central bright fringe, between the first minima on both sides, has total width $2\lambda L/a$.

其中 $y_\text{min}$ 在屏上从图样中心量起，$L$ 是缝到屏的距离，所有长度使用同一单位。由此得出两个结论。两侧第一级极小之间的中央亮纹总宽度为 $2\lambda L/a$。

And a narrower slit gives a wider pattern ($y \propto 1/a$), a longer wavelength gives a wider pattern ($y \propto \lambda$), and a more distant screen gives a wider pattern ($y \propto L$). Strong spreading needs a slit of the order of the wavelength, consistent with the rule above.

并且缝越窄，图样越宽（$y \propto 1/a$），波长越长，图样越宽（$y \propto \lambda$），屏越远，图样越宽（$y \propto L$）。强烈的扩展需要量级与波长相当的缝，这与上面的规则一致。

The Shape of the Opening开口的形状

The diffraction pattern produced by a wave passing through an opening depends on the shape of the opening. A long, narrow rectangular slit spreads light mainly across its narrow dimension, giving bright and dark bands that run parallel to the slit.

波穿过开口所产生的衍射图样取决于开口的形状。细长的矩形狭缝主要沿其窄的方向扩展光，产生平行于狭缝的明暗条纹。

A circular aperture spreads light in all directions around its axis, giving a bright central disc surrounded by dark and faint bright rings. A square opening spreads light along both of its sides.

圆孔使光围绕其轴线向各个方向扩展，产生一个明亮的中央圆斑，周围是暗环和微弱的亮环。方形开口沿它的两边都扩展光。

In every case a smaller opening (in comparison with the wavelength) gives a more widely spread pattern. Visual representations of single-slit patterns are useful in determining the physical properties of the slit and of the interacting waves: for instance, the spacing of the dark bands gives $\lambda/a$.

无论哪种情形，开口（相对于波长）越小，图样扩展得越宽。单缝图样的示意图有助于确定缝和相互作用的波的物理性质：例如，暗纹的间距给出 $\lambda/a$。

Common Mistake: Using $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$ for bright fringes In a single slit the equation $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$ with $m = 1, 2, 3, \dots$ gives the positions of the dark bands. The center ($m = 0$) is the brightest point, not a minimum, and the distance from the center to the first dark band is the half-width of the central maximum. Do not mix this with the double-slit equation $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$, which gives the bright maxima and does include $m = 0$.
常见错误：用 $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$ 求亮纹 对单缝，方程 $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$（$m = 1, 2, 3, \dots$）给出的是暗纹的位置。中心（$m = 0$）是最亮的点，而不是极小，中心到第一条暗纹的距离是中央亮纹的半宽。不要把它与双缝方程 $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$ 混淆，后者给出的是亮极大，并且包含 $m = 0$。
Worked Example: Sound Around a Corner, Light Not例题：声音能拐弯，光不能

A doorway is $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$ wide. A $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$ sound ($v = 343\,\mathrm{m/s}$) and a beam of light of wavelength $5.0 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$ both pass through it. Compare the doorway width with each wavelength and say which wave diffracts noticeably.

一个门口宽 $0.90\,\mathrm{m}$。一个 $500\,\mathrm{Hz}$ 的声音（$v = 343\,\mathrm{m/s}$）和一束波长为 $5.0 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}$ 的光都穿过它。比较门宽与各自波长，并说明哪种波的衍射明显。

Step 1: Wavelength of the sound第 1 步：声波的波长
$$\lambda_\text{sound} = \frac{v}{f} = \frac{343}{500} = 0.686\,\mathrm{m}, \qquad \frac{a}{\lambda_\text{sound}} = \frac{0.90}{0.686} = 1.3$$
Step 2: The light第 2 步：光
$$\frac{a}{\lambda_\text{light}} = \frac{0.90}{5.0 \times 10^{-7}} = 1.8 \times 10^{6}$$
The doorway is only about $1.3$ wavelengths for the sound, so the sound spreads strongly into the room; it is almost two million wavelengths for the light, so the light barely spreads and casts a sharp shadow. Why this works: diffraction is most pronounced when the opening is comparable to the wavelength, and the ratio $a/\lambda$ measures that.对声音来说门口只有约 $1.3$ 个波长，所以声音向室内强烈扩展；对光来说它有近两百万个波长，所以光几乎不扩展，投下清晰的影子。为什么这样做：开口与波长相当时衍射最明显，而比值 $a/\lambda$ 正是对此的度量。
Worked Example: A Laser Through a Narrow Slit例题：激光通过窄缝

A laser of wavelength $650\,\mathrm{nm}$ shines on a single slit of width $0.040\,\mathrm{mm}$. A screen is $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ behind the slit. Find the angle and the screen position of the first minimum, the width of the central bright fringe, and the position of the second minimum.

波长为 $650\,\mathrm{nm}$ 的激光照射宽度为 $0.040\,\mathrm{mm}$ 的单缝。屏在缝后 $2.0\,\mathrm{m}$ 处。求第一级极小的角度和屏上位置、中央亮纹的宽度，以及第二级极小的位置。

Step 1: Convert to meters and find the angle第 1 步：换算成米并求角度
$$\lambda = 6.5 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}, \quad a = 4.0 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m}, \quad \sin\theta_1 = \frac{\lambda}{a} = 0.01625 \;\Rightarrow\; \theta_1 = 0.93^\circ$$

Step 2: First minimum and central width第 2 步：第一级极小与中央宽度
$$y_1 = \frac{\lambda L}{a} = \frac{(6.5 \times 10^{-7})(2.0)}{4.0 \times 10^{-5}} = 0.0325\,\mathrm{m} = 3.3\,\mathrm{cm}, \qquad W = 2y_1 = 6.5\,\mathrm{cm}$$

The minima are evenly spaced, each $3.25\,\mathrm{cm}$ apart. Why this works: for small angles $y_\text{min} = m\lambda L/a$ is directly proportional to $m$.各级极小等间距，相邻两个相隔 $3.25\,\mathrm{cm}$。为什么这样做：小角时 $y_\text{min} = m\lambda L/a$ 与 $m$ 成正比。

Two Slits: Interference of Light双缝：光的干涉

Now shine monochromatic light of wavelength $\lambda$ on two narrow slits a distance $d$ apart, with a screen a distance $L$ beyond them. Each slit diffracts the light and acts as a new source of wavefronts, and the two sets of wavefronts overlap on the way to the screen.

现在用波长为 $\lambda$ 的单色光照射相距 $d$ 的两条窄缝，屏在它们后面距离 $L$ 处。每条缝都使光发生衍射，并成为新的波前波源，两组波前在到达屏的途中重叠。

The pattern is caused by a combination of wave diffraction and wave interference. If we consider interference only, treating each slit as a point source, a double slit creates a pattern of uniformly spaced (evenly spaced) maxima, and the constructive and destructive interference of the wavefronts from the two slits produces bright and dark bands on the screen.

这个图样是由波的衍射与波的干涉的结合造成的。如果只考虑干涉，把每条缝当作点波源，双缝产生的是等间距（均匀间隔）的极大，两条缝发出的波前的相长干涉和相消干涉在屏上产生明暗条纹。

Path Difference Decides Bright or Dark路程差决定亮暗

Whether a point on the screen is bright or dark depends on the path length difference $\Delta$ between the two wavefronts that arrive there.

屏上某点是亮还是暗，取决于到达该点的两个波前之间的路程差 $\Delta$。

For light arriving at an angle $\theta$ from the normal to the slits (a direction measured from the line midway between the slits to the point on the screen), the path from the farther slit is longer by $\Delta = d\sin\theta$.

对于与缝的法线成角 $\theta$ 到达的光（角度从两缝正中间的那条线量到屏上的点），较远的那条缝的路径更长，多出 $\Delta = d\sin\theta$。

Where the path length difference is a whole number of wavelengths, the waves arrive in phase and interfere constructively (bright); where it is a half-integer number of wavelengths, they arrive out of phase and interfere destructively (dark).

路程差是波长的整数倍处，两列波同相到达，发生相长干涉（亮）；路程差是波长的半整数倍处，两列波反相到达，发生相消干涉（暗）。

The Bright-Fringe Condition亮纹条件

Double slit: bright maxima双缝：亮极大
$$d\sin\theta = m\lambda, \qquad m = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots$$

Here $d$ is the distance between the two slits (m), $\theta$ is the angle from the central line to the $m$th bright fringe, $\lambda$ is the wavelength (m), and $m$ is the order of the maximum ($m = 0$ is the central maximum, where the path difference is zero).

其中 $d$ 为两缝之间的距离（m），$\theta$ 为从中心线到第 $m$ 条亮纹的夹角，$\lambda$ 为波长（m），$m$ 为极大的级次（$m = 0$ 是中央极大，路程差为零）。

This equation locates maxima, the opposite of the single-slit equation $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$ for minima, so check which kind of opening a problem describes before choosing between them. For small angles, with $\theta \lt 10^\circ$, the small-angle approximation $\sin\theta \approx \tan\theta = y/L$ gives the distance $y_\text{max}$ from the middle of the central bright fringe to the $m$th order of maximum brightness on the screen.

这个方程确定的是极大，与确定极小的单缝方程 $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$ 正好相反，所以在二者之间选择之前，要先看清题目描述的是哪种开口。对小角，即 $\theta \lt 10^\circ$ 时，小角近似 $\sin\theta \approx \tan\theta = y/L$ 给出屏上从中央亮纹中心到第 $m$ 级最大亮度处的距离 $y_\text{max}$。

Double slit, small angles: position of the $m$th maximum双缝，小角：第 $m$ 级极大的位置
$$y_\text{max} = \frac{m\lambda L}{d}$$

Consecutive maxima differ by $\Delta y = \lambda L/d$, which is the same for every pair: the fringes are evenly spaced. A larger slit separation $d$ squeezes the fringes together; a longer wavelength or a more distant screen spreads them out. Compare Topic 14.7: there a narrower slit spreads the pattern, here a wider separation compresses it.

相邻极大之间相差 $\Delta y = \lambda L/d$，每一对都相同：条纹等间距。缝间距 $d$ 越大，条纹挤得越紧；波长越长或屏越远，条纹越分散。与专题 14.7 比较：那里缝越窄图样越展开，这里间距越大图样越压缩。

First Principles: Where $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$ Comes From第一性原理：$d\sin\theta = m\lambda$ 从何而来

Principle: superposition. Two waves that leave the slits in phase add to a maximum wherever their paths differ by a whole number of wavelengths.

出发原理：叠加原理（superposition）。两列从缝出发时同相的波，在路程差为波长整数倍的地方相加成极大。

  1. The screen is far away ($L \gg d$), so the two rays to one point on it are almost parallel, both at angle $\theta$.
  2. Drop a perpendicular from $S_1$ onto the ray from $S_2$. The extra length is the side of a right triangle with hypotenuse $d$: $\Delta = d\sin\theta$.
  3. Bright where $\Delta = m\lambda$, so $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$.
  4. On the screen $y = L\tan\theta \approx L\sin\theta$ for small $\theta$, so $y_\text{max} = m\lambda L/d$.
  1. 屏很远（$L \gg d$），所以射向屏上同一点的两条光线几乎平行，都与中心线成 $\theta$ 角。
  2. 从 $S_1$ 向来自 $S_2$ 的光线作垂线。多出的长度是斜边为 $d$ 的直角三角形的一条边：$\Delta = d\sin\theta$。
  3. 当 $\Delta = m\lambda$ 时为亮纹，所以 $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$。
  4. 小角时屏上 $y = L\tan\theta \approx L\sin\theta$，所以 $y_\text{max} = m\lambda L/d$。

Limiting check: $m = 0$ gives $\theta = 0$, the bright center where the paths are equal. As $\lambda \to 0$ the spacing $\lambda L/d \to 0$, the fringes merge, and light seems to travel in straight lines. Doubling $d$ halves the spacing.

极限检验：$m = 0$ 给出 $\theta = 0$，即两条路程相等的中央亮纹。当 $\lambda \to 0$ 时，间距 $\lambda L/d \to 0$，条纹并在一起，光看起来沿直线传播。$d$ 加倍则间距减半。

Fringes Inside the Single-Slit Envelope单缝包络内的条纹

The two-point-source picture ignores the width of each slit. A real slit has a width $a$, so each one produces its own single-slit diffraction pattern, with a wide central maximum.

双点波源的图像忽略了每条缝的宽度。实际的缝有宽度 $a$，所以每条缝都产生各自的单缝衍射图样，有一个宽的中央极大。

When the diffraction and the interference are considered together, the double slit creates an interference pattern of maxima and minima superimposed within the envelope created by single-slit diffraction. The fringes follow $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$, but their brightness is limited by the single-slit envelope, falling to zero where the envelope has a minimum.

同时考虑衍射和干涉时，双缝产生的是极大与极小叠加在单缝衍射所形成的包络之内的干涉图样。条纹位置遵循 $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$，但它们的亮度受单缝包络限制，在包络为极小处降为零。

Young's Experiment: Evidence that Light Is a Wave杨氏实验：光是波的证据

Interference patterns produced by light interacting with a double slit indicate that light has wave properties. The source of this discovery was Young's double-slit experiment. Alternating bright and dark bands cannot be explained by streams of tiny bullets traveling in straight lines, which would simply make two bright stripes behind the two slits.

光与双缝相互作用所产生的干涉图样表明光具有波动性。这一发现的来源是杨氏双缝实验（Young's double-slit experiment）。明暗相间的条纹无法用沿直线飞行的微小弹丸流来解释，那样只会在两条缝后面形成两条亮带。

Only waves can cancel each other in some places and reinforce in others. The interference pattern is therefore evidence for the wave model of light, which fits with light being an electromagnetic wave (Topic 14.4).

只有波才能在某些地方相互抵消、在另一些地方相互加强。因此干涉图样是光的波动模型的证据，这与光是电磁波（专题 14.4）的认识一致。

History: Thomas Young Measures the Colors历史：托马斯·杨测量颜色的波长

Between 1801 and 1807 Thomas Young reported to London audiences that light from two closely spaced sources makes bright and dark bands where the beams overlap. Newton's particle model, then widely accepted, could not explain light added to light giving darkness.

1801 年至 1807 年间，托马斯·杨在伦敦的演讲中报告：来自两个靠得很近的光源的光，在光束重叠处形成明暗条纹。当时广为接受的牛顿微粒模型无法解释光加光为何会得到暗。

Young used the band spacing to estimate the wavelengths of the colors, finding red longer than violet. See Double-slit experiment (Wikipedia).

杨用条纹间距估算了各种颜色的波长，发现红光比紫光长。参见 双缝实验（Wikipedia）。

Diffraction Gratings衍射光栅

A diffraction grating is a collection of many evenly spaced parallel slits or openings. It produces an interference pattern that is the combination of numerous diffraction patterns superimposed on each other.

衍射光栅（diffraction grating）是由许多等间距的平行狭缝或开口构成的集合。它产生的干涉图样是许多衍射图样相互叠加的结果。

The maxima are at the same places as for two slits with the same spacing $d$, given by $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$, but with many slits the bright fringes become much sharper and brighter and the dark regions between them become wide. For a grating, $d$ is the distance between adjacent slits: a grating ruled with $N$ lines per meter has $d = 1/N$.

极大的位置与间距同为 $d$ 的双缝相同，由 $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$ 给出，但缝数很多时，亮纹变得更尖锐、更明亮，它们之间的暗区变宽。对光栅而言，$d$ 是相邻两缝之间的距离：每米刻有 $N$ 条线的光栅，$d = 1/N$。

Because the angle of each maximum depends on $\lambda$, a grating separates colors.

因为每个极大的角度取决于 $\lambda$，光栅能把颜色分开。

When white light is incident on a diffraction grating, the center maximum ($m = 0$, where $\theta = 0$ for every wavelength) is white, and the higher-order maxima disperse the white light into a rainbow of colors, with the longest-wavelength light (red) appearing farthest from the central maximum, since $\sin\theta = m\lambda/d$ is largest for the largest $\lambda$.

当白光入射到衍射光栅上时，中央极大（$m = 0$，对各种波长 $\theta = 0$）是白色的，更高级次的极大把白光色散成一道彩虹，其中波长最长的光（红）出现在离中央极大最远的位置，因为 $\lambda$ 最大时 $\sin\theta = m\lambda/d$ 最大。

Violet, the shortest visible wavelength, is nearest the center. Because $\sin\theta$ cannot exceed $1$, only orders with $m \le d/\lambda$ exist. At large angles, use $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$ directly instead of the small-angle form.

可见光中波长最短的紫光离中心最近。由于 $\sin\theta$ 不能超过 $1$，只存在 $m \le d/\lambda$ 的级次。在大角度时，要直接用 $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$，而不用小角形式。

Common Mistake: Mixing up the slit width $a$ and the slit separation $d$ The slit width $a$ controls the broad single-slit envelope, and the separation $d$ controls the fringes inside it. Using $a$ in $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$ gives the wrong fringe spacing, and using $d$ in the single-slit equation gives the wrong width for the central maximum. Ask two questions: is this a maximum or a minimum, and is the length in the problem the width of one opening or the distance between two?
常见错误：混淆缝宽 $a$ 与缝间距 $d$ 缝宽 $a$ 控制宽阔的单缝包络，缝间距 $d$ 控制包络内部的条纹。把 $a$ 代入 $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$ 会得到错误的条纹间距，把 $d$ 代入单缝方程会得到错误的中央极大宽度。要问自己两个问题：这是极大还是极小？题目中的长度是单个开口的宽度，还是两个开口之间的距离？
Worked Example: Fringe Positions in Young's Experiment例题：杨氏实验中的条纹位置

Light of wavelength $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ passes through two slits $0.20\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart and falls on a screen $1.5\,\mathrm{m}$ away. Find the fringe spacing, the distance from the center to the third bright fringe, and check that the small-angle form applies.

Step 1: Fringe spacing第 1 步：条纹间距
$$\Delta y = \frac{\lambda L}{d} = \frac{(6.0 \times 10^{-7})(1.5)}{2.0 \times 10^{-4}} = 4.5 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m} = 4.5\,\mathrm{mm}$$
Step 2: Third bright fringe第 2 步：第三条亮纹
$$y_3 = \frac{3\lambda L}{d} = 3(4.5\,\mathrm{mm}) = 13.5\,\mathrm{mm}$$
Step 3: Check the angle第 3 步：检验角度

Light at an Interface: Transmission, Reflection, Absorption and Phase Change界面处的光：透射、反射、吸收与相位变化

When light travels from one medium into another, such as from air into glass, some of the light is transmitted into the second medium, some is reflected back, and some is absorbed by the material. Thin-film interference depends on the reflected part, and in particular on what the reflection does to the phase of the wave.

光从一种介质进入另一种介质时，例如从空气进入玻璃，一部分光被透射进入第二种介质，一部分被反射回来，还有一部分被材料吸收。薄膜干涉依赖于反射的那一部分，特别是反射对波的相位所起的作用。

The phase change of a reflected ray depends on the relative indices of refraction $n$ of the two materials (the index of refraction $n$ tells how much the speed of light is reduced in a medium, $v = c/n$, so a larger $n$ means slower light).

反射光线的相位变化取决于两种材料折射率 $n$ 的相对大小（折射率 $n$ 表示光速在介质中减小的程度，$v = c/n$，所以 $n$ 越大，光越慢）。

The rule mirrors the string reflections of Topic 14.3, where a wave entering a slower medium came back inverted:

这条规则与专题 14.3 中弦上的反射相呼应：进入波速较小的介质的波，反射回来时倒转：

  • A phase change of 180 degrees (equivalent to half a wavelength of shift, or $\pi$ radians) occurs when a light ray is reflected from a medium with a greater index of refraction than the medium through which the ray is traveling.
  • No phase change occurs when a light ray is reflected from a medium with a lower index of refraction than the medium through which the ray is traveling.
  • The phase of a wave does not change when it is refracted as it passes from one medium into another: only the reflected ray can pick up a phase change, not the transmitted ray.
  • 当光线从折射率比光所在介质更大的介质表面反射时，发生 180 度相位变化（相当于半个波长的移动，即 $\pi$ 弧度）。
  • 当光线从折射率比光所在介质更小的介质表面反射时，没有相位变化。
  • 波从一种介质折射进入另一种介质时，相位不变：只有反射光线才可能产生相位变化，透射光线不会。

How a Thin Film Makes Interference薄膜如何产生干涉

Thin-film interference occurs when light interacts with a medium whose thickness is comparable to the light's wavelength. Light that strikes the film is partly reflected at the top surface. The rest enters the film, travels to the bottom surface, partly reflects there, and travels back up through the film and out.

薄膜干涉（thin-film interference）发生在光与厚度和光的波长相当的介质相互作用时。射到薄膜上的光一部分在上表面被反射，其余部分进入薄膜，传到下表面，在那里又被部分反射，再向上穿过薄膜射出。

The two reflected waves, one from the first interface and one from the second interface after the trip through the film, come out together and interfere, giving a single wave that is the sum of the two.

这两列反射波，一列来自第一个界面，另一列来自穿过薄膜后的第二个界面，一起射出并发生干涉，合成一列波，它是二者之和。

Whether the sum is bright or dark depends on the relationship between the thickness of the film, the wavelength of the light, any phase shifts on reflection, and the angle at which the incident light strikes the film.

合成波是亮还是暗，取决于膜厚、光的波长、反射时的相位变化，以及入射光射向薄膜的角度之间的关系。

Conditions for Bright and Dark Film at Normal Incidence垂直入射时亮膜与暗膜的条件

The quantitative analysis of thin-film interference is limited to light that is normal (perpendicular) to the surface; at other angles the path through the film is longer and depends on the angle, so we describe the effect only qualitatively.

对薄膜干涉的定量分析只限于垂直（法向）射向表面的光；在其他角度，光在薄膜中的路径更长，并且与角度有关，所以我们只对其做定性描述。

At normal incidence, ray 2 travels down and back through the film, an extra path of $2t$, where $t$ is the film's thickness. That path lies inside the film, where the wavelength is not the wavelength in air.

垂直入射时，光线 2 向下再向上穿过薄膜，多走的路程为 $2t$，其中 $t$ 是膜厚。这段路程在薄膜内部，那里的波长不是空气中的波长。

The frequency of a wave does not change at a boundary (Topic 14.3) but the speed falls to $1/n$ of its vacuum value, so the wavelength inside the film is

波的频率在边界处不变（专题 14.3），但波速减小为原来的 $1/n$，所以膜内的波长为

Wavelength inside the film膜内波长
$$\lambda_\text{film} = \frac{\lambda}{n}$$

where $\lambda$ is the wavelength in air (or vacuum) and $n$ is the film's index of refraction. The path difference $2t$ is then compared with $\lambda_\text{film}$. What also matters is the phase shifts, which must be counted case by case:

其中 $\lambda$ 是空气（或真空）中的波长，$n$ 是薄膜的折射率。然后把路程差 $2t$ 与 $\lambda_\text{film}$ 比较。同样重要的是相位变化，必须逐种情形来数：

  • Case 1: exactly one of the two reflections has a 180 degree phase change. The two reflected waves start half a cycle apart. Constructive interference (bright film) needs $2t = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda_\text{film}$ with $m = 0, 1, 2, \dots$; destructive interference (dark film) needs $2t = m\lambda_\text{film}$ with $m = 1, 2, \dots$. (A film much thinner than the wavelength is dark.)
  • Case 2: both reflections have a phase change, or neither does. The shifts cancel, so the waves start in step. Constructive interference needs $2t = m\lambda_\text{film}$ with $m = 1, 2, \dots$; destructive interference needs $2t = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda_\text{film}$ with $m = 0, 1, 2, \dots$.
  • 情形 1：两次反射中恰好有一次发生 180 度相位变化。两列反射波一开始就相差半个周期。相长干涉（亮膜）需要 $2t = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda_\text{film}$，$m = 0, 1, 2, \dots$；相消干涉（暗膜）需要 $2t = m\lambda_\text{film}$，$m = 1, 2, \dots$。（比波长薄得多的膜是暗的。）
  • 情形 2：两次反射都有相位变化，或者都没有。两次相位变化相互抵消，所以两列波一开始是同步的。相长干涉需要 $2t = m\lambda_\text{film}$，$m = 1, 2, \dots$；相消干涉需要 $2t = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda_\text{film}$，$m = 0, 1, 2, \dots$。
Thin film at normal incidence: use the phase shifts to pick one of the two forms垂直入射的薄膜：用相位变化选择情形
$$2t = m\,\frac{\lambda}{n} \quad\;\quad 2t = \left(m + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\frac{\lambda}{n}$$

Count first, then choose: write down the indices in order (top medium, film, bottom medium) and mark each of the two reflections as shifted (reflected from a higher index) or not shifted (reflected from a lower index).

先数，后选：按顺序（上方介质、薄膜、下方介质）写下折射率，并把两次反射各标为有相位变化（从折射率更高的介质反射）或没有（从折射率更低的介质反射）。

Here $t$ is the film thickness (m), $\lambda$ the wavelength in air (m) and $n$ the film's index. Different phase shifts mean the same thickness can give bright reflection in one situation (a soap film in air) and dark in another (an antireflection coating on glass).

其中 $t$ 为膜厚（m），$\lambda$ 为空气中的波长（m），$n$ 为薄膜折射率。相位变化不同，意味着同样的厚度在一种情形下可以使反射变亮（空气中的肥皂膜），在另一种情形下使反射变暗（玻璃上的增透膜）。

Soap Bubbles and Oil Films肥皂泡和油膜

Practical examples of thin-film interference include the color variations seen in soap bubbles and oil films. A soap film in air has air above and below: the top reflection comes from a higher index (shifted) and the bottom reflection from a lower index (not shifted), so it is Case 1.

薄膜干涉的实际例子包括肥皂泡和油膜上所见的颜色变化。空气中的肥皂膜上下都是空气：上表面的反射来自折射率更高的介质（有相位变化），下表面的反射来自折射率更低的介质（无相位变化），所以属于情形 1。

White light contains all the colors, and a given thickness reinforces some wavelengths and cancels others. The film's thickness is not the same everywhere (it drains downward in a bubble and varies across an oil slick), so different places reinforce different colors.

白光含有各种颜色，某一厚度会加强一些波长、抵消另一些波长。薄膜各处厚度并不相同（肥皂泡中薄膜向下流淌，油膜各处也不均匀），所以不同位置加强的颜色不同。

The spectrum of colors observed in oil films and soap bubbles arises from differences in the thickness of the film. At the very top of a vertical soap film the thickness shrinks toward zero and the film looks dark, which fits the Case 1 condition.

油膜和肥皂泡上所观察到的颜色光谱，源于薄膜厚度的差异。竖直肥皂膜的最上端，厚度趋于零，薄膜显得发暗，这与情形 1 的条件相符。

Antireflection Coatings增透膜

An antireflection coating is a thin film on glass (a lens, a camera, a solar cell) chosen to eliminate reflected light of one wavelength. It applies the relationships between the indices of refraction, the phase shifts and wave interference to create destructive interference of the light reflected from the two surfaces of the coating.

增透膜（antireflection coating）是镀在玻璃（镜头、相机、太阳能电池）上的薄膜，选择它的目的是消除某一波长的反射光。它利用折射率、相位变化和波的干涉之间的关系，使从增透膜两个表面反射的光发生相消干涉。

The simplest antireflection coating has a thickness equal to one quarter of the wavelength of the light in the coating, $t = \lambda_\text{film}/4 = \lambda/(4n)$, and the index of refraction of the coating is greater than that of air and less than that of the surface on which it is applied. This assumes the incident light is normal to the surface.

最简单的增透膜的厚度等于光在膜中波长的四分之一，$t = \lambda_\text{film}/4 = \lambda/(4n)$，膜的折射率大于空气的折射率、小于它所镀表面的折射率。这里假设入射光垂直于表面。

Why this works: with $n_\text{air} \lt n_\text{coating} \lt n_\text{glass}$, the reflection at the top of the coating is from a higher index (shifted) and the reflection at the glass is also from a higher index (shifted).

为什么这样做：当 $n_\text{air} \lt n_\text{coating} \lt n_\text{glass}$ 时，膜上表面的反射来自折射率更高的介质（有相位变化），玻璃界面处的反射也来自折射率更高的介质（有相位变化）。

Both reflections are shifted, so it is Case 2, and destructive interference needs $2t = \tfrac{1}{2}\lambda_\text{film}$, which is $t = \lambda_\text{film}/4$. The light that is not reflected is transmitted into the glass, so a good coating gives a brighter image, not a loss of energy.

两次反射都有相位变化，属于情形 2，相消干涉需要 $2t = \tfrac{1}{2}\lambda_\text{film}$，即 $t = \lambda_\text{film}/4$。没有被反射的光透射进了玻璃，所以好的增透膜带来更亮的像，而不是能量损失。

Common Mistake: Using the wavelength in air, or forgetting a phase shift Writing $2t = \lambda/4$ with the air wavelength is wrong: the path is inside the film, so use $\lambda/n$. A second common slip is to apply one rule to every film. A soap film in air (one phase change) is bright when $2t = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda/n$, while an antireflection coating on glass (two phase changes) is dark at the same condition. Count the shifts for each new setup.
常见错误：用空气中的波长，或漏掉一次相位变化 用空气中的波长写成 $2t = \lambda/4$ 是错的：路程在薄膜内部，所以要用 $\lambda/n$。第二个常见的失误是对每种薄膜都套用同一条规则。空气中的肥皂膜（一次相位变化）在 $2t = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda/n$ 时是亮的，而玻璃上的增透膜（两次相位变化）在同一条件下是暗的。对每个新装置都要重新数相位变化。
Worked Example: A Soap Film in Air例题：空气中的肥皂膜

A soap film ($n = 1.33$) hangs in air and is lit at normal incidence by light of wavelength $600\,\mathrm{nm}$. Find the two smallest nonzero thicknesses for which the film looks bright in reflection and the smallest nonzero thickness for which it looks dark.

一层肥皂膜（$n = 1.33$）悬在空气中，被波长 $600\,\mathrm{nm}$ 的光垂直照射。求使薄膜在反射中显得亮的两个最小非零厚度，以及使它显得暗的最小非零厚度。

Step 1: Count the phase shifts第 1 步：数相位变化
Top surface: air to film, reflection from a higher index, so a 180 degree shift. Bottom surface: film to air, reflection from a lower index, so no shift. Exactly one shift, so this is Case 1.上表面：空气到薄膜，从折射率更高的介质反射，所以有 180 度相位变化。下表面：薄膜到空气，从折射率更低的介质反射，所以没有相位变化。恰好一次相位变化，所以属于情形 1。
Step 2: Wavelength in the film第 2 步：膜内波长
$$\lambda_\text{film} = \frac{600}{1.33} = 451\,\mathrm{nm}$$
Step 3: Bright and dark thicknesses第 3 步：亮与暗的厚度
$$\text{bright: } t = \left(m + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\frac{\lambda_\text{film}}{2} = 113\,\mathrm{nm},\ 338\,\mathrm{nm}; \qquad \text{dark: } t = \frac{\lambda_\text{film}}{2} = 226\,\mathrm{nm}$$
The bright condition is $2t = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda_\text{film}$ with $m = 0$ and $m = 1$; the dark condition is $2t = \lambda_\text{film}$ with $m = 1$. Why this works: with one phase change the two reflected waves already start half a cycle apart, so they add when the extra path is a half-integer multiple of $\lambda_\text{film}$.亮的条件为 $2t = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda_\text{film}$，取 $m = 0$ 和 $m = 1$；暗的条件为 $2t = \lambda_\text{film}$，取 $m = 1$。为什么这样做：有一次相位变化时，两列反射波一开始就相差半个周期，所以当多出的路程是 $\lambda_\text{film}$ 的半整数倍时，它们相加。
Worked Example: Designing an Antireflection Coating例题：设计增透膜

A lens of glass ($n = 1.52$) is to be coated with a thin layer of magnesium fluoride ($n = 1.38$) to eliminate the reflection of $550\,\mathrm{nm}$ light at normal incidence. Check that the coating index is suitable, find the minimum thickness, and say what happens to the light that is not reflected.

要在玻璃透镜（$n = 1.52$）上镀一层氟化镁薄膜（$n = 1.38$），以消除垂直入射的 $550\,\mathrm{nm}$ 光的反射。检查膜的折射率是否合适，求最小厚度，并说明未被反射的光会怎样。

Step 1: Check the indices第 1 步：检查折射率
$1.00 \lt 1.38 \lt 1.52$: the coating index is greater than that of air and less than that of the glass. Both reflections are from a higher index, so both have a 180 degree shift and it is Case 2: the reflected waves start in step, so destructive interference needs an extra path of half a wavelength in the film.$1.00 \lt 1.38 \lt 1.52$：膜的折射率大于空气、小于玻璃。两次反射都来自折射率更高的介质，所以都有 180 度相位变化，属于情形 2：两列反射波一开始同步，所以相消干涉要求在膜内多走半个波长。
Step 2: Minimum thickness第 2 步：最小厚度

This is a quarter of the wavelength in the coating ($550/1.38 = 398.6\,\mathrm{nm}$, and a quarter of that is $99.6\,\mathrm{nm}$). Why this works: the quarter-wavelength film makes the extra path $2t$ equal to half a wavelength, so the two reflected waves arrive out of phase.这是膜中波长的四分之一（$550/1.38 = 398.6\,\mathrm{nm}$，其四分之一是 $99.6\,\mathrm{nm}$）。未被反射的光透射进玻璃，所以透镜透过更多的光。为什么这样做：四分之一波长的膜使多走的路程 $2t$ 等于半个波长，所以两列反射波反相到达。

A film of oil ($n = 1.45$) floats on water ($n = 1.33$), with air above. Light of wavelength $530\,\mathrm{nm}$ is incident normally. Decide whether the reflections have phase changes and find the smallest nonzero oil thickness that strongly reflects $530\,\mathrm{nm}$ light.

一层油膜（$n = 1.45$）浮在水（$n = 1.33$）上，上方是空气。波长 $530\,\mathrm{nm}$ 的光垂直入射。判断两次反射是否有相位变化，并求强烈反射 $530\,\mathrm{nm}$ 光的最小非零油膜厚度。

Step 1: Phase shifts第 1 步：相位变化
Air to oil ($1.00 \to 1.45$): reflection from a higher index, shifted. Oil to water ($1.45 \to 1.33$): reflection from a lower index, not shifted. One shift only, so this is Case 1 and strong reflection needs $2t = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda/n$.空气到油（$1.00 \to 1.45$）：从折射率更高的介质反射，有相位变化。油到水（$1.45 \to 1.33$）：从折射率更低的介质反射，无相位变化。只有一次相位变化，所以属于情形 1，强反射要求 $2t = (m + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda/n$。

At the top of a vertical soap film, where it has drained very thin, the film looks dark in reflected white light. Explain why.在竖直肥皂膜最上端，薄膜已经流得非常薄，在反射的白光中它显得发暗。解释原因。

The film is much thinner than the wavelength, so the extra path $2t$ is nearly zero. One of the two reflections (the top surface, from a higher index) has a 180 degree phase change and the other has none, so the two reflected waves are out of phase and cancel for every wavelength: destructive interference, so the film is dark.

The frequency of a wave does not change when it passes from one medium into another, but the speed in the film is reduced to $1/n$ of its value outside.

Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Saying a larger amplitude or higher frequency makes a wave faster: speed is set by the medium.
  2. Letting the frequency change at a boundary: it stays fixed while speed and wavelength change.
  3. Turning a Doppler question into a calculation: give a comparison (higher, lower or equal) with the reason, not a formula.
  4. Using $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$ for bright fringes, or mixing the slit width $a$ with the separation $d$.
  5. Applying the closed-pipe odd-harmonic rule to an open-open pipe, or the all-harmonics rule to a closed-open one.
  6. Forgetting the phase change on reflection from a higher index, or using the air wavelength in the film.
  7. Using the small-angle approximation for a grating's large angles.
最常见的错误
  1. 说振幅更大或频率更高会使波传得更快：波速由介质决定。
  2. 让频率在边界处发生变化：频率保持不变，改变的是波速和波长。
  3. 把多普勒问题变成计算题：只需给出比较（更高、更低或相等）及理由，而不是公式。
  4. 用 $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$ 求亮纹，或把缝宽 $a$ 与缝间距 $d$ 混淆。
  5. 把闭管的奇次谐波规则用到两端开口的管上，或把全部谐波的规则用到一端闭合的管上。
  6. 漏掉从更高折射率介质反射时的相位变化，或在膜内使用空气中的波长。
  7. 对光栅的大角度使用小角近似。

What does a wave transfer?波传递什么？
Energy between two locations, without transferring matter. Mechanical waves need a medium; electromagnetic waves do not.在两个位置之间传递能量，不传递物质。机械波需要介质；电磁波不需要。
Speed of a wave on a string?弦上的波速？
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{F_T}{\mu}}$$Set by tension and mass per unit length, not by the source.由张力和单位长度质量决定，与波源无关。
Transverse versus longitudinal?横波与纵波？
Transverse: disturbance perpendicular to travel. Longitudinal: parallel to travel. Sound is longitudinal (compressions and rarefactions).横波：扰动垂直于传播方向。纵波：平行于传播方向。声波是纵波（压缩区与稀疏区）。
Period, frequency and wave speed?周期、频率与波速？
$$T = \frac{1}{f}, \qquad v = f\lambda$$The source sets $f$; the medium sets $v$; $\lambda = v/f$.波源决定 $f$；介质决定 $v$；$\lambda = v/f$。
When is a reflected wave inverted?反射波何时倒转？
When the transmitted wave enters a medium where the speed decreases (light string to heavy string). The frequency never changes at a boundary.当透射波进入波速减小的介质时（轻弦到重弦）。频率在边界处永不改变。
Intensity, and who can be polarized?强度，以及谁能被偏振？
$$I = \frac{P}{A}$$Transverse waves can be polarized; longitudinal waves cannot. Polarization may reduce intensity.横波可以被偏振；纵波不能。偏振可能使强度减小。
Electromagnetic spectrum in order of decreasing wavelength?按波长由长到短的电磁波谱顺序？
Radio, microwave, infrared, visible (red to violet), ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma. All travel at $c$ in a vacuum, $c = f\lambda$.无线电波、微波、红外线、可见光（红到紫）、紫外线、X 射线、伽马射线。真空中都以 $c$ 传播，$c = f\lambda$。
Doppler effect rule (qualitative)?多普勒效应的规则（定性）？
Approaching: observed frequency above the rest frequency. Separating: below. No relative motion: equal. Greater relative velocity, greater difference.靠近：观测频率高于静止频率。远离：低于。无相对运动：相等。相对速度越大，差越大。
Beat frequency?拍频？
$$f_\text{beat} = |f_1 - f_2|$$From two waves of slightly different frequency.由两列频率略有不同的波产生。
Standing waves: both ends fixed (or both open)?驻波：两端固定（或两端开口）？
$$\lambda_n = \frac{2L}{n}, \quad f_n = n\,\frac{v}{2L}$$All harmonics, $n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$; a string fixed at both ends has $n$ antinodes.所有谐波，$n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$；两端固定的弦有 $n$ 个波腹。
Standing waves: one node end, one antinode end?驻波：一端波节、一端波腹？
$$\lambda_n = \frac{4L}{n}, \quad f_n = n\,\frac{v}{4L}$$Odd harmonics only, $n = 1, 3, 5, \dots$只有奇次谐波，$n = 1, 3, 5, \dots$
Single slit: where are the dark bands?单缝：暗纹在哪里？
$$a\sin\theta = m\lambda, \quad y_\text{min} = \frac{m\lambda L}{a}$$Minima, $m = 1, 2, 3, \dots$; small angles only for $y$.极小，$m = 1, 2, 3, \dots$；$y$ 的形式仅适用于小角。
Double slit or grating: where are the bright fringes?双缝或光栅：亮纹在哪里？
$$d\sin\theta = m\lambda, \quad y_\text{max} = \frac{m\lambda L}{d}$$Maxima, $m = 0, \pm 1, \dots$; inside the single-slit envelope.极大，$m = 0, \pm 1, \dots$；位于单缝包络之内。
White light on a grating?白光入射光栅？
White central maximum; higher orders form a rainbow with red (longest wavelength) farthest from the center, violet nearest.中央极大是白色；更高级次形成彩虹，红光（波长最长）离中心最远，紫光最近。
Phase change on reflection of light?光反射时的相位变化？
180 degrees when reflecting from a higher index of refraction; none from a lower index; none on refraction.从折射率更高的介质反射时为 180 度；从更低的介质反射时没有；折射时没有。
Thin film at normal incidence?垂直入射的薄膜？
$$\lambda_\text{film} = \frac{\lambda}{n}, \quad t_\text{coating} = \frac{\lambda}{4n}$$Count phase shifts case by case; the extra path is $2t$.逐种情形数相位变化；多走的路程为 $2t$。

11. White light is incident on a diffraction grating. Which description of the pattern is correct?白光入射到衍射光栅上。下列哪项对图样的描述是正确的？
The center maximum is red, and violet is farthest from the center中央极大是红色的，紫光离中心最远
The center maximum is white, and violet is farthest from the center in each order中央极大是白色的，每一级中紫光离中心最远
The center maximum is white, and red is farthest from the center in each order中央极大是白色的，每一级中红光离中心最远
The colors do not separate; a grating works only for single-wavelength light颜色不会分开；光栅只对单一波长的光起作用
Correct! At $m = 0$ every wavelength has $\theta = 0$, so the center is white. For higher orders $\sin\theta = m\lambda/d$ is largest for the longest wavelength, red.正确！在 $m = 0$ 处，每种波长都有 $\theta = 0$，所以中心是白色的。对更高级次，$\sin\theta = m\lambda/d$ 在波长最长的红光处最大。
Use $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$: the angle increases with the wavelength, so red (the longest wavelength) is deflected the most and violet the least. The center ($m = 0$) has no path difference for any color, so it is white. A grating separates colors; that is how it is used to analyze light.使用 $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$：角度随波长增大，所以红光（波长最长）偏折最大，紫光最小。中心（$m = 0$）对任何颜色都没有路程差，所以是白色的。光栅能把颜色分开，这正是用它分析光的原因。

12. A coating of index $1.38$ on glass of higher index is to remove the reflection of light of wavelength $552\,\mathrm{nm}$ in air at normal incidence. What is the minimum coating thickness?要在折射率更高的玻璃上镀一层折射率为 $1.38$ 的膜，以消除空气中波长 $552\,\mathrm{nm}$ 的光在垂直入射时的反射。膜的最小厚度是多少？
$138\,\mathrm{nm}$
$100\,\mathrm{nm}$
$200\,\mathrm{nm}$
$400\,\mathrm{nm}$
Correct! The thickness is a quarter of the wavelength in the coating: $t = \lambda/(4n) = 552/(4 \times 1.38) = 100\,\mathrm{nm}$.正确！厚度是膜中波长的四分之一：$t = \lambda/(4n) = 552/(4 \times 1.38) = 100\,\mathrm{nm}$。
The minimum thickness is $\lambda/(4n) = 100\,\mathrm{nm}$. $138\,\mathrm{nm}$ is a quarter of the wavelength in air, forgetting the index; $200\,\mathrm{nm}$ is half the wavelength in the film, which would be the path difference $2t$, not the thickness; and $400\,\mathrm{nm}$ is the whole wavelength in the film.最小厚度为 $\lambda/(4n) = 100\,\mathrm{nm}$。$138\,\mathrm{nm}$ 是空气中波长的四分之一，忘了折射率；$200\,\mathrm{nm}$ 是膜内波长的一半，那应是路程差 $2t$，而不是厚度；$400\,\mathrm{nm}$ 是膜内的整个波长。
13. The top of a vertical soap film in air has drained until it is far thinner than the wavelength of visible light. In reflected white light, how does that part of the film look, and why?空气中竖直肥皂膜的上端已经流得比可见光波长薄得多。在反射的白光中，这部分薄膜看起来怎样？为什么？
Dark: one of the two reflections has a 180 degree phase change, so the reflected waves cancel暗：两次反射中有一次有 180 度相位变化，所以反射波相互抵消
Bright: the two reflected waves are in phase亮：两列反射波同相
Dark: such a thin film absorbs all the light暗：这么薄的膜吸收了全部的光
Colored bands: each wavelength is reinforced in turn彩色条带：各种波长依次被加强
Correct! With $2t \approx 0$, the reflected waves differ only by the 180 degree shift at the top surface, so they are out of phase for every wavelength and interfere destructively.正确！$2t \approx 0$ 时，两列反射波只差上表面的 180 度相位变化，所以对每种波长都反相，发生相消干涉。
Count the phase changes: the top reflection (air to soap, higher index) has a 180 degree change and the bottom reflection (soap to air, lower index) has none. With almost no extra path, the waves are out of phase for all colors, so the film is dark. The darkness is from interference, not from absorption, and no color bands appear because no wavelength is reinforced.数相位变化：上表面反射（空气到肥皂，折射率更高）有 180 度相位变化，下表面反射（肥皂到空气，折射率更低）没有。几乎没有额外路程时，两列波对所有颜色都反相，所以薄膜是暗的。变暗是由于干涉而不是吸收，也不会出现彩色条带，因为没有任何波长被加强。
