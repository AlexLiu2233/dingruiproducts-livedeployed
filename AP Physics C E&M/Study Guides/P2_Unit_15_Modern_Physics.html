Quantum Theory and Wave-Particle Duality量子理论与波粒二象性

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe why quantum theory was developed and the observations that classical mechanics could not explain.
  • Describe light both as a wave and as a stream of photons, and state the properties of a photon.
  • Calculate the energy of a photon from its frequency or wavelength, in joules and in electron-volts.
  • Calculate the speed of photons in a medium from the index of refraction.
  • Calculate a de Broglie wavelength and predict how it changes when the momentum changes.
  • Explain when quantum theory is needed for a particle, and what quantized values of energy and momentum mean for a bound system.
  • 描述（describe）为什么要发展量子理论，以及经典力学无法解释的观测现象。
  • 描述（describe）光既可以看作波，也可以看作光子流，并说出光子的性质。
  • 由频率或波长计算（calculate）光子的能量，单位用焦耳和电子伏。
  • 由折射率计算（calculate）光子在介质中的速度。
  • 计算（calculate）德布罗意波长，并预测动量变化时它如何变化。
  • 解释（explain）什么时候需要用量子理论处理粒子，以及束缚体系中能量与动量的量子化是什么意思。

Why Quantum Theory Was Needed为什么需要量子理论

Quantum theory was developed to explain observations of matter and energy that classical mechanics could not explain.

量子理论（quantum theory）是为了解释经典力学无法解释的物质与能量的观测现象而发展起来的。

Three of them are central to this unit: the atomic spectra of gases (atoms emit and absorb light only at certain sharp colors), blackbody radiation (the way the color and intensity of glowing matter depend on its temperature), and the photoelectric effect (light knocking electrons out of a metal).

本单元有三个核心现象：气体的原子光谱（原子只在某些尖锐的特定颜色处发射和吸收光）、黑体辐射（发光物质的颜色与强度如何依赖温度），以及光电效应（光把电子从金属中打出来）。

Classical physics, which treats energy as a continuous quantity and light as a pure wave, gives wrong predictions for all three. Quantum theory is necessary to describe the properties of matter at atomic and subatomic scales. In it, fundamental particles can show both particle-like and wave-like behavior.

经典物理把能量看作连续量、把光看作纯粹的波，对这三者都给出错误的预测。要描述原子和亚原子尺度上物质的性质，必须使用量子理论。在量子理论中，基本粒子既可以表现出粒子性（particle-like），也可以表现出波动性（wave-like）。

Light as a Wave and as Photons光既是波也是光子

Interference and diffraction show that light behaves as a wave with frequency $f$ and wavelength $\lambda$, related by $c = f\lambda$. Other experiments, which you meet in the next topics, show that light also behaves as a stream of discrete particles called photons. Neither picture is "the" truth: you choose the model that explains the experiment.

干涉和衍射表明光表现为波，频率为 $f$、波长为 $\lambda$，二者满足 $c = f\lambda$。你在后面的专题中会看到，另一些实验表明光同样表现为一束束分立的粒子，称为光子（photon）。两种图像都不是"唯一的真相"：要根据实验选择能解释它的模型。

A photon is a massless, electrically neutral particle. Its energy is proportional to the frequency of the light, and the constant of proportionality is Planck's constant $h$. Photons travel in straight lines unless they interact with matter, as when they are absorbed, emitted or scattered.

光子是无质量（massless）、电中性的粒子，其能量与光的频率成正比，比例常数是普朗克常量 $h$。光子沿直线传播，除非与物质发生相互作用，例如被吸收、发射或散射。

Photon energy光子能量
$$E = hf = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$$

$E$ is the photon energy (J or eV), $h = 6.63 \times 10^{-34}\;\mathrm{J\,s} = 4.14 \times 10^{-15}\;\mathrm{eV\,s}$ is Planck's constant, $f$ is the frequency (Hz), $\lambda$ is the wavelength (m) and $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\;\mathrm{m/s}$. The product $hc = 1240\;\mathrm{eV\,nm}$ is convenient when $\lambda$ is in nanometers.

$E$ 为光子能量（J 或 eV），$h = 6.63 \times 10^{-34}\;\mathrm{J\,s} = 4.14 \times 10^{-15}\;\mathrm{eV\,s}$ 为普朗克常量，$f$ 为频率（Hz），$\lambda$ 为波长（m），$c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\;\mathrm{m/s}$。当 $\lambda$ 以纳米为单位时，用乘积 $hc = 1240\;\mathrm{eV\,nm}$ 很方便。

Keep the two energy units straight. The electron-volt is the energy an electron gains crossing a potential difference of one volt: $1\;\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\;\mathrm{J}$. Visible photons carry a few eV, which is why atomic physics uses eV and not J.

要分清两种能量单位。电子伏是电子经过 1 V 电势差获得的能量：$1\;\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\;\mathrm{J}$。可见光光子的能量只有几个 eV，所以原子物理用 eV 而不用 J。

A larger frequency means a shorter wavelength and a more energetic photon: a violet photon carries about twice the energy of a red one.

频率越大，波长越短，光子能量越大：紫光光子的能量约为红光光子的两倍。

Common Mistake: "A massless photon has no momentum" A photon has zero mass, but it still carries energy $E = hf$ and momentum $p = h/\lambda$. Mass is not required for a particle to carry energy and momentum. Never try to find a photon's energy from $\tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$: its energy comes from its frequency.
常见错误：“光子没有质量，所以没有动量” 光子质量为零，但仍携带能量 $E = hf$ 和动量 $p = h/\lambda$。粒子携带能量和动量并不需要有质量。千万不要用 $\tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$ 去求光子的能量：光子的能量来自它的频率。

The Speed of a Photon光子的速度

The speed of a photon depends on the medium it travels through. In free space, all photons travel at the speed of light, $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\;\mathrm{m/s}$, whatever their frequency.

光子的速度取决于它所在的介质。在自由空间中，所有光子的速度都等于光速 $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\;\mathrm{m/s}$，与频率无关。

In a material, the speed of photons is in general inversely proportional to the index of refraction $n$ of that medium, so photons travel more slowly in glass than in air. The frequency of the light does not change on entering the medium, so the wavelength shrinks by the same factor $n$.

在材料中，光子的速度一般与该介质的折射率（index of refraction）$n$ 成反比，所以光子在玻璃中比在空气中慢。光进入介质时频率不变，所以波长缩短相同的倍数 $n$。

Photon speed in a medium介质中的光子速度
$$v = \frac{c}{n}$$

$v$ is the speed of photons in the medium (m/s), $c$ the speed in free space and $n \ge 1$ the index of refraction (no unit).

$v$ 为介质中光子的速度（m/s），$c$ 为自由空间中的速度，$n \ge 1$ 为折射率（无单位）。

Matter Waves: The de Broglie Wavelength物质波：德布罗意波长

If light can behave like particles, particles can behave like waves. Variations of Young's double-slit experiment show this: a beam of electrons sent at two narrow slits builds up an interference pattern of bright and dark bands on a detector, even when the electrons arrive one at a time.

既然光可以表现得像粒子，粒子也可以表现得像波。杨氏双缝实验的各种变形说明了这一点：把电子束射向两条狭缝，探测器上会逐渐形成明暗相间的干涉条纹，即使电子是一个一个到达的。

A wave model of matter is quantified by the de Broglie wavelength, which depends on the momentum of the particle.

物质的波动模型用德布罗意波长（de Broglie wavelength）定量描述，它取决于粒子的动量。

de Broglie wavelength德布罗意波长
$$\lambda = \frac{h}{p} = \frac{h}{mv}$$

$\lambda$ is the wavelength (m), $p = mv$ the momentum of the particle (kg m/s), $m$ its mass (kg) and $v$ its speed (m/s). The same relation $p = h/\lambda$ gives the momentum of a photon.

$\lambda$ 为波长（m），$p = mv$ 为粒子的动量（kg m/s），$m$ 为质量（kg），$v$ 为速率（m/s）。同一关系式 $p = h/\lambda$ 也给出光子的动量。

Where the de Broglie Relation Comes From德布罗意关系从何而来

First principles: why a matter wave has $\lambda = h/p$第一性原理：为什么物质波满足 $\lambda = h/p$

Principle. A photon carries energy $E = hf$, and anything that moves at $c$ with no mass has momentum $p = E/c$. In 1924 de Broglie proposed that the same link between momentum and wavelength holds for every particle.

原理。光子携带能量 $E = hf$，而以 $c$ 运动的无质量粒子具有动量 $p = E/c$。1924 年，德布罗意提出：动量与波长之间的这一联系对所有粒子都成立。

  1. For a photon, $p = E/c = hf/c$.
  2. Use $c = f\lambda$, so $f/c = 1/\lambda$: $p = h/\lambda$, or $\lambda = h/p$.
  3. Apply it to a particle of mass $m$ at speed $v \ll c$: $p = mv$, so $\lambda = h/(mv)$.
  1. 对光子，$p = E/c = hf/c$。
  2. 利用 $c = f\lambda$，即 $f/c = 1/\lambda$：$p = h/\lambda$，或 $\lambda = h/p$。
  3. 用于质量为 $m$、速率 $v \ll c$ 的粒子：$p = mv$，所以 $\lambda = h/(mv)$。

Limiting check. The units are $\mathrm{J\,s}/(\mathrm{kg\,m/s}) = \mathrm{m}$. As $mv$ grows, $\lambda \to 0$: a thrown baseball has $\lambda \approx 10^{-34}\;\mathrm{m}$ and shows no wave effects. An electron at $1.0 \times 10^{6}\;\mathrm{m/s}$ has $\lambda = 7.3 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$, comparable to atomic spacings, so it diffracts.

极限检验。单位为 $\mathrm{J\,s}/(\mathrm{kg\,m/s}) = \mathrm{m}$。$mv$ 增大时 $\lambda \to 0$：投出的棒球 $\lambda \approx 10^{-34}\;\mathrm{m}$，看不到任何波动效应。速率为 $1.0 \times 10^{6}\;\mathrm{m/s}$ 的电子 $\lambda = 7.3 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$，与原子间距相当，所以会发生衍射。

The wavelength increases as the momentum decreases: halve the momentum and $\lambda$ doubles. Because $h$ is tiny, an everyday object has an unmeasurably small wavelength.

动量减小时波长增大：动量减半，$\lambda$ 加倍。由于 $h$ 极小，日常物体的波长小到无法测量。

Quantum theory is necessary to describe systems where the de Broglie wavelength is comparable to the size of the system, such as an electron near an atom or a slit of nanometer width. When $\lambda$ is much smaller than every length in the problem, classical mechanics works.

当德布罗意波长与体系的尺寸相当（comparable）时，例如原子附近的电子或纳米宽度的狭缝，就必须用量子理论描述。当 $\lambda$ 远小于问题中所有长度时，经典力学就够用了。

Quantized Values for Bound Systems束缚体系的量子化取值

A free particle can have any energy. A particle confined to a small region, such as an electron held by a nucleus, is a bound system, and quantum theory says its energy and momentum can take only certain separate values.

自由粒子可以具有任意能量。被限制在很小区域内的粒子，例如被原子核束缚的电子，构成束缚体系（bound system），量子理论指出它的能量和动量只能取某些分立的值。

We call such values quantized: the allowed values are discrete, like the steps of a staircase, and the system cannot be found between them. The next topics show where these discrete values come from (the electron's matter wave must fit the orbit) and what they mean for the light atoms emit and absorb.

我们把这样的值称为量子化（quantized）的：允许的取值是离散的（discrete），像楼梯的台阶一样，体系不可能处于两级之间。后面的专题会说明这些离散值从哪里来（电子的物质波必须与轨道相匹配），以及它们对原子发射和吸收的光意味着什么。

Worked Example: Energy of a Green Photon例题：绿光光子的能量

Green light has a wavelength of $500\;\mathrm{nm}$ in free space. Find the energy of one photon in eV and in J, and the frequency of the light.

绿光在自由空间中的波长为 $500\;\mathrm{nm}$。求一个光子的能量（分别以 eV 和 J 为单位）以及光的频率。

Step 1: Energy in eV第 1 步：以 eV 为单位的能量
$$E = \frac{hc}{\lambda} = \frac{1240\;\mathrm{eV\,nm}}{500\;\mathrm{nm}} = 2.48\;\mathrm{eV}$$
Step 2: Convert to joules第 2 步：换算成焦耳
$$E = (2.48)(1.60 \times 10^{-19}) = 3.97 \times 10^{-19}\;\mathrm{J}$$
Step 3: Frequency第 3 步：频率
$$f = \frac{c}{\lambda} = \frac{3.00 \times 10^{8}}{500 \times 10^{-9}} = 6.00 \times 10^{14}\;\mathrm{Hz}$$
Check: $hf = (4.14 \times 10^{-15})(6.00 \times 10^{14}) = 2.48\;\mathrm{eV}$. Why this works: using $hc$ in eV nm keeps the wavelength in nanometers and gives the answer directly in eV, which avoids unit conversions.检验：$hf = (4.14 \times 10^{-15})(6.00 \times 10^{14}) = 2.48\;\mathrm{eV}$。为什么成立：使用以 eV nm 为单位的 $hc$，波长可直接用纳米，结果直接得到 eV，省去单位换算。
Worked Example: When Does an Electron Behave as a Wave?例题：电子什么时候表现为波？

An electron is accelerated from rest through a potential difference of $100\;\mathrm{V}$, so its kinetic energy is $100\;\mathrm{eV}$. Find its de Broglie wavelength and compare it with an atom, about $1 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$ across. Then find the wavelength of a $0.145\;\mathrm{kg}$ baseball moving at $40\;\mathrm{m/s}$.

一个电子从静止开始经 $100\;\mathrm{V}$ 电势差加速，动能为 $100\;\mathrm{eV}$。求它的德布罗意波长，并与直径约 $1 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$ 的原子比较。再求质量 $0.145\;\mathrm{kg}$、速率 $40\;\mathrm{m/s}$ 的棒球的波长。

Step 1: Kinetic energy in joules第 1 步：以焦耳为单位的动能
$$K = (100)(1.60 \times 10^{-19}) = 1.60 \times 10^{-17}\;\mathrm{J}$$
Step 2: Momentum from $K = p^2/2m$第 2 步：由 $K = p^2/2m$ 求动量
$$p = \sqrt{2mK} = \sqrt{2(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(1.60 \times 10^{-17})} = 5.40 \times 10^{-24}\;\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$$
Step 3: Electron wavelength第 3 步：电子的波长
$$\lambda = \frac{h}{p} = \frac{6.63 \times 10^{-34}}{5.40 \times 10^{-24}} = 1.23 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$$
This is about the size of an atom, so the wave nature of the electron matters and quantum theory is needed. Electrons of this energy diffract from the regular layers of atoms in a crystal.这与原子的尺寸相当，所以电子的波动性很重要，必须用量子理论。这种能量的电子会被晶体中规则排列的原子层衍射。
Step 4: The baseball第 4 步：棒球
$$\lambda = \frac{h}{mv} = \frac{6.63 \times 10^{-34}}{(0.145)(40)} = 1.14 \times 10^{-34}\;\mathrm{m}$$
This is about $10^{24}$ times smaller than an atom and far smaller than anything in the situation, so no wave effect can ever be seen. Why this works: comparing $\lambda$ with the size of the system decides which theory to use.这比原子小约 $10^{24}$ 倍，远小于情境中的任何长度，所以永远观察不到波动效应。为什么成立：比较 $\lambda$ 与体系的尺寸，就能决定使用哪种理论。
Worked Example: Finding h from Matter-Wave Data例题：由物质波数据求 h

A student measures the de Broglie wavelength of particles at three momenta and gets $(p, \lambda)$ = $(1.00, 6.63)$, $(2.00, 3.32)$ and $(4.00, 1.66)$ with $p$ in $10^{-24}\;\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$ and $\lambda$ in $10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$. Which quantity should be plotted against $\lambda$ to get a straight line, and what does the slope give?

一名学生在三个动量下测量粒子的德布罗意波长，得到 $(p, \lambda)$ = $(1.00, 6.63)$、$(2.00, 3.32)$ 和 $(4.00, 1.66)$，其中 $p$ 的单位为 $10^{-24}\;\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$，$\lambda$ 的单位为 $10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$。应该用什么量对 $\lambda$ 作图才能得到直线？斜率给出什么？

Step 1: Linearize第 1 步：线性化
$$\lambda = h\left(\frac{1}{p}\right)$$
The equation has the form $y = mx$ with $y = \lambda$ and $x = 1/p$, so the line passes through the origin and the slope is $h$. Plotting $\lambda$ against $p$ would give a curve.方程具有 $y = mx$ 的形式，其中 $y = \lambda$，$x = 1/p$，所以直线过原点，斜率为 $h$。若用 $\lambda$ 对 $p$ 作图，得到的是曲线。
Step 2: Slope from the end points第 2 步：用两端点求斜率
$$x:\; 1.00,\; 0.50,\; 0.25 \;(10^{24}) \qquad \frac{(6.63 - 1.66) \times 10^{-10}}{(1.00 - 0.25) \times 10^{24}} = 6.63 \times 10^{-34}\;\mathrm{J\,s}$$
The slope, the ratio shown above, equals Planck's constant, which is Figure 15.1. Why this works: a straight line through the origin confirms that $\lambda \propto 1/p$, the de Broglie relation, and its slope gives $h$ with no other input.斜率（上面给出的比值）等于普朗克常量，这正是图 15.1。为什么成立：过原点的直线证实 $\lambda \propto 1/p$，即德布罗意关系，并且斜率不需要其他输入就能给出 $h$。
Exam tip考试提示

Decide the energy unit before you start. With a wavelength in nanometers, $E = 1240/\lambda$ gives eV directly; with SI values use $E = hf$ in joules and convert with $1\;\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\;\mathrm{J}$ only once.

开始前先确定能量单位。波长以纳米为单位时，$E = 1240/\lambda$ 直接得到 eV；用 SI 数值时，用 $E = hf$ 得到焦耳，只换算一次 $1\;\mathrm{eV} = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\;\mathrm{J}$。

When a question asks you to "justify" that a particle shows wave behavior, compare its de Broglie wavelength with a length in the situation (slit spacing, atomic spacing, system size) and state whether they are comparable.

当题目要求"论证"（justify）某粒子表现出波动性时，把它的德布罗意波长与情境中的某个长度（缝间距、原子间距、体系尺寸）比较，并说明二者是否相当。

Inside the Atom: Nucleus and Electrons原子内部：原子核与电子

Atoms have internal structure. An atom consists of a small, positively charged nucleus surrounded by one or more negatively charged electrons. The nucleus is made up of protons and neutrons, together called nucleons.

原子具有内部结构。原子由一个小而带正电的原子核（nucleus）和围绕它的一个或多个带负电的电子组成。原子核由质子和中子组成，二者合称核子。

A proton carries charge $+e$, a neutron carries no charge, and an electron carries charge $-e$, where $e = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\;\mathrm{C}$. A neutral atom has equal numbers of protons and electrons. An ion is an atom with a nonzero net electric charge: it has gained or lost electrons.

质子带电荷 $+e$，中子不带电，电子带电荷 $-e$，其中 $e = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\;\mathrm{C}$。电中性原子的质子数与电子数相等。离子（ion）是净电荷不为零的原子：它得到或失去了电子。

The number and arrangement of electrons determine how atoms interact with one another and with light. The numbers of neutrons and protons in an atom are written in nuclear notation:

电子的数目和排布决定了原子之间以及原子与光如何相互作用。原子中中子和质子的数目用核符号（nuclear notation）表示：

Nuclear notation核符号
$${}^{A}_{Z}\mathrm{X} \qquad A = Z + N$$

$\mathrm{X}$ is the chemical symbol, $Z$ is the atomic number (the number of protons), $N$ is the number of neutrons and $A$ is the mass number (the nucleon number, protons plus neutrons). All three are whole numbers with no unit.

$\mathrm{X}$ 为元素符号，$Z$ 为原子序数（质子数），$N$ 为中子数，$A$ 为质量数（核子数，即质子数加中子数）。三者都是没有单位的整数。

Elements, Isotopes and the Mass of an Atom元素、同位素与原子的质量

Each element has a unique number of protons, so $Z$ identifies the element: every atom with $Z = 6$ is carbon. Atoms of the same element can have different numbers of neutrons.

每种元素都有独一无二的质子数，所以 $Z$ 确定了元素：所有 $Z = 6$ 的原子都是碳。同一种元素的原子可以有不同的中子数。

The total number of neutrons and protons, $A$, identifies the isotope: ${}^{12}_{6}\mathrm{C}$ and ${}^{14}_{6}\mathrm{C}$ are both carbon, with 6 and 8 neutrons. Isotopes of one element have nearly the same chemistry, because they have the same number of electrons.

中子与质子的总数 $A$ 确定同位素（isotope）：${}^{12}_{6}\mathrm{C}$ 和 ${}^{14}_{6}\mathrm{C}$ 都是碳，分别有 6 个和 8 个中子。同一元素的各同位素电子数相同，所以化学性质几乎相同。

The mass of an atom is dominated by the total mass of the protons and neutrons in its nucleus. Each nucleon has a mass of about $1\;\mathrm{u} = 1.66 \times 10^{-27}\;\mathrm{kg}$, while an electron has only $9.11 \times 10^{-31}\;\mathrm{kg}$, about $1800$ times less.

原子的质量主要由原子核中质子和中子的总质量决定。每个核子的质量约为 $1\;\mathrm{u} = 1.66 \times 10^{-27}\;\mathrm{kg}$，而电子只有 $9.11 \times 10^{-31}\;\mathrm{kg}$，约小 $1800$ 倍。

Since the nucleus is only about $10^{-15}\;\mathrm{m}$ across while the atom is about $10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$ across, nearly all the mass sits in a tiny, dense core.

原子核的线度只有约 $10^{-15}\;\mathrm{m}$，而原子约为 $10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$，所以几乎全部质量集中在一个微小而致密的核心中。

History: from the gold foil to the Bohr atom历史：从金箔实验到玻尔原子

In 1909 Hans Geiger and Ernest Marsden, working in Ernest Rutherford's laboratory, fired alpha particles at a very thin gold foil. Most went straight through, but a very small fraction bounced back through large angles. In 1911 Rutherford explained this with a tiny, dense, positive nucleus that holds nearly all of the atom's mass.

1909 年，汉斯·盖革（Hans Geiger）和欧内斯特·马斯登（Ernest Marsden）在欧内斯特·卢瑟福（Ernest Rutherford）的实验室里，用 α 粒子轰击极薄的金箔。大多数粒子直接穿过，但有极少一部分以很大的角度被弹回。1911 年，卢瑟福用一个微小、致密、带正电并集中了原子几乎全部质量的原子核解释了这一结果。

In 1913 Niels Bohr gave this nuclear atom a set of allowed orbits and obtained the hydrogen spectrum. The pages below describe the scattering experiment and the model it led to.

1913 年，尼尔斯·玻尔（Niels Bohr）为这个核式原子规定了一组允许轨道，并由此得出了氢原子光谱。下面的页面介绍了这次散射实验以及由它产生的模型。

The Bohr Model: Circular Orbits玻尔模型：圆形轨道

The Bohr model of the atom is based on classical physics. It was the historical representation of the atom that led to the description of the hydrogen atom in terms of discrete energy states.

玻尔模型（Bohr model）以经典物理为基础。它是历史上最早的原子图像，由它得出了用分立能量状态描述氢原子的方法。

In the Bohr model the electron moves around the nucleus in a circular orbit determined by the electron's charge and mass and by the electric force between the electron and the nucleus. For a single electron orbiting a nucleus of charge $+e$, the Coulomb force supplies the net centripetal force:

在玻尔模型中，电子在圆形轨道（circular orbit）上绕核运动，轨道由电子的电荷和质量以及电子与原子核之间的电力决定。对绕电荷为 $+e$ 的原子核运动的单个电子，由库仑力提供向心的合力：

Coulomb force and net force on a circling electron库仑力与做圆周运动电子所受的合力
$$F_e = k\frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2} \qquad F_\mathrm{net} = \frac{m v^2}{r}$$

$k = 8.99 \times 10^{9}\;\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$ is the Coulomb constant, $q_1$ and $q_2$ are the charges (C), $r$ is the separation (m), $m$ is the electron mass and $v$ its speed. For the electron and a proton, $q_1 q_2 = e^2$ in magnitude.

$k = 8.99 \times 10^{9}\;\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$ 为库仑常量，$q_1$ 与 $q_2$ 为电荷（C），$r$ 为间距（m），$m$ 为电子质量，$v$ 为其速率。对电子与质子，取大小时 $q_1 q_2 = e^2$。

Setting $F_e = F_\mathrm{net}$ gives $k e^2/r^2 = m v^2/r$, so $m v^2 = k e^2/r$. The kinetic energy is then $K = \tfrac{1}{2} m v^2 = k e^2/(2r)$. The electron-nucleus system also has electric potential energy $U = -k e^2/r$, so the total energy is $E = K + U = -k e^2/(2r)$.

令 $F_e = F_\mathrm{net}$，得 $k e^2/r^2 = m v^2/r$，即 $m v^2 = k e^2/r$。于是动能 $K = \tfrac{1}{2} m v^2 = k e^2/(2r)$。电子与原子核组成的体系还有电势能 $U = -k e^2/r$，所以总能量 $E = K + U = -k e^2/(2r)$。

It is negative because the electron is bound: the larger the orbit, the closer $E$ is to zero. A classical orbit could have any radius and so any energy, which does not match the discrete spectra of atoms. The Bohr model fixes this by allowing only certain orbits.

它是负的，因为电子被束缚：轨道越大，$E$ 越接近零。经典轨道可以有任意半径，因而有任意能量，这与原子的分立光谱不符。玻尔模型通过只允许某些轨道来解决这个问题。

The Standing-Wave Model and Allowed Energy States驻波模型与允许的能量状态

Why are only some orbits allowed? The standing-wave model of electrons accounts for the existence of specific allowed energy states of an electron in an atom.

为什么只有某些轨道被允许？电子的驻波（standing wave）模型解释了原子中电子存在特定允许能量状态的原因。

The electron has a de Broglie wavelength $\lambda = h/(mv)$. A wave that travels once around a loop closes on itself smoothly only if the loop's circumference holds a whole number of wavelengths. Otherwise the wave meets itself out of step, cancels, and no stable pattern remains.

电子具有德布罗意波长 $\lambda = h/(mv)$。沿环路绕行一周的波，只有当环路的周长（circumference）恰好容纳整数个波长时，才能首尾平滑相接；否则波与自身错相相遇，相互抵消，不能形成稳定的图样。

So the orbit's circumference must be an integer multiple of the electron's de Broglie wavelength:

所以轨道周长必须是电子德布罗意波长的整数倍：

Standing-wave condition驻波条件
$$2\pi r = n\lambda = n\frac{h}{mv} \qquad n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$$

$r$ is the orbit radius (m), $n$ is a positive whole number that labels the orbit and the energy state, and $\lambda$ is the de Broglie wavelength of the electron. Each allowed $n$ has its own radius, speed and energy.

$r$ 为轨道半径（m），$n$ 为标记轨道和能量状态的正整数，$\lambda$ 为电子的德布罗意波长。每个允许的 $n$ 都有各自的半径、速率和能量。

Only the radii that satisfy this condition, together with the force equation above, are allowed, so only certain energy states exist. For hydrogen these energies are $E_n = -13.6\;\mathrm{eV}/n^2$, which Topic 15.3 uses. The analysis of electron structure in this course is limited to energy levels: it does not include such advanced descriptions as orbitals, orbital shapes or probability functions.

只有同时满足这一条件和上面力方程的半径才被允许，所以只存在某些能量状态。对氢原子，这些能量为 $E_n = -13.6\;\mathrm{eV}/n^2$，专题 15.3 会用到。本课程对电子结构的分析仅限于能级（energy levels）：不包括轨道（orbitals）、轨道形状或概率函数（probability functions）这类更高深的描述。

Deriving the Hydrogen Energy Levels推导氢原子能级

First principles: the hydrogen levels from two conditions第一性原理：由两个条件推出氢原子能级

Principle. Newton's second law for circular motion, with the Coulomb force as the net force ($m v^2 = k e^2/r$), plus the standing-wave condition $2\pi r = n h/(mv)$. This shows where the $E_n = -13.6\;\mathrm{eV}/n^2$ used in Topic 15.3 comes from.

原理。圆周运动的牛顿第二定律，库仑力作为合力（$m v^2 = k e^2/r$），再加上驻波条件 $2\pi r = n h/(mv)$。由此可以看出专题 15.3 所用的 $E_n = -13.6\;\mathrm{eV}/n^2$ 从何而来。

  1. Standing wave: $m v r = n h/(2\pi)$, so $v = n h/(2\pi m r)$.
  2. Substitute into $m v^2 = k e^2/r$ and solve: $r_n = \dfrac{n^2 h^2}{4\pi^2 m k e^2} = n^2 (5.29 \times 10^{-11}\;\mathrm{m})$.
  3. Use $E = -k e^2/(2r)$ from above: $E_n = -\dfrac{k e^2}{2 r_n} = -\dfrac{13.6\;\mathrm{eV}}{n^2}$.
  1. 驻波条件：$m v r = n h/(2\pi)$，所以 $v = n h/(2\pi m r)$。
  2. 代入 $m v^2 = k e^2/r$ 并求解：$r_n = \dfrac{n^2 h^2}{4\pi^2 m k e^2} = n^2 (5.29 \times 10^{-11}\;\mathrm{m})$。
  3. 利用上面的 $E = -k e^2/(2r)$：$E_n = -\dfrac{k e^2}{2 r_n} = -\dfrac{13.6\;\mathrm{eV}}{n^2}$。

Limiting check. For $n = 1$ the radius is $5.29 \times 10^{-11}\;\mathrm{m}$, the orbit of the worked example below, and $E_1 = -13.6\;\mathrm{eV}$ matches hydrogen's measured ionization energy. As $n \to \infty$, $r_n \to \infty$ and $E_n \to 0$: the electron is just free.

极限检验。$n = 1$ 时半径为 $5.29 \times 10^{-11}\;\mathrm{m}$，正是下面例题中的轨道，且 $E_1 = -13.6\;\mathrm{eV}$ 与实测的氢原子电离能一致。$n \to \infty$ 时 $r_n \to \infty$，$E_n \to 0$：电子恰好自由。

Common Mistake: Treating the Bohr orbit like a planet's orbit A planet can orbit at any radius, but an electron cannot: only orbits whose circumference is a whole number of de Broglie wavelengths are allowed. Also, $n$ is not the number of electrons or of orbits an atom has; it labels one allowed state. Do not describe electron structure with orbitals or probability clouds here: only energy levels are used.
常见错误：把玻尔轨道当作行星轨道 行星可以在任意半径上运行，电子却不行：只有周长恰为德布罗意波长整数倍的轨道才被允许。另外，$n$ 不是原子拥有的电子数或轨道数，而是标记一个允许状态的数。这里不要用轨道（orbital）或概率云来描述电子结构：只使用能级。
Worked Example: Counting Particles in Atoms and Ions例题：数一数原子和离子中的粒子

Find the number of protons, neutrons and electrons in (a) ${}^{37}_{17}\mathrm{Cl}$ with net charge $-e$ (a chloride ion), and (b) the neutral atom ${}^{35}_{17}\mathrm{Cl}$. How are the two atoms related?

求 (a) 净电荷为 $-e$ 的 ${}^{37}_{17}\mathrm{Cl}$（氯离子）和 (b) 中性原子 ${}^{35}_{17}\mathrm{Cl}$ 中质子、中子和电子的数目。这两种原子是什么关系？

Step 1: Protons and neutrons from $Z$ and $A$第 1 步：由 $Z$ 和 $A$ 得质子数和中子数
$$Z = 17; \quad N_a = 37 - 17 = 20, \quad N_b = 35 - 17 = 18$$
Step 2: Electrons from the net charge第 2 步：由净电荷得电子数
$$\text{(a)}:\; 17 + 1 = 18 \qquad \text{(b)}:\; 17$$
The ion in (a), with net charge $-e$, has 18 electrons; the neutral atom in (b) has 17. Both have 17 protons, so both are chlorine, but they have different mass numbers (37 and 35), so they are different isotopes. The ion in (a) has one extra electron, giving the net charge $-e$. Worked Example: The Lowest Bohr Orbit of Hydrogen例题：氢原子最低的玻尔轨道

In the lowest orbit of hydrogen the electron circles the proton at $r = 5.29 \times 10^{-11}\;\mathrm{m}$. Using the Coulomb force as the net centripetal force, find the electron's speed and kinetic energy, then show that the orbit holds exactly one de Broglie wavelength.

在氢原子最低的轨道上，电子绕质子运动，半径 $r = 5.29 \times 10^{-11}\;\mathrm{m}$。以库仑力作为向心的合力，求电子的速率和动能，然后证明该轨道恰好容纳一个德布罗意波长。

Step 1: Speed from $F_e = F_\mathrm{net}$第 1 步：由 $F_e = F_\mathrm{net}$ 求速率
$$v = \sqrt{\frac{k e^2}{m r}} = \sqrt{\frac{(8.99 \times 10^{9})(1.60 \times 10^{-19})^2}{(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(5.29 \times 10^{-11})}} = 2.19 \times 10^{6}\;\mathrm{m/s}$$
Step 2: Kinetic energy第 2 步：动能
$$K = \tfrac{1}{2} m v^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(2.19 \times 10^{6})^2 = 2.18 \times 10^{-18}\;\mathrm{J} = 13.6\;\mathrm{eV}$$
Step 3: de Broglie wavelength against circumference第 3 步：德布罗意波长与周长比较
$$\lambda = \frac{h}{mv} = \frac{6.63 \times 10^{-34}}{(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(2.19 \times 10^{6})} = 3.32 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}, \qquad 2\pi r = 3.32 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$$
The circumference equals one wavelength, so $n = 1$. The potential energy is $U = -2K = -27.2\;\mathrm{eV}$, so $E = K + U = -13.6\;\mathrm{eV}$, the energy of the lowest state of hydrogen. Why this works: the same orbit satisfies both the force equation and the standing-wave condition, which is what makes it an allowed state.周长等于一个波长，所以 $n = 1$。势能 $U = -2K = -27.2\;\mathrm{eV}$，因此 $E = K + U = -13.6\;\mathrm{eV}$，正是氢原子最低态的能量。为什么成立：同一条轨道同时满足力方程和驻波条件，这就是它成为允许状态的原因。
Worked Example: Is This Orbit Allowed?例题：这条轨道被允许吗？

A model predicts that an electron with speed $1.10 \times 10^{6}\;\mathrm{m/s}$ moves in a circular orbit of radius $2.12 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$. Use the standing-wave condition to decide whether this orbit is allowed, and which $n$ it has.

某模型预测，速率为 $1.10 \times 10^{6}\;\mathrm{m/s}$ 的电子在半径 $2.12 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$ 的圆形轨道上运动。用驻波条件判断这条轨道是否被允许，以及它的 $n$ 是多少。

Step 1: Wavelength and circumference第 1 步：波长与周长
$$\lambda = \frac{h}{mv} = \frac{6.63 \times 10^{-34}}{(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(1.10 \times 10^{6})} = 6.62 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}, \qquad 2\pi r = 2\pi(2.12 \times 10^{-10}) = 1.33 \times 10^{-9}\;\mathrm{m}$$
Step 2: Ratio第 2 步：比值
$$n = \frac{2\pi r}{\lambda} = \frac{1.33 \times 10^{-9}}{6.62 \times 10^{-10}} = 2.01 \approx 2$$
The ratio is a whole number within rounding, so the orbit is allowed and has $n = 2$: the circumference holds two wavelengths. Had the ratio been $2.5$, the wave would not close on itself and the orbit would be forbidden. Why this works: a whole-number ratio is the only way for the electron wave to be a stable standing wave around the nucleus.比值在舍入误差内是整数，所以这条轨道被允许，$n = 2$：周长容纳两个波长。如果比值是 $2.5$，波不能首尾相接，这条轨道就是被禁止的。为什么成立：整数比是电子波在原子核周围形成稳定驻波的唯一方式。

它们是同一种元素，因为质子数（8，氧）相同。它们是不同的同位素，因为核子总数不同：$A = 16$ 和 $A = 18$。

原子核：约 $12\;\mathrm{u} = 12(1.66 \times 10^{-27}) = 1.99 \times 10^{-26}\;\mathrm{kg}$。六个电子：$6(9.11 \times 10^{-31}) = 5.5 \times 10^{-30}\;\mathrm{kg}$，约为核质量的 $0.03\%$。原子的质量主要由原子核决定。

The electron's motion comes from classical ideas: a Coulomb force acting as the net centripetal force on a circular orbit. Energy Transfer by Photons: Absorption and Emission光子传递能量：吸收与发射

Energy is transferred when an atom absorbs or emits a photon. We model the atom as a system consisting of a nucleus and an electron. Its energy states are the allowed energies from Topic 15.2.

原子吸收或发射光子时，会传递能量。我们把原子模型化为由一个原子核和一个电子组成的体系，它的能量状态就是专题 15.2 中的允许能量。

Energy can be absorbed or emitted by the atom only if the amount of energy corresponds to the energy difference between two atomic energy states. A photon of any other energy passes the atom by without being absorbed.

只有当能量的大小对应于原子两个能量状态之间的能量差（energy difference）时，原子才能吸收或发射这份能量。能量为其他值的光子会从原子旁掠过而不被吸收。

  • Absorption: an atom in a given energy state may absorb a photon of the appropriate energy and make a transition to a higher energy state, called an excited state.
  • Emission: an atom in an excited state may emit a photon of the appropriate energy and spontaneously move to a lower energy state.
  • The system view: because the atom is a system of an electron and a nucleus, a change in the energy state corresponds to a change in the interaction energy between the electron and the nucleus.
  • 吸收：处于某一能量状态的原子可以吸收能量合适的光子，跃迁到较高的能量状态，称为激发态（excited state）。
  • 发射：处于激发态的原子可以发射能量合适的光子，自发地跃迁到较低的能量状态。
  • 体系的观点：因为原子是由电子和原子核组成的体系，能量状态的改变对应于电子与原子核之间相互作用能的改变。

A transition between two energy states corresponds to a photon of a single frequency, and therefore a single wavelength, set by the energy difference:

两个能量状态之间的一次跃迁对应于一个单一频率、因而单一波长的光子，由能量差决定：

Photon in a transition跃迁中的光子
$$\Delta E = hf = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$$

$\Delta E = |E_a - E_b|$ is the energy difference between the two states, whose energies are $E_a$ and $E_b$ (eV or J), $f$ is the photon frequency (Hz), $\lambda$ the wavelength (m or nm) and $hc = 1240\;\mathrm{eV\,nm}$. A larger energy gap gives a higher frequency and a shorter wavelength.

$\Delta E = |E_a - E_b|$ 为两个状态之间的能量差，两个状态的能量分别为 $E_a$ 和 $E_b$（eV 或 J），$f$ 为光子频率（Hz），$\lambda$ 为波长（m 或 nm），$hc = 1240\;\mathrm{eV\,nm}$。能量差越大，频率越高，波长越短。

Energy-Level Diagrams of Single-Electron Atoms单电子原子的能级图

An energy-level diagram shows each allowed energy state of an atom as a horizontal line, with higher energy higher on the page. A transition is an arrow between two lines: downward for emission, upward for absorption.

能级图（energy-level diagram）把原子每个允许的能量状态画成一条水平线，能量越高，位置越高。跃迁是两条线之间的箭头：向下表示发射，向上表示吸收。

In AP Physics 2, only energy-level diagrams of single-electron atoms (hydrogen and hydrogen-like ions) are considered. For hydrogen the energies, with zero at the point where the electron is just free, are given by $E_n = -13.6\;\mathrm{eV}/n^2$, so the first levels are $-13.60$, $-3.40$, $-1.51$ and $-0.85\;\mathrm{eV}$ for $n = 1, 2, 3, 4$.

在 AP Physics 2 中，只考虑单电子原子（氢和类氢离子）的能级图。对氢原子，把电子刚好自由时的能量取为零，各能量由 $E_n = -13.6\;\mathrm{eV}/n^2$ 给出，所以 $n = 1, 2, 3, 4$ 的最初几个能级为 $-13.60$、$-3.40$、$-1.51$ 和 $-0.85\;\mathrm{eV}$。

The energies are negative because the electron is bound to the nucleus. The levels crowd together as $n$ increases and approach zero.

能量是负的，因为电子被原子核束缚。随着 $n$ 增大，能级越来越密，并趋近于零。

Spectra Identify Elements光谱可以识别元素

Atoms of each element have a unique set of allowed energy levels, and thereby a unique set of absorption and emission frequencies. This set of frequencies determines the element's spectrum, like a fingerprint. Two kinds of spectrum are used:

每种元素的原子都有一套独一无二的允许能级，因而有一套独一无二的吸收和发射频率。这一组频率决定了元素的光谱（spectrum），就像指纹一样。常用两种光谱：

  • An emission spectrum is the light given off by a hot, glowing gas: it has bright lines at the frequencies of the transitions from excited states to lower states. It can be used to determine the elements in a source of light, such as a star.
  • An absorption spectrum is seen when light with all frequencies passes through a cooler gas: the atoms remove photons at the frequencies they can absorb, so dark lines appear in an otherwise continuous spectrum, at the same frequencies the element emits. It can be used to determine the elements composing a substance by observing what light the substance has absorbed.
  • 发射光谱（emission spectrum）是炽热发光气体发出的光：在从激发态跃迁到较低态的各频率处有亮线。它可以用来确定光源（例如恒星）中含有的元素。
  • 吸收光谱（absorption spectrum）出现在包含所有频率的光穿过较冷气体时：原子把它们能够吸收的频率的光子移走，于是在连续谱中出现暗线，位置与该元素发射的频率相同。通过观察物质吸收了哪些光，可以确定组成该物质的元素。

Binding Energy and Ionization结合能与电离

The binding energy (also called the ionization energy) is the energy required to remove an electron from an atom, which makes the atom ionized. An atom in the lowest energy level, the ground state, requires the greatest amount of energy to remove the electron.

结合能（binding energy，也称电离能）是把一个电子从原子中移走所需的能量，这会使原子变成离子（ionized）。处于最低能级（基态，ground state）的原子，移走电子所需的能量最多。

For hydrogen, ionization from $n = 1$ takes $13.60\;\mathrm{eV}$, from $n = 2$ only $3.40\;\mathrm{eV}$, and from $n = 3$ only $1.51\;\mathrm{eV}$: the binding energy of level $n$ is $|E_n|$.

对氢原子，从 $n = 1$ 电离需要 $13.60\;\mathrm{eV}$，从 $n = 2$ 只需 $3.40\;\mathrm{eV}$，从 $n = 3$ 只需 $1.51\;\mathrm{eV}$：第 $n$ 级的结合能为 $|E_n|$。

A photon with more than this energy can ionize the atom, and the surplus energy appears as the kinetic energy of the freed electron.

能量超过此值的光子可以使原子电离，多出的能量成为被释放电子的动能。

Common Mistake: "Any photon with enough energy is absorbed" A bound atom accepts a photon only if its energy matches a gap between two levels. A $11.0\;\mathrm{eV}$ photon has more than the $10.2\;\mathrm{eV}$ needed to reach $n = 2$ but less than $12.09\;\mathrm{eV}$ needed for $n = 3$, and it matches no gap, so ground-state hydrogen does not absorb it. The only exception is a photon with at least the ionization energy, which can free the electron.
常见错误：“能量够大的光子都会被吸收” 束缚的原子只接受能量恰好等于两能级之间能量差的光子。一个 $11.0\;\mathrm{eV}$ 的光子比到达 $n = 2$ 所需的 $10.2\;\mathrm{eV}$ 多，但少于到达 $n = 3$ 所需的 $12.09\;\mathrm{eV}$，与任何能量差都不匹配，所以基态氢原子不会吸收它。唯一的例外是能量不小于电离能的光子，它可以使电子自由。

Step 1: Energy difference第 1 步：能量差
$$\Delta E = E_3 - E_2 = (-1.51) - (-3.40) = 1.89\;\mathrm{eV}$$
Step 2: Wavelength第 2 步：波长
$$\lambda = \frac{hc}{\Delta E} = \frac{1240\;\mathrm{eV\,nm}}{1.89\;\mathrm{eV}} = 656\;\mathrm{nm}$$

Thermal Radiation and the Blackbody Model热辐射与黑体模型

Matter spontaneously converts some of its internal thermal energy into electromagnetic energy. A stove element glows red, a lamp filament glows white, and even your body emits infrared radiation that a thermal camera can see. This is thermal radiation, and its color and brightness depend on temperature.

物质会自发地把一部分内部热能转化为电磁能。炉子的发热元件发红光，灯丝发白光，连你的身体也发出红外辐射，热像仪可以看到。这就是热辐射（thermal radiation），它的颜色和亮度取决于温度。

To study it, physicists use an idealized model, the blackbody: a body that absorbs all radiation that falls on it. A blackbody reflects nothing, so at room temperature it looks black.

为了研究它，物理学家使用一种理想化模型，即黑体（blackbody）：吸收落在它上面全部辐射的物体。黑体不反射任何辐射，所以在室温下看起来是黑的。

If a blackbody is in equilibrium at a constant temperature, it must in turn emit energy: otherwise it would keep absorbing and its temperature would rise. In equilibrium the energy emitted equals the energy absorbed.

如果黑体在恒定温度下处于平衡（equilibrium），它必须反过来发射能量：否则它会不断吸收，温度会升高。平衡时，发射的能量等于吸收的能量。

The Blackbody Spectrum黑体光谱

A blackbody emits a continuous spectrum, that is, light at every wavelength, not just at a few sharp lines like an atom. The spectrum depends only on its temperature: the material, shape and history of the body do not matter.

黑体发射连续光谱（continuous spectrum），也就是每个波长上都有光，而不像原子那样只在少数尖锐谱线处发光。光谱只取决于物体的温度：物体的材料、形状和经历都无关紧要。

It is usually shown by plotting the intensity per unit wavelength as a function of wavelength. Every such curve rises from zero at short wavelength, reaches a single peak, and falls slowly at long wavelength.

通常用单位波长强度（intensity）随波长变化的曲线来表示。每条这样的曲线都从短波长处的零开始上升，到达唯一的峰值，然后在长波长处缓慢下降。

Raising the temperature has two effects on the whole curve: it rises at every wavelength, so the total radiated intensity increases, and the peak moves to a shorter wavelength. The two curves never cross.

升高温度对整条曲线有两个影响：曲线在每个波长处都升高，所以总辐射强度增大；峰值移向更短的波长。两条曲线永不相交。

Classical Physics Fails: Planck's Law经典物理的失败：普朗克定律

The distribution of intensity in a blackbody's spectrum, as a function of temperature, cannot be modeled using only classical physics concepts. A classical wave model of the radiation predicts that the intensity should keep growing at shorter and shorter wavelengths, which is not what is observed.

黑体光谱的强度分布随温度的变化，无法只用经典物理概念来模拟。辐射的经典波动模型预言强度应在越来越短的波长处不断增大，这与观测不符。

A blackbody's spectrum is described correctly by Planck's law, which assumes that the energy of light is quantized: light of frequency $f$ is exchanged in packets of energy $E = hf$.

普朗克定律（Planck's law）正确地描述了黑体光谱，它假设光的能量是量子化的：频率为 $f$ 的光以能量为 $E = hf$ 的份额交换。

High-frequency packets are large, so a hot body rarely has enough thermal energy to emit them, and the curve falls at short wavelengths just as observed. This was one of the first successes of quantum theory.

高频的份额很大，所以热物体很少有足够的热能发射它们，曲线在短波长处下降，与观测一致。这是量子理论最早的成功之一。

Wien's Law: The Peak Wavelength维恩定律：峰值波长

The peak wavelength emitted by a blackbody, the wavelength at which it emits the greatest amount of radiation per unit wavelength, decreases with increasing temperature, as described by Wien's law. The product of the peak wavelength and the absolute temperature is a constant:

黑体发射的峰值波长，即单位波长发射辐射最多的那个波长，随温度升高而减小，这由维恩定律（Wien's law）描述。峰值波长与热力学温度的乘积是常量：

Wien's law维恩定律
$$\lambda_\mathrm{max} T = 2.90 \times 10^{-3}\;\mathrm{m\,K}$$

$\lambda_\mathrm{max}$ is the peak wavelength (m) and $T$ is the absolute temperature (K). Doubling $T$ halves $\lambda_\mathrm{max}$. Always use kelvin: $T = T_\mathrm{C} + 273$.

$\lambda_\mathrm{max}$ 为峰值波长（m），$T$ 为热力学温度（K）。$T$ 加倍，$\lambda_\mathrm{max}$ 减半。一定要用开尔文：$T = T_\mathrm{C} + 273$。

The Stefan-Boltzmann Law: Radiated Power斯特藩-玻尔兹曼定律：辐射功率

The rate at which energy is emitted, the power, of a blackbody is proportional to the surface area of the body and to the temperature of the body raised to the fourth power, as described by the Stefan-Boltzmann law:

黑体发射能量的速率，即功率，与物体的表面积成正比，并与物体温度的四次方成正比，这由斯特藩-玻尔兹曼定律（Stefan-Boltzmann law）描述：

Stefan-Boltzmann law斯特藩-玻尔兹曼定律
$$P = \sigma A T^4$$

$P$ is the power emitted (W), $\sigma = 5.67 \times 10^{-8}\;\mathrm{W/(m^2\,K^4)}$ is the Stefan-Boltzmann constant, $A$ is the surface area (m²) and $T$ is the absolute temperature (K).

$P$ 为发射功率（W），$\sigma = 5.67 \times 10^{-8}\;\mathrm{W/(m^2\,K^4)}$ 为斯特藩-玻尔兹曼常量，$A$ 为表面积（m²），$T$ 为热力学温度（K）。

The fourth power makes the power very sensitive to temperature: doubling $T$ multiplies $P$ by $2^4 = 16$, while doubling $A$ only doubles $P$. A hot star with a small radius can radiate more than a cool star with a large radius. For a sphere, $A = 4\pi r^2$.

四次方使功率对温度非常敏感：$T$ 加倍，$P$ 变为 $2^4 = 16$ 倍，而 $A$ 加倍只使 $P$ 加倍。半径小的热恒星辐射的功率可能比半径大的冷恒星还多。对球体，$A = 4\pi r^2$。

Common Mistake: Sketching the hotter curve shifted sideways A hotter blackbody does not just move the same curve to shorter wavelengths. Its curve is higher everywhere (more power at every wavelength) and its peak is also at a shorter wavelength. Also, Wien's law gives where the peak is, not how much power is emitted: for the power use $P = \sigma A T^4$.
常见错误：把较热的曲线画成整体左移 较热的黑体并不是把同一条曲线整体移向短波长。它的曲线在每个波长处都更高（每个波长上的功率都更大），同时峰值也在更短的波长处。另外，维恩定律给出的是峰值位置，而不是发射了多少功率：求功率要用 $P = \sigma A T^4$。

The Effect and Its Threshold Frequency光电效应与阈频率

The photoelectric effect is the emission of electrons when electromagnetic radiation is incident upon a photoactive material, for example a clean metal surface. The emitted electrons are called photoelectrons.

光电效应（photoelectric effect）是电磁辐射入射到光敏材料（例如洁净的金属表面）上时发射电子的现象。发射出的电子称为光电子（photoelectrons）。

The emission of electrons requires a minimum frequency of the incident light, called the threshold frequency $f_0$. Light at the threshold frequency or higher induces electron emission, regardless of the number of photons that strike the material. Light below the threshold frequency never does, however bright it is or however long it shines.

发射电子要求入射光有一个最低频率，称为阈频率（threshold frequency）$f_0$。频率达到或高于阈频率的光就会引起电子发射，与照到材料上的光子数无关。频率低于阈频率的光无论多亮、照射多久，都不会引起发射。

Why the Wave Model Fails and Photons Succeed波动模型为何失败而光子模型成功

A classical wave carries energy spread over its wavefront, so brighter light should give each electron more energy, and dim light of any color should eventually free electrons by building up energy. Experiments show otherwise.

经典波携带的能量分布在波前上，所以更亮的光应该给每个电子更多能量，任何颜色的暗光最终也应该通过积累能量把电子释放出来。实验结果恰恰相反。

The energy of the emitted electrons does not depend on the number of photons incident upon the material (the intensity), and no electrons come out below the threshold frequency. This provides evidence that light is a collection of discrete, quantized energy packets called photons.

发射电子的能量与入射到材料上的光子数（即光强）无关，并且低于阈频率时没有电子出来。这为光是由分立的、量子化的能量包（称为光子）组成提供了证据。

Each photon gives all its energy $hf$ to a single electron in one event. A photon below the threshold frequency has too little energy to free an electron, and a second photon cannot help, because electrons do not store energy from one photon until the next arrives.

每个光子在一次事件中把全部能量 $hf$ 交给一个电子。低于阈频率的光子能量太少，不能释放电子，第二个光子也帮不上忙，因为电子不会把一个光子的能量储存起来等下一个光子到来。

So the two ways to change the light have different effects. More photons per second (greater intensity) at a fixed frequency means more photoelectrons per second, which is a larger current, but each electron has the same maximum energy. A higher frequency means more energetic photons, so the electrons have more energy.

所以改变光的两种方式效果不同。频率不变、每秒光子数增多（光强增大），意味着每秒光电子数增多，即电流增大，但每个电子的最大能量不变。频率升高意味着光子能量更大，所以电子的能量更大。

History: Planck, Einstein and Millikan历史：普朗克、爱因斯坦与密立根

In 1900 Max Planck could fit the measured blackbody spectrum only by assuming that energy is exchanged in packets $hf$ (Topic 15.4). In 1905 Albert Einstein went further: light itself travels as quanta, and one quantum frees one electron, so $K_\text{max} = hf - \phi$.

1900 年，马克斯·普朗克（Max Planck）发现，只有假设能量以 $hf$ 的份额交换，才能拟合实测的黑体光谱（专题 15.4）。1905 年，阿尔伯特·爱因斯坦（Albert Einstein）更进一步：光本身就以量子的形式传播，一个量子释放一个电子，所以 $K_\text{max} = hf - \phi$。

Robert Millikan doubted the photon idea, yet his stopping-potential measurements, published in 1916, confirmed the straight line and gave its slope $h$. Einstein's 1921 Nobel Prize cited his law of the photoelectric effect.

罗伯特·密立根（Robert Millikan）曾怀疑光子的观点，但他在 1916 年发表的遏止电势测量结果证实了这条直线，并给出其斜率 $h$。爱因斯坦的 1921 年诺贝尔奖特别提到了他的光电效应定律。

Work Function and Maximum Kinetic Energy逸出功与最大动能

The maximum kinetic energy of an emitted electron is related to the frequency of the incident light and to the work function $\phi$ of the material. The work function is the minimum energy required to emit an electron from atoms in the material.

发射电子的最大动能与入射光的频率以及材料的逸出功（work function）$\phi$ 有关。逸出功是使电子从材料的原子中发射出来所需的最小能量。

A photon delivers $hf$. The electron must spend at least $\phi$ to escape, and the energy left over is its kinetic energy. The most energetic electrons are those that lose only $\phi$:

光子带来 $hf$。电子逃逸至少要付出 $\phi$，剩下的能量就是它的动能。能量最大的电子只损失了 $\phi$：

Maximum kinetic energy of photoelectrons光电子的最大动能
$$K_\text{max} = hf - \phi$$

$K_\text{max}$ is the maximum kinetic energy (J or eV), $hf$ is the photon energy, and $\phi$ is the work function (J or eV). Emission needs $K_\text{max} \ge 0$, so the threshold frequency is $f_0 = \phi/h$.

$K_\text{max}$ 为最大动能（J 或 eV），$hf$ 为光子能量，$\phi$ 为逸出功（J 或 eV）。要发射电子需要 $K_\text{max} \ge 0$，所以阈频率 $f_0 = \phi/h$。

Work functions for materials are given in problems; you are not expected to know their values, or which properties of a material influence the size of its work function. Work in one unit: if $\phi$ is in eV, use $E = hf$ in eV (with $h = 4.14 \times 10^{-15}\;\mathrm{eV\,s}$) or $E = 1240/\lambda$ with $\lambda$ in nm.

材料的逸出功在题目中会给出；你不需要知道它们的数值，也不需要知道材料的哪些性质会影响逸出功的大小。计算时统一单位：如果 $\phi$ 以 eV 为单位，就用以 eV 为单位的 $E = hf$（取 $h = 4.14 \times 10^{-15}\;\mathrm{eV\,s}$），或用 $\lambda$ 以 nm 为单位的 $E = 1240/\lambda$。

First principles: Einstein's equation is energy conservation for one photon第一性原理：爱因斯坦方程就是单个光子的能量守恒

Principle. Conservation of energy for the system of one photon and one electron, with the photon absorbed whole.

原理。对一个光子和一个电子组成的体系应用能量守恒，光子被整个吸收。

  1. Energy in: the photon gives its whole energy $hf$ to one electron.
  2. Energy out: escaping costs at least $\phi$, so the least-bound electrons leave with $K_\text{max} = hf - \phi$.
  3. Stopping: the field does work $-eV_\text{stop}$ on the fastest electron and stops it, so $eV_\text{stop} = K_\text{max}$ and $V_\text{stop} = (h/e)f - \phi/e$.
  1. 输入的能量：光子把全部能量 $hf$ 交给一个电子。
  2. 输出的能量：逃逸至少要付出 $\phi$，所以束缚最弱的电子以 $K_\text{max} = hf - \phi$ 离开。
  3. 遏止：电场对最快的电子做功 $-eV_\text{stop}$ 使其停下，所以 $eV_\text{stop} = K_\text{max}$，$V_\text{stop} = (h/e)f - \phi/e$。

Limiting check. At $f = f_0 = \phi/h$, $K_\text{max} = 0$: electrons only just escape. Below $f_0$ the equation would give $K_\text{max} \lt 0$, which is impossible, so no electrons leave. Intensity appears nowhere in the chain, so it cannot change $K_\text{max}$.

极限检验。$f = f_0 = \phi/h$ 时 $K_\text{max} = 0$：电子刚好能逃逸。低于 $f_0$ 时方程会给出 $K_\text{max} \lt 0$，这不可能，所以没有电子离开。整个推导中没有出现光强，所以光强不能改变 $K_\text{max}$。

The Stopping-Potential Experiment遏止电势实验

In a typical experiment to demonstrate the photoelectric effect and to determine the work function of a metal, two metal plates are placed in a vacuum chamber, so that the electrons do not collide with air molecules, and are connected to a variable source of potential difference.

在演示光电效应并测定金属逸出功的典型实验中，两块金属板放在真空（vacuum）腔内，使电子不会与空气分子碰撞，并与一个可变的电势差电源相连。

One plate is illuminated by monochromatic light (a single frequency), which ejects electrons. The potential difference between the plates is adjusted, with the collecting plate made negative relative to the emitting plate, until no current is measured in the circuit.

其中一块板被单色光（monochromatic，单一频率）照射，电子被打出。调节两板间的电势差（使收集板相对发射板为负），直到电路中测不到电流为止。

That value is the stopping potential $V_\text{stop}$. Even the most energetic electron is then turned back before it reaches the collecting plate, so its kinetic energy has all been converted to electric potential energy:

这个值就是遏止电势（stopping potential）$V_\text{stop}$。此时连最有能量的电子也在到达收集板之前被折回，它的动能已经全部转化为电势能：

Stopping potential遏止电势
$$eV_\text{stop} = K_\text{max} = hf - \phi$$

$e = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\;\mathrm{C}$ is the elementary charge and $V_\text{stop}$ is in volts. If $K_\text{max}$ is in eV, $V_\text{stop}$ in volts has the same numerical value.

$e = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\;\mathrm{C}$ 为元电荷，$V_\text{stop}$ 的单位为伏。若 $K_\text{max}$ 以 eV 为单位，则以伏为单位的 $V_\text{stop}$ 数值与之相同。

The Graph of $K_\text{max}$ against Frequency$K_\text{max}$ 对频率的图像

Rewrite $K_\text{max} = hf - \phi$ in the form $y = mx + b$: with $y = K_\text{max}$ and $x = f$, the slope is $h$ and the vertical intercept is $-\phi$.

把 $K_\text{max} = hf - \phi$ 写成 $y = mx + b$ 的形式：取 $y = K_\text{max}$、$x = f$，斜率为 $h$，纵轴截距为 $-\phi$。

Measuring $V_\text{stop}$ at several frequencies and plotting $eV_\text{stop}$ against $f$ gives a straight line whose slope is Planck's constant for any metal. The line meets the frequency axis at $f_0$. Using different metals gives parallel lines that cross the axis at different thresholds.

在若干频率下测量 $V_\text{stop}$，并用 $eV_\text{stop}$ 对 $f$ 作图，得到一条直线，对任何金属其斜率都是普朗克常量。直线与频率轴相交于 $f_0$。换用不同金属得到的是相互平行、与频率轴交点（阈值）不同的直线。

Common Mistake: "Brighter light gives faster electrons" Intensity changes how many photons arrive each second, so it changes how many photoelectrons are emitted each second (the current), but not the maximum kinetic energy of each. The maximum kinetic energy, and the stopping potential, change only when the frequency changes (or the metal does). Below the threshold frequency, no intensity is enough.
常见错误：“更亮的光使电子更快” 光强改变的是每秒到达的光子数，因此改变每秒发射的光电子数（电流），但不改变每个电子的最大动能。最大动能和遏止电势只在频率改变（或金属改变）时才变化。低于阈频率时，无论多强的光都不够。

Step 1: Photon energy and $K_\text{max}$第 1 步：光子能量与 $K_\text{max}$
$$E = \frac{1240}{300} = 4.13\;\mathrm{eV}, \qquad K_\text{max} = 4.13 - 2.30 = 1.83\;\mathrm{eV}$$
Step 2: Speed of the fastest electrons第 2 步：最快电子的速率
$$K_\text{max} = (1.83)(1.60 \times 10^{-19}) = 2.93 \times 10^{-19}\;\mathrm{J}, \qquad v = \sqrt{\frac{2K_\text{max}}{m}} = 8.02 \times 10^{5}\;\mathrm{m/s}$$
Step 3: Stopping potential第 3 步：遏止电势
$$V_\text{stop} = \frac{K_\text{max}}{e} = 1.83\;\mathrm{V}$$
Step 4: Threshold第 4 步：阈值
$$f_0 = \frac{\phi}{h} = \frac{2.30}{4.14 \times 10^{-15}} = 5.56 \times 10^{14}\;\mathrm{Hz}, \qquad \lambda_0 = \frac{1240}{2.30} = 539\;\mathrm{nm}$$
The $300\;\mathrm{nm}$ light has a shorter wavelength than $539\;\mathrm{nm}$, so it is above threshold. Why this works: a photon of $4.13\;\mathrm{eV}$ pays $2.30\;\mathrm{eV}$ to free the electron, and the remainder is kinetic energy.$300\;\mathrm{nm}$ 的光波长比 $539\;\mathrm{nm}$ 短，所以高于阈值。为什么成立：一个 $4.13\;\mathrm{eV}$ 的光子付出 $2.30\;\mathrm{eV}$ 使电子逸出，其余成为动能。

The Compton Effect康普顿效应

In Compton scattering, a photon interacts with a free electron (one that is not bound to an atom, or bound so loosely that the binding energy is negligible, and initially at rest).

在康普顿散射（Compton scattering）中，光子与一个自由电子（不受原子束缚，或束缚极弱以至于束缚能可以忽略，且初始静止）相互作用。

The Compton effect is that the photon that emerges from the interaction has a lower energy and a longer wavelength than the incoming photon. The magnitude of the change is related to the direction of the photon after the collision: the more the photon is deflected, the larger the change.

康普顿效应是指：从相互作用中出射的光子，能量比入射光子低、波长比入射光子长。变化的大小与碰撞后光子的方向有关：光子偏转得越多，变化越大。

The lost energy has gone to the electron, which recoils.

损失的能量交给了电子，电子被反冲出去。

Evidence That Light Is Made of Photons光由光子组成的证据

Compton scattering provides evidence that light is a collection of discrete, quantized energy packets called photons. A classical wave would drive the electron to oscillate and re-radiate at the same wavelength, so no shift would appear.

康普顿散射为光是由分立的、量子化的能量包（称为光子）组成提供了证据。经典的波会使电子振动并以相同的波长再辐射，所以不会出现波长改变。

The measured shift is explained by treating a photon as a particle and applying conservation of energy and conservation of momentum to the collision between the photon and the electron.

测得的波长改变，可以通过把光子看作粒子，并对光子与电子的碰撞应用能量守恒（conservation of energy）和动量守恒（conservation of momentum）来解释。

The transfer of a photon's energy to an electron results in the energy, momentum, frequency and wavelength of the photon changing: $E$ and $p$ and $f$ decrease and $\lambda$ increases.

光子能量转移给电子，使光子的能量、动量、频率和波长发生变化：$E$、$p$ 和 $f$ 减小，$\lambda$ 增大。

For this to work, the photon needs a momentum. It is given by the same relation as the de Broglie wavelength (Topic 15.1), and it is also $E/c$:

要做到这一点，光子必须有动量。它由与德布罗意波长（专题 15.1）相同的关系给出，也等于 $E/c$：

Photon energy and photon momentum光子能量与光子动量
$$E = hf = \frac{hc}{\lambda} \qquad p = \frac{h}{\lambda} = \frac{E}{c}$$

$E$ is the photon energy (J or eV), $p$ the photon momentum (kg m/s), $\lambda$ the wavelength (m), $h = 6.63 \times 10^{-34}\;\mathrm{J\,s}$ and $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\;\mathrm{m/s}$. The momentum points along the direction of travel.

$E$ 为光子能量（J 或 eV），$p$ 为光子动量（kg m/s），$\lambda$ 为波长（m），$h = 6.63 \times 10^{-34}\;\mathrm{J\,s}$，$c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\;\mathrm{m/s}$。动量沿传播方向。

Conservation Laws in Two Dimensions二维情形下的守恒定律

AP Physics 2 includes full quantitative and qualitative treatments of conservation of momentum in two dimensions, so the collision is analyzed with components.

AP Physics 2 包括对二维动量守恒的完整定量和定性处理，所以要用分量来分析这次碰撞。

Let the incoming photon travel along $+x$ with wavelength $\lambda$, hit an electron at rest, and let the photon leave at angle $\theta$ above the axis with wavelength $\lambda'$, while the electron recoils with momentum $p_e$ at angle $\varphi$ below the axis.

设入射光子沿 $+x$ 传播、波长为 $\lambda$，撞上静止的电子；散射光子以与轴成 $\theta$ 角向上射出，波长为 $\lambda'$，电子以动量 $p_e$ 沿轴下方 $\varphi$ 角反冲。

Momentum is a vector, so each component is conserved separately, and energy is conserved as a scalar:

动量是矢量，所以每个分量分别守恒，而能量作为标量守恒：

Conservation equations for the collision碰撞的守恒方程
$$\frac{hc}{\lambda} = \frac{hc}{\lambda'} + K_e \qquad x:\; \frac{h}{\lambda} = \frac{h}{\lambda'}\cos\theta + p_e\cos\varphi \qquad y:\; 0 = \frac{h}{\lambda'}\sin\theta - p_e\sin\varphi$$

$K_e$ is the kinetic energy given to the electron, $\theta$ is the photon's scattering angle and $\varphi$ is the electron's recoil angle. The electron starts at rest, so it has no initial momentum or kinetic energy.

$K_e$ 为电子得到的动能，$\theta$ 为光子的散射角，$\varphi$ 为电子的反冲角。电子起初静止，所以初始动量和动能都为零。

The Wavelength Shift and the Scattering Angle波长改变量与散射角

Solving the conservation laws for the change in wavelength gives a result that depends only on the scattering angle. The change in wavelength experienced by a photon after colliding with an electron is related to how much the photon's direction changes:

对守恒定律求解波长的改变量，得到的结果只取决于散射角。光子与电子碰撞后波长的改变量，与光子方向改变了多少有关：

Compton wavelength shift康普顿波长改变量
$$\Delta\lambda = \lambda' - \lambda = \frac{h}{m_e c}\left(1 - \cos\theta\right)$$

$\Delta\lambda$ is the increase in wavelength (m), $\theta$ is the scattering angle of the photon, $m_e = 9.11 \times 10^{-31}\;\mathrm{kg}$ and $h/(m_e c) = 2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$.

$\Delta\lambda$ 为波长的增加量（m），$\theta$ 为光子的散射角，$m_e = 9.11 \times 10^{-31}\;\mathrm{kg}$，$h/(m_e c) = 2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$。

Read the formula. At $\theta = 0$ (no deflection) $\Delta\lambda = 0$. At $\theta = 90^\circ$, $\cos\theta = 0$ and $\Delta\lambda = 2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$. At $\theta = 180^\circ$ (the photon bounces straight back), $\Delta\lambda$ is largest, $4.86 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$.

来读一读这个公式。$\theta = 0$（不偏转）时 $\Delta\lambda = 0$。$\theta = 90^\circ$ 时，$\cos\theta = 0$，$\Delta\lambda = 2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$。$\theta = 180^\circ$（光子被直接弹回）时，$\Delta\lambda$ 最大，为 $4.86 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$。

The shift does not depend on the incoming wavelength, so it is only noticeable when $\lambda$ is small: for X-rays near $10^{-11}\;\mathrm{m}$ the shift is a large fraction of $\lambda$, while for visible light near $5 \times 10^{-7}\;\mathrm{m}$ it is about $10^{5}$ times smaller than $\lambda$ and cannot be seen.

改变量与入射波长无关，所以只有 $\lambda$ 很小时才明显：对接近 $10^{-11}\;\mathrm{m}$ 的 X 射线，改变量占 $\lambda$ 的很大一部分；对接近 $5 \times 10^{-7}\;\mathrm{m}$ 的可见光，改变量比 $\lambda$ 小约 $10^{5}$ 倍，看不出来。

Common Mistake: Saying the photon "slows down" The scattered photon still travels at $c$ in free space. What changes is its energy, frequency and momentum, and its wavelength gets longer. Do not mix this up with a particle losing speed. Also, do not add the photon's energy in one unit and the electron's in another: convert to eV or to J consistently.
常见错误：说光子“减速”了 散射后的光子在自由空间中仍以 $c$ 传播。改变的是它的能量、频率和动量，波长变长。不要把这与粒子减速混为一谈。另外，不要把光子的能量与电子的能量用不同单位相加：要统一换算成 eV 或 J。
Worked Example: X-ray Scattered through 90 Degrees例题：X 射线散射 90 度

An X-ray photon of wavelength $0.0500\;\mathrm{nm}$ scatters from a free electron at rest and leaves at $90^\circ$ to its original direction. Find the new wavelength, the photon energy before and after, and the kinetic energy of the electron.

波长 $0.0500\;\mathrm{nm}$ 的 X 射线光子被静止的自由电子散射，出射方向与原方向成 $90^\circ$。求新的波长、散射前后光子的能量，以及电子的动能。

Step 1: Wavelength shift第 1 步：波长改变量
$$\Delta\lambda = (2.43 \times 10^{-12})(1 - \cos 90^\circ) = 2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}, \qquad \lambda' = 5.00 \times 10^{-11} + 0.243 \times 10^{-11} = 5.243 \times 10^{-11}\;\mathrm{m}$$
Step 2: Photon energies第 2 步：光子能量
$$E = \frac{1240}{0.0500} = 24\,800\;\mathrm{eV}, \qquad E' = \frac{1240}{0.05243} = 23\,650\;\mathrm{eV}$$
Step 3: Energy given to the electron第 3 步：电子得到的能量
$$K_e = E - E' = 24\,800 - 23\,650 = 1150\;\mathrm{eV}$$
The photon lost about $4.6\%$ of its energy, and the electron recoils with $1.15\;\mathrm{keV}$. Why this works: the total energy is conserved, so the energy the photon lost is exactly the kinetic energy the electron gained.光子损失了约 $4.6\%$ 的能量，电子以 $1.15\;\mathrm{keV}$ 反冲。为什么成立：总能量守恒，所以光子损失的能量恰好等于电子得到的动能。
Worked Example: Momentum Components of the Recoil例题：反冲电子的动量分量

For the collision in the previous example, find the magnitude and direction of the electron's momentum using conservation of momentum, and check that its kinetic energy agrees with the energy lost by the photon.

The minus sign means the electron moves downward, below the axis, opposite to the scattered photon's vertical momentum.负号表示电子向下运动，位于轴的下方，与散射光子的竖直动量方向相反。

The Strong Force and Conservation Laws强力与守恒定律

A nucleus contains protons that repel each other electrically, yet it holds together.

原子核中的质子之间有电斥力，核却能结合在一起。

The reason is the strong force, which is exerted at nuclear scales (about $10^{-15}\;\mathrm{m}$) and dominates the interactions of nucleons (protons and neutrons): it attracts any two nucleons that are very close and is much stronger than the electric repulsion at that distance. At larger separations it has no noticeable effect.

原因是强力（strong force）：它作用于核的尺度（约 $10^{-15}\;\mathrm{m}$），主导核子（质子和中子）之间的相互作用：它吸引任何靠得很近的两个核子，在这个距离上比电斥力强得多。距离更大时它没有明显作用。

Possible nuclear reactions are constrained by the law of conservation of nucleon number: the total number of protons and neutrons (the mass number $A$) is the same before and after.

可能发生的核反应受核子数守恒定律制约：质子和中子的总数（质量数 $A$）在反应前后相同（nucleon number is conserved）。

Electric charge is also conserved, so the sum of the atomic numbers $Z$ is unchanged. The behavior of the particles in a reaction is constrained, too, by conservation of energy (including mass-energy equivalence) and conservation of momentum.

电荷也守恒，所以原子序数 $Z$ 之和不变。反应中粒子的行为还受能量守恒（包括质能等价）和动量守恒的制约。

Mass-Energy Equivalence质能等价

In all nuclear reactions, mass and energy may be exchanged because of mass-energy equivalence. A system with a rest mass $m$ has an energy $mc^2$, so if the products have less total mass than the reactants, the difference, the mass defect $\Delta m$, appears as released energy.

在所有核反应中，由于质能等价，质量和能量可以相互转换。静质量为 $m$ 的体系具有能量 $mc^2$，所以如果产物的总质量小于反应物，其差，即质量亏损（mass defect）$\Delta m$，就以释放能量的形式出现。

Because $c^2$ is huge, a tiny mass change gives a large energy: nuclear reactions release millions of times more energy per atom than chemical ones.

由于 $c^2$ 极大，很小的质量变化就对应很大的能量：每个原子的核反应释放的能量比化学反应大几百万倍。

Mass-energy equivalence质能等价
$$E = mc^2 \qquad \Delta E = \Delta m\,c^2 \qquad 1\;\mathrm{u} = 931\;\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$$

$E$ is the energy (J), $m$ the mass (kg) and $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\;\mathrm{m/s}$. With masses in atomic mass units, $\Delta E\;(\mathrm{MeV}) = 931 \times \Delta m\;(\mathrm{u})$, where $1\;\mathrm{u} = 1.66 \times 10^{-27}\;\mathrm{kg} = 931\;\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$.

$E$ 为能量（J），$m$ 为质量（kg），$c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}\;\mathrm{m/s}$。质量以原子质量单位给出时，$\Delta E\;(\mathrm{MeV}) = 931 \times \Delta m\;(\mathrm{u})$，其中 $1\;\mathrm{u} = 1.66 \times 10^{-27}\;\mathrm{kg} = 931\;\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$。

The energy that is released in nuclear processes appears as the kinetic energy of the products, or as photons (gamma rays), or as both.

核过程中释放的能量以产物的动能、光子（伽马射线）或二者兼有的形式出现。

Conservation of momentum decides how the kinetic energy is shared. If a nucleus at rest splits into two products, they move in opposite directions with equal momentum magnitudes, so the lighter product gets the larger share of the kinetic energy ($K = p^2/2m$).

动量守恒决定动能如何分配。若静止的核分裂成两个产物，它们沿相反方向运动，动量大小相等，所以质量较小的产物得到较大份额的动能（$K = p^2/2m$）。

Fusion, Fission and Binding Energy聚变、裂变与结合能

Nuclear fusion is the process by which two or more smaller (lighter) nuclei combine to form a larger nucleus, as well as subatomic particles. Nuclear fission is the process by which the nucleus of an atom splits into two or more smaller nuclei, as well as subatomic particles.

核聚变（nuclear fusion）是两个或多个较小（较轻）的核结合成一个较大的核，同时产生亚原子粒子的过程。核裂变（nuclear fission）是一个原子核分裂成两个或多个较小的核，同时产生亚原子粒子的过程。

Which processes release energy depends on the binding energy of the nuclei: the energy needed to separate a nucleus into its individual nucleons.

哪种过程释放能量，取决于核的结合能（binding energy）：把一个核拆分为单个核子所需的能量。

It equals the mass defect of the nucleus times $c^2$, where the mass defect is the mass of the separate nucleons minus the mass of the nucleus. The binding energy per nucleon is greatest for nuclei of intermediate mass (near iron).

它等于该核的质量亏损乘以 $c^2$，其中质量亏损是分离核子的质量减去该核的质量。中等质量的核（铁附近）每个核子的结合能最大。

  • Fusion of light nuclei gives a more tightly bound nucleus, so the products have less mass and energy is released.
  • Fission of a heavy nucleus into two medium-mass nuclei also gives more tightly bound products and releases energy: it may occur spontaneously.
  • If the products are less tightly bound than the starting nucleus, the process needs an energy input. Nuclear fission may occur spontaneously or may require an energy input, depending on the binding energy of the nucleus.
  • 轻核聚变得到结合得更紧的核，所以产物质量更小，释放能量。
  • 重核裂变为两个中等质量的核，产物也结合得更紧，同样释放能量：它可以自发发生。
  • 如果产物比起始核结合得更松，该过程就需要输入能量。核裂变可能自发发生，也可能需要输入能量，取决于该核的结合能。

Radioactive Decay and Half-Life放射性衰变与半衰期

Radioactive decay is the spontaneous transformation of a nucleus into one or more different nuclei, or to a lower energy level of the same nucleus. "Spontaneous" means that nothing triggers it.

放射性衰变（radioactive decay）是原子核自发地转变为一个或多个不同的核，或转变为同一个核的较低能级。“自发”（spontaneous）意味着没有任何东西触发它。

The time at which an individual nucleus undergoes decay is indeterminable: you cannot predict when a particular nucleus will decay. But for a large sample, the decay rates can be described using probability, like a very large number of coin flips.

单个原子核发生衰变的时刻是不可确定的：你无法预测某一个特定的核何时衰变。但对于大样品，衰变速率可以用概率（probability）来描述，就像抛大量硬币一样。

Each nucleus has the same fixed chance to decay in any short interval, whatever its age, so the sample as a whole follows a predictable pattern.

每个核在任何短时间内衰变的机会都相同且固定，与它的“年龄”无关，所以样品整体遵循可预测的规律。

The half-life, $t_{1/2}$, of a radioactive material is the time it takes for half of the initial number of radioactive nuclei to have spontaneously decayed. After one half-life, $\tfrac{1}{2}$ remain; after two, $\tfrac{1}{4}$; after three, $\tfrac{1}{8}$, and so on, with each half-life halving what is left, never reaching exactly zero.

放射性物质的半衰期（half-life）$t_{1/2}$ 是起初的放射性核有一半自发衰变所需的时间。经过一个半衰期，剩下 $\tfrac{1}{2}$；两个，剩下 $\tfrac{1}{4}$；三个，剩下 $\tfrac{1}{8}$，依此类推，每个半衰期都使剩余的量减半，永远不会恰好为零。

Different unstable isotopes can have vastly different half-lives, ranging from fractions of a second to billions of years; problems give you the half-life.

不同的不稳定同位素的半衰期可以相差悬殊，从不到一秒到数十亿年；题目会给出半衰期。

The Decay Constant and the Decay Equations衰变常量与衰变方程

The decay constant $\lambda$ measures how quickly a material decays: it is the chance per unit time that any one nucleus decays. A large $\lambda$ means a short half-life.

衰变常量（decay constant）$\lambda$ 衡量物质衰变的快慢：它是任何一个核在单位时间内衰变的机会。$\lambda$ 越大，半衰期越短。

A material's decay constant may be used to predict the number of nuclei remaining in a sample after a period of time, or the age of a material if the initial amount is known. The two are related by $\lambda = \ln 2 / t_{1/2}$, where $\ln 2 = 0.693$:

利用物质的衰变常量，可以预测经过一段时间后样品中剩余的核数，或在已知初始量时推算物质的年龄。二者的关系是 $\lambda = \ln 2 / t_{1/2}$，其中 $\ln 2 = 0.693$：

Decay constant and nuclei remaining衰变常量与剩余核数
$$\lambda = \frac{\ln 2}{t_{1/2}} = \frac{0.693}{t_{1/2}} \qquad N = N_0\,e^{-\lambda t} = N_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{t/t_{1/2}}$$

$N$ is the number of undecayed nuclei at time $t$, $N_0$ the initial number, $\lambda$ the decay constant (1/s, 1/h or 1/yr) and $t_{1/2}$ the half-life. Use the same time unit for $t$, $t_{1/2}$ and $1/\lambda$. The two forms of $N$ are the same: $e^{-\lambda t} = e^{-(\ln 2)\,t/t_{1/2}} = (1/2)^{t/t_{1/2}}$.

$N$ 为 $t$ 时刻未衰变的核数，$N_0$ 为初始核数，$\lambda$ 为衰变常量（1/s、1/h 或 1/yr），$t_{1/2}$ 为半衰期。$t$、$t_{1/2}$ 和 $1/\lambda$ 要用同一时间单位。$N$ 的两种形式是相同的：$e^{-\lambda t} = e^{-(\ln 2)\,t/t_{1/2}} = (1/2)^{t/t_{1/2}}$。

To find an age, rearrange: with the fraction $N/N_0$ left, the number of half-lives elapsed is $n = \ln(N_0/N)/\ln 2$ and $t = n\,t_{1/2}$. A fraction of exactly $1/2^n$ gives a whole number of half-lives; other fractions need a calculator.

求年龄时重新整理：剩余分数为 $N/N_0$ 时，经过的半衰期个数为 $n = \ln(N_0/N)/\ln 2$，$t = n\,t_{1/2}$。分数恰为 $1/2^n$ 时，半衰期个数是整数；其他分数要用计算器。

First principles: exponential decay from a constant chance per unit time第一性原理：由单位时间内恒定的衰变机会得出指数衰变

Principle. Each nucleus has the same fixed chance of decaying per unit time, whatever its age. So in equal time intervals the same fraction of the remaining nuclei decays.

原理。每个核在单位时间内衰变的机会相同且固定，与它的“年龄”无关。所以在相等的时间间隔内，剩余核中衰变的比例相同。

  1. Over one half-life the surviving fraction is $\tfrac{1}{2}$, by the definition of $t_{1/2}$.
  2. The chance does not depend on age, so each later half-life multiplies by $\tfrac{1}{2}$ again: $N = N_0 (1/2)^{t/t_{1/2}}$.
  3. Write $\tfrac{1}{2} = e^{-\ln 2}$: $N = N_0 e^{-(\ln 2/t_{1/2})\,t} = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$ with $\lambda = \ln 2/t_{1/2}$.
  1. 由 $t_{1/2}$ 的定义，经过一个半衰期，剩余比例为 $\tfrac{1}{2}$。
  2. 衰变机会与年龄无关，所以之后每个半衰期再乘以 $\tfrac{1}{2}$：$N = N_0 (1/2)^{t/t_{1/2}}$。
  3. 写成 $\tfrac{1}{2} = e^{-\ln 2}$：$N = N_0 e^{-(\ln 2/t_{1/2})\,t} = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$，其中 $\lambda = \ln 2/t_{1/2}$。

Limiting check. At $t = 0$, $N = N_0$. As $t \to \infty$, $N \to 0$ but never reaches it. A non-whole number of half-lives also works: after $1.5\,t_{1/2}$, $N/N_0 = 0.354$, between $\tfrac{1}{2}$ and $\tfrac{1}{4}$ as it must be.

极限检验。$t = 0$ 时 $N = N_0$。$t \to \infty$ 时 $N \to 0$，但永远达不到零。非整数个半衰期同样适用：经过 $1.5\,t_{1/2}$ 后 $N/N_0 = 0.354$，正好介于 $\tfrac{1}{2}$ 与 $\tfrac{1}{4}$ 之间。

Common Mistake: "After two half-lives, everything has decayed" Each half-life removes half of what is left, not half of the original. After two half-lives, one quarter remains, and three quarters have decayed. Also, the half-life describes a large sample: it does not tell you when one particular nucleus will decay, and a nucleus that has survived for a long time is no more likely to decay next.
常见错误：“两个半衰期后全部衰变完” 每个半衰期去掉的是剩余部分的一半，而不是原来的一半。两个半衰期后剩下四分之一，已衰变四分之三。另外，半衰期描述的是大样品：它并不能告诉你某一个特定的核何时衰变，而且已经存活了很久的核并不会更容易接着衰变。
Worked Example: Energy Released in Deuterium-Tritium Fusion例题：氘氚聚变释放的能量

The fusion reaction ${}^{2}_{1}\mathrm{H} + {}^{3}_{1}\mathrm{H} \to {}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He} + {}^{1}_{0}\mathrm{n}$ has atomic masses $2.014102\;\mathrm{u}$, $3.016049\;\mathrm{u}$, $4.002603\;\mathrm{u}$ and $1.008665\;\mathrm{u}$. Check that nucleon number and charge are conserved, find the energy released, and say how it is shared between the helium nucleus and the neutron if the reactants start with negligible momentum.

聚变反应 ${}^{2}_{1}\mathrm{H} + {}^{3}_{1}\mathrm{H} \to {}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He} + {}^{1}_{0}\mathrm{n}$ 中各原子质量分别为 $2.014102\;\mathrm{u}$、$3.016049\;\mathrm{u}$、$4.002603\;\mathrm{u}$ 和 $1.008665\;\mathrm{u}$。检验核子数和电荷是否守恒，求释放的能量，并说明若反应物起初动量可以忽略，能量如何在氦核与中子之间分配。

Step 1: Check the conservation of nucleon number and charge第 1 步：检验核子数和电荷守恒
$$A:\; 2 + 3 = 5 = 4 + 1 \qquad Z:\; 1 + 1 = 2 = 2 + 0$$
Step 2: Mass defect and energy第 2 步：质量亏损与能量
$$\Delta m = (2.014102 + 3.016049) - (4.002603 + 1.008665) = 0.018883\;\mathrm{u}, \qquad \Delta E = (0.018883)(931) = 17.6\;\mathrm{MeV}$$
Step 3: Sharing the kinetic energy第 3 步：动能的分配
$$p_\mathrm{He} = p_\mathrm{n} \Rightarrow \frac{K_\mathrm{n}}{K_\mathrm{He}} = \frac{m_\mathrm{He}}{m_\mathrm{n}} = 3.97 \;\Rightarrow\; K_\mathrm{n} = 14.0\;\mathrm{MeV}, \quad K_\mathrm{He} = 3.5\;\mathrm{MeV}$$
The reaction releases $17.6\;\mathrm{MeV}$, mostly as the kinetic energy of the light neutron. Why this works: equal and opposite momenta give $K = p^2/2m$ inversely proportional to mass, so the lighter product carries most of the energy.反应释放 $17.6\;\mathrm{MeV}$，大部分成为轻的中子的动能。为什么成立：动量大小相等、方向相反，$K = p^2/2m$ 与质量成反比，所以较轻的产物带走大部分能量。
Worked Example: Fission of Uranium-235例题：铀-235 的裂变

A uranium-235 nucleus absorbs a neutron and splits: ${}^{235}_{92}\mathrm{U} + {}^{1}_{0}\mathrm{n} \to {}^{141}_{56}\mathrm{Ba} + {}^{92}_{36}\mathrm{Kr} + 3\,{}^{1}_{0}\mathrm{n}$. The atomic masses are $235.043930$, $1.008665$, $140.914411$ and $91.926156\;\mathrm{u}$. Find the energy released per fission.

铀-235 核吸收一个中子后分裂：${}^{235}_{92}\mathrm{U} + {}^{1}_{0}\mathrm{n} \to {}^{141}_{56}\mathrm{Ba} + {}^{92}_{36}\mathrm{Kr} + 3\,{}^{1}_{0}\mathrm{n}$。各原子质量为 $235.043930$、$1.008665$、$140.914411$ 和 $91.926156\;\mathrm{u}$。求每次裂变释放的能量。

Step 1: Check the equation第 1 步：检验方程
$$A:\; 235 + 1 = 236 = 141 + 92 + 3 \qquad Z:\; 92 + 0 = 92 = 56 + 36 + 0$$
Step 2: Mass before and after第 2 步：反应前后的质量
$$m_i = 235.043930 + 1.008665 = 236.052595\;\mathrm{u}, \qquad m_f = 140.914411 + 91.926156 + 3(1.008665) = 235.866562\;\mathrm{u}$$
Here $m_i$ is the total mass before the reaction and $m_f$ is the total mass after it.这里 $m_i$ 是反应前的总质量，$m_f$ 是反应后的总质量。
Step 3: Energy第 3 步：能量
$$\Delta m = m_i - m_f = 0.186033\;\mathrm{u}, \qquad \Delta E = (0.186033)(931) = 173\;\mathrm{MeV}$$
About $173\;\mathrm{MeV}$ is released, mainly as kinetic energy of the fragments. Why this works: the products are more tightly bound, so their total mass is smaller, and the missing mass appears as energy. The extra neutrons can go on to cause further fission.约释放 $173\;\mathrm{MeV}$，主要成为碎片的动能。为什么成立：产物结合得更紧，所以总质量更小，缺失的质量以能量形式出现。多出的中子还可以引起进一步的裂变。
Worked Example: Binding Energy of Helium-4例题：氦-4 的结合能

Using atomic masses, ${}^{1}_{1}\mathrm{H} = 1.007825\;\mathrm{u}$, neutron $= 1.008665\;\mathrm{u}$ and ${}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He} = 4.002603\;\mathrm{u}$, find the binding energy of helium-4 and the binding energy per nucleon.

用原子质量 ${}^{1}_{1}\mathrm{H} = 1.007825\;\mathrm{u}$、中子 $= 1.008665\;\mathrm{u}$ 和 ${}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He} = 4.002603\;\mathrm{u}$，求氦-4 的结合能以及每个核子的结合能。

Step 1: Mass defect第 1 步：质量亏损
$$\Delta m = 2(1.007825) + 2(1.008665) - 4.002603 = 0.030377\;\mathrm{u}$$
Step 2: Binding energy第 2 步：结合能
$$E_b = (0.030377)(931) = 28.3\;\mathrm{MeV}, \qquad \frac{E_b}{A} = \frac{28.3}{4} = 7.07\;\mathrm{MeV}$$
The ratio $E_b/A$ is the binding energy per nucleon. It takes $28.3\;\mathrm{MeV}$ to pull the helium nucleus apart into 2 protons and 2 neutrons, and the same amount is released when they come together. Using hydrogen atoms (not bare protons) makes the electron masses cancel. Why this works: the whole nucleus is lighter than its separate nucleons, and that missing mass is the binding energy.比值 $E_b/A$ 是每个核子的结合能。把氦核拆成 2 个质子和 2 个中子需要 $28.3\;\mathrm{MeV}$，它们结合时释放同样多的能量。用氢原子（而不是裸质子）使电子质量互相抵消。为什么成立：整个核比分开的核子轻，缺失的那部分质量就是结合能。
Worked Example: Nuclei Remaining and the Age of a Sample例题：剩余核数与样品年龄

A sample starts with $N_0 = 8.0 \times 10^{20}$ nuclei of an isotope whose half-life is $12.0\;\mathrm{h}$. Find the number left after $36\;\mathrm{h}$ and after $20\;\mathrm{h}$. A different sample of an isotope with half-life $5730\;\mathrm{yr}$ has $30\%$ of its original nuclei left: how old is it?

一份样品起初有 $N_0 = 8.0 \times 10^{20}$ 个某同位素的核，半衰期为 $12.0\;\mathrm{h}$。求 $36\;\mathrm{h}$ 后和 $20\;\mathrm{h}$ 后剩下的核数。另一份半衰期为 $5730\;\mathrm{yr}$ 的同位素样品剩下原来核数的 $30\%$：它有多老？

Step 1: A whole number of half-lives第 1 步：整数个半衰期
$$\frac{36}{12.0} = 3 \;\Rightarrow\; N = N_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{8.0 \times 10^{20}}{8} = 1.0 \times 10^{20}$$
Step 2: A fractional number of half-lives第 2 步：非整数个半衰期
$$\lambda = \frac{0.693}{12.0} = 0.0578\;\mathrm{h^{-1}}, \qquad N = N_0 e^{-\lambda t} = (8.0 \times 10^{20})\,e^{-(0.0578)(20)} = 2.5 \times 10^{20}$$
Step 3: Age from the remaining fraction第 3 步：由剩余分数求年龄
$$n = \frac{\ln(1/0.30)}{\ln 2} = 1.74, \qquad t = n\,t_{1/2} = (1.74)(5730) = 9.95 \times 10^{3}\;\mathrm{yr}$$
The age lies between one and two half-lives, as it should: $\tfrac{1}{2} \gt 0.30 \gt \tfrac{1}{4}$, and $n = 1.74$ fits. Why this works: both forms of the decay law give the same answer, so use whichever is easier for the numbers.年龄介于一到两个半衰期之间，这是应当的：$\tfrac{1}{2} \gt 0.30 \gt \tfrac{1}{4}$，而 $n = 1.74$ 符合。为什么成立：衰变定律的两种形式给出相同的答案，数字方便用哪种就用哪种。
Worked Example: Finding a Half-Life from Count Data例题：由计数数据求半衰期

A detector counts the undecayed nuclei of a sample as $N = 1000$, $630$, $400$ and $250$ (in thousands) at $t = 0$, $10$, $20$ and $30\;\mathrm{min}$. Find the decay constant and half-life, and say which graph is a straight line.

探测器记录样品中未衰变核的数目在 $t = 0$、$10$、$20$、$30\;\mathrm{min}$ 时分别为 $N = 1000$、$630$、$400$、$250$（单位：千个）。求衰变常量和半衰期，并说明哪种图像是直线。

Step 1: Linearize第 1 步：线性化
$$\ln N = \ln N_0 - \lambda t$$
Plot $\ln N$ against $t$: the graph is a straight line with slope $-\lambda$. The values of $\ln N$ are $6.908$, $6.446$, $5.991$ and $5.521$.用 $\ln N$ 对 $t$ 作图：图像是斜率为 $-\lambda$ 的直线。$\ln N$ 的值为 $6.908$、$6.446$、$5.991$ 和 $5.521$。
Step 2: Slope, $\lambda$ and half-life第 2 步：斜率、$\lambda$ 与半衰期
$$\lambda = -\frac{5.521 - 6.908}{30 - 0} = 0.0462\;\mathrm{min^{-1}}, \qquad t_{1/2} = \frac{0.693}{0.0462} = 15.0\;\mathrm{min}$$
Check by halving: $1000 \to 500$ at $15\;\mathrm{min}$ and $\to 250$ at $30\;\mathrm{min}$, which matches the last count. Why this works: an exponential decay turns into a straight line when you take the natural log of $N$, so the slope gives $\lambda$ directly.用减半法检验：$15\;\mathrm{min}$ 时 $1000 \to 500$，$30\;\mathrm{min}$ 时 $\to 250$，与最后一个读数吻合。为什么成立：对 $N$ 取自然对数后，指数衰减变成直线，斜率直接给出 $\lambda$。

Particles Emitted in Nuclear Decay核衰变中发射的粒子

Some processes by which nuclei decay emit subatomic particles with unique properties. In AP Physics 2 you need four of them.

一些核衰变过程会发射具有独特性质的亚原子粒子。在 AP Physics 2 中，你需要认识其中四种。

  • An alpha particle, or helium nucleus, consists of two protons and two neutrons. It is symbolized by $\alpha$ or ${}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He}$. Only helium-4 nuclei are considered.
  • Neutrinos and antineutrinos are subatomic particles that have no electrical charge (they are neutral) and negligible mass. They are symbolized by $\nu$ and $\bar{\nu}$ respectively. They interact with matter only via the weak force and the gravitational force, so they interact very little with normal matter.
  • A positron, or antielectron, is a subatomic particle with an electric charge opposite to that of an electron ($+e$) and the same mass as an electron. It is symbolized by ${}^{0}_{+1}\mathrm{e}$ or $e^{+}$. The ordinary electron is ${}^{0}_{-1}\mathrm{e}$ or $e^{-}$.
  • A gamma photon is a photon, as in Topic 15.1, with energy usually in the MeV range; it is symbolized by $\gamma$.
  • α 粒子（alpha particle），即氦核，由两个质子和两个中子组成，符号为 $\alpha$ 或 ${}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He}$。只考虑氦-4 核。
  • 中微子和反中微子是不带电荷（呈电中性）、质量可以忽略的亚原子粒子，符号分别为 $\nu$ 和 $\bar{\nu}$。它们只通过弱力和引力与物质相互作用，所以与普通物质的相互作用极少。
  • 正电子（positron），即反电子，是电荷与电子相反（$+e$）、质量与电子相同的亚原子粒子，符号为 ${}^{0}_{+1}\mathrm{e}$ 或 $e^{+}$。普通电子为 ${}^{0}_{-1}\mathrm{e}$ 或 $e^{-}$。
  • 伽马光子（gamma photon）就是专题 15.1 中的光子，能量通常在 MeV 量级，符号为 $\gamma$。

What Is Conserved: Nucleon Number, Lepton Number and Charge守恒的量：核子数、轻子数与电荷

In all nuclear decays, nucleon number (the number of neutrons and protons), lepton number (the number of electrons and neutrinos) and charge are all conserved.

在所有核衰变中，核子数（中子和质子的总数）、轻子数（电子和中微子的数目）和电荷都守恒（charge is conserved）。

Since charge is conserved, every decay equation must balance the sum of the mass numbers $A$ and the sum of the charges (atomic numbers $Z$, with $-1$ for an electron and $+1$ for a positron) on both sides.

由于电荷守恒，每个衰变方程两边都必须配平质量数 $A$ 之和与电荷之和（原子序数 $Z$，电子记作 $-1$，正电子记作 $+1$）。

Beta decays emit a lepton pair together, an electron with an antineutrino or a positron with a neutrino, so the lepton number is the same before and after.

β 衰变总是成对发射轻子：电子与反中微子，或正电子与中微子，所以轻子数在衰变前后相同。

The decay type tells you which particle of the pair appears; telling a neutrino from an antineutrino by their properties is beyond this course.

衰变类型决定了这一对中出现哪个粒子；依据性质区分中微子与反中微子，超出了本课程范围。

Decay Energy and Course Boundaries衰变能量与课程范围

Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Saying brighter light gives faster photoelectrons: intensity changes the number of electrons, not the maximum energy.
  2. Mixing eV and J in one calculation.
  3. Forgetting that the scattered photon in Compton scattering has a longer wavelength and lower energy.
  4. Using Celsius in Wien's law or the Stefan-Boltzmann law.
  5. Thinking that two half-lives remove all the nuclei: one quarter remains.
  6. Writing a decay equation whose mass numbers or charges do not balance, or leaving out the lepton pair in a beta decay.
  7. Describing electron structure with orbitals or probability clouds instead of energy levels.
最常见的错误
  1. 说更亮的光使光电子更快：光强改变的是电子数目，而不是最大能量。
  2. 在一次计算中混用 eV 和 J。
  3. 忘记康普顿散射中散射光子的波长更长、能量更低。
  4. 在维恩定律或斯特藩-玻尔兹曼定律中使用摄氏温度。
  5. 以为两个半衰期后所有的核都衰变了：还剩四分之一。

Multiplying instead of dividing, with $580$ left in nanometers, gives $1.68\;\mathrm{K}$, inverting the ratio gives $2.00 \times 10^{-4}\;\mathrm{K}$, and converting $580\;\mathrm{nm}$ as $5.8 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$ gives $5.00 \times 10^{6}\;\mathrm{K}$.维恩定律给出 $T = (2.90 \times 10^{-3}\;\mathrm{m\,K})/\lambda_\mathrm{max}$，其中 $\lambda_\mathrm{max}$ 以米为单位：$5000\;\mathrm{K}$。相乘而不是相除（并且 $580$ 仍以纳米为单位）得 $1.68\;\mathrm{K}$，把比值倒过来得 $2.00 \times 10^{-4}\;\mathrm{K}$，把 $580\;\mathrm{nm}$ 错换成 $5.8 \times 10^{-10}\;\mathrm{m}$ 得 $5.00 \times 10^{6}\;\mathrm{K}$。
6. A blackbody has its absolute temperature tripled and its surface area halved. By what factor does the power it radiates change?一个黑体的热力学温度变为 3 倍，表面积变为一半。它辐射的功率变为原来的多少倍？
$4.5$
$81$
$1.5$
$40.5$
Correct! $P \propto A T^4$, so the factor is $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 3^4 = 40.5$.正确！$P \propto A T^4$，所以倍数为 $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 3^4 = 40.5$。
Power is proportional to $A T^4$: $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 81 = 40.5$. The value $81$ forgets the smaller area, $4.5$ uses $T^2$ instead of $T^4$, and $1.5$ uses $T$ to the first power.功率与 $A T^4$ 成正比：$\tfrac{1}{2} \times 81 = 40.5$。$81$ 忘了面积变小，$4.5$ 用的是 $T^2$ 而不是 $T^4$，$1.5$ 用的是 $T$ 的一次方。

Questions 7 to 9: Photoelectric Effect and Compton Scattering第 7 至 9 题：光电效应与康普顿散射

7. Light of wavelength $400\;\mathrm{nm}$ strikes a metal with work function $2.30\;\mathrm{eV}$. What is the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons?波长 $400\;\mathrm{nm}$ 的光照射逸出功为 $2.30\;\mathrm{eV}$ 的金属。光电子的最大动能是多少？
$3.10\;\mathrm{eV}$
$5.40\;\mathrm{eV}$
$0.80\;\mathrm{eV}$
$2.30\;\mathrm{eV}$
Correct! $K_\text{max} = 1240/400 - 2.30 = 3.10 - 2.30 = 0.80\;\mathrm{eV}$.正确！$K_\text{max} = 1240/400 - 2.30 = 3.10 - 2.30 = 0.80\;\mathrm{eV}$。
Subtract the work function from the photon energy: $3.10 - 2.30 = 0.80\;\mathrm{eV}$. The value $3.10\;\mathrm{eV}$ forgets the work function, $5.40\;\mathrm{eV}$ adds it instead, and $2.30\;\mathrm{eV}$ confuses $K_\text{max}$ with $\phi$.用光子能量减去逸出功：$3.10 - 2.30 = 0.80\;\mathrm{eV}$。$3.10\;\mathrm{eV}$ 忘了逸出功，$5.40\;\mathrm{eV}$ 错把它加上，$2.30\;\mathrm{eV}$ 把 $K_\text{max}$ 与 $\phi$ 混淆了。
8. Monochromatic light above the threshold frequency shines on a metal in a photoelectric experiment, and its intensity is doubled at the same frequency. What happens?在光电效应实验中，频率高于阈频率的单色光照射金属，在频率不变的情况下把光强加倍。会发生什么？
The current doubles and the stopping potential is unchanged电流加倍，遏止电势不变
Both the current and the stopping potential double电流和遏止电势都加倍
The current is unchanged and the stopping potential doubles电流不变，遏止电势加倍
Neither changes二者都不变
Correct! Twice as many photons arrive each second, so twice as many electrons are emitted, but each photon still has energy $hf$, so $K_\text{max}$ and $V_\text{stop}$ are unchanged.正确！每秒到达的光子数加倍，所以发射的电子数加倍，但每个光子的能量仍为 $hf$，所以 $K_\text{max}$ 和 $V_\text{stop}$ 不变。
Intensity sets the number of photons per second, and so the number of photoelectrons and the current. The maximum energy of one electron, and so the stopping potential, depends on the frequency and the work function, not on the intensity.光强决定每秒的光子数，因而决定光电子数和电流。单个电子的最大能量，即遏止电势，取决于频率和逸出功，而不是光强。
9. A photon is scattered straight back ($\theta = 180^\circ$) by a free electron at rest. By how much does its wavelength change? Use $h/(m_e c) = 2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$.一个光子被静止的自由电子直接散射回去（$\theta = 180^\circ$）。它的波长改变了多少？取 $h/(m_e c) = 2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$。
$+2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$
$+4.86 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$
$-2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$
$0$
Correct! $\Delta\lambda = (2.43 \times 10^{-12})(1 - \cos 180^\circ) = (2.43 \times 10^{-12})(2) = 4.86 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$, the largest possible increase.正确！$\Delta\lambda = (2.43 \times 10^{-12})(1 - \cos 180^\circ) = (2.43 \times 10^{-12})(2) = 4.86 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$，这是可能的最大增量。
Since $\cos 180^\circ = -1$, the factor $1 - \cos\theta$ equals $2$, giving $+4.86 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$. Using $1 - \cos\theta = 1$ gives $2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$ (that is the $90^\circ$ result), using $\cos\theta$ alone gives $-2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$ (but the wavelength always increases), and the shift is zero only for no deflection.因为 $\cos 180^\circ = -1$，所以 $1 - \cos\theta$ 等于 $2$，得到 $+4.86 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$。取 $1 - \cos\theta = 1$ 得 $2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$（那是 $90^\circ$ 的结果），只用 $\cos\theta$ 得 $-2.43 \times 10^{-12}\;\mathrm{m}$（但波长总是增大），而只有不偏转时改变量才为零。

10. A nuclear reaction has a mass defect of $0.0200\;\mathrm{u}$. How much energy does it release? ($1\;\mathrm{u}$ corresponds to $931\;\mathrm{MeV}$.)某核反应的质量亏损为 $0.0200\;\mathrm{u}$。它释放多少能量？（$1\;\mathrm{u}$ 对应 $931\;\mathrm{MeV}$。）
$18.6\;\mathrm{MeV}$
$4.66 \times 10^{4}\;\mathrm{MeV}$
$0.0200\;\mathrm{MeV}$
$1.86 \times 10^{-2}\;\mathrm{MeV}$
Correct! $\Delta E = (0.0200)(931) = 18.6\;\mathrm{MeV}$.正确！$\Delta E = (0.0200)(931) = 18.6\;\mathrm{MeV}$。
Multiply the mass defect in u by $931\;\mathrm{MeV/u}$: $18.6\;\mathrm{MeV}$. Dividing $931$ by $0.0200$ gives $4.66 \times 10^{4}$, reading the mass defect as the energy gives $0.0200$, and using $931\;\mathrm{keV/u}$ gives $1.86 \times 10^{-2}\;\mathrm{MeV}$.把以 u 为单位的质量亏损乘以 $931\;\mathrm{MeV/u}$：$18.6\;\mathrm{MeV}$。用 $931$ 除以 $0.0200$ 得 $4.66 \times 10^{4}$，把质量亏损的数值当作能量得 $0.0200$，用 $931\;\mathrm{keV/u}$ 则得 $1.86 \times 10^{-2}\;\mathrm{MeV}$。
11. An isotope has a half-life of $4.0$ days. What fraction of a sample remains after $10$ days?某同位素的半衰期为 $4.0$ 天。$10$ 天后样品还剩多少分数？
$0.25$
$0.125$
$0.177$
$0.40$
Correct! $10/4.0 = 2.5$ half-lives, so the fraction is $(1/2)^{2.5} = 0.177$.正确！$10/4.0 = 2.5$ 个半衰期，所以剩余分数为 $(1/2)^{2.5} = 0.177$。
The elapsed time is $2.5$ half-lives, so $(1/2)^{2.5} = 0.177$. The value $0.25$ counts only two half-lives, $0.125$ rounds up to three, and $0.40$ is the ratio $4.0/10$, which has no meaning here.经过的时间是 $2.5$ 个半衰期，所以 $(1/2)^{2.5} = 0.177$。$0.25$ 只数了两个半衰期，$0.125$ 进位成了三个，而 $0.40$ 是比值 $4.0/10$，在这里没有意义。
12. A nucleus ${}^{90}_{38}\mathrm{Sr}$ undergoes beta-minus decay. Which is the daughter nucleus?原子核 ${}^{90}_{38}\mathrm{Sr}$ 发生 β⁻ 衰变。子核是哪一个？
${}^{86}_{36}\mathrm{Kr}$
${}^{90}_{37}\mathrm{Rb}$
${}^{91}_{39}\mathrm{Y}$

Correct! Beta-minus decay keeps $A = 90$ and raises $Z$ by 1 to $39$, with an electron and an antineutrino emitted.正确！β⁻ 衰变保持 $A = 90$，并使 $Z$ 增加 1 变为 $39$，同时发射一个电子和一个反中微子。
In beta-minus decay a neutron becomes a proton, so $A$ is unchanged and $Z$ goes up by 1: ${}^{90}_{39}\mathrm{Y}$. The nucleus ${}^{86}_{36}\mathrm{Kr}$ is the result of alpha decay, ${}^{90}_{37}\mathrm{Rb}$ is the result of beta-plus decay, and ${}^{91}_{39}\mathrm{Y}$ wrongly raises $A$ by one.β⁻ 衰变中一个中子变为质子，所以 $A$ 不变，$Z$ 增加 1：${}^{90}_{39}\mathrm{Y}$。${}^{86}_{36}\mathrm{Kr}$ 是 α 衰变的结果，${}^{90}_{37}\mathrm{Rb}$ 是 β⁺ 衰变的结果，${}^{91}_{39}\mathrm{Y}$ 则错误地使 $A$ 增加了 1。
