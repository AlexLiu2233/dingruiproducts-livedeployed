Topic 11.1主题 11.1

The chi-square statistic and distribution卡方统计量与卡方分布

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Compute the chi-square statistic $\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$ from observed and expected counts.
  • Explain why each difference is squared and divided by the expected count, and why $\chi^2$ is never negative.
  • Describe the chi-square distribution: right-skewed, set by its degrees of freedom, with mean equal to the degrees of freedom.
  • Find a right-tail p-value with the chi-square cdf and say what it measures.
  • 由观测计数与期望计数计算卡方统计量 $\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$。
  • 说明为什么每个差要平方并除以期望计数，以及 $\chi^2$ 为什么不会为负。
  • 描述卡方分布：右偏，由自由度决定，均值等于自由度。
  • 用卡方累积分布函数求右尾 p 值，并说明它度量什么。

Many AP Statistics questions are about categorical data in counts. A chi-square procedure asks one question: how far are the observed counts from the expected counts we would see if a claim (the null hypothesis) were true? The answer is a single number, the chi-square statistic: $$\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$$ where $O$ is an observed count, $E$ is the expected count for the same category or cell, and the sum of these terms runs over every category (or every cell of a table). Expected counts are always computed assuming the null hypothesis is true.

许多 AP 统计学问题涉及以计数表示的分类数据。卡方过程只问一个问题：如果某个说法（原假设）成立，我们应看到的期望计数与实际观测计数相差多远？答案是一个数，即卡方统计量：$$\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$$ 其中 $O$ 是观测计数，$E$ 是同一类别（或单元格）的期望计数，对所有类别（或表中所有单元格）求和。期望计数总是在假设原假设成立的前提下计算。

Each piece of the formula has a job. Observed and expected counts both add to the same total, so the raw differences $O-E$ always sum to zero; squaring stops positive and negative differences from cancelling. Dividing by $E$ puts differences on a fair scale: being 10 over matters a lot when you expected 20 but hardly at all when you expected 2000. Each term is that category's contribution to $\chi^2$. The statistic equals $0$ only when every observed count equals its expected count, and the farther the counts drift from expectation, the larger it gets. Sample size matters too: if every observed count is multiplied by the same factor $c$ while the proportions stay the same, every expected count is multiplied by $c$ as well, and $\frac{(cO-cE)^2}{cE}=c\,\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$, so the chi-square statistic is multiplied by $c$. The same pattern in a larger sample therefore gives stronger evidence.

公式的每一部分都有作用。观测计数与期望计数的总和相同，所以原始差 $O-E$ 的总和恒为零；平方避免正负差互相抵消；除以 $E$ 让差值有公平的尺度：期望 20 却多出 10 影响很大，期望 2000 时则几乎无关紧要。每一项是该类别对 $\chi^2$ 的贡献。只有每个观测计数都等于期望计数时统计量才为 $0$，计数偏离期望越远，统计量越大。样本量也有影响：如果每个观测计数都乘以同一个因子 $c$，而比例不变，那么每个期望计数也乘以 $c$，并且 $\frac{(cO-cE)^2}{cE}=c\,\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$，所以卡方统计量变为原来的 $c$ 倍。因此同样的模式出现在更大的样本中，会给出更强的证据。

Must know

$\chi^2\ge 0$ always. A large $\chi^2$ is evidence against the null hypothesis, and the p-value is the area to the right of the observed $\chi^2$ under the chi-square curve. There is no left tail and no two-sided version for these tests, and counts (not percentages) go into the formula.

必备知识

$\chi^2\ge 0$ 恒成立。$\chi^2$ 大说明有证据反对原假设，p 值是卡方曲线下观测 $\chi^2$ 右侧的面积。这类检验没有左尾，也没有双侧版本，公式中代入的是计数（而不是百分比）。

The chi-square distribution卡方分布

Chi-square distributions are continuous curves defined only for values at or above $0$. Each one is right-skewed, and its exact shape is set by one number, the degrees of freedom (df). With small df the curve is strongly skewed and piles up near $0$; as df grows the curve moves to the right, flattens and becomes more symmetric. The mean of a chi-square distribution equals its df, so a statistic close to the df is typical when the null hypothesis is true, and one far above it is surprising. Figure 8.1 shows three of these curves.

卡方分布是只在 $0$ 及以上取值的连续曲线。每条曲线都是右偏的，具体形状由一个数，即自由度（df）决定。自由度小时曲线强烈右偏并集中在 $0$ 附近；自由度增大后曲线右移、变平、更接近对称。卡方分布的均值等于它的自由度，所以原假设成立时，接近自由度的统计量是典型的，远高于自由度的统计量则令人意外。图 8.1 给出三条这样的曲线。

To get a p-value, find the area to the right of the observed statistic. On a TI-84 use the chi-square cdf from the DISTR menu, χ²cdf(lower, upper, df), with a very large upper bound: p-value $=$ χ²cdf($\chi^2$, 1E99, df). A table of critical values works too: for $\alpha=0.05$ the critical values are $5.991$ (df $2$), $7.815$ (df $3$), $9.488$ (df $4$) and $11.070$ (df $5$), so a statistic below the critical value for its df has a p-value above $0.05$.

求 p 值就是求观测统计量右侧的面积。TI-84 上使用 DISTR 菜单中的卡方累积分布函数 χ²cdf(下限, 上限, df)，上限取一个很大的数：p 值 $=$ χ²cdf($\chi^2$, 1E99, df)。也可以查临界值表：$\alpha=0.05$ 时，临界值为 $5.991$（df $2$）、$7.815$（df $3$）、$9.488$（df $4$）、$11.070$（df $5$），所以统计量低于其自由度对应的临界值时，p 值大于 $0.05$。

Worked Example 8.1: compute the statistic, df and p-value from counts例题 8.1：由计数计算统计量、自由度与 p 值

A school cafeteria offers four entrées and a manager wonders whether students choose them equally often. A random sample of $100$ students picks: $18$, $22$, $30$ and $30$. If demand were equal, the expected count is $25$ for each entrée. (All data here are illustrative.)

  1. Contributions: $\frac{(18-25)^2}{25}=1.96$, $\frac{(22-25)^2}{25}=0.36$, $\frac{(30-25)^2}{25}=1.00$, and $1.00$ again for the last entrée.
  2. Add them: $\chi^2=1.96+0.36+1.00+1.00=4.32$.
  3. Degrees of freedom: $4$ entrées gives $\text{df}=4-1=3$.
  4. p-value: χ²cdf($4.32$, 1E99, $3$) $=0.2289$.
  5. Interpretation: the statistic $4.32$ is only a little above the mean of $3$ for this distribution. If demand really were equal, about $23\%$ of random samples would drift at least this far from equal by chance alone, so this is not unusual.

某学校食堂提供四种主菜，经理想知道学生选择它们的频率是否相同。随机抽取 $100$ 名学生，选择人数分别为 $18$、$22$、$30$、$30$。若需求相同，每种主菜的期望计数都是 $25$。（本例数据均为虚构示例。）

  1. 各项贡献：$\frac{(18-25)^2}{25}=1.96$，$\frac{(22-25)^2}{25}=0.36$，$\frac{(30-25)^2}{25}=1.00$，最后一种主菜同样为 $1.00$。
  2. 相加：$\chi^2=1.96+0.36+1.00+1.00=4.32$。
  3. 自由度：$4$ 种主菜，$\text{df}=4-1=3$。
  4. p 值：χ²cdf($4.32$, 1E99, $3$) $=0.2289$。
  5. 解释：统计量 $4.32$ 只略高于该分布的均值 $3$。若需求确实相同，约 $23\%$ 的随机样本仅凭偶然就会偏离到这种程度，所以这并不异常。
Worked Example 8.2: the same statistic with different degrees of freedom例题 8.2：同一统计量，不同自由度

A statistic of $\chi^2=10.4$ is reported. Find the p-value when (a) $\text{df}=5$ and (b) $\text{df}=2$.

  1. (a) χ²cdf($10.4$, 1E99, $5$) $=0.0647$. This agrees with the table: $10.4<11.070$, the critical value for $\text{df}=5$, so the p-value exceeds $0.05$.
  2. (b) χ²cdf($10.4$, 1E99, $2$) $=0.0055$. Now $10.4>5.991$, so the p-value is below $0.05$.
  3. The same number is ordinary for a curve with a larger mean and extreme for one with a smaller mean. Always report df beside $\chi^2$.

某检验报告的统计量为 $\chi^2=10.4$。分别求 (a) $\text{df}=5$ 和 (b) $\text{df}=2$ 时的 p 值。

  1. (a) χ²cdf($10.4$, 1E99, $5$) $=0.0647$。与查表一致：$10.4<11.070$（$\text{df}=5$ 的临界值），所以 p 值大于 $0.05$。
  2. (b) χ²cdf($10.4$, 1E99, $2$) $=0.0055$。此时 $10.4>5.991$，所以 p 值小于 $0.05$。
  3. 同一个数值，对均值较大的曲线是普通的，对均值较小的曲线则是极端的。报告 $\chi^2$ 时务必同时给出自由度。
Exam tip考试提示

A chi-square p-value is always a right-tail area, so never double it and never use a left tail. When you show work, write the formula with the actual counts substituted, for example $\frac{(18-25)^2}{25}$, and always state the degrees of freedom beside the statistic.

卡方检验的 p 值永远是右尾面积，不要加倍，也不要用左尾。写过程时，把实际计数代入公式，例如 $\frac{(18-25)^2}{25}$，并始终在统计量旁写出自由度。

Common mistakes
  • Leaving out the square, or dividing by $O$ instead of $E$.
  • Putting percentages or proportions into the formula instead of counts.
  • Reporting a statistic without its degrees of freedom, or finding the p-value with the wrong df.
常见错误
  • 漏掉平方，或除以 $O$ 而不是 $E$。
  • 把百分比或比例而不是计数代入公式。
  • 报告统计量时不写自由度，或用错误的自由度求 p 值。

1. Can $\chi^2$ be negative? Explain.1. $\chi^2$ 能为负吗？请说明。

No. Every term is $\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$ with a square on top and a positive expected count underneath, so each term is at least $0$ and so is their sum.

不能。每一项 $\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$ 分子是平方、分母是正的期望计数，所以每项不小于 $0$，总和也是非负的。

2. A test has $\text{df}=4$ and $\chi^2=4.2$. Without calculating, is this strong evidence against the null hypothesis?2. 某检验 $\text{df}=4$，$\chi^2=4.2$。不计算的话，这是反对原假设的有力证据吗？

No. The mean of a chi-square distribution equals its df, which is $4$, so $4.2$ is typical. The p-value is large (it is about $0.38$).

不是。卡方分布的均值等于自由度，即 $4$，所以 $4.2$ 是典型值。p 值很大（约 $0.38$）。

3. Find the contribution to $\chi^2$ for a category with $O=12$, $E=8$, and for one with $O=4$, $E=8$. What do you notice?3. 分别求下列两个类别对 $\chi^2$ 的贡献：$O=12$、$E=8$ 的类别，以及 $O=4$、$E=8$ 的类别。你注意到什么？

Both give $\frac{(\pm 4)^2}{8}=2.0$. Squaring makes being too high and too low count the same; the chi-square statistic does not record direction.

两者都是 $\frac{(\pm 4)^2}{8}=2.0$。平方使偏高与偏低同样计数；卡方统计量不记录方向。

Topic 11.2主题 11.2

Goodness of fit: hypotheses and conditions拟合优度：假设与条件

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Recognize a goodness-of-fit situation: one sample, one categorical variable, a claimed distribution.
  • Write the null and alternative hypotheses in context.
  • Compute expected counts $E=np$ from the claimed proportions.
  • Check the conditions (random, 10%, all expected counts at least 5) and state the degrees of freedom $k-1$.
  • 识别拟合优度情境：一个样本、一个分类变量、一个给定的分布。
  • 结合情境写出原假设与备择假设。
  • 由给定比例计算期望计数 $E=np$。
  • 检验条件（随机、10%、所有期望计数至少为 5）并写出自由度 $k-1$。

A chi-square goodness-of-fit test asks whether the distribution of one categorical variable in a population matches a claimed distribution. The data are one random sample, each individual is placed in exactly one of $k$ categories, and the claim gives a proportion $p_1,p_2,\dots,p_k$ for every category (the proportions add to $1$).

卡方拟合优度检验用于判断总体中一个分类变量的分布是否与给定的分布相符。数据是一个随机样本，每个个体恰好属于 $k$ 个类别之一，给定的说法为每个类别指定比例 $p_1,p_2,\dots,p_k$（比例之和为 $1$）。

The null hypothesis says the population proportions equal the claimed ones. The alternative hypothesis says at least one proportion differs; it is never directional and it does not say that every proportion differs. Write both in context, naming the population and the variable. In symbols, $H_0$: $p_i$ equals its claimed value for every category $i$, and $H_a$: at least one $p_i$ differs from its claimed value.

原假设说总体比例等于给定的比例，备择假设说至少有一个比例不同；它没有方向，也不是说每个比例都不同。两者都要结合情境，写明总体与变量。用符号表示：$H_0$：$p_i$ 等于其给定值，对每个类别 $i$ 都成立；$H_a$：至少有一个 $p_i$ 与其给定值不同。

The expected count for category $i$ is $E_i=np_i$: the sample size times the claimed proportion (not the sample's proportion). Expected counts need not be whole numbers, and they add to $n$.

类别 $i$ 的期望计数为 $E_i=np_i$：样本量乘以给定的比例（不是样本比例）。期望计数不必是整数，且总和为 $n$。

The degrees of freedom for goodness of fit are $\text{df}=k-1$, the number of categories minus one: once $k-1$ category counts are known, the last is fixed by the total $n$. It is not $n-1$.

拟合优度检验的自由度为 $\text{df}=k-1$，即类别数减一：已知 $k-1$ 个类别的计数后，最后一个由总数 $n$ 确定。它不是 $n-1$。

Worked Example 8.3: hypotheses, expected counts and conditions例题 8.3：假设、期望计数与条件

A company claims its trail mix is $40\%$ peanuts, $30\%$ raisins, $20\%$ candy pieces and $10\%$ cereal. An analyst takes a random sample of $200$ pieces from one day's production (far more than $2000$ pieces) and records each piece's type. (Illustrative scenario.)

  1. Hypotheses. $H_0$: the distribution of piece type in this day's production is as claimed, $p_1=0.40$, $p_2=0.30$, $p_3=0.20$, $p_4=0.10$. $H_a$: at least one of these proportions is different from the claimed value.
  2. Expected counts. $E=200(0.40)=80$, $200(0.30)=60$, $200(0.20)=40$, $200(0.10)=20$; they add to $200$.
  3. Conditions. Random: the analyst took a random sample. 10%: $200$ is less than $10\%$ of the day's output, which is more than $2000$ pieces. Large counts: the smallest expected count is $20$, which is at least $5$.
  4. Degrees of freedom. There are $k=4$ categories, so $\text{df}=4-1=3$.

某公司声称其什锦坚果由 $40\%$ 花生、$30\%$ 葡萄干、$20\%$ 糖果块和 $10\%$ 谷物组成。分析员从某天的产品中随机抽取 $200$ 块（该天产量远超 $2000$ 块），记录每块的类型。（虚构情境。）

  1. 假设。$H_0$：该天产品中各类型的分布与声称一致，$p_1=0.40$，$p_2=0.30$，$p_3=0.20$，$p_4=0.10$。$H_a$：这些比例中至少有一个与声称值不同。
  2. 期望计数。$E=200(0.40)=80$，$200(0.30)=60$，$200(0.20)=40$，$200(0.10)=20$；总和为 $200$。
  3. 条件。随机：分析员抽取的是随机样本。10%：$200$ 小于该天产量的 $10\%$，而产量超过 $2000$ 块。大计数：最小的期望计数是 $20$，不小于 $5$。
  4. 自由度。共有 $k=4$ 个类别，所以 $\text{df}=4-1=3$。
Worked Example 8.4: when the large-counts condition fails例题 8.4：大计数条件不满足时

A different claim lists five categories with proportions $0.40$, $0.30$, $0.20$, $0.08$ and $0.02$, and the random sample has $n=60$. (Illustrative scenario.)

  1. Expected counts: $60(0.40)=24$, $60(0.30)=18$, $60(0.20)=12$, $60(0.08)=4.8$, $60(0.02)=1.2$.
  2. Two expected counts are below $5$, so the large-counts condition fails and the chi-square test is not valid as set up.
  3. Fix 1: combine the last two categories into one sensible "other" category with proportion $0.10$. The expected counts become $24$, $18$, $12$, $6$, all at least $5$, and the new df is $4-1=3$. Decide to combine before looking at the results.
  4. Fix 2: take a larger sample. The smallest category needs $n(0.02)\ge 5$, so $n\ge 250$.

另一个说法列出五个类别，比例为 $0.40$、$0.30$、$0.20$、$0.08$、$0.02$，随机样本量 $n=60$。（虚构情境。）

  1. 期望计数：$60(0.40)=24$，$60(0.30)=18$，$60(0.20)=12$，$60(0.08)=4.8$，$60(0.02)=1.2$。
  2. 有两个期望计数小于 $5$，大计数条件不满足，按原设置卡方检验无效。
  3. 办法一：把最后两个类别合并为一个合理的“其他”类别，比例 $0.10$。期望计数变为 $24$、$18$、$12$、$6$，都不小于 $5$，新的自由度为 $4-1=3$。合并要在看结果之前决定。
  4. 办法二：增大样本量。最小的类别需要 $n(0.02)\ge 5$，即 $n\ge 250$。
Exam tip考试提示

Check the large-counts condition with the expected counts and show them, for example "expected counts are $80, 60, 40, 20$, all at least $5$". Checking the observed counts does not test the condition. Write the hypotheses in context: say what population and what variable.

用期望计数检验大计数条件并写出它们，例如“期望计数为 $80, 60, 40, 20$，都不小于 $5$”。检查观测计数并不能检验这个条件。假设要结合情境：说明是什么总体、什么变量。

Common mistakes
  • Computing $E$ from the sample proportions, which makes $O=E$ every time.
  • Writing $\text{df}=n-1$ instead of the number of categories minus one.
  • Writing $H_a$ as "all proportions differ" or giving it a direction.
常见错误
  • 用样本比例计算 $E$，这样 $O=E$ 总成立。
  • 把自由度写成 $\text{df}=n-1$，而不是类别数减一。
  • 把 $H_a$ 写成“所有比例都不同”或给它加方向。

Carrying out a goodness-of-fit test进行拟合优度检验

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Compute the test statistic, the degrees of freedom and the p-value, by hand and with the X2GOF-Test (chi-square goodness-of-fit test) on a calculator.
  • Write a conclusion that compares the p-value with $\alpha$ and is stated in context, using the words "convincing evidence" or "fail to reject" correctly.
  • Use each category's contribution to $\chi^2$ to see which category drives the result.
  • 按四步完成拟合优度检验：陈述、计划、计算、结论。
  • 手算并用计算器的 X2GOF-Test（卡方拟合优度检验）求检验统计量、自由度与 p 值。
  • 写出把 p 值与 $\alpha$ 比较、并结合情境的结论，正确使用“有令人信服的证据”或“不能拒绝”。

Carry out the test in four steps. State the hypotheses in context and a significance level $\alpha$. Plan by naming the test (chi-square goodness-of-fit test) and checking the random, 10% and large-counts conditions. Do the calculation: the test statistic $\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$, the degrees of freedom $k-1$, and the p-value from the right tail. Conclude by comparing the p-value with $\alpha$, in context.

按四步完成检验。陈述：结合情境写出假设和显著性水平 $\alpha$。计划：说出检验名称（卡方拟合优度检验）并检验随机、10% 与大计数条件。计算：检验统计量 $\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$、自由度 $k-1$，以及右尾 p 值。结论：结合情境，把 p 值与 $\alpha$ 比较。

On a TI-84, put the observed counts in L1 and the expected counts in L2, then press STAT, use the right arrow to reach TESTS, and choose the goodness-of-fit test (called X2GOF-Test, shown with the Greek letter chi on the screen) with Observed: L1, Expected: L2 and df $=k-1$. The output gives the test statistic, the p-value, the df and a list of each category's contribution. This menu item is on newer calculator operating systems; if yours lacks it, compute (L1-L2)^2/L2 into L3, take sum(L3) for $\chi^2$, and use the chi-square cdf from Topic 11.1 for the p-value.

If the p-value is less than $\alpha$, reject $H_0$: there is convincing evidence that the population distribution differs from the claimed distribution in at least one category. If the p-value is at least $\alpha$, fail to reject $H_0$: the data do not provide convincing evidence of a difference. That is not evidence that the claim is true; it only says this sample is consistent with it. Always finish with the context (population and variable).

若 p 值小于 $\alpha$，拒绝 $H_0$：有令人信服的证据表明总体分布至少在一个类别上与给定分布不同。若 p 值不小于 $\alpha$，不能拒绝 $H_0$：数据没有提供令人信服的差异证据。这并不证明该说法是真的，只说明这个样本与它相容。结论最后一定要回到情境（总体与变量）。

Which categories drive the result哪些类别造成了结果

The statistic is a sum of one contribution $\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$ per category. The category with the largest contribution is where the data disagree most with the claim. The test statistic does not carry a direction, so to say how a category differs, compare its observed and expected counts directly.

统计量是每个类别一项贡献 $\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$ 的总和。贡献最大的类别就是数据与说法分歧最大之处。统计量本身不带方向，要说明某类别如何不同，需直接比较它的观测计数与期望计数。

  • 检验所有期望计数不小于 $5$，并检查它们与观测表的边际合计一致。

A two-way table cross-classifies counts by two categorical variables (or by group and outcome). Each row has a row total, each column has a column total, and the table total (grand total) $n$ is the sum of all cells. These totals are the marginal counts, and they are all you need to build the expected counts.

双向表按两个分类变量（或按组别与结果）对计数做交叉分类。每行有行合计，每列有列合计，表总计（总合计）$n$ 是所有单元格之和。这些合计就是边际计数，构造期望计数只需要它们。

Here is the idea. Suppose the null hypothesis holds: every row is spread across the columns in the same way as the whole sample. The overall proportion of the sample in a column is $\frac{\text{column total}}{\text{table total}}$, so a row with $\text{row total}$ individuals should have about $\text{row total}\times\frac{\text{column total}}{\text{table total}}$ of them in that column.

思路如下。假设原假设成立：每一行在各列上的分布都与整个样本相同。样本中属于某一列的总体比例是 $\frac{\text{column total}}{\text{table total}}$，所以有 $\text{row total}$ 个个体的一行，在该列中应有约 $\text{row total}\times\frac{\text{column total}}{\text{table total}}$ 个。

Must know

Expected count in a cell $=\dfrac{(\text{row total})(\text{column total})}{\text{table total}}$. Keep decimals (do not round to whole numbers), and remember each row and each column of expected counts adds to the same total as in the observed table.

必备知识

单元格的期望计数 $=\dfrac{(\text{row total})(\text{column total})}{\text{table total}}$。保留小数（不要取整），并记住期望计数表的每行每列之和都与观测表相同。

Homogeneity and independence: design and hypotheses同质性与独立性：设计与假设

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Write the null and alternative hypotheses for each test in context.
  • State the degrees of freedom $(r-1)(c-1)$ for a two-way table.
  • Check the conditions: random samples or random assignment, 10%, and all expected counts at least $5$.

Two different questions lead to the same table, the same expected counts, the same statistic and the same degrees of freedom. What differs is how the data were collected, and therefore what the null hypothesis says. A test of homogeneity compares the distribution of one categorical variable across several groups. A test of independence asks whether two categorical variables are associated within one population.

两类不同的问题会得到同样的表、同样的期望计数、同样的统计量和同样的自由度。不同之处在于数据是怎样收集的，因此原假设的表述也不同。同质性检验比较一个分类变量在多个组中的分布。独立性检验问同一总体中两个分类变量是否有关联。

For a table with $r$ rows and $c$ columns (not counting the totals), $\text{df}=(r-1)(c-1)$. The conditions are: the data come from random samples (or from a randomized experiment); the 10% condition holds for each sample drawn without replacement; and every expected count is at least $5$.

对有 $r$ 行 $c$ 列的表（不含合计），$\text{df}=(r-1)(c-1)$。条件包括：数据来自随机样本（或随机化实验）；每个不放回抽取的样本满足 10% 条件；每个期望计数都不小于 $5$。

The four steps are the same as for goodness of fit. State the hypotheses that match the design. Plan with the test name and the conditions, including the expected counts. Do the calculation. Conclude by comparing the p-value with $\alpha$ in context.

Association is not causation

Rejecting $H_0$ gives convincing evidence of an association (independence) or of different distributions (homogeneity). The test says nothing about direction or about why. A cause-and-effect conclusion is allowed only when the data come from a randomized experiment, because random assignment, not the chi-square calculation, rules out confounding. After a survey or other observational study, say "associated", never "causes", and state the conclusion in context.

关联不等于因果

拒绝 $H_0$ 只说明有令人信服的证据表明存在关联（独立性）或分布不同（同质性）。检验不说明方向，也不说明原因。只有数据来自随机化实验时才能下因果结论，因为排除混杂因素的是随机分配，而不是卡方计算。对于调查或其他观察研究，只能说“有关联”，不能说“导致”，并结合情境写出结论。

Test, statistic, df, p-valueChi-square test of independence: $\chi^2=32.10$, $\text{df}=(3-1)(3-1)=4$, p-value $=1.8\times 10^{-6}$.
ConclusionBecause the p-value is far below $0.05$, we reject $H_0$. There is convincing evidence of an association between age group and preferred news source among adults in this city.
Where and why not causeThe largest contributions are adults $55$ and over choosing print ($30$ observed against $18$ expected, $8.0$) and choosing online ($27$ against $45$, $7.2$), and adults $18$ to $34$ choosing online ($63$ against $45$, $7.2$). Selecting an inference procedure选择推断过程

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Choose among goodness of fit, homogeneity, independence and the $z$ or $t$ procedures from the design and the data type.
  • Justify the choice in one sentence that names the design.
  • Explain why a chi-square test on a $2\times 2$ table matches a two-proportion $z$ test, and when each is the better tool.
  • Recognize when chi-square is not appropriate (means, quantitative data, a one-sided question).
  • 根据设计与数据类型，在拟合优度、同质性、独立性以及 $z$ 或 $t$ 过程中做出选择。
  • 用一句话说明所选检验并点明设计。
  • 说明为什么 $2\times 2$ 表的卡方检验与两比例 $z$ 检验等价，以及各自何时更合适。
  • 识别不能用卡方的情形（均值、定量数据、单侧问题）。

Selecting the appropriate inference procedure is a skill of its own. Ask three questions in order. 1. What type of data? Counts in categories point to a chi-square test or a $z$ procedure for proportions; measurements point to $t$ procedures for means or to inference for a slope. 2. How were the data collected? One sample or several separate samples, and how many variables. 3. What is the question? Does one variable match a claimed distribution, is a distribution the same across groups, or are two variables associated? Figure 8.6 summarizes which test to choose.

Worked Example 8.13: which procedure? classify five studies例题 8.13：用哪种检验？分类五项研究

Choose the procedure for each study. (Illustrative scenarios.)

  1. (a) One random sample of $500$ voters is classified by party ($3$ parties) and region ($4$ regions); is there an association? Chi-square test of independence, $\text{df}=(3-1)(4-1)=6$.
  2. (b) A random sample of $250$ shoppers is classified by payment type (cash, card, mobile), and a company report claims shares of $30\%$, $60\%$ and $10\%$. Chi-square goodness-of-fit test, $\text{df}=3-1=2$, expected counts $75$, $150$, $25$.
  3. (c) Random samples of $100$ parts from each of $3$ factories are classified as defective or not. Chi-square test of homogeneity ($3\times 2$ table), $\text{df}=2$.
  4. (d) Random samples of students at two schools report hours of sleep, and we compare the means. Two-sample $t$ test: the data are quantitative, so chi-square does not apply.
  5. (e) A random sample of $400$ voters is used to test whether the proportion supporting a measure is greater than $0.5$. One-proportion $z$ test: one proportion and a one-sided alternative.

为每项研究选择检验过程。（虚构情境。）

  1. (a) 对 $500$ 名选民的一个随机样本按政党（$3$ 个）和地区（$4$ 个）分类；二者有关联吗？卡方独立性检验，$\text{df}=(3-1)(4-1)=6$。
  2. (b) 对 $250$ 名顾客的随机样本按支付方式（现金、银行卡、移动支付）分类，公司报告声称占比为 $30\%$、$60\%$、$10\%$。卡方拟合优度检验，$\text{df}=3-1=2$，期望计数 $75$、$150$、$25$。
  3. (c) 各随机抽取 $100$ 个零件，分别来自 $3$ 家工厂，并分类为次品或非次品。卡方同质性检验（$3\times 2$ 表），$\text{df}=2$。
  4. (d) 从两所学校分别随机抽取学生报告睡眠时间，并比较均值。双样本 $t$ 检验：数据是定量的，不能用卡方。
  5. (e) 用 $400$ 名选民的随机样本检验支持某提案的比例是否大于 $0.5$。单比例 $z$ 检验：一个比例且备择假设为单侧。
Worked Example 8.14: a 2x2 chi-square test and the two-proportion z test agree例题 8.14：2x2 卡方检验与双比例 z 检验结果一致

Two treatments are randomly assigned to $120$ patients each. Improved: $84$ of $120$ on treatment 1 and $66$ of $120$ on treatment 2. (Illustrative data.)

  1. Chi-square: the improved column total is $150$ and the not-improved total is $90$, so the expected counts are $75$ and $45$ in each row. Contributions are $\frac{(84-75)^2}{75}=1.08$ and $\frac{(36-45)^2}{45}=1.80$ in row 1, and the same in row 2. So $\chi^2=5.76$, $\text{df}=(2-1)(2-1)=1$, p-value $=0.0164$.
  2. Two-proportion $z$: $\hat p_1=0.70$, $\hat p_2=0.55$, pooled $\hat p=\frac{150}{240}=0.625$, and $SE=\sqrt{0.625(0.375)\left(\frac1{120}+\frac1{120}\right)}=0.0625$. Then $z=\frac{0.70-0.55}{0.0625}=2.4$, and the two-sided p-value is $0.0164$.
  3. The two agree: $z^2=2.4^2=5.76=\chi^2$, with the same p-value. Use the $z$ procedure if you need a one-sided alternative, the direction of the difference or a confidence interval. The chi-square test only reports that the distributions differ.

两种治疗方法被随机分配给患者，每组 $120$ 人。好转人数：治疗 1 有 $84$ 人（共 $120$ 人），治疗 2 有 $66$ 人（共 $120$ 人）。（虚构数据。）

  1. 卡方：好转一列合计为 $150$，未好转合计为 $90$，所以每行的期望计数为 $75$ 和 $45$。第一行的贡献为 $\frac{(84-75)^2}{75}=1.08$ 和 $\frac{(36-45)^2}{45}=1.80$，第二行相同。于是 $\chi^2=5.76$，$\text{df}=(2-1)(2-1)=1$，p 值 $=0.0164$。
  2. 双比例 $z$：$\hat p_1=0.70$，$\hat p_2=0.55$，合并比例 $\hat p=\frac{150}{240}=0.625$，$SE=\sqrt{0.625(0.375)\left(\frac1{120}+\frac1{120}\right)}=0.0625$。于是 $z=\frac{0.70-0.55}{0.0625}=2.4$，双侧 p 值为 $0.0164$。
  3. 两者一致：$z^2=2.4^2=5.76=\chi^2$，p 值相同。若需要单侧备择假设、差异的方向或置信区间，就用 $z$ 过程。卡方检验只能说明分布不同。

Common mistakes
  • Running a chi-square test on percentages, on means, or on quantitative measurements.
  • Using chi-square for a question that needs a direction, such as "is group 1 higher", where a one-sided $z$ test is needed.
  • Using goodness of fit when there are two categorical variables, or homogeneity when there is a single sample.
常见错误
  • 对百分比、均值或定量测量值做卡方检验。
  • 对需要方向的问题（如“第 1 组是否更高”）使用卡方，这时应使用单侧 $z$ 检验。
  • 有两个分类变量时用拟合优度检验，或只有一个样本时用同质性检验。

Unit quiz单元自测

1. A goodness-of-fit test compares one sample with a claimed distribution that has $5$ categories. What are the degrees of freedom?

Answer: B. $\text{df}=k-1=5-1=4$. The df depends on the number of categories, not the sample size.

1. 拟合优度检验把一个样本与有 $5$ 个类别的给定分布比较。自由度是多少？

答案：B。 $\text{df}=k-1=5-1=4$。自由度取决于类别数，而不是样本量。

2. In a two-way table the row total is $40$, the column total is $70$ and the table total is $200$. What is the expected count for that cell?

Answer: B. Multiply the two totals and divide by the table total: $\frac{(40)(70)}{200}=14$.

2. 双向表中某单元格所在行合计为 $40$，所在列合计为 $70$，表总计为 $200$。该单元格的期望计数是多少？

答案：B。 两个合计相乘再除以表总计：$\frac{(40)(70)}{200}=14$。

3. A chi-square test of independence uses a table with $3$ rows and $4$ columns of data. What are the degrees of freedom?

Answer: C. $\text{df}=(3-1)(4-1)=6$.

3. 卡方独立性检验使用的数据表有 $3$ 行 $4$ 列。自由度是多少？

答案：C。 $\text{df}=(3-1)(4-1)=6$。

4. Random samples of $100$ customers are taken from each of four stores, and each customer is classified by satisfaction level. Which procedure is appropriate?

Answer: B. There are separate random samples and one categorical variable, so the test of homogeneity applies. There is no single claimed distribution, only one sample would suggest independence, and the data are categorical, not quantitative.

4. 从四家商店各随机抽取 $100$ 名顾客，每位顾客按满意度分类。应使用哪种检验？

答案：B。 这里是分别抽取的随机样本且只有一个分类变量，所以应使用同质性检验。没有单一的给定分布；只有一个样本时才会考虑独立性检验；数据是分类的而不是定量的。

5. A die test gives $\chi^2=12.88$ with $\text{df}=5$ and p-value $0.0245$. What is the correct conclusion at $\alpha=0.01$?

Answer: B. Since $0.0245>0.01$ we fail to reject $H_0$. Failing to reject is not acceptance, and the test never states a direction.

5. 某骰子检验得到 $\chi^2=12.88$，$\text{df}=5$，p 值 $0.0245$。在 $\alpha=0.01$ 下正确的结论是什么？

答案：B。 因为 $0.0245>0.01$，不能拒绝 $H_0$。不能拒绝不等于接受，而且检验从不给出方向。

6. A $2\times 2$ chi-square test gives $\chi^2=5.76$. What is $|z|$ for the equivalent two-proportion $z$ test?

Answer: C. For a $2\times 2$ table $\chi^2=z^2$, so $|z|=\sqrt{5.76}=2.4$.

6. 一个 $2\times 2$ 卡方检验得到 $\chi^2=5.76$。$|z|$ 是多少？它对应等价的双比例 $z$ 检验。

答案：C。 对 $2\times 2$ 表有 $\chi^2=z^2$，所以 $|z|=\sqrt{5.76}=2.4$。

7. Every observed count in a two-way table is multiplied by $3$, so the proportions in each row do not change. What happens to the chi-square statistic?

Answer: B. Each expected count is also multiplied by $3$, and $\frac{(3O-3E)^2}{3E}=3\,\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$, so the statistic is multiplied by $3$.

7. 双向表中每个观测计数都乘以 $3$，因此每行的比例不变。卡方统计量会怎样变化？

答案：B。 每个期望计数也乘以 $3$，并且 $\frac{(3O-3E)^2}{3E}=3\,\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$，所以统计量变为原来的 $3$ 倍。

8. A random sample of $90$ people is classified into three categories with observed counts $40$, $30$ and $20$. The claim is that the three categories are equally likely. What are the chi-square statistic and the degrees of freedom?

Answer: C. Each expected count is $\frac{90}{3}=30$. The contributions are $\frac{(40-30)^2}{30}=3.33$, $0$ and $\frac{(20-30)^2}{30}=3.33$, so $\chi^2=6.67$ and $\text{df}=3-1=2$. The p-value is $0.036$. Dividing by the observed counts gives $7.50$, and leaving out the division gives $200$.

8. 对 $90$ 人的随机样本分为三类，观测计数为 $40$、$30$、$20$。给定的说法是三类的可能性相等。卡方统计量与自由度分别是多少？

答案：C。 每个期望计数为 $\frac{90}{3}=30$。各项贡献为 $\frac{(40-30)^2}{30}=3.33$、$0$ 和 $\frac{(20-30)^2}{30}=3.33$，所以 $\chi^2=6.67$，$\text{df}=3-1=2$。p 值为 $0.036$。除以观测计数得到 $7.50$，漏掉除法则得到 $200$。

9. A random sample of $400$ adults in a city is classified by region and by preferred way to commute. A chi-square test of independence gives a p-value of $0.003$. Which conclusion is best at $\alpha=0.05$?

Answer: B. Because $0.003<0.05$ we reject $H_0$, which gives evidence of an association in context. A survey is observational, so it cannot show cause, and the p-value is not the probability that $H_0$ is true.

9. 对某市 $400$ 名成年人的随机样本，按地区和首选通勤方式分类。卡方独立性检验得到 p 值 $0.003$。在 $\alpha=0.05$ 下哪个结论最恰当？

答案：B。 因为 $0.003<0.05$，拒绝 $H_0$，这给出了结合情境的关联证据。调查是观察性的，不能说明因果；p 值也不是 $H_0$ 为真的概率。
