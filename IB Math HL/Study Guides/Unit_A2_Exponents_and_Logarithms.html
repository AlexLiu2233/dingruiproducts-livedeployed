Integer Exponents整数指数 SL 1.5

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State the seven integer exponent laws and the condition $a, b \ne 0$ under which they hold.
  • Explain why $a^{0} = 1$ and $a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^{n}}$ are forced by the product law.
  • Simplify products and quotients of powers, giving the answer with positive exponents.
  • Distinguish $(ab)^{n} = a^{n}b^{n}$ (true) from $(a+b)^{n} = a^{n}+b^{n}$ (false).
  • Solve equations such as $2^{x} \cdot 4^{x+1} = 32$ by writing both sides as powers of one base.
  • 写出（state）七条整数指数律，以及它们成立的条件 $a, b \ne 0$。
  • 解释（explain）为何乘积律迫使 $a^{0} = 1$ 与 $a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^{n}}$ 成立。
  • 化简（simplify）幂的乘积与商，结果写成正指数形式。
  • 区分 $(ab)^{n} = a^{n}b^{n}$（正确）与 $(a+b)^{n} = a^{n}+b^{n}$（错误）。
  • 把两边写成同一底数的幂，求解（solve）$2^{x} \cdot 4^{x+1} = 32$ 这类方程。
Seven exponent laws cover every manipulation in this unit. For $a, b \ne 0$ and integers $m, n$: $$ a^{m}\,a^{n} = a^{m+n}, \quad \frac{a^{m}}{a^{n}} = a^{m-n}, \quad (a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn}, \quad (ab)^{n} = a^{n}b^{n}, $$ $$ \left(\tfrac{a}{b}\right)^{n} = \tfrac{a^{n}}{b^{n}}, \qquad a^{0} = 1, \qquad a^{-n} = \tfrac{1}{a^{n}}. $$ These hold for any integer $n$ (positive, negative, zero) and extend to rational and real $n$ once we redefine "exponentiation" via roots and limits.
七条指数运算律覆盖了本单元所有指数化简。对 $a, b \ne 0$、整数 $m, n$： $$ a^{m}\,a^{n} = a^{m+n}, \quad \frac{a^{m}}{a^{n}} = a^{m-n}, \quad (a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn}, \quad (ab)^{n} = a^{n}b^{n}, $$ $$ \left(\tfrac{a}{b}\right)^{n} = \tfrac{a^{n}}{b^{n}}, \qquad a^{0} = 1, \qquad a^{-n} = \tfrac{1}{a^{n}}. $$ 这些对任意整数 $n$（正、负、零）均成立；通过根式与极限的延拓，可推广到分数与实数 $n$。
Definition For a positive integer $n$, $a^{n}$ is defined as repeated multiplication: $a^{n} = \underbrace{a \cdot a \cdots a}_{n\text{ factors}}$. The definition is extended to $n = 0$ by setting $a^{0} = 1$ (for $a \ne 0$), and to negative integers $n = -k$ by $a^{-k} = 1/a^{k}$. These extensions are forced by the requirement that the product rule $a^{m}a^{n} = a^{m+n}$ remain valid across all integer exponents.
定义 对正整数 $n$，$a^{n}$ 定义为反复相乘：$a^{n} = \underbrace{a \cdot a \cdots a}_{n\text{ 个因子}}$。再令 $a^{0} = 1$（$a \ne 0$），$a^{-k} = 1/a^{k}$（负整数）将定义推广。这些推广不是凭空规定，而是为了让乘积律 $a^{m}a^{n} = a^{m+n}$ 在所有整数指数上仍成立。
▸ Going deeper: why $a^{0} = 1$ and $a^{-n} = 1/a^{n}$▸ 深入：为什么 $a^{0} = 1$ 与 $a^{-n} = 1/a^{n}$

Start from the product rule $a^{m}a^{n} = a^{m+n}$, which is obvious for positive integer exponents (just count factors). Apply it with $n = 0$:

从对正整数显然成立的乘积律 $a^{m}a^{n} = a^{m+n}$ 出发（数因子即可证明）。取 $n = 0$：

$$ a^{m} \cdot a^{0} \;=\; a^{m+0} \;=\; a^{m}. $$

Dividing both sides by $a^{m}$ (which requires $a \ne 0$) gives $a^{0} = 1$. Then apply the rule with $m + n = 0$, i.e. $n = -m$:

两边除以 $a^{m}$（要求 $a \ne 0$）得 $a^{0} = 1$。再取 $m + n = 0$，即 $n = -m$：

$$ a^{m} \cdot a^{-m} \;=\; a^{0} \;=\; 1 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad a^{-m} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{a^{m}}. $$

So both definitions are forced: they're the unique extensions of the integer-power definition that preserve the product rule. This is the standard "extension by consistency" argument that runs through all of A2 (and reappears in A4 for $i^{0}$, $i^{-1}$).

所以这两个定义是被迫的：它们是保持乘积律成立的唯一推广。这就是贯穿整个 A2 的"按一致性延拓"思路（A4 中关于 $i^{0}$、$i^{-1}$ 的处理也是同一个套路）。

Simplify $\bigl(4x^{0}y^{-2}\bigr)^{-1} \cdot \bigl(2x^{-3}y\bigr)^{2}$ for $x, y \ne 0$.化简 $\bigl(4x^{0}y^{-2}\bigr)^{-1} \cdot \bigl(2x^{-3}y\bigr)^{2}$（$x, y \ne 0$）。

Clear the zero exponent first. $x^{0} = 1$ regardless of what $x$ is, so it drops out of the first bracket entirely:

先消去零指数。无论 $x$ 取何值，$x^{0} = 1$，第一个括号里的 $x^{0}$ 直接消失：

$$ \bigl(4x^{0}y^{-2}\bigr)^{-1} \;=\; \bigl(4y^{-2}\bigr)^{-1} \;=\; 4^{-1}y^{2} \;=\; \frac{y^{2}}{4}. $$

Expand the second bracket. Apply $(ab)^{n} = a^{n}b^{n}$:

展开第二个括号。用 $(ab)^{n} = a^{n}b^{n}$：

$$ \bigl(2x^{-3}y\bigr)^{2} \;=\; 2^{2}x^{-6}y^{2} \;=\; 4x^{-6}y^{2}. $$

Multiply the two results.

相乘两个结果。

$$ \frac{y^{2}}{4} \cdot 4x^{-6}y^{2} \;=\; x^{-6}y^{4} \;=\; \frac{y^{4}}{x^{6}}. $$

为何这题特别检验零指数律。常见错误是把 $x^{0}$ 当作让整项消失为 $0$，而不是化为乘法单位元 $1$，这正是上面"深入"推导从乘积律证明的那个区别。

Rational exponents are roots. For $a > 0$ and positive integer $n$: $$ a^{1/n} \;=\; \sqrt[n]{a}, \qquad a^{m/n} \;=\; \sqrt[n]{a^{m}} \;=\; \bigl(\sqrt[n]{a}\bigr)^{m}. $$ Combine with $a^{-k} = 1/a^{k}$ for negative rationals. All seven integer laws extend unchanged. Restriction: $a > 0$ avoids ambiguity (e.g. $(-1)^{1/2}$ is not real).
分数指数就是根式（radical）。对 $a > 0$、正整数 $n$： $$ a^{1/n} \;=\; \sqrt[n]{a}, \qquad a^{m/n} \;=\; \sqrt[n]{a^{m}} \;=\; \bigl(\sqrt[n]{a}\bigr)^{m}. $$ 负分数指数再配上 $a^{-k} = 1/a^{k}$。所有七条整数指数律原样适用。限制：取 $a > 0$ 以避免歧义（例如 $(-1)^{1/2}$ 在实数中无定义）。
Why $a^{1/n} = \sqrt[n]{a}$ The defining property of $\sqrt[n]{a}$ is "the unique positive number whose $n$-th power is $a$." For the exponent definition to extend consistently (preserving $(a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn}$), we need $a^{1/n}$ to satisfy $(a^{1/n})^{n} = a^{(1/n) \cdot n} = a^{1} = a$. Only one positive number does that: $\sqrt[n]{a}$.
为何 $a^{1/n} = \sqrt[n]{a}$ $\sqrt[n]{a}$ 的定义性质是"$n$ 次幂等于 $a$ 的唯一正数"。要让指数律 $(a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn}$ 在分数情形仍成立，$a^{1/n}$ 必须满足 $(a^{1/n})^{n} = a^{(1/n) \cdot n} = a^{1} = a$。能满足这一条件的唯一正数就是 $\sqrt[n]{a}$。

Common Mistake: Negative bases and rational exponents The restriction $a > 0$ above is not cosmetic. For an odd denominator, a negative base still has a real root: $(-8)^{1/3} = \sqrt[3]{-8} = -2$. But rewrite the same exponent with an even numerator and denominator, $1/3 = 2/6$, and $\bigl((-8)^{2}\bigr)^{1/6} = 64^{1/6} = 2$, the wrong sign, even though $1/3$ and $2/6$ are the same fraction. Squaring first destroys the sign information that the odd cube root needed. This is also why calculators sometimes reject or disagree on $(-8)^{1/3}$: many evaluate $a^{m/n}$ internally as $e^{(m/n)\ln a}$, and $\ln$ of a negative number has no real value. Rule of thumb: simplify the fractional exponent to lowest terms before deciding whether a negative base is defined, and never cancel a common factor between numerator and denominator once a root of a negative number is involved.
常见错误：负底数与分数指数 上面 $a > 0$ 的限制并非可有可无。当分母为奇数时，负底数仍有实数根：$(-8)^{1/3} = \sqrt[3]{-8} = -2$。但若把同一个指数写成分子分母都为偶数的形式 $1/3 = 2/6$，则 $\bigl((-8)^{2}\bigr)^{1/6} = 64^{1/6} = 2$，符号错了，尽管 $1/3$ 与 $2/6$ 本是同一个分数。先平方会抹去奇次根所依赖的符号信息。这也是为何计算器有时会拒算或对 $(-8)^{1/3}$ 给出不同结果：许多计算器内部把 $a^{m/n}$ 按 $e^{(m/n)\ln a}$ 求值，而负数的 $\ln$ 在实数范围内无定义。经验法则：先把分数指数化到最简，再判断负底数是否有定义；一旦涉及负数开根，切勿随意约去分子分母的公因数。
