Q1EASY 4.1 Linear Momentum4.1 线动量No Calculator

A $0.50~\mathrm{kg}$ ball travels east at $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$. The magnitude of its momentum is一个质量为 $0.50~\mathrm{kg}$ 的小球以 $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 的速度向东运动。其动量大小为

  • (A) $0.5~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$
  • (B) $2.0~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$
  • (C) $4.0~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$
  • (D) $8.0~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$
Answer:答案： (B)
$$|\vec p| = m v = 0.50(4.0) = 2.0~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$$
Trap (D) squares $v$: $0.50 \times 4.0^2 = 8.0$; (C) drops $m$ and leaves $4.0$; (A) drops $v$ and leaves $0.5$.干扰项 (D) 把 $v$ 平方：$0.50 \times 4.0^2 = 8.0$；(C) 漏乘 $m$，只剩 $4.0$；(A) 漏乘 $v$，只剩 $0.5$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $0.5~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$ comes from dropping the speed $v$ and keeping only the mass $m=0.50~\mathrm{kg}$. (C) $4.0~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$ comes from dropping the mass $m$ and setting the momentum equal to the speed $v=4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$. (D) $8.0~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$ comes from squaring the speed as in kinetic energy, $m v^2=0.50(4.0)^2=8.0$.(A) $0.5~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$ 来自只保留质量 $m=0.50~\mathrm{kg}$ 而漏掉速度 $v$。 (C) $4.0~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$ 来自只保留速度 $v=4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 而漏掉质量 $m$。 (D) $8.0~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$ 来自把速度平方，误用动能形式，$m v^2=0.50(4.0)^2=8.0$。
Insight.要点。 Momentum is mass times velocity, so its magnitude is $mv$ and it carries direction. Do not confuse momentum with kinetic energy, which scales as $v^2$ and is always nonnegative.动量等于质量乘速度，其大小为 $mv$ 且具有方向。不要与动能混淆；动能按 $v^2$ 变化且恒为非负。
Q2EASY 4.2 Impulse4.2 冲量No Calculator

A constant force of $10~\mathrm{N}$ acts on an object for $3.0~\mathrm{s}$. The magnitude of the impulse delivered to the object is一个 $10~\mathrm{N}$ 的恒力作用在某物体上，持续时间为 $3.0~\mathrm{s}$。该力对物体施加的冲量大小为

  • (A) $3.3~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$
  • (B) $13~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$
  • (C) $30~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$
  • (D) $300~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$J = F\,\Delta t = 10(3.0) = 30~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$$
Trap (D) is $J g$ (extra factor of 10).干扰项 (D) 多乘了 $g$（额外的 10 倍因子）。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $3.3~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ comes from using the wrong impulse formula $J=F/\Delta t$ while still labeling the answer with impulse units, $10/3.0\approx 3.3~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$. (B) $13~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ comes from adding the numerical force and time as if their units matched, $10+3.0=13$. (D) $300~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ comes from treating the $10~\mathrm{N}$ force as a $10~\mathrm{kg}$ mass, computing its weight $10(10)=100~\mathrm{N}$ with $g\approx 10~\mathrm{m/s^2}$, and then multiplying by $3.0~\mathrm{s}$: $100\times 3.0=300$.(A) $3.3~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ 来自误用冲量公式 $J=F/\Delta t$，却仍按冲量单位书写，$10/3.0\approx 3.3~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$。(B) $13~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ 来自把力与时间的数值当作单位相同直接相加，$10+3.0=13$。(D) $300~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ 来自把 $10~\mathrm{N}$ 的力当成 $10~\mathrm{kg}$ 的质量，用 $g\approx 10~\mathrm{m/s^2}$ 算重量 $10(10)=100~\mathrm{N}$，再乘 $3.0~\mathrm{s}$：$100\times 3.0=300$。
Insight.要点。 Impulse is the product of a constant force and its duration: $J=F\Delta t$. Its units are $N\cdot s$, equivalent to $kg\cdot m/s$.冲量是恒力与作用时间的乘积：$J=F\Delta t$。其单位是 $N\cdot s$，等价于 $kg\cdot m/s$。
Q3EASY 4.3 Conservation of Momentum4.3 动量守恒No Calculator

Two carts of equal mass sit at rest on a frictionless track, in contact with a compressed spring between them. After the spring is released, cart A moves left at $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$. Cart B then moves两辆质量相等的小车静止在无摩擦轨道上，中间夹着一根压缩弹簧。弹簧释放后，小车 A 以 $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 向左运动，则小车 B 的运动情况为

  • (A) right at $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$以 $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 向右
  • (B) right at $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$以 $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 向右
  • (C) left at $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$以 $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 向左
  • (D) right at $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$以 $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 向右
Answer:答案： (B)
Total $\vec p$ was zero at rest and stays zero (no external horizontal force). With equal masses, equal-and-opposite speeds:系统静止时总动量 $\vec p$ 为零，无水平外力故保持为零。质量相等，速度大小相等方向相反：
$$m(-2.0) + m\,v_B = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; v_B = +2.0~\mathrm{m/s}~\text{(right)}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from halving cart A's speed after writing equal masses as sharing the recoil equally, $v_B=2.0/2=1.0$. (C) $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ left comes from keeping the same sign for both final velocities, $m(-2.0)+m(-2.0)\neq0$, instead of giving B the opposite momentum. (D) $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from computing the relative speed of separation, $2.0+2.0=4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$, and reporting that relative speed as cart B's velocity in the track frame.(A) $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把等质量两车的反冲速度再平分，$v_B=2.0/2=1.0$。(C) $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 向左来自给两车末速度保留同一符号，$m(-2.0)+m(-2.0)\neq0$，而没有给 B 相反的动量。(D) $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把两车分离的相对速度 $2.0+2.0=4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 当成 B 在轨道参考系中的速度。
Insight.要点。 With no external horizontal force, the total momentum of an initially resting system stays zero. Equal masses then recoil with equal and opposite speeds, regardless of the internal spring details.无水平外力时，初始静止系统的总动量保持为零。质量相等时，两物体以大小相等、方向相反的速度反冲，与内部弹簧细节无关。
Q4EASY 4.4 Inelastic vs. Elastic4.4 非弹性碰撞与弹性碰撞No Calculator

In which type of collision is the total kinetic energy of the system not conserved?在哪种碰撞类型中，系统的总动能不守恒？

  • (A) Elastic collision弹性碰撞
  • (B) Perfectly inelastic collision完全非弹性碰撞
  • (C) Two billiard balls colliding glancingly without spinning两个台球在无自旋情况下的斜碰
  • (D) Two pucks bouncing off a frictionless wall两个冰球从无摩擦墙壁反弹
Answer:答案： (B)
Perfectly inelastic collisions have the maximum possible KE loss (the two bodies share a common final velocity). Elastic collisions preserve KE by definition; idealized billiard and frictionless-wall scenarios are taken as elastic. For the colliding pair in (A) to (C) momentum is conserved and only KE differs; in (D) the wall exerts an external impulse, so the pucks' momentum changes while their KE is kept.完全非弹性碰撞的动能损失最大（两物体碰后共享同一末速度）。弹性碰撞根据定义保持动能守恒；理想化的台球碰撞和无摩擦墙壁反弹均视为弹性碰撞。在 (A) 至 (C) 中，碰撞双方的总动量守恒，差别仅在于动能；在 (D) 中，墙壁施加外部冲量，冰球的动量改变而动能保持不变。
Insight.要点。 Momentum is conserved in every collision, but kinetic energy is not conserved in inelastic collisions. The classification depends only on kinetic energy; momentum never becomes optional.所有碰撞中动量都守恒，但非弹性碰撞中动能不守恒。碰撞分类只取决于动能；动量守恒始终成立。
Q5MEDIUM 4.2 Impulse from $F$-$t$ Graph4.2 由 $F$-$t$ 图求冲量No Calculator

A force exerted on a $2.0~\mathrm{kg}$ object varies linearly from $0$ at $t = 0$ to $20~\mathrm{N}$ at $t = 0.40~\mathrm{s}$, then drops back to $0$ at $t = 0.80~\mathrm{s}$ (a triangular pulse). The object starts at rest. Its speed at $t = 0.80~\mathrm{s}$ is作用在 $2.0~\mathrm{kg}$ 物体上的力从 $t = 0$ 时的 $0$ 线性增大到 $t = 0.40~\mathrm{s}$ 时的 $20~\mathrm{N}$，再降回 $t = 0.80~\mathrm{s}$ 时的 $0$（三角脉冲）。物体由静止开始，$t = 0.80~\mathrm{s}$ 时的速度大小为

  • (A) $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $8.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $16~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (B)
Impulse is the area under the $F$-$t$ curve:冲量等于 $F$-$t$ 曲线下的面积：
$$J = \tfrac{1}{2}\,\text{base}\,\cdot\,\text{height} = \tfrac{1}{2}(0.80)(20) = 8.0~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$$
Then $\Delta p = J \Rightarrow v = J/m = 8.0/2.0 = 4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$. Trap (C) uses a rectangular pulse (no half-factor).则 $\Delta p = J \Rightarrow v = J/m = 8.0/2.0 = 4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$。干扰项 (C) 按矩形脉冲计算（漏掉了 $\tfrac{1}{2}$ 因子）。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from using only the rising half of the pulse, $J=\tfrac{1}{2}(0.40)(20)=4.0~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$, then $v=4.0/2.0=2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$. (C) $8.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from using a rectangular area without the half factor, $J=(0.80)(20)=16~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$, then $v=16/2.0=8.0~\mathrm{m/s}$. (D) $16.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from using that rectangular impulse as the speed without dividing by $m$, $0.80(20)=16$.(A) $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自只用三角脉冲上升的一半，$J=\tfrac{1}{2}(0.40)(20)=4.0~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$，再算 $v=4.0/2.0=2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$。 (C) $8.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把面积按矩形算而漏掉二分之一因子，$J=(0.80)(20)=16~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$，再算 $v=16/2.0=8.0~\mathrm{m/s}$。 (D) $16.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把这个矩形冲量直接当作速度而没有除以 $m$，$0.80(20)=16$。
Insight.要点。 Impulse equals the area under an $F$-$t$ graph. For a triangular pulse, use $\tfrac12\,\text{base}\cdot\text{height}$ before dividing by mass to find the speed change.冲量等于 $F$-$t$ 图下的面积。对于三角脉冲，先按 $\tfrac12\times$ 底 $\times$ 高求面积，再除以质量得速度变化。
Q6MEDIUM 4.2 Vector Change in Momentum4.2 动量的矢量变化No Calculator

A $0.20~\mathrm{kg}$ ball strikes a wall horizontally at $5.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ and rebounds straight back at $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$. The magnitude of the impulse delivered by the wall to the ball is一个 $0.20~\mathrm{kg}$ 的小球以 $5.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 水平撞墙，以 $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 原路反弹。墙壁对小球施加的冲量大小为

  • (A) $0.20~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$
  • (B) $1.0~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$
  • (C) $1.8~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$
  • (D) $9.0~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$
Answer:答案： (C)
Treat incoming as $+x$. Then $v_i = +5$, $v_f = -4$ (rebound reverses direction).以入射方向为 $+x$，则 $v_i = +5$，$v_f = -4$（反弹方向相反）。
$$\Delta p = m(v_f - v_i) = 0.20(-4 - 5) = -1.8~\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$$
Magnitude is $1.8~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$. Trap (B) computes $m|v_f - v_i|/2$ (or treats the rebound speed as not changing sign).大小为 $1.8~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$。干扰项 (B) 计算了 $m|v_f - v_i|/2$（或未考虑反弹速度的方向变化）。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $0.20~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ comes from subtracting the speeds without reversing the rebound sign, $0.20(5.0-4.0)=0.20$. (B) $1.0~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ comes from using only the incoming momentum, $m|v_i|=0.20(5.0)=1.0$, and ignoring the rebound momentum. (D) $9.0~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ comes from adding the speeds and omitting the mass, $5.0+4.0=9.0$.(A) $0.20~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ 来自没有把反弹速度改号就直接相减，$0.20(5.0-4.0)=0.20$。 (B) $1.0~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ 来自只用入射动量，$m|v_i|=0.20(5.0)=1.0$，漏掉反弹动量。 (D) $9.0~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$ 来自把速度相加并漏掉质量，$5.0+4.0=9.0$。
Insight.要点。 A rebound changes the sign of the velocity, so $\Delta v=v_f-v_i$ has a larger magnitude than the simple speed difference. Choose one direction as positive and keep it through the subtraction.反弹使速度方向改变，因此 $\Delta v=v_f-v_i$ 的大小大于单纯的速率差。选定一个正方向，并在相减过程中保持一致。
Q7MEDIUM 4.4 Perfectly Inelastic Collision4.4 完全非弹性碰撞No Calculator

A $3.0~\mathrm{kg}$ cart moving at $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ collides with and sticks to a stationary $1.0~\mathrm{kg}$ cart on a frictionless track. The speed of the combined carts immediately after the collision is一辆质量为 $3.0~\mathrm{kg}$、速度为 $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 的小车在无摩擦轨道上与静止的 $1.0~\mathrm{kg}$ 小车碰撞并粘在一起。碰后两车合体的速度大小为

  • (A) $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $3.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$m_1 v_1 = (m_1 + m_2)\,v' \;\Longrightarrow\; v' = \frac{3.0(4.0)}{4.0} = 3.0~\mathrm{m/s}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from dividing the initial speed rather than the initial momentum by the total mass, $4.0/(3.0+1.0)=1.0$. (B) $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from using the elastic unequal-mass formula with the given final mass of the moving cart, $(3.0-1.0)/(3.0+1.0)(4.0)=2.0$, instead of a sticking collision. (D) $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from assuming the moving cart keeps its speed and the sticking does not slow it, $v'=v_1=4.0$.(A) $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自用总质量去除初速度而不是初始动量，$4.0/(3.0+1.0)=1.0$。 (B) $2.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把完全非弹性碰撞误用成不等质量弹性公式，$(3.0-1.0)/(3.0+1.0)(4.0)=2.0$。 (D) $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自认为运动小车保持原速，粘连不会使它减速，$v'=v_1=4.0$。
Insight.要点。 For a perfectly inelastic collision, use momentum conservation with the combined mass: $m_1v_1=(m_1+m_2)v'$. The final speed is the momentum divided by the total mass, not by either mass alone.完全非弹性碰撞中，用合体质量列动量守恒：$m_1v_1=(m_1+m_2)v'$。末速度等于总动量除以总质量，而不是除以某一个质量。

A $60~\mathrm{kg}$ skater stands at rest on frictionless ice and throws a $3.0~\mathrm{kg}$ ball horizontally at $8.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the ground. The skater's recoil speed is一名 $60~\mathrm{kg}$ 的溜冰者静止站在无摩擦冰面上，水平抛出一个 $3.0~\mathrm{kg}$ 的球，球相对地面的速度为 $8.0~\mathrm{m/s}$。溜冰者的反冲速度大小为

  • (A) $0.20~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $0.40~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $1.6~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $8.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (B)
Total $\vec p$ stays zero:总动量 $\vec p$ 保持为零：
$$60\,v_s + 3.0(8.0) = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; |v_s| = \frac{24}{60} = 0.40~\mathrm{m/s}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $0.20~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from halving the ball momentum before recoil, $(3.0(8.0)/2)/60=0.20$, as if the skater shares only half the momentum. (C) $1.6~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from confusing momentum with kinetic energy, setting $60v_s=\tfrac12(3.0)(8.0)^2=96$, so $v_s=1.6$. (D) $8.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from assuming the skater recoils with the ball's speed and omitting the mass ratio, $v_s=v_b=8.0$.(A) $0.20~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自先把球的动量减半再反冲，$(3.0(8.0)/2)/60=0.20$，误以为滑冰者只分担一半动量。 (C) $1.6~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把动量与动能混淆，写成 $60v_s=\tfrac12(3.0)(8.0)^2=96$，得 $v_s=1.6$。 (D) $8.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自认为滑冰者以球的速度反冲并漏掉质量比，$v_s=v_b=8.0$。
Insight.要点。 Recoil is momentum conservation from rest: $0=m_sv_s+m_bv_b$. The lighter object gets the larger speed, but the momenta of the two pieces are equal and opposite.反冲是静止系统动量守恒的应用：$0=m_sv_s+m_bv_b$。质量小的物体速度更大，但两部分的动量大小相等、方向相反。
Q9MEDIUM 4.4 Equal-Mass Elastic Collision4.4 等质量弹性碰撞No Calculator

In a one-dimensional elastic collision, a moving puck strikes an identical stationary puck. Immediately after the collision,在一维弹性碰撞中，一个运动的冰球撞击一个相同质量且静止的冰球。碰撞后瞬间，

  • (A) the moving puck stops and the target puck takes on its original velocity.运动的冰球静止，目标冰球获得原来的速度。
  • (B) both pucks move forward with half the original speed.两个冰球均以原速度的一半向前运动。

For equal-mass 1-D elastic collisions, momentum and KE conservation force a full velocity exchange (use the standard $v_1' = ((m_1-m_2)/(m_1+m_2))v_1$ with $m_1 = m_2$ to get $v_1' = 0$, $v_2' = v_1$). Trap (B) would conserve momentum but lose KE; (C) would conserve KE but not momentum.对于等质量一维弹性碰撞，动量和动能守恒要求速度完全交换（代入标准公式 $v_1' = ((m_1-m_2)/(m_1+m_2))v_1$，当 $m_1 = m_2$ 时得 $v_1' = 0$，$v_2' = v_1$）。干扰项 (B) 动量守恒但动能不守恒；(C) 动能守恒但动量不守恒。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) both pucks move forward with half the original speed comes from applying the perfectly inelastic equal-mass result, common speed $v/2$, instead of an elastic collision. (C) the moving puck rebounds at full speed and the target stays at rest comes from treating the identical target as a fixed wall, which is the infinite-mass limit, not equal masses. (D) both pucks come to rest comes from setting the total final momentum to zero even though the initial momentum is $mv$, not zero.(B) 两球都以原速度的一半向前运动 来自把完全非弹性等质量结果当作弹性碰撞，取共同速度 $v/2$。 (C) 运动球以原速反弹而目标球静止 来自把相同质量的目标球当成固定墙，这是无限大质量极限，不是等质量。 (D) 两球都静止 来自把最终总动量设为零，尽管初始动量是 $mv$ 而不是零。
Insight.要点。 In an equal-mass one-dimensional elastic collision with one initially at rest, the two objects simply exchange velocities. This is the cleanest special case of the elastic collision formulas.质量相等的一维弹性碰撞中，若一物体初始静止，则两物体交换速度。这是弹性碰撞公式最简洁的特例。

The fraction of the projectile's original kinetic energy that is dissipated (converted to heat, sound, deformation, etc.) is质量为 $m$、速度为 $v$ 的抛射体与质量为 $M$ 的静止靶体发生完全非弹性碰撞。抛射体原有动能中被耗散（转化为热能、声能、形变能等）的比例为

  • (A) $\dfrac{m}{m+M}$
  • (B) $\dfrac{M}{m+M}$
  • (C) $\dfrac{1}{2}$
  • (D) $0$
Answer:答案： (B)
Final speed $v' = m v/(m+M)$. Energy fraction retained:末速度 $v' = m v/(m+M)$。保留的动能比例：
$$\frac{K_f}{K_i} = \frac{\tfrac{1}{2}(m+M)v'^2}{\tfrac{1}{2} m v^2} = \frac{m}{m+M}$$
Hence fraction lost is $1 - m/(m+M) = M/(m+M)$. Limits: $M \to 0$ (no target) gives no loss; $M \to \infty$ (immovable target) gives full loss.故损失比例为 $1 - m/(m+M) = M/(m+M)$。极限情况：$M \to 0$（无靶体）无损失；$M \to \infty$（不可移动靶体）全部损失。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $\dfrac{m}{m+M}$ comes from computing the retained fraction $K_f/K_i=m/(m+M)$ and reporting it as the lost fraction. (C) $\dfrac{1}{2}$ comes from assuming every perfectly inelastic collision with a stationary target loses half the kinetic energy, which is only the equal-mass case. (D) $0$ comes from assuming a perfectly inelastic collision conserves kinetic energy because momentum is conserved.(A) $\dfrac{m}{m+M}$ 来自算出了保留比例 $K_f/K_i=m/(m+M)$，却把它当作损失比例。 (C) $\dfrac{1}{2}$ 来自认为每个打静止目标的完全非弹性碰撞都损失一半动能，而这只是等质量情形。 (D) $0$ 来自认为完全非弹性碰撞因为动量守恒所以动能也守恒。
Insight.要点。 In a perfectly inelastic collision with a stationary target, the retained kinetic-energy fraction is $m/(m+M)$, so the lost fraction is $M/(m+M)$. Check the limits $M\to 0$ and $M\to\infty$.与静止靶体发生完全非弹性碰撞时，保留的动能比例为 $m/(m+M)$，损失比例为 $M/(m+M)$。用 $M\to 0$ 与 $M\to\infty$ 检验。

$$J = \int_0^{0.50} 24 t\,dt = \bigl[12 t^2\bigr]_0^{0.50} = 12(0.25) = 3.0~\mathrm{N \cdot s}$$
(b) Speed at $t = 0.50~\mathrm{s}$: $v = J/m = 3.0/0.50 = 6.0~\mathrm{m/s}$.$t = 0.50~\mathrm{s}$ 时的速度：$v = J/m = 3.0/0.50 = 6.0~\mathrm{m/s}$。

$$\bar F = \frac{J}{\Delta t} = \frac{3.0}{0.50} = 6.0~\mathrm{N},\qquad F_\text{peak} = F(0.50) = 12~\mathrm{N}$$
For a linear ramp from zero, $\bar F = F_\text{peak}/2$, consistent with the triangular-pulse identity (area $= \tfrac{1}{2}\,\text{base}\,\cdot\,\text{height}$).对于从零线性增大的力，$\bar F = F_\text{peak}/2$，与三角脉冲面积公式（面积 $= \tfrac{1}{2} \times$ 底 $\times$ 高）一致。

$$F = \frac{J}{\Delta t} = \frac{3.0}{0.10} = 30~\mathrm{N}$$

$$v_1' - v_2' = -(v_1 - v_2) = -(4.0 - (-2.0)) = -6.0$$
Combined with momentum $0.30 v_1' + 0.10 v_2' = 0.30(4.0) + 0.10(-2.0) = 1.0$:结合动量方程 $0.30 v_1' + 0.10 v_2' = 0.30(4.0) + 0.10(-2.0) = 1.0$：
$$0.30 v_1' + 0.10(v_1' + 6.0) = 1.0 \;\Longrightarrow\; 0.40 v_1' = 0.40 \;\Longrightarrow\; v_1' = 1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$$
$$v_2' = v_1' + 6.0 = 7.0~\mathrm{m/s}$$

$$K_i = \tfrac{1}{2}(0.30)(16) + \tfrac{1}{2}(0.10)(4) = 2.40 + 0.20 = 2.60~\mathrm{J}$$
$$K_f = \tfrac{1}{2}(0.30)(1) + \tfrac{1}{2}(0.10)(49) = 0.15 + 2.45 = 2.60~\mathrm{J}~\checkmark$$

$$v_\text{cm} = \frac{m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2}{m_1 + m_2} = \frac{1.0}{0.40} = +2.5~\mathrm{m/s}$$

$$K=\frac{p^2}{2m}:\qquad K_m=\frac{p^2}{2m},\qquad K_{2m}=\frac{p^2}{4m}\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;K_m=2K_{2m}$$

Puck A ($0.30~\mathrm{kg}$) slides at $5.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ along $+x$ and strikes stationary puck B ($0.30~\mathrm{kg}$) on a frictionless table. After the collision, A moves at $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ at $36.87^\circ$ above the $+x$ axis. External horizontal impulse during contact is negligible.冰球 A（$0.30~\mathrm{kg}$）以 $5.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 沿 $+x$ 方向滑动，在无摩擦桌面上撞击静止的冰球 B（$0.30~\mathrm{kg}$）。碰后 A 以 $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 沿 $+x$ 轴上方 $36.87^\circ$ 方向运动。接触期间外部水平冲量可忽略。

(a) Velocity of B.B 的速度。
$$\vec v_{Af}=4.0(\cos 36.87^\circ,\ \sin 36.87^\circ)=(3.2,\ 2.4)~\mathrm{m/s}$$
Equal masses, so momentum conservation becomes $\vec u_A=\vec v_{Af}+\vec v_{Bf}$:两球质量相等，动量守恒化为 $\vec u_A=\vec v_{Af}+\vec v_{Bf}$：
$$\vec v_{Bf}=(5.0-3.2,\ 0-2.4)=(1.8,\ -2.4)~\mathrm{m/s},\qquad |\vec v_{Bf}|=\sqrt{1.8^2+2.4^2}=3.0~\mathrm{m/s}$$
Direction: $\tan^{-1}(2.4/1.8)\approx 53.1^\circ$ below the $+x$ axis.方向：$+x$ 轴下方 $\tan^{-1}(2.4/1.8)\approx 53.1^\circ$。
(b) Elastic and perpendicular.弹性与垂直。
$$K_i=\tfrac12(0.30)(5.0)^2=3.75~\mathrm{J},\qquad K_f=\tfrac12(0.30)(4.0^2+3.0^2)=3.75~\mathrm{J}$$
$$\vec v_{Af}\cdot\vec v_{Bf}=(3.2)(1.8)+(2.4)(-2.4)=5.76-5.76=0$$
Kinetic energy is unchanged, so the collision is elastic; the zero dot product shows the final velocities are perpendicular ($36.87^\circ+53.13^\circ=90^\circ$).动能不变，故为弹性碰撞；点积为零说明两末速度互相垂直（$36.87^\circ+53.13^\circ=90^\circ$）。
(c) General proof.一般性证明。 With equal masses and B initially at rest, momentum and kinetic-energy conservation give在质量相等且 B 初始静止时，动量守恒与动能守恒分别给出
$$\vec u=\vec v_A+\vec v_B,\qquad u^2=v_A^2+v_B^2$$
Squaring the first equation (dot it with itself):将第一式自身点乘（平方）：
$$u^2=v_A^2+v_B^2+2\,\vec v_A\cdot\vec v_B\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;\vec v_A\cdot\vec v_B=0$$
So whenever both final velocities are nonzero (a glancing collision), they are perpendicular. It is not an accident of these numbers; it uses all three assumptions: equal masses, a stationary target, and an elastic collision.因此只要两个末速度都不为零（斜碰），它们就互相垂直。这不是本题数据的巧合，而是同时用到了三个假设：质量相等、靶球静止、弹性碰撞。
(d) Perfectly inelastic version.完全非弹性情形。
$$(0.30)(5.0)=(0.60)V\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\;V=2.5~\mathrm{m/s}$$
The joined pucks move along $+x$, the direction of the original momentum.粘合后的冰球沿 $+x$ 方向运动，即原动量的方向。
$$K_f=\tfrac12(0.60)(2.5)^2=1.875~\mathrm{J},\qquad \frac{K_i-K_f}{K_i}=\frac{3.75-1.875}{3.75}=0.50$$
Half of the initial kinetic energy becomes internal energy, consistent with $m_B/(m_A+m_B)=\tfrac12$.一半初动能转化为内能，与 $m_B/(m_A+m_B)=\tfrac12$ 一致。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 False moves: (a) using only the original line, $v_{Bx}=5.0-4.0=1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ and $v_{By}=0$, which ignores A's northward component. (b) showing the collision is elastic by saying equal masses always make it elastic, without checking $K_i=K_f=3.75~\mathrm{J}$. (d) using only one puck's mass in the final kinetic energy, $K_f=\tfrac12(0.30)(2.5)^2=0.9375~\mathrm{J}$, and reporting a lost fraction $(3.75-0.9375)/3.75=0.75$ instead of $0.50$.错误做法：（a）只沿原来的一条直线算，$v_{Bx}=5.0-4.0=1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 且 $v_{By}=0$，漏掉 A 的向北分量。 （b）用等质量总是弹性来证明碰撞是弹性的，没有检查 $K_i=K_f=3.75~\mathrm{J}$。 （d）在末动能里只用一个小球的质量，$K_f=\tfrac12(0.30)(2.5)^2=0.9375~\mathrm{J}$，报出损失比例 $(3.75-0.9375)/3.75=0.75$ 而不是 $0.50$。
Insight.要点。 In two dimensions, conserve momentum as a vector, component by component; elasticity is an extra condition you must check with kinetic energy. For equal masses with a stationary target, "elastic" and "perpendicular final velocities" are the same statement.二维问题中要把动量当作矢量逐分量守恒；是否弹性是另外一个条件，必须用动能检验。对于靶球静止的等质量碰撞，"弹性"与"末速度互相垂直"是同一个命题。

You have a $0.50~\mathrm{kg}$ cart A, a $1.00~\mathrm{kg}$ cart B, a level track, two motion sensors, a balance, and magnetic bumpers. You want to test whether momentum is conserved and whether a magnetic-bumper collision is consistent with being elastic. Take east as positive. In each trial B starts at rest; the measured velocities are:你有一辆 $0.50~\mathrm{kg}$ 的小车 A、一辆 $1.00~\mathrm{kg}$ 的小车 B、一条水平轨道、两个运动传感器、一台天平以及磁性缓冲器。你要检验动量是否守恒，以及磁性缓冲器碰撞是否符合弹性碰撞。取向东为正。每次实验中 B 初始静止，测得速度如下：

(a) Procedure.实验步骤。 Measure both cart masses with the balance. Level the track and choose east as positive. Use the two motion sensors to record signed velocities of both carts immediately before and immediately after contact. Keep the bumpers, collision location, and analysis windows fixed while varying A's incoming speed over at least four trials. The system is both carts. Use short before/after windows close to contact, and measure the coast-down acceleration in a run with no collision to estimate the external friction/tilt impulse.用天平测量两车质量。调平轨道并取向东为正。用两个运动传感器记录两车在接触前、后瞬间的带符号速度。保持缓冲器、碰撞位置与分析时间窗不变，改变 A 的入射速度，至少做四次实验。系统为两辆小车。选取紧邻接触的短时间窗，并在无碰撞的滑行实验中测量减速度，以估计摩擦或倾斜带来的外部冲量。
(b) Graph.作图。 For each trial compute $p_i=m_Av_{Ai}+m_Bv_{Bi}$ and $p_f=m_Av_{Af}+m_Bv_{Bf}$. Plot $p_f$ (vertical, $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$) against $p_i$ (horizontal, $\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$). (c) Best-fit slope.最佳拟合斜率。
$$(p_i,\ p_f)=(0.400,\ 0.395),\ (0.600,\ 0.590),\ (0.800,\ 0.780),\ (1.000,\ 0.970)~\mathrm{kg\cdot m/s}$$
$$\sum p_ip_f=2.106,\qquad \sum p_i^2=2.160,\qquad b=\frac{2.106}{2.160}\approx 0.975$$
The slope is $2.5\%$ below the ideal value of $1$, inside the $4\%$ uncertainty, so the data support momentum conservation at this precision. They do not prove the system was perfectly isolated.斜率比理想值 $1$ 低 $2.5\%$，在 $4\%$ 的不确定度之内，因此在该精度下数据支持动量守恒。但这并不能证明系统完全孤立。
(d) Kinetic-energy ratio, Trial 3.实验 3 的动能比。
$$K_i=\tfrac12(0.50)(1.60)^2=0.640~\mathrm{J},\qquad K_f=\tfrac12(0.50)(-0.52)^2+\tfrac12(1.00)(1.04)^2=0.608~\mathrm{J}$$
$$\frac{K_f}{K_i}=\frac{0.6084}{0.640}\approx 0.951$$
The $4.9\%$ shortfall is within the $8\%$ uncertainty, so the trial is consistent with an elastic collision (but does not prove it).$4.9\%$ 的差值在 $8\%$ 的不确定度之内，因此该次实验与弹性碰撞相符（但并不能证明它一定是弹性的）。
(e) Targeted check.针对性检验。 Accept any check whose outcome differs between the two causes. Model answer: repeat the runs with the carts moving in the opposite direction along the same track. A tilt impulse is fixed to the track, so its effect on the signed momentum change reverses relative to the motion; a sensor timing or scale bias follows the sensor and analysis convention instead. A separate coast-down measurement combined with varying the analysis-window width is also defensible.任何能使两种原因给出不同结果的检验均可接受。参考答案：让小车沿同一轨道反方向运动并重复实验。倾斜带来的冲量固定于轨道，因此它对带符号动量变化的影响相对于运动方向会反转；而传感器计时或比例偏差则随传感器和分析约定而定。单独测量滑行减速度并改变分析时间窗宽度，也是合理的方案。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 False moves: (b) plotting $v_{Bf}$ against $v_{Ai}$ and expecting a slope of $1$; this drops the masses, while the total momenta $p_i=m_Av_{Ai}+m_Bv_{Bi}$ and $p_f=m_Av_{Af}+m_Bv_{Bf}$ plotted against each other in either orientation give the ideal slope $1$ and intercept $0$. (d) computing only cart $A$'s final kinetic energy, $K_{A,f}=\tfrac12(0.50)(-0.52)^2=0.0676~\mathrm{J}$, and using $K_f/K_i=0.0676/0.640\approx 0.106$; this omits cart $B$'s $0.5408~\mathrm{J}$ and wrongly rejects an elastic outcome. (e) saying only to take more data or to reverse the carts without predicting how a track-fixed impulse and a sensor timing or scale bias differ; the point requires a check with distinct outcomes, such as a no-collision coast-down plus varying the analysis-window width, or an explicit bias model.错误操作：(b) 画 $v_{Bf}$ 对 $v_{Ai}$ 的图并期望斜率为 $1$；这样丢掉了质量，而总动量 $p_i=m_Av_{Ai}+m_Bv_{Bi}$ 与 $p_f=m_Av_{Af}+m_Bv_{Bf}$ 彼此作图（任一作为横轴）才给出理想斜率 $1$ 和截距 $0$。(d) 只计算小车 $A$ 的末动能 $K_{A,f}=\tfrac12(0.50)(-0.52)^2=0.0676~\mathrm{J}$，并用 $K_f/K_i=0.0676/0.640\approx 0.106$；这漏掉了小车 $B$ 的 $0.5408~\mathrm{J}$，因而错误地否定弹性结果。(e) 只说多取数据或把小车反向，却不说明轨道固定的冲量与传感器计时或尺度偏差会怎样给出不同结果；得分点要求检查方案能区分二者，例如无碰撞滑行测量并改变分析窗口宽度，或给出明确的偏差模型。
Insight.要点。 An experimental claim needs a system, a measurement interval, and a comparison with a stated uncertainty. "Close enough" is not a conclusion, and "human error" is not an explanation unless it predicts the direction of the deviation.实验结论需要明确系统、测量时间段，并与给定的不确定度比较。"差不多"不是结论；"人为误差"若不能预测偏差方向，也不算解释。
