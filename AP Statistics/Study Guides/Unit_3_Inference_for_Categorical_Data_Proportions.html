Topics 3.1 and 3.2 · Selecting statistical methods

Estimators and sampling distributions of proportions

Must know

An estimator is a statistic used to estimate a population parameter. The sample proportion $\hat p$ is an unbiased estimator of the population proportion $p$: across all samples of size $n$, the mean of $\hat p$ equals $p$. Its sampling distribution has standard deviation $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$, and it is approximately normal when $np\ge10$ and $n(1-p)\ge10$.

Inference starts with a question about a population and answers it from a sample. Before computing anything, name the parameter in words and in symbols, then name the statistic that estimates it. For a categorical variable with two outcomes, the parameter is the population proportion $p$, and the estimator is the sample proportion $\hat p$: the number of successes divided by the sample size.

$50$$\sqrt{0.42(0.58)/50}\approx0.0698$$np=21$ and $n(1-p)=29$, both at least 10
$100$$\sqrt{0.42(0.58)/100}\approx0.0494$$np=42$ and $n(1-p)=58$
$400$$\sqrt{0.42(0.58)/400}\approx0.0247$$np=168$ and $n(1-p)=232$

Synthetic teaching model used throughout this guide: a city population with true proportion 0.42. No column is a real survey.

Example 3.1 — Parameter, estimator, estimate
  1. The parameter is $p=0.42$, the true proportion of adults in the city who ride the express bus at least weekly.
  2. The estimator is $\hat p$, the proportion in a random sample of adults.
  3. In one sample of $n=100$, the estimate is $\hat p=0.47$. Estimates change from sample to sample; the parameter does not.
Example 3.2 — Describing the sampling distribution
  1. Center: the mean of $\hat p$ is $p=0.42$, so a single estimate of 0.47 is not evidence of bias.
  2. Spread: $\sqrt{0.42(0.58)/100}\approx0.0494$, so typical samples land within about 0.05 of 0.42.
  3. Shape: $np=42$ and $n(1-p)=58$ both clear 10, so the distribution is approximately normal and normal-area reasoning applies.
Go deeper: why the spread shrinks like $\sqrt{n}$

The standard deviation $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$ falls by a factor of 2 whenever the sample size is multiplied by 4: precision improves with the square root of the sample size, not linearly. The 10% condition also matters: sampling without replacement behaves like independent sampling when $n$ is no more than 10% of the population.

主题 3.1 与 3.2 · 选择统计方法

估计量与比例的抽样分布

必备知识

估计量是用来估计总体参数的统计量。样本比例 $\hat p$ 是总体比例 $p$ 的无偏估计量：在所有容量为 $n$ 的样本上，$\hat p$ 的均值等于 $p$。其抽样分布的标准差为 $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$；当 $np\ge10$ 且 $n(1-p)\ge10$ 时近似正态。

推断从关于总体的问题出发，用样本来回答。动手计算之前，先用文字和符号命名参数，再命名用于估计它的统计量。对于只有两种结果的分类变量，参数是总体比例 $p$，估计量是样本比例 $\hat p$：成功次数除以样本量。

  2. 估计量为 $\hat p$，即随机样本中的比例。

Topic 3.3 · Analyzing data

Constructing a confidence interval for a proportion

Must know

A one-proportion $z$-interval is $\hat p\pm z^{*}\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/n}$. The standard error uses the sample estimate $\hat p$, and $z^{*}$ is the critical value for the confidence level. Conditions: a random sample, the 10% condition, and large counts, $n\hat p\ge10$ and $n(1-\hat p)\ge10$.

The interval is a point estimate plus and minus a margin of error. Its center is the estimate; its width is set by the confidence level through $z^{*}$ and by the sample size through the standard error.

Confidence level$z^{*}$
80%1.282
90%1.645
95%1.960
99%2.576
Example 3.3 — A 95% interval for the breakfast survey
  1. Estimate: a random sample of 90 students finds 54 who eat breakfast daily, so $\hat p=54/90=0.60$.
  2. Conditions: the sample is random; 90 students is far less than 10% of the school; $n\hat p=54$ and $n(1-\hat p)=36$ are both at least 10.
  3. Standard error: $\sqrt{0.60(0.40)/90}\approx0.0516$ and the margin of error is $1.96(0.0516)\approx0.1012$.
  4. Interval: $0.60\pm0.1012$, or about $(0.499,\ 0.701)$. On the TI-84, STAT then TESTS then A:1-PropZInt with $x=54$, $n=90$, C-Level .95 returns the same interval.
Go deeper: what 95% confidence means

In repeated random sampling, about 95% of intervals built this way capture the true proportion $p$. The confidence attaches to the method, not to one interval: once computed, $(0.499,0.701)$ either contains $p$ or it does not. Saying there is a 95% chance that $p$ is inside this interval is the wrong interpretation.

主题 3.3 · 分析数据

构造比例的置信区间

必备知识

单比例 $z$ 区间为 $\hat p\pm z^{*}\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/n}$。标准误使用样本估计值 $\hat p$，$z^{*}$ 是置信水平对应的临界值。条件：随机样本、10% 条件、大计数条件（$n\hat p\ge10$ 且 $n(1-\hat p)\ge10$）。

区间由点估计加减误差范围构成：中心是估计值；宽度由置信水平（通过 $z^{*}$）与样本量（通过标准误）共同决定。

例 3.3 — 早餐调查的 95% 区间
  1. 估计：90 名学生的随机样本中有 54 人每天吃早餐，故 $\hat p=54/90=0.60$。
  2. 条件：样本随机；90 远小于全校的 10%；$n\hat p=54$ 与 $n(1-\hat p)=36$ 均不小于 10。

深入理解：95% 置信的含义

在重复随机抽样中，约 95% 的此种区间会包含真实比例 $p$。置信附着于方法而非某一个区间：一旦算出，$(0.499,0.701)$ 要么包含 $p$、要么不包含。说“$p$ 落在此区间内的概率是 95%”是错误的解释。

Topic 3.4 · Interpreting results

Justifying a claim from a confidence interval

Must know

A confidence interval evaluates a claim by what it contains. If the claimed value lies inside the interval, the data do not contradict it. If the entire interval lies on one side of the claimed value, the data provide evidence against the claim at that confidence level.

Use the interval from the same study. For the breakfast survey the 95% interval is $(0.499,0.701)$, so claims about $p$ are checked against the range 0.499 to 0.701, not against the single estimate 0.60.

Example 3.4 — Two claims, two verdicts
  1. Claim A: 65% of students eat breakfast daily. Since $0.65$ lies inside $(0.499,0.701)$, the data are consistent with this claim.
  2. Claim B: fewer than 45% of students eat breakfast daily. Since the whole interval lies above $0.45$, the data provide evidence against this claim.
  3. What the interval cannot do: confirm that $p$ equals a single value. Values near the endpoints are still possible, just less supported.
Go deeper: interval length and the size of a claim

A wide interval can be consistent with many claims at once; a narrow interval separates them. That is why sample size matters for decision-making: the same interval width that leaves two policies indistinguishable with $n=90$ may separate them with $n=900$, where the standard error is about one third as large.

主题 3.4 · 解释结果

用置信区间论证主张

必备知识

置信区间通过“包含哪些值”来评价主张。若主张的数值落在区间内，数据不与主张矛盾；若整个区间位于该数值的一侧，则在该置信水平下数据提供了反对该主张的证据。

应使用同一项研究得出的区间。早餐调查的 95% 区间为 $(0.499,0.701)$，因此关于 $p$ 的主张要与 0.499 至 0.701 这个范围比较，而不是与单点估计 0.60 比较。

例 3.4 — 两个主张，两种裁决
  1. 主张 A：65% 的学生每天吃早餐。由于 $0.65$ 落在 $(0.499,0.701)$ 内，数据与该主张一致。
  2. 主张 B：每天吃早餐的学生少于 45%。由于整个区间都在 $0.45$ 之上，数据提供了反对该主张的证据。
  3. 区间做不到的事：确认 $p$ 恰好等于某个具体值。靠近端点的值仍有可能，只是支持度更低。
深入理解：区间长度与主张的粒度

较宽的区间可以同时与许多主张相容，较窄的区间才能把它们区分开。这正是样本量对决策重要的原因：$n=90$ 时无法区分的两种方案，在 $n=900$ 时可能被区分开，因为标准误约为原来的三分之一。

Topics 3.5 and 3.6 · Setting up a test; p-values

Hypotheses for a proportion; p-values

Must know

Hypotheses are statements about the parameter $p$, never about $\hat p$: $H_0$ states a value $p_0$, and $H_a$ states one of $p>p_0$, $p\lt p_0$, or $p\ne p_0$. The test statistic is $z=\frac{\hat p-p_0}{\sqrt{p_0(1-p_0)/n}}$; the standard error uses the null value. The p-value is the probability, computed assuming $H_0$ is true, of a result at least as extreme as the observed one in the direction of $H_a$.

Alternativep-value regionExtremeness
$H_a:p>p_0$upper tail beyond $z$values above the observed one
$H_a:p\lt p_0$lower tail beyond $z$values below the observed one
$H_a:p\ne p_0$both tails, doubledvalues farther from $p_0$ in either direction
Example 3.5 — Setting up the breakfast test
  1. A student council claims that a majority of students would support a later start time. With a skeptical starting point, $H_0:p=0.5$ and $H_a:p>0.5$, where $p$ is the true proportion who support the change.
  2. Conditions: a random sample of 90 students; 90 is less than 10% of the school; the null model gives $np_0=45$ and $n(1-p_0)=45$, both at least 10.
  3. The observed value is $\hat p=0.60$; the test asks how unusual that is when $p=0.5$.
Example 3.6 — The test statistic and the p-value
  1. Standard error from the null: $\sqrt{0.5(0.5)/90}\approx0.0527$.
  2. Test statistic: $z=\frac{0.60-0.50}{0.0527}\approx1.897$.
  3. One-sided p-value: the upper-tail area beyond $z=1.897$ is about $0.0289$. On the TI-84, STAT then TESTS then 5:1-PropZTest with $p_0=0.5$, $x=54$, $n=90$, and $>p_0$ returns this p-value.
Go deeper: what a p-value is not

The p-value is not the probability that $H_0$ is true, and $1-p$ is not the probability that $H_a$ is true. It is a statement about the data under the null model: 0.0289 is the rate at which this test would produce a result at least this extreme if the population proportion were exactly 0.5 with a random sample of 90.

主题 3.5 与 3.6 · 提出假设与 p 值

比例的假设检验：假设与 p 值

必备知识

假设是关于参数 $p$ 的陈述，绝不写成 $\hat p$：$H_0$ 给出某个值 $p_0$，$H_a$ 取 $p>p_0$、$p\lt p_0$ 或 $p\ne p_0$ 之一。检验统计量 $z=\frac{\hat p-p_0}{\sqrt{p_0(1-p_0)/n}}$，标准误使用原假设的取值。p 值是在 $H_0$ 成立的前提下，得到与观测结果同样极端或更极端（方向与 $H_a$ 一致）的结果的概率。

备择假设p 值所在区域“更极端”的含义
$H_a:p>p_0$$z$ 以上的上尾高于观测值的比例
$H_a:p\lt p_0$$z$ 以下的下尾低于观测值的比例
$H_a:p\ne p_0$两侧尾部，需加倍无论方向、离 $p_0$ 更远的比例
例 3.5 — 建立早餐检验
  1. 学生会声称多数学生支持推迟上课时间。从保守的立场出发，$H_0:p=0.5$，$H_a:p>0.5$，其中 $p$ 为支持该变化的真实比例。
  2. 条件：90 名学生的随机样本；90 小于全校的 10%；原假设模型给出 $np_0=45$ 与 $n(1-p_0)=45$，均不小于 10。
  3. 观测值为 $\hat p=0.60$；检验要回答的是：当 $p=0.5$ 时，出现这样的结果有多不寻常。
例 3.6 — 检验统计量与 p 值
  1. 原假设下的标准误：$\sqrt{0.5(0.5)/90}\approx0.0527$。
  2. 检验统计量：$z=\frac{0.60-0.50}{0.0527}\approx1.897$。
  3. 单侧 p 值：$z=1.897$ 以上的上尾面积约为 $0.0289$。TI-84 上依次按 STAT、TESTS、5:1-PropZTest，输入 $p_0=0.5$、$x=54$、$n=90$，选择 $>p_0$，即得该 p 值。
深入理解：p 值不是什么

p 值不是“$H_0$ 为真的概率”，$1-p$ 也不是“$H_a$ 为真的概率”。它描述的是在原假设模型下数据的表现：若总体比例恰为 0.5、样本量为 90，该检验给出至少如此极端结果的比率是 0.0289。

Topics 3.7 and 3.8 · Carrying out a test; potential errors

Decisions, conclusions, and the two errors

Must know

Compare the p-value with the significance level $\alpha$. If p-value $<\alpha$, reject $H_0$; otherwise fail to reject $H_0$. The conclusion names the parameter in context and stops where the evidence stops: failing to reject is not proof that $H_0$ is true.

Decision$H_0$ true$H_0$ false
Reject $H_0$Type I error, probability $\alpha$Correct decision
Fail to reject $H_0$Correct decisionType II error, probability $\beta$
Example 3.7 — Completing the breakfast test
  1. With $\alpha=0.05$ and p-value $\approx0.0289$, reject $H_0$ because $0.0289<0.05$.
  2. Conclusion in context: there is convincing evidence that more than half of the school supports a later start time.
  3. What $\alpha=0.05$ means: if the true proportion were exactly 0.5, this test would wrongly reject in about 5% of samples of this size.
Example 3.8 — The two errors in this study
  1. Type I error: rejecting $H_0$ when $p=0.5$ is true, and acting as if a majority supports the change. The consequence is adopting a schedule based on a false signal.
  2. Type II error: failing to reject when $p>0.5$, and missing real majority support. The consequence is shelving a change students actually want.
  3. Power is $1-\beta$: the probability of detecting a true $p$ above 0.5. Power rises with larger samples, with true proportions farther from 0.5, and with a larger $\alpha$.
Go deeper: which error deserves the caution

Neither error is automatically worse; the setting decides. A medical screening test is built to keep the Type II error small even at the cost of more Type I errors, because missing a real case is the costly failure. For the schedule vote, the school district may weigh a false majority signal more heavily and choose a smaller $\alpha$, accepting lower power.

主题 3.7 与 3.8 · 执行检验与潜在错误

检验决策、结论与两类错误

必备知识

用 p 值与显著性水平 $\alpha$ 比较：p 值 $<\alpha$ 时拒绝 $H_0$，否则不拒绝 $H_0$。结论要结合情景命名参数，并且只说到证据支持的范围：不拒绝并不是证明 $H_0$ 为真。

决策$H_0$ 为真$H_0$ 为假
拒绝 $H_0$第一类错误，概率 $\alpha$正确决策
不拒绝 $H_0$正确决策第二类错误，概率 $\beta$
例 3.7 — 完成早餐检验
  1. 取 $\alpha=0.05$，p 值约为 $0.0289$，因 $0.0289<0.05$ 而拒绝 $H_0$。
  2. 结合情景的结论：有令人信服的证据表明，该校超过一半的学生支持推迟上课时间。
  3. $\alpha=0.05$ 的含义：若真实比例恰为 0.5，此类样本量下该检验约有 5% 的概率错误地拒绝。
例 3.8 — 本研究中的两类错误
  1. 第一类错误：当 $p=0.5$ 为真时拒绝 $H_0$，把变化当作已获多数支持。后果是依据虚假信号改变作息安排。
  2. 第二类错误：当 $p>0.5$ 时不拒绝，错过真实存在的多数支持。后果是搁置学生确实需要的改变。
  3. 功效为 $1-\beta$，即检出真实高于 0.5 的比例的概率。样本量越大、真实比例离 0.5 越远、$\alpha$ 越大，功效越高。
深入理解：哪类错误更需防范

两类错误没有绝对的主次，取决于具体情景。医学筛查宁可多一些第一类错误，也要把第二类错误控制得很小，因为漏掉真实病例代价最大。就上课时间投票而言，若学区更担心虚假的多数信号，可以选择更小的 $\alpha$，并接受较低的功效。

Topics 3.9 to 3.11 · Two proportions

The difference of two proportions: distribution, interval, claims

Must know

The sampling distribution of $\hat p_1-\hat p_2$ has mean $p_1-p_2$ and standard deviation $\sqrt{\frac{p_1(1-p_1)}{n_1}+\frac{p_2(1-p_2)}{n_2}}$. A two-proportion $z$-interval uses that expression with the sample proportions, and the distribution is approximately normal when each group meets the large-counts condition.

Two groups, two proportions, one comparison. The parameter is the difference $p_1-p_2$ and the estimate is $\hat p_1-\hat p_2$. Both sample sizes matter for the spread: enlarging one group helps, but the other group still contributes its variability.

ModelSD of $\hat p_1-\hat p_2$
$p_1=0.40$, $n_1=200$; $p_2=0.32$, $n_2=250$$\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{200}+\dfrac{0.32(0.68)}{250}}\approx0.0455$
Both sample sizes raised to 400$\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{400}+\dfrac{0.32(0.68)}{400}}\approx0.0338$
Example 3.9 — Describing the distribution
  1. Center: the mean of $\hat p_1-\hat p_2$ is $0.40-0.32=0.08$.
  2. Spread: from the table, about $0.0455$, so the difference is not measured precisely even with hundreds per group.
  3. Shape: $n_1p_1=80$, $n_1(1-p_1)=120$, $n_2p_2=80$, $n_2(1-p_2)=170$ are all large, so the distribution is approximately normal.
Example 3.10 — A 95% interval for $p_1-p_2$
  1. Sample 1: $x_1=88$ of $n_1=200$, so $\hat p_1=0.44$. Sample 2: $x_2=150$ of $n_2=400$, so $\hat p_2=0.375$; the difference is $0.065$.
  2. Standard error: $\sqrt{\frac{0.44(0.56)}{200}+\frac{0.375(0.625)}{400}}\approx0.0426$.
  3. Margin of error: $1.96(0.0426)\approx0.0836$, so the interval is $0.065\pm0.0836$, about $(-0.019,\ 0.149)$.
Example 3.11 — Reading claims from the interval
  1. Claim: the two proportions differ. Since $0$ lies inside $(-0.019,0.149)$, the data do not provide convincing evidence of a difference.
  2. Claim: the first proportion exceeds the second by 2 percentage points. Since $0.02$ lies inside the interval, that size of difference remains plausible.
  3. Both readings are about the parameter difference; the interval says nothing about where any particular individual belongs.
Go deeper: intervals use the unpooled standard error

An interval makes no assumption that $p_1=p_2$, so it estimates each variance separately. The test of $H_0:p_1-p_2=0$ does assume a common proportion and pools; each standard error belongs in its own place.

主题 3.9 至 3.11 · 两比例之差

两比例之差的分布、区间与论证

必备知识

$\hat p_1-\hat p_2$ 的抽样分布均值为 $p_1-p_2$，标准差为 $\sqrt{\frac{p_1(1-p_1)}{n_1}+\frac{p_2(1-p_2)}{n_2}}$。两比例 $z$ 区间把总体比例换成样本比例后使用该式；两组都满足大计数条件时，分布近似正态。

两组、两个比例、一个比较。参数是差 $p_1-p_2$，估计量是 $\hat p_1-\hat p_2$。两个样本量都影响离散程度：把一组做大有用，但另一组的波动仍在起作用。

模型$\hat p_1-\hat p_2$ 的标准差
$p_1=0.40$，$n_1=200$；$p_2=0.32$，$n_2=250$$\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{200}+\dfrac{0.32(0.68)}{250}}\approx0.0455$
两组样本量都增至 400$\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{400}+\dfrac{0.32(0.68)}{400}}\approx0.0338$
例 3.9 — 描述该分布
  1. 中心：$\hat p_1-\hat p_2$ 的均值为 $0.40-0.32=0.08$。
  2. 离散程度：由上表约为 $0.0455$，即使每组数百人，差值的测量也难称精确。
  3. 形状：$n_1p_1=80$、$n_1(1-p_1)=120$、$n_2p_2=80$、$n_2(1-p_2)=170$ 都很大，分布近似正态。
例 3.10 — $p_1-p_2$ 的 95% 区间
  1. 样本一：$n_1=200$ 中 $x_1=88$，故 $\hat p_1=0.44$；样本二：$n_2=400$ 中 $x_2=150$，故 $\hat p_2=0.375$；差为 $0.065$。
  2. 标准误：$\sqrt{\frac{0.44(0.56)}{200}+\frac{0.375(0.625)}{400}}\approx0.0426$。
  3. 误差范围：$1.96(0.0426)\approx0.0836$，区间为 $0.065\pm0.0836$，即约 $(-0.019,\ 0.149)$。
例 3.11 — 由区间读主张
  1. 主张：两比例存在差异。由于 $0$ 落在 $(-0.019,0.149)$ 内，数据没有提供令人信服的差异证据。
  2. 主张：第一组比第二组高 2 个百分点。由于 $0.02$ 落在区间内，这一大小的差异仍然可能。
  3. 两种解读都针对参数之差；区间不说明某个个体属于哪一组。
深入理解：区间使用未合并标准误

区间不假设 $p_1=p_2$，因此分别估计两个方差；检验 $H_0:p_1-p_2=0$ 时才假设共同比例并合并。两种标准误各有其位，不可混用。

Topics 3.12 and 3.13 · Testing a difference

Significance test for the difference of two proportions

Must know

For $H_0:p_1-p_2=0$, combine the counts first: $\hat p_c=\frac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2}$. The test statistic is $z=\frac{\hat p_1-\hat p_2}{\sqrt{\hat p_c(1-\hat p_c)\left(\frac{1}{n_1}+\frac{1}{n_2}\right)}}$, and the p-value comes from the standard normal in the direction of $H_a$.

The pooled estimate is the single proportion implied by the null hypothesis. It is used only inside the standard error of the test, never reported as an estimate. Conditions: independent random samples, the 10% condition for each group, and large counts computed with the pooled value in both groups.

Example 3.12 — Setting up the test
  1. Question: do the two populations differ in the proportion who pass a skills check?
  2. Hypotheses: $H_0:p_1-p_2=0$ and $H_a:p_1-p_2\ne0$, where $p_1$ and $p_2$ are the true pass proportions.
  3. Data: $x_1=88$ of $n_1=200$ and $x_2=150$ of $n_2=400$. Conditions hold: independent random samples, and pooled counts that are all large.
Example 3.13 — Carrying out the test
  1. Pooled estimate: $\hat p_c=\frac{88+150}{200+400}=\frac{238}{600}\approx0.3967$.
  2. Standard error: $\sqrt{0.3967(0.6033)\left(\frac{1}{200}+\frac{1}{400}\right)}\approx0.0424$.
  3. Test statistic: $z=\frac{0.44-0.375}{0.0424}\approx1.534$, and the two-sided p-value is about $0.125$.
  4. Decision at $\alpha=0.05$: since $0.125>0.05$, fail to reject $H_0$. There is not enough evidence to conclude that the two pass proportions differ. On the TI-84, STAT then TESTS then 6:2-PropZTest reports $z$ and the p-value directly.
Go deeper: the same data can give two verdicts

Under the one-sided alternative $H_a:p_1>p_2$, the same $z=1.534$ gives a p-value near 0.063, still above 0.05. A difference that looks interesting inside an interval can fail a test when the sample is small: the interval in Example 3.10 and this test describe the same pair of samples.

主题 3.12 与 3.13 · 差值检验

两比例之差的显著性检验

必备知识

当 $H_0:p_1-p_2=0$ 时先合并计数：$\hat p_c=\frac{x_1+x_2}{n_1+n_2}$。检验统计量 $z=\frac{\hat p_1-\hat p_2}{\sqrt{\hat p_c(1-\hat p_c)\left(\frac{1}{n_1}+\frac{1}{n_2}\right)}}$，p 值按 $H_a$ 的方向取自标准正态分布。

合并比例是原假设所隐含的“共同比例”，只用于检验的标准误，绝不作为要报告的点估计。条件：两个独立的随机样本、每组满足 10% 条件、两组按合并比例计算的大计数条件都满足。

例 3.12 — 建立检验
  1. 问题：两个总体通过技能测验的比例是否不同？
  2. 假设：$H_0:p_1-p_2=0$，$H_a:p_1-p_2\ne0$，其中 $p_1$、$p_2$ 为真实通过比例。
  3. 数据：$n_1=200$ 中 $x_1=88$；$n_2=400$ 中 $x_2=150$。条件满足：独立随机样本，按合并比例计算的计数都很大。
例 3.13 — 执行检验
  1. 合并估计：$\hat p_c=\frac{88+150}{200+400}=\frac{238}{600}\approx0.3967$。
  2. 标准误：$\sqrt{0.3967(0.6033)\left(\frac{1}{200}+\frac{1}{400}\right)}\approx0.0424$。
  3. 检验统计量：$z=\frac{0.44-0.375}{0.0424}\approx1.534$，双侧 p 值约为 $0.125$。
  4. $\alpha=0.05$ 时的决策：$0.125>0.05$，不拒绝 $H_0$，没有足够证据说明两个通过比例不同。TI-84 上依次按 STAT、TESTS、6:2-PropZTest，可直接读出 $z$ 与 p 值。
深入理解：同一组数据可能得出两种结论

若取单侧 $H_a:p_1>p_2$，同样的 $z=1.534$ 给出约 0.063 的 p 值，仍大于 0.05。区间内看起来有意思的差异，在样本偏小时可能通不过检验：例 3.10 的区间与本例的检验来自同一组两个样本。

Topics 3.14 and 3.15 · Chi-square tests

Chi-square tests for homogeneity and independence

Must know

Use homogeneity when independent random samples are taken from each of several populations or treatments and the question is whether one categorical variable has the same distribution in all of them. Use independence when a single sample is classified by two categorical variables and the question is whether the variables are associated. Expected count $=\frac{\text{row total}\times\text{column total}}{\text{table total}}$, the statistic is $\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}$, $df=(r-1)(c-1)$, and every expected count should be at least 5.

Example 3.14 — Setting up the homogeneity test
  1. $H_0$: the distribution of plans is the same for both school populations. $H_a$: the distributions differ.
  2. Expected count for urban and four-year college: $\frac{(100)(140)}{300}\approx46.67$. If the distributions matched, about 46.7 urban students would choose a four-year college.
  3. The samples are independent random samples of graduates, and the smallest expected count is about 23.3, so every expected count clears 5.
Example 3.15 — Carrying out the test
  1. Contributions: $\frac{(44-46.67)^2}{46.67}\approx0.152$; $\frac{(36-30)^2}{30}=1.200$; $\frac{(20-23.33)^2}{23.33}\approx0.476$; $\frac{(96-93.33)^2}{93.33}\approx0.076$; $\frac{(54-60)^2}{60}=0.600$; $\frac{(50-46.67)^2}{46.67}\approx0.238$.

  3. With $df=2$ the upper-tail probability is about $0.254$. At $\alpha=0.05$ there is not enough evidence that the distributions of plans differ between the two school populations. On the TI-84, enter the observed table as matrix [A] and run STAT then TESTS then C:$\chi^2$-Test.
Go deeper: why expected counts must be large

The chi-square statistic is compared with a chi-square distribution, and that comparison is accurate only when each cell contributes a stable amount of information. Small expected counts make the statistic jumpy, so a cell with an expected count below 5 calls for combining categories before testing.

必备知识

深入理解：四舍五入与精度

计算过程保留完整精度，报告时检验统计量保留两到三位小数、概率保留三到四位。区间写作 $(0.512,0.688)$ 与“$\hat p=0.600$、误差范围 0.088”表达同样的精度；区间端点与误差范围必须互相吻合。

Free Response

FRQ model responses

Must know

Free-response points come from naming each condition and linking it to the study, showing the computation, and writing a conclusion in context with the correct scope. A bare number earns little; a number with its procedure and its meaning earns the marks.

Model response 1 — 7-mark one-proportion interval
  1. (a) Parameter and conditions, 2 marks. Let $p$ be the true proportion of the school's students who eat breakfast daily. The 90 students were selected at random, 90 is less than 10% of the school, and $n\hat p=54$ with $n(1-\hat p)=36$ are both at least 10, so the normal model applies.
  2. (b) Interval, 3 marks. $\hat p=54/90=0.60$; $SE=\sqrt{0.60(0.40)/90}\approx0.0516$; margin $=1.96(0.0516)\approx0.1012$; interval $0.60\pm0.1012=(0.4988,\ 0.7012)$, about $(0.499,\ 0.701)$.
  3. (c) Interpretation, 2 marks. We are 95% confident that the true proportion of this school's students who eat breakfast daily is between about 0.499 and 0.701.
Model response 2 — 8-mark two-proportion test
  1. (a) Hypotheses and conditions, 3 marks. $H_0:p_1-p_2=0$, $H_a:p_1-p_2\ne0$, where $p_1$ and $p_2$ are the true proportions who pass the check in the two training programs. Independent random samples; both sample sizes are less than 10% of their populations; pooled counts are all large.
  2. (b) Computation, 3 marks. $\hat p_1=84/200=0.42$, $\hat p_2=92/250=0.368$; $\hat p_c=176/450\approx0.3911$; $SE=\sqrt{0.3911(0.6089)\left(\frac{1}{200}+\frac{1}{250}\right)}\approx0.0463$; $z=\frac{0.42-0.368}{0.0463}\approx1.123$; two-sided p-value $\approx0.261$.
  3. (c) Conclusion, 2 marks. Because $0.261>0.05$, fail to reject $H_0$; there is not enough evidence to conclude that the two programs differ in their pass proportions.
Go deeper: how these responses are scored

Marks attach to statements that could be independently verified: naming the parameter, checking a condition and tying it to the study, showing the substituted formula, and stating the decision with its scope. A response that jumps from data to "the proportion is 0.6" loses the condition and interpretation marks even when the arithmetic is right.

简答题示范

简答题模型作答

必备知识

简答题的分数来自：逐条命名条件并结合研究情景说明、写出计算过程、以及写出范围正确的结论。只写一个数字得分很低；数字连同程序与含义才能拿到分。

模型作答一 — 7 分单比例区间
  1. (a) 参数与条件，2 分。设 $p$ 为该校学生每天吃早餐的真实比例。90 名学生为随机抽取，90 小于全校的 10%，且 $n\hat p=54$、$n(1-\hat p)=36$ 均不小于 10，故可用正态模型。
  2. (b) 区间，3 分。$\hat p=54/90=0.60$；$SE=\sqrt{0.60(0.40)/90}\approx0.0516$；误差范围 $=1.96(0.0516)\approx0.1012$；区间 $0.60\pm0.1012=(0.4988,\ 0.7012)$，约 $(0.499,\ 0.701)$。
  3. (c) 解释，2 分。我们有 95% 的把握，该校学生每天吃早餐的真实比例约在 0.499 与 0.701 之间。
模型作答二 — 8 分两比例检验
  1. (a) 假设与条件，3 分。$H_0:p_1-p_2=0$，$H_a:p_1-p_2\ne0$，$p_1$、$p_2$ 为两个培训项目通过测验的真实比例。两个样本相互独立且随机；两组样本量均小于各自总体的 10%；按合并比例计算的计数都很大。
  2. (b) 计算，3 分。$\hat p_1=84/200=0.42$，$\hat p_2=92/250=0.368$；$\hat p_c=176/450\approx0.3911$；$SE=\sqrt{0.3911(0.6089)\left(\frac{1}{200}+\frac{1}{250}\right)}\approx0.0463$；$z=\frac{0.42-0.368}{0.0463}\approx1.123$；双侧 p 值 $\approx0.261$。
  3. (c) 结论，2 分。由于 $0.261>0.05$，不拒绝 $H_0$；不足以说明两个项目的通过比例存在差异。
深入理解：这份作答如何被评分

分数落在可以被独立核对的陈述上：命名参数、核验条件并结合研究、展示代入公式、写出带范围的决策。若从数据直接跳到“比例为 0.6”，即使算术正确也会丢掉条件分与解释分。

教学用分值划分与练习册公布的标准一致；并非 College Board 官方评分标准。

Diagnostics

Common errors and their corrections

Must know

Most lost points in Unit 3 come from four families: the wrong standard error, a probability statement read too broadly, a decision written without context, and conditions skipped. The table collects the versions seen most often in draft work.

CodeCommon errorCorrection
M13Using $\hat p$ in the standard error of a test.A test computes the standard error from the null value: $\sqrt{p_0(1-p_0)/n}$.
M14Writing "there is a 95% chance that $p$ lies in the interval".Confidence describes the method: 95% of such intervals capture $p$.
M15Reporting the interval width as the margin of error.The margin of error is half the width.
M16Reading the p-value as $P(H_0\ \text{true})$.The p-value is $P(\text{result at least this extreme}\mid H_0)$.
M17"Reject $H_0$" as the entire conclusion.Add the parameter, direction, and population: what was concluded, and about whom.
M18Swapping Type I and Type II errors.Type I rejects a true $H_0$; Type II fails to reject a false $H_0$.
M19Pooling in an interval, or leaving the test unpooled.Intervals use each group's own proportion; the test of a zero difference pools.
M20Using the combined sample size inside one group's standard error.Each group contributes its own $n$: $\frac{1}{n_1}+\frac{1}{n_2}$, never $\frac{2}{n_1+n_2}$.
M21Building an expected count from the wrong totals.Expected $=$ row total $\times$ column total $\div$ table total, for every cell.
M22Using $n-1$ for the chi-square degrees of freedom.$df=(r-1)(c-1)$ counts the cells free to vary.
M23Never checking the size of the expected counts.Report the smallest expected count and confirm it is at least 5.
M24Concluding "no difference" after failing to reject.Fail to reject means the evidence is inconclusive, not that the values are equal.
Go deeper: why the standard error fork matters most

M13 and M19 are the same idea seen twice: the standard error must match the claim being made. An interval estimates without assuming equality; a test of equality assumes it under the null. Getting this wrong changes the statistic, the p-value, and often the decision.

Curriculum: these are the recurring misreadings seen in draft work for topics 3.1 to 3.15.

诊断

常见错误与修正

必备知识

单元 3 的失分大多来自四类问题：标准误用错、误读概率陈述的含义、决策不结合情景、以及跳过条件核验。下表汇总了草稿中最常出现的版本。

编号常见错误修正
M13检验的标准误中代入 $\hat p$。检验的标准误由原假设取值计算：$\sqrt{p_0(1-p_0)/n}$。
M14写成“$p$ 落在此区间内的概率是 95%”。置信描述的是方法：95% 的此类区间会包含 $p$。
M15把区间宽度当作误差范围。误差范围是宽度的一半。
M16把 p 值读成 $P(H_0\ \text{为真})$。p 值是 $P(\text{至少如此极端的结果}\mid H_0)$。
M17结论只写“拒绝 $H_0$”。补上参数、方向与总体：对谁、得出什么结论。
M18混淆第一类与第二类错误。第一类错误拒绝为真的 $H_0$；第二类错误不拒绝为假的 $H_0$。
M19在区间中合并，或在检验中不合并。区间用各组自身比例；差为零的检验才合并。
M20某一组的标准误里用两组合计样本量。每组贡献各自的 $n$：$\frac{1}{n_1}+\frac{1}{n_2}$，而非常用的 $\frac{2}{n_1+n_2}$。
M21用错合计构造期望计数。每格都按 行合计 $\times$ 列合计 $\div$ 总计 计算。
M22卡方自由度误用 $n-1$。$df=(r-1)(c-1)$，即可以自由变动的格子数。
M23从不检查期望计数的大小。写出最小期望计数并确认不小于 5。
M24不拒绝之后直接写“没有差异”。不拒绝说明证据不足，而不是数值相等。
深入理解：为什么标准误的分野最关键

M13 与 M19 是同一个想法的两种表现：标准误必须与所做的主张匹配。区间不假设相等，检验在原假设下假设相等。用错会改变统计量、p 值，往往还改变决策。

Mixed assessment

Unit quiz

1. A random sample of 200 commuters finds 140 who support a fare change. Which interval is the 90% one-proportion z-interval?

Answer: A. The estimate is $0.70$ and $SE=\sqrt{0.70(0.30)/200}\approx0.0324$. With $z^{*}=1.645$ the margin is about $0.0533$, giving $(0.647,0.753)$.

  • A. Correct: 90% pairs $z^{*}=1.645$ with the unpooled standard error.
  • B. This is the 95% interval: $z^{*}=1.96$ is too wide a multiplier for 90%.
  • C. This uses one standard error as the margin; the multiplier is missing.
  • D. This is the 99% interval, which pays for extra confidence with extra width.
2. What is the standard deviation of the sampling distribution of $\hat p$ when $p=0.35$ and $n=400$?

Answer: A. $\sqrt{0.35(0.65)/400}=\sqrt{0.00056875}\approx0.0238$.

  • A. Correct: $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$ with the values substituted.
  • B. This is the variance $p(1-p)/n$, not the standard deviation.
  • C. This is $\sqrt{p(1-p)}$, the standard deviation of a single observation.
  • D. Halving would be right only if the sample size were quadrupled from 100.
3. A report claims that fewer than 30% of households compost. Which hypotheses test this claim?

Answer: A. The claim is a direction on the parameter: the test uses $H_a:p<0.30$, with the equality in $H_0$.

  • A. Correct: the parameter, the equality in $H_0$, and the direction from the claim.
  • B. The direction is reversed; the claim points below 0.30.
  • C. Hypotheses are about the parameter $p$, not the statistic $\hat p$.
  • D. A two-sided alternative does not match a directional claim.
4. A test of $H_0:p=0.5$ against $H_a:p\ne0.5$ gives a p-value of 0.07. Which interpretation is correct?

Answer: A. The p-value is a probability about data given the null model, not a probability about the hypotheses.

  • A. Correct: a data statement under the null model.
  • B. This reads the p-value as $P(H_0)$, which it is not.
  • C. $1-p$ is not the probability of the alternative either.
  • D. The p-value is a tail area, not a share of the sample.
5. A hospital tests $H_0:p=0.10$ against $H_a:p>0.10$, where $p$ is the readmission rate. Which outcome is a Type II error?

Answer: A. A Type II error fails to reject a false $H_0$: the real rate is higher, and the test misses it.

  • A. Correct: a false $H_0$ that the test fails to reject.
  • B. This is a Type I error.
  • C. This is a correct decision.
  • D. This is also a correct decision.
6. A 95% confidence interval for $p_1-p_2$ is $(-0.02,\ 0.10)$. Which conclusion is supported?

Answer: A. An interval containing 0 leaves a zero difference plausible, so the data do not establish a difference.

  • A. Correct: 0 inside means no evidence of a nonzero difference.
  • B. Inconclusive is not the same as exactly equal.
  • C. Evidence of a direction needs the whole interval above 0.
  • D. Intervals do not rank the values inside them.
7. In a test of $H_0:p_1-p_2=0$ with $x_1=40$ of $n_1=100$ and $x_2=60$ of $n_2=200$, what is the pooled estimate?

Answer: A. $\hat p_c=(40+60)/(100+200)=100/300\approx0.3333$: counts over counts, not an average of proportions.

  • A. Correct: pool the counts, then divide.
  • B. This averages the two proportions and overweights the smaller group.
  • C. This is only $\hat p_1$.
  • D. This is only $\hat p_2$.
8. Independent random samples are taken from three schools, and each student is classified by whether they plan to attend college. How many degrees of freedom does the chi-square test use?

Answer: A. The table has $r=3$ rows and $c=2$ columns, so $df=(3-1)(2-1)=2$.

  • A. Correct: $(r-1)(c-1)=2$.
  • B. This is the number of populations, not the degrees of freedom.
  • C. This mixes the row and column counts.
  • D. This is the number of cells, not the degrees of freedom.
综合自测

单元自测

1. 200 名通勤者的随机样本中有 140 人支持票价调整。哪一个区间是 90% 的单比例 $z$ 区间？

答案：A。 估计值为 $0.70$，$SE=\sqrt{0.70(0.30)/200}\approx0.0324$。取 $z^{*}=1.645$ 得误差范围约 $0.0533$，区间为 $(0.647,0.753)$。

  • A. 正确：90% 对应 $z^{*}=1.645$，并使用未合并的标准误。
  • B. 这是 95% 区间：$z^{*}=1.96$ 对 90% 来说过宽。
  • C. 这里只用了一个标准误作为误差范围，缺少临界值倍数。
  • D. 这是 99% 区间：更高的置信换来更宽的区间。
2. 当 $p=0.35$、$n=400$ 时，$\hat p$ 抽样分布的标准差是多少？

答案：A。 $\sqrt{0.35(0.65)/400}=\sqrt{0.00056875}\approx0.0238$。

  • A. 正确：代入 $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$。
  • B. 这是方差 $p(1-p)/n$，不是标准差。
  • C. 这是 $\sqrt{p(1-p)}$，即单次观测的标准差。
  • D. 只有样本量从 100 变为 4 倍时才应减半。
3. 一份报告称不到 30% 的家庭进行堆肥。检验该主张应使用哪组假设？

答案：A。 主张针对参数的方向：$H_a:p<0.30$，等号放在 $H_0$。

  • A. 正确：参数、等号在 $H_0$、方向来自主张。
  • B. 方向反了：主张指向低于 0.30。
  • C. 假设针对参数 $p$，不是统计量 $\hat p$。
  • D. 双侧备择与有方向的主张不符。
4. 对 $H_0:p=0.5$ 与 $H_a:p\ne0.5$ 的检验得到 p 值 0.07。哪种解释正确？

答案：A。 p 值是在原假设模型下关于数据的概率，不是关于假设的概率。

  • A. 正确：在原假设下关于数据的陈述。
  • B. 这把 p 值读成了 $P(H_0)$，并非如此。
  • C. $1-p$ 也不是备择假设为真的概率。
  • D. p 值是尾部面积，不是样本的占比。
5. 某医院检验 $H_0:p=0.10$ 与 $H_a:p>0.10$，其中 $p$ 为再入院率。哪种结果属于第二类错误？

答案：A。 第二类错误是未拒绝为假的 $H_0$：真实比例更高，检验却未发现。

  • A. 正确：未拒绝为假的 $H_0$。
  • B. 这是第一类错误。
  • C. 这是正确决策。
  • D. 这也是正确决策。
6. $p_1-p_2$ 的 95% 置信区间为 $(-0.02,\ 0.10)$。可以支持哪种结论？

答案：A。 区间包含 0 时，差值为零仍然可能，因此数据未确立差异。

  • A. 正确：0 在区间内说明没有非零差异的证据。
  • B. 证据不足不等于恰好相等。
  • C. 要支持方向，需要整个区间都在 0 之上。
  • D. 区间并不对区间内的取值排序。
7. 在检验 $H_0:p_1-p_2=0$ 时，$n_1=100$ 中 $x_1=40$，$n_2=200$ 中 $x_2=60$，合并估计是多少？

答案：A。 $\hat p_c=(40+60)/(100+200)=100/300\approx0.3333$：先合并计数再相除，而不是对比例取平均。

  • A. 正确：合并计数后再相除。
  • B. 这是两个比例的简单平均，会给较小的组过大权重。
  • C. 这只是 $\hat p_1$。
  • D. 这只是 $\hat p_2$。
8. 从三所学校分别抽取独立随机样本，记录每名学生是否打算上大学。卡方检验的自由度是多少？

答案：A。 表格有 $r=3$ 行、$c=2$ 列，故 $df=(3-1)(2-1)=2$。

  • A. 正确：$(r-1)(c-1)=2$。
  • B. 这是总体个数，不是自由度。
  • C. 这里混淆了行数与列数。
  • D. 这是格子数，不是自由度。
