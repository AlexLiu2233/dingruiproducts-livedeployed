Topic 10.1专题 10.1

Electrostatics with Conductors导体的静电学

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe the charge distribution of an ideal conductor in electrostatic equilibrium: all excess charge sits on the surface and the interior carries no net charge.
  • Explain why the electric field is zero inside the conducting material and why the whole conductor, surface included, is one equipotential.
  • Justify that the field at the surface is perpendicular to it, and calculate $E=\sigma/\epsilon_0$ just outside a conductor with a Gaussian pillbox.
  • Compare the surface charge density at sharp points and edges with that on flat or gently curved regions.
  • Predict the induced charge on a conductor placed in an external field, and the direction of the force on a neutral conductor near a point charge.
  • Explain electrostatic shielding by a closed conducting shell, including a shell with a charge inside its cavity.
  • 描述（describe）理想导体在静电平衡时的电荷分布：所有多余电荷都在表面，导体内部没有净电荷。
  • 解释（explain）为什么导体材料内部的电场为零，以及为什么整个导体（包括表面）是一个等势体。
  • 论证（justify）表面处的电场垂直于表面，并用高斯柱面（pillbox）计算导体外表面附近的 $E=\sigma/\epsilon_0$。
  • 比较（compare）尖端和棱边处与平坦或曲率较小处的表面电荷密度。
  • 预测（predict）置于外电场中的导体上的感应电荷，以及中性导体靠近点电荷时所受力的方向。
  • 解释（explain）封闭导体壳的静电屏蔽，包括空腔内有电荷的导体壳。

A conductor is the first material you meet in this course whose charge is free to move. The whole topic follows from one idea: charge keeps moving until the field that drives it is zero. Every property below is a consequence of that single condition combined with Gauss's law and the definition of potential from Units 8 and 9.

导体是本课程中第一种电荷可以自由移动的材料。整个专题由一个想法推出：电荷会一直移动，直到驱动它的电场为零。下面的每条性质，都是这个条件与第 8、9 单元的高斯定律和电势定义共同导出的结果。

What you must know
  • An ideal conductor has electrons that move freely. In electrostatic equilibrium, $\vec E=0$ everywhere inside the conducting material.
  • All excess charge resides on the surface. There is no net charge in the interior.
  • The conductor, surface included, is one equipotential, at a potential that is generally not zero.
  • Just outside the surface $\vec E$ is perpendicular to it, with magnitude $E=\sigma/\epsilon_0$.
  • Surface charge density is greater at points and edges than on planar areas.
  • A conductor polarizes in an external field, and a closed conducting shell shields its interior from external fields.
必须掌握
  • 理想导体中的电子可以自由移动。静电平衡时，导体材料内部各处 $\vec E=0$。
  • 所有多余电荷都分布在表面，内部没有净电荷。
  • 导体（包括表面）是一个等势体，其电势一般不为零。
  • 紧靠表面外侧，$\vec E$ 垂直于表面，大小为 $E=\sigma/\epsilon_0$。
  • 尖端和棱边处的表面电荷密度大于平坦区域。
  • 导体在外电场中会极化，封闭的导体壳能把内部与外电场屏蔽开。

The physical model: free charge that keeps moving until it can't物理模型：自由电荷一直移动，直到无法再移动

A metal has roughly one mobile electron per atom, moving through a lattice of fixed positive ions. If a field $\vec E$ existed anywhere in the metal it would exert $\vec F=q\vec E$ on those electrons and they would drift. Electrostatic equilibrium is the state in which no charge is drifting, and the only way that can happen is for the field inside the material to be zero. An external field pushes electrons to one side and leaves the other side positive. The redistributed charge sets up a field of its own that exactly cancels the applied field inside the metal. In a good conductor this takes so short a time that the AP course treats it as instantaneous.

金属中大约每个原子提供一个可自由移动的电子，它们在固定的正离子晶格中运动。若金属内部某处存在电场 $\vec E$，它会对这些电子施加力 $\vec F=q\vec E$，电子就会漂移。静电平衡（electrostatic equilibrium）就是没有电荷再漂移的状态，而这只有在材料内部场强为零时才可能。外电场把电子推向一侧，另一侧留下正电；重新分布的电荷产生自己的电场，在金属内部恰好抵消外加电场。在良导体中，这个过程所需时间极短，AP 课程把它视为瞬时完成。

Notice what the argument does not say. It makes no claim about the field outside the conductor, inside a cavity that holds a charge, or while a current flows (Unit 11). It applies to the conducting material, after the charges have stopped moving.

也要注意这个论证没有说什么：它没有断言导体外部的场、含有电荷的空腔内的场，或有电流流动时的场（第 11 单元）。它只适用于电荷停止运动之后的导体材料内部。

Four consequences, each derived from a law you already know四条推论，每条都来自已学过的定律

  1. The conductor is an equipotential. Join any two points $P_1$, $P_2$ in the material by a path that stays in the material. Then $V_2-V_1=-\int_{P_1}^{P_2}\vec E\cdot d\vec l=0$ because $\vec E=0$ along the whole path. The common value is set by the conductor's charge and surroundings, so it is usually not zero.
  2. Excess charge lives on the surface. Draw a Gaussian surface of any shape entirely inside the material. The field is zero on it, so the flux is zero and Gauss's law gives $Q_{\rm enc}=0$. This works for every closed surface in the material, however small, so the bulk has no net charge density. Any net charge sits on a boundary: the outer surface, or the wall of a cavity.
  3. The surface field is normal. A component of $\vec E$ tangent to the surface would push surface charge along it, contradicting equilibrium. So $E_\parallel=0$ and the field meets the surface at a right angle. This is the same statement as the surface being an equipotential, because field lines cross equipotentials at right angles.
  4. The field just outside has magnitude $\sigma/\epsilon_0$. This needs the short Gauss's-law derivation below.
  1. 导体是等势体。在材料内任取两点 $P_1$、$P_2$，用始终在材料内的路径连接，则 $V_2-V_1=-\int_{P_1}^{P_2}\vec E\cdot d\vec l=0$，因为路径上处处 $\vec E=0$。这个公共电势由导体自身电荷和周围环境决定，通常不为零。
  2. 多余电荷分布在表面。在材料内部任作一个形状任意的高斯面，其上场强为零，通量为零，由高斯定律得 $Q_{\rm enc}=0$。材料内的任何封闭面（无论多小）都如此，所以体内净电荷密度为零。任何净电荷都在边界上：外表面或空腔壁。
  3. 表面处的场垂直于表面。若 $\vec E$ 有沿表面的切向分量，它会推动表面电荷沿表面移动，与平衡矛盾。因此 $E_\parallel=0$，电场与表面成直角。这与“表面是等势面”是同一个结论，因为电场线与等势面处处垂直。
  4. 紧靠外侧的场强为 $\sigma/\epsilon_0$。这需要下面这段简短的高斯定律推导。
Field just outside a conductor (Gaussian pillbox)导体外表面附近的场（高斯柱面）
$$E\,\Delta A+0+0=\frac{\sigma\,\Delta A}{\epsilon_0}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad E=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}\ \text{(normal to the surface)}$$

Take a small cylinder with end-cap area $\Delta A$ and axis along the surface normal, one cap just outside and one just inside the metal. The inner cap has $E=0$. The curved side has $\vec E\perp d\vec A$ or $\vec E=0$, so it contributes no flux. The field points away from the surface where $\sigma>0$ and toward it where $\sigma\lt 0$.

取一个小圆柱，端面积为 $\Delta A$，轴线沿表面法线，一个端面在金属外侧紧贴表面，另一个在金属内侧。内侧端面上 $E=0$；侧面上 $\vec E\perp d\vec A$ 或 $\vec E=0$，不贡献通量。$\sigma>0$ 处场背离表面，$\sigma\lt 0$ 处场指向表面。

AP trap: $\sigma/\epsilon_0$ versus $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$ An isolated infinite sheet gives $E=\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$ on each side. The field just outside a conductor is twice that, because it is the field of every charge in the problem. The local patch contributes $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$ outward and $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$ inward; all the other charges together cancel the inward part inside the metal and double the outward part outside. Use $\sigma/\epsilon_0$ at a conductor's surface and $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$ for a lone sheet.
AP 陷阱：$\sigma/\epsilon_0$ 与 $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$ 孤立的无限大带电平面在两侧各产生 $E=\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$。导体外表面附近的场是它的两倍，因为这是题中所有电荷共同产生的场：局部面元向外、向内各贡献 $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$，其余所有电荷的总场在金属内部抵消向内的部分、在外部使向外的部分加倍。导体表面用 $\sigma/\epsilon_0$，孤立平面用 $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$。

Cavities, induced charge, and shielding空腔、感应电荷与屏蔽

Suppose a conductor has a hollow cavity. A Gaussian surface in the metal that surrounds the cavity has zero field on it, so it encloses zero net charge: (charge in the cavity) + (charge on the cavity wall) $=0$. Two standard results follow.

设导体内有一个空腔。在金属中作一个包围空腔的高斯面，其上场强为零，所以包围的净电荷为零：（空腔内的电荷）+（空腔壁上的电荷）$=0$。由此得到两个标准结果。

Empty cavity. The wall carries no charge at all. If one part of the wall were positive and another negative, a field line would cross the cavity between them, and the potential would drop along it. That is impossible because the wall is one equipotential. So $\vec E=0$ throughout an empty cavity, whatever charges or fields exist outside the conductor. This is electrostatic shielding: a closed conducting shell surrounds a region that is free of external electric fields, the principle behind a Faraday cage.

空腔内没有电荷。空腔壁上完全没有电荷。若壁上某处为正、另一处为负，就会有一条电场线穿过空腔连接它们，沿线电势下降，而这不可能，因为空腔壁是一个等势面。所以无论导体外部有什么电荷或电场，空腔内处处 $\vec E=0$。这就是静电屏蔽（electrostatic shielding）：封闭的导体壳包围的区域不受外电场影响，法拉第笼就是这个原理。

Cavity containing charge $q$. The wall carries induced charge $-q$, arranged so that the field of $q$ plus the field of the wall charge is zero in the metal. The wall distribution depends on where $q$ sits and is nonuniform if $q$ is off center. Conservation then fixes the outer surface: $Q_{\rm outer}=Q_{\rm conductor}+q$. On a spherical outer surface that charge spreads uniformly, even if $q$ is off center, because the metal cuts the outside off from the cavity. Outside observers can tell how much charge is inside, but not where it is.

空腔内含有电荷 $q$。空腔壁带有感应电荷 $-q$，其分布使 $q$ 的场与壁上电荷的场在金属内相加为零。壁上分布取决于 $q$ 的位置，$q$ 偏离中心时分布不均匀。电荷守恒再决定外表面：$Q_{\rm outer}=Q_{\rm conductor}+q$。球形外表面上这部分电荷是均匀分布的，即使 $q$ 偏离中心也一样，因为金属把外部与空腔隔开。外部观察者能知道里面有多少电荷，但不知道它在哪里。

Shielding works in one direction. A closed shell shields its empty cavity from outside charges, but it does not hide a charge inside: the outer surface still carries $+q$ and produces an external field. Grounding the shell removes that outer charge (Topic 10.2).

屏蔽是单向的。封闭导体壳能把空腔与外部电荷隔开，但不能隐藏腔内的电荷：外表面仍带 $+q$，并在外部产生电场。把导体壳接地可以除去这部分外表面电荷（专题 10.2）。

Worked Example: Point charge inside a charged shell例题：带电导体壳内的点电荷

A point charge $q=-2.0\;\text{nC}$ sits at the center of a thick conducting spherical shell with inner radius $a=0.10\;\text{m}$ and outer radius $b=0.15\;\text{m}$. The shell carries net charge $Q_s=+3.0\;\text{nC}$. Use $k=8.99\times10^9\;\text{N}{\cdot}\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$. (a) Find the charge on each shell surface. (b) Find $E$ at $r=0.05$, $0.12$ and $0.20\;\text{m}$. (c) Find the shell's potential and the potential at $r=0.05\;\text{m}$, taking $V(\infty)=0$.

一个点电荷 $q=-2.0\;\text{nC}$ 位于厚导体球壳的中心，球壳内半径 $a=0.10\;\text{m}$、外半径 $b=0.15\;\text{m}$，球壳净电荷 $Q_s=+3.0\;\text{nC}$。取 $k=8.99\times10^9\;\text{N}{\cdot}\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$。(a) 求球壳各表面上的电荷。(b) 求 $r=0.05$、$0.12$、$0.20\;\text{m}$ 处的 $E$。(c) 取 $V(\infty)=0$，求球壳的电势及 $r=0.05\;\text{m}$ 处的电势。

Step 1第 1 步
A Gaussian sphere inside the metal ($a\lt r\lt b$) has zero flux, so $q+Q_{\rm in}=0$ and the inner surface carries $Q_{\rm in}=+2.0\;\text{nC}$.金属内的高斯球面（$a\lt r\lt b$）通量为零，所以 $q+Q_{\rm in}=0$，内表面带电 $Q_{\rm in}=+2.0\;\text{nC}$。
Step 2第 2 步
The shell's total charge is fixed: $Q_{\rm in}+Q_{\rm out}=+3.0\;\text{nC}$, so the outer surface carries $Q_{\rm out}=+1.0\;\text{nC}$.球壳总电荷固定：$Q_{\rm in}+Q_{\rm out}=+3.0\;\text{nC}$，所以外表面带电 $Q_{\rm out}=+1.0\;\text{nC}$。
Step 3第 3 步
At $r=0.05\;\text{m}$ (cavity), spherical symmetry gives a field set by $q$ alone. It points radially inward because the enclosed charge is negative.在 $r=0.05\;\text{m}$（空腔内），球对称给出只由 $q$ 决定的场，因为内部电荷为负，场沿径向向内。
$$E=\frac{k|q|}{r^2}=\frac{(8.99\times10^9)(2.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.05)^2}=7.2\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 4第 4 步
At $r=0.12\;\text{m}$ (inside the metal) $E=0$. Check: $Q_{\rm enc}=-2.0+2.0=0$.在 $r=0.12\;\text{m}$（金属内）$E=0$。检验：$Q_{\rm enc}=-2.0+2.0=0$。
Step 5第 5 步
At $r=0.20\;\text{m}$ the enclosed charge is $-2.0+2.0+1.0=+1.0\;\text{nC}$, so the field is outward.在 $r=0.20\;\text{m}$，包围的电荷为 $-2.0+2.0+1.0=+1.0\;\text{nC}$，场向外。
$$E=\frac{(8.99\times10^9)(1.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.20)^2}=2.2\times10^{2}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 6第 6 步
Outside the shell the field is that of $+1.0\;\text{nC}$ at the center, and the metal is one equipotential, so the shell's potential is its surface value.壳外的场等同于中心处 $+1.0\;\text{nC}$ 产生的场，且金属是一个等势体，所以球壳的电势就是其表面值。
$$V(b)=\frac{kQ_{\rm enc}}{b}=\frac{(8.99\times10^9)(1.0\times10^{-9})}{0.15}=59.9\;\text{V}$$
Step 7第 7 步
Integrate inward from the inner wall, using $E_r=kq/r'^2$ in the cavity.从内壁向内积分，空腔内 $E_r=kq/r'^2$。
$$V(r)=V(a)+kq\left(\frac1r-\frac1a\right)=59.9+(8.99\times10^9)(-2.0\times10^{-9})(20-10)\approx-1.2\times10^{2}\;\text{V}$$
Step 8第 8 步
Check by superposition: each spherical layer contributes $kQ/R$ at points inside it.用叠加法检验：每个球面层在其内部各点贡献 $kQ/R$。
$$V(0.05)=k\left[\frac{q}{0.05}+\frac{Q_{\rm in}}{0.10}+\frac{Q_{\rm out}}{0.15}\right]=-359.6+179.8+59.9=-119.9\;\text{V}$$

Why this works. The metal has zero field but sits at $+60\;\text{V}$, and the cavity has nonzero field. Zero field and zero potential are different claims.

原理。金属内场强为零，但电势为 $+60\;\text{V}$；空腔内场强不为零。场强为零与电势为零是两种不同的说法。

Worked Example: Two routes to the surface field of a sphere例题：求球面场的两种方法

An isolated conducting sphere of radius $R=0.20\;\text{m}$ carries $Q=+4.0\;\text{nC}$. Find the field just outside its surface in two ways and confirm they agree. Use $\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{C}^2/(\text{N}{\cdot}\text{m}^2)$.

半径 $R=0.20\;\text{m}$ 的孤立导体球带电 $Q=+4.0\;\text{nC}$。用两种方法求其表面外侧的场，并验证二者一致。取 $\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{C}^2/(\text{N}{\cdot}\text{m}^2)$。

Step 1第 1 步
By symmetry the charge spreads uniformly over the surface.由对称性，电荷均匀分布在球面上。
$$\sigma=\frac{Q}{4\pi R^2}=\frac{4.0\times10^{-9}}{4\pi(0.20)^2}=7.96\times10^{-9}\;\text{C/m}^2$$
Step 2第 2 步
Pillbox result:高斯柱面结果：
$$E=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}=\frac{7.96\times10^{-9}}{8.85\times10^{-12}}=9.0\times10^{2}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 3第 3 步
Spherical Gaussian surface at $r=R^+$:在 $r=R^+$ 取球形高斯面：
$$E=\frac{kQ}{R^2}=\frac{(8.99\times10^9)(4.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.20)^2}=9.0\times10^{2}\;\text{N/C}$$

Why this works. The two agree because $\dfrac{kQ}{R^2}=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\dfrac{Q}{R^2}=\dfrac{1}{\epsilon_0}\dfrac{Q}{4\pi R^2}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}$. The pillbox result is local: it also holds on irregular conductors with no symmetric Gaussian surface, provided you know $\sigma$ at that point.

原理。二者一致是因为 $\dfrac{kQ}{R^2}=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\dfrac{Q}{R^2}=\dfrac{1}{\epsilon_0}\dfrac{Q}{4\pi R^2}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}$。柱面结果是局部的：只要知道该点的 $\sigma$，它在没有对称高斯面的不规则导体上同样成立。

Polarization of a conductor in an external field导体在外电场中的极化

A conductor placed in an external field is polarized: mobile charge shifts until the field inside the material is zero again. This is a direct consequence of the conductor remaining an equipotential. If the conductor is neutral, the near side acquires charge of sign opposite to the source and the far side acquires equal charge of the same sign as the source, so the net charge stays zero.

置于外电场中的导体会被极化（polarized）：可移动电荷发生移动，直到导体内部的场重新为零。这是导体仍保持等势体的直接结果。若导体不带电，靠近场源的一侧出现与场源异号的电荷，远侧出现等量的与场源同号的电荷，净电荷仍为零。

Worked Example: A neutral conductor near a point charge例题：靠近点电荷的中性导体

A small uncharged metal sphere hangs from an insulating thread near a fixed positive point charge. Does the sphere feel a net force? If so, in which direction? Justify your answer without calculation.

一个不带电的小金属球用绝缘线悬挂，靠近一个固定的正点电荷。金属球是否受到净力？若有，方向如何？不用计算，说明理由。

Step 1第 1 步
The source's field pulls mobile electrons toward the near side. The near side becomes negative and the far side equally positive, so the net charge is still zero.场源的电场把可移动电子拉向近侧。近侧变为负、远侧变为等量的正，净电荷仍为零。
Step 2第 2 步
The induced negative charge is closer to the source than the induced positive charge, and the source's field weakens with distance. The attraction on the near side is therefore stronger than the repulsion on the far side.感应负电荷比感应正电荷更靠近场源，而场源的场随距离减弱。因此近侧受到的吸引力大于远侧受到的排斥力。
Step 3第 3 步
The net force on the sphere is toward the source, an attraction. If the source is negative, the near side becomes positive and the force is again attractive.球所受净力指向场源，是吸引力。若场源为负，近侧变为正，力仍是吸引力。

Why this works. This is why a charged rod attracts neutral objects. A complete answer links the location of the induced charge, the distance dependence of the field, and the resulting force imbalance.

原理。这就是带电棒能吸引中性物体的原因。完整的回答要把感应电荷的位置、场随距离的变化，以及由此产生的力的不平衡联系起来。

Sharp points and nonuniform surface charge (qualitative)尖端与不均匀的表面电荷（定性）

On a sphere, symmetry makes $\sigma$ uniform. On any other shape $\sigma$ varies along the surface, and it is larger at points and edges than on planar areas. Topic 10.2 shows why: two far-apart spheres joined by a wire share one potential, which forces $\sigma\propto1/R$, so the smaller sphere carries the larger surface density. A pointed end behaves like a small-radius sphere. It has the largest $\sigma$ and, since $E=\sigma/\epsilon_0$, the strongest field just outside. Strong fields near tips can ionize the surrounding air (corona discharge), which is why high-voltage equipment is rounded and lightning rods are pointed. The CED treats this comparison qualitatively: you should be able to rank the surface fields at different points, not calculate $\sigma$ on an arbitrary shape.

球面上，对称性使 $\sigma$ 均匀。其他形状上 $\sigma$ 沿表面变化，尖端和棱边处大于平坦区域。专题 10.2 说明了原因：用导线相连的两个相距很远的球共享同一电势，这迫使 $\sigma\propto1/R$，所以较小的球表面电荷密度更大。尖端的行为像一个半径很小的球，其 $\sigma$ 最大，又因 $E=\sigma/\epsilon_0$，紧靠外侧的场也最强。尖端附近的强场可以使周围空气电离（电晕放电），这就是高压设备做成圆滑形状、避雷针做成尖形的原因。CED 对此只做定性比较：你需要能对表面不同点的场强排序，而不是计算任意形状上的 $\sigma$。

Representation translation: graphs for a charged conducting sphere For a conducting sphere of radius $R$ carrying charge $Q$:
  • $E(r)$ is zero for $r\lt R$, jumps discontinuously to $kQ/R^2$ at the surface, then falls as $1/r^2$. The jump equals $\sigma/\epsilon_0$.
  • $V(r)$ is constant at $kQ/R$ for $r\le R$, then falls as $kQ/r$. It is continuous at $r=R$ with a kink there, because a jump in $V$ would need an infinite field.
  • Consistency: $E_r=-dV/dr$. The flat part of $V$ goes with $E=0$.
A common error is drawing $V$ dropping to zero inside the conductor.
表征转换：带电导体球的图像 对半径为 $R$、带电 $Q$ 的导体球：
  • $E(r)$ 在 $r\lt R$ 时为零，在表面不连续地跳到 $kQ/R^2$，然后按 $1/r^2$ 下降。跳变量等于 $\sigma/\epsilon_0$。
  • $V(r)$ 在 $r\le R$ 时恒为 $kQ/R$，之后按 $kQ/r$ 下降。它在 $r=R$ 处连续但有折点，因为 $V$ 若跳变就需要无穷大的场。
  • 一致性：$E_r=-dV/dr$。$V$ 的水平段对应 $E=0$。
常见错误是把导体内部的 $V$ 画成降到零。
Exam tip考试提示

For a conductor, write the two facts separately: the field in the material is zero, and the potential in the material equals the surface potential. A cavity that holds a charge is the usual place a response goes wrong: the field in that cavity is not zero. When you state induced charges, name the surface (cavity wall or outer surface), the sign, and the reason (Gauss's law in the metal for the wall, charge conservation for the outer surface).

写导体的结论时，把两个事实分开写：材料内的场为零，材料内的电势等于表面电势。含有电荷的空腔是最容易出错的地方：该空腔内的场不为零。说明感应电荷时，要指出是哪个表面（空腔壁还是外表面）、电荷的符号及理由（空腔壁由金属内的高斯定律得出，外表面由电荷守恒得出）。

Topic 10.1 describes one conductor alone. Here two or more conductors are connected, and the question becomes which way charge flows and where it ends up. Two conditions answer it every time: connected conductors reach the same potential, and the total charge of an isolated set of conductors is conserved.

专题 10.1 讲的是单个导体。这里把两个或多个导体连在一起，问题变成电荷往哪个方向流、最后停在哪里。有两个条件每次都能回答：相连的导体电势相同；孤立的一组导体的总电荷守恒。

What you must know
  • Conductors in electrical contact exchange charge until the surfaces of each are at the same electric potential.
  • Two identical conductors that touch end with equal charges.
  • For far-apart spheres joined by a wire, $Q\propto R$, $\sigma\propto1/R$ and the surface field $E\propto1/R$.
  • Ground is an idealized reference at $V=0$ that can absorb or provide any amount of charge without changing its potential.
  • Grounding a conductor in an external field induces charge on it. A grounded conductor is at $V=0$, not necessarily at $Q=0$.
必须掌握
  • 电接触的导体之间交换电荷，直到各导体表面处于同一电势。
  • 两个相同的导体接触后电荷相等。

  • “地”是 $V=0$ 的理想参考点，可以吸收或提供任意数量的电荷而电势不变。
  • 在外电场中将导体接地会在其上感应出电荷。接地导体的电势为 $V=0$，但电荷不一定为 $Q=0$。

Why equal potential is the equilibrium condition为什么等电势是平衡条件

Two conductors at potentials $V_1$ and $V_2$ are joined by a thin wire. The wire is a conductor, so the combined object must become one equipotential before equilibrium is possible. The direction of flow follows from energy: if a small positive charge $dq$ moves from conductor 1 to conductor 2, the electrostatic energy changes by $dU=(V_2-V_1)\,dq$. Charge moves in the direction that lowers $U$: positive charge flows from higher to lower potential, and electrons flow from lower to higher potential. The flow stops when $V_1=V_2$. The time this takes is negligible on the scale of the problem.

两个电势分别为 $V_1$、$V_2$ 的导体用细导线相连。导线是导体，所以整体必须成为一个等势体才可能平衡。流动方向由能量决定：若小电荷 $dq$（正）从导体 1 移到导体 2，静电能变化 $dU=(V_2-V_1)\,dq$。电荷朝使 $U$ 减小的方向移动：正电荷从高电势流向低电势，电子从低电势流向高电势。当 $V_1=V_2$ 时流动停止。这个过程所需的时间在题目的尺度上可以忽略。

Equal potential gives one equation and charge conservation gives a second. With two unknown final charges those two equations are enough. With more conductors, each wire adds one equal-potential condition.

等电势给出一个方程，电荷守恒给出第二个方程。两个未知的最终电荷，这两个方程就够了。导体更多时，每根导线增加一个等电势条件。

Two far-apart spheres joined by a thin wire用细导线相连的两个相距很远的球
$$\frac{kQ_1}{R_1}=\frac{kQ_2}{R_2},\quad Q_1+Q_2=Q_{\rm tot}\ \Rightarrow\ Q_1=\frac{R_1}{R_1+R_2}Q_{\rm tot},\ \ \frac{\sigma_1}{\sigma_2}=\frac{E_{1}}{E_{2}}=\frac{R_2}{R_1}$$

“Far apart” means the separation is large compared with both radii, so each sphere's potential comes from its own charge only, $V=kQ/R$. For close or concentric conductors this shortcut fails: use superposition, or Gauss's law plus a line integral.

“相距很远”指间距远大于两个半径，这样每个球的电势只来自自身电荷，$V=kQ/R$。对靠得近或同心的导体，这个捷径不成立：要用叠加，或高斯定律加线积分。

Worked Example: Connected spheres: charges, fields, and the energy that disappears例题：相连的球：电荷、场与消失的能量

球 1 半径 $R_1=0.050\;\text{m}$，带电 $+6.0\;\text{nC}$。球 2 半径 $R_2=0.10\;\text{m}$，不带电。两球相距很远，用一根细长导线相连。求 (a) 最终电荷，(b) 公共电势，(c) 表面场强，(d) 总静电能的变化。取 $k=8.99\times10^9\;\text{N}{\cdot}\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$。

Ground and charging by induction接地与感应起电

Ground is an idealized reference point with zero electric potential. It can absorb or supply an unlimited amount of charge without its potential changing. Connecting a conductor to ground therefore fixes the conductor's potential at $V=0$ and lets charge flow as needed to achieve it. At each step of a charging sequence ask: is the conductor isolated (its charge is fixed) or grounded (its potential is fixed)?

“地”（ground）是电势为零的理想参考点，它可以吸收或提供任意数量的电荷而电势不变。把导体接地就把导体的电势固定在 $V=0$，并让电荷按需流动以达到这个电势。在起电过程的每一步都要问：导体是孤立的（电荷固定），还是接地的（电势固定）？

Charge can be induced on a conductor by grounding it in the presence of an external field. The sign of the induced charge is opposite to the sign of the source, and the source itself loses no charge.

在外电场存在时把导体接地，可以在导体上感应出电荷。感应电荷的符号与场源相反，场源本身不损失电荷。

原理。感应使球带上与棒相反的电荷，棒本身的电荷不变。这里地线中电子的流向是从地到球。

What capacitance measures电容衡量什么

Put $+Q$ on one conductor and $-Q$ on the other. The field between them is proportional to $Q$, because every contribution to it scales with charge. So is the line integral of that field, $\Delta V$. The ratio $Q/\Delta V$ therefore does not depend on $Q$: it depends only on the shapes, the spacing and the material between the conductors. That ratio is the capacitance. A large capacitance means a lot of charge is stored for a small potential difference. Doubling the charge on a given capacitor doubles $\Delta V$ and leaves $C$ unchanged.

在一个导体上放 $+Q$，另一个放 $-Q$。它们之间的场与 $Q$ 成正比，因为场的每一部分贡献都随电荷等比例变化，这个场的线积分 $\Delta V$ 也是如此。所以比值 $Q/\Delta V$ 与 $Q$ 无关：它只取决于导体的形状、间距和板间的材料。这个比值就是电容（capacitance）。电容大意味着在较小的电势差下就能存储较多电荷。把给定电容器上的电荷加倍，$\Delta V$ 也加倍，而 $C$ 不变。

A parallel-plate capacitor consists of two separated parallel conducting plates that hold equal amounts of charge with opposite signs: $+Q$ on one plate and $-Q$ on the other. The field between them is proportional to $Q$, because every charge adds its own contribution, and the potential difference $\Delta V$ between the plates is proportional to that field. The ratio $Q/\Delta V$ therefore does not depend on $Q$: it depends only on the physical properties of the capacitor, namely the shape and spacing of the plates and the material between them. That ratio is the capacitance, $C=Q/\Delta V$, where $Q$ is the magnitude of the charge on either plate. A large capacitance means a lot of charge is stored for a small potential difference. Doubling the charge on a given capacitor doubles $\Delta V$ and leaves $C$ unchanged.

平行板电容器由两块分开的平行导体板组成，两板带有等量异号电荷：一块带 $+Q$，另一块带 $-Q$。板间的场与 $Q$ 成正比，因为每个电荷都贡献各自的场，而两板之间的电势差 $\Delta V$ 与这个场成正比。所以比值 $Q/\Delta V$ 与 $Q$ 无关：它只取决于电容器的物理性质，即极板的形状、间距和板间的材料。这个比值就是电容（capacitance），$C=Q/\Delta V$，其中 $Q$ 是任一极板上电荷的大小。电容大意味着在较小的电势差下就能存储较多电荷。把给定电容器上的电荷加倍，$\Delta V$ 也加倍，而 $C$ 不变。

The four-step method used for every geometry
  1. Place $+Q$ on one conductor and $-Q$ on the other.
  2. Use symmetry and Gauss\'s law to find $\vec E$ in the gap.
  3. Integrate from the negative to the positive conductor to get the positive quantity $\Delta V=-\int_-^+\vec E\cdot d\vec l$.
  4. Form $C=Q/\Delta V$. The charge $Q$ must cancel; if it does not, there is an error.
适用于每种几何形状的四步法
  1. 在一个导体上放 $+Q$，在另一个上放 $-Q$。
  2. 用对称性和高斯定律求间隙中的 $\vec E$。
  3. 从负导体到正导体积分，得到正的量 $\Delta V=-\int_-^+\vec E\cdot d\vec l$。
  4. 计算 $C=Q/\Delta V$。$Q$ 必须消去；若没有消去，说明有错误。

Parallel-plate capacitor平行板电容器

Two plates of area $A$ are a distance $d$ apart, with $d$ much smaller than the plate dimensions. Except near the edges the field between the plates is uniform, constant in magnitude and direction, and perpendicular to the plates; outside it is nearly zero. The charges sit on the facing inner surfaces with density $\sigma=Q/A$. The field is proportional to $\sigma$ on either plate.

两块面积为 $A$ 的极板相距 $d$，$d$ 远小于极板尺寸。除边缘附近外，板间的场是均匀的，大小和方向都不变，并垂直于极板；板外的场几乎为零。电荷分布在相对的内表面上，密度为 $\sigma=Q/A$。场与任一极板上的 $\sigma$ 成正比。

$$\begin{aligned}\text{Gauss (pillbox through the positive plate):}\ &E\,\Delta A=\frac{\sigma\,\Delta A}{\epsilon_0}\ \Rightarrow\ E=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}=\frac{Q}{\epsilon_0A}\\ \text{Line integral across the gap:}\ &\Delta V=\int_0^dE\,dx=Ed=\frac{Qd}{\epsilon_0A}\\ \text{Capacitance:}\ &C=\frac{Q}{\Delta V}=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}\end{aligned}$$

Superposition gives the same field. Each plate, treated as a sheet, contributes $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$. Between the plates the two contributions point the same way and add to $\sigma/\epsilon_0$; outside they point opposite ways and cancel. With a dielectric of dielectric constant $\kappa$ filling the gap, $\epsilon_0\to\kappa\epsilon_0$ and the result is $C=\kappa\epsilon_0A/d$ (Topic 10.4). The capacitance is proportional to the area of one plate and inversely proportional to the separation.

用叠加原理得到同样的场。每块板视为一个带电平面，贡献 $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$。板间两个贡献方向相同，相加为 $\sigma/\epsilon_0$；板外方向相反，相互抵消。若间隙充满介电常数（dielectric constant）为 $\kappa$ 的介质，则 $\epsilon_0\to\kappa\epsilon_0$，结果为 $C=\kappa\epsilon_0A/d$（专题 10.4）。电容与一块极板的面积成正比，与板间距成反比。

The field between the plates has the same strength everywhere in the gap, so moving a positive charge $q$ from the negative plate to the positive plate, against the field, takes work $qEd$. Dividing the work by $q$ gives the potential difference between the plates, so the field magnitude is determined by the voltage and the separation:

间隙中各处的场强相同，所以把正电荷 $q$ 逆着场从负极板移到正极板，需要做功 $qEd$。用功除以 $q$ 就得到两板之间的电势差，所以场强由电压和间距决定：

$$E=\frac{\Delta V}{d}$$

Here $E$ is the field magnitude in V/m (equivalently N/C), $\Delta V$ is the potential difference between the plates and $d$ is their separation. The field points from the positive plate to the negative plate. Because $d$ is much smaller than the plate dimensions, the field between the plates is uniform: constant in both magnitude and direction, except near the edges of the plates. AP Physics 2 ignores edge effects unless a problem says otherwise.

这里 $E$ 是场强大小，单位 V/m（等价于 N/C），$\Delta V$ 是两板之间的电势差，$d$ 是板间距。场的方向从正极板指向负极板。由于 $d$ 远小于极板尺寸，板间的场是均匀的：大小和方向都不变，只有极板边缘附近例外。除非题目另有说明，AP 物理 2 忽略边缘效应。

The capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor is proportional to the area $A$ of one plate and inversely proportional to the separation $d$. More area holds more charge at the same field, so $C$ grows with $A$. A larger separation needs a larger $\Delta V=Ed$ to maintain the same field and charge, so $C$ falls as $d$ grows. The constant of proportionality is the product $\kappa\epsilon_0$, where $\kappa$ is the dielectric constant of the material between the plates ($\kappa=1$ for vacuum, and very nearly 1 for air) and $\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{F/m}$ is the electric permittivity of free space.

平行板电容器的电容与一块极板的面积 $A$ 成正比，与板间距 $d$ 成反比。面积越大，在相同的场下能容纳的电荷越多，所以 $C$ 随 $A$ 增大。间距越大，要维持相同的场和电荷就需要更大的 $\Delta V=Ed$，所以 $C$ 随 $d$ 增大而减小。比例常数是乘积 $\kappa\epsilon_0$，其中 $\kappa$ 是板间材料的介电常数（dielectric constant，真空中 $\kappa=1$，空气中非常接近 1），$\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{F/m}$ 是真空电容率（permittivity of free space）。

The battery fixes $\Delta V=12\;\text{V}$, so the charge (magnitude) on each plate is电池固定 $\Delta V=12\;\text{V}$，所以每块板上的电荷（大小）为
$$Q=C\,\Delta V=(1.77\times10^{-10})(12)=2.12\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$$
Step 3第 3 步
Surface density and field; check with $\Delta V/d$:面电荷密度与场强；用 $\Delta V/d$ 检验：
$$\sigma=\frac{Q}{A}=1.06\times10^{-7}\;\text{C/m}^2,\quad E=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}=1.2\times10^{4}\;\text{V/m}=\frac{12}{1.0\times10^{-3}}$$
Step 4第 4 步
Stored energy:储能：
$$U=\tfrac12C(\Delta V)^2=\tfrac12(1.77\times10^{-10})(12)^2=1.27\times10^{-8}\;\text{J}$$

Why this works. The field points from the positive plate to the negative plate. A farad is a very large unit; capacitors in circuits are usually rated in $\mu\text{F}$, nF or pF.

原理。场的方向从正极板指向负极板。法拉是很大的单位，电路中的电容器通常以 $\mu\text{F}$、nF 或 pF 标称。

A charged particle between the plates带电粒子在极板之间

The field between the plates is uniform, so a charged particle there feels a constant force $F=qE$ and undergoes constant acceleration $a=qE/m$. Its motion has the same structure as projectile motion near Earth's surface: uniform velocity along the plates, uniform acceleration across them. The same kinematics from Mechanics applies, with $qE/m$ in place of $g$.

板间的场是均匀的，所以其中的带电粒子受恒力 $F=qE$，做匀加速运动，$a=qE/m$。它的运动与地球表面附近的抛体运动结构相同：沿极板方向匀速，垂直于极板方向匀加速。力学中的运动学公式同样适用，只是用 $qE/m$ 代替 $g$。

The field between the plates is uniform, so a charged particle there feels a constant electric force $F=qE$ and, by Newton's second law, undergoes constant acceleration $a=qE/m$, where $q$ is the particle's charge, $m$ its mass and $E=\Delta V/d$. Its motion has the same structure as projectile motion near Earth's surface: uniform velocity along the plates, uniform acceleration across them, and a parabolic path, with $qE/m$ in place of $g$. A positive charge accelerates in the direction of the field and a negative charge accelerates opposite to it.

板间的场是均匀的，所以其中的带电粒子受恒定的电场力 $F=qE$，由牛顿第二定律做匀加速运动，$a=qE/m$，其中 $q$ 是粒子的电荷，$m$ 是质量，$E=\Delta V/d$。它的运动与地球表面附近的抛体运动结构相同：沿极板方向匀速，垂直于极板方向匀加速，轨迹是抛物线，只是用 $qE/m$ 代替 $g$。正电荷沿场的方向加速，负电荷沿与场相反的方向加速。

Worked Example: An electron crossing a capacitor例题：穿过电容器的电子

Two horizontal plates $d=0.020\;\text{m}$ apart have a potential difference of $200\;\text{V}$ and length $L=0.050\;\text{m}$. An electron enters midway between them with speed $v_0=2.0\times10^7\;\text{m/s}$ parallel to the plates. Find its deflection on leaving the plates and the angle of its velocity. Neglect gravity ($e=1.602\times10^{-19}\;\text{C}$, $m_e=9.109\times10^{-31}\;\text{kg}$).

两块水平极板相距 $d=0.020\;\text{m}$，电势差 $200\;\text{V}$，长度 $L=0.050\;\text{m}$。一个电子以速率 $v_0=2.0\times10^7\;\text{m/s}$ 平行于极板从两板正中射入。求它离开极板时的偏移量和速度方向的角度。忽略重力（$e=1.602\times10^{-19}\;\text{C}$，$m_e=9.109\times10^{-31}\;\text{kg}$）。

Step 1第 1 步
Field and acceleration (directed toward the positive plate, since the electron is negative):场强与加速度（电子带负电，加速度指向正极板）：
$$E=\frac{\Delta V}{d}=\frac{200}{0.020}=1.0\times10^{4}\;\text{V/m},\quad a=\frac{eE}{m_e}=1.76\times10^{15}\;\text{m/s}^2$$
Step 2第 2 步
Time to cross, from the uniform motion along the plates:穿过极板的时间，由沿极板方向的匀速运动得到：
$$t=\frac{L}{v_0}=\frac{0.050}{2.0\times10^{7}}=2.5\times10^{-9}\;\text{s}$$
Step 3第 3 步
Deflection across the plates:垂直于极板方向的偏移：
$$y=\tfrac12at^2=\tfrac12(1.76\times10^{15})(2.5\times10^{-9})^2=5.5\times10^{-3}\;\text{m}$$
Step 4第 4 步
Exit velocity components and angle:离开时的速度分量与角度：
$$v_y=at=4.4\times10^{6}\;\text{m/s},\quad \tan\theta=\frac{v_y}{v_0}=0.22\ \Rightarrow\ \theta\approx12^{\circ}$$

Why this works. The deflection $5.5\;\text{mm}$ is less than $d/2=10\;\text{mm}$, so the electron does clear the plate. The electric force is about $1.6\times10^{-15}\;\text{N}$ against a weight of about $9\times10^{-30}\;\text{N}$, so neglecting gravity is justified.

原理。偏移量 $5.5\;\text{mm}$ 小于 $d/2=10\;\text{mm}$，所以电子能飞出极板。电场力约为 $1.6\times10^{-15}\;\text{N}$，重力约 $9\times10^{-30}\;\text{N}$，忽略重力是合理的。

Spherical and cylindrical capacitors球形与圆柱形电容器

The same four steps apply to concentric spheres and to coaxial cylinders. Only the Gaussian surface and the field change. These two geometries, together with parallel plates, are the only ones the CED expects you to analyse quantitatively.

同样的四步法适用于同心球和同轴圆柱，只是高斯面和场不同。这两种几何形状加上平行板，是 CED 要求定量分析的全部电容器类型。

Concentric spherical capacitor (inner radius $a$, outer radius $b$)同心球形电容器（内半径 $a$，外半径 $b$）
$$\begin{aligned}E&=\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0r^2}\ (a\lt r\lt b),\qquad \Delta V=\int_a^b\frac{Q\,dr}{4\pi\epsilon_0r^2}=\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\,\frac{b-a}{ab}\\ C&=\frac{Q}{\Delta V}=4\pi\epsilon_0\,\frac{ab}{b-a}\end{aligned}$$

Gaussian surface: a sphere of radius $r$, so $E(4\pi r^2)=Q/\epsilon_0$. The result is the field of a point charge between the shells.

高斯面取半径为 $r$ 的球面，所以 $E(4\pi r^2)=Q/\epsilon_0$。结果是壳间的点电荷场。

Coaxial cylindrical capacitor (radii $a\lt b$, length $L\gg b$)同轴圆柱形电容器（半径 $a\lt b$，长度 $L\gg b$）
$$\begin{aligned}E&=\frac{Q}{2\pi\epsilon_0Lr}\ (a\lt r\lt b),\qquad \Delta V=\int_a^b\frac{Q\,dr}{2\pi\epsilon_0Lr}=\frac{Q}{2\pi\epsilon_0L}\ln\frac ba\\ C&=\frac{Q}{\Delta V}=\frac{2\pi\epsilon_0L}{\ln(b/a)}\end{aligned}$$

Gaussian surface: a coaxial cylinder of radius $r$ and length $L$, so $E(2\pi rL)=Q/\epsilon_0$. End effects are neglected, and $C$ depends only on the ratio $b/a$.

高斯面取半径 $r$、长度 $L$ 的同轴圆柱面，所以 $E(2\pi rL)=Q/\epsilon_0$。忽略端部效应，$C$ 只取决于比值 $b/a$。

Check your result with limiting cases
  • Sphere with $b\to\infty$: $C\to4\pi\epsilon_0a$, the capacitance of an isolated sphere (the $C=R/k$ used in Topic 10.2).
  • Sphere with a thin gap $b=a+d$: $C\approx4\pi\epsilon_0a^2/d=\epsilon_0A/d$ with $A=4\pi a^2$.
  • Cylinder with a thin gap $b=a+d$: $\ln(1+d/a)\approx d/a$, so $C\approx2\pi\epsilon_0aL/d=\epsilon_0A/d$ with $A=2\pi aL$.
A stated limiting-case check is a good way to back up a derived expression.
用极限情形检验结果
  • 球形，$b\to\infty$：$C\to4\pi\epsilon_0a$，即孤立球的电容（专题 10.2 用到的 $C=R/k$）。
  • 球形，薄间隙 $b=a+d$：$C\approx4\pi\epsilon_0a^2/d=\epsilon_0A/d$，其中 $A=4\pi a^2$。
  • 圆柱形，薄间隙 $b=a+d$：$\ln(1+d/a)\approx d/a$，所以 $C\approx2\pi\epsilon_0aL/d=\epsilon_0A/d$，其中 $A=2\pi aL$。
写出极限情形的检验，是支持推导结果的好办法。
Worked Example: Concentric spherical capacitor例题：同心球形电容器

A vacuum spherical capacitor has shells of radii $a=0.030\;\text{m}$ and $b=0.060\;\text{m}$, carrying $+2.0\;\text{nC}$ (inner) and $-2.0\;\text{nC}$ (outer). Find $C$, $\Delta V$ and the field just outside the inner shell.

真空球形电容器的内外壳半径分别为 $a=0.030\;\text{m}$、$b=0.060\;\text{m}$，分别带 $+2.0\;\text{nC}$（内）和 $-2.0\;\text{nC}$（外）。求 $C$、$\Delta V$ 以及紧靠内壳外侧的场强。

Step 1第 1 步
Capacitance:电容：
$$C=4\pi\epsilon_0\frac{ab}{b-a}=4\pi(8.85\times10^{-12})\frac{(0.030)(0.060)}{0.030}=6.67\times10^{-12}\;\text{F}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Potential difference (inner shell at the higher potential); check with the integral:电势差（内壳电势较高）；用积分检验：
$$\Delta V=\frac{Q}{C}=\frac{2.0\times10^{-9}}{6.67\times10^{-12}}=3.0\times10^{2}\;\text{V}=kQ\left(\frac1a-\frac1b\right)$$
Step 3第 3 步
Field just outside the inner shell, directed radially outward:紧靠内壳外侧的场强，沿径向向外：
$$E(a^+)=\frac{kQ}{a^2}=\frac{(8.99\times10^9)(2.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.030)^2}=2.0\times10^{4}\;\text{N/C}$$

Why this works. The field is strongest at the inner conductor and falls as $1/r^2$, so it is not uniform and $\Delta V=Ed$ would be wrong.

原理。场在内导体处最强，并按 $1/r^2$ 下降，所以场不均匀，用 $\Delta V=Ed$ 是错误的。

Worked Example: Coaxial cable例题：同轴电缆

If the inner conductor is $100\;\text{V}$ above the outer one, find the charge per unit length and the field magnitude at $r=a$ and $r=b$.

Step 2第 2 步
Charge on that length and charge per unit length:这段长度上的电荷与单位长度电荷：

Step 4第 4 步
Evaluate at the two surfaces:在两个表面处取值：

Why this works. Here $E\propto1/r$, so $\Delta V/d$ is never a valid shortcut for a cylindrical or spherical gap.

原理。这里 $E\propto1/r$，所以对圆柱形或球形间隙，用 $\Delta V/d$ 永远不是有效的捷径。

Energy stored in a capacitor电容器中储存的能量

The potential energy stored in a capacitor equals the work done by an external agent to separate that amount of charge. Charging means moving charge from the negative conductor to the positive one against the field. When charge $q$ has already been moved, the potential difference is $q/C$, so moving the next $dq$ takes work $dW=(q/C)\,dq$.

电容器中储存的电势能等于外力把这么多电荷分开所做的功。充电就是逆着场把电荷从负导体搬到正导体。当已经搬了电荷 $q$ 时，电势差为 $q/C$，所以再搬 $dq$ 需要做功 $dW=(q/C)\,dq$。

$$U=\int_0^Q\frac{q}{C}\,dq=\frac{Q^2}{2C}=\tfrac12Q\,\Delta V=\tfrac12C(\Delta V)^2$$

The potential energy stored in a capacitor equals the work done by an external agent to separate that amount of charge. Charging means moving charge from the negative plate to the positive plate against the field. When charge $q$ has already been moved, the potential difference is $q/C$, so it rises steadily from $0$ to $\Delta V$ as the charge rises from $0$ to $Q$. The total work is the charge moved times the average potential difference, $\tfrac12\Delta V$:

电容器中储存的电势能等于外力把这么多电荷分开所做的功。充电就是逆着场把电荷从负极板搬到正极板。当已经搬了电荷 $q$ 时，电势差为 $q/C$，所以随着电荷从 $0$ 增加到 $Q$，电势差从 $0$ 平稳上升到 $\Delta V$。总功等于搬运的电荷乘以平均电势差 $\tfrac12\Delta V$：

$$U=Q\left(\tfrac12\Delta V\right)=\tfrac12Q\,\Delta V=\tfrac12C(\Delta V)^2=\frac{Q^2}{2C}$$

The factor $\tfrac12$ appears because the potential difference rises from $0$ to $\Delta V$ during charging: on average each bit of charge is moved through half the final potential difference. On a graph of $\Delta V$ against $q$ the line has slope $1/C$, and $U$ is the area under it, a triangle of base $Q$ and height $\Delta V$.

出现因子 $\tfrac12$，是因为充电过程中电势差从 $0$ 升到 $\Delta V$：平均而言，每一小份电荷只经过了最终电势差的一半。在 $\Delta V$ 对 $q$ 的图像上，直线的斜率为 $1/C$，$U$ 是线下的面积，即底为 $Q$、高为 $\Delta V$ 的三角形。

The energy can also be located in the field. For parallel plates $U=\tfrac12\dfrac{\epsilon_0A}{d}(Ed)^2=\left(\tfrac12\epsilon_0E^2\right)(Ad)$, so the energy per unit volume is $u=\tfrac12\epsilon_0E^2$. For the spherical capacitor, integrating $u$ over thin shells of volume $4\pi r^2dr$ gives $U=\dfrac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0}\displaystyle\int_a^b\dfrac{dr}{r^2}=\dfrac{Q^2}{2C}$, the same value as the work calculation. Two methods agreeing is a useful check when a question asks you to verify the stored energy.

能量也可以定位在场中。对平行板，$U=\tfrac12\dfrac{\epsilon_0A}{d}(Ed)^2=\left(\tfrac12\epsilon_0E^2\right)(Ad)$，所以单位体积的能量为 $u=\tfrac12\epsilon_0E^2$。对球形电容器，在体积为 $4\pi r^2dr$ 的薄球壳上对 $u$ 积分，得 $U=\dfrac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0}\displaystyle\int_a^b\dfrac{dr}{r^2}=\dfrac{Q^2}{2C}$，与做功计算的结果相同。当题目要求验证储能时，两种方法相互一致是有用的检验。

When the plates are pulled apart, pushed together or resized, first decide which quantity stays fixed, then follow the chain $C\to Q\text{ or }\Delta V\to E\to U$. The table doubles the plate separation, $d\to2d$.

当极板被拉开、推近或改变大小时，先判断哪个量保持不变，再沿着 $C\to Q\text{ 或 }\Delta V\to E\to U$ 的链条推理。下表把板间距加倍，$d\to2d$。

Worked Example: Pulling the plates of an isolated capacitor apart例题：拉开孤立电容器的极板

A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum has plate area $A=0.020\;\text{m}^2$ and separation $d=1.0\;\text{mm}$. A battery charges it to $20\;\text{V}$ and is then disconnected. The plates are slowly pulled apart to $d'=2.0\;\text{mm}$. Find the charge, the potential difference, the field and the stored energy before and after, and the work done by the external agent. Use $\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{F/m}$.

真空中的平行板电容器，极板面积 $A=0.020\;\text{m}^2$，间距 $d=1.0\;\text{mm}$。用电池把它充电到 $20\;\text{V}$，然后断开电池。极板被缓慢拉开到 $d'=2.0\;\text{mm}$。求拉开前后的电荷、电势差、场强和储能，以及外力所做的功。取 $\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{F/m}$。

Step 1第 1 步
Before: capacitance, charge, field and energy.拉开前：电容、电荷、场强和能量。
$$C=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}=\frac{(8.85\times10^{-12})(0.020)}{1.0\times10^{-3}}=1.77\times10^{-10}\;\text{F},\quad Q=C\,\Delta V=3.54\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$$ $$E=\frac{\Delta V}{d}=\frac{20}{1.0\times10^{-3}}=2.0\times10^{4}\;\text{V/m},\quad U=\tfrac12Q\,\Delta V=\tfrac12(3.54\times10^{-9})(20)=3.54\times10^{-8}\;\text{J}$$
Step 2第 2 步
The capacitor is isolated, so the charge on the plates stays $Q=3.54\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$. Doubling $d$ halves the capacitance:电容器是孤立的，所以极板上的电荷保持 $Q=3.54\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$。把间距 $d$ 加倍使电容减半：
$$C'=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d'}=8.85\times10^{-11}\;\text{F}$$
Step 3第 3 步
Potential difference and field after:拉开后的电势差和场强：
$$\Delta V'=\frac{Q}{C'}=\frac{3.54\times10^{-9}}{8.85\times10^{-11}}=40\;\text{V},\quad E'=\frac{\Delta V'}{d'}=\frac{40}{2.0\times10^{-3}}=2.0\times10^{4}\;\text{V/m}$$
Step 4第 4 步
Stored energy after, and the work done by the external agent (equal to the change in stored energy):拉开后的储能，以及外力所做的功（等于储能的变化）：
$$U'=\tfrac12Q\,\Delta V'=\tfrac12(3.54\times10^{-9})(40)=7.08\times10^{-8}\;\text{J},\quad W=U'-U=3.54\times10^{-8}\;\text{J}$$

Why this works. The field is unchanged because it depends only on the charge on the plates, and the stored energy doubles because the same field now fills twice the volume. The agent does positive work because the plates attract each other, so the agent must pull against the attraction.

原理。场强不变，因为它只取决于极板上的电荷；储能加倍，是因为同样的场现在充满了两倍的体积。外力做正功，因为极板相互吸引，外力必须克服这种吸引力。

Capacitors in parallel and in series电容器的并联与串联

Parallel. The plates are joined by wires, so each connected set is one equipotential and every capacitor has the same $\Delta V$. The charges add, so $C_{\rm eq}=\sum C_i$: parallel capacitors add plate area. Series. The two joined plates between neighbours form an isolated conductor that starts neutral, so every capacitor in the string carries the same $Q$ and the potential differences add: $1/C_{\rm eq}=\sum1/C_i$. Series capacitors add gap width, and $C_{\rm eq}$ is smaller than the smallest capacitor. These are the reverse of the resistor rules in Unit 11.

并联。极板用导线相连，每个相连的极板组是一个等势体，各电容器的 $\Delta V$ 相同。电荷相加，所以 $C_{\rm eq}=\sum C_i$：并联相当于增加极板面积。串联。相邻电容器之间相连的两块极板构成一个起初不带电的孤立导体，所以串联支路中每个电容器的 $Q$ 相同，电势差相加：$1/C_{\rm eq}=\sum1/C_i$。串联相当于增加间隙宽度，$C_{\rm eq}$ 比最小的电容还小。这与第 11 单元的电阻规则正好相反。

Worked Example: A small network例题：一个小网络

$C_1=2.0\;\mu\text{F}$ and $C_2=6.0\;\mu\text{F}$ are in series. This pair is in parallel with $C_3=1.5\;\mu\text{F}$, and the network is connected across a $12\;\text{V}$ battery. Find the equivalent capacitance, the charge and voltage of each capacitor, and the total stored energy.

Polarization: why an insulator lowers the field极化：为什么绝缘体会降低场

In a dielectric the charges are bound to their atoms or molecules and cannot travel through the material, so it is not as free to move charge as a conductor is. An applied field still shifts them slightly. In nonpolar molecules it pulls the electron cloud a little off the nucleus and creates an induced dipole. In polar molecules such as water, which already have permanent dipoles, it partly lines them up. In either case the interior stays neutral on average, because the positive end of each dipole sits beside the negative end of its neighbour. At the two faces the cancellation fails: a thin layer of negative bound charge appears on the face next to the positive plate, and a layer of positive bound charge on the face next to the negative plate. These bound layers produce a field that is opposite in direction to the external field and weakens the field inside the material.

电介质中的电荷束缚在原子或分子上，不能在材料中穿行，所以它移动电荷的自由程度不如导体。外加场仍会使它们略有位移。在非极性分子中，外场把电子云从原子核稍微拉开，形成感应偶极子；在水这样已有固有偶极矩的极性分子中，外场使它们部分排列整齐。两种情形下，内部平均仍是中性的，因为每个偶极子的正端紧挨着邻居的负端。在两个表面处这种抵消失效：靠近正极板的表面出现一薄层负的束缚电荷，靠近负极板的表面出现一层正的束缚电荷。这些束缚电荷层产生的场与外场方向相反，使材料内的场减弱。

A conductor is the extreme case. Its charge moves freely, so it cancels the applied field completely inside the material. A dielectric cancels only part of it. A conductor behaves like $\kappa\to\infty$, and the formulas below reduce correctly in that limit. The dielectric constant relates the permittivity of the material to that of free space, $\kappa=\epsilon/\epsilon_0$, and $\kappa\ge1$.

导体是极端情形：它的电荷可自由移动，所以在材料内部完全抵消外加场。电介质只抵消一部分。导体的行为相当于 $\kappa\to\infty$，下面的公式在这个极限下也能正确还原。介电常数（dielectric constant）把材料的电容率与真空电容率联系起来，$\kappa=\epsilon/\epsilon_0$，且 $\kappa\ge1$。

A conductor is the extreme case. Its charge moves freely, so it cancels the applied field completely inside the material. A dielectric cancels only part of it. The dielectric constant relates the permittivity of the material to that of free space, $\kappa=\epsilon/\epsilon_0$, where $\epsilon$ is the permittivity of the material, and $\kappa\ge1$ (vacuum has $\kappa=1$).

导体是极端情形：它的电荷可自由移动，所以在材料内部完全抵消外加场。电介质只抵消一部分。介电常数（dielectric constant）把材料的电容率与真空电容率联系起来，$\kappa=\epsilon/\epsilon_0$，其中 $\epsilon$ 是材料的电容率，且 $\kappa\ge1$（真空中 $\kappa=1$）。

Field, capacitance and bound charge with a dielectric filling the gap介质充满间隙时的场、电容与束缚电荷
$$\begin{aligned}E&=\frac{E_0}{\kappa}=\frac{\sigma_f}{\kappa\epsilon_0}\quad(\text{same free charge})\\ \Delta V&=Ed=\frac{Qd}{\kappa\epsilon_0A}\ \Rightarrow\ C=\frac{Q}{\Delta V}=\frac{\kappa\epsilon_0A}{d}=\kappa C_0\\ E&=\frac{\sigma_f-\sigma_b}{\epsilon_0}=\frac{\sigma_f}{\kappa\epsilon_0}\ \Rightarrow\ \sigma_b=\sigma_f\left(1-\frac1\kappa\right)\end{aligned}$$

A pillbox that encloses free charge $\sigma_f$ and bound charge $-\sigma_b$ gives the net-charge form in the last line. The same substitution $\epsilon_0\to\kappa\epsilon_0$ applies to the spherical and cylindrical results when the dielectric fills the whole gap. $Q$ in $C=Q/\Delta V$ is always the free charge on the plates.

包围自由电荷 $\sigma_f$ 和束缚电荷 $-\sigma_b$ 的高斯柱面给出最后一行的净电荷形式。介质充满整个间隙时，球形和圆柱形电容器的结果同样只需代换 $\epsilon_0\to\kappa\epsilon_0$。$C=Q/\Delta V$ 中的 $Q$ 始终是极板上的自由电荷。

Capacitance and field with a dielectric filling the gap介质充满间隙时的电容与场强
$$C=\frac{Q}{\Delta V}=\frac{\kappa\epsilon_0A}{d}=\kappa C_0,\qquad C_0=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}$$ $$\Delta V=\frac{Q}{\kappa C_0}=\frac{\Delta V_0}{\kappa},\qquad E=\frac{\Delta V}{d}=\frac{E_0}{\kappa}$$

$C_0$ is the capacitance with vacuum between the plates, $\kappa$ is the dielectric constant, and the subscript $0$ marks the values before the dielectric is inserted. The second line applies to an isolated capacitor, where the charge $Q$ on the plates stays fixed. Inserting the dielectric induces a field in the dielectric opposite in direction to the field between the plates, so the net field is weaker by the factor $\kappa$. For the same charge a weaker field means a smaller $\Delta V=Ed$, so $C=Q/\Delta V$ is larger by the same factor. $Q$ is always the charge on one plate.

$C_0$ 是板间为真空时的电容，$\kappa$ 是介电常数，下标 $0$ 表示插入电介质之前的值。第二行适用于孤立电容器，此时极板上的电荷 $Q$ 保持不变。插入电介质会在电介质中感应出与板间场方向相反的场，所以净场以因子 $\kappa$ 减弱。电荷相同时，场越弱，$\Delta V=Ed$ 越小，所以 $C=Q/\Delta V$ 以同样的倍数增大。$Q$ 始终是单个极板上的电荷。

Worked Example: Inserting a dielectric into an isolated capacitor例题：把电介质插入孤立电容器

A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum has plate area $A=0.040\;\text{m}^2$ and separation $d=2.0\;\text{mm}$. A battery charges it to $20\;\text{V}$ and is then disconnected. A sheet with dielectric constant $\kappa=4.0$ is slid in until it fills the gap. Find the capacitance, charge, potential difference, field and stored energy before and after. Use $\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{F/m}$.

真空中的平行板电容器，极板面积 $A=0.040\;\text{m}^2$，间距 $d=2.0\;\text{mm}$。用电池把它充电到 $20\;\text{V}$，然后断开电池。把介电常数 $\kappa=4.0$ 的介质板推入，直到充满间隙。求插入前后的电容、电荷、电势差、场强和储能。取 $\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{F/m}$。

Step 1第 1 步
Before the sheet is inserted:插入介质板之前：
$$C_0=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}=\frac{(8.85\times10^{-12})(0.040)}{2.0\times10^{-3}}=1.77\times10^{-10}\;\text{F},\quad Q=C_0\,\Delta V_0=3.54\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$$ $$E_0=\frac{\Delta V_0}{d}=1.0\times10^{4}\;\text{V/m},\quad U_0=\tfrac12Q\,\Delta V_0=3.54\times10^{-8}\;\text{J}$$
Step 2第 2 步
The capacitor is isolated, so $Q$ stays $3.54\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$. The dielectric multiplies the capacitance by $\kappa$:电容器是孤立的，所以 $Q$ 保持 $3.54\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$。电介质使电容变为 $\kappa$ 倍：
$$C=\kappa C_0=(4.0)(1.77\times10^{-10})=7.08\times10^{-10}\;\text{F}$$
Step 3第 3 步
Potential difference and field after:插入后的电势差和场强：
$$\Delta V=\frac{Q}{C}=\frac{3.54\times10^{-9}}{7.08\times10^{-10}}=5.0\;\text{V},\quad E=\frac{\Delta V}{d}=\frac{5.0}{2.0\times10^{-3}}=2.5\times10^{3}\;\text{V/m}$$
Step 4第 4 步
Stored energy after:插入后的储能：
$$U=\tfrac12Q\,\Delta V=\tfrac12(3.54\times10^{-9})(5.0)=8.85\times10^{-9}\;\text{J}=\frac{U_0}{\kappa}$$

Why this works. With $Q$ fixed, $\Delta V$, $E$ and $U$ each fall by the factor $\kappa$, and $E$ falls because the field induced in the dielectric opposes the field of the plates. The stored energy decreases because the plates attract the polarized sheet into the gap, so the sheet gains kinetic energy or does work on whatever holds it.

原理。$Q$ 固定时，$\Delta V$、$E$、$U$ 都以因子 $\kappa$ 减小，$E$ 减小是因为电介质中感应出的场与极板的场方向相反。储能减少，是因为极板把被极化的介质板吸入间隙，介质板获得动能，或对握住它的物体做功。

Worked Example: Inserting a dielectric with the battery connected例题：电池保持连接时插入电介质

The same kind of capacitor has plate area $A=0.020\;\text{m}^2$ and separation $d=1.0\;\text{mm}$ in vacuum and stays connected to a $12\;\text{V}$ battery. A dielectric with $\kappa=3.0$ is inserted to fill the gap. Find the new charge, field and stored energy, and compare them with the values before.

同类电容器在真空中极板面积 $A=0.020\;\text{m}^2$，间距 $d=1.0\;\text{mm}$，并与 $12\;\text{V}$ 电池保持连接。插入 $\kappa=3.0$ 的电介质，充满间隙。求新的电荷、场强和储能，并与插入前比较。

Step 1第 1 步
Before the dielectric is inserted:插入电介质之前：
$$C_0=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}=1.77\times10^{-10}\;\text{F},\quad Q_0=C_0\,\Delta V=2.12\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$$ $$E_0=\frac{\Delta V}{d}=1.2\times10^{4}\;\text{V/m},\quad U_0=\tfrac12Q_0\,\Delta V=1.27\times10^{-8}\;\text{J}$$
Step 2第 2 步
The battery holds $\Delta V=12\;\text{V}$ fixed, so the dielectric changes $C$ and the battery supplies extra charge:电池使 $\Delta V=12\;\text{V}$ 保持不变，所以电介质改变的是 $C$，电池向极板补充电荷：
$$C=\kappa C_0=5.31\times10^{-10}\;\text{F},\quad Q=C\,\Delta V=(5.31\times10^{-10})(12)=6.37\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$$
Step 3第 3 步
The field depends on $\Delta V$ and $d$, both unchanged:场强取决于 $\Delta V$ 和 $d$，二者都没有变：
$$E=\frac{\Delta V}{d}=\frac{12}{1.0\times10^{-3}}=1.2\times10^{4}\;\text{V/m}=E_0$$
Step 4第 4 步
Stored energy after:插入后的储能：
$$U=\tfrac12Q\,\Delta V=\tfrac12(6.37\times10^{-9})(12)=3.82\times10^{-8}\;\text{J}\approx3U_0$$

Why this works. The charge and the stored energy both rise by the factor $\kappa$ while the field is unchanged. The extra charge on the plates makes a larger field between the plates, and the opposing field induced in the dielectric reduces it back to the original value.

原理。电荷和储能都增大为原来的 $\kappa$ 倍，而场强不变。极板上增加的电荷使板间的场变大，电介质中感应出的反向场又把它减回原来的值。

Worked Example: Isolated capacitor, $\kappa=3$例题：孤立电容器，$\kappa=3$

A capacitor $C_0$ is charged to $\Delta V_0$ and disconnected. A slab with $\kappa=3$ is slid in until it fills the gap. Find the new $C$, $\Delta V$, $E$, $U$ and the bound charge in terms of the initial values, then account for the change in energy.

电容 $C_0$ 的电容器充电到 $\Delta V_0$ 后断开电源。把 $\kappa=3$ 的介质板推入，直到充满间隙。用初始值表示新的 $C$、$\Delta V$、$E$、$U$ 和束缚电荷，然后说明能量的变化去了哪里。

Step 1第 1 步
Isolation fixes the free charge, $Q_f=Q_0$.孤立使自由电荷固定，$Q_f=Q_0$。
Step 2第 2 步
The capacitance triples, so the potential difference and (at fixed spacing) the field fall to one-third:电容变为 3 倍，所以电势差和（间距不变时的）场强降为三分之一：
$$C_f=3C_0,\qquad \Delta V_f=\frac{Q_0}{3C_0}=\frac{\Delta V_0}{3},\qquad E_f=\frac{E_0}{3}$$
Step 3第 3 步
Stored energy:储能：
$$U_f=\frac{Q_0^2}{2(3C_0)}=\frac{U_0}{3}$$
Step 4第 4 步
Bound charge:束缚电荷：
$$\sigma_b=\sigma_f\left(1-\tfrac13\right)=\tfrac23\sigma_f$$

Why this works. Where did $\tfrac23U_0$ go? The fringing field at the plate edges pulls the polarized slab into the gap, so the field does positive work on the slab. A slab sliding in freely gains kinetic energy; one lowered slowly does work on the agent holding it.

原理。那 $\tfrac23U_0$ 去了哪里？极板边缘的边缘场把被极化的介质板拉入间隙，场对介质板做正功。自由滑入的介质板获得动能；缓慢放入时，场对握住它的外力做功。

Worked Example: Battery-connected capacitor, $\kappa=3$例题：接电池的电容器，$\kappa=3$

The same slab is inserted while the battery stays connected. Find the new charge and energy, and account for the battery's work.

在电池保持连接的情况下插入同一块介质板。求新的电荷和能量，并说明电池做的功。

Step 1第 1 步
The battery fixes $\Delta V_f=\Delta V_0$, so the charge triples and the battery pushes an extra $2Q_0$ onto the plates:电池固定 $\Delta V_f=\Delta V_0$，所以电荷变为 3 倍，电池额外向极板送去 $2Q_0$：
$$Q_f=3C_0\,\Delta V_0=3Q_0$$
Step 2第 2 步
Stored energy increases:储能增加：
$$U_f=\tfrac12(3C_0)\Delta V_0^2=3U_0,\qquad \Delta U=+2U_0$$
Step 3第 3 步
Work done by the battery, using $U_0=\tfrac12Q_0\Delta V_0$:电池做的功，用 $U_0=\tfrac12Q_0\Delta V_0$：
$$W_{\rm batt}=\Delta V_0\,\Delta Q=\Delta V_0(2Q_0)=4U_0$$
Step 4第 4 步
Energy balance $W_{\rm batt}+W_{\rm ext}=\Delta U$ gives the work done by an external hand controlling the slab:能量平衡 $W_{\rm batt}+W_{\rm ext}=\Delta U$ 给出控制介质板的外力所做的功：
$$W_{\rm ext}=2U_0-4U_0=-2U_0$$

Why this works. The field pulls the slab in, so a hand that controls the slab does negative work. The slab is drawn in in both cases, even though the stored energy falls when isolated and rises when connected: half of the battery's work is stored and the other half goes into pulling the slab in.

原理。场把介质板拉入，所以控制介质板的手做负功。无论孤立还是接电池，介质板都被拉入，尽管储能在孤立时减小、在接电池时增大：电池所做的功一半储存起来，另一半用于把介质板拉入。

Partially filled gaps: series and parallel models部分充满的间隙：串联与并联模型

Slab across part of the gap (thickness $t\lt d$, parallel to the plates). The free charge density is the same throughout, so $E=\sigma_f/\epsilon_0$ in the air and $\sigma_f/(\kappa\epsilon_0)$ in the slab. The potential differences add, so the layers act as capacitors in series, $C=\dfrac{\epsilon_0A}{d-t+t/\kappa}$. The slab's position within the gap does not matter. A metal slab ($\kappa\to\infty$) gives $C=\epsilon_0A/(d-t)$: it just shortens the gap. Dielectric filling a fraction $f$ of the plate area (side by side). Both regions have the same $\Delta V$ and the same $E=\Delta V/d$, so they act in parallel, $C=C_0[(1-f)+f\kappa]$.

介质板只占间隙的一部分（厚度 $t\lt d$，平行于极板）。自由电荷密度处处相同，所以空气中 $E=\sigma_f/\epsilon_0$，介质板中 $E=\sigma_f/(\kappa\epsilon_0)$。电势差相加，所以各层相当于串联的电容器，$C=\dfrac{\epsilon_0A}{d-t+t/\kappa}$。介质板在间隙中的位置无关紧要。金属板（$\kappa\to\infty$）给出 $C=\epsilon_0A/(d-t)$：它只是缩短了间隙。介质占极板面积的比例 $f$（并排放置）。两个区域有相同的 $\Delta V$ 和相同的 $E=\Delta V/d$，所以相当于并联，$C=C_0[(1-f)+f\kappa]$。

Worked Example: A slab across half the gap例题：占间隙一半厚度的介质板

Plates of area $A=0.010\;\text{m}^2$ are $d=4.0\;\text{mm}$ apart. A slab with $\kappa=4.0$ and thickness $t=2.0\;\text{mm}$ lies flat against one plate, and the capacitor is connected to a $50\;\text{V}$ battery. Find $C$, $Q$ and the field in the air and in the slab, and confirm that the voltages add to $50\;\text{V}$.

面积 $A=0.010\;\text{m}^2$ 的极板相距 $d=4.0\;\text{mm}$。一块 $\kappa=4.0$、厚度 $t=2.0\;\text{mm}$ 的介质板平放紧贴一块极板，电容器接在 $50\;\text{V}$ 电池上。求 $C$、$Q$ 以及空气中和介质板中的场强，并验证电压之和为 $50\;\text{V}$。

Step 1第 1 步

$$C=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d-t+t/\kappa}=\frac{(8.85\times10^{-12})(0.010)}{0.0020+0.00050}=3.54\times10^{-11}\;\text{F}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Without the slab $C_0=\epsilon_0A/d=2.21\times10^{-11}\;\text{F}$, so $C/C_0=1.6$, less than $\kappa=4$ because only half the gap is filled. Series check with the two layers:没有介质板时 $C_0=\epsilon_0A/d=2.21\times10^{-11}\;\text{F}$，所以 $C/C_0=1.6$，小于 $\kappa=4$，因为只填充了一半间隙。用两层串联检验：
$$C_{\rm air}=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{t}=4.43\times10^{-11}\;\text{F},\ \ C_{\rm slab}=\frac{\kappa\epsilon_0A}{t}=1.77\times10^{-10}\;\text{F},\ \ \left(\frac1{C_{\rm air}}+\frac1{C_{\rm slab}}\right)^{-1}=3.54\times10^{-11}\;\text{F}$$

Charge on the plates:极板上的电荷：
$$Q=C\,\Delta V=(3.54\times10^{-11})(50)=1.77\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$$
Step 4第 4 步
Fields:场强：
$$E_{\rm air}=\frac{Q}{\epsilon_0A}=2.0\times10^{4}\;\text{V/m},\qquad E_{\rm slab}=\frac{E_{\rm air}}{\kappa}=5.0\times10^{3}\;\text{V/m}$$
Step 5第 5 步
Voltages across each layer:各层上的电压：
$$\Delta V_{\rm air}=(2.0\times10^{4})(0.0020)=40\;\text{V},\quad \Delta V_{\rm slab}=(5.0\times10^{3})(0.0020)=10\;\text{V},\quad 40+10=50\;\text{V}$$

Why this works. Most of the voltage drop is across the air layer, the region with the smaller capacitance. This is the same pattern as the series network in Topic 10.3.

原理。大部分电压降落在空气层上，那是电容较小的区域。这与专题 10.3 中的串联网络是同样的规律。

Experimental design: determining $\kappa$实验设计：测定 $\kappa$

Use a parallel-plate capacitor of fixed $A$ and $d$. Measure $C_0$ with an air gap, then insert a sheet of the material that fills the gap completely and measure $C$. The model predicts $\kappa=C/C_0$. A more robust design charges the capacitor to several known voltages and measures $Q$ each time, for example by discharging through a charge sensor: the slope of $Q$ against $\Delta V$ is $C$, with and without the dielectric, and the ratio of the slopes is $\kappa$. Controlled variables are plate area, spacing, alignment and full coverage. An air gap between the sheet and a plate turns the arrangement into the series model and makes $\kappa$ come out too low. Repeat trials and fit a line rather than rely on one reading.

AP traps for 10.4
  • A dielectric reduces the field only at fixed free charge. With a battery connected and the gap filled, $E=\Delta V/d$ is unchanged: the extra free charge balances the bound charge.
  • Do not multiply by $\kappa$ when only part of the gap is filled. Use the series or parallel model.
  • Energy decreases in the isolated case and increases in the connected case, yet the slab is pulled in both times.
  • $\kappa\ge1$ always. An answer with $\kappa\lt 1$ signals an algebra error.
  • Bound charge is not free charge. $Q$ in $C=Q/\Delta V$ means the free charge.
10.4 的 AP 陷阱
  • 只有在自由电荷固定时，电介质才使场减小。接电池且间隙充满时，$E=\Delta V/d$ 不变：额外的自由电荷与束缚电荷平衡。
  • 只充满部分间隙时不要直接乘 $\kappa$，要用串联或并联模型。
  • 孤立时能量减小，接电池时能量增大，但介质板两种情形下都被拉入。
  • $\kappa\ge1$ 恒成立。答案中出现 $\kappa\lt 1$ 说明代数有误。
  • 束缚电荷不是自由电荷。$C=Q/\Delta V$ 中的 $Q$ 指自由电荷。
AP traps for 10.6 with dielectrics
  • A dielectric reduces the field only at fixed charge. With a battery connected, $E=\Delta V/d$ is unchanged because $\Delta V$ and $d$ are both fixed.
  • Energy decreases in the isolated case ($U=Q^2/2C$ with $Q$ fixed) and increases in the connected case ($U=\tfrac12C\Delta V^2$ with $\Delta V$ fixed).
  • $\kappa\ge1$ always. An answer with $\kappa$ below 1 signals an algebra error.
  • $Q$ in $C=Q/\Delta V$ is the charge on one plate, not the net charge of the capacitor.
带电介质的 10.6 的 AP 陷阱
  • 只有在电荷固定时，电介质才使场减小。接电池时 $E=\Delta V/d$ 不变，因为 $\Delta V$ 和 $d$ 都固定。
  • 孤立时能量减小（$Q$ 固定，$U=Q^2/2C$），接电池时能量增大（$\Delta V$ 固定，$U=\tfrac12C\Delta V^2$）。
  • $\kappa\ge1$ 恒成立。答案中 $\kappa$ 小于 1 说明代数有误。
  • $C=Q/\Delta V$ 中的 $Q$ 是单个极板上的电荷，不是电容器的净电荷。

A dielectric question is answered only when it states what is held fixed. Write one sentence naming the constraint (isolated, so $Q$ is fixed; or connected, so $\Delta V$ is fixed), then give the factor for each of $C$, $Q$, $\Delta V$, $E$ and $U$. When a question asks why the field decreases, name the mechanism: the polarized dielectric produces a field opposite to the external field. Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Saying zero field inside a conductor means zero potential. The potential equals the surface value.
  2. Using $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$ for the field outside a conductor. It is $\sigma/\epsilon_0$.
  3. Setting $Q_1=Q_2$ for connected unequal spheres. Equal potential is the condition.
  4. Treating grounded as $Q=0$. Grounded means $V=0$.
  5. Using $\Delta V=Ed$ across a spherical or cylindrical gap.
  6. Using the wrong energy formula for the fixed quantity.
  7. Multiplying by $\kappa$ when the dielectric fills only part of the gap.
最常见的错误
  1. 认为导体内场强为零就意味着电势为零。其电势等于表面值。
  2. 对导体外部的场用 $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$，应为 $\sigma/\epsilon_0$。
  3. 对相连的不等大小的球令 $Q_1=Q_2$。条件是电势相等。
  4. 把接地当作 $Q=0$。接地表示 $V=0$。
  5. 在球形或圆柱形间隙上使用 $\Delta V=Ed$。
  6. 对固定的量用错能量公式。
  7. 介质只充满部分间隙时仍乘以 $\kappa$。
Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Using the wrong energy formula for the fixed quantity.
  2. Putting the plate separation in millimeters into $C=\kappa\epsilon_0A/d$ instead of meters.
  3. Saying a dielectric always lowers the field. It does so only at fixed charge.
  4. Treating $Q$ in $C=Q/\Delta V$ as the net charge of the capacitor. It is the charge on one plate.
最常见的错误
  1. 对固定的量用错能量公式。
  2. 在 $C=\kappa\epsilon_0A/d$ 中把以毫米为单位的板间距直接代入，而没有换算成米。
  3. 认为电介质总是使场减小。只有在电荷固定时才如此。
  4. 把 $C=Q/\Delta V$ 中的 $Q$ 当作电容器的净电荷。它是单个极板上的电荷。

The wall carries $-q$ by Gauss's law in the metal, leaving $+q$ outside, not $0$ or $-q$. That outer charge is uniform, not concentrated near $q$: the nonuniform charge is on the inner wall.由金属内的高斯定律，内壁带 $-q$，外表面是 $+q$，而不是 $0$ 或 $-q$。外表面的电荷是均匀的，不集中在 $q$ 附近：不均匀的是内壁上的电荷。
3. (Physics C) Two conducting spheres of radii $R$ and $3R$ are far apart and joined by a thin wire. Their total charge is $Q$. What is the final charge on the smaller sphere?（物理 C）半径为 $R$ 和 $3R$ 的两个导体球相距很远，用细导线相连，总电荷为 $Q$。较小球的最终电荷是：
$Q/4$
$Q/3$
$Q/2$
$3Q/4$
Correct. Equal potential gives $Q_1/R=Q_2/(3R)$, so $Q_2=3Q_1$ and $Q_1=Q/4$.正确。等电势给出 $Q_1/R=Q_2/(3R)$，所以 $Q_2=3Q_1$，$Q_1=Q/4$。
$Q/3$ is the radius ratio $R/3R$, not the share. An equal share $Q/2$ holds only for identical conductors, and $3Q/4$ is the larger sphere's charge.$Q/3$ 是半径之比 $R/3R$，不是电荷份额。平分的 $Q/2$ 只适用于相同的导体，而 $3Q/4$ 是较大球的电荷。
4. (Physics C) A negatively charged rod is held near a neutral metal sphere on an insulating base. The sphere is grounded, the ground wire is removed, and then the rod is taken away. The final charge on the sphere is:（物理 C）一根带负电的棒靠近绝缘底座上的中性金属球。球被接地，随后断开地线，再拿走棒。球最终的电荷是：
Negative, like the rod负，与棒相同
Neutral, because the rod never touched it中性，因为棒从未接触它
Positive正
Undetermined without the radius不知道半径则无法确定
Correct. While grounded, electrons repelled by the rod flow to the Earth. Removing the ground first traps the electron deficit, so the sphere ends positive.正确。接地期间，被棒排斥的电子流入地。先断开地线就把电子的缺失留在球上，所以球最终带正电。
Induction leaves a charge opposite in sign to the rod, and contact is not required because the ground wire provides the charge path. The radius changes how much charge, not its sign.感应使球带上与棒相反的电荷，不需要接触，因为地线提供了电荷的通路。半径只影响电荷的多少，不影响符号。
5. A parallel-plate capacitor has plate area $0.10\;\text{m}^2$ and separation $2.0\;\text{mm}$ in vacuum, and is connected to a $50\;\text{V}$ battery. What is the charge on each plate? ($\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{F/m}$)真空中的平行板电容器，极板面积 $0.10\;\text{m}^2$，间距 $2.0\;\text{mm}$，接在 $50\;\text{V}$ 电池上。每块极板上的电荷是多少？（$\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{F/m}$）
$22\;\text{nC}$
$11\;\text{nC}$
$44\;\text{nC}$
$220\;\text{nC}$
Correct. $C=\epsilon_0A/d=(8.85\times10^{-12})(0.10)/(2.0\times10^{-3})=4.43\times10^{-10}\;\text{F}$ and $Q=C\Delta V=2.2\times10^{-8}\;\text{C}=22\;\text{nC}$.正确。$C=\epsilon_0A/d=(8.85\times10^{-12})(0.10)/(2.0\times10^{-3})=4.43\times10^{-10}\;\text{F}$，$Q=C\Delta V=2.2\times10^{-8}\;\text{C}=22\;\text{nC}$。
$11\;\text{nC}$ inserts a factor $\tfrac12$ (that belongs to energy, not charge). $44\;\text{nC}$ doubles $C$, as results from using $d=1.0\;\text{mm}$ instead of $2.0\;\text{mm}$. $220\;\text{nC}$ results from reading $d$ as $0.20\;\text{mm}$.$11\;\text{nC}$ 多乘了因子 $\tfrac12$（那属于能量，不属于电荷）。$44\;\text{nC}$ 把 $C$ 加倍，相当于用了 $d=1.0\;\text{mm}$ 而不是 $2.0\;\text{mm}$。$220\;\text{nC}$ 是把 $d$ 当成了 $0.20\;\text{mm}$。
6. (Physics C) A spherical capacitor has inner radius $R$ and outer radius $2R$, with vacuum between. Its capacitance is:（物理 C）球形电容器的内半径为 $R$，外半径为 $2R$，中间是真空。它的电容是：
$4\pi\epsilon_0R$
$8\pi\epsilon_0R$
$2\pi\epsilon_0R$
$16\pi\epsilon_0R$
Correct. $C=4\pi\epsilon_0\,ab/(b-a)=4\pi\epsilon_0(R)(2R)/R=8\pi\epsilon_0R$.正确。$C=4\pi\epsilon_0\,ab/(b-a)=4\pi\epsilon_0(R)(2R)/R=8\pi\epsilon_0R$。
$4\pi\epsilon_0R$ is the isolated sphere with no outer shell; the nearby shell raises $C$ above it, so any smaller answer is impossible. The geometric factor is $ab/(b-a)=2R$, not $R/2$ or $4R$.$4\pi\epsilon_0R$ 是没有外壳的孤立球；附近的外壳使 $C$ 高于它，所以更小的答案都不可能。几何因子是 $ab/(b-a)=2R$，不是 $R/2$ 或 $4R$。
7. An isolated, charged parallel-plate capacitor stores energy $U_0$. The plate separation is slowly doubled. The stored energy becomes:一个孤立的带电平行板电容器储能 $U_0$。板间距被缓慢加倍。储能变为：
$U_0/2$
$2U_0$
$U_0$
$4U_0$
Correct. $Q$ is fixed and $C$ halves, so $U=Q^2/(2C)$ doubles. The agent pulling the plates apart supplies the energy.正确。$Q$ 固定，$C$ 减半，所以 $U=Q^2/(2C)$ 加倍。把极板拉开的外力提供了这部分能量。
$U_0/2$ is the battery-connected result. $U_0$ would need the force on the plates to vanish. $4U_0$ would need $U\propto d^2$, but $U=Q^2d/(2\epsilon_0A)$ is linear in $d$.$U_0/2$ 是接电池时的结果。$U_0$ 要求极板间的力为零。$4U_0$ 要求 $U\propto d^2$，但 $U=Q^2d/(2\epsilon_0A)$ 对 $d$ 是线性的。
8. A dielectric with $\kappa=3$ is inserted to fill the gap of a capacitor that stays connected to a battery. By what factor does the stored energy change?把 $\kappa=3$ 的电介质插入一个仍与电池相连的电容器，充满间隙。储能变为原来的多少倍？
$3$
$1/3$
$9$
$1$
Correct. $\Delta V$ is fixed and $C\to3C_0$, so $U=\tfrac12C\Delta V^2$ triples.正确。$\Delta V$ 固定，$C\to3C_0$，所以 $U=\tfrac12C\Delta V^2$ 变为 3 倍。
$1/3$ is the isolated result ($Q$ fixed). Only $C$ changes by $\kappa$, so $U$ scales as $\kappa$, not $\kappa^2$. The battery does work, so the energy does not stay the same.$1/3$ 是孤立时的结果（$Q$ 固定）。只有 $C$ 变为 $\kappa$ 倍，所以 $U$ 按 $\kappa$ 变化，而不是 $\kappa^2$。电池做功，所以能量不会保持不变。
9. (Physics C) A dielectric with $\kappa=5$ fills one-third of the plate area of a capacitor $C_0$, through the full gap, and the rest is air. The new capacitance is:（物理 C）$\kappa=5$ 的电介质填满电容器 $C_0$ 三分之一的极板面积（贯穿整个间隙），其余是空气。新的电容是：
$\tfrac73C_0$
$\tfrac53C_0$
$5C_0$
$\tfrac35C_0$
Correct. Side-by-side regions are in parallel: $C=\tfrac23C_0+\tfrac13(5C_0)=\tfrac73C_0$.正确。并排的区域并联：$C=\tfrac23C_0+\tfrac13(5C_0)=\tfrac73C_0$。
$\tfrac53C_0$ keeps only the dielectric part and forgets the air region. $5C_0$ applies only when the dielectric fills the whole gap. A capacitance below $C_0$ is impossible because a dielectric never lowers $C$.$\tfrac53C_0$ 只保留了介质部分而忘了空气部分。$5C_0$ 只适用于介质充满整个间隙。小于 $C_0$ 的电容不可能，因为电介质不会降低 $C$。
10. A proton and an alpha particle (charge $2e$, mass $4m_p$) are each released from rest midway between two charged plates. What is the ratio of the alpha particle's acceleration to the proton's?一个质子和一个 α 粒子（电荷 $2e$，质量 $4m_p$）分别从两个带电极板正中间由静止释放。α 粒子与质子的加速度之比是：
$1/2$
$2$
$1$
$4$
Correct. The field is uniform, so $a=qE/m$. The ratio is $(2e/4m_p)/(e/m_p)=1/2$.正确。场是均匀的，所以 $a=qE/m$。比值为 $(2e/4m_p)/(e/m_p)=1/2$。
Both the charge and the mass differ, so neither alone gives the ratio. Doubling the charge would double $a$, but quadrupling the mass divides it by 4, a net factor of $1/2$.电荷和质量都不同，任何一个单独都不能给出比值。电荷加倍使 $a$ 加倍，质量变为 4 倍使 $a$ 除以 4，净效果是 $1/2$。
