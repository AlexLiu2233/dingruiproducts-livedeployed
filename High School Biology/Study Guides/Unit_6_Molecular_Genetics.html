The double helix in four facts.双螺旋四要点。
  • Structure: DNA is a double-stranded helix. Each strand is a polymer of nucleotides; each nucleotide has a sugar (deoxyribose), a phosphate group, and one of four nitrogenous bases.结构：DNA 是双链螺旋。每条链是核苷酸聚合体；每个核苷酸含一个糖（脱氧核糖）、一个磷酸基团和四种含氮碱基之一。
  • Base pairing (Chargaff's rules): Adenine (A) pairs with Thymine (T) via two hydrogen bonds; Guanine (G) pairs with Cytosine (C) via three hydrogen bonds. The two strands are antiparallel (one runs 5'→3', the other 3'→5').碱基配对（查哥夫定律）：腺嘌呤（A）与胸腺嘧啶（T）通过两个氢键配对；鸟嘌呤（G）与胞嘧啶（C）通过三个氢键配对。两条链反向平行（一条 5'→3'，另一条 3'→5'）。
  • Information: The sequence of bases encodes genetic information. A gene is a specific sequence of DNA that codes for a functional product (usually a protein). The human genome is about 3 billion base pairs per set of 23 chromosomes; a body cell has two sets (23 chromosome pairs, about 6 billion base pairs).信息：碱基序列编码遗传信息。基因是编码功能产物（通常是蛋白质）的特定 DNA 序列。人类基因组每套 23 条染色体约含 30 亿碱基对；体细胞有两套（23 对染色体，约 60 亿碱基对）。
  • RNA differs from DNA: RNA is single-stranded; uses ribose sugar instead of deoxyribose; uses Uracil (U) instead of Thymine (T); shorter than a full chromosome.RNA 与 DNA 的区别：RNA 是单链；使用核糖而非脱氧核糖；使用尿嘧啶（U）代替胸腺嘧啶（T）；比完整染色体短。

Worked Example 2: Chargaff's rules and hydrogen bonds例题 2：查哥夫定律与氢键

A double-stranded DNA fragment from a bacterium is 1000 base pairs long, and 18% of its bases are cytosine. Find (a) the percentage of each of the four bases and (b) the total number of hydrogen bonds holding the two strands together (an A-T pair has 2, a G-C pair has 3).某细菌的双链 DNA 片段长 1000 个碱基对，其中 18% 的碱基是胞嘧啶。求：(a) 四种碱基各占的百分比；(b) 把两条链连在一起的氢键总数（A-T 对有 2 个，G-C 对有 3 个）。

(a) Percentages. In double-stranded DNA, A = T and G = C. So G = 18% as well, and G + C = 36%. The rest is 100% − 36% = 64%, shared equally by A and T, so A = T = 32%.(a) 百分比。双链 DNA 中 A = T，G = C。所以 G 也是 18%，G + C = 36%。其余 100% − 36% = 64% 由 A 和 T 平分，故 A = T = 32%。

(b) Hydrogen bonds. G-C pairs: 36% of 1000 = 360. A-T pairs: 1000 − 360 = 640. Hydrogen bonds: 360 × 3 + 640 × 2 = 1080 + 1280 = 2360.(b) 氢键。G-C 对：1000 的 36% = 360 对。A-T 对：1000 − 360 = 640 对。氢键数：360 × 3 + 640 × 2 = 1080 + 1280 = 2360。

Why it works. Every A on one strand faces a T on the other, and every G faces a C, so the counts are equal in pairs. Common error: applying A = T to a single-stranded molecule (a single-stranded DNA virus, or any RNA), where the base counts need not match.为什么成立。一条链上的每个 A 都对着另一条链上的 T，每个 G 都对着 C，所以成对的数量相等。常见错误：把 A = T 用在单链分子（单链 DNA 病毒或任何 RNA）上；单链分子的碱基数不一定相等。

Rosalind Franklin's X-ray crystallography (Photo 51, 1952) revealed the helical structure and dimensions. DNA ReplicationDNA 复制

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe semi-conservative replication and state where and when it happens in a eukaryotic cell.
  • Predict the density of DNA after each generation in the Meselson-Stahl experiment and say which models each result rules out.
  • Explain with the 5'→3' rule why one new strand is made continuously (leading) and the other in Okazaki fragments (lagging).
  • State the roles of helicase, primase, DNA polymerase and DNA ligase.
  • 描述半保留复制，并说明它在真核细胞中发生的位置和时期。
  • 预测梅塞尔森-斯塔尔实验中每一代 DNA 的密度，并说明每个结果排除了哪些模型。
  • 用 5'→3' 规则解释为什么一条新链连续合成（前导链），另一条以冈崎片段合成（后随链）。
  • 说出解旋酶、引物酶、DNA 聚合酶和 DNA 连接酶的作用。
Semi-conservative replication: the key rule.半保留复制：核心规则。
  • Semi-conservative: when DNA replicates, each new double helix contains one original (parental) strand and one newly synthesized strand. Both daughter DNA molecules are identical to the parent.半保留：DNA 复制时，每个新双螺旋含一条原有（亲本）链和一条新合成链。两个子代 DNA 分子均与亲本相同。
  • Where: occurs in the nucleus (eukaryotes) before cell division (S phase of the cell cycle).位置：在细胞分裂前发生于细胞核（真核生物）（细胞周期 S 期）。
  • Steps (conceptual): (1) The two parental strands separate (unwind). (2) Free nucleotides pair up with each template strand by base-pairing rules (A-T, G-C). (3) New strands are synthesized in the 5'→3' direction. (4) Two complete double helices result.步骤（概念性）：(1) 两条亲本链分离（解旋）。(2) 游离核苷酸按碱基配对规则（A-T、G-C）与每条模板链配对。(3) 新链按 5'→3' 方向合成。(4) 产生两个完整双螺旋。

Why two kinds of new strand? DNA polymerase can only add nucleotides to a 3' end, so every new strand grows 5'→3'. The two template strands run in opposite directions (they are antiparallel), so at one fork the new strand on one template grows toward the fork and can be made in one piece: the leading strand. The new strand on the other template can only grow away from the fork. It is built backwards in short pieces called Okazaki fragments, each started as the fork opens further and later joined by DNA ligase: the lagging strand.为什么有两种新链？DNA 聚合酶只能在 3' 端添加核苷酸，所以每条新链都按 5'→3' 延伸。两条模板链方向相反（反向平行），因此在同一个复制叉处，一条模板上的新链朝复制叉方向延伸，可以一次连续合成：这是前导链（leading strand）。另一条模板上的新链只能背离复制叉延伸，要分成许多短片段向后合成，这些短片段叫冈崎片段（Okazaki fragments），复制叉每打开一段就起始一个，之后由 DNA 连接酶连接：这是后随链（lagging strand）。

Worked Example 3: Semi-conservative replication prediction例题 3：半保留复制预测

A researcher labels all DNA in a cell with a heavy nitrogen isotope (¹⁵N). The cell then replicates once in a medium containing only light nitrogen (¹⁴N). Predict the composition of the daughter DNA molecules and explain why this supports the semi-conservative model.研究者用重氮同位素（¹⁵N）标记细胞内所有 DNA。该细胞随后在仅含轻氮（¹⁴N）的培养基中复制一次。预测子代 DNA 分子的组成，并解释这如何支持半保留模型。

Result:结果： All daughter DNA molecules are hybrid: one ¹⁵N strand (original) and one ¹⁴N strand (new). In density-gradient centrifugation, all molecules band at an intermediate density, not at the heavy position. This is what Meselson and Stahl found in 1958 after one generation. It rules out conservative replication (which would give one heavy band and one light band). Dispersive replication also predicts a single intermediate band after one generation, so the models are separated one generation later: semi-conservative replication gives two bands (hybrid and light) after generation 2, while dispersive replication keeps a single band (see Worked Example 4). Semi-conservative replication predicts one band at intermediate density after one replication: confirmed.所有子代 DNA 分子均为杂合体：一条 ¹⁵N 链（原有）和一条 ¹⁴N 链（新合成）。在密度梯度离心中，所有分子聚集在中间密度带，而非重密度带。这正是梅塞尔森和斯塔尔在 1958 年复制一代后的发现，排除了保留复制（将产生一条重带和一条轻带）。弥散复制在第一代后同样预测出现一条中间密度带，所以要再复制一代才能区分：第 2 代后半保留复制出现两条带（杂合带和轻带），而弥散复制仍只有一条带（见例题 4）。半保留复制预测复制一次后出现一条中间密度带：得到证实。

Worked Example 4: Heavy DNA after three generations例题 4：三代之后的重 DNA

A bacterium whose DNA is entirely ¹⁵N (both strands heavy) is moved to a medium with only ¹⁴N and copies its DNA three times (three generations). Starting from one DNA molecule, find after generation 3: (a) the number of DNA molecules, (b) how many are hybrid (¹⁵N/¹⁴N) and how many are fully light (¹⁴N/¹⁴N), (c) the fraction of all strands that still carry ¹⁵N, and (d) the bands a density-gradient tube shows after generation 2 under the semi-conservative and the dispersive models.一个 DNA 全部为 ¹⁵N（两条链都是重链）的细菌被转入只含 ¹⁴N 的培养基，并把 DNA 复制三次（三代）。从一个 DNA 分子开始，求第 3 代之后：(a) DNA 分子数；(b) 其中杂合（¹⁵N/¹⁴N）和完全轻（¹⁴N/¹⁴N）的分子各有多少；(c) 仍带有 ¹⁵N 的链占所有链的比例；(d) 第 2 代之后，在半保留模型和弥散模型下密度梯度管中各出现什么条带。

(a) Molecules. Each replication doubles the number of molecules: 2³ = 8.(a) 分子数。每复制一次分子数加倍：2³ = 8。

(b) Hybrid and light. The two original ¹⁵N strands are never destroyed. Each ends up in a different molecule, so there are always exactly 2 hybrid molecules after generation 1, and 8 − 2 = 6 fully light molecules. No fully heavy molecule is left after generation 1.(b) 杂合与轻。两条原有的 ¹⁵N 链从不会被破坏。它们分别落入不同的分子，所以第 1 代之后始终恰有 2 个杂合分子，其余 8 − 2 = 6 个是完全轻的分子。第 1 代之后不再有完全重的分子。

(c) Strands. 8 molecules have 16 strands, and 2 of them are ¹⁵N: 2/16 = 1/8 = 12.5%.(c) 链。8 个分子共有 16 条链，其中 2 条是 ¹⁵N：2/16 = 1/8 = 12.5%。

(d) Generation 2. There are 4 molecules: 2 hybrid and 2 light. Semi-conservative replication predicts two bands of equal amount (intermediate and light). Dispersive replication predicts that every molecule is a mixture (about one quarter ¹⁵N), so a single band. Meselson and Stahl saw two bands, which supports the semi-conservative model.(d) 第 2 代。共 4 个分子：2 个杂合，2 个轻。半保留复制预测出现两条量相等的带（中间带和轻带）。弥散复制预测每个分子都是混合体（约四分之一为 ¹⁵N），因此只有一条带。梅塞尔森和斯塔尔看到的是两条带，这支持半保留模型。

The lac operon (prokaryotic gene regulation): Honors SBI4U D3.3lac 操纵子（原核基因调控）：荣誉 SBI4U D3.3

An operon is a cluster of functionally related genes in prokaryotes controlled by a single promoter. The lac operon of E. coli controls lactose digestion. Key components: (1) Promoter: where RNA polymerase binds. (2) Operator: a DNA switch sequence between the promoter and the genes. (3) Structural genes (lacZ, lacY, lacA): encode the enzymes for lactose metabolism. Gel electrophoresis: separating DNA fragments by size凝胶电泳：按大小分离 DNA 片段

DNA fragments are loaded into wells at one end of an agarose gel. What is the difference between transcription and translation?转录和翻译有什么区别？

Transcription copies a DNA gene into messenger RNA in the nucleus, while translation reads that mRNA at the ribosome to assemble amino acids into a protein. Together they carry out gene expression.转录是在细胞核中将 DNA 基因复制成信使 RNA，而翻译则是在核糖体上读取该 mRNA，将氨基酸组装成蛋白质。两者共同完成基因表达。

How does DNA replication work?DNA 复制是如何进行的？

DNA replication is semiconservative: the double helix unwinds and each original strand serves as a template, so each new molecule contains one old strand and one newly built complementary strand.DNA 复制是半保留复制：双螺旋解开，每条原始链作为模板，因此每个新分子都包含一条旧链和一条新合成的互补链。

What types of mutations can change the DNA sequence?哪些类型的突变会改变 DNA 序列？

Mutations include point mutations such as substitutions, plus insertions and deletions that can cause frameshifts. Their effect ranges from silent to harmful depending on how they alter the protein.突变包括如替换这样的点突变，以及可导致移码的插入和缺失。其影响从无影响到有害不等，取决于它们如何改变蛋白质。

How to use this guide如何使用本指南

Molecular genetics sits at the heart of every senior biology curriculum. All four curricula agree on the conceptual core: DNA carries hereditary information, it is copied by replication, read out by transcription into RNA, and decoded by translation into protein. They diverge sharply on depth. US NGSS HS-LS1-1 explicitly excludes the biochemistry of protein synthesis: the conceptual DNA→protein→specialized-cell explanation is the expectation. Alberta Biology 30 Unit C GO3 uses "in general" throughout, sitting between NGSS and Ontario. Ontario SBI4U Strand D goes deepest: it names polymerases I/II/III, helicase, ligase, Okazaki fragments, codon/anticodon, and the operon model: all carried here with Honors SBI4U flags. BC Anatomy & Physiology 12 covers DNA replication and gene expression at a level between NGSS and SBI4U.
