Topic 7.1主题 7.1

Variability in Statistics统计量的变异

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Distinguish a parameter from a statistic and name each in context, with its symbol.
  • Explain sampling variability: why different random samples from one population give different statistics.
  • Define a sampling distribution as the distribution of a statistic over repeated samples of the same size.
  • Describe how a simulation of many random samples approximates a sampling distribution.
  • State whether a sentence is about the population, one sample, or the sampling distribution.
  • 在具体情境中区分参数与统计量，并写出各自的符号。
  • 解释抽样变异：为什么来自同一总体的不同随机样本会得到不同的统计量。
  • 把抽样分布定义为同样大小的重复样本所得统计量的分布。
  • 说明如何用大量随机样本的模拟来近似抽样分布。
  • 判断一句话说的是总体、单个样本，还是抽样分布。

A parameter is a number that describes a population, such as the population proportion $p$, the mean $\mu$, or the standard deviation $\sigma$. It is fixed, and usually unknown. A statistic is a number computed from a sample, such as $\hat{p}$, $\bar{x}$, or $s$. A statistic is what you actually observe, and it is used to estimate the parameter. Because the sample is chosen by chance, a different random sample gives a different statistic: this is sampling variability, also called sample-to-sample variation.

参数是描述总体的数值，如总体比例 $p$、均值 $\mu$、标准差 $\sigma$。参数是固定的，而且通常未知。统计量（statistic）是由样本算出的数值，如 $\hat{p}$、$\bar{x}$、$s$。统计量是你真正观察到的值，用来估计参数。样本由机会决定，所以换一个随机样本就得到不同的统计量：这就是抽样变异（sampling variability，即样本与样本之间的差异）。

Now imagine taking repeated random samples of the same size $n$ from the same population and computing the statistic for each one. The sampling distribution of the statistic is the distribution of all the values it could take, with the probability of each. Three different distributions must never be mixed up: the population distribution (values of individuals), the distribution of one sample (the $n$ data values you collected), and the sampling distribution (values of a statistic over repeated samples). Its centre, shape and spread are what later topics will calculate.

现在设想从同一总体中反复抽取同样大小 $n$ 的随机样本（repeated samples），每次都计算统计量。统计量的抽样分布（sampling distribution）就是它所有可能取值的分布及各值的概率。必须分清三个不同的分布：总体分布（个体的取值）、单个样本的分布（你收集到的 $n$ 个数据）和抽样分布（重复抽样所得统计量的取值）。后续主题要计算的就是抽样分布的中心、形状与离散程度。

Real sampling distributions are hard to list exactly, so we simulate them: use software or a random number generator to draw many random samples, record the statistic each time, and make a dotplot or histogram. In Figure 5.1 a computer drew 40 random samples of size 50 from a population with $p=0.60$. The 40 sample proportions have mean 0.6035 and range from 0.44 to 0.76 (their SD is 0.076). A simulation shows the pattern; it does not give the exact sampling distribution.

真实的抽样分布很难逐一列出，所以我们用模拟来逼近：用软件或随机数生成器抽取许多随机样本，每次记录统计量，再作点图或直方图。图 5.1 中，计算机从 $p=0.60$ 的总体中抽取了 40 个容量为 50 的随机样本。这 40 个样本比例的平均值为 0.6035，范围从 0.44 到 0.76（标准差 0.076）。模拟显示的是规律，并不是精确的抽样分布。

A simulation can also estimate an unknown population standard deviation. Suppose a simulation (illustrative) draws 500 random samples of size $n=25$ and records the sample mean $\bar{x}$ each time, and the 500 means have standard deviation $2.4$. Because $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$, we can solve for the population value: $\sigma\approx\sigma_{\bar{x}}\sqrt{n}\approx2.4\sqrt{25}=12$. Do not stop at $2.4$: that is the spread of the sample means, not of individual values. The estimate is approximate and changes a little from one simulation to the next, and the formula for $\sigma_{\bar{x}}$ assumes a random sample that is under $10\%$ of the population.

模拟也可以用来估计未知的总体标准差。假设一次模拟（示意数据）抽取了 500 个容量 $n=25$ 的随机样本，每次记录样本均值 $\bar{x}$，这 500 个均值的标准差为 $2.4$。因为 $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$，可以反过来求总体的值：$\sigma\approx\sigma_{\bar{x}}\sqrt{n}\approx2.4\sqrt{25}=12$。不要停在 $2.4$：它是样本均值的离散程度，而不是单个数据的离散程度。这个估计只是近似值，每次模拟结果会略有不同；而且 $\sigma_{\bar{x}}$ 的公式要求样本是随机的，并且不超过总体的 $10\%$。

Worked Example 5.1: parameter and statistic in context例题 5.1：结合情境识别参数与统计量

A district has 2,400 high school students. A random sample of 80 of them is asked about part-time work: 35 of the 80 have a job, and the sample mean of hours worked last week is $\bar{x}=6.3$ hours.

  1. Population: all 2,400 students in the district. Sample: the 80 students asked.
  2. Parameters: $p$, the proportion of all 2,400 students who have a part-time job, and $\mu$, the mean hours worked last week by all 2,400 students. Both are unknown.
  3. Statistics: $\hat{p}=35/80=0.4375$ and $\bar{x}=6.3$ hours.
  4. In context: "About 43.8% of the 80 sampled students have a part-time job; this estimates the proportion of all students in the district who do." A new random sample of 80 would give different values of $\hat{p}$ and $\bar{x}$.
  5. Why this works: a number from the sample is a statistic; the same quantity for everyone in the district is the parameter.

某学区有 2,400 名高中生。随机抽取其中 80 人询问兼职情况：80 人中 35 人有工作，上周工作时间的样本均值为 $\bar{x}=6.3$ 小时。

  1. 总体：该学区全部 2,400 名学生。样本：被询问的 80 名学生。
  2. 参数：$p$，全部 2,400 名学生中有兼职的比例；$\mu$，全部 2,400 名学生上周工作时间的均值。两者都未知。
  3. 统计量：$\hat{p}=35/80=0.4375$，$\bar{x}=6.3$ 小时。
  4. 结合情境：“被抽中的 80 名学生中约 43.8% 有兼职，这是对全学区有兼职学生比例的估计。”再抽一个容量 80 的随机样本，$\hat{p}$ 与 $\bar{x}$ 的值就会不同。
  5. 原理：来自样本的数值是统计量；同一个量在全学区所有人身上的取值才是参数。
Worked Example 5.2: reading a simulated sampling distribution例题 5.2：读懂模拟得到的抽样分布

Use Figure 5.1: 40 simulated sample proportions from samples of size 50 when the true proportion is $p=0.60$. (a) What does one dot represent? (b) What does the whole dotplot show? (c) A new survey of 50 randomly chosen students gives $\hat{p}=0.52$. Is that evidence that $p$ is not 0.60?

  1. (a) One dot is the sample proportion $\hat{p}$ from one random sample of 50 students, not one student.
  2. (b) The dotplot approximates the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ for samples of size 50: it centres near 0.60 (mean 0.6035), is roughly symmetric, and varies from 0.44 to 0.76.
  3. (c) No. A value of 0.52 (26 out of 50) appears twice among the 40 dots, so a sample like that happens easily by chance when $p=0.60$. One sample result inside the usual spread is not evidence that $p\neq 0.60$.
  4. Why this works: to judge whether a statistic is unusual, compare it with the spread of its sampling distribution, not with the parameter alone.

使用图 5.1：当真实比例 $p=0.60$ 时，由容量 50 的样本模拟得到 40 个样本比例。(a) 一个点代表什么？(b) 整个点图显示什么？(c) 对 50 名随机抽取的学生再做一次调查，得到 $\hat{p}=0.52$。这能说明 $p$ 不是 0.60 吗？

  1. (a) 一个点是一个容量 50 的随机样本的样本比例 $\hat{p}$，而不是一名学生。
  2. (b) 点图近似于容量 50 时 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布：中心在 0.60 附近（平均值 0.6035），大致对称，取值从 0.44 到 0.76。
  3. (c) 不能。0.52（50 人中 26 人）在 40 个点中出现了两次，所以当 $p=0.60$ 时这样的样本很容易靠机会出现。落在通常波动范围之内的一次样本结果，不能证明 $p\neq 0.60$。
  4. 原理：要判断一个统计量是否异常，应把它与其抽样分布的离散程度比较，而不是只与参数比较。
Worked Example 5.2b: using a simulation to judge a claim例题 5.2b：用模拟判断一个说法

A school newspaper claims that 40% of the students at a large school bike to class. A random sample of 50 students gives 12 who bike. A student tests the claim by simulation: she draws 200 random samples of size 50 from a population with $p=0.40$ and records $\hat{p}$ each time. Only 3 of the 200 simulated values are 0.24 or lower. Does the sample give convincing evidence that fewer than 40% of the students bike?

  1. What the simulation shows: each dot is one $\hat{p}$ from one random sample of 50 students, and there are 200 dots because the simulation was repeated 200 times. The size of each sample is $n=50$, not 200.
  2. The statistic: $\hat{p}=12/50=0.24$, which is $0.16$ below the claimed value $0.40$.
  3. Compare with the sampling distribution: $3/200=0.015$, so a result as low as 0.24 turns up in about 1.5% of samples when the claim is true. A Normal model gives a similar answer: $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{0.40(0.60)/50}\approx0.0693$, $z=\dfrac{0.24-0.40}{0.0693}\approx-2.31$, area about $0.010$.
  4. Conclusion in context: a complete answer states that if 40% of all students at this school bike, a random sample of 50 would give $\hat{p}\le0.24$ only about 1.5% of the time, so the sample gives convincing evidence that fewer than 40% of the students at the school bike. The 1.5% comes from one simulation and would change a little in a new run.
  5. Why this works: a sample result is judged by where it falls in the sampling distribution that the claim predicts, and the simulation builds that distribution without any formula.

校报声称某大型学校有 40% 的学生骑车上学。随机抽取 50 名学生，其中 12 人骑车。一名学生用模拟检验这个说法：她从 $p=0.40$ 的总体中抽取 200 个容量为 50 的随机样本，每次记录 $\hat{p}$。200 个模拟值中只有 3 个不超过 0.24。这个样本是否提供了令人信服的证据，说明骑车的学生少于 40%？

  1. 模拟显示什么：每个点是一个容量 50 的随机样本的 $\hat{p}$，共有 200 个点是因为模拟重复了 200 次。每个样本的容量是 $n=50$，而不是 200。
  2. 统计量：$\hat{p}=12/50=0.24$，比声称的 $0.40$ 低 $0.16$。
  3. 与抽样分布比较：$3/200=0.015$，所以当该说法成立时，约 1.5% 的样本会得到不超过 0.24 的结果。正态模型给出相近的答案：$\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{0.40(0.60)/50}\approx0.0693$，$z=\dfrac{0.24-0.40}{0.0693}\approx-2.31$，面积约为 $0.010$。
  4. 结合情境的结论：完整答案要点：若该校 40% 的学生骑车，随机抽取 50 人时只有约 1.5% 的样本会得到 $\hat{p}\le0.24$，所以样本提供了令人信服的证据，说明该校骑车的学生少于 40%。这个 1.5% 来自一次模拟，重新运行会略有变化。
  5. 原理：判断一个样本结果，要看它落在该说法所预测的抽样分布的什么位置；模拟不用公式就能建立这个分布。
Exam tip考试提示

Every sentence about variability must say which distribution it describes: the population, one sample, or the sampling distribution of a named statistic ("the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ for samples of size 50"). Answers are clearer and more complete when they say "the statistic varies from sample to sample" rather than "the sample varies". Always state the parameter in context: its symbol, what it measures, and for which population.

凡是谈到变异的句子，都要说明描述的是哪个分布：总体、单个样本，还是某个具名统计量的抽样分布（如“容量 50 的样本中 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布”）。说“统计量随样本而变”比说“样本在变”更清楚、更完整。参数一定要结合情境陈述：符号、衡量什么、针对哪个总体。

Common mistakes
  • Calling $\bar{x}$ a parameter because it "describes the data". A summary of a sample is always a statistic.
  • Thinking a statistic is wrong because it differs from the parameter. Differences of this kind are expected sampling variability.
  • Confusing a sample histogram of $n$ individuals with the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}$ or $\hat{p}$.
常见错误
  • 因为“描述了数据”就把 $\bar{x}$ 叫作参数。样本的概括数值永远是统计量。
  • 因为统计量与参数不同就认为它错了。这种差异属于正常的抽样变异。
  • 把 $n$ 个个体的样本直方图与 $\bar{x}$ 或 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布混为一谈。
Common Mistake: counting the repetitions as the sample size

A student looks at Figure 5.1, sees 40 dots and writes $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{0.60(0.40)/40}\approx0.0775$. That uses $n=40$, which is wrong: each dot came from a sample of $n=50$, so the spread is $\sqrt{0.60(0.40)/50}\approx0.0693$. The number of dots is how many times the simulation was repeated; more repetitions give a smoother picture of the same sampling distribution and do not change its centre or spread. Fix: say what $n$ is (the size of each sample) and what the number of dots is (the number of repetitions) before using either.

常见错误：把重复次数当成样本容量

某学生看着图 5.1 数出 40 个点，就写 $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{0.60(0.40)/40}\approx0.0775$。这里用了 $n=40$，是错的：每个点来自容量 $n=50$ 的样本，所以离散程度是 $\sqrt{0.60(0.40)/50}\approx0.0693$。点的个数是模拟重复的次数；重复越多，画出的同一个抽样分布越平滑，但中心和离散程度不会改变。改正：使用前先说清 $n$ 是什么（每个样本的容量）、点的个数是什么（重复次数）。

Topic 7.2主题 7.2

Sampling distributions and the Central Limit Theorem抽样分布与中心极限定理

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Distinguish a parameter from a statistic and describe a sampling distribution.
  • State the mean, standard deviation and shape of the sampling distribution of $\hat p$ and of $\bar x$, with conditions.
  • Apply the Central Limit Theorem and explain what happens as $n$ grows.
  • Calculate probabilities for $\hat p$ and $\bar x$ with the normal model (including a normal-distribution review).
  • 区分参数与统计量，并描述抽样分布。
  • 陈述 $\hat p$ 与 $\bar x$ 的抽样分布的均值、标准差与形状及其条件。
  • 应用中心极限定理，并说明 $n$ 增大时会发生什么。
  • 用正态模型计算 $\hat p$ 与 $\bar x$ 的概率（含正态分布复习）。

A parameter is a number describing a population ($p$, $\mu$); a statistic is a number computed from a sample ($\hat p$, $\bar x$). A statistic varies from sample to sample. Its sampling distribution is the distribution of values of the statistic over all possible samples of the same size from the same population.

参数是描述总体的数（$p$、$\mu$）；统计量是由样本算出的数（$\hat p$、$\bar x$）。统计量会随样本而变化。它的抽样分布是：从同一总体抽取所有同样大小的样本时，该统计量取值的分布。

Must know

Central Limit Theorem (CLT): for a population with mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$, the sampling distribution of $\bar x$ is approximately normal when $n$ is large, whatever the shape of the population; the approximation improves as $n$ grows. If the population is itself normal, $\bar x$ is normal for every $n$. Increasing $n$ shrinks the spread by the factor $\sqrt n$ but does not change the center.

必备知识

中心极限定理（CLT）：对均值 $\mu$、标准差 $\sigma$ 的总体，$n$ 较大时 $\bar x$ 的抽样分布近似为正态，与总体形状无关；$n$ 越大近似越好。若总体本身正态，则对任意 $n$，$\bar x$ 都是正态。增大 $n$ 使离散程度按 $\sqrt n$ 缩小，但不改变中心。

Explore it: the CLT visualizer lets you change the population shape and the sample size $n$ and shows the histogram of many sample means. See also the binomial sampling simulator for $\hat p$.

动手探索：CLT 可视化工具可改变总体形状与样本量 $n$，并显示大量样本均值的直方图。关于 $\hat p$ 还可参见二项抽样模拟器。

Also on the 2027 exam: review of the normal distribution (topic 2.11)

A normal distribution is symmetric and described by $\mu$ and $\sigma$. Standardize with $z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$. The empirical rule gives about $68\%$, $95\%$ and $99.7\%$ within $1$, $2$ and $3$ standard deviations. Calculator: normalcdf(lower, upper, μ, σ) for areas and invNorm(area, μ, σ) for percentiles (TI-Nspire: Normal Cdf and Inverse Normal). Use $\sigma/\sqrt n$ or $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$ as the standard deviation when the question is about a sample statistic.

正态分布对称，由 $\mu$ 与 $\sigma$ 描述。用 $z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$ 标准化。经验法则：在 $1$、$2$、$3$ 个标准差内分别约有 $68\%$、$95\%$、$99.7\%$。计算器：求面积用 normalcdf(下限, 上限, μ, σ)，求百分位数用 invNorm(面积, μ, σ)（TI-Nspire：Normal Cdf 与 Inverse Normal）。当问题涉及样本统计量时，标准差要用 $\sigma/\sqrt n$ 或 $\sqrt{p(1-p)/n}$。

Topic 7.3主题 7.3

The Central Limit Theorem中心极限定理

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State the Central Limit Theorem and describe the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}$ it predicts.
  • Explain how the shape of the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}$ depends on the shape of the population and on $n$.
  • Decide whether a Normal model for $\bar{x}$ is justified from the population shape and the sample size.
  • Explain why the centre of the sampling distribution stays at $\mu$ while its spread decreases as $n$ increases.
  • Calculate a probability for a sample mean from a skewed population when $n$ is large.
  • 陈述中心极限定理，并描述它所预测的 $\bar{x}$ 的抽样分布。
  • 解释 $\bar{x}$ 抽样分布的形状如何依赖于总体的形状与 $n$。
  • 根据总体形状与样本量，判断能否对 $\bar{x}$ 使用正态模型。
  • 解释为什么抽样分布的中心保持在 $\mu$，而离散程度随 $n$ 增大而变小。
  • 当 $n$ 较大时，计算来自偏斜总体的样本均值的概率。

If the population is Normal (a normally distributed population), the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}$ is exactly Normal for every sample size, even $n=1$. If the population is not Normal, the shape of the sampling distribution depends on $n$. The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) says that when the sample is a random sample and $n$ is large, the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}$ is approximately Normal whatever the shape of the population.

若总体服从正态分布（population is Normal），则 $\bar{x}$ 的抽样分布对任何样本量（包括 $n=1$）都恰好是正态的。若总体不是正态的，抽样分布的形状取决于 $n$。中心极限定理（Central Limit Theorem，CLT）指出：当样本为随机样本且 $n$ 较大时，无论总体形状如何，$\bar{x}$ 的抽样分布都近似正态（approximately Normal）。

The usual guideline is $n \ge 30$: with at least 30 observations, treat $\bar{x}$ as approximately Normal. It is a guideline, not a law. A population that is strongly skewed or has outliers may need a larger $n$, while a nearly symmetric population needs much less. For small $n$ from a non-Normal population, you cannot claim a Normal shape. In every case the centre of the sampling distribution stays at $\mu$, and the spread shrinks as $n$ grows (Figure 5.3).

常用的经验标准是 $n \ge 30$：观测不少于 30 个（at least 30）时，可把 $\bar{x}$ 看作近似正态。这是经验标准，不是定律。强烈偏斜（skewed）或有离群值的总体可能需要更大的 $n$，近似对称的总体则要小得多。对来自非正态总体、$n$ 较小的样本，不能断言形状为正态。无论哪种情形，抽样分布的中心都保持在 $\mu$，离散程度随 $n$ 增大而变小（图 5.3）。

Topic 7.4主题 7.4

Biased and Unbiased Point Estimates有偏与无偏的点估计

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define a point estimate and identify the statistic used to estimate a given parameter.
  • Explain what it means for a statistic to be an unbiased estimator in terms of the mean of its sampling distribution.
  • Distinguish bias from variability using a picture of a sampling distribution.
  • Explain why a larger sample reduces variability but does not remove bias.
  • Identify an estimator or sampling method as biased from a description and state the direction of the bias.
  • 定义点估计，并为给定参数指出用来估计它的统计量。
  • 用抽样分布的均值解释“无偏估计量”的含义。
  • 借助抽样分布的图区分偏差与变异。
  • 解释为什么更大的样本降低变异，却不能消除偏差。
  • 根据描述判断某个估计量或抽样方法是否有偏，并说明偏差的方向。

A point estimate is the value of a statistic used as a single-number estimate of a parameter: $\bar{x}$ estimates $\mu$, and $\hat{p}$ estimates $p$. A statistic is an unbiased estimator of a parameter when the mean of its sampling distribution equals the parameter. Unbiased does not mean any one estimate is correct: individual values land above or below the parameter, but they balance out over repeated samples. Both $\bar{x}$ (for $\mu$) and $\hat{p}$ (for $p$) are unbiased when the data come from a random sample.

点估计（point estimate）是用统计量的值作为参数的单一数值估计：$\bar{x}$ 估计 $\mu$，$\hat{p}$ 估计 $p$。当统计量的抽样分布的均值等于参数时，该统计量是该参数的无偏估计量（unbiased estimator）。无偏并不表示某一次估计是准确的：个别取值有高有低，但在重复抽样中会相互抵消。当数据来自随机样本时，$\bar{x}$（估计 $\mu$）与 $\hat{p}$（估计 $p$）都是无偏的。

Bias is about the centre: a biased statistic has a sampling distribution whose mean is not the parameter, so it is systematically too high or too low. Variability is about the spread of the sampling distribution: how much the statistic changes from sample to sample. The best statistic has low bias and low variability (Figure 5.4). Bias comes from the method (a flawed sampling plan or a poor estimator); variability is reduced by taking a larger sample. A larger sample makes the sampling distribution narrower around the same wrong centre if the method is biased, so it does not remove bias.

偏差（bias）关乎中心：有偏统计量的抽样分布的均值不等于参数，因此系统性地偏高或偏低。变异（variability）关乎抽样分布的离散程度：统计量随样本变化的幅度。最好的统计量偏差小且变异小（图 5.4）。偏差来自方法（有缺陷的抽样方案或不好的估计量）；变异则可通过更大的样本（larger sample）降低。如果方法有偏，更大的样本只会让抽样分布围绕同一个错误的中心变窄，所以并不能消除偏差。

Worked Example 5.7: checking bias by listing every sample例题 5.7：列出所有样本来检验偏差

A tiny population has five values: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, so $\mu=6$ and the largest value is 10. List every possible sample of size 2 (no repeats). Compare the mean of the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}$ with $\mu$, and the mean of the sampling distribution of the sample maximum with 10.

  4. Why this works: unbiasedness is a statement about the average over all samples, which is exactly what listing every sample gives.

  4. 原理：无偏性说的是所有样本上的平均，而列出全部样本恰好给出这个平均。
Worked Example 5.8: bias versus variability in a poll例题 5.8：民调中的偏差与变异

A company surveys customers by calling only landline numbers from a customer list that includes many households with no landline. (a) It surveys 500 customers and then 5,000. Does the larger sample fix the problem? (b) For a random sample with $p=0.5$, compare the standard deviation of $\hat{p}$ when $n=100$ and $n=400$.

A larger sample is just a more precise estimate of the wrong value: variability falls, bias stays. The fix is a better method, such as random selection from the full list with calls to every phone type.
  2. (b) $\sqrt{\dfrac{0.5(0.5)}{100}}=0.05$ and $\sqrt{\dfrac{0.5(0.5)}{400}}=0.025$. Quadrupling $n$ halves the variability of $\hat{p}$.

某公司只拨打客户名单中的座机号码来调查客户，而名单中许多家庭没有座机。(a) 它先调查了 500 名客户，又调查了 5,000 名。更大的样本能解决问题吗？(b) 对 $p=0.5$ 的随机样本，比较 $\hat{p}$ 的标准差在 $n=100$ 和 $n=400$ 时的大小。

  2. (b) $\sqrt{\dfrac{0.5(0.5)}{100}}=0.05$，$\sqrt{\dfrac{0.5(0.5)}{400}}=0.025$。$n$ 增为 4 倍，$\hat{p}$ 的变异减半。
  3. 原理：样本量只出现在离散程度的公式中，不影响中心，所以无法消除偏差。
Worked Example 5.8b: choosing between estimators from a simulation例题 5.8b：根据模拟在估计量之间做选择

To estimate a population mean $\mu=50$, a student simulates three estimators. Each is computed from 1,000 random samples of size $n=25$, and the mean and standard deviation of the 1,000 values are recorded. Estimator A: mean 50.1, SD 6.2. Estimator B: mean 54.8, SD 1.1.   1. (a) A: bias about $50.1-50=+0.1$ (essentially none) but large variability (SD 6.2). B: bias $54.8-50=+4.8$ (it overestimates) and small variability (SD 1.1). C: bias about $49.98-50=-0.02$ (essentially none) and small variability (SD 1.3).
  2. (b) C. Check the centre first: A and C are centred on $\mu$ and B is not. Among A and C, C has the far smaller spread. B has the smallest SD, but its values agree with each other around the wrong value, $4.8/1.1\approx4.4$ standard deviations above $\mu$.

  4. Conclusion: a complete answer states that C has low bias and low variability, that B's small SD does not make up for its bias, and that a larger sample reduces variability only.
  5. Why this works: bias is the gap between the centre of the sampling distribution and the parameter; variability is the spread around that centre. Judge both, in that order.

  4. 结论：完整答案要点：C 偏差低且变异低；B 的小标准差弥补不了它的偏差；更大的样本只降低变异。
  5. 原理：偏差是抽样分布的中心与参数之间的差距；变异是围绕该中心的离散程度。两者都要判断，并且先看偏差。

Bias, unbiased estimators and variability偏差、无偏估计量与变异

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Distinguish bias from variability and explain how sample size affects each.
  • Verify unbiasedness by listing a small sampling distribution.
  • Explain why a larger sample does not fix a biased sampling method.
  • 定义无偏估计量并举例。

  • 说明为什么更大的样本不能纠正有偏的抽样方法。

A statistic used to estimate a parameter is an estimator. It is unbiased if the mean of its sampling distribution equals the parameter it estimates. Variability describes the spread of the sampling distribution. A good estimator has little bias and little variability (Figure 8).

用来估计参数的统计量称为估计量。若它的抽样分布的均值等于所估计的参数，则称其无偏。变异描述抽样分布的离散程度。好的估计量偏差小且变异小（图 8）。

Must know

The sample proportion $\hat p$ is an unbiased estimator of $p$, and the sample mean $\bar x$ is an unbiased estimator of $\mu$, when samples are random. The sample variance $s^2$ (dividing by $n-1$) is unbiased for $\sigma^2$, although $s$ is slightly biased low for $\sigma$. A larger random sample reduces variability but is not what removes bias; bias comes from the sampling method or the estimator itself.

必备知识

在随机抽样下，样本比例 $\hat p$ 是 $p$ 的无偏估计量，样本均值 $\bar x$ 是 $\mu$ 的无偏估计量。样本方差 $s^2$（除以 $n-1$）是 $\sigma^2$ 的无偏估计量，但 $s$ 对 $\sigma$ 略有偏低。更大的随机样本可降低变异，但消除不了偏差；偏差来自抽样方法或估计量本身。

Worked Example 4.13a: Listing a sampling distribution by hand

A tiny population has the three values $2,\,4,\,6$ (so $\mu=4$ and population variance $\sigma^2=8/3$). Take every possible sample of size $2$ with replacement ($9$ equally likely samples). Show that $\bar x$ is unbiased, and show that the sample maximum is a biased estimator of the population maximum $6$.

  1. Sample means for $(2,2),(2,4),(2,6),(4,2),(4,4),(4,6),(6,2),(6,4),(6,6)$ are $2,3,4,3,4,5,4,5,6$.
  2. $\mu_{\bar x}=(2+3+4+3+4+5+4+5+6)/9=36/9=4=\mu$. So $\bar x$ is unbiased. Also $\sigma_{\bar x}^2=\frac{4+1+0+1+0+1+0+1+4}{9}=\frac{12}{9}=\frac43=\frac{\sigma^2}{n}=\frac{8/3}{2}$.
  3. Sample maxima are $2,4,6,4,4,6,6,6,6$, with mean $44/9\approx4.89$. This is below the true maximum $6$, so the sample maximum is biased low (it can never exceed the population maximum and is usually below it).
  4. Check on variance: sample variances $s^2=(a-b)^2/2$ are $0,2,8,2,0,2,8,2,0$ with mean $24/9=8/3=\sigma^2$, so dividing by $n-1$ is unbiased. Dividing by $n$ would give mean $4/3$, which is biased low.

  1. $(2,2),(2,4),(2,6),(4,2),(4,4),(4,6),(6,2),(6,4),(6,6)$ 的样本均值为 $2,3,4,3,4,5,4,5,6$。

  3. 样本最大值为 $2,4,6,4,4,6,6,6,6$，均值 $44/9\approx4.89$。低于真实最大值 $6$，所以样本最大值是偏低的有偏估计（它不可能超过总体最大值，且通常低于它）。
  4. 方差验证：样本方差 $s^2=(a-b)^2/2$ 取值 $0,2,8,2,0,2,8,2,0$，均值 $24/9=8/3=\sigma^2$，所以除以 $n-1$ 是无偏的。若除以 $n$，均值为 $4/3$，偏低。
Worked Example 4.13b: A big sample with a biased method

A school wants the proportion $p$ of students who support a later start time. An online poll that anyone may answer gets $n=1500$ responses with $\hat p=0.72$. A later random check of the whole school shows the true proportion is $p=0.60$. Compare the variability of the poll with the size of its error.

  1. If the sample had been random, $\sigma_{\hat p}=\sqrt{0.6(0.4)/1500}=\sqrt{0.00016}\approx0.0126$.
  2. The error $0.72-0.60=0.12$ is about $0.12/0.0126\approx9.5$ standard deviations, far too large for chance variation.
  3. So the discrepancy is bias from the voluntary-response method (people with strong opinions respond), not sampling variability.
  4. Increasing $n$ would shrink $\sigma_{\hat p}$ further but would not move the center of the biased sampling distribution toward $0.60$. Only a better sampling method (random selection) removes the bias.

Worked Example 4.13c: Computing the bias caused by nonresponse

A district surveys randomly chosen families about a later school start. In the population, $p=0.60$ of families support it. For a random sample of size $n$ from a population with proportion $p$, the sampling distribution of the sample proportion $\hat{p}$ has centre $\mu_{\hat{p}}=p$ (the mean of $\hat{p}$ equals $p$, so $\hat{p}$ is unbiased) and standard deviation $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{p(1-p)}{n}}$. The spread is smaller for larger $n$ (Figure 5.5), and for $p$ nearer 0 or 1.

对容量为 $n$、来自比例为 $p$ 的总体的随机样本，样本比例 $\hat{p}$ 的抽样分布的中心为 $\mu_{\hat{p}}=p$（$\hat{p}$ 的均值等于 $p$，所以 $\hat{p}$ 无偏），标准差为 $\sigma_{\hat{p}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{p(1-p)}{n}}$。$n$ 越大离散程度越小（图 5.5），$p$ 越接近 0 或 1 离散程度也越小。

The Normal model for $\hat{p}$ needs three conditions. Random: the data come from a random sample (or a randomized experiment). 10% condition: when sampling without replacement, $n\le 0.10N$, so that individual selections are close to independent and the formula for $\sigma_{\hat{p}}$ holds. Large Counts: $np \ge 10$ and $n(1-p) \ge 10$, which say that the expected numbers of successes and failures are both at least 10, so the distribution is not skewed against the 0 or 1 boundary. Check these with the population value $p$ when it is known.

对 $\hat{p}$ 使用正态模型需要三个条件。随机：数据来自随机样本（或随机化实验）。10% 条件：无放回抽样时 $n\le 0.10N$，这样各次选取近似独立，$\sigma_{\hat{p}}$ 的公式才成立。大计数条件（Large Counts）：$np \ge 10$ 且 $n(1-p) \ge 10$，即成功与失败的期望个数都至少为 10，分布才不会被 0 或 1 的边界挤得偏斜。已知总体比例 $p$ 时，用总体的值来检验这些条件。

  5. Why this works: once the conditions are checked, $\hat{p}$ behaves like a Normal variable with the stated centre and spread.

Sampling Distribution for a Difference in Sample Proportions样本比例之差的抽样分布

Take independent random samples of sizes $n_1$ and $n_2$ from two populations with proportions $p_1$ and $p_2$. The difference in sample proportions $\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2$ has a sampling distribution with mean $\mu_{\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2}=p_1-p_2$ and standard deviation $\sigma_{\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{p_1(1-p_1)}{n_1}+\dfrac{p_2(1-p_2)}{n_2}}$. Because the samples are independent, the variances of $\hat{p}_1$ and $\hat{p}_2$ add; the standard deviations do not. The variability of a difference is larger than that of either proportion alone, even though the centre is subtracted.

独立地随机抽取容量为 $n_1$ 与 $n_2$ 的样本，它们分别来自比例为 $p_1$ 与 $p_2$ 的两个总体。样本比例之差（difference in sample proportions）$\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2$ 的抽样分布，均值为 $\mu_{\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2}=p_1-p_2$，标准差为 $\sigma_{\hat{p}_1-\hat{p}_2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{p_1(1-p_1)}{n_1}+\dfrac{p_2(1-p_2)}{n_2}}$。因为样本相互独立，$\hat{p}_1$ 与 $\hat{p}_2$ 的方差相加，标准差不能相加。尽管中心是相减得到的，差的变异却比任何单个比例的变异都大。

Conditions: both samples are random; the two samples are independent of each other (not matched or paired); the 10% condition holds in each population ($n_1\le0.10N_1$ and $n_2\le0.10N_2$); and Large Counts holds for each sample, meaning $n_1p_1$, $n_1(1-p_1)$, $n_2p_2$ and $n_2(1-p_2)$ are all at least 10. Let the population have mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$, and take a random sample of size $n$. The sample mean $\bar{x}$ has a sampling distribution with mean $\mu_{\bar{x}}=\mu$ (the mean of the sampling distribution equals the population mean, so $\bar{x}$ is unbiased) and standard deviation $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$. Averages vary less than individuals, and you need four times as many observations to cut the standard deviation in half (Figure 5.7).

设总体的均值为 $\mu$、标准差为 $\sigma$，抽取容量为 $n$ 的随机样本。样本均值（sample mean）$\bar{x}$ 的抽样分布，均值为 $\mu_{\bar{x}}=\mu$（抽样分布的均值等于总体均值，所以 $\bar{x}$ 无偏），标准差为 $\sigma_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$。平均值的波动比个体小，而要把标准差减半，需要 4 倍的观测数（图 5.7）。

Conditions for a Normal model for $\bar{x}$. Random: a random sample (or randomized experiment). 10% condition: when sampling without replacement, $n\le 0.10N$, so the formula $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$ is valid. Normal or large: the population is Normal (then $\bar{x}$ is Normal for any $n$), or $n\ge30$ so the Central Limit Theorem applies, or the sample data show no strong skew or outliers when $n$ is small. If the population is Normal you may say "the population is Normal" only if the problem states it or the sample plot supports it.

对 $\bar{x}$ 使用正态模型的条件。随机：随机样本（或随机化实验）。10% 条件：无放回抽样时 $n\le 0.10N$，这样公式 $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$ 才有效。正态或样本量大：总体是正态的（此时 $\bar{x}$ 在任何 $n$ 下都是正态的）；或 $n\ge30$，使中心极限定理（Central Limit Theorem）适用；或 $n$ 较小时，样本数据没有强烈偏斜或离群值。只有当题目说明总体是正态的，或样本图支持这一点时，才能写“总体是正态的”。

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Check the conditions for two independent samples: random, independent, 10% condition, and Normal or large for each sample.
  • Calculate a probability for a difference of sample means using a Normal model.
  • Interpret the result in context, naming both populations and the direction of the difference.
  • Describe how a simulation (dotplot or histogram) approximates a sampling distribution and compare it with the formula.
  • 写出 $\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2$ 的抽样分布的均值与标准差。
  • 检验两个独立样本的条件：随机、独立、10% 条件，以及每个样本的正态或样本量大。
  • 用正态模型计算样本均值之差的概率。
  • 结合情境解释结果，点明两个总体与差的方向。
  • 说明如何用模拟（点图或直方图）近似抽样分布，并与公式比较。

Take independent random samples of sizes $n_1$ and $n_2$ from two populations with means $\mu_1$, $\mu_2$ and standard deviations $\sigma_1$, $\sigma_2$. The difference in sample means $\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2$ has a sampling distribution with mean $\mu_{\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2}=\mu_1-\mu_2$ and standard deviation $\sigma_{\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sigma_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{\sigma_2^2}{n_2}}$. As in 5.6, the variances add because the samples are independent.

独立随机抽取容量为 $n_1$ 与 $n_2$ 的样本，它们分别来自均值为 $\mu_1$、$\mu_2$，标准差为 $\sigma_1$、$\sigma_2$ 的两个总体。样本均值之差（difference in sample means）$\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2$ 的抽样分布，均值为 $\mu_{\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2}=\mu_1-\mu_2$，标准差为 $\sigma_{\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sigma_1^2}{n_1}+\dfrac{\sigma_2^2}{n_2}}$。与 5.6 一样，因为样本独立，所以方差相加。

Conditions: the two samples are random and independent of each other; the 10% condition holds in both populations; and each sample is from a Normal population or is large ($n\ge30$ in each group) so the Central Limit Theorem applies to each sample mean. Check each sample: if both samples are large the difference is approximately Normal, and if both populations are Normal the difference is exactly Normal for any sample sizes.

条件：两个样本是随机的，并且彼此独立；两个总体都满足10% 条件；每个样本都来自正态总体，或样本量大（每组 $n\ge30$），使中心极限定理适用于每个样本均值。要检验每个样本：两个样本都大时，差近似正态；两个总体都是正态时，无论样本量多大，差都恰好是正态的。

Worked Example 5.15: a difference of sample means例题 5.15：样本均值之差

Independent random samples of $n_1=40$ and $n_2=50$ students are taken. (a) Find the probability that $\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2$ exceeds 6.   5. In context: there is about a 19% chance that the mean score in the District 1 sample is more than 6 points higher than in the District 2 sample, and only about a 4% chance that the District 2 sample mean beats District 1's although District 1 has the higher population mean.

It approximates the sampling distribution of $\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2$. Its centre should be near $\mu_1-\mu_2$, and its spread near $\sqrt{\sigma_1^2/n_1+\sigma_2^2/n_2}$.

Prompt. At a large university, 40% of students use the shuttle bus (illustrative). A random sample of $n=100$ students is taken. (a) Describe the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$: centre, standard deviation, and shape, with the conditions. (b) Find the probability that more than 50% of the sample use the shuttle. (c) How would the standard deviation change if $n=400$?

PartWhat a complete answer includes
(a)Centre $\mu_{\hat{p}}=0.40$; SD $\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{100}}=0.0490$. Conditions: random sample; 10% condition (100 students is less than 10% of the students at a large university); $np=40\ge10$ and $n(1-p)=60\ge10$. So the sampling distribution is approximately Normal.
(b)$z=\dfrac{0.50-0.40}{0.0490}\approx2.04$; normalcdf(0.50, 1E99, 0.40, 0.0490) $=0.0206$. In context: there is about a 2% chance that more than 50% of 100 randomly chosen students use the shuttle.
(c)$\sqrt{\dfrac{0.40(0.60)}{400}}=0.0245$, half as large. Quadrupling the sample size halves the standard deviation, and the centre stays at 0.40.
