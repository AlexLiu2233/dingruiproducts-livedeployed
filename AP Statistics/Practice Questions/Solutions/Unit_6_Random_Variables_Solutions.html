A synthetic charity game costs 3 dollars to play. You win 10 dollars with probability 0.2, 4 dollars with probability 0.3, and 0 dollars with probability 0.5. What is the expected net gain per play?
某合成慈善游戏每次参加费用为 3 美元。以概率 0.2 赢得 10 美元，以概率 0.3 赢得 4 美元，以概率 0.5 赢得 0 美元。每次参加的期望净收益是多少？
Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Expected winnings are $10(0.2)+4(0.3)+0(0.5)=3.20$ dollars. The net gain subtracts the certain cost of 3 dollars: $3.20-3.00=+0.20$ dollars per play on average. Equivalently the net gains are $7,\,1,\,-3$ with the same probabilities: $7(0.2)+1(0.3)-3(0.5)=0.20$.期望奖金为 $10(0.2)+4(0.3)+0(0.5)=3.20$ 美元。净收益要减去确定的 3 美元费用：平均每次 $3.20-3.00=+0.20$ 美元。等价地，净收益为 $7,\,1,\,-3$，概率不变：$7(0.2)+1(0.3)-3(0.5)=0.20$。

Why the options attract选项误区
  1. Stops at the expected winnings $3.20$ and forgets the certain 3-dollar cost of playing.停在期望奖金 $3.20$，忘记了确定的 3 美元费用。
  2. Correct: weights every outcome by its probability, then nets out the cost.正确：按概率加权后再扣除费用。
  3. The largest payout is not the expected value; it occurs only with probability $0.2$.最高奖金不是期望值；它只以概率 $0.2$ 出现。
  4. $(7+1-3)/3\approx1.67$ weights the three net gains equally, but each outcome needs its own probability.$(7+1-3)/3\approx1.67$ 对三个净收益等权平均，但每个结果应使用各自的概率。
An expected value weights every outcome by its probability; subtract any certain cost after weighting, or build it into each net gain.期望值要按概率给每个结果加权；确定的成本可在加权后再减，或直接计入每个净收益。

Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The standard deviation measures the typical distance from the mean. Both means are 50. Spinner P's outcomes are 5 points from 50 and Spinner Q's are 30 points from 50, so P has the smaller spread. Check: $SD_P=5$ and $SD_Q=30$ points.标准差衡量与均值的典型距离。两个均值都是 50。转盘 P 的结果离 50 为 5 分，转盘 Q 的离 50 为 30 分，所以 P 的离散程度更小。验算：$SD_P=5$，$SD_Q=30$ 分。

Why the options attract (M 10)选项误区（M 10）
  1. Correct: compares each outcome's distance from the shared mean.正确：比较各结果到共同均值的距离。
  2. Equal means do not force equal spread; here the spreads are 5 and 30.均值相等并不意味着离散程度相等；这里分别是 5 和 30。

  4. The deviations $-30$ and $+30$ do cancel when added, which is exactly why they are squared before averaging: $SD_Q=\sqrt{0.5(900)+0.5(900)}=30$, larger than $SD_P=5$.偏差 $-30$ 与 $+30$ 相加确实会抵消，所以才要先平方再平均：$SD_Q=\sqrt{0.5(900)+0.5(900)}=30$，大于 $SD_P=5$。
Spread lives in the distances of the outcomes from the mean; two games with the same mean can carry very different risk.离散程度体现在各结果到均值的距离上；均值相同的两个游戏，风险可能大不相同。

Answer: D答案：D

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

For $Y=a+bX$, $\mu_Y=a+b\mu_X=8-3(5)=-7$ and $\sigma_Y=|b|\sigma_X=3(2)=6$. Adding the constant 8 shifts the center but does not change the spread, and the sign of $b$ drops out.对 $Y=a+bX$，$\mu_Y=a+b\mu_X=8-3(5)=-7$，$\sigma_Y=|b|\sigma_X=3(2)=6$。加常数 8 只平移中心，不改变离散程度，$b$ 的符号也不影响标准差。

Why the options attract选项误区
  1. A standard deviation cannot be negative; it is $|b|\sigma_X=6$.标准差不可能为负；应为 $|b|\sigma_X=6$。

Two independent synthetic quantities have $X$ with mean 40 and standard deviation 6, and $Y$ with mean 30 and standard deviation 4. A student writes: 'For $D=X-Y$ the mean is $40-30=10$ and the standard deviation is $6-4=2$.' Which evaluation is correct?
两个相互独立的合成变量：$X$ 的均值为 40、标准差（standard deviation）为 6，$Y$ 的均值为 30、标准差为 4。学生写道：“对 $D=X-Y$，均值为 $40-30=10$，标准差为 $6-4=2$。”哪项评价正确？

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Means subtract: $\mu_D=40-30=10$, so the student's mean is right. Because $X$ and $Y$ are independent, $\sigma_D^2=\sigma_X^2+\sigma_Y^2=36+16=52$ (variances add for a difference too), so $\sigma_D=\sqrt{52}\approx7.21$. The student's value 2 is smaller than either input SD, which is impossible when independent variation is being combined.均值相减：$\mu_D=40-30=10$，学生的均值正确。因为 $X$ 与 $Y$ 独立，$\sigma_D^2=\sigma_X^2+\sigma_Y^2=36+16=52$（求差时方差（variance）同样相加），所以 $\sigma_D=\sqrt{52}\approx7.21$。学生得到的 2 比任何一个输入的标准差都小，合并独立变异时这是不可能的。

Why the options attract (M 16)选项误区（M 16）
  1. Correct: keeps the mean and replaces the SD rule with 'variances add'.正确：保留均值，并把标准差规则改为“方差相加”。
  2. Subtracting variances is the same kind of error as subtracting SDs: $36-16=20$ gives $4.47$, but independent variation always adds.方差相减和标准差相减是同一类错误：$36-16=20$ 得 $4.47$，但独立的变异总是相加。

  4. Standard deviations never subtract: the spread of a difference is at least as large as each input's spread.标准差从不相减：差的离散程度不小于各输入的离散程度。
For independent variables, sums and differences have the same variance, $\sigma_X^2+\sigma_Y^2$. Only the means subtract.对独立变量，和与差的方差相同，都是 $\sigma_X^2+\sigma_Y^2$；只有均值相减。
