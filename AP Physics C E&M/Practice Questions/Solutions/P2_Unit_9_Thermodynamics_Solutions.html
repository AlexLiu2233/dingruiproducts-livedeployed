Q1EASY 9.1 Kinetic Theory9.1 分子动理论No Calculator

A sealed container holds a gas at a constant temperature. Which statement best explains why the gas exerts a steady pressure on the container walls?一个密闭容器内装有温度保持不变的气体。下列哪一项最能解释气体为什么对容器壁施加稳定的压强？

  • (A) Each atom strikes the wall and rebounds, and the perpendicular forces of a huge number of such collisions add up to a steady force per unit area.每个原子撞击器壁后反弹，数量极大的此类碰撞产生的垂直作用力叠加成每单位面积上稳定的力。
  • (B) The atoms repel one another with long-range forces, and these forces push the outer layer of atoms against the wall.原子之间存在长程斥力，这些力把外层原子推向器壁。
  • (C) The weight of the atoms presses on the bottom of the container, so the pressure exists only at the bottom wall.原子的重力压在容器底部，所以压强只存在于底部的器壁。
  • (D) The atoms stick to the wall for a short time and then leave, and the sticking produces the force.原子在器壁上短暂停留后离开，这种停留产生了力。
Answer:答案： (A)
Each rebound changes the atom's momentum perpendicular to the wall, so the wall pushes on the atom and, by Newton's third law, the atom pushes on the wall. With an enormous number of collisions each second the individual impulses blend into a steady force:每次反弹都会改变原子垂直于器壁方向的动量，所以器壁对原子有力，根据牛顿第三定律，原子也对器壁有力。每秒发生的碰撞数量极大，各次冲量融合成稳定的力：
$$P=\frac{F_\perp}{A}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) borrows the idea of repulsion between particles; in the ideal-gas model the atoms exert forces only during collisions, so no long-range push is involved. (C) confuses gas pressure with the weight of a solid: the pressure comes from collisions and acts on every wall and throughout the gas, not only at the bottom. (D) invents sticking; the collisions are elastic rebounds, and a rebound gives twice the momentum change that stopping would.(B) 借用了粒子间排斥的想法；在理想气体模型中，原子只在碰撞时才有相互作用力，不存在长程推力。 (C) 把气体压强与固体的重力混为一谈：压强来自碰撞，作用在每一面器壁上并存在于整个气体内部，而不只在底部。 (D) 凭空设想了黏附；碰撞是弹性反弹，反弹引起的动量变化是停下来的两倍。
Insight.要点。 Gas pressure is a statistical average of many collisions, and it exists throughout the gas, not only at the boundary.气体压强是大量碰撞的统计平均，它存在于整个气体内部，而不只在边界上。

An ideal gas is at a temperature of $27^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The average kinetic energy of one atom of the gas is closest to一定量理想气体的温度为 $27^\circ\mathrm{C}$。该气体中一个原子的平均动能最接近

  • (A) $5.59\times10^{-22}~\mathrm{J}$
  • (B) $4.14\times10^{-21}~\mathrm{J}$
  • (C) $6.21\times10^{-21}~\mathrm{J}$
  • (D) $1.24\times10^{-20}~\mathrm{J}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$T = 27+273 = 300~\mathrm{K}$$
$$K_\text{avg} = \tfrac{3}{2}k_B T = \tfrac{3}{2}(1.38\times10^{-23})(300) = 6.21\times10^{-21}~\mathrm{J}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) puts $T=27$ into the formula without changing to kelvin: $\tfrac{3}{2}(1.38\times10^{-23})(27)=5.59\times10^{-22}~\mathrm{J}$. (B) drops the factor $\tfrac{3}{2}$ and uses $k_BT=(1.38\times10^{-23})(300)=4.14\times10^{-21}~\mathrm{J}$. (D) uses $3k_BT=1.24\times10^{-20}~\mathrm{J}$, writing $3$ where the formula has $\tfrac{3}{2}$.(A) 没有换成开尔文就把 $T=27$ 代入：$\tfrac{3}{2}(1.38\times10^{-23})(27)=5.59\times10^{-22}~\mathrm{J}$。 (B) 丢掉了因子 $\tfrac{3}{2}$，用 $k_BT=(1.38\times10^{-23})(300)=4.14\times10^{-21}~\mathrm{J}$。 (D) 用 $3k_BT=1.24\times10^{-20}~\mathrm{J}$，把公式中的 $\tfrac{3}{2}$ 写成了 $3$。
Insight.要点。 $K_\text{avg}=\tfrac{3}{2}k_BT$ needs the kelvin temperature, and it is the same for every ideal gas at that temperature.$K_\text{avg}=\tfrac{3}{2}k_BT$ 必须使用开尔文温度，并且在该温度下对任何理想气体都相同。
Q3MEDIUM 9.1 Temperature and Atomic Mass9.1 温度与原子质量No Calculator

Helium atoms (mass $4~\mathrm{u}$) and argon atoms (mass $40~\mathrm{u}$) are in two separate containers at the same temperature. The ratio $v_\text{rms}(\text{He})/v_\text{rms}(\text{Ar})$ and the ratio $K_\text{avg}(\text{He})/K_\text{avg}(\text{Ar})$ are, respectively,氦原子（质量 $4~\mathrm{u}$）和氩原子（质量 $40~\mathrm{u}$）分别装在两个温度相同的容器中。比值 $v_\text{rms}(\text{He})/v_\text{rms}(\text{Ar})$ 与比值 $K_\text{avg}(\text{He})/K_\text{avg}(\text{Ar})$ 分别为

  • (A) $\sqrt{10}$ and和 $1$
  • (B) $10$ and和 $1$
  • (C) $1$ and和 $1$
  • (D) $\sqrt{10}$ and和 $\sqrt{10}$
Answer:答案： (A)
$$K_\text{avg}=\tfrac{3}{2}k_BT\ \Rightarrow\ \frac{K_\text{He}}{K_\text{Ar}}=1$$
$$\tfrac{1}{2}mv_\text{rms}^2=\tfrac{3}{2}k_BT\ \Rightarrow\ \frac{v_\text{He}}{v_\text{Ar}}=\sqrt{\frac{m_\text{Ar}}{m_\text{He}}}=\sqrt{\frac{40}{4}}=\sqrt{10}\approx3.2$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) forgets the square root and uses the mass ratio $10$ itself for the speed ratio. (C) reasons that equal temperature means equal speed; equal temperature gives equal average kinetic energy, so the lighter atoms must be faster. (D) treats $K_\text{avg}$ as proportional to $v_\text{rms}$ rather than $v_\text{rms}^2$, which gives a ratio of $\sqrt{10}$ for the energies.(B) 忘了开平方，直接把质量比 $10$ 当作速率比。 (C) 以为温度相同则速率相同；温度相同给出的是平均动能相同，所以较轻的原子必然更快。 (D) 把 $K_\text{avg}$ 当成与 $v_\text{rms}$ 而不是 $v_\text{rms}^2$ 成正比，使能量之比成了 $\sqrt{10}$。
Insight.要点。 At one temperature every ideal gas has the same $K_\text{avg}$; the lighter atoms reach it with a higher speed, $v_\text{rms}\propto\sqrt{T/m}$.在同一温度下，所有理想气体的 $K_\text{avg}$ 相同；较轻的原子靠更大的速率达到这一数值，$v_\text{rms}\propto\sqrt{T/m}$。
Q4HARD 9.1 Collisions and Pressure9.1 碰撞与压强Calculator

A steady stream of argon atoms, each of mass $6.64\times10^{-26}~\mathrm{kg}$ and speed $400~\mathrm{m/s}$, strikes a flat wall of area $4.0\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{m^2}$. Each atom arrives at $60^\circ$ to the normal of the wall and rebounds elastically from the smooth wall, so its component of velocity parallel to the wall is unchanged. $2.0\times10^{22}$ atoms strike the wall each second. The average pressure the stream exerts on the wall is closest to一股稳定的氩原子流撞击面积为 $4.0\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{m^2}$ 的平整墙面，每个原子质量为 $6.64\times10^{-26}~\mathrm{kg}$，速率为 $400~\mathrm{m/s}$。每个原子与墙面法线成 $60^\circ$ 角入射，并从光滑的墙面弹性反弹，平行于墙面的速度分量不变。每秒有 $2.0\times10^{22}$ 个原子撞击墙面。这股原子流对墙面的平均压强最接近

  • (A) $6.6\times10^{2}~\mathrm{Pa}$
  • (B) $1.3\times10^{3}~\mathrm{Pa}$
  • (C) $2.3\times10^{3}~\mathrm{Pa}$
  • (D) $2.7\times10^{3}~\mathrm{Pa}$
Answer:答案： (B)
Only the component normal to the wall reverses, so each atom's momentum change is along the normal:只有垂直于墙面的分量反向，所以每个原子的动量变化沿法线方向：
$$\Delta p = 2mv\cos60^\circ = 2(6.64\times10^{-26})(400)(0.50) = 2.66\times10^{-23}~\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$$
$$F=\frac{N\,\Delta p}{\Delta t}=(2.0\times10^{22})(2.66\times10^{-23})=0.531~\mathrm{N}$$
$$P=\frac{F}{A}=\frac{0.531}{4.0\times10^{-4}}=1.3\times10^{3}~\mathrm{Pa}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) uses $\Delta p=mv\cos60^\circ$, as if each atom only stopped instead of rebounding, which halves the result to $6.6\times10^{2}~\mathrm{Pa}$. (C) takes the component of the velocity along the wall, $\Delta p=2mv\sin60^\circ$, which gives $2.3\times10^{3}~\mathrm{Pa}$. (D) uses the whole speed, $\Delta p=2mv$, as if every atom hit head-on, which gives $2.7\times10^{3}~\mathrm{Pa}$.(A) 取 $\Delta p=mv\cos60^\circ$，好像每个原子只是停下而没有反弹，结果减半为 $6.6\times10^{2}~\mathrm{Pa}$。 (C) 用了速度沿墙面的分量，$\Delta p=2mv\sin60^\circ$，得到 $2.3\times10^{3}~\mathrm{Pa}$。 (D) 用整个速率，$\Delta p=2mv$，好像每个原子都正面撞击，得到 $2.7\times10^{3}~\mathrm{Pa}$。
Insight.要点。 Pressure is the sum of the perpendicular impulses per second divided by the area. Resolve each velocity into normal and parallel parts before using momentum.压强等于每秒垂直冲量之和除以面积。运用动量之前，先把每个速度分解成法向和平行两个分量。
Q5EASY 9.2 Ideal Gas Law9.2 理想气体定律Calculator

A $4.0~\mathrm{L}$ container holds an ideal gas at an absolute pressure of $2.0\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$ and a temperature of $27^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The number of moles of gas is closest to一个 $4.0~\mathrm{L}$ 的容器内装有理想气体，绝对压强为 $2.0\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$，温度为 $27^\circ\mathrm{C}$。气体的物质的量（摩尔数）最接近

  • (A) $0.32~\mathrm{mol}$
  • (B) $3.6~\mathrm{mol}$
  • (C) $3.2\times10^{2}~\mathrm{mol}$
  • (D) $2.0\times10^{4}~\mathrm{mol}$
Answer:答案： (A)
$$V=4.0~\mathrm{L}=4.0\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{m^3},\qquad T=300~\mathrm{K}$$
$$n=\frac{PV}{RT}=\frac{(2.0\times10^{5})(4.0\times10^{-3})}{(8.31)(300)}=0.32~\mathrm{mol}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) uses $T=27$ instead of $300~\mathrm{K}$: $800/[(8.31)(27)]=3.6~\mathrm{mol}$. (C) leaves the volume as $4.0$ instead of converting to $4.0\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{m^3}$, which makes $n$ a thousand times too large, $3.2\times10^{2}~\mathrm{mol}$. (D) divides by the volume instead of multiplying by it, $P/(VRT)=(2.0\times10^{5})/[(4.0\times10^{-3})(8.31)(300)]=2.0\times10^{4}~\mathrm{mol}$.(B) 用 $T=27$ 而不是 $300~\mathrm{K}$：$800/[(8.31)(27)]=3.6~\mathrm{mol}$。 (C) 没有把体积换算成 $4.0\times10^{-3}~\mathrm{m^3}$，而是直接用 $4.0$，使 $n$ 大了一千倍，为 $3.2\times10^{2}~\mathrm{mol}$。 (D) 用体积去除而不是乘以体积，$P/(VRT)=(2.0\times10^{5})/[(4.0\times10^{-3})(8.31)(300)]=2.0\times10^{4}~\mathrm{mol}$。
Insight.要点。 Convert to SI and kelvin before using $PV=nRT$, and solve it as $n=PV/(RT)$: the volume is in the numerator.使用 $PV=nRT$ 之前先换成国际单位和开尔文，并解出 $n=PV/(RT)$：体积在分子上。
Q6MEDIUM 9.2 Proportional Reasoning9.2 比例推理No Calculator

A fixed amount of ideal gas is sealed in a rigid container at $27^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The gas is warmed to $54^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The final pressure is about how many times the initial pressure?一定量的理想气体密封在刚性容器中，温度为 $27^\circ\mathrm{C}$。把气体加热到 $54^\circ\mathrm{C}$。末态压强约为初态压强的多少倍？

  • (A) $0.92$
  • (B) $1.0$
  • (C) $1.09$
  • (D) $2.0$
Answer:答案： (C)
With $n$ and $V$ fixed, $P\propto T$ in kelvin:$n$ 和 $V$ 不变时，$P$ 与开尔文温度 $T$ 成正比：
$$\frac{P_2}{P_1}=\frac{T_2}{T_1}=\frac{54+273}{27+273}=\frac{327}{300}=1.09$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) inverts the ratio, $300/327=0.92$, as if pressure fell when the gas is heated. (B) reasons that a fixed volume means a fixed pressure; the volume is fixed, but the pressure still rises with temperature. (D) takes the ratio of the Celsius readings, $54/27=2.0$.(A) 把比值颠倒，$300/327=0.92$，好像气体受热后压强下降。 (B) 认为体积不变则压强不变；体积虽然不变，压强仍随温度升高。 (D) 取摄氏读数之比，$54/27=2.0$。
Insight.要点。 Doubling the Celsius reading does not double the temperature. Gas-law ratios need kelvin.摄氏读数翻倍并不意味着温度翻倍。气体定律中的比值必须用开尔文。
Q7HARD 9.2 Absolute Zero from Data9.2 由数据外推绝对零度Calculator

A student measures the absolute pressure of a fixed amount of gas in a rigid sealed container at three temperatures. The data are $P=1.000\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$ at $0^\circ\mathrm{C}$, $P=1.185\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$ at $50^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $P=1.370\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$ at $100^\circ\mathrm{C}$. A straight line through the data is extended to zero pressure. The temperature at which the line reaches zero pressure is closest to一名学生在三个温度下测量密封在刚性容器中的一定量气体的绝对压强。数据为：$0^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 时 $P=1.000\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$，$50^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 时 $P=1.185\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$，$100^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 时 $P=1.370\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$。将通过这些数据点的直线外推到压强为零。直线与零压强相交处的温度最接近

  • (A) $-370^\circ\mathrm{C}$
  • (B) $-270^\circ\mathrm{C}$
  • (C) $-100^\circ\mathrm{C}$
  • (D) $+270^\circ\mathrm{C}$
Answer:答案： (B)
Let $\beta$ be the slope of the line of $P$ against $T$:设 $\beta$ 为 $P$ 对 $T$ 直线的斜率：
$$\beta=\frac{1.370\times10^{5}-1.000\times10^{5}}{100-0}=370~\mathrm{Pa/^\circ C}$$
$$P=1.000\times10^{5}+370\,T=0\ \Rightarrow\ T=-\frac{1.000\times10^{5}}{370}=-270^\circ\mathrm{C}$$
The three points are collinear (the middle one gives the same slope), and the intercept agrees with absolute zero.三个数据点共线（中间一点给出相同的斜率），截距与绝对零度吻合。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) divides the pressure at $100^\circ\mathrm{C}$ by the slope, $1.370\times10^{5}/370=370$, and measures it down from $0^\circ\mathrm{C}$. (C) divides the pressure rise $0.370\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$ by the slope instead of the pressure itself, $-(0.370\times10^{5})/370=-100$. (D) finds $1.000\times10^{5}/370=270$ but drops the minus sign, which would put the zero of pressure above the data.(A) 用 $100^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 处的压强除以斜率，$1.370\times10^{5}/370=370$，并从 $0^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 向下量取。 (C) 用压强增量 $0.370\times10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa}$ 而不是压强本身除以斜率，$-(0.370\times10^{5})/370=-100$。 (D) 算出 $1.000\times10^{5}/370=270$，却丢掉了负号，使零压强点落在数据的上方。
Insight.要点。 Extrapolate from a point on the line to $P=0$ using the slope. A line of $P$ against $T$ for a fixed amount of gas at fixed volume reaches zero pressure near $-273^\circ\mathrm{C}$.从直线上的一点出发，用斜率外推到 $P=0$。一定量气体在体积固定时，$P$ 对 $T$ 的直线在约 $-273^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 处达到零压强。

The piston is pushed in until the volume is halved, and at the same time the gas is warmed to $177^\circ\mathrm{C}$. No gas escapes. The final absolute pressure divided by the initial absolute pressure is带有密封活塞的汽缸中装有温度为 $27^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 的理想气体。把活塞推入使体积减半，同时把气体加热到 $177^\circ\mathrm{C}$。没有气体漏出。末态绝对压强与初态绝对压强之比为

  • (B) $2.0$
  • (C) $13$
  • (D) $3.0$

$$\frac{P_2}{P_1}=\frac{V_1}{V_2}\cdot\frac{T_2}{T_1}=2\cdot\frac{450}{300}=3.0$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) keeps only the temperature ratio, $450/300=1.5$, and forgets that the volume was halved. (B) keeps only the volume factor, $2$, and forgets that the gas was warmed. (C) uses the Celsius readings, $2\times177/27=13$.(A) 只保留温度比 $450/300=1.5$，忘了体积减半。 (B) 只保留体积因子 $2$，忘了气体被加热。 (C) 使用摄氏读数，$2\times177/27=13$。
Insight.要点。 When several variables change, multiply the factors: halving $V$ doubles $P$, and the temperature ratio in kelvin multiplies it again.多个变量同时变化时，把各个因子相乘：体积减半使 $P$ 变为两倍，再乘以开尔文温度之比。
Q9EASY 9.3 Thermal Equilibrium9.3 热平衡No Calculator

Two blocks in thermal contact have reached thermal equilibrium. Which statement is correct?两个相互热接触的物块已达到热平衡。下列哪一项正确？

  • (A) No energy is exchanged between the atoms of the two blocks any more.两个物块的原子之间不再交换能量。
  • (B) The atoms of both blocks have stopped moving.两个物块中的原子都已停止运动。
  • (C) The two blocks necessarily have equal internal energy.两个物块的内能必然相等。
  • (D) No net energy is transferred between the blocks, although individual collisions still pass energy in both directions.两个物块之间没有净能量传递，尽管个别碰撞仍在两个方向上传递能量。
Answer:答案： (D)
Thermal equilibrium means no net energy is transferred by thermal processes. Atoms at the contact surface still collide; energy passes from higher-energy atoms to lower-energy atoms and sometimes the other way, but the two flows balance and the temperatures are equal.热平衡是指通过热过程没有净能量传递。接触面上的原子仍在碰撞；能量大多从高能原子传给低能原子，有时也反过来，但两个方向的传递相互抵消，两物块温度相等。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) mistakes a zero net transfer for no transfer at all; collisions across the contact continue. (B) confuses equilibrium with absolute zero: the atoms keep moving at the shared temperature. (C) treats equal temperature as equal internal energy; the internal energy also depends on the amount and kind of material, so the blocks can have different energies at the same temperature.(A) 把净传递为零误认为完全没有传递；穿过接触面的碰撞仍在继续。 (B) 把平衡与绝对零度混淆：原子在共同的温度下继续运动。 (C) 把温度相等当成内能相等；内能还取决于物质的量和种类，所以两物块在同一温度下可以有不同的内能。
Insight.要点。 Equilibrium is a balance of exchanges, not the end of them. Equal temperature, not equal energy, marks thermal equilibrium.平衡是各种交换相互抵消，而不是交换的终止。热平衡的标志是温度相等，而不是能量相等。
Q10MEDIUM 9.3 Thermal Processes9.3 热过程No Calculator

Three situations are described. (i) Sunlight crossing empty space warms the panel of a satellite. (ii) One end of a metal rod is held in a flame and the other end becomes hot. (iii) Warm air from a radiator rises and warms the upper part of a room. The main thermal process in (i), (ii) and (iii) is, respectively,描述三种情形。(i) 穿过真空的阳光使卫星的太阳能板升温。(ii) 金属棒的一端放在火焰中，另一端变热。(iii) 暖气片附近的暖空气上升，使房间上部变暖。(i)、(ii)、(iii) 中起主要作用的热过程依次为

  • (A) conduction, radiation, convection热传导、热辐射、热对流
  • (B) radiation, convection, conduction热辐射、热对流、热传导
  • (C) radiation, conduction, convection热辐射、热传导、热对流

The probability of finding exactly two atoms in each half is how many times the probability of finding all four atoms in the left half?一个孤立的盒子里有四个可区分的气体原子，每个原子在左半部或右半部出现的可能性相同。把四个原子分配到两半的每一种具体方式出现的可能性都相等。发现两半各恰有两个原子的概率是发现四个原子全在左半部的概率的多少倍？

  • (A) $1$
  • (B) $6$
  • (C) $3$
  • (D) $4$
Answer:答案： (B)
There are $2^4=16$ equally likely assignments. Exactly one of them puts all four atoms on the left. The assignments with two atoms on each side are the ways of choosing which two atoms are on the left, $\binom{4}{2}=6$:共有 $2^4=16$ 种等可能的分配方式。其中恰有一种把四个原子都放在左边。每边两个原子的分配方式，就是选出哪两个原子在左边的方法数，$\binom{4}{2}=6$：
$$\frac{P_{2,2}}{P_{4,0}}=\frac{6/16}{1/16}=6$$
Here $P_{2,2}$ is the probability of two atoms in each half and $P_{4,0}$ that of all four in the left half. The evenly spread arrangement can be realized in more ways, so it is the more probable, higher-entropy state.这里 $P_{2,2}$ 是两半各有两个原子的概率，$P_{4,0}$ 是四个原子全在左半部的概率。均匀分布的排列方式有更多种实现方法，所以它是概率更大、熵更高的状态。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) treats the two descriptions as equally likely; each particular assignment is, but the 2-2 split contains six of them and the 4-0 split contains one. (C) counts the 2-2 assignments in complementary pairs, $6/2=3$, as if swapping left and right gave the same arrangement. (D) counts only the $4$ ways of choosing a single atom, which is the count for a 3-1 split.(A) 把两种描述当作同样可能；每一种具体分配确实等可能，但 2-2 分布包含六种，而 4-0 分布只含一种。 (C) 把 2-2 的分配按互补成对计数，$6/2=3$，好像左右互换后是同一种排列。 (D) 只数了选出单个原子的 $4$ 种方法，这是 3-1 分布的计数。
Insight.要点。 The probable state is the one with the most arrangements. With many atoms the even spread is overwhelmingly more likely, which is the direction an isolated gas moves in.概率大的状态就是排列方式最多的状态。原子数很多时，均匀分布的可能性大得压倒一切，这正是孤立气体变化的方向。

State状态ABCD
$P~(10^{5}~\mathrm{Pa})$$1.0$$2.0$$2.0$$1.0$
$V~(10^{-3}~\mathrm{m^3})$$1.0$$1.0$$2.0$$2.0$
(a) Temperatures from $T=PV/(nR)$, with $nR=(0.050)(8.31)=0.4155~\mathrm{J/K}$:由 $T=PV/(nR)$ 求温度，其中 $nR=(0.050)(8.31)=0.4155~\mathrm{J/K}$：
