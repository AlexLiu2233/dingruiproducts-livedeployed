Q1EASY 1.1 Scalars and Vectors1.1 标量与矢量No Calculator

Which pair lists one scalar and one vector, in that order?下列哪组依次列出了一个标量和一个矢量？

  • (A) velocity, speed速度，速率
  • (B) distance, displacement路程，位移
  • (C) displacement, distance位移，路程
  • (D) acceleration, force加速度，力
Answer:答案： (B)
Distance is a path length (scalar, sign-free); displacement is a vector with magnitude and direction. (A) reverses the order. (C) flips them. (D) lists two vectors.路程是路径长度（标量，无符号）；位移是有大小和方向的矢量。(A) 颠倒了顺序，(C) 将两者互换，(D) 列出的是两个矢量。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) velocity, speed reverses the required order: velocity is a vector and speed is a scalar, so this pair is vector then scalar. (C) displacement, distance identifies the right pair but flips it: displacement is a vector and distance is a scalar, giving vector then scalar. (D) acceleration, force lists two vectors: both acceleration and force need direction, so the pair contains no scalar.(A) 速度、速率把要求的顺序颠倒了：速度是矢量而速率是标量，所以这是矢量在前、标量在后。(C) 位移、路程选对了两个量但顺序颠倒：位移是矢量而路程是标量，得到矢量在前、标量在后。(D) 加速度、力列出的是两个矢量：加速度和力都需要方向，因此这一对中没有标量。
Insight.要点。 Distinguish a scalar from a vector by one test: does the quantity need a direction to be complete? Distance only asks how far along the path, while displacement also asks in which direction. Apply that test before looking at the answer choices.区分标量与矢量只需一个检验：该量是否需要方向才算完整？路程只问沿路径走了多远，而位移还要问朝哪个方向。先做这个检验，再看选项。
Q2EASY 1.2 Position, Velocity, Acceleration1.2 位置、速度、加速度No Calculator

A particle's position is $x(t) = 3t^2 - 12t + 5$ (SI units). Its velocity at $t=2~\mathrm{s}$ is质点的位置为 $x(t) = 3t^2 - 12t + 5$（SI 单位）。其在 $t=2~\mathrm{s}$ 时的速度为

  • (A) $-12~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $6~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $12~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (B)
$$v(t) = \frac{dx}{dt} = 6t - 12$$
$$v(2) = 6(2) - 12 = 0~\mathrm{m/s}$$
The particle is momentarily at rest at $t=2~\mathrm{s}$ (a turning point of $x(t)$). Choice (A) is $v(0)$; (D) is $v(4)$.质点在 $t=2~\mathrm{s}$ 时瞬间静止（即 $x(t)$ 的转折点）。选项 (A) 是 $v(0)$，(D) 是 $v(4)$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $-12~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from evaluating the derivative at $t=0$: $v(0)=6(0)-12=-12$. (C) $6~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from using the acceleration $a=6~\mathrm{m/s^2}$ as the velocity at $t=2$. (D) $12~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from differentiating only $3t^2$, giving $v=6t$, and substituting $t=2$ while dropping the $-12$ term.(A) $-12~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把导数在 $t=0$ 处求值：$v(0)=6(0)-12=-12$。(C) $6~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把加速度 $a=6~\mathrm{m/s^2}$ 当成 $t=2$ 时的速度。(D) $12~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自只对 $3t^2$ 求导得到 $v=6t$，并在代入 $t=2$ 时丢掉了 $-12$ 项。
Insight.要点。 Differentiate the position function before substituting $t=2$; a zero velocity means the particle is instantaneously at rest, not that it was never moving. Check the sign of $v(t)$ on either side to confirm the turning point.先对位置函数求导，再代入 $t=2$；速度为零表示质点瞬时静止，而不是从未运动。检查 $v(t)$ 在两侧的符号以确认转折点。

Q5MEDIUM 1.3 Kinematic Equations1.3 运动学方程No Calculator

A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly to $20~\mathrm{m/s}$ over $5~\mathrm{s}$. The distance it travels in this interval is一辆汽车从静止出发，在 $5~\mathrm{s}$ 内匀加速至 $20~\mathrm{m/s}$。该过程中行驶的路程为

  • (A) $25~\mathrm{m}$
  • (B) $50~\mathrm{m}$
  • (C) $75~\mathrm{m}$
  • (D) $100~\mathrm{m}$
Answer:答案： (B)
Use the average-velocity shortcut for uniform acceleration:利用匀加速运动的平均速度简算：
$$d = \bar v\,t = \frac{v_0 + v}{2}\,t = \frac{0 + 20}{2}\,(5) = 50~\mathrm{m}$$
Equivalently, $a = \tfrac{20}{5} = 4~\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and $d = \tfrac{1}{2}at^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}(4)(25) = 50~\mathrm{m}$.等价地，$a = \tfrac{20}{5} = 4~\mathrm{m/s^2}$，$d = \tfrac{1}{2}at^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}(4)(25) = 50~\mathrm{m}$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $25~\mathrm{m}$ comes from applying an extra factor $1/2$ to the average speed: $\frac12\bar v t=\frac12(10)(5)=25$. (C) $75~\mathrm{m}$ comes from substituting the average speed $10~\mathrm{m/s}$ as the initial speed in $d=\frac{v_0+v}{2}t$: $\frac{10+20}{2}(5)=75$. (D) $100~\mathrm{m}$ comes from ignoring acceleration and using the final speed as constant: $d=vt=(20)(5)=100$.(A) $25~\mathrm{m}$ 来自在平均速度上多加一个 $1/2$ 因子：$\frac12\bar v t=\frac12(10)(5)=25$。(C) $75~\mathrm{m}$ 来自把平均速度 $10~\mathrm{m/s}$ 当作初速度代入 $d=\frac{v_0+v}{2}t$：$\frac{10+20}{2}(5)=75$。(D) $100~\mathrm{m}$ 来自忽略加速度、把末速度当恒定速度：$d=vt=(20)(5)=100$。
Insight.要点。 The average-velocity shortcut $d=(v_0+v)t/2$ is valid only for uniform acceleration. When $a$ varies with time, distance is the integral of $v(t)$, not the midpoint times $t$.平均速度简算 $d=(v_0+v)t/2$ 只在匀加速时成立。当 $a$ 随时间变化时，路程应是对 $v(t)$ 的积分，而不是中点速度乘 $t$。
Q6MEDIUM 1.3 Free Fall1.3 自由落体No Calculator

A ball is dropped from rest from a height $H$ above level ground. Air resistance is negligible. Its speed just before impact is一只球从距水平地面高度 $H$ 处由静止释放，空气阻力不计。其落地前瞬间的速率为

  • (A) $\sqrt{gH}$
  • (B) $\sqrt{2gH}$
  • (C) $2gH$
  • (D) $gH$
Answer:答案： (B)
Apply $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2g\,\Delta y$ with $v_0 = 0$ and $\Delta y = H$:代入 $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2g\,\Delta y$，其中 $v_0 = 0$，$\Delta y = H$：
$$v^2 = 2gH \quad\Longrightarrow\quad v = \sqrt{2gH}$$
Choices (C) and (D) have wrong units (m²/s² and m²/s², not m/s).选项 (C) 和 (D) 的单位有误（m²/s²，而非 m/s）。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $\sqrt{gH}$ comes from using the too short fall time $t=\sqrt{H/g}$ in $v=gt$, which omits the factor $2$ in $H=\frac12gt^2$. (C) $2gH$ comes from reaching $v^2=2gH$ but never taking the square root. (D) $gH$ comes from using $v=gt$ with the height $H$ inserted as if it were the fall time.(A) $\sqrt{gH}$ 来自在 $v=gt$ 中使用过短的落地时间 $t=\sqrt{H/g}$，这漏掉了 $H=\frac12gt^2$ 中的因子 $2$。(C) $2gH$ 来自得到 $v^2=2gH$ 后没有开平方。(D) $gH$ 来自把高度 $H$ 当作落地时间代入 $v=gt$。
Insight.要点。 Choose the equation with the knowns and unknown you actually have: $v_0=0$, displacement $H$, and acceleration $g$ point to $v^2=v_0^2+2gH$. Dimensional analysis also rejects any answer not in $m/s$.选择包含已知量与所求量的方程：$v_0=0$、位移 $H$、加速度 $g$，故用 $v^2=v_0^2+2gH$。量纲分析也可排除任何单位不是 $m/s$ 的答案。
Q7MEDIUM 1.2 Non-Constant Acceleration1.2 非恒定加速度No Calculator

A particle has acceleration $a(t) = 6t~\mathrm{m/s^2}$ and starts from rest at $x=0$. Its position at $t=2~\mathrm{s}$ is一质点的加速度为 $a(t) = 6t~\mathrm{m/s^2}$，从 $x=0$ 处由静止开始运动。其在 $t=2~\mathrm{s}$ 时的位置为

  • (A) $4~\mathrm{m}$
  • (B) $8~\mathrm{m}$
  • (C) $12~\mathrm{m}$
  • (D) $24~\mathrm{m}$
Answer:答案： (B)
Integrate twice from rest at the origin:从原点由静止开始对加速度积分两次：
$$v(t) = \int_0^t 6\tau\,d\tau = 3t^2$$
$$x(t) = \int_0^t 3\tau^2\,d\tau = t^3$$
$$x(2) = 2^3 = 8~\mathrm{m}$$
Trap (D) is $a(2) \cdot t$, treating $a$ as constant.陷阱 (D) 将 $a$ 视为常数，错误地计算了 $a(2) \cdot t$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $4~\mathrm{m}$ comes from reaching $x(t)=t^3$ correctly but evaluating $2^3$ as $2^2=4$. (C) $12~\mathrm{m}$ comes from stopping after one integration, since $v(2)=3(2)^2=12~\mathrm{m/s}$, and reporting that velocity as position. (D) $24~\mathrm{m}$ comes from treating $a(2)=12~\mathrm{m/s^2}$ as constant and using $x=\frac12 a(2)t^2$: $\frac12(12)(2)^2=24$.(A) $4~\mathrm{m}$ 来自已经正确得到 $x(t)=t^3$，却把 $2^3$ 算成 $2^2=4$。(C) $12~\mathrm{m}$ 来自只积分一次就停下，因为 $v(2)=3(2)^2=12~\mathrm{m/s}$，然后把该速度当作位置。(D) $24~\mathrm{m}$ 来自把 $a(2)=12~\mathrm{m/s^2}$ 当作恒定加速度并使用 $x=\frac12 a(2)t^2$：$\frac12(12)(2)^2=24$。
Insight.要点。 The constant-acceleration formulas are shortcuts for integrals. Here $a$ depends on $t$, so integrate twice: first $a\to v$, then $v\to x$. Never treat $a(2)$ as if it were the constant acceleration over the interval.匀加速公式本质上是积分的捷径。这里 $a$ 随 $t$ 变化，所以需要积分两次：先由 $a$ 得 $v$，再由 $v$ 得 $x$。不要把 $a(2)$ 当成整个区间的恒定加速度。
Q8MEDIUM 1.4 Relative Motion1.4 相对运动No Calculator

A boat heads due north across a river at $4~\mathrm{m/s}$ relative to the water. The river flows due east at $3~\mathrm{m/s}$. The boat's speed relative to the bank is一艘船相对于水以 $4~\mathrm{m/s}$ 向正北方向横渡河流，河流以 $3~\mathrm{m/s}$ 向正东方流动。船相对于河岸的速率为

  • (A) $1~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $5~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $7~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $\sqrt{7}~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (B)
Velocities add as vectors. The components are perpendicular (north and east), so the resultant speed is速度按矢量相加。两分量相互垂直（北向与东向），合速率为
$$|\vec v_\text{bank}| = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5~\mathrm{m/s}$$
Trap (C) adds magnitudes directly (only valid for parallel vectors); (A) subtracts them (only for anti-parallel).陷阱 (C) 直接相加大小（仅对平行矢量有效）；(A) 相减（仅对反向平行矢量有效）。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $1~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from subtracting the perpendicular speeds as if they were anti-parallel: $4-3=1$. (C) $7~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from adding the magnitudes directly as if the vectors were parallel: $3+4=7$. (D) $\sqrt{7}~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from adding the speeds under the square root without squaring them: $\sqrt{3+4}=\sqrt{7}$.(A) $1~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把垂直方向的速度当成反平行相减：$4-3=1$。(C) $7~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把大小直接相加，好像两矢量平行：$3+4=7$。(D) $\sqrt{7}~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把速度相加后直接开方而没有先平方：$\sqrt{3+4}=\sqrt{7}$。
Insight.要点。 Perpendicular velocity components combine by the Pythagorean theorem, never by adding $3+4$. Write $\vec v_{\text{bank}}=\vec v_{\text{boat/water}}+\vec v_{\text{water/bank}}$ and only then take the magnitude.相互垂直的速度分量用勾股定理合成，绝不能直接相加 $3+4$。先写 $\vec v_{\text{岸}}=\vec v_{\text{船/水}}+\vec v_{\text{水/岸}}$，再取模。

A particle moves along the $x$-axis with position $x(t)=2t^3-9t^2+12t+5$ (SI units) for $t \ge 0$. At which time(s) is the particle momentarily at rest?一质点沿 $x$ 轴运动，位置为 $x(t)=2t^3-9t^2+12t+5$（SI 单位），$t \ge 0$。质点在哪些时刻瞬时静止？

  • (A) $t=1~\mathrm{s}$ only仅 $t=1~\mathrm{s}$
  • (B) $t=2~\mathrm{s}$ only仅 $t=2~\mathrm{s}$
  • (C) $t=1~\mathrm{s}$ and $t=2~\mathrm{s}$$t=1~\mathrm{s}$ 和 $t=2~\mathrm{s}$
  • (D) $t=0$, $1$, and $2~\mathrm{s}$$t=0$、$1$ 和 $2~\mathrm{s}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$v(t)=\frac{dx}{dt}=6t^2-18t+12=6(t-1)(t-2)$$
So $v=0$ at $t=1~\mathrm{s}$ and $t=2~\mathrm{s}$. At $t=0$, $v(0)=12~\mathrm{m/s}\neq 0$, so (D) confuses the start of the clock with rest. Choosing a single time usually means the quadratic was only partly factored.因此 $v=0$ 出现在 $t=1~\mathrm{s}$ 与 $t=2~\mathrm{s}$。在 $t=0$ 时 $v(0)=12~\mathrm{m/s}\neq 0$，故 (D) 把计时起点误当作静止。只选一个时刻通常是二次式没有完全因式分解。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $t=1~\mathrm{s}$ only comes from setting just the factor $t-1$ to zero and missing the second factor. (B) $t=2~\mathrm{s}$ only comes from setting just the factor $t-2$ to zero and discarding the first root.   • (A) $4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $5.6~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $16.5~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $24~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (B)
Integrate with $x(0)=0$, and locate the stops: $v=3(t-1)(t-3)=0$ at $t=1$ and $t=3~\mathrm{s}$.取 $x(0)=0$ 积分，并找出停止时刻：$v=3(t-1)(t-3)=0$ 于 $t=1$ 与 $t=3~\mathrm{s}$。
$$x(t)=t^3-6t^2+9t:\quad x(1)=4,\quad x(3)=0,\quad x(5)=20~(\mathrm{m})$$
$$d=4+4+20=28~\mathrm{m},\qquad \bar s=\frac{d}{\Delta t}=\frac{28}{5}=5.6~\mathrm{m/s}$$
(A) is the average velocity $20/5=4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$, which is what the signed area under the $v$-$t$ graph gives directly. (C) averages the endpoint velocities $v(0)=9$ and $v(5)=24$, valid only for constant acceleration. (D) is $v(5)$.(A) 是平均速度 $20/5=4.0~\mathrm{m/s}$，即 $v$-$t$ 图像下带符号面积直接给出的量。(C) 对端点速度 $v(0)=9$ 与 $v(5)=24$ 取平均，只在匀加速时成立。(D) 是 $v(5)$。

A cart moves along a straight track with acceleration $a(t)=12t-6~\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $0 \le t \le 4~\mathrm{s}$. At $t=0$ its velocity is $v=5~\mathrm{m/s}$ and its position is $x=0$.一小车沿直轨道运动，加速度为 $a(t)=12t-6~\mathrm{m/s^2}$，$0 \le t \le 4~\mathrm{s}$。在 $t=0$ 时其速度为 $v=5~\mathrm{m/s}$，位置为 $x=0$。

(a) Velocity and position.速度与位置。
$$v(t)=5+\int_0^t (12\tau-6)\,d\tau=6t^2-6t+5$$
$$x(t)=\int_0^t (6\tau^2-6\tau+5)\,d\tau=2t^3-3t^2+5t$$
(b) No reversal; minimum speed.不反向；最小速率。 A reversal needs $v$ to change sign. The discriminant of $6t^2-6t+5$ is $36-120=-84\lt 0$, so $v$ has no real zeros and stays positive. Its minimum is where $a=0$, at $t=0.5~\mathrm{s}$:反向要求 $v$ 变号。$6t^2-6t+5$ 的判别式为 $36-120=-84\lt 0$，故 $v$ 没有实根且恒为正。其最小值出现在 $a=0$ 即 $t=0.5~\mathrm{s}$ 处：
$$v_\text{min}=v(0.5)=1.5-3+5=3.5~\mathrm{m/s}$$
(c) Displacement, average velocity, average acceleration.位移、平均速度、平均加速度。
$$\Delta x=x(4)-x(0)=128-48+20=100~\mathrm{m},\qquad \bar v=\frac{100}{4}=25~\mathrm{m/s}$$
$$v(4)=96-24+5=77~\mathrm{m/s},\qquad \bar a=\frac{77-5}{4}=18~\mathrm{m/s^2}$$
(d) New model: turning points, displacement, distance.新模型：转向点、位移、路程。
$$v(t)=5+\int_0^t(6\tau-9)\,d\tau=3t^2-9t+5,\qquad x(t)=t^3-4.5t^2+5t$$
$$v=0:\; t=\frac{9\pm\sqrt{21}}{6}\approx 0.736~\mathrm{s},\;2.264~\mathrm{s}$$
$v$ changes sign at each root (simple roots), so the cart reverses twice.
