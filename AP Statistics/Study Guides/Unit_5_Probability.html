Topic 5.1主题 5.1

Introducing probability概率入门

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe a random process and list its sample space and events.
  • Interpret a probability as a long-run relative frequency and state the law of large numbers.
  • Use equally likely outcomes and the complement rule to find probabilities.
  • Explain why short-run streaks do not change the probability of the next trial.
  • 描述随机过程，并列出其样本空间与事件。
  • 把概率解释为长期相对频率，并陈述大数定律。
  • 利用等可能结果与补集规则求概率。
  • 说明为何短期的连续结果不会改变下一次试验的概率。

A random process produces outcomes that cannot be predicted individually but follow a regular pattern over many repetitions. The sample space $S$ is the list of all possible outcomes, and an event is any collection of outcomes. The probability $P(A)$ of an event is the proportion of times $A$ would occur in the long run if the random process were repeated indefinitely under the same conditions.

随机过程的单次结果无法预测，但在大量重复下呈现规律。样本空间 $S$ 是所有可能结果的列表，事件是若干结果的集合。事件 $A$ 的概率 $P(A)$ 是：在相同条件下无限次重复该随机过程时，$A$ 发生的长期比例。

Must know

Every probability satisfies $0\le P(A)\le 1$, and $P(S)=1$. When all $N$ outcomes are equally likely, $P(A)=\dfrac{\text{number of outcomes in }A}{N}$. The complement $A^{c}$ ("not $A$") has $P(A^{c})=1-P(A)$.

必备知识

任一概率满足 $0\le P(A)\le 1$，且 $P(S)=1$。当 $N$ 个结果等可能时，$P(A)=\dfrac{A\text{ 含的结果数}}{N}$。补集 $A^{c}$（“非 $A$”）满足 $P(A^{c})=1-P(A)$。

The law of large numbers大数定律

The law of large numbers states that as the number of repetitions grows, the relative frequency of an event approaches its probability. Figure 1 shows one simulated run of fair-coin flips: the running proportion of heads is erratic for small $n$ and settles near $0.5$ for large $n$. Note what the law does not say: the count of heads does not have to "catch up" after a streak. The coin has no memory, so each flip still has probability $0.5$ of heads.

大数定律指出：重复次数越多，事件的相对频率越接近其概率。图 1 是公平硬币抛掷的一次模拟：$n$ 较小时正面比例起伏不定，$n$ 较大时稳定在 $0.5$ 附近。注意定律并没有说连续出现某结果后正面的次数要“补回来”。硬币没有记忆，每次抛掷正面的概率仍是 $0.5$。

Choosing a sample space you can count选择可以计数的样本空间

Equally likely outcomes are a modelling decision, not a default. Before using $P(A)=\dfrac{n(A)}{N}$, ask whether every listed outcome really has the same chance. The eleven possible sums of two dice, $2$ through $12$, are not equally likely: only $(1,1)$ gives a sum of $2$, but six ordered pairs give a sum of $7$. The remedy is to list outcomes that are equally likely, here the $36$ ordered pairs, and then count how many of them make the event happen. The same care applies to a fair coin flipped twice: $HH, HT, TH, TT$ are equally likely, but "one head" is not a single outcome because it contains $HT$ and $TH$.

等可能结果是一种建模选择，而不是默认前提。在使用 $P(A)=\dfrac{n(A)}{N}$ 之前，先问每个列出的结果是否真的机会相同。两颗骰子的十一种可能的和（$2$ 到 $12$）并不等可能：只有 $(1,1)$ 得到和 $2$，而有六个有序数对得到和 $7$。补救办法是列出等可能的结果，这里即 $36$ 个有序数对，再数其中有多少个使事件发生。抛两次公平硬币时同样要小心：$HH, HT, TH, TT$ 等可能，但“一次正面”不是单个结果，因为它包含 $HT$ 与 $TH$。

A probability can also come from data. If $120/400=0.30$ is the proportion of recorded days with rain, that relative frequency estimates the probability of rain on a similar day. It is an estimate, and a different record would give a slightly different value. Say which kind of probability a problem gives you (a count of equally likely outcomes, or a long-run relative frequency) before you interpret it.

概率也可以来自数据。若 $120/400=0.30$ 是有降雨的记录日所占的比例，这个相对频率就是对类似日子降雨概率的估计。它只是估计，换一份记录会得到略有不同的值。在解释之前，先说明题目给出的是哪一种概率（等可能结果的计数，还是长期相对频率）。

Worked examples例题

Worked Example 4.1a: Two dice and the complement rule

Two fair six-sided dice are rolled. Find $P(\text{sum}=7)$, $P(\text{sum}\ge 10)$ and $P(\text{at least one 6})$.

  1. The sample space has $6\times 6=36$ equally likely ordered pairs, so each pair has probability $1/36$.
  2. Sum 7: $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$ gives 6 pairs, so $P=6/36=1/6\approx0.1667$.
  3. Sum at least 10: $(4,6),(5,5),(6,4),(5,6),(6,5),(6,6)$ gives 6 pairs, so $P=6/36=1/6\approx0.1667$.
  4. At least one 6: count the complement "no 6" first. There are $5\times5=25$ pairs with no 6, so $P=1-25/36=11/36\approx0.3056$.
例题 4.1a：两颗骰子与补集规则

掷两颗公平的六面骰子。求 $P(\text{sum}=7)$、$P(\text{sum}\ge 10)$ 与 $P(\text{at least one 6})$。

  1. 样本空间含 $6\times 6=36$ 个等可能的有序数对，每个的概率为 $1/36$。
  2. 和为 7：$(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$ 共 6 对，所以 $P=6/36=1/6\approx0.1667$。
  3. 和不小于 10：$(4,6),(5,5),(6,4),(5,6),(6,5),(6,6)$ 共 6 对，所以 $P=6/36=1/6\approx0.1667$。
  4. 至少一个 6：先数补集“没有 6”。无 6 的数对有 $5\times5=25$ 个，所以 $P=1-25/36=11/36\approx0.3056$。
Worked Example 4.1b: A committee of two

A club has four officers: Ana and Ben (seniors), Cai and Dee (juniors). Two officers are chosen at random for a committee. Find the probability that the committee has at least one senior.

  1. List the sample space (order does not matter): $\{AB, AC, AD, BC, BD, CD\}$, six equally likely committees.
  2. "At least one senior" fails only for $CD$, so use the complement: $P(\text{no senior})=1/6$.
  3. $P(\text{at least one senior})=1-1/6=5/6\approx0.833$. Check by counting directly: $AB, AC, AD, BC, BD$ are 5 of the 6 committees.
例题 4.1b：两人委员会

某社团有四名干部：Ana 和 Ben（高三），Cai 和 Dee（高二）。随机选两人组成委员会。求委员会中至少有一名高三学生的概率。

  1. 列出样本空间（不计顺序）：$\{AB, AC, AD, BC, BD, CD\}$，共六种等可能的委员会。
  2. “至少一名高三学生”只有 $CD$ 不满足，故用补集：$P(\text{no senior})=1/6$。
  3. $P(\text{at least one senior})=1-1/6=5/6\approx0.833$。直接数可验证：$AB, AC, AD, BC, BD$ 共 6 种中的 5 种。
Worked Example 4.1c: Is a sum of two dice a prime number?

Maya rolls two fair dice and says: "There are $11$ possible sums, $2$ through $12$, and $5$ of them ($2, 3, 5, 7, 11$) are prime, so the probability of a prime sum is $5/11$." Evaluate her reasoning, find the correct probability, and interpret it for $360$ rolls.

  1. The $11$ sums are not equally likely, so counting sums is not valid. Use the $36$ equally likely ordered pairs instead.
  2. Count the pairs for each prime sum: sum $2$ has $1$ pair, sum $3$ has $2$, sum $5$ has $4$, sum $7$ has $6$ and sum $11$ has $2$. That is $1+2+4+6+2=15$ pairs.
  3. The correct probability is $15/36\approx0.417$, not $5/11\approx0.455$.
  4. Check with the complement: the non-prime sums $4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12$ have $3+5+5+4+3+1=21$ pairs, and $15+21=36$.
  5. Interpretation: in the long run about $41.7\%$ of rolls of two fair dice give a prime sum. In $360$ rolls we would expect about $150$, but the actual count varies from run to run and need not equal $150$ exactly.
  6. A complete answer names the sample space ($36$ ordered pairs), says why they are equally likely, shows the count and the probability, and interprets the result as a long-run proportion.
例题 4.1c：两颗骰子的和是质数吗？

Maya 掷两颗公平的骰子，并说：“可能的和有 $11$ 种，即 $2$ 到 $12$，其中 $5$ 个（$2, 3, 5, 7, 11$）是质数，所以和为质数的概率是 $5/11$。”评价她的推理，求出正确的概率，并就 $360$ 次投掷作出解释。

  1. 这 $11$ 种和并不等可能，所以数“和”的个数是无效的。应改用 $36$ 个等可能的有序数对。
  2. 数出每个质数和对应的数对：和 $2$ 有 $1$ 对，和 $3$ 有 $2$ 对，和 $5$ 有 $4$ 对，和 $7$ 有 $6$ 对，和 $11$ 有 $2$ 对，共 $1+2+4+6+2=15$ 对。
  3. 正确的概率是 $15/36\approx0.417$，而不是 $5/11\approx0.455$。
  4. 用补集验证：非质数的和 $4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12$ 共有 $3+5+5+4+3+1=21$ 对，而 $15+21=36$。
  5. 解释：长期来看，两颗公平骰子的投掷中约有 $41.7\%$ 的和为质数。投掷 $360$ 次时，预计约有 $150$ 次，但实际次数每次运行都会变化，不一定恰为 $150$。
  6. 完整的答案要指出样本空间（$36$ 个有序数对），说明它们为何等可能，给出计数和概率，并把结果解释为长期比例。
Common Mistake: The law of averages

The "law of averages" is not the law of large numbers. After five heads in a row, the next flip is still $50\%$ likely to be heads, and a probability of $0.3$ does not guarantee that exactly 3 of 10 trials succeed.

常见错误：平均律

“平均律”不是大数定律。连续五次正面之后，下一次仍有 $50\%$ 的概率为正面；概率为 $0.3$ 也不保证 10 次试验中恰有 3 次成功。

Common Mistake: Counting outcomes that are not equally likely

Listing the sums $2$ to $12$ and writing $P(\text{sum}=7)=1/11$ treats outcomes that have different numbers of ordered pairs as if they were equally likely. The fix is to list the $36$ ordered pairs, count the pairs that give the sum, and divide by $36$, so that $P(\text{sum}=7)=6/36$.

常见错误：把不等可能的结果当作等可能

列出和 $2$ 到 $12$ 并写 $P(\text{sum}=7)=1/11$，是把含有不同个数有序数对的结果当作等可能。正确做法是列出 $36$ 个有序数对，数出得到该和的数对，再除以 $36$，于是 $P(\text{sum}=7)=6/36$。

AP exam tipAP 考试提示

Always interpret a probability as a long-run proportion and name the event: "If many such days were observed under the same conditions, about $30\%$ would have rain." Do not write that it "will" rain on $3$ of $10$ days.

解释概率时，始终说成长期比例并点明事件：“在相同条件下观察大量这样的日子，约有 $30\%$ 会下雨。”不要写成“10 天中会有 3 天下雨”。

Check your understanding自我检测
1. A forecast says there is a $30\%$ chance of rain tomorrow. Interpret this probability.1. 预报称明天有 $30\%$ 的降雨概率。解释这个概率。

In the long run, on about $30\%$ of days with conditions like tomorrow's, it rains. It does not mean it rains for $30\%$ of the day.

在长期中，与明天条件相似的日子里约有 $30\%$ 会下雨。这不是说一天中有 $30\%$ 的时间在下雨。

2. A fair coin has landed heads $5$ times in a row. What is the probability of heads on the next flip, and why?2. 一枚公平硬币已连续 $5$ 次正面朝上。下一次正面的概率是多少？为什么？

$0.5$. Flips are independent and the coin has no memory; the law of large numbers concerns the long-run proportion, not a correction of streaks.

$0.5$。各次抛掷相互独立，硬币没有记忆；大数定律讲的是长期比例，而非对连续结果的补偿。

$P(\text{red})=5/10=0.5$, so $P(\text{not red})=1-0.5=0.5$ (equivalently $(3+2)/10$).

$P(\text{红})=5/10=0.5$，所以 $P(\text{不是红})=1-0.5=0.5$（等价于 $(3+2)/10$）。

Topic 5.2主题 5.2

Mutually exclusive events and the addition rule互斥事件与加法规则

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Decide whether two events are mutually exclusive.
  • Apply the addition rule for mutually exclusive events.
  • Explain why mutually exclusive events with positive probability are not independent.
  • Use a Venn diagram to organize events.
  • 判断两个事件是否互斥。
  • 对互斥事件应用加法规则。
  • 说明为什么概率为正的互斥事件不是独立的。
  • 用维恩图整理事件。

Two events are mutually exclusive (also called disjoint) if they cannot occur at the same time: they share no outcomes, so $P(A\text{ and }B)=0$. For such events the probabilities simply add. The same rule extends to any list of events no two of which overlap.

若两个事件不可能同时发生，则称它们互斥（也称不相交）：二者没有共同结果，故 $P(A\text{ 且 }B)=0$。此时概率直接相加。该规则可推广到任意两两不重叠的一组事件。

Must know

If $A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive, $P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A)+P(B)$. For events with overlap, the general addition rule in Topic 5.4 applies instead. If $P(A)>0$ and $P(B)>0$ and $A,B$ are mutually exclusive, then $P(B\mid A)=0\ne P(B)$, so $A$ and $B$ are dependent.

必备知识

若 $A$、$B$ 互斥，则 $P(A\text{ 或 }B)=P(A)+P(B)$。事件有重叠时，应使用主题 5.4 的一般加法规则。若 $P(A)>0$、$P(B)>0$ 且 $A,B$ 互斥，则 $P(B\mid A)=0\ne P(B)$，所以 $A$ 与 $B$ 不独立。

Translating words into events把文字翻译成事件

Most errors in this topic start with the wording, so read each phrase as an event. "$A$ or $B$" is inclusive: $A$ happens, $B$ happens, or both, written $A\cup B$. "Neither" means not $A$ and not $B$, which is the complement of the union, so $P(\text{neither})=1-P(A\cup B)$. "Exactly one" means $A$ or $B$ but not both, so $P(\text{exactly one})=P(A\cup B)-P(A\cap B)$. "Not both" is the complement of $A\cap B$.

本主题的多数错误源于措辞，所以要把每个短语读成一个事件。“$A$ 或 $B$”是包含式的：$A$ 发生、$B$ 发生，或二者都发生，记作 $A\cup B$。“两者都不”指非 $A$ 且非 $B$，即并集的补集，所以 $P(\text{neither})=1-P(A\cup B)$。“恰好一个”指 $A$ 或 $B$ 发生但不同时发生，所以 $P(\text{exactly one})=P(A\cup B)-P(A\cap B)$。“不同时发生”是 $A\cap B$ 的补集。

When the categories of one variable are mutually exclusive and exhaustive, as in the commute survey, their probabilities add to $1$. That gives a way to find a missing value: if three of four categories have probabilities $0.18$, $0.12$ and $0.35$, the fourth is $1-0.65=0.35$. Check that a whole list sums to $1$ before you use it.

当一个变量的各类别互斥且穷尽时（如上学方式调查），它们的概率之和为 $1$。由此可以求缺失的值：若四个类别中有三个的概率为 $0.18$、$0.12$ 和 $0.35$，则第四个为 $1-0.65=0.35$。使用整张列表之前，先检查它的和是否为 $1$。

Worked examples例题

Worked Example 4.2a: How students get to school

In a synthetic survey of students, each student reports one main commute mode: $P(\text{walk})=0.18$, $P(\text{bike})=0.12$, $P(\text{bus})=0.35$ and $P(\text{car})=0.35$. Find $P(\text{walk or bike})$, $P(\text{not car})$ and $P(\text{walk or bus})$.

  1. The categories are mutually exclusive (one main mode per student) and exhaustive: $0.18+0.12+0.35+0.35=1.00$.
  2. $P(\text{walk or bike})=0.18+0.12=0.30$.
  3. $P(\text{not car})=1-0.35=0.65$, which equals $0.18+0.12+0.35=0.65$.
  4. $P(\text{walk or bus})=0.18+0.35=0.53$.
例题 4.2a：学生如何上学

在一项合成的学生调查中，每名学生只报告一种主要上学方式：$P(\text{walk})=0.18$，$P(\text{bike})=0.12$，$P(\text{bus})=0.35$，$P(\text{car})=0.35$。求 $P(\text{walk or bike})$、$P(\text{not car})$ 与 $P(\text{walk or bus})$。

  1. 各类别互斥（每人只有一种主要方式）且穷尽：$0.18+0.12+0.35+0.35=1.00$。
  2. $P(\text{walk or bike})=0.18+0.12=0.30$。
  3. $P(\text{not car})=1-0.35=0.65$，也等于 $0.18+0.12+0.35=0.65$。
  4. $P(\text{walk or bus})=0.18+0.35=0.53$。
Worked Example 4.2b: Counts by class year

A school council survey lists $300$ students: $90$ first-years, $80$ second-years, $70$ juniors and $60$ seniors. A student is chosen at random. Find $P(\text{first-year or senior})$ and $P(\text{not a junior})$, and decide whether "first-year" and "plays a sport" are mutually exclusive.

  1. Class years are mutually exclusive, so $P(\text{first-year or senior})=\dfrac{90}{300}+\dfrac{60}{300}=\dfrac{150}{300}=0.50$.
  2. $P(\text{not a junior})=1-\dfrac{70}{300}=\dfrac{230}{300}\approx0.767$.
  3. "First-year" and "plays a sport" are not mutually exclusive: a first-year student can play a sport, so the events can occur together and the simple sum would overcount.
例题 4.2b：按年级的人数

某校学生会调查列出 $300$ 名学生：$90$ 名一年级、$80$ 名二年级、$70$ 名三年级、$60$ 名四年级。随机选一名学生。求 $P(\text{first-year or senior})$、$P(\text{not a junior})$，并判断“一年级”与“参加体育运动”是否互斥。

  1. 年级互斥，故 $P(\text{first-year or senior})=\dfrac{90}{300}+\dfrac{60}{300}=\dfrac{150}{300}=0.50$。
  2. $P(\text{not a junior})=1-\dfrac{70}{300}=\dfrac{230}{300}\approx0.767$。
  3. “一年级”与“参加体育运动”不互斥：一年级学生可以参加体育运动，两事件可同时发生，直接相加会重复计数。
Worked Example 4.2c: Physics, chemistry or both

At a synthetic school, $200$ students were surveyed: $80$ take physics ($A$), $70$ take chemistry ($B$) and $30$ take both. A student is chosen at random. Find $P(A\cup B)$, $P(\text{neither})$ and $P(\text{exactly one})$, and explain why $P(A)+P(B)$ is not the answer to the first.

  1. $P(A)=80/200=0.40$, $P(B)=70/200=0.35$ and $P(A\cap B)=30/200=0.15$.
  2. The events are not mutually exclusive, because $30$ students take both. Adding $0.40+0.35=0.75$ counts those $30$ students twice.
  3. $P(A\cup B)=0.40+0.35-0.15=0.60$. Check by counting: $80+70-30=120$ students, and $120/200=0.60$.
  4. $P(\text{neither})=1-0.60=0.40$, which is $80$ students. The tempting value $1-0.15=0.85$ is only the probability that a student does not take both.
  5. $P(\text{exactly one})=0.60-0.15=0.45$. Check: $50$ take only physics and $40$ take only chemistry, and $90/200=0.45$.
  6. A complete answer defines the events, notes that they overlap, uses the general rule, and states each result in context: about $45\%$ of the surveyed students take exactly one of the two courses.
例题 4.2c：物理、化学或两者都选

The conditional probability of $B$ given $A$ is the probability that $B$ occurs when we already know that $A$ occurred. Knowing $A$ shrinks the sample space to the outcomes in $A$.

在已知 $A$ 发生的条件下，$B$ 发生的概率称为条件概率。已知 $A$ 后，样本空间缩小为 $A$ 中的结果。

$$P(B\mid A)=\frac{P(A\text{ and }B)}{P(A)},\qquad P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\,P(B\mid A)$$
Must know

The condition goes in the denominator. In a two-way table, $P(B\mid A)$ is the cell count divided by the total of the row or column for $A$, not by the grand total. Also, in general $P(B\mid A)\ne P(A\mid B)$.

必备知识

条件放在分母。在二维表格中，$P(B\mid A)$ 是单元格计数除以 $A$ 所在行或列的合计，而不是除以总计。一般地，$P(B\mid A)\ne P(A\mid B)$。

Also on the 2027 exam: two-way table summaries

The revised course also asks you to describe two categorical variables with joint, marginal and conditional relative frequencies. They are probabilities in disguise: a joint relative frequency is $P(A\cap B)$ (cell divided by the table total), a marginal relative frequency is $P(A)$ (row or column total divided by the table total), and a conditional relative frequency is $P(B\mid A)$ (cell divided by its row or column total). The variables are associated when the conditional distributions differ between groups, for example in a segmented bar chart.

对应 2027 考试新增内容：二维表格的汇总

新课程还要求用联合、边际与条件相对频率描述两个分类变量。它们其实就是概率：联合相对频率是 $P(A\cap B)$（单元格除以总计），边际相对频率是 $P(A)$（行或列合计除以总计），条件相对频率是 $P(B\mid A)$（单元格除以其所在行或列的合计）。当各组的条件分布不同时（例如在分段条形图中），两变量存在关联。

Tree diagrams树状图

A tree diagram lays out a sequence of stages. Each branch carries a conditional probability, so the probabilities leaving any one node sum to $1$. Multiply along a path to get a joint probability, and add the paths that share the event you want. To reverse a conditional, divide one joint probability by the total probability of the given event.

树状图按阶段排列。每条分支标注条件概率，因此从同一节点发出的概率之和为 $1$。沿一条路径相乘得联合概率；把包含目标事件的各路径相加。要把条件概率反转，就用一个联合概率除以所给事件的总概率。

Total probability and sampling without replacement全概率与不放回抽样

A tree also computes an unconditional probability from conditional pieces. If $A$ and $A^{c}$ split the sample space, then $P(B)=P(A)\,P(B\mid A)+P(A^{c})\,P(B\mid A^{c})$: add the joint probabilities of every path that ends in $B$. This is how $0.1128$ was found for a positive test in Worked Example 4.3b, and it is the denominator whenever a conditional is reversed.

树状图还可以由条件概率的各部分算出无条件概率。若 $A$ 与 $A^{c}$ 把样本空间分成两部分，则 $P(B)=P(A)\,P(B\mid A)+P(A^{c})\,P(B\mid A^{c})$：把所有以 $B$ 结尾的路径的联合概率相加。例题 4.3b 中检测阳性的 $0.1128$ 就是这样得到的，每当要反转条件概率时，它也是分母。

When items are drawn without replacement, the probabilities on the second stage depend on the first draw, because the pool has changed. From a box of $12$ batteries with $3$ defective, the chance the second is defective is $2/11$ if the first was defective and $3/11$ if it was not. Do not reuse first-stage probabilities on later branches. This is exactly a situation in which the draws are not independent.

不放回地抽取时，第二阶段的概率取决于第一次抽取，因为剩下的总体已经改变。从含 $3$ 个次品的 $12$ 节电池中抽取，若第一节是次品，第二节是次品的概率为 $2/11$；若第一节不是次品，则为 $3/11$。不要把第一阶段的概率沿用到后面的分支。这正是各次抽取不独立的典型情形。

Three habits keep a conditional problem organized. Write the events with letters. Write the wanted probability in $P(B\mid A)$ notation. Then decide whether the data are a table (divide within one row or column) or stages (multiply along paths, then divide).

三个习惯可使条件概率题条理清楚。用字母写出事件。用 $P(B\mid A)$ 的记号写出所求概率。再判断数据是表格（在一行或一列内相除）还是分阶段（沿路径相乘，再相除）。

  • 区分独立事件与互斥事件。

The union "$A$ or $B$" means $A$ occurs, $B$ occurs, or both. The general addition rule subtracts the overlap that was counted twice. Events are independent if knowing that one occurred does not change the probability of the other.

并集“$A$ 或 $B$”指 $A$ 发生、$B$ 发生，或二者都发生。一般加法规则要减去被重复计算的重叠部分。若得知一个事件发生并不改变另一个事件的概率，则这两个事件独立。

$$P(A\text{ or }B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\text{ and }B)$$
Must know

Events $A$ and $B$ are independent exactly when any one of these holds: $P(B\mid A)=P(B)$, or $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$, or $P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\,P(B)$. For independent events, $P(A\text{ and }B)=P(A)\,P(B)$ extends to any number of events. Independence is a statement about probabilities, not about whether the events seem unrelated.

必备知识

事件 $A$、$B$ 独立，当且仅当下列任一式成立：$P(B\mid A)=P(B)$，或 $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$，或 $P(A\text{ 且 }B)=P(A)\,P(B)$。对独立事件，$P(A\text{ 且 }B)=P(A)\,P(B)$ 可推广到任意多个事件。独立是关于概率的陈述，而不是凭直觉判断事件“看起来是否无关”。

When sampling without replacement from a large population, the trials are not exactly independent, but they are treated as approximately independent when the sample is less than $10\%$ of the population (the $10\%$ condition).

从大总体中不放回抽样时，各次试验并非严格独立；当样本量不超过总体的 $10\%$（$10\%$ 条件）时，可近似视为独立。

Three checks for independence检验独立性的三种方法

Use whichever check the data make easiest, and say which one you used. The three checks always agree, because each rearranges the others: if $P(B\mid A)=P(B)$, the multiplication rule gives $P(A\cap B)=P(A)\,P(B\mid A)=P(A)\,P(B)$. With a two-way table, compare a conditional relative frequency with the matching marginal one. For "at least one" success in several independent trials, work with the complement: if $q$ is the probability of no success, then the probability of at least one success is $1-q$, and for independent trials $q$ is a product. When the trials are draws from a population without replacement, the 10% condition (the sample is less than 10% of the population) is what allows the product. Mutually exclusive events with positive probabilities fail every one of the three checks.

哪种检验方便数据就用哪种，并写明用的是哪一种。三种检验总是一致，因为每一种都可由其他推出：若 $P(B\mid A)=P(B)$，由乘法规则得 $P(A\cap B)=P(A)\,P(B\mid A)=P(A)\,P(B)$。对二维表格，比较一个条件相对频率与对应的边际相对频率。对多次独立试验中“至少一次”成功，改用补集：设 $q$ 为一次也不成功的概率，则至少成功一次的概率为 $1-q$，而独立试验中 $q$ 是乘积。若是从总体中不放回抽取，则需满足 10% 条件（样本不超过总体的 10%）才可连乘。概率为正的互斥事件，三种检验都通不过。

Writing and justifying independence claims写出并论证独立性的结论

Independence is a claim you test and then state in context. When a problem gives exact probabilities, check one equality and conclude. When the numbers come from a sample table, they will seldom match exactly, so report the two numbers you compared, say whether they are equal or different in this table, and describe the association (or the lack of it) in context. Judging whether a small gap could be sampling variation is a job for the inference units later in the course.

独立性是需要检验、然后结合情境陈述的结论。题目给出精确概率时，检验一个等式即可下结论。数字来自样本表格时，它们很少恰好相等，所以要写出所比较的两个数，说明在这张表中二者相等还是不等，并结合情境描述关联（或没有关联）。判断一个很小的差距是否可能只是抽样变异，是本课程后面推断单元的任务。

Independence also gives a shortcut for unions. If $A$ and $B$ are independent, then $P(A\cup B)=1-\big(1-P(A)\big)\big(1-P(B)\big)$, because "neither" is a product of two complements. For $P(A)=0.5$ and $P(B)=0.4$ this gives $1-0.5\times0.6=0.70$, the same value as $0.5+0.4-0.2$.

独立性还给出并集的捷径。若 $A$ 与 $B$ 独立，则 $P(A\cup B)=1-\big(1-P(A)\big)\big(1-P(B)\big)$，因为“两者都不”是两个补集概率的乘积。对 $P(A)=0.5$、$P(B)=0.4$，得到 $1-0.5\times0.6=0.70$，与 $0.5+0.4-0.2$ 的结果相同。

Independence is not "no relationship I can imagine". It is a property of the probabilities in the model. If trials are draws without replacement from a small group, the product rule does not apply and conditional probabilities are needed, as in Topic 5.3.

独立性不是“我想不出有什么联系”，而是模型中概率的性质。若各次试验是从小群体中不放回地抽取，则乘积规则不适用，需要使用条件概率，如主题 5.3 所示。

A simulation imitates a random process with a model that has the same probabilities, so that it can be repeated cheaply many times. It is used when an exact calculation is hard or when you want to check one. A complete description has five parts, in order.

模拟用概率相同的模型模仿随机过程，因而可以低成本地重复多次。当精确计算困难，或想检验精确计算时就可使用。完整的描述依次包括五部分。

  1. State the model and its assumptions (for example, each box holds one of 5 prizes with equal probability, independently).
  2. Assign digits so the digit probabilities match the model, and say what to ignore (for example, digits 6 to 9 and 0 are skipped).
  3. Define one trial: what is generated, when it stops, and what is recorded.
  4. Repeat for many trials (state the number).
  5. Estimate with the proportion of trials in which the event occurred, and conclude in context.
  1. 说明模型及其假设（例如，每个盒子独立地等可能含 5 种奖品之一）。
  2. 分配数字，使数字的概率与模型一致，并说明忽略哪些数字（例如跳过 6 到 9 和 0）。

Choosing and checking a simulation选择并检验模拟

Designing one trial is the hard part. Ask what is random, what a single trial consists of, and when it stops. For "at least $8$ of $10$ shots", one trial is ten digits and a count. For "the first success", one trial stops at the first success and records the number of attempts. Record in each trial exactly the quantity the question needs, and nothing else.

设计一次试验是难点。要问清什么是随机的、一次试验由什么构成、何时停止。对“$10$ 次投篮中至少 $8$ 次”，一次试验就是十个数字再数一个个数。对“第一次成功”，一次试验在第一次成功时停止并记录尝试次数。每次试验只记录题目所需要的那个量，不要多记。

Digit rules must give every outcome its right share. For three outcomes with probabilities $0.5$, $0.3$ and $0.2$, one digit is enough: $0$ to $4$, $5$ to $7$ and $8$ to $9$. A probability such as $0.35$ or $0.125$ needs two or three digits. When the sample is drawn without replacement, such as $3$ different students out of $10$, label the units $0$ to $9$ and ignore a digit that repeats within the trial.

数字规则必须让每个结果得到恰当的份额。对概率为 $0.5$、$0.3$、$0.2$ 的三个结果，一位数字就够了：$0$ 到 $4$、$5$ 到 $7$、$8$ 到 $9$。像 $0.35$ 或 $0.125$ 这样的概率需要两位或三位数字。不放回抽样时（例如从 $10$ 名学生中抽 $3$ 名不同的学生），把各个单位标为 $0$ 到 $9$，并忽略同一次试验中重复出现的数字。

More trials give a steadier estimate, because of the law of large numbers, but only for the model that was written down. If the model's probabilities are wrong, or independence was assumed where it fails, a million trials still estimates the wrong probability. When an exact answer is available, compare the simulated proportion with it to see how large the typical gap is.

试验次数越多，估计越稳定，这是大数定律的结果，但只针对所写下的那个模型。若模型的概率有误，或在不独立时假设了独立，即使做一百万次试验，估计的仍是错误的概率。如果有精确答案，就把模拟比例与它比较，看一般的差距有多大。

Worked Example 4.5a: How many cereal boxes to collect all five prizes?

Each cereal box contains one of $5$ prizes, equally likely and independent. Design and carry out $10$ trials of a simulation to estimate the average number of boxes needed to collect all $5$ prizes.

  1. Model: every box independently holds prize $1$ to $5$ with probability $0.2$ each.
  2. Digits: use randInt(1,5); the digit is the prize in that box.
  3. Trial: generate digits one at a time until all of $1,2,3,4,5$ have appeared; record the number of digits. Trial $1$ produced $1,3,5,5,1,2,5,5,5,4$, so all five prizes needed $10$ boxes.
  4. Ten trials gave: $10,\,11,\,12,\,9,\,28,\,15,\,12,\,7,\,9,\,9$ boxes (computer-generated digits).
  5. Estimate: the mean is $(10+11+12+9+28+15+12+7+9+9)/10=122/10=12.2$ boxes. Two of the $10$ trials needed more than $12$ boxes, an estimated probability of $0.20$.
  6. Conclusion: a child should expect to buy roughly $12$ boxes. The exact long-run mean is $5(1+\tfrac12+\tfrac13+\tfrac14+\tfrac15)\approx11.4$, so ten trials land close, but a different ten trials would give a different estimate.

Worked Example 4.5b: A 70% free-throw shooter

A player makes each free throw with probability $0.70$, independently. Use $20$ simulated sets of $10$ shots to estimate the probability she makes at least $8$ of the $10$.

  1. Digits: one random digit per shot; $0$ to $6$ means made ($7$ of $10$ digits, probability $0.70$) and $7$ to $9$ means missed.
  2. Trial: generate $10$ digits and count the made shots. For trial $1$ the digits $4,0,7,0,8,5,3,7,7,8$ give $5$ made.
  3. Twenty trials (made out of 10): $5,5,6,9,6,6,7,7,9,5,7,7,8,7,8,6,5,9,5,8$.
  4. Estimate: $8$ or more occurred in $6$ trials ($9,9,8,8,9,8$), so the estimate is $6/20=0.30$.
  5. Comparison: A later unit on random variables computes the exact probability $0.3828$. With only $20$ trials the estimate typically misses by about $\sqrt{0.38\times0.62/20}\approx0.11$, so $0.30$ is unremarkable.
例题 4.5b：命中率 70% 的罚球手

Worked Example 4.5c: A committee drawn without replacement

A club of $10$ students has $4$ seniors. A committee of $3$ is chosen at random. Describe and carry out $10$ trials of a simulation to estimate the probability that the committee has at least $2$ seniors.

  1. Model: every group of $3$ different students is equally likely to be the committee.
  2. Digits: label the students $0$ to $9$, with $0$ to $3$ the seniors.
  3. Trial: read random digits, ignoring any digit already chosen, until $3$ different digits are chosen; record how many of them are in $0$ to $3$.
  4. Ten trials (digits chosen, then number of seniors): $945\to0$, $807\to1$, $302\to3$, $157\to1$, $368\to1$, $193\to2$, $036\to2$, $426\to1$, $219\to2$, $972\to1$ (computer-generated digits).
  5. Estimate: at least $2$ seniors occurred in $4$ trials ($302$, $193$, $036$, $219$), so the estimate is $4/10=0.40$.
  6. Check: there are $120$ equally likely committees; $36$ have exactly $2$ seniors and $4$ have all $3$, so the exact probability is $40/120\approx0.333$. The estimate differs because only $10$ trials were run.
  7. A complete answer states the model, digit rule, trial and stopping rule, number of repetitions and estimate, and concludes in context: about $40\%$ of the simulated committees had at least $2$ seniors.

Common Mistake: A vague simulation design

Assigning digits that do not match the probability (for example one digit per prize when there are $6$ prizes), failing to say how a trial stops, or reporting a simulation proportion as if it were the exact probability.

常见错误：模拟设计含糊

数字分配与概率不符（例如有 $6$ 种奖品却每种奖品只分一个数字）、没有说明一次试验如何停止，或把模拟得到的比例当作精确概率。

Common Mistake: Letting a digit repeat in a draw without replacement

If a trial must choose $3$ different students, a digit that appears twice has to be skipped. Counting the same student twice creates a committee that cannot happen, and it distorts the estimate. Say "ignore repeated digits" in the description of the trial.

常见错误：不放回抽取时允许数字重复

若一次试验要选出 $3$ 名不同的学生，重复出现的数字必须跳过。把同一名学生算两次，会产生不可能出现的委员会，并扭曲估计值。在试验的描述中要写明“忽略重复的数字”。

3. A player makes each shot with probability $0.6$, independently. What is the probability she makes at least one of $4$ shots?

Answer: D. $P(+)=0.02\times0.95+0.98\times0.10=0.019+0.098=0.117$, so $P(D\mid+)=0.019/0.117\approx0.162$. Choice A is $P(+)$, choice B is $P(+\mid D)$ and choice C is the joint probability $P(D\cap+)$.

10. 在一个合成的筛查项目中，$2\%$ 的人患有某种状况。患病者检测呈阳性的比例为 $95\%$，未患病者呈阳性的比例为 $10\%$。某人检测呈阳性。此人患病的概率是多少？

答案：D。 $P(+)=0.02\times0.95+0.98\times0.10=0.019+0.098=0.117$，所以 $P(D\mid+)=0.019/0.117\approx0.162$。选项 A 是 $P(+)$，选项 B 是 $P(+\mid D)$，选项 C 是联合概率 $P(D\cap+)$。

11. A game is won with probability $0.2$ each time, independently. What is the probability of winning at least once in $3$ plays?

Answer: B. Use the complement: losing all three has probability $0.8^{3}=0.512$, so at least one win has probability $1-0.512=0.488$. Choice A is $3\times0.2$, an expected count, and choice C is the probability of no wins.

11. 某游戏每次获胜的概率为 $0.2$，各次独立。玩 $3$ 次至少获胜一次的概率是多少？

答案：B。 用补集：三次全输的概率为 $0.8^{3}=0.512$，所以至少赢一次的概率为 $1-0.512=0.488$。选项 A 是 $3\times0.2$，是期望次数；选项 C 是一次都没赢的概率。

12. Outcome A has probability $0.5$, outcome B has $0.3$ and outcome C has $0.2$. Which assignment of a single random digit matches these probabilities?

Answer: A. Five, three and two of the ten digits give $0.5$, $0.3$ and $0.2$. Choice B gives $0.6, 0.3, 0.1$, choice C gives $0.5, 0.4, 0.1$, and choice D makes the three outcomes equally likely.

12. 结果 A 的概率为 $0.5$，B 为 $0.3$，C 为 $0.2$。下列哪种对单个随机数字的分配与这些概率相符？

答案：A。 十个数字中分别取五个、三个和两个，得到 $0.5$、$0.3$、$0.2$。选项 B 给出 $0.6, 0.3, 0.1$，选项 C 给出 $0.5, 0.4, 0.1$，选项 D 使三个结果等可能。
