Electric Potential Energy电势能

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Derive $U_E=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_1q_2}{r}$ by integrating the external force from infinity, and explain what its sign says about the interaction.
  • Use $U_E=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_1q_2}{r}=\frac{kq_1q_2}{r}$ for two point charges, and explain what its sign says about the interaction.
  • Calculate the total electric potential energy of a system of up to four point charges by summing the energy of every distinct pair.
  • Sketch $U_E(r)$ for like and for unlike charges, and compare the energy of two configurations.
  • Explain why electric potential energy belongs to a system rather than to one charge, and why its value depends on the chosen zero.
  • Relate the work done by the electric force to the change in potential energy, $W_{\text{field}}=-\Delta U_E$, and distinguish it from the external work.
  • 描述（describe）两个点电荷组成的系统的电势能：外力把它们从无穷远处移到当前位置所必须做的功。

  • 对两个点电荷使用（use）$U_E=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_1q_2}{r}=\frac{kq_1q_2}{r}$，并解释其符号说明了相互作用的什么性质。
  • 对至多四个点电荷组成的系统，把每一对电荷的电势能相加，计算（calculate）系统的总电势能。

Unit 8 described what a charge does to the space around it (the electric field) and what force results on another charge. Unit 9 adds the energy view.

第 8 单元描述了电荷对周围空间的作用（电场）以及由此在另一个电荷上产生的力。第 9 单元加入能量的视角。

Because the electrostatic force is conservative, the work it does depends only on the start and end configurations, so a single number, the electric potential energy, can be assigned to each arrangement of charges.

静电力是保守力（conservative force），它做的功只取决于起止构型，因此可以给电荷的每一种排布赋予一个数值，即电势能（electric potential energy）。

Energy methods often replace vector force sums with scalar arithmetic: they ignore the path, they ignore direction, and they handle motion that a constant-acceleration model cannot.

能量方法常常用标量运算代替矢量求和：它不关心路径，不关心方向，还能处理匀加速模型无法描述的运动。

The earlier topics of this unit described what a charge does to the space around it (the electric field) and what force results on another charge. This topic adds the energy view. Because the electrostatic force is conservative, the work it does depends only on the start and end arrangements, so a single number, the electric potential energy, can be assigned to each arrangement of charges. Energy methods often replace vector force sums with scalar arithmetic: they ignore the path, they ignore direction, and they handle motion that a constant-acceleration model cannot.

本单元前面的专题描述了电荷对周围空间的作用（电场）以及由此在另一个电荷上产生的力。本专题加入能量的视角。静电力是保守力（conservative force），它做的功只取决于起止排布，因此可以给电荷的每一种排布赋予一个数值，即电势能（electric potential energy）。能量方法常常用标量运算代替矢量求和：它不关心路径，不关心方向，还能处理匀加速模型无法描述的运动。

Constants used in this guide $k=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}=8.99\times10^{9}\ \mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$, $e=1.60\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C}$, $m_e=9.11\times10^{-31}\ \mathrm{kg}$, $m_p=1.67\times10^{-27}\ \mathrm{kg}$, and $1\ \mathrm{eV}=1.60\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{J}$. The exam provides reference materials, so use the values it gives; the rounded values here keep the arithmetic readable.
本指南使用的常数 $k=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}=8.99\times10^{9}\ \mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$，$e=1.60\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C}$，$m_e=9.11\times10^{-31}\ \mathrm{kg}$，$m_p=1.67\times10^{-27}\ \mathrm{kg}$，$1\ \mathrm{eV}=1.60\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{J}$。考试会提供参考资料（reference materials），请使用其中给出的数值；这里取的近似值只是为了让运算更易读。

Definition: work to assemble the system定义：组装系统所需的功

Consider two point charges. Hold $q_1$ fixed and start $q_2$ infinitely far away, where the two charges do not interact.

考虑两个点电荷。固定 $q_1$，让 $q_2$ 从无穷远处出发，此时两者没有相互作用。

An external agent moves $q_2$ slowly (so its kinetic energy never changes) until the separation is $r$. The electric potential energy of the two-charge system is the work $W_{\text{ext}}$ the external agent did. This definition fixes the zero: $U_E=0$ when the charges are infinitely far apart.

外界缓慢移动 $q_2$（使其动能始终不变），直到间距为 $r$。两电荷系统的电势能就是外界所做的功 $W_{\text{ext}}$。这个定义同时确定了零点：两电荷相距无穷远时 $U_E=0$。

Two forces act on $q_2$ during the trip: the electric force $\vec F_E$ from $q_1$ and the external force. Moving slowly means they cancel, $\vec F_{\text{ext}}=-\vec F_E$. The electric force does work $W_{\text{field}}=-W_{\text{ext}}$ on the way in, so the definition can also be written $\Delta U_E=-W_{\text{field}}=+W_{\text{ext}}$ for any slow change of configuration.

移动过程中 $q_2$ 受两个力：$q_1$ 施加的电场力 $\vec F_E$ 和外力。缓慢移动意味着两者抵消，$\vec F_{\text{ext}}=-\vec F_E$。电场力在这一过程中做功 $W_{\text{field}}=-W_{\text{ext}}$，所以对任何缓慢的构型变化，定义也可以写成 $\Delta U_E=-W_{\text{field}}=+W_{\text{ext}}$。

Derivation: the potential energy of two point charges推导：两个点电荷的电势能

Bring $q_2$ in along a radial line. Let $r'$ be the separation at an intermediate moment. The electric force on $q_2$ along the outward radial direction $\hat r$ is $F_E=\frac{q_1q_2}{4\pi\epsilon_0 r'^2}$ (positive means repulsion). The external force is $F_{\text{ext}}=-F_E$, and the separation runs from $\infty$ to $r$.

沿径向把 $q_2$ 移入。设中间时刻的间距为 $r'$。$q_2$ 受到的电场力沿向外径向 $\hat r$ 的分量为 $F_E=\frac{q_1q_2}{4\pi\epsilon_0 r'^2}$（为正表示排斥）。外力为 $F_{\text{ext}}=-F_E$，间距从 $\infty$ 变到 $r$。

$$U_E=W_{\text{ext}}=\int_{\infty}^{r}F_{\text{ext}}\,dr'=-\frac{q_1q_2}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\int_{\infty}^{r}\frac{dr'}{r'^2}=-\frac{q_1q_2}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left[-\frac{1}{r'}\right]_{\infty}^{r}=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_1q_2}{r}$$

The upper limit $r$ contributes $-1/r$ and the lower limit contributes $0$ because $1/r'\to0$ at infinity. The integral converges, which is why infinity is a convenient zero for point charges.

上限 $r$ 给出 $-1/r$，下限给出 $0$，因为在无穷远处 $1/r'\to0$。积分收敛，这正是以无穷远作零点对点电荷方便的原因。

From the definition to the formula从定义到公式

The external agent must balance the electric force at every step as $q_2$ comes in slowly from infinitely far away, so the external work is the area under the graph of the force against the separation $r'$. Because the force falls as $1/r'^2$, almost none of this area lies at large separations, and the total area from infinitely far away down to a separation $r$ works out to $\frac{kq_1q_2}{r}$, the result stated in the box below.

外界必须在 $q_2$ 从无穷远处缓慢移入的每一步都与电场力平衡，所以外力做的功就是力对间距 $r'$ 的图像下的面积。由于力按 $1/r'^2$ 减小，这块面积几乎都不在大间距处；从无穷远处一直到间距 $r$ 的总面积为 $\frac{kq_1q_2}{r}$，即下面方框中给出的结果。

Electric potential energy of two point charges两点电荷的电势能
$$U_E=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_1q_2}{r}=\frac{kq_1q_2}{r}$$

Each $q$ carries its own sign. $U_E>0$ for like charges, $U_E<0$ for unlike charges, $U_E\to0$ as $r\to\infty$.

每个 $q$ 都带自己的符号。同号电荷 $U_E>0$，异号电荷 $U_E<0$，$r\to\infty$ 时 $U_E\to0$。

Reading the sign. Like charges repel, so an external agent must push them together: $W_{\text{ext}}>0$ and $U_E>0$, and the system stores energy that is released if the charges are let go.

读懂符号。同号电荷互相排斥，外界必须把它们推到一起：$W_{\text{ext}}>0$，$U_E>0$，系统储存的能量在放手时释放。

Unlike charges attract, so the external agent must hold them back as they approach: $W_{\text{ext}}<0$ and $U_E<0$. A negative $U_E$ means the pair is bound relative to the infinitely separated state; energy $|U_E|$ must be supplied to pull them apart to infinity.

异号电荷互相吸引，电荷靠近时外界必须拉住它们：$W_{\text{ext}}<0$，$U_E<0$。$U_E$ 为负表示这一对电荷相对于相距无穷远的状态是束缚的；要把它们拉开到无穷远，必须提供能量 $|U_E|$。

Graph. Plot $U_E$ against $r$. For like charges the curve is a positive hyperbola, $U_E\propto 1/r$: it rises without bound as $r\to0$ and falls toward the axis as $r$ grows, never crossing it.

图像。以 $r$ 为横轴画 $U_E$。同号电荷的曲线是正的双曲线，$U_E\propto 1/r$：$r\to0$ 时无限上升，$r$ 增大时趋近横轴但不穿过它。

For unlike charges it is the mirror image below the axis, a negative hyperbola that dives to $-\infty$ as $r\to0$ and rises toward $0$ from below.

异号电荷的曲线是它关于横轴的镜像，即负的双曲线，$r\to0$ 时降向 $-\infty$，并从下方趋近 $0$。

The force is the negative slope, $F_r=-\dfrac{dU_E}{dr}$: for like charges the slope is negative, so $F_r>0$ (repulsion); for unlike charges the slope is positive, so $F_r<0$ (attraction). Differentiating $U_E=kq_1q_2/r$ returns $F_r=kq_1q_2/r^2$, the Coulomb force, as it must.

力是曲线斜率的负值，$F_r=-\dfrac{dU_E}{dr}$：同号电荷斜率为负，故 $F_r>0$（排斥）；异号电荷斜率为正，故 $F_r<0$（吸引）。对 $U_E=kq_1q_2/r$ 求导得 $F_r=kq_1q_2/r^2$，正是 Coulomb 力，必然如此。

Graph. Plot $U_E$ against $r$. For like charges the curve is a positive hyperbola, $U_E\propto 1/r$: it rises without bound as $r\to0$ and falls toward the axis as $r$ grows, never crossing it. For unlike charges it is the mirror image below the axis, a negative hyperbola that dives toward $-\infty$ as $r\to0$ and rises toward $0$ from below. The force is related to the slope of the curve: for like charges the energy falls as the separation grows, so the charges repel; for unlike charges the energy rises toward zero as the separation grows, so they attract; and a steeper curve means a stronger force.

图像。以 $r$ 为横轴画 $U_E$。同号电荷的曲线是正的双曲线，$U_E\propto 1/r$：$r\to0$ 时无限上升，$r$ 增大时趋近横轴但不穿过它。异号电荷的曲线是它关于横轴的镜像，即负的双曲线，$r\to0$ 时降向 $-\infty$，并从下方趋近 $0$。力与曲线的斜率有关：同号电荷的能量随间距增大而减小，所以互相排斥；异号电荷的能量随间距增大而升向零，所以互相吸引；曲线越陡，力越大。

Systems of several charges多个电荷组成的系统

Assemble the system one charge at a time from infinity. When the second charge arrives it interacts with the first; when the third arrives it interacts with both earlier charges; and so on. Each distinct pair therefore contributes exactly once, and the total is a scalar sum over pairs.

把系统中的电荷逐个从无穷远处移来。第二个电荷到达时与第一个相互作用；第三个到达时与前两个都相互作用；依此类推。因此每一对不同的电荷恰好贡献一次，总能量是对所有电荷对的标量求和。

The work needed is independent of the order, because the force is conservative.

由于力是保守的，所需的功与移入顺序无关。

Total electric potential energy总电势能
$$U_E=\sum_{i<j}\frac{kq_iq_j}{r_{ij}}$$

$N$ charges have $N(N-1)/2$ distinct pairs: 3 charges give 3 terms, 4 charges give 6 terms. The sum is a scalar sum with signs included, and only four or fewer charges (or a clearly symmetric arrangement) are expected.

$N$ 个电荷有 $N(N-1)/2$ 个不同的电荷对：3 个电荷有 3 项，4 个电荷有 6 项。这是带符号的标量求和；考试只涉及至多四个电荷（或明显对称的排布）。

$N$ charges have $N(N-1)/2$ distinct pairs: 3 charges give 3 terms, 4 charges give 6 terms. The sum is a scalar sum with signs included. AP Physics 2 limits electric potential energy calculations to configurations of four or fewer point charges.

$N$ 个电荷有 $N(N-1)/2$ 个不同的电荷对：3 个电荷有 3 项，4 个电荷有 6 项。这是带符号的标量求和。AP 物理 2 把电势能的计算限定在四个或更少点电荷组成的构型。

Common Mistake: counting a pair twice, or counting a charge alone Writing $U_E=\frac{kq_1q_2}{r_{12}}+\frac{kq_2q_1}{r_{21}}$ counts one interaction twice. A single isolated charge has no electric potential energy; the energy belongs to the arrangement. When a problem says "the potential energy of the charge", it means the system of that charge and the fixed charges that surround it, so name the system.
常见错误：把一对电荷算两次，或单独计算一个电荷 写成 $U_E=\frac{kq_1q_2}{r_{12}}+\frac{kq_2q_1}{r_{21}}$ 就把同一个相互作用算了两次。孤立的单个电荷没有电势能；能量属于电荷的排布。题目说"该电荷的电势能"时，指的是这个电荷与周围固定电荷组成的系统，所以要明确写出系统。
Worked Example: Assembling three point charges例题：组装三个点电荷

A charge $q_A=+2.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ is at the origin, $q_B=+1.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ is at $(0.30\ \mathrm{m},\,0)$ and $q_C=-3.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ is at $(0,\,0.40\ \mathrm{m})$. Find the electric potential energy of the three-charge system, taking $U_E=0$ for infinite separation, and say what the sign means. Use $k=8.99\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

电荷 $q_A=+2.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ 位于原点，$q_B=+1.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ 位于 $(0.30\ \mathrm{m},\,0)$，$q_C=-3.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ 位于 $(0,\,0.40\ \mathrm{m})$。取无穷远处 $U_E=0$，求三电荷系统的电势能，并说明符号的含义。取 $k=8.99\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$。

Step 1第 1 步
Find the three separations. $A$ to $B$ is $0.30\ \mathrm{m}$ and $A$ to $C$ is $0.40\ \mathrm{m}$, and $B$ to $C$ is the hypotenuse of a 3-4-5 triangle.求三个间距。$A$ 到 $B$ 为 $0.30\ \mathrm{m}$，$A$ 到 $C$ 为 $0.40\ \mathrm{m}$，$B$ 到 $C$ 是 3-4-5 直角三角形的斜边。
$$r_{AB}=0.30\ \mathrm{m},\quad r_{AC}=0.40\ \mathrm{m},\quad r_{BC}=\sqrt{0.30^2+0.40^2}=0.50\ \mathrm{m}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Write one term for each of the three distinct pairs, keeping each sign.为三个不同的电荷对各写一项，保留各自的符号。
$$U_E=k\left(\frac{q_Aq_B}{r_{AB}}+\frac{q_Aq_C}{r_{AC}}+\frac{q_Bq_C}{r_{BC}}\right)$$
Step 3第 3 步
Evaluate each pair separately (in joules).分别计算每一对（单位：焦耳）。
$$U_{AB}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(2.0\times10^{-6})(1.0\times10^{-6})}{0.30}=+0.060\ \mathrm{J},\quad U_{AC}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(2.0\times10^{-6})(-3.0\times10^{-6})}{0.40}=-0.135\ \mathrm{J}$$
Step 4第 4 步
The last pair, then the total.最后一对，再求总和。
$$U_{BC}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(1.0\times10^{-6})(-3.0\times10^{-6})}{0.50}=-0.054\ \mathrm{J},\qquad U_E=0.060-0.135-0.054=-0.129\ \mathrm{J}$$
Step 5第 5 步
Interpret. $U_E=-0.13\ \mathrm{J}$ is negative: the attraction of $C$ to $A$ and $B$ outweighs the repulsion of $A$ and $B$. An external agent would have done $W_{\text{ext}}=-0.13\ \mathrm{J}$ to assemble the system, and it would have to supply $+0.13\ \mathrm{J}$ to separate all three charges to infinity.解读。$U_E=-0.13\ \mathrm{J}$ 为负：$C$ 对 $A$、$B$ 的吸引超过了 $A$、$B$ 之间的排斥。外界组装该系统时做功 $W_{\text{ext}}=-0.13\ \mathrm{J}$，要把三个电荷全部拉到无穷远则必须提供 $+0.13\ \mathrm{J}$。
Why this works为什么可行
Energy is a scalar, so the pair terms add as plain numbers once each charge carries its sign. The geometry only enters through the three distances.能量是标量，只要每个电荷带上自己的符号，各对的能量就是普通数字相加。几何只通过三个距离进入计算。
Worked Example: Four equal charges on a square例题：正方形四角的四个等量电荷

Four identical charges $q=+2.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ sit at the corners of a square of side $a=0.10\ \mathrm{m}$. (a) Find the electric potential energy of the system. (b) Find the work an external agent must do to remove one charge to infinity, leaving the other three fixed.

四个相同的电荷 $q=+2.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ 位于边长 $a=0.10\ \mathrm{m}$ 的正方形四角。(a) 求系统的电势能。(b) 求外界把其中一个电荷移到无穷远（其余三个固定）所需做的功。

Step 1第 1 步
Count the pairs: four sides (separation $a$) and two diagonals (separation $\sqrt2\,a$). That is $4\cdot3/2=6$ pairs.数电荷对：四条边（间距 $a$）和两条对角线（间距 $\sqrt2\,a$），共 $4\cdot3/2=6$ 对。
$$U_E=\frac{kq^2}{a}\left(4+\frac{2}{\sqrt2}\right)=\frac{kq^2}{a}\left(4+\sqrt2\right)$$
Step 2第 2 步
Evaluate the common factor, then the total.先算公共因子，再算总和。
$$\frac{kq^2}{a}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(2.0\times10^{-6})^2}{0.10}=0.36\ \mathrm{J},\qquad U_E=(0.36)(5.414)=1.95\ \mathrm{J}$$
Step 3第 3 步
For (b), the charge removed to infinity takes its interactions with it: two sides and one diagonal. Electric potential and potential difference电势与电势差

Place a small positive test charge $q$ at a point in the field of fixed source charges. The potential energy of the system is proportional to $q$, so the ratio does not depend on $q$: that ratio is the electric potential at the point, $V=U_E/q$.

在固定源电荷的场中某点放一个小的正试探电荷 $q$。系统的势能与 $q$ 成正比，所以比值与 $q$ 无关：这个比值就是该点的电势（electric potential），$V=U_E/q$。

Its unit is the volt, $1\ \mathrm{V}=1\ \mathrm{J/C}$. Potential is a scalar: it has a sign but no direction. Like potential energy it needs a zero; for charges contained in a finite region we take $V=0$ infinitely far away.

单位是伏特，$1\ \mathrm{V}=1\ \mathrm{J/C}$。电势是标量：有正负，没有方向。与势能一样，它需要一个零点；对分布在有限区域内的电荷，取无穷远处 $V=0$。

实际测量的是差值。$A$、$B$ 两点之间的电势差（potential difference）是试探电荷在两点之间移动时，单位电荷电势能的变化：

Potential difference电势差
$$\Delta V=V_B-V_A=\frac{\Delta U_E}{q}=\frac{W_{\text{ext}}}{q}=-\frac{W_{\text{field}}}{q}$$

$W_{\text{ext}}$ is the work by an outside agent moving $q$ slowly from $A$ to $B$. Rearranged, $\Delta U_E=q\,\Delta V$, which is the working tool of Topic 9.3.

$$\frac{4.0}{x}=\frac{2.0}{0.60-x}\ \Rightarrow\ 2.4-4.0x=2.0x\ \Rightarrow\ x=0.40\ \mathrm{m}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Beyond $q_2$ ($x>0.60$), $r_1=x$ and $r_2=x-0.60$. Nothing on the far side of $q_1$ works, because there $r_1$ is the nearer distance but $q_1$ is the larger charge.在 $q_2$ 外侧（$x>0.60$），$r_1=x$，$r_2=x-0.60$。$q_1$ 外侧没有解，因为那里 $r_1$ 是较近的距离，但 $q_1$ 又是较大的电荷。
$$\frac{4.0}{x}=\frac{2.0}{x-0.60}\ \Rightarrow\ 4.0x-2.4=2.0x\ \Rightarrow\ x=1.2\ \mathrm{m}$$
Step 3第 3 步
For $E=0$ the field vectors must be opposite and equal. That happens only outside the pair on the side of the smaller charge, at $x>0.60$, where $q_1$ pushes toward $+x$ and $q_2$ pulls toward $-x$: $\dfrac{4.0}{x^2}=\dfrac{2.0}{(x-0.60)^2}$, so $x=\sqrt2\,(x-0.60)$.$E=0$ 要求两个场矢量等大反向。这只发生在两电荷之外、电量较小的一侧，即 $x>0.60$：$q_1$ 的场沿 $+x$，$q_2$ 的场沿 $-x$：$\dfrac{4.0}{x^2}=\dfrac{2.0}{(x-0.60)^2}$，所以 $x=\sqrt2\,(x-0.60)$。
$$x=\frac{0.60\sqrt2}{\sqrt2-1}=2.05\ \mathrm{m}$$
Step 4第 4 步
Evaluate $V$ at that point by scalar superposition.用标量叠加求该点的 $V$。
$$V=k\left(\frac{4.0\times10^{-9}}{2.05}+\frac{-2.0\times10^{-9}}{1.45}\right)=(8.99\times10^{9})(5.7\times10^{-10})=5.1\ \mathrm{V}$$
Step 5第 5 步
The two answers differ: $V=0$ at $0.40\ \mathrm{m}$ and $1.2\ \mathrm{m}$, where $E\neq0$ (at $1.2\ \mathrm{m}$ it is $25\ \mathrm{N/C}$ toward $-x$), and $E=0$ at $2.05\ \mathrm{m}$, where $V=5.1\ \mathrm{V}$.两个答案不同：$V=0$ 在 $0.40\ \mathrm{m}$ 与 $1.2\ \mathrm{m}$ 处，那里 $E\neq0$（在 $1.2\ \mathrm{m}$ 处为 $25\ \mathrm{N/C}$，沿 $-x$）；$E=0$ 在 $2.05\ \mathrm{m}$ 处，那里 $V=5.1\ \mathrm{V}$。
Why this works为什么可行
$V$ adds as signed scalars and $E$ adds as vectors, so the two conditions are different equations with different solutions.$V$ 按带符号标量相加，$E$ 按矢量相加，所以两个条件是不同的方程，解也不同。

Potential difference from chemical processes化学过程产生的电势差

Not every potential difference comes from fixed charges you can place with your hand. In a battery, chemical reactions at the two electrodes separate positive and negative charge: charge moves against the electric field inside the cell, so the terminals end up at different potentials.

并非所有电势差都来自可以用手放置的固定电荷。在电池中，两个电极处的化学反应把正负电荷分开：电荷在电池内部逆着电场移动，于是两个端子处于不同的电势。

The reactions do work on each unit of charge that crosses the cell, and that work per unit charge is the potential difference of the cell (its emf).

化学反应对每个通过电池的单位电荷做功，这个单位电荷的功就是电池的电势差（电动势，emf）。

An ideal $9.0\ \mathrm{V}$ battery gives $9.0\ \mathrm{J}$ to every coulomb that goes from the negative to the positive terminal inside it, so moving $0.50\ \mathrm{C}$ through the cell involves $4.5\ \mathrm{J}$. Unit 11 uses this idea for circuits.

理想的 $9.0\ \mathrm{V}$ 电池在内部把每库仑电荷从负极送到正极时提供 $9.0\ \mathrm{J}$，因此使 $0.50\ \mathrm{C}$ 通过电池涉及 $4.5\ \mathrm{J}$。第 11 单元在电路中用到这个概念。

Conductors in electrical contact相互接触的导体

When two conductors are in electrical contact, electrons redistribute between them until the surfaces of the conductors are at the same electric potential. After that no charge flows between them, because there is no potential difference to drive it. If a charged metal sphere touches a larger neutral one, for example, charge is shared until both surfaces are at the same potential; the two spheres do not end up with equal charge, only with equal potential.

两个导体相互接触时，电子在它们之间重新分布，直到导体的表面处于相同的电势。此后它们之间不再有电荷流动，因为没有电势差来驱动。例如，带电的金属球接触一个更大的不带电金属球时，电荷在两者之间分配，直到两个表面电势相同；两球最终电荷并不相等，相等的只是电势。

Potential difference from the field由电场求电势差

Moving a test charge $q$ through a small displacement $d\vec\ell$, the electric force does work $dW_{\text{field}}=q\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell$. Since $dV=-dW_{\text{field}}/q$,

试探电荷 $q$ 发生一小段位移 $d\vec\ell$ 时，电场力做功 $dW_{\text{field}}=q\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell$。由于 $dV=-dW_{\text{field}}/q$，

Potential difference as a line integral of the field电势差作为电场的线积分
$$V_B-V_A=-\int_A^B\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell$$

Moving with the field ($\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell>0$) lowers $V$; moving against it raises $V$. The result is the same for every path from $A$ to $B$ because the electrostatic force is conservative.

顺着电场移动（$\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell>0$）使 $V$ 降低；逆着电场移动使 $V$ 升高。由于静电力是保守力，从 $A$ 到 $B$ 的任何路径结果都相同。

Path independence lets you pick the easiest route: a radial segment and an arc concentric with the source, for example, because the dot product vanishes along the arc. For a uniform field the integral is a product: $\Delta V=-\vec E\cdot\Delta\vec r=-E\,\Delta s\cos\theta$. Moving a distance $d$ along the field lowers the potential by $Ed$, which gives the magnitude $E=|\Delta V|/d$ between large parallel plates.

路径无关使你可以选最容易的路线：例如一段径向线段加上一段与源电荷同心的圆弧，因为沿圆弧点积为零。对匀强电场，积分变成乘积：$\Delta V=-\vec E\cdot\Delta\vec r=-E\,\Delta s\cos\theta$。沿电场方向移动距离 $d$，电势降低 $Ed$，这就给出两块大平行板之间的场强大小 $E=|\Delta V|/d$。

Potential difference and the electric field电势差与电场

Move a positive test charge $q$ a distance $d$ in the direction of a uniform electric field $E$. The field does positive work $qEd$, so the potential energy falls by $qEd$ and the potential falls by $Ed$. Moving against the field raises the potential by the same amount. Turning this around, the average electric field between two points equals the potential difference between them divided by the distance between them:

让正试探电荷 $q$ 沿匀强电场 $E$ 的方向移动距离 $d$。电场做正功 $qEd$，所以势能减小 $qEd$，电势降低 $Ed$。逆着电场移动则电势升高同样的量。反过来说，两点之间的平均电场等于它们之间的电势差除以它们之间的距离：

Average electric field from a potential difference由电势差求平均电场
$$E=\frac{|\Delta V|}{d}$$

$E$ is the average magnitude of the electric field between the two points, $|\Delta V|$ is the magnitude of the potential difference between them, and $d$ is the distance between them. The field points from the higher-potential point toward the lower-potential point, and its units are $\mathrm{V/m}=\mathrm{N/C}$. Between two large parallel plates the field is uniform, so $E$ is the field everywhere between them.

$E$ 是两点之间电场大小的平均值，$|\Delta V|$ 是它们之间电势差的大小，$d$ 是它们之间的距离。电场由电势较高的点指向电势较低的点，单位为 $\mathrm{V/m}=\mathrm{N/C}$。两块大平行板之间的电场是匀强的，所以 $E$ 就是两板之间各处的场强。

Worked Example: Work, potential difference and field例题：功、电势差与电场

An external agent moves a $+2.0\ \mathrm{nC}$ test charge slowly from point $A$ to point $B$ and does $6.0\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$ of work on it. (a) Find the potential difference $V_B-V_A$. (b) The points are $0.030\ \mathrm{m}$ apart along a uniform field. Find the field magnitude and say which point is at the higher potential.

外界把 $+2.0\ \mathrm{nC}$ 的试探电荷缓慢地从 $A$ 点移到 $B$ 点，对它做功 $6.0\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$。(a) 求电势差 $V_B-V_A$。(b) 两点沿匀强电场方向相距 $0.030\ \mathrm{m}$。求场强大小，并说明哪一点电势较高。

Step 1第 1 步
Slow motion means the kinetic energy does not change, so the work done by the agent equals the change in potential energy, $\Delta U=6.0\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$. Divide by the charge.缓慢移动意味着动能不变，所以外界做的功等于势能的变化，$\Delta U=6.0\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$。再除以电荷量。
$$\Delta V=\frac{\Delta U}{q}=\frac{6.0\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}}{2.0\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{C}}=300\ \mathrm{V}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Use the average field relation with the distance between the points.用平均电场关系式，代入两点间的距离。
$$E=\frac{|\Delta V|}{d}=\frac{300\ \mathrm{V}}{0.030\ \mathrm{m}}=1.00\times10^{4}\ \mathrm{V/m}$$
Step 3第 3 步
$V_B-V_A$ is positive, so $B$ is at the higher potential. The field points toward decreasing potential, from $B$ toward $A$.$V_B-V_A$ 为正，所以 $B$ 点电势较高。电场指向电势降低的方向，即由 $B$ 指向 $A$。
Why this works为什么可行
A positive charge gains potential energy when it is moved to higher potential, so the agent had to do positive work against the field. The units check: $\mathrm{J/C}=\mathrm{V}$ and $\mathrm{V/m}$ is a field unit.正电荷移向较高电势时势能增加，所以外界必须逆着电场做正功。单位检验：$\mathrm{J/C}=\mathrm{V}$，$\mathrm{V/m}$ 是场强单位。
Worked Example: Parallel plates on a battery例题：接在电池上的平行板

Two large parallel plates $5.0\ \mathrm{mm}$ apart are connected to a $12\ \mathrm{V}$ battery. (a) Find the field between them. (b) Taking the negative plate as $V=0$, find the potential at a point $2.0\ \mathrm{mm}$ from the negative plate. (c) Find the change in electric potential energy when a proton moves from the positive plate to the negative plate.

两块相距 $5.0\ \mathrm{mm}$ 的大平行板接在 $12\ \mathrm{V}$ 电池上。(a) 求两板间的电场。(b) 取负极板为 $V=0$，求距负极板 $2.0\ \mathrm{mm}$ 处的电势。(c) 求质子从正极板移到负极板时电势能的变化。

Step 1第 1 步
The field between the plates is uniform and points from the positive to the negative plate, with magnitude $|\Delta V|/d$.两板间的电场是匀强的，方向由正极板指向负极板，大小为 $|\Delta V|/d$。
$$E=\frac{12\ \mathrm{V}}{5.0\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m}}=2.4\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Potential changes linearly with distance along the field. Moving $2.0\ \mathrm{mm}$ from the negative plate toward the positive plate is moving against the field, so $V$ rises.沿电场方向电势随距离线性变化。从负极板朝正极板移动 $2.0\ \mathrm{mm}$ 是逆着电场移动，所以 $V$ 升高。
$$V=0+E\,(2.0\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m})=4.8\ \mathrm{V}$$
Step 3第 3 步
The proton goes from $+12\ \mathrm{V}$ to $0\ \mathrm{V}$, so $\Delta V=-12\ \mathrm{V}$.质子从 $+12\ \mathrm{V}$ 到 $0\ \mathrm{V}$，所以 $\Delta V=-12\ \mathrm{V}$。
$$\Delta U_E=q\,\Delta V=(1.60\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C})(-12\ \mathrm{V})=-1.9\times10^{-18}\ \mathrm{J}=-12\ \mathrm{eV}$$
Why this works为什么可行
A uniform field makes $V$ a linear function of position, so ratios of distances give ratios of potential. The sign of $\Delta U_E$ is negative because a positive charge moving with the field loses potential energy.匀强电场使 $V$ 成为位置的线性函数，所以距离之比就是电势之比。$\Delta U_E$ 为负，因为正电荷顺着电场移动会失去势能。

Potential of continuous charge distributions连续电荷分布的电势

For a continuous distribution, treat each small element $dq$ as a point charge, then sum by integration:

对连续分布，把每个小电荷元 $dq$ 当作点电荷，再用积分求和：

Potential of a continuous distribution连续分布的电势
$$V=\int\frac{k\,dq}{r},\qquad dq=\lambda\,d\ell\ \ \text{(line)}$$

$r$ is the distance from the element $dq$ to the field point. Because $V$ is a scalar, there are no vector components to resolve and no cancellation to worry about; this is simpler than the field integral.

$r$ 是电荷元 $dq$ 到场点的距离。因为 $V$ 是标量，不需要分解分量，也不必担心矢量抵消；这比场强积分简单。

Method. (1) Sketch the distribution and the field point. (2) Choose a coordinate and write $dq=\lambda\,dx$ (or $\lambda R\,d\theta$ on an arc). (3) Write $r$ for that element, in terms of the same coordinate. (4) Set limits and integrate. The calculus is expected for four distributions only; the derivations follow.

方法。(1) 画出分布和场点。(2) 选坐标并写出 $dq=\lambda\,dx$（圆弧上为 $\lambda R\,d\theta$）。(3) 用同一个坐标写出该电荷元的 $r$。(4) 定出积分限并积分。微积分只要求用于四类分布；推导如下。

Ring, on the axis. A thin ring of radius $a$ carries total charge $Q$ spread uniformly. For a point on the axis a distance $x$ from the center, every element $dq$ is the same distance $r=\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$ away, so $r$ comes out of the integral:

圆环，轴线上一点。半径为 $a$ 的细圆环带总电荷 $Q$，均匀分布。对轴线上距圆心 $x$ 的点，每个电荷元 $dq$ 到场点的距离都是 $r=\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$，所以 $r$ 可以提到积分号外：

$$V=\int\frac{k\,dq}{\sqrt{a^2+x^2}}=\frac{k}{\sqrt{a^2+x^2}}\int dq=\frac{kQ}{\sqrt{a^2+x^2}}$$

Semicircular arc, at its center. A uniformly charged arc of radius $R$ has $dq=\lambda R\,d\theta$. Every element is exactly $R$ from the center, so for an arc of any angle with total charge $Q$ the potential at the center is $V=\int k\,dq/R=kQ/R$. The same result holds for part of a semicircle (a quarter circle, say): use the charge actually on the arc.

半圆弧，圆心处。半径为 $R$ 的均匀带电圆弧有 $dq=\lambda R\,d\theta$。每个电荷元到圆心的距离恰好都是 $R$，所以对总电荷为 $Q$ 的任意角度圆弧，圆心处的电势为 $V=\int k\,dq/R=kQ/R$。半圆弧的一部分（例如四分之一圆）结果相同：代入圆弧上实际所带的电荷。

Finite line, on the perpendicular bisector. A rod of length $L$ lies along the $x$ axis from $-L/2$ to $L/2$ with uniform $\lambda=Q/L$. For a point at height $y$ above the middle, an element at $x$ is $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ away:

有限长带电线，中垂线上一点。长为 $L$ 的细棒沿 $x$ 轴从 $-L/2$ 到 $L/2$，均匀线密度 $\lambda=Q/L$。对中点正上方高度 $y$ 处的点，位于 $x$ 的电荷元到场点的距离为 $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$：

$$V=k\lambda\int_{-L/2}^{L/2}\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}=k\lambda\Big[\ln\!\big(x+\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\big)\Big]_{-L/2}^{L/2}=2k\lambda\ln\!\left(\frac{L/2+\sqrt{L^2/4+y^2}}{y}\right)$$

(The last step uses $\big(\tfrac L2+s\big)\big(-\tfrac L2+s\big)=s^2-\tfrac{L^2}{4}=y^2$ with $s=\sqrt{L^2/4+y^2}$.) Finite line, collinear point. A rod along $x$ from $0$ to $L$, and a point on the axis a distance $d$ beyond its end: an element at $x$ is $r=L+d-x$ away, so

（最后一步用到 $\big(\tfrac L2+s\big)\big(-\tfrac L2+s\big)=s^2-\tfrac{L^2}{4}=y^2$，其中 $s=\sqrt{L^2/4+y^2}$。）有限长带电线，共线点。棒沿 $x$ 轴从 $0$ 到 $L$，场点在轴上、距棒端 $d$ 处：位于 $x$ 的电荷元到场点的距离为 $r=L+d-x$，所以

$$V=k\lambda\int_0^L\frac{dx}{L+d-x}=k\lambda\Big[-\ln(L+d-x)\Big]_0^L=k\lambda\ln\!\left(\frac{L+d}{d}\right)$$

Long wire or cylinder, at a distance from the axis. The charge is infinite, so $V$ cannot be set to zero at infinity (the integral diverges logarithmically). Integrate the field instead, from a chosen reference radius. From Unit 8, $E=\lambda/(2\pi\epsilon_0 r)=2k\lambda/r$ radially outward, so between radii $r_1$ and $r_2$:

长直导线或圆柱，到轴线某距离处。电荷无限多，所以 $V$ 不能在无穷远处取零（积分对数发散）。改为从选定的参考半径出发对场强积分。由第 8 单元，$E=\lambda/(2\pi\epsilon_0 r)=2k\lambda/r$，沿径向向外，所以在半径 $r_1$ 与 $r_2$ 之间：

$$V(r_2)-V(r_1)=-\int_{r_1}^{r_2}\frac{2k\lambda}{r}\,dr=-2k\lambda\ln\frac{r_2}{r_1}$$

Apply $V=kQ/r$ (the whole charge is at one distance), then at the center $x=0$ where $r=a$.应用 $V=kQ/r$（全部电荷处于同一距离），再取圆心 $x=0$，此时 $r=a$。

When all the charge is the same distance from the field point, the integral collapses to $kQ/r$ with no calculus left, and the potential is large near the charge and decreases with distance for positive charge.当全部电荷到场点的距离相同时，积分化为 $kQ/r$，不再需要微积分；正电荷附近电势高，并随距离增大而降低。

Worked Example: Potential difference near a long charged wire例题：长带电导线附近的电势差

A long straight wire carries uniform $\lambda=+3.0\ \mathrm{nC/m}$. Find the potential difference $V(0.50\ \mathrm{m})-V(0.10\ \mathrm{m})$ and explain why $V=0$ cannot be taken at infinity. Use $k=8.99\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

一根长直导线均匀带电，$\lambda=+3.0\ \mathrm{nC/m}$。求电势差 $V(0.50\ \mathrm{m})-V(0.10\ \mathrm{m})$，并解释为什么不能取无穷远处 $V=0$。取 $k=8.99\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$。

Step 1第 1 步
The field of a long line is radial with $E=2k\lambda/r$. Integrate it from $r_1=0.10\ \mathrm{m}$ to $r_2=0.50\ \mathrm{m}$, moving outward along the field.长直线电荷的场沿径向，$E=2k\lambda/r$。从 $r_1=0.10\ \mathrm{m}$ 向外沿电场积分到 $r_2=0.50\ \mathrm{m}$。
$$V(r_2)-V(r_1)=-\int_{0.10}^{0.50}\frac{2k\lambda}{r}\,dr=-2k\lambda\ln\frac{0.50}{0.10}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Substitute the numbers.代入数值。
$$2k\lambda=2(8.99\times10^{9})(3.0\times10^{-9})=54\ \mathrm{V},\qquad \Delta V=-54\ln5=-87\ \mathrm{V}$$
Step 3第 3 步
The potential is lower farther out, as expected for moving along the field of a positive line. If we tried $r_2\to\infty$ the logarithm would grow without bound, so no finite $V$ exists relative to infinity; the line itself extends to infinity. We choose any convenient radius $r_0$ with $V(r_0)=0$ and write $V(r)=-2k\lambda\ln(r/r_0)$.越往外电势越低，这与沿正线电荷的电场方向移动的预期一致。若令 $r_2\to\infty$，对数无界增长，所以相对于无穷远不存在有限的 $V$；因为线电荷本身一直延伸到无穷远。我们选任意方便的半径 $r_0$ 作为 $V(r_0)=0$，并写出 $V(r)=-2k\lambda\ln(r/r_0)$。
Why this works为什么可行
Only the difference between two radii is physical here. Doubling the distance always changes $V$ by the same amount, $2k\lambda\ln2$, which is the signature of a logarithmic potential.这里只有两个半径之间的差值才有物理意义。距离每增大一倍，$V$ 的变化量总是相同的，为 $2k\lambda\ln2$，这正是对数型电势的特征。

Highly symmetric arrangements高度对称的排布

Scalar superposition works for any number of point charges, but AP Physics 2 asks you to carry it out for four or fewer, or for more only when high symmetry makes the sum easy. The usual symmetric case is a set of equal charges that are all the same distance $r$ from the point of interest, such as equal charges spaced evenly around a circle with the point at its center. Every term in the sum is then the same, so $V=N\frac{kq}{r}$, where $N$ is the number of charges.

标量叠加对任意个点电荷都成立，但 AP 物理 2 只要求对四个或更少的电荷进行计算，更多的电荷只在高度对称、求和变得简单时才会出现。常见的对称情形是一组等量电荷到所求点的距离 $r$ 都相同，例如等量电荷沿圆周均匀排列、所求点在圆心。此时求和中每一项都相同，所以 $V=N\frac{kq}{r}$，其中 $N$ 是电荷的个数。

Worked Example: Six equal charges around a hexagon例题：正六边形上的六个等量电荷

Six identical point charges, each $q=+2.0\ \mathrm{nC}$, sit at the corners of a regular hexagon of side $a=0.20\ \mathrm{m}$. Find the electric potential at the center of the hexagon, taking $V=0$ at infinity, and say what the symmetry tells you about the electric field there. Use $k=8.99\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

六个相同的点电荷，每个 $q=+2.0\ \mathrm{nC}$，位于边长 $a=0.20\ \mathrm{m}$ 的正六边形的六个顶点。取无穷远处 $V=0$，求六边形中心处的电势，并说明对称性对该处的电场有什么启示。取 $k=8.99\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$。

Step 1第 1 步
The corners of a regular hexagon are all the same distance from the center, and that distance equals the side length.正六边形的顶点到中心的距离全都相同，并且等于边长。
$$r=a=0.20\ \mathrm{m}$$
Step 2第 2 步
All six charges are equal and equally far away, so the six terms of the scalar sum are identical.六个电荷相等且距离相同，所以标量求和中的六项完全相同。
$$V=6\,\frac{kq}{r}=6\,\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(2.0\times10^{-9})}{0.20}=6\,(89.9\ \mathrm{V})=539\ \mathrm{V}$$
Step 3第 3 步
The potential is positive because every charge is positive. By symmetry the six field vectors at the center cancel in opposite pairs, so $E=0$ there even though $V=539\ \mathrm{V}$.每个电荷都为正，所以电势为正。由对称性，中心处的六个场矢量两两反向抵消，所以那里 $E=0$，尽管 $V=539\ \mathrm{V}$。
Why this works为什么可行
Potentials from charges of the same sign never cancel because they add as signed numbers, while the fields add as vectors and do cancel by symmetry.同号电荷产生的电势按带符号的数相加，不会抵消；而电场按矢量相加，由于对称性会抵消。

Field from potential由电势求电场

Along any small step $dx$, $dV=-\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell=-E_x\,dx$. So the field component in a direction is the negative of the rate of change of potential in that direction:

沿任意小位移 $dx$，$dV=-\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell=-E_x\,dx$。所以某方向的电场分量等于电势在该方向变化率的负值：

Field component from the potential由电势求电场分量
$$E_x=-\frac{dV}{dx}$$

The field points in the direction of decreasing potential. Where the $V(x)$ graph is flat ($dV/dx=0$), the component $E_x$ is zero, even if $V$ itself is not zero.

电场指向电势降低的方向。在 $V(x)$ 图像平坦处（$dV/dx=0$），分量 $E_x$ 为零，即使 $V$ 本身不为零。

The same rule applies to $E_y$ and $E_z$ with the rate of change in those directions. The field has no component along a direction in which $V$ does not change.

同样的规则适用于 $E_y$、$E_z$，只需取电势在相应方向上的变化率。在 $V$ 不变的方向上，电场没有分量。

Worked Example: The field of a ring from its potential例题：由电势求圆环的电场

Use $V(x)=kQ/\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$ for a ring on its axis to find $E_x(x)$, then evaluate it for $Q=+6.0\ \mathrm{nC}$, $a=0.12\ \mathrm{m}$, $x=0.16\ \mathrm{m}$.

利用圆环轴线上的 $V(x)=kQ/\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$ 求 $E_x(x)$，再对 $Q=+6.0\ \mathrm{nC}$、$a=0.12\ \mathrm{m}$、$x=0.16\ \mathrm{m}$ 求值。

Step 1第 1 步
Differentiate $V=kQ(a^2+x^2)^{-1/2}$ with the chain rule and apply $E_x=-dV/dx$.用链式法则对 $V=kQ(a^2+x^2)^{-1/2}$ 求导，并应用 $E_x=-dV/dx$。
$$E_x=-kQ\left(-\tfrac12\right)(a^2+x^2)^{-3/2}(2x)=\frac{kQx}{(a^2+x^2)^{3/2}}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Substitute. Here $a^2+x^2=(0.20)^2$, so $(a^2+x^2)^{3/2}=(0.20)^3=8.0\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m^3}$ and $kQ=(8.99\times10^{9})(6.0\times10^{-9})=54\ \mathrm{V\,m}$.代入数值。此处 $a^2+x^2=(0.20)^2$，所以 $(a^2+x^2)^{3/2}=(0.20)^3=8.0\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m^3}$，$kQ=(8.99\times10^{9})(6.0\times10^{-9})=54\ \mathrm{V\,m}$。
$$E_x=\frac{(54)(0.16)}{8.0\times10^{-3}}=1.1\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$$
Step 3第 3 步
The field points along $+x$ (away from the positive ring), and $E_x=0$ at $x=0$, where $V(x)$ has its maximum and zero slope. This is the same field that Topic 8.4 finds by integrating $dE$ with components.电场沿 $+x$（背离带正电的圆环），在 $x=0$ 处 $E_x=0$，该处 $V(x)$ 取最大值，斜率为零。这与专题 8.4 通过对 $dE$ 分解分量积分得到的场强相同。
Why this works为什么可行
Differentiating a scalar function is far easier than integrating vector components, so when $V(x)$ is known, take the negative slope to get $E_x$.对标量函数求导比对矢量分量积分容易得多，所以已知 $V(x)$ 时，取其斜率的负值就得到 $E_x$。

Graph translation: V(x) and E(x)图像转换：V(x) 与 E(x)

The two relations are inverses of each other. From a $V(x)$ graph, $E_x$ at any $x$ is the negative of the slope.

这两个关系互为逆运算。由 $V(x)$ 图像，任意 $x$ 处的 $E_x$ 是斜率的负值。

From an $E_x(x)$ graph, the change in potential between two positions is the negative of the signed area under the graph: $V(x_2)-V(x_1)=-\int_{x_1}^{x_2}E_x\,dx$. A flat segment of $V$ is a zero-field region; a steep segment is a strong field; a positive slope means the field points in $-x$.

由 $E_x(x)$ 图像，两位置之间的电势变化是图线下带符号面积的负值：$V(x_2)-V(x_1)=-\int_{x_1}^{x_2}E_x\,dx$。$V$ 的平坦段是零场区域；陡峭段是强场；斜率为正表示电场指向 $-x$。

Worked Example: Build V(x) from a piecewise field例题：由分段电场构建 V(x)

Along the $x$ axis the field is $E_x=+200\ \mathrm{V/m}$ for $0<x<0.20\ \mathrm{m}$, $E_x=0$ for $0.20<x<0.50\ \mathrm{m}$, and $E_x=-100\ \mathrm{V/m}$ for $0.50<x<0.90\ \mathrm{m}$. Given $V(0)=0$, find $V$ at $x=0.20$, $0.50$ and $0.90\ \mathrm{m}$ and describe the graph of $V(x)$.

Step 1第 1 步
Apply $\Delta V=-\int E_x\,dx$ one segment at a time (the field is constant in each, so each integral is a rectangle).逐段应用 $\Delta V=-\int E_x\,dx$（每段内电场为常量，所以每个积分都是一个矩形）。
$$V(0.20)=0-(200)(0.20)=-40\ \mathrm{V}$$
Step 2第 2 步
The middle segment has no field, so the potential does not change. The last segment has a negative field, so $V$ rises.中间一段没有电场，电势不变。最后一段电场为负，所以 $V$ 升高。
$$V(0.50)=-40-(0)(0.30)=-40\ \mathrm{V},\qquad V(0.90)=-40-(-100)(0.40)=0\ \mathrm{V}$$
Step 3第 3 步

Equipotential lines and field maps等势线与电场图

An equipotential line (isoline of potential) joins points at the same $V$. Four facts link a map of equipotentials to the field:

等势线（电势的等值线）连接 $V$ 相同的点。等势线图与电场之间有四条联系：

  • The field is perpendicular to the equipotential at every point. Moving along the line, $dV=0=-\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell$, so $\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell=0$.
  • Equivalently, the field has no component along an isoline, and the field does no work on a charge moved along one.
  • The field points toward decreasing potential, from higher-valued to lower-valued lines.
  • Where lines are closer, $V$ changes faster with distance, so the field is stronger: $|E|\approx|\Delta V|/\Delta s$ with $\Delta s$ the perpendicular spacing between lines.
  • 电场在每一点都垂直于等势线。沿线移动时 $dV=0=-\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell$，所以 $\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell=0$。
  • 换言之，电场沿等势线没有分量，电荷沿等势线移动时电场不做功。
  • 电场指向电势降低的方向，从数值较高的线指向数值较低的线。
  • 等势线越密处，$V$ 随距离变化越快，电场越强：$|E|\approx|\Delta V|/\Delta s$，其中 $\Delta s$ 为等势线之间的垂直间距。
  • The electric field is perpendicular to the equipotential line at every point, because the potential does not change along the line.
  • Equivalently, the field has no component along an isoline, and the field does no work on a charge moved along one, since $\Delta U_E=q\Delta V=0$ when $V$ does not change.
  • The field points toward decreasing potential, from higher-valued to lower-valued lines.
  • Where lines are closer, $V$ changes faster with distance, so the field is stronger: $E\approx\frac{|\Delta V|}{\Delta s}$ with $\Delta s$ the perpendicular spacing between lines.
  • 电场在每一点都垂直于等势线，因为沿线电势不变。
  • 换言之，电场沿等势线没有分量；电荷沿等势线移动时电场不做功，因为 $V$ 不变时 $\Delta U_E=q\Delta V=0$。
  • 电场指向电势降低的方向，从数值较高的线指向数值较低的线。
  • 等势线越密处，$V$ 随距离变化越快，电场越强：$E\approx\frac{|\Delta V|}{\Delta s}$，其中 $\Delta s$ 为等势线之间的垂直间距。

Maps work in both directions: from a field-vector map, draw lines that cross every vector at right angles; from an isoline map, draw vectors perpendicular to the lines, pointing downhill in $V$.

这种图可以双向使用：由电场矢量图，画出与每个矢量垂直的线；由等势线图，画出垂直于线、指向 $V$ 降低方向的矢量。

Near a point charge the lines are concentric circles (crowded close to the charge); between large parallel plates they are evenly spaced parallel lines. Such maps predict the motion of charged objects: a positive charge released from rest accelerates toward lower $V$; a negative charge toward higher $V$.

点电荷附近的等势线是同心圆（靠近电荷处更密）；大平行板之间则是等间距的平行线。这样的图可以预测带电物体的运动：静止释放的正电荷向 $V$ 较低处加速；负电荷向 $V$ 较高处加速。

Worked Example: Reading an equipotential map例题：读等势线图

In a region the equipotentials are straight lines parallel to the $y$ axis, labelled $40\ \mathrm{V}$ at $x=2.0\ \mathrm{cm}$, $30\ \mathrm{V}$ at $x=4.0\ \mathrm{cm}$ and $20\ \mathrm{V}$ at $x=6.0\ \mathrm{cm}$. 某区域内等势线是平行于 $y$ 轴的直线，在 $x=2.0\ \mathrm{cm}$ 处标 $40\ \mathrm{V}$，$x=4.0\ \mathrm{cm}$ 处标 $30\ \mathrm{V}$，$x=6.0\ \mathrm{cm}$ 处标 $20\ \mathrm{V}$。(a) 求电场的方向与大小。(b) 求 $+2.0\ \mathrm{nC}$ 的电荷从 $(2.0\ \mathrm{cm},\,1.0\ \mathrm{cm})$ 移到 $(6.0\ \mathrm{cm},\,8.0\ \mathrm{cm})$ 时的 $\Delta U_E$。

Step 1第 1 步
The field is perpendicular to the vertical lines, so it is along the $x$ axis, and it points toward lower $V$: that is $+x$. Its magnitude comes from the change in $V$ per perpendicular distance between lines.电场垂直于这些竖直线，所以沿 $x$ 轴，并指向较低的 $V$，即 $+x$。大小由相邻等势线之间每单位垂直距离上 $V$ 的变化得出。
$$|E|=\frac{|\Delta V|}{\Delta s}=\frac{10\ \mathrm{V}}{0.020\ \mathrm{m}}=5.0\times10^{2}\ \mathrm{V/m}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Only the $x$ coordinate matters because the potential depends on $x$ alone: $V$ goes from $40\ \mathrm{V}$ to $20\ \mathrm{V}$. The $y$ displacement is along an equipotential and contributes nothing.只有 $x$ 坐标起作用，因为电势只取决于 $x$：$V$ 从 $40\ \mathrm{V}$ 变为 $20\ \mathrm{V}$。$y$ 方向的位移沿等势线，不产生贡献。
$$\Delta U_E=q\,\Delta V=(2.0\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{C})(20-40\ \mathrm{V})=-4.0\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{J}$$
Why this works为什么可行
Equipotentials turn a two-dimensional picture into a one-dimensional calculation: only the change of $V$ across the lines counts, not the sideways travel along them.等势线把二维图像变成一维计算：只有跨越等势线时 $V$ 的变化才起作用，沿线的横向移动不起作用。

$$\Delta U_E=q\Delta V=(-1.60\times10^{-19})(-30)=+4.8\times10^{-18}\ \mathrm{J}$$
Step 3第 3 步
It stops where all of its kinetic energy has become potential energy, so $\Delta K=0-K_A=-q\Delta V$. Solve for the potential difference from $A$ to the stopping point.它在全部动能都变成势能处停止，所以 $\Delta K=0-K_A=-q\Delta V$。解出从 $A$ 到停止点的电势差。
$$\Delta V=\frac{K_A}{q}=\frac{4.10\times10^{-18}\ \mathrm{J}}{-1.60\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C}}=-25.6\ \mathrm{V}$$
Step 4第 4 步
The electron stops where $V=-25.6\ \mathrm{V}$, before it reaches $B$ at $-30\ \mathrm{V}$. Then the force on it, which points toward higher potential for a negative charge, sends it back toward $A$.电子在 $V=-25.6\ \mathrm{V}$ 处停下，还没有到达 $-30\ \mathrm{V}$ 的 $B$ 点。随后它受的力（负电荷所受的力指向较高电势）把它送回 $A$ 一侧。
Why this works为什么可行
A negative charge loses kinetic energy as it moves toward lower potential. The stopping point is where its kinetic energy equals the potential energy it has gained, $|q|\,|\Delta V|$.负电荷朝较低电势运动时动能减小。停止点就是它的动能等于所增加的势能 $|q|\,|\Delta V|$ 的位置。

Two interacting objects: closest approach and release两个相互作用的物体：最近距离与释放

When the second charge is not fixed, two conservation laws work together. Momentum conservation (Unit 4) relates the speeds: if the system starts at rest and only the pair interacts, $m_1v_1=m_2v_2$ in opposite directions.

当第二个电荷不固定时，两个守恒定律要一起用。动量守恒（第 4 单元）联系两个速率：如果系统从静止开始、只有这一对物体相互作用，则 $m_1v_1=m_2v_2$，方向相反。

Energy conservation then converts the pair energy $kq_1q_2/r$ into the sum of the two kinetic energies. For a head-on approach, the closest separation is where the relative speed is zero and all of the initial relative kinetic energy has become pair energy.

然后能量守恒把电荷对的能量 $kq_1q_2/r$ 转化为两个动能之和。对于正碰接近，最近距离出现在相对速度为零的时刻，这时全部初始相对动能都变成了电荷对的势能。

Closest approach to a fixed charge朝固定电荷运动的最近距离

When the source charge is fixed, or so heavy that it hardly moves, the system is the moving charge and the source. A charge moving toward a like source slows down as the pair energy $\frac{kq_1q_2}{r}$ grows. At the closest distance its speed is momentarily zero, so all of its initial kinetic energy has become potential energy of the pair.

当源电荷固定、或重得几乎不动时，系统就是运动的电荷与源电荷。电荷朝同号源电荷运动时，随着电荷对的能量 $\frac{kq_1q_2}{r}$ 增大而减速。在最近距离处它的速度瞬时为零，所以全部初始动能都变成了这一对电荷的势能。

Worked Example: Distance of closest approach例题：最近距离

An alpha particle ($q=+2e$) with kinetic energy $5.0\ \mathrm{MeV}$, far from a gold nucleus ($q=+79e$), is fired straight at it. Treat the nucleus as fixed (it is far heavier) and find the closest distance. Use $k=8.99\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$ and $e=1.60\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C}$.

动能为 $5.0\ \mathrm{MeV}$ 的 α 粒子（$q=+2e$）从远处正对金原子核（$q=+79e$）射去。把原子核视为固定（它重得多），求最近距离。取 $k=8.99\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$，$e=1.60\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm{C}$。

Step 1第 1 步
System: the alpha particle and the nucleus. Far away, $U_E=0$ and $K=5.0\ \mathrm{MeV}$; at closest approach the alpha stops momentarily, so $K=0$. Convert the energy.系统：α 粒子与原子核。远处 $U_E=0$，$K=5.0\ \mathrm{MeV}$；在最近点 α 粒子瞬时停止，所以 $K=0$。先换算能量。
$$K_i=(5.0\times10^{6})(1.60\times10^{-19})\ \mathrm{J}=8.0\times10^{-13}\ \mathrm{J}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Conservation of energy: $K_i+0=0+U_E(r_{\min})$.能量守恒：$K_i+0=0+U_E(r_{\min})$。
$$K_i=\frac{k(2e)(79e)}{r_{\min}}\ \Rightarrow\ r_{\min}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(2)(79)(1.60\times10^{-19})^2}{8.0\times10^{-13}}$$
Step 3第 3 步
Evaluate.计算。
$$r_{\min}=4.5\times10^{-14}\ \mathrm{m}$$
Why this works为什么可行
The result scales as $r_{\min}\propto1/K_i$: doubling the kinetic energy halves the distance of closest approach, and doubling the speed (four times the energy) makes it one quarter. This is how scattering experiments estimate nuclear sizes.结果满足 $r_{\min}\propto1/K_i$：动能加倍使最近距离减半；速度加倍（能量变为四倍）使最近距离变为四分之一。散射实验正是这样估计原子核大小的。
Worked Example: Two charged spheres released together例题：两个带电小球同时释放

Two small spheres each carry $+3.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$. Their masses are $m_1=2.0\ \mathrm{g}$ and $m_2=1.0\ \mathrm{g}$. They are held $0.10\ \mathrm{m}$ apart and released from rest in empty space. Find the speed of each when they are very far apart. Use $k=8.99\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.

两个小球各带 $+3.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$，质量分别为 $m_1=2.0\ \mathrm{g}$ 和 $m_2=1.0\ \mathrm{g}$。把它们固定在相距 $0.10\ \mathrm{m}$ 处，然后在真空中从静止释放。求它们相距很远时各自的速率。取 $k=8.99\times10^{9}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$。

Step 1第 1 步
System: both spheres. Initially the pair energy is stored in the arrangement, and the kinetic energy is zero.系统：两个小球。开始时电荷对的能量储存在排布中，动能为零。
$$U_i=\frac{kq^2}{r}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(3.0\times10^{-6})^2}{0.10}=0.81\ \mathrm{J}$$
Step 2第 2 步
Momentum is conserved (no external force), and the total starts at zero, so the lighter sphere moves faster: $m_1v_1=m_2v_2$ gives $v_2=2v_1$.动量守恒（没有外力），总动量初始为零，所以较轻的小球更快：$m_1v_1=m_2v_2$ 给出 $v_2=2v_1$。
$$v_2=\frac{m_1}{m_2}\,v_1=2v_1$$
Step 3第 3 步
Energy conservation: all of $U_i$ becomes kinetic energy when they are far apart ($U_f=0$).能量守恒：相距很远（$U_f=0$）时，全部 $U_i$ 变成动能。
$$0.81=\tfrac12(2.0\times10^{-3})v_1^2+\tfrac12(1.0\times10^{-3})(2v_1)^2=(3.0\times10^{-3})v_1^2$$
Step 4第 4 步
Solve.求解。
$$v_1=\sqrt{270}=16\ \mathrm{m/s},\qquad v_2=33\ \mathrm{m/s}$$
Step 5第 5 步
Check: $\tfrac12(2.0\times10^{-3})(16.4)^2=0.27\ \mathrm{J}$ and $\tfrac12(1.0\times10^{-3})(32.9)^2=0.54\ \mathrm{J}$, which sum to $0.81\ \mathrm{J}$. The lighter sphere carries twice as much of the energy.检验：$\tfrac12(2.0\times10^{-3})(16.4)^2=0.27\ \mathrm{J}$，$\tfrac12(1.0\times10^{-3})(32.9)^2=0.54\ \mathrm{J}$，两者之和为 $0.81\ \mathrm{J}$。较轻的小球分得两倍的能量。
Why this works为什么可行
Two unknown speeds need two equations, and the two conservation laws supply them. Using only energy on one sphere would be wrong, because the other sphere takes some of the energy.两个未知速率需要两个方程，两个守恒定律正好提供。只对一个小球用能量是错误的，因为另一个小球也带走一部分能量。

Common errors常见错误

Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Using $E=kQ/r^2$ where $V=kQ/r$ is needed, or the reverse.
  2. Adding potentials as vectors or dropping the sign of a charge.
  3. Counting a pair twice (or forgetting a pair) when summing $U_E$ over several charges.
  4. Treating $V=0$ and $E=0$ as the same condition.
  5. Giving the field the same sign as the slope of $V$: it is the negative of it.
  6. Moving a negative charge toward lower potential and expecting it to speed up.
  7. Using $\Delta K=-q\,\Delta V$ when an external agent also does work, or forgetting the second object in a two-body problem.
最常见的错误
  1. 在需要 $V=kQ/r$ 的地方用了 $E=kQ/r^2$，或者反过来。
  2. 把电势当矢量相加，或者丢掉电荷的符号。
  3. 对多个电荷求 $U_E$ 时把一对算两次（或漏掉一对）。
  4. 把 $V=0$ 与 $E=0$ 当成同一个条件。
  5. 让场的符号与 $V$ 的斜率相同：它应是斜率的负值。
  6. 让负电荷朝较低电势运动，却期待它加速。
  7. 在外界也做功时仍用 $\Delta K=-q\,\Delta V$，或者在两体问题中忘记第二个物体。
Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Using $E=kQ/r^2$ where $V=kQ/r$ is needed, or the reverse.
  2. Adding potentials as vectors or dropping the sign of a charge.
  3. Counting a pair twice (or forgetting a pair) when summing $U_E$ over several charges.
  4. Treating $V=0$ and $E=0$ as the same condition.
  5. Moving a negative charge toward lower potential and expecting it to speed up.
  6. Using $\Delta K=-q\Delta V$ when an external agent also does work.
  7. Using the potential $V$ where the potential difference $\Delta V$ is needed.

Potential of point charges?点电荷的电势？

(Physics C) Potential difference from the field?（物理 C）由电场求电势差？
$$\Delta V=-\int\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell$$ The negative signed area under $E_x(x)$.$$\Delta V=-\int\vec E\cdot d\vec\ell$$ $E_x(x)$ 图线下带符号面积的负值。
How does $\vec E$ meet an equipotential?$\vec E$ 与等势线怎样相交？
At right angles, pointing toward lower $V$; no field component along the line.垂直相交，指向较低的 $V$；沿线没有场分量。
(Physics C) $V$ at the center of an arc of charge $Q$, radius $R$?（物理 C）电荷 $Q$、半径 $R$ 的圆弧圆心处的 $V$？

Which way does a free charge move?自由电荷往哪个方向运动？
Positive toward lower $V$; negative toward higher $V$. Either way, $U_E$ falls and $K$ rises.正电荷走向较低的 $V$；负电荷走向较高的 $V$。无论哪种，$U_E$ 减小而 $K$ 增大。

(Physics C) Long charged wire: where is $V=0$?（物理 C）长带电导线：$V=0$ 在哪里？
Not at infinity (the logarithm diverges); choose a reference radius, $\Delta V=-2k\lambda\ln(r_2/r_1)$.不能取无穷远（对数发散）；选一个参考半径，$\Delta V=-2k\lambda\ln(r_2/r_1)$。
(Physics C) Where is $E$ zero in terms of $V(x)$?（物理 C）用 $V(x)$ 表述，$E$ 在哪里为零？

Questions 1-3: energy and potential of charge systems第 1-3 题：电荷系统的能量与电势

1. Charges $+4.0\ \mathrm{nC}$ and $-4.0\ \mathrm{nC}$ are $0.20\ \mathrm{m}$ apart. Their electric potential energy (zero at infinity) is:电荷 $+4.0\ \mathrm{nC}$ 与 $-4.0\ \mathrm{nC}$ 相距 $0.20\ \mathrm{m}$。它们的电势能（无穷远处为零）为：
$+7.2\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$
$-7.2\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$
$-3.6\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$
$-1.4\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{J}$
Correct! $U_E=\dfrac{(8.99\times10^{9})(4.0\times10^{-9})(-4.0\times10^{-9})}{0.20}=-7.2\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$. Unlike charges give a negative energy.正确！$U_E=\dfrac{(8.99\times10^{9})(4.0\times10^{-9})(-4.0\times10^{-9})}{0.20}=-7.2\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$。异号电荷的能量为负。
$U_E=kq_1q_2/r$ with the signs kept: $-7.2\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$. A positive sign ignores the opposite charges, $-3.6\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$ halves the result, and $-1.4\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{J}$ uses $r=0.10\ \mathrm{m}$.$U_E=kq_1q_2/r$，保留符号：$-7.2\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$。正号忽略了电荷异号，$-3.6\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{J}$ 把结果减半，$-1.4\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{J}$ 用了 $r=0.10\ \mathrm{m}$。
2. Three identical $+1.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ charges sit at the corners of an equilateral triangle of side $0.30\ \mathrm{m}$. The electric potential energy of the system is:三个相同的 $+1.0\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ 电荷位于边长 $0.30\ \mathrm{m}$ 的等边三角形的顶点。系统的电势能为：
$0.030\ \mathrm{J}$
$0.060\ \mathrm{J}$
$0.090\ \mathrm{J}$
$0.18\ \mathrm{J}$
Correct! Three distinct pairs, each $kq^2/a=(8.99\times10^{9})(1.0\times10^{-6})^2/0.30=0.030\ \mathrm{J}$, so $U_E=3(0.030)=0.090\ \mathrm{J}$.正确！三个不同的电荷对，每对 $kq^2/a=(8.99\times10^{9})(1.0\times10^{-6})^2/0.30=0.030\ \mathrm{J}$，所以 $U_E=3(0.030)=0.090\ \mathrm{J}$。
Each pair contributes $0.030\ \mathrm{J}$ and there are three pairs. $0.030\ \mathrm{J}$ counts one pair, $0.060\ \mathrm{J}$ two pairs, and $0.18\ \mathrm{J}$ counts each pair twice.每对贡献 $0.030\ \mathrm{J}$，共三对。$0.030\ \mathrm{J}$ 只算了一对，$0.060\ \mathrm{J}$ 算了两对，$0.18\ \mathrm{J}$ 把每对算了两次。
3. Four $+2.0\ \mathrm{nC}$ charges sit at the corners of a square of side $0.10\ \mathrm{m}$. The electric potential at the center of the square is closest to:四个 $+2.0\ \mathrm{nC}$ 的电荷位于边长 $0.10\ \mathrm{m}$ 的正方形的四角。正方形中心的电势最接近：
$0$
$5.1\times10^{2}\ \mathrm{V}$
$7.2\times10^{2}\ \mathrm{V}$
$1.0\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V}$
Correct! Each corner is $0.10/\sqrt2=0.0707\ \mathrm{m}$ from the center, and the potentials add as scalars: $V=4(8.99\times10^{9})(2.0\times10^{-9})/0.0707=1.0\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V}$. The field there is zero, the potential is not.正确！每个角到中心的距离为 $0.10/\sqrt2=0.0707\ \mathrm{m}$，电势按标量相加：$V=4(8.99\times10^{9})(2.0\times10^{-9})/0.0707=1.0\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V}$。那里的场为零，电势不为零。
The center is half a diagonal, $0.0707\ \mathrm{m}$, from every corner. Zero confuses $V$ with $E$; $7.2\times10^{2}\ \mathrm{V}$ uses $0.10\ \mathrm{m}$ (the side), and $5.1\times10^{2}\ \mathrm{V}$ uses the full diagonal.中心到每个角的距离是半条对角线，$0.0707\ \mathrm{m}$。零把 $V$ 与 $E$ 混淆了；$7.2\times10^{2}\ \mathrm{V}$ 用了 $0.10\ \mathrm{m}$（边长），$5.1\times10^{2}\ \mathrm{V}$ 用了整条对角线。

Questions 4-6: field, speed, and ring potential第 4-6 题：场强、速度与圆环电势

4. In a region of uniform field, $V$ falls from $60\ \mathrm{V}$ at $x=0$ to $20\ \mathrm{V}$ at $x=4.0\ \mathrm{cm}$. The field is:在匀强电场区域中，$V$ 从 $x=0$ 处的 $60\ \mathrm{V}$ 降到 $x=4.0\ \mathrm{cm}$ 处的 $20\ \mathrm{V}$。电场为：
$1.0\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$ toward $+x$$1.0\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$，沿 $+x$
$1.0\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$ toward $-x$$1.0\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$，沿 $-x$
$10\ \mathrm{V/m}$ toward $+x$$10\ \mathrm{V/m}$，沿 $+x$
$1.5\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$ toward $+x$$1.5\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$，沿 $+x$
Correct! $E_x=-\Delta V/\Delta x=-(20-60)/0.040=+1.0\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$. The field points toward decreasing potential, $+x$.正确！$E_x=-\Delta V/\Delta x=-(20-60)/0.040=+1.0\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$。电场指向电势降低的方向，即 $+x$。
Use the potential change $40\ \mathrm{V}$ over $0.040\ \mathrm{m}$ (convert centimetres to metres), and point the field toward lower $V$. $10\ \mathrm{V/m}$ forgets the conversion; $1.5\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$ divides the starting potential, not the change, by the distance.用 $0.040\ \mathrm{m}$ 内 $40\ \mathrm{V}$ 的电势变化（厘米要换成米），并让电场指向较低的 $V$。$10\ \mathrm{V/m}$ 忘了单位换算；$1.5\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{V/m}$ 用起始电势而不是变化量去除以距离。
5. A proton starts from rest and is accelerated through a potential difference of $2.0\ \mathrm{kV}$. Its final speed is closest to:质子从静止出发，经 $2.0\ \mathrm{kV}$ 的电势差加速。它的末速率最接近：
$4.4\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{m/s}$
$6.2\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{m/s}$
$2.7\times10^{7}\ \mathrm{m/s}$
$3.8\times10^{11}\ \mathrm{m/s}$
Correct! $\tfrac12m_pv^2=e\,\Delta V$ gives $v=\sqrt{2(1.60\times10^{-19})(2000)/(1.67\times10^{-27})}=6.2\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{m/s}$.正确！由 $\tfrac12m_pv^2=e\,\Delta V$ 得 $v=\sqrt{2(1.60\times10^{-19})(2000)/(1.67\times10^{-27})}=6.2\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{m/s}$。
$v=\sqrt{2e\,\Delta V/m_p}$. $4.4\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{m/s}$ drops the factor of $2$, $2.7\times10^{7}\ \mathrm{m/s}$ uses the electron mass, and $3.8\times10^{11}\ \mathrm{m/s}$ is $v^2$ (the square root was not taken).$v=\sqrt{2e\,\Delta V/m_p}$。$4.4\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{m/s}$ 漏了因子 $2$，$2.7\times10^{7}\ \mathrm{m/s}$ 用了电子质量，$3.8\times10^{11}\ \mathrm{m/s}$ 是 $v^2$（没有开平方）。
6. (Physics C) The potential at the center of a uniformly charged thin ring (radius $a$) is $V_0$. At the axis point a distance $\sqrt3\,a$ from the center, the potential is:（物理 C）均匀带电细圆环（半径 $a$）圆心处的电势为 $V_0$。在轴线上距圆心 $\sqrt3\,a$ 处，电势为：
$0$
$V_0/\sqrt3$
$V_0/2$
$V_0/4$
Correct! $V=kQ/\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$. At the center $V_0=kQ/a$. At $x=\sqrt3\,a$, $r=\sqrt{a^2+3a^2}=2a$, so $V=kQ/(2a)=V_0/2$.正确！$V=kQ/\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$。圆心处 $V_0=kQ/a$。在 $x=\sqrt3\,a$ 处，$r=\sqrt{a^2+3a^2}=2a$，所以 $V=kQ/(2a)=V_0/2$。
The ring is at distance $r=\sqrt{a^2+x^2}=2a$ from the point. $V_0/\sqrt3$ treats $x$ as the distance, and $V_0/4$ squares the distance (that is how the field falls, not the potential). The potential does not vanish.圆环上各点到该点的距离为 $r=\sqrt{a^2+x^2}=2a$。$V_0/\sqrt3$ 把 $x$ 当作距离，$V_0/4$ 把距离平方（那是场的变化规律，不是电势的）。电势不为零。

$+8.0\ \mathrm{V/m}$

The field is the negative of the slope, so the sign flips. $-8.0\ \mathrm{V/m}$ keeps the slope sign, $0$ would need a flat graph, and $-0.125\ \mathrm{V/m}$ takes a reciprocal.场是斜率的负值，所以符号要反过来。$-8.0\ \mathrm{V/m}$ 保留了斜率的符号，$0$ 需要图像平坦，$-0.125\ \mathrm{V/m}$ 取了倒数。
8. A proton and an alpha particle ($q=+2e$, mass about $4m_p$) start from rest and are accelerated through the same potential difference. The ratio of final speeds $v_\alpha/v_p$ is:质子和 α 粒子（$q=+2e$，质量约为 $4m_p$）从静止出发，经过同一电势差加速。末速率之比 $v_\alpha/v_p$ 为：
$1/\sqrt2$
$1/2$
$\sqrt2$
$2$
Correct! $v=\sqrt{2q\,\Delta V/m}$, so $v_\alpha/v_p=\sqrt{(2/4)}=1/\sqrt2\approx0.71$. The alpha gains twice the kinetic energy but has four times the mass.正确！$v=\sqrt{2q\,\Delta V/m}$，所以 $v_\alpha/v_p=\sqrt{(2/4)}=1/\sqrt2\approx0.71$。α 粒子获得两倍的动能，但质量是四倍。

9. A charge is moved slowly from point $P$ to point $Q$ along an equipotential line. The work done by the electric field is:一个电荷沿等势线从 $P$ 点缓慢移到 $Q$ 点。电场做的功为：
positive正
negative负
zero零
dependent on the mass of the charge取决于电荷的质量
