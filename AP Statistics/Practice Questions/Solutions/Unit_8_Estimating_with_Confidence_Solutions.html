Q 1第1题MEDIUM中等3.3, 3.4 One-proportion confidence intervals and claims3.3, 3.4 单比例置信区间与说法论证CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A random sample of 180 students from a district of more than 5,000 students is asked whether they support a later start to the school day; 126 say yes (synthetic). Which 95% confidence interval for the population proportion is correct?
从一个有 5,000 多名学生的学区中随机抽取 180 名学生，询问他们是否支持推迟上学时间，126 人回答支持（合成数据）。下面哪个总体比例的 95% 置信区间（confidence interval）正确？
Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Conditions: the sample is random, $180$ is less than 10% of the district, and $126$ and $54$ are both at least 10. With $\hat p=126/180=0.70$ the standard error is $\sqrt{0.70(0.30)/180}\approx0.0342$. For 95% confidence $z^*=1.960$, so the margin of error is $1.960(0.0342)\approx0.0669$ and the interval is $0.70\pm0.0669$, about $(0.633, 0.767)$. On the TI-84 this is 1-PropZInt with $x=126$, $n=180$, C-Level $0.95$.条件：样本随机，$180$ 不到该学区学生的 10%，且 $126$ 与 $54$ 都至少为 10。由 $\hat p=126/180=0.70$ 得标准误（standard error） $\sqrt{0.70(0.30)/180}\approx0.0342$。95% 置信水平（confidence level）对应 $z^*=1.960$，所以误差范围（margin of error）为 $1.960(0.0342)\approx0.0669$，区间为 $0.70\pm0.0669$，约 $(0.633, 0.767)$。在 TI-84 上用 1-PropZInt，输入 $x=126$、$n=180$、C-Level $0.95$。

Why the options attract (M 20)选项误区（M 20）
  1. Uses one standard error as the whole margin: $0.0342$ gives $(0.666, 0.734)$. The confidence level supplies the multiplier $z^*$, which is missing here.把一个标准误当作整个误差范围：$0.0342$ 给出 $(0.666, 0.734)$。置信水平决定乘数 $z^*$，这里漏掉了它。
  2. Correct: estimate plus and minus $z^*=1.960$ standard errors.正确：估计值加减 $1.960$ 个标准误。
  3. Uses $z^*=1.645$, the multiplier for 90% confidence, and gets the narrower interval $(0.644, 0.756)$.用了 90% 置信水平对应的 $z^*=1.645$，得到偏窄的区间 $(0.644, 0.756)$。
  4. Doubles the margin: $2(0.0669)=0.1339$ gives $(0.566, 0.834)$. The $\pm1.960$ standard errors already make up the full 95% interval.把误差范围加倍：$2(0.0669)=0.1339$ 给出 $(0.566, 0.834)$。$\pm1.960$ 个标准误已经是完整的 95% 区间。
A confidence interval is the estimate plus and minus $z^*$ standard errors, with $z^*$ fixed by the confidence level.置信区间是估计值加减 $z^*$ 个标准误，其中 $z^*$ 由置信水平决定。

A random sample of 400 voters from a large city is asked about a ballot measure, and $\hat p=0.36$ of them are in favor (synthetic). Using $\hat p\pm z^*\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/n}$, which 90% confidence interval for the proportion of all voters in the city who favor the measure is correct?
从一个大城市随机抽取 400 名选民询问一项公投议案，$\hat p=0.36$ 的人赞成（合成数据）。使用 $\hat p\pm z^*\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/n}$，该市全体选民中赞成议案比例的 90% 置信区间（confidence interval）哪个正确？
Answer: D答案：D

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The sample is random, $400$ is less than 10% of a large city's voters, and $400(0.36)=144$ and $400(0.64)=256$ are at least 10. The standard error is $\sqrt{0.36(0.64)/400}=0.024$. For 90% confidence $z^*=1.645$, so the margin is $1.645(0.024)\approx0.0395$ and the interval is $0.36\pm0.0395$, about $(0.321, 0.399)$.样本随机，$400$ 不到大城市选民的 10%，且 $400(0.36)=144$、$400(0.64)=256$ 都至少为 10。标准误（standard error）为 $\sqrt{0.36(0.64)/400}=0.024$。90% 置信水平（confidence level）对应 $z^*=1.645$，误差范围（margin of error）为 $1.645(0.024)\approx0.0395$，区间为 $0.36\pm0.0395$，约 $(0.321, 0.399)$。

Why the options attract (M 20)选项误区（M 20）
  1. Uses the standard error $0.024$ alone as the margin and leaves out the multiplier 1.645.只把标准误 $0.024$ 当作误差范围，漏掉了乘数 1.645。
  2. Uses $z^*=1.960$, the multiplier for 95% confidence: $1.960(0.024)=0.047$ gives $(0.313, 0.407)$.用了 95% 置信水平的乘数 $z^*=1.960$：$1.960(0.024)=0.047$ 给出 $(0.313, 0.407)$。
  3. Puts $n=100$ inside the square root, which doubles the standard error to $0.048$ and gives $(0.281, 0.439)$.在根号里用了 $n=100$，使标准误翻倍为 $0.048$，得到 $(0.281, 0.439)$。
  4. Correct: $0.36\pm1.645(0.024)$.正确：$0.36\pm1.645(0.024)$。
Interval arithmetic is three steps: the standard error from the sample, the multiplier from the confidence level, then estimate plus and minus the margin.区间计算分三步：由样本得出标准误，由置信水平得出乘数，再用估计值加减误差范围。

A 95% confidence interval for a population proportion, built from a random sample, is $(0.42, 0.50)$. What are the sample proportion and the margin of error?
由一个随机样本得到的总体比例的 95% 置信区间（confidence interval）为 $(0.42, 0.50)$。样本比例（sample proportion）和误差范围（margin of error）分别是多少？
Answer: A答案：A

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The interval is symmetric about the estimate, $\hat p\pm ME$. So $\hat p$ is the midpoint, $(0.42+0.50)/2=0.46$, and the margin of error is half the width, $(0.50-0.42)/2=0.04$.区间关于估计值对称，形式为 $\hat p\pm ME$。所以 $\hat p$ 是中点 $(0.42+0.50)/2=0.46$，误差范围是宽度的一半 $(0.50-0.42)/2=0.04$。

Why the options attract (M 2)选项误区（M 2）
  1. Correct: midpoint for the estimate, half the width for the margin.正确：中点是估计值，半宽是误差范围。
  2. Divides the width by 1 instead of 2 and reports the full width $0.08$; the interval extends one margin on each side of $\hat p$.宽度除以 1 而不是 2，给出整个宽度 $0.08$；区间在 $\hat p$ 两侧各延伸一个误差范围。
  3. Divides the width by 4, giving $0.02$; the margin is half of the width, not a quarter.宽度除以 4 得 $0.02$；误差范围是宽度的一半，不是四分之一。
  4. Takes the upper endpoint as the estimate. The estimate sits at the center, so the endpoints are $\hat p-ME$ and $\hat p+ME$.把上端点当作估计值。估计值位于中心，端点是 $\hat p-ME$ 与 $\hat p+ME$。
Estimate = midpoint of the interval; margin of error = half the width.估计值是区间中点；误差范围是区间宽度的一半。

Q 4第4题MEDIUM中等3.3, 4.2 Margin of error and sample size3.3, 4.2 误差范围与样本容量CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A polling team will select a random sample and wants a 95% confidence interval for a population proportion with a margin of error of at most $0.025$. With no prior information they plan with $\hat p(1-\hat p)=0.25$, the conservative choice $\hat p=0.5$. What is the smallest sample size that meets the requirement?
民调小组将抽取一个随机样本，希望得到误差范围（margin of error）至多为 $0.025$ 的总体比例 95% 置信区间（confidence interval）。没有先验信息时，他们按 $\hat p(1-\hat p)=0.25$（即保守取值 $\hat p=0.5$）规划。满足要求的最小样本容量是多少？
Answer: C答案：C

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

Solve $1.960\sqrt{0.25/n}\le0.025$. Then $n\ge\left(\dfrac{1.960}{0.025}\right)^2(0.25)=1536.64$. A sample size must be a whole number and the margin must not exceed $0.025$, so round up: $n=1537$.解 $1.960\sqrt{0.25/n}\le0.025$，得 $n\ge\left(\dfrac{1.960}{0.025}\right)^2(0.25)=1536.64$。样本容量必须是整数，且误差范围不能超过 $0.025$，所以向上取整：$n=1537$。

Why the options attract (M 20)选项误区（M 20）
  1. Truncates $1536.64$ to $1536$. At $n=1536$ the margin is $1.960\sqrt{0.25/1536}$, slightly above $0.025$, so the requirement is not met; always round up.把 $1536.64$ 截断为 $1536$。$n=1536$ 时误差范围 $1.960\sqrt{0.25/1536}$ 略大于 $0.025$，不满足要求；必须向上取整。
  2. Uses $z^*=2$ instead of $1.960$: $(2/0.025)^2(0.25)=1600$. That overshoots the requirement.用 $z^*=2$ 代替 $1.960$：$(2/0.025)^2(0.25)=1600$，超出了要求。
  3. Correct: $1536.64$ rounded up.正确：$1536.64$ 向上取整。
  4. Drops the factor $\hat p(1-\hat p)=0.25$: $(1.960/0.025)^2=6146.56$ rounds up to $6147$, four times too many.漏掉因子 $\hat p(1-\hat p)=0.25$：$(1.960/0.025)^2=6146.56$ 取整为 $6147$，是所需样本量的四倍。
Sample size comes from solving margin of error $\le$ target for $n$; round up, and use $\hat p(1-\hat p)=0.25$ when nothing is known.样本容量由“误差范围不超过目标值”解出 $n$；向上取整，且在没有信息时取 $\hat p(1-\hat p)=0.25$。

Survey B            | [---------------]
          +    +    +    +    +    +    +
          0.40 0.45 0.50 0.55 0.60 0.65 0.70
Which claim is justified by the intervals?
对两个独立的成年人随机样本调查了某项提案（合成数据）。图中画出了每次调查中全体成年人支持该提案比例的 95% 置信区间（confidence interval），竖线标出 $0.50$。
调查 A     [--------|-------]

          +    +    +    +    +    +    +
          0.40 0.45 0.50 0.55 0.60 0.65 0.70
这些区间支持哪项主张？
Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

A claim that more than half of all adults support the proposal needs every plausible value to exceed $0.50$. Survey B's interval $(0.52, 0.68)$ lies entirely above $0.50$, so it gives convincing evidence of a majority. Survey A's interval $(0.41, 0.58)$ contains $0.50$ and values below it, so a minority has not been ruled out. Its sample proportion is the midpoint $0.495$, below $0.50$.要说明多于一半的成年人支持该提案，需要区间内的每个合理取值都大于 $0.50$。调查 B 的区间 $(0.52, 0.68)$ 完全在 $0.50$ 以上，给出多数支持的有力证据。调查 A 的区间 $(0.41, 0.58)$ 含有 $0.50$ 及更小的值，不能排除少数支持。它的样本比例（sample proportion）是中点 $0.495$，低于 $0.50$。

  1. Reads only the upper endpoint. Survey A's interval also contains proportions as low as $0.41$.只看上端点。调查 A 的区间还含有低至 $0.41$ 的比例。

  4. Treats the statistic as if it were the parameter. Survey A's $\hat p=0.495$ is below $0.50$, and a sample proportion alone says nothing about plausibility for $p$.把统计量当成参数。调查 A 的 $\hat p=0.495$ 低于 $0.50$，而单看样本比例无法说明 $p$ 的合理取值。

Q 6第6题MEDIUM中等3.3, 4.2 Margin of error and sample size3.3, 4.2 误差范围与样本容量CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
Two researchers independently draw random samples of the same size from the same large population and obtain the same sample proportion, strictly between 0 and 1. Researcher A reports a 90% confidence interval and Researcher B reports a 95% confidence interval. Without computing either, which interval is wider and why?
两位研究者各自独立地从同一个大总体中抽取容量相同的随机样本，得到相同的样本比例（sample proportion）（严格介于 0 与 1 之间）。研究者 A 给出 90% 置信区间（confidence interval），研究者 B 给出 95% 置信区间。不用计算，哪个区间更宽，为什么？
Answer: D答案：D

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The width is $2z^*\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/n}$. The same $n$ and $\hat p$ give the same standard error, positive because $\hat p$ is strictly between 0 and 1. The only change is the multiplier: $z^*=1.960$ for 95% against $1.645$ for 90%, so B's interval is wider by the factor $1.960/1.645\approx1.19$.宽度为 $2z^*\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/n}$。$n$ 与 $\hat p$ 相同，标准误（standard error）就相同（因 $\hat p$ 严格介于 0 与 1 之间而为正）。唯一的区别是乘数：95% 对应 $z^*=1.960$，90% 对应 $1.645$，所以 B 的区间更宽，比值为 $1.960/1.645\approx1.19$。

Why the options attract (M 20)选项误区（M 20）
  1. Confuses narrow with precise. A narrower interval at lower confidence is less likely to capture $p$; it is not better.把窄当成精确。置信水平较低时区间更窄，但更不容易覆盖 $p$，并不更好。
  2. Equal samples give equal standard errors, but the multipliers differ ($1.645$ against $1.960$), so the widths differ.相同样本给出相同标准误，但乘数不同（$1.645$ 与 $1.960$），所以宽度不同。
  3. Right interval, wrong reason. The standard error $\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/n}$ depends only on $\hat p$ and $n$, not on the confidence level.区间选对了，理由错了。标准误 $\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/n}$ 只取决于 $\hat p$ 和 $n$，与置信水平无关。
  4. Correct: the confidence level enters through the multiplier $z^*$.正确：置信水平通过乘数 $z^*$ 起作用。
More confidence costs precision: the same data give a wider interval at a higher level.更高的置信水平以精确度为代价：同样的数据，水平越高区间越宽。

Answer: A答案：A

  1. Correct: $\sigma$ unknown, so $t$ with $df=n-1=24$.正确：$\sigma$ 未知，用 $df=n-1=24$ 的 $t$。

  4. Treats $s=210$ as if it were a known population value and claims $n=25$ is enough for $z$; the $z$ interval is for a known $\sigma$.把 $s=210$ 当成已知的总体值，并认为 $n=25$ 足以用 $z$；$z$ 区间用于 $\sigma$ 已知的情形。

Q 9第9题MEDIUM中等4.2, 4.3 One-sample t intervals and claims4.2, 4.3 单样本 t 区间与说法论证CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
A student builds a 95% confidence interval for the mean number of pages of all 4,000 books in a school library, using the first 15 books on one shelf as the sample. Which statement about the conditions for a one-sample $t$ interval is correct?
一名学生用某个书架上的前 15 本书作样本，构造学校图书馆全部 4,000 本书平均页数的 95% 置信区间（confidence interval）。关于单样本 $t$ 区间的条件，哪个陈述正确？
Answer: B答案：B

Reasoning推理1 point1 分

The first condition for any interval is a random sample (or randomized experiment).
