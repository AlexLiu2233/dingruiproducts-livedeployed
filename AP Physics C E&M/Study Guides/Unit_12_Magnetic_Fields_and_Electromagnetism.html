Topic 12.1专题 12.1

Magnetic Fields磁场

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Describe a magnetic field as a vector field that exerts forces on moving charges, currents, and magnetic materials, and explain why it is produced by dipoles and never by monopoles.
  • State Gauss's law for magnetism, $\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A} = 0$, and use it to explain why magnetic field lines form closed loops.
  • Explain, using the fact that magnetic poles never occur in isolation, why magnetic field lines form closed loops.
  • Sketch the field of a bar magnet, including the field lines inside the magnet, and predict how a compass aligns at a given point.
  • Explain how the alignment of magnetic dipoles accounts for permanent magnetism, induced magnetism, and why a broken magnet gives two dipoles.
  • Distinguish ferromagnetic, paramagnetic, and diamagnetic behavior in an external field.
  • Describe magnetic permeability, state the value of the vacuum permeability $\mu_0$, and explain why the permeability of matter is not a constant.
  • 描述（describe）磁场是一个对运动电荷、电流和磁性材料施力的矢量场，并解释为什么磁场由磁偶极子产生而绝不由磁单极子产生。
  • 陈述（state）高斯磁定律 $\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A} = 0$，并用它解释磁感线为什么是闭合曲线。
  • 利用磁极从不孤立存在这一事实，解释磁感线为什么是闭合曲线。
  • 画出（sketch）条形磁铁的磁场，包括磁铁内部的磁感线，并预测磁针在某点的指向。
  • 解释（explain）磁偶极子的排列如何说明永磁性与感应磁性，以及为什么掰断的磁铁得到两个偶极子。
  • 区分（distinguish）铁磁性、顺磁性与抗磁性材料在外磁场中的行为。
  • 描述（describe）磁导率，陈述真空磁导率 $\mu_0$ 的数值，并解释物质的磁导率为什么不是常数。

Magnetism starts from a simple observation: a compass needle swings toward a magnet, and a magnet picks up iron without touching it. As with the electric field, the interaction is carried by a field. A magnetic field $\vec{B}$ is a vector field: at every point in space it has a magnitude and a direction, and it can be used to find the magnetic force on moving electric charges, on electric currents, and on magnetic materials. Its SI unit is the tesla, $1\ \text{T} = 1\ \text{N}/(\text{A}\cdot\text{m})$.

磁学始于一个简单的观察：磁针会转向磁铁，磁铁不接触也能吸起铁。与电场一样，这种相互作用由场（field）来传递。磁场 $\vec{B}$ 是矢量场：空间每一点都有大小与方向，可用来求磁场对运动电荷、电流和磁性材料的作用力。其 SI 单位是特斯拉，$1\ \text{T} = 1\ \text{N}/(\text{A}\cdot\text{m})$。

Properties of the magnetic field磁场的性质

A magnetic field is represented by a vector field map: arrows (or field lines) whose direction at each point is the direction of $\vec{B}$ there, drawn closer together where $|\vec{B}|$ is larger. Magnetic fields are produced by magnetic dipoles, or by combinations of dipoles, and never by monopoles. A dipole has a north and a south polarity. A compass is a small dipole that is free to rotate: placed in a field, it turns until its north end points along $\vec{B}$.

磁场用矢量场图表示：每一点箭头（或磁感线）的方向就是该处 $\vec{B}$ 的方向，$|\vec{B}|$ 越大处画得越密。磁场由磁偶极子（magnetic dipole）或偶极子的组合产生，绝不由磁单极子产生。偶极子有北极与南极。磁针是可自由转动的小偶极子：放入磁场后，它会转到北极端沿 $\vec{B}$ 的方向。

Because no isolated north or south pole has ever been found, every field line must close on itself. This is stated by Gauss's law for magnetism (Maxwell's second equation): the net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero.

由于从未发现孤立的北极或南极，每条磁感线都必须自行闭合。这就是高斯磁定律（Gauss's law for magnetism，麦克斯韦第二方程）：通过任何闭合曲面的净磁通量为零。

Because no isolated north or south pole has ever been found, every magnetic field line must close on itself: magnetic field lines form closed loops. A line that is drawn leaving a north pole continues on and returns to a south pole.

由于从未发现孤立的北极或南极，每条磁感线都必须自行闭合：磁感线构成闭合回路（closed loops）。从北极画出的磁感线会继续延伸，并回到南极。

Gauss's law for magnetism (Maxwell's second equation)高斯磁定律（麦克斯韦第二方程）
$$\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A} = 0$$

Every field line that enters a closed surface also leaves it. There is no magnetic charge to act as a source or sink.

进入闭合曲面的每条磁感线也必然穿出。不存在可作源或汇的磁荷。

Compare this with Gauss's law for electricity from Unit 8: $\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A} = Q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$. Electric field lines can start and end on charges, so the electric flux through a closed surface can be nonzero. Magnetic field lines cannot start or end anywhere. Maxwell's equations are the collection of four equations that fully describe electromagnetism; this is the second, and you will meet the fourth in Topic 12.4.

与第 8 单元的电场高斯定律 $\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A} = Q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$ 对比：电场线可以起止于电荷，所以通过闭合曲面的电通量可以不为零；磁感线却不能在任何地方起止。麦克斯韦方程组是完整描述电磁学的四个方程，这是其中第二个，第四个将在专题 12.4 遇到。

Compare this with electric field lines (Unit 10): electric field lines can start on positive charges and end on negative charges, but magnetic field lines cannot start or end anywhere, because magnetic poles never occur in isolation.

与电场线（第 10 单元）对比：电场线可以起始于正电荷、终止于负电荷，而磁感线不能在任何地方起止，因为磁极从不孤立存在。

For a bar magnet, the external field points away from one end (defined as the north pole) and returns to the other end (the south pole). Inside the magnet the lines continue from south to north to close the loops. That inside segment is the part students most often leave out of a sketch.

对条形磁铁，外部磁场从一端（定义为北极）指向外，并回到另一端（南极）。磁铁内部的磁感线则从南极指向北极，使回路闭合。这段内部磁感线是学生画图时最常漏掉的部分。

Common Mistake: field lines begin at the north pole Outside the magnet the lines run from N to S, but they do not start at N or end at S: they continue through the magnet from S to N. A sketch in which lines stop at the poles contradicts $\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A} = 0$.
常见错误：磁感线从北极开始 磁铁外部磁感线由 N 指向 S，但它们并不在 N 开始或在 S 终止：它们在磁铁内部从 S 继续指向 N。若画成磁感线在两极终止，就违背了 $\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{A} = 0$。
Common Mistake: field lines begin at the north pole Outside the magnet the lines run from N to S, but they do not start at N or end at S: they continue through the magnet from S to N. A sketch in which lines stop at the poles contains lines with a beginning and an end, which is impossible because magnetic field lines form closed loops.
常见错误：磁感线从北极开始 磁铁外部磁感线由 N 指向 S，但它们并不在 N 开始或在 S 终止：它们在磁铁内部从 S 继续指向 N。若画成磁感线在两极终止，就出现了有起点和终点的磁感线，这是不可能的，因为磁感线是闭合回路。
Worked Example: Net flux through a closed surface near a pole例题：靠近磁极的闭合曲面的净磁通量

A small closed surface is drawn around the north-pole end of a bar magnet, so the surface cuts across the magnet's body. The outward magnetic flux through the curved side wall is $+1.8\times10^{-5}\ \text{Wb}$ and through the end cap outside the magnet is $+3.1\times10^{-5}\ \text{Wb}$. The remaining face, the cap of area $5.0\ \text{cm}^2$ that lies inside the magnet, is perpendicular to the field. Find the flux through this cap and the magnitude of the average field there.

在条形磁铁北极一端周围作一个小闭合曲面，使曲面截断磁铁本体。通过弯曲侧壁的向外磁通量为 $+1.8\times10^{-5}\ \text{Wb}$，通过磁铁外部端盖的向外磁通量为 $+3.1\times10^{-5}\ \text{Wb}$。其余一面是位于磁铁内部、面积为 $5.0\ \text{cm}^2$ 的端盖，与磁场垂直。求通过该端盖的磁通量及此处平均磁场的大小。

Step 1第 1 步
Apply Gauss's law for magnetism: the three fluxes must sum to zero.应用高斯磁定律：三部分磁通量之和必须为零。
$$\Phi_{\text{side}} + \Phi_{\text{out}} + \Phi_{\text{in}} = 0$$
Step 2第 2 步
Solve for the flux through the cap inside the magnet.解出通过磁铁内部端盖的磁通量。
$$\Phi_{\text{in}} = -(1.8\times10^{-5} + 3.1\times10^{-5})\ \text{Wb} = -4.9\times10^{-5}\ \text{Wb}$$
Step 3第 3 步
The negative sign means the flux is directed into the closed surface: the lines run through the magnet from S to N, entering the surface there. Use $\Phi = BA$ for a perpendicular, uniform field, with $A = 5.0\ \text{cm}^2 = 5.0\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2$.负号表示磁通量指向闭合曲面内部：磁感线在磁铁内部由 S 指向 N，在此处进入曲面。对垂直且均匀的磁场用 $\Phi = BA$，其中 $A = 5.0\ \text{cm}^2 = 5.0\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2$。
$$|B| = \dfrac{|\Phi_{\text{in}}|}{A} = \dfrac{4.9\times10^{-5}\ \text{Wb}}{5.0\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2} = 0.098\ \text{T}$$
Why this works为什么这样做
Flux that leaves the surface through the pole must come back in somewhere else, and since the surface is closed that somewhere is the cap inside the magnet. Closed field lines are the physical content of the zero.从磁极处穿出曲面的磁通量必须从别处重新进入，而曲面是闭合的，这个别处就是磁铁内部的端盖。零的物理含义正是磁感线闭合。

Magnetic dipoles and the behavior of materials磁偶极子与材料的磁性

Magnetic dipoles result from the circular or rotational motion of electric charge. In magnetic materials this is chiefly the motion of electrons (their orbital motion and their intrinsic spin). A current loop is the simplest dipole: it has a north face and a south face, and its field far away looks like that of a small bar magnet. This origin explains several facts at once.

磁偶极子来自电荷的圆周或旋转运动。在磁性材料中，这主要是电子的运动（轨道运动与内禀自旋）。电流环是最简单的偶极子：它有北面与南面，远处的场看上去像一根小条形磁铁。这一来源同时解释了好几个事实。

  • No isolated poles. Break a bar magnet in half and each half is a complete dipole with its own north and south pole, because the dipoles inside it are still there.
  • Like poles repel, opposite poles attract. Two north poles (or two south poles) push apart; a north and a south pole pull together.
  • Falloff with distance. The field of a dipole decreases as you move away from it; for an ideal point dipole it falls off faster than the $1/r^2$ of a point charge (as $1/r^3$ along its axis).
  • Falloff with distance. The magnitude of the field of a dipole decreases with increasing distance from the dipole.
  • Alignment. A dipole in a field tends to align with the field, which is why a compass needle points along $\vec{B}$.
  • Permanent and induced magnetism. Both are properties of a system, produced by the alignment of dipoles: permanent when the alignment persists, induced when an external field creates it.
  • 没有孤立的磁极。把条形磁铁掰成两半，每一半都是完整的偶极子，各有自己的 N 极和 S 极，因为其内部的偶极子仍然存在。
  • 同极相斥，异极相吸。两个 N 极（或两个 S 极）互相推开；一个 N 极与一个 S 极互相吸引。
  • 随距离的衰减。偶极子的磁场随离开偶极子的距离增大而减小；对理想点偶极子，它比点电荷的 $1/r^2$ 衰减更快（沿其轴线按 $1/r^3$）。
  • 随距离的衰减。偶极子磁场的大小随离偶极子的距离增大而减小。
  • 取向。偶极子在磁场中倾向于与场对齐，所以磁针沿 $\vec{B}$ 的方向。
  • 永磁性与感应磁性。两者都是系统的性质，由偶极子的排列产生：排列持续存在即为永磁，由外场造成即为感应磁性。

A material's composition decides how it responds to an external field. Three behaviors cover all matter:

材料的组成决定它对外磁场的响应。三类行为涵盖了所有物质：

FM
Ferromagnetic: iron, nickel, cobalt

Atomic dipoles are coupled into magnetic domains. An external field can align the domains, and the material can stay permanently magnetized after the field is removed. Strong attraction to a magnet.

FM
铁磁性（ferromagnetic）：铁、镍、钴

原子偶极子耦合成磁畴。外磁场可使磁畴排列整齐，撤去外场后材料可保持永久磁化。对磁铁有强吸引。

PM
Paramagnetic: aluminum, titanium, magnesium

Interacts only weakly with an external field. The dipoles do not stay aligned after the field is removed, so there is no lasting magnetization. Weak attraction.

PM
顺磁性（paramagnetic）：铝、钛、镁

与外磁场只有微弱相互作用。撤去外场后偶极子不会保持排列，所以没有持久磁化。表现为微弱吸引。

DM
Diamagnetic: all materials

Every material has diamagnetism: its electronic structure creates a usually weak alignment of dipole moments opposite to the external field. It is visible only when the stronger effects are absent, and it gives a weak repulsion.

DM
抗磁性（diamagnetic）：所有材料

每种材料都具有抗磁性：其电子结构使偶极矩产生通常很弱的、与外磁场相反的排列。只有在更强的效应不存在时才能看出，表现为微弱排斥。

Earth itself can be approximated as a magnetic dipole. Its field lines leave the surface in the southern hemisphere and enter in the northern. A compass needle's north end points toward geographic north because that end is attracted to the pole near geographic north, which is a magnetic south pole of Earth's dipole.

地球本身可近似为一个磁偶极子。其磁感线在南半球离开地表，在北半球进入。磁针北端指向地理北方，是因为该端被地理北极附近的极吸引，而那里是地球偶极子的磁南极。

Magnetic permeability磁导率

Magnetic permeability $\mu$ measures how much magnetization a material develops in response to an external field. Free space has a constant, defined value, the vacuum permeability, which appears in the equations of this unit:

磁导率 $\mu$ 衡量材料在外磁场作用下产生多大磁化。自由空间有一个恒定的确定值，即真空磁导率（vacuum permeability），它会出现在本单元的各个公式中：

Vacuum permeability真空磁导率
$$\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}\ \text{T}\cdot\text{m/A}$$

Analogous to $\epsilon_0$ for electric fields. Both are properties of empty space.

与电场中的 $\epsilon_0$ 类似。两者都是真空的性质。

The permeability of matter differs from $\mu_0$ and comes from the matter's composition and arrangement. It is not a constant for a given material: it varies with temperature, orientation, and the strength of the external field. For a ferromagnet it can be hundreds or thousands of times $\mu_0$ and changes as the material saturates; for paramagnets and diamagnets it differs from $\mu_0$ only slightly.

物质的磁导率不同于 $\mu_0$，由物质的组成与排列决定。对于给定材料它不是常数：会随温度、取向和外磁场强度变化。铁磁体的磁导率可达 $\mu_0$ 的数百乃至数千倍，并随材料趋于饱和而变化；顺磁体和抗磁体的磁导率与 $\mu_0$ 只有很小的差别。

Worked Example: Classify a material from its behavior例题：由行为判断材料类别

Three samples are placed near a strong magnet and then the magnet is removed. Sample A is pulled strongly toward the magnet and afterwards picks up paper clips. Sample B is pulled weakly toward the magnet and afterwards picks up nothing. Sample C is pushed weakly away from the magnet. Classify each sample and name the dipole behavior responsible.

把三个样品放在强磁铁附近，然后移走磁铁。样品 A 被强烈吸向磁铁，之后还能吸起回形针。样品 B 被微弱吸向磁铁，之后什么也吸不起。样品 C 被微弱推离磁铁。对每个样品分类，并指出起作用的偶极子行为。

Step 1第 1 步
Sample A: strong attraction and lasting magnetization means the dipoles stayed aligned after the field was removed. That is ferromagnetic (for example iron), with domains aligned by the external field.样品 A：强吸引并且持久磁化，说明撤去外场后偶极子仍保持排列。这是铁磁性（例如铁），磁畴被外磁场排列整齐。
Step 2第 2 步
Sample B: weak attraction means the dipoles aligned with the field, but picking up nothing afterwards means the alignment did not persist. That is paramagnetic (for example aluminum).样品 B：微弱吸引说明偶极子沿场方向排列，而之后什么也吸不起说明排列没有保持。这是顺磁性（例如铝）。
Step 3第 3 步
Sample C: a weak push away means the induced dipole moments point opposite to the external field. That is diamagnetism, which is present in every material but dominates only when paramagnetic and ferromagnetic effects are absent.样品 C：微弱的排斥说明感应偶极矩与外磁场相反。这是抗磁性，它存在于每种材料中，但只有在顺磁与铁磁效应不存在时才占主导。
Why this works为什么这样做
Each class is defined by two observable things: the sign of the response (attract or repel) and whether the alignment survives when the external field is removed.每一类都由两个可观察的量定义：响应的符号（吸引或排斥），以及撤去外场后排列是否保持。
Worked Example: Cut a magnet and place a compass例题：掰断磁铁并放置磁针

A bar magnet is cut in half across its middle. (a) Describe the poles of each half. (b) A compass is placed on the axis of the original magnet, near its north end, outside the magnet. Which way does the compass needle point, and how would the needle's torque change if the compass were moved much farther away?

把条形磁铁从中间横向切成两半。(a) 描述每一半的磁极。(b) 把磁针放在原磁铁的轴线上、靠近其北端、在磁铁外部。磁针指向哪个方向？如果把磁针移到更远处，磁针所受的力矩将如何变化？

Step 1第 1 步
Each half is still a dipole: the cut face of the north half becomes a new south pole, and the cut face of the south half becomes a new north pole. No isolated pole appears.每一半仍是偶极子：北半段的切面成为新的南极，南半段的切面成为新的北极。不会出现孤立的磁极。
Step 2第 2 步
Outside the magnet the field points away from the north end. A compass aligns its north end with $\vec{B}$, so near the north end of the magnet the needle's north end points directly away from the magnet.磁铁外部的磁场从北端指向外。磁针的北端与 $\vec{B}$ 对齐，所以在磁铁北端附近，磁针的北端指向远离磁铁的方向。
Step 3第 3 步
Far away, $|\vec{B}|$ is smaller, so the torque on the needle when it is displaced from alignment is smaller. The needle still points along $\vec{B}$, but it settles more weakly, and Earth's field becomes comparable to the magnet's field.在远处 $|\vec{B}|$ 较小，所以磁针偏离对齐位置时所受力矩较小。磁针仍沿 $\vec{B}$ 指向，但恢复得更弱，地球磁场将与磁铁的磁场相当。
Why this works为什么这样做
A dipole's field direction at a point decides the compass direction; its magnitude decides how firmly the needle is held there.某点偶极子场的方向决定磁针的指向，其大小决定磁针被固定得有多牢。

The field of a moving charge运动电荷的磁场

A single charge $q$ moving with velocity $\vec{v}$ (much less than the speed of light) produces, at a point displaced from it by $\vec{r}$, the magnetic field

以速度 $\vec{v}$（远小于光速）运动的单个电荷 $q$，在相对它位移为 $\vec{r}$ 的点产生的磁场为

Magnetic field of a moving point charge运动点电荷的磁场
$$\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\,\frac{q\,\vec{v}\times\hat{r}}{r^2}$$

Magnitude: $B = \dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{|q|\,v\sin\theta}{r^2}$, where $\theta$ is the angle between $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{r}$. This is the Biot-Savart law (Topic 12.3) for a single charge.

大小：$B = \dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{|q|\,v\sin\theta}{r^2}$，其中 $\theta$ 是 $\vec{v}$ 与 $\vec{r}$ 的夹角。这是单个电荷的毕奥-萨伐尔定律（专题 12.3）。

Three features follow directly from the cross product. The field depends on the charge's velocity and on the distance to the point (as $1/r^2$, like an electric field). Its direction is perpendicular to both $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{r}$, given by the right-hand rule: point the fingers of your right hand along $\vec{v}$, curl them toward $\hat{r}$, and the thumb gives the direction of $\vec{B}$ for $q>0$ (reverse it for $q<0$). The magnitude is a maximum when $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{r}$ are perpendicular, and it is zero along the line of motion, where $\sin\theta = 0$.

由叉乘可直接得出三个特点。磁场取决于电荷的速度和到该点的距离（如电场一样按 $1/r^2$）。其方向同时垂直于 $\vec{v}$ 和 $\vec{r}$，由右手定则给出：右手四指沿 $\vec{v}$，向 $\hat{r}$ 弯曲，拇指方向即 $q>0$ 时 $\vec{B}$ 的方向（$q<0$ 时反向）。当 $\vec{v}$ 与 $\vec{r}$ 垂直时大小最大，沿运动直线上 $\sin\theta = 0$，磁场为零。

A single moving charged object produces a magnetic field; a charge at rest does not. At a point in space, the magnetic field produced by the object depends on the object's velocity and on the distance between the point and the object: a faster charge produces a stronger field, and the field gets weaker as the distance from the charge grows.

单个运动的带电物体会产生磁场，静止的电荷则不会。空间某点由该物体产生的磁场取决于物体的速度和该点到物体的距离：电荷运动得越快，磁场越强；离电荷越远，磁场越弱。

The direction of the field at a point is perpendicular to both the velocity of the object and the position vector from the object to that point, and it is found with the right-hand rule. For a positive charge, point the thumb of your right hand along the velocity; your curled fingers show the direction in which the field circles the line of motion. For a negative charge, reverse the result. The magnitude of the field is a maximum at points where the velocity and the position vector are perpendicular, and it is zero at points on the line of motion.

该点磁场的方向同时垂直于物体的速度和从物体指向该点的位置矢量，用右手定则确定。对正电荷，右手拇指沿速度方向，弯曲的四指表示磁场绕运动直线环绕的方向；对负电荷，结果反向。在速度与位置矢量垂直的点，磁场大小最大；在运动直线上的点，磁场为零。

The field of a single moving charge is tiny. Measurable fields come from enormous numbers of charges moving together, which is a current (Topic 12.3).

单个运动电荷的磁场非常微弱。可测量的磁场来自大量电荷的共同运动，也就是电流（专题 12.3）。

The magnetic force on a moving charge运动电荷所受的磁力

A magnetic field exerts a force on a charge moving within it. The magnitude and direction of the force depend on the cross product of the charge's velocity and the field:

磁场对在其中运动的电荷施加力。力的大小与方向取决于电荷速度与磁场的叉乘：

Magnetic force on a moving charge运动电荷所受磁力
$$\vec{F}_B = q\,\vec{v}\times\vec{B},\qquad F_B = |q|\,v\,B\sin\theta$$

The force is perpendicular to both $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{B}$. For $q<0$ it points opposite to $\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$.

力同时垂直于 $\vec{v}$ 和 $\vec{B}$。对 $q<0$，力与 $\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$ 反向。

  • Zero force if $\vec{v}=0$, or if $\vec{v}$ is parallel or antiparallel to $\vec{B}$ ($\sin\theta = 0$). Maximum force when $\vec{v}\perp\vec{B}$.
  • Because $\vec{F}_B\perp\vec{v}$, the magnetic force does no work: $\vec{F}_B\cdot\vec{v}=0$. It changes the direction of the velocity but not the speed or kinetic energy.
  • Right-hand rule: fingers along $\vec{v}$, curl toward $\vec{B}$, thumb along $\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$. Flip the answer for a negative charge.
  • 若 $\vec{v}=0$，或 $\vec{v}$ 与 $\vec{B}$ 平行或反平行（$\sin\theta = 0$），则力为零。$\vec{v}\perp\vec{B}$ 时力最大。
  • 因为 $\vec{F}_B\perp\vec{v}$，磁力不做功：$\vec{F}_B\cdot\vec{v}=0$。它改变速度的方向，但不改变速率或动能。
  • 右手定则：四指沿 $\vec{v}$，向 $\vec{B}$ 弯曲，拇指沿 $\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$。负电荷的结果要反向。

A magnetic field may exert a force on a charged object moving in that field. The magnitude of the force is proportional to the magnitude of the charge, to the speed of the object, and to the magnitude of the field, and it also depends on the angle between the velocity and the field:

磁场可能对在其中运动的带电物体施加力。力的大小与电荷量的大小、物体的速率和磁场的大小成正比，并且还取决于速度与磁场之间的夹角：

Magnetic force on a moving charge运动电荷所受磁力
$$F_B = |q|\,v\,B\sin\theta$$

Here $F_B$ is the magnitude of the magnetic force (N), $|q|$ the magnitude of the charge (C), $v$ the speed (m/s), $B$ the magnitude of the magnetic field (T), and $\theta$ the angle between $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{B}$.

其中 $F_B$ 为磁力的大小（N），$|q|$ 为电荷量的大小（C），$v$ 为速率（m/s），$B$ 为磁场的大小（T），$\theta$ 为 $\vec{v}$ 与 $\vec{B}$ 的夹角。

范围说明：0、90 和 180 度 AP 物理 2 把运动电荷所受磁力大小的定量处理限于速度与磁场夹角为 0、90 和 180 度的情形。其他角度只作定性分析。
  • Zero force if $v=0$ (a charge at rest feels no magnetic force), or if $\vec{v}$ is parallel or antiparallel to $\vec{B}$ ($\theta = 0$ or $180^\circ$, so $\sin\theta = 0$). Maximum force $F_B = |q|vB$ when $\vec{v}\perp\vec{B}$ ($\theta = 90^\circ$).
  • At any other angle the force is not zero but is smaller than the maximum, and it grows as the angle approaches $90^\circ$. This is treated qualitatively only.
  • The force is perpendicular to both $\vec{B}$ and $\vec{v}$. Right-hand rule: fingers along the velocity, curl them toward the field, and the thumb gives the force on a positive charge. Reverse the answer for a negative charge.
  • Because the force is perpendicular to the velocity, the magnetic force does no work. It changes the direction of the velocity but not the speed or kinetic energy.
  • 若 $v=0$（静止的电荷不受磁力），或 $\vec{v}$ 与 $\vec{B}$ 平行或反平行（$\theta = 0$ 或 $180^\circ$，即 $\sin\theta = 0$），则力为零。$\vec{v}\perp\vec{B}$（$\theta = 90^\circ$）时力最大，$F_B = |q|vB$。
  • 在其他任何角度，力不为零但小于最大值，并随角度接近 $90^\circ$ 而增大。这只作定性处理。
  • 力同时垂直于 $\vec{B}$ 和 $\vec{v}$。右手定则：四指沿速度方向，向磁场弯曲，拇指给出正电荷所受的力。对负电荷，结果要反向。
  • 因为力垂直于速度，磁力不做功。它改变速度的方向，但不改变速率或动能。

The Hall effect霍尔效应

The Hall effect describes the potential difference created across a conductor by an external magnetic field that has a component perpendicular to the direction in which charges move in the conductor. Take a flat strip of width $w$ (the direction across which we measure) and thickness $t$ (the dimension along $\vec{B}$), carrying current $I$ with $\vec{B}$ perpendicular to the strip. The magnetic force pushes the carriers, whatever their sign, toward one edge. Charge piles up there and leaves the opposite edge with the opposite charge, so a transverse electric field $E_H$ builds until its force cancels the magnetic force:

霍尔效应指外磁场在导体中产生电势差，该磁场有垂直于导体中电荷运动方向的分量。取宽为 $w$（测量电压的方向）、厚为 $t$（沿 $\vec{B}$ 的尺寸）的扁平导体条，载有电流 $I$，$\vec{B}$ 垂直于导体条。磁力把载流子（无论正负）推向一侧边缘。电荷在那里堆积，使对边带相反的电荷，于是横向电场 $E_H$ 不断增大，直到其力与磁力抵消：

Hall voltage霍尔电压
$$qE_H = qv_dB\;\Rightarrow\; V_H = E_H w = v_d B w,\qquad I = nqv_d(wt)\;\Rightarrow\; V_H = \frac{IB}{nqt}$$

Here $n$ is the carrier number density and $v_d$ the drift speed. The sign of the Hall voltage reveals the sign of the carriers.

其中 $n$ 为载流子数密度，$v_d$ 为漂移速率。霍尔电压的符号揭示载流子的电性。

The Hall effect describes the potential difference created across a conductor by an external magnetic field that has a component perpendicular to the direction in which the charges move in the conductor. Consider a flat conductor that carries a current in a perpendicular magnetic field. The magnetic force pushes the moving charge carriers, whatever their sign, toward one edge of the conductor. Charge collects on that edge and leaves the opposite edge with the opposite charge, so a potential difference, the Hall voltage, appears across the conductor. It stops growing when the electric force from the collected charge on a carrier balances the magnetic force on it, so that the carriers again move straight along the conductor.

霍尔效应指外磁场在导体两侧产生电势差，该磁场有垂直于导体中电荷运动方向的分量。设一块扁平导体在垂直磁场中载有电流。磁力把运动的载流子（无论正负）推向导体的一侧边缘。电荷在那里堆积，使对边带相反的电荷，于是导体两侧出现电势差，即霍尔电压。当堆积的电荷对载流子的电场力与磁力平衡时，电压不再增大，载流子重新沿导体直线运动。

Electrons and positive carriers that make up the same conventional current are pushed toward the same edge. That edge therefore becomes negative if the carriers are electrons and positive if the carriers are positive, so the polarity of the Hall voltage reveals the sign of the charge carriers.

构成同一传统电流的电子和正载流子被推向同一侧边缘。所以载流子为电子时该边带负电，载流子为正电荷时该边带正电，霍尔电压的极性因而揭示载流子的电性。

The field of a long, straight wire长直导线的磁场

The magnetic field vectors around a long, straight, current-carrying wire are tangent to concentric circles centered on the wire. The field has no component toward the wire, away from the wire, or parallel to the wire.

长直载流导线周围的磁场矢量与以导线为中心的同心圆相切。磁场没有指向导线、背离导线或平行于导线的分量。

At a point in space, the magnitude of the field of a long, straight, current-carrying wire is proportional to the current in the wire and inversely proportional to the perpendicular distance from the central axis of the wire to the point:

空间某点处，长直载流导线磁场的大小与导线中的电流成正比，与导线中心轴到该点的垂直距离成反比：

Field of a long, straight wire长直导线的磁场
$$B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$

Here $B$ is the field magnitude (T), $I$ the current (A), $r$ the perpendicular distance from the central axis of the wire to the point (m), and $\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}\ \text{T}\cdot\text{m/A}$ is the vacuum permeability. The field is the sum of the fields of all the moving charges in the wire, and by symmetry it has the same magnitude at every point on a circle around the wire. Doubling the current doubles the field; doubling the distance halves it.

其中 $B$ 为磁场大小（T），$I$ 为电流（A），$r$ 为导线中心轴到该点的垂直距离（m），$\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}\ \text{T}\cdot\text{m/A}$ 为真空磁导率。该磁场是导线中所有运动电荷磁场之和，由对称性，在绕导线的圆上每一点大小相同。电流加倍，磁场加倍；距离加倍，磁场减半。

Direction: use the right-hand rule. Point the thumb of your right hand along the conventional current; your curled fingers show the direction in which the field circles the wire.

方向：用右手定则。右手拇指沿传统电流方向，弯曲的四指表示磁场绕导线环绕的方向。

A current-carrying loop produces a magnetic field that behaves like the field of a small bar magnet. At the center of the loop the field is directed along the axis of the loop, perpendicular to the plane of the loop. To find its direction, curl the fingers of your right hand in the direction of the current around the loop; your thumb points along the field at the center.

在两根或多根载流导线附近，某点的磁场用矢量加法求得：对每根导线分别用 $B = \mu_0 I/(2\pi r)$ 和右手定则，再把各个磁场按矢量相加。方向相同的磁场相加，方向相反的磁场相减。

The Biot-Savart law毕奥-萨伐尔定律

A small segment of wire of length $d\vec{\ell}$ (pointing along the conventional current $I$) produces, at a point displaced from the segment by $\vec{r}$, the field

长度为 $d\vec{\ell}$（沿传统电流 $I$ 的方向）的导线小段，在相对该段位移为 $\vec{r}$ 的点产生磁场

Biot-Savart law毕奥-萨伐尔定律
$$d\vec{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\,\frac{I\,d\vec{\ell}\times\hat{r}}{r^2},\qquad \vec{B} = \int d\vec{B}$$

It follows from the moving-charge field by replacing $q\vec{v}$ with $I\,d\vec{\ell}$. Magnitude of one piece: $dB = \dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{I\,d\ell\,\sin\theta}{r^2}$.

把运动电荷的磁场中的 $q\vec{v}$ 换成 $I\,d\vec{\ell}$ 即得。单个小段的大小：$dB = \dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{I\,d\ell\,\sin\theta}{r^2}$。

Because $d\vec{B}\propto d\vec{\ell}\times\hat{r}$, the field vectors around a small segment are tangent to concentric circles centered on the wire. The field has no component toward or away from the segment, and none parallel to it, since the cross product is perpendicular to both $d\vec{\ell}$ and $\hat{r}$. Use the right-hand rule: thumb along the current, fingers curl in the direction of $\vec{B}$.

由于 $d\vec{B}\propto d\vec{\ell}\times\hat{r}$，小段周围的磁场矢量与以导线为中心的同心圆相切。磁场没有指向或背离该段的分量，也没有平行于它的分量，因为叉乘同时垂直于 $d\vec{\ell}$ 和 $\hat{r}$。用右手定则：拇指沿电流，四指弯曲的方向就是 $\vec{B}$ 的方向。

Boundary: only the standard geometries Quantitative Biot-Savart work is limited to a point on the perpendicular bisector of a straight conductor, a point on the central axis of a circular loop, and the center of a circular-loop segment. Learn these three derivations well; use symmetry and superposition for combinations of them.
范围说明：只考查标准几何形状 毕奥-萨伐尔定律的定量计算限于：直导线中垂线上的一点、圆环中心轴线上的一点，以及圆环一段圆弧的中心。请熟练掌握这三个推导；对它们的组合用对称性与叠加原理。

Straight wire: the perpendicular bisector直导线：中垂线

Place a straight wire of length $L$ along the $x$-axis from $-L/2$ to $L/2$, carrying $I$ in $+x$, and find $\vec{B}$ at $P=(0,a)$. For a piece at position $x$, $r=\sqrt{x^2+a^2}$ and $\sin\theta = a/r$. All pieces give $d\vec{B}$ in the same direction (out of the page, by the right-hand rule), so the magnitudes can be added directly:

把长为 $L$ 的直导线沿 $x$ 轴从 $-L/2$ 放到 $L/2$，电流 $I$ 沿 $+x$，求 $P=(0,a)$ 处的 $\vec{B}$。对位置为 $x$ 的小段，$r=\sqrt{x^2+a^2}$，$\sin\theta = a/r$。所有小段产生的 $d\vec{B}$ 方向相同（由右手定则垂直纸面向外），所以大小可直接相加：

Derivation推导
$$B = \int_{-L/2}^{L/2}\frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi}\frac{a\,dx}{(x^2+a^2)^{3/2}} = \frac{\mu_0 I a}{4\pi}\left[\frac{x}{a^2\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}\right]_{-L/2}^{L/2} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi a}\,\frac{L/2}{\sqrt{a^2+(L/2)^2}}$$

For $L\gg a$ the last factor tends to $1$ and the field becomes $B=\mu_0 I/(2\pi a)$, the field of a long straight wire (derived again with Ampere's law in Topic 12.4). The factor $\sin\theta = (L/2)/\sqrt{a^2+(L/2)^2}$ is always less than $1$, so a finite wire gives a smaller field than an infinite one at the same distance.

当 $L\gg a$ 时最后一个因子趋近于 $1$，磁场变为 $B=\mu_0 I/(2\pi a)$，即长直导线的磁场（专题 12.4 将用安培环路定律再次导出）。因子 $\sin\theta = (L/2)/\sqrt{a^2+(L/2)^2}$ 总是小于 $1$，所以在同样距离处，有限长导线的磁场比无限长的小。

一根 $0.40\ \text{m}$ 长的直导线载有 $5.0\ \text{A}$ 电流。求在其中垂线上距导线 $0.10\ \text{m}$ 处的磁场，并与同一距离处无限长导线的磁场比较。

$$\sin\theta = \frac{0.20}{\sqrt{0.10^2+0.20^2}} = 0.894$$
Step 2第 2 步

$$B_\infty = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi a} = \frac{(2\times10^{-7})(5.0)}{0.10} = 1.0\times10^{-5}\ \text{T}$$
Step 3第 3 步
The finite wire:有限长导线：
$$B = B_\infty\sin\theta = 8.9\times10^{-6}\ \text{T}$$
Step 4第 4 步
This is about $10.6\%$ less than $B_\infty$. The direction is given by the right-hand rule: tangent to a circle around the wire.它比 $B_\infty$ 小约 $10.6\%$。方向由右手定则确定：与绕导线的圆相切。

Circular loop: the central axis圆环：中心轴线

Take a circular loop of radius $R$ in the $xy$ plane carrying current $I$, and a point on the axis at distance $z$ from its center. Every piece $d\vec{\ell}$ is perpendicular to the vector $\vec{r}$ from the piece to the point, so $|d\vec{\ell}\times\hat{r}|=d\ell$ and $r^2 = R^2+z^2$. By symmetry the components of $d\vec{B}$ perpendicular to the axis cancel in pairs from opposite sides of the loop. Only the axial component survives, and it is $dB\cdot(R/r)$:

取 $xy$ 平面内半径为 $R$、电流为 $I$ 的圆环，以及轴线上距圆心 $z$ 处的一点。每一小段 $d\vec{\ell}$ 都垂直于从该段指向该点的矢量 $\vec{r}$，所以 $|d\vec{\ell}\times\hat{r}|=d\ell$，且 $r^2 = R^2+z^2$。由对称性，$d\vec{B}$ 垂直于轴线的分量在圆环对边成对抵消。只有轴向分量保留，它等于 $dB\cdot(R/r)$：

Derivation推导
$$B_z = \oint\frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi}\frac{d\ell}{r^2}\frac{R}{r} = \frac{\mu_0 I R}{4\pi (R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\oint d\ell = \frac{\mu_0 I R^2}{2\,(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}$$
Loop field on the axis and at the center圆环轴线上与圆心处的磁场
$$B_z = \frac{\mu_0 I R^2}{2\,(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}},\qquad B_{\text{center}} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2R}$$

Set $z=0$ for the center. The direction is along the axis, given by the right-hand rule: curl the fingers of your right hand along the current and the thumb points along $\vec{B}$.

令 $z=0$ 得圆心处的磁场。方向沿轴线，由右手定则确定：右手四指沿电流弯曲，拇指指向 $\vec{B}$ 的方向。

For an arc subtending angle $\varphi$ (in radians) at the center, all pieces are the same distance $R$ from the center and perpendicular to the radius, so the contributions simply add in proportion to arc length $R\varphi$:

对圆心角为 $\varphi$（弧度）的圆弧，所有小段到圆心的距离都是 $R$，并垂直于半径，所以各贡献按弧长 $R\varphi$ 成比例相加：

Center of a circular arc圆弧的圆心
$$B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi R}\,\varphi$$

A full loop is $\varphi=2\pi$ and gives $\mu_0I/2R$. A straight section pointing directly toward or away from the center has $d\vec{\ell}\parallel\hat{r}$, so it contributes zero there.

整圆 $\varphi=2\pi$，给出 $\mu_0I/2R$。正对着或背着圆心的直线段有 $d\vec{\ell}\parallel\hat{r}$，所以在圆心处贡献为零。

Worked Example: Loop on and off the center例题：圆心与轴线上的圆环磁场

A circular loop of radius $R=0.050\ \text{m}$ carries $I=3.0\ \text{A}$. Find the field (a) at its center and (b) on its axis at $z=0.12\ \text{m}$ from the center. (c) What fraction of the center value is the field at $z=R$?

Step 1第 1 步
At the center:圆心处：
$$B_{\text{center}} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2R} = \frac{(4\pi\times10^{-7})(3.0)}{2(0.050)} = 3.8\times10^{-5}\ \text{T}$$
Step 2第 2 步
On the axis, with $R^2+z^2 = 0.0025+0.0144 = 0.0169\ \text{m}^2$:在轴线上，$R^2+z^2 = 0.0025+0.0144 = 0.0169\ \text{m}^2$：
$$B_z = \frac{(4\pi\times10^{-7})(3.0)(0.050)^2}{2\,(0.0169)^{3/2}} = 2.1\times10^{-6}\ \text{T}$$
Step 3第 3 步
At $z=R$, $(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}=(2R^2)^{3/2}=2^{3/2}R^3$, so在 $z=R$ 处，$(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}=(2R^2)^{3/2}=2^{3/2}R^3$，所以
$$\frac{B_z}{B_{\text{center}}} = \frac{1}{2^{3/2}} = 0.354$$
Why this works为什么这样做
The field falls rapidly along the axis. At $z\gg R$ the loop field behaves like $1/z^3$, the signature of a magnetic dipole (compare Topic 12.1).沿轴线磁场迅速减小。当 $z\gg R$ 时圆环的磁场表现为 $1/z^3$，这是磁偶极子的特征（对比专题 12.1）。
Worked Example: Two concentric semicircles例题：两个同心半圆弧

A wire carries $I=6.0\ \text{A}$ along a closed path made of a semicircular arc of radius $b=0.050\ \text{m}$ traversed counterclockwise, a straight radial section, a semicircular arc of radius $a=0.020\ \text{m}$ traversed clockwise, and a second straight radial section. All arcs share the center $P$. Find the field at $P$.

导线载有 $I=6.0\ \text{A}$，沿一条闭合路径流动：半径 $b=0.050\ \text{m}$ 的半圆弧按逆时针走，一段径向直线，半径 $a=0.020\ \text{m}$ 的半圆弧按顺时针走，另一段径向直线。所有圆弧共圆心 $P$。求 $P$ 处的磁场。

The radial straight sections point along $\hat{r}$, so $d\vec{\ell}\times\hat{r}=0$ and they add nothing at $P$.径向直线段沿 $\hat{r}$，所以 $d\vec{\ell}\times\hat{r}=0$，它们在 $P$ 处没有贡献。
Step 2第 2 步

$$B_b = \frac{\mu_0 I}{4b} = 3.8\times10^{-5}\ \text{T (out)},\qquad B_a = \frac{\mu_0 I}{4a} = 9.4\times10^{-5}\ \text{T (in)}$$

Superpose, taking out of the page as positive:叠加，取垂直纸面向外为正：
$$B_P = \frac{\mu_0 I}{4}\left(\frac{1}{b}-\frac{1}{a}\right) = -5.7\times10^{-5}\ \text{T}$$
Step 4第 4 步
The field at $P$ has magnitude $5.7\times10^{-5}\ \text{T}$ and points into the page, because the smaller arc is closer to $P$ and wins.$P$ 处磁场大小为 $5.7\times10^{-5}\ \text{T}$，方向垂直纸面向内，因为较小的圆弧离 $P$ 更近，贡献更大。
Why this works为什么这样做
Break the wire into pieces whose fields are known, check the direction of each with the right-hand rule, and add with signs.把导线拆成磁场已知的几段，用右手定则检查每段的方向，再带符号相加。

The magnetic force on a current-carrying wire载流导线所受的磁力

A wire contains many moving charges, and the magnetic force on each adds up. For a straight segment of length $L$ in a uniform field, and for a general piece $d\vec{\ell}$ in any field:

导线中有许多运动电荷，每个电荷所受磁力叠加。对均匀磁场中长为 $L$ 的直导线，以及任意磁场中的一般小段 $d\vec{\ell}$：

Force on a current-carrying wire载流导线所受的力
$$\vec{F} = I\,\vec{L}\times\vec{B},\qquad d\vec{F} = I\,d\vec{\ell}\times\vec{B}$$

Magnitude $F=ILB\sin\theta$. $\vec{L}$ points along the conventional current. For a curved wire in a uniform field, $\vec{F}=I\vec{L}_{\text{chord}}\times\vec{B}$, where $\vec{L}_{\text{chord}}$ is the straight vector from the start to the end of the wire.

大小 $F=ILB\sin\theta$。$\vec{L}$ 沿传统电流方向。对均匀磁场中的弯曲导线，$\vec{F}=I\vec{L}_{\text{chord}}\times\vec{B}$，其中 $\vec{L}_{\text{chord}}$ 是从导线起点到终点的直线矢量。

A magnetic field may exert a force on a current-carrying wire. The magnitude of the force is proportional to the current, to the length of the portion of the wire within the field, and to the magnitude of the field, and it also depends on the angle between the direction of the current in the wire and the direction of the magnetic field:

磁场可能对载流导线施加力。力的大小与电流、导线处于磁场中的那部分长度和磁场的大小成正比，并且还取决于导线中电流方向与磁场方向之间的夹角：

Force on a current-carrying wire载流导线所受的力
$$F = ILB\sin\theta$$

Here $F$ is the magnitude of the force (N), $I$ the current (A), $L$ the length of wire within the field (m), $B$ the magnitude of the field (T), and $\theta$ the angle between the direction of the current and the direction of the field. The force is zero when the current is parallel or antiparallel to the field ($\theta = 0$ or $180^\circ$) and is a maximum, $F = ILB$, when the current is perpendicular to the field.

其中 $F$ 为力的大小（N），$I$ 为电流（A），$L$ 为导线处于磁场中的长度（m），$B$ 为磁场的大小（T），$\theta$ 为电流方向与磁场方向之间的夹角。当电流与磁场平行或反平行（$\theta = 0$ 或 $180^\circ$）时力为零；当电流垂直于磁场时力最大，$F = ILB$。

The force is perpendicular to both the wire and the field, and its direction follows the right-hand rule: point the fingers of your right hand along the conventional current, curl them toward the magnetic field, and your thumb gives the direction of the force.

力垂直于导线和磁场，方向遵循右手定则：右手四指沿传统电流方向，向磁场弯曲，拇指就给出力的方向。

Two parallel wires each create a field at the other. Wire 1 produces $B_1=\mu_0I_1/(2\pi d)$ at wire 2, so wire 2 feels a force per unit length $F/L = I_2B_1$. The result is symmetric, and the direction follows from the right-hand rule twice: parallel currents attract; antiparallel currents repel.

两根平行导线各在对方处产生磁场。导线 1 在导线 2 处产生 $B_1=\mu_0I_1/(2\pi d)$，所以导线 2 受到单位长度的力 $F/L = I_2B_1$。结果是对称的，方向由两次右手定则得出：同向电流相吸，反向电流相斥。

Force per unit length between parallel wires平行导线之间单位长度的力
$$\frac{F}{L} = \frac{\mu_0 I_1 I_2}{2\pi d}$$

(a) A straight wire $0.50\ \text{m}$ long carries $6.0\ \text{A}$ at $30^\circ$ to a uniform field of $0.040\ \text{T}$. Find the force. Step 1第 1 步
(a) Use $F=ILB\sin\theta$:(a) 用 $F=ILB\sin\theta$：
$$F = (6.0)(0.50)(0.040)\sin30^\circ = 0.060\ \text{N}$$
Step 2第 2 步
The direction is along $\vec{L}\times\vec{B}$, perpendicular to both the wire and the field.方向沿 $\vec{L}\times\vec{B}$，垂直于导线和磁场。
Step 3第 3 步
(b) Use $F/L=\mu_0 I_1I_2/(2\pi d)$:(b) 用 $F/L=\mu_0 I_1I_2/(2\pi d)$：
$$\frac{F}{L} = \frac{(2\times10^{-7})(10)(20)}{0.050} = 8.0\times10^{-4}\ \text{N/m}$$

Currents in the same direction attract.同向电流相吸。
Why this works为什么这样做

The Biot-Savart law always works but often leads to a hard integral. When a current distribution has enough symmetry, there is a shortcut, just as Gauss's law is a shortcut for electric fields. Ampere's law relates the line integral of the magnetic field around any closed imaginary path, called an Amperian loop, to the current that passes through the loop:

毕奥-萨伐尔定律总是适用，但往往导致困难的积分。当电流分布有足够的对称性时，就有捷径，就像高斯定律是电场的捷径一样。安培环路定律（Ampere's law）把磁场沿任意闭合假想路径（称为安培环路）的线积分，与穿过该环路的电流联系起来：

Ampere's law安培环路定律
$$\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{\ell} = \mu_0\,I_{\text{enc}}$$

$I_{\text{enc}}$ is the net current through any surface bounded by the loop. Choose the loop direction, then curl the fingers of your right hand along it: currents along your thumb are positive, currents the other way are negative.

$I_{\text{enc}}$ 是穿过以该环路为边界的任意曲面的净电流。先选定环路方向，右手四指沿此方向弯曲：沿拇指方向的电流为正，反方向的电流为负。

Ampere's law is true always, useful sometimes To pull $B$ out of the integral you need a loop on which $\vec{B}$ is either parallel to $d\vec{\ell}$ with constant magnitude, or perpendicular to it (or zero). That requires symmetry. The CED expects quantitative use only for long straight wires, long solenoids, and conducting cylinders or slabs carrying a current density.
安培环路定律永远成立，但只有时才好用 要把 $B$ 从积分号中提出来，需要一个环路，使 $\vec{B}$ 在环路上要么与 $d\vec{\ell}$ 平行且大小恒定，要么垂直于它（或为零）。这需要对称性。CED 只期望对长直导线、长螺线管以及载有电流密度的导电圆柱或平板进行定量应用。

Long straight wire长直导线

By symmetry the field of a long straight wire has the same magnitude all around a circle of radius $r$ centered on the wire, and is tangent to it. Take that circle as the Amperian loop. Then $\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{\ell}=B(2\pi r)$ and $I_{\text{enc}}=I$:

由对称性，长直导线的磁场在以导线为中心、半径为 $r$ 的圆周上各处大小相同，并与圆周相切。取该圆为安培环路。则 $\oint \vec{B}\cdot d\vec{\ell}=B(2\pi r)$，$I_{\text{enc}}=I$：

Field of a long straight wire长直导线的磁场
$$B(2\pi r) = \mu_0 I\;\Rightarrow\; B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$

This agrees with the limit of the finite-wire Biot-Savart result in Topic 12.3.

这与专题 12.3 中有限长导线毕奥-萨伐尔结果的极限一致。

Cylinders and slabs carrying current density载有电流密度的圆柱与平板

Take a long cylindrical conductor of radius $R$ with current density $J(r)$ along its axis. Use circular Amperian loops of radius $r$ about the axis. The enclosed current is the integral of $J$ over the area inside the loop, using rings of area $dA = 2\pi r'\,dr'$:

Enclosed current and field in a cylinder圆柱中的电流与磁场
$$I_{\text{enc}}(r)=\int_0^{r}J(r')\,2\pi r'\,dr',\qquad B(r)=\frac{\mu_0\,I_{\text{enc}}(r)}{2\pi r}$$

For $r>R$, $I_{\text{enc}}=I$ (the total), so outside the cylinder $B=\mu_0 I/2\pi r$, exactly as for a thin wire carrying the same current.

The long solenoid长螺线管

Unless otherwise stated, a solenoid is assumed to be very long, with a uniform field inside and a negligible field outside. Take a rectangular Amperian loop of length $\ell$ with one side inside the solenoid, parallel to the axis. The other three sides contribute nothing: the end sides are perpendicular to $\vec{B}$ (or lie where $B\approx0$), and the outside side lies where $B\approx 0$. If the solenoid has $n$ turns per unit length, the loop encloses $n\ell$ turns, so $I_{\text{enc}}=n\ell I$:

除非另有说明，螺线管都假定为很长，内部磁场均匀，外部磁场可忽略。取长为 $\ell$ 的矩形安培环路，其一边在螺线管内、平行于轴线。其余三边没有贡献：两端边垂直于 $\vec{B}$（或位于 $B\approx0$ 处），外侧边位于 $B\approx 0$ 处。若螺线管单位长度有 $n$ 匝，环路包围 $n\ell$ 匝，所以 $I_{\text{enc}}=n\ell I$：

Field inside a long solenoid长螺线管内部的磁场
$$B\,\ell = \mu_0\,n\ell\,I\;\Rightarrow\; B_{\text{sol}} = \mu_0 n I$$

$B$ depends on the turns per unit length $n=N/\ell_{\text{sol}}$ and the current, not on the solenoid's radius. Direction: curl the fingers of the right hand along the current in the turns and the thumb gives $\vec{B}$ along the axis.

$B$ 取决于单位长度匝数 $n=N/\ell_{\text{sol}}$ 和电流，与螺线管半径无关。方向：右手四指沿线圈中电流弯曲，拇指给出沿轴线的 $\vec{B}$。

Superposition of fields磁场的叠加

The principle of superposition lets you combine the fields found above: the net field at a point is the vector sum of the fields of each wire, loop, segment, or cylinder. Find each contribution separately with its own right-hand rule, then add the vectors. A coaxial cable shows the idea: the inner conductor carries $I$ one way and the outer shell $I$ back, so Ampere's law gives $B=\mu_0I/2\pi r$ between them and $B=0$ outside both (net enclosed current zero).

叠加原理使你可以组合上面得到的各个磁场：某点的净磁场是各导线、线圈、线段或圆柱各自磁场的矢量和。分别用右手定则求出每个贡献，再做矢量相加。同轴电缆就体现了这一思想：内导体载有 $I$ 向一个方向，外壳载有 $I$ 返回，所以安培环路定律给出两者之间 $B=\mu_0I/2\pi r$，两者之外 $B=0$（净环路电流为零）。

Top Mistakes to Avoid
  1. Forgetting the field lines inside a magnet: they close on themselves.
  2. Crossing in the wrong order ($\vec{B}\times\vec{v}$ instead of $\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$) or forgetting to reverse for a negative charge.
  3. Forgetting to reverse the right-hand-rule direction for a negative charge.
  4. Saying the magnetic force changes a particle's speed or does work.
  5. Using the whole $v$ in $r=mv/qB$ for helical motion: only $v_\perp$ belongs there.
  6. Treating $B=\mu_0I/2\pi r$ as valid inside a cylinder, or an infinite-wire formula for a short wire.
  7. Taking the current through the loop from the wrong sign convention in Ampere's law.
  8. Using $N$ instead of $n=N/\ell$ in $B=\mu_0nI$.
  9. Treating the field of a wire as pointing toward or away from the wire: it circles around the wire.
  10. Using $F=ILB$ without checking the angle: the force is zero when the current is parallel to the field.
  11. Adding the fields of two wires as plain numbers: they are vectors, and fields in opposite directions subtract.
最常见的错误
  1. 忘了磁铁内部的磁感线：它们是自行闭合的。
  2. 叉乘次序错（用 $\vec{B}\times\vec{v}$ 代替 $\vec{v}\times\vec{B}$），或对负电荷忘了反向。
  3. 对负电荷忘了把右手定则得到的方向反向。
  4. 说磁力改变粒子的速率或做功。
  5. 在螺旋运动的 $r=mv/qB$ 中用整个 $v$：只有 $v_\perp$ 属于那里。
  6. 把 $B=\mu_0I/2\pi r$ 用在圆柱内部，或把无限长导线公式用于短导线。
  7. 在安培环路定律中对穿过环路的电流使用了错误的符号约定。
  8. 在 $B=\mu_0nI$ 中用 $N$ 代替 $n=N/\ell$。
  9. 把导线的磁场当作指向导线或背离导线：它是绕导线环绕的。
  10. 不检查夹角就使用 $F=ILB$：电流与磁场平行时力为零。
  11. 把两根导线的磁场当作普通数字相加：它们是矢量，方向相反的磁场要相减。
