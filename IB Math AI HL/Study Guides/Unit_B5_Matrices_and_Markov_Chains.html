Order and elements阶与元素

A matrix with $m$ rows and $n$ columns has order $m \times n$ (rows first, always). The order of a matrix decides which operations are allowed, so state it before you calculate. The element in row $i$ and column $j$ is written $a_{ij}$. Two matrices are equal when they have the same order and every pair of corresponding elements is equal, which lets you set up equations element by element. A matrix with one column is a column vector; this guide writes points, state vectors and unknowns as column vectors.

有 $m$ 行 $n$ 列的矩阵，其阶（order）为 $m \times n$（永远先行后列）。矩阵的阶决定哪些运算可以进行，所以计算前先写出阶。第 $i$ 行第 $j$ 列的元素记作 $a_{ij}$。两个矩阵同阶且对应元素逐一相等时，称它们相等，因此可以逐个元素列方程。只有一列的矩阵称为列向量（column vector）；本指南中的点、状态向量与未知量都写成列向量。

Addition, scalar multiples and the product of two matrices加法、数乘与两个矩阵的乘积

Addition and subtraction work element by element and need matrices of the same order. A scalar multiple $kA$ multiplies every element by $k$. Matrix multiplication is different: the element in row $i$, column $j$ of $AB$ is row $i$ of $A$ multiplied term by term with column $j$ of $B$, then summed. So the product of two matrices exists only when the number of columns of $A$ equals the number of rows of $B$:

加减法逐个元素进行，要求两矩阵同阶。数乘 $kA$ 把每个元素乘以 $k$。矩阵乘法（matrix multiplication）不同：$AB$ 第 $i$ 行第 $j$ 列的元素，是 $A$ 的第 $i$ 行与 $B$ 的第 $j$ 列对应项相乘后求和。所以只有当 $A$ 的列数等于 $B$ 的行数时，两矩阵的乘积才存在：

$$ (m \times n)\,(n \times p) \;\to\; m \times p, \qquad AB \neq BA \text{ in general}, \qquad AI = IA = A. $$

The identity matrix $I$ is square with 1s on the leading diagonal and 0s elsewhere; it plays the role of the number 1. The zero matrix $O$ has every element 0. Common misconception: because $AB$ and $BA$ are usually different, you cannot "divide" or cancel from either side freely; you must multiply on the same side of both sides of an equation.

单位矩阵（identity matrix）$I$ 为方阵，主对角线上为 1，其余为 0，作用相当于数字 1。零矩阵（zero matrix）$O$ 的所有元素都是 0。常见误解：由于 $AB$ 与 $BA$ 通常不同，不能随意在任一侧"相除"或约去；对等式两边做乘法时必须乘在同一侧。

Worked Example: sums and products of 2 × 2 matrices例题：2 × 2 矩阵的和与积

Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -2 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$. Find (a) $2A - B$, (b) $AB$, (c) $BA$, and comment on (b) and (c).设 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$，$B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -2 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$。求 (a) $2A - B$，(b) $AB$，(c) $BA$，并比较 (b) 与 (c)。

(a) Double every element of $A$, then subtract element by element: $2A - B = \begin{pmatrix} 4 - 1 & -2 - 0 \\ 6 + 2 & 8 - 5 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 8 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

(a) 先把 $A$ 的每个元素乘以 2，再逐个元素相减：$2A - B = \begin{pmatrix} 4 - 1 & -2 - 0 \\ 6 + 2 & 8 - 5 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 8 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$。

(b) Row by column: $AB = \begin{pmatrix} 2(1) + (-1)(-2) & 2(0) + (-1)(5) \\ 3(1) + 4(-2) & 3(0) + 4(5) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & -5 \\ -5 & 20 \end{pmatrix}$.

(b) 行乘列：$AB = \begin{pmatrix} 2(1) + (-1)(-2) & 2(0) + (-1)(5) \\ 3(1) + 4(-2) & 3(0) + 4(5) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & -5 \\ -5 & 20 \end{pmatrix}$。

(c) $BA = \begin{pmatrix} 1(2) + 0(3) & 1(-1) + 0(4) \\ -2(2) + 5(3) & -2(-1) + 5(4) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 11 & 22 \end{pmatrix}$.

(c) $BA = \begin{pmatrix} 1(2) + 0(3) & 1(-1) + 0(4) \\ -2(2) + 5(3) & -2(-1) + 5(4) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 11 & 22 \end{pmatrix}$。

Comment. $AB \neq BA$: the order of the factors matters. On a GDC, enter $A$ and $B$ in the matrix editor and evaluate $A \times B$ and $B \times A$ to confirm.

说明。$AB \neq BA$：因子的先后顺序很重要。在 GDC 的矩阵编辑器中输入 $A$ 与 $B$，分别计算 $A \times B$ 与 $B \times A$ 加以确认。

Why this works. Each element of a product combines a whole row with a whole column, so swapping the factors pairs different rows with different columns.

为什么可行。乘积的每个元素都由一整行与一整列组合而成，交换因子后，配对的行与列就不同了。

Worked Example: a product of data matrices in context例题：情境中的数据矩阵乘积

The prices, in dollars, are $\mathbf{p} = \begin{pmatrix} 3.5 \\ 2.5 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$. (a) State the order of $Q$ and of $Q\mathbf{p}$. (b) Find $Q\mathbf{p}$ and interpret it. (a) $Q$ 为 $2 \times 3$，$\mathbf{p}$ 为 $3 \times 1$。内侧数字相同（3 与 3），所以 $Q\mathbf{p}$ 存在，阶为 $2 \times 1$。

(c) $\mathbf{p}Q$ would be $(3 \times 1)(2 \times 3)$: the inner numbers 1 and 2 differ, so the product is not defined.

(c) $\mathbf{p}Q$ 为 $(3 \times 1)(2 \times 3)$：内侧数字 1 与 2 不同，所以乘积无定义。

Why this works. Each row of $Q$ meets the price column, so each entry is "quantity times price, summed over drinks": the total revenue for one day.

为什么可行。$Q$ 的每一行与价格列相乘，所以每个元素都是"数量乘价格，对各饮品求和"，即某一天的总收入。

做乘法前，把两个阶并排写出，例如 $(2 \times 3)(3 \times 1)$：若内侧数字不同，"求 $BA$"的答案就是"无定义"，并写出这个理由。情境题中，要说明乘积的行与列分别代表什么，金额保留到分；只给出一个矩阵是不完整的解读。

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Find the inverse of a $2 \times 2$ matrix by hand, and of a $3 \times 3$ matrix with a GDC.
  • Determine whether a matrix is singular, and find parameter values that make it singular.
  • Write a system of linear equations as $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ and solve it as $\mathbf{x} = A^{-1}\mathbf{b}$.
  • Solve matrix equations such as $XA = B$, multiplying by the inverse on the correct side.
  • 手算 $2 \times 2$ 矩阵的行列式，并用 GDC 计算（calculate）更大方阵的行列式。
  • 手算 $2 \times 2$ 矩阵的逆矩阵，用 GDC 求 $3 \times 3$ 矩阵的逆矩阵。
  • 判断（determine）矩阵是否奇异，并求使其奇异的参数值。
  • 把线性方程组写成 $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$，并解为 $\mathbf{x} = A^{-1}\mathbf{b}$。
  • 解 $XA = B$ 一类的矩阵方程，在正确的一侧乘以逆矩阵。

The determinant and the inverse matrix行列式与逆矩阵

For a square matrix $A$, the inverse matrix $A^{-1}$ is the matrix with $AA^{-1} = A^{-1}A = I$. For a $2 \times 2$ matrix the determinant and the inverse are

对方阵 $A$，逆矩阵（inverse matrix）$A^{-1}$ 满足 $AA^{-1} = A^{-1}A = I$。对 $2 \times 2$ 矩阵，行列式（determinant）与逆矩阵为

$$ A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \det A = ad - bc, \qquad A^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad - bc}\begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix}. $$

Swap the leading-diagonal elements, change the signs of the other two, and divide by the determinant. If $\det A \neq 0$, the matrix has an inverse. If $\det A = 0$, there is nothing to divide by and the matrix has no inverse: it is singular. For a $3 \times 3$ matrix, find $\det A$ and $A^{-1}$ with the GDC matrix menu; the same rule applies, so check the determinant is non-zero before relying on the inverse.

交换主对角线元素，另两个元素变号，再除以行列式。若 $\det A \neq 0$，矩阵有逆矩阵。若 $\det A = 0$，无法相除，矩阵没有逆矩阵，称为奇异矩阵（singular）。对 $3 \times 3$ 矩阵，用 GDC 的矩阵菜单求 $\det A$ 与 $A^{-1}$；规则相同，所以使用逆矩阵前先确认行列式不为零。

Solving a system of linear equations解线性方程组

A system of linear equations with as many equations as unknowns can be written $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$, where $A$ is the coefficient matrix, $\mathbf{x}$ the column of unknowns and $\mathbf{b}$ the column of constants. Multiplying both sides on the left by $A^{-1}$ gives $A^{-1}A\mathbf{x} = A^{-1}\mathbf{b}$, so $\mathbf{x} = A^{-1}\mathbf{b}$. For $XA = B$, multiply on the right instead: $X = BA^{-1}$. Common misconception: $\mathbf{b}A^{-1}$ is not the same as $A^{-1}\mathbf{b}$ (for a column $\mathbf{b}$ it is not even defined). If $\det A = 0$, this method fails; the system then has either no solution or infinitely many, and you must say which by examining the equations.

方程个数与未知数个数相同的线性方程组（system of linear equations）可写成 $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$，其中 $A$ 为系数矩阵，$\mathbf{x}$ 为未知数列向量，$\mathbf{b}$ 为常数列向量。两边左乘 $A^{-1}$ 得 $A^{-1}A\mathbf{x} = A^{-1}\mathbf{b}$，所以 $\mathbf{x} = A^{-1}\mathbf{b}$。对 $XA = B$，则要右乘：$X = BA^{-1}$。常见误解：$\mathbf{b}A^{-1}$ 与 $A^{-1}\mathbf{b}$ 不同（当 $\mathbf{b}$ 是列向量时前者甚至无定义）。若 $\det A = 0$，此方法失效；方程组要么无解，要么有无穷多解，需要检查方程才能判断是哪一种。

Worked Example: a 2 × 2 inverse by hand and a system例题：手算 2 × 2 逆矩阵并解方程组

Solve $3x + 2y = 7$ and $5x + 4y = 11$ using an inverse matrix.用逆矩阵解方程组 $3x + 2y = 7$，$5x + 4y = 11$。

Matrix form. $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 11 \end{pmatrix}$.

矩阵形式。$\begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 11 \end{pmatrix}$。

Determinant. $\det A = 3(4) - 2(5) = 12 - 10 = 2 \neq 0$, so $A^{-1}$ exists.

行列式。$\det A = 3(4) - 2(5) = 12 - 10 = 2 \neq 0$，所以 $A^{-1}$ 存在。

Inverse. $A^{-1} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 4 & -2 \\ -5 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

逆矩阵。$A^{-1} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 4 & -2 \\ -5 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$。

Solve. $\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 4(7) - 2(11) \\ -5(7) + 3(11) \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$.

求解。$\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 4(7) - 2(11) \\ -5(7) + 3(11) \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$。

Check. $3(3) + 2(-1) = 7$ and $5(3) + 4(-1) = 11$. So $x = 3$, $y = -1$.

检验。$3(3) + 2(-1) = 7$，$5(3) + 4(-1) = 11$。所以 $x = 3$，$y = -1$。

Why this works. $A^{-1}$ undoes $A$, just as dividing undoes multiplying, so applying it to both sides isolates the unknowns.

为什么可行。$A^{-1}$ 抵消 $A$ 的作用，就像除法抵消乘法，所以两边同乘它就能分离出未知数。

Let the prices per kg be $x$, $y$ and $z$ dollars. Write the system as a matrix equation and solve it.在市场上，Ana 买 2 千克苹果、1 千克梨、3 千克李子共付 17 美元；Ben 买 1、4、2 千克共付 27 美元；Chen 买 3、2、1 千克共付 21 美元。设每千克价格分别为 $x$、$y$、$z$ 美元。把方程组写成矩阵方程并求解。

矩阵方程。$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 1 & 4 & 2 \\ 3 & 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 17 \\ 27 \\ 21 \end{pmatrix}$。

A matrix moves points矩阵移动点

Write the point $(x, y)$ as the column vector $\binom{x}{y}$. The matrix $M = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$ sends it to $M\binom{x}{y} = \binom{ax + by}{cx + dy}$. The origin never moves, and $M\binom{1}{0} = \binom{a}{c}$, $M\binom{0}{1} = \binom{b}{d}$: the columns of $M$ are the images of the two unit vectors. That is the fastest way to build any transformation matrix: decide where $\binom{1}{0}$ and $\binom{0}{1}$ go and write them as columns. A translation by $\binom{p}{q}$ is not a matrix product; it is added: $\binom{x'}{y'} = M\binom{x}{y} + \binom{p}{q}$.

把点 $(x, y)$ 写成列向量 $\binom{x}{y}$。矩阵 $M = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$ 把它映到 $M\binom{x}{y} = \binom{ax + by}{cx + dy}$。原点不动，且 $M\binom{1}{0} = \binom{a}{c}$，$M\binom{0}{1} = \binom{b}{d}$：$M$ 的两列就是两个单位向量的像。这是构造任何变换矩阵最快的方法：确定 $\binom{1}{0}$ 与 $\binom{0}{1}$ 被映到哪里，再把它们写成两列。平移 $\binom{p}{q}$（translation）不是矩阵乘法，而是相加：$\binom{x'}{y'} = M\binom{x}{y} + \binom{p}{q}$。

Standard matrices (all about the origin; $\theta$ anticlockwise):

标准矩阵（均以原点为中心；$\theta$ 为逆时针方向）：

$$ \text{rotation by } \theta: \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \text{reflection in } y = (\tan\theta)x: \begin{pmatrix} \cos 2\theta & \sin 2\theta \\ \sin 2\theta & -\cos 2\theta \end{pmatrix} $$ $$ \text{enlargement, factor } k: \begin{pmatrix} k & 0 \\ 0 & k \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \text{horizontal stretch, factor } k: \begin{pmatrix} k & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \text{vertical stretch, factor } k: \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & k \end{pmatrix} $$

Special cases worth knowing: reflection in the $x$-axis $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$, in the $y$-axis $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, in $y = x$ $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$; rotation $90^{\circ}$ anticlockwise $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. A rotation by $\theta$ clockwise is a rotation by $-\theta$ anticlockwise.

值得记住的特例：关于 $x$ 轴的反射（reflection）$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$，关于 $y$ 轴 $\begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$，关于 $y = x$ $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$；逆时针旋转（rotation）$90^{\circ}$ 为 $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$。顺时针旋转 $\theta$ 即逆时针旋转 $-\theta$。

Composite transformations and the area scale factor复合变换与面积缩放因子

If $M_{1}$ is applied first and $M_{2}$ second, the image is $M_{2}(M_{1}\mathbf{x}) = (M_{2}M_{1})\mathbf{x}$, so the single matrix for the composite transformation is $M_{2}M_{1}$: the first transformation sits on the right. Because matrix products do not commute, reversing the order usually gives a different transformation. Every shape's area is multiplied by the same number, the area scale factor $|\det M|$. A negative determinant means the transformation also reverses orientation (as a reflection does); a zero determinant squashes the plane onto a line or a point, so there is no inverse transformation.

若先作 $M_{1}$ 再作 $M_{2}$，像为 $M_{2}(M_{1}\mathbf{x}) = (M_{2}M_{1})\mathbf{x}$，所以复合变换（composite transformation）的单一矩阵是 $M_{2}M_{1}$：先作的变换写在右边。由于矩阵乘法不满足交换律，颠倒顺序通常得到不同的变换。所有图形的面积都乘以同一个数，即面积缩放因子（area scale factor）$|\det M|$。行列式为负，说明变换还翻转了定向（与反射一样）；行列式为零，则平面被压到一条直线或一个点上，不存在逆变换。

Definition and meaning定义与意义

A non-zero vector $\mathbf{v}$ is an eigenvector of a square matrix $A$ when $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$ for some number $\lambda$, the eigenvalue belonging to $\mathbf{v}$. Geometrically, $A$ does not turn $\mathbf{v}$: it only scales it by $\lambda$ (and reverses it if $\lambda < 0$). So the line through the origin in the direction of $\mathbf{v}$ is mapped onto itself. In Figure 1, $M\binom{1}{1} = \binom{3}{3} = 3\binom{1}{1}$: the diagonal $y = x$ is stretched by factor 3, so $\binom{1}{1}$ is an eigenvector with eigenvalue 3. Any non-zero multiple of an eigenvector is also an eigenvector for the same eigenvalue.

若对某个数 $\lambda$ 有 $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$，则非零向量 $\mathbf{v}$ 称为方阵 $A$ 的特征向量（eigenvector），这个数就是属于 $\mathbf{v}$ 的特征值（eigenvalue）。几何上，$A$ 不改变 $\mathbf{v}$ 的方向，只把它缩放 $\lambda$ 倍（$\lambda < 0$ 时反向）。所以过原点、沿 $\mathbf{v}$ 方向的直线被映到自身。在图 1 中，$M\binom{1}{1} = \binom{3}{3} = 3\binom{1}{1}$：对角线 $y = x$ 被拉伸 3 倍，所以 $\binom{1}{1}$ 是特征值为 3 的特征向量。特征向量的任何非零倍数，也是同一特征值的特征向量。

The characteristic equation特征方程

Rewrite $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$ as $(A - \lambda I)\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$. A non-zero solution $\mathbf{v}$ can exist only if $A - \lambda I$ has no inverse (otherwise multiplying by the inverse would force $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$), so $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$. For $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$ this characteristic equation is

把 $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$ 改写为 $(A - \lambda I)\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$。只有当 $A - \lambda I$ 没有逆矩阵时才可能有非零解 $\mathbf{v}$（否则两边乘以逆矩阵会得到 $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$），所以 $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$。对 $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$，这个特征方程（characteristic equation）为

$$ \det(A - \lambda I) = (a - \lambda)(d - \lambda) - bc = \lambda^{2} - (a + d)\lambda + (ad - bc) = 0. $$

The left side is the characteristic polynomial. Its two roots satisfy $\lambda_{1} + \lambda_{2} = a + d$ (the trace) and $\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2} = \det A$, a quick check on your eigenvalues. A GDC polynomial solver gives the roots; some models also have an eigenvalue command. Common misconception: $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$ always satisfies $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$, which is why it is excluded; an eigenvector must be non-zero.

左边就是特征多项式。两根满足 $\lambda_{1} + \lambda_{2} = a + d$（迹，trace）与 $\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2} = \det A$，可用来快速检验特征值。GDC 的多项式求解器可给出根；有些型号也有直接求特征值的命令。常见误解：$\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$ 总满足 $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$，正因如此才被排除；特征向量必须是非零向量。

Why diagonalise为什么要对角化

Put eigenvectors $\mathbf{v}_{1}$, $\mathbf{v}_{2}$ as the columns of $P$ and the matching eigenvalues on the diagonal of $D$, in the same order. Then $AP = PD$ (column by column this is just $A\mathbf{v}_{i} = \lambda_{i}\mathbf{v}_{i}$), and when $P$ has an inverse, $A = PDP^{-1}$. To diagonalise $A$ is to find such a $P$ and $D$. This course uses the case of two distinct real eigenvalues; distinct eigenvalues guarantee two independent eigenvectors, so $P$ is invertible.

把特征向量 $\mathbf{v}_{1}$、$\mathbf{v}_{2}$ 作为 $P$ 的两列，把对应的特征值按相同顺序放在 $D$ 的对角线上。于是 $AP = PD$（逐列看，这正是 $A\mathbf{v}_{i} = \lambda_{i}\mathbf{v}_{i}$），当 $P$ 可逆时，$A = PDP^{-1}$。对角化（diagonalise）$A$，就是求出这样的 $P$ 与 $D$。本课程只处理两个不同实特征值的情形；不同的特征值保证有两个线性无关的特征向量，所以 $P$ 可逆。

$$ A = PDP^{-1} \quad\Rightarrow\quad A^{n} = (PDP^{-1})(PDP^{-1})\cdots(PDP^{-1}) = PD^{n}P^{-1}, \qquad D^{n} = \begin{pmatrix} \lambda_{1}^{n} & 0 \\ 0 & \lambda_{2}^{n} \end{pmatrix}. $$

Every inner $P^{-1}P$ collapses to $I$, and a diagonal matrix is raised to a power element by element. So $A^{n}$ becomes a formula in $n$. In the long run the term with the largest $|\lambda|$ dominates: a power $\lambda^{n}$ with $|\lambda| < 1$ tends to $0$. Common misconception: swapping the columns of $P$ without swapping the diagonal of $D$ gives a wrong product; the $i$th column of $P$ must belong to the $i$th diagonal entry of $D$.

中间每个 $P^{-1}P$ 都化为 $I$，而对角矩阵的幂只需对各对角元素取幂。所以 $A^{n}$ 变成关于 $n$ 的公式。长期来看，$|\lambda|$ 最大的项占主导：当 $|\lambda| < 1$ 时 $\lambda^{n}$ 趋于 $0$。常见误解：交换 $P$ 的两列而不交换 $D$ 的对角元素，乘积就错了；$P$ 的第 $i$ 列必须对应 $D$ 的第 $i$ 个对角元素。

Markov chains and the column convention马尔可夫链与列约定

A Markov chain is a process that moves between a fixed set of states in steps, where the probability of the next state depends only on the current state, not on the earlier history. The transition matrix $T$ stores these probabilities. This guide uses the column convention throughout: the element in row $i$, column $j$ is the probability of moving from state $j$ to state $i$, so each column lists where everything in one state goes and each column sums to 1. The state vector $\mathbf{s}_{n}$ is a column of probabilities (or counts) for each state after $n$ steps, starting from the initial state $\mathbf{s}_{0}$:

马尔可夫链（Markov chain）是按步在一组固定状态之间转移的过程，下一状态的概率只取决于当前状态，与更早的历史无关。转移矩阵（transition matrix）$T$ 存放这些概率。本指南全程使用列约定：第 $i$ 行第 $j$ 列的元素是从状态 $j$ 转移到状态 $i$ 的概率，所以每一列列出某一状态中的全部去向，且每一列之和为 1。状态向量（state vector）$\mathbf{s}_{n}$ 是 $n$ 步后各状态的概率（或数量）组成的列向量，从初始状态（initial state）$\mathbf{s}_{0}$ 出发：

$$ \mathbf{s}_{n+1} = T\mathbf{s}_{n}, \qquad \mathbf{s}_{n} = T^{n}\mathbf{s}_{0}, \qquad T\mathbf{s} = \mathbf{s} \text{ with entries summing to } 1 \text{ (steady state)}. $$

Some books write the transpose instead: rows sum to 1, the state is a row vector and $\mathbf{s}_{n} = \mathbf{s}_{0}T^{n}$. The two conventions give the same probabilities, but mixing them gives nonsense. Common misconception: multiplying a row-convention matrix by a column state vector silently produces wrong numbers, so add up a column of the given matrix before you start; if the columns do not sum to 1 but the rows do, transpose it.

有些书使用转置的写法：每一行之和为 1，状态是行向量，$\mathbf{s}_{n} = \mathbf{s}_{0}T^{n}$。两种约定得到相同的概率，但混用就会出错。常见误解：把行约定的矩阵乘以列状态向量，不会报错却会得到错误的数，所以开始前先把给定矩阵的一列加起来；若各列之和不为 1 而各行之和为 1，就先转置。

The steady state稳态

A steady-state vector $\mathbf{s}$ does not change from one step to the next: $T\mathbf{s} = \mathbf{s}$. That says $\mathbf{s}$ is an eigenvector of $T$ for eigenvalue 1, scaled so its entries sum to 1. To find it, solve $(T - I)\mathbf{s} = \mathbf{0}$: these equations are dependent, so replace one of them by "entries sum to 1". For a chain in which some power of $T$ has every entry positive (true for every chain in this unit), $\mathbf{s}_{n}$ approaches this same steady state from every initial state, so it gives the long-run proportion of time, or of the population, in each state.

稳态（steady state）向量 $\mathbf{s}$ 从一步到下一步保持不变：$T\mathbf{s} = \mathbf{s}$。这说明 $\mathbf{s}$ 是 $T$ 关于特征值 1 的特征向量，并按元素和为 1 缩放。求法是解 $(T - I)\mathbf{s} = \mathbf{0}$：这些方程线性相关，所以用"各元素之和为 1"替换其中一个。若 $T$ 的某次幂所有元素都为正（本单元所有链都满足），则无论初始状态如何，$\mathbf{s}_{n}$ 都趋于同一个稳态，所以它给出长期处于各状态的时间比例或总体比例。

Exam Strategy and Common Pitfalls考试策略与常见陷阱

Order matters顺序很重要
  • Write the orders first. $(m \times n)(n \times p)$ is defined and gives $m \times p$; anything else is not defined.
  • 先写出阶。$(m \times n)(n \times p)$ 有定义且结果为 $m \times p$；其他情形都无定义。

  • 逆矩阵乘在正确的一侧。$A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ 得 $\mathbf{x} = A^{-1}\mathbf{b}$；$XA = B$ 得 $X = BA^{-1}$。
  • Composites read right to left. "$M_{1}$ then $M_{2}$" is the single matrix $M_{2}M_{1}$.
  • 复合变换从右往左读。"先 $M_{1}$ 后 $M_{2}$"的单一矩阵是 $M_{2}M_{1}$。
Eigen-work and Markov chains特征值问题与马尔可夫链
  • Check eigenvalues with trace and determinant. $\lambda_{1} + \lambda_{2} = a + d$ and $\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2} = \det A$ catch most slips in the characteristic equation.
  • 用迹与行列式检验特征值。$\lambda_{1} + \lambda_{2} = a + d$ 与 $\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2} = \det A$ 能发现特征方程中的大多数错误。
  • Match $P$ and $D$ column by column, then test your $A^{n}$ formula at $n = 1$.
  • $P$ 与 $D$ 逐列对应，再用 $n = 1$ 检验 $A^{n}$ 的公式。
  • Check the convention of a transition matrix. In this guide columns sum to 1 and $\mathbf{s}_{n} = T^{n}\mathbf{s}_{0}$; if a given matrix has rows summing to 1, it is the transpose.
  • 检查转移矩阵的约定。本指南中各列之和为 1，$\mathbf{s}_{n} = T^{n}\mathbf{s}_{0}$；若给定矩阵各行之和为 1，它就是转置形式。
  • A steady state needs two conditions: $T\mathbf{s} = \mathbf{s}$ and entries summing to 1.
  • 稳态需要两个条件：$T\mathbf{s} = \mathbf{s}$，且各元素之和为 1。

How to use this guide本指南使用说明

B5 is one of the HL-only units of this course: it covers the 2021 sub-topics AHL 1.14 (matrices, determinants, inverses, systems), AHL 1.15 (eigenvalues, eigenvectors, diagonalisation, powers), AHL 3.9 (matrix transformations) and AHL 4.19 (Markov chains). The sections build on each other: products and inverses (B5.1, B5.2) feed transformations (B5.3); eigenvectors (B5.4) explain both the invariant lines of a transformation and the powers of a matrix (B5.5); and the steady state of a Markov chain (B5.6) is the eigenvector for eigenvalue 1.B5 是本课程的纯 HL 单元之一，涵盖 2021 年大纲子主题 AHL 1.14（矩阵、行列式、逆矩阵、方程组）、AHL 1.15（特征值、特征向量、对角化、矩阵幂）、AHL 3.9（矩阵变换）与 AHL 4.19（马尔可夫链）。各节层层递进：乘积与逆矩阵（B5.1、B5.2）用于几何变换（B5.3）；特征向量（B5.4）既解释变换的不变直线，也解释矩阵的幂（B5.5）；而马尔可夫链的稳态（B5.6）正是特征值 1 的特征向量。
