$f(x)=x^{5/3}-5x^{2/3}$, so $f'(x)=\dfrac53x^{2/3}-\dfrac{10}{3}x^{-1/3}=\dfrac{5(x-2)}{3x^{1/3}}$ for $x\ne0$. (M1·A1)$f(x)=x^{5/3}-5x^{2/3}$，故 $f'(x)=\dfrac53x^{2/3}-\dfrac{10}{3}x^{-1/3}=\dfrac{5(x-2)}{3x^{1/3}}$，$x\ne0$。(M1·A1)

$f'=0$ at $x=2$; $f'$ does not exist at $x=0$. Sign chart: $f'\gt0$ for $x\lt0$, $f'\lt0$ for $0\lt x\lt2$, and $f'\gt0$ for $x\gt2$. Thus $x=0$ gives a local maximum (critical point where $f'$ does not exist), and $x=2$ gives a local minimum. (A1·R1)$f'=0$ 于 $x=2$；$f'$ 在 $x=0$ 处不存在。符号表：$x\lt0$ 时 $f'\gt0$，$0\lt x\lt2$ 时 $f'\lt0$，$x\gt2$ 时 $f'\gt0$。故 $x=0$ 给出极大值（$f'$ 不存在的临界点），$x=2$ 给出极小值。(A1·R1)

At $x=0$, $f'(x)\to+\infty$ as $x\to0^-$ and $f'(x)\to-\infty$ as $x\to0^+$, so the graph has a vertical tangent and a cusp at $(0,0)$; $f$ is continuous but not differentiable there. (R1)在 $x=0$ 处，$x\to0^-$ 时 $f'(x)\to+\infty$，$x\to0^+$ 时 $f'(x)\to-\infty$，故图像在 $(0,0)$ 处有竖直切线与尖点；$f$ 在该处连续但不可导。(R1)

(c) Second derivative, concavity and inflection points(c) 二阶导数、凹凸性与拐点 M1·A1·A1·R1

Differentiate $f'(x)=\dfrac53x^{2/3}-\dfrac{10}{3}x^{-1/3}$: $f''(x)=\dfrac{10}{9}x^{-1/3}+\dfrac{10}{9}x^{-4/3}=\dfrac{10(x+1)}{9x^{4/3}}$ for $x\ne0$. (M1·A1)对 $f'(x)=\dfrac53x^{2/3}-\dfrac{10}{3}x^{-1/3}$ 求导：$f''(x)=\dfrac{10}{9}x^{-1/3}+\dfrac{10}{9}x^{-4/3}=\dfrac{10(x+1)}{9x^{4/3}}$，$x\ne0$。(M1·A1)

Since $x^{4/3}\gt0$ for $x\ne0$, the sign of $f''$ is the sign of $x+1$. Thus $f''\lt0$ on $(-\infty,-1)$ and $f''\gt0$ on $(-1,0)\cup(0,\infty)$. Hence $f$ is concave down on $(-\infty,-1)$ and concave up on $(-1,0)\cup(0,\infty)$. (A1)由于 $x\ne0$ 时 $x^{4/3}\gt0$，$f''$ 的符号即 $x+1$ 的符号。故 $f''\lt0$ 于 $(-\infty,-1)$，$f''\gt0$ 于 $(-1,0)\cup(0,\infty)$。因此 $f$ 在 $(-\infty,-1)$ 上下凹，在 $(-1,0)\cup(0,\infty)$ 上上凹。(A1)

At $x=-1$, $f''$ changes from negative to positive, so $x=-1$ is an inflection point; $f(-1)=1\cdot(-6)=-6$. At $x=0$, $f''$ does not exist, but $f''\gt0$ on both sides of $0$, so concavity does not change. Therefore $x=0$ is not an inflection point. (R1)在 $x=-1$ 处，$f''$ 由负变正，故 $x=-1$ 是拐点；$f(-1)=1\cdot(-6)=-6$。在 $x=0$ 处，$f''$ 不存在，但 $f''$ 在 $0$ 两侧均为正，故凹凸性不改变。因此 $x=0$ 不是拐点。(R1)

(d) Second Derivative Test at $x=0$ and $x=2$(d) 在 $x=0$ 与 $x=2$ 处使用二阶导数判别法 R1·A1

At $x=0$, the Second Derivative Test cannot be used because it requires $f'(0)=0$ and $f''(0)$ to exist; here $f'(0)$ and $f''(0)$ do not exist. (R1)在 $x=0$ 处不能使用二阶导数判别法，因为它要求 $f'(0)=0$ 且 $f''(0)$ 存在；此处 $f'(0)$ 与 $f''(0)$ 均不存在。(R1)

At $x=2$: $f'(2)=0$ and $f''(2)=\dfrac{10(3)}{9\cdot 2^{4/3}}=\dfrac{10}{3\cdot 2^{4/3}}\gt0$. By the Second Derivative Test, $f$ has a local minimum at $x=2$. (A1)在 $x=2$ 处：$f'(2)=0$ 且 $f''(2)=\dfrac{10(3)}{9\cdot 2^{4/3}}=\dfrac{10}{3\cdot 2^{4/3}}\gt0$。由二阶导数判别法，$f$ 在 $x=2$ 处有极小值。(A1)

(e) Sketch with labelled points(e) 标出关键点的草图 A1·A1·A1

Sketch: label the intercepts $(0,0)$ and $(5,0)$; the local maximum at the cusp $(0,0)$ with vertical tangent; the local minimum at $\left(2,-3\sqrt[3]{4}\right)$; and the inflection point $(-1,-6)$. The graph comes from $-\infty$ on the left, rises concave down to the inflection, becomes concave up, rises to the cusp maximum $(0,0)$, falls to the minimum $\left(2,-3\sqrt[3]{4}\right)$, then rises through $(5,0)$ to $+\infty$. (A1·A1·A1)草图：标出截距 $(0,0)$ 与 $(5,0)$；尖点极大值 $(0,0)$（竖直切线）；极小值 $\left(2,-3\sqrt[3]{4}\right)$；拐点 $(-1,-6)$。曲线从左侧 $-\infty$ 上升，经拐点前为下凹，之后转为上凹，升至尖点极大值 $(0,0)$，再降至极小值 $\left(2,-3\sqrt[3]{4}\right)$，然后上升穿过 $(5,0)$ 趋向 $+\infty$。(A1·A1·A1)

Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) Treating $x^{2/3}$ as negative for $x\lt0$ makes $x-5$ and $x^{2/3}$ both negative as $x\to-\infty$, so the student reports $f\to\infty$ instead of $f\to-\infty$. (b) Discarding $x=0$ because $f'(0)$ does not exist leaves only $x=2$ as a critical point, so the local maximum at the cusp $(0,0)$ is missed. (c) Listing $x=0$ as an inflection point because $f''(0)$ does not exist gives inflections at $x=-1$ and $x=0$, but $f''\gt0$ on both sides of $0$, so concavity does not change there.（a）把 $x\lt0$ 时的 $x^{2/3}$ 当作负数，于是当 $x\to-\infty$ 时认为 $x-5$ 与 $x^{2/3}$ 都为负，错报 $f\to\infty$，而正确结果是 $f\to-\infty$。 （b）因为 $f'(0)$ 不存在就把 $x=0$ 排除在临界点之外，只留下 $x=2$，于是漏掉尖点 $(0,0)$ 处的局部极大。 （c）因为 $f''(0)$ 不存在就把 $x=0$ 列为拐点，得到拐点 $x=-1$ 与 $x=0$，但 $f''$ 在 $0$ 两侧都为正，所以凹凸性没有改变。
Insight.要点。 A point where $f''$ does not exist is not automatically an inflection point: the concavity must actually change. At $x=0$ the cusp is a local maximum because $f'$ changes sign, but $f''$ stays positive on both sides, so the curve remains concave up and the point is not an inflection. At $x=-1$, $f''$ changes sign, so it is an inflection even though $f'$ does not vanish there.$f''$ 不存在的点不自动是拐点：必须真正发生凹凸性改变。在 $x=0$ 处，由于 $f'$ 变号，尖点是极大值；但 $f''$ 在两侧均为正，曲线仍上凹，故该点不是拐点。在 $x=-1$ 处，$f''$ 变号，所以即使 $f'$ 不为零，它仍是拐点。
