Changing the unit relabels the same seedlings and adds no data at 20 weeks, so the prediction ($\hat v=96-0.4(140)=40$ cm) is an extrapolation and the linear trend may not continue. 1 point for converting to 20 weeks and comparing with 2 to 12; 1 for reasoning that recoding adds no information.140 天即 $x=140/7=20$ 周，远超拟合所用的 2 至 12 周。换单位只是给同样的幼苗换了标签，并没有增加 20 周处的数据，所以该预测（$\hat v=96-0.4(140)=40$ cm）是外推，线性趋势可能不再持续。换算为 20 周并与 2 至 12 周比较 1 分；说明重新编码不增加信息 1 分。

Where this goes wrong (M24)常见错误（M24）

Recoding by a positive multiple leaves $r$ alone but divides the slope by the same factor; reflecting a variable ($v=c-y$) flips the sign of both the slope and $r$. “Only the units changed” is true of $p$ and false of the reflection. Another slip is to call $1-r^2$ the new $r^2$ because the relationship was “reversed.”按正倍数重新编码不改变 $r$，但斜率要除以同一因子；反射一个变量（$v=c-y$）会使斜率和 $r$ 同时变号。“只改变了单位”对 $p$ 成立，对反射不成立。另一个失误是因为关系被“反转”就把 $1-r^2$ 当作新的 $r^2$。

A linear recoding rescales the slope and intercept and can flip the sign of $r$, while $r^2$ and the quality of the fit stay the same.线性重新编码会改变斜率与截距的尺度，并可能使 $r$ 变号，而 $r^2$ 与拟合优度保持不变。
