  2. Compute the mean and the standard deviation of each game; verify your part (a) comparison.计算两种游戏的均值与标准差，并验证 (a) 的比较。 [3]
  3. A participant prefers steady payouts. Which game should they choose, and what does its standard deviation say in dollar terms?若参与者偏好稳定的收益，应选择哪种游戏？其标准差以美元计说明了什么？ [2]
Both games have mean 10 dollars. Distances from the mean: Game A outcomes are 10 dollars away; Game B outcomes are 2 dollars away, so B is less spread out. Computed: $SD_A=10$ dollars, $SD_B=2$ dollars. For steady payouts choose Game B; its typical distance from the mean is 2 dollars.两种游戏的均值都是 10 美元。与均值的距离：游戏 A 的两个结果距离 10 美元；游戏 B 的两个结果距离 2 美元，因此 B 更集中。计算得 $SD_A=10$ 美元，$SD_B=2$ 美元。偏好稳定收益者应选游戏 B；其与均值的典型距离为 2 美元。

(a) 3 points3 分

Both means are 10, so the comparison is about distances alone. Game A pays outcomes 0 and 20, which are 10 dollars from 10. Game B pays 8 and 12, which are 2 dollars from 10. Smaller typical distance means smaller spread, so B is the steadier game.两个均值都是 10，因此只需比较距离。游戏 A 的结果 0 与 20 距 10 为 10 美元；游戏 B 的结果 8 与 12 距 10 为 2 美元。典型距离更小表示离散程度更小，故 B 更稳定。

(b) 3 points3 分

Means: $0.5(0)+0.5(20)=10$ and $0.5(8)+0.5(12)=10$. Squared deviations: Game A, $0.5(100)+0.5(100)=100$, so $SD_A=10$ dollars. Game B, $0.5(4)+0.5(4)=4$, so $SD_B=2$ dollars. This confirms the (a) comparison: B's SD is smaller.均值：$0.5(0)+0.5(20)=10$，$0.5(8)+0.5(12)=10$。平方离差：游戏 A 为 $0.5(100)+0.5(100)=100$，故 $SD_A=10$ 美元；游戏 B 为 $0.5(4)+0.5(4)=4$，故 $SD_B=2$ 美元。验证了 (a) 的比较：B 的标准差更小。

(c) 2 points2 分

Choose Game B. Its standard deviation of 2 dollars says a typical payout sits about 2 dollars from the mean of 10 dollars, versus about 10 dollars for Game A.选择游戏 B。其 2 美元的标准差表示收益与均值 10 美元之间的典型距离约为 2 美元，而游戏 A 约为 10 美元。

Where this goes wrong (M 7)常见错误（M 7）

Comparing the means (equal here) instead of spreads; reporting the variance as the spread; or assuming equal means force equal standard deviations (M 7).比较均值（此处相同）而不比较离散程度；把方差当作离散程度；或认为均值相同则标准差必然相同（M 7）。

Spread lives in the distances of outcomes from the mean; equal means can hide very different risks.离散程度体现在各结果与均值的距离中；均值相同的分布可能风险差异很大。
