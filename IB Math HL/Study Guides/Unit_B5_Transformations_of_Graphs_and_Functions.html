Unit B5: Transformations of
Graphs and Functions单元 B5：图像与函数的变换

Functions are rarely studied in isolation. A new graph is often nothing more than a familiar one shifted, stretched, reflected, or composed under absolute value or reciprocal. This unit catalogues the standard moves: vertical and horizontal translation, vertical and horizontal stretch (or compression), reflection in either axis, and at HL the absolute-value and reciprocal transformations. The pattern is mechanical once the algebra is read with care: every change to the input acts on the $x$-coordinates in the opposite sense, and every change to the output acts on the $y$-coordinates in the direct sense.函数很少孤立出现。新图像往往不过是熟悉图像经过平移、伸缩、反射，或在 HL 阶段再经过绝对值与倒数变换之后的结果。本单元梳理各种标准变换：纵向与横向平移、纵向与横向伸缩、关于两坐标轴的反射，以及 HL 才学的绝对值变换与倒数变换。规律一旦理清便相当机械：对输入的任何改动按相反方向作用于 $x$ 坐标，对输出的任何改动按相同方向作用于 $y$ 坐标。

Topic B5.1 · Vertical TranslationsTopic B5.1 · 纵向平移

Vertical Translations纵向平移 SL 2.11

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State the effect of $y = f(x) + k$ on a graph: a vertical translation by $k$ (up when $k > 0$, down when $k < 0$).
  • Explain why only the $y$-coordinates change: each point $(p, q)$ maps to $(p, q + k)$.
  • Determine the new range, $y$-intercept and horizontal asymptote after a vertical translation.
  • Calculate new $x$-intercepts by solving $f(x) + k = 0$, since they are not images of the old ones.
  • Write a translation by the vector $\binom{0}{k}$ as an equation, and read $k$ back from an equation.
  • Sketch $y = f(x) + k$ from a labelled graph of $y = f(x)$.
  • 写出（state）$y = f(x) + k$ 对图像的作用：纵向平移 $k$（$k > 0$ 向上，$k < 0$ 向下）。
  • 解释（explain）为何只有 $y$ 坐标改变：每个点 $(p, q)$ 映到 $(p, q + k)$。
  • 确定（determine）纵向平移后的值域、$y$ 轴截距与水平渐近线。
  • 通过解 $f(x) + k = 0$ 计算（calculate）新的 $x$ 轴截距，因为它们不是旧截距的像。
  • 把按向量 $\binom{0}{k}$ 的平移（translation）写成方程，并能由方程读出 $k$。
  • 由已标注的 $y = f(x)$ 图像画出（sketch）$y = f(x) + k$。
The rule. If $y = f(x)$ has graph $G$, then $$ y \;=\; f(x) + k $$ has graph $G$ translated up by $k$ if $k > 0$, and down by $|k|$ if $k < 0$. The transformation acts on the output, so every $y$-coordinate increases by $k$ while every $x$-coordinate is unchanged.

Effect on domain and range. Domain is unchanged. Range shifts by $k$: if $f$ has range $[m, M]$, then $f + k$ has range $[m + k, M + k]$.
规则。设 $y = f(x)$ 的图像为 $G$，则 $$ y \;=\; f(x) + k $$ 的图像是 $G$ 沿 $y$ 轴 向上平移 $k$ 个单位（$k > 0$）或 向下平移 $|k|$ 个单位（$k < 0$）。该变换作用于输出，每个 $y$ 坐标加上 $k$，每个 $x$ 坐标保持不变。

对定义域和值域的影响。定义域不变。值域整体平移 $k$：若 $f$ 的值域为 $[m, M]$，则 $f + k$ 的值域为 $[m + k, M + k]$。
Worked Example B5.1: shifted parabolaB5.1 例题：平移的抛物线

Starting from $y = x^{2}$, sketch $y = x^{2} - 3$. State the vertex, the range, and the $x$-intercepts of the new graph.由 $y = x^{2}$ 画出 $y = x^{2} - 3$。写出新图像的顶点、值域与 $x$ 截距。

Identify the transformation. Here $k = -3$, so the parabola $y = x^{2}$ is translated $3$ units down.

识别变换。此处 $k = -3$，故 $y = x^{2}$ 向下平移 $3$ 个单位。

Vertex. The vertex of $y = x^{2}$ is $(0, 0)$. After shifting down by $3$, the vertex becomes $(0, -3)$.

顶点。$y = x^{2}$ 的顶点为 $(0, 0)$。向下平移 $3$ 单位后变为 $(0, -3)$。

Range. Range of $y = x^{2}$ is $[0, \infty)$. The new range is $[-3, \infty)$.

值域。$y = x^{2}$ 的值域为 $[0, \infty)$，新值域为 $[-3, \infty)$。

Intercepts. Solve $x^{2} - 3 = 0$: $x = \pm \sqrt{3}$. The graph cuts the $x$-axis at $(\pm \sqrt{3}, 0)$. The $y$-intercept is $(0, -3)$.

截距。解 $x^{2} - 3 = 0$：$x = \pm \sqrt{3}$。图像在 $(\pm \sqrt{3}, 0)$ 处交 $x$ 轴。$y$ 截距为 $(0, -3)$。

Worked Example B5.1b: translated exponentialB5.1b 例题：平移后的指数函数

Let $f(x) = 2^{x}$. The graph of $g$ is the graph of $f$ translated by the vector $\binom{0}{-4}$. Write down $g(x)$, then state the horizontal asymptote, the range and both intercepts of the graph of $g$.设 $f(x) = 2^{x}$。$g$ 的图像是 $f$ 的图像按向量 $\binom{0}{-4}$ 平移所得。写出 $g(x)$，再写出 $g$ 的图像的水平渐近线、值域与两个截距。

Equation. The vector $\binom{0}{-4}$ moves every point $4$ units down and none sideways, so $g(x) = f(x) - 4 = 2^{x} - 4$.

方程。向量 $\binom{0}{-4}$ 把每个点向下移 $4$ 个单位、不作横向移动，故 $g(x) = f(x) - 4 = 2^{x} - 4$。

Asymptote. As $x \to -\infty$, $2^{x} \to 0$, so $f$ has horizontal asymptote $y = 0$. Every $y$-value drops by $4$, so $g$ has horizontal asymptote $y = -4$.

渐近线。$x \to -\infty$ 时 $2^{x} \to 0$，故 $f$ 的水平渐近线为 $y = 0$。每个 $y$ 值都减 $4$，故 $g$ 的水平渐近线为 $y = -4$。

Range. The range of $f$ is $(0, \infty)$, so the range of $g$ is $(-4, \infty)$.

值域。$f$ 的值域为 $(0, \infty)$，故 $g$ 的值域为 $(-4, \infty)$。

$y$-intercept. $g(0) = 2^{0} - 4 = 1 - 4 = -3$, so the intercept is $(0, -3)$, the image of $(0, 1)$ on $y = 2^{x}$.

$y$ 轴截距。$g(0) = 2^{0} - 4 = 1 - 4 = -3$，截距为 $(0, -3)$，即 $y = 2^{x}$ 上 $(0, 1)$ 的像。

$x$-intercept. Solve $2^{x} - 4 = 0$: $2^{x} = 4 = 2^{2}$, so $x = 2$ and the intercept is $(2, 0)$. It is the image of $(2, 4)$ on $y = 2^{x}$, not of an old intercept, because $y = 2^{x}$ has none.

$x$ 轴截距。解 $2^{x} - 4 = 0$：$2^{x} = 4 = 2^{2}$，故 $x = 2$，截距为 $(2, 0)$。它是 $y = 2^{x}$ 上 $(2, 4)$ 的像，而不是旧截距的像，因为 $y = 2^{x}$ 根本没有这种截距。

Why this works. A vertical translation moves every $y$-value by $k$ (asymptote, range, $y$-intercept) and leaves every $x$-value alone; a new $x$-intercept has to be found by solving $f(x) + k = 0$.

原理。纵向平移把每个 $y$ 值（渐近线、值域、$y$ 轴截距）都移动 $k$，$x$ 值保持不变；新的 $x$ 轴截距须解 $f(x) + k = 0$ 求得。

Going deeper: why every $y$-coordinate increases by exactly $k$深入：为何每个 $y$ 坐标恰好增加 $k$

Take a point $(a, b)$ on the graph of $y = f(x)$, so $b = f(a)$. The transformed equation is $y = f(x) + k$. Evaluating at $x = a$ gives $y = f(a) + k = b + k$. The new graph therefore passes through $(a, b + k)$ for every original point $(a, b)$. The $x$-coordinate is preserved, the $y$-coordinate increases by $k$, and the figure as a whole rises by $k$.

设 $(a, b)$ 是 $y = f(x)$ 的图像上一点，即 $b = f(a)$。变换后方程 $y = f(x) + k$ 在 $x = a$ 处取值 $y = f(a) + k = b + k$。新图像因此过点 $(a, b + k)$。$x$ 坐标不变，$y$ 坐标加 $k$，整张图上升 $k$。

Pitfall: $+ k$ inside versus outside陷阱：括号内外的 $+ k$ $y = f(x) + k$ is a vertical shift. $y = f(x + k)$ is a horizontal shift. The $+ k$ outside the function is vertical (up by $k$); the $+ k$ inside the function is horizontal (left by $k$, opposite sign). Before moving anything, check which side of the bracket the constant is on.$y = f(x) + k$ 是纵向平移，$y = f(x + k)$ 是横向平移。括号外的 $+ k$ 表示向上平移 $k$；括号内的 $+ k$ 表示向左平移 $k$（符号相反）。动手前先看常数在括号内还是括号外。
The function $f$ has range $[-1, 4]$. The range of $g(x) = f(x) + 5$ is:函数 $f$ 的值域为 $[-1, 4]$。$g(x) = f(x) + 5$ 的值域为：

Adding $5$ outside the function shifts every output up by $5$. Endpoints $-1$ and $4$ become $4$ and $9$, so the range is $[4, 9]$.在函数外加 $5$ 把每个输出整体上移 $5$。端点 $-1$ 与 $4$ 分别变为 $4$ 与 $9$，故值域为 $[4, 9]$。
A vertical translation shifts every $y$-value by the same constant. Add $5$ to each endpoint of the original range.纵向平移把每个 $y$ 值整体加上同一常数。把原值域两端点各加 $5$ 即可。
Exam tip考试提示

When a translation is given as a column vector, the top entry is the horizontal move and the bottom entry the vertical move: $\binom{0}{-4}$ means $y = f(x) - 4$. After sketching, label the image of every labelled point of the original graph with its new coordinates, and move any horizontal asymptote with the curve: a translated curve still drawn approaching $y = 0$ is an incorrect sketch. Describe the move with its direction, for example "translation 4 units down".

平移以列向量给出时，上面一项是横向移动，下面一项是纵向移动：$\binom{0}{-4}$ 表示 $y = f(x) - 4$。画完后，把原图每个已标注点的像连同新坐标标出，并让水平渐近线随曲线一起移动：平移后的曲线若仍画成趋近 $y = 0$，就是错误的草图。描述变换时写明方向，例如 "translation 4 units down"（向下平移 4 个单位）。

Only $y$-values change, each by $-3$: the point maps to $(3, -1 - 3) = (3, -4)$ and the range becomes $[-2 - 3, 5 - 3] = [-5, 2]$.

只有 $y$ 值改变，每个都加 $-3$：该点映到 $(3, -1 - 3) = (3, -4)$，值域变为 $[-2 - 3, 5 - 3] = [-5, 2]$。

$y = 3^{x}$ 的渐近线为 $y = 0$，纵向平移 $c$ 后变为 $y = c$，故 $c = -6$。由 $3^{x} - 6 = 0$ 得 $3^{x} = 6$，$x = \log_{3} 6 = \dfrac{\ln 6}{\ln 3} \approx 1.63$。截距为 $(\log_{3} 6, 0) \approx (1.63, 0)$。

Horizontal Translations横向平移 SL 2.11

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State the effect of $y = f(x - h)$ on a graph: a horizontal translation by $h$ (right when $h > 0$, left when $h < 0$).
  • Explain why the sign looks reversed: the point $(p, q)$ maps to $(p + h, q)$.
  • Determine the new domain, vertical asymptotes and $x$-intercepts after a horizontal translation.
  • Combine a horizontal and a vertical translation as the vector $\binom{h}{k}$, giving $y = f(x - h) + k$.
  • Sketch $y = f(x - h)$ from a labelled graph of $y = f(x)$.
  • 写出（state）$y = f(x - h)$ 对图像的作用：横向平移 $h$（$h > 0$ 向右，$h < 0$ 向左）。
  • 解释（explain）符号为何看似相反：点 $(p, q)$ 映到 $(p + h, q)$。
  • 确定（determine）横向平移后的定义域、竖直渐近线与 $x$ 轴截距。
  • 把横向与纵向平移合成为向量 $\binom{h}{k}$，得到 $y = f(x - h) + k$。
  • 由已标注的 $y = f(x)$ 图像画出（sketch）$y = f(x - h)$。
The rule. If $y = f(x)$ has graph $G$, then $$ y \;=\; f(x - h) $$ has graph $G$ translated right by $h$ if $h > 0$, and left by $|h|$ if $h < 0$. The sign convention is the opposite of what most students expect: subtracting from the input shifts to the right.

Effect on domain and range. Range is unchanged. Domain shifts by $h$: if $f$ has domain $[a, b]$, then $f(x - h)$ has domain $[a + h, b + h]$.
规则。设 $y = f(x)$ 的图像为 $G$，则 $$ y \;=\; f(x - h) $$ 的图像是 $G$ 向右平移 $h$ 个单位（$h > 0$）或 向左平移 $|h|$ 个单位（$h < 0$）。符号方向恰与多数学生的直觉相反：输入减去一个量，图像向右移。

对定义域和值域的影响。值域不变。定义域整体平移 $h$：若 $f$ 的定义域为 $[a, b]$，则 $f(x - h)$ 的定义域为 $[a + h, b + h]$。
Why the sign is opposite为何符号方向相反 The new graph passes through the point where the new input $x - h$ equals the original input $x'$. Solving $x - h = x'$ gives $x = x' + h$, so the point originally at $x'$ now sits at $x' + h$, that is, $h$ units to the right. The minus sign in $f(x - h)$ becomes a plus sign in the geometric shift.新图像经过的那一点须使新输入 $x - h$ 等于原输入 $x'$。由 $x - h = x'$ 解出 $x = x' + h$，即原本位于 $x'$ 的点如今位于 $x' + h$，恰好向右移 $h$ 个单位。$f(x - h)$ 中的减号在几何平移上反而成了加号。
Worked Example B5.2: shifted parabolaB5.2 例题：横向平移的抛物线

Sketch $y = (x - 2)^{2}$ starting from $y = x^{2}$. State the vertex and the axis of symmetry.由 $y = x^{2}$ 画出 $y = (x - 2)^{2}$。写出顶点与对称轴。

Identify the transformation. Comparing with $y = f(x - h)$ and $f(x) = x^{2}$, we have $h = 2$. The parabola is translated $2$ units to the right.

识别变换。与 $y = f(x - h)$ 对照（$f(x) = x^{2}$），得 $h = 2$。抛物线向右平移 $2$ 个单位。

Vertex. The vertex of $y = x^{2}$ is $(0, 0)$. After shifting right by $2$ the vertex becomes $(2, 0)$.

顶点。$y = x^{2}$ 的顶点为 $(0, 0)$。向右平移 $2$ 单位后变为 $(2, 0)$。

Axis of symmetry. Originally $x = 0$, now $x = 2$.

对称轴。原为 $x = 0$，现为 $x = 2$。

Sanity check. $y = (x - 2)^{2}$ evaluated at $x = 2$ gives $y = 0$, confirming the vertex sits at $(2, 0)$. Evaluating at $x = 0$ gives $y = 4$, so the $y$-intercept is $(0, 4)$.

核对。$y = (x - 2)^{2}$ 在 $x = 2$ 取 $y = 0$，确认顶点为 $(2, 0)$。$x = 0$ 取 $y = 4$，故 $y$ 截距为 $(0, 4)$。

The graph of $y = f(x)$ is translated so that the new graph has equation $y = f(x + 4)$. The translation is:$y = f(x)$ 经过平移得到 $y = f(x + 4)$。该平移为：

Write $f(x + 4) = f(x - (-4))$, so $h = -4$. A negative $h$ shifts the graph $|h| = 4$ units to the left.把 $f(x + 4)$ 写作 $f(x - (-4))$，故 $h = -4$。负 $h$ 表示图像向左平移 $|h| = 4$ 个单位。
Inside the function, $+4$ corresponds to $-h = 4$, that is, $h = -4$. The shift is therefore $4$ units to the left.函数内的 $+4$ 对应 $-h = 4$，即 $h = -4$。故平移方向向左 $4$ 个单位。
Worked Example B5.2b: effect on domainB5.2b 例题：对定义域的影响

$f(x) = \sqrt{x}$ has domain $[0, \infty)$. State the domain of $g(x) = f(x + 3)$.$f(x) = \sqrt{x}$ 的定义域为 $[0, \infty)$。求 $g(x) = f(x + 3)$ 的定义域。

Identify $h$. Write $f(x+3) = f(x - (-3))$, so $h = -3$: a shift $3$ units to the left.

识别 $h$。把 $f(x+3)$ 写作 $f(x - (-3))$，故 $h = -3$：向左平移 $3$ 个单位。

Shift the domain by $h$. Using the domain rule $[a,b] \to [a+h, b+h]$ with $a=0$, $b=\infty$, $h=-3$:

把定义域平移 $h$。用定义域规则 $[a,b] \to [a+h, b+h]$，代入 $a=0$、$b=\infty$、$h=-3$：

$$ \text{domain of } g \;=\; [0 + (-3), \infty) \;=\; [-3, \infty). $$

Direct check. $g(x) = \sqrt{x+3}$ requires $x + 3 \ge 0$, i.e. $x \ge -3$. This matches the shifted domain exactly: the algebra and the transformation rule agree.

直接验证。$g(x) = \sqrt{x+3}$ 要求 $x + 3 \ge 0$，即 $x \ge -3$。与平移后的定义域完全吻合：代数计算与变换规则一致。

Exam tip考试提示

For a translation by $\binom{h}{k}$, replace $x$ by $x - h$ and add $k$: the vector $\binom{-2}{5}$ applied to $y = x^{2}$ gives $y = (x + 2)^{2} + 5$, not $y = (x - 2)^{2} + 5$. Vertical stretch. If $y = f(x)$ has graph $G$, then $$ y \;=\; a \, f(x) $$ stretches $G$ vertically by factor $|a|$ (compression if $|a| < 1$). If $a < 0$ the graph is also reflected in the $x$-axis. Every $y$-coordinate is multiplied by $a$; $x$-coordinates are unchanged.

Horizontal stretch. $$ y \;=\; f(b x) $$ stretches $G$ horizontally by factor $1/|b|$ (compression if $|b| > 1$, since then $1/|b| < 1$). If $b < 0$ the graph is also reflected in the $y$-axis. Every $x$-coordinate is divided by $b$; $y$-coordinates are unchanged.

横向伸缩。 $$ y \;=\; f(b x) $$ 把 $G$ 横向拉伸 $1/|b|$ 倍（$|b| > 1$ 时 $1/|b| < 1$，即压缩）。若 $b < 0$ 还要关于 $y$ 轴反射。每个 $x$ 坐标除以 $b$，$y$ 坐标不变。

Reflection in the $x$-axis. $$ y \;=\; -f(x) $$ flips the graph upside-down. Every $y$-coordinate is negated; $x$-coordinates are unchanged. Fixed points are exactly the zeros of $f$.

Reflection in the $y$-axis. $$ y \;=\; f(-x) $$ flips the graph left-to-right. Every $x$-coordinate is negated; $y$-coordinates are unchanged. Fixed points are the points where the original graph crosses the $y$-axis.

Composite transformations. Moves inside the bracket act on $x$ and moves outside act on $y$; the two groups do not interact, so either group may be done first. Inside the bracket, undo the operations on $x$ in reverse order: for $y = f(b(x - h))$, first stretch horizontally with scale factor $\tfrac{1}{b}$, then translate by $h$. Outside, follow the order of operations: for $y = a f(x) + k$, first stretch vertically with scale factor $a$, then translate by $k$.

How to use this guide本指南使用说明

Transformation questions usually take one of two forms. One is a short sketch: "Given the graph of $y = f(x)$, sketch $y = 2 f(x - 3) + 1$." The other hides the transformation inside a longer modelling question, where reading $y = a \sin(b(x - c)) + d$ correctly is the crux. The structural ideas in this unit are the keys to both.变换题通常有两种形式：一种是简短的图像题（"已知 $y = f(x)$ 的图像，画出 $y = 2 f(x - 3) + 1$"）；另一种把变换藏在较长的建模题里，关键在于正确读懂 $y = a \sin(b(x - c)) + d$。本单元的核心思想对两类题都管用。

!
If you are cramming如果你在临阵磨枪

Memorise the four-row table: $f(x) + k$ shifts up, $f(x - h)$ shifts right, $a f(x)$ stretches vertically by $a$, $f(b x)$ stretches horizontally by $1/b$. Sign in $h$ and reciprocal in $b$ are the two traps. Then practise sketching $y = a \sin(b(x - c)) + d$ from $y = \sin x$ end to end.

背熟四行表：$f(x) + k$ 上移、$f(x - h)$ 右移、$a f(x)$ 纵向拉伸 $a$ 倍、$f(b x)$ 横向拉伸 $1/b$ 倍。$h$ 的符号与 $b$ 的倒数是两个易错点。再完整画一遍 $y = \sin x$ 到 $y = a \sin(b(x - c)) + d$ 的整套变换。

★
If you are going for a 7如果你目标是 7 分

Master the order of composite transformations: inside the bracket the operations act on the input and are undone in reverse order (a translation by $h$ appears as $x - h$, a stretch with scale factor $\tfrac{1}{b}$ as $bx$), while outside the bracket they act on the output in the order written. Check every composite by mapping one labelled point.

掌握复合变换的顺序：括号内的运算作用于输入，按相反顺序撤销（平移 $h$ 写作 $x - h$，比例系数 $\tfrac{1}{b}$ 的伸缩写作 $bx$）；括号外的运算作用于输出，按书写顺序进行。每个复合变换都映射一个已标注点来检查。

At HL, also dissect $y = |f(x)|$ versus $y = f(|x|)$ and learn to sketch $y = 1/f(x)$ from the key features of $f$: zeros, extrema, asymptotes.

HL 学生还要分清 $y = |f(x)|$ 与 $y = f(|x|)$，并能由 $f$ 的关键特征（零点、极值、渐近线）画出 $y = 1/f(x)$。

对 SL 而言，B5.1 至 B5.4 就是本单元的全部：目标是能由已标注的图像手画任意 $y = a f(b(x - h)) + k$，并标出关键点的像。
