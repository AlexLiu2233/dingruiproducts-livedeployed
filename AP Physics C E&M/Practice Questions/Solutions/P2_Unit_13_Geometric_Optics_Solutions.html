Q1EASY 13.1 Law of Reflection13.1 反射定律No Calculator

A narrow beam of light strikes a flat mirror and makes an angle of $35^\circ$ with the surface of the mirror. The angle between the incident ray and the reflected ray at the point of incidence is一束细光束射到平面镜上，与镜面的夹角为 $35^\circ$。在入射点处，入射光线与反射光线之间的夹角为

  • (A) $35^\circ$
  • (B) $55^\circ$
  • (C) $70^\circ$
  • (D) $110^\circ$
Answer:答案： (D)
$$\theta_i = 90^\circ - 35^\circ = 55^\circ,\qquad \theta_r = \theta_i = 55^\circ$$
$$\theta_i + \theta_r = 55^\circ + 55^\circ = 110^\circ$$
Here $\theta_i$ is the angle of incidence and $\theta_r$ the angle of reflection, both measured from the normal to the mirror; the two rays lie on opposite sides of the normal, so their angles add.其中 $\theta_i$ 是入射角，$\theta_r$ 是反射角，两者都从镜面的法线量起；两条光线在法线的两侧，所以两个角相加。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $35^\circ$ is the angle between the incident ray and the mirror surface, not the angle between the two rays. (B) $55^\circ$ is only the angle of incidence $90^\circ-35^\circ$; it leaves out the equal angle of the reflected ray on the other side of the normal. (C) $70^\circ=2(35^\circ)$ doubles the angle with the surface, but the law of reflection uses angles from the normal, not from the surface.(A) $35^\circ$ 是入射光线与镜面之间的夹角，不是两条光线之间的夹角。 (B) $55^\circ$ 只是入射角 $90^\circ-35^\circ$，漏掉了法线另一侧反射光线的同样大小的角。 (C) $70^\circ=2(35^\circ)$ 把与镜面的夹角翻倍，但反射定律用的是从法线量起的角，不是从镜面量起的角。
Insight.要点。 Draw the normal first and put both angles at the normal. An angle given "with the surface" is $90^\circ$ minus the angle of incidence.先画出法线，再把两个角都标在法线处。题中说的“与镜面的夹角”等于 $90^\circ$ 减去入射角。
Q2EASY 13.1 Diffuse Reflection13.1 漫反射No Calculator

A beam of parallel light rays strikes a sheet of white paper, and the reflected light leaves the paper in many different directions. The best explanation is that一束平行光线射到一张白纸上，反射光向许多不同的方向射出。最好的解释是

  • (A) the law of reflection does not hold on a rough surface.反射定律在粗糙表面上不成立。
  • (B) the paper absorbs the light and then emits it again in random directions.纸吸收了光，然后向随机的方向重新发射。
  • (C) each ray obeys the law of reflection, but the normal to the paper points in different directions at the places where the rays strike it.每条光线都遵守反射定律，但光线射到的各处，纸面的法线方向各不相同。
  • (D) the paper changes the wavelength of the light, so the rays separate.纸改变了光的波长，所以光线分开了。
Answer:答案： (C)
$$\theta_r = \theta_i$$
Here $\theta_i$ and $\theta_r$ are the angles of incidence and reflection at one point of the surface, each measured from the local normal at that point. Paper is rough on a small scale, so the local normal changes from point to point; parallel incident rays therefore have different angles of incidence and leave in different directions. This is diffuse reflection.其中 $\theta_i$ 和 $\theta_r$ 是表面上某一点的入射角和反射角，都从该点的法线量起。纸在微观上是粗糙的，所以各点的法线方向不同；平行的入射光线因此有不同的入射角，并向不同方向射出。这就是漫反射。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) the law holds at every point of every surface; what changes on a rough surface is the direction of the normal, not the law. (B) describes absorption and re-emission, which is not the ray model of diffuse reflection: each ray reflects at its own facet. (D) reflection does not change the wavelength of the light; the rays separate only because their angles of incidence differ.(A) 反射定律在每个表面的每一点都成立；粗糙表面上改变的是法线的方向，而不是定律本身。 (B) 说的是吸收再发射，不是漫反射的光线模型：每条光线在自己的小面上反射。 (D) 反射不改变光的波长；光线分开只是因为各自的入射角不同。
Insight.要点。 Specular and diffuse reflection obey the same law; they differ in whether the normal keeps one direction or varies across the patch of light.镜面反射和漫反射遵守同一条定律；区别在于被光照亮的区域内，法线是保持一个方向还是变化不定。
Q3MEDIUM 13.1 Rotating a Mirror13.1 转动平面镜No Calculator

A fixed narrow beam of light strikes a flat mirror. The mirror is rotated by $12^\circ$ about the point where the beam strikes it, and the incident beam does not move. Through what angle does the reflected beam turn?一束固定的细光束射到平面镜上。平面镜绕光束的入射点转过 $12^\circ$，入射光束不动。反射光束转过的角度为

  • (A) $6^\circ$
  • (B) $12^\circ$
  • (C) $24^\circ$
  • (D) $48^\circ$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$\Delta\phi = 2\delta = 2(12^\circ) = 24^\circ$$
Here $\delta$ is the rotation of the mirror and $\Delta\phi$ is the angle through which the reflected beam turns. Rotating the mirror by $\delta$ rotates the normal by $\delta$, which changes the angle of incidence by $\delta$. The reflected ray then moves by $\delta$ because $\theta_r$ changed and by another $\delta$ because the normal itself turned.其中 $\delta$ 是平面镜转过的角度，$\Delta\phi$ 是反射光束转过的角度。平面镜转过 $\delta$，法线也转过 $\delta$，使入射角改变 $\delta$。于是反射光线一方面因 $\theta_r$ 改变而移动 $\delta$，另一方面因法线本身转动又移动 $\delta$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $6^\circ=12^\circ/2$ halves the rotation instead of doubling it. (B) $12^\circ$ counts only the change of the angle of reflection and forgets that the normal turned as well. (D) $48^\circ=4(12^\circ)$ applies the factor of $2$ twice, once for the law of reflection and again for the geometry.(A) $6^\circ=12^\circ/2$ 把转角减半，而不是加倍。 (B) $12^\circ$ 只计入反射角的变化，忘了法线也转动了。 (D) $48^\circ=4(12^\circ)$ 把因子 $2$ 用了两次，一次来自反射定律，另一次来自几何关系。
Insight.要点。 A reflected beam turns twice as far as the mirror. That doubling is why a small mirror rotation can be seen as a large motion of a light spot.反射光束转过的角度是平面镜的两倍。这一倍增使很小的转动也能表现为光斑的明显移动。
Q4MEDIUM 13.1 Rays and Waves13.1 光线与波No Calculator

A narrow laser beam of wavelength $650~\mathrm{nm}$ passes through a slit that is $0.050~\mathrm{mm}$ wide. On a screen several meters away, the spot is much wider than the slit. Which statement is correct?一束波长为 $650~\mathrm{nm}$ 的细激光束穿过宽为 $0.050~\mathrm{mm}$ 的狭缝。在几米外的屏上，光斑比狭缝宽得多。下列哪一项说法正确？

  • (A) The ray model explains it, because the rays refract at the edges of the slit and fan out.光线模型可以解释，因为光线在狭缝边缘折射并向外散开。
  • (B) The ray model cannot explain it, because the spreading of light after a narrow opening depends on the wave nature of light.光线模型不能解释，因为光通过狭窄开口后的扩散取决于光的波动性。
  • (C) The ray model explains it, because the rays reflect from the edges of the slit.光线模型可以解释，因为光线在狭缝边缘反射。
  • (D) The ray model cannot explain it, because a laser beam is not coherent.光线模型不能解释，因为激光束不是相干的。
Answer:答案： (B)
$$\frac{w}{\lambda} = \frac{5.0\times10^{-5}~\mathrm{m}}{6.5\times10^{-7}~\mathrm{m}} \approx 77$$
Here $w$ is the width of the slit and $\lambda$ is the wavelength of the light. A ray is a straight line, so the ray model predicts a spot about as wide as the slit. The observed spreading is a wave effect (diffraction), which is treated in Unit 14. The slit is only about $77$ wavelengths wide, small enough for the wave nature of the light to show.其中 $w$ 是狭缝的宽度，$\lambda$ 是光的波长。光线是直线，所以光线模型预言光斑的宽度与狭缝差不多。观察到的扩散是波动效应（衍射），在第 14 单元中学习。狭缝只有约 $77$ 个波长宽，小到足以显示光的波动性。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) refraction needs a change of medium, and a straight ray at the edge of an opening does not fan out. (C) reflection from the edges would send rays in the directions given by the law of reflection, and the ray model still gives a sharp-edged spot. (D) a laser is a coherent, monochromatic source; it can be modeled as a single ray until it meets a very small opening.(A) 折射需要介质改变，而开口边缘处的直线光线不会向外散开。 (C) 在边缘反射会让光线按反射定律的方向射出，光线模型给出的仍是边缘清晰的光斑。 (D) 激光是相干的单色光源；在遇到非常小的开口之前，可以把它建模为一条光线。
Insight.要点。 Rays suit mirrors, lenses and boundaries much larger than a wavelength. Spreading, interference and diffraction need the wave model.光线模型适用于远大于波长的镜面、透镜和界面。光的扩散、干涉和衍射需要波动模型。
Q5HARD 13.1 Two Reflections13.1 两次反射No Calculator

Two flat mirrors meet at a right angle. A ray that lies in a plane perpendicular to both mirrors strikes the first mirror with an angle of incidence of $40^\circ$, reflects, strikes the second mirror, and reflects again. The angle between the direction of travel of the final ray and the direction of travel of the original incident ray is两块平面镜成直角相接。一条位于与两镜都垂直的平面内的光线，以 $40^\circ$ 的入射角射到第一块镜上，反射后射到第二块镜上，再次反射。最终光线的传播方向与原入射光线的传播方向之间的夹角为

  • (A) $0^\circ$
  • (B) $80^\circ$
  • (C) $100^\circ$
  • (D) $180^\circ$
Answer:答案： (D)
$$(v_x,\,v_y)\;\longrightarrow\;(v_x,\,-v_y)\;\longrightarrow\;(-v_x,\,-v_y)$$
Here $v_x$ and $v_y$ are the components of the ray's direction along the first mirror and along the second mirror. Reflection from the first mirror reverses the component perpendicular to it, $v_y$; reflection from the second mirror reverses the component perpendicular to it, $v_x$. The final direction is exactly opposite to the original direction, whatever the angle of incidence, so the angle between the two directions is $180^\circ$.其中 $v_x$ 和 $v_y$ 是光线方向沿第一块镜和沿第二块镜的分量。在第一块镜上反射，垂直于它的分量 $v_y$ 反向；在第二块镜上反射，垂直于它的分量 $v_x$ 反向。无论入射角多大，最终方向都与原方向正好相反，所以两个方向之间的夹角是 $180^\circ$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $0^\circ$ assumes the second reflection exactly undoes the first, but both components must be reversed. (B) $80^\circ=2(40^\circ)$ is the angle between the incident and reflected rays at the first mirror, which is not the change of direction of travel. (C) $100^\circ=180^\circ-2(40^\circ)$ is how far the direction turns at the first mirror alone; it stops before the second reflection.(A) $0^\circ$ 以为第二次反射恰好抵消第一次，但两个分量都要反向。 (B) $80^\circ=2(40^\circ)$ 是第一块镜处入射光线与反射光线之间的夹角，不是传播方向的改变量。 (C) $100^\circ=180^\circ-2(40^\circ)$ 是只经过第一块镜时方向转过的角度，没有算第二次反射。
Insight.要点。 Two perpendicular mirrors send a ray straight back toward its source, for every angle of incidence.两块互相垂直的平面镜会把光线沿原方向反射回去，与入射角无关。
Q6EASY 13.2 Plane Mirror13.2 平面镜No Calculator

A person stands $2.0~\mathrm{m}$ in front of a large flat mirror. The distance between the person and the person's image is一个人站在一面大平面镜前 $2.0~\mathrm{m}$ 处。此人与其像之间的距离为

  • (A) $1.0~\mathrm{m}$
  • (B) $2.0~\mathrm{m}$
  • (C) $4.0~\mathrm{m}$
  • (D) $6.0~\mathrm{m}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$\lvert s_\text{i}\rvert = s_\text{o} = 2.0~\mathrm{m},\qquad d = s_\text{o} + \lvert s_\text{i}\rvert = 2.0 + 2.0 = 4.0~\mathrm{m}$$
Here $s_\text{o}$ is the distance from the mirror to the object, $\lvert s_\text{i}\rvert$ is the distance from the mirror to the image (behind the mirror), and $d$ is the distance between the object and its image. The image of a plane mirror is as far behind the mirror as the object is in front of it.其中 $s_\text{o}$ 是物到镜面的距离，$\lvert s_\text{i}\rvert$ 是像到镜面的距离（在镜后），$d$ 是物与像之间的距离。平面镜所成的像在镜后的距离等于物在镜前的距离。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $1.0~\mathrm{m}$ halves the distance, as if the image lay midway between the person and the mirror. (B) $2.0~\mathrm{m}$ is only the distance from the mirror to the image. (D) $6.0~\mathrm{m}=2.0+2.0+2.0$ adds the person's distance to the mirror a third time.(A) $1.0~\mathrm{m}$ 把距离减半，好像像在人与镜面的中点。 (B) $2.0~\mathrm{m}$ 只是镜面到像的距离。 (D) $6.0~\mathrm{m}=2.0+2.0+2.0$ 把人到镜面的距离多加了一次（共三次）。
Insight.要点。 The object-to-image distance for a plane mirror is twice the object-to-mirror distance, and the image is virtual, upright and the same size.平面镜的物像距离是物到镜面距离的两倍；像是虚像、正立、与物等大。
Q7MEDIUM 13.2 Mirror Equation13.2 镜面方程Calculator

An object is $30~\mathrm{cm}$ in front of a concave spherical mirror whose focal length is $20~\mathrm{cm}$. The image is一个物体位于焦距为 $20~\mathrm{cm}$ 的凹面镜前 $30~\mathrm{cm}$ 处。所成的像

  • (A) $60~\mathrm{cm}$ in front of the mirror, real在镜前 $60~\mathrm{cm}$ 处，是实像
  • (B) $12~\mathrm{cm}$ in front of the mirror, real在镜前 $12~\mathrm{cm}$ 处，是实像
  • (C) $60~\mathrm{cm}$ behind the mirror, virtual在镜后 $60~\mathrm{cm}$ 处，是虚像
  • (D) $10~\mathrm{cm}$ in front of the mirror, real在镜前 $10~\mathrm{cm}$ 处，是实像
Answer:答案： (A)
$$\frac{1}{s_\text{i}} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{s_\text{o}} = \frac{1}{20~\mathrm{cm}} - \frac{1}{30~\mathrm{cm}} = \frac{1}{60~\mathrm{cm}}$$
$$s_\text{i} = +60~\mathrm{cm}$$
Here $s_\text{o}$ is the object distance, $s_\text{i}$ the image distance and $f$ the focal length, all measured from the mirror. A positive $s_\text{i}$ means a real image, in front of the mirror.其中 $s_\text{o}$ 是物距，$s_\text{i}$ 是像距，$f$ 是焦距，都从镜面量起。$s_\text{i}$ 为正表示像是实像，在镜前。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) $12~\mathrm{cm}$ adds the reciprocals, $1/s_\text{i}=1/20+1/30=1/12$, instead of subtracting. (C) swaps the two terms, $1/s_\text{i}=1/30-1/20=-1/60$, which gives $-60~\mathrm{cm}$ and a virtual image. (D) $10~\mathrm{cm}=30-20$ subtracts the distances instead of the reciprocals.(B) $12~\mathrm{cm}$ 把倒数相加，$1/s_\text{i}=1/20+1/30=1/12$，而不是相减。 (C) 把两项的次序弄反，$1/s_\text{i}=1/30-1/20=-1/60$，得到 $-60~\mathrm{cm}$ 和虚像。 (D) $10~\mathrm{cm}=30-20$ 是把距离相减，而不是把倒数相减。
Insight.要点。 The mirror equation relates reciprocals. An object beyond the focal point of a concave mirror gives a real image; the magnification here is $-2$, so it is also inverted and enlarged.镜面方程联系的是倒数。凹面镜的物体在焦点之外时成实像；这里放大率为 $-2$，所以像还是倒立、放大的。
Q8EASY 13.2 Convex Mirror Image13.2 凸面镜成像No Calculator

A real object is placed in front of a convex spherical mirror at any distance from it. The image formed is always把一个实物放在凸面镜前任意距离处。所成的像总是

  • (A) real, inverted and reduced实像、倒立、缩小
  • (B) virtual, upright and enlarged虚像、正立、放大
  • (C) real, upright and reduced实像、正立、缩小
  • (D) virtual, upright and reduced虚像、正立、缩小
Answer:答案： (D)
$$\frac{1}{s_\text{i}} = \frac{1}{f}-\frac{1}{s_\text{o}}\lt 0,\qquad M = -\frac{s_\text{i}}{s_\text{o}} = \frac{\lvert f\rvert}{\lvert f\rvert + s_\text{o}},\quad 0 \lt M \lt 1$$
Here $s_\text{o}$ is the object distance, $s_\text{i}$ the image distance, $f$ the focal length (negative for a convex mirror) and $M$ the magnification. Both terms of $1/s_\text{i}$ are negative, so $s_\text{i}\lt0$: the image is virtual. $M$ is positive and below $1$: the image is upright and reduced.其中 $s_\text{o}$ 是物距，$s_\text{i}$ 是像距，$f$ 是焦距（凸面镜为负），$M$ 是放大率。$1/s_\text{i}$ 的两项都是负的，所以 $s_\text{i}\lt0$：像是虚像。$M$ 为正且小于 $1$：像是正立、缩小的。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) is the pattern for a concave mirror with the object far away; a diverging mirror never brings rays to a real focus. (B) is the pattern for a concave mirror with the object inside its focal point; a convex mirror never enlarges. (C) combines a real image with an upright one, which a single real object cannot produce with a mirror: real images of real objects are inverted.(A) 是凹面镜在物体很远时的情形；发散的镜面不会使光线会聚成实焦点。 (B) 是凹面镜在物体位于焦点以内时的情形；凸面镜不会放大。 (C) 把实像与正立结合在一起，而单个实物经镜面成的实像一定是倒立的。
Insight.要点。 A convex mirror always gives a virtual, upright, reduced image that lies between the mirror and its focal point.凸面镜总是成虚像、正立、缩小的像，像位于镜面与焦点之间。
Q9HARD 13.2 Convex Mirror Magnification13.2 凸面镜的放大率Calculator

A convex spherical mirror has a radius of curvature of $60~\mathrm{cm}$. An object $18~\mathrm{cm}$ tall stands $60~\mathrm{cm}$ in front of the mirror. The height and orientation of the image are一面凸面镜的曲率半径为 $60~\mathrm{cm}$。一个高 $18~\mathrm{cm}$ 的物体立在镜前 $60~\mathrm{cm}$ 处。像的高度和朝向为

  • (A) $9.0~\mathrm{cm}$, upright$9.0~\mathrm{cm}$，正立
  • (B) $6.0~\mathrm{cm}$, upright$6.0~\mathrm{cm}$，正立
  • (C) $18~\mathrm{cm}$, inverted$18~\mathrm{cm}$，倒立
  • (D) $54~\mathrm{cm}$, upright$54~\mathrm{cm}$，正立
Answer:答案： (B)
$$f = -\frac{R}{2} = -30~\mathrm{cm},\qquad \frac{1}{s_\text{i}} = -\frac{1}{30} - \frac{1}{60} = -\frac{3}{60}\;\Rightarrow\; s_\text{i} = -20~\mathrm{cm}$$
$$M = -\frac{s_\text{i}}{s_\text{o}} = +\frac{20}{60} = +\frac{1}{3},\qquad h_\text{i} = M h_\text{o} = \frac{18~\mathrm{cm}}{3} = 6.0~\mathrm{cm}$$
Here $R$ is the radius of curvature, $f$ the focal length, $s_\text{o}$ and $s_\text{i}$ the object and image distances, $M$ the magnification, and $h_\text{o}$ and $h_\text{i}$ the object and image heights. The positive $M$ means the image is upright.其中 $R$ 是曲率半径，$f$ 是焦距，$s_\text{o}$ 和 $s_\text{i}$ 是物距和像距，$M$ 是放大率，$h_\text{o}$ 和 $h_\text{i}$ 是物高和像高。$M$ 为正表示像是正立的。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $9.0~\mathrm{cm}$ takes the focal length to be the radius, $f=-60~\mathrm{cm}$, which gives $s_\text{i}=-30~\mathrm{cm}$ and $M=+\tfrac12$. Mathematical Routines. A narrow laser beam in air strikes a flat mirror with an angle of incidence of $35^\circ$. A flat screen is placed perpendicular to the reflected beam, $4.0~\mathrm{m}$ from the point of incidence, and the beam makes a spot on it.数学运算。空气中的一束细激光束以 $35^\circ$ 的入射角射到平面镜上。一块平面屏垂直于反射光束放置，距入射点 $4.0~\mathrm{m}$，光束在屏上形成一个光斑。

(a) The law of reflection gives $\theta_r=\theta_i=35^\circ$ from the normal, so the reflected ray makes $90^\circ-35^\circ$ with the surface:反射定律给出，从法线量起 $\theta_r=\theta_i=35^\circ$，所以反射光线与镜面的夹角为 $90^\circ-35^\circ$：
$$90^\circ - 35^\circ = 55^\circ$$
(b) The two rays lie on opposite sides of the normal, so their angles add:两条光线在法线的两侧，所以两个角相加：
$$\theta_i + \theta_r = 35^\circ + 35^\circ = 70^\circ$$
(c) Rotating the mirror by $\delta$ rotates its normal by $\delta$:平面镜转过 $\delta$，它的法线也转过 $\delta$：
$$\theta_i' = 35^\circ + 8.0^\circ = 43^\circ$$
$$\Delta\phi = 2\delta = 2(8.0^\circ) = 16^\circ$$
The reflected ray moves by $\delta$ because the angle of reflection changed and by another $\delta$ because the normal turned.反射光线一方面因反射角改变而移动 $\delta$，另一方面因法线转动又移动 $\delta$。
(d) The beam turns through $16^\circ$ about the point of incidence, and the screen is $L=4.0~\mathrm{m}$ away and perpendicular to the original reflected beam:光束绕入射点转过 $16^\circ$，屏距入射点 $L=4.0~\mathrm{m}$，且垂直于原来的反射光束：
$$d = L\tan\Delta\phi = (4.0~\mathrm{m})\tan16^\circ = (4.0)(0.287)~\mathrm{m} \approx 1.1~\mathrm{m}$$
Symbols: $\theta_i$ and $\theta_r$ are the angles of incidence and reflection from the normal, $\theta_i'$ is the new angle of incidence, $\delta$ is the rotation of the mirror, $\Delta\phi$ is the angle through which the reflected beam turns, $L$ is the distance from the point of incidence to the screen and $d$ is the distance the spot moves.符号：$\theta_i$ 和 $\theta_r$ 是从法线量起的入射角和反射角，$\theta_i'$ 是新的入射角，$\delta$ 是平面镜转过的角度，$\Delta\phi$ 是反射光束转过的角度，$L$ 是入射点到屏的距离，$d$ 是光斑移动的距离。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 False moves: (a) giving $35^\circ$, which is the angle of incidence, as the angle with the surface; (c) saying the beam turns by only $8.0^\circ$, the rotation of the mirror; (d) using the rotation of the mirror in place of the turn of the beam, $(4.0)\tan8.0^\circ=0.56~\mathrm{m}$.常见错误：(a) 把入射角 $35^\circ$ 当作与镜面的夹角；(c) 以为光束只转过平面镜的转角 $8.0^\circ$；(d) 用平面镜的转角代替光束转过的角度，得到 $(4.0)\tan8.0^\circ=0.56~\mathrm{m}$。
Insight.要点。 Measure every angle from the normal, and remember that a reflected beam turns twice as far as the mirror does.每个角都从法线量起，并记住反射光束转过的角度是平面镜转角的两倍。

Translation Between Representations. A thin diverging lens has a focal length of $f=-18~\mathrm{cm}$. A real object $6.0~\mathrm{cm}$ tall stands on the principal axis, $24~\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens.表征转换。一个薄发散透镜的焦距为 $f=-18~\mathrm{cm}$。一个高 $6.0~\mathrm{cm}$ 的实物立在主光轴上，距透镜 $24~\mathrm{cm}$。

(a) Rays that arrive parallel to the axis of a diverging lens are refracted so that they spread apart, and they appear to have come from one point on the object's side of the lens, the focal point. This focal point is not a place where light actually meets, so by the sign convention the focal length of a diverging element is negative.平行于发散透镜主光轴入射的光线被折射后向外散开，看起来像是从透镜物方一侧的一点发出的，这一点就是焦点。这个焦点不是光线真正相交的地方，所以按符号约定，发散元件的焦距为负。
(b) From the lens equation:由透镜方程：
$$\frac{1}{s_\text{i}} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{s_\text{o}} = -\frac{1}{18} - \frac{1}{24} = -\frac{7}{72}\;\Rightarrow\; s_\text{i} = -10.3~\mathrm{cm}$$
The negative sign means a virtual image, on the same side of the lens as the object.负号表示虚像，与物体在透镜的同一侧。
(c) The magnification and the image height:放大率和像高：
$$M = -\frac{s_\text{i}}{s_\text{o}} = +\frac{10.3}{24} = +0.43,\qquad h_\text{i} = M h_\text{o} = (0.43)(6.0~\mathrm{cm}) = 2.6~\mathrm{cm}$$
$M$ is positive and less than $1$, so the image is upright and reduced.$M$ 为正且小于 $1$，所以像是正立、缩小的。
(d) The lens equation gives $1/s_\text{i}=-1/s_\text{o}+1/f$, so the line has slope $-1$ (no units) and vertical intercept:透镜方程给出 $1/s_\text{i}=-1/s_\text{o}+1/f$，所以直线的斜率为 $-1$（无单位），纵轴截距为：
$$\frac{1}{f} = -\frac{1}{18~\mathrm{cm}} = -0.056~\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$$
The negative intercept means a negative focal length, which shows a diverging lens.截距为负表示焦距为负，说明这是发散透镜。
Symbols: $f$ is the focal length, $s_\text{o}$ the object distance, $s_\text{i}$ the image distance (negative for a virtual image), $M$ the magnification, and $h_\text{o}$ and $h_\text{i}$ the object and image heights.符号：$f$ 是焦距，$s_\text{o}$ 是物距，$s_\text{i}$ 是像距（虚像为负），$M$ 是放大率，$h_\text{o}$ 和 $h_\text{i}$ 是物高和像高。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 False moves: (a) calling the focal point of a diverging lens a place where the rays meet; (b) using $f=+18~\mathrm{cm}$ as for a converging lens, which gives $1/s_\text{i}=1/18-1/24$ and a real image $72~\mathrm{cm}$ from the lens; (c) giving a negative $M$ and an inverted image because the sign of $s_\text{i}$ was dropped; (d) reporting the intercept as $+0.056~\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$.常见错误：(a) 把发散透镜的焦点说成光线相交的地方；(b) 像会聚透镜那样取 $f=+18~\mathrm{cm}$，得到 $1/s_\text{i}=1/18-1/24$ 和离透镜 $72~\mathrm{cm}$ 的实像；(c) 丢掉 $s_\text{i}$ 的符号，得到负的 $M$ 和倒立的像；(d) 把截距写成 $+0.056~\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$。
Insight.要点。 A diverging lens always gives a virtual, upright, reduced image of a real object, and the sign of the intercept on the reciprocal graph carries the same information as the sign of $f$.发散透镜对实物总是成虚像、正立、缩小的像，倒数图上截距的符号与 $f$ 的符号包含同样的信息。
AP rubric, 8 points.AP 评分标准，共 8 分。 (a) 1: states that parallel rays spread apart and appear to come from a focal point on the object's side · 1: explains that this focal point is not where light meets, so $f$ is negative · (b) 1: applies the lens equation with $f=-18~\mathrm{cm}$ · 1: obtains $s_\text{i}=-10.3~\mathrm{cm}$ and states virtual, on the object's side · (c) 1: obtains $M=+0.43$ and $h_\text{i}=2.6~\mathrm{cm}$ · 1: states upright and reduced · (d) 1: states slope $-1$ and intercept $1/f=-0.056~\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ · 1: links the negative intercept to a negative $f$ and a diverging lens.
