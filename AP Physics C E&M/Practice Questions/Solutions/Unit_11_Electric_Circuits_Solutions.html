A circuit schematic is drawn with conventional current. A battery drives a steady current through a single resistor. Which statement is correct?某电路图按约定电流绘制。电池驱动稳恒电流通过一个电阻。下列哪项陈述正确？

  • (A) Conventional current leaves the negative terminal, passes through the resistor, and enters the positive terminal.约定电流从负极流出，经过电阻，进入正极。
  • (B) Conventional current leaves the positive terminal, passes through the resistor, and returns to the negative terminal. Electrons drift the opposite way.约定电流从正极流出，经过电阻，回到负极。电子的漂移方向与之相反。
  • (C) Conventional current and electron drift point the same way through the resistor.在电阻中，约定电流与电子漂移方向相同。
  • (D) Current is zero inside the battery and nonzero only in the resistor.电池内部电流为零，只有电阻中有电流。
Answer:答案： (B)
Schematics use conventional current, the direction positive charge would move: out of the positive terminal, around the external circuit, and back to the negative terminal. The charge carriers in a metal are electrons, which drift in the opposite direction.电路图采用约定电流，即正电荷的运动方向：从正极流出，经外电路，回到负极。金属中的载流子是电子，其漂移方向与之相反。

Insight.要点。 Conventional current is a bookkeeping choice, but it is the one every AP schematic uses unless told otherwise.约定电流只是一种记法，但除非另有说明，AP 电路图都采用它。

With $A=1.0~\mathrm{mm^2}=1.0\times10^{-6}~\mathrm{m^2}$ and $I=nqv_dA$:$$v_d=\frac{I}{neA}=\frac{2.0}{(8.5\times10^{28})(1.60\times10^{-19})(1.0\times10^{-6})}=1.5\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{m/s}$$取 $A=1.0~\mathrm{mm^2}=1.0\times10^{-6}~\mathrm{m^2}$，由 $I=nqv_dA$：$$v_d=\frac{I}{neA}=\frac{2.0}{(8.5\times10^{28})(1.60\times10^{-19})(1.0\times10^{-6})}=1.5\times10^{-4}~\mathrm{m/s}$$

The power dissipated in $R_1$ after the wire is connected is closest to一个理想的 $12~\mathrm{V}$ 电池与 $20~\Omega$ 电阻 $R_1$ 和 $40~\Omega$ 电阻 $R_2$ 串联。随后用一根电阻可忽略的导线直接并接在 $R_2$ 两端。接上导线后 $R_1$ 消耗的功率最接近

  • (A) $0.80~\mathrm{W}$
  • (B) $1.8~\mathrm{W}$
  • (C) $7.2~\mathrm{W}$
  • (D) $12~\mathrm{W}$
Answer:答案： (C)
The wire has no resistance, so charge flows through it instead of through $R_2$. This is a short circuit across $R_2$, and the circuit is $R_1$ alone across the battery.$$I=\frac{\varepsilon}{R_1}=\frac{12}{20}=0.60~\mathrm{A},\qquad P_1=I^2R_1=(0.60)^2(20)=7.2~\mathrm{W}$$导线没有电阻，电荷从导线而不是从 $R_2$ 通过。这就是对 $R_2$ 的短路，电路变成只有 $R_1$ 接在电池两端。$$I=\frac{\varepsilon}{R_1}=\frac{12}{20}=0.60~\mathrm{A},\qquad P_1=I^2R_1=(0.60)^2(20)=7.2~\mathrm{W}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $0.80~\mathrm{W}$ is the power in $R_1$ before the wire is connected, $(0.20)^2(20)$, as if the wire changed nothing. (B) $1.8~\mathrm{W}$ treats the wire as removing $R_1$ instead of $R_2$: $I=12/40=0.30~\mathrm{A}$ and $P=(0.30)^2(20)$. (D) $12~\mathrm{W}$ is $IR_1=12~\mathrm{V}$ read as a power; the current was multiplied by the resistance instead of by the potential difference.(A) $0.80~\mathrm{W}$ 是接上导线之前 $R_1$ 的功率，$(0.20)^2(20)$，好像导线没有任何影响。(B) $1.8~\mathrm{W}$ 把导线当成去掉了 $R_1$ 而不是 $R_2$：$I=12/40=0.30~\mathrm{A}$，$P=(0.30)^2(20)$。(D) $12~\mathrm{W}$ 是 $IR_1=12~\mathrm{V}$，把电势差当成了功率；电流乘的是电阻，而不是电势差。
Insight.要点。 A short circuit is a path along which charge flows with no change in potential difference. The bypassed resistor carries no current, and the rest of the circuit carries more.短路是电荷流动时电势差不变的通路。被旁路的电阻不再有电流，而电路其余部分的电流变大。

A uniform wire has resistance $R$. It is drawn out to twice its original length with no change in volume or resistivity. Its new resistance is一根均匀导线的电阻为 $R$。把它拉长到原长的两倍，体积和电阻率均不变。其新电阻为

  • (A) $2R$
  • (B) $4R$
  • (C) $8R$
  • (D) $R$

Constant volume means $AL$ is fixed, so doubling $L$ halves $A$.$$R'=\rho\frac{2L}{A/2}=4\rho\frac{L}{A}=4R$$体积不变意味着 $AL$ 不变，$L$ 加倍则 $A$ 减半。$$R'=\rho\frac{2L}{A/2}=4\rho\frac{L}{A}=4R$$

(D) $R$ assumes resistance depends only on the material and volume.(A) $2R$ 只考虑了长度变长，忽略了导线变细。(C) $8R$ 多算了，相当于面积变为原来的四分之一。(D) $R$ 误以为电阻只取决于材料和体积。
Insight.要点。 Stretching at fixed volume changes both factors in $R=\rho L/A$ in the same direction, so the effect is squared.体积不变地拉伸会使 $R=\rho L/A$ 中的两个因子同向变化，效果按平方计。
