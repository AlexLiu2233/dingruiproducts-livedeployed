Setting Up Differential Equations and Separation of Variables建立微分方程与分离变量法 HL AHL 5.14

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Translate a statement of proportionality or a rate of change in context into a differential equation, defining the constant and its sign.
  • Explain the difference between a general solution (with an arbitrary constant) and a particular solution fixed by an initial condition.
  • Solve $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x)g(y)$ by separation of variables, including exponential growth and decay and Newton's law of cooling.
  • Use two pieces of data to find both the constant of integration and the constant of proportionality.
  • Check a solution by substituting back into the differential equation, and state the domain on which it is valid.
  • 把比例关系或情境中的变化率（rate of change）翻译成微分方程，并说明常数的含义与正负。
  • 解释通解（含任意常数）与由初始条件确定的特解之间的区别。
  • 用分离变量法求解 $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x)g(y)$，包括指数增长与衰减以及牛顿冷却定律。
  • 利用两组数据同时求出积分常数与比例常数。
  • 把解代回微分方程进行检验，并说明解成立的定义域。

From a statement to a differential equation从文字叙述到微分方程

A differential equation (DE) is an equation that contains a derivative of an unknown function. When a quantity $y$ changes with time $t$, its rate of change is $\dfrac{dy}{dt}$. The phrase "proportional to" means "equals a constant times": "the rate of change of $y$ is proportional to $y$" becomes $\dfrac{dy}{dt} = ky$, where $k$ is the constant of proportionality. Decide the sign of $k$ from the words: if $y$ is growing, $k > 0$; for "the rate of decrease is proportional to $y$" write $\dfrac{dy}{dt} = -ky$ with $k > 0$. Newton's law of cooling says the rate of change of an object's temperature $T$ is proportional to the difference between $T$ and the surroundings $T_{s}$, which gives $\dfrac{dT}{dt} = -k(T - T_{s})$ with $k > 0$: the object warms when $T < T_{s}$ and cools when $T > T_{s}$.

微分方程（differential equation，DE）是含有未知函数导数的方程。量 $y$ 随时间 $t$ 变化时，其变化率为 $\dfrac{dy}{dt}$。"与……成正比"（proportional to）的意思是"等于一个常数乘以……"：例如"$y$ 的变化率与 $y$ 成正比"写成 $\dfrac{dy}{dt} = ky$，其中 $k$ 为比例常数。$k$ 的正负由文字决定：$y$ 增长时 $k > 0$；"减少的速率与 $y$ 成正比"则写成 $\dfrac{dy}{dt} = -ky$，且 $k > 0$。牛顿冷却定律指出，物体温度 $T$ 的变化率与 $T$ 和环境温度 $T_{s}$ 之差成正比，即 $\dfrac{dT}{dt} = -k(T - T_{s})$，$k > 0$：当 $T < T_{s}$ 时物体升温，当 $T > T_{s}$ 时物体降温。

Separation of variables分离变量法

If the equation can be written $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x)\,g(y)$, with $x$-terms and $y$-terms multiplying, you can separate the variables: move every $y$ to one side with $dy$ and every $x$ to the other with $dx$, then integrate each side. One constant of integration is enough, because the two constants merge into one:

若方程可以写成 $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x)\,g(y)$，即 $x$ 的部分与 $y$ 的部分相乘，就可以分离变量（separation of variables）：把含 $y$ 的项连同 $dy$ 移到一侧，含 $x$ 的项连同 $dx$ 移到另一侧，再分别积分。只需要一个积分常数，因为两侧的常数可合并为一个：

$$ \int \frac{1}{g(y)}\,dy = \int f(x)\,dx + c $$

The result with the arbitrary constant $c$ is the general solution: a whole family of curves. An initial condition such as $y(0) = 80$ picks one curve; the result is the particular solution. Two cautions. Dividing by $g(y)$ assumes $g(y) \neq 0$, so check separately whether a constant solution with $g(y) = 0$ (for example $y = 0$) is also a solution. And when you integrate $\dfrac{1}{y}$, write $\ln|y|$; the sign of $y$ is fixed by the initial condition, so you can usually drop the modulus and say which sign you are using.

含任意常数 $c$ 的结果称为通解（general solution），表示一整族曲线。给定初始条件（initial condition），例如 $y(0) = 80$，就选出其中一条曲线，得到特解（particular solution）。有两点要注意。除以 $g(y)$ 默认了 $g(y) \neq 0$，所以要单独检查使 $g(y) = 0$ 的常数函数（例如 $y = 0$）是否也是解。对 $\dfrac{1}{y}$ 积分时应写作 $\ln|y|$；$y$ 的符号由初始条件决定，因此通常可以去掉绝对值，但要说明所取的符号。

Worked Example: exponential decay from two data points例题：由两组数据求指数衰减

The mass $m$ grams of a radioactive sample decreases at a rate proportional to $m$. Initially $m = 80$ and after 5 days $m = 60$. (a) Write down a differential equation. (b) Solve it for $m$ in terms of $t$ (days). (c) Find $m$ after 10 days. (d) Find the half-life, to 3 significant figures.某放射性样品的质量 $m$ 克以与 $m$ 成正比的速率减少。开始时 $m = 80$，5 天后 $m = 60$。(a) 写出微分方程。(b) 求 $m$ 关于 $t$（天）的表达式。(c) 求 10 天后的 $m$。(d) 求半衰期，保留 3 位有效数字。

(a) The rate of decrease is proportional to $m$, so $\dfrac{dm}{dt} = -km$ with $k > 0$.

(a) 减少的速率与 $m$ 成正比，所以 $\dfrac{dm}{dt} = -km$，$k > 0$。

(b) Separate: $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{m}\,dm = \int -k\,dt$, so $\ln m = -kt + c$ (here $m > 0$). Then $m = Ae^{-kt}$ with $A = e^{c}$. The initial condition $m(0) = 80$ gives $A = 80$. The second datum $m(5) = 60$ gives $60 = 80e^{-5k}$, so $e^{-5k} = \dfrac{3}{4}$ and $k = \dfrac{1}{5}\ln\dfrac{4}{3} = 0.0575$ (3 s.f.). Since $e^{-5k} = \dfrac{3}{4}$, we can write $m = 80\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{t/5}$.

(b) 分离变量：$\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{m}\,dm = \int -k\,dt$，所以 $\ln m = -kt + c$（此处 $m > 0$）。于是 $m = Ae^{-kt}$，其中 $A = e^{c}$。初始条件 $m(0) = 80$ 给出 $A = 80$。第二组数据 $m(5) = 60$ 给出 $60 = 80e^{-5k}$，故 $e^{-5k} = \dfrac{3}{4}$，$k = \dfrac{1}{5}\ln\dfrac{4}{3} = 0.0575$（3 位有效数字）。由于 $e^{-5k} = \dfrac{3}{4}$，也可写成 $m = 80\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{t/5}$。

(c) $m(10) = 80\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{2} = 80 \times 0.5625 = 45$ grams.

(c) $m(10) = 80\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{2} = 80 \times 0.5625 = 45$ 克。

(d) Half of 80 is 40, so $40 = 80e^{-kt}$, giving $t = \dfrac{\ln 2}{k} = \dfrac{5\ln 2}{\ln(4/3)} = 12.0$ days (3 s.f.). On a GDC, store the exact value of $k$, then either solve $80e^{-kt} = 40$ with the equation solver or find the intersection of $y = 80e^{-kx}$ with $y = 40$.

(d) 80 的一半是 40，所以 $40 = 80e^{-kt}$，得 $t = \dfrac{\ln 2}{k} = \dfrac{5\ln 2}{\ln(4/3)} = 12.0$ 天（3 位有效数字）。用 GDC 时，先存储 $k$ 的精确值，再用方程求解器解 $80e^{-kt} = 40$，或求 $y = 80e^{-kx}$ 与 $y = 40$ 的交点。

Check by substitution. From $m = 80e^{-kt}$, $\dfrac{dm}{dt} = -80ke^{-kt} = -km$, which is the differential equation, and $m(0) = 80$.

代回检验。由 $m = 80e^{-kt}$ 得 $\dfrac{dm}{dt} = -80ke^{-kt} = -km$，正是原微分方程，且 $m(0) = 80$。

Why this works. The constant $A$ is the starting value and $k$ is the decay rate; two pieces of data are needed because there are two unknown constants.

为什么可行。常数 $A$ 是初始值，$k$ 是衰减率；有两个未知常数，所以需要两组数据。

Worked Example: Newton's law of cooling例题：牛顿冷却定律

A drink at $90^{\circ}$C is placed in a room at $20^{\circ}$C. Its temperature $T$ satisfies $\dfrac{dT}{dt} = -k(T - 20)$ with $k > 0$, where $t$ is in minutes. After 10 minutes $T = 60$. (a) Solve the equation for $T$. (b) Find $T$ after 20 minutes. (c) Find when $T = 30$. (d) What happens to $T$ as $t \to \infty$?一杯 $90^{\circ}$C 的饮料放在 $20^{\circ}$C 的房间里。温度 $T$ 满足 $\dfrac{dT}{dt} = -k(T - 20)$，$k > 0$，$t$ 的单位为分钟。10 分钟后 $T = 60$。(a) 求 $T$。(b) 求 20 分钟后的 $T$。(c) 求 $T = 30$ 的时刻。(d) 当 $t \to \infty$ 时 $T$ 如何变化？

(a) Separate: $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{T - 20}\,dT = \int -k\,dt$. The drink stays above 20, so $T - 20 > 0$ and $\ln(T - 20) = -kt + c$, that is $T = 20 + Ae^{-kt}$. From $T(0) = 90$: $A = 70$. From $T(10) = 60$: $40 = 70e^{-10k}$, so $e^{-10k} = \dfrac{4}{7}$ and $k = \dfrac{1}{10}\ln\dfrac{7}{4} = 0.0560$ (3 s.f.). Hence $T = 20 + 70e^{-kt}$.

(a) 分离变量：$\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{T - 20}\,dT = \int -k\,dt$。饮料温度始终高于 20，所以 $T - 20 > 0$，$\ln(T - 20) = -kt + c$，即 $T = 20 + Ae^{-kt}$。由 $T(0) = 90$ 得 $A = 70$。由 $T(10) = 60$ 得 $40 = 70e^{-10k}$，所以 $e^{-10k} = \dfrac{4}{7}$，$k = \dfrac{1}{10}\ln\dfrac{7}{4} = 0.0560$（3 位有效数字）。因此 $T = 20 + 70e^{-kt}$。

(b) $e^{-20k} = \left(\dfrac{4}{7}\right)^{2} = \dfrac{16}{49}$, so $T(20) = 20 + 70 \times \dfrac{16}{49} = 20 + 22.857\ldots = 42.9^{\circ}$C (3 s.f.).

(b) $e^{-20k} = \left(\dfrac{4}{7}\right)^{2} = \dfrac{16}{49}$，所以 $T(20) = 20 + 70 \times \dfrac{16}{49} = 20 + 22.857\ldots = 42.9^{\circ}$C（3 位有效数字）。

(c) $30 = 20 + 70e^{-kt}$ gives $e^{-kt} = \dfrac{1}{7}$, so $t = \dfrac{\ln 7}{k} = \dfrac{10\ln 7}{\ln(7/4)} = 34.8$ minutes (3 s.f.). A GDC graph of $y = 20 + 70e^{-kx}$ and $y = 30$ confirms the intersection.

(c) $30 = 20 + 70e^{-kt}$ 得 $e^{-kt} = \dfrac{1}{7}$，所以 $t = \dfrac{\ln 7}{k} = \dfrac{10\ln 7}{\ln(7/4)} = 34.8$ 分钟（3 位有效数字）。在 GDC 上画出 $y = 20 + 70e^{-kx}$ 与 $y = 30$ 的图像，可以确认交点。

(d) $e^{-kt} \to 0$, so $T \to 20$: the drink approaches room temperature and never goes below it.

(d) $e^{-kt} \to 0$，所以 $T \to 20$：饮料温度趋近室温，且永远不会低于室温。

Check by substitution. $\dfrac{dT}{dt} = -70ke^{-kt} = -k(T - 20)$, and $T(0) = 20 + 70 = 90$.

代回检验。$\dfrac{dT}{dt} = -70ke^{-kt} = -k(T - 20)$，且 $T(0) = 20 + 70 = 90$。

Worked Example: a separable equation and its domain例题：可分离方程及其定义域

Solve $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2xy^{2}$ with $y(0) = 1$, and state the values of $x$ for which your solution is valid.求解 $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2xy^{2}$，$y(0) = 1$，并说明所得解成立的 $x$ 的取值范围。

Here $f(x) = 2x$ and $g(y) = y^{2}$. For $y \neq 0$: $\displaystyle\int y^{-2}\,dy = \int 2x\,dx$, so $-\dfrac{1}{y} = x^{2} + c$. The condition $y(0) = 1$ gives $-1 = c$, so $-\dfrac{1}{y} = x^{2} - 1$ and

这里 $f(x) = 2x$，$g(y) = y^{2}$。当 $y \neq 0$ 时：$\displaystyle\int y^{-2}\,dy = \int 2x\,dx$，所以 $-\dfrac{1}{y} = x^{2} + c$。条件 $y(0) = 1$ 给出 $-1 = c$，于是 $-\dfrac{1}{y} = x^{2} - 1$，即

$$ y = \frac{1}{1 - x^{2}}. $$

$$ y = \frac{1}{1 - x^{2}}. $$

Domain. The formula fails at $x = \pm 1$, where $y$ becomes unbounded. The solution through $(0, 1)$ is the branch on $-1 < x < 1$, the interval that contains the initial point. The constant function $y = 0$ is also a solution, lost when we divided by $y^{2}$, but it does not pass through $(0, 1)$.

定义域。该公式在 $x = \pm 1$ 处失效，此时 $y$ 无界。过 $(0, 1)$ 的解是 $-1 < x < 1$ 上的那一支，即包含初始点的区间。常数函数 $y = 0$ 也是解，它在除以 $y^{2}$ 时丢失了，但不经过 $(0, 1)$。

Check by substitution. $y = (1 - x^{2})^{-1}$ gives $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2x(1 - x^{2})^{-2} = 2xy^{2}$, and $y(0) = 1$.

代回检验。$y = (1 - x^{2})^{-1}$ 给出 $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2x(1 - x^{2})^{-2} = 2xy^{2}$，且 $y(0) = 1$。

Why this works. A solution of a differential equation is a function on an interval; a formula can be defined on more than one interval, so the initial condition decides which piece is the answer.

为什么可行。微分方程的解是某个区间上的函数；一个公式可能在多个区间上有定义，所以由初始条件决定哪一段才是答案。

Slope Fields and Solution Curves斜率场与解曲线 HL AHL 5.15

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Compute the gradient $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x, y)$ at chosen points and draw the corresponding short segments of a slope field.
  • Read a slope field: where the gradient is zero, positive or negative, and where it is the same along rows or columns.
  • Sketch a solution curve through a given point by following the segments.
  • Explain why two different solution curves do not cross, and identify constant (horizontal) solutions.
  • Match a differential equation to its slope field using values of the gradient.
  • 在选定的点计算梯度 $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x, y)$，并画出斜率场（slope field）中相应的短线段。
  • 读斜率场：梯度何处为零、为正、为负，以及何处沿行或沿列保持相同。
  • 沿着线段方向，画出过给定点的解曲线（solution curve）。
  • 解释为什么两条不同的解曲线不会相交，并识别常数（水平）解。
  • 利用梯度的取值，把微分方程与其斜率场对应起来。

What a slope field shows斜率场表示什么

A first-order equation $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x, y)$ tells you the gradient of a solution at every point $(x, y)$, even when you cannot solve it. A slope field (direction field) draws a short segment of gradient $f(x, y)$ at each point of a grid. A solution curve is a curve that is tangent to the segments everywhere: start at the initial point and keep following the direction of the nearest segments. To build a slope field by hand, make a table of $f(x, y)$ at grid points and sketch each segment; Figure 1 uses spacing $0.5$ in $x$ and $1$ in $y$.

一阶方程 $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = f(x, y)$ 给出解在每一点 $(x, y)$ 处的梯度，即使无法求出解析解。斜率场（方向场）在网格的每个点画一小段梯度为 $f(x, y)$ 的线段。解曲线是处处与线段相切的曲线：从初始点出发，沿附近线段的方向一路画下去。手工作斜率场时，先列出网格点上 $f(x, y)$ 的值，再画各线段；图 1 在 $x$ 方向取间距 $0.5$，在 $y$ 方向取间距 $1$。

Features to read: the segments are horizontal where $f(x, y) = 0$ (stationary points of solutions); if $f$ depends on $x$ only, segments are identical along each vertical line; if $f$ depends on $y$ only, they are identical along each horizontal line. A horizontal line $y = c$ with $f(x, c) = 0$ for every $x$ is a constant solution. Because there is exactly one segment at each point, two different solution curves of the equations in this unit never cross.

需要读出的特征：在 $f(x, y) = 0$ 处线段水平（解的驻点）；若 $f$ 只与 $x$ 有关，则每条竖直线上的线段相同；若 $f$ 只与 $y$ 有关，则每条水平线上的线段相同。若水平线 $y = c$ 使得对所有 $x$ 都有 $f(x, c) = 0$，则它是常数解（constant solution）。由于每个点只有一条线段，本单元中的方程的两条不同解曲线永远不会相交。

Worked Example: reading and checking the slope field of $dy/dx = xy$例题：读并验证 $dy/dx = xy$ 的斜率场

For $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = xy$: (a) find the gradient at $(1, 1)$, $(2, 3)$, $(-1, 2)$ and $(0, 5)$; (b) describe the field on the axes and its symmetry; (c) sketch the solution through $(0, 1)$ and confirm it by separation of variables.对于 $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = xy$：(a) 求 $(1, 1)$、$(2, 3)$、$(-1, 2)$ 与 $(0, 5)$ 处的梯度；(b) 描述坐标轴上的线段与场的对称性；(c) 画出过 $(0, 1)$ 的解曲线，并用分离变量法验证。

(a) Substitute $f(x, y) = xy$: $f(1, 1) = 1$, $f(2, 3) = 6$ (steep), $f(-1, 2) = -2$, $f(0, 5) = 0$.

(a) 代入 $f(x, y) = xy$：$f(1, 1) = 1$，$f(2, 3) = 6$（很陡），$f(-1, 2) = -2$，$f(0, 5) = 0$。

Separation: $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{y}\,dy = \int x\,dx$ gives $\ln y = \dfrac{x^{2}}{2} + c$ for $y > 0$; $y(0) = 1$ gives $c = 0$, so $y = e^{x^{2}/2}$. (c) 从 $(0, 1)$ 出发，线段水平；当 $x > 0$ 且 $y > 0$ 时梯度为正且不断增大，所以曲线越来越陡，左侧由对称性同理。分离变量：$\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{y}\,dy = \int x\,dx$ 在 $y > 0$ 时给出 $\ln y = \dfrac{x^{2}}{2} + c$；$y(0) = 1$ 得 $c = 0$，所以 $y = e^{x^{2}/2}$。在 $x = 1$ 检验：$y = e^{0.5} = 1.6487$，$\dfrac{dy}{dx} = xy = 1.6487$，而 $e^{x^{2}/2}$ 的导数是 $xe^{x^{2}/2}$，符合要求。

Why this works. The segments are tangent lines of the solutions; following them traces the solution that the equation forces.

为什么可行。线段就是解的切线；沿着它们画，就描出了方程所决定的那条解。

Worked Example: a field that cannot be separated: $dy/dx = x - y$例题：无法分离变量的场：$dy/dx = x - y$

For $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = x - y$ with $y(0) = 1$: (a) find where the segments are horizontal and the sign of the gradient on either side; (b) describe the solution curve through $(0, 1)$; (c) verify by substitution that $y = x - 1 + 2e^{-x}$ is the solution and find its stationary point.对于 $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = x - y$，$y(0) = 1$：(a) 求线段水平之处，以及两侧梯度的正负；(b) 描述过 $(0, 1)$ 的解曲线；(c) 代入验证 $y = x - 1 + 2e^{-x}$ 是该解，并求其驻点。

(a) $x - y = 0$ on the line $y = x$, so segments there are horizontal. Below the line ($y < x$) the gradient $x - y$ is positive; above it ($y > x$) it is negative. For example $f(0, 1) = -1$ and $f(2, 0) = 2$.

(a) 在直线 $y = x$ 上 $x - y = 0$，所以那里的线段水平。直线下方（$y < x$）梯度 $x - y$ 为正；上方（$y > x$）为负。例如 $f(0, 1) = -1$，$f(2, 0) = 2$。

(b) The curve starts at $(0, 1)$, above the line, with gradient $-1$, so it falls until it meets the line $y = x$, where its gradient is $0$, and afterwards it rises, because it is then below the line. So there is a minimum on the line $y = x$.

(b) 曲线从直线上方的 $(0, 1)$ 出发，梯度为 $-1$，因此下降，直到与直线 $y = x$ 相遇，此处梯度为 $0$；之后曲线位于直线下方，梯度为正，开始上升。所以最小值出现在直线 $y = x$ 上。

(c) This equation is not separable, so we verify instead. $y = x - 1 + 2e^{-x}$ gives $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 1 - 2e^{-x}$, while $x - y = x - (x - 1 + 2e^{-x}) = 1 - 2e^{-x}$: equal. Also $y(0) = -1 + 2 = 1$. Stationary point: $1 - 2e^{-x} = 0$ gives $x = \ln 2 = 0.693$, and $y = \ln 2 - 1 + 1 = \ln 2$, so it lies on $y = x$ as predicted. Since $\dfrac{d^{2}y}{dx^{2}} = 2e^{-x} > 0$, it is a minimum at $(0.693, 0.693)$.

(c) 该方程不能分离变量，所以改用代入验证。$y = x - 1 + 2e^{-x}$ 给出 $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 1 - 2e^{-x}$，而 $x - y = x - (x - 1 + 2e^{-x}) = 1 - 2e^{-x}$：相等。又 $y(0) = -1 + 2 = 1$。驻点：$1 - 2e^{-x} = 0$ 得 $x = \ln 2 = 0.693$，$y = \ln 2 - 1 + 1 = \ln 2$，位于 $y = x$ 上，与预测一致。因为 $\dfrac{d^{2}y}{dx^{2}} = 2e^{-x} > 0$，所以 $(0.693, 0.693)$ 是最小值点。

Why this works. The slope field gives the qualitative shape without any solving; the explicit solution then confirms it, and Euler's method (next topic) approximates it numerically.

为什么可行。斜率场不用求解就给出曲线的大致形状；显式解再加以确认，而欧拉法（下一节）则给出数值近似。

Euler's method欧拉法

When a differential equation cannot be solved exactly, or you only need numbers, Euler's method follows the tangent line for a short horizontal distance $h$, the step size (step length), then recomputes the gradient and repeats. From the initial point $(x_{0}, y_{0})$:

当微分方程无法精确求解，或只需要数值时，欧拉法（Euler's method）沿切线前进一小段水平距离 $h$，即步长（step size），然后重新计算梯度并重复。从初始点 $(x_{0}, y_{0})$ 出发：

$$ x_{n+1} = x_{n} + h, \qquad y_{n+1} = y_{n} + h\,f(x_{n}, y_{n}). $$

To reach $x = x_{end}$ you need $\dfrac{x_{end} - x_{0}}{h}$ steps. Each row of the table needs $x_{n}$, $y_{n}$, the gradient $f(x_{n}, y_{n})$, and the new $y_{n+1}$. Carry the unrounded values forward and round only the numbers you display, to the number of decimal places the question states. On a GDC, put $x$ in one column of a spreadsheet and $y$ in the next, enter the formula for the first step, and fill down; or store the update in a recursion. Always check the first step by hand.

要到达 $x = x_{end}$，需要 $\dfrac{x_{end} - x_{0}}{h}$ 步。表格每一行需要 $x_{n}$、$y_{n}$、梯度 $f(x_{n}, y_{n})$ 与新的 $y_{n+1}$。往后传递的是未取近似的数值，只对显示的数字按题目规定的小数位数取近似。用 GDC 时，在电子表格的一列放 $x$，下一列放 $y$，输入第一步的公式后向下填充；也可以把更新式存为递推。第一步务必手算核对。

Accuracy. Each step replaces the curve by its tangent, and errors accumulate, so a smaller step size usually gives a closer estimate (at the cost of more steps). Where the solution curve is concave up ($y'' > 0$), tangent lines lie below the curve and Euler's estimates are too small; where it is concave down they are too large.

精度。每一步都用切线代替曲线，误差会累积，所以步长越小，估计值通常越接近真值（代价是步数更多）。在解曲线向上凹（$y'' > 0$）之处，切线位于曲线下方，欧拉估计值偏小；在向下凹之处则偏大。

Two quantities that depend on each other相互依赖的两个量

Many models track two quantities whose rates of change each depend on both quantities, such as a predator and its prey, or the amounts of a substance in two connected tanks. Such a coupled system of first-order equations is written with $t$ as the independent variable:

许多模型同时追踪两个量，其中每个量的变化率都依赖于两个量，例如捕食者与猎物，或两个相连水箱中某种物质的含量。这样的一阶方程耦合方程组以 $t$ 为自变量，写作：

$$ \frac{dx}{dt} = f(t, x, y), \qquad \frac{dy}{dt} = g(t, x, y). $$

Euler's method for a coupled system with step size $h$ updates both variables at the same time, always using the values from row $n$:

耦合方程组的欧拉法，步长为 $h$，同时更新两个变量，并且始终使用第 $n$ 行的数值：

$$ t_{n+1} = t_{n} + h, \qquad x_{n+1} = x_{n} + h\,f(t_{n}, x_{n}, y_{n}), \qquad y_{n+1} = y_{n} + h\,g(t_{n}, x_{n}, y_{n}). $$

The most common slip is to use the new $x_{n+1}$ when computing $y_{n+1}$; that mixes two different times. Compute both rates in row $n$ first, then both new values. An equilibrium (constant solution) of the system is a point where both rates are zero, $f = 0$ and $g = 0$, so neither quantity changes. On a GDC spreadsheet use one column each for $t$, $x$, $y$, and fill down formulas that refer to the row above.

最常见的错误是在计算 $y_{n+1}$ 时用了新的 $x_{n+1}$，这样就把两个不同时刻的数混在了一起。应先在第 $n$ 行算出两个变化率，再算两个新值。方程组的平衡点（常数解）是两个变化率同时为零的点，即 $f = 0$ 且 $g = 0$，此时两个量都不再变化。用 GDC 电子表格时，$t$、$x$、$y$ 各占一列，向下填充引用上一行的公式。

The linear system and its equilibrium线性方程组及其平衡点

A coupled linear system has the form $\dfrac{dx}{dt} = ax + by$, $\dfrac{dy}{dt} = cx + dy$, which in matrix form is $\dfrac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt} = A\mathbf{x}$ with $\mathbf{x} = \binom{x}{y}$ and $A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$. The equilibrium point is where $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$. If $\det A \neq 0$ the only equilibrium is the origin. It is stable if trajectories that start near it move towards it as $t \to \infty$, and unstable otherwise.

耦合线性方程组的形式为 $\dfrac{dx}{dt} = ax + by$，$\dfrac{dy}{dt} = cx + dy$，写成矩阵形式即 $\dfrac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt} = A\mathbf{x}$，其中 $\mathbf{x} = \binom{x}{y}$，$A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$。平衡点（equilibrium point）满足 $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$。若 $\det A \neq 0$，唯一的平衡点是原点。如果从平衡点附近出发的轨线在 $t \to \infty$ 时趋向它，则称它是稳定的，否则是不稳定的。

Eigenvalues, eigenvectors and the general solution特征值、特征向量与通解

Try a solution of the form $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{v}e^{\lambda t}$. Substituting gives $\lambda\mathbf{v}e^{\lambda t} = A\mathbf{v}e^{\lambda t}$, so $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$: $\lambda$ must be an eigenvalue of $A$ with eigenvector $\mathbf{v}$. The eigenvalues solve $\lambda^{2} - (a + d)\lambda + (ad - bc) = 0$. For two distinct real eigenvalues $\lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2}$ with eigenvectors $\mathbf{v}_{1}, \mathbf{v}_{2}$ the general solution is

尝试形如 $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{v}e^{\lambda t}$ 的解。代入得 $\lambda\mathbf{v}e^{\lambda t} = A\mathbf{v}e^{\lambda t}$，即 $A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$：$\lambda$ 必须是 $A$ 的特征值（eigenvalue），$\mathbf{v}$ 是特征向量（eigenvector）。特征值满足 $\lambda^{2} - (a + d)\lambda + (ad - bc) = 0$。当有两个互异实特征值 $\lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2}$，对应特征向量 $\mathbf{v}_{1}, \mathbf{v}_{2}$ 时，通解为

$$ \mathbf{x}(t) = c_{1}e^{\lambda_{1}t}\mathbf{v}_{1} + c_{2}e^{\lambda_{2}t}\mathbf{v}_{2}. $$

The constants $c_{1}, c_{2}$ come from the initial values: put $t = 0$ and solve $c_{1}\mathbf{v}_{1} + c_{2}\mathbf{v}_{2} = \mathbf{x}(0)$. A GDC returns eigenvalues and eigenvectors for a matrix you enter; it may scale an eigenvector differently from your hand method, and any non-zero multiple is correct. The trace and determinant give a check: $\lambda_{1} + \lambda_{2} = a + d$ and $\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2} = ad - bc$.

常数 $c_{1}, c_{2}$ 由初始值确定：令 $t = 0$，解 $c_{1}\mathbf{v}_{1} + c_{2}\mathbf{v}_{2} = \mathbf{x}(0)$。GDC 可以对输入的矩阵给出特征值与特征向量；它给出的特征向量可能与手算相差一个倍数，任何非零倍数都是正确的。迹与行列式可用来检验：$\lambda_{1} + \lambda_{2} = a + d$，$\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2} = ad - bc$。

Turning one second-order equation into two first-order equations把一个二阶方程化为两个一阶方程

A second-order differential equation contains $y''$, for example $y'' = f(t, y, y')$. Euler's method only knows how to advance first-order equations, so introduce a new variable for the first derivative, $z = y'$. Then $y'' = z'$ and the single equation becomes a pair of coupled first-order equations (a system of coupled first-order equations):

二阶微分方程（second-order differential equation）含有 $y''$，例如 $y'' = f(t, y, y')$。欧拉法只能推进一阶方程，所以为一阶导数引入新变量 $z = y'$。于是 $y'' = z'$，这一个方程就变成一对耦合的一阶方程（耦合一阶方程组）：

$$ \frac{dy}{dt} = z, \qquad \frac{dz}{dt} = f(t, y, z). $$

Both initial values are needed: $y(t_{0})$ and $y'(t_{0}) = z(t_{0})$. Now apply the coupled Euler method of topic E5.4 with the pair $(y, z)$: $y_{n+1} = y_{n} + h\,z_{n}$ and $z_{n+1} = z_{n} + h\,f(t_{n}, y_{n}, z_{n})$. If the equation is linear with constant coefficients, such as $y'' + py' + qy = 0$, the system is $\dfrac{d}{dt}\binom{y}{z} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -q & -p \end{pmatrix}\binom{y}{z}$, whose eigenvalues satisfy $\lambda^{2} + p\lambda + q = 0$, so topic E5.5 classifies its behaviour.

两个初始值都需要：$y(t_{0})$ 与 $y'(t_{0}) = z(t_{0})$。然后对 $(y, z)$ 使用 E5.4 的耦合欧拉法：$y_{n+1} = y_{n} + h\,z_{n}$，$z_{n+1} = z_{n} + h\,f(t_{n}, y_{n}, z_{n})$。若方程是常系数线性方程，如 $y'' + py' + qy = 0$，则方程组为 $\dfrac{d}{dt}\binom{y}{z} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -q & -p \end{pmatrix}\binom{y}{z}$，其特征值满足 $\lambda^{2} + p\lambda + q = 0$，因此可以用 E5.5 的方法判断其行为。
