The Mole and Stoichiometry摩尔与化学计量

Chemistry is a quantitative science: every balanced equation is a recipe that tells you exactly how many atoms, molecules, or moles of each substance react and form. This guide builds the stoichiometry toolkit from the ground up, from Avogadro's number and the mole concept, through molar mass and the three-way conversion between moles, mass, and particles, to percent composition and empirical/molecular formulae, balancing chemical equations by inspection, mole-ratio stoichiometry, limiting and excess reactants, and finally percent yield. Worked examples and KaTeX formulas throughout.化学是一门定量科学：每一个配平的化学方程式都是一张配方，精确告诉你各物质参与反应和生成的原子数、分子数或摩尔数。本指南从基础开始逐步搭建化学计量学工具箱，从阿伏伽德罗常数（Avogadro's number，阿伏伽德罗常数）和摩尔（mole，摩尔）概念出发，经摩尔质量（molar mass，摩尔质量）以及摩尔、质量与粒子数之间的三向换算，到质量分数与实验式（empirical formula，实验式）/分子式（molecular formula，分子式），再到配平化学方程式、摩尔比化学计量（stoichiometry，化学计量），最后落脚于限量反应物（limiting reactant，限量反应物）与产率（percent yield，产率）。全程使用例题与公式。

The Mole and Avogadro's Number摩尔与阿伏伽德罗常数

Learning objectives学习目标
  • State Avogadro's number with its unit, $6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}}$, and explain why it is defined through ${}^{12}\text{C}$.
  • Name the representative particle (atom, molecule, ion or formula unit) before counting.
  • Calculate the number of particles from moles with $N = n N_A$, and moles from particles with $n = N/N_A$.
  • Calculate the number of atoms of one element in a sample of a compound.
  • Explain why one mole of different substances holds the same number of particles but has different masses.
  • 写出阿伏伽德罗常数及其单位 $6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}}$，并解释它为何通过 ${}^{12}\text{C}$ 来定义。
  • 计数之前先指明代表性粒子（原子、分子、离子或化学式单元，representative particle）。
  • 用 $N = n N_A$ 由摩尔数计算粒子数，用 $n = N/N_A$ 由粒子数计算摩尔数。
  • 计算化合物样品中某一元素的原子数。
  • 解释为什么不同物质的 1 摩尔含有相同数目的粒子，却有不同的质量。
The mole is chemistry's counting unit: it bridges atoms to grams.摩尔是化学的计数单位：它将原子数与克数连接起来。
  • 1 mole (mol)1 摩尔（mol）: exactly $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ representative particles (atoms, molecules, ions, formula units). This number is Avogadro's number $N_A$.：恰好 $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ 个代表性粒子（原子、分子、离子、化学式单元）。这个数字就是阿伏伽德罗常数 $N_A$。
  • Why $6.022 \times 10^{23}$?为什么是 $6.022 \times 10^{23}$？ It is defined so that 1 mol of ${}^{12}\text{C}$ atoms has a mass of exactly $12\ \mathrm{g}$. The same number of any element's atoms has a mass in grams numerically equal to its atomic mass in u.它的定义使得 1 mol ${}^{12}\text{C}$ 原子的质量恰好等于 $12\ \mathrm{g}$。任何元素的同等数量原子，其克数在数值上等于其以 u 为单位的原子质量。

Key conversion:关键换算：

$$ N = n \times N_A \qquad n = \frac{N}{N_A} $$ $N$ = number of particles, $n$ = moles, $N_A = 6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}}$. NGSS HS-PS1-7 and SCH3U D3.2 both identify Avogadro's number and the mole as the atomic-to-macroscopic bridge as assessed content.$N$ = 粒子数，$n$ = 摩尔数，$N_A = 6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}}$。NGSS HS-PS1-7 与 SCH3U D3.2 均将阿伏伽德罗常数和摩尔作为原子-宏观桥梁列为评估内容。

Choosing the representative particle选择代表性粒子

A mole counts any kind of particle, so the question or your answer must say which one. $1\ \mathrm{mol}$ of $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ is $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ molecules, which is $2 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23}$ hydrogen atoms and $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ oxygen atoms. For an ionic solid such as $\text{NaCl}$ the representative particle is the formula unit, and $1\ \mathrm{mol}$ of $\text{NaCl}$ contains $1\ \mathrm{mol}$ of $\text{Na}^+$ ions and $1\ \mathrm{mol}$ of $\text{Cl}^-$ ions. This matters because the count of atoms depends on the formula: $1\ \mathrm{mol}$ of $\text{O}_2$ is $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ molecules but $1.204 \times 10^{24}$ atoms.1 摩尔可以数任何一种粒子，所以题目或你的答案必须说明数的是哪一种。$1\ \mathrm{mol}$ 的 $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ 是 $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ 个分子，即 $2 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23}$ 个氢原子和 $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ 个氧原子。对于 $\text{NaCl}$ 这类离子固体，代表性粒子是化学式单元（formula unit），$1\ \mathrm{mol}$ $\text{NaCl}$ 含有 $1\ \mathrm{mol}$ $\text{Na}^+$ 离子和 $1\ \mathrm{mol}$ $\text{Cl}^-$ 离子。这很重要，因为原子数取决于化学式：$1\ \mathrm{mol}$ $\text{O}_2$ 是 $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ 个分子，但有 $1.204 \times 10^{24}$ 个原子。

Common error: writing "$1\ \mathrm{mol}$ of oxygen" without saying $\text{O}$ or $\text{O}_2$. The two differ by a factor of $2$ in atoms, so write the formula every time.常见错误：只写“$1\ \mathrm{mol}$ 氧”而不说明是 $\text{O}$ 还是 $\text{O}_2$。两者的原子数相差 $2$ 倍，所以每次都要写出化学式。

Worked Example 1: Particles from moles例题 1：由摩尔数求粒子数

How many molecules are in $2.50\ \mathrm{mol}$ of water, $\text{H}_2\text{O}$?$2.50\ \mathrm{mol}$ 的水（$\text{H}_2\text{O}$）中含有多少个分子？

Apply $N = n \times N_A$.应用 $N = n \times N_A$。

$$ N = 2.50\ \mathrm{mol} \times 6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}} = 1.506 \times 10^{24}\ \text{molecules}. $$

Each of those molecules contains 2 H atoms and 1 O atom, so there are $3 \times 1.506 \times 10^{24} = 4.52 \times 10^{24}$ total atoms.每个分子含有 2 个 H 原子和 1 个 O 原子，故总原子数为 $3 \times 1.506 \times 10^{24} = 4.52 \times 10^{24}$。

Worked Example 2: Atoms of one element in a compound例题 2：化合物中某元素的原子数

A sample contains $3.00\ \mathrm{mol}$ of ammonia, $\text{NH}_3$. How many hydrogen atoms does it contain?某样品含有 $3.00\ \mathrm{mol}$ 氨（$\text{NH}_3$）。其中含有多少个氢原子？

Step 1: Molecules of $\text{NH}_3$.第 1 步：$\text{NH}_3$ 的分子数。

$$ N = 3.00\ \mathrm{mol} \times 6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}} = 1.81 \times 10^{24}\ \text{molecules}. $$

Step 2: Hydrogen atoms.第 2 步：氢原子数。 Each $\text{NH}_3$ molecule has $3$ H atoms.每个 $\text{NH}_3$ 分子含 $3$ 个 H 原子。

$$ N_\text{H} = 3 \times 1.8066 \times 10^{24} = 5.42 \times 10^{24}\ \text{H atoms}. $$

Why this works: the subscript in the formula is the number of atoms per molecule, so the atom count is the molecule count times that subscript. Equivalently, $3.00\ \mathrm{mol}$ of $\text{NH}_3$ holds $9.00\ \mathrm{mol}$ of H atoms.原理：化学式中的下标就是每个分子中的原子数，所以原子数等于分子数乘以该下标。等价地，$3.00\ \mathrm{mol}$ $\text{NH}_3$ 含有 $9.00\ \mathrm{mol}$ H 原子。

Sense check: a laboratory amount in moles is a small number, a particle count is enormous.

指令词：state 要求给出数值和单位，calculate 要求写出计算过程。给出 $N_A$ 时要带单位 $\mathrm{mol^{-1}}$，答案中要点明粒子种类，例如“$1.81 \times 10^{24}$ 个 $\text{NH}_3$ 分子”。常见错误：该乘以 $N_A$ 时却除以它，以及题目问原子数时却只数了分子数。合理性检查：实验室里的物质的量是较小的数，粒子数则是极大的数。

Molar Mass and Conversions摩尔质量与换算

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Calculate the molar mass of an element or compound from atomic masses given to two decimal places.
  • Convert between mass and moles with $n = m/M$ and $m = nM$.
  • Chain mass, moles and number of particles with dimensional analysis, writing each conversion factor so the units cancel.
  • Use the molar volume of a gas at STP, $22.4\ \mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}}$, to convert between moles and volume.
  • Choose the conversion path on the mole road map for any starting quantity.
  • 根据保留两位小数的原子质量，计算元素或化合物的摩尔质量。
  • 用 $n = m/M$ 和 $m = nM$ 在质量与摩尔数之间换算。
  • 用量纲分析（dimensional analysis）连接质量、摩尔数和粒子数，并让每个换算因子（conversion factor）的单位能够消去。
  • 用 STP 下气体的摩尔体积 $22.4\ \mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}}$ 在摩尔数与体积之间换算。
  • 对任意起始量，在摩尔路线图上选出换算路径。
Molar mass ($M$) is the mass of one mole in grams.摩尔质量（$M$）是一摩尔物质的克数。
  • For an element对于元素: $M$ in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ numerically equals the atomic mass in $\mathrm{u}$. E.g. Fe: $M = 55.85\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.：$M$（单位 $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$）在数值上等于以 $\mathrm{u}$ 为单位的原子质量。例如 Fe：$M = 55.85\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$。
  • For a compound对于化合物: sum the atomic masses of all atoms in the formula. E.g. $\text{H}_2\text{O}$: $M = 2(1.008) + 16.00 = 18.02\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.：将化学式中所有原子的原子质量相加。例如 $\text{H}_2\text{O}$：$M = 2(1.008) + 16.00 = 18.02\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$。

The three-way roadmap:三向换算路线图：

$$ n = \frac{m}{M} \qquad m = n \times M \qquad N = n \times N_A $$ $n$ = moles, $m$ = mass (g), $M$ = molar mass ($\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$), $N$ = number of particles. SCH3U D2.3 and D3.2 assess calculations using moles, number of particles, and atomic mass. BC Chemistry 11 lists "calculation of mass and molar quantities using significant figures" as an elaboration of "dimensional analysis."$n$ = 摩尔数，$m$ = 质量（g），$M$ = 摩尔质量（$\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$），$N$ = 粒子数。SCH3U D2.3 和 D3.2 评估涉及摩尔数、粒子数和原子质量的计算。BC Chemistry 11 将"使用有效数字计算质量和摩尔量"列为"量纲分析"的细化内容。

Dimensional analysis and the mole road map量纲分析与摩尔路线图

Dimensional analysis (the factor-label method) multiplies a quantity by conversion factors that each equal $1$, such as $\dfrac{1\ \mathrm{mol}}{44.01\ \mathrm{g}}$, arranged so the unwanted unit cancels. The mole is the hub of the road map in Figure 1: to convert between any two of mass, particles and gas volume, go through moles. For a gas at STP ($0\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and $101.3\ \mathrm{kPa}$) one mole of any ideal gas occupies about $22.4\ \mathrm{L}$, so $V = n \times 22.4\ \mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}}$.量纲分析（因子标签法，factor-label method）把一个量乘以若干个都等于 $1$ 的换算因子，例如 $\dfrac{1\ \mathrm{mol}}{44.01\ \mathrm{g}}$，并安排好使多余的单位消去。摩尔是图 1 路线图的枢纽：质量、粒子数、气体体积中任意两个量之间的换算都要经过摩尔。对 STP 下的气体（$0\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$、$101.3\ \mathrm{kPa}$），1 摩尔任何理想气体约占 $22.4\ \mathrm{L}$，所以 $V = n \times 22.4\ \mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}}$。

Common error: using $22.4\ \mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}}$ for a liquid or solid (liquid water is not $22.4\ \mathrm{L}$ per mole), or for a gas that is not at STP. The molar volume applies only to gases at the stated conditions.常见错误：把 $22.4\ \mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}}$ 用于液体或固体（液态水每摩尔并不是 $22.4\ \mathrm{L}$），或用于不在 STP 的气体。摩尔体积只适用于所述条件下的气体。

Worked Example 3: Grams to moles to particles例题 3：克→摩尔→粒子数

A student dissolves $8.80\ \mathrm{g}$ of carbon dioxide, $\text{CO}_2$, in water. (a) Calculate the molar mass of $\text{CO}_2$. (b) Find the number of moles. (c) Find the number of molecules.一名学生将 $8.80\ \mathrm{g}$ 二氧化碳（$\text{CO}_2$）溶于水。(a) 计算 $\text{CO}_2$ 的摩尔质量。(b) 求摩尔数。(c) 求分子数。

(a) Molar mass.(a) 摩尔质量。

$$ M(\text{CO}_2) = 12.01 + 2(16.00) = 44.01\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}. $$

(b) Moles.(b) 摩尔数。

$$ n = \frac{m}{M} = \frac{8.80\ \mathrm{g}}{44.01\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}} = 0.200\ \mathrm{mol}. $$

(c) Molecules.(c) 分子数。

$$ N = 0.200\ \mathrm{mol} \times 6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}} = 1.20 \times 10^{23}\ \text{molecules.} $$
Worked Example 4: Mass of a gas to moles, volume and molecules例题 4：气体质量换算为摩尔数、体积和分子数

A cylinder holds $11.0\ \mathrm{g}$ of carbon dioxide gas at STP. (a) How many moles is that? (b) What volume does it occupy? (c) How many molecules does it contain?一个钢瓶中装有 STP 下的 $11.0\ \mathrm{g}$ 二氧化碳气体。(a) 这是多少摩尔？(b) 它占多大体积？(c) 含有多少个分子？

(a) Moles.(a) 摩尔数。 $M(\text{CO}_2) = 44.01\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ from the previous example.由上一例得 $M(\text{CO}_2) = 44.01\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$。

$$ n = \frac{11.0\ \mathrm{g}}{44.01\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}} = 0.250\ \mathrm{mol}. $$

(b) Volume at STP.(b) STP 下的体积。

$$ V = 0.250\ \mathrm{mol} \times 22.4\ \mathrm{L\,mol^{-1}} = 5.60\ \mathrm{L}. $$

(c) Molecules.(c) 分子数。

$$ N = 0.250\ \mathrm{mol} \times 6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}} = 1.51 \times 10^{23}\ \text{molecules}. $$

Why this works: one mole links all three quantities, so a single value of $n$ gives the volume and the particle count without converting grams to litres directly.原理：摩尔把三个量联系在一起，所以求出一个 $n$ 就能得到体积和粒子数，不需要直接把克换成升。

The law of definite proportions定比定律

The law of definite proportions (also called the law of constant composition) says that a pure compound always contains the same elements in the same fixed mass ratio, whatever its source or how it was made. Water is a clear example. Its percent composition is $\dfrac{2(1.008)}{18.02} \times 100\% = 11.2\%$ hydrogen and $\dfrac{16.00}{18.02} \times 100\% = 88.8\%$ oxygen, whether the water fell as rain, came from a tap or was made by burning hydrogen. A $9.00\ \mathrm{g}$ sample therefore always holds $1.01\ \mathrm{g}$ of H and $7.99\ \mathrm{g}$ of O, a mass ratio $\text{H} : \text{O} = 1 : 7.94$.定比定律（也叫恒定组成定律）指出：纯净的化合物无论来源或制备方法如何，所含元素总是相同，且质量比固定。水是很好的例子。它的质量分数是氢 $\dfrac{2(1.008)}{18.02} \times 100\% = 11.2\%$，氧 $\dfrac{16.00}{18.02} \times 100\% = 88.8\%$，无论水来自雨水、自来水，还是由氢气燃烧制得。因此 $9.00\ \mathrm{g}$ 水中总是含有 $1.01\ \mathrm{g}$ H 和 $7.99\ \mathrm{g}$ O，质量比 $\text{H} : \text{O} = 1 : 7.94$。

The reason is the formula itself: every $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ molecule has $2$ H atoms and $1$ O atom, and each kind of atom has a fixed mass, so the mass ratio cannot change. A different ratio means a different compound. Hydrogen peroxide, $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$, is also made of hydrogen and oxygen but is $\dfrac{2(1.008)}{34.01} \times 100\% = 5.93\%$ hydrogen. A mixture such as salt water or air has no fixed ratio, so the law applies only to pure compounds.原因在于化学式本身：每个 $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ 分子都含 $2$ 个 H 原子和 $1$ 个 O 原子，每种原子的质量固定，所以质量比不会改变。质量比不同就是不同的化合物。过氧化氢 $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ 同样由氢和氧组成，但氢的质量分数是 $\dfrac{2(1.008)}{34.01} \times 100\% = 5.93\%$。盐水或空气这类混合物没有固定的比例，所以该定律只适用于纯净的化合物。

Common error: mixing up the atom ratio and the mass ratio. In water the atom ratio is $\text{H} : \text{O} = 2 : 1$, but the mass ratio is $1 : 7.94$ because an O atom is about $16$ times heavier than an H atom.常见错误：混淆原子数之比与质量比。水中原子数之比是 $\text{H} : \text{O} = 2 : 1$，但质量比是 $1 : 7.94$，因为一个 O 原子约比一个 H 原子重 $16$ 倍。

Why the ratio is in moles, not grams为什么比值针对摩尔数而不是质量

The coefficients of a balanced equation count particles, and a mole is a fixed count of particles, so the coefficient ratio is a ratio of moles. In $\text{N}_2 + 3\text{H}_2 \to 2\text{NH}_3$, $1\ \mathrm{mol}$ of $\text{N}_2$ reacts with $3\ \mathrm{mol}$ of $\text{H}_2$. In grams that is $28.02\ \mathrm{g}$ of $\text{N}_2$ with $3 \times 2.016 = 6.048\ \mathrm{g}$ of $\text{H}_2$, which is not a $1 : 3$ ratio. Masses only enter at the start and the end of the road map, through the molar masses.配平方程式的系数数的是粒子，而 1 摩尔是固定数目的粒子，所以系数之比就是摩尔数之比。在 $\text{N}_2 + 3\text{H}_2 \to 2\text{NH}_3$ 中，$1\ \mathrm{mol}$ $\text{N}_2$ 与 $3\ \mathrm{mol}$ $\text{H}_2$ 反应。换成克，就是 $28.02\ \mathrm{g}$ $\text{N}_2$ 对 $3 \times 2.016 = 6.048\ \mathrm{g}$ $\text{H}_2$，并不是 $1 : 3$。质量只在路线图的起点和终点通过摩尔质量进入计算。

Going deeper: why comparing moles of product identifies the limiting reactant深入：为什么比较产物摩尔数能识别限量反应物

The limiting reactant is the one that runs out first. For a general reaction $a\text{A} + b\text{B} \to c\text{C}$, the amount of product C that reactant A can produce is $n_C^{(A)} = n_A \cdot (c/a)$, and the amount B can produce is $n_C^{(B)} = n_B \cdot (c/b)$. The reaction stops when whichever product supply runs out first is exhausted, so the actual yield of C is限量反应物是最先耗尽的那个。对于通式反应 $a\text{A} + b\text{B} \to c\text{C}$，反应物 A 能产生的 C 的摩尔数为 $n_C^{(A)} = n_A \cdot (c/a)$，B 能产生的为 $n_C^{(B)} = n_B \cdot (c/b)$。反应在哪种供应先耗尽时停止，故 C 的实际产量为

$$ n_C = \min\!\left(n_A \cdot \frac{c}{a},\; n_B \cdot \frac{c}{b}\right) $$

and the limiting reactant is whichever argument gives the minimum. An equivalent test divides each reactant's moles by its stoichiometric coefficient: compare $n_A/a$ to $n_B/b$. The smaller ratio belongs to the limiting reactant, because that reactant is proportionally least available relative to what the equation demands. For instance, if $n_A/a = 0.50$ and $n_B/b = 0.99$, then A runs out when only half the stoichiometric demand has been met, while B still has nearly twice as much as needed, so A is limiting. Common Questions常见问题

How do I find the limiting reactant?如何确定限量反应物？

Convert each reactant's mass to moles, divide by its coefficient in the balanced equation, and the reactant with the smallest result is the limiting reactant that controls how much product forms.将每种反应物的质量换算为摩尔数，再除以其在配平方程式中的系数，结果最小的反应物即为决定产物生成量的限量反应物。

How do you calculate percent yield?如何计算产率？

Percent yield equals the actual yield divided by the theoretical yield, multiplied by 100. The theoretical yield comes from stoichiometry using the limiting reactant.产率等于实际产量除以理论产量再乘以 100。理论产量由限量反应物通过化学计量计算得出。

What is the difference between empirical and molecular formula?实验式和分子式有什么区别？

The empirical formula is the simplest whole-number ratio of atoms, while the molecular formula gives the actual number of atoms and is a whole-number multiple of the empirical formula.实验式是原子的最简整数比，而分子式给出实际的原子数目，是实验式的整数倍。

Every curriculum we map to includes it, and the four agree on a shared core: the mole concept, molar mass, mole-ratio stoichiometry, and balancing equations. If you are cramming the night before如果你在临阵磨枪

Memorise five things: one mole $= 6.022 \times 10^{23}$ particles (Avogadro's number); moles $= $ mass $\div$ molar mass ($n = m/M$); the mole ratio from the balanced equation converts between moles of different substances; the limiting reactant is the one that runs out first; percent yield $= (\text{actual}/\text{theoretical}) \times 100\%$. Read every cram-cheat box.背熟五件事：1 摩尔 $= 6.022 \times 10^{23}$ 个粒子（阿伏伽德罗常数）；摩尔数 $= $ 质量 $\div$ 摩尔质量（$n = m/M$）；配平方程式中的摩尔比用于在不同物质的摩尔数之间换算；限量反应物是最先耗尽的那个；产率 $= （\text{actual}/\text{theoretical}）\times 100\%$。读每个速记框。
