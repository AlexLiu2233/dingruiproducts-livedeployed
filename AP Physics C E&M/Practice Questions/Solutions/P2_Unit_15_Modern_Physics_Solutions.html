Q1EASY 15.1 Photon Properties15.1 光子的性质No Calculator

Which of the following statements about photons is correct?下列关于光子的说法，哪一项是正确的？

  • (A) A photon has a very small but nonzero mass, which is why it can carry momentum.光子有很小但不为零的质量，所以它才能携带动量。
  • (B) The energy of a photon is proportional to its wavelength.光子的能量与它的波长成正比。
  • (C) The energy of each photon is larger in brighter light of the same frequency.频率相同时，光越亮，每个光子的能量越大。
  • (D) A photon is massless and electrically neutral, and its energy is proportional to its frequency.光子质量为零且不带电，其能量与频率成正比。
Answer:答案： (D)
$$E = hf = \frac{hc}{\lambda},\qquad p = \frac{h}{\lambda},\qquad m_\text{photon}=0,\quad q_\text{photon}=0$$
Here $E$ is the photon energy, $h$ is Planck's constant, $f$ is the frequency, $\lambda$ is the wavelength and $p$ is the photon momentum.其中 $E$ 是光子能量，$h$ 是普朗克常量，$f$ 是频率，$\lambda$ 是波长，$p$ 是光子动量。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) assumes that momentum needs mass; a photon carries $p=h/\lambda$ with zero mass. (B) mixes up the two variables: since $E=hc/\lambda$, the energy is inversely proportional to the wavelength, not proportional to it. (C) confuses brightness with photon energy; a brighter beam has more photons per second, but each still has $E=hf$ for the same $f$.(A) 以为动量必须有质量；光子质量为零，却有 $p=h/\lambda$。 (B) 把变量弄混了：因为 $E=hc/\lambda$，能量与波长成反比，而不是成正比。 (C) 把亮度当成光子能量；光越亮，每秒的光子数越多，但频率 $f$ 相同时每个光子仍是 $E=hf$。
Insight.要点。 Photon energy is set by frequency alone; intensity only counts how many photons arrive.光子的能量只由频率决定；光强只决定到达的光子有多少个。

A photon has a wavelength of $620~\mathrm{nm}$ in free space. Its energy is closest to一个光子在自由空间中的波长为 $620~\mathrm{nm}$。其能量最接近

  • (A) $3.2\times10^{-19}~\mathrm{eV}$
  • (B) $0.50~\mathrm{eV}$
  • (C) $2.00~\mathrm{eV}$
  • (D) $7.7\times10^{5}~\mathrm{eV}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$E = \frac{hc}{\lambda} = \frac{1240~\mathrm{eV\,nm}}{620~\mathrm{nm}} = 2.00~\mathrm{eV}$$
Here $E$ is the photon energy, $h$ is Planck's constant, $c$ is the speed of light and $\lambda$ is the wavelength.其中 $E$ 是光子能量，$h$ 是普朗克常量，$c$ 是光速，$\lambda$ 是波长。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) converts the correct answer to joules, $(2.00)(1.60\times10^{-19})=3.2\times10^{-19}$, but keeps the label eV. (B) inverts the ratio: $620/1240=0.50$. (D) multiplies instead of dividing: $(1240)(620)=7.7\times10^{5}$.(A) 把正确答案换算成焦耳，$(2.00)(1.60\times10^{-19})=3.2\times10^{-19}$，却仍然标成 eV。 (B) 把比值倒过来：$620/1240=0.50$。 (D) 用乘法代替除法：$(1240)(620)=7.7\times10^{5}$。
Insight.要点。 With $\lambda$ in nanometers, $E~(\mathrm{eV})=1240/\lambda$ gives the answer directly in eV, and a longer wavelength means a smaller energy.波长以纳米为单位时，$E~(\mathrm{eV})=1240/\lambda$ 直接得到以 eV 为单位的结果；波长越长，能量越小。
Q3EASY 15.1 Photon Speed in a Medium15.1 介质中光子的速度Calculator

Photons travel from free space into glass whose index of refraction is $1.50$. The speed of the photons in the glass is closest to光子从自由空间进入折射率为 $1.50$ 的玻璃。光子在玻璃中的速度最接近

  • (A) $1.33\times10^{8}~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $2.00\times10^{8}~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $3.00\times10^{8}~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $4.50\times10^{8}~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (B)
$$v = \frac{c}{n} = \frac{3.00\times10^{8}}{1.50} = 2.00\times10^{8}~\mathrm{m/s}$$
Here $v$ is the speed of the photons in the glass, $c$ is their speed in free space and $n$ is the index of refraction.其中 $v$ 是光子在玻璃中的速度，$c$ 是光子在自由空间中的速度，$n$ 是折射率。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) divides by $n^2$: $3.00\times10^{8}/2.25=1.33\times10^{8}$. (C) keeps the free-space speed; every photon travels at $c$ only in free space, and in a medium the speed is inversely proportional to $n$. (D) multiplies by $n$: $(3.00\times10^{8})(1.50)=4.50\times10^{8}$, which would make the photons faster than $c$.(A) 除以 $n^2$：$3.00\times10^{8}/2.25=1.33\times10^{8}$。 (C) 保留自由空间中的速度；只有在自由空间中所有光子的速度才都是 $c$，在介质中速度与 $n$ 成反比。 (D) 乘以 $n$：$(3.00\times10^{8})(1.50)=4.50\times10^{8}$，这会使光子比 $c$ 还快。
Insight.要点。 Since $n\ge1$, the speed of photons in a material is never greater than $c$.因为 $n\ge1$，光子在材料中的速度不会大于 $c$。
Q4MEDIUM 15.1 de Broglie Wavelength15.1 德布罗意波长No Calculator

A slow electron has de Broglie wavelength $\lambda$. It is accelerated until its kinetic energy is four times as large (it is still slow enough for $K=p^2/2m$ to hold). Its de Broglie wavelength is now一个慢速电子的德布罗意波长为 $\lambda$。它被加速，直到动能变为原来的四倍（速度仍然很小，$K=p^2/2m$ 仍然成立）。此时它的德布罗意波长为

  • (A) $\lambda/4$
  • (B) $\lambda/2$
  • (C) $2\lambda$
  • (D) $4\lambda$
Answer:答案： (B)
$$K=\frac{p^2}{2m}\;\Rightarrow\; p=\sqrt{2mK}\;\Rightarrow\; p'=\sqrt{4}\,p=2p$$
$$\lambda'=\frac{h}{p'}=\frac{h}{2p}=\frac{\lambda}{2}$$
Here $K$ is the kinetic energy, $p$ the momentum, $m$ the electron mass, $h$ Planck's constant and $\lambda$ the de Broglie wavelength; primes mark the values after the acceleration.其中 $K$ 是动能，$p$ 是动量，$m$ 是电子质量，$h$ 是普朗克常量，$\lambda$ 是德布罗意波长；带撇的量是加速之后的值。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) treats $\lambda$ as inversely proportional to the kinetic energy, so a factor of $4$ in $K$ gives $\lambda/4$. (C) sees that the momentum doubles but then uses $\lambda\propto p$, so $\lambda$ would double. (D) treats $\lambda$ as proportional to the kinetic energy.(A) 把 $\lambda$ 当成与动能成反比，$K$ 变为 $4$ 倍就得到 $\lambda/4$。 (C) 看出动量加倍，却用了 $\lambda\propto p$，于是 $\lambda$ 也加倍。 (D) 把 $\lambda$ 当成与动能成正比。
Insight.要点。 Go from the energy to the momentum first: $p\propto\sqrt{K}$, and then $\lambda=h/p$ is inversely proportional to $p$.先由能量求动量：$p\propto\sqrt{K}$，再用 $\lambda=h/p$，$\lambda$ 与 $p$ 成反比。

  • (A) $13$, $14$ and $16$
  • (B) $13$, $14$ and $13$
  • (C) $14$, $13$ and $10$
  • (D) $13$, $14$ and $10$
Answer:答案： (D)
$$Z=13,\qquad N=A-Z=27-13=14$$
$$N_e=Z-3=13-3=10$$
Here $Z$ is the number of protons, $N$ the number of neutrons and $N_e$ the number of electrons.其中 $Z$ 是质子数，$N$ 是中子数，$N_e$ 是电子数。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) adds the charge to the electron count, $13+3=16$, but a positive ion has lost electrons. (B) ignores the net charge and counts the electrons of the neutral atom, $13$. (C) swaps the roles of the two numbers, reading $A-Z=14$ as the protons and $Z=13$ as the neutrons.(A) 把电荷加到电子数上，$13+3=16$，但带正电的离子是失去了电子。 (B) 忽略净电荷，数的是中性原子的电子数 $13$。 (C) 把两个数的作用弄反，把 $A-Z=14$ 当作质子数，把 $Z=13$ 当作中子数。
Insight.要点。 The lower number is the protons, $A-Z$ is the neutrons, and the electrons are $Z$ minus the net charge in units of $e$.下方的数是质子数，$A-Z$ 是中子数，电子数等于 $Z$ 减去以 $e$ 为单位的净电荷。
Q6MEDIUM 15.2 Standing-Wave Condition15.2 驻波条件Calculator

An electron moves at $4.38\times10^{5}~\mathrm{m/s}$ in a circular orbit of radius $1.32\times10^{-9}~\mathrm{m}$ around a nucleus. The number of de Broglie wavelengths of the electron that fit around the circumference of the orbit is closest to一个电子以 $4.38\times10^{5}~\mathrm{m/s}$ 的速率绕原子核做半径为 $1.32\times10^{-9}~\mathrm{m}$ 的圆周运动。绕轨道一周的长度上包含的该电子德布罗意波长的个数最接近

  • (A) $0.8$
  • (B) $1.6$
  • (C) $5.0$
  • (D) $10$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$\lambda=\frac{h}{mv}=\frac{6.63\times10^{-34}}{(9.11\times10^{-31})(4.38\times10^{5})}=1.66\times10^{-9}~\mathrm{m}$$
$$2\pi r=2\pi(1.32\times10^{-9})=8.29\times10^{-9}~\mathrm{m},\qquad \frac{2\pi r}{\lambda}=\frac{8.29\times10^{-9}}{1.66\times10^{-9}}\approx5.0$$
Here $\lambda$ is the de Broglie wavelength, $h$ is Planck's constant, $m$ is the electron mass, $v$ is its speed and $r$ is the orbit radius.其中 $\lambda$ 是德布罗意波长，$h$ 是普朗克常量，$m$ 是电子质量，$v$ 是它的速率，$r$ 是轨道半径。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) divides the radius, not the circumference, by $\lambda$: $1.32\times10^{-9}/1.66\times10^{-9}=0.80$. (B) uses the diameter $2r$ as the length of the path: $2.64\times10^{-9}/1.66\times10^{-9}=1.6$. (D) uses $4\pi r$, which is twice the circumference: $1.66\times10^{-8}/1.66\times10^{-9}=10$.(A) 用半径而不是周长去除以 $\lambda$：$1.32\times10^{-9}/1.66\times10^{-9}=0.80$。 (B) 用直径 $2r$ 作为路径长度：$2.64\times10^{-9}/1.66\times10^{-9}=1.6$。 (D) 用了 $4\pi r$，是周长的两倍：$1.66\times10^{-8}/1.66\times10^{-9}=10$。
Insight.要点。 The standing-wave condition compares the circumference $2\pi r$ with $\lambda$; a whole number such as $5$ means the orbit is an allowed energy state.驻波条件是把周长 $2\pi r$ 与 $\lambda$ 相比；得到像 $5$ 这样的整数，说明该轨道是允许的能量状态。
Q7HARD 15.2 Bohr Orbit15.2 玻尔轨道No Calculator

In the Bohr model, the electric force of the nucleus on an electron in a circular orbit supplies the net centripetal force. Compare two circular orbits of the same electron around the same nucleus. In the second orbit the radius is four times that of the first, and the electron's speed in the first orbit is $v$. The electron's speed in the second orbit is在玻尔模型中，原子核对做圆周运动的电子的电场力提供向心的合力。比较同一个电子绕同一个原子核的两条圆轨道：第二条轨道的半径是第一条的四倍，电子在第一条轨道上的速率为 $v$。电子在第二条轨道上的速率为

  • (A) $v/2$
  • (B) $v/4$
  • (C) $v/16$
  • (D) $2v$
Answer:答案： (A)
$$\frac{ke^2}{r^2}=\frac{mv^2}{r}\;\Rightarrow\; v=\sqrt{\frac{ke^2}{mr}}\;\Rightarrow\; v\propto r^{-1/2}$$
$$\frac{v_2}{v_1}=\left(\frac{r_2}{r_1}\right)^{-1/2}=4^{-1/2}=\frac{1}{2}$$
Here $k$ is the Coulomb constant, $e$ is the elementary charge, $m$ is the electron mass, $r$ is the orbit radius and $v$ is the speed.其中 $k$ 是库仑常量，$e$ 是元电荷，$m$ 是电子质量，$r$ 是轨道半径，$v$ 是速率。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) assumes $v\propto1/r$, which is what keeping the angular momentum $mvr$ fixed would give; here the electric force sets the speed through $F_e=F_\text{net}$. (C) assumes $v\propto1/r^2$ by copying the dependence of the electric force on $r$. (D) assumes $v\propto r^{1/2}$, which is what a centripetal force $F$ that stays the same as the radius grows would give, $v=\sqrt{Fr/m}$; but the electric force weakens as $r$ grows.(B) 以为 $v\propto1/r$，这相当于角动量 $mvr$ 保持不变；而这里是电场力通过 $F_e=F_\text{net}$ 决定速率。 (C) 照搬电场力对 $r$ 的依赖，以为 $v\propto1/r^2$。 (D) 以为 $v\propto r^{1/2}$，这相当于向心力 $F$ 在半径增大时保持不变，$v=\sqrt{Fr/m}$；但电场力会随 $r$ 增大而减弱。
Insight.要点。 Set $F_e=F_\text{net}$ and solve for $v$ before comparing: the $r$ in $mv^2/r$ cancels one factor of $r$ in $ke^2/r^2$.先令 $F_e=F_\text{net}$ 并解出 $v$，再作比较：$mv^2/r$ 中的 $r$ 与 $ke^2/r^2$ 中的一个 $r$ 约去。
Q8MEDIUM 15.3 Emission Wavelength15.3 发射波长Calculator

The energies of the lowest four levels of hydrogen are $E_1=-13.60~\mathrm{eV}$, $E_2=-3.40~\mathrm{eV}$, $E_3=-1.51~\mathrm{eV}$ and $E_4=-0.85~\mathrm{eV}$. A hydrogen atom makes a transition from $n=4$ to $n=2$ and emits one photon. The wavelength of the photon is closest to氢原子最低四个能级的能量为 $E_1=-13.60~\mathrm{eV}$，$E_2=-3.40~\mathrm{eV}$，$E_3=-1.51~\mathrm{eV}$，$E_4=-0.85~\mathrm{eV}$。一个氢原子从 $n=4$ 跃迁到 $n=2$，发射一个光子。该光子的波长最接近

  • (A) $97~\mathrm{nm}$
  • (B) $292~\mathrm{nm}$
  • (C) $486~\mathrm{nm}$
  • (D) $1.46\times10^{3}~\mathrm{nm}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$\Delta E=E_4-E_2=(-0.85)-(-3.40)=2.55~\mathrm{eV}$$
$$\lambda=\frac{hc}{\Delta E}=\frac{1240~\mathrm{eV\,nm}}{2.55~\mathrm{eV}}=486~\mathrm{nm}$$
Here $E_n$ is the energy of level $n$, $\Delta E$ is the energy of the emitted photon, $h$ is Planck's constant, $c$ is the speed of light and $\lambda$ is the photon wavelength.其中 $E_n$ 是第 $n$ 能级的能量，$\Delta E$ 是所发射光子的能量，$h$ 是普朗克常量，$c$ 是光速，$\lambda$ 是光子波长。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) takes the lower level as $n=1$: $\Delta E=12.75~\mathrm{eV}$ gives $1240/12.75=97~\mathrm{nm}$. (B) adds the magnitudes of the two energies, $0.85+3.40=4.25~\mathrm{eV}$, giving $1240/4.25=292~\mathrm{nm}$. (D) uses only $|E_4|=0.85~\mathrm{eV}$ as the photon energy, giving $1240/0.85=1.46\times10^{3}~\mathrm{nm}$.(A) 把低能级取成 $n=1$：$\Delta E=12.75~\mathrm{eV}$，得 $1240/12.75=97~\mathrm{nm}$。 (B) 把两个能量的绝对值相加，$0.85+3.40=4.25~\mathrm{eV}$，得 $1240/4.25=292~\mathrm{nm}$。 (D) 只用 $|E_4|=0.85~\mathrm{eV}$ 作为光子能量，得 $1240/0.85=1.46\times10^{3}~\mathrm{nm}$。
Insight.要点。 The photon carries the difference between the two levels, so subtract the signed energies before converting with $hc$.光子带走的是两个能级之差，所以先把带符号的能量相减，再用 $hc$ 换算。

Mathematical Routines. In a Bohr-type model of a hydrogen atom, an electron moves at a speed of $5.47\times10^{5}~\mathrm{m/s}$ in a circular orbit of radius $8.46\times10^{-10}~\mathrm{m}$ around a proton. Take $k=8.99\times10^{9}~\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$.数学运算。在玻尔型的氢原子模型中，一个电子以 $5.47\times10^{5}~\mathrm{m/s}$ 的速率绕质子做半径为 $8.46\times10^{-10}~\mathrm{m}$ 的圆周运动。取 $k=8.99\times10^{9}~\mathrm{N\,m^2/C^2}$。

(a) De Broglie wavelength:德布罗意波长：
$$\lambda=\frac{h}{mv}=\frac{6.63\times10^{-34}}{(9.11\times10^{-31})(5.47\times10^{5})}=1.33\times10^{-9}~\mathrm{m}$$
(b) An orbit is allowed if its circumference holds a whole number of wavelengths:如果轨道的周长恰好容纳整数个波长，这条轨道就是允许的：
$$2\pi r=2\pi(8.46\times10^{-10})=5.32\times10^{-9}~\mathrm{m},\qquad \frac{2\pi r}{\lambda}=\frac{5.32\times10^{-9}}{1.33\times10^{-9}}\approx4.0$$

(c) The electric force and the force needed for the circular motion:电场力以及圆周运动所需的力：
$$F_e=\frac{ke^2}{r^2}=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(1.60\times10^{-19})^2}{(8.46\times10^{-10})^2}=3.22\times10^{-10}~\mathrm{N}$$
$$F_\text{net}=\frac{mv^2}{r}=\frac{(9.11\times10^{-31})(5.47\times10^{5})^2}{8.46\times10^{-10}}=3.22\times10^{-10}~\mathrm{N}$$
The two agree, so the Coulomb force can supply the net centripetal force for this orbit.两者相等，所以库仑力可以提供这条轨道所需的向心的合力。

$$\frac{2\pi r'}{\lambda}=\frac{2\pi(7.00\times10^{-10})}{1.33\times10^{-9}}=3.31$$
This is not a whole number, so the wave does not close on itself and the proposed orbit is not allowed.这不是整数，所以波不能首尾相接，提议的轨道是不允许的。
