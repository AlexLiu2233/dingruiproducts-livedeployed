Q 6第6题HARD较难2.11 Empirical rule2.11 经验法则CALCULATOR PERMITTED可用计算器[1]
Scores on a 100-point exam have mean 78 and standard deviation 12, but the histogram is strongly skewed to the left with a few very low scores. A student writes: "By the empirical rule, about 95% of the scores are between 54 and 102, so about 2.5% of students scored above 102." Which critique is correct?
一次满分 100 分的考试，分数均值（mean） 78、标准差（standard deviation） 12，但直方图明显左偏，有几个很低的分数。一名学生写道：“由经验法则（empirical rule），约 95% 的分数在 54 到 102 之间，所以约 2.5% 的学生得分高于 102。”哪项评价正确？
  1. (A) The empirical rule describes Normal distributions only. This distribution is skewed, so the 95% claim is unsupported, and no score above 100 is possible, so the 2.5% claim fails.经验法则只描述正态分布。这个分布是偏态的，所以 95% 的说法没有依据；而且不可能有高于 100 的分数，因此 2.5% 的说法不成立。
  2. (B) There is no flaw: $78\pm2(12)$ is 54 to 102, so the arithmetic is right and the rule applies.没有问题：$78\pm2(12)$ 就是 54 到 102，计算正确，经验法则适用。
  3. (C) The student should have said 5% scored above 102, since 95% are in the middle.该生应当说有 5% 的人高于 102，因为中间有 95%。
  4. (D) There is no flaw: the empirical rule applies to any distribution whose mean and standard deviation are known.没有问题：只要知道均值和标准差，经验法则适用于任何分布。
