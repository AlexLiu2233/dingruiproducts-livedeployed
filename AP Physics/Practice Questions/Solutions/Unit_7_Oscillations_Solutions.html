Q1EASY7.2 Mass-Spring Period7.2 弹簧质量系统周期Calculator

$m = 0.50~\mathrm{kg}$, $k = 200~\mathrm{N/m}$. SHM period?$m = 0.50~\mathrm{kg}$，$k = 200~\mathrm{N/m}$，求简谐运动周期。

  • (A) $0.10~\mathrm{s}$
  • (B) $0.16~\mathrm{s}$
  • (C) $0.31~\mathrm{s}$
  • (D) $0.63~\mathrm{s}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{m/k} = 2\pi\sqrt{0.50/200} = 2\pi(0.050) \approx 0.314~\mathrm{s}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $0.10~\mathrm{s}$ comes from using $T=2\sqrt{m/k}=2(0.050)=0.10~\mathrm{s}$, omitting the factor $\pi$. (B) $0.16~\mathrm{s}$ comes from using $T=\pi\sqrt{m/k}=\pi(0.050)\approx0.16~\mathrm{s}$, taking the coefficient as $\pi$ instead of $2\pi$. (D) $0.63~\mathrm{s}$ comes from using $T=4\pi\sqrt{m/k}=4\pi(0.050)\approx0.63~\mathrm{s}$, doubling the correct period by replacing $2\pi$ with $4\pi$.(A) $0.10~\mathrm{s}$ 来自误用 $T=2\sqrt{m/k}=2(0.050)=0.10~\mathrm{s}$，漏掉了因子 $\pi$。(B) $0.16~\mathrm{s}$ 来自误用 $T=\pi\sqrt{m/k}=\pi(0.050)\approx0.16~\mathrm{s}$，把系数当成 $\pi$ 而不是 $2\pi$。(D) $0.63~\mathrm{s}$ 来自误用 $T=4\pi\sqrt{m/k}=4\pi(0.050)\approx0.63~\mathrm{s}$，把 $2\pi$ 换成 $4\pi$ 使周期加倍。
Insight.要点。 The mass-spring period is $T=2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$. It increases with mass and decreases with stiffness; amplitude does not appear for ideal SHM.弹簧振子周期为 $T=2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$。周期随质量增大、随劲度系数增大而减小；理想简谐运动中振幅不出现。
Q2EASY7.5 Simple Pendulum Period7.5 单摆周期Calculator

Simple pendulum, $L = 1.0~\mathrm{m}$, $g = 9.8~\mathrm{m/s^2}$.单摆，$L = 1.0~\mathrm{m}$，$g = 9.8~\mathrm{m/s^2}$。

  • (A) $1.0~\mathrm{s}$
  • (B) $2.0~\mathrm{s}$
  • (C) $4.0~\mathrm{s}$
  • (D) $6.3~\mathrm{s}$
Answer:答案： (B)
$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{L/g} = 2\pi\sqrt{1.0/9.8} \approx 2.01~\mathrm{s}$$
The classic 1-m seconds-pendulum result.这是经典的1米秒摆结果。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $1.0~\mathrm{s}$ comes from replacing the factor $2\pi$ by $\pi$ in $T=2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$, giving $T=\pi\sqrt{1.0/9.8}\approx1.0~\mathrm{s}$. (C) $4.0~\mathrm{s}$ comes from doubling the coefficient to $4\pi$ in $T=4\pi\sqrt{L/g}$, giving $T=4\pi\sqrt{1.0/9.8}\approx4.0~\mathrm{s}$. (D) $6.3~\mathrm{s}$ comes from forgetting to divide by $g$ inside the square root, $T=2\pi\sqrt{L}=2\pi\sqrt{1.0}\approx6.3~\mathrm{s}$.(A) $1.0~\mathrm{s}$ 来自把 $T=2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$ 中的系数 $2\pi$ 误写成 $\pi$，得到 $T=\pi\sqrt{1.0/9.8}\approx1.0~\mathrm{s}$。 (C) $4.0~\mathrm{s}$ 来自把系数错误地加倍为 $4\pi$，写成 $T=4\pi\sqrt{L/g}$，得到 $T=4\pi\sqrt{1.0/9.8}\approx4.0~\mathrm{s}$。 (D) $6.3~\mathrm{s}$ 来自根号内漏除以 $g$，写成 $T=2\pi\sqrt{L}=2\pi\sqrt{1.0}\approx6.3~\mathrm{s}$。
Insight.要点。 A simple pendulum has $T=2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$, independent of mass and amplitude in the small-angle limit. The one-meter pendulum is the classic two-second period.单摆周期为 $T=2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$，小角度极限下与质量和振幅无关。一米单摆是经典的两秒周期。
Q3EASY7.1 Defining SHM7.1 简谐运动定义No Calculator

An object undergoes simple harmonic motion when the net force on it is当作用在物体上的合力满足以下条件时，物体做简谐运动：

  • (A) constant in magnitude and direction大小和方向均恒定
  • (B) proportional to its speed and opposite in direction与速度成正比且方向相反
  • (C) proportional to its displacement from equilibrium and opposite in direction与偏离平衡位置的位移成正比且方向相反
  • (D) proportional to the square of its displacement from equilibrium与偏离平衡位置的位移的平方成正比
Answer:答案： (C)
SHM ⇔ $F = -kx$ (linear restoring force). Trap (B) is the damping law; (D) gives anharmonic motion.简谐运动 ⇔ $F = -kx$（线性回复力）。选项(B)是阻尼规律；选项(D)给出非简谐运动。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) A student chooses a constant force because a constant acceleration seems to give repetitive motion; for example $F=mg$ near Earth gives constant acceleration and no linear restoring force toward $x=0$, so the motion is not SHM. (B) A student remembers that drag opposes velocity and treats $F\propto -v$ as the defining SHM force; this gives damping and removes energy rather than producing undamped oscillation. (D) A student confuses the quadratic potential energy $U\propto x^2$ with the force law and writes a force proportional to the square of displacement, such as $F\propto -x^2$; then $m\ddot x=-kx^2$, which is not linear and gives non-sinusoidal motion.(A) 学生把“合力恒定”误认为产生往复运动的条件，因为恒定加速度看起来能给出固定模式；例如靠近地表时 $F=mg$ 只给出恒定加速度，没有指向 $x=0$ 的线性回复力，所以不是简谐运动。 (B) 学生只记得阻尼力与速度反向，把 $F\propto -v$ 当作简谐运动的定义；这个力做负功并耗散能量，得到阻尼振动而非无阻尼简谐运动。 (D) 学生把二次势能 $U\propto x^2$ 与力律混淆，写成正比于位移平方的力，例如 $F\propto -x^2$；于是 $m\ddot x=-kx^2$，方程非线性，运动不是正弦式。
Insight.要点。 Simple harmonic motion is defined by a linear restoring force $F=-kx$. The minus sign means the force always points back toward equilibrium, which is what produces oscillation.简谐运动由线性回复力 $F=-kx$ 定义。负号表示力始终指向平衡位置，这正是产生振动的根源。
Q4EASY7.4 Energy at Amplitude7.4 振幅处的能量No Calculator

Block on a spring at $x = +A$. Its energy is弹簧上的滑块位于 $x = +A$ 处，其能量为

  • (A) entirely kinetic全部为动能
  • (B) entirely potential全部为势能
  • (C) half kinetic and half potential动能与势能各占一半
  • (D) zero (the spring is fully relaxed)零（弹簧完全松弛）
Answer:答案： (B)
At $x = \pm A$ the block is momentarily at rest (turning point), so $K = 0$ and $E = U = \tfrac{1}{2}kA^2$. Trap (D) confuses "no motion" with "no energy", the spring is fully stretched here, not relaxed.在 $x = \pm A$ 处，滑块瞬间静止（转折点），故 $K = 0$，$E = U = \tfrac{1}{2}kA^2$。选项(D)将"无运动"误认为"无能量"，此处弹簧是完全伸长的，而非松弛。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) Assigning the maximum speed $v=A\omega$ at the turning point gives $K=\tfrac12 mA^2\omega^2=\tfrac12 kA^2$ and hides that $v=0$ there. (C) Setting $K=U=\tfrac12E$ at the endpoint splits the energy equally, but at $x=A$ the speed is zero, so $K=0$. (D) Using $v=0$ to conclude both $K=0$ and $U=0$ confuses the turning point with the relaxed position; at $x=A$ the spring is fully stretched.(A) 在端点误用最大速度 $v=A\omega$，得到 $K=\tfrac12 mA^2\omega^2=\tfrac12 kA^2$，忽略了该处 $v=0$。(C) 在端点令 $K=U=\tfrac12E$，把能量平均分成两半，但 $x=A$ 处速度为零，所以 $K=0$。(D) 由 $v=0$ 同时断定 $K=0$ 且 $U=0$，把端点与弹簧原长位置混淆；在 $x=A$ 处弹簧被拉到最大形变。
Insight.要点。 At the turning points $x=\pm A$, speed is zero, so all energy is potential: $E=\tfrac12 kA^2$. Zero speed does not mean zero energy; the spring is maximally stretched or compressed.在转折点 $x=\pm A$ 处速度为零，故全部能量都是势能：$E=\tfrac12 kA^2$。速度为零不代表能量为零；弹簧处于最大伸长或压缩状态。

  • (B) $A$
  • (C) $A\omega$

$$v(t) = \dot x = -A\omega\sin(\omega t),\quad v(0) = -A\omega\sin 0 = 0$$

(C) $A\omega$ comes from using the maximum speed $A\omega$ at the turning point and choosing the positive direction, although the speed at $x=A$ is zero. (D) $-A\omega$ comes from using the same maximum speed with the negative sign, for example by setting $\sin0$ to $1$ in $v=-A\omega\sin(\omega t)$.(B) $A$ 来自算出 $x(0)=A\cos0=A$ 后把位置当作速度。(C) $A\omega$ 来自在端点误用最大速度 $A\omega$ 并取正方向，但 $x=A$ 处速度为零。(D) $-A\omega$ 来自把同一最大速度取负号，例如在 $v=-A\omega\sin(\omega t)$ 中把 $\sin0$ 当成 $1$。
Insight.要点。 For $x=A\cos(\omega t)$, velocity is the derivative $-A\omega\sin(\omega t)$. At $t=0$ the particle is at the turning point, so the velocity is naturally zero.对 $x=A\cos(\omega t)$，速度是其导数 $-A\omega\sin(\omega t)$。$t=0$ 时粒子位于转折点，速度自然为零。
Q6MEDIUM7.4 Maximum Speed7.4 最大速度Calculator

$m = 0.20~\mathrm{kg}$, $k = 50~\mathrm{N/m}$, $A = 0.10~\mathrm{m}$. Maximum speed?$m = 0.20~\mathrm{kg}$，$k = 50~\mathrm{N/m}$，$A = 0.10~\mathrm{m}$，求最大速度。

  • (A) $0.5~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (B) $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (C) $1.6~\mathrm{m/s}$
  • (D) $2.5~\mathrm{m/s}$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$v_{\max} = A\omega = A\sqrt{k/m} = 0.10\sqrt{50/0.20} = 0.10\sqrt{250} \approx 1.58~\mathrm{m/s}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $0.5~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from using $v=2A/T$ with the SHM period $T=2\pi\sqrt{m/k}\approx0.397~\mathrm{s}$, treating one peak-to-peak distance $2A$ as the distance covered in a full period: $2(0.10)/0.397\approx0.50~\mathrm{m/s}$. (B) $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from computing the average speed over a full cycle as $v_{\mathrm{avg}}=4A/T=4(0.10)/0.397\approx1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ instead of the maximum speed. (D) $2.5~\mathrm{m/s}$ comes from energy conservation $\tfrac12 m v^2=\tfrac12 kA^2$ and solving only for $v^2=kA^2/m=50(0.10)^2/0.20=2.5$, then omitting the square root and reporting $v^2$ as the speed.(A) $0.5~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自用 $v=2A/T$，其中简谐周期 $T=2\pi\sqrt{m/k}\approx0.397~\mathrm{s}$，学生把一个完整周期内走过的距离误当成峰到峰的距离 $2A$：$2(0.10)/0.397\approx0.50~\mathrm{m/s}$。 (B) $1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自把完整周期内的平均速率 $v_{\mathrm{avg}}=4A/T=4(0.10)/0.397\approx1.0~\mathrm{m/s}$ 当作最大速率。 (D) $2.5~\mathrm{m/s}$ 来自用能量守恒 $\tfrac12 m v^2=\tfrac12 kA^2$ 只解出 $v^2=kA^2/m=50(0.10)^2/0.20=2.5$，然后漏掉开平方，把 $v^2$ 的值当成速率。
Insight.要点。 Maximum speed occurs at equilibrium and equals $A\omega=A\sqrt{k/m}$. The mass appears under a square root, and the result must have units of speed.最大速度出现在平衡位置，等于 $A\omega=A\sqrt{k/m}$。质量在根号下，且结果必须具有速度单位。

Q9MEDIUM7.4 Energy Fraction at $x = A/2$7.4 $x = A/2$ 处的能量比例No Calculator

Fraction of $E$ that is kinetic at $x = A/2$?在 $x = A/2$ 处，$E$ 中动能所占的比例？

  • (A) $1/4$
  • (B) $1/2$
  • (C) $3/4$
  • (D) $7/8$
Answer:答案： (C)
$$\frac{U(A/2)}{E} = \frac{\tfrac{1}{2}k(A/2)^2}{\tfrac{1}{2}kA^2} = \tfrac{1}{4}$$
So $K/E = 1 - \tfrac{1}{4} = \tfrac{3}{4}$.故 $K/E = 1 - \tfrac{1}{4} = \tfrac{3}{4}$。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $1/4$ comes from computing the potential-energy fraction at $x=A/2$ as $U/E=(A/2)^2/A^2=1/4$ and stopping before converting to kinetic energy. (B) $1/2$ comes from treating the energy split as linear in $x$, so at $x=A/2$ the student assigns $U=E/2$ and $K=E/2$ instead of using $U\propto x^2$. (D) $7/8$ comes from computing $U=\tfrac12 k(A/2)^2=kA^2/8$ but then using $E=kA^2$ by omitting the $\tfrac12$ in the total energy; this gives $K/E=1-1/8=7/8$.(A) $1/4$ 来自先算 $x=A/2$ 处的势能占比 $U/E=(A/2)^2/A^2=1/4$，然后停在这里，没有再用 $K/E=1-U/E$ 求动能占比。 (B) $1/2$ 来自把能量分配当作与 $x$ 成正比，于是在 $x=A/2$ 处写成 $U=E/2$ 和 $K=E/2$，而没有使用 $U\propto x^2$。 (D) $7/8$ 来自正确算出 $U=\tfrac12 k(A/2)^2=kA^2/8$，但把总能量写成 $E=kA^2$，漏掉了总能量中的 $\tfrac12$；于是 $K/E=1-1/8=7/8$。
Insight.要点。 At $x=A/2$, potential energy is one quarter of the total because $U\propto x^2$. Therefore kinetic energy is three quarters of the total, not half.在 $x=A/2$ 处，由于 $U\propto x^2$，势能为总能量的四分之一。因此动能为总能量的四分之三，而不是二分之一。
Q10MEDIUM7.5 Physical Pendulum (Rod)7.5 实体摆（均匀杆）No Calculator

Uniform rod $M$, $L$, pivoted at one end. Period of small-amplitude swing?均匀杆，质量 $M$，长度 $L$，绕一端转动，求小振幅摆动周期。

  • (A) $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}$
  • (B) $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{2g}}$
  • (C) $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2L}{3g}}$
  • (D) $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3L}{2g}}$

$T = 2\pi\sqrt{I/(Mgd)}$ with $I = \tfrac{1}{3}ML^2$ and $d = L/2$:$T = 2\pi\sqrt{I/(Mgd)}$，其中 $I = \tfrac{1}{3}ML^2$，$d = L/2$：
$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\tfrac{1}{3}ML^2}{Mg(L/2)}} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2L}{3g}}$$
Note this is faster than the simple pendulum of length $L$ (since $2/3 < 1$), the mass is distributed closer to the pivot.注意，这比摆长为 $L$ 的单摆更快（因为 $2/3 < 1$），因为质量分布更靠近转轴。

$$I\ddot\theta = -M g\,(L/2)\,\theta,\quad I = \tfrac{1}{3}ML^2$$
$$\ddot\theta = -\frac{Mg(L/2)}{(1/3)ML^2}\,\theta = -\frac{3g}{2L}\,\theta$$
Canonical SHM with $\omega^2 = 3g/(2L)$, so这是标准简谐运动，$\omega^2 = 3g/(2L)$，故
$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2L}{3g}}$$
(b) Plug numbers:代入数值：
$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2(1.20)}{3(9.8)}} = 2\pi\sqrt{0.0816} \approx 1.79~\mathrm{s}$$
(c) Exact energy conservation: the rod's CM falls by $(L/2)(1 - \cos\theta_0)$ as $\theta$ goes from $\theta_0$ to $0$.精确能量守恒：当 $\theta$ 从 $\theta_0$ 变为 $0$ 时，质心下降 $(L/2)(1 - \cos\theta_0)$。
$$M g\,(L/2)(1 - \cos\theta_0) = \tfrac{1}{2}I\omega^2 = \tfrac{1}{6}ML^2\omega^2$$
$$\omega^2 = \frac{3g(1 - \cos\theta_0)}{L} = \frac{3(9.8)(1 - 0.866)}{1.20} \approx 3.28~\mathrm{rad^2/s^2}$$
$$\omega \approx 1.81~\mathrm{rad/s}$$
