Q1EASY易 🇺🇸 US美 SAT-style MCQSAT 风格选择题 §1 Slope斜率 · HSF-IF.B.6 [3 marks][3 分]

Slope of the line through $(-4, 7)$ and $(2, -5)$.求过点 $(-4, 7)$ 与 $(2, -5)$ 的直线斜率。

Answer:答案：  (A)  $m = -2$

(a) Apply slope formula套用斜率公式 M1·A1·A1

$$ m \;=\; \frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}} \;=\; \frac{-5 - 7}{2 - (-4)} \;=\; \frac{-12}{6} \;=\; -2. $$ So the slope is $-2$, matching option (A).故斜率为 $-2$，对应选项 (A)。
Why the distractors fail.干扰项分析。
(B) $-\tfrac{1}{2}$: the reciprocal, comes from swapping numerator and denominator in the slope formula.是真值的倒数，来源是在斜率公式中把分子分母颠倒。
(C) $\tfrac{1}{2}$: reciprocal with the sign also dropped, two errors at once.既颠倒又丢掉负号，一步走错两次。
(D) $2$: dropped the negative sign from $-12 / 6$.在算 $-12 / 6$ 时丢掉了负号。
Pin down "rise over run", direction-locked.把"纵向变化除以横向变化"锁定方向。 The sign of slope is fixed the moment you pick which point is $(x_{1}, y_{1})$, but only if you keep that choice consistent in both numerator and denominator. The common slip is writing $\dfrac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{1} - x_{2}}$, which flips the sign. Either lock the order before you compute, or use the magnitude $\left|\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}\right|$ and read the sign off the geometry: as $x$ increases from $-4$ to $2$, $y$ falls from $7$ to $-5$, so $m < 0$.一旦选定哪个点是 $(x_{1}, y_{1})$，斜率符号就定了——前提是分子与分母都保持同一选择。常见的错是写成 $\dfrac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{1} - x_{2}}$，这会把符号翻反。要么在动笔前锁定顺序，要么先算绝对值 $\left|\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}\right|$，再从几何上读出符号：$x$ 从 $-4$ 增到 $2$ 时，$y$ 从 $7$ 降到 $-5$，所以 $m < 0$。
Q2EASY易 🇺🇸 US美 SAT-style MCQSAT 风格选择题 §2 Slope-Intercept斜截式 · HSA-REI.B.3 [3 marks][3 分]

Rewrite $4x - 3y = 12$ in slope-intercept form.将 $4x - 3y = 12$ 化为斜截式。

Answer:答案：  (B)  $y = \tfrac{4}{3}\,x - 4$

(a) Isolate $y$分离 $y$ M1·A1

Start from $4x - 3y = 12$. Subtract $4x$ from both sides:从 $4x - 3y = 12$ 出发，两边减去 $4x$： $$ -3y \;=\; -4x + 12. $$ Divide every term by $-3$, flipping every sign:两边同除以 $-3$，每一项都变号： $$ y \;=\; \tfrac{-4}{-3}\,x + \tfrac{12}{-3} \;=\; \tfrac{4}{3}\,x - 4. $$

(b) Read $(m, b)$ and match读出 $(m, b)$ 并比对 A1

Slope $m = \tfrac{4}{3}$, $y$-intercept $b = -4$. Hence option (B).斜率 $m = \tfrac{4}{3}$，$y$ 轴截距 $b = -4$，故选 (B)。
Why the distractors fail.干扰项分析。
(A) $y = -\tfrac{4}{3} x + 4$: sign error on both the slope and the intercept, divided by $+3$ instead of $-3$.斜率与截距均符号反，是把分母写成 $+3$ 而非 $-3$ 所致。
(C) $y = 4x - 12$: "moved" the $-3y$ across the equals sign without dividing the rest of the equation.把 $-3y$ "搬"过等号，却没把右边一并除以 $-3$。
(D) $y = \tfrac{3}{4} x - 3$: read off the equation as if it were already $y = mx + b$, treating the $-3$ multiplier as a constant.当作方程已是 $y = mx + b$ 形式直接读取，把 $-3$ 当成常数处理。
Read slope only from $y = mx + b$ form.只能从 $y = mx + b$ 形式读出斜率。 A line in standard form $Ax + By = C$ has slope $-A/B$ and $y$-intercept $C/B$, not $A$ and $C$. Many students glance at $4x - 3y = 12$ and answer "slope 4". On the SAT the only safe move is to isolate $y$ before reading anything off the equation. The second trap is sign-handling: dividing by a negative coefficient flips every term, both the $x$-coefficient and the constant.标准式 $Ax + By = C$ 的斜率是 $-A/B$、$y$ 轴截距是 $C/B$，而不是 $A$ 和 $C$。不少同学瞥一眼 $4x - 3y = 12$ 就答"斜率 4"。在 SAT 上唯一稳妥的做法是先分离 $y$，再从方程读取信息。第二个陷阱是符号处理：除以负系数会让每一项都变号，$x$ 的系数与常数项都得跟着翻。
Q3EASY易 🇨🇦 ON安 ON Provincial-style安大略省考风格 §1 Rate of Change变化率 · MPM1D Linear Relations线性关系 [4 marks][4 分]

Pool drains linearly: $V(3) = 4200$ L, $V(11) = 2600$ L. (a) Rate. (b) Interpret sign. (c) Restriction on $t$.泳池线性排水：$V(3) = 4200$ 升，$V(11) = 2600$ 升。(a) 求变化率。(b) 解释符号含义。(c) 写出 $t$ 的限制。

Answer:答案：  (a) $\dfrac{\Delta V}{\Delta t} = -200$ L/min升/分  ·  (b) negative $\Rightarrow$ water is leaving负值 $\Rightarrow$ 水在流失  ·  (c) $0 \le t \le 24$ min分

(a) Compute the rate, with units计算变化率（带单位） M1·A1

Using two data points $(t, V) = (3, 4200)$ and $(11, 2600)$:取两组数据 $(t, V) = (3, 4200)$ 与 $(11, 2600)$： $$ \frac{\Delta V}{\Delta t} \;=\; \frac{2600 - 4200}{11 - 3} \;=\; \frac{-1600}{8} \;=\; -200 \;\; \text{L/min}. $$

(b) Interpret the sign解释符号含义 A1

The negative sign means $V$ is decreasing over time: the pool is losing $200$ litres of water every minute. This is consistent with "the pool is being drained."负号表示 $V$ 随时间递减：泳池每分钟流失 $200$ 升水，与题干"泳池正在排水"一致。

(c) Domain restriction定义域限制 A1

The linear model is $V(t) = -200 t + b$. Using $V(3) = 4200$: $b = 4200 + 600 = 4800$, so $V(t) = 4800 - 200 t$. Physically the volume cannot go below zero: $V(t) \ge 0 \;\Leftrightarrow\; t \le 24$. Time also cannot run backwards from the start of the drain: $t \ge 0$. Hence $0 \le t \le 24$ minutes.线性模型为 $V(t) = -200 t + b$。代入 $V(3) = 4200$：$b = 4200 + 600 = 4800$，故 $V(t) = 4800 - 200 t$。物理上水量不能为负：$V(t) \ge 0 \;\Leftrightarrow\; t \le 24$。同时排水开始后时间不能倒流：$t \ge 0$。综合得 $0 \le t \le 24$ 分钟。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A common slip is to take the change in volume but miss the decreasing direction, giving $200$ L/min instead of $-200$ L/min. Another near miss is to use only one data point and divide by time, which does not give the rate between the two moments. (b) If a student says the negative sign means the pool is filling, they have reversed the meaning of the sign. Here the negative rate means the volume is dropping by $200$ L each minute. (c) Writing only $t \ge 0$ misses the depletion time. The model also stops at $t = 24$ when $V(t)=0$, so the upper bound is part of the restriction.（a）常见错误是只算体积变化，却忽略减少的方向，得到 $200$ L/min，而不是 $-200$ L/min。另一个失误是只用一个数据点再除以时间，这样不能得到两时刻之间的变化率。（b）如果把负号理解成泳池在加水，就把符号含义反了。这里负速率表示水量每分钟减少 $200$ 升。（c）只写 $t \ge 0$ 会漏掉排空时刻。这个模型在 $t = 24$ 且 $V(t)=0$ 时结束，所以上界也是限制的一部分。
"Restriction on $t$" wants the physical boundary, not just $t \ge 0$."对 $t$ 的限制"要的是物理边界，不仅仅是 $t \ge 0$。 Provincial markers reward students who recognise that a linear model of a physical quantity has two domain boundaries: a start time (often $t = 0$) and a depletion time (set the modelled quantity to zero and solve). Writing only "$t \ge 0$" leaves the upper-bound mark on the table, the pool can't keep draining once it is empty at $t = 24$. State both endpoints explicitly with units.省考阅卷人乐于奖励能看出"物理量的线性模型有两个定义域边界"的学生：一个起始时刻（通常 $t = 0$）和一个耗尽时刻（令被建模量为零再求解）。只写 "$t \ge 0$" 会丢掉上界这一分，因为 $t = 24$ 时池子已空，不可能继续排水。两个端点都要明确写出并带上单位。
Marks, 4 in total.评分要点，共 4 分。 (a) 1: uses the two points to compute $\Delta V / \Delta t$ correctly (M1) · 1: gives $-200$ L/min with units (A1) · (b) 1: states that a negative rate means the volume is decreasing, so the pool is draining (A1) · (c) 1: gives a valid time restriction with both endpoints, $0 \le t \le 24$ min (A1)（a）1：用两个点正确计算 $\Delta V / \Delta t$（M1） · 1：给出带单位的 $-200$ L/min（A1） · （b）1：说明负速率表示体积减少，也就是泳池在排水（A1） · （c）1：给出包含两端点的时间限制 $0 \le t \le 24$ min（A1）
Q4MEDIUM中 🇺🇸 US美 SAT-style MCQSAT 风格选择题 §2 Vertical Line竖直线 · HSF-IF.C.7a [3 marks][3 分]

Equation of the line through $(5, -8)$ and $(5, 17)$.求过点 $(5, -8)$ 与 $(5, 17)$ 的直线方程。

Answer:答案：  (C)  $x = 5$

(a) Recognise the vertical-line pattern识别竖直线特征 M1·A1

Both given points share the same $x$-coordinate, $x = 5$. Attempting the slope formula gives两点的 $x$ 坐标相同，都是 $x = 5$。若硬套斜率公式： $$ m \;=\; \frac{17 - (-8)}{5 - 5} \;=\; \frac{25}{0}, $$ which is undefined. A line with undefined slope is vertical, and a vertical line consists of every point whose $x$-coordinate equals the common value.分母为零、斜率不存在。斜率不存在的直线为竖直线，由所有 $x$ 坐标都等于该共同值的点组成。

(b) Write the equation写出方程 A1

Hence $x = 5$, option (C).故 $x = 5$，选 (C)。
Why the distractors fail.干扰项分析。
(A) $y = 5$: a horizontal line at height 5, students confuse "constant $x$" with "constant $y$".这是过 $y = 5$ 的水平线；学生把"$x$ 恒等"误作"$y$ 恒等"。
(B) $y = -8$: uses only one point and writes a horizontal line through it.只用了一个点，写出过该点的水平线。
(D) $y = \tfrac{25}{2} x$: reads $\Delta y / \Delta x = 25 / 2$ from the two points, but $\Delta x = 0$, not $2$. This distractor invites the student who tries to "force" the slope formula.从两点读出 $\Delta y / \Delta x = 25 / 2$，但 $\Delta x = 0$ 而非 $2$。此干扰项专门"诱惑"那些硬套斜率公式的学生。
A vertical line is not a function.竖直线不是函数。 The single most-tested vertical-line trap on the SAT: students see two points and reach for $y = mx + b$, which fails because $m$ is undefined. The honest move is to inspect the $x$-coordinates first: if they match, the line is $x = (\text{that value})$ and you are done. The same diagnostic works for horizontal lines: matching $y$-coordinates $\Rightarrow$ $y = (\text{that value})$. Train the eye to compare coordinates before computing.SAT 上最常考的竖直线陷阱：学生一看到两点就伸手去写 $y = mx + b$，结果因 $m$ 不存在而翻车。正确做法是先比较 $x$ 坐标：若相同，直线即 $x = (\text{该值})$，秒答。同一招也用于水平线：$y$ 坐标相同 $\Rightarrow$ $y = (\text{该值})$。养成下笔前先比坐标的习惯。
Q5MEDIUM中 🇨🇦 BC卑 BC Provincial-style卑诗省考风格 §3 Graphing作图 · FMPC10 linear functions一次函数 [5 marks][5 分]

$3x + 4y = 12$. (a) Intercepts. (b) Sketch with intercepts and one lattice point. (c) Slope and sign.$3x + 4y = 12$。(a) 求截距。(b) 画图，标出截距与另一格点。(c) 写出斜率及其符号。

Answer:答案：  (a) $x$-int $(4, 0)$, $y$-int $(0, 3)$$x$ 轴截距 $(4, 0)$，$y$ 轴截距 $(0, 3)$  ·  (b) line through $(4,0), (0,3), (-4, 6)$过 $(4,0)$、$(0,3)$、$(-4, 6)$ 的直线  ·  (c) $m = -\tfrac{3}{4}$, negative$m = -\tfrac{3}{4}$，为负

(a) Intercepts截距 A1·A1

$y$-intercept: set $x = 0 \Rightarrow 4y = 12 \Rightarrow y = 3$. So $(0, 3)$.$y$ 轴截距：令 $x = 0 \Rightarrow 4y = 12 \Rightarrow y = 3$，得 $(0, 3)$。
$x$-intercept: set $y = 0 \Rightarrow 3x = 12 \Rightarrow x = 4$. So $(4, 0)$.$x$ 轴截距：令 $y = 0 \Rightarrow 3x = 12 \Rightarrow x = 4$，得 $(4, 0)$。

(b) Sketch with an extra lattice point作图并补一个格点 M1·A1

A convenient lattice point: pick $x = -4$, giving $3(-4) + 4y = 12 \Rightarrow 4y = 24 \Rightarrow y = 6$. So $(-4, 6)$ lies on the line. Plot all three points $(4, 0)$, $(0, 3)$, $(-4, 6)$, label them, and draw a straight line through them. The line falls from upper-left to lower-right.方便的格点：取 $x = -4$，则 $3(-4) + 4y = 12 \Rightarrow 4y = 24 \Rightarrow y = 6$，故 $(-4, 6)$ 在直线上。画出三点 $(4, 0)$、$(0, 3)$、$(-4, 6)$，标注后连成直线。该线从左上向右下倾斜。

(c) Slope and sign斜率与符号 A1

Using the two intercepts:用两截距计算： $$ m \;=\; \frac{3 - 0}{0 - 4} \;=\; -\tfrac{3}{4}. $$ Slope is negative. (Cross-check: rearrange $3x + 4y = 12 \Rightarrow y = -\tfrac{3}{4}\,x + 3$, same slope.)斜率为负。（交叉验证：把 $3x + 4y = 12$ 化为 $y = -\tfrac{3}{4}\,x + 3$，斜率一致。）
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A common error is to read the intercepts straight from the constant term, giving $(12,0)$ and $(0,12)$, instead of solving each one variable equation. Another slip is to swap the coordinates, for example writing $(3,0)$ and $(0,4)$. (b) A student may plot the intercepts correctly but draw the line with the wrong third point, often from a sign slip such as $(-4,-6)$ instead of $(-4,6)$. That still gives a line, but not the right one. (c) A natural mistake is to take the slope as $\frac34$ by ignoring that the line falls from left to right. The correct slope is negative, so the sign must be included.（a）常见错误是直接把常数项当作截距，写成 $(12,0)$ 和 $(0,12)$，而不是分别解一元方程。另一个失误是把坐标对调，比如写成 $(3,0)$ 和 $(0,4)$。（b）有些学生虽然把两个截距画对了，却因为第三个点的符号错误画成 $(-4,-6)$，而不是 $(-4,6)$。这样仍然是一条直线，但不是正确的那条。（c）一个自然错误是把斜率看成 $\frac34$，因为忽略了这条线是从左上向右下下降的。正确斜率为负，符号不能漏掉。
Intercept method beats slope-intercept for sketching.作图时，截距法胜过斜截式。 When a line is in standard form $Ax + By = C$, the fastest route to a sketch is the intercept method: set the other variable to zero twice. This gives you two well-defined lattice points (if $A$, $B$, $C$ behave) and a line in three pen strokes. Converting to $y = mx + b$ first is wasted work for a sketch. The third lattice point is a check, if it doesn't fall on the line drawn through the intercepts, your arithmetic is wrong. Provincial markers look for that third point as evidence the student didn't just trace two dots.当直线为标准式 $Ax + By = C$ 时，最快的作图法就是截距法：分别令另一变量为零两次。若 $A$、$B$、$C$ 取整，立即得到两个清晰的格点，三笔画完。先转 $y = mx + b$ 反而是绕远路。第三个格点是核对工具——若它不落在由两截距连成的直线上，说明算错了。省考阅卷人正是把这第三个点视作"学生不是只描了两个点"的证据。

Q6MEDIUM中 🇺🇸 US美 AP-feeder FRQAP 衔接简答题 §4 Parallel & Perpendicular平行与垂直 · HSF-LE.A.2 [7 marks][7 分]

$\ell_{0}: 2x + 5y = 10$. (a) Slope-intercept form and $m_{0}$. (b) Parallel line through $(-3, 1)$. (c) Perpendicular line through $(4, -2)$.$\ell_{0}: 2x + 5y = 10$。(a) 化为斜截式并求 $m_{0}$。(b) 求过 $(-3, 1)$ 的平行线。(c) 求过 $(4, -2)$ 的垂直线。

Answer:答案：  (a) $y = -\tfrac{2}{5}x + 2$, $m_{0} = -\tfrac{2}{5}$  ·  (b) $y = -\tfrac{2}{5}x - \tfrac{1}{5}$  ·  (c) $y = \tfrac{5}{2}x - 12$

(a) Slope-intercept form of $\ell_{0}$$\ell_{0}$ 的斜截式 M1·A1

From $2x + 5y = 10$, isolate $y$:由 $2x + 5y = 10$ 分离 $y$： $$ 5y \;=\; -2x + 10 \;\Longrightarrow\; y \;=\; -\tfrac{2}{5}\,x + 2. $$ So $m_{0} = -\tfrac{2}{5}$ and the $y$-intercept is $2$.故 $m_{0} = -\tfrac{2}{5}$，$y$ 轴截距为 $2$。

(b) Parallel through $(-3, 1)$过 $(-3, 1)$ 的平行线 M1·A1

Parallel $\Rightarrow$ same slope: $m = -\tfrac{2}{5}$. Use point-slope through $(-3, 1)$:平行 $\Rightarrow$ 斜率相同：$m = -\tfrac{2}{5}$。用点斜式过 $(-3, 1)$： $$ y - 1 \;=\; -\tfrac{2}{5}\,(x - (-3)) \;=\; -\tfrac{2}{5}\,x - \tfrac{6}{5}. $$ Hence $y = -\tfrac{2}{5}\,x - \tfrac{6}{5} + 1 = -\tfrac{2}{5}\,x - \tfrac{1}{5}$.故 $y = -\tfrac{2}{5}\,x - \tfrac{6}{5} + 1 = -\tfrac{2}{5}\,x - \tfrac{1}{5}$。

(c) Perpendicular through $(4, -2)$过 $(4, -2)$ 的垂直线 M1·A1·A1

Perpendicular slope is the negative reciprocal of $m_{0}$:垂直方向的斜率为 $m_{0}$ 的负倒数： $$ m_{\perp} \;=\; -\frac{1}{m_{0}} \;=\; -\frac{1}{-2/5} \;=\; \tfrac{5}{2}. $$ Point-slope through $(4, -2)$:用点斜式过 $(4, -2)$： $$ y - (-2) \;=\; \tfrac{5}{2}\,(x - 4) \;\Longrightarrow\; y + 2 \;=\; \tfrac{5}{2}\,x - 10. $$ So $y = \tfrac{5}{2}\,x - 12$.故 $y = \tfrac{5}{2}\,x - 12$。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A common slip is to read the slope from the $x$ coefficient in standard form and stop there. From $2x+5y=10$, the slope is not $2$ or $\frac25$, it is $-\frac25$. (b) A natural error is to use the wrong slope for the parallel line, or to keep the right slope but change the point sign, which can produce a line such as $y=-\frac25x+\frac{11}{5}$ instead of the correct one. (c) A frequent mistake is to use the reciprocal $-\frac52$ or the positive reciprocal $\frac25$ for a perpendicular line. Either slip gives a different equation, not $y=\frac52x-12$.（a）常见错误是把标准式里的 $x$ 系数直接当成斜率，看到这里就停下。由 $2x+5y=10$ 得到的斜率不是 $2$ 也不是 $\frac25$，而是 $-\frac25$。（b）一个自然错误是平行线用了错误斜率，或者斜率对了却把点的符号写反了，这样会得到像 $y=-\frac25x+\frac{11}{5}$ 这样的直线，而不是正确答案。（c）常见失误是把垂线斜率写成 $-\frac52$，或者只取正倒数 $\frac25$。这两种都不会得到 $y=\frac52x-12$。

Q13MEDIUM中 🇺🇸 US美 AP-feeder FRQAP 衔接简答题 §5 Special Systems: None or Infinitely Many特殊方程组：无解或无穷多解 · HSA-REI.C.6 [11 marks][11 分]

(a) Check $(5, 1)$ in $x + y = 6$, $2x - y = 6$. (b) $y = 3x + 2$, $y = 3x - 4$. (c) $x + y = 10$, $2x + 2y = 20$. (d) $k$ for no solution.(a) 检验 $(5, 1)$ 是否满足 $x + y = 6$、$2x - y = 6$。(b) $y = 3x + 2$、$y = 3x - 4$。(c) $x + y = 10$、$2x + 2y = 20$。(d) 使方程组无解的 $k$。

Answer:答案：  (a) wrong; $(4, 2)$不正确；$(4, 2)$  ·  (b) no solution (parallel lines)无解（平行线）  ·  (c) infinitely many (same line)无穷多解（同一直线）  ·  (d) $k = 3$

(a) Check the claim in both equations在两个方程中检验 M1·R1·A1

First equation: $5 + 1 = 6$ $\checkmark$. Second equation: $2(5) - 1 = 9 \ne 6$. A pair that satisfies only one equation is not a solution, so the claim is wrong. To solve, substitute $y = 6 - x$ into the second equation:第一个方程：$5 + 1 = 6$ $\checkmark$。第二个方程：$2(5) - 1 = 9 \ne 6$。只满足一个方程的数对不是解，所以该说法错误。求解：把 $y = 6 - x$ 代入第二个方程： $$ 2x - (6 - x) = 6 \;\Longrightarrow\; 3x = 12 \;\Longrightarrow\; x = 4, \quad y = 6 - 4 = 2. $$ Check: $4 + 2 = 6$ $\checkmark$ and $8 - 2 = 6$ $\checkmark$. The solution is $(4, 2)$.检验：$4 + 2 = 6$ $\checkmark$，$8 - 2 = 6$ $\checkmark$。解为 $(4, 2)$。

(b) A false statement means no solution矛盾式意味着无解 M1·R1·R1

Substitute $y = 3x + 2$ into the second equation: $3x + 2 = 3x - 4$, so $2 = -4$. The $x$-terms cancel and what remains is false for every $x$, so no pair satisfies both equations: the system has no solution. On a graph the lines have the same slope $3$ but different $y$-intercepts ($2$ and $-4$), so they are parallel and never meet.把 $y = 3x + 2$ 代入第二个方程：$3x + 2 = 3x - 4$，即 $2 = -4$。$x$ 项相消，剩下的式子对任何 $x$ 都不成立，所以没有数对能同时满足两个方程：方程组无解。图像上两直线斜率同为 $3$，但 $y$ 轴截距不同（$2$ 与 $-4$），故互相平行、永不相交。

(c) A true statement means infinitely many solutions恒等式意味着无穷多解 M1·R1·R1

From $y = 10 - x$: $2x + 2(10 - x) = 20$, so $2x + 20 - 2x = 20$, i.e. $20 = 20$. This is true for every $x$, so it does not rule anything out: every pair on the line $x + y = 10$ is a solution, e.g. $(0, 10)$, $(3, 7)$, $(10, 0)$. The second equation is exactly $2$ times the first, so both describe the same line — there are infinitely many solutions, not none.由 $y = 10 - x$：$2x + 2(10 - x) = 20$，即 $20 = 20$。它对任何 $x$ 都成立，因此没有排除任何值：直线 $x + y = 10$ 上的每一点都是解，例如 $(0, 10)$、$(3, 7)$、$(10, 0)$。第二个方程恰好是第一个的 $2$ 倍，两者表示同一条直线——方程组有无穷多解，而不是无解。

(d) Parallel, distinct lines平行且不重合的直线 A1·R1

Setting $kx + 1 = 3x - 2$ gives $(k - 3)x = -3$. If $k = 3$ this reads $0 = -3$, which is impossible, so there is no solution. (For any $k \ne 3$ there is exactly one solution, $x = \tfrac{-3}{k - 3}$.) Equivalently, $k = 3$ makes the slopes equal while the intercepts $1$ and $-2$ differ.令 $kx + 1 = 3x - 2$，得 $(k - 3)x = -3$。若 $k = 3$，则为 $0 = -3$，不可能，方程组无解。（$k \ne 3$ 时恰有一解 $x = \tfrac{-3}{k - 3}$。）等价地说，$k = 3$ 使两直线斜率相等而截距 $1$ 与 $-2$ 不同。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A common error is to check only the first equation and stop when $(5,1)$ works for $x+y=6$. The second equation gives $2(5)-1=9$, so the claim fails. (b) A natural mistake is to substitute correctly, reach $2=-4$, and then try to force an $x$ value from a statement that has no variable left. That usually leads to the wrong conclusion about the number of solutions. (c) If a student sees $20=20$ and says there is no solution, they are reading a true identity the wrong way. A true leftover statement means every point on the line works, so there are infinitely many solutions. (d) A frequent error is to think different slopes are needed for no solution. In fact, no solution here comes from the same slope $k=3$ but different intercepts, so the lines are parallel and distinct.（a）常见错误是只检查第一个方程，看到 $(5,1)$ 对 $x+y=6$ 成立就停下来。第二个方程给出 $2(5)-1=9$，所以这个说法不成立。（b）一个自然错误是代入后得到 $2=-4$，却还想从一个已经没有变量的式子里强行求出 $x$。这样通常会把解的个数判断错。（c）如果学生看到 $20=20$ 就说没有解，那就是把一个真的恒等式看反了。这个真命题表示直线上每个点都满足方程，所以有无穷多解。（d）常见错误是以为没有解需要不同斜率。其实这里没有解来自同斜率 $k=3$ 但截距不同，所以两条直线平行且不重合。
Read the leftover statement.读懂剩下的式子。 When the variable cancels, the answer is not a pair but a reason. A false leftover ($2 = -4$) means parallel lines and no solution; a true leftover ($20 = 20$) means the same line and infinitely many solutions. A complete answer to (b) or (c) contains no pair of numbers at all.变量相消时，答案不是数对，而是一个理由。剩下的式子为假（$2 = -4$）表示平行线、无解；为真（$20 = 20$）表示同一直线、无穷多解。(b) 或 (c) 的完整答案中根本不含数对。

$y = x^{2}$ and $y = x + 6$: substitute, factor, find all pairs, interpret graphically.$y = x^{2}$ 与 $y = x + 6$：代入、因式分解、求出所有解对，并作图像解释。

Answer:答案：  (a) $x = 3$ or或 $x = -2$  ·  (b) $(3, 9)$, $(-2, 4)$  ·  (c) two intersection points两个交点

(a) Substitute and factor代入并因式分解 M1·M1·A1

Both equations give $y$, so set them equal:两个方程都给出 $y$，令其相等： $$ x^{2} = x + 6 \;\Longrightarrow\; x^{2} - x - 6 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; (x - 3)(x + 2) = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; x = 3 \;\text{or}\; x = -2. $$

(b) Back-substitute and check回代并检验 A1·A1

$x = 3$: $y = 3 + 6 = 9$, and $3^{2} = 9$ $\checkmark$. $x = -2$: $y = -2 + 6 = 4$, and $(-2)^{2} = 4$ $\checkmark$. The solutions are $(3, 9)$ and $(-2, 4)$.$x = 3$：$y = 9$，且 $3^{2} = 9$ $\checkmark$。$x = -2$：$y = 4$，且 $(-2)^{2} = 4$ $\checkmark$。解为 $(3, 9)$ 与 $(-2, 4)$。

(c) Why 0, 1 or 2为何是 0、1 或 2 个 R1·R1

Substituting always produces a quadratic equation, which has $0$, $1$ or $2$ real roots. Geometrically, a line can miss the parabola, touch it at one point (a tangent), or cut through it at two points. Here the line $y = x + 6$ cuts the parabola twice, at $(-2, 4)$ and $(3, 9)$, matching the two roots.代入后总得到一个二次方程，它有 $0$、$1$ 或 $2$ 个实根。几何上，直线可能与抛物线不相交、相切于一点，或穿过抛物线交于两点。本题直线 $y = x + 6$ 与抛物线交于两点 $(-2, 4)$ 与 $(3, 9)$，与两个根对应。
Where this goes wrong.错在哪里。 (a) A common slip is to move $x+6$ to the left as $x^{2} - x + 6 = 0$, which uses the wrong sign on the constant term and breaks the factorisation to $x = 3$ or $x = -2$. (b) Another mistake is to find the $x$ values but then not pair each one with its matching $y$, so a student may stop at $x = 3$ and $x = -2$ instead of writing the ordered pairs $(3, 9)$ and $(-2, 4)$. (c) A common error is to think a line and a parabola can meet in only one or two points because there is one of each shape, but the graph can also miss entirely; here the line really cuts the parabola at two points, so the answer is two intersection points.(a) 常见错误是把 $x+6$ 移到左边时写成 $x^{2} - x + 6 = 0$，常数项符号错了，这样就得不到 $x = 3$ 或 $x = -2$ 的因式分解。 (b) 另一个错误是先求出 $x$ 值，却没有把每个 $x$ 配上对应的 $y$，于是只写到 $x = 3$ 和 $x = -2$，而没有写成有序数对 $(3, 9)$ 和 $(-2, 4)$。 (c) 常见误区是以为一条直线和一条抛物线只能交于一个或两个点，因为图形各只有一个，但它们也可能完全不相交；这里直线确实与抛物线相交于两点，所以答案是两个交点。
Same method, new shape.同一方法，新的形状。 Substitution does not care whether the equations are linear; it reduces two equations to one.
