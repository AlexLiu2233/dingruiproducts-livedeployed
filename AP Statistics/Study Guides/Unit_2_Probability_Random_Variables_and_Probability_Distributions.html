Topics 2.1 and 2.2 · Selecting statistical methods

Two categorical variables: tables and summaries

Must know

A two-way table organizes two categorical variables. Joint relative frequencies divide a cell by the table total, marginal relative frequencies divide a row or column total by the table total, and conditional relative frequencies divide a cell by its row or column total. An association exists when the conditional distributions differ across the groups being compared.

When both variables are categorical, the question is not "how much" but "how do the groups compare". Start by naming the two variables, the individuals, and the total. Every summary you report should say which total it was divided by.

Reads at least one book a monthOwns an e-reader: YesOwns an e-reader: NoTotal
Yes10575180
No4575120
Total150150300

Synthetic teaching data used throughout this guide: 300 surveyed students, none of it real research.

Example 2.1 — Marginal versus conditional
  1. Marginal: $P(\text{reads})=180/300=0.60$ and $P(\text{owns})=150/300=0.50$.
  2. Conditional: $P(\text{reads}\mid\text{owns})=105/150=0.70$ and $P(\text{reads}\mid\text{does not own})=75/150=0.50$.
  3. Because $0.70\ne0.50$, the conditional distributions differ; in this sample the variables are associated.
Example 2.2 — Reading a segmented bar chart

Draw one stacked bar per e-reader group with segment heights 0.70/0.30 and 0.50/0.50. The two bars are visibly different, which is the graphical statement of association. If the segments matched exactly, the bars would be identical and there would be no evidence of association.

Go deeper: why divide by the row or column total?

A conditional relative frequency re-bases the table on the group you are comparing. Dividing by the overall total mixes the groups back together and can hide a real difference. Always report which total you divided by: "among students who own an e-reader" is not the same claim as "among all students".

主题 2.1 与 2.2 · 选择统计方法

两个分类变量：表格与汇总

必备知识

二维表格整理两个分类变量。联合相对频率用单元格除以总计数；边际相对频率用行或列合计除以总计数；条件相对频率用单元格除以所属行或列的合计。当比较的各组条件分布不同时，两个变量存在关联。

当两个变量都是分类变量时，问题不是“多少”，而是“各组如何比较”。先命名两个变量、观测单位与总计数，并始终说明每个比分除以哪个总计数。

是10575180
否4575120
合计150150300

本指南贯穿使用的合成教学数据：调查 300 名学生，均非真实研究。

例 2.1 — 边际与条件相对频率
  1. 边际：$P(\text{读书})=180/300=0.60$，$P(\text{拥有})=150/300=0.50$。
  2. 条件：$P(\text{读书}\mid\text{拥有})=105/150=0.70$，$P(\text{读书}\mid\text{不拥有})=75/150=0.50$。
  3. 由于 $0.70\ne0.50$，两组条件分布不同，本样本中两变量有关联。
例 2.2 — 读取分段条形图

按两组分别绘制堆叠条形，段高为 0.70/0.30 与 0.50/0.50。两条形状明显不同，这就是关联的图形陈述；若各段完全一致，两条重合，则无关联证据。

深入理解：为何除以行或列合计？

条件相对频率把表格重新建立在所比较的组上。除以总计数会把各组混合在一起，可能掩盖真实差异。始终说明除以了哪个总计数：“在拥有电子阅读器的学生中”与“在所有学生中”是不同的结论。

Topic 2.3 · Selecting statistical methods

Estimating probabilities using simulation

Must know

A simulation estimates a probability by running a model of the random phenomenon many times and recording the proportion of trials in which the event occurs. The estimate is not the exact probability; more trials tend to make the estimate more stable, but no number of trials repairs a wrong model.

Simulations make abstract probabilities concrete. Four steps: define the random phenomenon and the event, state the model (how one trial is generated), run many independent trials, then report the proportion of trials where the event occurred. Each trial must follow the same model and must not be influenced by the previous trial.

Example 2.3 — Free throws by simulation

A player's free throws are modeled with success probability 0.70. Use one random digit per shot: 0 through 6 = made, 7 through 9 = missed. Twenty simulated shots:

Shot12345678910
Digit outcomeMMSMSMMMSM
Shot11121314151617181920
Digit outcomeMSMMMSMMSM

Fourteen of the twenty simulated shots were made, so the estimated probability of a made free throw is $14/20=0.70$. A different set of twenty digits would give a different estimate; that variability is expected, not an error.

Go deeper: what a simulation can and cannot show

The estimate inherits the model: digits 0 through 6 correspond to probability 0.70 only because the model says so. A simulation cannot discover the true probability by itself, and a single run of twenty trials is a rough estimate. Increasing the number of trials reduces the typical distance between the estimate and the model probability, but a biased model stays biased at any number of trials.

主题 2.3 · 选择统计方法

用模拟估计概率

必备知识

模拟通过多次运行随机现象的模型，记录事件发生的比例，以此估计概率。估计值并非精确概率；试验次数越多，估计通常越稳定，但再多次数也无法弥补错误的模型。

模拟把抽象的概率变得具体。四个步骤：定义随机现象与事件，声明模型（单次试验如何生成），运行许多独立试验，最后报告事件发生的比例。每次试验必须遵循同一模型，且不受前一次试验影响。

例 2.3 — 用模拟估计罚球命中率

假设罚球命中概率为 0.70，每次投一个随机数字：0 至 6 为命中，7 至 9 为未中。20 次模拟结果：

第次12345678910
结果MMSMSMMMSM
第次11121314151617181920
结果MSMMMSMMSM

20 次中命中 14 次，估计命中概率为 $14/20=0.70$。换一组随机数字会得到不同估计值，这种波动是正常现象。

深入理解：模拟能说明什么

估计结果继承模型：数字 0 至 6 对应概率 0.70，仅因模型如此声明。模拟本身无法发现真实概率，20 次试验只是粗略估计。增加试验次数可减小估计与模型概率的典型距离，但模型有偏时，任何次数都无法纠正。

Topics 2.4 and 2.5 · Selecting statistical methods

Introduction to probability; mutually exclusive events

Must know

The sample space lists every possible outcome; an event is a subset of outcomes. Every probability lies between 0 and 1, and the probabilities of all outcomes in a sample space sum to 1. The complement rule gives $P(A^{c})=1-P(A)$. The addition rule is $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)$. Events are mutually exclusive when they share no outcomes, so $P(A\cap B)=0$ and the rule reduces to $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)$.

Probability statements are written about named events, not about vague outcomes. "At least one" is usually computed as the complement of "none"; "A or B" means at least one of the two occurs, not one or the other exclusively.

Example 2.4 — The addition rule with overlap

For a randomly chosen student, let $A$ be "owns an e-reader" with $P(A)=0.45$, and $B$ be "reads at least one book a month" with $P(B)=0.30$, where $P(A\cap B)=0.10$.

  1. $P(A\cup B)=0.45+0.30-0.10=0.65$: 65% of students own an e-reader or read monthly (or both).
  2. $P(\text{neither})=P((A\cup B)^{c})=1-0.65=0.35$.
  3. Because $P(A\cap B)=0.10\ne0$, $A$ and $B$ are not mutually exclusive in this model.
Example 2.5 — Mutually exclusive events

A single randomly drawn card cannot be both a heart and a spade. With 13 hearts and 13 spades in a standard 52-card deck, $P(\text{heart or spade})=\tfrac{13}{52}+\tfrac{13}{52}=\tfrac{26}{52}=0.50$: the simple addition works exactly because the events share no outcomes.

Go deeper: subtracting the overlap is not optional

Adding two probabilities double-counts the outcomes in both events. The subtraction repairs exactly that double count, so it is needed whenever the events can occur together. For mutually exclusive events the overlap is empty, the subtraction removes nothing, and both formulas agree.

主题 2.4 与 2.5 · 选择统计方法

概率入门与互斥事件

必备知识

样本空间列出所有可能结果，事件是结果的子集。任何概率在 0 与 1 之间，样本空间内所有结果的概率之和为 1。互补规则 $P(A^{c})=1-P(A)$；加法规则 $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)$。互斥事件没有公共结果，$P(A\cap B)=0$，加法规则简化为直接相加。

概率陈述应针对有名字的事件，而非模糊描述。“至少一个”通常用“一个都没有”的互补计算；“A 或 B”指两者至少发生一个，并非二者只取其一。

例 2.4 — 含重叠的加法规则

随机选一名学生，记 $A$ 为“拥有电子阅读器”，$P(A)=0.45$；$B$ 为“每月至少读一本书”，$P(B)=0.30$，且 $P(A\cap B)=0.10$。

  1. $P(A\cup B)=0.45+0.30-0.10=0.65$：65% 的学生拥有阅读器或每月读书（含两者兼有）。
  2. $P(\text{两者均无})=1-0.65=0.35$。
  3. 因 $P(A\cap B)=0.10\ne0$，此模型中 $A$ 与 $B$ 不互斥。
例 2.5 — 互斥事件

从 52 张牌中抽一张，不可能既是红桃又是黑桃。红桃 13 张、黑桃 13 张，$P(红桃或黑桃)=\tfrac{26}{52}=0.50$：事件无公共结果，直接相加即可。

深入理解：为何必须减去重叠

两个概率相加会把同时属于两事件的结果重复计入，减去重叠部分恰好修正这一重复。当事件不可能同时发生时，重叠为空，两种写法一致。

Topics 2.6 and 2.7 · Selecting statistical methods

Conditional probability; independent events and unions

Must know

Conditional probability is $P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}$ for $P(B)>0$: it re-bases the sample space on the knowledge that $B$ occurred. Events $A$ and $B$ are independent exactly when $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$, equivalently $P(A\cap B)=P(A)\cdot P(B)$. For independent events the addition rule becomes $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A)\cdot P(B)$.

Independence is a property of the probability model, not of one set of observed data. In a two-way table, equal conditional distributions suggest independence in the population the sample represents; a difference suggests association. The test is the same multiplication: compare the joint probability with the product of the two marginal probabilities.

Example 2.6 — Conditional probability from a table

Using the 300-student table from Section 2.1: $P(\text{reads}\mid\text{owns})=\frac{P(\text{reads}\cap\text{owns})}{P(\text{owns})}=\frac{0.35}{0.50}=0.70$. Reading the table directly gives $105/150=0.70$, as expected.

Example 2.7 — Testing independence and combining events
  1. Compare $P(\text{reads}\cap\text{owns})=0.35$ with $P(\text{reads})\cdot P(\text{owns})=0.60\times0.50=0.30$. They differ, so in this sample the events are not independent; the variables are associated.
  2. Two warnings in a fire-alarm system fail independently with probabilities 0.02 and 0.05. Then $P(\text{both fail})=0.02\times0.05=0.001$.
  3. $P(\text{at least one fails})=0.02+0.05-0.001=0.069$ by the addition rule.
Go deeper: mutually exclusive is not the same as independent

Mutually exclusive events cannot occur together, so for events with positive probability, $P(A\mid B)=0\ne P(A)$: they cannot be independent. Independence says that learning $B$ happened does not change the probability of $A$; mutual exclusivity says learning $B$ happened rules $A$ out entirely. Do not swap the two.

主题 2.6 与 2.7 · 选择统计方法

条件概率、独立性与事件并

必备知识

当 $P(B)>0$ 时，条件概率 $P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}$：它把样本空间重新建立在"已知 B 发生"之上。事件 $A$ 与 $B$ 独立当且仅当 $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$，等价于 $P(A\cap B)=P(A)\cdot P(B)$。对独立事件，加法规则变形为 $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A)\cdot P(B)$。

独立性是概率模型的性质，而不是某一份观测数据的性质。在二维表格中，若各条件分布相等，提示样本所代表的总体中两变量可能独立；若不同，则提示存在关联。两种情形用同一次乘法检验：比较联合概率与两个边际概率之积。

例 2.6 — 由表格求条件概率

沿用 2.1 节 300 名学生数据：$P(\text{读书}\mid\text{拥有})=\frac{P(\text{读书}\cap\text{拥有})}{P(\text{拥有})}=\frac{0.35}{0.50}=0.70$。直接读表为 $105/150=0.70$，两者一致。

例 2.7 — 独立性检验与事件并
  1. 比较 $P(\text{读书}\cap\text{拥有})=0.35$ 与 $P(\text{读书})\cdot P(\text{拥有})=0.60\times0.50=0.30$。两者不等，本样本中两事件不独立，两变量相关。
  2. 火灾报警系统两个独立报警器失效概率分别为 0.02 与 0.05，则 $P(\text{同时失效})=0.02\times0.05=0.001$。
  3. 由加法规则 $P(\text{至少一个失效})=0.02+0.05-0.001=0.069$。
深入理解：互斥不等于独立

互斥事件不可能同时发生：对正概率事件，$P(A\mid B)=0\ne P(A)$，因此不可能独立。独立表示"知道 B 发生不改变 A 的概率"；互斥表示"知道 B 发生则 A 必然被排除"。两者不可混用。

Topic 2.8 · Selecting statistical methods

Random variables and probability distributions

Must know

A random variable assigns a numerical value to each outcome of a random phenomenon. A discrete random variable has a countable set of possible values, described by a probability distribution: each probability is between 0 and 1, and the probabilities over all possible values sum to 1. Probabilities of events such as $P(X\ge2)$ are found by adding the probabilities of the values involved.

Distinguish the random variable from its observed value. $X$ is the variable; $x$ is a possible value. Write distributions as a table or formula, and check the two validity conditions before computing anything.

Example 2.8 — A discrete distribution and an event

Let $X$ be the number of days a student reads in 3 sampled days, with this synthetic distribution:

$x$0123
$P(X=x)$0.100.300.400.20
  1. Validity: every probability is between 0 and 1, and $0.10+0.30+0.40+0.20=1$.
  2. $P(X\ge2)=P(X=2)+P(X=3)=0.40+0.20=0.60$.
  3. $P(X<2)=P(X=0)+P(X=1)=0.40$, which is also $1-P(X\ge2)$ by the complement rule.
Go deeper: why the values must sum to 1

The possible values of a discrete random variable partition the sample space: every outcome leads to exactly one value. Total probability 1 for the sample space therefore becomes total probability 1 for the distribution. A table whose probabilities sum to 0.95 has a missing outcome or an arithmetic error.

主题 2.8 · 选择统计方法

随机变量与概率分布

必备知识

随机变量把随机现象的每个结果对应为一个数值。离散随机变量取值可数，由概率分布描述：每个概率在 0 与 1 之间，所有可能取值的概率之和为 1。事件如 $P(X\ge2)$ 的概率等于相关取值概率之和。

注意区分随机变量与其观测值：$X$ 是变量，$x$ 是某个可能取值。用表格或公式写出分布，先验证两条有效性条件，再进行计算。

例 2.8 — 离散分布与事件概率

设 $X$ 为某学生在抽样的 3 天中读书的天数，合成分布如下：

$x$0123
$P(X=x)$0.100.300.400.20
  1. 有效性：各概率均在 0 与 1 之间，且 $0.10+0.30+0.40+0.20=1$。
  2. $P(X\ge2)=P(X=2)+P(X=3)=0.40+0.20=0.60$。
  3. $P(X<2)=P(X=0)+P(X=1)=0.40$，也等于 $1-P(X\ge2)$（互补规则）。
深入理解：为何概率之和必须为 1

离散随机变量的所有可能取值划分了整个样本空间：每个结果恰好对应一个取值。样本空间总概率为 1，因此分布总概率也为 1。若表中概率之和为 0.95，说明遗漏了结果或存在计算错误。

Topic 2.9 · Selecting statistical methods

Parameters of random variables

Must know

The mean (expected value) of a discrete random variable is $\mu_X=\sum x\,P(X=x)$, a weighted average of the values. The variance is $\sigma_X^{2}=\sum (x-\mu_X)^{2}P(X=x)$ and the standard deviation $\sigma_X$ is its square root. The mean describes the long-run average value of $X$; the standard deviation describes typical distance from that mean, in the same units as $X$.

These are parameters of the distribution, computed from probabilities, not statistics computed from data. Expected value is not the most likely value, and for the linear transformation $Y=a+bX$ the parameters follow $\mu_Y=a+b\mu_X$ and $\sigma_Y=|b|\cdot\sigma_X$.

Example 2.9 — Mean and standard deviation of $X$

Continuing the reading-days distribution $x$: 0, 1, 2, 3 with probabilities 0.10, 0.30, 0.40, 0.20:

  1. $\mu_X=0(0.10)+1(0.30)+2(0.40)+3(0.20)=1.7$ days.
  2. Deviations are $-1.7,-0.7,0.3,1.3$; weighted squares give $\sigma_X^{2}=0.289+0.147+0.036+0.338=0.81$, so $\sigma_X=0.9$ days.
  3. Interpretation: across many students, the number of reading days averages 1.7, typically differing from 1.7 by about 0.9 days.
Go deeper: a transformation

If a program adds one required reading day, $Y=X+1$, then $\mu_Y=2.7$ days and $\sigma_Y=0.9$ days: adding a constant moves the center only. Doubling the recording unit, $Z=2X$, gives $\mu_Z=3.4$ and $\sigma_Z=1.8$: scaling moves both, because spread follows the unit.

主题 2.9 · 选择统计方法

随机变量的参数

必备知识

离散随机变量的均值（期望值）$\mu_X=\sum x\,P(X=x)$，是取值的加权平均。方差 $\sigma_X^{2}=\sum (x-\mu_X)^{2}P(X=x)$，标准差 $\sigma_X$ 为其算术平方根。均值描述 $X$ 的长期平均取值；标准差描述取值与均值的典型距离，单位与 $X$ 相同。

这些是由概率算出的分布参数，而非由数据算出的统计量。期望值不等于最可能取值；对线性变换 $Y=a+bX$，参数满足 $\mu_Y=a+b\mu_X$、$\sigma_Y=|b|\cdot\sigma_X$。

例 2.9 — $X$ 的均值与标准差

沿用读书天数分布：取值 0、1、2、3，概率 0.10、0.30、0.40、0.20：

  1. $\mu_X=0(0.10)+1(0.30)+2(0.40)+3(0.20)=1.7$ 天。
  2. 离差为 $-1.7,-0.7,0.3,1.3$，加权平方和为 $\sigma_X^{2}=0.289+0.147+0.036+0.338=0.81$，故 $\sigma_X=0.9$ 天。
  3. 解释：在大量学生中，平均读书 1.7 天，与 1.7 的典型差距约 0.9 天。
深入理解：线性变换

若项目增加一天必读日，$Y=X+1$，则 $\mu_Y=2.7$ 天、$\sigma_Y=0.9$ 天：加常数只平移中心。若记录单位加倍，$Z=2X$，则 $\mu_Z=3.4$、$\sigma_Z=1.8$：缩放同时改变中心与离散程度。

Topic 2.10 · Selecting statistical methods

The binomial distribution

Must know

A binomial setting requires: a fixed number $n$ of trials, two outcomes per trial (success or failure), the same success probability $p$ on every trial, and independent trials. Then $P(X=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^{k}(1-p)^{n-k}$ for $k=0,1,\dots,n$, with mean $\mu=np$ and standard deviation $\sigma=\sqrt{np(1-p)}$.

Check all four conditions before using the binomial model. Sampling without replacement can still be treated as binomial when the sample is small relative to the population, but the conditions must be stated. "At least" and "at most" probabilities are cumulative sums; the complement is usually faster: $P(X\ge k)=1-P(X\le k-1)$.

Example 2.10 — Free throws

A player shoots $n=10$ free throws, each made with probability $p=0.70$, independently.

  1. $P(X=8)=\binom{10}{8}(0.70)^{8}(0.30)^{2}=45(0.05764801)(0.09)\approx0.2335$.
  2. $P(X\ge8)=P(8)+P(9)+P(10)\approx0.2335+0.1211+0.0282=0.3828$.
  3. $\mu=10(0.70)=7$ made throws; $\sigma=\sqrt{10(0.70)(0.30)}=\sqrt{2.1}\approx1.45$ throws.
Go deeper: what changes and what does not

When $p=0.50$ the distribution is symmetric; when $p\ne0.50$ it is skewed (right-skewed for small $p$, left-skewed for large $p$). Changing $n$ with $p$ fixed changes both center and spread; increasing $n$ makes the shape less skewed. Interpret $\mu=7$ as the long-run average number of made throws over many repetitions of the 10-shot sequence.

主题 2.10 · 选择统计方法

二项分布

必备知识

二项分布的前提：试验次数 $n$ 固定；每次试验只有两种结果（成功或失败）；每次成功概率 $p$ 相同；各次试验相互独立。此时 $P(X=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^{k}(1-p)^{n-k}$（$k=0,1,\dots,n$），均值 $\mu=np$，标准差 $\sigma=\sqrt{np(1-p)}$。

使用二项模型前必须核对四个条件。"至少""至多"类概率是累计和，通常用补事件更快：$P(X\ge k)=1-P(X\le k-1)$。

例 2.10 — 罚球

某球员罚球 $n=10$ 次，每次命中概率 $p=0.70$，且相互独立。

  1. $P(X=8)=\binom{10}{8}(0.70)^{8}(0.30)^{2}=45(0.05764801)(0.09)\approx0.2335$。
  2. $P(X\ge8)=P(8)+P(9)+P(10)\approx0.2335+0.1211+0.0282=0.3828$。
  3. $\mu=10(0.70)=7$ 次命中；$\sigma=\sqrt{10(0.70)(0.30)}=\sqrt{2.1}\approx1.45$ 次。
深入理解：形状与参数

$p=0.50$ 时分布对称；$p\ne0.50$ 时偏斜（$p$ 小则右偏，$p$ 大则左偏）。固定 $p$ 而增大 $n$，中心与离散程度都改变，偏斜减轻。$\mu=7$ 应解释为大量重复 10 次投篮序列的长期平均命中数。

Topics 2.11 and 2.12 · Selecting statistical methods

The normal distribution; sampling distributions and the CLT

Must know

A normal distribution is described by its mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$, symmetric with total area 1 under the curve. Standardizing gives $z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$; table probabilities are areas under the standard normal curve. The empirical rule puts about 68%, 95% and 99.7% of values within 1, 2 and 3 standard deviations of the mean. For the sampling distribution of a sample mean: center $\mu_{\bar x}=\mu$, spread $\sigma_{\bar x}=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$, and by the central limit theorem the shape is approximately normal for large $n$, more closely so as $n$ grows.

Sketch and label before computing: mark $\mu$, shade the direction of the probability, and confirm which distribution the question is about. A probability about the population uses $\sigma$; a probability about a sample mean uses $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$.

Example 2.11 — Areas of a normal distribution

Synthetic reading speeds: $X\sim N(70,8)$ words per minute.

  1. $P(X<62)$: $z=\frac{62-70}{8}=-1$, so the probability is about 0.1587.
  2. $P(62<X<78)=P(-1<z<1)\approx0.6826$ by the empirical rule.
  3. The 90th percentile: $x=70+1.2816(8)\approx80.25$ words per minute (inverse normal).
Example 2.12 — Sampling distribution of a mean

The same population has $\mu=70$, $\sigma=8$. Take random samples of $n=36$.

  1. $\mu_{\bar x}=70$ words per minute; $\sigma_{\bar x}=\frac{8}{\sqrt{36}}\approx1.33$ words per minute.
  2. $P(\bar x>72)$: $z=\frac{72-70}{1.333}\approx1.5$, so the probability is about 0.0668.
  3. The central limit theorem supports the normal approximation for the sampling distribution when samples are independent and $n$ is large; larger $n$ makes the approximation better.
Go deeper: population versus sampling distribution

Individual values vary by about $\sigma=8$; sample means vary far less, by about $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$. That is why averages of many observations are more stable than single observations, and why a $\sqrt{n}$ divisor appears in the standard error of a single sample mean or proportion.

主题 2.11 与 2.12 · 选择统计方法

正态分布、样本均值的抽样分布与中心极限定理

必备知识

正态分布由均值 $\mu$ 与标准差 $\sigma$ 描述，关于 $\mu$ 对称，曲线下总面积为 1。标准化 $z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$，查表所得即标准正态曲线下面积。经验法则：约 68%、95%、99.7% 的取值落在均值 1、2、3 个标准差内。样本均值 $\bar x$ 的抽样分布：中心 $\mu_{\bar x}=\mu$，离散程度 $\sigma_{\bar x}=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$；当 $n$ 较大且抽样独立时，中心极限定理保证其形状近似正态，且 $n$ 越大近似越好。

计算前先画图标注：标出 $\mu$，标出所求概率的方向，并确认题目问的是总体分布还是样本均值的分布。涉及总体取值的概率用 $\sigma$；涉及样本均值的概率用 $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$。

例 2.11 — 正态曲线下面积

合成阅读速度数据：$X\sim N(70,8)$ 词/分。

  1. $P(X<62)$：$z=\frac{62-70}{8}=-1$，概率约 0.1587。
  2. $P(62<X<78)=P(-1<z<1)\approx0.6826$（经验法则）。
  3. 第 90 百分位数：$x=70+1.2816(8)\approx80.25$ 词/分（反正态）。
例 2.12 — 均值的抽样分布

同一总体 $\mu=70$、$\sigma=8$，抽取 $n=36$ 的随机样本。

  1. $\mu_{\bar x}=70$ 词/分；$\sigma_{\bar x}=\frac{8}{\sqrt{36}}\approx1.33$ 词/分。
  2. $P(\bar x>72)$：$z=\frac{72-70}{1.333}\approx1.5$，概率约 0.0668。
  3. 当样本独立且 $n$ 较大时，中心极限定理支持对 $\bar x$ 的抽样分布使用正态近似；$n$ 越大近似越好。
深入理解：总体分布与抽样分布

单个取值的典型波动约为 $\sigma=8$；样本均值的波动小得多，约为 $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$。这就是"平均更稳定"的统计原因，也是单个样本均值或比例的标准误中出现 $\sqrt{n}$ 的原因。

Quick review

Exam writing toolkit

Must know

A complete probability response has four moves: name the model or distribution, present the expression, substitute the numbers, and state the answer with context. For normal and sampling-distribution questions, also label the boundaries and shade the direction before computing.

  • Expression first. Write $P(X\ge8)=1-P(X\le7)$ before any number appears; graders read the structure as the mathematical communication.
  • Substitute, then compute. $\binom{10}{8}(0.70)^8(0.30)^2$ shows where the numbers came from; a bare calculator value does not.
  • Direction and units. "About 0.2335" is incomplete; "the probability that the player makes exactly 8 of 10 free throws is about 0.2335" is complete.
  • Use the complement. "At least one", "at least 8", and "neither" are usually fastest as $1-$ the complement.
  • Check conditions. Binomial conditions stated; independence plausible; sampling questions name $n$ and whether the question is about $X$ or $\bar x$.
  • Multiple choice. Eliminate by structure: wrong complement direction, wrong denominator, or a spread that forgets $\sqrt{n}$ are the frequent distractors.
Go deeper: how marks attach to words

AP-style keys award the setup and the conclusion separately. A correct number with no named distribution earns partial credit; a named distribution with an arithmetic slip earns the set-up credit and loses the answer credit. Write both.

Diagnostics

Common errors and their corrections

Each code below is used by the companion Practice and Solutions files so that a wrong option or a wrong step can name its misconception.

CodeThe errorThe correction
M1Dividing a cell by the table total when the question asks about a group.Conditional relative frequencies divide by the row or column total; say which total you used.
M2Reporting a simulation estimate as the exact probability.An estimate inherits its model; more trials reduce variability, never bias.
M3Calling events independent because they cannot happen together.Mutually exclusive positive-probability events are dependent, not independent.
M4Adding $P(A)+P(B)$ with no subtraction when the events overlap.Use $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)$; subtract exactly the double-counted part.
M5Reversing a conditional: reporting $P(B\mid A)$ for $P(A\mid B)$.The denominator is the event after the bar; check which group the condition names.
M6Judging association from one conditional proportion alone.Compare conditional distributions across groups, or compare $P(A\cap B)$ with $P(A)P(B)$.
M7Treating the expected value as the most likely value.$\mu_X$ is a long-run weighted average; the mode can be a different number.
M8Reporting variance or forgetting to square/root when units matter.Variance is in squared units; report the standard deviation in the original units.
M9Using binomial methods when $n$ or $p$ changes between trials.Check all four binomial conditions; a changing probability is not binomial.
M10Computing a normal probability with the tail direction reversed or without standardizing.Sketch, shade, standardize $z$, then read the smaller/left tail as the table gives it.
M11Claiming the population itself must be normal for the CLT.The CLT is about the sampling distribution of $\bar x$; larger $n$ improves the normal approximation.
M12Using $\sigma$ where the question is about $\bar x$, or $\sigma_{\bar x}$ where it is about an individual value.Sample means vary by $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$; individual values vary by $\sigma$.

常见错误与修正

下列编号供配套练习与详解使用，使错误选项或错误步骤能够点名具体误区。

编号常见错误修正
M1问的是分组却用总体合计作分母。条件相对频率除以行或列合计；并注明所用口径。
M2把模拟估计值当作精确概率。估计继承模型；增加次数只减少波动，不能消除偏差。
M3因两事件不可能同时发生就称其独立。正概率的互斥事件是相关的，不是独立的。
M4事件有重叠时仍直接相加 $P(A)+P(B)$。用 $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)$，恰好减去重复计入部分。
M5条件概率方向颠倒：把 $P(B\mid A)$ 当作 $P(A\mid B)$。竖线后的事件作分母；核对条件指向哪一组。
M6只看一个条件比例就判断相关。跨组比较条件分布，或比较 $P(A\cap B)$ 与 $P(A)P(B)$。
M7把期望值当作最可能取值。$\mu_X$ 是长期加权平均；众数可能另有所属。
M8方差单位遗忘平方，或该开方时未开方。方差单位为平方单位；报告标准差时用原单位。
M9$n$ 或 $p$ 在试验间变化时仍用二项方法。核对全部四条二项条件；概率变化则不适用。
M10正态概率方向弄反，或未标准化直接查表。先画图、标注、标准化 $z$，再按表读取左尾面积。
M11声称总体本身必须正态才能用 CLT。CLT 说的是 $\bar x$ 的抽样分布；$n$ 越大，正态近似越好。
M12问 $\bar x$ 时用 $\sigma$，或问单个取值时用 $\sigma_{\bar x}$。样本均值波动为 $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$；单个取值波动为 $\sigma$。

Mixed assessment

Unit quiz

1. A company's 250 employees are classified by whether they work remotely and whether they use public transit. Remote: 30 transit, 70 no transit. On-site: 45 transit, 105 no transit. What is $P(\text{transit}\mid\text{remote})$?

Answer: B. The condition says remote, so divide by the remote total: $30/100=0.30$.

  • A. Divided the cell by the table total instead of the remote total.
  • B. Correct: conditional on remote, the base is the 100 remote employees.
  • C. Used the on-site conditional $45/150=0.30$? It equals this one; the question asked about remote.
  • D. Mixed a partial column total into the denominator.
2. For the same table, remote employees use transit at proportion 0.30 and on-site employees at proportion 0.30. What does this suggest?

Answer: B. Equal conditional distributions indicate no association in the sample; causal language is never supported by an observational classification.

  • A. Association would require the conditional proportions to differ.
  • B. Correct: 0.30 equals 0.30, so the conditionals agree.
  • C. Classification cannot establish causation.
  • D. Equal conditionals are possible and need no error.
3. A simulation runs 40 independent trials and the event occurs in 9. What is the estimated probability, and what happens with more trials?

Answer: A. The estimate is $9/40=0.225$. More trials reduce the typical distance from the model probability, but an estimate never becomes exact by counting alone.

  • A. Correct: 9/40 and a stability statement about more trials.
  • B. No finite number of trials makes the estimate exact.
  • C. 9/40, not 0.09, and more trials do reduce variability.
  • D. The estimate is successes over trials, 9/40, not trials over 100.
4. Events $A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive with $P(A)=0.35$ and $P(B)=0.45$. Which statement is correct?

Answer: B. Mutually exclusive means $P(A\cap B)=0$, so $P(A\cup B)=0.35+0.45=0.80$. Positive-probability mutually exclusive events are not independent.

  • A. Mutual exclusivity implies dependence, not independence.
  • B. Correct: 0.80 with a dependence statement.
  • C. 0.1575 comes from $0.35\times0.45$, which applies to independent events.
  • D. The product rule applies to independent events; here the overlap is 0.
5. For events with $P(A)=0.40$, $P(B)=0.50$ and $P(A\cap B)=0.20$, what is $P(A\mid B)$, and are the events independent?

Answer: A. $P(A\mid B)=0.20/0.50=0.40=P(A)$, and the product test also passes, so the events are independent.

  • A. Correct: the conditional equals $P(A)$ and the product test passes.
  • B. $P(A\mid B)$ divides the joint by $P(B)$: it is 0.40.
  • C. Small probabilities do not preclude independence.
  • D. The conditional divides by $P(B)$, not by 1.
6. A discrete random variable has distribution $P(0)=0.20$, $P(1)=0.40$, $P(2)=0.30$, $P(3)=0.10$. What is $P(X\ge2)$?

Answer: B. $P(X\ge2)=P(2)+P(3)=0.30+0.10=0.40$; the complement of $P(X\le1)=0.60$.

  • A. This is $P(X=2)$ alone; the event includes 3.
  • B. Correct: 0.30 + 0.10.
  • C. 0.60 is the complement event $P(X\le1)$.
  • D. Total probability is 1; the event is not the whole space.
7. Ten independent trials each succeed with probability 0.60. Which expression gives $P(X=2)$?

Answer: A. Two successes and eight failures: $\binom{10}{2}(0.60)^{2}(0.40)^{8}\approx0.0106$.

  • A. Correct: choose 2 of 10, successes to the power 2.
  • B. Missing the combinatorial factor for which trials succeed.
  • C. Swapped the success and failure exponents.
  • D. Neither the factor nor the failure probability is right.
8. An approximately normal population has $\mu=50$, $\sigma=10$. Random samples of $n=25$ are taken. What is $P(\bar x>54)$, approximately?

Answer: A. $\sigma_{\bar x}=10/\sqrt{25}=2$, so $z=2$; the upper tail beyond $z=2$ is about 0.0228.

  • A. Correct: standardize with $\sigma/\sqrt{n}=2$.
  • B. Used $z=1$; the divisor must be $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$.
  • C. Mixed the tail direction with a two-tail share.
  • D. This is the left-tail area, not the requested direction.
综合自测

单元自测

1. 某公司 250 名员工按是否远程办公与是否使用公共交通分类。远程：公交 30 人、非公交 70 人；坐班：公交 45 人、非公交 105 人。$P(\text{公交}\mid\text{远程})$ 等于多少？

答案：B。 条件为远程，故除以远程合计：$30/100=0.30$。

  • A. 把单元格除以了总体合计，而非远程合计。
  • B. 正确：在远程组内，基数是 100。
  • C. 这是坐班组的条件比例；题目问远程组。
  • D. 分母混入了部分列合计。
2. 同一表格中，远程组使用公交比例为 0.30，坐班组也为 0.30。这说明什么？

答案：B。 条件分布相等说明样本中无相关证据；观察性分类不支持因果结论。

  • A. 相关要求条件比例不同。
  • B. 正确：0.30 与 0.30 相等，条件分布一致。
  • C. 分类数据不能建立因果。
  • D. 条件比例相等完全可能，无需错误解释。
3. 某模拟进行 40 次独立试验，事件发生 9 次。估计概率是多少？增加试验次数会怎样？

答案：A。 估计为 $9/40=0.225$。增加次数可减少与模型概率的典型距离，但估计不会因此变成精确值。

  • A. 正确：9/40，并说明增加次数使估计更稳定。
  • B. 任何有限次数都不能把估计变为精确值。
  • C. 应为 9/40；增加次数可减小波动。
  • D. 估计是成功次数除以试验次数，即 9/40，而不是试验次数除以 100。
4. 事件 $A$ 与 $B$ 互斥，$P(A)=0.35$，$P(B)=0.45$。哪项正确？

答案：B。 互斥意味着 $P(A\cap B)=0$，故 $P(A\cup B)=0.35+0.45=0.80$。正概率的互斥事件不独立。

  • A. 互斥意味着相关，不是独立。
  • B. 正确：0.80，且不独立。
  • C. 0.1575 来自乘法，那是独立事件的情形。
  • D. 乘法规则用于独立事件；此处重叠为 0。
5. $P(A)=0.40$、$P(B)=0.50$、$P(A\cap B)=0.20$。$P(A\mid B)$ 是多少？两事件是否独立？

答案：A。 $P(A\mid B)=0.20/0.50=0.40=P(A)$，乘法检验也通过，故两事件独立。

  • A. 正确：条件概率等于 $P(A)$，乘法检验通过。
  • B. $P(A\mid B)$ 用联合概率除以 $P(B)$，结果是 0.40。
  • C. 概率较小不影响独立性判断。
  • D. 条件概率的分母是 $P(B)$，不是 1。
6. 离散随机变量的分布为 $P(0)=0.20$、$P(1)=0.40$、$P(2)=0.30$、$P(3)=0.10$。$P(X\ge2)$ 是多少？

答案：B。 $P(X\ge2)=P(2)+P(3)=0.30+0.10=0.40$，即 $1-P(X\le1)=1-0.60$。

  • A. 这只是 $P(X=2)$，事件还包含 3。
  • B. 正确：0.30+0.10。
  • C. 0.60 是补事件 $P(X\le1)$。
  • D. 全样本概率为 1，该事件不是全集。
7. 10 次独立试验，每次成功概率 0.60。哪个表达式给出 $P(X=2)$？

答案：A。 2 次成功、8 次失败：$\binom{10}{2}(0.60)^{2}(0.40)^{8}\approx0.0106$。

  • A. 正确：从 10 次中选 2 次，成功概率的指数为 2。
  • B. 缺少选择哪些试验成功的组合因子。
  • C. 成功与失败的指数写反了。
  • D. 组合因子与失败概率都不对。
8. 近似正态的总体 $\mu=50$、$\sigma=10$，抽取 $n=25$ 的随机样本。$P(\bar x>54)$ 约是多少？

答案：A。 $\sigma_{\bar x}=10/\sqrt{25}=2$，故 $z=2$；$z=2$ 右侧尾部面积约为 0.0228。

  • A. 正确：用 $\sigma/\sqrt{n}=2$ 标准化。
  • B. 误用 $z=1$；分母必须是 $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$。
  • C. 与双侧面积混淆，方向也不对。
  • D. 这是左尾面积，与所求方向相反。
