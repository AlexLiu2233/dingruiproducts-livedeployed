Charge and the point-charge model电荷与点电荷模型

Charge is a fundamental property of matter, like mass, but it comes in two signs. It is a scalar: it has a sign and a magnitude but no direction.

电荷是物质的基本属性，和质量类似，但有两种符号。它是标量（scalar）：有正负和大小，没有方向。

Charge is quantized: the elementary charge $e = 1.60\times10^{-19}\;\text{C}$ is the smallest indivisible amount, an electron carries $-e$, a proton carries $+e$, and a neutron carries none. Any net charge you will meet is therefore $q = Ne$ for an integer $N$, with $N$ counting an excess or a deficit of electrons.

电荷是量子化的（quantized）：元电荷 $e = 1.60\times10^{-19}\;\text{C}$ 是最小的不可再分电荷量，电子带 $-e$，质子带 $+e$，中子不带电。因此你遇到的任何净电荷都等于 $q = Ne$（$N$ 为整数），$N$ 表示多出或缺少的电子数。

A coulomb is enormous: it is about $6.25\times10^{18}$ elementary charges, so everyday static charges are microcoulombs ($10^{-6}\;\text{C}$) or nanocoulombs ($10^{-9}\;\text{C}$).

1 库仑是很大的电荷量，约为 $6.25\times10^{18}$ 个元电荷，所以日常的静电荷只有微库仑（$10^{-6}\;\text{C}$）或纳库仑（$10^{-9}\;\text{C}$）量级。

A point charge is a model: the size of the charged object is negligible compared with the distances in the problem, so all its charge can be treated as sitting at one point.

点电荷（point charge）是一种模型：带电物体的尺寸与题中涉及的距离相比可以忽略，因此可以把它的全部电荷看作集中在一点。

A charged sphere of radius $2\;\text{cm}$ is a good point charge when other objects are meters away and a poor one when another object is $3\;\text{cm}$ from its surface. The model depends on the situation, not on the object.

半径 $2\;\text{cm}$ 的带电球，当其他物体在数米之外时是很好的点电荷，而当另一物体离其表面只有 $3\;\text{cm}$ 时就不是了。这个模型取决于具体情境，而不取决于物体本身。

Coulomb's law库仑定律

Experiment shows that the electrostatic force between two point charges is proportional to the magnitude of each charge and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them. The force acts along the line joining the charges.

实验表明，两个点电荷之间的静电力与每个电荷的大小成正比，与它们之间距离的平方成反比，方向沿两电荷的连线。

Coulomb's law (magnitude)库仑定律（大小）
$$F = k\,\frac{|q_1||q_2|}{r^2} = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\,\frac{|q_1||q_2|}{r^2}$$

$k = 1/(4\pi\epsilon_0) \approx 8.99\times10^{9}\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$ and $\epsilon_0 \approx 8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{C}^2/(\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2)$ is the permittivity of free space.

$k = 1/(4\pi\epsilon_0) \approx 8.99\times10^{9}\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$，$\epsilon_0 \approx 8.85\times10^{-12}\;\text{C}^2/(\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2)$ 为真空电容率。

The direction rule is a statement about signs. Charges of the same sign repel; charges of opposite sign attract. The two forces form a Newton's third law pair: equal magnitudes, opposite directions, on different objects, even when $|q_1| \neq |q_2|$.

方向规则其实是关于符号的陈述：同号电荷相斥，异号电荷相吸。这两个力构成一对牛顿第三定律的作用力与反作用力：大小相等、方向相反、作用在不同物体上，即使 $|q_1| \neq |q_2|$ 也是如此。

In vector form, with $\vec r_{12} = \vec r_1 - \vec r_2$ pointing from charge 2 to charge 1 and $\hat r_{12} = \vec r_{12}/r$ its unit vector, the force on charge 1 due to charge 2 is

用矢量形式表示，设 $\vec r_{12} = \vec r_1 - \vec r_2$ 由电荷 2 指向电荷 1，$\hat r_{12} = \vec r_{12}/r$ 为其单位矢量，则电荷 2 对电荷 1 的力为

Coulomb's law (vector form)库仑定律（矢量形式）
$$\vec F_{12} = k\,\frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}\,\hat r_{12}$$

Keep the signs of $q_1$ and $q_2$ here: a positive product points along $\hat r_{12}$ (repulsion), a negative product points opposite (attraction).

此处保留 $q_1$ 与 $q_2$ 的正负号：乘积为正则沿 $\hat r_{12}$ 方向（排斥），乘积为负则与之反向（吸引）。

The direction rule is a statement about signs. Charges of the same sign repel; charges of opposite sign attract. The force on each charge is directed along the line joining the two charges. The two forces form a Newton's third law pair: equal magnitudes, opposite directions, on different objects, even when $|q_1| \neq |q_2|$. Use the formula above with the magnitudes of the charges to find the size of the force, then decide the direction from the signs: away from the other charge for repulsion, toward it for attraction.

方向规则其实是关于符号的陈述：同号电荷相斥，异号电荷相吸。每个电荷所受的力都沿两电荷的连线。这两个力构成一对牛顿第三定律的作用力与反作用力：大小相等、方向相反、作用在不同物体上，即使 $|q_1| \neq |q_2|$ 也是如此。用上面的公式和电荷的绝对值求力的大小，再根据符号判断方向：排斥时背离另一个电荷，吸引时指向另一个电荷。

The inverse-square dependence gives quick proportional reasoning. Doubling the separation divides the force by $2^2 = 4$; tripling one charge multiplies the force by $3$; doubling both charges and the separation leaves the force unchanged.

平方反比关系便于快速作比例推理：距离加倍，力除以 $2^2 = 4$；一个电荷变为原来的 $3$ 倍时，力也变为 3 倍；两个电荷和距离都加倍，力不变。

Worked Example: Force between two charges例题：两个电荷之间的力

A charge $q_1 = +3.0\;\mu\text{C}$ is at $x = 0$ and a charge $q_2 = -5.0\;\mu\text{C}$ is at $x = 0.20\;\text{m}$. Find the force on $q_1$, then the force when the separation is doubled.

电荷 $q_1 = +3.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 位于 $x = 0$，电荷 $q_2 = -5.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 位于 $x = 0.20\;\text{m}$。求 $q_1$ 受到的力，再求距离加倍后的力。

Step 1: Decide the direction first第 1 步：先判断方向
The charges have opposite signs, so they attract: the force on $q_1$ points toward $q_2$, that is, in the $+x$ direction.两电荷异号，相互吸引：$q_1$ 受力指向 $q_2$，即沿 $+x$ 方向。
Step 2: Magnitude第 2 步：大小
Use magnitudes of the charges in Coulomb's law:代入电荷的绝对值使用库仑定律：
$$F = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(3.0\times10^{-6})(5.0\times10^{-6})}{(0.20)^2} = 3.4\;\text{N}$$
Step 3: Result第 3 步：结果
$\vec F_{1} = +3.4\;\text{N}\;\hat x$, and $q_2$ feels an equal $3.4\;\text{N}$ force in the $-x$ direction.$\vec F_{1} = +3.4\;\text{N}\;\hat x$，$q_2$ 受到大小同为 $3.4\;\text{N}$、沿 $-x$ 方向的力。
Step 4: Double the separation第 4 步：距离加倍
Because $F \propto 1/r^2$, the force becomes one quarter of its value:因为 $F \propto 1/r^2$，力变为原来的四分之一：
$$F' = \frac{3.4}{4} \approx 0.84\;\text{N}$$
Step 5: Why this works第 5 步：为什么这样做
Magnitudes come from the formula with absolute values; direction comes from the sign rule. Keeping them separate prevents sign mistakes.大小由取绝对值的公式给出，方向由符号规则给出。把两者分开处理可以避免符号错误。

Superposition and the four-charge boundary叠加原理与四电荷的范围

When several charges act on one charge, each pair interacts as if the others were absent, and the net force is the vector sum of the individual forces. Draw each force on a diagram first, resolve into components, add the components, and only then combine them into a magnitude and an angle.

当多个电荷作用在同一个电荷上时，每一对电荷的相互作用都和其他电荷不存在时一样，合力是各个力的矢量和（vector sum）。先画出每个力，分解成分量，把分量相加，最后再合成大小与角度。

AP Physics C: E&M asks you to calculate the electric force from four or fewer interacting charged objects; a larger collection is allowed only when it is highly symmetric, for example equal charges at the corners of a regular polygon, where components cancel by symmetry.

AP Physics C: E&M 只要求计算至多四个相互作用的带电物体之间的电力；更多的电荷只在高度对称时才会出现，例如正多边形各顶点上的等量电荷，其分量因对称而抵消。

(Fields of continuous charge distributions are still expected, and they are the subject of Topics 8.4 and 8.6.)

（连续电荷分布的电场仍在考查范围内，将在 8.4 和 8.6 专题中讨论。）

When several charges act on one charge, each pair interacts as if the others were absent, and the net force is the vector sum of the individual forces. Draw each force on a diagram first, resolve into components, add the components, and only then combine them into a magnitude and an angle. AP Physics 2 limits calculations of the electric force to four or fewer interacting charged objects; a larger collection is allowed only in situations of high symmetry, for example equal charges at the corners of a regular polygon, where components cancel by symmetry.

当多个电荷作用在同一个电荷上时，每一对电荷的相互作用都和其他电荷不存在时一样，合力是各个力的矢量和（vector sum）。先画出每个力，分解成分量，把分量相加，最后再合成大小与角度。AP Physics 2 只要求计算至多四个相互作用的带电物体之间的电力；更多的电荷只在高度对称的情形下才允许分析，例如正多边形各顶点上的等量电荷，其分量因对称而抵消。

Worked Example: Net force from two charges例题：两个电荷的合力

A charge $q = +1.0\;\mu\text{C}$ is at the origin. A charge $+3.0\;\mu\text{C}$ is at $(0.10\;\text{m},\,0)$ and a charge $-2.0\;\mu\text{C}$ is at $(0,\,0.20\;\text{m})$. Find the net force on $q$.

电荷 $q = +1.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 位于原点，电荷 $+3.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 位于 $(0.10\;\text{m},\,0)$，电荷 $-2.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 位于 $(0,\,0.20\;\text{m})$。求 $q$ 受到的合力。

Step 1: Force from the $+3.0\;\mu\text{C}$ charge第 1 步：来自 $+3.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 电荷的力
Like charges repel, so the force on $q$ points away from $(0.10,0)$, along $-x$:同号相斥，所以 $q$ 受力背离 $(0.10,0)$，沿 $-x$ 方向：
$$F_1 = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(1.0\times10^{-6})(3.0\times10^{-6})}{(0.10)^2} = 2.7\;\text{N}$$
Step 2: Force from the $-2.0\;\mu\text{C}$ charge第 2 步：来自 $-2.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 电荷的力
Opposite charges attract, so the force points toward $(0,0.20)$, along $+y$:异号相吸，所以受力指向 $(0,0.20)$，沿 $+y$ 方向：
$$F_2 = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(1.0\times10^{-6})(2.0\times10^{-6})}{(0.20)^2} = 0.45\;\text{N}$$
Step 3: Add the components第 3 步：分量相加
The forces are perpendicular, so the components are the forces themselves:两个力互相垂直，因此分量就是这两个力本身：
$$F_x = -2.7\;\text{N},\quad F_y = +0.45\;\text{N}$$
Step 4: Magnitude and angle第 4 步：大小与角度
Combine the components into a magnitude and a direction:把分量合成为大小与方向：
$$|\vec F| = \sqrt{(2.7)^2 + (0.45)^2} \approx 2.7\;\text{N},\qquad \tan\alpha = \frac{0.45}{2.7}\;\Rightarrow\;\alpha \approx 9.5^{\circ}\text{ above the }-x\text{ axis}$$
Step 5: Why this works第 5 步：为什么这样做
The two interactions are independent, so superposition applies; the small $+y$ component is the only thing the far charge changes.两个相互作用互不影响，所以可以叠加；较远的电荷只贡献了一个较小的 $+y$ 分量。
Worked Example: Four charges at the corners of a square例题：正方形四个顶点上的四个电荷

Four equal charges $q = +2.0\;\mu\text{C}$ sit at the corners of a square of side $a = 0.10\;\text{m}$. Find the net force on one of them.

四个相等的电荷 $q = +2.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 位于边长 $a = 0.10\;\text{m}$ 的正方形的四个顶点。求其中一个电荷受到的合力。

Step 1: Three forces on the corner charge第 1 步：角上电荷受到的三个力
Let $F_0 = kq^2/a^2$. The two adjacent charges each give $F_0$, one along each side direction (away from the neighbours). The opposite corner is at distance $\sqrt2\,a$ and gives $F_0/2$ along the diagonal, away from the center.令 $F_0 = kq^2/a^2$。两个相邻电荷各产生大小为 $F_0$ 的力，分别沿两条边的方向（背离相邻电荷）。对角的电荷距离为 $\sqrt2\,a$，产生沿对角线、背离中心、大小为 $F_0/2$ 的力。
Step 2: Evaluate $F_0$第 2 步：计算 $F_0$
$$F_0 = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(2.0\times10^{-6})^2}{(0.10)^2} = 3.6\;\text{N}$$
Step 3: Add along the diagonal第 3 步：沿对角线相加
The two side forces are perpendicular and equal, so their resultant is $\sqrt2\,F_0$ along the diagonal. All three forces then point the same way:两个边上的力互相垂直且相等，合力为沿对角线的 $\sqrt2\,F_0$。于是三个力的方向相同：
$$F = \sqrt2\,F_0 + \frac{F_0}{2} = F_0\left(\sqrt2 + \tfrac12\right) = 3.6\times1.91 \approx 6.9\;\text{N}$$
Step 4: Why this works第 4 步：为什么这样做
This is four interacting charges with a symmetric layout, so it sits inside the course boundary; by symmetry the net force must lie along the diagonal, which is why only one direction needs summing.这是四个电荷、排布对称的情形，在课程范围之内；由对称性可知合力必沿对角线，因此只需在一个方向上求和。

Electric force versus gravitational force电力与引力的比较

Both forces fall off as $1/r^2$, but they differ in two ways. Electrostatic forces can attract or repel because charge has two signs; gravitational forces between masses are always attractive. And between any two particles that have both mass and charge, the electric force is usually vastly larger than the gravitational force.

两种力都按 $1/r^2$ 衰减，但有两点不同。静电力可以吸引也可以排斥，因为电荷有两种符号；质量之间的引力总是吸引。而且对于同时有质量和电荷的两个粒子，电力通常远大于引力。

Gravity still dominates at large scales (planets, stars) because large objects are almost exactly electrically neutral: positive and negative charge cancel, while mass only adds.

在行星、恒星这样的大尺度上引力仍占主导，是因为大物体几乎完全电中性：正负电荷相互抵消，而质量只会累加。

Worked Example: Electric versus gravitational force例题：电力与引力

Compare the electric and gravitational forces between two protons separated by any distance $r$, and between a proton and an electron. Use $e = 1.60\times10^{-19}\;\text{C}$, $m_p = 1.67\times10^{-27}\;\text{kg}$, $m_e = 9.11\times10^{-31}\;\text{kg}$ and $G = 6.67\times10^{-11}\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$.

比较相距任意距离 $r$ 的两个质子之间，以及一个质子和一个电子之间的电力与引力。取 $e = 1.60\times10^{-19}\;\text{C}$，$m_p = 1.67\times10^{-27}\;\text{kg}$，$m_e = 9.11\times10^{-31}\;\text{kg}$，$G = 6.67\times10^{-11}\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$。

Step 1: Form the ratio第 1 步：写出比值
Both forces scale as $1/r^2$, so $r$ cancels in the ratio:两种力都与 $1/r^2$ 成正比，比值中 $r$ 被约去：
$$\frac{F_E}{F_G} = \frac{k e^2/r^2}{G m_p^2/r^2} = \frac{k e^2}{G m_p^2}$$
Step 2: Two protons第 2 步：两个质子

Permittivity, polarization, conductors and insulators电容率、极化、导体与绝缘体

The constant $\epsilon_0$ in Coulomb's law is the electric permittivity of free space. Materials also have a permittivity, and it differs from the free-space value.

库仑定律中的常数 $\epsilon_0$ 是真空电容率（electric permittivity of free space）。材料也有电容率，其值不同于真空值。

Permittivity measures how strongly a material polarizes in an applied electric field: the field slightly rearranges the electrons, separating positive and negative charge inside the material, and that induced separation partly opposes the applied field. The easier it is for electrons to change configuration, the larger the permittivity.

电容率衡量材料在外加电场中被极化（polarize）的程度：电场使电子发生轻微重排，在材料内部分离出正负电荷，这种感应分离会部分抵消外加电场。电子越容易改变排布，电容率就越大。

For two point charges embedded in a uniform material, $\epsilon_0$ in Coulomb's law is replaced by the material's permittivity $\epsilon$, which reduces the force.

对于放在均匀材料中的两个点电荷，库仑定律中的 $\epsilon_0$ 要换成该材料的电容率 $\epsilon$，使力减小。

The constant $\epsilon_0$ in Coulomb's law is the electric permittivity of free space: free space has a constant value of permittivity that appears in physical relationships. The permittivity of a material or medium measures the degree to which it is polarized in the presence of an electric field. Polarization can be modeled as the induced rearrangement of electrons by an external electric field, which separates positive and negative charge within the material. The permittivity of matter differs from the free-space value because of the matter's composition and arrangement: in a given material it is determined by how easily the electrons can change configuration, and the easier that is, the larger the permittivity.

库仑定律中的常数 $\epsilon_0$ 是真空电容率（electric permittivity of free space）：真空的电容率是一个恒定值，会出现在各种物理关系中。材料或介质的电容率衡量它在电场中被极化（polarize）的程度。极化可以建模为外电场引起的电子重新排布，使材料内部的正、负电荷分离。物质的电容率与真空值不同，这取决于物质的组成与排列：在给定材料中，它由电子改变排布的难易程度决定，电子越容易改变排布，电容率就越大。

最后，你在力学中画过的几乎所有力，例如支持力、摩擦力和张力，本质上都是电力。桌子和书中的原子通过各自的电子相互排斥；由于粒子间的相互作用数量极大，人们便用一个简单模型的接触力（contact force）来代替它们的总和。

Conservation of charge and the system boundary电荷守恒与系统边界

Conservation of charge: the net charge of a system is constant unless charge is transferred between the system and its surroundings. Charge is never created or destroyed in these processes; it moves.

电荷守恒（conservation of charge）：除非系统与外界之间发生电荷转移，否则系统的净电荷保持不变。在这些过程中电荷既不会产生也不会消失，只是发生了移动。

In practice charging a system almost always means moving electrons to or from it, because the protons are bound in nuclei. Gaining electrons gives a negative net charge; losing electrons gives a positive net charge.

实际上，让系统带电几乎总是意味着让电子流入或流出，因为质子束缚在原子核中。得到电子则净电荷为负；失去电子则净电荷为正。

The phrase “unless charge is transferred” means you must choose a system. If two spheres touch, each sphere's net charge can change, but the net charge of the pair (the system containing both) cannot, as long as nothing else is connected.

“除非发生电荷转移”这句话意味着你必须先选定一个系统。两个球接触时，每个球的净电荷都可能改变，但只要没有别的物体相连，由两球组成的系统的净电荷就不会变。

The same bookkeeping works for friction: a cloth and a rod rubbed together exchange electrons, one ending up positive and the other negative by exactly the same amount.

摩擦起电也遵循同样的记账方式：布与棒相互摩擦时交换电子，一个带正电，另一个带负电，电荷量恰好相等。

Conductors, insulators and induced charge separation导体、绝缘体与感应电荷分离

Bring a charged rod near a neutral object without touching it. The object's net charge is still zero, yet it is attracted. The reason is induced charge separation, also called polarization: the electric force from the rod shifts charge within the object, and the object's net charge stays zero.

把一根带电棒靠近一个中性物体但不接触。物体的净电荷仍为零，却会被吸引。原因是感应电荷分离（induced charge separation），也叫极化：棒的电力使物体内部的电荷发生移动，而物体的净电荷仍为零。

In a conductor, free electrons move over large distances, so the near side of a neutral metal sphere gains charge of the opposite sign to the rod and the far side gains the same sign.

在导体中，自由电子可以移动很远，所以中性金属球靠近棒的一侧会出现与棒异号的电荷，远离的一侧出现同号电荷。

In an insulator, electrons cannot leave their atoms; each atom or molecule distorts slightly, leaving a thin layer of induced charge on the surface.

在绝缘体中，电子无法离开原子，每个原子或分子只发生轻微形变，在表面留下一层很薄的感应电荷。

Why is the neutral object attracted? The opposite-sign induced charge is closer to the rod than the same-sign induced charge is. Because the electric force falls with distance, the attraction is stronger than the repulsion, and the net force on the object is toward the rod. This holds whichever sign the rod has.

为什么中性物体会被吸引？异号的感应电荷离棒更近，同号的感应电荷离棒更远。由于电力随距离减小，吸引力大于排斥力，物体受到的合力指向棒。无论棒带哪种电荷，结论都一样。

Three ways to charge an object三种起电方式

Friction. Two materials are rubbed together and electrons transfer from one to the other; the two end with equal and opposite charges.

摩擦起电。两种材料相互摩擦，电子从一个转移到另一个，两者最终带等量异号电荷。

Contact (conduction). A charged object touches another, and electrons flow between them until the charge is shared; if the total charge is not zero, the two end with charges of the same sign as that total.

接触起电（传导）。带电物体接触另一个物体，电子在两者之间流动直到电荷被分配；若总电荷不为零，两者最终带与总电荷同号的电荷。

For two identical conducting spheres that touch and separate, the total charge is shared equally, so each ends with half of the total, whatever the initial charges were. Induction. An object is charged without ever touching the charged object, using grounding.

两个相同的导体球接触后分开，总电荷被平分，无论开始时各带多少电荷，每个球最终都得到总电荷的一半。感应起电。借助接地，使物体在从未接触带电物体的情况下带电。

Grounding means electrically connecting an object to a much larger, approximately neutral system, usually Earth.

接地（grounding）是指把物体与一个大得多、近似为中性的系统（通常是地球）用导线连接。

Earth can supply or accept as many electrons as needed without a noticeable change in its own charge. A grounding connection is how electrons enter or leave the object. The steps for charging a neutral metal sphere on an insulating stand by induction with a negative rod are:

地球可以提供或接收任意多的电子，而自身电荷没有明显变化。接地连接就是电子进出物体的通道。用带负电的棒通过感应给绝缘支架上的中性金属球充电，步骤如下：

  1. Bring the negative rod near the sphere without touching: electrons in the sphere move away from the rod, leaving the near side positive and the far side negative.
  2. Connect the sphere to ground while the rod stays in place: electrons flow from the sphere into the ground, away from the rod.
  3. Remove the ground connection while the rod stays in place: the sphere is now isolated with a net positive charge.
  4. Remove the rod: the positive charge spreads over the conducting sphere.
  1. 在不接触的情况下把负电棒靠近球：球内电子远离棒，靠近棒的一侧带正电，远离的一侧带负电。
  2. 保持棒的位置不变，把球接地：电子从球流入大地，远离棒。
  3. 保持棒的位置不变，断开接地连接：球此时成为孤立的、带净正电荷的物体。
  4. 移走棒：正电荷分布到整个导体球上。

The sphere ends with the sign opposite to the rod, and the rod loses no charge. The order matters: if the ground is removed after the rod is taken away, the electrons return from Earth and the sphere is neutral again.

球最终带的电荷与棒异号，而棒并未失去电荷。顺序很重要：如果先移走棒再断开接地，电子会从地球返回，球又变回中性。

Definition of the electric field电场的定义

Place a small test charge $q$ at a point and measure the electric force $\vec F$ on it. The electric field at that point is the ratio of the force to the test charge:

在某点放一个小的试探电荷（test charge）$q$，测量它所受的电力 $\vec F$。该点的电场（electric field）定义为力与试探电荷之比：

Electric field电场
$$\vec E = \frac{\vec F}{q}$$

Units: $\text{N/C}$. A vector quantity. Equivalently, $\vec F = q\vec E$.

单位：$\text{N/C}$。是矢量。等价地，$\vec F = q\vec E$。

A test charge is a point charge small enough in magnitude that it does not significantly disturb the sources producing the field; otherwise it would push charges around on nearby conductors and change the very field being measured.

试探电荷是电量足够小的点电荷，以至于不会明显扰动产生电场的源电荷；否则它会推动附近导体上的电荷，改变所要测量的电场本身。

The field points away from isolated positive charges and toward isolated negative charges. A positive charge feels a force in the same direction as $\vec E$; a negative charge feels a force in the direction opposite to $\vec E$.

电场背离孤立的正电荷、指向孤立的负电荷。正电荷受力与 $\vec E$ 同向；负电荷受力与 $\vec E$ 反向。

Field of a point charge and superposition点电荷的电场与叠加

Dividing Coulomb's law by the test charge gives the field of a point charge $Q$ at distance $r$, directed along the line from the source:

把库仑定律除以试探电荷，得到电荷 $Q$ 在距离 $r$ 处的电场，方向沿源电荷出发的连线：

Field of a point charge点电荷的电场
$$\vec E = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{Q}{r^2}\,\hat r = k\,\frac{Q}{r^2}\,\hat r$$

$\hat r$ points from the source to the field point. For $Q>0$ the field points away from the source, and for $Q<0$ toward it.

$\hat r$ 由源电荷指向场点。$Q>0$ 时电场背离源电荷，$Q<0$ 时指向源电荷。

The net electric field at a location is the vector sum of the fields from each nearby charged object. The strategy is the same as for forces: sketch the direction of each contribution at the field point, resolve into components, add, and combine. Because only one source is used per term, the inverse-square form is used for each source separately.

某位置的合电场是附近各带电物体产生的电场的矢量和。方法与求力时相同：先在场点画出每个贡献的方向，分解成分量，相加，再合成。每一项只对应一个源电荷，因此分别对每个源电荷使用平方反比形式。

Worked Example: Field and force from a point charge例题：点电荷的电场与受力

A point charge $Q = +5.0\;\text{nC}$ is at the origin. Find the electric field at $x = 0.30\;\text{m}$, and the force on a $-2.0\;\mu\text{C}$ charge placed there.

点电荷 $Q = +5.0\;\text{nC}$ 位于原点。求 $x = 0.30\;\text{m}$ 处的电场，以及放在该处的 $-2.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 电荷所受的力。

Step 1: Field第 1 步：电场
The source is positive, so the field points away from it, along $+x$:源电荷为正，电场背离它，沿 $+x$ 方向：
$$E = \frac{kQ}{r^2} = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(5.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.30)^2} = 5.0\times10^{2}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 2: Force on the negative charge第 2 步：负电荷受力
The magnitude is $F = |q|E$, and a negative charge is pushed opposite to the field:大小为 $F = |q|E$，负电荷受力与电场方向相反：
$$F = (2.0\times10^{-6})(5.0\times10^{2}) = 1.0\times10^{-3}\;\text{N},\quad\text{toward the source}$$
Step 3: Why this works第 3 步：为什么这样做
The field is a property of the location; changing the charge placed there changes only the force.电场是该位置的性质；改变放在那里的电荷只会改变力。

Zero-field points电场为零的位置

A net field can be zero at a point where the contributions cancel. Between two charges of the same sign the fields point in opposite directions, so a zero point can lie between them.

在各个贡献相互抵消的点，合电场可以为零。两个同号电荷之间，电场方向相反，所以零点可以在两者之间。

For two charges of opposite sign, the fields point the same way between the charges, so the zero point lies outside the pair, on the side of the charge with the smaller magnitude, where the larger charge's field is weakened by distance. Set the two magnitudes equal and solve for the distance.

两个异号电荷之间，电场方向相同，所以零点在这对电荷之外，位于电量较小的电荷一侧，那里较大电荷的电场因距离更远而被削弱。令两个大小相等，解出距离即可。

Worked Example: Locating a zero-field point例题：确定电场为零的位置

A charge $+4.0\;\mu\text{C}$ is at $x = 0$ and a charge $-1.0\;\mu\text{C}$ is at $x = 0.60\;\text{m}$. Find the point on the $x$ axis where the net electric field is zero.

电荷 $+4.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 位于 $x = 0$，电荷 $-1.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 位于 $x = 0.60\;\text{m}$。求 $x$ 轴上合电场为零的点。

Step 1: Choose the region第 1 步：选择区域
Between the charges both fields point in $+x$ (away from $+4.0$, toward $-1.0$), so they cannot cancel. To the left, the larger charge is nearer, so it wins. The zero must lie to the right of $-1.0\;\mu\text{C}$, a distance $d$ beyond it.两电荷之间两个电场都沿 $+x$（背离 $+4.0$，指向 $-1.0$），不可能抵消。在左侧，较大电荷更近，起主导作用。因此零点必在 $-1.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 的右侧、距它 $d$ 处。
Step 2: Equate magnitudes第 2 步：令大小相等
At that point the $+4.0\;\mu\text{C}$ field points $+x$ and the $-1.0\;\mu\text{C}$ field points $-x$ (toward it):在该点，$+4.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 的电场沿 $+x$，$-1.0\;\mu\text{C}$ 的电场沿 $-x$（指向它）：
$$\frac{k(4.0)}{(0.60+d)^2} = \frac{k(1.0)}{d^2}\;\Rightarrow\;\frac{2}{0.60+d} = \frac{1}{d}\;\Rightarrow\; d = 0.60\;\text{m}$$
Step 3: Result第 3 步：结果
The zero-field point is at $x = 0.60 + 0.60 = 1.20\;\text{m}$. Check: $4.0/(1.20)^2 = 1.0/(0.60)^2$.电场为零的点在 $x = 0.60 + 0.60 = 1.20\;\text{m}$。检验：$4.0/(1.20)^2 = 1.0/(0.60)^2$。
Step 4: Why this works第 4 步：为什么这样做
Cancelling requires opposite directions and equal magnitudes; the direction analysis picks the region, and the equal-magnitude equation fixes the position.抵消需要方向相反且大小相等；方向分析确定区域，大小相等的方程确定位置。
Worked Example: Field on the perpendicular bisector of two charges例题：两个电荷中垂线上的电场

Two charges of $+3.0\;\text{nC}$ sit at $(\pm0.040\;\text{m},\,0)$. Find the net electric field at the point $(0,\,0.030\;\text{m})$.

两个 $+3.0\;\text{nC}$ 的电荷位于 $(\pm0.040\;\text{m},\,0)$。求点 $(0,\,0.030\;\text{m})$ 处的合电场。

Step 1: Distance and angle第 1 步：距离与角度
Each charge is $r = \sqrt{0.040^2 + 0.030^2} = 0.050\;\text{m}$ from the field point, and the line to it makes an angle with the $y$ axis whose cosine is $0.030/0.050 = 0.60$.每个电荷到场点的距离为 $r = \sqrt{0.040^2 + 0.030^2} = 0.050\;\text{m}$，连线与 $y$ 轴夹角的余弦为 $0.030/0.050 = 0.60$。
Step 2: One charge's field第 2 步：单个电荷的电场
$$E_1 = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(3.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.050)^2} = 1.08\times10^{4}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 3: Add components第 3 步：分量相加
The $x$ components cancel by symmetry. The $y$ components add, each away from its source:$x$ 分量因对称而抵消，$y$ 分量相加，各自背离其源电荷：
$$E_y = 2E_1\cos\theta = 2(1.08\times10^{4})(0.60) = 1.3\times10^{4}\;\text{N/C}\;\text{along}\;+y$$
Step 4: General form第 4 步：一般形式
For two charges at $(\pm a, 0)$, each of charge $q$, the same steps give the general result for a point $(0,y)$:对位于 $(\pm a, 0)$、电荷均为 $q$ 的两个电荷，同样的步骤给出点 $(0,y)$ 处的一般结果：
$$E_y = \frac{2kqy}{(a^2+y^2)^{3/2}}$$

Representing electric fields电场的表示方法

A vector field map draws an arrow at many points: the arrow's direction is the field direction there and its length shows the magnitude. Field-line diagrams are simplified models of such maps.

矢量场图（vector field map）在许多点上画箭头：箭头方向是该处的电场方向，长度表示大小。电场线图（field-line diagram）是这类场图的简化模型。

Their rules are: lines begin on positive charge (or at infinity) and end on negative charge (or at infinity); the tangent to a line at a point gives the field direction there; lines never cross, because the field has one direction at each point.

规则如下：电场线从正电荷（或无穷远）出发，终止于负电荷（或无穷远）；某点处电场线的切线方向就是该点的电场方向；电场线不会相交，因为每一点的电场只有一个方向。

The density of lines (the number per area perpendicular to the lines) shows the relative magnitude, so where the lines crowd together the field is strong. A line diagram does not give magnitudes by itself, only comparisons within the same diagram.

电场线的疏密（单位垂直面积上的线数）表示场强的相对大小，线越密的地方场强越大。电场线图本身不给出大小，只能在同一幅图内做比较。

To read a field-line diagram, compare the spacing of the lines. Where the lines are drawn closer together the field is stronger, and where they spread apart the field is weaker. Near a small charged sphere the lines are crowded and the field is strong; far from it the same lines are far apart and the field is weak. The field direction at a point is along the tangent to the line there: away from positive charge and toward negative charge. A vector field map carries the same information with arrow direction and arrow length.

阅读电场线图时，要比较电场线的疏密。电场线画得越靠近的地方场强越大，电场线越稀疏的地方场强越小。在带电小球附近，电场线密集、场强大；远离小球处，同样的电场线相距很远、场强小。某点的电场方向沿该处电场线的切线：背离正电荷、指向负电荷。矢量场图则用箭头的方向和长度表达同样的信息。

Fields of conductors and insulators导体与绝缘体的电场

In a conductor in electrostatic equilibrium the charge carriers have stopped moving. That can only happen if the net force on each carrier is zero, so the electric field inside the conductor is zero, and any excess charge sits on the surface.

处于静电平衡的导体（conductor）中，电荷载流子已停止运动。这只可能在每个载流子所受合力为零时出现，所以导体内部的电场为零，多余的电荷分布在表面。

At the surface the field is perpendicular to the surface: a parallel component would push surface charge along the conductor.

在表面处电场垂直于表面：若有平行分量，就会推动表面电荷沿导体移动。

For an isolated sphere with a spherically symmetric charge distribution, the field outside equals the field of a point charge with the same net charge located at the center (Topic 8.6 proves this with Gauss's law).

对于电荷呈球对称分布的孤立球体，球外的电场等于位于球心、带同样净电荷的点电荷的电场（8.6 专题将用高斯定律证明这一点）。

In a conductor in electrostatic equilibrium the charge carriers have stopped moving. That can only happen if the net force on each carrier is zero, so the electric field inside the conductor is zero, and any excess charge sits on the surface. At the surface the field is perpendicular to the surface: a parallel component would push surface charge along the conductor. For an isolated sphere with a spherically symmetric charge distribution, the field outside equals the field of a point charge with the same net charge located at the center.

处于静电平衡的导体（conductor）中，电荷载流子已停止运动。这只可能在每个载流子所受合力为零时出现，所以导体内部的电场为零，多余的电荷分布在表面。在表面处电场垂直于表面：若有平行分量，就会推动表面电荷沿导体移动。对于电荷呈球对称分布的孤立球体，球外的电场等于位于球心、带同样净电荷的点电荷的电场。

An insulator is different. Its charge cannot move freely, so excess charge on an insulator can be spread throughout the interior as well as on the surface, and the electric field inside an insulator can be nonzero.

绝缘体（insulator）则不同。它的电荷不能自由移动，因此绝缘体上多余的电荷既可以分布在表面，也可以分布在整个内部，绝缘体内部的电场可以不为零。

Scope. AP Physics 2 limits electric-field calculations to four or fewer charged objects; an analysis with more charges is allowed only in situations of high symmetry. Electric fields within insulators are analyzed only qualitatively.

范围。AP Physics 2 只要求计算至多四个带电物体产生的电场；电荷更多时，只在高度对称的情形下才允许分析。对绝缘体内部的电场只要求作定性分析（qualitative）。

Worked Example: Motion of a charge in a uniform field例题：电荷在匀强电场中的运动

An electron is released from rest in a uniform field of magnitude $2.0\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$. Find its acceleration, its speed after moving $0.020\;\text{m}$, and compare the electric force with its weight. Use $m_e = 9.11\times10^{-31}\;\text{kg}$ and $e = 1.60\times10^{-19}\;\text{C}$.

常见错误：混淆电场与力 $\vec E$ 是某点的电场，与试探电荷无关；$\vec F = q\vec E$ 才与之有关。不要因为力指向源电荷就说负电荷处的电场指向源电荷：电场方向由源电荷决定，只有力的方向随 $q$ 的符号翻转。

Setting up the integral建立积分

Field of a continuous distribution连续分布的电场
$$\vec E = \int d\vec E = \int \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\,\frac{dq}{r^2}\,\hat r$$

Here $\hat r$ points from the element $dq$ to the field point. Use $dq = \lambda\,d\ell$ (line), $\sigma\,dA$ (surface) or $\rho\,dV$ (volume).

这里 $\hat r$ 由电荷元 $dq$ 指向场点。用 $dq = \lambda\,d\ell$（线）、$\sigma\,dA$（面）或 $\rho\,dV$（体）。

  1. Draw the distribution, the field point and a representative element $dq$ at a general position.
  2. Write $dq$ in terms of one integration variable (a length $x$ or an angle $\theta$) and the density.
  3. Write $r$ and the direction of $d\vec E$ from the geometry; draw $d\vec E$ on the diagram.
  4. Use symmetry: pair elements so perpendicular components cancel, and keep only the components that add.
  5. Integrate over the whole distribution with the correct bounds, then check units and limiting cases.
  1. 画出电荷分布、场点以及处于一般位置的典型电荷元 $dq$。
  2. 用一个积分变量（长度 $x$ 或角度 $\theta$）和密度表示 $dq$。
  3. 根据几何关系写出 $r$ 和 $d\vec E$ 的方向，并在图上画出 $d\vec E$。
  4. 利用对称性：把电荷元成对考虑，使垂直分量抵消，只保留相加的分量。
  5. 在整个分布上用正确的积分限积分，然后检查单位和极限情形。

A thin ring on its axis细圆环轴线上的电场

Take a ring of radius $a$ and total charge $Q$ with its axis along $x$, and a field point $P$ at distance $x$ from the center. Every element $dq$ is the same distance $r = \sqrt{a^2 + x^2}$ from $P$, so each produces $dE = k\,dq/r^2$.

取半径为 $a$、总电荷为 $Q$ 的圆环，轴线沿 $x$ 方向，场点 $P$ 到圆心的距离为 $x$。每个电荷元 $dq$ 到 $P$ 的距离都是 $r = \sqrt{a^2 + x^2}$，所以每个产生的电场为 $dE = k\,dq/r^2$。

The component of $d\vec E$ perpendicular to the axis cancels with the element on the opposite side of the ring. The component along the axis is $dE\cos\alpha$, where $\cos\alpha = x/r$ is the same for all elements:

$d\vec E$ 垂直于轴线的分量与圆环对侧的电荷元抵消。沿轴线的分量是 $dE\cos\alpha$，其中 $\cos\alpha = x/r$ 对所有电荷元都相同：

Ring of charge on its axis圆环轴线上的电场
$$E_x = \int \frac{k\,dq}{r^2}\,\frac{x}{r} = \frac{kx}{(a^2+x^2)^{3/2}}\int dq = \frac{kQx}{(a^2+x^2)^{3/2}}$$

Directed along the axis, away from the ring for $Q>0$. It is zero at the center ($x=0$), approaches $kQ/x^2$ for $x \gg a$, and has a maximum at $x = a/\sqrt2$ where $E_{\max} = \dfrac{2}{3\sqrt3}\dfrac{kQ}{a^2} \approx 0.385\,\dfrac{kQ}{a^2}$.

沿轴线方向，$Q>0$ 时背离圆环。在圆心（$x=0$）处为零，当 $x \gg a$ 时趋近 $kQ/x^2$，在 $x = a/\sqrt2$ 处取最大值 $E_{\max} = \dfrac{2}{3\sqrt3}\dfrac{kQ}{a^2} \approx 0.385\,\dfrac{kQ}{a^2}$。

To find the maximum, set $dE/dx = 0$: differentiating $x(a^2+x^2)^{-3/2}$ gives $(a^2+x^2) - 3x^2 = 0$, so $x = a/\sqrt2$. The graph of $E$ against $x$ is an odd function: it starts at zero, rises to a maximum at $x = a/\sqrt2$, then decays as $1/x^2$; on the negative side the field points the opposite way.

求最大值时令 $dE/dx = 0$：对 $x(a^2+x^2)^{-3/2}$ 求导得 $(a^2+x^2) - 3x^2 = 0$，所以 $x = a/\sqrt2$。$E$ 随 $x$ 变化的图像是奇函数：从零开始，在 $x = a/\sqrt2$ 处升到最大，然后按 $1/x^2$ 衰减；在负半轴电场方向相反。

Worked Example: Ring of charge: derive and evaluate例题：带电圆环：推导并计算

A thin ring of radius $a = 0.10\;\text{m}$ carries uniform charge $Q = +6.0\;\mu\text{C}$. Find the electric field on the axis at $x = 0.20\;\text{m}$ from the center, and compare it with the value obtained by treating the ring as a point charge at its center.

半径 $a = 0.10\;\text{m}$ 的细圆环均匀带电 $Q = +6.0\;\mu\text{C}$。求轴线上距圆心 $x = 0.20\;\text{m}$ 处的电场，并与把圆环当作位于圆心的点电荷所得结果比较。

Step 1: Element and symmetry第 1 步：电荷元与对称性
Each element $dq$ is at $r = \sqrt{a^2+x^2}$; perpendicular components cancel in pairs, so only $dE\cos\alpha$ with $\cos\alpha = x/r$ survives.每个电荷元 $dq$ 到场点的距离为 $r = \sqrt{a^2+x^2}$；垂直分量成对抵消，只剩 $dE\cos\alpha$，其中 $\cos\alpha = x/r$。
Step 2: Integrate第 2 步：积分
Because $r$ and $\cos\alpha$ are constants, $\int dq = Q$:因为 $r$ 与 $\cos\alpha$ 都是常量，且 $\int dq = Q$：
$$E_x = \frac{kQx}{(a^2+x^2)^{3/2}}$$
Step 3: Evaluate第 3 步：代入数值
With $a^2 + x^2 = 0.010 + 0.040 = 0.050\;\text{m}^2$ and $(0.050)^{3/2} = 0.01118\;\text{m}^3$:由 $a^2 + x^2 = 0.010 + 0.040 = 0.050\;\text{m}^2$ 和 $(0.050)^{3/2} = 0.01118\;\text{m}^3$：
$$E_x = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(6.0\times10^{-6})(0.20)}{0.01118} = 9.6\times10^{5}\;\text{N/C},\;\text{along }+x$$
Step 4: Compare with a point charge第 4 步：与点电荷比较
$$\frac{kQ}{x^2} = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(6.0\times10^{-6})}{(0.20)^2} = 1.35\times10^{6}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 5: Why this works第 5 步：为什么这样做
The point-charge value is about $40\%$ too large at $x = 2a$, because the elements are not all on the axis; it becomes accurate only for $x \gg a$.在 $x = 2a$ 处，点电荷的结果偏大约 $40\%$，因为各电荷元并不都在轴线上；只有当 $x \gg a$ 时它才准确。

A finite line charge有限长线电荷

Take a straight wire of length $L$ with total charge $Q$ and uniform linear charge density $\lambda = Q/L$.

取一根长为 $L$、总电荷为 $Q$、线电荷密度均匀为 $\lambda = Q/L$ 的直导线。

On the perpendicular bisector: place the wire on the $x$ axis from $-L/2$ to $L/2$ and the field point at $(0,\,y)$. An element $dq = \lambda\,dx$ at position $x$ is at distance $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$. The $x$ components cancel between elements at $+x$ and $-x$; the $y$ component of each is $dE\,(y/r)$:

在中垂线上：把导线放在 $x$ 轴上，从 $-L/2$ 到 $L/2$，场点在 $(0,\,y)$。位置 $x$ 处的电荷元 $dq = \lambda\,dx$ 到场点的距离为 $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$。位于 $+x$ 和 $-x$ 的电荷元的 $x$ 分量相互抵消；每个电荷元的 $y$ 分量为 $dE\,(y/r)$：

Finite line charge, perpendicular bisector有限长线电荷，中垂线上
$$E_y = \int_{-L/2}^{L/2} \frac{k\lambda\,y\,dx}{(x^2+y^2)^{3/2}} = \frac{k\lambda\,y\,x}{y^2\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\Bigg|_{-L/2}^{L/2} = \frac{kQ}{y\sqrt{y^2 + L^2/4}}$$

Limits: for $y \gg L$ it becomes $kQ/y^2$ (point charge); for $L \to \infty$ at fixed $\lambda$ it becomes $E = 2k\lambda/y = \lambda/(2\pi\epsilon_0 y)$, the field of an infinitely long uniformly charged wire.

极限：当 $y \gg L$ 时变为 $kQ/y^2$（点电荷）；当 $L \to \infty$ 而 $\lambda$ 不变时变为 $E = 2k\lambda/y = \lambda/(2\pi\epsilon_0 y)$，即无限长均匀带电导线的电场。

The integral used here is $\int dx/(x^2+y^2)^{3/2} = x/\big(y^2\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\big)$, which you can verify by differentiating. The infinite-wire result $E = 2k\lambda/r$ points radially away from a positive wire and falls as $1/r$, not $1/r^2$.

这里用到的积分是 $\int dx/(x^2+y^2)^{3/2} = x/\big(y^2\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\big)$，可以通过求导验证。无限长导线的结果 $E = 2k\lambda/r$ 在正电荷导线外沿径向向外，随 $1/r$ 而不是 $1/r^2$ 减小。

For an infinitely long uniformly charged cylinder, the field outside at a distance $r$ from the central axis is the same as that of a line charge on the axis carrying the cylinder's charge per unit length (Topic 8.6 shows why).

对于无限长均匀带电圆柱，距中心轴线 $r$ 处的外部电场，等于位于轴线上、带有该圆柱单位长度电荷的线电荷的电场（8.6 专题将说明原因）。

Step 2: Direction第 2 步：方向

Step 3: Compare with the infinite wire第 3 步：与无限长导线比较
The charge density is $\lambda = Q/L = 1.0\times10^{-8}\;\text{C/m}$, so线电荷密度为 $\lambda = Q/L = 1.0\times10^{-8}\;\text{C/m}$，所以
$$\frac{2k\lambda}{y} = \frac{2(8.99\times10^{9})(1.0\times10^{-8})}{0.30} = 6.0\times10^{2}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 4: Why this works第 4 步：为什么这样做
The infinite-wire formula overestimates by about $25\%$ here because the rod ends are not far compared with $y$.这里无限长导线公式偏大约 $25\%$，因为与 $y$ 相比，棒的端点并不算远。

At a collinear point: place the rod along the $x$ axis from $x=0$ to $x=L$, and a field point on the axis a distance $d$ beyond the end, at $x = L + d$. Now every element's field points along the axis and no component cancels, so the integral adds magnitudes. An element $dq = \lambda\,dx$ at position $x$ is at distance $L + d - x$:

在共线点：把棒放在 $x$ 轴上，从 $x=0$ 到 $x=L$，场点在轴上、棒端点外距离 $d$ 处，即 $x = L + d$。此时每个电荷元的电场都沿轴线方向，没有分量抵消，积分只需把大小相加。位置 $x$ 处的电荷元 $dq = \lambda\,dx$ 到场点的距离为 $L + d - x$：

Finite line charge, collinear point有限长线电荷，共线点
$$E = \int_0^L \frac{k\lambda\,dx}{(L+d-x)^2} = k\lambda\left[\frac{1}{L+d-x}\right]_0^L = k\lambda\left(\frac1d - \frac1{L+d}\right) = \frac{kQ}{d\,(L+d)}$$

Directed along the axis, away from the rod for $Q>0$. For $d \gg L$ it becomes $kQ/d^2$.

沿轴线方向，$Q>0$ 时背离棒。当 $d \gg L$ 时变为 $kQ/d^2$。

Worked Example: Rod: field at a collinear point例题：带电棒：共线点处的电场

$$E = \frac{kQ}{d(L+d)} = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(5.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.20)(0.70)} = 3.2\times10^{2}\;\text{N/C}$$

$$\frac{kQ}{(0.45)^2} = 2.2\times10^{2}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 3: Why this works第 3 步：为什么这样做
The nearer half of the rod contributes disproportionately because of the $1/r^2$ weighting, so the point-charge estimate is too small.由于 $1/r^2$ 的权重，棒较近的一半贡献更大，所以点电荷估算偏小。

A semicircular arc at its center半圆弧圆心处的电场

Let a semicircular arc of radius $R$ and total charge $Q$ be centered at the origin, running over polar angles $0 \le \theta \le \pi$, so $\lambda = Q/(\pi R)$. An element has length $R\,d\theta$, so $dq = \lambda R\,d\theta$, and every element is exactly $R$ from the center.

设半径为 $R$、总电荷为 $Q$ 的半圆弧以原点为圆心，极角范围 $0 \le \theta \le \pi$，于是 $\lambda = Q/(\pi R)$。电荷元的长度为 $R\,d\theta$，所以 $dq = \lambda R\,d\theta$，每个电荷元到圆心的距离恰好都是 $R$。

For a positive arc, the field at the center from $dq$ points directly away from $dq$, that is, along $-(\cos\theta\,\hat x + \sin\theta\,\hat y)$. By symmetry about the $y$ axis the $x$ components cancel and the $y$ components add:

对于带正电的圆弧，$dq$ 在圆心产生的电场直接背离 $dq$，即沿 $-(\cos\theta\,\hat x + \sin\theta\,\hat y)$ 方向。由关于 $y$ 轴的对称性，$x$ 分量抵消，$y$ 分量相加：

Semicircular arc at its center半圆弧圆心处的电场
$$E_y = -\int_0^{\pi} \frac{k\lambda R\,d\theta}{R^2}\sin\theta = -\frac{k\lambda}{R}\Big[-\cos\theta\Big]_0^{\pi} = -\frac{2k\lambda}{R} = -\frac{2kQ}{\pi R^2}$$

The field has magnitude $2kQ/(\pi R^2)$ and points along the symmetry axis, away from the midpoint of a positive arc. For a quarter arc ($0\le\theta\le\pi/2$) both components survive: $|E_x| = |E_y| = k\lambda/R$, so the magnitude is $\sqrt2\,k\lambda/R$ directed at $45^{\circ}$ to the axes.

电场大小为 $2kQ/(\pi R^2)$，沿对称轴方向，对正电圆弧而言背离圆弧的中点。对四分之一圆弧（$0\le\theta\le\pi/2$），两个分量都保留：$|E_x| = |E_y| = k\lambda/R$，所以大小为 $\sqrt2\,k\lambda/R$，方向与坐标轴成 $45^{\circ}$。

Also remember that field components must be integrated, not magnitudes: adding magnitudes for a ring or arc double-counts contributions that cancel. Finally, $dq$ must be written with the variable you integrate over; a leftover $dq$ in the integrand is an unfinished setup.
常见错误：对棒使用点电荷公式 只有当到分布的距离远大于分布的尺寸时，$kQ/r^2$ 才正确。还要记住应当对电场的分量求积分，而不是对大小求积分：对圆环或圆弧把大小相加，会把本应抵消的贡献重复计入。最后，$dq$ 必须用你的积分变量表示；被积函数中遗留 $dq$ 说明积分尚未建立完。

Topic 8.4 gave a way to find the field of a distribution by adding contributions. Topic 8.5 introduces a different quantity, electric flux, which does not give the field at a point but measures how much field passes through a surface. Flux is the ingredient that makes Gauss's law possible in Topic 8.6.

8.4 专题给出了通过叠加各贡献来求分布电场的方法。8.5 专题引入另一个不同的量，即电通量；它不给出某点的电场，而是衡量有多少电场穿过某个曲面。通量是 8.6 专题中高斯定律得以成立的要素。

What flux measures通量衡量什么

Flux describes the amount of a given quantity that passes through a given area. A familiar example is the volume of water per second flowing through a hoop held in a stream: it is largest when the hoop faces the flow and zero when the hoop is edge-on, because then no water crosses it.

通量（flux）描述的是通过给定面积的某个量的多少。一个熟悉的例子是流过放在溪流中的圆环的水的体积流量：圆环正对水流时最大，侧向时为零，因为此时没有水穿过。

Electric flux has the same geometric structure, with the electric field in place of the flow velocity; nothing actually flows.

电通量具有相同的几何结构，只是用电场代替流速；并没有东西真的在流动。

Uniform field: the area vector and the dot product匀强电场：面积矢量与点积

The area vector $\vec A$ has magnitude equal to the area and direction perpendicular to the plane of the surface. For a closed surface the area vector points outward. For an electric field $\vec E$ that is constant over a flat area $A$, the electric flux is the dot product:

面积矢量（area vector）$\vec A$ 的大小等于面积，方向垂直于曲面所在平面。对于闭合曲面，面积矢量指向外侧。对于在平面 $A$ 上保持恒定的电场 $\vec E$，电通量是点积：

Electric flux (uniform field)电通量（匀强电场）
$$\Phi_E = \vec E\cdot\vec A = EA\cos\theta$$

$\theta$ is the angle between $\vec E$ and the area vector $\vec A$ (the normal to the surface), not the angle between $\vec E$ and the surface. Units: $\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$.

$\theta$ 是 $\vec E$ 与面积矢量 $\vec A$（曲面的法线）之间的夹角，而不是 $\vec E$ 与曲面之间的夹角。单位：$\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$。

The sign comes from the dot product. If $\vec E$ has a component along $\vec A$ the flux is positive; if $\vec E$ is opposite to $\vec A$ the flux is negative; if $\vec E$ is parallel to the surface ($\theta = 90^{\circ}$) the flux is zero.

符号来自点积。若 $\vec E$ 有沿 $\vec A$ 的分量，则通量为正；若 $\vec E$ 与 $\vec A$ 反向，则通量为负；若 $\vec E$ 与曲面平行（$\theta = 90^{\circ}$），则通量为零。

On a closed surface, positive flux means field leaving the enclosed region and negative flux means field entering it. Flux is a scalar.

对闭合曲面而言，通量为正表示电场从被包围的区域穿出，通量为负表示电场进入该区域。通量是标量。

Worked Example: Flux through a tilted loop例题：通过倾斜线圈的通量

A flat loop of area $0.040\;\text{m}^2$ sits in a uniform field of magnitude $2.5\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$. The field makes an angle of $30^{\circ}$ with the plane of the loop. Find the electric flux through the loop.

面积 $0.040\;\text{m}^2$ 的平面线圈位于大小为 $2.5\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$ 的匀强电场中。电场与线圈所在平面的夹角为 $30^{\circ}$。求通过线圈的电通量。

Step 1: Use the angle with the normal第 1 步：使用与法线的夹角
The angle between $\vec E$ and the area vector (the normal) is $90^{\circ} - 30^{\circ}$:$\vec E$ 与面积矢量（法线）的夹角为 $90^{\circ} - 30^{\circ}$：
$$\theta = 60^{\circ}$$
Step 2: Evaluate第 2 步：计算
$$\Phi_E = EA\cos\theta = (2.5\times10^{3})(0.040)\cos60^{\circ} = 50\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$$
Step 3: Sign第 3 步：符号
The sign depends on which side the normal is chosen; choosing the opposite normal gives $-50\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$. State which normal you used.符号取决于法线选在哪一侧；选相反的法线则得 $-50\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$。要说明你使用的是哪一个法线。
Step 4: Why this works第 4 步：为什么这样做
Only the component of $\vec E$ perpendicular to the loop passes through it, and that is $E\cos\theta$ with $\theta$ measured from the normal.只有垂直于线圈的 $\vec E$ 分量穿过线圈，它等于 $E\cos\theta$，其中 $\theta$ 从法线量起。

Nonuniform fields: the surface integral非匀强电场：曲面积分

When the field changes over the surface or the surface is curved, divide the surface into small elements $d\vec A$ over each of which $\vec E$ is nearly constant, and add the contributions. The total electric flux through a surface is the surface integral of the electric field:

当电场在曲面上变化，或曲面是弯曲的时，把曲面分成许多小面元 $d\vec A$，在每个面元上 $\vec E$ 近似恒定，再把各部分贡献相加。通过曲面的总电通量是电场的曲面积分：

Electric flux (general)电通量（一般情形）
$$\Phi_E = \int \vec E\cdot d\vec A$$

For a closed surface the integral is written $\oint \vec E\cdot d\vec A$, with $d\vec A$ pointing outward everywhere.

对闭合曲面，积分写作 $\oint \vec E\cdot d\vec A$，各处的 $d\vec A$ 都指向外侧。

The art is the choice of $dA$. If $E$ depends on one coordinate, choose a strip on which that coordinate is constant, so $E$ comes out of the integral over the strip's width.

关键在于选取 $dA$。若 $E$ 只依赖一个坐标，就选取该坐标为常量的窄条，使 $E$ 在窄条宽度内可提出积分号。

Worked Example: Flux of a field that varies across the surface例题：通过场强随位置变化的曲面的通量

A rectangular surface lies in the $yz$ plane with $0 \le y \le 0.20\;\text{m}$ and width $w = 0.10\;\text{m}$ in $z$. The field is $\vec E = c\,y\,\hat x$ with $c = 100\;\text{N/(C}\cdot\text{m)}$. Find the flux through the rectangle with the area vector along $+x$.

一个矩形曲面位于 $yz$ 平面内，$0 \le y \le 0.20\;\text{m}$，在 $z$ 方向宽度为 $w = 0.10\;\text{m}$。电场为 $\vec E = c\,y\,\hat x$，$c = 100\;\text{N/(C}\cdot\text{m)}$。面积矢量沿 $+x$，求通过该矩形的通量。

Step 1: Choose the area element第 1 步：选取面积元
The field depends only on $y$, so take a strip of height $dy$ and width $w$: $dA = w\,dy$, with $d\vec A$ along $+x$, parallel to $\vec E$.电场只依赖 $y$，所以取高 $dy$、宽 $w$ 的窄条：$dA = w\,dy$，$d\vec A$ 沿 $+x$，与 $\vec E$ 平行。
Step 2: Integrate第 2 步：积分
$$\Phi_E = \int_0^{0.20} (c\,y)(w\,dy) = c\,w\,\frac{y^2}{2}\Big|_0^{0.20} = (100)(0.10)\frac{(0.20)^2}{2} = 0.20\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$$
Step 3: Why this works第 3 步：为什么这样做
Using the average field $E(0.10) = 10\;\text{N/C}$ times the area $0.020\;\text{m}^2$ gives the same value because $E$ is linear in $y$.用平均场强 $E(0.10) = 10\;\text{N/C}$ 乘以面积 $0.020\;\text{m}^2$ 也得到同样的值，因为 $E$ 对 $y$ 是线性的。

Closed surfaces闭合曲面

For a closed surface, compute the flux through each piece with the outward area vector and add. In a uniform field the net flux through any closed surface is zero: whatever enters on one side leaves on the other.

对于闭合曲面，用指向外侧的面积矢量计算每一块的通量，然后相加。在匀强电场中，通过任何闭合曲面的净通量为零：在一侧进入的恰好从另一侧穿出。

A nonzero net flux requires the field to be nonuniform in a particular way, and that particular way is related to the charge inside (Topic 8.6). Zero net flux does not mean zero field: the field can be large at every point of the surface, with inward and outward contributions cancelling.

净通量不为零，要求电场以某种特定方式变化，而这种方式与曲面内部的电荷有关（8.6 专题）。净通量为零不意味着电场为零：曲面上每一点的电场都可能很大，只是进入与穿出的贡献相互抵消。

$$\Phi_{+x} = +E_xA = +12\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C},\qquad \Phi_{-x} = -E_xA = -12\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$$
Step 3: Faces perpendicular to $y$ and $z$第 3 步：垂直于 $y$ 轴和 $z$ 轴的面
Similarly with $E_y$; the field has no $z$ component, so the $\pm z$ faces get zero:同理用 $E_y$；电场没有 $z$ 分量，所以 $\pm z$ 面的通量为零：
$$\Phi_{+y} = +16,\quad \Phi_{-y} = -16,\quad \Phi_{\pm z} = 0\;\;(\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C})$$
Step 4: Net flux第 4 步：净通量
The contributions cancel in pairs:各项成对抵消：
$$\Phi_{\text{net}} = 12 - 12 + 16 - 16 + 0 + 0 = 0$$
Step 5: Why this works第 5 步：为什么这样做
A uniform field has no sources or sinks inside the cube, so the net flux is zero even though four faces carry nonzero flux.匀强电场在立方体内没有源或汇，所以尽管有四个面通量不为零，净通量仍为零。

Flux of a point charge: the bridge to Gauss's law点电荷的通量：通向高斯定律的桥梁

Surround a point charge $Q$ with a sphere of radius $r$. By symmetry, the field has the same magnitude $kQ/r^2$ everywhere on the sphere and is perpendicular to it, so $\vec E$ is parallel to $d\vec A$ and $E$ comes out of the integral:

用半径为 $r$ 的球面包围点电荷 $Q$。由对称性，球面上各处电场大小都是 $kQ/r^2$，且垂直于球面，所以 $\vec E$ 与 $d\vec A$ 平行，$E$ 可以提到积分号外：

Flux through a sphere around a point charge点电荷周围球面的通量
$$\Phi_E = \oint E\,dA = \frac{kQ}{r^2}\,(4\pi r^2) = 4\pi kQ = \frac{Q}{\epsilon_0}$$

The radius cancels: the flux is the same for every sphere around the charge. This is the first step toward Gauss's law.

半径被约去：围绕该电荷的每个球面的通量都相同。这是通向高斯定律的第一步。

Worked Example: Flux through a sphere and a hemisphere例题：通过球面与半球面的通量

A point charge $Q = +2.0\;\text{nC}$ is at the center of a sphere of radius $0.10\;\text{m}$. Find the flux through the whole sphere, through a hemisphere, and the flux if the radius is doubled.

Step 2: Hemisphere第 2 步：半球面

常见错误：通量的三种错误 (1) 使用 $\vec E$ 与曲面的夹角而不是与法线的夹角：这会把 $\cos$ 与 $\sin$ 互换。(2) 把通量当作矢量；尽管它由矢量构成，通量是标量。(3) 认为净通量为零就意味着电场为零：电场在曲面上各点都可能不为零，只是进入与穿出的通量相互抵消。

Coulomb's law and the integrals of Topic 8.4 find fields by adding pieces. Gauss's law finds some fields by a shortcut that works only when the geometry is very symmetric: it ties the total flux through a closed surface to the charge inside, and symmetry then tells you the field on that surface.

The law and its meaning定律及其含义

Gauss's law高斯定律
$$\oint \vec E\cdot d\vec A = \frac{Q_{\text{enc}}}{\epsilon_0}$$

$Q_{\text{enc}}$ is the net charge inside the closed surface; $\vec E$ is the total field from all charges, inside and outside.

$Q_{\text{enc}}$ 是闭合曲面内的净电荷；$\vec E$ 是所有电荷（面内与面外）产生的总电场。

A Gaussian surface is an imaginary three-dimensional closed surface. If the enclosed charge stays the same, the total flux does not change when the surface is made larger, smaller or reshaped: in Topic 8.5 the spherical flux was $Q/\epsilon_0$ for every radius.

高斯面（Gaussian surface）是想象出来的三维闭合曲面。若所围电荷不变，曲面变大、变小或改变形状时总通量都不变：在 8.5 专题中，对每个半径球面通量都是 $Q/\epsilon_0$。

Charge outside the surface contributes zero net flux, because each field line from an outside charge that enters the surface also leaves it.

曲面外的电荷对净通量的贡献为零，因为外部电荷发出的每条进入曲面的电场线也会穿出曲面。

That outside charge still contributes to $\vec E$ at points of the surface, so Gauss's law gives the field only when symmetry tells you that all charges together produce a field of known direction and constant magnitude on the surface. The sign matters: negative enclosed charge gives negative (inward) net flux.

但外部电荷仍然对曲面上各点的 $\vec E$ 有贡献，所以只有当对称性告诉你全部电荷共同产生的电场在曲面上的方向已知、大小恒定时，高斯定律才能给出电场。符号很重要：所围电荷为负时，净通量为负（向内）。

Worked Example: Flux through a cube around a point charge例题：点电荷周围立方体的通量

A point charge $Q = +12\;\text{nC}$ is at the center of a cube. Find the net flux through the cube and through each face. What changes if the charge is moved off center but stays inside, and if the charge is moved outside the cube?

点电荷 $Q = +12\;\text{nC}$ 位于立方体中心。求通过立方体的净通量和通过每个面的通量。如果电荷移到偏离中心但仍在立方体内，或移到立方体外，会有什么变化？

Step 1: Net flux第 1 步：净通量
By Gauss's law, with the full charge enclosed:由高斯定律，全部电荷都被包围：
$$\Phi_{\text{net}} = \frac{Q}{\epsilon_0} = \frac{12\times10^{-9}}{8.85\times10^{-12}} = 1.4\times10^{3}\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$$
Step 2: Each face第 2 步：每个面
The six faces are equivalent by symmetry, so each carries one sixth:六个面由对称性等价，所以每个面承担六分之一：
$$\Phi_{\text{face}} = \frac{\Phi_{\text{net}}}{6} = 2.3\times10^{2}\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$$
Step 3: Move the charge第 3 步：移动电荷
Off center but still inside, the net flux is unchanged at $1.4\times10^{3}\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$, but the faces no longer share it equally: the nearest face carries more. Outside the cube, the net flux is zero, with equal inward and outward flux.偏离中心但仍在内部时，净通量不变，仍为 $1.4\times10^{3}\;\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$，但各面不再平分：最近的面承担更多。在立方体之外时，净通量为零，进入与穿出的通量相等。
Step 4: Why this works第 4 步：为什么这样做
Gauss's law needs no symmetry to give the net flux; symmetry is needed only to divide the flux among the faces.高斯定律给出净通量并不需要对称性；对称性只在把通量分配给各个面时才需要。

Choosing a Gaussian surface选取高斯面

Gaussian surfaces are constructed so that, on each region of the surface, the field produced by the enclosed charge is either perpendicular to the surface with constant magnitude, or parallel to it.

高斯面的构造方式，是使面上每个区域中由所围电荷产生的电场要么垂直于曲面且大小恒定，要么与曲面平行。

Over a perpendicular region, $\vec E\cdot d\vec A = E\,dA$ and $E$ factors out, leaving $E$ times the area of that region; over a parallel region the dot product is zero. Three symmetries are used:

在垂直区域内，$\vec E\cdot d\vec A = E\,dA$，$E$ 可以提出，只剩 $E$ 乘以该区域的面积；在平行区域内点积为零。常用三种对称性：

If you cannot argue it from the symmetry of the sources, Gauss's law will not give $E$. For example, the field near a charged cube or a dipole cannot be found this way.

在写出积分之前先检验对称性论证：为什么 $\vec E$ 的方向必须是径向、沿轴线或沿法线？如果无法从源电荷的对称性论证这一点，高斯定律就不能给出 $E$。例如，带电立方体或电偶极子附近的电场就不能用这种方法求得。

Spherical symmetry球对称

Take a concentric sphere of radius $r$. Then $\oint \vec E\cdot d\vec A = E\,(4\pi r^2) = Q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$, so

取半径为 $r$ 的同心球面。于是 $\oint \vec E\cdot d\vec A = E\,(4\pi r^2) = Q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$，故

Spherically symmetric distribution球对称分布
$$E(r) = \frac{Q_{\text{enc}}(r)}{4\pi\epsilon_0\,r^2}$$

Outside the distribution, $Q_{\text{enc}}$ is the total charge, so the field equals that of a point charge at the center (the result stated in Topic 8.3). For a thin shell or a conductor's surface charge, $Q_{\text{enc}} = 0$ for $r<R$ and so $E = 0$ inside. For a uniformly charged solid insulating sphere of radius $R$ and charge $Q$, $Q_{\text{enc}} = Q\,r^3/R^3$ for $r<R$, giving $E = kQr/R^3 = \rho r/(3\epsilon_0)$.

在分布之外，$Q_{\text{enc}}$ 是总电荷，所以电场等于位于球心的点电荷的电场（8.3 专题中给出的结果）。对薄球壳或导体的表面电荷，$r<R$ 时 $Q_{\text{enc}} = 0$，所以内部 $E = 0$。对半径为 $R$、电荷为 $Q$ 的均匀带电实心绝缘球，$r<R$ 时 $Q_{\text{enc}} = Q\,r^3/R^3$，得 $E = kQr/R^3 = \rho r/(3\epsilon_0)$。

Worked Example: Point charge inside a charged thin shell例题：带电薄球壳内的点电荷

A point charge $Q_1 = +3.0\;\text{nC}$ sits at the center of a thin insulating spherical shell of radius $0.10\;\text{m}$ that carries a uniform charge $Q_s = -5.0\;\text{nC}$. Find $\vec E$ at $r = 0.05\;\text{m}$, just inside the shell, just outside the shell, and at $r = 0.20\;\text{m}$.

一个点电荷 $Q_1 = +3.0\;\text{nC}$ 位于半径 $0.10\;\text{m}$ 的薄绝缘球壳的中心，球壳均匀带电 $Q_s = -5.0\;\text{nC}$。求 $r = 0.05\;\text{m}$ 处、球壳内侧附近、球壳外侧附近以及 $r = 0.20\;\text{m}$ 处的 $\vec E$。

Step 1: Inside the shell第 1 步：球壳内部
For $r<0.10\;\text{m}$ the Gaussian sphere encloses only $Q_1$:当 $r<0.10\;\text{m}$ 时，高斯球面只包围 $Q_1$：
$$E(0.05) = \frac{kQ_1}{r^2} = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(3.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.05)^2} = 1.1\times10^{4}\;\text{N/C},\;\text{outward}$$
Step 2: Just inside the shell第 2 步：球壳内侧附近
$$E = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(3.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.10)^2} = 2.7\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C},\;\text{outward}$$
Step 3: Outside the shell第 3 步：球壳外部
For $r>0.10\;\text{m}$ the enclosed charge is $Q_1 + Q_s = -2.0\;\text{nC}$:当 $r>0.10\;\text{m}$ 时，所围电荷为 $Q_1 + Q_s = -2.0\;\text{nC}$：
$$E(0.10^+) = \frac{k(-2.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.10)^2} = -1.8\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C},\qquad E(0.20) = \frac{k(-2.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.20)^2} = -4.5\times10^{2}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 4: Interpret the signs第 4 步：解读符号
The negative values mean the field points inward. The field jumps across the shell, by $-1.8\times10^{3} - 2.7\times10^{3} = -4.5\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$, which equals $\sigma/\epsilon_0$ for the shell's surface charge density $\sigma = Q_s/(4\pi(0.10)^2)$.负值表示电场向内。电场在球壳处发生跳变，跳变量为 $-1.8\times10^{3} - 2.7\times10^{3} = -4.5\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$，等于球壳表面电荷密度 $\sigma = Q_s/(4\pi(0.10)^2)$ 对应的 $\sigma/\epsilon_0$。

$$E(0.040) = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(6.0\times10^{-9})(0.040)}{(0.080)^3} = 4.2\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 2: At the surface第 2 步：球面处
$$E(R) = \frac{kQ}{R^2} = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(6.0\times10^{-9})}{(0.080)^2} = 8.4\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 3: Outside第 3 步：外部
$$E(0.16) = \frac{kQ}{(0.16)^2} = 2.1\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$$

Half the radius gives half the surface field inside (linear in $r$), while doubling the radius outside gives a quarter (inverse square); the two formulas agree at $r = R$.在内部，半径减半则电场为球面值的一半（对 $r$ 线性）；在外部，半径加倍则电场为四分之一（平方反比）；两个公式在 $r = R$ 处一致。

Charge from a density function由电荷密度函数求电荷

When the charge density depends on position, $Q_{\text{enc}}$ must be found by integrating it over the enclosed region: $Q = \int \lambda\,d\ell$ in one dimension, $\int \sigma\,dA$ in two dimensions and $\int \rho\,dV$ in three. For a spherically symmetric $\rho(r)$ use thin shells, $dV = 4\pi r^2\,dr$; for a cylindrically symmetric $\rho(r)$ of length $\ell$ use $dV = 2\pi r\ell\,dr$.

当电荷密度依赖于位置时，必须对所围区域积分来求 $Q_{\text{enc}}$：一维为 $Q = \int \lambda\,d\ell$，二维为 $\int \sigma\,dA$，三维为 $\int \rho\,dV$。对球对称的 $\rho(r)$，用薄球壳，$dV = 4\pi r^2\,dr$；对长度为 $\ell$、柱对称的 $\rho(r)$，用 $dV = 2\pi r\ell\,dr$。

Worked Example: Nonuniform charge density in a sphere例题：球内非均匀的电荷密度

An insulating sphere of radius $R = 0.10\;\text{m}$ has volume charge density $\rho = \rho_0\,r/R$ with $\rho_0 = 3.0\times10^{-6}\;\text{C/m}^3$. Find the total charge, and $E$ at $r = 0.050\;\text{m}$ and $r = 0.20\;\text{m}$.

半径 $R = 0.10\;\text{m}$ 的绝缘球的体电荷密度为 $\rho = \rho_0\,r/R$，$\rho_0 = 3.0\times10^{-6}\;\text{C/m}^3$。求总电荷，以及 $r = 0.050\;\text{m}$ 和 $r = 0.20\;\text{m}$ 处的 $E$。

Step 1: Enclosed charge at radius $r$第 1 步：半径 $r$ 内所围电荷
Integrate shells of thickness $dr'$:对厚度为 $dr'$ 的薄球壳积分：
$$Q_{\text{enc}}(r) = \int_0^r \rho_0\frac{r'}{R}\,4\pi r'^2\,dr' = \frac{4\pi\rho_0}{R}\frac{r^4}{4} = \frac{\pi\rho_0 r^4}{R}$$
Step 2: Total charge第 2 步：总电荷
Set $r = R$:令 $r = R$：
$$Q = \pi\rho_0R^3 = \pi(3.0\times10^{-6})(0.10)^3 = 9.4\times10^{-9}\;\text{C}$$
Step 3: Field inside第 3 步：内部电场
Gauss's law with a concentric sphere gives $E = Q_{\text{enc}}/(4\pi\epsilon_0 r^2)$, so inside $E = \rho_0 r^2/(4\epsilon_0 R)$:对同心球面应用高斯定律得 $E = Q_{\text{enc}}/(4\pi\epsilon_0 r^2)$，所以内部 $E = \rho_0 r^2/(4\epsilon_0 R)$：
$$E(0.050) = \frac{(3.0\times10^{-6})(0.050)^2}{4(8.85\times10^{-12})(0.10)} = 2.1\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 4: Field outside第 4 步：外部电场
Outside, $Q_{\text{enc}} = Q$:在外部，$Q_{\text{enc}} = Q$：
$$E(0.20) = \frac{kQ}{r^2} = \frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(9.4\times10^{-9})}{(0.20)^2} = 2.1\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 5: Why this works第 5 步：为什么这样做
Spherical symmetry holds for any $\rho(r)$, so the same Gaussian sphere works; only $Q_{\text{enc}}$ changes, and it comes from an integral.对任何 $\rho(r)$，球对称性都成立，所以同样的高斯球面适用；改变的只有 $Q_{\text{enc}}$，它由积分得出。

Cylindrical symmetry柱对称

Use a coaxial cylinder of radius $r$ and length $\ell$. The end caps are parallel to $\vec E$, so only the curved side counts: $E\,(2\pi r\ell) = Q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$. For an infinite line with charge per length $\lambda$, $Q_{\text{enc}} = \lambda\ell$, so $E = \lambda/(2\pi\epsilon_0 r) = 2k\lambda/r$. This matches the limit found by integration in Topic 8.4.

取半径为 $r$、长为 $\ell$ 的同轴圆柱面。两个端面与 $\vec E$ 平行，所以只有侧面起作用：$E\,(2\pi r\ell) = Q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$。对单位长度电荷为 $\lambda$ 的无限长直线，$Q_{\text{enc}} = \lambda\ell$，所以 $E = \lambda/(2\pi\epsilon_0 r) = 2k\lambda/r$。这与 8.4 专题中积分得到的极限结果一致。

For a long uniformly charged solid cylinder of radius $R$ and charge density $\rho$: inside, $Q_{\text{enc}} = \rho\pi r^2\ell$ gives $E = \rho r/(2\epsilon_0)$; outside, $Q_{\text{enc}} = \rho\pi R^2\ell$ gives $E = \rho R^2/(2\epsilon_0 r)$, the same as a line charge $\lambda = \rho\pi R^2$ on the axis.

对半径为 $R$、电荷密度为 $\rho$ 的长均匀带电实心圆柱：内部 $Q_{\text{enc}} = \rho\pi r^2\ell$，得 $E = \rho r/(2\epsilon_0)$；外部 $Q_{\text{enc}} = \rho\pi R^2\ell$，得 $E = \rho R^2/(2\epsilon_0 r)$，与轴线上 $\lambda = \rho\pi R^2$ 的线电荷相同。

一根很长的绝缘圆柱，半径 $R = 0.020\;\text{m}$，体电荷密度均匀为 $\rho = +5.0\times10^{-6}\;\text{C/m}^3$。求 $r = 0.010\;\text{m}$ 处、表面处以及 $r = 0.040\;\text{m}$ 处的 $E$。

$$E(0.010) = \frac{\rho r}{2\epsilon_0} = \frac{(5.0\times10^{-6})(0.010)}{2(8.85\times10^{-12})} = 2.8\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 2: Surface第 2 步：表面
$$E(R) = \frac{\rho R}{2\epsilon_0} = \frac{(5.0\times10^{-6})(0.020)}{2(8.85\times10^{-12})} = 5.6\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 3: Outside第 3 步：外部
The field falls as $1/r$ outside, so at twice the radius it is half the surface value:外部电场按 $1/r$ 下降，所以在两倍半径处是表面值的一半：
$$E(0.040) = \frac{\rho R^2}{2\epsilon_0 r} = \frac{(5.0\times10^{-6})(0.020)^2}{2(8.85\times10^{-12})(0.040)} = 2.8\times10^{3}\;\text{N/C}$$

By symmetry $\vec E$ is radial and depends only on $r$; the end caps are parallel to $\vec E$ and contribute nothing.由对称性，$\vec E$ 沿径向且只依赖 $r$；端面与 $\vec E$ 平行，没有贡献。

Planar symmetry面对称

For an infinite plane of charge with surface charge density $\sigma$, take a pillbox with two caps of area $A$ parallel to the plane, one on each side. By symmetry $\vec E$ points away from a positive sheet on both sides, so the flux through the two caps is $2EA$, and the sides have zero flux. Then $2EA = \sigma A/\epsilon_0$:

对面电荷密度为 $\sigma$ 的无限大带电平面，取一个药盒形高斯面，两个端面面积为 $A$，与平面平行，分别位于两侧。由对称性，$\vec E$ 在正电平面两侧都背离平面，所以通过两个端面的通量为 $2EA$，侧面通量为零。于是 $2EA = \sigma A/\epsilon_0$：

Infinite plane of charge无限大带电平面
$$E = \frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}$$

The field is uniform and independent of distance from the plane. Two parallel planes with $+\sigma$ and $-\sigma$ superpose to give $E = \sigma/\epsilon_0$ between them and zero outside. For an infinite slab of thickness $t$ and uniform density $\rho$, centered on the plane $x = 0$, $E = \rho x/\epsilon_0$ for $|x|<t/2$ and $E = \rho t/(2\epsilon_0)$ outside.

电场是均匀的，与到平面的距离无关。带 $+\sigma$ 和 $-\sigma$ 的两个平行平面叠加后，两者之间 $E = \sigma/\epsilon_0$，外部为零。对厚度为 $t$、密度均匀为 $\rho$、中心在 $x = 0$ 平面上的无限大平板，$|x|<t/2$ 时 $E = \rho x/\epsilon_0$，板外 $E = \rho t/(2\epsilon_0)$。

Worked Example: Parallel planes of charge例题：平行带电平面

Two large parallel sheets carry uniform charge densities $+\sigma$ and $-\sigma$ with $\sigma = 3.0\;\mu\text{C/m}^2$. Find the field of one sheet alone, then the net field between the sheets and outside them.

两个大的平行薄板分别均匀带电，电荷密度为 $+\sigma$ 和 $-\sigma$，$\sigma = 3.0\;\mu\text{C/m}^2$。求单个薄板单独产生的电场，然后求两板之间和两板之外的合电场。

Step 1: One sheet第 1 步：单个薄板
$$E_1 = \frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0} = \frac{3.0\times10^{-6}}{2(8.85\times10^{-12})} = 1.7\times10^{5}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 2: Between the sheets第 2 步：两板之间
Both fields point from the positive sheet toward the negative sheet, so they add:两个电场都由正板指向负板，所以相加：
$$E_{\text{between}} = 2E_1 = \frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0} = 3.4\times10^{5}\;\text{N/C}$$
Step 3: Outside第 3 步：两板之外
The two fields have equal magnitude and opposite direction, so $E_{\text{outside}} = 0$.两个电场大小相等、方向相反，所以 $E_{\text{outside}} = 0$。
Step 4: Why this works第 4 步：为什么这样做
The planar-symmetry field does not depend on distance, so superposition of two planes is just vector addition of two constants.面对称的电场与距离无关，所以两个平面的叠加只是两个常量的矢量相加。

对薄球壳，内部 $E = 0$，在 $r=R$ 处跳变到 $kQ/R^2$，然后按 $1/r^2$ 下降：面电荷使 $E$ 发生跳变。对长直线或圆柱，外部 $E$ 按 $1/r$ 下降。对平面，每一侧 $E$ 是常量，穿过平面时方向反转。所围电荷改变符号时，$E$ 也改变符号。

Common Mistake: Using Gauss's law without symmetry Gauss's law is always true, but $E\,A = Q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$ is valid only when $E$ is constant and perpendicular over the whole surface. A charge outside the surface does not change the flux, but it may change $E$ on the surface and so spoil the symmetry argument.
常见错误：没有对称性就使用高斯定律 高斯定律总是成立，但 $E\,A = Q_{\text{enc}}/\epsilon_0$ 只有在整个曲面上 $E$ 恒定且垂直时才成立。另外，$Q_{\text{enc}}$ 只计高斯面内的电荷，而 $E$ 是所有电荷产生的电场。曲面外的电荷不改变通量，但可能改变曲面上的 $E$，从而破坏对称性论证。
