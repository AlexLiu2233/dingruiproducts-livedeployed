Point charges $+q$ and $+4q$ are held a distance $d$ apart. Which statement about the electric forces the two charges exert on each other is correct?点电荷 $+q$ 和 $+4q$ 相距 $d$ 固定。关于这两个电荷相互施加的电力，下列说法正确的是？

  • (A) The force on $+4q$ is four times as large as the force on $+q$.作用在 $+4q$ 上的力是作用在 $+q$ 上的力的四倍。
  • (B) The two forces have equal magnitudes and point in opposite directions.两个力大小相等、方向相反。
  • (C) The two forces have equal magnitudes and point in the same direction.两个力大小相等、方向相同。
  • (D) The force on $+q$ is four times as large as the force on $+4q$.作用在 $+q$ 上的力是作用在 $+4q$ 上的力的四倍。
Answer:答案： (B)
By Coulomb's law each charge feels $F=k(q)(4q)/d^2=4kq^2/d^2$. The two forces are an action-reaction pair, so they have equal magnitude and opposite direction (repulsion, since the charges are alike).由库仑定律，每个电荷受力 $F=k(q)(4q)/d^2=4kq^2/d^2$。这两个力是一对作用力与反作用力，大小相等、方向相反（同种电荷，表现为排斥）。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) The force on $+4q$ is four times as large as the force on $+q$: this choice sizes the force by the source charge alone, as if the larger charge pushed harder. Each force is $k(q)(4q)/d^2$ and depends on the product of both charges. (C) The two forces have equal magnitudes and point in the same direction: this choice gets the magnitude right but ignores that like charges repel. Each force points away from the other charge, so the two forces are opposite. (D) The force on $+q$ is four times as large as the force on $+4q$: this choice is the mirror image of the first error: it gives the larger force to the smaller charge, as if force varied inversely with a charge's own size. Coulomb's law is symmetric in $q_1$ and $q_2$.(A) 作用在 $+4q$ 上的力是作用在 $+q$ 上的力的四倍：只按施力电荷的大小来判断力的大小，仿佛电荷越大推力越大。实际上每个力都是 $k(q)(4q)/d^2$，取决于两个电荷的乘积。 (C) 两个力大小相等、方向相同：大小判断正确，但忽略了同种电荷相互排斥。每个力都背离另一个电荷，因此两个力方向相反。 (D) 作用在 $+q$ 上的力是作用在 $+4q$ 上的力的四倍：是第一种错误的镜像：把较大的力给了较小的电荷，仿佛力与自身电荷成反比。库仑定律对 $q_1$ 和 $q_2$ 是对称的。
Insight.要点。 A Coulomb force depends on the product $q_1q_2$ and on $r^2$; neither charge is favored. Larger charge means a larger field, not a larger force on itself.库仑力取决于乘积 $q_1q_2$ 和 $r^2$，对两个电荷是对等的。电荷越大，产生的场越强，而不是自身受力越大。

While the rod is still near, the sphere is briefly connected to ground by a wire; the wire is removed, and then the rod is removed. The sphere ends up一个电中性的导体球放在绝缘支架上。将带负电的棒靠近但不接触。棒仍在附近时，用导线将球短暂接地；撤去导线，再移走棒。最终球

  • (B) negatively charged, with the charge spread uniformly over its surface.带负电，电荷均匀分布在表面。
  • (C) positively charged, with the charge spread uniformly over its surface.带正电，电荷均匀分布在表面。
  • (D) positively charged, with the charge concentrated on the side that faced the rod.带正电，电荷集中在曾面向棒的一侧。
Answer:答案： (C)
The negative rod pushes mobile electrons in the sphere to the far side, and the ground wire lets them leave. Removing the wire first traps a net electron deficit, so the sphere is positive. After the rod is removed, the excess positive charge redistributes uniformly over the isolated conducting sphere.负电棒把球内的自由电子推向远端，接地导线让它们离开。先撤去导线，球便留下净电子亏缺而带正电。移走棒后，孤立导体球上多余的正电荷重新均匀分布。
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) neutral: this choice treats grounding as discharging the sphere. The rod repels electrons through the wire into ground while it is near, so the sphere has lost electrons when the wire is removed. (B) negatively charged, with the charge spread uniformly over its surface: this choice is the result of touching the rod (charging by contact). Here the rod never touches the sphere and it pushes electrons out, not in. (D) positively charged, with the charge concentrated on the side that faced the rod: this choice has the right sign but the wrong distribution. The one-sided separation lasts only while the rod is present; on an isolated conductor the excess charge spreads out once the rod is gone.(A) 呈电中性：把接地当作使球放电。棒在附近时把电子经导线排向大地，撤去导线时球已失去电子。 (B) 带负电，电荷均匀分布在表面：这是接触起电的结果。本题中棒从未接触球，且是把电子推出而不是送入。 (D) 带正电，电荷集中在曾面向棒的一侧：符号正确但分布错误。单侧的电荷分离只在棒存在时出现；棒移走后，孤立导体上的多余电荷会重新散开。
Insight.要点。 Charging by induction always leaves the opposite sign to the inducing rod, and the order matters: ground removed first, rod removed second.感应起电得到的电荷符号总与施感棒相反，且次序很关键：先撤接地，再移走棒。

The magnitude is $E=k|q|/r^2$. Because the source is negative, the field points toward it.大小为 $E=k|q|/r^2$。因为施场电荷为负，场指向该电荷。

Field direction is the direction of the force on a positive test charge, which a negative source attracts. (C) $6.0\times10^{4}~\mathrm{N/C}$, toward the charge: this choice uses $kq/r$ instead of $kq/r^2$: $(8.99\times10^{9})(2.0\times10^{-6})/0.30=6.0\times10^{4}$. Two fixed point charges, $+Q$ at $x=0$ and $+4Q$ at $x=3d$, lie on the $x$-axis. At what position on the axis is the net electric field zero, so that a small test charge placed there feels no net force?两个固定的点电荷 $+Q$（位于 $x=0$）和 $+4Q$（位于 $x=3d$）在 $x$ 轴上。轴上哪个位置的合电场为零，使得放在那里的小试探电荷不受净力？

  • (A) $x=3d/2$
  • (B) $x=3d/5$
  • (C) $x=2d$
  • (D) $x=d$
Answer:答案： (D)
Between two like charges the fields oppose, so the zero is between them. Setting the magnitudes equal at distance $x$ from $+Q$ gives $x=d$. Outside the pair the fields point the same way and cannot cancel.两个同种电荷之间的场方向相反，零场点在两者之间。令距离 $+Q$ 为 $x$ 处的场强相等，得 $x=d$。在两电荷之外两个场同向，无法抵消。
$$\frac{kQ}{x^2}=\frac{k(4Q)}{(3d-x)^2}\;\Rightarrow\;3d-x=2x\;\Rightarrow\;x=d$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (A) $x=3d/2$: this choice picks the midpoint, which would work only for equal charges. At the midpoint the larger charge's field dominates. (B) $x=3d/5$: this choice drops the squares: $1/x=4/(3d-x)$ gives $3d/5$. The fields go as $1/r^2$, so the condition is $1/x^2=4/(3d-x)^2$. (C) $x=2d$: this choice inverts the distance ratio and puts the point nearer the larger charge, where its field is even stronger. The zero lies nearer the smaller charge.(A) $x=3d/2$：选了中点，而中点只对等量电荷成立。在中点处较大电荷的场占优。 (B) $x=3d/5$：漏掉了平方：$1/x=4/(3d-x)$ 得到 $3d/5$。场按 $1/r^2$ 变化，应列 $1/x^2=4/(3d-x)^2$。 (C) $x=2d$：把距离比弄反，使该点更靠近较大电荷，那里它的场更强。零场点应更靠近较小的电荷。
Insight.要点。 For two like charges the zero sits between them, closer to the smaller one, with a distance ratio equal to the square root of the charge ratio.对两个同种电荷，零场点在两者之间且靠近较小者，距离之比等于电荷之比的平方根。

$$F=\frac{(8.99\times10^{9})(2.0\times10^{-6})(1.0\times10^{-6})}{(0.30)^2}=0.20~\mathrm{N}$$
Why the other options attract.其他选项的诱因。 (B) $0.20~\mathrm{N}$, attractive: this choice has the right size but the wrong nature. Both spheres end up positive ($+2.0$ and $+1.0~\mu\mathrm{C}$), and like charges repel. (C) $0.40~\mathrm{N}$, repulsive: this choice skips the second contact and uses $+2.0~\mu\mathrm{C}$ on both $A$ and $B$. Touching $C$ takes half of $B$'s charge. $$\Phi_\mathrm{face}=\frac{1}{6}\cdot\frac{q}{\varepsilon_0}=\frac{678}{6}=1.1\times10^{2}~\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/C}$$

$$\Phi_\mathrm{far\ face}=\frac{85}{3}=28~\mathrm{N\cdot m^2/C}$$
