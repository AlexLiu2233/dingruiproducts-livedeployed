Q1MEDIUMAP MC5.1 Beneficial and Harmful Effects

A city deploys a new traffic navigation app that routes drivers around congestion in real time. City planners praise the app for reducing average commute times, but residents of a quiet neighborhood complain that the app now sends heavy traffic through their streets, increasing noise and accidents near a school.某城市部署了一款新的交通导航应用，可以实时引导司机绕开拥堵路段。城市规划者称赞该应用缩短了平均通勤时间，但一个安静社区的居民抱怨说，该应用现在把大量车流引入他们的街道，增加了噪音以及一所学校附近的事故。

Which statement best explains this situation using the AP CSP framework on the effects of computing innovations?根据 AP CSP 关于计算创新影响的框架，哪项陈述最能解释这种情况？

  • (A) The app is purely beneficial because reducing average commute time is a measurable benefit该应用纯粹是有益的，因为缩短平均通勤时间是可衡量的好处
  • (B) The app is harmful overall and should be removed, because any harm outweighs all benefits该应用总体上是有害的，应该被下架，因为任何危害都超过所有益处
  • (C) A single effect of a computing innovation can be viewed as both beneficial and harmful by different people, and not every effect is anticipated in advance计算创新的同一个影响可以被不同的人视为既有益又有害，而且并非所有影响都能事先预料到
  • (D) The neighborhood residents are experiencing a digital divide because they lack access to the app该社区居民正在经历数字鸿沟，因为他们无法使用该应用
Answer:答案： (C)
  • The app was designed for a specific purpose: reducing congestion and commute times.
  • Routing traffic through a quiet neighborhood is an unintended consequence of that design.
  • IOC-1.A.4 states that a single effect can be viewed as both beneficial and harmful by different people: planners see shorter commutes, residents see noise and danger.
  • IOC-1.A.3 states that not every effect of a computing innovation is anticipated in advance.
Option A is wrong because a measurable benefit does not make an innovation purely beneficial; harms can coexist. Option B is wrong because the framework does not say any harm automatically outweighs all benefits; effects are weighed, not absolutized. Option D misuses the term digital divide, which is about differing access to computing and the Internet, not about disliking an app's effects.
  • 该应用的设计目的是减少拥堵、缩短通勤时间。
  • 把车流引入安静社区是这一设计的非预期后果（unintended consequence）。
  • IOC-1.A.4 指出：同一个影响可以被不同的人视为既有益又有害：规划者看到通勤缩短，居民看到噪音和危险。
  • IOC-1.A.3 指出：并非计算创新的所有影响都能被事先预料到。
选项 A 错误，因为存在可衡量的益处并不意味着创新纯粹有益，危害可以同时存在。选项 B 错误，因为该框架并没有说任何危害都自动超过所有益处，影响需要权衡而不是绝对化。选项 D 误用了数字鸿沟（digital divide）一词，它指的是对计算设备和互联网的访问差异，而不是不喜欢某应用带来的影响。
Q2HARDAP MC5.1 Beneficial and Harmful Effects

A social media company releases a free photo-filter app intended for casual entertainment. Within a year, independent journalists use the app's image-tagging feature to document evidence in conflict zones, while other groups use the same feature to identify and harass protesters. The app's user base grows to hundreds of millions faster than the company expected.一家社交媒体公司发布了一款面向日常娱乐的免费滤镜应用。一年之内，独立记者使用该应用的图像标记功能记录冲突地区的证据，而另一些团体则利用同一功能识别并骚扰抗议者。该应用的用户数比公司预期更快地增长到数亿。

Which statement is best supported by the AP CSP enduring understanding about the impact of computing innovations?根据 AP CSP 关于计算创新影响的学科大概念（enduring understanding），哪项陈述最有依据？

  • (A) Rapid sharing and a very large user base can produce significant impacts beyond the programmer's intended purpose or control, including both beneficial and harmful uses快速传播和庞大的用户群可能产生超出程序员预期目的或控制的重大影响，包括有益和有害两方面的用途
  • (B) The company should have been able to anticipate every possible use, so it bears full responsibility for all outcomes公司本应能够预料到所有可能的用途，因此对所有后果承担全部责任
  • (C) Because the app was designed for entertainment, the journalism use is not a real effect of the innovation由于该应用是为娱乐设计的，新闻用途不算该创新的真实影响
  • (D) The harmful uses prove the app has no beneficial effects on society有害用途证明该应用对社会没有有益影响
Answer:答案： (A)
  • IOC-1.B.6: rapid sharing of a program or running it with a very large number of users can result in significant impacts beyond the intended purpose or control of the programmer.
  • IOC-1.B.1: computing innovations can be used in ways their creators had not originally intended, here both journalism (beneficial) and harassment (harmful).
  • IOC-1.B.4: it is not possible for a programmer to consider all the ways an innovation can be used.
Option B is wrong because IOC-1.B.4 says anticipating every use is impossible, so full anticipation cannot be expected. Option C is wrong because unintended uses are still real effects of the innovation. Option D is wrong because the journalism use is a clear beneficial effect; harmful uses do not erase beneficial ones.
  • IOC-1.B.6：程序被快速分享或被海量用户使用，可能产生超出程序员预期目的或控制的重大影响。
  • IOC-1.B.1：计算创新可能被以创造者最初未曾设想的方式使用，本例中既有新闻用途（有益），也有骚扰用途（有害）。
  • IOC-1.B.4：程序员不可能考虑到创新的所有使用方式。
选项 B 错误，因为 IOC-1.B.4 说明预料所有用途是不可能的，不能要求完全预料。选项 C 错误，因为非预期用途仍然是创新的真实影响。选项 D 错误，因为新闻用途是明确的有益影响，有害用途并不能抹杀有益影响。
Q3MEDIUMAP MC5.2 Digital Divide

A state survey collects data on household Internet access. The results are summarized below. - Households with annual income above $75,000: 96% have broadband Internet at home - Households with annual income below $30,000: 63% have broadband Internet at home - Urban households: 93% have broadband - Rural households: 71% have broadband某州对家庭互联网接入情况进行了调查，结果汇总如下。 - 年收入超过 75,000 美元的家庭：96% 拥有家庭宽带 - 年收入低于 30,000 美元的家庭：63% 拥有家庭宽带 - 城市家庭：93% 拥有宽带 - 农村家庭：71% 拥有宽带

Based on the data, which statement best describes the digital divide illustrated here?根据这些数据，哪项陈述最准确地描述了这里体现的数字鸿沟（digital divide）？

  • (A) The divide exists only between countries, so this within-state data cannot show a digital divide数字鸿沟只存在于国家之间，因此州内数据无法体现数字鸿沟
  • (B) Access to the Internet varies with both socioeconomic and geographic characteristics, raising issues of equity and access for both groups and individuals互联网接入随社会经济（socioeconomic）和地理特征而变化，对群体和个人都引发了公平与接入问题
  • (C) The divide will disappear on its own as devices get cheaper, so no action by organizations or governments is relevant随着设备越来越便宜，鸿沟会自行消失，因此组织或政府的行动无关紧要
  • (D) Only individuals are affected by the digital divide; groups as a whole cannot be affected数字鸿沟只影响个人，群体整体不会受到影响
Answer:答案： (B)
  • The data shows a 33-point gap by income (socioeconomic) and a 22-point gap by urban vs rural (geographic).
  • IOC-1.C.1 and IOC-1.C.2: Internet access varies between socioeconomic, geographic, and demographic characteristics, and this differing access is the digital divide.
  • IOC-1.C.3 and IOC-1.C.4: the divide affects both groups and individuals and raises issues of equity, access, and influence.
Option A is wrong because the divide exists within countries and regions, not only between countries. Option C is wrong because IOC-1.C.5 says the divide is affected by actions of individuals, organizations, and governments; it does not fix itself. Option D directly contradicts IOC-1.C.3, which says the divide can affect both groups and individuals.
  • 数据显示收入差距带来 33 个百分点的差异（社会经济因素），城乡差距带来 22 个百分点的差异（地理因素）。
  • IOC-1.C.1 和 IOC-1.C.2：互联网接入因社会经济、地理和人口特征而不同，这种接入差异就是数字鸿沟。
  • IOC-1.C.3 和 IOC-1.C.4：数字鸿沟同时影响群体和个人，并引发公平、接入和影响力方面的问题。
选项 A 错误，因为数字鸿沟也存在于国家和区域内部，不只是国家之间。选项 C 错误，因为 IOC-1.C.5 指出数字鸿沟受个人、组织和政府行动的影响，它不会自行消失。选项 D 直接与 IOC-1.C.3 矛盾，该条明确指出鸿沟可同时影响群体和个人。
Q4HARDAP MC5.2 Digital Divide

A school district moves all homework assignments, grades, and college-application resources to an online-only portal to save printing costs. Officials note that 12% of students live in households without reliable home Internet, concentrated in lower-income neighborhoods. The district says students without access can use the public library, which is open weekdays until 6 p.m. and closed weekends.某学区为节省印刷成本，把所有家庭作业、成绩和大学申请资源都迁移到仅在线使用的门户网站上。官员们注意到，12% 的学生生活在没有可靠家庭互联网的家庭中，且集中在低收入社区。学区表示，无法上网的学生可以使用公共图书馆，图书馆工作日开放到下午 6 点，周末闭馆。

Which analysis best evaluates this policy in terms of the digital divide?从数字鸿沟的角度，哪项分析最能评价这项政策？

  • (A) The policy eliminates the divide because a library exists somewhere in the district该政策消除了数字鸿沟，因为学区内某处有图书馆
  • (B) The policy is neutral because it treats every student identically by giving them the same online portal该政策是中立的，因为它给每个学生提供相同的在线门户，一视同仁
  • (C) The policy mainly affects device ownership, not Internet access, so it is unrelated to the digital divide该政策主要影响设备拥有量而非互联网接入，因此与数字鸿沟无关
  • (D) The policy can deepen inequity: identical treatment is not equitable when access differs, and limited library hours may not provide meaningful access for working families该政策可能加剧不平等：当接入条件不同时，相同对待并不等于公平，而且图书馆有限的开放时间可能无法为有工作的家庭提供真正可用的接入
Answer:答案： (D)
  • IOC-1.C.2: the digital divide is differing access to computing devices and the Internet based on socioeconomic, geographic, or demographic characteristics; here 12% lack reliable access, concentrated by income.
  • IOC-1.C.4: the divide raises issues of equity and access. Moving required resources online-only shifts the burden onto students with the least access.
  • Equal treatment (same portal) is not equitable treatment when starting conditions differ; limited library hours constrain the workaround.
  • IOC-1.C.5: actions of organizations, like this district, affect the divide, for better or worse.
Option A is wrong because the mere existence of one access point does not provide meaningful, timely access for all students. Option B confuses equal treatment with equity; identical resources given to students with unequal access produces unequal outcomes. Option C is wrong because the divide explicitly includes Internet access, which is exactly what the 12% lack.
  • IOC-1.C.2：数字鸿沟是基于社会经济、地理或人口特征的计算设备与互联网接入差异；本例中 12% 的学生缺乏可靠接入，且按收入集中分布。
  • IOC-1.C.4：数字鸿沟引发公平与接入问题。把必需资源改为仅在线，会把负担转嫁给接入条件最差的学生。
  • 相同对待（同样的门户）不等于公平对待，因为起点条件不同；图书馆有限的开放时间也限制了这一替代方案。
  • IOC-1.C.5：组织（如本学区）的行动会影响数字鸿沟，可能使之好转或恶化。
选项 A 错误，因为仅仅存在一个接入点并不能为所有学生提供有意义且及时的接入。选项 B 混淆了平等对待与公平，把相同资源给予接入条件不同的学生会产生不平等的结果。选项 C 错误，因为数字鸿沟明确包括互联网接入，而这正是那 12% 学生所缺乏的。
Q5HARDAP MC5.3 Computing Bias

A company builds a résumé-screening algorithm trained on ten years of its own hiring data. Historically, the company hired mostly men for engineering roles. After deployment, the algorithm scores résumés from graduates of women's colleges significantly lower, even when their skills match those of highly scored candidates. The programmers insist they never wrote any rule about gender.某公司构建了一个简历筛选算法，用公司自己十年的招聘数据进行训练。历史上，该公司工程岗位录用的主要是男性。部署后，该算法给女子学院毕业生的简历打分明显偏低，即使她们的技能与高分候选人相当。程序员坚称他们从未编写过任何与性别有关的规则。

Which statement best explains this outcome?哪项陈述最能解释这一结果？

  • (A) The algorithm cannot be biased because no programmer wrote a gender rule into the code该算法不可能有偏见，因为没有程序员在代码中写入性别规则
  • (B) The bias must have been introduced by the résumé applicants themselves, so the company has no way to reduce it偏见一定是由简历申请人自己引入的，因此公司无法减少它
  • (C) Machine learning systems are objective by definition, so the lower scores reflect real skill differences机器学习系统按定义就是客观的，因此较低的分数反映了真实的技能差异
  • (D) Bias can enter through the data used by an innovation, not only through explicitly written rules; historical hiring patterns biased the training data偏见不仅可以通过显式编写的规则进入系统，还可以通过创新所使用的数据进入；历史招聘模式使训练数据带上了偏见
Answer:答案： (D)
  • IOC-1.D.1: computing innovations can reflect existing human biases because of biases written into the algorithms OR biases in the data used by the innovation.
  • Here the training data encodes a decade of mostly-male hiring, so the model learned patterns correlated with that history, including proxies like women's colleges.
  • IOC-1.D.2: programmers should take action to reduce bias, for example by auditing training data and testing outcomes across groups.
  • IOC-1.D.3: biases can be embedded at all levels of software development, including data collection.
Option A is wrong because bias does not require an explicit rule; biased data is sufficient. Option B is wrong because the applicants did nothing to cause the pattern, and IOC-1.D.2 says programmers should act to reduce bias. Option C is wrong because models trained on human data inherit human biases; they are not objective by definition.
  • IOC-1.D.1：计算创新可能反映现存的人类偏见，原因既可以是写入算法的偏见，也可以是创新所用数据中的偏见。
  • 本例中，训练数据记录了十年来以男性为主的录用历史，因此模型学到了与这段历史相关的模式，包括像女子学院这样的代理特征。
  • IOC-1.D.2：程序员应采取行动减少偏见，例如审计训练数据并跨群体测试结果。
  • IOC-1.D.3：偏见可能嵌入软件开发的各个层面，包括数据收集环节。
选项 A 错误，因为偏见不需要显式规则，有偏的数据就足够了。选项 B 错误，因为申请人并未造成这种模式，且 IOC-1.D.2 指出程序员应采取行动减少偏见。选项 C 错误，因为在人类数据上训练的模型会继承人类的偏见，它们并非按定义客观。
Q6HARDAP MC5.3 Computing Bias

A hospital uses an algorithm to identify patients who need extra care. The algorithm predicts each patient's future health care needs from their past health care spending. An audit finds that Black patients with the same illness severity as white patients are consistently scored as needing less care, because historically less money was spent on Black patients at equal levels of illness.某医院使用一种算法来识别需要额外护理的患者。该算法根据患者过去的医疗支出预测其未来的医疗需求。审计发现，病情严重程度相同的黑人患者与白人患者相比，一直被评分为需要更少的护理，因为历史上在相同病情下，花在黑人患者身上的医疗费用更少。

Which change would most directly reduce the bias in this system, consistent with the AP CSP framework?根据 AP CSP 框架，哪项改动能最直接地减少该系统的偏见？

  • (A) Keep the spending variable but add more years of the same spending data so the model has more examples保留支出变量，但增加更多年份的同类支出数据，让模型有更多样本
  • (B) Hide the race field from the algorithm so it cannot see each patient's race向算法隐藏种族字段，使它看不到每位患者的种族
  • (C) Replace past spending as the target variable with a direct measure of illness or health need, and test outcomes across patient groups用直接的疾病或健康需求指标替换以过去支出作为目标变量，并跨患者群体测试结果
  • (D) Add a rule that manually raises scores for some patients to cancel out the pattern添加一条规则，手动提高部分患者的分数以抵消该模式
Answer:答案： (C)
  • The root cause is the choice of target variable: past spending is a biased proxy for health need because historical spending was unequal at equal illness.
  • IOC-1.D.1: bias can come from the data used by the innovation. Spending data encodes systemic inequity.
  • IOC-1.D.2: programmers should take action to reduce bias. Using a direct health-need measure removes the biased proxy, and auditing outcomes across groups verifies improvement.
Option A is wrong because more of the same biased data reinforces the pattern instead of reducing it. Option B is a common trap: hiding the race field does not address the cause: the biased proxy (past spending) remains, and proxies can reintroduce bias even when a protected attribute such as race is hidden. Option D is wrong because ad hoc manual adjustments do not fix the underlying biased target and can introduce new unfairness.
  • 根本原因在于目标变量的选择：过去支出是健康需求的有偏代理，因为历史上相同病情下的支出就是不平等的。
  • IOC-1.D.1：偏见可能来自创新所使用的数据。支出数据编码了系统性的不平等。
  • IOC-1.D.2：程序员应采取行动减少偏见。使用直接的健康需求指标可以去除有偏代理，并跨群体审计结果以验证改进。
选项 A 错误，因为更多同样有偏的数据只会强化而非减少该模式。选项 B 是常见陷阱：隐藏种族字段并没有解决根源：有偏的代理变量（过去支出）仍然存在，即使隐藏了种族等受保护属性，代理变量仍会重新引入偏见。选项 D 错误，因为临时的手动调整并不能修复底层有偏的目标变量，还可能引入新的不公平。
Q7MEDIUMAP MC5.4 Crowdsourcing

A research team studying bird migration builds a mobile app that lets volunteers worldwide submit photos, locations, and timestamps of bird sightings from their own phones. Hundreds of thousands of volunteers contribute observations, and the team combines the submissions to map migration routes at a scale no single research group could fund.一个研究鸟类迁徙的团队开发了一款移动应用，让全球志愿者用自己的手机提交鸟类观测的照片、位置和时间戳。数十万志愿者贡献了观测数据，团队汇总这些提交内容，绘制出任何单一研究团队都无力资助的大规模迁徙路线图。

Which statement best classifies this project and explains why it works?哪项陈述最能对该项目的性质进行分类并解释其成功的原因？

  • (A) It is data mining, because the team extracts patterns from the submissions这是数据挖掘，因为团队从提交内容中提取模式
  • (B) It is crowdsourcing and citizen science: distributed individuals, many not scientists, contribute relevant data from their own computing devices, enabled by widespread Internet access这是众包（crowdsourcing）和公民科学（citizen science）：分布各地的个人（其中许多并非科学家）使用自己的计算设备贡献相关数据，这得益于互联网的普及
  • (C) It is symmetric encryption, because many people share the same app这是对称加密，因为许多人共享同一个应用
  • (D) It cannot produce reliable science because volunteers are not professional scientists它无法产生可靠的科学成果，因为志愿者不是专业科学家
Answer:答案： (B)
  • IOC-1.E.4: crowdsourcing is the practice of obtaining input or information from a large number of people via the Internet.
  • IOC-1.E.3: citizen science is research conducted in whole or in part by distributed individuals, many of whom may not be scientists, who contribute relevant data using their own computing devices. This project matches both definitions exactly.
  • IOC-1.E.1: widespread access to information and public data facilitates identifying problems, developing solutions, and sharing results.
Option A is wrong because data mining describes analysis technique, not the distributed data-collection model the question asks about. Option C is nonsense in context; encryption is unrelated to collecting observations. Option D is wrong because IOC-1.E.2 and IOC-1.E.3 confirm that citizen science is a real, effective model; scale and validation methods make the data useful.
  • IOC-1.E.4：众包是通过互联网从大量人群获取输入或信息的做法。
  • IOC-1.E.3：公民科学是全部或部分由分布各地的个人完成的科学研究，其中许多人并非科学家，他们使用自己的计算设备贡献相关数据。该项目与这两个定义完全吻合。
  • IOC-1.E.1：信息和公共数据的广泛获取促进了问题的识别、解决方案的开发和成果的传播。

  • 因此 Ben 用 Amara 公布的公钥加密，只有 Amara 保密的私钥才能解密该文件。
  • IOC-2.B.6：证书颁发机构签发数字证书，基于信任模型验证安全通信中所用加密密钥的所有权。银行网站向 Amara 出示的证书正是起这个作用。
  • 相比之下，对称密钥加密（symmetric key encryption）使用同一把共享密钥进行加密和解密，是不同的方案。
选项 A 错误，因为发送者从不需要接收者的私钥，分享私钥会彻底破坏该方案的安全性。选项 B 混淆了公钥加密与对称加密，二者是不同的方案。选项 D 错误，因为公钥只能加密，解密需要私钥，而截获者没有私钥。
Q15HARDAP MC5.6 Safe Computing

A small company wants to reduce the risk of unauthorized access to its laptops and servers. The IT lead proposes four measures. Measure 1: install and update virus and malware scanning software Measure 2: apply operating system and application updates promptly Measure 3: require strong passwords and multifactor authentication Measure 4: connect to any available open Wi-Fi network to save mobile data costs一家小公司希望降低其笔记本电脑和服务器被未经授权访问的风险。IT 负责人提出了四项措施。 措施 1：安装并更新病毒和恶意软件扫描程序 措施 2：及时安装操作系统和应用程序更新 措施 3：要求使用强密码和多因素身份验证 措施 4：为节省移动数据费用，连接任何可用的开放 Wi-Fi 网络

Which of these measures would help protect the company's computing resources, and which would not?这些措施中，哪些有助于保护公司的计算资源，哪一项无助于保护？

  • (A) Measures 1, 2, and 3 help; measure 4 increases risk because data sent over public networks can be intercepted, for example through a rogue access point措施 1、2、3 有帮助；措施 4 会增加风险，因为在公共网络上传输的数据可能被截获，例如通过恶意接入点（rogue access point）
  • (B) Only measure 3 helps; scanning software and updates are unrelated to unauthorized access只有措施 3 有帮助；扫描软件和系统更新与未经授权的访问无关
  • (C) All four measures help, because open Wi-Fi is encrypted by default四项措施都有帮助，因为开放 Wi-Fi 默认是加密的
  • (D) Measures 1 and 4 help; updates usually introduce new bugs, so measure 2 should be avoided措施 1 和 4 有帮助；更新通常会引入新缺陷，所以应避免措施 2
Answer:答案： (A)
  • IOC-2.B.7: virus and malware scanning software can help protect a computing system against infection. Measure 1 helps.
  • IOC-2.B.10: all real-world systems have errors or design flaws that can be exploited, and regular software updates help fix errors that could compromise a system. Measure 2 helps.
  • IOC-2.B.1: authentication measures such as strong passwords and multifactor authentication protect devices and information from unauthorized access. Measure 3 helps.
  • IOC-2.C.3 and IOC-2.C.4: data sent over public networks can be intercepted, analyzed, and modified, for example through a rogue access point giving unauthorized access to secure networks. Measure 4 increases risk rather than reducing it.
Option B is wrong because scanning and updates directly address malware and exploitable flaws. Option C is wrong because open Wi-Fi is not encrypted by default, and rogue access points are a named threat. Option D is wrong because updates fix security flaws, and open Wi-Fi does not help.
  • IOC-2.B.7：病毒和恶意软件扫描程序可以帮助保护计算系统免受感染。措施 1 有帮助。
  • IOC-2.B.10：所有真实系统都存在可被利用的错误或设计缺陷，定期更新软件有助于修复可能危及系统的错误。措施 2 有帮助。
  • IOC-2.B.1：强密码和多因素身份验证等身份验证措施可以保护设备和信息免受未经授权的访问。措施 3 有帮助。
  • IOC-2.C.3 和 IOC-2.C.4：在公共网络上传输的数据可能被截获、分析和篡改，例如通过能未经授权访问安全网络的恶意接入点。措施 4 会增加而非降低风险。
选项 B 错误，因为扫描和更新直接针对恶意软件和可被利用的缺陷。选项 C 错误，因为开放 Wi-Fi 默认并不加密，恶意接入点是被明确点名的威胁。选项 D 错误，因为更新修复的是安全漏洞，而开放 Wi-Fi 毫无帮助。
