Equivalent Expressions: Factor, Expand, Combine等价表达式：分解、展开、合并

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Define equivalent expressions and test whether two expressions are equivalent.
  • Expand products and combine like terms without sign errors.
  • Factor out a greatest common factor and factor quadratics by inspection.
  • Recognise a difference of squares and a perfect square trinomial and use them in both directions.
  • Use the structure of an expression, treating a chunk as one symbol, to avoid solving for every variable.
  • 定义等价表达式 (equivalent expressions)，并判断两个表达式是否等价。
  • 展开乘积并合并同类项 (like terms)，不出符号错误。
  • 提取最大公因式，并用观察法分解二次式。
  • 识别平方差 (difference of squares) 与完全平方 (perfect square) 三项式，并正反两个方向使用。
  • 利用表达式的结构 (structure)，把一块式子当作一个整体，避免把每个变量都解出来。

What equivalent expressions are什么是等价表达式

Two expressions are equivalent expressions when they give the same value for every allowed value of the variable. $2(x+3)$ and $2x+6$ are equivalent; $x^2+9$ and $(x+3)^2$ are not, because at $x=1$ they give $10$ and $16$. The first Advanced Math testing point is exactly this skill: rewrite an expression in a different form that makes a feature visible, such as a root, a common factor, or a vertex.

若变量取任何允许的值时两个表达式的值都相同，它们就是等价表达式 (equivalent expressions)。$2(x+3)$ 与 $2x+6$ 等价；$x^2+9$ 与 $(x+3)^2$ 不等价，因为 $x=1$ 时分别得 $10$ 和 $16$。Advanced Math 的第一个考查点正是这项技能：把表达式改写成另一种形式，使根、公因式或顶点之类的特征一目了然。

Expand means multiply out brackets with the distributive property; factor means write a sum as a product. They are inverse moves. Combine like terms (same variable part) by adding coefficients only: $3x^2+5x^2=8x^2$, but $3x^2+5x$ cannot be combined.

展开 (expand) 是用分配律去括号；因式分解 (factor) 是把和写成积。两者互为逆运算。合并同类项时只把系数相加：$3x^2+5x^2=8x^2$，但 $3x^2+5x$ 不能合并。

Rewriting using structure利用结构改写

A difference of squares is any expression of the form $a^2-b^2$, where $a$ and $b$ can themselves be expressions: $9x^2-16=(3x)^2-4^2=(3x-4)(3x+4)$. A perfect square trinomial has first and last terms that are squares and a middle term equal to twice their product: $4x^2+20x+25=(2x+5)^2$ because $2\cdot 2x\cdot 5=20x$. Seeing the pattern is the point; both are structure that is hidden until you look at the exponents and coefficients.

平方差 (difference of squares) 是形如 $a^2-b^2$ 的式子，其中 $a$、$b$ 本身也可以是表达式：$9x^2-16=(3x)^2-4^2=(3x-4)(3x+4)$。完全平方 (perfect square) 三项式的首末项是平方，中间项等于两者乘积的两倍：$4x^2+20x+25=(2x+5)^2$，因为 $2\cdot 2x\cdot 5=20x$。关键是认出模式；这两种结构只有看指数和系数时才显现。

Structure also lets you skip work. If a question asks for $(x+y)^2$ and gives $x^2+y^2$ and $xy$, you never need $x$ and $y$ separately. Likewise $x^4-16$ is a difference of squares with $a=x^2$ and $b=4$, so it factors again: $(x^2-4)(x^2+4)=(x-2)(x+2)(x^2+4)$.

利用结构还能省去计算。若题目要求 $(x+y)^2$，并给出 $x^2+y^2$ 和 $xy$，就不必单独求出 $x$ 和 $y$。同理 $x^4-16$ 是 $a=x^2$、$b=4$ 的平方差，还能继续分解：$(x^2-4)(x^2+4)=(x-2)(x+2)(x^2+4)$。

Worked Example: expand and combine like terms例题：展开并合并同类项

Write $(3x-2)(x+4)-(x^2-5x+1)$ as a single polynomial in standard form.

  1. Expand the product: $(3x-2)(x+4)=3x^2+12x-2x-8=3x^2+10x-8$.
  2. Distribute the minus sign over the bracket: $-(x^2-5x+1)=-x^2+5x-1$.
  3. Combine like terms: $3x^2-x^2=2x^2$, $10x+5x=15x$, $-8-1=-9$.
  4. Result: $2x^2+15x-9$.

Why it works: subtracting a bracket flips every sign inside it. Check with $x=1$: the original is $(1)(5)-(1-5+1)=5+3=8$ and $2+15-9=8$.

把 $(3x-2)(x+4)-(x^2-5x+1)$ 写成标准形式的单个多项式。

  1. 展开乘积：$(3x-2)(x+4)=3x^2+12x-2x-8=3x^2+10x-8$。
  2. 把负号分配到括号内：$-(x^2-5x+1)=-x^2+5x-1$。
  3. 合并同类项：$3x^2-x^2=2x^2$，$10x+5x=15x$，$-8-1=-9$。
  4. 结果：$2x^2+15x-9$。

原理：减去一个括号会使括号内每一项变号。用 $x=1$ 检验：原式为 $(1)(5)-(1-5+1)=5+3=8$，而 $2+15-9=8$。

Worked Example: factor with a common factor and a difference of squares例题：提公因式并用平方差分解

Factor $12x^2-27$ completely.

  1. Pull out the greatest common factor $3$: $12x^2-27=3(4x^2-9)$.
  2. Spot the structure: $4x^2=(2x)^2$ and $9=3^2$, so $4x^2-9=(2x-3)(2x+3)$.
  3. Result: $3(2x-3)(2x+3)$.

Why it works: always take the common factor first; otherwise the difference of squares is hidden. Expanding back gives $3(4x^2-9)=12x^2-27$.

把 $12x^2-27$ 分解到不能再分。

  1. 先提取最大公因数 $3$：$12x^2-27=3(4x^2-9)$。
  2. 识别结构：$4x^2=(2x)^2$，$9=3^2$，所以 $4x^2-9=(2x-3)(2x+3)$。
  3. 结果：$3(2x-3)(2x+3)$。

原理：总是先提公因式，否则平方差会被掩盖。展开回去得 $3(4x^2-9)=12x^2-27$。

Worked Example: use structure instead of solving for the variables例题：利用结构而不是求变量

If $x^2+y^2=29$ and $xy=10$, what is the value of $(x+y)^2$?

  1. Expand: $(x+y)^2=x^2+2xy+y^2$.
  2. Group the known pieces: $(x^2+y^2)+2xy=29+2(10)$.
  3. Result: $29+20=49$.

Why it works: the question asks for a combination that the givens already contain. Solving for $x$ and $y$ would take far longer and is unnecessary. (For instance $x=2,\,y=5$ and $x=5,\,y=2$ both give $49$.)

若 $x^2+y^2=29$ 且 $xy=10$，求 $(x+y)^2$ 的值。

  1. 展开：$(x+y)^2=x^2+2xy+y^2$。
  2. 把已知部分合并：$(x^2+y^2)+2xy=29+2(10)$。
  3. 结果：$29+20=49$。

原理：题目所求的组合，已知条件已经包含。把 $x$、$y$ 解出来既慢又没必要。（例如 $x=2,\,y=5$ 与 $x=5,\,y=2$ 都得 $49$。）

Common Mistake: sign and square errors
  • Writing $(x+3)^2=x^2+9$. The middle term $6x$ is missing; a square of a sum is never the sum of squares.
  • Forgetting to distribute a minus sign: $-(x-4)=-x-4$ is wrong; it is $-x+4$.
  • Calling $x^2+16$ a difference of squares. The sum $x^2+16$ does not factor over the real numbers.
常见错误：符号与平方错误
  • 写成 $(x+3)^2=x^2+9$。漏掉了中间项 $6x$；和的平方绝不等于平方的和。
  • 忘记分配负号：$-(x-4)=-x-4$ 是错的，应为 $-x+4$。
  • 把 $x^2+16$ 当作平方差。平方和 $x^2+16$ 在实数范围内不能分解。

Quadratic Equations: Factoring, Square Roots, the Formula二次方程：因式分解、开平方与求根公式

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Solve a quadratic equation by factoring with the zero product property.
  • Solve $(x-h)^2=c$ type equations by taking square roots, remembering both signs.
  • Use completing the square to solve a quadratic equation and to find a vertex.
  • Apply the quadratic formula and simplify radical answers.
  • Use the discriminant $b^2-4ac$ to decide between two real solutions, one real solution, and no real solutions.
  • 用因式分解 (factoring) 和零积性质 (zero product property) 解二次方程 (quadratic equation)。
  • 对形如 $(x-h)^2=c$ 的方程开平方，并记得取正负两个值。
  • 用配方法 (completing the square) 解二次方程并求顶点。
  • 运用求根公式 (quadratic formula) 并化简根式答案。
  • 用判别式 (discriminant) $b^2-4ac$ 判断有两个实数解、一个实数解还是无实数解。

Three ways to solve, one standard form三种解法，同一标准形式

A quadratic equation in standard form is $ax^2+bx+c=0$ with $a\neq 0$. Get everything on one side first. The zero product property says that if $m\cdot n=0$ then $m=0$ or $n=0$; this is why factoring works. To solve $x^2-5x-14=0$ you look for two numbers with product $-14$ and sum $-5$.

二次方程的标准形式是 $ax^2+bx+c=0$（$a\neq 0$）。先把所有项移到一侧。零积性质说明：若 $m\cdot n=0$，则 $m=0$ 或 $n=0$；这就是因式分解有效的原因。解 $x^2-5x-14=0$ 时，找两个积为 $-14$、和为 $-5$ 的数。

When the equation is already a square, take square roots and keep both signs: $(x-4)^2=25$ gives $x-4=5$ or $x-4=-5$. When it does not factor, completing the square rewrites $x^2+bx$ as $(x+\tfrac b2)^2-\left(\tfrac b2\right)^2$. The same move turns standard form into vertex form, which Section 2.4 uses.

若方程已是平方形式，就开平方并保留正负号：$(x-4)^2=25$ 得 $x-4=5$ 或 $x-4=-5$。若不能因式分解，配方把 $x^2+bx$ 改写成 $(x+\tfrac b2)^2-\left(\tfrac b2\right)^2$。同样的步骤把标准形式变成顶点式，第 2.4 节会用到。

For $ax^2+bx+c=0$: $x=\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$
Discriminant $D=b^2-4ac$: $D\gt 0$ two real solutions; $D=0$ one real solution; $D\lt 0$ no real solutions.

The discriminant is the part under the square root. It is the fastest way to answer questions that ask how many solutions there are, or for what value of a constant there is exactly one solution, because you never need the solutions themselves. Geometrically (Figure 2.1) the discriminant tells you whether the parabola crosses, touches, or misses the x-axis.

判别式就是根号下的部分。对于问有几个解，或常数取何值时恰有一个解的题目，它是最快的方法，因为根本不需要求出解。从几何上看（图 2.1），判别式说明抛物线与 x 轴是相交、相切还是不相交。

Worked Example: solve by factoring例题：用因式分解求解

Solve $x^2-5x-14=0$.

  1. Find two numbers with product $-14$ and sum $-5$: $-7$ and $2$.
  2. Factor: $(x-7)(x+2)=0$.
  3. Zero product property: $x-7=0$ or $x+2=0$, so $x=7$ or $x=-2$.

Check: $49-35-14=0$ and $4+10-14=0$.

解 $x^2-5x-14=0$。

  1. 找两个积为 $-14$、和为 $-5$ 的数：$-7$ 和 $2$。
  2. 分解：$(x-7)(x+2)=0$。
  3. 零积性质：$x-7=0$ 或 $x+2=0$，所以 $x=7$ 或 $x=-2$。

检验：$49-35-14=0$，$4+10-14=0$。

Worked Example: complete the square例题：配方

Solve $x^2+6x-4=0$ by completing the square.

  1. Move the constant: $x^2+6x=4$.
  2. Add $\left(\tfrac62\right)^2=9$ to both sides: $x^2+6x+9=13$.
  3. Write the left side as a square: $(x+3)^2=13$.
  4. Take square roots: $x+3=\pm\sqrt{13}$, so $x=-3\pm\sqrt{13}$ (about $0.606$ and $-6.606$).

Why it works: adding the square of half the coefficient builds a perfect square trinomial, which collapses to one squared bracket.

用配方法解 $x^2+6x-4=0$。

  1. 移项：$x^2+6x=4$。
  2. 两边加上 $\left(\tfrac62\right)^2=9$：$x^2+6x+9=13$。
  3. 左边写成平方：$(x+3)^2=13$。
  4. 开平方：$x+3=\pm\sqrt{13}$，所以 $x=-3\pm\sqrt{13}$（约 $0.606$ 与 $-6.606$）。

原理：加上一次项系数一半的平方，就构成完全平方三项式，从而合成一个平方括号。

Worked Example: quadratic formula with an irrational answer例题：求根公式与无理数答案

Solve $3x^2-4x-1=0$.

  1. Identify $a=3$, $b=-4$, $c=-1$.
  2. Discriminant: $D=(-4)^2-4(3)(-1)=16+12=28\gt 0$, so there are two real solutions.
  3. $x=\dfrac{4\pm\sqrt{28}}{6}=\dfrac{4\pm 2\sqrt7}{6}=\dfrac{2\pm\sqrt7}{3}$.
  4. Numerically $x\approx 1.549$ or $x\approx -0.215$.

Why it works: $\sqrt{28}=2\sqrt7$ lets you cancel a common factor of $2$ in the numerator and denominator.

解 $3x^2-4x-1=0$。

  1. 确定 $a=3$，$b=-4$，$c=-1$。
  2. 判别式：$D=(-4)^2-4(3)(-1)=16+12=28\gt 0$，所以有两个实数解。
  3. $x=\dfrac{4\pm\sqrt{28}}{6}=\dfrac{4\pm 2\sqrt7}{6}=\dfrac{2\pm\sqrt7}{3}$。
  4. 数值上 $x\approx 1.549$ 或 $x\approx -0.215$。

原理：$\sqrt{28}=2\sqrt7$，使分子分母能约去公因数 $2$。

Worked Example: use the discriminant with an unknown constant例题：对含未知常数的方程用判别式

For which positive value of $k$ does $x^2+kx+9=0$ have exactly one real solution? For which values of $k$ does it have no real solutions?

  1. Discriminant: $D=k^2-4(1)(9)=k^2-36$.
  2. Exactly one solution needs $D=0$: $k^2=36$, so $k=6$ or $k=-6$; the positive value is $k=6$.
  3. No real solutions needs $D\lt 0$: $k^2\lt 36$, so $-6\lt k\lt 6$.

Check: with $k=6$, $x^2+6x+9=(x+3)^2$ has the single solution $x=-3$.

$k$ 取哪个正值时 $x^2+kx+9=0$ 恰有一个实数解？$k$ 取哪些值时无实数解？

  1. 判别式：$D=k^2-4(1)(9)=k^2-36$。
  2. 恰有一个解需 $D=0$：$k^2=36$，所以 $k=6$ 或 $k=-6$；正值为 $k=6$。
  3. 无实数解需 $D\lt 0$：$k^2\lt 36$，所以 $-6\lt k\lt 6$。

检验：$k=6$ 时 $x^2+6x+9=(x+3)^2$，只有唯一解 $x=-3$。

A line and a parabola直线与抛物线

A system asks for the points that satisfy both equations. For a line and a parabola, substitute the expression for $y$ from the line into the quadratic. You get one quadratic in $x$, whose solutions are the x-coordinates of the intersections; put each back into the line (the simpler equation) to find $y$.

方程组要求同时满足两个方程的点。对直线与抛物线，把直线的 $y$ 表达式代入二次式，得到一个关于 $x$ 的二次方程，其解就是交点的横坐标；再代入直线（较简单的方程）求出 $y$。

Because the substitution produces a quadratic, a line and a parabola can meet in two points, one point (the line is tangent), or no points, and the discriminant of that quadratic counts them exactly as in Section 2.2. A line and a circle behave the same way. In a question that says "exactly one solution", set the discriminant of the combined quadratic equal to zero.

由于代入后得到二次方程，直线与抛物线可相交于两点、一点（直线相切）或没有交点，该二次方程的判别式像第 2.2 节一样给出个数。直线与圆的情形相同。若题目说“恰有一个解”，就令合并后二次方程的判别式等于零。

Radical and rational equations根式方程与分式方程

In a radical equation such as $\sqrt{x+7}=x+1$, isolate the radical, then square both sides. Squaring is not reversible: it also turns $\sqrt{u}=-v$, which has no solution when $v\gt 0$, into $u=v^2$. So an extraneous solution can appear, and every candidate must be substituted back into the original equation.

在根式方程 (radical equation) 如 $\sqrt{x+7}=x+1$ 中，先孤立根式，再两边平方。平方不可逆：它会把 $v\gt 0$ 时无解的 $\sqrt{u}=-v$ 也变成 $u=v^2$。因此可能出现增根 (extraneous solution)，每个候选解都必须代回原方程检验。

A rational equation has the unknown in a denominator. Multiply both sides by the common denominator, solve, and then discard any value that makes an original denominator zero, since division by zero is undefined. Such a value is extraneous too.

分式方程 (rational equation) 的未知数在分母中。两边乘以公分母求解，再舍去使原分母为零的值，因为除以零无意义。这样的值同样是增根。

Solve $\sqrt{x+7}=x+1$.

  3. Check $x=2$: $\sqrt{9}=3$ and $2+1=3$. It works.
  4. Check $x=-3$: $\sqrt{4}=2$ but $-3+1=-2$. It fails, so $x=-3$ is extraneous.
  5. Solution: $x=2$ only.

Quadratic functions: vertex form, zeros, axis of symmetry二次函数：顶点式、零点、对称轴

A quadratic function can be written in vertex form $f(x)=a(x-h)^2+k$. The vertex is $(h,k)$, the axis of symmetry is the vertical line $x=h$, and the parabola opens up when $a\gt 0$ (then $k$ is the minimum) and down when $a\lt 0$ (then $k$ is the maximum). From standard form $ax^2+bx+c$, the vertex has $x=-\dfrac{b}{2a}$; substitute to get $k$. Completing the square (Section 2.2) converts standard form into vertex form.

二次函数可写成顶点式 (vertex form) $f(x)=a(x-h)^2+k$。顶点是 $(h,k)$，对称轴是竖直线 $x=h$；$a\gt 0$ 时开口向上（$k$ 为最小值），$a\lt 0$ 时开口向下（$k$ 为最大值）。由标准形式 $ax^2+bx+c$，顶点横坐标为 $x=-\dfrac{b}{2a}$，代入求 $k$。配方（第 2.2 节）把标准形式变为顶点式。

The zeros (also called roots) of $f$ are the x-values where $f(x)=0$, the x-intercepts of the graph. Two zeros are always the same distance from the axis of symmetry, so the axis is the midpoint of the zeros: with zeros $1$ and $5$ the axis is $x=3$. That gives a fast route to the vertex without completing the square.

$f$ 的零点（也叫根）是使 $f(x)=0$ 的 x 值，即图像的 x 轴截距。两个零点到对称轴的距离相等，所以对称轴是两零点的中点：零点为 $1$ 和 $5$ 时对称轴为 $x=3$。这是不配方就得到顶点的快捷方法。

Polynomial functions: factors, zeros, end behavior多项式函数：因式、零点、端点行为

A polynomial is a sum of terms $a_nx^n$ with whole-number exponents; its degree is the highest exponent. The remainder theorem says the remainder when $p(x)$ is divided by $x-c$ equals $p(c)$. The factor theorem follows: $x-c$ is a factor of $p(x)$ exactly when $p(c)=0$, that is, when $c$ is a zero. So testing small integer values of $c$ both finds factors and avoids long division.

多项式 (polynomial) 是指数为非负整数的项 $a_nx^n$ 之和，次数 (degree) 是最高指数。余数定理 (remainder theorem) 说：$p(x)$ 除以 $x-c$ 的余数等于 $p(c)$。由此得因式定理 (factor theorem)：当且仅当 $p(c)=0$，即 $c$ 是零点时，$x-c$ 才是 $p(x)$ 的因式。所以试几个小整数 $c$ 既能找到因式，又能避免长除法。

Odd degree: the ends go opposite ways (rising on the right if the leading coefficient is positive). The number of times the graph can cross the x-axis is at most the degree.

The absolute value $|u|$ is the distance of $u$ from $0$, so $|u|=c$ with $c\gt 0$ means $u=c$ or $u=-c$: two cases (two equations). If $c=0$ there is one solution and if $c\lt 0$ there is none. The graph of $f(x)=|x-h|+k$ is a V with vertex $(h,k)$; it opens up and its minimum value is $k$. When the right side contains $x$, solve both cases and then check each answer in the original equation, because the right side must be non-negative.

绝对值 (absolute value) $|u|$ 是 $u$ 到 $0$ 的距离，所以当 $c\gt 0$ 时 $|u|=c$ 意味着 $u=c$ 或 $u=-c$：两种情形 (two cases，即两个方程)。$c=0$ 时只有一个解，$c\lt 0$ 时无解。$f(x)=|x-h|+k$ 的图像是顶点为 $(h,k)$ 的 V 形，开口向上，最小值为 $k$。若右边含 $x$，两种情形都要解，然后把每个答案代回原方程检验，因为右边必须非负。

Exponential growth and decay指数增长与衰减

An exponential function has the variable in the exponent: $f(x)=a\cdot b^x$ with $a\neq 0$, $b\gt 0$, $b\neq 1$. The number $a$ is the initial value ($f(0)=a$). When $b\gt 1$ the function shows growth and $b$ is the growth factor; when $0\lt b\lt 1$ it shows decay and $b$ is the decay factor. A quantity that changes by a fixed percent each period is exponential, not linear: a fixed amount each period would be linear.

指数函数的变量在指数位置：$f(x)=a\cdot b^x$，$a\neq 0$，$b\gt 0$，$b\neq 1$。$a$ 是初始值（$f(0)=a$）。$b\gt 1$ 时为增长，$b$ 是增长因子；$0\lt b\lt 1$ 时为衰减，$b$ 是衰减因子。每期按固定百分数变化的量是指数型而非线性；每期变化固定数量才是线性。

Percent change
Change of $r$ per period (as a decimal) means $b=1+r$. Growth of $8\%$ gives $b=1.08$; loss of $15\%$ gives $b=1-0.15=0.85$.
百分数变化
每期变化 $r$（小数）意味着 $b=1+r$。增长 $8\%$ 得 $b=1.08$；损失 $15\%$ 得 $b=1-0.15=0.85$。

Exponent rules and rational exponents指数法则与有理指数

The product rule is $x^m\cdot x^n=x^{m+n}$ and the quotient rule is $x^m/x^n=x^{m-n}$. The power rule is $(x^m)^n=x^{mn}$, and $(xy)^n=x^ny^n$. A negative exponent flips: $x^{-n}=1/x^n$. A rational exponent (also called a fractional exponent) combines a power and a root: $x^{m/n}=\left(\sqrt[n]{x}\right)^m=\sqrt[n]{x^m}$. So $8^{2/3}=(\sqrt[3]{8})^2=2^2=4$.

乘法法则 (product rule) 是 $x^m\cdot x^n=x^{m+n}$，除法法则是 $x^m/x^n=x^{m-n}$。幂的法则 (power rule) 是 $(x^m)^n=x^{mn}$，以及 $(xy)^n=x^ny^n$。负指数取倒数：$x^{-n}=1/x^n$。有理指数 (rational exponent，也叫fractional exponent) 把乘方与开方合在一起：$x^{m/n}=\left(\sqrt[n]{x}\right)^m=\sqrt[n]{x^m}$。所以 $8^{2/3}=(\sqrt[3]{8})^2=2^2=4$。

To solve an equation with the unknown in an exponent, write both sides with the same base and equate the exponents. Take the root first when you evaluate a rational exponent; the numbers stay small.

解未知数在指数位置的方程时，把两边写成同一底数，再令指数相等。计算有理指数时先开方，数字会保持较小。

Transformations of graphs图像的变换

A transformation changes a graph in a predictable way. A shift (also called a translation) moves it without changing its shape: $f(x)+k$ moves it up $k$, and $f(x-h)$ moves it right $h$. The sign inside the bracket works against intuition: $f(x+3)$ moves the graph left $3$, because the input must be $3$ smaller to give the same output. A reflection flips it: $-f(x)$ in the x-axis and $f(-x)$ in the y-axis. A factor $a$ outside stretches vertically; a factor $b$ inside compresses horizontally by $\frac1b$.

变换 (transformation) 以可预测的方式改变图像。平移 (shift/translation) 不改变形状：$f(x)+k$ 向上移 $k$，$f(x-h)$ 向右移 $h$。括号内的符号与直觉相反：$f(x+3)$ 使图像向左移 $3$，因为输入要小 $3$ 才能得到同样的输出。反射 (reflection) 把图像翻折：$-f(x)$ 关于 x 轴，$f(-x)$ 关于 y 轴。外部的因数 $a$ 做竖直拉伸；内部的因数 $b$ 使图像水平压缩为原来的 $\frac1b$。

Composition of functions复合函数

A composition feeds the output of one function into another. $f(g(x))$ means apply $g$ first, then $f$: replace every $x$ in $f$ with the whole expression $g(x)$. Order matters; in general $f(g(x))\neq g(f(x))$. A function table or a point works the same way: find $g(2)$, then apply $f$ to that result.

复合 (composition) 把一个函数的输出作为另一个函数的输入。$f(g(x))$ 表示先作用 $g$ 再作用 $f$：把 $f$ 中每个 $x$ 都换成整个表达式 $g(x)$。顺序很重要；一般 $f(g(x))\neq g(f(x))$。函数表或一个点也一样：先求 $g(2)$，再把 $f$ 作用于该结果。

Worked Example: shift a function and track a point例题：平移函数并追踪一个点

Let $f(x)=x^2-2x$ and $g(x)=f(x+2)-1$. Write $g(x)$ as a formula and say where the point $(4,8)$ of $f$ goes.

  1. $f(x+2)=(x+2)^2-2(x+2)=x^2+4x+4-2x-4=x^2+2x$.
  2. Subtract $1$: $g(x)=x^2+2x-1$.
  3. $g$ is $f$ shifted $2$ left and $1$ down, so $(4,8)$ moves to $(2,7)$.
  4. Check: $g(2)=4+4-1=7$.

Why it works: $g(2)=f(4)-1=8-1=7$; the input shift of $+2$ means the old point $x=4$ now appears at $x=2$.

设 $f(x)=x^2-2x$，$g(x)=f(x+2)-1$。把 $g(x)$ 写成公式，并说明 $f$ 上的点 $(4,8)$ 移到哪里。

  1. $f(x+2)=(x+2)^2-2(x+2)=x^2+4x+4-2x-4=x^2+2x$。
  2. 减 $1$：$g(x)=x^2+2x-1$。
  3. $g$ 是 $f$ 向左平移 $2$、向下平移 $1$，所以 $(4,8)$ 移到 $(2,7)$。
  4. 检验：$g(2)=4+4-1=7$。

原理：$g(2)=f(4)-1=8-1=7$；输入处的 $+2$ 意味着原来 $x=4$ 的点现在出现在 $x=2$。

Worked Example: reflect and shift a vertex例题：反射并平移顶点

The graph of $f$ has vertex $(2,5)$. Find the vertex of $y=-f(x+1)+3$.

  1. $f(x+1)$ shifts left $1$: $(2,5)\to(1,5)$.
  2. The minus sign is a reflection in the x-axis: $(1,5)\to(1,-5)$.
  3. Adding $3$ shifts up $3$: $(1,-5)\to(1,-2)$.

The new vertex is $(1,-2)$. Why it works: apply the operations in the order the function applies them: inside shift first, then the outside reflection, then the outside shift.

$f$ 的图像顶点为 $(2,5)$。求 $y=-f(x+1)+3$ 的顶点。

  1. $f(x+1)$ 向左平移 $1$：$(2,5)\to(1,5)$。
  2. 负号是关于 x 轴的反射：$(1,5)\to(1,-5)$。
  3. 加 $3$ 向上平移 $3$：$(1,-5)\to(1,-2)$。

新顶点为 $(1,-2)$。原理：按函数运算的先后顺序施加操作：先是内部平移，然后外部反射，最后外部平移。

Worked Example: composition in both orders例题：两种次序的复合

Let $f(x)=2x+3$ and $g(x)=x^2-1$. Find $f(g(x))$, $g(f(x))$, and the values of $x$ with $f(g(x))=19$.

  1. $f(g(x))=2(x^2-1)+3=2x^2+1$.
  2. $g(f(x))=(2x+3)^2-1=4x^2+12x+8$.
  3. Numbers: $g(2)=3$, then $f(3)=9$; the formula gives $2(4)+1=9$.
  4. Solve $2x^2+1=19$: $x^2=9$, so $x=3$ or $x=-3$.

Why it works: the two compositions give different functions (for example at $x=1$ they give $3$ and $24$), so always check which function is on the inside.

设 $f(x)=2x+3$，$g(x)=x^2-1$。求 $f(g(x))$、$g(f(x))$，以及使 $f(g(x))=19$ 的 $x$。

  1. $f(g(x))=2(x^2-1)+3=2x^2+1$。
  2. $g(f(x))=(2x+3)^2-1=4x^2+12x+8$。
  3. 数值：$g(2)=3$，再 $f(3)=9$；公式给出 $2(4)+1=9$。
  4. 解 $2x^2+1=19$：$x^2=9$，所以 $x=3$ 或 $x=-3$。

原理：两种复合得到不同的函数（例如 $x=1$ 时分别为 $3$ 和 $24$），所以要始终确认哪个函数在内层。

Common Mistake: reading shifts and compositions backwards
  • Thinking $f(x+3)$ shifts right. It shifts left $3$.
  • Computing $f(g(x))$ by applying $f$ first. The inside function $g$ acts first.
  • Treating $-f(x+1)$ as $-f(x)+1$. The minus sign multiplies the whole output, but the $+1$ is inside and shifts horizontally.
