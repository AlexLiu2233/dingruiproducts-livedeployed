Polynomial
Functions多项式函数

Polynomials are the natural generalisation of the parabola you mastered in the Quadratic Functions guide. This unit names the parts (degree, leading coefficient, constant term), does the arithmetic (addition, multiplication, long and synthetic division), and then unlocks roots through the remainder theorem, the factor theorem, and the rational root theorem. From there we graph by zeros, multiplicity, and end behaviour, count turning points against the degree, and finish with complex conjugate roots and polynomial inequalities via sign analysis. The unit feeds into Rational and Radical Expressions, where polynomial factoring rebuilds the numerator and denominator, AP Calc Unit 2 (derivative of a polynomial), and IB Math AA HL B2 (polynomial functions with the full factor / remainder / sum-and-product machinery).多项式（polynomial）是你在二次函数单元掌握的抛物线的自然推广。本单元先命名各部分：次数（degree）、首项系数（leading coefficient）、常数项（constant term），再做运算（加、乘、长除法与综合除法），然后用余数定理（remainder theorem）、因式定理（factor theorem）和有理根定理（rational root theorem）打开求根。接下来按零点、重根（multiplicity）与末端行为（end behaviour）作图，按次数估拐点（turning point）数量，最后以复共轭根（complex conjugate root）和符号分析下的多项式不等式（polynomial inequality）收尾。本单元承接的下游课程包括：有理与根式表达式（多项式因式分解重建分子与分母）、AP Calculus Unit 2（多项式求导）和 IB Math AA HL B2（多项式函数，完整的因式 / 余数 / 根与系数机制）。

Learning objectives学习目标

  • Describe end behaviour in both directions from the degree and the sign of the leading coefficient.

  • State the maximum number of turning points from the degree.
  • 写出多项式的次数、首项系数与常数项。
  • 由次数与首项系数的符号描述两个方向的末端行为。
  • 不展开乘积，直接确定其次数与首项系数。
  • 由次数写出拐点数的上界。
A polynomial in $x$.$x$ 的多项式。 Finite sum of $a_{k} x^{k}$ with $k$ a non-negative integer:$a_{k} x^{k}$（$k$ 为非负整数）的有限和： $$ p(x) \;=\; a_{n} x^{n} + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + \cdots + a_{1} x + a_{0}, \qquad a_{n} \ne 0. $$
  • Degree次数 $n$ , largest exponent with non-zero coefficient (linear $1$, quadratic $2$, cubic $3$, quartic $4$, quintic $5$)., 非零系数对应的最高次（一次 $1$、二次 $2$、三次 $3$、四次 $4$、五次 $5$）。
  • Leading coefficient首项系数 $a_{n}$ , controls end behaviour., 决定末端行为。
  • Constant term常数项 $a_{0} = p(0)$ , the $y$-intercept., 即 $y$ 截距。
  • Monic首一 , leading coefficient $1$. Terms vs. factors: terms are added, factors are multiplied., 首项系数为 $1$。项与因式：项是相加的，因式是相乘的。
End behaviour.末端行为。 Only $a_{n} x^{n}$ matters as $x \to \pm \infty$. Four cases by parity of $n$ and sign of $a_{n}$: even-positive both ends to $+\infty$ (like $x^{2}$); even-negative both to $-\infty$; odd-positive left $\to -\infty$, right $\to +\infty$ (like $x^{3}$); odd-negative the reverse.当 $x \to \pm \infty$ 时只看 $a_{n} x^{n}$。按 $n$ 的奇偶和 $a_{n}$ 的符号分四种：偶 + 正两端均趋 $+\infty$（似 $x^{2}$）；偶 + 负两端均趋 $-\infty$；奇 + 正左 $\to -\infty$、右 $\to +\infty$（似 $x^{3}$）；奇 + 负相反。

That four-word list is the right vocabulary checklist for any BC PC12 polynomial question; the same vocabulary surfaces in MHF4U (Ontario) and in US Pre-Calc / AP Pre-Calc, often word-for-word.BC PC12 在多项式函数内容阐释中原文列出四项图象特征 , 次数、极值、零点、末端行为。这四个词是 BC PC12 任何多项式题的术语清单；同一组术语也常原文出现在 MHF4U（安大略）以及美国 Pre-Calc / AP Pre-Calc 中。
Worked Example 1: Identify the parts例题 1：辨识各部分

For $p(x) = -3 x^{4} + 5 x^{2} - 2 x + 7$, state the degree, leading coefficient, constant term, and end behaviour.对 $p(x) = -3 x^{4} + 5 x^{2} - 2 x + 7$，写出次数、首项系数、常数项及末端行为。

Identify.辨识。 $p(x) = -3 x^{4} + 0 \cdot x^{3} + 5 x^{2} - 2 x + 7$ (note the missing $x^{3}$ term).$p(x) = -3 x^{4} + 0 \cdot x^{3} + 5 x^{2} - 2 x + 7$（注意 $x^{3}$ 项缺失）。

Degree次数 $= 4$. Leading coefficient首项系数 $a_{4} = -3$. Constant term常数项 $a_{0} = 7 = p(0)$.

End behaviour.末端行为。 $n = 4$ even and $a_{n} = -3 < 0$: both ends fall, $p(x) \to -\infty$ as $x \to \pm \infty$.$n = 4$ 为偶，$a_{n} = -3 < 0$：两端均下降，$x \to \pm \infty$ 时 $p(x) \to -\infty$。

Evaluate.小结。 Quartic, opens "down on both ends," $y$-intercept $(0, 7)$, at most $3$ turning points (general: degree $n \Rightarrow$ at most $n - 1$ turning points). The next sections find where it crosses zero.四次多项式，"两端向下"，$y$ 截距 $(0, 7)$，至多 $3$ 个拐点（一般规律：次数 $n \Rightarrow$ 至多 $n - 1$ 个拐点）。后续章节将求其零点。

Worked Example 2: Degree, leading coefficient and end behaviour from different forms例题 2：由不同形式判断次数、首项系数与末端行为

For each polynomial, state the degree, leading coefficient, constant term, end behaviour and the maximum number of turning points: (a) $g(x) = 4 x^{2} + 2 x - x^{3} - 5$; (b) $h(x) = (x^{2} - 1)(2 x^{2} + 3)$.对每个多项式，写出次数、首项系数、常数项、末端行为及拐点数上界：(a) $g(x) = 4 x^{2} + 2 x - x^{3} - 5$；(b) $h(x) = (x^{2} - 1)(2 x^{2} + 3)$。

(a) Rewrite in descending order.(a) 按降幂重写。 $g(x) = -x^{3} + 4 x^{2} + 2 x - 5$. The highest power is $x^{3}$, so the degree is $3$, the leading coefficient is $-1$ and the constant term is $-5$.$g(x) = -x^{3} + 4 x^{2} + 2 x - 5$。最高次幂为 $x^{3}$，故次数为 $3$，首项系数为 $-1$，常数项为 $-5$。

Odd degree with a negative leading coefficient: as $x \to -\infty$, $g(x) \to +\infty$; as $x \to +\infty$, $g(x) \to -\infty$. At most $3 - 1 = 2$ turning points.奇次且首项系数为负：$x \to -\infty$ 时 $g(x) \to +\infty$；$x \to +\infty$ 时 $g(x) \to -\infty$。至多 $3 - 1 = 2$ 个拐点。

(b) Multiply the leading terms.(b) 把各因式的首项相乘。 The leading term of the product is $x^{2} \cdot 2 x^{2} = 2 x^{4}$, so the degree is $4$ and the leading coefficient is $2$. The constant term is $(-1)(3) = -3$.乘积的首项为 $x^{2} \cdot 2 x^{2} = 2 x^{4}$，故次数为 $4$，首项系数为 $2$。常数项为 $(-1)(3) = -3$。

Even degree with a positive leading coefficient: both ends rise, $h(x) \to +\infty$ as $x \to \pm \infty$. At most $3$ turning points.偶次且首项系数为正：两端上升，$x \to \pm \infty$ 时 $h(x) \to +\infty$。至多 $3$ 个拐点。

Evaluate.复核。 Expanding (b) gives $2 x^{4} + x^{2} - 3$: degree $4$, leading coefficient $2$, constant term $-3$. The shortcut and the expansion agree.展开 (b) 得 $2 x^{4} + x^{2} - 3$：次数 $4$，首项系数 $2$，常数项 $-3$。捷径与展开结果一致。

Going deeper · Why only the leading term decides end behaviour深入 · 为何只有首项决定末端行为

Factor $a_{n} x^{n}$ out: $p(x) = a_{n} x^{n} \left( 1 + \frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n} x} + \cdots + \frac{a_{0}}{a_{n} x^{n}} \right)$. As $|x| \to \infty$, every term in the bracket except the constant $1$ heads to $0$, so the bracket approaches $1$ and $p(x)$ behaves like $a_{n} x^{n}$ for large $|x|$. That is the whole "only the leading term matters" rule: lower-order terms are eventually negligible. The same argument explains why polynomials of different degree must diverge from each other , the algebraic content behind HSF-LE.A.3's "exponential eventually beats polynomial" claim, applied within the polynomial family itself.把 $a_{n} x^{n}$ 提出：$p(x) = a_{n} x^{n} \left( 1 + \frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n} x} + \cdots + \frac{a_{0}}{a_{n} x^{n}} \right)$。当 $|x| \to \infty$ 时，括号内除常数 $1$ 外每一项均趋 $0$，故括号趋 $1$，$|x|$ 大时 $p(x)$ 表现如 $a_{n} x^{n}$。这就是"只看首项"的全部含义：低次项最终可忽略。同样的论证解释了不同次数的多项式必然彼此发散 , 这就是 HSF-LE.A.3"指数终将超越多项式"在多项式族内部的代数版本。

Section 2 · Arithmetic and division第 2 节 · 运算与除法

Polynomial Arithmetic and Division多项式运算与除法

Learning objectives学习目标
  • Add, subtract and multiply polynomials and state the degree of the result.
  • Divide by a polynomial of any degree using long division, inserting zero coefficients for missing powers.
  • Divide by a linear factor $(x - a)$ using synthetic division.
  • Write the result as $p(x) = d(x) q(x) + r(x)$ and check it.
  • 对多项式做加、减、乘运算并写出结果的次数。
  • 用长除法除以任意次数的多项式，缺项补零系数。
  • 用综合除法除以线性因式 $(x - a)$。
  • 把结果写成 $p(x) = d(x) q(x) + r(x)$ 并检验。
Addition and multiplication.加法与乘法。 Add by combining like terms (same exponent). Multiply by the distributive law term-by-term; the degree of a product is the sum of the degrees.相加合并同类项（同指数）；按分配律逐项相乘；乘积的次数等于各因式次数之和。

Long division.长除法。 To divide $p(x)$ by $d(x)$:用 $d(x)$ 除 $p(x)$：
  1. Arrange both in descending order, including a $0$ coefficient for any missing power.两者均按降幂排列，缺项用 $0$ 系数补齐。
  2. Divide the leading term of $p$ by the leading term of $d$ to get the next term of the quotient.$p$ 的首项除以 $d$ 的首项，得到商的下一项。
  3. Multiply $d$ by that term and subtract from $p$.用该项乘 $d$，从 $p$ 中减去。
  4. Repeat with the new "remainder so far" until its degree is less than $\deg d$.对新的"当前余数"重复以上步骤，直到其次数小于 $\deg d$。
The result is $p(x) = d(x) \cdot q(x) + r(x)$ with $\deg r < \deg d$.最终 $p(x) = d(x) \cdot q(x) + r(x)$，且 $\deg r < \deg d$。

Synthetic division.综合除法。 A streamlined shortcut for dividing by a linear factor $(x - a)$:除以线性因式 $(x - a)$ 的速记法：
  1. Write the coefficients of $p$ in descending order (use $0$ for missing powers).按降幂写出 $p$ 的系数（缺项补 $0$）。
  2. Put $a$ on the left.把 $a$ 放在左侧。
  3. Bring down the first coefficient. Multiply by $a$ and add to the next coefficient. Repeat.把首系数下移；乘 $a$ 加到下一系数；如此循环。
  4. The final number is $p(a)$ , the remainder. The other numbers, in order, are the coefficients of the quotient (degree one less than $p$).最后一个数即 $p(a)$ , 余数；其余数字按序为商的系数（次数比 $p$ 低 $1$）。
Synthetic division is exactly long division by $(x - a)$, rewritten as a number-crunching procedure.综合除法本质上就是除以 $(x - a)$ 的长除法，写成纯数字流程而已。
Worked Example 3: Long division of $p(x) = 2 x^{3} - 5 x^{2} + 3 x - 7$ by $d(x) = x - 2$例题 3：用 $d(x) = x - 2$ 长除 $p(x) = 2 x^{3} - 5 x^{2} + 3 x - 7$

Find the quotient $q(x)$ and the remainder $r$.求商 $q(x)$ 与余数 $r$。

Set up.列式。 Coefficients of $p$: $2, -5, 3, -7$.$p$ 的系数：$2, -5, 3, -7$。

Step 1.第 1 步。 $2 x^{3} \div x = 2 x^{2}$; multiply by $d$: $2 x^{3} - 4 x^{2}$; subtract: $-x^{2}$. Bring down: dividend $-x^{2} + 3 x - 7$.$2 x^{3} \div x = 2 x^{2}$；乘 $d$ 得 $2 x^{3} - 4 x^{2}$；相减得 $-x^{2}$。下移：被除式变为 $-x^{2} + 3 x - 7$。

Step 2.第 2 步。 $-x^{2} \div x = -x$; multiply: $-x^{2} + 2 x$; subtract: $x$. Bring down: dividend $x - 7$.$-x^{2} \div x = -x$；乘得 $-x^{2} + 2 x$；相减得 $x$。下移：被除式变为 $x - 7$。

Step 3.第 3 步。 $x \div x = 1$; multiply: $x - 2$; subtract: $-5$. Degree $0 < \deg d$, stop.$x \div x = 1$；乘得 $x - 2$；相减得 $-5$。次数 $0 < \deg d$，停止。

Read off.读出结果。 $q(x) = 2 x^{2} - x + 1$, $r = -5$, so $2 x^{3} - 5 x^{2} + 3 x - 7 = (x - 2)(2 x^{2} - x + 1) - 5$. Sanity check at $x = 2$: left $= 16 - 20 + 6 - 7 = -5$, matching $r$ , previewing the remainder theorem in §3.$q(x) = 2 x^{2} - x + 1$、$r = -5$，故 $2 x^{3} - 5 x^{2} + 3 x - 7 = (x - 2)(2 x^{2} - x + 1) - 5$。$x = 2$ 处核验：左 $= 16 - 20 + 6 - 7 = -5$，与 $r$ 一致 , 这就是 §3 余数定理的预演。

Worked Example 4: Long division by a quadratic with a missing term例题 4：用二次式长除并处理缺项

Divide $p(x) = x^{4} - 3 x^{2} + 5 x - 2$ by $d(x) = x^{2} + x - 1$. State the quotient and the remainder.用 $d(x) = x^{2} + x - 1$ 除 $p(x) = x^{4} - 3 x^{2} + 5 x - 2$，写出商与余式。

Set up.列式。 The $x^{3}$ term is missing, so write $p(x) = x^{4} + 0 x^{3} - 3 x^{2} + 5 x - 2$.缺 $x^{3}$ 项，故写成 $p(x) = x^{4} + 0 x^{3} - 3 x^{2} + 5 x - 2$。

Step 1.第 1 步。 $x^{4} \div x^{2} = x^{2}$. Multiply: $x^{2}(x^{2} + x - 1) = x^{4} + x^{3} - x^{2}$. Subtract: $-x^{3} - 2 x^{2} + 5 x - 2$.$x^{4} \div x^{2} = x^{2}$。相乘：$x^{2}(x^{2} + x - 1) = x^{4} + x^{3} - x^{2}$。相减：$-x^{3} - 2 x^{2} + 5 x - 2$。

Going deeper · Why the rational root theorem is true深入 · 有理根定理为何成立

If $p(x) = a_{n} x^{n} + \cdots + a_{0}$ has integer coefficients and rational root $p/q$ in lowest terms, then $p(p/q) = 0$. Multiply through by $q^{n}$ to clear denominators:若 $p(x) = a_{n} x^{n} + \cdots + a_{0}$ 系数为整且最简有理根 $p/q$，则 $p(p/q) = 0$。两边乘 $q^{n}$ 清分母：

$$ a_{n} p^{n} + a_{n-1} p^{n-1} q + \cdots + a_{1} p q^{n-1} + a_{0} q^{n} \;=\; 0. $$

Every term except the last is divisible by $p$, so $p \mid a_{0} q^{n}$; since $\gcd(p, q) = 1$, this forces $p \mid a_{0}$. Symmetrically, every term except the first is divisible by $q$, so $q \mid a_{n} p^{n}$ and $q \mid a_{n}$. That is the theorem. Without integer coefficients the result fails; if the polynomial has rational coefficients, clear denominators first.除最后一项外每项均含 $p$，故 $p \mid a_{0} q^{n}$；又 $\gcd(p, q) = 1$，故 $p \mid a_{0}$。对称地，除第一项外每项均含 $q$，故 $q \mid a_{n} p^{n}$，即 $q \mid a_{n}$。定理得证。若系数非整数则结论失败；若为有理数，先清分母即可。

Going deeper · Why the conjugate root theorem is true深入 · 共轭根定理为何成立

Complex conjugation satisfies $\overline{z + w} = \bar{z} + \bar{w}$ and $\overline{z w} = \bar{z} \cdot \bar{w}$. If $p(x) = a_{n} x^{n} + \cdots + a_{0}$ has real coefficients (so $\bar{a}_{k} = a_{k}$) and $p(z) = 0$, apply conjugation to both sides: $\overline{p(z)} = a_{n} \bar{z}^{n} + \cdots + a_{0} = p(\bar{z})$, while the left side is $\overline{0} = 0$. So $p(\bar{z}) = 0$, i.e. $\bar{z}$ is also a root. The proof fails for non-real coefficients (then $\bar{a}_{k} \ne a_{k}$) , the conjugate-pair statement is specifically about real polynomials.复共轭满足 $\overline{z + w} = \bar{z} + \bar{w}$ 与 $\overline{z w} = \bar{z} \cdot \bar{w}$。若 $p(x) = a_{n} x^{n} + \cdots + a_{0}$ 系数为实（故 $\bar{a}_{k} = a_{k}$）且 $p(z) = 0$，两边取共轭：$\overline{p(z)} = a_{n} \bar{z}^{n} + \cdots + a_{0} = p(\bar{z})$，而左边 $= \overline{0} = 0$。故 $p(\bar{z}) = 0$，即 $\bar{z}$ 也是根。系数非实时（$\bar{a}_{k} \ne a_{k}$）证明失败 , 共轭对结论专属实多项式。

Vocabulary and end behaviour术语与末端行为

Common Questions常见问题

What is the factor theorem?什么是因式定理？

The factor theorem says that (x - c) is a factor of a polynomial exactly when substituting x = c makes the polynomial equal zero, so c is a root.因式定理指出，当代入 x = c 使多项式等于零时，(x - c) 恰好是该多项式的一个因式，因此 c 是一个根。

How do I find the rational roots of a polynomial?如何求多项式的有理根？

The rational root theorem lists candidate roots as factors of the constant term over factors of the leading coefficient; test each with synthetic or direct substitution.有理根定理把候选根列为常数项的因数除以首项系数的因数，再用综合除法或直接代入逐一检验。

How does the degree affect the end behavior of a polynomial graph?次数如何影响多项式图像的末端走向？

The leading term sets the end behavior: even degree sends both ends the same direction, odd degree sends them opposite ways, and the leading sign decides up or down.首项决定末端走向：偶次时两端走向相同，奇次时两端走向相反，首项系数的正负决定向上还是向下。
